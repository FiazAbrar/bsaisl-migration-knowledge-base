# Going abroad from Bangladesh: open questions

Questions this doc can't yet answer, grouped by part. Each is a "Need to know" in `doc.md`.

- Does the Ministry publish one current list of all agencies approved to send women domestic workers?
- Do customs officers still apply USD 5,000, the figure on Bangladesh Customs' own page?
- Did labour migration from Bangladesh to the UAE stop in 2024, as RMMRU (the Refugee and Migratory Movements Research Unit) reported, or did about 47,000 workers go that year, as Prothom Alo reported in 2026?
- Was the amendment to the Overseas Employment and Migrants Act that brought sub-agents (registered dalals) under the law passed in 2023 or in 2024?
  - Can a worker search RAIMS (the Recruitment Agents Information System, BMET's agency database) to check an agency or sub-agent?
- Has the grading system for recruiting agencies that the State Minister for expatriates' welfare announced in May 2026 been introduced, and where are the grades published?
- Does Probashi Kallyan Bank (PKB, the government bank for migrant workers) ask for two guarantors or one for a migration loan in 2026?

## Dalal scams: the usual tricks and how to spot them

- Has BMET registered any sub-agents yet, and where exactly is the public list (which page of bmet.gov.bd or OEP)?

- What does a village dalal keep per worker on a typical Saudi or Malaysia visa in 2026, and what does the sub-agent above him keep?
- What is the official total for the Gulf (Wafid) medical in Bangladesh in 2026, and where is it published?
- What monthly interest do village moneylenders charge migrants in 2026, and is land usually mortgaged by a written deed or informally?
- Are forged nikahnamas (Muslim marriage contracts) used for Gulf or Italian family visas, and how does a family check one against the kazi's register?
- What does a Serbia, Croatia or Malta work permit sell for in Dhaka in 2026?
- Did Croatia suspend work visas for Bangladeshis, and is it still suspended?
- Are migrants or their families asked to lend their bank or bKash accounts to move other people's money, and has anyone been charged for it?
- Which CID or police anti-trafficking unit phone number takes complaints directly from families, and does it answer?
- Does BRAC's Migration Welfare Centre take walk-in fraud cases, and at which addresses?
- RMMRU's programme director said in April 2026 that about 31% of people who go abroad are cheated, and its executive director said in July 2026 that about 51% are: which RMMRU studies give these figures, from which year and sample, and which is the current one?
- In which unions does the information pilot run through Union Digital Centres (UDCs, the government's local service centres), funded by Italy and backed by IOM, and what can a would-be migrant get there?
- Counts of licensed recruiting agencies in 2026 range from about 2,200 to about 2,900: how many hold a valid licence now?
  - On which BMET page is the current list?
- Has the direct recruitment of 6,000 drivers for Dubai, which the minister for expatriates' welfare announced in August 2026, started?
  - Where does a driver apply?
- Is section 35 of the Overseas Employment and Migrants Act 2013, which punishes acting as an unregistered sub-agent, enforced against dalals in 2026, and has anyone been convicted under it? (Speakers in 2026 say there is no law for intermediaries, though a 2023 amendment and 2025 rules provide for sub-agent registration.)
- How does a worker whose passport was seized from an agency in a BMET raid (such as the September 2026 raid on Triple A Overseas) get it back from BMET's custody, and at which office?
- Does BOESL (Bangladesh Overseas Employment and Services Limited, the government's recruiting company) charge a candidate anything after final selection, paid by pay order, or nothing at all, as the Special Response Battalion (SRB) said in October 2026?
- Were the 9 October 2026 raids on job-abroad fraud rings at Technical Mor, Dhaka (12 arrested) and in Shyamoli, Dhaka (20 arrested) the same raid, and which arrest count is right?
- Has BMET carried out the recommendations of its inquiry into fake clearances made through its app (among them OTP, session time-out, an IT audit and testing by the Bangladesh Computer Council, BCC)?
  - Has BMET's server moved to the BCC?
- Did the ACC file its case over forged emigration clearances for women in June 2025 or on 3 July 2025, and is it one case or two?
- How can a worker check, before paying, whether the labour wing of the Bangladesh embassy in the destination country attested the demand letter behind his visa?
  - How can he see which recruiting agency it was attested for?
- The State Minister for expatriates' welfare said in May 2026 that agreements with some countries now give migrants legal remedy there: which countries' agreements let a worker file a case and recover compensation in that country?
  - How does a worker start such a case?
- How does a cheated worker bring a complaint to the union or upazila complaint settlement committee?
  - Which of these committees actually meet?
- Does WARBE Development Foundation (a migrant workers' rights organisation) mediate refunds for people who walk in with a complaint, and at which offices?
- What phone number does BNWLA (the Bangladesh National Women Lawyers' Association) give returnees who need a lawyer, and does it take cases outside Cox's Bazar?
