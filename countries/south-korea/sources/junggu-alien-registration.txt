Alien Registration

A foreigner who intends to stay in the Republic of Korea for a period of time exceeding 90 days shall be issued an alien registration card. A foreigner who has completed alien registration becomes entitled to health insurance benefits, and his/her children may enter a Korean or international school.

Foreigners shall carry their alien registration card at all times.

Applicable persons

Foreigners who intend to stay in the Republic of Korea for 91 days or longer

Those who have lost their Korean nationality and have obtained a foreign nationality

Foreigners born in the Republic of Korea who intend to stay in Korea for more than 91 days from the date on which they obtained a status of stay

※ The old alien registration card issued according to previous regulations is valid until reissued

Registration period

Foreigners who intend to stay in the Republic of Korea for 91 days or more : Within 90 days from the date of entry

Foreigners who have been granted a status of stay or obtained permission to change the status of stay : Immediately after the applicant obtains permission

Registration procedure and receiving your Korean Residence Card

The applicant for Korean Residence Card must submit the following documents to the immigration office, branch office, immigration service center, or Multicultural Migrant Plus Center that has jurisdiction over his/her place of residence.

APPLICATION FORM (REPORT FORM) FOREIGN RESIDENT REGISTRATION, passport, one color photo (3.5cm×4.5cm), required documents according to visa status, and a fee of 35,000 KRW (effective January 1, 2025).

The validity period of the Korean Residence Card is until the permitted period of stay (however, in the case of a Permanent Residence Card, until the expiration date of the card) and it must be renewed or updated before the period of stay expires.

You may receive an alien registration card in person at the immigration office or branch office where you filed the application, or by mail. It will take about four weeks to receive your card.

※ You will need to make a reservation in advance via the Hi Korea website (www.hikorea.go.kr) before you visit the immigration office. Documents for alien registration can be checked through the Hi Korea website or the Immigration Contact Center (1345 Call Center)
