[ Subscribing to Four Major Social Insurance Programs ]

 This content provides legal information on subscription to the four major social
 insurances (i.e., national pension, national health insurance, employment insurance,
 and industrial accident compensation insurance) for foreign workers.

National pension

What is "national pension"?

- "National pension" is a system designed to contribute to the stability of people's
    lives and the promotion of welfare by having individuals steadily pay insurance
    contributions while they have income, and then providing pension payments from
    the state when there is no income due to old age, disability, or death (Article 1
    of National Pension Act).

benefits, and the types of benefits under National Pension Act are as follows

   National pension eligibility for foreign nationals as workplace subscribers

- Foreign nationals residing in the Republic of Korea may enroll for national
    pension on equal terms with Korean nationals, and foreign nationals aged 18 or
    older and under 60 who work at a workplace that participates in national pension
    become workplace subscribers (Articles 6 and 126(1) of National Pension Act).

-  However,  any       of  the          following  are  excluded                 from

   eligibility [National Pension Service website (https://www.nps.or.kr)].

                 National pension enrollment method for foreign nationals as workplace
      subscribers
      - When a foreign national acquires the status of a workplace subscriber, the

           employer must file a workplace subscriber qualification acquisition report with
           the National Pension Service (Subparagraph 1 of Article 54 of Enforcement
           Rule of the National Pension Act).

               National pension contributions and payment

      - For national pension premiums, the workplace subscriber pays a contribution equal
           to 65/1,000 of the standard monthly income, and the employer pays a burden equal
           to 65/1,000 of the standard monthly income (Article 88(3) of National Pension
           Act).

      - The employer must deduct the contribution portion borne by the workplace
           subscriber from the monthly wages payable to that person and pay it, and if part
           of the insurance premium is subsidized by the state, the employer must deduct and
           pay only the remaining amount after subtracting the subsidized amount (Article
           90(1) of National Pension Act).
           National health insurance

               What is "national health insurance"?

      - "National health insurance" is a system intended to contribute to the improvement
           of public health and promote social security by providing insurance benefits for
           the prevention, diagnosis, treatment, and rehabilitation of disease and injury,
           for childbirth and death, and for health promotion (Article 1 of National
           Health Insurance Act).
               National health insurance types and details

         National health insurance eligibility for foreign nationals as the employee

insured

- A foreign national staying in Korea becomes an employee insured if they are a
    worker, public official, or teaching staff member at an applicable workplace and
    are registered under Subparagraph 3 of Article 6(1) of Resident Registration
    Act, filed a domestic residence report under Article 6 of Act on the
    Immigration and Legal Status of Overseas Koreans, or registered as a foreigner
    under Article 31 of Immigration Act (Article 109(2) of National Health
    Insurance Act).

- However, any of the following are excluded from eligibility (Article 6(2) of 
    National Health Insurance Act; Article 9 of Enforcement Decree of the
    National Health Insurance Act).

                National health insurance enrollment method for foreign nationals as the
      employee insured
      - When a foreign national staying in Korea becomes an employee insured, the employer

           must submit a health insurance employee-insured qualification acquisition report
           with the required documents to the National Health Insurance Service within 14
           days from the date on which the person becomes the as the employee insured
           (Article 61(1) of Enforcement Rule of the National Health Insurance Act).

               National health insurance premiums and payment

      - The monthly remuneration-based insurance premium of an employee insured (i.e.,
           workers, public officials, and private-school teaching staff) is borne equally by
           the employee insured and the employer (i.e., business owner, state or local
           government, and private school founder/operator) at fifty (50) percent each of
           the premium amount (main sentence of Article 76(1) of National Health
           Insurance Act).
         · However, if the employee insured is teaching staff working at a private school,
             the employee insured bears fifty (50) percent, the private school
             founder/operator bears thirty (30) percent, and the state bears twenty (20)
             percent of the premium amount (proviso to Article 76(1) of National Health
             Insurance Act).

      - The employer must deduct from remuneration the monthly premium amount to be borne
           by the employee insured and pay it (former part of Article 77(3) of National
           Health Insurance Act).

What is "employment insurance"?

- "Employment insurance" is a system designed to contribute to economic and social
    development by preventing unemployment, promoting employment, developing and
    improving the vocational skills of employees, enhancing the state's functions of
    vocational guidance and job placement, and providing benefits necessary for
    livelihood when workers become unemployed so as to stabilize their lives and
    encourage job-seeking activities (Article 1 of Employment Insurance Act).

Employment insurance enrollment method for foreign workers

- When an employer or subcontractor employs foreign workers, or when a foreign
    national seeks to enroll in employment insurance, the employer must submit a
    foreign national employment insurance enrollment application to the Korea Workers'
    Compensation & Welfare Service (main sentence of Article 2(1) of Enforcement
    Rule of the Employment Insurance Act).

        Employment insurance premiums and payment

- For employment insurance premiums, in general, the unemployment-benefit portion is
    shared equally by the worker and the employer, each bearing an amount calculated
    by multiplying the total remuneration by one-half of the unemployment-benefit
    premium rate, and the full amount of the premium for employment stabilization and
    vocational skill development is borne by the employer (main sentence of Article
    13(2) and Article 13(4) of Act on the Collection of Insurance Premiums for
    Employment Insurance and Industrial Accident Compensation Insurance, etc.).

- The employer may withhold from the worker's remuneration an amount equivalent to
    the worker's share of the employment insurance premium and pay it (Article 16(1)
    of Act on the Collection of Insurance Premiums for Employment Insurance and
    Industrial Accident Compensation Insurance, etc.).

    Industrial accident compensation insurance

What is "industrial accident compensation insurance"?

- "Industrial accident compensation insurance" is a system intended to contribute
    to the protection of workers by promptly and fairly compensating for occupational
    accidents suffered by workers; establishing and operating necessary insurance
    facilities to promote the rehabilitation and social reintegration of injured
    workers; and implementing projects for accident prevention and other projects to
    promote workers' welfare (Article 1 of Industrial Accident Compensation
    Insurance Act).

        Industrial accident compensation insurance eligibility for foreign workers

- All workers, including foreign workers, are covered by industrial accident
    compensation insurance regardless of nationality or status of stay (main sentence
    of Article 6 of Industrial Accident Compensation Insurance Act).

- However, taking into account risk level, scale, and location, any business falling
    under one of the following is excluded from coverage (proviso to Article 6 of 
    Industrial Accident Compensation Insurance Act; Article 2(1) of Enforcement
    Decree of the Industrial Accident Compensation Insurance Act).

        Businesses excluded from industrial accident compensation insurance coverage

       Businesses where accident compensation is provided under other laws

 Employment within a household

       Agriculture, forestry (excluding logging), fishery, and hunting businesses
         that are not corporations and have fewer than five regular workers

workers
- An employer who wishes to enroll in industrial accident compensation insurance
