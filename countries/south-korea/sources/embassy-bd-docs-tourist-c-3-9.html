      Documents for Tourist Visa(C-3-9) Application

      DATE
      2024-09-22
      FILE
      • (new)VISA APPLICATION FORM(사증신청서).pdf

      All visa applicants are requested to fill up the [e-Form(Visa)] at the [Korea Visa Portal] website from 4 May 2025. After filling up & print the application form with bar code, the applicant should visit to the Consular section of Embassy with other documents required.



      e-Form (Visa)

      What is 'e-form' (visa application)?

      • An applicant can fill out an electronic visa application form online and submit it to the Visa Portal before he/she visits a Korean diplomatic mission in his/her country of residence.

      • In order to complete the application process, you must print the e-form (with a bar code) that you filled out on the Visa Portal (with other required documents) and visit the Korean embassy in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

      Application Procedure





      Required Documents for Your e-Form

      Scanned image files of passport pages, digital photograph (in natural color, size: 3.5cm x 4.5cm)

      Required Document Before Visiting the Diplomatic Mission

      Visa application form with a bar code, passport, photograph(natural color, 3.5cm x 4.5cm), visa fee and required documents

       e-Form(visa) applcation : https://www.visa.go.kr/openPage.do?MENU_ID=10204 (Select language to English)


      ▶ Documents for Tourist Visa(C-3-9) Application


      ※ Applicants must visit in person to submit visa application.

       

      General Requirement

       

      1. Visa Application Form (Attached) <- Please use the attached visa application form.

          ※ Please fill out the form faithfully so that the contents of each form are not omitted.

      2. Passport Sized 1 Color Photo of Applicant (Taken Within Last 6 Months)

      3. Passport with Minimum Validity of 6 Months.

      4. Photocopy of Valid Passport

      5. Valid Air-ticket Booking Slip

      6. Cover or Forwarding Letter of Applicant

       

      For Tourist Visa(C-3-9) Application

       

      1. Tour itinerary (Detail tour plan in Korea)

      2. Accommodation Reservation slip

      3. Personal bank statement and Bank Solvency Certificate (Last Minimum 6 months)

      4. Applicant’s Company Certificate of corporate or Trade License, if available (copy of initial and current one)

      5. Applicant’s Income Tax Certificate Tax-challan (ট্যাক্স-চালান) copy and Tax Return Acknowledegement Receipt

      6. Employment Certificate issued from the Applicant’s company, if available, Office ID Card Copy

        ※ Recommendation Letter issued by the Institute, if the applicant is a student

      7. Invitation letter & Affidavit of support notarized, if available


       

       

      ▶ Fee (Fees are non-refundable for cases in which visa processing results are not permitted, cancellation of the applicant, or issuance of a single-entry visa after applying for a multiple-entry visa.)


      - Less than 90 days(Single) : $40

      - Double : $70

      - Multiple : $90
