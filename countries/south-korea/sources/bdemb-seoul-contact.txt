Direction

Subway:
Get off at the ITAEWON Station, Line No.-6, Exit-4. You can rent a Taxi and the price would be 4,000won. Or, walk to the Chungwha Apartment. Enter the Chungwha Apartment. Cross through the Apartment. Turn right and see Hotel Mondrian Seoul Itaewon. Opposite side of Hotel Mondrian Seoul Itaewon, you will see Pumyang Building. Beside that you get to the Destination.

By Bus:
Get off the bus stop Yongsan District Office(용산구청)/Mondrian Seoul Itaewon Hotel. Bus No. 100, 143, 401, 406, 740. Then walk to Embassy about 300 meter following the map.
