Embassy of the Republic of Korea in People's Republic of Bangladesh

			(From 1 Jul, 2025) Adjustment of the exchange rate standard for consular service fees(1USD =125BDT)

DATE

2025-06-24

In consideration of exchange rate fluctuations, 
the exchange rate standard of consular service fees will be changed as follows.
- Exchange rate standard : USD 1 = BDT 125
- Effective date : 1 Jul, 2025
