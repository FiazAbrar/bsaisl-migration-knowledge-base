Notice to Visa Applicants
The Embassy of the Republic of Korea would like to remind all visa
applicants that the visa application process(except visa fees) does not require
any financial transactions. 
The Embassy of the Republic of Korea or any Korean
person will never ask for money to be wired or transferred for any visa-related
purposes.
Please be cautious of any individuals or entities that claim to be
affiliated with the Embassy of the Republic of Korea and request financial
transactions in exchange for visa services or assistance. These are likely to
be fraudulent schemes designed to take advantage of unsuspecting applicants.
If you encounter any suspicious activity or individuals, please
report it to the Embassy of the Republic of Korea immediately. We are committed
to providing a safe and secure visa application process for all applicants.
TEL
: +88-02-5881-2088-90, 
Email : consuldhaka@mofa.go.kr
Thank you.
