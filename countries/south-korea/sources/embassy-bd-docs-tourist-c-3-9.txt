e-Form (Visa)
What is 'e-form' (visa application)?
An applicant can fill out an electronic visa application form online and submit it to the Visa Portal before he/she visits a Korean diplomatic mission in his/her country of residence.
In order to complete the application process, you must print the e-form (with a bar code) that you filled out on the Visa Portal (with other required documents) and
 visit the Korean embassy
 in Dhaka, Bangladesh.
Application Procedure
Required Documents for Your e-Form
Scanned image files of passport pages, digital photograph (in natural color, size: 3.5cm x 4.5cm)
Required Document Before Visiting the Diplomatic Mission
Visa application form with a bar code, passport, photograph(natural color, 3.5cm x 4.5cm), visa fee and re
quired documents

Documents for Tourist Visa(C-3-9) Application
※ Applicants must visit in person to submit visa application.

General Requirement

1. Visa Application Form (Attached) 
<- Please use the attached visa application form.
    ※ Please fill out the form faithfully so that the contents of each form are not omitted.
2. Passport Sized 1 Color Photo of Applicant (Taken Within Last 6 Months)
3. Passport with Minimum Validity of 6 Months.
4. Photocopy of Valid Passport
5. Valid Air-ticket Booking Slip
6. Cover or Forwarding Letter of Applicant

For Tourist Visa(C-3-9) Application

1. Tour itinerary (Detail tour plan in Korea)
2. Accommodation Reservation slip
3. Personal bank statement and Bank Solvency Certificate (Last Minimum 6 months)
4. Applicant’s Company Certificate of corporate or Trade License, if available (copy of initial and current one)
5. 
Applicant’s Income Tax Certificate Tax-challan 
(
ট্যাক্স-চালান) 
copy and Tax Return Acknowledegement Receipt
6. Employment Certificate issued from the Applicant’s company, if available, 
Office ID Card Copy
  ※

Recommendation Letter issued by the Institute, if the applicant is a student
7. Invitation letter & Affidavit of support notarized, if available

Fee

(Fees are non-refundable for cases in which visa processing results are not permitted, cancellation of the applicant, or issuance of a single-entry visa after applying for a multiple-entry visa.)

- Double : $70
- Multiple : $90
