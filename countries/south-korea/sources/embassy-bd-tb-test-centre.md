## Notice on change to designated tuberculosis test center

DATE  
2023-10-26

□ Notice on change to designated tuberculosis test center

\

1. Applicable to : Citizens of countries with high risk of tuberculosis (including Bangladesh)

  - When applying for a long-term stay visa for the purpose of staying for more than 90 days

  - When applying for a seasonal worker(C-4-1~4) or a caregiver for a tuberculosis patient,\

     A tuberculosis test results must be submitted

\

  ※ Exemptions : 0~5 year old children, pregnant women, A-1, A-2, A-3 visa applicants

\

2. Information on designated TB test center

[TABLE]

\

  ※ Prescription point, which was previously a designated TB test center, had its designation canceled. ▧
