[Notice] Granting Visa Status to Foreign Children to Ensure Educational Access

The Ministry of Justice has launched an initiative to support foreign children who have been residing in Korea 

for extended periods and attending school without authorized visa status. 

To uphold their right to education, the Ministry has decided to extend the initiative until March 31, 2028.

Under this initiative, children who meet the eligibility requirements will either be granted a visa or allowed 

to change their current visa status.

Please refer to the attached document for detailed information on eligibility criteria, 

as well as measures concerning the children's parents and underage siblings.

The document is available in six languages: Korean, English, Chinese, Vietnamese, Mongolian, and Russian.

* 
Please refer to the attached application form. Additional documents may be required. 

Please visit the immigration office responsible for your place of residence 

and inquire about the necessary documentation.
