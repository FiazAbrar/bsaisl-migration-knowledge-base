## [**Embassy of the Republic of Korea in People's Republic of Bangladesh**](/bd-en/index.do)

# 

## Notice on Passport Collection for Visa Applications Submitted at the Embassy

DATE  
2026-09-01

## Notice on Passport Collection

\

This notice applies only to applicants who submitted their visa applications directly at the Embassy of the Republic of Korea in Bangladesh by September 1, 2026, prior to the opening of the Korea Visa Application Center.

\

Applicants whose visa issuance has been confirmed may collect their passports directly from the Embassy without waiting for two working days after the visa decision.

\

Please bring the original receipt when collecting your passport.

\

### Collection Hours

- ○11:00 AM – 12:00 PM\

- ○2:00 PM – 3:30 PM

\*Available on any working day.

\

Note: This notice does not apply to applications submitted through the Korea Visa Application Center.
