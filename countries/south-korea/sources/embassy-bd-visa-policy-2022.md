## Changes to visa issuance policy (beginning on June 1st, 2022)

DATE  
2022-05-31

From June 1st, the Visa  Issuance Policy of Republic of Korea will be changed as follows.

\

1. Resume short-term visa issuance such as Tourist Visa(C-3-9)

​    For the documents : [https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/bd-en/brd/m_23302/view.do?seq=1&page=1](https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/bd-en/brd/m_23302/view.do?seq=1&page=1)\

\

2. Remove "Consent for Isolation" document from all visa applications.

\

3. Multiple-entry short-term visas such as C-3 that were suspended from April 13th, 2020 due to COVID-19, they can be used for entry to Korea as long as they are still valid. 

​

For the application of Tourist visa, All applicants are requested to send a e-mail([consuldhaka@mofa.go.kr](mailto:consuldhaka@mofa.go.kr)) in advance for visiting date. [\
](mailto:consuldhaka@mofa.go.kr)
