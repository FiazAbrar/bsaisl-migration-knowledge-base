- (Application for Industrial Accident Compensation) If a worker needs at least four days of medical care due to a work-
    related accident, they must apply for medical care benefits with the Korea Workers' Compensation & Welfare Service
    (Article 40 (1) and (3) and Article 41 (1) of ãInOEdustrial Accident Compensation Insurance Actã).·

- (Recognition of an Industrial Accident) If the accident is determined to be work-related and medical care benefits are
    approved, the worker will be entitled to industrial accident compensation (Articles 20 and 21 of ãEOEnforcement Rule
    of the Industrial Accident Compensation Insurance Actã).·

          Q. If I am injured in a traffic accident while commuting to work, am I still entitled to receive industrial accident

               compensation?
          A. Yes, if the traffic accident that occurred during your commute is accepted as a "commuting accident," you are entitled

               to receive industrial accident compensation.
                 For an accident that occurs while commuting to be recognized as a work-related injury, it must fall under one of the
                 following cases (Article 37 (1) 3 of ãInOEdustrial Accident Compensation Insurance Actã).·

          1. An accident that occurs while commuting under the control and supervision of the employer, such as using
               transportation provided by the employer or equivalent means
