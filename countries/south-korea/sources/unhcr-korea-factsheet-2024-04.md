## FACT SHEET

# Republic of Korea

#### April 2024

Since 1994, 4,096 persons have been recognized as refugees or granted humanitarian status holders by the Government of the Republic of Korea.

### Main activities

#### Support to RoK's national asylum system

■ RoK acceded to the 1951 Convention and its 1967 Protocol in 1992 and began registering asylum-seekers in 1994. The Government's first recognition of refugee status came in 2001. The Refugee Act, its Presidential Decree and Enforcement Regulations came into force on 1 July 2013. It is the first stand-alone comprehensive refugee law in the region and represents a significant enhancement of refugee protection.

■ UNHCR works alongside various CSOs to improve the quality of services provided to the people we serve. These will include offering consultations in legal aid, accommodation, employment as well as social integration and empowerment.
