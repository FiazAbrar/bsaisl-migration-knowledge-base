                        visa process or favourable decision-making.

Photograph (4 copies) 

Description: 

                            Support your application with high-quality personal
                            photographs.

                            Want to ensure that your photos meet the required
                            standards, or forget to bring an image of your own?
                            Not to worry!

                            Our friendly staff available to photograph you
                            during your visit to the Visa Application Centre -
                            ensuring that you have a photograph that meets the
                            quality standards required for visa submission.
                            It’s quick, affordable and easy - and our
                            staff can handle it all for you.

Photocopy (1st 4 pages) 

Description: 

                            Save time and protect your valuable original
                            documents with our Photocopying service.

                            The quality of your photocopied documents is
                            extremely important to your application, and with
                            our Photocopying service, you can be sure that your
                            documents are ready for your visa application.

                            We offer fast, high-powered machines working on
                            top-quality paper at our Visa Application Centre.

It’s fast, easy and affordable.

                            This service can be availed at the Visa Application
                            Centre during your visit.

 SMS

Description: 

                            Stay up to date on the progress of your application,
                            with regular SMS updates.

                            The SMS service keeps you in the loop at all times -
                            sending an automated message to your mobile phone at
                            key stages of the application process.

                            Which means no needless worry, no long periods of
                            uncertainty, and no wondering about the status of
                            your application.

Please note:

                            These updates are currently only available in
                            English.

 Form Filling

Description: 

                            Our team will help you successfully complete your
                            paperwork at the Visa Application Centre.

                            Our staff will guide you through the process from
                            start to finish, explaining the questions, and
                            recording your answers.

Please note:

                            We cannot answer questions on your behalf or fill in
                            answers based on assumptions.

                            This service is only available in selected Centres.

Printing Service (1st 3 pages) 

Description: 

                            Support your visa application with high-quality
                            printed documents.

                            Whether you need an application form or a supporting
                            document, we offer high-quality printing services at
                            our Visa Application Centre.

                            Our top-quality inks and papers ensure that your
                            application is clear, legible and fit for submission
                            - meeting the exacting standards required for your
                            visa application.

                            This service can be availed at the Visa Application
                            Centre during your visit.

Courier Service 

Description: 

                            Get your documents delivered quickly and directly to
                            your home or office.

                            Safe, fast and convenient - we’ll arrange the
                            return of your documents and passports directly to
                            you, at a time and location of your choosing.

                            Save time and travel costs, with no need to arrange
                            multiple visits to the Visa Application Centre.

Premium Lounge 

Description: 

                            For a more personalized service at the Visa
                            Application Centre, you can purchase access to our
                            Premium Lounge. You can also purchase this service
                            during your visit to our Visa Application Centre at
                            any given time, simply by informing a staff member.

This service includes:

 Direct access to a Premium Service counter where
                              our staff will verify the documents provided, and
                              take payment in a single location

 Cold and hot beverages available at your disposal

A dedicated team member assisting during the
                              submission process

Printing, Photocopy and SMS

The option of submitting any missing documents
                              before 14:00 the same day

                            This service does not guarantee the right to obtain
                            a visa, nor faster processing of your visa
                            application by the Mission. Using the Premium Lounge
                            service does not guarantee the success of your visa
                            application or affect the service standard.
