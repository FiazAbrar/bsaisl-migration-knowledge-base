Special EPS-TOPIK

Overview

Prevents illegal stay by encouraging foreign workers whose period of stay has expired to voluntarily return to their home countries.

					Supports the re-employment of skilled workers through designated job placement
*

* Designated job placement is available for those who have worked for more than one year at their last workplace and have passed the Special EPS-TOPIK after applying to take the test for the same industry as their last workplace.

Eligibility Requirements for Special EPS-TOPIK

Re-employed
 foreign workers who voluntarily departed within their period of stay (departure date: January 1, 2010, or later)

※ If it's confirmed that the candidate did not voluntarily depart, the test will be invalidated regardless of the score.

※ Includes individuals with G-1 status who voluntarily departed within their period of stay

Male or Female from 18 to 39 years old

No criminal record

No experience of deportation from Republic of Korea

No restriction on immigration (reason for disqualification)

Not staying in Korea for more than 5 years with E-9 visa

Not staying in Korea for more than 5 years with E-10 visa

Not staying in Korea for a combined period of more than 5 years under E-9 and E-10 visa
