   Reporting wage arrears

   Petition or criminal complaint

- A foreign worker who has not been paid wages may request

   that      the       overdue        wages         be   paid        (petition)        or       request

   that      the       employer       be     punished           for       violating     the     Labor

   Standards           Act            (criminal          complaint)            [Ministry        of

   Employment and Labor website (https://labor.moel.go.kr)  Civil Application &

   Inquiry  Civil Service Guide  How to Resolve Unpaid Wages 

   Petition/Complaint].

   How to apply

-  You        may      file        a  wage          arrears       petition     online      via  the

   Ministry       of   Employment            and         Labor       website,    or        you  may

   visit      the      competent             local       employment       and    labor       office's

   customer        service         having           jurisdiction     over        the         workplace

   location,       receive            prior  counseling,             and       then     submit  a

   petition        or        file     a             criminal      complaint          [Ministry  of

   Employment and Labor website (https://labor.moel.go.kr)  Civil Application &

   Inquiry  Civil Service Guide  How to Resolve Unpaid Wages 

   Petition/Complaint].

   Processing procedure

- After a wage arrears report is filed, the general processing steps are as follows.

Resolution through civil proceedings

Provisional attachment, civil action, etc.

- A foreign worker who has not been paid wages may also seek relief through: 

provisional attachment of the employer's assets,  the small-claims procedure

(for civil cases where the amount in dispute is KRW 30,000,000 or less, enabling

simplified  processing),              filing  a   civil    lawsuit,   and       

compulsory    enforcement   [Ministry         of    Employment  and        Labor

website (https://labor.moel.go.kr)  Civil Application & Inquiry  Civil Service

Guide  How to Resolve Unpaid Wages  Civil Litigation].

How to apply

- You may file a civil lawsuit with the district court
    having jurisdiction over the workplace location or the
    worker's address; after obtaining a final and conclusive
    judgment, you may carry out compulsory enforcement
    [Ministry of Employment and Labor website (https://labor.moel.go.kr)  Civil
    Application & Inquiry  Civil Service Guide  How to Resolve Unpaid Wages  Civil
    Litigation].

 Legal aid services of the Korea Legal Aid Corporation

   A foreign worker whose final three months' average monthly wage at the time of
  wage arrears is less than KRW 4,000,000 may receive free legal aid (e.g., legal
  counseling, representation in litigation) from the Korea Legal Aid Corporation
  (Article 33-3 of Legal Aid Act; Article 7 (1) 8 of Enforcement Rule of
  the Legal Aid Act; Article 5 (2) 1 of Rules on the Handling of Legal Aid
  Cases).

   How to apply for free legal aid

    Hotline: 132

    Website: Korea Legal Aid Corporation (https://www.klac.or.kr)

           Support programs for workers with wage arrears

               Substitute payment

      - Under this system, the state pays a worker's unpaid wages, etc., in place of the
           employer. A foreign worker who has not been paid wages may claim substitute
           payment for wage arrears from the Korea Workers' Compensation & Welfare Service if
           the following requirements are met (Article 7-2 (1) of Wage Claim Guarantee
           Act; Articles 7 (3) and 8(4) of Enforcement Decree of the Wage Claim
           Guarantee Act; Criteria for Wage Amounts of Currently Employed Workers
           Eligible for Substitute Payment of Unpaid Wages).

                        Eligibility for substitute payment (for employees currently employed)

                  At the time of filing a lawsuit or petition, you must be an employee whose
                    employment contract has not ended (daily laborers whose employment contract
                    period is less than one month are excluded).

                  The three-month average of ordinary wages must be less than a hundred-ten
                    (110) percent of the minimum wage.

                  You must have been employed by the employer operating the relevant
                    business for at least six months up to the date on which the most recent
                    wage arrears occurred before the date of filing a lawsuit or petition.

                  Withintwo years from the day after the most recent wage arrears occurred
                    prior tofiling a lawsuit or petition, you must file a lawsuit, etc., and
                    obtain a finaland conclusive court judgment, etc.; orwithin one year, you
                    must file a petition, etc., and obtain a confirmation ofunpaid wages,
                    etc./employer while still employed.

      - (Scope and Maximum Amount) Based on the date of filing a lawsuit or petition, you
           may receive substitute payment for up to three months of unpaid wages (including
           temporary layoff benefits and maternity leave benefits before and after
           childbirth), up to a maximum of KRW 7,000,000, by counting backward from the date
           on which the most recent wage arrears occurred (Article 7-2 (2) of Wage Claim
           Guarantee Act; Chapter I(2) of Notice on the Maximum Amount of Substitute
           Payment for Unpaid Wages).

      - (How to Apply) Submit a claim to Korea Workers' Compensation & Welfare Service
           with an original or copy of the final and conclusive court judgment (or an
           original or copy of the confirmation of unpaid wages, etc./employer issued by the
           employment and labor office) attached (Article 5 (2) of Enforcement Rule of
           the Wage Claim Guarantee Act).
               Wage payment guarantee insurance

      - Foreign workers with a status of stay for non-professional employment (E-9) or

           visit and employment (H-2) [excluding foreign workers with a status of stay for
           visit and employment (H-2) who are engaged in the construction industry] may,
           when wage arrears occur, apply for insurance benefits under the wage payment
           guarantee insurance taken out by the employer (Article 23 (1) of Act on the
           Employment of Foreign Workers; Article 27 (1) and (2) of Enforcement Decree
           of the Act on the Employment of Foreign Workers).
      - (Maximum Amount) You may receive insurance benefits for unpaid wages up to
           a maximum of KRW 4,000,000 (Notice on the Guaranteed Amount under the Wage
           PaymentGuarantee Insurance for Foreign Workers).
      - (How to Apply) Report the wage arrears to the Ministry of Employment and Labor,
           obtain a confirmation of unpaid wages, etc./employer, and submit a claim for
           insurance benefits to the guarantee insurance company (Seoul Guarantee
           Insurance).
