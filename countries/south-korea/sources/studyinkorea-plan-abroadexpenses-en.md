## Average Study Expenses in Korea

To prepare for a successful study abroad experience, it is important to select a university and academic program while considering the associated costs. National universities receive financial support from the Korean government, making their tuition fees generally lower than those of private universities. Since tuition fees vary by university, prospective students should check the tuition fees of their desired universities in advance. .
