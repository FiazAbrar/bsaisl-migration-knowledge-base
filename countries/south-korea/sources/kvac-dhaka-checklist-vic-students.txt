Documents for Visa Applicants in Possession of a Visa Issuance Confirmation (VIC)

 Applicants must visit in person to submit visa application.

For Student Visa Applicants (D-2, D-4) in Possession of a VIC

1. Visa Application Form with bar code

  The required form consists of 2 pages and is titled
   "Application for Visa for Those in Possession of Visa Issuance Confirmation."

2. Passport Sized 1 Color Photo of Applicant (Taken Within Last 6 Months)
3. Passport with Minimum Validity of 6 Months.
4. Photocopy of Valid Passport
5. Certificate of Admission (Original or Copy) issued by Education Organization/University's
Authority
6. University Payment Certificate
7. Parents' Bangladesh ID cards and Passport copies (if possible)
8. Police Clearance Certificate issued by Bangladesh Police (within 3 months)
9. TB Test Result issued by Designated tuberculosis test center (within 3 months)
10. Valid Air-ticket Booking Slip

Note: Fees are non-refundable for cases in which visa processing results are not permitted,
cancellation of the applicant, or issuance of a single-entry visa after applying for a
multiple-entry visa.
