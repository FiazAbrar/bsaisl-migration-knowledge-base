## Notice on the Opening of the Korea Visa Application Center and Visa Procedure Changes

DATE  
2026-08-25

# Notice on the Opening of the Korea Visa Application Center 

# and Upcoming Changes to Visa Application Procedures

\

The Embassy of the Republic of Korea in Bangladesh will introduce changes to visa application procedures in connection with the scheduled opening of the Korea Visa Application Center. Applicants preparing to apply for a visa are advised to take note of the following information.

\

## 1. Opening of the Korea Visa Application Center

ㅇ Following the opening of the Visa Application Center, applications for ordinary visas and passport collection will be handled exclusively through the Visa Application Center.

ㅇ The Embassy will continue to accept applications for and issue passports related to diplomatic and official visas confirmed through diplomatic notes.

- ●Official Name: Korea Visa Application Center, Dhaka, Bangladesh

- ●Location: 2F B&T Nehaleeya Tower / Plot 80, Block B Kemal Ataturk Avenue, Dhaka 1213, Bangladesh

- ●Contact: +889606777156

- ●Scheduled Opening Date: September 2, 2026 (Wednesday)

## 2. Changes to Visa Application Procedures

ㅇ The Visa Application Center will operate an online appointment system.

ㅇ Details regarding the online appointment procedure and the date from which appointments can be made will be announced separately on August 27, 2026 (Thursday).

\

## 3. Additional Information

ㅇ  Notarization and police clearance certificate-related services will continue to be handled by the Embassy as before.

ㅇ  Detailed information regarding the official opening date, operating hours, online appointment procedures, visa application procedures, required documents, fees, and other matters related to the operation of the Visa Application Center will be announced separately at a later date.

ㅇ  Applicants preparing to apply for a visa are advised to check future announcements regarding the Visa Application Center carefully.

\

The Embassy of the Republic of Korea in Bangladesh will make every effort to enhance the convenience of visa applicants and provide more efficient visa-related services through the opening of the Visa Application Center.
