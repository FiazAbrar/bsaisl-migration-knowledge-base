           National health insurance
               What is "national health insurance"?

       - "National health insurance" is a system intended to contribute to the improvement
           of public health and promote social security by providing insurance benefits for
           the prevention, diagnosis, treatment, and rehabilitation of disease and injury,
           for childbirth and death, and for health promotion (Article 1 of National
           Health Insurance Act).

       - Under National Health Insurance Act, insurance benefits include benefits for
           medical care, medical care reimbursement, health screening, and benefits for
           assistive devices for persons with disabilities as follows.

National health insurance coverage eligibility

- Citizens residing in the Republic of Korea become insured persons or dependents
    under national health insurance. However, the following persons are excluded
    (Article 5(1) of National Health Insurance Act).

   � Persons receiving medical care under Medical Care Assistance Act;

   � Persons receiving medical protection under Act on the Honorable Treatment of
      Persons of Distinguished Service to Independence and Act on the Honorable
      Treatment of and Support for Persons of Distinguished Service to the State
      (hereinafter "beneficiaries of medical protection for persons of merit").
      Nevertheless, any of the following persons become an insured person or a
      dependent:

    A beneficiary of medical protection for persons of merit who applies to the
      insurer for national health insurance coverage;

    A person who had been covered by national health insurance and then became a
      beneficiary of medical protection for persons of merit but did not apply to the
      insurer for exclusion from national health insurance coverage.

       "Dependent" means a person who is among any of the following and chiefly relies

           National Health Insurance Act (Article 5(2) of National Health Insurance
           Act).

                                                                           Dependents

            1. Spouses of the employee insured;

2. Lineal ascendants of the employee insured (including lineal ascendants of
    their spouse);

3. Lineal descendants of the employee insured (including lineal descendants of
    their spouse) and their spouse;

4. Siblings of the employee insured.

                  Insurance premium and responsibility of payment for the employee insured
             The monthly payroll-based insurance premium for the employee insured shall be

               borne fifty (50) percent each by the employee insured and by the party
               applicable below (main sentence of Article 76(1) and Items a and c of
               Subparagraph 2 of Article 3 of National Health Insurance Act).
            1. Where the employee insured is a worker: the employer of the workplace to
                 which the worker belongs;
            2. Where the employee insured is a public official: the state or local
                 government to which the public official belongs;
            3. Where the employee insured is a faculty member (excluding teachers working
                 at private schools): founder/operator of the private school to which the
                 faculty member belongs.
             However, where the employee insured is a teacher working at a private school,
               fifty (50) percent of the premium is borne by the employee insured, thirty
               (30) percent by the founder/operator of the private school to which the
               teacher belongs, and twenty (20) percent by the state (proviso to Article
               76(1) of National Health Insurance Act).

              Insurance premium and responsibility of payment for the self-employed insured
             Premiums for the self-employed insured shall be paid jointly by all self-

                employed insured in the household to which the insured person belongs (main
                sentence of Article 77(2) of National Health Insurance Act).

             However, minors without income and property and minors who meet the following
               standards considering income and property are exempt from the obligation to
               pay (proviso to Article 77(2) of National Health Insurance Act and main
               sentence of Article 46 of Enforcement Decree of the National Health
               Insurance Act).

            1. A minor belonging to a household that includes at least one adult and who
                 meets all of the following:

               � The total annual income under Article 41(1) of Enforcement Decree of the
                  National Health Insurance Act is KRW 1,000,000 or less; and

               � Has no property falling under Subparagraph 1 of Article 42(1) of 
                  Enforcement Decree of the National Health Insurance Act.

            2. A minor whose parents are both deceased and whose total annual income under
                 Article 41(1) of Enforcement Decree of the National Health Insurance Act
                 is KRW 1,000,000 or less.

             However, a minor who has dividend income under Subparagraph 2 of Article
                 41(1) of Enforcement Decree of the National Health Insurance Act or
                 business income arising from a business registered under Article 168(1) of 
                 Income Tax Act as referred to in Subparagraph 3 of Article 41(1) of 
                 Enforcement Decree of the National Health Insurance Act, is excluded
                 (proviso to Article 46 of Enforcement Decree of the National Health
                 Insurance Act).

           For more information on premium calculation methods, premium
               rates, premium reductions, and related matters, please visit the
               NHIS website (www.nhis.or.kr) or contact the NHIS hotline (1577-1000).

           National health insurance enrollment for foreign nationals

       Enrollment of foreign nationals residing in the Republic of Korea
      Q. Can foreign nationals enroll in national health insurance?

                                                                    8 / 13
 

      A. Yes. Overseas Koreans or foreign nationals residing in Korea (hereinafter "
          foreign nationals") may become the employee insured if certain conditions are met
          (Article 109(2) of National Health Insurance Act), and are mandatorily enrolled
          as the self-employed insured if they reside in Korea for at least six months
          (Article 109(3) of National Health Insurance Act; Article 61-2(1) of 
          Enforcement Rule of the National Health Insurance Act).
            On the other hand, foreign nationals entering with a status of stay for study
            abroad or as a marriage migrant (F-6) are, as an exception, eligible for
            national health insurance immediately upon entry (Article 109(3) of National
            Health Insurance Act; Article 61-2(1) of Enforcement Rule of the National
            Health Insurance Act).
               Requirements for foreign nationals as the employee insured

      - A foreign national residing in Korea becomes the employee insured when all of the
           following requirements are met (Article 109(2) of National Health Insurance
           Act).

                                                                    9 / 13
 

                               Requirements for foreign nationals as the employee insured
             The person must be a worker, public official, or faculty member at a

                workplace subject to coverage; and

             The person must fall under one of the following:

            1. A person registered pursuant to Subparagraph 3 of Article 6(1) of 
                Resident Registration Act;

            2. A person who filed a domestic residence report pursuant to Article 6 of 
                Act on the Immigration and Legal Status of Overseas Koreans; or

            3. A person who completed alien registration pursuant to Article 31 of 
                Immigration Act.

            However, a person falling under any of the following cannot become the
               employee insured (Articles 6(2) and 109(2) of National Health Insurance
               Act; Article 9 of Enforcement Decree of the National Health Insurance
               Act).

            1. Daily laborers whose employment period is less than 1 month

            2. Public officials who assume office by winning an election, and who do not
                 receive monthly remuneration or salary equivalent thereto

            3. Non-regular workers or short-time workers whose prescribed working hours are
                less than 60 hours per month

            4. Non-regular teaching staff or part-time public officials and teaching staff
                whose prescribed working hours are less than 60 hours per month

            5. Workers and employers of businesses whose place of business is not fixed

            6. An employer of a business with no workers or that employs only persons
                falling under Item 3 above.

               Requirements for foreign nationals as the self-employed insured

      - A foreign national residing in Korea who is not an employee insured becomes a
           self-employed insured when all of the following requirements are met (Article
           109(3) of National Health Insurance Act; Paragraphs 1 and 2 of Article 61-2
           and attached Table 9 of Enforcement Rule of the National Health Insurance Act;
           Article 4(3) of Standards for the Application of National Health Insurance
           to Long-Term Resident Overseas Koreans and Foreigners).

                           Requirements for foreign nationals as the self-employed insured
             The person has resided in Korea for at least six months, or there are grounds

                to expect the person will reside continuously in Korea for at least six
                months, by falling under one of the following:

            1. Has obtained permanent residence status pursuant to Subparagraph 2 of
                 Article 10 of Immigration Act;

            2. Has obtained a status of stay for non-professional employment (E-9) under
                 Subparagraph 21 of attached Table 1-2 of Enforcement Decree of the
                 Immigration Act;

            3. Has obtained a status of stay as a marriage migrant under Subparagraph 27 of
                 attached Table 1-2 of Enforcement Decree of the Immigration Act;

            4. Has obtained a status of stay for study or research that qualifies under
                 subparagraph 5 of attached Table 1-2 of Enforcement Decree of the
                 Immigration Act [limited to study by overseas Koreans or foreign nationals
                 with a status of stay for study (D-2) or as an overseas Korean (F-4)]; or

            5. Has obtained a status of stay for study to receive education at a school
                 under Article 2 of Elementary and Secondary Education Act [limited to
                 study by overseas Koreans or foreign nationals with a status of stay as a
                 general trainee (D-4) or overseas Korean (F-4)].

            1. A person registered pursuant to Subparagraph 3 of Article 6(1) of

           National health insurance enrollment method for foreign nationals

               Automatic national health insurance enrollment for foreign nationals
      - For foreign nationals who reside in Korea for at least six months, the National

           Health Insurance Service processes enrollment automatically without a separate
           reporting procedure (Subparagraph 1 of Article 76-2(1) of Enforcement Decree
           of the National Health Insurance Act).
          A foreign national who enters with a status of stay for study or as a marriage

             migrant is processed for automatic enrollment immediately upon entry. However,
             enrollment becomes available after alien registration (Subparagraph 2 of Article
             76-2(1) of Enforcement Decree of the National Health Insurance Act).

      - Notwithstanding the above, depending on the circumstances, documents may be
           required to verify family relationships or facts of marriage/divorce (limited to
           household members), and documents to verify levels of income and property, for
           the purpose of national health insurance enrollment (Article 4(4) and attached
           Table 1 of Standards for the Application of National Health Insurance to
           Long-Term Resident Overseas Koreans and Foreigners).
           For more information on national health insurance enrollment
               for foreign nationals and the acquisition of dependent status,
               please contact the NHIS hotline for foreign nationals (033-811-2000).
