			Notice on cases of submitting counterfeit documents when applying for a visa or notarization

DATE

2022-09-19

Please note.
Recently, cases have been discovered, such as using forged bank statements, forged foreign visas, forged Immigration stamps, and forged university degree certificates and so on.
Please be advised that if you submit forged items when applying for a visa or notarizing documents, you may be denied a visa, banned from entering the Republic of Korea, and arrested as criminal suspects.
