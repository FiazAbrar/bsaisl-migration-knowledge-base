Dear Managing Director

1. I have the honour to convey the warm greetings of the Government and people

continued commitment and the Ministryl dedicated efforts to strengthen and

improve the EPS system. Bangladesh remains one of the most promising personne!

sending countries for the Republic of Korea, and we value this longstanding

partnership.

2. Recently, however, a significant number of Bangladeshi EPS candidates have

faced difficulties due to the deletion of their names from the rostel which has

understandably caused concern among the affected workers and relevant authorities.

3. ln this regard, BOESL, the Bangladesh Embassy in Seoul, and the Ministry of

Expatriates' Welfare and Overseas Employment have all conveyed their concerns and

have expressed hope that the HRDK Headquarters in Ulsan witl give favourable

consideration to the appeals submitted by the roster deleted workers.

4. I wish to assure you that HRDK has taken these concerns very seriously. After

extensive internal consultations and a detailed review of EPS practices across the 17

particiPating countries, HRDK would like to respectfully share the following

observations:

a. Roster deletion is an integral part of the EPS system, and reinstatement (auto roster)
is not permitted under the established guidelines.

participating in the lottery process those who are subject to the existing conditions.

b. ln order to ensure proper preparation for the deleted candidates, the

examination scheduled for the first half of the upcoming 2026 UBT Exam has been
extended to the second half of the year.

c. ln recognition of the challenges faced by the roster deleted EPS personnel,

HRDK has extended a special consideration to Bangladesh by increasing the annual
qualifiers quota from 500 to 2,000, thereby creating additional opportunities for

Bangladeshi applicants under the EPS program.

affected personnel and offer them a renewed opportunity to pursue employment in
the Republic of Korea.
