			[Notice] Warning of Fake Reservation Documents

DATE

2024-02-12

The Embassy of the Republic of Korea notice some agency or individual are making 
fake reservation documents(consular appointment)
 and selling these counterfeit papers to consular service seekers. 
This is a criminal offence and punishable under law.
The Embassy of the Republic of Korea would be bound to take the following legal action for such type of criminal activities;
1. Individual would be banned by visa restriction
2. Agencies would be charged for criminal offence through legal proceedings
Please be more cautious with any kind of fake, fraudulent and fabricated deal provided by any individual or agency. 
The Embassy of the Republic of Korea in Bangladesh is committed to ensure transparent consular services for all.
If you encounter any suspicious activity mentioned above, please report it to the Embassy of the Republic of Korea immediately.
