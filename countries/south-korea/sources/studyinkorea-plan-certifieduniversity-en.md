## International Education Quality Assurance System (IEQAS)

What is the International Education Quality Assurance System (IEQAS)?\
To attract outstanding international students, the Ministry of Justice and the Ministry of Education annually evaluate and certify universities with high internationalization capabilities. If you are preparing to study in Korea, please check the list of IEQAS-certified universities and consider applying.

### Characteristics of Accredited Universities

#### 

Accreditation is granted to universities that meet both basic requirements and evaluation criteria in areas such as strategy and recruitment, student support, and management and outcomes for international students. Certified universities benefit from relaxed visa screening criteria for international students and additional allowances for part-time work hours during weekdays.

### What Are Excellent Accredited Universities?

Among certified universities, those demonstrating outstanding internationalization capacity are separately designated as 'Excellent Certified Universities'. These universities receive benefits such as simplified visa issuance procedures and preferential consideration in the selection of participants for internationalization projects by the Ministry of Education.

- Non-Accredited universities undergo inspections, and if they fail to meet certain standards, they are designated as institutions requiring stricter visa screening for language proficiency, financial capability, and other factors.
