Embassy of the Republic of Korea in People's Republic of Bangladesh

			[Notice] Warning of Fake Visa Documents

DATE

2023-12-28

FILE

Fake Documents.pdf

The Embassy of the Republic of Korea would like to remind all visa applicants to be more cautious with any kind of visa issuance guarantee given by any individuals or agency. It is totally fake, fraudulent, fabricated and criminal acts to assure someone with visa issuance by any individual or agency; on behalf of Korean immigration or the Embassy of the Republic of Korea in Bangladesh.

Nobody is capable to issue Korean Visa except the Embassy of the Republic of Korea in Bangladesh. We are duly concerned that perhaps some recruiting/travel agency fraudulently demanding money from mass people by giving them 
fake E-7(Welding), C-4-1-4(Agricultural) visa issuance certificate
 in the name of Korean immigration and Korean Embassy in Bangladesh.
For your kind information we would like to announce that 
currently very few E-7 visas (Welding Visa)
 for Bangladeshi workers is issuing jointly proceed by enlisted Korean and Bangladeshi compliance recruiting agencies. So far, 
no agricultural visa (C-4-1-4)
 is issued from the embassy of the Republic of Korea in Bangladesh.
Please be cautious of any individuals or entities that claim to be affiliated with the Embassy of the Republic of Korea and request financial transactions in exchange for visa issuance or assistance. Korean Embassy does not affiliate with any agencies. So these are likely to be fraudulent schemes designed to take advantage of unsuspecting applicants.
The Embassy of the Republic of Korea in Bangladesh is committed to ensure safe and lawful migration to South Korea for all Bangladeshi travelers.
If you encounter any suspicious activity mentioned above, please report it to the Embassy of the Republic of Korea immediately.
