Dhaka, Bangladesh | 02 September 2026

VFS Global launches South Korea visa services in Bangladesh
with modern and easily accessible application center in Dhaka

    · Korea visa applicants can now visit our centrally located visa application center in Dhaka for
         a seamless application submission experience.

    · Applicants can book an appointment online to visit our center for submitting their applications
         with ease.

VFS Global, the global leader in trusted technology services, empowering secure mobility for
governments and citizens, has announced the launch of South Korea visa services in Bangladesh
through a state-of-the-art Korea Visa Application Center in Dhaka, providing an enhanced visa
application experience for Korean visa applicants in the country.

Korean visa applicants across Bangladesh will now be able to submit their applications at the new Korea Visa
Application Center in Dhaka by booking a prior appointment online.

The inauguration of the new Korea Visa Application Center was done on 2 September 2026 by H.E. Mr. Kim
Ji-joon, Ambassador of the Republic of Korea to Bangladesh and Ms. Yummi Talwar, Chief Operating
Officer - South Asia, VFS Global, in the presence of other dignitaries from the Embassy of the Republic of
Korea.

Ms. Yummi Talwar, Chief Operating Officer - South Asia, VFS Global, said, "The launch of our Korea Visa
Application Center marks a significant step in improving the visa application submission experience for travellers
across Bangladesh. This spacious, modern, and centrally located facility offers a comfortable and convenient
application environment, supported by well-trained customer service representatives who will assist applicants
at every step of their journey."

VFS Global has been a trusted partner for the Government of the Republic of Korea for visa processing services
since 2011. The company offers visa services on behalf of the Republic of Korea across 5 countries through a
network of 7 dedicated visa application centres globally.

VFS Global's role in the visa application process is limited to front-end administrative tasks only, which include
collecting visa application forms, required documentation as per the checklist, and returning the passports to
applicants after the decision is made. VFS Global has no role in the decision-making process of granting or
refusing a visa. Decision making on the granting or refusal of visa is the sole prerogative and sole responsibility
of the concerned Embassy.

Korea Visa Application Center in Dhaka:

     · Address: B&T Nehaleeya Tower, 2nd floor, Plot-80, Block-B, Kamal Ataturk Avenue, Banani, Dhaka-
          1213, Bangladesh

     · Business Hours: 9am to 2pm

Applicants are encouraged to obtain the latest information on visa requirements, fees, appointment booking,
and available services only through the official website of the Embassy of the Republic of Korea and the official
VFS Global website.

About VFS Global
As the global leader in trusted technology services, empowering secure mobility for governments and citizens,
VFS Global embraces technological innovation, including Generative AI, to support governments and diplomatic
missions worldwide. The company manages non-judgmental and administrative tasks related to applications
for visa, passport, and consular services for its client governments, increasing productivity and enabling them
to focus entirely on the critical task of assessment.

ethical practices and sustainability while serving as the trusted partner to 71 client governments. Operating
over 4,200 Application Centres in 169 countries, VFS Global has efficiently processed more than 557 million
transactions* since 2001.
