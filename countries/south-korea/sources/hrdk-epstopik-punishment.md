# Punishment of Wrongdoer

- Restrictions on application about cheaters
  - Restrict for four years from the time of cheating

- Cheating
  - Communication with other applicant regarding test during the test
  - Exchange of answer sheet for the purpose of cheating.
  - Checking other applicant's answer sheet or questionnaire before writing answer during the test.
  - Showing or telling the answer to other applicant.
  - Possessing, using or exchanging question related object
  - Answer the question with help of others
  - Swapping name and application number with other applicants.
  - Substitute test-taking
  - Using or Carring a cell phone during the test
  - Other illegal or inappropriate behavior.

- EPS Fraud Reporting Online Center
  - The HRDK operates the \[ EPS Fraud Reporting Online Center \] to receive reports related to fraudulent activities or\
    suspicions of unfair behavior in the point-based selection system for foreign workers.\
    These reports help address organizational fraud, corruption, or other unjust actions.\
    The identity of the reporter and the reported content will be kept strictly confidential and the issues will be verified\
    and investigated through the EPS Center, with the results communicated to the reporter.

    The types of fraud that can be reported include

    Organized fraudulent activity planning, Possession and use of prohibited items,\
    Taking the test on behalf of another person Cheating or exchanging answers,\
    Forging identification cards and admission tickets Communication during the exam

    However, simple complaints, reports without verified facts, or defamatory content without evidence will not be considered in the scope of the report.

    To report

    fill out the "Fraud Report Form" and send it to the email below.\
    The form can be downloaded from the "Employment Permit System Korean Language Proficiency Test (EPS-TOPIK)"\
    website (https://epstopik.hrdkorea.or.kr).

    EPS Fraud Reporting Online Center Email : cleaneps@hrdkorea.or.kr

    [Download Fraud Report Form](/epstopik/eps_klt/FraudReportForm.hwp "Download Fraud Report Form")
