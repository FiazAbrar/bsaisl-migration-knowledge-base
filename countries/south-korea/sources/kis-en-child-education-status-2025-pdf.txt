   Status of stay to be granted to foreign children staying in Korea for

  a long term to guarantee their right to education (25.4.1. ~ '28.3.31.)

 Application Period and Eligibility

  Application Period

   From '25. 4. 1. to '28. 3. 31.

  Eligibility

   Born in Korea or entered Korea as an infant or a young child (before the

          age of six)

    Have lived in Korea for at least six years
    On the date of application, applicant is attending either an elementary,

          middle or high school in Korea or have graduated from a high school in

          Korea.

   Entered Korea at the age of six or older
     Have lived in Korea for at least seven years
    On the date of application, applicant is attending either an elementary,

           middle or high school in Korea or have graduated from a high school in

           Korea.

 Action Plans for Applicants

  Action Plan for Children

   If a child is currently attending an elementary, middle, or high

     school

      - Grants visa for studying (D-4) until high school graduation

   If a child has graduated from highschool

      - Will be able to change visa if he/she meets condition for studying
         or working conditions
    - Those who are not eligible for visa change for studying or
        working, he/she will be granted or changed to G-1 visa

  If a child failed to meet conditions maintaining their visa (e.g.

    expelled from school, committed crime)
    - will result in visa cancellation or refusal of visa extension in accordance with

        the Immigration Act

 Action Plan for Minor Brothers and Sisters of a Child

  If an eligible child is granted a visa, brothers and sisters of the

           child will be granted G-1 visa to guarantee stable family life.

 Action Plan for Child's Parents

  Action Plan

    - As a general rule, parents of eligible children are supposed to leave Korea.

         Considering that their child is a minor, however, they will be permitted to
         temporarily stay in Korea to raise their child until the child graduates from
         high school or becomes an adult.

    - However, parent who is not protecting/raising children in reality will
       be excluded from eligibility.

  Paying penalty for parents breaking law

    - When applying for visa issuance for their child, parents will be notified about

          their obligation to pay fine for their illegal status of stay.

     * if parentls lack ability to pay fine or if there is a reason behind, they may
      receive additional reduction

    - After paying the fine, parents can stay in Korea temporarily on a G-1 visa

         until their child graduates from high school. In the meantime, they will also
         be allowed to engage in activities beyond their visa status to raise their
         child.

  Temporary stay and Complete Departure

    - If a child graduated from highschool or becomes an adult, he/she
        must leave Korea on his/her own. If he/she stays in Korea
          illegally without leaving, he/she will be deported and restricted
          from re-entry

   Increase Parental Responsibility

     - Impose additional condition such as KIIP class attendance* to make
         sure parents neglect their duty for raising and educating their child

      * KIIP(Ministry of Justice), Education on raising child (related ministry)
