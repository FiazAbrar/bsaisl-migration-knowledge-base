MHTC’s trip to Bangladesh proved highly successful with the formalisation of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU), officially establishing a partnership with [Qualitas Healthcare Bangladesh](https://www.linkedin.com/company/qualitas-healthcare-bangladesh/). This collaboration aims to enhance healthcare services for Bangladeshi patients, providing them with top-notch, extensive, yet affordable care.

The MoU was signed by Dr. Mohamed Ali Abu Bakar, CEO of MHTC, and Dr. Ramon Varughese, the Chief Medical Officer at Qualitas Health Malaysia and Country Head of Qualitas Health Bangladesh.
