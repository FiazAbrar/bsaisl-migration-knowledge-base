# Figures at a glance in Malaysia

As of August 2026, there are some 225,740 refugees and asylum-seekers registered with UNHCR in Malaysia.

As of August 2026, there are **225,740 refugees and asylum-seekers** registered with UNHCR in Malaysia.

- Approximately 90% are from Myanmar, including Rohingya and other ethnic groups who have fled conflict, violence, and persecution.
- The remaining population comprises refugees and asylum-seekers from countries including Pakistan (3%), Yemen (2%), Afghanistan (1%), Somalia (1%), and other nationalities (3%).

**45%** of refugees and asylum-seekers are men, while **23%** are women. Boys and girls each account for **16%** of the population.

There are approximately **72,236 children below the age of 18**, representing **32%** of the total registered population.

_UNHCR statistics does not map or reflect new arrivals into the country._

_Any increase in numbers of registered refugees are as a result of UNHCR's own capacity to conduct registration._
