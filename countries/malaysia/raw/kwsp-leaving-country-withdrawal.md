## Leaving Country Withdrawal

# EPF Leaving Country Withdrawal

Members who have given up your Malaysian citizenship to migrate to another country, expatriates, Permanent Residents (PR) or foreign workers returning to your home country can choose to withdraw all of your EPF savings.

- Application Within Malaysia
- Overseas Application

## Who Can Apply

Malaysians/ PR who have renounced their citizenship/ PR OR Expatriate/ foreign workers who are no longer employed/ intend to leave Malaysia

Have savings with the EPF

## What You Can Withdraw

• Entire savings with the EPF

## What You Need

- Form KWSP 9K (AHL) and Checklist
- Copy of valid passport
- Form KWSP 3 (Pindaan) - for mail submissions if thumbprint verification fails
- A bank verification document confirming the account holder's details.

### Additional Documents Required For Malaysian and Non-Malaysian Members

Proof of Identity is needed for the following situations:

- **If passport number does not match EPF records**
Copy of old passport used when EPF account was registered
- **If original passport is not with the applicant**
Temporary travel permit from the Embassy of applicant's country in Malaysia
- **If member's particulars are incomplete or mismatched in EPF records**
Letter Confirming the Identification of Applicant from employer
**OR**
Letter Confirming the Identification of Applicant from applicant's embassy
**OR**
Verification Form of Member's Contribution from employer

### Foreign Worker

1. **Proof Of Departure**

    Check Out Memo from the Immigration Department of Malaysia

## Leaving Country Withdrawal Channel

### Withdrawal Channels

Self-Service Terminal (SST) EPF Offices

_\*\*Leaving Country Withdrawal can be applied through the SST at EPF offices only._

### Payment Methods

1. **Local Payments**

    a. **Full payment in Ringgit Malaysia (RM) will be credited to your bank account**

    If you hold an active account with our panel bank and your identification document number matches the bank records.

    b. **You will be issued a Bankers Cheque**

    If payment to your bank account is unsuccessful.

2. **Overseas Payments**
Full payment will be made via foreign demand draft (FDD) in one of the following currencies:

    i. Brunei Dollar (BND)

    ii. Singapore Dollar (SGD)

If your chosen currency is not listed above, full payment will be made via foreign telegraphic transfer (FTT).

### Important Reminder

**Certify your documents**

Ensure ALL copies of documents have been certified and acknowledged by the authorised persons.

1. Applicants In Malaysia:

- EPF Officer
- Malaysia Embassy Officer/ High Commission/ Consulate of Applicant's Country in Malaysia
- Applicant's Employer

2. Applicants Abroad:

- Malaysian Embassy
- Malaysian High Commission/Consulate
- Malaysian Trades Commission
- Public Notary

**Submit to EPF**

Send your completed submissions at your nearest EPF office or mail it to the EPF.
