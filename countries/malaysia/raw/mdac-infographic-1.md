MALAYSIA DIGITAL ARRIVAL CARD (MDAC) FOR FOREIGN VISITORS
HOW TO REGISTER?
STEP 1 : BEFORE ARRIVING MALAYSIA
Complete and submit MDAC within 3 days before arrival in Malaysia
https://imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/mdac/main
STEP 2 : WHEN ARRIVING MALAYSIA
Show MDAC registration and verify your passport at the Immigration counter
