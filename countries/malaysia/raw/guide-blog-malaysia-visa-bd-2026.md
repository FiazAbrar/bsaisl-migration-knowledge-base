Planning to visit Malaysia in 2026? Good news—getting a **[Malaysia visa from Bangladesh](https://worldfamoustourism.blogspot.com/)** is relatively simple if you follow the correct process. This guide covers everything: requirements, costs, processing time, and application steps.

## 📌 Types of Malaysia Visa for Bangladeshis

Before applying, choose the right visa type:

1.  **Tourist Visa (Single Entry)**

    - Valid for short travel (up to 30 days)

2.  **eVisa (Online Visa)**

    - Apply online without visiting embassy

3.  **eNTRI Visa (Electronic Travel Registration)**

    - Faster and cheaper option for short visits

## 📄 Required Documents

To apply for a Malaysia visa, you need:

- Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
- Recent passport-size photo (white background)
- National ID card copy
- Bank statement (last 3–6 months)
- Confirmed return air ticket
- Hotel booking or invitation letter
- Visa application form

## 💰 Malaysia Visa Cost from Bangladesh (2026)

- eVisa: ৳5,000 – ৳8,000

## 🖥️ How to Apply (Step-by-Step)

### Option 1: Apply Online (Recommended)

1.  Visit official Malaysia visa portal
2.  Create an account
3.  Fill in your details
4.  Upload documents
5.  Pay visa fee online
6.  Download your visa after approval

### Option 2: Through Travel Agency

- Submit documents to a trusted agency
- Pay service charge
- They handle the process for you

## ⏱️ Processing Time

- eNTRI: 1–3 working days
- eVisa: 3–5 working days
- Sticker Visa: 5–10 working days
