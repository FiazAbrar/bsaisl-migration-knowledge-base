FOMEMA Medical Examination Registration Fees
The registration fees for the FOMEMA Medical Examination are inclusive of the health screenings listed below:
Registration Fees, Subject to 8% SST: Male Foreign Workers RM 207; Female Foreign Workers RM 217
Mental Health Assessment
Doctor Assessment: Physical Examination by a Doctor; Blood Pressure; Height and Weight; Body Mass Index (BMI); Vision Test
Medical Report: Fitness for Work Report by the Doctor
Chest X-ray
Urine Tests: Full Analysis (Urine Albumin and Urine Sugar); Drug Test (Opiates, Methamphetamine and Cannabinoids); Pregnancy Test *Only applicable for female workers
Blood Tests: ABO Blood Grouping; Infectious Disease Screening
