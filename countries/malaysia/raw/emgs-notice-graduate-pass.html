What is the Graduate Pass?

The Graduate Pass is a Social Visit Pass that enables international students to stay in Malaysia for a year after graduation. It lets you travel and work in permitted job sectors.

Benefits of the Graduate Pass

Work in Malaysia. No employer sponsorship needed.

Start your career in Malaysia straight after graduation, without the need for company sponsorship.

Valid for 12 months
 after graduation.

Enjoy a 12-month extension to live and work in Malaysia, enriching your experience.

Your dependents
 can join you*.

Bring your dependents along and experience Malaysia together as a family.

Apply for the Graduate Pass

Apply for the Graduate Pass today and enjoy an additional 12 months to live and work in Malaysia. Don’t miss out on this chance to gain valuable work experience and make the most of your time in Malaysia. Apply for the Graduate Pass now and take the next big step in your journey!
