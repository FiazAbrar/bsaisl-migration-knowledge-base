                             EMPLOYMENT INJURY SCHEME FOR FOREIGN WORKERS

This scheme provides protection to employees against accidents or occupational diseases
                               arising out of and in the course of their employment.

Effective 1 January 2019, employers are required to register their foreign workers with Social Security
Organisation (SOCSO) to receive protection through the Employment Injury Scheme under the
