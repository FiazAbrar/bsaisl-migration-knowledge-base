# Couple arrested in international fake visa operation

By [Kalbana Perimbanayagam](/authors/kalbana-perimbanayagam)

February 25, 2026 @ 6:08pm

Items seized in the operation against fake visa syndicate - PIC COURTESY OF IMMIGRATION DEPT

[![](https://www.nst.com.my/assets/images/summary-banner-mobile.png)](/newssummary/1385161?summary=1385161&date=1772014106)

KUALA LUMPUR: The Immigration Department has dismantled an international fake visa syndicate known as David Lawrence following a three-month surveillance and intelligence operation.

Its Kuala Lumpur deputy director (control) Marina Habibon said the syndicate offered forged international visas to European countries at fees of up to RM20,000 per document.

She said the operation, led by the department's Intelligence Unit under its Enforcement Division, resulted in the arrest of a Bangladeshi couple in their 30s who are believed to have been active for the past eight months.

"The syndicate processed fake international visas for clients in Bangladesh.

"Customers' documents were sent to Malaysia via international courier before being forged and used for travel to European countries," she said in a statement.

"Acting on intelligence, enforcement officers raided a house in Sungai Besi here on Feb 10, which was used as the syndicate's operation base."

Marina said several Bangladeshi passports, fake foreign immigration stamps, suspected forged immigration slips, mobile phones, a laptop and other equipment used in the activities were seized during the raid.

On Feb 23, the two suspects were charged at the Kuala Lumpur Sessions Court and pleaded guilty to nine charges.

"The court imposed a total fine of RM112,000. Failing payment, the accused were ordered to serve 45 months' imprisonment," she said.

She added that upon completion of their sentence, both individuals would be deported to their home country and blacklisted from entering Malaysia permanently.

Marina said stern action would continue to be taken against any party involved in document forgery and immigration offences under the Immigration Act 1959/63 (Act 155) and other relevant laws.

She urged the public to channel information on illegal activities to the department to ensure the country's safety and sovereignty remain protected.
