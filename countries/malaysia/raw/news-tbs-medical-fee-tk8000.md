# Govt fixes Tk8,000 medical test fee for Malaysia-bound workers

## The ministry urged Malaysia-bound workers not to pay more than Tk8,000 under any circumstances and warned them against contacting brokers or middlemen for medical examinations.

**UNB**

31 October, 2026, 01:15 pm

Last modified: 31 October, 2026, 09:02 pm

Representational image. Photo: Collected

**The Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare and Overseas Employment has fixed the medical examination fee for workers seeking employment in Malaysia at Tk8,000 per person.**

No medical centre would be allowed to charge more than the fixed amount for the required medical tests, said a press release issued on today (31 October).

The ministry urged Malaysia-bound workers not to pay more than Tk8,000 under any circumstances and warned them against contacting brokers or middlemen for medical examinations.

#### [![The Business Standard Google News](/sites/all/themes/sloth/images/google_news.svg) Keep updated, follow The Business Standard's Google news channel](https://news.google.com/publications/CAAqBwgKMIivlQsw2ZKrAw?ceid=US:en&oc=3)

It also clarified that no medical centre would operate under any specific recruiting agency.

Selected workers would be free to undergo medical examinations at any authorised medical centre.

It further said that if a worker decided to proceed with a different recruiting agency after receiving a medical slip through another agency, the previous agency would have to forward the medical report without delay.

No objection or excuse from the agency issuing the slip would be accepted in this regard.

Workers have been advised to immediately inform the ministry if any medical centre demands more than the fixed fee or subjects them to harassment, unnecessary delays or other irregularities.

Complaints can be sent to the ministry through email at [\[email protected\]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#493a2c2a3b2c3d283b3009393b262b283a2120672e263f672b2d).

The ministry also reminded workers that no fee is required for a MiGRAMS slip.

It said the ministry would regularly monitor and inspect the activities of medical centres and take strict legal action against those found involved in irregularities.

It also urged Malaysia-bound workers, recruiting agencies and medical centres to strictly follow the instructions to ensure a transparent, orderly and systematic overseas employment process.
