There are multitudes of various job opportunities in the free market, research and the public sector, or you may opt to become self-employed.

#### Career in Malaysia

There are many expatriates and foreign workers in Malaysia, international companies are found throughout the country. Employer will need to file a permit application with the local government in Malaysia. Depend on the type of job and skills, different working passes (Employment Pass and Temporary Employment Pass for example) are issued when obtaining a work permit.

#### Malaysian Labour Market at a Glance

Malaysia's labour market is diverse, with key sectors including manufacturing, services, agriculture, and increasingly, technology. The country offers opportunities for skilled expatriates and locals, supported by initiatives to enhance education and skill development. Economic growth drives employment, with a focus on industrialization and service sector expansion.

#### Finance

Malaysia is also known for its innovative financial sector, particularly in Islamic banking and finance. Malaysia is ranked as first according to the Islamic Finance Development Indicator score, the largest Islamic banking service provider in the Asia Pacific, and second only to Saudi Arabia in terms of the size of banking assets.

#### Palm Oil

As one of the world’s largest palm oil exporters, the country accounts for 39 % of world palm oil production and 44% of all world exports. The majority of Malaysia’s palm oil products are exported to China, the EU, Pakistan, US and India, and is evidence of Malaysia’s contribution on global stage.

#### Oil, Gas and Energy

The oil, gas, and energy industry in Malaysia is a crucial economic sector, led by PETRONAS and rich in offshore reserves. Key activities include exploration, production, refining, and LNG export. Despite challenges like oil price volatility and environmental concerns, there is a strong focus on renewable energy and sustainability to diversify and ensure long-term industry growth.

Most research positions at universities are fixed term and connected to a specific project. But there are also research jobs outside universities, for instance, at research institutions or in industry. Often big companies have their own in-house research departments.

#### attractive and stable job opportunities

Following decades of strong growth, forward-looking policy reforms have successfully diversified its economy from one that was agriculture and commodity-based in the mid-1980’s into a robust hub for manufacturing, finance and other services today. The service and manufacturing sectors are projected by local authorities to grow by 5.7 per cent and 4.8 per cent respectively, and the IMF is projecting the job market to be stable with an overall economic growth of 4.7 per cent.

#### job search checklist

Before you set out to explore job opportunities, make sure that you are prepared in order to have a successful job search.

[* *Start looking early](#jslist)

- Gives you a head start in a competitive job market.
- More time to explore options and tailor your applications to specific roles.
- Early applications show proactivity and enthusiasm to employers.
- Avoids last-minute stress of job hunting post-graduation.

[* *Don’t necessarily limit your search](#jslist2)

- Explore a wide range of industries and sectors for potential opportunities.
- Broadening your scope increases your chances of finding the right fit.
- Consider non-traditional career paths or roles you hadn’t initially thought of.
- The flexibility to pivot might lead to unexpected yet rewarding career paths.

[* *Take advantage of what your university offers](#jslist3)

- Career services can provide tailored advice, resume critiques, and job search support.
- Alumni networks often offer mentorship and job opportunities.
- Workshops and seminars to enhance job readiness.
- University job portals may list exclusive opportunities for students.

[* *Check our job fairs and network](#jslist4)

- Direct access to employers who are actively hiring.
- Chance to make a strong personal impression on potential employers.
- Gather information about companies and industries of interest.
- Networking can often lead to hidden job opportunities that aren’t publicly advertised.

[* *Do your research on the permit application for a work pass](#jslist5)

- Understanding visa requirements avoids delays or rejections in your application.
- Different countries have different rules, so research is essential.
- Some companies might sponsor your work visa, so check with potential employers.
- Have all your necessary documentation prepared ahead of time.

#### expand your horizons

Many degree holders only look at their studied field and look for a job that matches exactly what they did in their studies. They look past everything else and creates unnecessary limitations. Jobseekers should expand their horizons as their jobs do not have to be an exact reflection of their studies. It is important to be able to transfer your knowledge and skills into related fields.

#### skill development

Continuously enhance your skills and qualifications to remain competitive in the job market. Take proactive steps to enhance your skills and qualifications to meet the evolving demands of employers. This could involve enrolling in additional training programs, workshops, or online courses to acquire new skills or deepen your expertise in your field. Consider pursuing relevant certifications or professional designations that are recognised and valued by employers in your industry. Additionally, seek out opportunities to gain practical experience through internships, volunteer work, or freelance projects that allow you to apply and refine your skills in real-world settings.

#### job search checklist

Stay proactive, network effectively, and continuously develop your skills to maximise your job search success.

[* *Research](#jst)

- Gather detailed information about potential employers and industries.
- Identify job requirements and tailor your applications accordingly.
- Stay updated on trends and future demands in your field.
- Understand the workplace culture and expectations of companies you’re interested in.

[* *Preparation](#jst2)

- Develop a well-structured resume and cover letter that highlights relevant skills.
- Practice for interviews and assessments to boost confidence.
- Ensure all necessary documentation is ready, including references and certificates.
- Prepare to discuss your strengths, weaknesses, and experiences effectively.

[* *skill development](#jst3)

- Continuously work on improving both soft and technical skills.
- Take online courses or attend workshops to stay updated on industry trends.
- Gain practical experience through internships, part-time jobs, or freelancing.
- Develop problem-solving and communication skills, which are valuable in any career.

[* *utilize resources](#jst4)

- University career centers can provide invaluable support in your job hunt.
- Online job boards, LinkedIn, and industry-specific sites can widen your search.
- Seek out mentors or career coaches for personalized guidance.
- Free or low-cost courses can boost your skills and employability.

[* *professional branding](#jst5)

- A strong LinkedIn profile can serve as your digital resume.
- Maintain a consistent and professional online presence.
- Networking is easier when you have a recognizable personal brand.
- Tailor your resume and cover letters to highlight your key strengths.

[* *Stay persistent](#jst6)

- The job market is competitive, and persistence often leads to success.
- Learn from rejections and improve your applications for future opportunities.
- Stay motivated by setting small, achievable job search goals.
- Success might take time, but continuous effort leads to results.
