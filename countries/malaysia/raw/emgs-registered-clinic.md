# Registered Malaysian Clinic

New international students are required to attend a medical screening at a panel clinic in Malaysia within 7 days of their arrival date. Below is a list of all the panel clinics in Malaysia. You can also use the EMGS mobile app to navigate to a clinic near your location. This list will be updated from time to time.
