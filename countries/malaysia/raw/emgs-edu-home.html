KPJ Healthcare University (KPJU) is a private university based in Nilai, Negeri Sembilan specialising in healthcare education across nursing, medicine, pharmacy, physiotherapy, medical imaging, and healthcare management. A subsidiary of KPJ Healthcare Berhad, it gives students direct access to clinical training and internships at KPJ's network of specialist hospitals nationwide.

Kuching, Sarawak
#294 QS World Rankings 2026

Swinburne University of Technology Sarawak Campus is a private university in Kuching, Sarawak, established in 2000 as the international branch campus of Swinburne University of Technology, Melbourne. It offers foundation, undergraduate, and postgraduate programmes in engineering, science, business, computing, and design, following the same syllabus as its Australian home campus.
