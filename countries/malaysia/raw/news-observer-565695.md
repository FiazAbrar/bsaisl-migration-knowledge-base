### 

# Malaysian visa applications temporarily suspended

## 

Published : Tuesday, 10 February, 2026 at 9:12 PM

*Observer Online Report*

[![](https://observerbd.com/images/ico_plus.png)](javascript:void(0);) [![](https://observerbd.com/images/ico_minus.png)](javascript:void(0);) [![](https://observerbd.com/images/ico_reg.png)](javascript:void(0);)

![](/2026/02/10/ob_61.1770736528.jpg)

X

Malaysian High Commission in Dhaka has announced that its online visa platform, MyVISA, will be temporarily unavailable until further notice due to ongoing system maintenance and stabilisation work.\
\
In a notification issued on Tuesday, the High Commission stated that all visa applications and approval processes will be paused during this period. \
\

Services on the website will resume only after the maintenance is completed, with an official announcement to follow.\
\
Applicants are advised to note that no access to MyVISA website; including submitting new applications or checking approvals, will be possible until the system is back online.\
\
