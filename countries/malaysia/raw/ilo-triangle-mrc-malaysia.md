TRIANGLE in ASEAN

Migrant Worker Resource Centres in Malaysia

TRIANGLE in ASEAN delivers assistance directly to migrant workers and their communities through a network of Migrant Worker Resource Centres (MRCs). These MRCs are managed in partnership with government institutions, trade unions and civil society organizations, and provide a range of services in countries of origin and destination. Below you may find the contact details of partners implementing MRCs in Malaysia.
