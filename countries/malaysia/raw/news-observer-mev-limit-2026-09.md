### 

# Malaysia limits multiple entry visas for B'desh, 30 nations

## 

Published : Friday, 4 September, 2026 at 9:56 AM

*Observer Online Report*

[![](https://observerbd.com/images/ico_plus.png)](javascript:void(0);) [![](https://observerbd.com/images/ico_minus.png)](javascript:void(0);) [![](https://observerbd.com/images/ico_reg.png)](javascript:void(0);)

![Collected Photo](/2026/09/04/ob_76.1788494476.jpg)

X

Collected Photo

Malaysia restricted multiple-entry visa (MEV) facilities for tourists from 31 countries across South Asia and Africa, including Bangladesh.

\

Under the new policy, Bangladeshi tourists will generally receive single-entry visas instead of one-year multiple-entry social visit visas. The Malaysian Immigration Department published the list of affected nations under the revised framework.

\

According to a directive from the Malaysian Immigration Department, multiple-entry visas will no longer be granted indiscriminately to citizens of all countries effective September 1, 2026.

\

However, multiple-entry visas will remain available for specific approved travel categories, including business, medical treatment, fly-and-cruise packages, and wedding tourism.

\

The official notification published on the Malaysian Immigration Department's website clarified that the restriction applies strictly to visit visas and social travel.

\

The policy change does not affect employment visas or work permits for Bangladeshi workers currently employed in Malaysia.
