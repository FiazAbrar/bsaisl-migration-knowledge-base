Appeal Procedure

Appeal Procedure

Employers or Agencies who wish to appeal for their foreign worker may register the appeal case within two (2) weeks from the certification date.

Appeal Registration Process

Notification

For a case allowed to appeal, the employer will be notified via email advising them to submit an appeal within two weeks from the certification date.

Registration

Registration of appeal can be done either through Employer/ Agency Portal at the portal.fomema.my 

Monitor

Employer / Agency can monitor the progress of the appeal case via respective Employer Portal at the portal.fomema.my

Resolve

All appeal cases will be resolved soonest possible once the documents are completed, and the case will be closed if no documents are received after 4 weeks.

Cases Not Allowed to Appeal

Tuberculosis (TB)

Audit of initial chest X-ray image by Panel Consultant Radiologist of FOMEMA confirms the present of radiological signs of TB.

Malaria

Malaria screening test result was positive and confirmed with BFMP test as malaria parasite present.

Pregnancy

Urine pregnancy test result was positive and confirmed with serum β-hCG test result as positive.
Note: β-hCG = Beta-Human Chorionic Gonadotropin

HIV

HIV 1 & 2 Ab was reactive during FOMEMA Medical Examination.

Appeal Process Flow

Register Appeal

Employer/ Agency may register the appeal via Employer / Agency Portal at portal.fomema.my

Additional Tests

All additional test results must be submitted to FOMEMA soonest possible. The case will be closed if no document is received after 4 weeks.

Please refer to the “Appeal To-Do List” for the list of additional test. portal.fomema.my

Check Appeal Status

The status of the appeal can be monitored from the Employer Portal at portal.fomema.my

Appeal Process Flow

Notification

The employer/agency will receive an email notification for cases that are eligible for appeal.

Registration

Registration of appeal cases on the FOMEMA Online Portal by the employer/agency.

Appeal Fee

All payments related to appeal cases are to be made to FOMEMA.

Case Close

The appeal will be resolved promptly once all documents are submitted. If no documents are received by Week 4, the case will be closed.

Appeal Status

The appeal status can be tracked on the designated portal.

Test Results Submission

The additional test results must be submitted by the appeal panel  to FOMEMA no later than four (4) weeks after the appeal registration date.

Appeal Status

STATUS INDICATION
Appeal submitted Application for appeal has been registered and the officer of the case is reviewing the case.
Pending Payment Employer / Agency has to make payment via FOMEMA Online Portal.
Doctor Examination Case already reviewed by the officer in-charge and for additional tests to be carried out. Employer need to refer GP.
Document Complete All forms/results have been received and case will be submitted for the next Appeal Committee hearing (weekly basis).
Awaiting Approval The case is being reviewed by Appeal Committee
Closed appeal The case has been closed either as successful or unsuccessful*. Kindly proceed to Immigration Department for further action. To print the appeal decision letter, please click “Decision Letter” button. *Reasons for unsuccessful appeal: Additional test results are abnormal or Documents for appeal process not complete.
