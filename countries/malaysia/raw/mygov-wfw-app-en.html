    3. Topics
    5. Working for Workers (WFW) Application

    • Topic
      Working for Workers (WFW) Application

    • Category

      CareerEmployee Rights and Benefits

    The Working for Workers (WFW) application is a digital initiative developed by the Ministry of Human Resources (KESUMA) to strengthen workers’ rights in Malaysia.

    This application provides an official and easily accessible channel for workers to directly report employment-related complaints to the authorities.

    Key Features of the WFW App:

    • Official Complaint Channel

    Workers can lodge complaints about employers, unfair working conditions, unpaid wages, or any violation of the Employment Act.

    • Information on Workers’ Rights 

    Provides references on labour laws, workers’ rights, and other support services.

    • 24/7 Access

    Complaints can be submitted at any time without the need to physically visit a labour office.
