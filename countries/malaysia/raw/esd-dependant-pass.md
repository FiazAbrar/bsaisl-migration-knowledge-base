## Dependant Pass

Employment Pass holders are eligible to apply for a Dependant Pass for their spouses, children under 18 years of age, and legally adopted children under 18 years of age.

- #### Before you apply

  Principal holder must have a valid Employment Pass

- #### Where to apply

  Go to the ESD website, log in through the company’s ESD account and submit the application

- #### Duration of pass

  Duration of pass is based on the principal applicant\
  \*If the passport validity of applicant is less than the principal applicant, the duration will be based on applicant’s passport validity

- #### Work rights

  Applicants are required to apply for the Employment Pass

\*Click here to find out how to apply for the [Employment Pass](https://esd.imi.gov.my/portal/expatriates/myxpats/key-services/employment-pass/)
