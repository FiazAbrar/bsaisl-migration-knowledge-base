Information
Immigration facilities are granted to citizens of the People's Republic of China who hold a Public Affairs Passport or an Ordinary Passport with a minimum validity of six (6) months, allowing them to enter Malaysia without a visa for social visit purposes.
Holders of a Public Affairs Passport or an Ordinary Passport are eligible to enter and stay in Malaysia for a period not exceeding 30 days per entry.
Each entry allows a stay of up to 30 days, and the total stay within any 180-day period is limited to 90 days.
