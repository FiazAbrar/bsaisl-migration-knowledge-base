Investor Pass ![](/assets/images/icon_tooltip.gif) The foreign investors are made up of the following three (3) categories:\
\
1. New Investor - potential investors who have no investment record in Malaysia.\
\
2. Investor in Pipeline – potential investors who have been identified interested in making investments or are currently in negotiations with the Malaysian Government or have applied to MIDA for project approvals.\
\
3. Existing Investor - investors who have investment records in Malaysia and SSM registered companies, but the investor is not under the employment of the company or any companies in Malaysia.
