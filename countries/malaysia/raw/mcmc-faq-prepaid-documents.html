Prepaid Registration

What are the documents required for Prepaid SIM card registration?

The Service Providers and their representatives would require verification against the original identification of the following: -
 

Malaysian MyKad issued by National Registration Department
Malaysian Permanent Residents MyPR card issued by National Registration Department
Malaysian Armed Forces MyTentera card issued by National Registration Department
Foreigners
Passport, Work Permit Identification cards & Student Identification cards
 
Other documents issued by the National Registration Department (“NRD”) and Immigration Department of Malaysia (“IDM”)
  1. Temporary Identity Card (MyKas);
  2. IMM 13 (limited to the state of Sabah and Wilayah Persekutuan Labuan only and verification is required from IDM prior to activation of services by Service Provider).
 
 
Please bring along the original documents for registration.
