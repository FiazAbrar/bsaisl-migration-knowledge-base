# On Arrival

Malaysia welcomes you with open arms as you embark on your exciting study journey.

From the moment you arrive, we are here to assist you with everything you need – from navigating immigration procedures to finding accommodation and adjusting to campus life.

**1** Arrival at the Airport

Welcome to Malaysia. As an international student, your journey to Malaysia begins at the airport.

##### LINKS

- [Malaysian Immigration](https://www.imi.gov.my/)
- [Malaysia Arrival Digital Card (MDAC)](https://www.imi.gov.my/index.php/en/pengumuman/malaysia-digital-arrival-card-mdac/)

#### Immigration and Customs

Upon arrival, international students may proceed to the ISAC counter at the Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA Terminal 1 and KLIA Terminal 2).

Check the list of the required documents here: [https://visa.educationmalaysia.gov.my/guidelines](https://visa.educationmalaysia.gov.my/guidelines)

Details on ISAC, click [here](/plan-your-studies/start-to-prepare/international-student-arrival-centre-(isac))

#### Transportation from the Airport

There are various modes of transportations available. However, you may also check with your university if they provide any pickup services.

- KLIA Transit / KLIA Express: The easiest way to the Kuala Lumpur City is using this train service.
- Taxis: Available at designated taxi stands. Use airport-authorized taxis or book via reputable apps like Grab.
- Airport Shuttles: Many airports offer shuttle services to major cities and universities. Check with your university if they provide a pickup service.
- Public Transit: Trains and buses are available at most major airports. Follow the signs or ask airport staff for directions.

**2** First Steps in Malaysia

Settling down in a foreign country can be overwhelming. It's a big adjustment, and it's natural to feel a mix of excitement, and anticipation. Worry not, here are some things to prepare ahead.

#### Temporary Accommodation

- University Dormitories: Check if the university offers short-term accommodation for new arrivals.
- Hotels/Hostels: Nearby hotels or hostels are a good option for temporary stays. Look for student discounts or special rates.
- Booking Tips: Use online platforms like Booking.com or Agoda to find suitable temporary accommodation. Read reviews and check the location's proximity to your university.

#### University Check-In

- Visit your university's international student office or designated check-in point.
- Submit required documents: passport, visa, acceptance letter, and any additional forms provided by the university.
- Receive your student ID card, welcome package, and orientation schedule.
- Attend orientation sessions to learn about campus resources, academic expectations, and student life.
- Participate in tours to familiarize yourself with the campus layout and facilities.

\

**3** Settling In

Before you embark on your exciting journey to Malaysia, ensure you have a few essentials in place. Things like opening a local bank account will streamline your financial transactions and provide convenient access to your funds. Additionally, a local SIM card with affordable rates will keep you connected with friends, family, and university resources.

#### Getting a Local SIM Card

- Major Mobile Service Providers: Maxis, Celcom, Digi, and U Mobile are popular options.
- Compare prepaid and postpaid plans based on your usage.
- Prepaid plans are flexible and easy to top-up.
- Postpaid plans offer better rates for long-term use but require a contract.

[Telecom and Internet Services](/living-in-malaysia/tips-on-everyday-life/telecom-and-internet-services "Accommodation for Students")\
\

**CONCLUSION**

Adapting to a new culture can be challenging, but with these tips, you will find it easier to integrate and enjoy your time in Malaysia. Respect the local customs, practice the language, and you will gain a richer experience. Welcome to your new adventure!
