## TOSHOST managed-solution FAQ

Everything you need to know about our enterprise managed hosting, global infrastructure and expert migration services.

### What is included in TOSHOST Managed Service?

Managed Excellence is a full-service layer including 24/7 proactive monitoring, an enterprise WAF, performance tuning for Laravel and WordPress, and daily immutable backups, all handled by L3 engineers.

### What levels of application support are provided?

We go beyond the OS to tune LiteSpeed/Nginx, MySQL/PostgreSQL and framework configurations for stacks like Laravel and WordPress to ensure maximum throughput.

### How is hardware reliability managed on dedicated servers?

We use proactive monitoring to detect hardware wear early. In the event of a failure we provide priority replacement with zero labour costs, backed by daily immutable backups.

### What network and uptime SLA do you guarantee?

We operate a multi-homed 10Gbps backbone with 100% NVMe storage and provide a financially-backed 99.99% uptime SLA with L7 DDoS protection.

### Is the free migration truly zero-downtime?

Yes. Our human-led concierge migration performs a live sync of files and databases with integrity testing before any DNS change, ensuring zero service interruption.

### How secure is the dedicated backup portal?

The portal uses immutable, multi-location backup technology that protects snapshots from ransomware, with one-click download from redundant off-site storage.

### Will I talk to a human engineer or a chatbot?

Support tickets are handled by our engineers. The client portal also has an AI assistant for quick knowledge-base answers.

### Can I choose my data center location?

Yes. We offer multiple strategic data center locations worldwide so you can select the region closest to your audience for the lowest latency and best performance.
