Empowering Communities

Community Engagement Safe Migration Policy Advocacy Partnerships Reintegration
  • Providing counselling, legal aid, safe migration information, and reintegration support.
  • Mobilizing migrants, returnees, and families through local outreach.
  • Strengthening platforms for migrant leadership and community advocacy.
  • Awareness campaigns on safe, rights-based, and gender-responsive migration.
  • Supporting victims of fraud, abuse, wage theft, and contract violations.
  • Coordinating with government and partners to improve protection mechanisms.
  • Influencing policies at local, national, regional, and global levels.
  • Engaging ministries, parliamentarians, and institutions to strengthen governance.
  • Advancing migrants’ representation in national dialogues.
  • Civil society, human rights groups, trade unions, NGOs, and private sector.
  • Government agencies and local authorities to improve service delivery.
  • International organizations to align national systems with global standards.
  • Economic and social reintegration support for returnee migrants.
  • Skills-building and livelihood strengthening to reduce vulnerability.
  • Promoting migration as a driver of sustainable development.

Civil Society Campaign

Migrant Rights Are Human Rights

#Resist • #Reclaim • #Realise

Human Rights are Non-Negotiable!

Despite global commitments, migrant rights violations continue to escalate across various regions. Civil society and migrant voices are increasingly silenced. The time has come to reclaim power, demand accountability, and ensure justice for all migrants.

Resist

Stand against criminalisation & exploitation.

Reclaim

Reclaim dignity & representation in governance.

Realise

Transform commitments into concrete action.
