Foreign Workers 
Personnel

Driven by the growing demand for Foreign Construction Workers, Construction Labor Exchange Center Berhad (CLAB) meets the needs of Organizations and Companies to bring in and redistribute Foreign Workers to various industries in Malaysia. With projects growing rapidly in the construction industry, we are responsible for handling all applications for Foreign Construction Workers.

Please log on to 
https://www.clab.com.my/foreign-worker
 for further information.
