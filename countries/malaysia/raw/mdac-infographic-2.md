MALAYSIA DIGITAL ARRIVAL CARD (MDAC) FOR FOREIGN VISITORS
WHO IS ELIGIBLE?
All foreign citizens travelling to Malaysia must complete the Malaysia Digital Arrival Card (MDAC)
Effective 01 January 2024, completion of this MDAC will be mandatory, except those list below:
- CITIZENS OF SINGAPORE;
- DIPLOMATIC AND OFFICIAL PASPPORT HOLDERS;
- MALAYSIA PERMANENT RESIDENTS AND LONG-TERM PASS HOLDERS;
- GENERAL CERTIFICATE OF IDENTITY (GCI) BRUNEI DARUSSALAM HOLDERS;
- BRUNEI MALAYSIA FREQUENT TRAVELER FACILITY HOLDERS;
- THAILAND BORDER PASS HOLDERS; AND
- INDONESIA CROSS-BORDER TRAVEL DOCUMENT (PLB) HOLDERS.
