General

**DEFINITIONS**

01. **"Applicant(s)"** refers to any individual submitting Malaysia eVISA application to the High Commission / Embassy / Consulate.
02. **"Embassy / Consulate"** refers to the High Commission / Embassy and / or Consulate General of Malaysia.
03. **"eVAL/ eVDR / VDR"** is a pre-conditional Visa Approval Letter issued by Immigration Department of Malaysia before approving the actual visa through eVISA platform.
04. **"eVISA"** is an electronic visa that enables foreign nationals to enter Malaysia.
05. **"eVISA for Medical"** is a visa that is issued through an electronic / online eVISA platform to foreign healthcare travellers / patients under MHTP to enable the individual to enter and stay in Malaysia for days subject to nationality eligibility with the purpose of seeking healthcare treatment, procedure or services.
06. **"eVISA paltform"** is an online application platform that enables foreign nationals to apply for an electronic visa to enter Malaysia at the comfort of their convenience.
07. **"eVISA Processing fee"** refers to the processing fee charged for visa processing system. eVISA convenience fee is not included in the processing fee.
08. **"eVISA Convenience Fee"** refers to services other than the basic service that provide convenience to applicant.
09. **"Immigration Officer"** refers to official(s) of Immigration Department of Malaysia.
10. **"Malaysia Healthcare Travel Council (MHTC)"** is an agency under the Ministry of Health Malaysia, to facilitate the overall development of the Malaysian healthcare travel industry, by coordinating industry collaborations and building valuable public-private partnerships, at home and abroad.
11. **"MHTC Member"** refers to private healthcare providers such as hospitals, ambulatory care services and dental services that are registered under MHTC membership programme.
12. **"MYVISA"** refers to the online visa application provided and maintain by the Malaysian Government including IT Infrastructure and support services according to the working procedures set by the High Commission / Embassy / Consulates.
13. **"Receipt"** refers to the document, which confirms that an eVISA online application has been made and payment of all fees has been accepted.
14. **"Visa fee"** refers to the visa fees payable to the Government of Malaysia, which will be accepted and collected by MYVISA at the rate prescribed by the Fees (Passport and Visas) Order 1967.

04. An **eVISA for Social Visit** have 2 types of visa **Single Entry Visa (SEV)** for a single journey to Malaysia for nationalities that required visa subjected to eligibility of nationality for each visit and a **Multiple Entry Visa (MEV)** for multiple journeys for all nationalities **EXCEPT** for Israel and North Korea with 30 days stay allowed for each entry, provided that the applicant's passport remains valid.
05. eVISA for **Visa Without Reference (VTR)** is valid for a country who needed visa for a single entry or multiple entry to Malaysia for the purpose of social visit.
06. eVISA for **Visa With Reference (VDR)** is valid for an applicant who have Visa Approval Letter who needed visa for a single journey to Malaysia for the purpose of study, professional / temporary employment, medical (under Medical Health Tourism Council) and social, subject to the eligibility of nationality. Upon arrival in Malaysia, they are required to obtain their long - term pass.
07. Applicants **under 18 years** old are **COMPULSORY** to upload their Birth Certificate.
08. Any applicant, who breach the Malaysian law, will be subject to criminal proceedings and administrative actions by the Government of Malaysia. Applicants are not allowed to accept any unauthorized employment, or attend school, or represent the foreign information media during their stay in Malaysia.
09. **Single Entry Visa (SEV)** obtained through the eVISA system allows for single journey to Malaysia within 6 months from the date of issuance. However, for Foreign Workers, Fly & Cruise, and Wedding Tourism categories, this visa is valid for 3 months from the date of issuance, with the condition that their passport remains valid.
10. **Multiple Entry Visa (MEV)** validity is three (3) months for the Fly & Cruise and Wedding Tourism social visitor categories, and for the Medical and Business Visitor categories, the validity is six (6) months from the date of issuance. The permissible duration of stay in Malaysia is 30 days per entry, provided that the passport remains valid.
