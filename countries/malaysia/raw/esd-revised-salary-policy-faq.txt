4 What is the definition of a Succession Plan?  A succession plan is a structured plan by the employer to
                                                prepare local employees to replace expatriate roles within the
                                                stipulated employment period.

                                                This plan may include the following:
                                                      Identifying roles and responsibilities that will be taken
                                                          over by local employees.
                                                      Training and mentoring, including knowledge transfer
                                                          from expatriates to local employees.
                                                      An appropriate timeline to ensure local employees are
                                                          ready with the required skills and capabilities; and
                                                      Planning to ensure smooth operations, so the
                                                          transition does not affect productivity or organisational
                                                          performance.

                                                The succession plan serves as a guide for employers in
                                                managing human resources sustainably while ensuring
                                                expatriates contribute tangible value to local talent
                                                development.
