Manage Booking
Enter the six-character airline PNR/booking reference and the passenger's last name.
Select Search, open the booking, and choose Exchange.
Select the required flight date and complete the applicable fare-difference or fee payment.
For further assistance, contact ibebiman@biman.gov.bd.
