# Malaysia doc: open "Need to know" items

Extracted from malaysia.src.md (7 Nov 2026). Grouped by the section they sit in.

- **Work visa: company worker (PLKS):** the maximum a private agency may take from a worker outside the 10,000 zero-cost places. News reports say that in Apr 2026 the two governments agreed that employers would bear the full cost of recruitment, at zero cost to workers, with fewer intermediaries and a Malaysian AI-based recruitment system, and that in Jun 2026 they agreed no syndicate would be involved (unofficial, not verified).

- **Work visa: company worker (PLKS):** whether every Malaysia job order's letter is posted. The letters seen from Oct 2023 to Feb 2025 also have an English "Recruitment Permission Letter" page with a permission ID such as MY-RL1274-2025-0000883 and a "block visa number", with the number of visas and their issue date: in some letters this is a Malaysian approval reference (KSM/FWCMSBW490000267/QA03/1 or KDN/16031/PHAWP2146128), in others a reference beginning BHC-LW (BHC-LW-36). The Sep 2025 letter has no English page. Compare the letter with what the agency tells you: the employer's name, the salary (RM1,500 in the letters from Oct 2023 to Feb 2025, RM1,700 in the Sep 2025 letter and in BOESL's Nov 2026 circular) and the maximum fee. A one-line BMET notice of 27 Sep 2023 asks agencies to send demand letters to its IT branch for recruitment permission for any country, including Malaysia, "ব্যতীত" (except, or without) the Malaysian KSM, KDN or FWCMS number, and says that otherwise BMET can't give permission; the notice doesn't explain this further. Among the letters seen, some cite a Malaysian KSM or KDN reference, others a reference beginning BHC-LW, and the Sep 2025 letter the Bangladesh High Commission's attestation letter.

An IMI briefing of Apr 2026, hosted by an industry association, gives RM750 for plantation and agriculture (unofficial, not verified); IMI's own table is the official rule.

- **Work visa: company worker (PLKS):** whether Malaysia requires a police clearance certificate for PLKS workers; it isn't in IMI's document list for the visa. If the agency asks for one, apply yourself at [pcc.police.gov.bd](https://pcc.police.gov.bd/) with a passport valid at least 3 months; the present address you give must be one in your passport. Upload a scan of your passport data page attested by a first-class gazetted officer, and your NID or birth certificate if the passport has no address. The fee is Tk 1,500 on the portal (treasury challan or card), while Bangladesh Police's own guidance says Tk 500. Track it by SMS: type PCC S and your reference number and send it to 26969; the police verify you at your present address. If you apply from abroad, someone else can collect it with an attested authorisation letter giving their name and NID number.

- **Business visit:** whether Bangladesh is one of the eligible nationalities. For those eligible, a business multiple-entry visa is valid 6 months with up to 30 days per entry; older official pages still describe 3 to 12 months. The costs are the eVISA's: RM20 visa fee for Bangladesh, RM105 processing and a card or e-wallet charge; a decision takes 2 to 7 working days, and a refusal comes without reasons and with no published appeal. Fly & Cruise visas need flight and cruise bookings and the sailing schedule, and Wedding Tourism visas a return ticket, proof of journey or itinerary; both are valid 3 months. A social visit pass doesn't allow professional work; that needs a work pass such as the Professional Visit Pass. Getting a pass by a false statement is an offence, with a fine up to RM10,000 or up to 5 years' jail. A spouse who comes along needs a visa of their own, as every Bangladeshi does; one MyVISA account can hold applications for up to 5 people (see Tourist visa, Step 2).

- **Professional Visit Pass:** how long a standard PVP takes through ESD. If refused, only the company can appeal, within 14 days in ESD Online; the appeal is decided within 30 working days. False information in an application can lead to rejection, cancellation of the approval, withdrawal of the pass, immigration action and blacklisting.
