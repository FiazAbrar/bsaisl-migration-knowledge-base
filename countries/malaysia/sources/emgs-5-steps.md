**1** Discover Malaysia

Uncover what Malaysia has to offer -  be it a quality education or a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity. Geographically diverse and culturally rich, Malaysia is a study destination of choice with its UNESCO World Heritage sites and uniquely Malaysian offerings.

Known for its affordable, high-quality standard of living and a thriving economy, Malaysia has grown to be a key economic player in the region and its an attractive destination for students, tourists, businesses and investors.

**2** CHOOSE WHAT TO STUDY

Picking the right university course is crucial. Choose a program that aligns with your interests and strengths for a fulfilling university experience. Malaysia's growing economy offers exciting opportunities in fields like medicine, manufacturing, and technology.

**3** PLAN YOUR BUDGET

Planning ahead for your budget is important for your peace of mind.

- Cover necessities like comfortable accommodation and sumptuous food
- Travel and sightsee Malaysia
- Delve into your passion and hobbies such as a dance class or visit to the cinema
- Plan for medical emergencies
