## Malaysia Rumah Kedua Ku (MM2H)

**NOTE:**

1.  All document must be translate in English by Institute Language and Literature. Link [http://lamanweb.dbp.gov.my/](http://lamanweb.dbp.gov.my/) or Embassy or Consulate General of Malaysia at abroad link [https://www.kln.gov.my/web/guest/malaysian-mission](https://www.kln.gov.my/web/guest/malaysian-mission).
2.  Certified True Copy (CTC) for of marriage certificate or birth certificate by Embassy or Consulate General of Malaysia abroad link [https://www.kln.gov.my/web/guest/malaysian-mission](https://www.kln.gov.my/web/guest/malaysian-mission) or public notary at Malaysia.

[8. APPLICATION GUIDELINES FOR PERMISSION TO PART-TIME WORKING]()

- Authorization Letter from the company (designation in company);

- Cover Letter explaining company activities and job justification;

- Offer Letter from the company (must mention duration of employment contract, duration of working hour and remuneration);

- Latest print out Companies Commission of Malaysia Form 9,24 & 49;

- Business licence from local authority;

- Recommendation letter from related agency (which ever related);

  (i) Education – Ministry of Education;

  (ii) Health – Ministry of Health;

  (iii) Manufacturing – Malaysia Industrial Development Authority;

  (iv) Securities – Securities Commission;

  (v) Banking. Financial and insurance – Central Bank Malaysia;

- Advertisement in local newspaper/ jobstreet for the intake of local citizen;

- Completed DP10 Form;

- Participant’s resume;

- Certified copy of participant’s passport; and

- Personal income tax relief letter from Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia (LHDN)-salary above RM10,000.00.

- Original passport (present at Malaysia Embassy/High Commision);
- Copy of conditional approval letter; and
- Latest exit endorsement/stamping cop from Malaysia

**GUIDELINES FOR WITHDRAWAL OF FIXED DEPOSIT**

APPLICATION GUIDELINES FOR WITHDRAWAL OF FIXED DEPOSIT FOR THE EXISTING MM2H PARTICIPANTS IN MALAYSIA

CLIENT CHARTER: 3 (THREE) WORKING DAYS

1. RESIDENTIAL PURCHASE

- Applicant or representative MM2H company must be present;
- Intention letter for individual/ agency representative letter for MM2H company;
- Copy of Passport (Particular Page and visa MM2H);
- Copy of Conditional Approval Letter;
- Copy of Fixed Deposit Certificate;
- Copy of sales & purchase agreement (all pages);
- Copies of receipt of a house payment.

NOTES:

- Participant may submit a copy of the sales and purchase agreement (SPA) without the receipt of a home payment provided that the SPA is validated (Certified True Copy) by the relevant authorities.
- Participant is allowed to request partial withdraw of Residential Properties ONLY.
- Duty stamp page and signature page MUST be certified.

2. EDUCATION

- Applicant or representative MM2H company must be present;
- Intention letter for individual/ agency representative letter for MM2H company;
- Copy of Passport (Particular Page and visa MM2H);
- Copy of Conditional Approval Letter;
- Copy of Fixed Deposit Certificate;
- Copies enrollment fee from school.

3. MEDICAL EXPENSES

- Applicant or representative MM2H company must be present;
- Intention letter for individual/ agency representative letter for MM2H company;
- Copy Of Passport Particular Page;
- Copy Of MM2H Visa;
- Copy Of Conditional Approval Letter;
- Copy Of Fixed Deposit Certificate;
- \*Copy proof of payment for Medical Expenses (Receipt)

NOTES:

- Participant may submit a receipt of payment for medicines purchased at Pharmacy or other related fee such as consultation, hospital admission as proof of medical expenses.

4. INTEREST 

- Applicant or representative MM2H company must be present;
- Intention letter for individual/ agency representative letter for MM2H company;
- Copy of Passport (Particular Page and visa MM2H);
- Copy of Conditional Approval Letter; and
- Copy of Fixed Deposit Certificate.

5. TRANSFER OF BANK

- Applicant or representative MM2H company must be present;
- Intention letter for individual/ agency representative letter for MM2H company;
- Copy of Passport (Particular Page and visa MM2H);
- Copy of Conditional Approval Letter; and
- Copy of Fixed Deposit Certificate.

6. JOINT OR SEPARATE ACCOUNT/CHANGE TERM OR TENURE/ CHANGE BRANCH OF BANK/ CONVENTIONAL BANK 

- Applicant or representative MM2H company must be present;
- Intention letter for individual/ agency representative letter for MM2H company;
- Copy of Passport (Particular Page and visa MM2H);
- Copy of Conditional Approval Letter; and
- Copy of Fixed Deposit Certificate.

**GENERAL NOTES:**

- MM2H participant are allowed to withdraw partial of the required Fixed Deposit starting **from the second year onwards** (effective from the date of MM2H Social Visit Pass endorsement in the passport) for approved expenses related to residential purchase, children’s education expenses, medical expenses as per below:

\(a\) RM50,000 for aged 50 years and above; and

\(b\) RM150,000 for aged below 50 years.

However, MM2H participant are required to maintain the balanced of Fixed Deposit until they terminate their participation from Programme as per below:

\(a\) RM 100,000 for aged 50 years and above; and

\(b\) RM 150,000 for aged below 50 years.

- Withdrawal of Fixed Deposit is on reimbursement basis and release of money will be made based on the amount requested.
- **All application for withdrawal of fixed deposit is no longer permitted to be submitted by e-mail.**
- Any incomplete documents will not be processed.
- Complete documents will be process within three (3) working days.
