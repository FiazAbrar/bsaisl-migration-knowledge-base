###### Note:

1. _The Application Fee is payable at the Pre-Approval stage and is non-refundable._
2. _The Processing Fee (inclusive of 8% SST) is payable only upon approval of the application._
3. _If an application is not approved, the applicant is not required to pay the Processing Fee._
4. _All fees are charged per applicant and are subject to change in accordance with prevailing government policies._
5. _The Grand Total reflects the combined amount of Pre-Approval and Post-Approval fees for each applicant._
