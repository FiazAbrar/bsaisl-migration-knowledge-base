# How to Get Your Malaysian Student Visa: The Complete EMGS Guide (2026)
ApplyBoard | September 23, 2026

## Understanding the eVAL (Electronic Visa Approval Letter)
The eVAL is the single most important document in your application. Formerly issued as a physical letter (VAL), it is now fully electronic — a downloadable PDF available directly through the EMGS portal once your application reaches 70% completion.

### Step 3 — Track Your Application
Once submitted, use your EMGS ID to track your application status at any time. The progress bar moves through key milestones:
- 32–35% — EMGS issues its internal Approval Letter; your application is forwarded to Immigration
- 70% — Immigration has approved your eVAL; download it from the EMGS portal
- 100%— Student Pass sticker issued and ready for collection

**What happens if my student visa application is rejected?**
You can appeal or reapply with corrected or additional documents. Your institution's international student office can guide you through the appeals process. Common rejection reasons include incomplete documentation, a failed medical screening, or unmet academic requirements.

**Can I apply for a Malaysian student visa without going through EMGS?**
No. All international student visa applications must be submitted through the EMGS system, either by your institution or directly through the EMGS portal. You cannot apply to the Immigration Department independently, and entering Malaysia on a tourist visa with the intention of converting it to a Student Pass is not permitted.

### Step 7 — Student Pass Endorsement
Your institution submits your passport and supporting documents to the Immigration Office. Processing generally takes another 2–4 weeks. You'll then receive your passport back with the Student Pass sticker, along with your i-Kad (identity card for foreign nationals) and insurance card.
