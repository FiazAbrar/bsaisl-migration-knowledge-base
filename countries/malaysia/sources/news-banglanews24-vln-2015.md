# Malaysia visa fee lacks transparency

Senior Correspondent

Publish: 12 December, 2015 15:35

**Dhaka:** The intending tourists to Malaysia are counting money six times higher than the fixed Tk 600, which is inscribed on the sticker.

There are allegations that Malaysian high commission in Dhaka appointed the organization called Visa Luar Negar (VLN). It is taking Taka 2,500 per visa.

Like many other countries Malaysia has entrusted their passport and visa documentation work to a private organization. But question arises about the transparency of the organization. VLN has been taking this excess money for the last two years.

On the other hand, the visa expectants alleged that from past August none was given multiple-visa that has shattered the hopes of the tourists who wants to visit several times. These people are to seek visa repeatedly that is helping the VLN financially.

The travel agents alleged that they cannot directly deposit passport for processing to VLN. The high commission has nominated 24 agents. The nominated or selected agents can only deal with VLN. Recently permission of some travel agents has been cancelled in accusation of irregularities.

The system has become so much complicated that one has to go to four doors to go to Malaysia enhancing the cost manifold.

An agent wishing anonymity told banglanews, 'We are asked to deposit Taka 3,100 per visa though the sticker bears the figure of Tk 600'.

There are booths to deposit money in the selected visa centers. But to VLN, money is to deposit through bank draft of Tk 3,100.
