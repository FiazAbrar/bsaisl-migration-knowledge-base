Registration Procedure

Registration Procedure

IMPORTANT

  • Employers are urged to register their foreign workers for FOMEMA medical examination test within 72 hours of their arrival in Malaysia and to be conducted at FOMEMA panel clinics
  • Medical examination results are typically released within 7 working days from the medical examination date
  • FOMEMA verifies compliance with the Ministry of Health Malaysia's standard operating procedures

As of December 2023, medical examination tests for foreign workers are mandatory to be conducted annually. Adhering to this ensures high standards of public health and national safety, aligning with our commitment to ensuring a healthy and capable workforce in Malaysia.

First Time Registration for Individual Employers

Documents Needed

  • Copy of your NRIC for Malaysian/Copy of your passport if you are non-Malaysian
  • Copy of the foreign worker's current working permit/calling VISA/Maid Online slip/Recalibration approval slip by the Immigration Department of Malaysia

First Time Registration for Companies

Documents Needed

  • Copy of your company’s registration documents (SSM Form 9 or equivalent)
  • Copy of the NRIC/Passport of contact person
  • Copy of the letter authorising the contact person to perform worker registrations
  • Copy of the foreign worker's current working permit/Calling VISA/Maid Online slip/Recalibration approval slip by the Immigration Department of Malaysia

First Time Registration for Agencies

Documents Needed

  • Copy of company’s registration document (SSM Form 9)
  • Copy of company’s registration document (SSM Form 49)
  • Copy of JTKSM license
  • Copy of Company Director’s NRIC

Appointing an Agency

Employers have the option to appoint an agency to manage the registration of foreign workers’ medical examination on the employer’s behalf. 

On Behalf of Individual Employers

Documents Needed

  • Authorisation letter from Employer
  • Copy of your NRIC for Malaysian/Copy of your passport if you are non-Malaysian
  • Copy of working permit/Calling VISA/Maid Online Slip/Recalibration approval slip by the Immigration Department of Malaysia

On Behalf of Companies

Documents Needed

  • Authorisation letter from Employer
  • Copy of your company’s registration documents (SSM Form 9 or equivalent)
  • Copy of the NRIC/Passport of contact person
  • Copy of the letter authorising the contact person to perform worker registrations
  • Copy of working permit/Calling VISA/Maid Online slip/Recalibration approval slip by the Immigration Department of Malaysia

Registration for Foreign Worker

IMPORTANT

  • There will be no documents required for registration of new arrival and renewal for foreign workers. Employers and agencies will only need to input their foreign worker details in the FOMEMA Online Portal.
  • For renewal, Employers may initiate the registration for their foreign worder at least three (3) months before the expiry of current work permit.
Registration fees for foreign workers

Special Renewal Cases

Special Renewal allows panel employers or agencies to re-register their foreign workers who were previously deemed unsuitable for employment. This re-registration process provides an opportunity for workers to undergo another round of medical examination, ensuring they meet the necessary requirements. Each application will be reviewed carefully, and subject to approval.

Documents Needed

  • Copy of foreign worker's passport
  • Copy of foreign worker's work permit/RTK slip/VDR slip/Maid Online slip
  • Special pass for expired working permit
  • Authorisation letter which can be downloaded from the FOMEMA Online Portal during registration
