## STANDARDISATION OF PASSPORT PHOTO BACKGROUND REQUIREMENT FOR EXPATRIATE APPLICATIONS

Dear Valued Clients, Further to our previous announcement regarding the enhancement of the Photo Verification feature, please be informed of an upcoming update to the passport photo requirements for expatriate applications. Effective 1 December 2026, all passport photos submitted via Xpats Gateway and ESD Online must have a white background.

## SECOND REMINDER: MyNext Onboarding and Internship Fulfilment under the 1:3 Internship Policy

Following our previous communication in May 2026, we would like to remind all companies employing expatriates to complete their onboarding on MyNext, TalentCorp’s internship placement and 1:3 Internship Policy monitoring platform and take the necessary steps to fulfil their internship requirements

## MYXPATS OPEN DAY 2026: SCHEDULE & REGISTRATION

We are pleased to inform you that the Malaysia Expatriate Services Centre (MYXpats) will be organising a series of Open Day sessions throughout 2026.

## ESD ONLINE ENHANCEMENT: PERMISSION TO STUDY AND PERMISSION TO WORK AT A SECOND LOCATION

Dear Valued Clients, Following our announcement on 15 August 2024 on the redeployment of Sub-Product services via ESD Online, additional enhancements have been implemented to further streamline the application process and better facilitate employers in meeting the needs of their expatriates.
