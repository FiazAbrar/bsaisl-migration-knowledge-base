# Who is eligible to participate? (Touch 'n Go eWallet Help Centre)

The Remittance Beta is open to foreign workers from 11 selected countries:

01. Indonesia
02. Bangladesh
03. The Philippines
04. Nepal
05. India
06. Pakistan
07. Thailand
08. Vietnam
09. Sri Lanka
10. Singapore
11. Cambodia

To participate, users must have:

a) A valid passport with at least 12 months' validity period.

b) A valid work visa in Malaysia.

c) No Malaysian bank account is required.
