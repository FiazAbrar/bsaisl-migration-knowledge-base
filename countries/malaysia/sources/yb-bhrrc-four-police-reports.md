# Malaysia: Four Bangladeshi workers file police reports after being promised construction jobs but arriving to no work & having passports taken, among other abuses

Business and Human Rights Centre. Article, 13 Mar 2024. Author: By Jason Thomas, Free Malaysia Today (Malaysia)

Summary. Date Reported: 13 Mar 2024. Location: Malaysia

Four Bangladeshi men filed police reports at the Sentul police headquarters in Malaysia, claiming that their employer had failed to provide them with jobs despite them being in the country for more than seven months. They have therefore arrived to no work or salary. The men, who said they are being housed in Chow Kit, came to Malaysia in October after being promised a monthly pay of RM1,500 to work for a construction company. The workers allege they, alongside other colleagues totally 161 workers, are suffering. They have no money to buy food. Their passports are being withheld by the employer, who asks for RM6,000 for the passports to be returned. The men said several of them had raised their plight with the labour department office in Subang Jaya on Jan 4 but received no feedback for two months. They were then directed to the department’s headquarters in Putrajaya.

Response. Action taken: The workers were informed by an officer at the headquarters that their employer had been told to either send them back to Bangladesh or help them secure new employers.

They said the employer was also asked to pay the wages due to them since they entered the country in October, adding that the office is still waiting for feedback from the employer.
