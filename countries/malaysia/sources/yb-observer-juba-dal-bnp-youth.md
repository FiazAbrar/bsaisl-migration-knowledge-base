# Juba Dal stages nationwide protest to resist campaign against Tarique

## 

Published : Friday, 18 July, 2025 at 12:00 AM

*Staff Correspondent*

[![](https://observerbd.com/images/ico_plus.png)](javascript:void(0);) [![](https://observerbd.com/images/ico_minus.png)](javascript:void(0);) [![](https://observerbd.com/images/ico_reg.png)](javascript:void(0);)

![](https://www.observerbd.com:443/2025/07/17/ob_1752772321.jpg)Juba Dal, the youth wing of the BNP, staged countrywide protests on Thursday condemning what they described as a recent insult to BNP's Acting Chairman Tarique Rahman. Leaders and activists warned that any future disparagement would be met with a strong response.\
\
Protest rallies were held simultaneously in all 64 districts and across 80 organisational units of Jubo Dal. At the central rally in front of the BNP headquarters in Nayapaltan, BNP Standing Committee member Salahuddin Ahmed said, "Although the people are demanding democracy, mob rule has taken hold across the country."\
\

From the morning, Jubo Dal activists gathered at Nayapaltan carrying placards. Following the rally, a large procession was brought out, which marched through Bijoynagar, Purana Paltan, Jatiya Press Club, Matsya Bhaban, and ended at Shahbagh intersection. The march caused a traffic standstill in Shahbagh for about 30 minutes.\
\
Salahuddin remarked that while the July movement aimed to restore democracy, it is now being undermined by what he called "mobocracy." He added, "The BNP initially supported the interim government formed on August 5, but now the people's uprising is being challenged."\
\
"Who is challenging the power of this mass uprising, and why" he asked, accusing the interim administration of failing to ensure law and order.\
\
Jubo Dal President Abdul Monayem Munna stated, "Whenever unrest breaks out, fingers are pointed at BNP. But our Acting Chairman Tarique Rahman has always opposed mob violence." He called on the Chief Advisor to identify those responsible for instigating violence and ensure their punishment.\
\

Munna further warned, "The people of Bangladesh will not tolerate any insult to Tarique Rahman. Even the Awami League has refrained from using such abusive language against our leadership."\
\
The Nayapaltan rally was presided over by Munna. Among others who addressed the gathering were Jubo Dal General Secretary Nurul Islam Nayan, Dhaka South Convener Khandaker Enamul Haque Enam, Member Secretary Rabiul Islam Nayan, Dhaka North Convener Sharifuddin Jewel, and Member Secretary Sajjadul Miraj.\
\
During the procession, participants chanted a mix of political and provocative slogans including "Jamaat-Shibir Razakar, leave Bengal now", "Break Jamaat's evil hand". "Tyranny fled to Delhi, now Razakar will flee to Pindi", "Catch Shibir members one by one and slaughter them",  "Catch Jamaat members and finish them".\
\
In Sylhet, a protest march was held at the Registry Ground. Sylhet District Jubo Dal President Advocate Mominul Islam Momin said the BNP, based on the ideology of Ziaur Rahman, remains the country's most popular party, with Jubo Dal as its front force. The procession ended at Chowhatta Point after passing through key city roads.\
\
