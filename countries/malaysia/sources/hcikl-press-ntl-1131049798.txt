                    Advisory for Indian Nationals travelling to Malaysia

Consequent to the opening of the regular international air travel to Malaysia, instances
have come to the notice of the High Commission of India that several Indian nationals
have been issued the `Not to land' (NTL) by the Malaysian Immigration authorities at
the International airport of entry in Malaysia, thus denying them entry into Malaysia.
Reasons for issuing NTL include (a) lacking sufficient funds, (b) verifiable proof of
accommodation, (c) genuine return air tickets, among others. In such cases, the Indian
nationals have to remain inside the Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) till the
airlines which brought them to Malaysia repatriates them back to the airport in India
from where they have boarded the flights. This leads to passengers remain stranded
at KLIA till their repatriation, causing undue hardships, which is surely avoidable.

All Indian nationals planning a visit to Malaysia are, therefore, advised to strictly follow
all guidelines and comply with entry related requirements issued by the Government
of Malaysia, particularly the proof of sufficient funds, verifiable proof of
accommodation, and genuine return air tickets.
