# Graduate Pass

## Graduate Pass

Apply for the Graduate Pass and enjoy an additional 12 months to work in Malaysia without the need for an employer sponsor. Immerse yourself in Malaysia's vibrant culture, gain valuable work experience, and seamlessly transition from a student pass to a work permit. Your dependents can also join you.

[apply now](/student-visa/social-visit-pass.html)

![Graduate Pass illustration](/media/cms_upload/graduate2-sec1-img.jpg)

### What is the Graduate Pass?

The Graduate Pass is a Social Visit Pass that enables international students to stay in Malaysia for a year after graduation. It lets you travel and work in permitted job sectors.

### EVERYTHING YOU SHOULD KNOW ABOUT THE GRADUATE PASS BEFORE YOU APPLY

#### Eligibility Requirements for International Students

- Your passport must be valid for at least 18 months.
- The Graduate Pass is only issued to students who have completed a Bachelor’s degree or higher.
- You must provide a letter of completion from your educational institution proving you have successfully completed your course.
- You must hold a valid Student Pass during the Graduate Pass application process.
- You must have an active health insurance policy for the duration of the Graduate Pass.
- You must provide a personal bond with a Malaysian citizen as your sponsor, who must have a minimum monthly salary of RM 1,500.
- The student must not have any legal convictions in Malaysia.

##### Additional Requirement for India and China

The extension of this service to Chinese and Indian nationals is valid until 31st December 2026. Approval will be considered on a case-by-case basis, subject to the following requirement:

The applicant must provide a letter of good conduct from either their educational institution or their respective embassy.

#### Supporting Documents for International Students

Passport bio data page

Valid student pass\
**Note:** *If the applicant was issued a check-out memo or is holding a social visit pass during processing, they are not eligible for a graduate pass.*

The passport must be valid for at least 18 months.

Confirmation of graduation or release letter from their educational institute, or a letter from the institution confirming: “The applicant is expected to complete the study on \[date\].”

Applicant must have a valid medical health insurance cover note for one year, purchased locally in Malaysia.\
**Note:** *Personal accident insurance is not accepted. If the applicant submits a monthly insurance plan, an advance payment for one year is required.*

Personal bond. The template is available on the EMGS website — [Click here (1)](/media/cms_upload/Click_Here_1_.pdf) to download the template. [Click here (2)](/media/cms_upload/Click_Here_2_.pdf) for the guideline to fill the information in the personal bond form. Please ensure the personal bond is duly stamped by the Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia (IRBM/LHDN).

Photo (Size: 217px X 280px)

A Sponsor Declaration Letter from a Malaysian citizen with a minimum monthly income of RM1,500 is required. The declaration letter must be signed and officially certified with the stamp of a Sessions Court Judge, Magistrate, Commissioner for Oaths, or Notary Public.\

The Sponsor Declaration Letter template can be downloaded by clicking [here (3)](/media/cms_upload/Sponsor_Declaration_Letter.doc)

**Note**: *For Graduate Pass applications in Sarawak, the sponsor must be a Malaysian citizen residing in Sarawak and is also required to submit the BK Form. The BK Form can be downloaded by [here (4)](/media/cms_upload/BK_form.pdf)*

Sponsor’s latest payslip for 3 months

- If employed:
  - Latest 3 months’ payslips
  - Payslips must include SOCSO, EPF deductions, and Income Tax (if applicable)\
    **Note:** *Payment vouchers are not accepted; sponsors with part‑time jobs are not accepted.*
- If self‑employed:
  - SSM license
  - Latest 3 months’ sponsor bank statements
  - Income tax (if applicable)

Sponsor's NRIC copy (front & back)

#### The Benefits of the Graduate Pass

- Multiple Entry Visa (MEV) which provides ease of entry and exit into the country.
- Dependents of the student are also eligible for the Dependent Graduate Pass with the same duration as the student.
- Can work part-time in permitted job sectors
