4. What is the type of pass
offered and its duration?
Professional Visit Pass (Pas Lawatan Ikhtisas) valid for
an initial stay of 3 to 12 months, with up to three (3) 12-
month renewals, for a maximum total stay of 48 months
subject to continued eligibility and approval.
5. Do I need to be in
Malaysia to apply?
No, you are not required to be in Malaysia to apply.
6. Am I allowed to enter or
stay in Malaysia while my
DE Rantau Nomad Pass
application is under
review?
Yes, you may remain in Malaysia on a valid visa (such as
a tourist visa) while your DE Rantau Nomad Pass
application is being processed.
However, once your application is approved while you
are in Malaysia, you will be required to exit the country. If
applicable to your nationality, you must obtain a Visa with
Reference (VDR) before re-entering Malaysia.
Upon re-entry, you may proceed with the endorsement
process to receive your DE Rantau e-Pass.
7. Is this open to all
nationalities?
The pass is open to applicants of all nationalities, with the
exception of citizens of Israel.
8. Is there any minimum age
requirement for the DE
Rantau Nomad Pass?
The main pass holder must be over eighteen (18) years
old.
9. Can I bring my family? Only immediate family members (dependent) of the main
pass holder are eligible to apply:
a. Husband / Wife/ Common-Law partner.
b. Child, adopted child or stepchild under the age of
eighteen (18) years.
c. Special needs child (no age limit) verified by experts.
d. Parent / parents to main pass holder only
e. At the time of renewal, biological children, adopted
children, and stepchildren aged above 18 years old
are not eligible for renewal under the dependant
category.
Kindly refer to Section C: Payment in the FAQ for
detailed information on the applicable fees.
10. Can my spouse work in
Malaysia under this pass?
No.
11. Can a dependent enter
Malaysia later and not
together with the main
applicant?
A dependent may either enter Malaysia together with
main pass holder or to enter individually after main pass
holder is in Malaysia.
12. Will this pass be multi-
entry?
Yes, this pass allows multiple entries.

13. What happens if my passport (or
my dependents' passport) has less
than six (6) months validity before
expiring at the point of application
and during the validity period of DE
Rantau Nomad pass?
The validity of the passport must be more than fourteen
(14) months upon submission of application. Thus, if the
validity is less than fourteen (14) months, you need to
apply for a new passport. This is to ensure compliance
with current immigration requirements and facilitate the
issuance of the renewed pass.
14. Does DE Rantau Nomad Pass
allow me to open a local bank
account?
Most banks in Malaysia do not accept Professional Visit
Pass for the applicant to open a bank account. You may
seek further clarifications with the respective bank of
interest.
15. Can my children attend school in
Malaysia while holding the DE
Rantau Nomad dependent pass?
For children of DE Rantau Nomad holders who wish to
study in Malaysia, please apply for a Student Pass via
the school.
Please check directly with the relevant schools for further
clarifications before applying. However, the Student pass
is not required for the dependent who is home schooling.
16. Can I stay in Sabah and Sarawak
using DE Rantau Nomad Pass?
At the moment, DE Rantau Nomad pass facilitates your
stay only in any states in Peninsular Malaysia and
Federal Territory of Labuan. You can still travel to Sabah
or Sarawak, but your entry into these two (2) states will
be using the tourist pass.
17. Can a tourist pass convert to a DE
Rantau pass?
No. Conversion from a tourist pass to a DE Rantau pass is
not permitted.
18. Should my outstanding tax be
cleared when applying for a
renewal?
Applicants must ensure that all outstanding tax liabilities
have been fully settled and provide proof of tax payment
as part of the renewal application.

7. What is a sponsor and who can
be a sponsor?
A sponsor is an organization that agrees to vouch for
your presence in Malaysia and assumes responsibility
for your stay, maintenance, and repatriation while you
reside in the country under the DE Rantau Nomad
Pass. For DE Rantau Nomad Pass MDEC shall be the
sponsoring organization. The following terms and
conditions apply:
1. Deposit to be paid to MDEC. The amount is
defined at Section C: Payment Question No 2
in the FAQ for specific instructions. The
payment can be made via online payment.
2. A personal bond agreement shall be signed
with MDEC as a sponsor.
The sponsorship arrangement is a regulatory
requirement under Malaysia's immigration framework
and must be completed before the pass can be
endorsed.

9. What happens if my application
is rejected?
No appeals will be accepted for rejected applications.
Applicants may re-apply in the future whenever they
meet the eligibility requirements and are ready to
proceed with a new application.
10. Will I get refund if my application
is rejected?
All applications submitted will be subject to a non-
refundable processing fee. This applies to all outcomes,
including cases of rejection, cancellation, or withdrawal
after submission.
11. Does submission of an
application guarantee approval?
No. Submission of an application does not guarantee
approval. All applications are subject to assessment
against prevailing programme requirements,
immigration regulations, security considerations and
other applicable policies.
Example: An applicant submits all required documents
and pays the application fee. During assessment,
additional compliance concerns are identified by the
relevant authority. The application may still be rejected
despite having submitted complete documents.
12. Can programme requirements
change after I submit my
application?
Yes. Immigration laws, policies, procedures and
programme requirements may be revised by the
relevant authorities from time to time. Applicants are
required to comply with the latest requirements in force.
Example: An applicant applies in January based on
existing requirements. In March, a new document
requirement is introduced. The applicant may be
required to submit the additional document before
processing can continue.
13. How will I be informed of updates
or additional document requests?
Applicants are responsible for regularly checking their
registered email address and the official DE Rantau
website for announcements, updates and requests for
information.
Example: An email requesting additional supporting
documents is sent to the registered email address.
Failure to respond may result in delays in processing.

14. Can processing take longer than
the published timeline?
Yes. Processing timelines are indicative and may be
affected by application volume, system maintenance,
public holidays, additional verifications, regulatory

Example: A temporary system disruption or additional

15. Can I request expedited
processing?
No. Applications are processed in accordance with
established procedures and assessment requirements.
Expedited processing cannot be guaranteed.
Example: An applicant requests urgent approval due to

16. Can I set my own deadline
because of travel plans, tenancy

No. Applicants should not make commitments based on
anticipated approval dates, as approval remains
subject to assessment and regulatory requirements.
Example: An applicant signs a rental agreement based
on the assumption that approval will be granted before
a specified date. If processing is delayed, the applicant
remains responsible for the commitment.
17. Should I purchase flight tickets
before receiving approval and
endorsement?
Applicants are strongly advised not to make non-
refundable travel arrangements before obtaining the
appropriate approval and completing the endorsement
process.
Example: An applicant purchases airline tickets before
pass approval is issued. If approval is delayed, any
losses incurred are the responsibility of the applicant.
18. Should I sign a tenancy
agreement, relocate my family or
pay school fees before
endorsement?
Applicants are strongly advised not to incur significant
expenses before obtaining the relevant approvals and
endorsements.
Example: An applicant pays rental deposits or school
fees before endorsement is completed. Any resulting
financial losses will not be borne by MDEC.
19. Can MDEC compensate me for
financial losses caused by
delays, policy changes or
rejection?
No. Applicants are responsible for their own travel,
accommodation, education, business and relocation
expenses incurred throughout the application process.
Example: An applicant books accommodation before
obtaining approval. MDEC is not responsible for the
costs if the application is delayed or unsuccessful.

28. I am currently on an Employment
Pass, and I plan to be on DE
Rantau Nomad Pass when my
current pass expires. How do I do
this? Do I need to leave Malaysia
before I apply for DE Rantau
Nomad Pass?
If you are currently holding an Employment Pass and
plan to transition to the DE Rantau Nomad Pass, the
process depends on the type of Employment Pass you
hold.
Employment Pass Category I & II (EP1/EP2):
· If your DE Rantau application is approved and your
current EP1/EP2 is still valid, you do not need to
leave Malaysia.
· If your EP1/EP2 expires while your application is still
pending panel approval, you are required to exit
Malaysia before continuing the process.
You may apply for the DE Rantau Nomad Pass while still
in Malaysia. To do so:
1. Obtain a release letter from your current
employer confirming the termination of your
employment.
2. Apply to shorten or cancel your Employment
Pass through the Immigration Department.
This allows you to remain in Malaysia during the
application and endorsement process without needing
to exit the country.
Employment Pass Category III:
If you are on an Employment Pass Category III, you are
required to exit Malaysia after your pass is cancelled.
You may only proceed with the endorsement of the DE
Rantau Nomad Pass after receiving approval and re-
entering Malaysia with the appropriate visa (e.g. Visa
with Reference (VDR) or eVISA, depending on your
nationality).
Please ensure your Employment Pass is properly
shortened or cancelled before endorsement, and that
you meet all documentation requirements for the DE
Rantau Nomad Pass.

No. You cannot convert your Student Pass to a DE
Rantau Nomad Pass.
However, you may apply for the DE Rantau Nomad
Pass while in Malaysia. You are required to provide a
release letter from your university to confirm the
completion of your study with a full transcript, to shorten
the student pass.

SECTION C: ENDORSEMENT PROCESS
No. Question Answer
1. What is the validity of the Approval
Letter?
The Approval Letter is valid for six (6) months from the
date of issuance and is non-extendable.
Applicants are required to complete the endorsement
process within this validity period. Failure to do so before
the expiry date will result in the need to submit a new
application, as extensions will not be granted under any
circumstances.
2. What should I do before starting
the endorsement process?
Before starting the endorsement process, applicants
must enter Malaysia using the appropriate visa or entry
permission stated in the Approval Letter (if applicable).
Applicants who require a Visa with Reference (VDR)
must obtain the VDR before travelling to Malaysia.
Upon arrival in Malaysia, applicants are required to clear
Immigration through a normal Immigration counter and
must not use the e-Gate/Autogate facility for their
first entry, as an Immigration entry stamp is required for
endorsement purposes.
Applicants must ensure that they obtain an Immigration
entry stamp and prepare all required supporting
documents including the approval letter and a copy of
Reference Visa (if applicable). Failure to do so may result
in the issuance of an entry stamp that is not suitable for
endorsement purposes.
3. When should I apply for the e-visa
or VDR?
Once your DE Rantau Nomad Pass application is
approved, you are advised to plan your travel and visa
arrangements before proceeding with the endorsement
process. The next steps will vary based on your current
location and nationality.
For Applicants Currently Abroad
If you are outside Malaysia:
Foreign nationals who require a visa to enter Malaysia
must apply for either:
· An eVISA via
https://malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my/evisa/evisa.jsp, or
· A Visa with Reference (VDR) through the
Malaysian Embassy/Consulate as stated in your
approval letter.
You must enter Malaysia using the appropriate visa to
proceed with the endorsement and issuance of your DE
Rantau e-Pass.
For Applicants Already in Malaysia
· If you are in Malaysia on a tourist or social visit pass,
you are required to exit Malaysia and re-enter once
your application is approved, using the correct visa
or entry method stated in the approval.
· If you are in Malaysia on a long-term pass (such as
an Employment Pass or Student Pass), please refer
to Section B: Questions 28 and 29 in the FAQ for
specific instructions, as additional conditions may
apply.

· Passport copy (all pages of passport);

· Valid health insurance policy covering Malaysia

· Income Tax e-Registration Slip issued by the

6. How do I complete the endorsement
process?
Once all required documents have been prepared:
1. Log in to the DE Rantau portal;
2. Open your approved application;
3. Upload the required documents;
4. Submit the endorsement application; and
5. Make the required endorsement-related payments.
MDEC and the relevant authorities will review the
submission before issuing the e-Pass.
Note:
· Applicants must remain in Malaysia until the
endorsement process is completed and the ePass
has been issued. This also applies to renewal
applications. Leaving Malaysia before completion of
endorsement may result in the application being
cancelled.
· For new applications: Endorsement must be
completed within 1 month from the date of entry into
Malaysia (based on the entry stamp).
For renewal applications: Endorsement must be
completed within 1 month from the approval date.
