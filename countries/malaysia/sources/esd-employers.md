## Employers

![](https://esd.imi.gov.my/portal/images/EMPLOYER-Chart.png)

- # BEFORE YOU SUBMIT +

  step 1Get to know ESD and create ESD account.

  step 2Complete all information needed and upload all required documentation

  step 3Submit the application. Immigration will notify if further information is needed.

- # DURING REGISTRATION +

  step 4Wait for application status. The status will be notified to company via the system

  step 5If approved, complete the Letter of Understanding and print out the document.

  step 6Make appointment with ESD to complete the registration process o receive full access to ESD website.

- # AFTER REGISTRATION +

  step 7Submit expatriate application via ESD online

  step 8Application status will be notified to the company via the system.

[REGISTER NOW](https://esd.imi.gov.my/portal/employers/registration/)\

The ESD allows companies to submit expatriate-related immigration applications via an integrated data platform. Therefore, companies will experience improvement in helping expatriates to secure approval for work permits.
