# INTERNATIONAL STUDENT ARRIVAL CENTRE (ISAC)

Welcome to ISAC - Student Arrival at the Airport, Made Easier. We are proud to be the only International Student Arrival Centre at the airport, in the world.

It is the 24-hour service centre dedicated to assisting international students arriving in Malaysia. Skip the lines and enjoy express clearance at the Kuala Lumpur International Airports

#### Overview

ISAC is a service centre that operates 24 hours and assists on the arrival of all international students before their official entry to the Malaysian Immigration Gate at the airports.

ISAC also assists in providing a first-hand experience for international students to Study in Malaysia by welcoming the students when they arrive at **KLIA Terminal 1 (T1)** and **KLIA Terminal 2 (T2)**.

ISAC KLIA T1 was established on April 2018 and ISAC KLIA T2 was established on April 2021.

#### ISAC KLIA Terminal 1 Renovations Complete! ✨

We’re excited to announce that the renovation of our International Student Arrival Centre (ISAC) counter at KLIA Terminal 1 is now complete! 🎉

The upgraded counters are now fully operational, providing a more comfortable and efficient experience for all international students arriving in Malaysia. Our friendly staff are ready to assist you with all services, as usual.

### Guide to ISAC - KLIA Terminal 1 (T1)

Arrival Hall in the main terminal building at Level 3, before the immigration clearance counters.

### Guide to ISAC - KLIA Terminal 2 (T2)

Level 3A after the exit from the Skybridge. International Students who arrive in Malaysia on Level 2 will be required to take the Passenger lifts to Level 3A.
