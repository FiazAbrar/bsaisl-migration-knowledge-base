Payments and Refunds

What payment gateway platforms are available on the FOMEMA Online Portal?

The FOMEMA Online Portal uses Fiuu (formerly known as Razer Merchant Services) as its official online payment gateway provider.

What payment methods are accepted through Fiuu?

Fiuu supports the following payment methods:
Card (Debit/Credit)
Online Banking (B2C & B2B)
QR and e-Wallet
ShopeePay
GrabPay
Boost
Touch n Go e-Wallet
MAE

Are there any charges for using these payment methods?

A standard charge of RM3.00 (convenient fee) applies for all payment methods.

Is Fiuu secure?

Fiuu is PCI-DSS Level 1 certified, signifying that it has implemented and maintains stringent security measures to protect customer and transaction data, complying with the rigorous standards set by the PCI Security Standards Council.

What is the difference between FPX B2B and FPX B2C?

FPX B2B is FPX Corporate for business bank accounts, while FPX B2C is FPX Consumer for personal bank accounts.

How can I review the foreign workers’ details before I proceed with the payment?

To review before making payment, you may follow these steps:
Click “Payment”.
Click “Show” to review the foreign worker’s name before you proceed with payment.

What should I do if I chose the wrong foreign worker and the payment code has been generated for this worker?

You may cancel the payment by following these steps:
Click “Payment”
Click “Edit”
Click “Cancel”

How much is the medical examination payment for foreign workers?

The medical examination fee for male foreign workers is RM207, whereas the fee for female foreign workers is RM217.
Both are subject to 8% SST.

Why do the medical examination fees increase?

The medical examination fee increase is due to three (3) additional tests in alignment with the latest health guidelines set by the Ministry of Health Malaysia.

Why is the medical examination fee higher for female workers?

The registration fee for female workers is higher because it includes the cost of a pregnancy test.

Does the payment need to be done immediately?

Payment can be done after the foreign worker has been selected. You may follow these steps:
Click on “Payment”
Click “Show” to confirm the foreign worker details
Click on “Edit Payment”
Click on “Pay Now”
Do note that the transaction is only valid for thirty (30) days from the purchase date.

How do I check if the payment made is successful?

To check if your payment is successful, you may follow these steps:
Click on “Payment”
Find the payment code
Refer to the status of payment
Do note that the transaction is only valid for thirty (30) days from the purchase date.

How can I generate the receipt after payment has been made?

To generate the receipt, you may follow these steps:
Click on “Payment”
Find the payment code
Click on “Receipt” to generate the receipt for the transaction made

Is there a limit to the number of payments I can make for foreign workers per transaction?

You can make payments for up to 25 foreign workers per single transaction.

How do I request for a refund on the FOMEMA Online Portal?

You may request for a refund by following these steps:
Login to the FOMEMA Online Portal
Click “Registration History”
Search for foreign worker’s name
Click “Refund”
Select “Reason”
Read disclaimer and click “Agree” to proceed
The refund will be credited to the registered employer’s account within fourteen (14) working days. Do note that RM27 
(subject to 8% SST)

will be deducted for each refund request for administrative and processing costs.

Why can’t I request for a refund of payment?

Refunds are not allowed after the medical examination has been carried out or after the expiry date of the form.

What happens if I miss the 30-day validity period after making a payment?

The validity is 30 days from the date of payment. If the medical examination is not completed within this period, a new registration and payment will be required. Refunds cannot be issued once the validity has expired, and we encourage you to complete the process within the 30-day timeframe to avoid any inconvenience.
