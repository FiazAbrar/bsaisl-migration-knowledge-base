Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

General

What is MYVISA?

MYVISA is an online application platform that enable foreign nationals to apply for an electronic visa (eVISA) to enter Malaysia at the comfort of their convenience.

Do all other foreign nationals need to apply for an eVISA to enter Malaysia?

No. eVISA is only compulsory for 31 specific nationalities, and it applies **only for entry into Malaysia for social visit purposes,** such as tourism, family visits, or short business trips.
Other nationalities or purposes of entry (such as employment, study, etc.) may require under Visa with Reference (VDR).

How can I apply for an eVISA?

eVISA can only be applied through our website: https://malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my

What types of eVISA are offered?

An eVISA offers a **Single Entry Visa (SEV)** for a single journey to Malaysia for nationalities that required visa subjected to eligibility of nationality for each visit and Effective 1st September 2026, **Multiple Entry Visa (MEV)** will no longer be available for all nationalities and will be limited to 31 eligible nationalities under the Fly & Cruise, Business, Medical Treatment, and Wedding Tourism purposes.

Can I apply my eVISA in Malaysia?

If you are applying as an individual, you are not allowed to apply for an eVISA within Malaysia. eVISA must be obtained before entering the country. However, if you are an employer, applications can only be submitted from within Malaysia.

How long does it take to obtain an eVISA?

It takes 2 - 7 working days to process.

The processing period for an eVISA is **2 to 7 working days,** excluding weekends, Malaysian public holidays, any other specific countries holidays, and **does not include applications that are incomplete, require additional documentation (query process), or are subject to an interview process.**

The 2 - 7 working days processing time also does not include unforeseen or uncontrollable events including, but not limited to, system maintenance downtime, Internet Service Provider (ISP) equipment failure, host equipment failure, communications network failure, power failure, natural events or acts of war.

Are there any options that we can apply for the visa online in groups?

Yes, an applicant can register up to five different passports. However, it is not recommended to have someone else apply on their behalf. It is strongly advised that applicants submit their own applications.

What is the minimum passport blank pages prior entry into Malaysia?

The minimum required blank passport page is three (3).

Which types of travel documents are eligible to apply for an eVISA?

**eVISA applications are available to holders of international, diplomatic, and official passports only.**

Can I apply eVISA using Travel Document other than listed travel documents?

No, you cannot apply eVISA using Travel Document. You may contact the nearest High Commission / Embassy / Consulate General of Malaysia near to you and check if you can apply for Normal Paper Visa.

I have applied for my eVISA from another website. How do I check my visa status?

Please note that **https://malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my** is the **ONLY official eVISA website.** Applications made through third-party sites are at the applicant's own risk and **cannot be verified** by the official system.

If another person / agent applied on my behalf, how do I check my visa status?

If your eVISA application was submitted by a third party or agent, please contact them directly to check your visa status. Applicants are **strongly advised to apply on their own** to avoid any future complications. The Immigration Department of Malaysia **will not be held responsible** for any issues arising from third-party applications, regardless of whether they are official or unofficial.

What should I fill in the "Address in Malaysia" if I am having short layover and not staying overnight in Malaysia?

Kindly fill in the address of the Malaysia's International Airport in the address of your application. You are also required to write a cover letter explaining that you are having short layover and not staying overnight in Malaysia and then upload the letter into your visa application.

How long will my eVISA and eVISA Note be valid for?

The 'eVISA NOTE' validity is **three (3) months for the Fly & Cruise and Wedding Tourism** social visitor categories. For the **Medical Visitor and Business Visitor categories, the validity is six (6) months. From the date of issuance.**

What are the additional supporting documents required to be uploaded when applying for an eVISA?

The required supporting documents depend on the **Purpose of Stay / Visit**:

**1) Business:** A Company Official Dispatch Letter or an Invitation Letter from the Malaysian Government / Government-Linked Companies (GLC).A Return Flight Ticket.

**2) Fly & Cruise:** Flight and Cruise Ticket Bookings and the Cruise Sailing Schedule.

**3) Medical Treatment:** A Referral Letter or Appointment Confirmation Letter from a registered Malaysian medical center.

**4) Wedding Tourism:** A Return Flight Ticket, Proof of Journey, or Travel Itinerary Document.

**5) Social :** Visitors are required to demonstrate financial sufficiency through cash, a valid debit or credit card, traveler's cheques, or Bank Negara Malaysia-recognized e-money, supported by bank statements for the last three (3) months, alongside a return ticket to their country of origin via flight or another mode of transport.

**All visitors must provide proof of accommodation booking or a residential address for the entire duration of their stay in the country.**

What happens to my active visa if my new eVISA application is approved?

Any previous visa that is still active will be automatically **cancelled** once your new eVISA application is approved.

What happens to my active visa if my new eVISA application is rejected?

If your new eVISA is rejected, the previous active visa remains valid.

How do applicants check the validity period of their visas?

Please refer to the "issue date" and "expiration date" columns in the eVISA notes to check the validity period.

How many days can I stay in Malaysia with an eVISA?

Applicant with an eVISA can stay in Malaysia based on their nationality's eligibility for each entry under **Single Entry Visa (SEV).** For the **Multiple Entry Visa (MEV),** each entry allows a 30-day stay. Failure to adhere to the stipulated stay duration may result in fines imposed by Malaysian authorities and potential denial of entry for a specified period during subsequent visits to Malaysia.

How long should I apply for an eVISA before my travel date?

It is recommended to apply for your eVISA **at least 14 days** before your intended departure to Malaysia.

How many applications can I submit to my account?

For one account, an applicant is permitted to apply for five (5) applications at a time, for a maximum of five (5) individuals only. The applicant is allowed to reapply once any of the five (5) applications submitted are fully processed.

Should I re-apply for a new eVISA if my departure date changes but the new date is within six (6) months?

No. You do not have to re-apply for a new eVISA within 6 months from the approved visa issuance date by Malaysian High Commission / Embassy / Consulate to enter Malaysia.

I have realized that some of the information that I provided in the eVISA application requires correction. What should I do?

If you are applying for a VTR, you can amend and update your information on the eVISA application page before making payment. However, if you are applying for a VDR, please refer to your Letter Issuing Office to resolve any discrepancies in your information before proceeding with the payment.

Upon receiving my issued eVISA, I realized that some of the information that I provided was wrong and needs corrections. What should I do?

You must re-apply for a new eVISA with the correct information as per your travel documents.

The information on my approved eVISA does not fully match the information on my travel document. Can I enter Malaysia with this eVISA?

No. Your eVISA will be considered invalid.

Upon successful payment, I have realized that some of the information that I provided was wrong and needs corrections. What should I do?

The applicant assumes responsibility and must re-apply for a new eVISA with the correct information as per the travel documents if there are any mistakes made in his or her application.

Does my eVISA guarantees me to enter Malaysia?

No, eVISA does not guarantee your entry to Malaysia as it is subjected to the approval of the officer at the Malaysia Entry Exit Points.

Why was my eVISA application rejected?

As stated in the Terms and Conditions during each application, all eVISA applications are considered on individual merit and the High Commission / Embassy / Consulate has the right to seek additional information at any stage of processing. The fact that an application for a visa has been received by the eVISA system does not guarantee that the eVISA will be issued. High Commission / Embassy / Consulate reserves the right to refuse an eVISA application without providing any reason. It is advisable that when you re-apply, kindly ensure that the application meets all requirements before submitting it to High Commission / Embassy / Consulate for approval.

What are the documents that may be checked upon my arrival at Malaysia's Entry Checkpoint?

Below are the documents which are required to be presented upon arrival at Malaysia's Entry Checkpoints::

1. Valid passport / travel document with a minimum validity of 6 months.
2. Valid eVISA printout or Digital eVISA (eVISA Note).
3. Boarding Pass, ticket or arrival passport (if applicable).
4. Confirmed returned flight ticket (if applicable).
5. Proof of paid accommodation.
6. Other supporting documents.

Am I required to submit any physical document when applying for my eVISA?

No, you are not required to submit any physical documents unless requested by the Malaysian High Commission / Embassy / Consulate.

Can I apply for eVISA if I am currently travelling in another country?

Yes, with a proof of reside or stay that you are in the country you are applying from.

Visa Exemption for People's Republic of China (PRC)

Do Chinese citizens need a visa to enter Malaysia?

Starting September 1st, 2026,Chinese citizens traveling on Ordinary or Public Affairs passports valid for a minimum of 6 months can enter Malaysia **visa-free** for social visits including tourism, business, or family visit up to **30 days per entry**. **Multiple Entry Visa (MEV) facilities are no longer required or available.**

How do I fill in the surname field if I do not have any surname or given name in my passport?

If your passport does not have a surname or given name, you can enter your name in either the **Given Name** or **Surname** field. Kindly ensure that the information entered matches the details in your passport. If there is no surname mentioned in your passport, you may leave the **Surname** field blank or enter your full name in the **Given Name** field.

How should I upload my flight tickets if I have more than one document?

If you have multiple documents (i.e., for hotel booking or air tickets), kindly merge all the documents into one file and then upload it into the respective field of your eVISA application.

What do I have to do if I do not have any invitation letter to provide in my eVISA application?

If you cannot provide the invitation letter, it is advisable that you write a cover letter in English (PDF format) and mention the reason why you cannot provide the documents requested by Malaysian High Commission /Embassy / Consulate.

What do I have to provide for accommodation documents if I am staying at a friend or relative's place?

Addressed to the Immigration Officer, please prepare a simple cover letter including your personal details and the address where you will be staying in Malaysia. Additionally, include the details of your friend or relative (such as their name, identification card number, and phone number). Kindly combine these documents into a single file and upload it under the **Proof of Paid Accommodation / Hotel Booking** section.

How do I write the cover letter for my eVISA application? What is the format of the cover letter?

There are no specific formats for these cover letters. It is advisable to write a simple letter addressed to the Immigration Officer and explaining your circumstances for them to review.

How do I upload my passport photo for eVISA for social application? It keeps stating that my picture does not meet the specifications.

Kindly ensure that your passport photo:

1. The photo must be a **studio-taken image** with a **plain white background** and must have been taken within the **last six (6) months**
2. It must show the **full face from chin to crown** without any headgear, and the dimensions must be **35mm wide × 50mm high**.
3. **Scanned and selfie photos are strictly not permitted**. Photos that do not meet these specifications may be **rejected**.

Can I apply for an eVISA for Medical treatment in Malaysia?

Yes, you can only apply for an eVISA for Medical if you are seeking treatment at a hospital that is a member of the Malaysia Healthcare Travel Council (MHTC). You must obtain an appointment letter from one of the MHTC member hospitals before applying for the eVISA.

When should I apply for the eVISA for Medical?

**You must submit your eVISA for Medical application at least fourteen(14) working days** prior to the travel date and hospital appointment date.

What documents must I present at the Malaysian Entry/Exit point for medical travel?

1. A valid passport with at least 6 months' validity (biodata page)
2. A copy of the eVISA for Medical (eVISA Note) and the eVAL printout.
3. An official appointment letter from an MHTC member hospital.
4. Complete information of accompanying companions (maximum of two persons allowed).

Is the eVISA application fee refundable?

All payments are strictly non-refundable, regardless of the application outcome.

My application was refused / rejected. Can I apply for a refund of the eVISA Application Charge?

The eVISA Application Charge is paid for the processing of the application and must be paid regardless of the application outcome. There are no provisions to refund the eVISA Application Charge solely on the basis that the visa application was withdrawn or refuse.

Can I make payment offline?
