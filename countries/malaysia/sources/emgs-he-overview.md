#### Overview

The country attracts both local and international students seeking affordable, industry-relevant education in a multicultural environment.

#### 11 FOREIGN BRANCH CAMPUSES IN MALAYSIA

Malaysia is home to 11 internationally renowned foreign branch campuses, offering students globally recognized degrees at a more affordable cost. These campuses bring world-class education closer to home, providing the same curriculum, quality, and certification as their main campuses abroad.
