###### About Us

## Welcome to TalentCorp!

We are the national agency under the Ministry of Human Resources mandated to **drive Malaysia's talent strategy towards becoming a dynamic talent hub.**

With over 30 key initiatives for students, graduates, professionals, and employers, we welcome you to learn about how our work can benefit you.

[Learn More](https://www.talentcorp.com.my/about-us/)
