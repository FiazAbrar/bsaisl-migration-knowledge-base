###### Welcome Message from HE High Commissioner

Welcome to the official website of the High Commission for the People’s Republic of Bangladesh to Malaysia. In this website, we offer all possible information on bilateral relations between Bangladesh and Malaysia including multifarious services provided by the High Commission.\
We have also made an effort to depict a few aspects of Bangladesh and its social, cultural and economic emancipation. It is our attempt to make the website user-friendly and, with that in view, we are in the process of continuous improvement. We welcome any constructive suggestions to make the website more effective, accessible and functional.

###### H.E. Mr. Manjurul Karim Khan Chowdhury\
High Commissioner
