            FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)

   The Commission Determination on the Mandatory Standards for the
        Registration of End-Users of Prepaid Public Cellular Services

The Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission ("Commission") has
issued the Commission Determination on the Mandatory Standards for the
Registration of End-Users of Prepaid Public Cellular Services, Determination No. 1 of
2026 ("Mandatory Standards").

These Mandatory Standards establish a legally binding framework governing the
conduct of prepaid SIM registration in Malaysia. They aim to protect consumers
against identity misuse, fraudulent registration, and unauthorised use of personal data.

About the Commission Determination on the Mandatory Standards for the
Registration of End-Users of Prepaid Public Cellular Services, Determination
No.1 of 2026

1. Who can register for a prepaid SIM card?

     All users, regardless of nationality, are required to register their prepaid SIM cards
     if they are subscribers to Malaysian Prepaid Public Cellular Services.

     Prepaid SIM card registration applies to the following categories:

       (a) Malaysian citizens;
       (b) Permanent Residents;
       (c) Temporary Residents;
       (d) Non-Malaysian (workers or students);
       (e) Non-Malaysian (tourists) who are visiting Malaysia for less than three (3)

                months;
       (f) Non-Malaysian - IMM13 Document Holder; and
       (g) Non-Malaysian - Dokumen Pendaftaran Pelarian Holder (or any

                equivalent terms).

5. Can someone else register a SIM card on my behalf?

    No. The SIM card must be registered by the user. However, for children aged
    twelve (12) to seventeen (17) years old, registration must be carried out by a
    parent or guardian, and the child must be present during the registration
    process.

7. Are pre-activated SIM cards allowed?

    No. Prepaid SIM cards are not allowed to be activated before registration.
    If you receive a SIM card that is already active before you register, it should not
    be used, as this is not permitted.

8. How long does it take for my SIM card to be activated?

    The activation status of your SIM card will be notified within 12 hours after
    your registration is completed and verified.

9. I am a tourist. How long can I use my SIM card?

    You can use the SIM card for a maximum period of three (3) months from the
    date of successful registration. Upon expiry of the threemonth period, the SIM
    card will be automatically terminated.

10. Can Service Providers or Dealers reuse my Identification Document for
       SIM registration or other purposes?

       No. Copies of Identification Documents used for that SIM registration must be
       watermarked and cannot be reused for any other registration. This measure
       helps protect your identity from misuse or fraud.

11. How do I replace my SIM card?
       SIM card replacement can only be done through your Service Provider. You
       may be required to perform identity verification and provide specific
       information to confirm that the number is registered under your name.

Additional FAQs for Subscriber Vigilance

12. What should I do to stay safe when registering a prepaid SIM?
       Always ensure that your SIM registration is done through authorised channels
       such as the Service Provider's official app, website, or an authorised Dealer.
       Never share your Identification Documents, photos, MyDigital ID or One-Time
       Password (OTP) with anyone claiming to "assist" with registration.

13. How can I avoid becoming a victim of fraudulent or unauthorised SIM
       registrations?
       Be cautious of individuals offering to register SIM cards on your behalf or
       requesting your personal details for "faster processing". These are common
       methods used in scams. Only provide your details directly to Service Provider
       platforms or authorised Dealers.

14. How do I check if a SIM card has been registered under my name without
       my knowledge?
       Your Service Provider has provided a free-of-charge mechanism that enables
       you to check and view all active prepaid SIMs registered under your identity.
       If you notice any numbers you did not register, contact your Service Provider
       immediately.
