## Citizenship of a child born in Malaysia (doc: "having a child"; family sections per visa)

- Born on/after Malaysia Day = citizen by operation of law only if a qualification in Second Schedule Part II applies [K0001].
- Rule: citizen if **at least one parent is, at the time of birth, a Malaysian citizen or permanent resident** (Sch.2 Pt II s.1(a)) [K0002]. A child of two Bangladeshi parents who are neither citizens nor PRs does not qualify under this rule.
- Only other route: child "not born a citizen of any country" (s.1(e)) [K0003]; any citizenship acquired within one year after birth counts as citizenship at birth for this rule (s.2(3)) [K0004].
- Pending change: Act A1752 (2025, not yet in force at the reprint) will make s.1(a) "a citizen" only, i.e. a PR parent will no longer be enough [K0005].
- Citizenship decisions of the Federal Government cannot be appealed or reviewed in court [K0006].

## What can get you jailed: entry, overstay, removal, bans (doc: "what can get you jailed"; "going home without valid papers")

- Entering without a valid Entry Permit or Pass (or s.55 exemption) [K0007]: fine up to RM10,000 or up to 5 years or both, plus up to 6 strokes of whipping (s.6(3)) [K0008].
- Prohibited immigrants: no means of support or no definite job awaiting [K0009]; unlawful entrants, people without valid or with forged travel documents, family of prohibited immigrants [K0010]. Appeal to the Minister (final) for a permit holder refused entry on arrival [K0011]. The Act sets no money amount for "means of support".
- Overstay: may not remain after the pass expires or is cancelled [K0016]; without reasonable cause: fine **not less than** RM10,000 or up to 5 years or both (s.15(4)) [K0015].
- Removal (deportation): after conviction under s.5/6/8/9 [K0017]; overstayers removed whether or not prosecuted [K0018], with no appeal against removal where the pass expired or was cancelled [K0019]; any non-citizen unlawfully entering or remaining is liable to removal [K0037]. Detention until removal can be arranged [K0020]; arrest without warrant and up to 30 days' detention while removal is decided [K0021]. Failing to produce a pass on demand = presumed unlawful [K0023].
- Bans: the Director General can bar a person from entry for a stated period or permanently [K0012]; when a pass is cancelled the holder is removed and "thereafter" prohibited from entering [K0013]; appeal to the Minister within 7 days by written petition [K0014]. Coming back after removal: fine up to RM10,000 or up to 5 years or both, up to 6 strokes, and removal again (s.36) [K0022].
- Working in breach of a pass: the pass "shall cease to be a valid Pass" when any of its conditions is broken (s.55B(2)) [K0025]. Employers of workers without a valid pass: RM10,000-50,000 or up to 12 months or both, per worker [K0024]; more than five at once: 6 months-5 years and whipping [K0026]. The worker-side offence for breaching pass conditions is regulation 39 of the Immigration Regulations 1963 (referred to in s.55E(7)) [K0029]; that regulation is not in raw/.
- Landlords/occupiers who let an illegal immigrant (including an overstayer) stay: RM5,000-30,000 or up to 12 months per person [K0029]. Harbouring: RM10,000-50,000 per person [K0033]. Smuggling people in: RM10,000-50,000 and 2-5 years and whipping [K0027].
- Forged papers: making, forging or altering a visa, permit or pass: RM30,000-100,000 **and** 5-10 years **and** up to 6 strokes (s.55D) [K0028]. False statements, using someone else's pass, getting a pass by false statement, using or holding a forged pass: fine up to RM10,000 or up to 5 years (s.56(1)) [K0030, K0031, K0032]. Attempts and abetment punished like the offence [K0034]. General penalty: up to RM10,000 or 5 years or both [K0035].

## Refugees and asylum (doc: "asylum", written to discourage)

- The Immigration Act (2006 reprint) contains no refugee or asylum provision: 0 matches for "refugee"/"asylum"; every non-citizen needs a valid permit, pass or exemption to enter [K0036]. The Federal Constitution reprint also has 0 matches (noted in K0036).

## Passports (doc: "rights at work" passport withholding; "scams"; "what can get you jailed")

- Holding another person's passport without lawful authority is an offence for any person (s.12(1)(f)): fine up to RM10,000 or up to 5 years or both [K0045, K0046]. The Act does not mention employers or consent; "lawful authority" is not defined.
- A holder who lets someone else possess their passport intending or knowing it may be used by another person also offends (s.12(1)(e)) [K0047].
- Forged or altered passport, or using/holding one: s.12(1)(a) [K0048]; false statement for one's own passport: s.12(1)(c) [K0049]; both up to RM10,000 or 5 years [K0046]. Getting a passport/visa for someone else by false statement: RM10,000-50,000 and 1-5 years (s.12A) [K0050]. Making a false passport: RM15,000-100,000 and 2-10 years and up to 6 strokes (s.12B) [K0051].
- Text is an unofficial consolidation to 1997 [K0052].

## Drugs and the death penalty (doc: "what can get you jailed")

- Act 846 abolishes the **mandatory** death penalty across seven laws, including the Dangerous Drugs Act 1952 [K0039]; commencement by gazette notification, date not in the file [K0040].
- Drug trafficking (DDA s.39B): Act 846 only lowers the minimum whipping (for those not sentenced to death) from 15 to 12 strokes and deletes s.39B(2A)-(2B); it does not remove death [K0041]. Applies to anyone convicted after commencement even for earlier offences [K0042].
- Murder (Penal Code s.302): death or 30-40 years, plus at least 12 strokes if not sentenced to death [K0043]. "Imprisonment for natural life" replaced by 30-40 years in several laws [K0044].

## Draft steps (for the doc writer)

**Having a child in Malaysia.** 1. Know that a baby born in Malaysia to two Bangladeshi parents who are not Malaysian citizens or PRs is not a Malaysian citizen by birth [K0002]; the stateless-child route does not apply if the child gets another citizenship within a year [K0003, K0004]. 2. Need to know: how to register the birth in Malaysia (JPN) and with the Bangladesh High Commission for Bangladeshi citizenship by descent, and the child's immigration status.

**Staying legal.** 1. Leave before the pass expires: overstaying is a minimum RM10,000 fine or up to 5 years [K0015], and removal with no appeal [K0018, K0019]. 2. Work only within the pass conditions: a broken condition voids the pass [K0025]. 3. Carry the pass or a copy accepted by Immigration: failing to show it means you are presumed illegal [K0023]. 4. Never use, buy or carry forged passes or passports [K0028, K0032, K0048, K0051].

**If an employer keeps your passport.** Keeping another person's passport without lawful authority is an offence under Passports Act s.12(1)(f), up to RM10,000 or 5 years [K0045]. Need to know: where a worker reports this (Immigration, police, Labour Department) and what happens in practice.

**After deportation.** A cancelled pass means a ban "thereafter" [K0013]; the DG may also ban for a stated period or permanently [K0012]; appeal within 7 days to the Minister [K0014]; returning unlawfully is a further offence [K0022].
