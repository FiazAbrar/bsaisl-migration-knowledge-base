## Answers to the open "Need to know" items

**Overtime rates and public holidays (doc step "Hours").** Overtime beyond normal daily hours: at least 1.5x the hourly rate [M0001, M0002]. Rest day: daily-rated get 1 day's wages (up to half normal hours) or 2 days' (more) [M0011]; monthly-rated get half a day's or 1 day's pay [M0012]; beyond normal hours on a rest day at least 2x hourly [M0013]; no one can be forced to work a rest day except shift work or emergencies [M0010]. Public holiday worked: 2 days' wages on top of holiday pay [M0019]; overtime on a public holiday at least 3x hourly [M0020]. Paid public holidays: 11 gazetted a year, 5 fixed (National Day, King's Birthday, state Ruler's Birthday or FT Day, Workers' Day, Malaysia Day) [M0014, M0015]; substitute day if it falls on a rest day [M0016]; employer posts the other six [M0017]; absence the day before/after loses holiday pay without a reasonable excuse [M0018]. Hourly rate = (monthly wage / 26) / normal daily hours [M0007, M0008, M0234, M0235]. Limits: 8 h/day, 45 h/week, 30-min break after 5 h [M0003]; never more than 12 h a day [M0004]; 45 h excludes free breaks [M0237]; wages can't be cut for the 48-to-45 change [M0061]. Workers earning over RM4,000 (except manual workers) are excluded from OT/rest-day/holiday pay and termination benefits [M0058, M0109]. Still not answered: the monthly overtime cap (set by regulations, not in the Act) [M0005].

**Leave.** Annual 8/12/16 days [M0021, M0022]; sick 14/18/22 + 60 hospitalisation (74-82 total) [M0023, M0024, M0025, M0239]; maternity 98 days [M0026]; paternity 7 days [M0027]; pregnant worker protection [M0240].

**How and when wages are paid (s.19, s.25).** Wage period max 1 month [M0028]; pay by the 7th day after it ends [M0029]; OT, rest-day and holiday pay by the last day of the next wage period [M0030]; into a bank account in the worker's own name [M0034, M0035]; cash/cheque only on the worker's written request plus JTKSM approval [M0036, M0037]; on termination with notice, pay by the last day [M0032]; if the worker quits without notice (s.13 or s.14(3)), within 3 days [M0033]; employer can't dictate how wages are spent [M0038]; late pay is an offence [M0040] and a breach of contract by the employer [M0041]; partial-month formula [M0043].

**Deductions (s.24); levy and recruitment costs.** Only deductions the Act allows [M0044]: overpayment, notice indemnity, interest-free advances, deductions authorised by other written law [M0045, M0046]; third-party payments, insurance and accommodation/food need the worker's written request plus the Director General's prior written permission [M0108, M0047, M0048], and accommodation/food only if for the worker's benefit [M0049]; max 50% of monthly wages [M0050, M0051]; unauthorised deduction is an offence [M0040]. Levy/recruitment costs: none of these files names them; the Act's closed list does not include them. **Still not answered:** an explicit official statement that the levy or recruitment fees may not be deducted (look in JTKSM/KESUMA levy circulars, the "Employer Mandatory Commitment" for levy, or the Minimum Standards of Housing regulations 2020 for rent caps).

**Termination and lay-off benefits.** Notice 4/6/8 weeks or as in the contract [M0052]; pay in lieu [M0053]; misconduct dismissal only after due inquiry [M0054]; worker can leave without notice if in danger [M0055]; foreign workers retrenched before locals in a similar job [M0057]; locals can't be replaced by foreigners [M0069]; untaken annual leave paid out [M0022]; return ticket: employer pays at contract end or when it terminates, worker pays if dismissed for misconduct or resigns [M0149, M0150]. **Still not answered:** the amounts of termination/lay-off benefits: s.60J leaves them to the Employment (Termination and Lay-Off Benefits) Regulations 1980, not in these files [M0056].

**Labour Court claim.** Where: JTKSM (Labour Department) office; the Director General of Labour hears wage/payment disputes without upper limit [M0081, M0082]. Free advice: 03-8000 8000, jtksm@mohr.gov.my, nearest Labour Office [M0118]. How: go in person and give a sworn statement [M0087, M0088, M0112]; bring passport, employer name/address, payslips, appointment letter/contract, termination letter [M0113]. Fee: free [M0232]; no fee for summonses [M0093]; free copy of decision [M0095]; the Act allows filing fees by regulation (none named) [M0094]. Time limits: 60 days to challenge a misconduct dismissal [M0083, M0117]; 90 days for a claim against a principal contractor [M0100]; general wage-claim limit not in these files. Steps: sworn statement, mention date, summons, hearing, order [M0114]; employer summoned, worker brings witnesses [M0089]; evidence on oath, decision [M0090]; decided in the employer's absence if he doesn't come [M0091]; joint claims allowed [M0092]; employer may not stop the worker appearing [M0099]. Order: 8% interest from day 31 [M0085]; non-compliance an offence [M0086]; enforced through the Sessions/Magistrate's Court [M0096]; seizure and sale, garnishment, prosecution, judgment-debtor summons [M0116]; third-party payment order [M0097]; appeal to the High Court [M0098] within 14 days [M0115]; employer about to flee: complain to a Magistrate [M0110]; DG can represent the worker in civil court [M0101]; civil suit as an alternative [M0102]. Separate route: written complaint (aduan) to the Labour Office, leading to inspection, rectification notice, then prosecution/compound [M0119, M0080, M0079]. **Still not answered:** how long a case takes; the general time limit for wage claims; the current filing fee regulations (brochure is 2017).

**Forced labour.** Offence, fine up to RM100,000 and/or 2 years [M0073]; definition and three elements [M0074, M0075]; employers convicted can't get approval to hire foreign workers [M0063]; complain to JTKSM (complaint powers s.79) [M0080]. Discrimination complaints [M0076, M0077, M0236]; sexual harassment [M0078]. **Still not answered:** a specific forced-labour hotline or form (none in these files).

**Unfair dismissal (Industrial Relations).** These files only show that the Labour Court can't hear a claim pending under the Industrial Relations Act 1967 [M0103] and the s.69(3) misconduct route (60 days; remedy limited to pay in lieu, not reinstatement) [M0083, M0084]. **Still not answered:** the IRA s.20 representation to the Industrial Relations Department (JPPM): time limit, form, and whether PLKS workers use it. The IRA is not in these files.

**Change of employer (PLKS).** Allowed only if the employer is bankrupt, closed, hit by calamity, restructured, renamed, or died [M0123, M0124]; same sector, not frozen [M0126]; not for 6P/PKPP/Rehiring/RTK, domestic helpers, non-PLKS, or PLKS 10th year+ [M0127]; PLKS valid 3+ months [M0130, M0131]. Who applies: the employers, not the worker [M0132, M0125]: JTKSM step on ePPAx (both employers register, old employer applies, new employer uploads in 7 days, JTK branch investigates, JTKSM decides) [M0121, M0122], then JTKSM support letter [M0128, M0140], then BPPA KESUMA counter in Putrajaya, Mon-Thu 8.30-4.30, queue 8.30-12 [M0134, M0135], interview with originals, 2 copies [M0136]. Documents: worker passport front page + active PLKS sticker, workers' list [M0139]; EPF/PERKESO statements [M0137]; statutory declarations [M0138]; stamped release agreement [M0125, M0141]. Approval depends on compliance and quota [M0129]. Fee: form is marked free; no fee stated [M0142]. **Still not answered:** processing time; any fee at Immigration to transfer the pass; any route for a worker whose employer is abusive (not a listed ground).

**Absconding.** Definition [M0213, M0214]; employer reports to Immigration with police report and form [M0215]; worker blacklisted and bond confiscated [M0216]; employer must also tell JTKSM within 14 days [M0066, M0067, M0241]. **Still not answered:** how a worker challenges a false absconding report or gets off the blacklist (nothing in these files).

**Passport retention.** MOHR policy: employers must not hold or keep the worker's passport, per the Passports Act 1966 [M0143].

**Housing (Act 446).** Applies in Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan [M0191]; Part IIIA for non-estate workers [M0192]; Certificate for Accommodation required [M0193]; employer notifies JTKSM within 30 days [M0194]; minimum standards apply [M0195] but are set in regulations [M0196]; unfit buildings banned [M0199]; decent amenities [M0200]; separate men/women, fire and wiring safety, medical help [M0201, M0202]; person in charge inspects twice a month, takes sick workers to clinic [M0203, M0204]; stay up to 4 weeks after short-notice termination [M0205]; no obligation for dependants [M0206]; estate-type housing (Part II): free water and electricity, no rent [M0207, M0208, M0209]. Who pays: employer provides housing [M0146]; rent may be deducted under Ministerial conditions [M0197, M0198] and, under the Employment Act, only with the worker's written request and JTKSM permission [M0048, M0049]. Complaints: JTKSM (DG of Labour) enforces and can inspect without notice [M0212, M0210, M0079]; penalties [M0211]. **Still not answered:** space per worker, beds, toilets, water per person, maximum rent: in the Employees' Minimum Standards of Housing, Accommodations and Amenities (Accommodation and Centralized Accommodation) Regulations 2020, not in these files.

**EPF (KWSP).** Proposed 2% worker + 2% employer, MOHR statement 3 Feb 2025 [M0163]; a bill to amend the EPF Act was expected [M0164]; rate held until review [M0165]. **Still not answered:** whether it is now law and the start date (search KWSP site, EPF (Amendment) Act 2025, Gazette).

**PERKESO / SOCSO.** EI Scheme since 1 Jan 2019 and Invalidity Scheme since 1 Jul 2024, covering foreign workers incl. domestic workers [M0166, M0167, M0168, M0169, M0176, M0190]. Contributions: employer 1.25% EI + 0.5% invalidity = 1.75%; worker 0.5% [M0170, M0171]; older EI-only rate 1.25% employer-only [M0180]; due by the 15th [M0181]; wages counted [M0185]. Eligibility and registration: valid passport and work pass; ASSIST portal or PERKESO office [M0173, M0182, M0183]; 12-digit FWSS number [M0184]; employer offence up to RM10,000/2 years [M0174, M0175]. Benefits: [M0172, M0186]; funeral RM6,500 if repatriated or RM2,000 if buried in Malaysia (2019) [M0187]; non-work death repatriation reimbursed up to RM4,500 (2022) [M0177, M0178]. Claims: Foreign Worker Claim Notification Form (Appendix C, Circular 3/2018) [M0188]; contacts 1-300-22-8000, perkeso@perkeso.gov.my [M0179, M0189]. Employer also pays funeral and repatriation of a deceased worker under MOHR policy [M0148]. **Still not answered:** benefit rates for temporary/permanent disablement, dependants' and invalidity pension amounts; claim time limits; who files the claim if the employer refuses; whether the RM6,500/RM2,000/RM4,500 amounts are current.

**FOMEMA, SPIKPA, insurance.** FOMEMA within 30 days / 1 month, PLKS only after fit, COM if unfit [M0223, M0144]; employer pays FOMEMA [M0144]; FOMEMA again for 2nd and 3rd-year renewals [M0221]; SPIKPA hospital insurance needed for renewal except plantation [M0220]; employer provides insurance under Act 4 [M0145]; biomedical screening before entry [M0160]; sick or infectious workers sent home [M0162]. **Still not answered:** SPIKPA premium and cover limits.

**Minimum wage.** Only that employers must follow minimum wage rules under Act 732 [M0153], basic wage equal to locals in the same job [M0152], and that the JTKSM brochure handles minimum-wage complaints [M0120]. **Still not answered:** the amount (not in these files).

**PLKS re-entry when going home on leave.** Only that not returning on the agreed date after a trip home counts as absconding [M0214]. **Still not answered:** re-entry permit/procedure.

## Draft steps (PLKS, rights at work and problems)

1. **Know your pay and hours.** Max 8 h/day, 45 h/week, 12 h with overtime [M0003, M0004]. Overtime 1.5x, rest day 2x beyond normal hours, public holiday 2 days' pay + 3x overtime [M0001, M0013, M0019, M0020]. 11 paid public holidays [M0014]. Leave [M0021, M0023, M0239].
2. **Check your wage.** Paid by the 7th into your own bank account [M0029, M0034]; overtime by the end of the next month [M0030]; deductions only as allowed, max half [M0044, M0050]; rent only with your written request and JTKSM approval [M0048].
3. **Keep your passport.** The employer must not hold it [M0143].
4. **Housing.** Must have a Certificate for Accommodation; men and women separate; sick workers taken to clinic [M0193, M0201, M0204]; complain to JTKSM [M0212].
5. **If not paid:** call 03-8000 8000 or go to the nearest Labour Office with your passport, payslips, contract and any termination letter; give a sworn statement; attend the mention and hearing with witnesses; the order carries interest and can be enforced by court; appeal within 14 days [M0118, M0113, M0112, M0114, M0089, M0085, M0096, M0115]. It's free [M0232].
6. **If dismissed for misconduct:** complain to JTKSM within 60 days [M0083].
7. **If forced to work or locked in:** it's a crime under s.90B [M0073, M0074].
8. **Changing employer:** only in the listed cases, applied for by the employers through JTKSM ePPAx and BPPA KESUMA [M0123, M0121, M0128, M0134].
9. **If reported as absconded:** see consequences [M0216]; route to fix: Need to know.
10. **Injury, illness, death:** PERKESO covers you; employer registers you and you get an FWSS number; claim with the Foreign Worker Claim Notification Form [M0166, M0184, M0188]; employer pays funeral and repatriation [M0148].
