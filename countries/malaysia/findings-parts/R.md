## Official versus news conflicts still open

- **OSC under KDN (official W0043; IMI slides Apr 2026) vs under KSM since 1 Jul 2026 (news R0001, RTM quoting the Human Resources Minister).** Under the source rule the official page is the rule. But the news is newer and quotes a Cabinet decision, and the other official page (W0044) also says KSM. The doc should show both. Need to know: MOHR's media statement (blocked).
- **FOMEMA medical: within 3 days of arrival (IMI slides, lead W0069) vs 30 days (IMI page W0047) / 1 month (MOHR policy).** The official rule is 30 days / 1 month.
- **Employment period: "10 + 3 years" (IMI slides, lead W0069) vs up to 10 years (official W0049, R0007).** The official rule is 10 years. The +3-year extension is reported only in the slides and in older news.
- **MoU date: 18 Dec 2022 (news W0015) vs 19 Dec 2021 (official W0027, R0010).** The official date is the rule (settled).
- **Tk 78,990 cost: news B0123 adds a Tk 37,000 agency service charge and employer-paid accommodation.** The official order (R0008, R0009) gives neither figure; it lists the agency service charge only as one of the items inside the Tk 78,990 cap. Don't write Tk 37,000 as fact.
