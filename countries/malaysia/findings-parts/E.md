# Findings, part E: professional and long-stay passes, family passes

Researched 6 Nov 2026. Ledger rows E0001-E0149 (ledger-parts/E.csv). Sources: esd.imi.gov.my (ESD/MYXpats), imi.gov.my, mm2h.gov.my and motac.gov.my (MOTAC), mdec.my (MDEC), talentcorp.com.my, the JTKSM ePPAx FAQ. Most IMI and ESD web pages carry no date, so their rows are `possibly-outdated`. The ESD FAQ pages print "Latest Update" with today's date, which the server generates, so I treated them as undated. Dated, current sources are the Revised Expatriate Salary Booklet (PDF dated 7 Aug 2026), its FAQ (12 Feb 2026), the PVP-Expert FAQ (Sept 2026), mm2h.gov.my ("Last Update 10/02/2026"), DE Rantau FAQ v11 (1 Nov 2026) and the Enhanced DE Rantau FAQ (Oct 2026).

Context for the doc: none of these passes is a route for an ordinary Bangladeshi worker. PLKS (factory, construction, plantation) workers **cannot bring family and cannot marry in Malaysia** [E0138, E0139, E0140]. The passes below are for graduates and professionals earning RM5,000+ a month, for wealthy retirees or investors, or for remote workers earning USD36,000+ a year from abroad. Bangladeshis are not excluded from any of them: MM2H is open to any country with diplomatic relations with Malaysia [E0100], and DE Rantau to every country except Israel [E0084]. A Bangladeshi needs a Visa with Reference (VDR), and the visa costs RM20 a year [E0045, E0046, E0047, E0058].

## 1. Employment Pass (EP)

**What:** a work pass for an "expatriate", the Malaysian term for a foreign professional or skilled worker, valid for one employer [E0004, E0005, E0006]. Issued for 1-60 months, multiple entry [E0015]. Valid only in Peninsular Malaysia; Sabah and Sarawak have their own immigration and ESD units [E0005; see IMI charter E0056 for Sabah/Sarawak units].

**Who decides:** the Expatriate Committee (EC), or a sector approving agency: MIDA (manufacturing), MDEC (ICT/MSC), Bank Negara (banking), Securities Commission, the Public Services Department, and the ECERDC and IRDA regions [E0004, E0007]. Paying the fee doesn't guarantee approval [E0049].

**Categories from 1 June 2026** (basic salary only, taxable in Malaysia; allowances, bonuses and pay outside Malaysia don't count) [E0008, E0013, E0017]:
- Cat I: RM20,000+ a month, up to 10 years, dependants allowed [E0009].
- Cat II: RM10,000-19,999, up to 10 years (succession plan required from 1 Jan 2027), dependants allowed [E0010].
- Cat III: RM5,000-9,999, up to 5 years with the same employer (succession plan from 1 Jan 2027), dependants now allowed [E0011]. In manufacturing and manufacturing-related services under MIDA the range is RM7,000-9,999 [E0018]. Before June 2026 Cat III was RM3,000-4,999, 12 months per application and no dependants [E0012]. Cat III holders issued before 1 June 2026 still can't bring dependants [E0019].
- Minimum qualification: degree + 3 years' relevant experience, or diploma + 5 years, or technical certificate + 7 years [E0022, E0023] (guidebook dated April 2025).
- The employer must be registered with ESD. Paid-up capital: RM250,000 if 100% local, RM350,000 for a joint venture, RM500,000 if 100% foreign, RM1m for foreign-owned trade companies [E0024].

**Who applies:** only the employer, never the worker [E0002, E0001]. The process from the booklet [E0016]: (1) company registers on ESD Online, about 5 working days; (2) JTKSM (Department of Labour) prior approval under Section 60K of the Employment Act, plus a MYFutureJobs (PERKESO) job advert acknowledgement, both on Xpats Gateway, about 10 working days [E0050, E0051, E0052, E0053]; (3) support letter from the approving agency if the sector is regulated; (4) EP application on ESD Online, 2 working days fast track or 5 normal [E0016, E0054, E0055]; (5) VDR for visa-required nationals [E0058]; (6) entry and ePASS endorsement, 1 working day, at the Expatriate Satellite Centre (ESC) at KLIA T1/T2 with no appointment, or at the MYXpats Centre [E0059]. An employer that hires without JTKSM approval faces a fine of up to RM100,000 and/or 5 years in jail [E0052].

**Documents:** colour scan of the full passport (more than 12 months' validity, 6 empty pages), photo, attested certificates, contract and job description; for renewals, the last 3 months' payslips, tax forms and receipts [E0025, E0026].

**Fees:** MYXpats application fee RM2,000 + 8% SST = RM2,160 [E0040]. Immigration endorsement: RM200 a year (RM300 for a MIDA key post) + RM125 processing + visa (Bangladesh RM20 a year) [E0042, E0045]. For a 12-month EP for a Bangladeshi that is RM200 + RM125 + RM20 = RM345 immigration fees plus the RM2,160 application fee (my arithmetic from E0040/E0042/E0045; the doc should show the parts, not a total). A rejected application gets 75% of the processing fee back unless the employer appeals [E0048].

**Appeal:** the employer appeals within 14 days in ESD Online; the EC-Appeal decides within up to 30 working days; after that the case can be referred to MOHA [E0063, E0064, E0021].

**Renewal:** can be filed 3 months before expiry, under the revised salary rules [E0020]. A new passport requires a Transfer of Endorsement, or the holder can be stopped from leaving Malaysia [E0065].

**Check it is real:** the ePASS has a QR code; anyone can check it at imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/eservices/epass?semakan, or email esdhelpdesk@imi.gov.my (1 working day) [E0060, E0061, E0062]. MYXpats helpdesk: +603-7839 7171, helpdesk@myxpats.com.my, Level 4 Surian Tower, Mutiara Damansara [E0066].

## 2. Dependant Pass and LTSVP for EP holders' families

- From 1 June 2026: the **Dependant Pass (DP)** covers a spouse and children under 18 (step and adopted included; disabled children of any age). The **LTSVP** (Long-Term Social Visit Pass) covers parents, parents-in-law, children aged 18-25 and a common-law spouse [E0014, E0027].
- The company applies through its ESD account, together with the EP or later [E0034, E0037]. Fees: RM540 application (RM500 + SST) [E0041], plus RM90 a year, RM50 processing and the visa fee [E0043]. The LTSVP needs a personal bond: RM750 for Bangladeshis [E0144]. Charter time 14 working days [E0029].
- Documents: photo, colour passport (12+ months), and a marriage or birth certificate translated into English and certified (CTC) by the Malaysian High Commission in Dhaka or the Bangladesh High Commission in KL, with a current-year CTC for new applications [E0030]. A common-law spouse needs an affidavit from the embassy; children 18-25 need a statutory declaration and embassy confirmation at every renewal [E0031].
- Validity follows the principal's pass, capped by the dependant's passport [E0034]. **Conflict** on the LTSVP maximum: 12 months [E0148] vs following the principal [E0147].
- **Can dependants work?** **Conflict:** the ESD guidebook and DP page say no, the dependant must get their own EP [E0028, E0035, E0145]; the older IMI FAQ (2021) says a DP spouse can work with an Immigration work endorsement [E0038]. LTSVP holders can't work or study (they need an EP or a Student Pass) [E0032].
- Domestic maid: EP holder earning RM5,000+ (1 maid), RM10,000+ (2), RM15,000+ (3) [E0033].

## 3. Can PLKS workers bring family?

No. "Pekerja Asing tidak dibenarkan membawa keluarga masuk dan tinggal di negara ini" (foreign workers are not allowed to bring their family to live in Malaysia) [E0138, E0140]. They also can't marry a local or another foreign worker while on PLKS [E0139]. A Bangladeshi worker's only official family route is a family member's own pass (a visit visa, which is another part's topic) or the worker moving to a professional pass after qualifying.

## 4. LTSVP for a foreign spouse of a Malaysian citizen

- Minimum 6 months, up to 5 years. The holder may work or run a business without an EP or PLKS once Immigration endorses permission in the passport [E0122, E0123, E0125]. The marriage must be registered under Malaysian law [E0129]. Immigration may visit the home or workplace to check (naziran) [E0124].
- New application: both spouses attend the state Immigration office with Imm.12, Imm.38 (2 copies), Imm.55 (2 copies), a sponsor statement, a security bond stamped RM10, a sworn declaration with photo, the spouse's IC and birth certificate, the marriage registration, a wedding photo, proof of income and address, and the spouse's Malaysian passport [E0126, E0127]. Charter: 25 working days [E0057].
- Work endorsement: at the Visa, Pass and Permit Division in the state of work, with the spouse present. Bring the spouse's MyKad copy, passport and pass, a sworn declaration, the marriage certificate, an offer letter, a contract stamped RM10 (stating position, period and salary), the information form and the SSM company printout [E0125].
- A false statement is an offence under s.56(1)(f): fine up to RM10,000 and/or 5 years in jail [E0128].
- After 3 years on a long-term pass, family of citizens can get the **Pas Residen** (Residence Pass): RM500 for 5 years; holders may work, study and run a business [E0131, E0132, E0133].

## 5. MM2H (Malaysia My Second Home), MOTAC

- Open to any country with diplomatic relations with Malaysia, which includes Bangladesh. Age 25+ (Silver/Gold/Platinum) or 21+ (SEZ/SFZ) [E0100].
- **Apply only through a MOTAC-licensed MM2H operator** [E0101]. Final approval and appeals rest with MOHA/Immigration [E0102]. The licensed list, with licence numbers (MM2Hxxx) and validity dates, is at mm2h.gov.my/agencies [E0116]. Since 1 Aug 2025 applications go through the system at mm2h.gov.my/portal (helpdesk 03-8891 7207), and from 1 Oct 2025 by online appointment [E0118].
- Tiers [E0106-E0109, E0114]:

| | Fixed deposit | Pass | Min. property | Participating fee | Renewal fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Platinum | USD 1,000,000 | 20 yrs | RM2,000,000 | RM200,000 | RM5,000 |
| Gold | USD 500,000 | 15 yrs | RM1,000,000 | RM3,000 | RM3,000 |
| Silver | USD 150,000 | 5 yrs | RM600,000 | RM1,000 | RM1,500 |
| SEZ/SFZ | USD 65,000 (21-49) / USD 32,000 (50+) | 10 yrs | Forest City, Johor only | RM1,000 | RM300 |

  Processing fee: RM5,000 per principal and RM2,500 per dependant [E0107]. Under 50s must spend 90 days a year in Malaysia [E0103]. The property must be bought within 1 year of endorsement and not sold for 10 years [E0119, E0103]. Up to 50% of the fixed deposit can be withdrawn for a house, education, medical or tourism costs [E0106]. Police may interview applicants at random [E0120].
- **Work:** Platinum only; Gold, Silver and SEZ can't work or run a business [E0112, E0113].
- Family: spouse, children under 21 (21-34 if single and unemployed), disabled children, parents and parents-in-law; maid for Platinum only [E0104]. **Conflict:** maximum child age 34 [E0110, E0146] vs 35 [E0111].
- Renewal: RM500 a year pass fee + RM0-50 visa [E0105].
- Sarawak runs its own S-MM2H. **Lead only:** from 2025, age 30+, RM500,000 fixed deposit in a Sarawak bank, 30 days a year in Sarawak [E0143]. The official MTCP pages were blocked.

## 6. DE Rantau Nomad Pass (MDEC)

- A type of Professional Visit Pass for remote workers and freelancers with foreign employers or clients. Open to all nationalities except Israel; age 18+ [E0084].
- Income: tech roles more than USD36,000 a year (raised from USD24,000 for applications from 1 Dec 2026), non-tech USD60,000 [E0085, E0098]. The contract must have started at least 3 months before applying; up to 3 contracts [E0085].
- Duration: 3-12 months, then up to three 12-month renewals, 48 months maximum. No route to PR [E0086, E0098].
- Apply at mdec.my/derantau. Processing 6-8 weeks; the e-Pass comes within 1 week of endorsement; check status at malaysiadigital.mdec.my [E0089, E0090]. Bangladeshis then get a VDR or eVISA and enter for endorsement [E0089].
- Fees: RM1,080 for the main applicant and RM540 per dependant (non-refundable), plus the RM360 a year pass fee and visa. Refundable bond for Bangladeshis: RM750 [E0091, E0092].
- Family: spouse or common-law partner, children under 18, special-needs children, parents [E0087]. **The spouse can't work** [E0088].
- No appeal [E0093]. Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan only; a tourist pass can't be converted [E0095]. Passport needs 14+ months' validity [E0096]. Documents include a letter of good conduct from the home country [E0097].
- **New applications are closed 1-30 Nov 2026 and reopen on 1 Dec 2026** [E0099].
- "Agents" can't guarantee approval [E0094].

## 7. Other passes

- **Professional Visit Pass (PVP-Expert):** short assignments of up to 12 months for an overseas company; no Dependant Pass [E0067]. Not for general workers or operators; the applicant waits abroad; false documents lead to blacklisting [E0068, E0070]. A Social Visit Pass does not allow work [E0069].
- **PLS@Xpats:** permission to work on a Social Visit Pass, for emergency or critical professional work, applied for by the company at mtp.imi.gov.my/plsXpats [E0071, E0072]; charter 3 working days [E0056]. **Need to know:** the duration (news says 30 days), sectors and fees; the FAQ page is empty (see blocked).
- **Investor Pass:** 6 months + 6, for foreign investors working through MIDA and other investment agencies, applied from abroad on Xpats Gateway with a business email. No dependants. RM1,296 + RM90 + RM500 + visa [E0075, E0076, E0077, E0073].
- **Residence Pass-Talent (RP-T, TalentCorp):** 10 years. Requires 3 consecutive years in Malaysia on an EP, RM15,000 basic salary, 2 years of tax paid, a qualification and 5 years' experience. The holder can change employer; the spouse can work [E0078, E0079, E0080].
- **MTEP (MDEC):** for tech founders. 1-year PVP (fee from RM2,700) or 5-year Resident Pass (from RM5,400, dependants allowed). Needs RM50,000 in bank statements, a good-conduct certificate and a sponsor [E0081, E0082, E0083].
- **PVIP (Premium Visa Programme):** listed on the IMI home page [E0074]. **Need to know:** official terms. No public official page was found; news reports a RM200,000 fee, RM40,000 a month offshore income, 20 years and appointed agents only.

## 8. Permanent residence

The Entry Permit (Permit Masuk) gives indefinite residence. Its categories are the wife, husband or child under 18 of a citizen, and "Warga Asing Sepenuhnya" (fully foreign nationals) [E0134]. It can be revoked [E0135]. Expatriates approved for PR get an LTSVP while waiting [E0137]. Status checks and a points calculator are at imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/eservices and app.imi.gov.my/epc [E0136]. DE Rantau gives no PR route [E0098]. PLKS rules (no family, no marriage, 5 years maximum) mean an ordinary worker can't build up the qualifying stay [E0138, E0139]. **Need to know:** the official criteria for "fully foreign" PR (the e-book is on Google Drive; see blocked).

## 9. Scams and checks

- EP/DP: the worker never applies; any "EP for sale" offer to an individual is outside the official process, because only a registered employer can apply [E0002]. Verify any ePASS by its QR code or at imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/eservices/epass?semakan [E0060]. PVP-Expert misuse and false information lead to blacklisting [E0070].
- MM2H: only MOTAC-licensed operators may submit; check the name and licence number at mm2h.gov.my/agencies [E0101, E0116]. Lead: MOTAC re-vetted all agents in 2024 after complaints [E0142].
- DE Rantau: no agent can guarantee approval [E0094].
- Practical lead: 3,604 Bangladeshis held active MM2H passes on 31 Jan 2024, 5th-largest nationality [E0141]. This needs the parlimen.gov.my answer (11 March 2024).

## Draft steps

### Employment Pass (for a Bangladeshi graduate or skilled professional)
1. **Check you qualify:** a degree + 3 years' experience, diploma + 5 years or technical certificate + 7 years, and a job offer of at least RM5,000 basic salary a month (RM7,000 in manufacturing) [E0022, E0011, E0018].
2. **Get a written offer from a registered Malaysian company.** It, not you, applies [E0002]. It must first be registered on ESD (paid-up capital RM250,000-1m) [E0024] and get JTKSM Section 60K approval and a MYFutureJobs advert acknowledgement on Xpats Gateway (about 10 working days) [E0050, E0051, E0016].
3. **Send the company your documents:** a colour scan of every passport page (12+ months valid), a photo, attested certificates and the signed contract [E0025].
4. **The company applies on ESD Online and pays RM2,160.** Decision in 2-5 working days; the approval letter is printed from the system [E0040, E0016, E0054].
5. **Get your Visa with Reference (VDR).** The company applies with the EP and collects it at MYXpats [E0058].
6. **Fly in and get the ePASS endorsed** at the ESC at KLIA T1/T2 (no appointment) or MYXpats. The company pays the immigration fees: RM200 a year + RM125 + RM20 visa [E0059, E0042, E0045].
7. **Check the ePASS** by its QR code or at imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/eservices/epass?semakan [E0060].
- If refused: only the company can appeal, within 14 days (decision in up to 30 working days) [E0063].
- Changing employer means a new application [E0006].
- Bringing family: see below.

### Bringing your family (EP holder)
1. Get your marriage and children's birth certificates translated into English and certified by the Malaysian High Commission in Dhaka (or the Bangladesh High Commission in KL), dated the current year [E0030].
2. Your company applies on ESD Online for a DP (spouse, children under 18) or an LTSVP (parents, in-laws, children 18-25, common-law spouse) and pays RM540 per person [E0014, E0041]. For an LTSVP, you sign a personal bond (RM750 for Bangladeshis) [E0144, E0031].
3. Decision in about 14 working days; then endorsement at RM90 a year + RM50 + visa [E0029, E0043].
4. Your family can't work on a DP or LTSVP unless they get their own EP (ESD); IMI's older FAQ mentions a work endorsement for a DP spouse (conflict) [E0028, E0038].

### PLKS workers
- You can't bring your family or marry in Malaysia while on PLKS [E0138, E0139].

### Married to a Malaysian
1. Register the marriage under Malaysian law [E0129].
2. Both go to the state Immigration office with the forms and documents in E0126; LTSVP of 6 months to 5 years; 25 working days [E0122, E0123, E0057].
3. To work, apply for the work endorsement with the offer letter and RM10-stamped contract [E0125].
4. After 3 years, you can apply for a Pas Residen (RM500 for 5 years) [E0131, E0133].

### MM2H
1. Pick a licensed operator from mm2h.gov.my/agencies and check its licence number and validity [E0116, E0101].
2. Have the fixed deposit (Silver USD150,000 and up) and money for the property (Silver RM600,000 and up) [E0106, E0108].
3. Pay a RM5,000 processing fee plus the participating fee; police may interview you; medical check at a MOTAC panel clinic [E0107, E0109, E0120].
4. Buy the home within 1 year; stay 90 days a year if under 50; only Platinum may work [E0119, E0103, E0112].

### DE Rantau
1. Check you earn USD36,000+ (tech) or USD60,000+ (non-tech) a year from foreign employers or clients, with contracts that started 3+ months ago [E0085].
2. Apply at mdec.my/derantau (closed 1-30 Nov 2026, reopens 1 Dec 2026); pay RM1,080 + RM540 per dependant [E0099, E0091].
3. Wait 6-8 weeks and check malaysiadigital.mdec.my [E0089, E0090].
4. Get a VDR or eVISA, enter, and endorse (RM360 a year pass fee + visa + RM750 refundable bond) [E0089, E0091, E0092].
