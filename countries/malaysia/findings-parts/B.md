# Findings, part B: Bangladesh-side steps, money home, coming home, corridor numbers

Researched 6 Nov 2026. Ledger ids B0001-B0145 in `ledger-parts/B.csv`: 64 verified, 67 possibly-outdated, 11 conflict, 2 lead, 1 unofficial-practice.

All ten PDFs in `official-pdfs/` were read in full. Seven were copied to raw/ and logged under their official URL; the 74-page WEWB annual report, the 28-page ministry charter and the 10-page PKB charter were read page by page from images (reader subagents transcribed the quotes, which I checked against the claims). The Saudi-only PDFs (SVP FAQ, Takamol poster, Saudi skill-test notice, Saudi permission letter) hold nothing Malaysia-specific; the only general points are BMET's 104 TTCs and the standard permission-letter conditions (worker chosen from the BMET database under s.19(3) of the 2013 Act; signed contract with a Bangla translation; payment by cheque/draft/pay order). These were not ledgered as facts for Malaysia.

Newer versions found online: the BMET citizen charter (page updated 30 Sep 2026) and the PKB charter (published 29 Sep 2026, identical to the local copy). The ministry's welfare-fee circular of 20 Sep 2026 changes the welfare fee. The WEWB citizen-charter PDF in `official-pdfs/` was not re-read page by page: its services are covered by the Board's live service pages (most updated 7 Oct 2026), which supersede it.

## Notes by doc section

### Step: register on OEP and train (all work routes)
- OEP registration: employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/login-with-otp in Chrome; details exactly as on the NID (drop dots, correct spaces); OTP; full form; pay by bKash/Nagad; download Registration Card [B0139]. The fee amount is not stated [B0139].
- TTC course: log in, Training portal, Trade/Skills, division/district/centre, Search, Apply, photo must match your face, Process to Enroll, Confirm, My Course [B0140]. 55 trades; short-course fee set by BMET (money receipt); project-funded courses are free and pay a stipend [B0037].
- **Malaysia skill test:** BMET's leaflet tells Malaysia-bound workers to pass the CIDB skill test, run at Khulna TTC, to be added in Dhaka, Chattogram and Rajshahi [B0001]. The leaflet is undated (Part A should confirm current CIDB arrangements).
- OEP hotline 09614445271 (on OEP page; ledger row failed on quote length; it is in raw/oep-country-clearance.md "### 09614445271").

### Before paying (checks, payment, complaints)
- Pay the agency only through the bank into the agency's account; keep receipts [B0003]. Have visa and papers checked at BMET Dhaka or the district office [B0005].
- **Visa check at BMET:** free, 3 working days; bring the visa and the passport page with the visa stamp. Online checks cover only Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Singapore and UAE: **not Malaysia** [B0032, B0033].
- **Complaint to BMET:** self-explanatory application with proof to the DG, BMET (complaint cell); inquiry committee and hearing; free; 30 working days; BMET recommends and the ministry decides [B0034, B0035]. GRS ladder: Director (Employment) diremi@bmet.gov.bd 3 months, then Secretary secretary@probashi.gov.bd 1 month, then grs.gov.bd 3 months [B0041].
- **Complaint to the ministry (enforcement branch):** application + proof + your full address + agency RL number to sasenforcement@probashi.gov.bd (02-41030255); hearing of both sides; starts in 3-5 working days, settled in 30 working days; free [B0044, B0045]. For agencies that sent workers without permission: application to the Secretary with smart card, ticket, contract, passport copy; 60 working days [B0046]. Mobile courts under the 2013 Act give immediate remedies, 7 working days [B0047]. Ministry GRS: adsadmin@probashi.gov.bd 30 working days, appeal adsorg@ 20, Cabinet Division 60 [B0053].
- If anyone offers to fix TTC admission/passing or clearance for money, tell the police [B0010].

### PKB migration loan
- Up to Tk 3 lakh, no collateral, 9% simple interest, up to 3 years including grace, monthly instalments, decided in 7 working days [B0017]. Eligibility: Bangladeshi, 18-60, applicant and guarantors permanent residents of the branch area, no loan default [B0013].
- Documents: free form, 3 photos, NID, passport, visa, BMET card (not needed for re-entry visa), contract (optional), 3 signed MICR cheques + statement, PKB savings account (min Tk 500) [B0014]. Re-entry visa: proof of legal remittance, proof of job abroad, cost statement [B0015]. Two guarantors (one solvent with cheques/statement/income proof; one family member), each with photo, NID, address proof [B0016].
- Fees: 0.5% processing + VAT, 0.5% documentation + VAT, Tk 900 per lakh risk cover; 16 Tk 10 revenue stamps; 3 Tk 100 adhesive stamps per guarantor [B0018]. Online application on mygov.bd (one application at a time; pay after decision) [B0019, B0020]. Helpline 16238, from abroad +880 9677-787878 [B0018]; loans dept migration@pkb.gov.bd, 01716-175560 [B0055]; PKB GRS gm2@pkb.gov.bd [B0056].
- Women migrant loan: same Tk 3 lakh / 9% / 3 years [B0025].
- The PKB loan page itself is undated (status possibly-outdated); the rehabilitation and women-loan pages are dated Sep and Aug 2026.

### Police clearance
- Online at pcc.police.gov.bd: passport valid 3+ months; present address = an address in the passport where you live; register, Apply, details, addresses, upload scans, submit [B0105]. Documents: attested scan of passport data page (gazetted officer, or embassy if abroad), NID/birth certificate if MRP has no address [B0106]. Status by SMS "PCC S <ref>" to 26969; helpdesk 01320001824/25 [B0107]. Someone else can collect with an attested authorisation letter [B0108].
- **Fee conflict:** portal says Tk 1,500 treasury challan (code 1-7301-0001-2681) or card [B0105]; police.gov.bd guidance says Tk 500 (code 1-2201-0001-2681) [B0109].

### Attestation/apostille
- Bangladesh joined the Apostille Convention (in force 30 Mar 2025); **Malaysia is not in the HCCH status table**, so an apostille alone is not the route for Malaysia [B0110]. Malaysia's own attestation requirements: Part A/C (BD HC KL "checklist for attestation" page exists on kualalumpur.mofa.gov.bd).

### Pre-departure orientation and BMET clearance
- Compulsory 3-day PDO at 70 institutes; fee set by the institute, paid against a money receipt [B0036, B0012].
- Agency-processed clearance: 3 working days; agency submits application, visa, attested contract, attested OEP registration card, Tk 300 stamp undertaking, original pay-order for welfare fee and smart card fee, original income-tax challan [B0026, B0027].
- **Own-visa clearance:** apply yourself at BMET emigration branch (Dhaka) or DEMO Chattogram: application, visa copy, contract if any, attested OEP card copy, Tk 300 non-judicial stamp declaration, pay-order for welfare fee + insurance; smart card fee Tk 250; 3 working days [B0029, B0030].
- **Amounts (current):** welfare fee **Tk 5,500 from 1 Oct 2026** (ministry circular 20 Sep 2026) [B0043], replacing Tk 3,500 still shown in the BMET charter [B0028]; insurance premium Tk 1,000 one-time [B0008, B0028]; emigration clearance fee Tk 250 per worker; source income tax Tk 400 (unattested visa) / Tk 250 (attested) for single visas, Tk 1,200 skilled / Tk 800 unskilled for group visas [B0028]. Expected total at clearance (my arithmetic, not stated anywhere): Tk 5,500 + 1,000 + 250 + tax.
- Airport desks check smart cards; 1,098 travellers with no/fake clearance were stopped in FY2023-24 [B0038, B0128].
- Before flying: copies of all papers (1 set at home, 3 with you), 2 bank accounts, labour wing contacts [B0002, B0004]; never carry others' packets [B0006].

### After arrival: joining the Welfare Board from Malaysia
- If you came without BMET clearance: apply at portal.wewb.gov.bd; processed by the High Commission labour wing; copy of online application, passport, work permit; fee **equivalent of Tk 6,500 in ringgit, including life insurance** (page 7 Oct 2026) [B0057, B0127]. Workers with BMET clearance don't need membership; check at portal.wewb.gov.bd/bmet-wewbm-check [B0058].

### Sending money home
- Use legal channels, not hundi [B0007].
- Costs from Malaysia: Bangladesh Bank says average cost up to USD 200 was 3.19% in Apr-Jun 2026 (overall 1.12%) [B0095]. World Bank (Q3 2025, older): average 7.51% for MYR 610; MoneyGram 5.05%, Western Union 5.15%, NBL/Agrani 5.32%, Merchantrade 5.49%, CBL 5.50%; banks 8.9-15.8% [B0096, B0097].
- Check a remitter is licensed on BNM's MSB licensee list / approved agents list; report illegal remittance to BNM's contact centre (BNMTELELINK 1-300-88-5465) [B0098-B0100]. Agrani Remittance House is a licensed Class B remitter with branches in KL, Rawang, Klang, Bukit Mertajam, JB, Seremban [B0101].
- bKash partners in Malaysia: Ria, CBL, Mycash Online, Mobilemoney Ripple, Merchantrade, Al Jadeed, Wise, Global Money Exchange, NBL, Aman Exchange, WorldRemit, Placid Express, Touch 'n Go, Western Union, MoneyGram; give receiver's bKash number and full name; ATM cash-out Tk 7 per thousand [B0102, B0103].
- 2.5% government incentive: stated by bKash [B0103] and news (TBS, June 2026) [B0104 lead]; Bangladesh Bank circular blocked.

### If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job
- Body from Malaysia: company letter to the High Commission; employer appoints casket company; papers (company letters, insurance claim letter, compensation letter, hospital letter, death certificate, Malaysian police report, passport) to the HC; family's claim/consent letter naming the receiver; tell the airport desk [B0119]. Bodies normally come home at the employer's cost; the Board pays if no employer/employer refuses/community can't help (Tk 10 lakh spent for Malaysia in FY24) [B0125]. Family applies to the Board (call 16135) [B0074]; or to the ministry (aswelfare@probashi.gov.bd) with death certificate etc., 7 working days, incl. undocumented workers [B0051]. Burial in Malaysia: family's written view sent via the Board [B0075].
- Airport: Tk 35,000 cheque for transport and burial for every body; collect within 3 months; documents: warish certificate, nominee photo and NID/passport, deceased's passport, NOC, death certificate, airway bill [B0066, B0067, B0039, B0038].
- Tk 3 lakh grant: if BMET clearance or Board membership; only for death in the country of clearance; no application needed if the airport cheque was taken; if buried abroad apply within 1 year; heirs = spouse, children, parents (else full siblings); documents: family certificate countersigned by UNO, Tk 400 stamp indemnity, embassy NOC/death certificate, bank certificate with routing number, photos, passport copy; paid by BEFTN with SMS [B0060-B0065]. (Saudi doc's "60 working days" is not on the current page.)
- Insurance (Jiban Bima Corporation): Tk 10 lakh death, total disability Tk 10 lakh, partial 10-90%, Tk 50,000 if job lost and back within 6 months; 5 years from card [B0008, B0009, B0085]; Board helps claim with clearance/membership proof and passport copy [B0059].
- Malaysian compensation for deaths: Labour Department Form H sent to heirs, returned via the HC [B0120]; labour wings recover dues and the Board pays heirs; legal cell helps with power of attorney [B0136, B0076].
- Injured/sick: Board helps bring home and admit to hospital [B0073]; treatment aid up to Tk 2 lakh, apply within 1 year of return, WhatsApp video check [B0071, B0072] (conflict with Tk 1.5 lakh in 2024 report [B0129]); free airport ambulance [B0070] (2024 report says "low-cost" [B0130]).
- Help bringing a living worker home: relative applies to the ministry with workplace/residence address, agency details, employer's mobile; 7 working days [B0052]. Unpaid wages: ministry Welfare-1 branch, 7 working days [B0048]. Harassment of women: aswelfare@, 7 working days [B0049]. Compensation for deaths via ministry [B0050].
- No Welfare Board safe home in Malaysia [B0079, B0135].

### Coming home
- Customs (HSIA, SRO 261/2025): duty-free 100 g gold jewellery, 200 g silver, 200 cigarettes, 2 used + 1 new phone once a year; one gold bar up to 117 g (10 tola) a year at Tk 5,000 per tola, declared; no alcohol for Bangladeshi passport holders; FMJ form over USD 10,000; declaration form on the plane, or within 7 days at the custom house [B0111-B0115]. Customs emergency +88 01319-056591 [B0116].
- Airport desk: help with customs and lost luggage, complaints from cheated returnees, food for returnees from jail/outpass, cash in special cases (war, disaster, layoff), ambulance [B0068, B0069]. Women returning within 1 year after abuse/fraud get Tk 5,000 cash [B0038].
- Wage Earners' Centre, Borua (Lonjonipara, Ward 48, Khilkhet): book at wec.wewb.gov.bd or in person with clearance/membership proof, passport and ticket copies; Tk 100 admission + Tk 200/night; 01310 350555, 01754 715720 [B0080, B0081]. Probashi Lounge at HSIA: free wifi, food up to 30% off, free phone, print/photocopy; show clearance/membership proof [B0082].
- Family protection: apply to the Board directly or via DC office/DEMO; Board writes to local administration (DC/SP) [B0078, B0132].
- Reintegration: PKB rehabilitation loan up to Tk 50 lakh (Tk 3 lakh unsecured), 9%, 10 years, 15 working days, trade licence compulsory [B0021-B0024]. RAISE project (cash incentives, counselling, training) ran July 2021-June 2026 [B0084, B0133]; district welfare centres since July 2023 [B0133]. BRAC Migration Programme: airport help, counselling, livelihood support, legal help [B0122].

## Draft steps

**Step 1 (Before paying), additions for Malaysia:**
1. Register on OEP: open employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/login-with-otp in Chrome, type your mobile number and the code sent to it, enter everything exactly as on your NID, pay by bKash or Nagad, and download the registration card [B0139]. Hotline 09614445271.
2. If you need a trade, enrol at a TTC through OEP: Training, Trade/Skills, choose division, district and centre, Search, Apply, Process to Enroll, Confirm [B0140, B0037]. BMET tells Malaysia-bound workers to take the CIDB skill test, now at Khulna TTC [B0001].
3. Pay the agency only into its bank account and keep receipts [B0003].
4. Check the visa at BMET (Kakrail) or your district manpower office: bring the visa and the passport page with the visa stamp; free, 3 working days. BMET's online check doesn't cover Malaysia [B0032, B0033, B0005].
5. Borrow from PKB if needed (terms above) [B0013-B0020].
6. Complain if cheated: BMET DG (30 working days) or ministry enforcement branch (3-5 days to start, 30 to settle); GRS ladder [B0034, B0044, B0041, B0053].

**Police clearance step:** register on pcc.police.gov.bd, Apply, fill details and the address where you live (it must be in your passport), upload the attested passport page, pay the fee shown (portal: Tk 1,500 challan or card; police.gov.bd guidance: Tk 500), track by SMS "PCC S <ref>" to 26969 [B0105-B0109]. **Need to know:** processing time.

**Orientation and clearance step:**
1. Do the 3-day PDO at a TTC; pay the centre's fee against a receipt [B0036].
2. BMET clearance: the agency applies; 3 working days [B0026]. Own visa: apply yourself at BMET Dhaka or DEMO Chattogram with the documents listed [B0029].
3. Fees: welfare fee Tk 5,500 (from 1 Oct 2026), insurance Tk 1,000, smart card/clearance fee Tk 250, income tax Tk 250-400 (single) or Tk 800-1,200 (group) [B0043, B0028, B0030].
4. At the airport the welfare desk checks your card; without one you are stopped [B0038, B0128].

**After arrival:** if you came without clearance, join the Welfare Board at portal.wewb.gov.bd through the labour wing; about Tk 6,500 in ringgit, with life insurance [B0057].

**Sending money home:** choose a BNM-licensed remitter (check BNM's list), compare costs (BB: 3.19% average for small sums; WB: MTOs ~5-5.5%, banks 9-16%), send to a bank or bKash account with the receiver's exact name; family gets the 2.5% incentive; report hundi operators to BNM [B0095-B0104].

**Death/injury/coming home:** as in the notes above.
