## Going home without valid papers

- PRM 2.0 rules: IMI posters (L0113-L0124, Sep 2026). Exclusions and the special-status route are from the Home Minister's May 2025 statement (T0002-T0004, Utusan) and The Star Apr 2026 (H0008).
- 5-day rule: the High Commission's First Secretary (Press), Sep 2026 (H0002, bdnews24 English and Bangla) and the eCentral guide Dec 2025 (T0007) both say the flight must be at least 5 days after the Immigration visit; IMI's poster says only "within 14 days" (L0116). Not a conflict (the poster is silent), so the doc states both limits.
- Walk-in: PRM 2.0 is walk-in (L0113). The 2024 programme used an online appointment system, sto.imi.gov.my, from 7 Mar 2024 (poster saved as raw/imi-prm-sto-poster-2024.html; it is a PNG saved under an .html name by fetch.py; not registered or cited). Not in the doc because PRM 2.0 is walk-in.
- Offices: the poster says 14 Enforcement offices in Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan; the eCentral guide (T0009) lists Putrajaya HQ plus the state offices (and wrongly adds Sabah and Sarawak); addresses re-checked against IMI's own contact page (T0010-T0015).
- Travel Permit: fee RM38 from 16 Jan 2026 (R0003) replaces RM44 (L0008, 2023). Processing: same day after 4 pm if applied 9:30-1:00 (L0016, FAQ Jan 2025) vs 1-2 working days, counter 10-4 (L0011/L0012, 2023 page). The current consular page (Aug 2026) gives neither; the newer FAQ is kept. Photos: current page two (L0003); 2025 FAQ three (L0014). Current page kept.
- Practice: crowds at KL (T0031), Seremban 150 a day, queues from 6 am (H0009). TP numbers (H0005, H0022) and depot deaths (F0054, F0056) kept out as statistics.
- Scams: High Commission warning (H0004), "no third party" (T0008). A Daily Sun report (Mar 2024) on detained Bangladeshis pushed to buy overpriced tickets (RM1,850 vs RM500-700) through one agency, and on fraudsters posing as High Commission staff, could not be read (blocked). A Bernama item on people impersonating Immigration officers is also blocked.
- Outside bodies: Tenaganita takes individual cases (A0086: 74 Bangladeshi workers won RM1.54m through it, Jun 2025); contacts T0036. No evidence found that ILO or IOM take individual cases in Malaysia; UNHCR's detention hotline (N0048) is for refugees and asylum seekers.
- Bans: official law only (K0012-K0022). Practice: deportees blacklisted (H0018, H0019, H0021); 2017 DG statement of 1-5 years and life for serious cases (H0012, H0013) and 2024 Daily Star Bangla report of 5 years or life (H0016) kept out of the doc text as old; length still a Need to know.
- Sample letter to the Ministry (bring-home request) is a template written from the charter's required contents (B0052, Z0015).
