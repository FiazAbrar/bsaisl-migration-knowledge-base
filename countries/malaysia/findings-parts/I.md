## Changes from the old draft and why

- **EPF (retirement fund):** the old draft said EPF for foreign workers was only "proposed (Feb 2025), no start date" [M0163, M0164]. EPF's own page says mandatory from October 2025 wages, 2% employer + 2% worker, for Visitor's Pass (Foreign Workers) holders except domestic helpers; automatic registration (notices sent from 20 Sep 2025); worker must visit an EPF office for thumbprint; full "Leaving the Country Withdrawal" exists; Akaun Fleksibel withdrawals from RM50 and age 50/55/60 withdrawals online from 31 Oct 2026 [I0014-I0018]. The "Your rights at work > Pension" bullet and "Need to know: whether EPF has started" elsewhere in the doc are now outdated; whoever owns those chunks should update them. The KWSP news page (kwsp.gov.my/en/w/news/epf-begins-mandatory-...) returned 403 to curl; the FAQ page was scraped by Firecrawl instead.
- **Passport police verification:** Home Ministry circular of 18 Feb 2025 (scanned PDF, transcribed) ends police verification for new passports based on the online-verified NID [I0009]. DW Feb 2025 confirms and quotes TIB: three-quarters of applicants paid bribes at police verification; money demanded "at every step" [I0010, I0011]. TIB 2017 (jagonews, scraped but not saved/cited): Tk 797 average bribe for verification, Tk 2,221 at the office; too old to cite.
- **Passport complaints:** helpline 16445 / 09666716445 (Natore RPO notice, Apr 2023) [I0024]; each passport office site lists a grievance redress officer and links to GRS [I0025, I0026].
- **FOMEMA result for workers:** IMI MyIMMs has a public "FOMEMA Application Status" by passport number + citizenship [I0001]. This resolves the old "Need to know: how a worker sees their own result" partly (status, not the report). D0028 (FMT 2025: no one sees the report) kept as practice.
- **SPIKPA:** MOH page: cashless at MOH hospitals, show valid passport, worker pays above limit, unpaid bills block renewal, employer needs JTK permission to deduct premium [I0019-I0021]. CONFLICT on the cover limit: MOH page (undated, describes 2011 scheme) says RM10,000/yr and RM120 premium; insurers (Tokio Marine, Chubb, eInsurans) say RM20,000/yr for RM120. Not saved; doc omits the amount. Need an official current figure.
- **PDO:** BMET's PDO page lists the 10 course topics, a pre-departure checklist, contract checks (get contract 2 days before) and luggage rules [I0003-I0006]. Official portal training.oep.gov.bd has enrollment-card and certificate downloads by passport/NID [I0007, I0008]. Fee: BMET charter says institute-set, paid against money receipt [B0036]. probashiapp.net (Oct 2026 SEO blog, scraped not saved) claims Tk 100 course + Tk 100 service fee via the Ami Probashi app, 2-year certificate validity, fake-certificate rackets charging Tk 2,000-10,000; not cited because the site is an unofficial app blog with unverifiable and partly generic content.
- **PNR / fake tickets:** TBS May 2024: agencies gave fake tickets, workers turned out of the airport, last-minute demands of Tk 50,000 more [I0012, I0013]. How to check: Biman FAQ Manage Booking with six-character PNR + last name [I0022] (biman-airlines.com 403 to curl, Firecrawl direct-quote extraction saved). Other carriers (US-Bangla, Malaysia Airlines, AirAsia) not checked.
- **Medical (Bio-Medical):** Ministry Facebook notices 28 and 31 Oct 2026 (Z0159-Z0168) remain the core. Notable: "the agency that made the medical slip" = the agency makes the MiGRAMS slip. Ministry says the approved list "will soon be published on the website"; probashi.gov.bd notices search shows only 2022 and 2023 lists (probashi-notice-medical-centre-list 17 Aug 2022, -list-published 25 Jul 2022, -enlistment 4 Apr 2023); not used. FWCMS list of 51 (Jugantor 6 Nov 2026, D0014) – all Dhaka/Gazipur addresses per Jugantor. Order conflict: FWCMS says Bio-Medical before eVDR [W0009]; Ministry 28 Oct says agencies must submit VDR papers to the centre [Z0162]. Doc states both rules where they apply without commenting; worth asking BMET/Ministry.
- **Dropped from the doc (kept here):** security bond RM500 vs RM750 conflict (Z0087, Z0088, Z0169) and levy table (M0226, M0227, Z0006) – employer costs, not worker actions; doc says the employer pays all of them [W0054]. i-Kad delivery conflict (vendor vs employer collects) and colour conflict (Z0092, Z0093): doc follows IMI's Feb 2026 poster [L0126, L0127]. ## Reality checks (what happens in practice)

- Medical: previous round, agencies took Tk 11,000 vs fixed Tk 6,500; five times as many did medicals as went (TBS Sep 2025, D0021, D0022); unfit-then-bribe at Gulf centres (D0023); unauthorised testing centre sealed 1 Oct 2026 (D0017). No reports yet (search 7 Nov 2026, past month) of overcharging since 31 Oct; minister repeated the Tk 8,000 cap in early Nov.
- VDR: levy deducted from wages (F0082, US TIP 2026).
- Arrival: workers arrived with valid permits and no jobs (ILO May 2026, A0095; Dhaka Tribune Jun 2026, A0014). Reuters May 2023 and Andy Hall May 2024 (KLIA overrun) found but not saved.
- FOMEMA: no report access (D0028); forged passports sold to FOMEMA-unfit workers (D0035).
- Passport retention: widespread (F0084, TIP 2026); a June 2026 case of workers complaining to JTK with Migrant Welfare Network (H0032). OHCHR Nov 2025 press release on systematic exploitation of Bangladeshi workers: 403, not saved.
- ILO/IOM: ILO representation procedure is for workers'/employers' organisations, not individuals (G0027); IOM hotline merged into 16135 (G0028).
