## Tourist visa (social visit): Step 2, Apply on MyVISA

**MyVISA outages.** From 10 Feb 2026 the High Commission of Malaysia in Dhaka said MyVISA would be unavailable "until further notice". All new applications and approvals stopped, with no end date given (unofficial: N0001, N0003, N0004; same event as V0106, V0107). Applicants were told to email "the service provider" for urgent help, but the address is hidden on the saved page (N0002). This suggests HC Dhaka still uses an outsourced provider.

The Malaysian mission in New Delhi posted official notices of a further global MyVISA outage and of its restoration on 23 Jul 2026 (verified: N0005, N0006; notice bodies are images, transcribed in the notes). The doc can say that MyVISA sometimes goes offline for maintenance and that restorations are announced on Malaysian mission pages [N0005, N0006].

**Need to know:** the date MyVISA reopened for Bangladeshi applicants after the 10 Feb 2026 pause. I searched English and Bangla news, kln.gov.my/web/bgd_dhaka news and posts, and IMI announcements. Nothing found; the HC Dhaka Facebook page is blocked. MyVISA was clearly working again by Sep 2026 (the MEV change, V0108).

**Apply yourself.** In Dec 2025 the Malaysian High Commissioner was asked about Bangladeshis turned back at the airport. He urged people to apply directly on the official eVISA portal and said its link is posted on the HC's Facebook page (unofficial: N0054). This supports the official V0005-V0007.

**Agencies in Dhaka and their fees.** History only (pre-MyVISA):
- 2015: HC Dhaka used a private processor, Visa Luar Negara (VLN), through 24 nominated travel agents. Applicants paid a Tk 3,100 bank draft against a Tk 600 sticker fee (N0021, N0022 (2015)).
- 2016: applicants paid Tk 5,400 with no receipt for Tk 4,800 of it (N0023 (2016)).
- 2023: MACC (the Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission) arrested an HC Dhaka enforcement officer for approving visas through the VLN system for US$300-500 bribes each since 2021. Tourist visas were reportedly sold to people actually going to work (N0024, N0025 (2023)).
- Current: a May 2026 travel blog puts agents' charges for an eVISA at Tk 5,000-8,000, against the official RM20 visa fee + RM105 processing charge (V0016, V0017). Low reliability: the same blog lists eNTRI, which is India-only (N0056).

**Need to know:** the current official list of authorised agencies or service providers in Dhaka and their service fee (as in V's Need to know; HC Dhaka "Agencies" page lists only Tourism Malaysia).

**Refusals.** In 2025 news, Malaysia was among the countries refusing Bangladeshi applicants most often. Travel operators blame fake documents, overstaying and people using tourist visas to work (N0026, N0027). There is no official refusal rate.

## Tourist visa: Step 4, MDAC

- IMI told travellers, on Facebook, to use only imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/mdac/main (N0030; consistent with official V0065).
- Feb 2026: sponsored Google ads led to fake sites such as mcadfast.com. They take personal details and then charge (AUD52/RM145). Press reports say MDAC is free and there is no paid "faster approval" (N0057, N0031 (2024)).
- Press lists the official channels as IMI's website, the MyNIISE app and QR codes in the airport arrival hall (N0058).

**Need to know (still open):** an official statement that MDAC is free. IMI's MDAC announcement page and its 2023 infographic (raw/imi-mdac-announcement, raw/imi-mdac-infographic-bm-2023) give no fee either way. In the doc: "reported free (unofficial, not verified)".

## Tourist visa: Step 5, At the border (denied entry / "Not to Land")

**What AKPS checks.** AKPS is the Malaysian Border Control and Protection Agency. In 2025 it ran early-morning screening of Dhaka flights at KLIA Terminal 1. It turned back Bangladeshis holding valid visas:
- 51 in Mar 2025 (N0007, N0008)
- 96 on 11 Jul (V0111)
- 123 on 23 Jul (N0013)
- 80 on 25 Jul (N0011)
- 40 on 12 Aug (N0009)
- 98 on 15 Aug (V0109)

Reasons given: fake hotel bookings, no return ticket, too little money or foreign currency, falsified documents, a vague purpose, and failing to give "satisfactory justification". Some people admitted they came for jobs (N0007, N0008, N0011, N0013). AKPS says entrants must show a valid passport and visa, enough money and credible accommodation (N0012). More than 200 Bangladeshis were deported after refusal in 2025 (N0014). In 2023, most NTL notices went to Bangladeshi, Pakistani and Indian travellers (N0016 (2023)).

**What happens after an NTL.** Refused travellers are deported within 24 hours (N0010). They are held in a designated airport area until the airline flies them back (N0015 (2023), N0020 (2022)). Since a July 2023 Cabinet decision, airlines and not third parties handle the return (N0015). A traveller who doesn't understand the questions can ask for a translator (Indian High Commission advisory, by analogy: N0019 (2022)). An NTL means you cannot enter until you meet the entry conditions, and blacklisting is one listed reason for an NTL (N0017, N0018 (2023, NST explainer)).

**Need to know (still open):** whether an NTL is recorded against future visa applications or entries, and whether the traveller is ever charged for the return flight. No official IMI or AKPS page found.

## Tourist visa: Don't work / Scams (tourist visas sold as jobs)

- Jan 2025: the BD High Commission in KL warned that fraudsters bring Bangladeshi women on tourist or other visas with false job promises. There is no agreement for recruiting female workers, and women were told not to travel for general work (news report: N0028).
- Aug 2025: the BD High Commission in KL warned that a ring collects passports and cash for non-existent Sabah jobs. There is no MoU covering Sabah (news report: N0029; the official advisory page is blocked).
- 2023: corruption at HC Dhaka included tourist visas issued to people actually going to work (N0024 (2023)).

**Need to know:** the official texts of both BD High Commission warnings (see blocked-parts/N.md).

## Student visa: practical notes

- **Working.** A mainstream Bangla guide (Prothom Alo, Jun 2025) tells students they can work up to 20 hours "combining semester breaks or weekends". The official rule allows work only in semester breaks or holidays, with Immigration approval. N0053 is status **conflict** with official S0013, S0039 and S0041; the official rule wins. The lead should add a note on S0013.
- **Overstaying.** Community members say many Bangladeshis who came on student visas stayed after their studies (N0036).
- **Forgery.** In Feb 2026 a Bangladeshi on a valid student pass was arrested for running a passport-forgery ring that helped Bangladeshis pass FOMEMA (foreign-worker medical) checks. He was held under s.56(1)(i) of the Immigration Act (N0035).
- **Graduate Pass.** In Aug 2025 a Bangladeshi adviser said Malaysia had agreed "in principle" to Graduate Pass visas for Bangladeshi students (N0032, N0033). Malaysia's Higher Education Minister then called this "false and baseless" (N0034). The official list still excludes Bangladesh (S0044). The doc can say that claims of a Graduate Pass for Bangladeshis were denied by the minister [S0044, N0034].
- **Existing student rows still stand:** S0134-S0140 (agents' fees Tk 6-8 lakh, students promised work, 2017 fake colleges).

**Need to know (still open):** EMGS (Education Malaysia Global Services) real processing times for Bangladeshi applicants. Only an undated forum and SEO pages claim "1-3 months"; nothing usable was found. Also open: any 2025-2026 official list of colleges whose permission to take international students was withdrawn (searched in Malay and English; none found).

## Medical visit

**Official MHTC figures and events.** MHTC is the Malaysia Healthcare Travel Council. Close to 70,000 Bangladeshi healthcare travellers visited Malaysia in 2025 (verified: N0039). Malaysia Healthcare Week ran in Dhaka on 5-12 Apr 2026, and in Dhaka and Chattogram on 16-21 Sep 2026. Hospitals taking part: Alpha IVF, IHH Healthcare, KL Fertility and Sunway Medical Centre in both cities, and KPJ Ampang Puteri in Dhaka (N0040). MHTC works through Bangladeshi "healthcare facilitators" and travel partners (N0041).

**Partner in Bangladesh.** Dhaka Tribune (Sep 2026) names Irving Aviation Limited as MHTC's Bangladesh partner. MHTC's own releases name none (N0042). MHTC named GD Assist in 2022 (V0078).

**Need to know (still open):** MHTC's current authorised partners in Bangladesh. In the doc, report the Irving Aviation name only as "unofficial, not verified". Demand is mainly for cardiology, oncology, transplants and surgery (N0043; corridor context only).

## Employment Pass (Bangladeshi professionals)

A "significant number" of Bangladeshi professionals work in Malaysia on Employment Passes (N0037). They fear the 1 June 2026 thresholds (official: E0008-E0011) will push employers to keep only exceptionally qualified Bangladeshis (N0038). This is corridor context only; there are no numbers.

## MM2H

In 2024 a Bangladesh Bank spokesperson said the central bank has never approved sending money to Malaysia for MM2H (Malaysia My Second Home), so that money did not leave legally (N0051, N0052 (2024)). E0141 has 3,604 Bangladeshi MM2H holders (Jan 2024).

**Need to know:** an official Bangladesh Bank or Foreign Exchange Regulation rule on whether a Bangladeshi resident may legally transfer the USD 150,000 MM2H deposit and buy property abroad. This matters for the "how" of MM2H for Bangladeshis.

## Asylum

**Bangladeshis are not a listed refugee group.** UNHCR registered 225,740 refugees and asylum-seekers (Aug 2026); about 90% are from Myanmar, and the other named origins are Pakistan, Yemen, Afghanistan and Somalia. Bangladesh is not named (verified: N0044). UNHCR's figures grow only with its own registration capacity (N0045). The doc can use this for "written to discourage": there is no listed Bangladeshi refugee population [N0044].

**Fake UNHCR cards.**
- A Bangladeshi-run syndicate in Kulim sold fake work permits and UNHCR cards for RM80-250 each; 19 Bangladeshis were arrested (Jun-Jul 2026) (N0049).
- A KL ring sold forged e-PLKS work passes for RM150 and fake police reports for RM70; 27 Bangladeshis were detained (Sep 2026) (N0050).

**How to check a UNHCR card is real (official).** UNHCR cards carry holograms, barcodes and a QR code. Anyone can check them with the free UNHCR Verify Plus app from Google Play or the App Store only. "Incompatible QR Code" means the document is not genuine. UNHCR Malaysia is at 570 Jalan Bukit Petaling, KL, tel 03 2118 4800; detention hotline +6012 630 5060 (N0046-N0048; booklet version Dec 2024, uploaded Jun 2026, so possibly-outdated). Use with S0109 and S0143 (UNHCR services are free).

## Draft steps (additions to existing V/S/E steps)

**Tourist, Step 2 (apply):**
1. Apply only on malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my yourself. The High Commissioner told Bangladeshis to apply directly on the official portal [V0005, N0054].
2. Agents reportedly charge Tk 5,000-8,000 against official charges of RM20 + RM105 (unofficial, not verified) [V0016, V0017, N0056]. Past Dhaka processors took unreceipted mark-ups [N0021 (2015), N0023 (2016)].
3. If MyVISA is down, wait for the mission's notice of restoration; outages happened in Feb and Jul 2026 [N0001, N0005, N0006].

**Tourist, Step 4 (MDAC):** search nothing; type imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/mdac/main or use MyNIISE [V0059, N0058]. Any site asking for payment is reported to be fake (unofficial, not verified) [V0065, N0057].

**Tourist, Step 5 (border):**
- Carry printed proof of a paid hotel booking, a return ticket and cash or a card for your stay [V0050, V0054].
- Expect questioning on early-morning Dhaka flights; refusals in 2025 were for fake bookings, no return ticket, too little money, or admitting a job search [N0007, N0011, N0013].
- If refused: you are held airside and the airline flies you back within about 24 hours; ask for a translator if needed [N0010, N0015 (2023), N0019 (2022)].

**Tourist, Scams:** a tourist visa with a "job" attached is a fraud. The BD High Commission KL has warned women and Sabah job-seekers [N0028, N0029]. A visit pass does not allow work [V0046].

**Student, Can students work?:** add that news guides overstate student work rights; the official rule is the one that applies [S0013, N0053].

**Medical, Step 2:** apply through an MHTC member hospital (V0073, V0076). **Need to know:** the current MHTC partner in Bangladesh; news names Irving Aviation (unofficial, not verified) [N0042].

**Asylum:** Bangladeshis are not among UNHCR Malaysia's listed nationalities [N0044]. Fake UNHCR cards are sold by syndicates and lead to arrest [N0049]. A genuine card can be checked with UNHCR Verify Plus [N0046, N0047].

**MM2H:** **Need to know:** Bangladesh Bank permission to move the deposit. In 2024 a BB spokesperson said no approval had ever been given (unofficial, not verified) [N0052 (2024)].
