## New how-to detail

- EMGS user guide PDF (created 19 Dec 2019, possibly outdated; mentions 6% tax and a physical VAL delivered only to the institution, both superseded): name as in passport MRZ, passport number must match offer letter, offer letter as one PDF (550 KB limit), payment via institution / telegraphic transfer (USD50 convenience fee) / card (3% fee), payment-problem email enquiry@emgs.com.my, institution must confirm the online application. ## Reality checks (student)
- Prothom Alo op-ed (USM senior lecturer, 19 Sep 2026): agents paid commission by institutions; free consultation = sales pitch; passports withheld for more payments; lapsed licences; work-on-student-visa lie; students caught working are deported, sometimes fined/jailed; verify offer letters with the international office by email/phone [O0017-O0020, S0134, S0135, S0137].
- Dhaka Post, 17 Oct 2026: three students, two agencies, about Tk 18.5 lakh; fake Genovasi offer letter; EMGS progress stuck 5-15% for three months; tuition not paid; moved to a Bangladeshi-owned English centre; two passports held by the agent (agent denies); families complained in writing to the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare (outcome not reported) [O0021-O0026]. Agency names are in the raw copy, not in the doc.
- Dhaka Tribune, 16 Jul 2026: Bhatara police case, court remand of education-consultancy officials over ~Tk 20 crore study-abroad fraud (91 students in one firm) [O0027].
- ApplyBoard guide (23 Sep 2026): tracker milestones 32-35% / 70% (eVAL downloadable) / 100%; says refusals can be appealed or re-applied via the institution (lead only, not official; doc keeps the appeal as Need to know) [O0013-O0016].
- 2017 R.AGE/Star series (Straits Times reprint saved raw/news-st-lured-students, not registered) and PIE News 2017 on EMGS pre-screening (raw/news-pie-emgs-visa-fraud, not registered): background only.
- Complaint channels: KPT SiSPAA at mohe.spab.gov.my (page updated 14 Jul 2025) [O0028]; MyGCC 24-hour call centre also listed on KPT contact page (raw/mohe-hubungi-sispaa, not logged). Bangladesh Ministry Complaint Resolution Cell minister.expat@gmail.com (R0012) is for Bangladeshis abroad; whether it acts on education-agent complaints is unknown. No evidence found either way on ILO/IOM/NGOs taking individual student-fraud cases.
