# Findings, part H: leaving Malaysia and coming home (practical pass)

Researched 6 Nov 2026. Ledger rows H0001-H0046 (ledger-parts/H.csv). 23 sources registered (22 news/NGO, `--official no`; 1 BRAC's own contact page, `--official yes`, as for the existing `brac-migration` source). Almost everything here is practice context: news rows are `unofficial` (what happens) or `lead` (a rule reported only by news, used only where an official search failed). Searched: English, Malay and Bangla (WebSearch), imi.gov.my / moha.gov.my / mofa.gov.bd (site-restricted), bb.org.bd circular list (Firecrawl single scrape: it loads, but no 2026 incentive circular is in the latest entries), brac.net.

## Leaving at the end of the job (doc: Work visa, "Leaving at the end of the job")

- **Employer must now be at the exit point for the Check-Out Memo (COM).** The Home Minister said in May 2025 that employers must be present at international exit points to manage the exit registration of foreign workers, as on entry; employers who don't comply may face limits on new quota applications [H0029, H0030]. Lead only: no KDN/IMI text found. Adds to the official COM steps L0071-L0074.
- **What goes wrong in practice:** Bangladeshi workers at a Malaysian factory went 3 months unpaid, with passports held by the company and about 35 left without a valid PLKS because the company didn't renew; they complained to the Labour Department (JTK, Jabatan Tenaga Kerja) with the Migrant Welfare Network platform [H0032]. Without a valid pass such a worker can't leave through the normal COM route; see PRM below.

## Going home on leave (doc: "Going home on leave", currently a Need to know)

- **Multiple-entry visa for Bangladeshi PLKS workers:** reported from 8 Aug 2025 (Home Ministry statement via FMT): workers already holding a PLKS and a single-entry visa don't apply separately; the multiple-entry visa (MEV) is given when the PLKS is renewed for the next year [H0027, H0028]. Lead only: the KDN/IMI circular (reported dated 10 Jul 2025) was not found on imi.gov.my or moha.gov.my.
- **Need to know (keep):** the official PLKS re-entry rule. Also whether IMI's MEV restriction from 1 Sept 2026 (V0009, written for visitors) touches workers' MEVs. The official rule that not returning on the agreed date counts as running away (L0058) stays the core line.

## If you want to change employer (cross-reference for part L/W)

- **Conflict:** The Star (May 2025) reports the same KDN-KSM meeting allowed employers to apply to move foreign workers **across sectors** (previously only within a sector, on closure, restructuring or expansion, or with a Labour Department committee's approval) [H0031], against the official but undated IMI pages L0055/W0052 (no change of employer or sector) and L0082 (outsourcing: same sector only). Status `conflict` on H0031. The doc must show the official line as the rule and add a Need to know: the current official process for a cross-sector transfer.

## Going home without valid papers (doc: same section)

**Travel Permit (TP) in practice**
- The High Commission issued about 83,000 travel permits to Bangladeshis May-Dec 2025 and about 100 a day in April 2026 [H0005]; 6,853 travel passes Jan-Jun 2026, 1,319 of them to people in detention camps [H0022]. (Two different counts from two outlets; the doc should not merge them.)
- High Commission staff visit 8 prisons and 13 detention camps regularly [H0023]; about 400 people got consular or legal help Jan-Jun 2026 [H0026].

**PRM 2.0 (Program Repatriasi Migran 2.0) in practice**
- The High Commission's own guidance (First Secretary Press, video on IMI's Facebook page, Sept 2026, reported by bdnews24 and Jugantor): valid passport or TP [H0001]; confirmed ticket dated **at least 5 days after the Immigration appointment and within 14 days** [H0002] (the official IMI poster L0116 gives only "within 14 days": the 5-day minimum is unofficial, not verified); appear in person, no representative; bring the original passport or TP, a copy of the biodata page, a copy of the relevant immigration pass, old and new passports if any, and the ticket [H0003]. "There is no need to pay any broker" [H0004].
- **Who can't use PRM 2.0:** people who registered under an earlier repatriation programme but didn't leave, people blacklisted by Immigration, people wanted by the authorities [H0008] (lead: an exact eligibility rule; not on the IMI poster, so Need to know with this report labelled unofficial).
- **Numbers:** Bangladeshis had the highest participation in Feb 2026 [H0007]; by mid-April 2026 Indonesians were first and Bangladeshis second, 204,523 gone out of 228,961 registered [H0006]. (Corridor section only.)
- **Crowds:** before Eid 2026 Klang Valley workers went to the Seremban office, which serves 150 a day; queues from 6am for an 8am opening; staff working to 10pm [H0009, H0010]; a Bangladeshi construction worker with work-pass problems used it to go home for Eid [H0011]. Practical point for the doc: go early, expect a cap per day, don't wait for the last weeks (the High Commission also says "do not wait until the last moment" [H0004 source]).

**Deportation and depots**
- On entering a depot, biometrics are taken and the person is blacklisted as a prohibited immigrant [H0015 (2022)].
- Deportees in 2026 (Kedah, Belantik depot; KLIA) were blacklisted in the Immigration (JIM) system to stop re-entry [H0018, H0019, H0021]; the air ticket is paid by the detainee, the family or the embassy [H0020]; in 2024, 39 Bangladeshis from the Pekan Nenas depot went home at their own cost after serving sentences [H0016, H0017 (2024)]. Official complement: L0017/L0018 (camp arranges the flight once there is a passport and a ticket; TP free in camps).

**How long the ban lasts (doc Need to know, step 5)**
- No official source found (searched imi.gov.my, Malay and English news, Akta 155 already in raw/). Only news: the Immigration DG said in 2017 that deported undocumented migrants can be blacklisted **1 to 5 years** by category of offence, and for **life** in serious cases, with a fingerprint blacklist (BioSL) [H0012, H0013, H0014 (2017)]; Daily Star Bangla (2024) reported **5 years or for ever** for 39 deported Bangladeshis [H0016 (2024)]; 2026 reports say "for a set period" without the length [H0019]. Official hook: prohibited immigrants include people removed from any country whom the DG deems undesirable (L0091).
- Whether **PRM 2.0 participants** are blacklisted: not found in any source (official or news). Keep as Need to know.

## If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job (doc: same section)

- Jan-Jun 2026: 13 bodies sent home at Bangladesh government cost; the High Commission helped with 584 more paid by employers, companies or relatives [H0024]; it recovered RM492,667 in unpaid wages and "condolence money" compensation [H0025].
- Cost in practice: about RM7,000 to send a body from Johor; one body lay 18 days in a hospital morgue because no relative was found, and a Bangladeshi community association paid [H0033 (2024)].
- At the airport the Welfare Board pays Tk35,000 per body, in practice as well (Apr 2026, incl. 2 bodies from Malaysia) [H0034], matching official B0066.
- Context: 4,813 bodies came home in 2024; Malaysia is among the main origins [H0036]; migrants say causes are often written as stroke or heart attack to avoid accident compensation [H0037]. (Context only; the doc rule is to ask for the police report and post-mortem papers already listed in L0023-L0025.)
- PERKESO: official PDFs already in raw/ (perkeso-foreign-worker-*), registered by another part. Not repeated here.

## Coming home (doc: same section)

- **Airport:** a BRAC Migration Programme official is stationed at Shahjalal airport [H0035]; Welfare Board Tk35,000 at the desk [H0034]. The Wage Earners' Centre at Khilkhet/Borua was reported in 2024 as temporarily disrupted because of a bad access road [H0046 (2024)]; official B0080 describes it as open, so the doc keeps B0080 and may add "check before going".
- **BRAC contact (doc Need to know):** brac.net gives only general contacts: BRAC Centre, 75 Mohakhali, Dhaka-1212; tel 88 02 2222 81265; info@brac.net [H0041, H0042]. No Migration Programme number on brac.net. A 2019 BRAC/IOM "Prottasha" returnee hotline (08000102030 toll free, 09610102030) [H0043 (2019)] was later reported merged into the government's Probashbondhu call centre (16135); current status unverified, so the doc should give the BRAC Centre contact and keep a narrower Need to know (Migration Programme's own line).
- **Reintegration:** RAISE (official B0084: July 2021-June 2026) paid direct cash to 29,518 returnees through district welfare centres ("Obhibashi Sheba Kendra") in RMMRU's districts alone [H0038]; many returnees stayed on the waiting list and RMMRU calls for a follow-up project [H0039]; returnees with trade skills were referred for Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) at TTCs [H0040]. For the doc: RAISE has ended; Need to know whether any successor scheme takes registrations now.

## Sending money home (doc: same section)

- **Risk of hundi to the family:** in 2022 Bangladesh Bank and BFIU (Bangladesh Financial Intelligence Unit) suspended about 5,000 agentships including 480 bKash/Nagad agent accounts over hundi, so remittances in those accounts couldn't be cashed [H0044 (2022)]; Bangladesh Bank's notice called sending money outside banking channels a punishable offence [H0045 (2022)] (lead: legal rule, needs the official notice).
- **2.5% incentive circular:** not found. bb.org.bd's circular list loads via Firecrawl (raw/bb-mediaroom-circular-list-fc.md) (the 10 newest entries, 26 Oct-5 Nov 2026, have no incentive circular); FEPD-2 Circular 09 of 29 Oct 2026 (consolidated FX rules, raw/bb-fepd2-circular-09-2026-10) does not mention the incentive. Existing lead B0104 (budget 11 Jun 2026) stands.

## Draft steps (for the doc writer)

**Leaving at the end of the job** (add after L0071-L0074 steps):
- Your employer must come to the airport with you and register your exit; this was announced in May 2025 (unofficial, not verified) [H0029]. If the company has stopped paying you, holds your passport or hasn't renewed your PLKS, complain to the Labour Department (JTK) and contact the High Commission's labour wing (B0117) [H0032; L-part steps].

**Going home on leave:**
- Need to know: the PLKS re-entry rule. Reported (unofficial, not verified): since 8 Aug 2025 Bangladeshi workers get a multiple-entry visa with their PLKS renewal, without a separate application [H0027, H0028]. Not returning on the agreed date counts as running away [L0058].

**Going home without valid papers:**
1. No passport: get a Travel Permit at the High Commission (official steps L0001-L0016). In practice it issued about 100 a day in April 2026 [H0005].
2. PRM 2.0 (official steps L0113-L0124). In practice: buy a ticket dated at least 5 days after your Immigration appointment and within 14 days (unofficial, not verified) [H0002]; go yourself, no agent; bring original passport or TP, a copy of the biodata page, a copy of your last pass, old and new passports, the ticket [H0003]; don't pay brokers [H0004]; expect daily caps and early queues [H0009, H0010]. Need to know: who is excluded (reported: earlier no-shows, blacklisted, wanted people [H0008], unofficial).
3. If detained: the depot takes fingerprints and blacklists you [H0015 (2022)]; you, your family or the embassy pay the ticket [H0020]; the High Commission visits camps and issues TPs there [H0023, H0022; official L0017-L0018].
4. Need to know: how long the ban lasts. Reported (unofficial, not verified): 1-5 years by offence, life for serious cases [H0012, H0013 (2017)]; "5 years or for ever" [H0016 (2024)]. Whether PRM participants are blacklisted: not found.

**If a worker dies:** add after step 2: in practice, sending a body from Malaysia has cost about RM7,000; where no employer pays, families and community groups have raised it, and the High Commission sent 13 bodies at government cost in Jan-Jun 2026 (unofficial, not verified) [H0033 (2024), H0024].

**Coming home:** BRAC: BRAC Centre, 75 Mohakhali, Dhaka-1212, tel 88 02 2222 81265, info@brac.net [H0041, H0042]; BRAC staff are at Shahjalal airport [H0035]. RAISE cash support has ended [B0084; H0039].
