## BOESL zero-cost jobs (Step 1.3; mentor: "reality check?")

- Circular image (5 Nov 2026) re-read from the PDF image on 7 Nov: window 06 Nov 10:00 to 08 Nov 16:00 (matches W0002 as corrected); apply with passport number, education, experience [G0031]; applying/interview/selection don't guarantee a job [G0032]; driving licence and experience count [G0033]; email info@boesl.gov.bd [G0034]. The window closes 8 Nov 2026, so the doc presents it as "the example".
- Reality check:
  - Amar Desh 28 Oct 2026: state minister Nurul Haque said going through BOESL may cost about Tk 1.55-1.60 lakh (airfare, medical, service charge) [G0010]. This conflicts with the zero-cost DHL circular and the "10,000 at zero cost" announcement; likely refers to ordinary (non-zero-cost) BOESL jobs, but not stated. Doc tells people to check the circular says no cost.
  - Daily Star Bangla 16 Oct 2026: eligibility and selection for the 10,000 not specified [G0009]; Asif Munir (migration expert) warns of false hopes.
  - Oversubscription: 2013 G2G, 1.4 million applied for ~10,000 [G0029, The Star 2013]; earlier reports (search only, not fetched) say 36,000 picked by lottery and only 4,000-8,000 actually sent. BOESL sent 1,381 Oct 2022-May 2024 [A0059].
  - No 2026 news found on lottery fairness or BOESL bribery (Bangla and English searches, 7 Nov).
- Fraud: BOESL warning (Aug 2025) of a ring using BOESL's name; BOESL has no agents [W0068].
