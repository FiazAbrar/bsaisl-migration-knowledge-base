## Work visa: domestic worker

Not open to Bangladeshis. Immigration's approved source countries for domestic helpers are Indonesia, Thailand, Cambodia, the Philippines, Sri Lanka, India, Vietnam, Laos and Nepal [L0194, L0076].

## Student visa (Student Pass)

A Student Pass lets you stay in Malaysia to study full time at an approved institution; it is not a work visa [S0023, S0042, S0013]. You apply through EMGS (Education Malaysia Global Services, a company under Malaysia's Ministry of Higher Education, KPT), for public and private institutions alike [Z0013, S0002, S0060]; the Immigration Department of Malaysia (IMI) gives final approval [S0003].

### Step 1: Check the institution and the course

Check before you pay anyone: agents earn commission from institutions and push the colleges paying most [O0020].

1. **Check the institution may take foreign students.** A private institution needs KPT approval, an accredited programme and the Home Ministry's (KDN's) permission to take international students; a public university needs no separate check [S0001]. KPT's Department of Higher Education (JPT) lists private institutions with that permission, updated monthly [O0001, O0042].
   1. On [jpt.mohe.gov.my, Senarai Daftar dan Statistik IPT Swasta](https://jpt.mohe.gov.my/portal/index.php/ms/ipt/ipt-swasta/25-senarai-daftar-dan-statistik-ipt-swasta), click the download icon beside "Senarai Daftar IPTS Kelulusan KDN" [O0001].
   2. Open "Tahun 2026", then the "AKTIF" (active) file with the highest number (in November 2026, file 7, the list up to 31 July 2026, updated 20 August 2026) [O0040, O0041, O0004].
   3. Search it for the institution's name; the campus address must match the offer letter [O0003].

   If the institution is missing, is in the "TIDAK AKTIF" (inactive) file of the same number, or is on the page's list of cancelled approvals ("Senarai IPTS Yang Telah Diluluskan Tindakan Pembatalan Kelulusan Penubuhan"), it can't get you a Student Pass, since IMI needs its KDN approval letter, in force, with every new application [O0041, O0005, O0002, O0006].
2. **Check the course is accredited** on the [Malaysian Qualifications Register (MQR)](https://www2.mqa.gov.my/mqr/) of MQA (the Malaysian Qualifications Agency): type the course in "Name of Qualification", choose the level and press Search [S0087]. Only accredited programmes appear, and only for the campus named; a course run anywhere else is not accredited [S0084, S0085]. "No record" can mean provisional accreditation only, never accredited, or failed [S0086].
3. **A language or training centre** needs approval from the Ministry of Education (KPM) and the Ministry of Human Resources (KESUMA), plus KDN's permission to take international students [O0043], and its students can't work part time or bring family [S0012, S0010]. To check one, contact KPM's Private Education Division: 03-8884 9536, bps@moe.gov.my, Aras 3, Blok E2, Kompleks E, 62604 Putrajaya; it acknowledges enquiries within 3 working days [O0044, O0045].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Is there a public list of KPM- or KESUMA-approved centres allowed to take international students?
   > - Where can you check a KESUMA-approved centre?

4. **Check you qualify:** 12 years of schooling and a recognised exam [S0043], and an English certificate, without which the application may be delayed and the pass won't cover the whole course [S0050]. The certificates accepted are:
   - MUET (the Malaysian University English Test) [S0050];
   - IELTS [S0050];
   - TOEFL iBT or Essentials [S0050];
   - PTE Academic [S0050];
   - Cambridge English [S0050];
   - Linguaskill [S0050];
   - OET [S0050];
   - ELS CIEP [S0050].
5. **Check the offer letter is real.** Agents have given Bangladeshi students fake ones [O0021]. The institution's international office can confirm by email or phone that it is genuine and the course is running [O0017].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Is there an official EMGS or KPT channel for checking an offer letter?
   > - Where can you find the international office's genuine contacts?

### Step 2: Apply from Bangladesh

Apply from outside Malaysia [S0002]. You or the institution can create the application [O0063], but only one you create and pay for yourself on the Education Malaysia website can be refunded to you [S0078]. You can track it without an agent (Step 4) [S0080, O0046].

1. **Get and accept an offer letter;** the form can't be submitted without it [S0024, S0047].
2. **You'll need:**
   - a 35 x 45 mm photo on white [S0045];
   - your passport's data, visa and observation pages [S0045], the passport valid at least 18 months (for a pass covering the whole course, EMGS recommends 12 months beyond the course) [S0046];
   - the offer letter, all pages, as one PDF [S0045, O0009];
   - EMGS's Health Declaration Form [S0045, S0048];
   - your certificates and transcripts [S0045];
   - your English certificate [S0050].

   Keep your passport: agents have held students' passports to demand more money [O0018, O0023].
3. **Open an account and apply** on [visa.educationmalaysia.gov.my](https://visa.educationmalaysia.gov.my/) or the EMGS HUB app [S0024]. Enter the institution and course exactly as in the offer letter, your name exactly as in the two coded lines at the bottom of your passport's data page, and the passport number on the offer letter [O0007, O0008]. EMGS then asks the institution to confirm [O0012].
4. **Pay** in one of three ways [O0010]:
   - through the institution [O0010];
   - by telegraphic transfer (bank-to-bank international transfer): converted to US dollars, plus USD 50 [O0047, O0048];
   - by Visa or MasterCard on the EMGS site, plus 3% [O0049].

   Tuition handed to agents has failed to reach institutions [O0022]. EMGS's [fee calculator](https://visa.educationmalaysia.gov.my/student-visa/calculate-application-fees) gives, for a new application at a university or private higher education institution:
   - processing: RM1,728 [S0060];
   - eVAL (electronic Visa Approval Letter, IMI's approval to enter as a student): RM159 [S0061, S0062];
   - medical screening in Malaysia: RM340 (RM350 in Sarawak) [S0061];
   - Student Pass: RM60 [S0061, S0062];
   - i-Kad (student identity card): RM50, or RM60.60 couriered [S0061, S0063];
   - EMGS health insurance: RM498 to RM644, or a policy through your institution [S0064];
   - visa: RM20 [V0017].

   8% service tax is added to processing fees [Z0094]. Public-university students may do the medical at their university [Z0095]. If a card payment fails but your bank took the money, email enquiry@emgs.com.my with the bank's confirmation, transaction ID and application number [O0011].
5. **Refunds.** The first year's processing and eVAL fees aren't refunded once invoiced; convenience fees and service tax never are [S0077, O0050, O0051]. Before the institution decides, you get a full refund if you ask within 90 days of paying [O0053], through the online refund form in your account, logged in with the email you applied with; card payments come back by telegraphic transfer [O0052, O0058].
6. **Lodge the RM750 personal bond** (the Bangladeshi rate) with the institution, which signs a bond with Immigration for you; it is returned when you finish or withdraw without breaking immigration rules [S0059, S0049]. The form can be handed in after arrival, for the Student Pass sticker [O0059].

   > **Need to know**
   > - How and when does the institution take the money?
   > - What receipt do you get?
   > - How do you claim it back?

7. **If EMGS asks for more documents,** send them within 14 working days of the return date [S0020], or the application closes, and a refund must be asked for within 6 months of closing [O0054, O0055].
8. **Wait.** EMGS checks your academic papers, then IMI checks your passport and security and issues the eVAL [S0003]; EMGS's charter is 14 working days for a complete application [S0026].

**If you're refused,** you can appeal [O0056]; the institution's international office handles the appeal or a new application with corrected documents [O0015]. Ask for any refund within 90 days of EMGS's notice [O0057].

> **Need to know**
> - What is the appeal form?
> - What is the appeal fee?
> - What is the appeal deadline?

### Step 3: eVAL and visa

The eVAL is valid 6 months; you may not enter before it is issued [S0027, S0028]. Then apply on [MyVISA](https://malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my/), from outside Malaysia, for a Single Entry Visa under Visa With Reference (VDR), the category needing an approval letter (the eVAL) [S0004, S0029, S0053, V0007, C0006]. Account and payment work as in Tourist visa, Step 2 [V0021, V0022]; the fee is RM20 [V0017].

> **Need to know**
> - Does MyVISA's RM105 processing charge apply to students?

### Step 4: How to check your application is real

1. **Track it** on [visa.educationmalaysia.gov.my](https://visa.educationmalaysia.gov.my/): Student Visa, Track My Application, enter passport number and nationality, tick the terms box, press Track My Application [S0080, O0060]. IMI's own status page sends Student Pass checks to EMGS [S0081].
2. **At 70%** Immigration has approved your eVAL; download it as a PDF from your EMGS account [O0013, O0014]. If progress stops, ask the institution's international office, which must confirm your application, or EMGS via [educationmalaysia.gov.my/get-in-touch/contact-us](https://educationmalaysia.gov.my/get-in-touch/contact-us) [O0012, O0084].

> **Need to know**
> - How can you check that an eVAL document itself is genuine?

### Step 5: Arrival and the pass

1. **Register the MDAC** (Malaysia Digital Arrival Card) within 3 days before arriving (see Tourist visa, Step 4) [V0058]; long-term pass holders are exempt [V0060].
2. **At Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA),** go to EMGS's 24-hour International Student Arrival Centre (ISAC) before immigration (Terminal 1: Level 3 arrival hall; Terminal 2: Level 3A after the Skybridge); its staff help you through immigration, with priority lanes for students [S0082, S0083, O0061, O0062].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which documents does ISAC ask to see?
   > - Does ISAC charge anything?

3. **Within 7 working days do the medical** at an EMGS panel clinic, listed on EMGS's site and app; without it EMGS can't start your pass [S0055, S0058, S0056]. If you fail, appeal at the same clinic within 3 days of being told [S0030]; if that fails, ask for any refund within 90 days of EMGS's notice [O0057].

   > **Need to know**
   > - How do you book the medical?
   > - What does the medical appeal cost?
   > - Which documents does the medical appeal need?
   > - What are the medical appeal's possible outcomes?

4. **The institution gets your pass sticker** with your original passport, its letter, EMGS's support letter, your stamped personal bond and at least 12 months' Malaysian health insurance [S0017]; IMI's client charter gives 5 working days [C0047].

### Keeping the pass

The pass usually lasts up to a year; renew as early as 3 months before expiry [S0032, S0033], yourself or through the institution, on STARS (EMGS's Student Application Registration System) or the EMGS website [O0063, O0064]. You'll need:

- a 35 x 45 mm photo on white [O0085];
- copies of your passport's data, visa and observation pages [O0085];
- your certificates and transcripts [O0085].

You need a CGPA (grade point average) of at least 2.0 and 80% attendance, or an explanation letter from the institution [S0051]. Renewal costs RM270 to EMGS (private institution) and RM60 to IMI [S0060, S0007]. Breaking any condition can get the pass cancelled [S0016].

> **Need to know**
> - How long does a renewal take?

**Changing course:** the institution may file a Variation application once, within 18 months after your first semester, if you finished at least one semester at bachelor's level or above; otherwise it ends your pass with a Check-Out Memo and you leave Malaysia before applying again [S0034, S0036]. With an eVAL but no pass yet, you must leave to change course [S0035].

### Can students work?

The rules:

- **Who:** only university and private higher education students, not language or training centre students [S0012].
- **When:** in semester breaks or holidays over 7 days [S0013, S0039].
- **How much:** at most 20 hours a week [S0013, S0039].
- **Where:** restaurants, petrol stations, mini markets, hotels or on campus [S0013, S0039].
- **Never:** as a cashier, front desk worker, hotel singer, masseur, musician or guest relations officer, or student recruiter [S0014, S0040].

Students working outside these rules are deported, sometimes fined or jailed [O0019]. Claims of an agreement giving Bangladeshi students graduate passes to work are false [N0034].

To get permission first, you'll need:

- the job offer letter [S0015];
- the institution's supporting letter with the break dates [S0015];
- your results and attendance report [S0015];
- copies of your data page and Student Pass [S0015];
- the employer's Labour Department (JTK) approval to employ a foreigner under section 60K of the Employment Act 1955 [S0015].

Go in person with an institution representative to IMI's Student Pass Unit at headquarters or the state Immigration office, which decides after checking the documents [S0041, S0015].

> **Need to know**
> - What is the fee for work permission?
> - How long does a decision take?

### Family

Only master's and PhD students may bring dependants (spouse, children under 18, disabled children of any age, parents and parents-in-law); a Bangladeshi bachelor's student can't, and dependants may not work [S0008, S0009, S0038, S0011]. The institution applies on STARS [S0021, O0064]. You'll need:

- Forms IMM.12 and IMM.38 [O0065];
- the institution's letters requesting the pass and confirming your Student Pass [O0065];
- a copy of every page of the dependant's passport, cover included (valid 18 months or more), and of your data page and pass [O0065];
- a 3.5 x 5.0 cm photo on white [O0065];
- 12 months' health insurance [O0065];
- a stamped personal bond (new applications) [O0065];
- proof of support, such as 3 months' bank statements or a scholarship letter [O0065];
- the marriage or birth certificate [O0066].

Bring the originals; incomplete applications aren't processed [O0069]. Bangla documents need a certified translation; a dependant already in Malaysia needs relationship documents certified by the Bangladesh High Commission [O0068, O0067]. The costs:

- EMGS: RM250 per dependant (RM140 to renew) [S0067];
- insurance, bought by each dependant [S0067];
- IMI: RM90 for the Long-Term Social Visit Pass [S0007].

That pass has a category for close family of a foreign student [C0019]. For a child born in Malaysia, see Having a child in Malaysia [S0114].

> **Need to know**
> - How long does a dependant's application take?
> - How is the result shown?
> - Does the close-family category cover a bachelor's student's family?

### School pupils

IMI gives Student Passes to foreign pre-school and school children [C0024] with a place at:

- a government or government-aided school under KPM [Z0110];
- an international, expatriate, private or religious school or Chinese independent secondary school registered with KPM's Private Education Division; an international school also needs KDN's permission [Z0110, Z0111].

The school's representative, a parent or a legal guardian applies, new or renewal, at IMI's Student Pass Unit at headquarters, the state Visa, Pass and Permit Division, or a branch offering Student Passes, by the school's address [Z0112, Z0113]. You'll need:

- Forms IMM.14 and IMM.38 [Z0114];
- a copy of every page of the child's passport, cover included (renewal: data page and current pass), valid 18 months or more [Z0115, Z0124];
- a 3.5 x 5.0 cm photo on white [Z0116];
- 12 months' health insurance [Z0118];
- a stamped personal bond (new) [Z0117];
- for a government or aided school, the state or district education office's approval letter [Z0119];
- the originals [Z0120].

It costs RM60 plus the visa fee [S0007]; IMI's client charter gives 5 working days [C0047]. The child's parents, legal guardian or adoptive parents, and siblings under 7 may get a Long-Term Social Visit Pass as companions, at RM90, with no work or business [Z0121, Z0122, Z0123, S0007].

### Scams

**Done to students.** Besides the traps in Steps 1 and 2, agents claim a student visa lets you work freely; it doesn't [S0135, S0013]. Agents have charged families Tk 6 to 8 lakh or more for admission and a visa, steering students to barely functioning colleges [S0134]; agencies have left EMGS applications stuck for months and, after arrival, moved students from the promised university into a Bangladeshi-owned English language centre, whose students can't work [O0025, O0026].

**Tried by applicants.** Getting a pass by a false statement, or using someone else's, carries a fine up to RM10,000 or up to 5 years' jail [K0031, K0030].

**If an agent cheated you or holds your passport,** in this order:

1. **Bangladesh police.** Register on [gd.police.gov.bd](https://gd.police.gov.bd/) or the Online GD app with your NID, a mobile number (for a one-time code) and a live photo; pick the district and station, describe what happened, when and where, and attach receipts and messages [I0034, I0038]. A GD (General Diary) comes back as a digital copy with its number and the investigating officer's details; for a case, take the printout or complaint code to that station [O0071, O0070]. Or call the CID (Criminal Investigation Department) hotline, 01320-019999, or control room, 01320-019998, which wants to hear of anyone holding passports or demanding unusual visa sums [O0072, O0033].
2. **The Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare's Complaint Resolution Cell,** minister.expat@gmail.com [R0012]. Sample: "My son [name, passport no.] paid [agency, address] Tk [amount] on [dates] for admission to [institution] in Malaysia. The fees were not paid and the agency holds his passport. Receipts attached. Please help recover the passport and money."

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does the Ministry act on complaints about education agents, which hold no recruiting licence?

3. **In Malaysia,** the Bangladesh High Commission's consular wing: consular.kualalumpur@gmail.com, +603 2604 0946, mission.kualalumpur@mofa.gov.bd, No 8, Lorong Yap Kwan Seng, 50450 Kuala Lumpur [L0031, L0032]. In an emergency call 999 (free) for the Royal Malaysia Police [O0073, O0074].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does the High Commission help students recover passports held by agents?

4. **Against the institution,** KPT's SiSPAA complaints system, [mohe.spab.gov.my](https://mohe.spab.gov.my/eApps/system/index.do) [O0028, O0082]: register ("Daftar Baharu"), log in, choose "Aduan/Maklum Balas Baharu"; "Semak Aduan/Maklum Balas" shows progress [O0079, O0083]. Give full name, passport number, phone and email, and attach the offer letter and receipts, or KPT may reject it; it doesn't take private money disputes with agents [O0080, O0081].

   > **Need to know**
   > - How long does KPT take to reply?

5. **Free legal help:** OKUP (Ovibashi Karmi Unnayan Program, a Bangladeshi migrants' organisation) advises and files cases for migrant workers, families and trafficking survivors: helpline 01842773300, 4th Floor, 466 Sawdagar Garden, Dania Post Office Road, Dhaka 1236 [O0076, O0077, O0078].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does OKUP take the cases of cheated students?

## Tourist visa (social visit)

Bangladeshis need an eVISA to visit for tourism, family or short business, and may stay up to 30 days [V0001, V0003]. Ordinary visits get a single-entry visa; multiple-entry is only for business, medical treatment, Fly & Cruise and wedding tourism [V0009]. It never allows work or study (see Don't work) [V0046].

### Step 1: Prepare

You'll need:

- a passport valid over 6 months from travel, with at least 3 blank pages [V0026, V0032];
- a studio photo, 35 x 50 mm, white background, full face, under 6 months old, no selfies or scans [V0033, V0034];
- scans of the data page and the page with your previous passport number [V0026];
- a confirmed return ticket [V0026, V0050];
- proof of paid accommodation, or, staying with friends or relatives, a cover letter to the immigration officer with your details, the address, and the host's name, Malaysian identity card number and phone [V0026, V0029];
- proof of money (cash, card or e-money backed by 3 months' bank statements) [V0027];
- for a child, the birth certificate [O0039];
- a credit card, or Alipay or WeChat Pay, to pay [O0037].

Merge several files for one upload field into one [V0031].

### Step 2: Apply on MyVISA

1. **Use only [malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my](https://malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my/),** IMI's only official eVISA site; you can't apply from inside Malaysia, and third-party applications can't be verified [V0005, V0006, V0007]. If an agent applies, only the agent sees the status, and IMI takes no responsibility [O0038]; agents in Bangladesh charge Tk 5,000 to 8,000 for an eVISA you can apply for yourself [N0056]. MyVISA has shut for weeks at a time (from 10 February 2026 and again in July 2026), suspending all applications and approvals until a notice says it is back [N0003, N0005, N0006].
2. **Open an account:** under Individual, Sign Up, fill in the form and CAPTCHA (picture code); change the emailed temporary password at first login [V0021]. Log in with email, the emailed 6-digit code and password [V0022]. One account holds 5 applications, for up to 5 people [V0023].
3. **Apply on a weekday, 9 am to 5 pm Bangladesh time** (the only hours open to Bangladesh) [V0025], at least 14 days before travel [V0013]. Write your name exactly as in your passport; with no surname, leave Surname blank or put the full name in Given Name [V0035]. Unpaid drafts expire after 7 working days [V0024].
4. **Pay** RM20 visa fee, RM105 processing and 0.8% (card) or 1.7% (e-wallet) convenience charge; nothing is refunded, even on refusal [V0016, V0017, V0019]. Visa agencies add their own fee [V0018].
5. **Watch the status** [V0040]:
   - "Document Submission or Information Update Required": upload what is asked [V0040].
   - "Interview Requested": confirm and attend, online or in person as the High Commission sets [V0040, O0034].
   - No response in 5 days means automatic rejection [V0041].

   A decision takes 2 to 7 working days, restarting after any re-upload [V0012, O0035].
6. **Download and print the eVISA** (Actions, Download eVISA) [V0037]. It allows one journey within 6 months of issue, whatever the travel date in that window [R0005, R0006]; it cancels any earlier visa still active [V0045]. A wrong detail from your own mistake means applying and paying again [O0036].
7. **If refused,** there is no appeal, and no reason need be given; you can reapply and pay again [V0042, V0043, V0019]. Questions and complaints: High Commission of Malaysia, Dhaka, dhaka@imi.gov.my (visa section), +88 02 4108 1918 (visa), House 19, Road 6, Baridhara Diplomatic Enclave, Sunday to Thursday 8.30 am to 4.30 pm [V0043, B0121].

### Step 3: Check your eVISA is real

At [malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my/evisa/check-evisa](https://malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my/evisa/check-evisa) enter your passport number, the visa sticker number and the CAPTCHA [V0067]; only official-site applications can be checked [V0006].

> **Need to know**
> - What does a genuine eVISA look like?
> - Where is its sticker number printed?

### Step 4: Fill in the Malaysia Digital Arrival Card (MDAC)

1. **Submit it within 3 days before arrival,** the submission day included [V0058, V0061], only at [imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/mdac/main](https://imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/mdac/main), which lists no fee [V0059]. Fake sites charge, such as malaysiacard.com (USD 29.99), and appear as sponsored search results [V0065, N0057].
2. **Fill in** your name, passport number, date and place of birth, nationality, sex, passport expiry, email, mobile, arrival and departure dates, flight number, mode of travel and last airport of departure [V0061].
3. **Check it** under "Check Registration" with passport number, nationality and PIN [V0062].
4. **Show it** with your passport at immigration [V0059].

### Step 5: At the border

**At Dhaka airport,** immigration police stop travellers (Malaysia included) whose visa, BMET clearance (a worker's emigration clearance), papers or answers don't match [O0031]. Carry what Malaysia checks: eVISA, return ticket, paid accommodation and proof of money [V0050, V0027].

> **Need to know**
> - What should you do if Bangladeshi immigration stops you?
> - Can you complain about being stopped?
> - Can you travel later after being stopped?

**At the Malaysian airport,** the officer checks passport, eVISA, MDAC, return ticket and paid accommodation [V0050, V0059]. A visa doesn't guarantee entry: you can be refused if you can't explain your plans, or can't prove you aren't a "prohibited immigrant", which includes lacking means of support [V0051, V0052, V0053, V0054]. Autogates aren't open to Bangladeshis [V0057]. Bangladeshis holding valid tourist visas are regularly turned back at KLIA for fake hotel bookings, no return ticket, too little money or no satisfactory reason [V0109, N0011, N0013]; travellers are told to carry RM500 to RM1,000, and officers may check phone photos [O0029, O0030]. Those refused go on a "Not to Land" list and the airline returns them within about 24 hours [N0010, N0015].

**If refused entry:** the Immigration Act's appeal to the Minister covers only Permit holders, not visitors [V0055, K0011].

> **Need to know**
> - Can a refused visitor appeal?
> - Is there an official minimum sum of money?
> - What does "Not to Land" mean for future visits?

### Scams

**Done to travellers.** Fraudsters bring Bangladeshi women on tourist visas promising jobs, though no agreement exists to recruit Bangladeshi women for Malaysia [N0028]. Rings in Kuala Lumpur sell Bangladeshis forged European visas for up to RM20,000 [D0043]; a man promised Italy was sent with fake Malaysian and French visas and turned back at the Malaysian airport [D0048]. Travel agencies have promised work permits, sent people on visit visas, held them and demanded ransom from families [O0032].

**Tried by travellers.** Getting a pass by a false statement carries a fine up to RM10,000 or up to 5 years' jail [K0031, K0030].

**If someone sold you a "job on a tourist visa", holds your passport, or holds you in Malaysia,** in this order:

1. **Held or in danger in Malaysia:** call 999 for the Royal Malaysia Police (PDRM) [O0073, O0074], then the Bangladesh High Commission's consular wing, +603 2604 0946, consular.kualalumpur@gmail.com [L0031].
2. **Family calls the Probashi call centre** of WEWB (the Wage Earners' Welfare Board, the government body for migrant workers' welfare), for problems of migrants and families: 16135 toll free in Bangladesh, +880 9610102030 from abroad, 24 hours [B0083, O0075].
3. **Bangladesh police:** online at [gd.police.gov.bd](https://gd.police.gov.bd/) or at the station (see Student visa, Scams), or the CID hotline, 01320-019999, for anyone holding passports or demanding unusual visa sums [I0034, O0070, O0072, O0033].
4. **Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare and Overseas Employment** (Probashi Kallyan Bhaban, 71-72 Old Elephant Road, Dhaka-1000) [G0025]:

   - **Against a recruiting agency:** email the enforcement branch, sasenforcement@probashi.gov.bd (02-41030255), with your proof, full address and the agency's RL (licence) number and address; free, action within 3 to 5 working days, settled within 30 [B0044, B0045].
   - **Against a licensed agency that sent you abroad without permission:** apply to the Secretary with your BMET smart card, ticket, contract and passport copies; free, settled within 60 working days of BMET's recommendation [B0046].
   - **Anything else:** the Complaint Resolution Cell, minister.expat@gmail.com [R0012].

   Sample: "I, [name, passport no.], paid [agent, address, phone] Tk [amount] on [date] for a job in Malaysia. I was sent on a tourist visa on [date] and [what happened]. Receipts and messages attached."
5. **Free legal help:** OKUP (see Student visa, Scams) helps trafficking survivors and migrants' families, helpline 01842773300 [O0076, O0077, O0078].

### Don't work

A visit pass allows no work, work training or study [V0046, V0104] and lapses once its conditions are broken [V0100]. Overstaying brings fines and can bar re-entry for a set period [V0047]. A visit pass is extended only for illness, an accident or war at home, with evidence and a return ticket, by applying in person on Form IMM.55 [V0069]. A directive of 8 June 2026 ordered a nationwide multi-agency crackdown on people working on tourist, student and other short passes [P0021].

## Family visit sponsored by a Malaysian citizen (VDR)

A Malaysian citizen (the sponsor) can invite a Bangladeshi relative through a VDR (Visa Dengan Rujukan, visa with reference): the sponsor applies in Malaysia, Immigration headquarters approves, and a Malaysian mission issues the visa [C0008, V0090, P0010, P0049, V0089]. Proof of relationship:

- **Parents or parents-in-law:** the sponsor's spouse's marriage certificate, birth certificate and passport, plus a notarial certificate endorsed by the Malaysian mission and Wisma Putra (Malaysia's foreign ministry) [P0008].
- **A child under 7:** the birth certificate and the sponsor's marriage certificate [P0008].
- **A spouse:** the marriage certificate [P0008].
- **Other relatives:** documentary proof [Z0028].

No work, work training or study [V0104].

### Step 1: Send your papers to the sponsor

You'll need:

- a passport valid more than 6 months from travel, every printed page scanned [P0010, P0016];
- 2 photos, 3.5 x 5.0 cm [P0004, P0010];
- the relationship documents above [P0008];
- for the sponsor's form, your marital history, job in Bangladesh with proof, earlier visits to and work in Malaysia, spouse and children, address and planned length of stay [P0012].

1. **Parents or parents-in-law: get the notarial certificate endorsed** [P0008] at the High Commission of Malaysia in Dhaka (contacts in Tourist visa, Step 2, item 7) and at Wisma Putra. Wisma Putra's Consular Division takes documents walk-in from the person or a representative with ID, usually ready the same or next working day: Wisma Putra No 1, Jalan Wisma Putra, Precinct 2, 62602 Putrajaya; 03-8000 8000; sah_admin@kln.gov.my; Monday to Thursday 8.30 am to 4 pm, Friday 8.30 to 12 and 2.45 to 4 pm [P0050, P0051]. Non-English documents need an English translation [P0052].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What must the certificate state?
   > - Which notary may make it?
   > - What are the endorsement fees in Dhaka and Putrajaya?
   > - How long does endorsement take in Dhaka and Putrajaya?
   > - Must the Bangladesh High Commission in Kuala Lumpur authenticate it first (Wisma Putra's general rule for documents from abroad) [P0052]?
   > - Can the sponsor take it to Wisma Putra?

2. **Send the sponsor copies of everything,** with your name in capitals, sex, place and country of birth, date of birth, nationality and passport details exactly as in your passport, for Form Imm.12 [P0003]. Ask the sponsor to name the High Commission in Dhaka as the issuing mission [P0012, B0121].

### Step 2: The sponsor applies in Malaysia

You'll need (the sponsor):

- MyKad (Malaysian identity card) [P0010];
- the 3 latest payslips showing RM2,000 a month or more, or, if self-employed, the SSM (Companies Commission of Malaysia) business registration and 3 months' bank statements [P0010, Z0027];
- an employer's confirmation letter [P0010, Z0027];
- the papers from Step 1 [P0010].

1. **Download the forms** from [IMI's VDR checklist](https://www.imi.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/SENARAI-SEMAK-Borang-VDR.pdf): Form Imm.12, the sponsor's declaration (Surat Akuan Penaja), the bond and the sponsor information form (Borang Maklumat Tambahan Penaja) [P0010, P0011, P0012].
2. **Type Form Imm.12** in 2 copies, with the relative's photo on each [P0004, Z0058].
3. **Swear the declaration** (job, employer, monthly income and full responsibility for the relative) before a Commissioner for Oaths at a government court, a Sessions Court judge, magistrate or notary public [P0011].
4. **Sign a personal bond, stamped RM10 at LHDN** (the Inland Revenue Board); the sponsor forfeits its sum if the relative breaks the visit's conditions [Z0026, P0006, P0014]. A cash security bond applies only to citizens of China and Nigeria [P0006].
5. **Add a cover letter** (Surat Iringan) and the employer's letter [P0010].
6. **Submit in person;** the sponsor signs the sponsor form before an Immigration officer [Z0025, P0012]. VDRs are decided by the Visa, Pass and Permit Division, Immigration headquarters, No. 15, Tingkat 1-7, Blok 2G4, Persiaran Perdana, Presint 2, 62550 Putrajaya; 03-8000 8000 [C0008, P0005]. Incomplete applications or ones without documentary proof are not accepted [P0009].
7. **Wait about 7 working days;** the result is a visa approval letter (eVAL) [V0090, P0010, Z0096, P0016].

> **Need to know**
> - Does a Bangladeshi relative's sponsor pay a cash deposit (the form asks about one, wang cagaran, and IMI's bond rate for Bangladeshis is RM750) [P0048, V0112]?
> - What must the cover letter contain?
> - Does a state Immigration office also take the application?
> - What are the counter hours?
> - Is there any IMI fee for the approval?
> - How does the approval reach the relative?
> - Can a refusal be appealed?

### Step 3: Apply for the eVISA

Bangladeshis still need an eVISA with the VDR approval [P0015]. You'll need [P0016, V0016, V0017]:

- a scan of your passport's data page;
- the approval letter;
- an email address;
- a card or e-wallet.

1. Apply on [malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my](https://malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my/) as in Tourist visa, Step 2, choosing Visa With Reference, with details exactly as on the approval letter; any difference must be corrected by the issuing office before you pay [V0021, C0006, Z0096, P0017].
2. Pay the same fees as a tourist eVISA; nothing is refunded [V0017, P0018, V0019]. If charged twice, or the status stays "New Application", don't pay again; use MyVISA's 24-hour Live Chat [P0019].
3. Download, print and check the eVISA as in Tourist visa, Steps 2 and 3 [V0037, V0067].

### Step 4: Travel

Register the MDAC (Malaysia Digital Arrival Card) within 3 days before arrival and carry the papers the border officer checks (see Tourist visa, Step 5) [V0058, V0050].

### Don't work, scams and complaints

Working ends a visit pass (see What can get you jailed); Immigration arrests Bangladeshis working on visit passes, and the sponsor loses the bond [V0104, V0100, P0020, P0022, P0014]. False statements are an offence (see Tourist visa, Scams) [K0031]. Immigration matters go only through Immigration counters or its official portal; no officer or third party may handle them outside those channels [P0038].

> **Need to know**
> - What scams run on this route, such as fake sponsors or brokers charging for a "family VDR"?

If a sponsor or broker cheats you:

1. **Before paying,** check a phone number, bank account or company name on the Royal Malaysia Police's Semak Mule portal [P0056].
2. **In Malaysia,** report at the nearest police station; in an emergency call 999, free [P0055, O0073, O0074]. If you paid online, first call the National Scam Response Centre (NSRC), 997, 24 hours, within 24 hours so the money can be blocked, and tell your bank [P0055].
3. **If an official asked for a bribe,** report to the MACC: 1-800-88-6000 (free), [portaladuan.sprm.gov.my](https://portaladuan.sprm.gov.my/), info@sprm.gov.my, SMS +6019-6000 696; No. 2, Lebuh Wawasan, Presint 7, 62250 Putrajaya [P0053, P0054].
4. **For help as a Bangladeshi,** contact the Bangladesh High Commission in Kuala Lumpur (see Contacts) [L0032, B0117].
