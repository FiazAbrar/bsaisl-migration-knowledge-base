## Business visit

A social visit pass covers meetings, conferences, inspections, audits, signing agreements, investment surveys and seminars, not professional or technical work or a job [V0068, V0094, V0100]. For up to 30 days of urgent professional work the Malaysian company gets PLS@Xpats permission (Step 3); longer work needs a Professional Visit Pass or Employment Pass [P0024, V0091].

### Step 1: Get the papers

You'll need: the papers in Tourist visa, Step 1, plus your company's dispatch letter or an invitation from a Malaysian government body or government-linked company [V0026, V0032, V0070].

### Step 2: Apply for the business eVISA on MyVISA

Apply, pay and check the eVISA as in Tourist visa, Steps 2 and 3, choosing the business purpose and uploading the letter and ticket; the fees, the 2 to 7 working days and the refusal rules are the same [V0070, V0037, V0067, V0017, V0016, V0019, V0012, V0042].

A business multiple-entry visa (6 months, up to 30 days per entry) is limited to 31 eligible nationalities [V0071, C0007].

> **Need to know**

### Step 3: Short professional work: PLS@Xpats

You'll need (the company) [P0028]:

- its request letter;
- a copy of every page of your passport, valid 6 months or more;
- its SSM registration;
- the work or project schedule;
- your return ticket.

1. The company applies free at [mtp.imi.gov.my/plsXpats/main](https://mtp.imi.gov.my/plsXpats/main), without ESD (IMI's Expatriate Services Division) registration; it takes 3 working days, for anyone 18 or over, not blacklisted and not Israeli [P0028, P0029, P0027].
2. You still need the eVISA, and must carry the approval letter or be refused entry for this work [P0026, P0030].

The approval is valid 30 days from approval for one entry and at most 30 days' work, not extendable, in Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan only, with no family pass [P0024, P0025, P0031]. Questions: eapp.imi.gov.my enquiry form [P0047].

### Scams and complaints

Fake invitations: a syndicate sold Bangladeshis fake invitation letters (such as to sports competitions), with return tickets and hotel bookings to fool officers, for RM2,000 to RM5,000 [P0040]. Don't pay anyone to "get" the visa: it is applied for and paid only online, for the Step 2 fees, and no officer or third party may handle it outside official channels [P0019, V0016, P0038]. False statements are an offence (see Tourist visa, Scams) [K0031].

If cheated:

- **In Malaysia:** use the route under Family visit (VDR), Don't work, scams and complaints [P0055, P0053].
- **In Bangladesh,** if an agency or broker took money for a "business visa" job: complain to BMET (see Work visa: company worker (PLKS), Scams) [B0034, B0047].

## MM2H (Malaysia My Second Home)

A long-stay pass for people with substantial savings, run by MOTAC (the Ministry of Tourism, Arts and Culture), for citizens of any country with diplomatic relations with Malaysia [E0101, E0100], aged 25 or over (21 for the SEZ/SFZ, Special Economic Zone / Special Financial Zone, tier) [Z0041, Z0043].

**Tiers** [E0106, E0108, E0114]:

- **Silver:** USD 150,000 fixed deposit and a home of at least RM600,000; 5 years.
- **Gold:** USD 500,000 and RM1,000,000; 15 years.
- **Platinum:** USD 1,000,000 and RM2,000,000; 20 years.
- **SEZ/SFZ:** USD 65,000 (age 21 to 49) or USD 32,000 (50 and over) and a home in Forest City, Johor; 10 years [E0106, E0115].

**Family** [E0104, E0110]:

- a spouse;
- children under 21 (biological, step or adopted);
- unmarried, unemployed children aged 21 to 34;
- disabled children of any age;
- parents and parents-in-law.

**Sarawak's S-MM2H.** Sarawak runs its own scheme, on these terms since 1 January 2025 [E0143]:

- age 30 or over;
- RM500,000 fixed deposit in a Sarawak bank;
- 30 days a year in Sarawak;
- a 5-year pass;
- applied for through the state's one-stop centre or licensed agents.

1. **Check the money can legally leave Bangladesh.** Bangladesh Bank controls money leaving Bangladesh under the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act 1947 [Z0103]; in 2024 it was reported not to have approved sending money abroad for MM2H [N0051]; a Malaysian MM2H licence doesn't by itself mean an agent meets Bangladesh's rules [Q0050].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What is Bangladesh Bank's written rule on sending money abroad for MM2H?
   > - How do you ask Bangladesh Bank for permission?

2. **Choose a licensed agent.** Every application goes through an MM2H operator licensed by MOTAC [E0101]. Check at [mm2h.gov.my/agencies](https://www.mm2h.gov.my/agencies) that the company name, licence number (MM2H plus digits) and licence dates match what the agent shows [E0116]. No agent can guarantee approval; some have taken tens of thousands of ringgit, then stopped answering, and applicants couldn't recover it [Q0047, Q0048].

3. **The agent applies** through the One Stop Centre MM2H on MOTAC's online system; the Ministry of Home Affairs approves through Immigration [Q0041, E0118]. Fees:
   - processing: RM5,000 for the main applicant and RM2,500 per dependant [E0107, E0109];
   - participation: RM1,000 (Silver, SEZ/SFZ), RM3,000 (Gold) or RM200,000 (Platinum) [E0107, E0109];
   - MOTAC's system also lists RM500 plus SST (sales and service tax) per application [E0118].

   You need a Certificate of Good Conduct (in Bangladesh, a police clearance certificate; see Work visa: company worker (PLKS), Step 4) and Malaysian police screening [Q0049].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Is the RM500 in MOTAC's system extra to the processing and participation fees?
   > - What is the full document list?
   > - What is the processing time?

4. **After approval.**
   1. Everyone has a medical check at a MOTAC panel clinic or hospital [Q0039].
   2. Open the fixed deposit in a licensed Malaysian bank; up to half can later be withdrawn for a home, education, medical care or tourism in Malaysia [Q0040].
   3. Buy a home within a year of endorsement and keep it 10 years unless selling to buy a dearer one [E0119, E0103].

   Immigration's client charter gives 1 working day to endorse the pass [C0047].

**Living on MM2H.**

- Under 50, spend at least 90 days a year in Malaysia [E0103].
- Only Platinum holders may work or run a business [E0112, E0113, Z0019, Z0064].
- Renewal is RM500 a year plus a visa fee of RM0 to RM50 by nationality [Q0042].
- MM2H years don't count toward permanent residence [Q0015].

**If refused.** Any appeal is for the Ministry of Home Affairs [Z0042].

> **Need to know**
> - How is an MM2H refusal appealed to the Ministry of Home Affairs?
> - By when must the appeal be made?

**If an agent cheats you.**

- Complain on MOTAC's SISPAA at [motac.spab.gov.my/eApps/system/index.do](https://motac.spab.gov.my/eApps/system/index.do) [Q0045]:
  1. Choose "Aduan/Maklum Balas Baharu".
  2. Give the agent's name and licence number, and what you paid and when. For example: "I paid RM[amount] on [date] to [agent], licence MM2H[number], for an MM2H application. The agent has not applied or answered since [date]. Receipts attached. Please investigate and help me recover the money."
  3. Attach receipts.
  4. Track it under "Semak Aduan/Maklum Balas" [Q0045].
- Or call the MM2H office: +603 8891 7568 or +603 8891 7434; MOTAC, No. 2, Tower 1, Jalan P5/6, Presint 5, 62200 Putrajaya [Q0043, Q0044].

## DE Rantau Nomad Pass

For remote workers and freelancers paid from outside Malaysia, run by MDEC (the Malaysia Digital Economy Corporation), for all nationalities except Israel, main applicant over 18 [E0084]. Minimum income: USD 36,000 a year in tech, USD 60,000 otherwise [E0085, E0098]. Up to 12 months at a time and 48 in all [E0086]; holders at the two-year limit will get two more years under conditions to be set [Q0004]. No route to permanent residence [E0098]. New applications are closed until 30 November 2026 and reopen on 1 December 2026 [E0099].

**Family** [E0087]:

- a spouse or common-law partner;
- children under 18 (including adopted and stepchildren);
- a special-needs child of any age;
- parents.

A spouse can't work [E0088].

1. **Prepare the documents** in English or with a certified translation, as clear PDFs, with the same name and income figures throughout [Q0051, Q0074].

   You'll need, as a remote employee:

   - a passport with 6 empty pages, valid over 14 months [Q0072, E0096];
   - a CV;
   - a signed contract showing remote work, title, duration and salary, with an employer not registered in Malaysia, starting at least 3 months before you apply [Q0072, Z0065];
   - 3 months' payslips and bank statements showing the same deposits [Q0073];
   - an income statement or tax return;
   - a Letter of Good Conduct (police clearance; see Work visa: company worker (PLKS), Step 4);
   - the personal bond form;
   - your highest education certificate [Q0072, E0097];
   - a medical insurance enrolment certificate valid in Malaysia for at least 3 months, covering dependants; it can be handed in after approval, before the pass is issued [Q0109].

   You'll need, as a freelancer [Q0075, Q0076, Z0067]:

   - the passport, CV, Letter of Good Conduct, bond form, certificate and insurance;
   - up to 3 signed client contracts showing scope, duration and payment terms;
   - 3 months' invoices;
   - proof of payment (PayPal, Stripe, Wise or bank records);
   - 3 months' bank statements above the threshold.

   > **Need to know**
   > - What is the minimum insurance cover?
   > - Which insurers are accepted?
   > - Do Malaysian clients count?

2. **Apply** at [mdec.my/derantau](https://mdec.my/derantau) and pay RM1,080 plus RM540 per dependant online, not refunded [E0089, E0091]. A decision takes 6 to 8 weeks after a complete application; track it at [malaysiadigital.mdec.my](https://malaysiadigital.mdec.my) [E0089]. No appeal, but you can reapply; no one can guarantee approval [E0093, E0094].

3. **After approval: enter and get the pass endorsed.** The approval letter is valid 6 months, not extendable; finish every step within it [Q0052].

   1. Get an eVISA (a visa applied for online) or, if the letter says so, a VDR (visa with reference) [Q0053]. Apply for the eVISA yourself on MyVISA, with the fees and times in Tourist visa, Step 2 [V0005, V0016]. A tourist or social visit pass can't be converted: leave and re-enter [Q0054, E0095].

      > **Need to know**
      > - What does a VDR for DE Rantau cost?
      > - What documents does it need?
      > - How long does it take?

   2. Use a normal Immigration counter, not the automatic gate, to get an entry stamp [Q0053].
   3. Within 1 month, upload on the DE Rantau portal, under your approved application [Q0057, Q0055, Q0058]:
      - the personal bond with a RM10 revenue stamp, endorsed by LHDN;
      - all passport pages;
      - the entry stamp;
      - the eVISA or VDR;
      - Malaysian health insurance;
      - an LHDN tax registration slip from mytax.hasil.gov.my.
   4. Pay Immigration RM90 per 3 months or RM360 a year, any multiple-entry visa charge, and a RM750 bond for a Bangladeshi, refunded when the pass expires [Z0040, E0092].
   5. Stay in Malaysia until the e-Pass is issued, about 1 week; leaving can cancel the application [Q0056, E0089].

**Where you can live.** Peninsular Malaysia and Labuan; Sabah or Sarawak need a tourist pass [E0095].

**If something goes wrong.** Contact MDEC's Expats Service Centre (expatctr@mdec.com.my, 03-8315 3106 / 3157) [Q0097] or the form at [mdec.my/contact-us](https://mdec.my/contact-us), enquiry type "Foreign Knowledge Workers" [Q0098]. **Permanent residence (Entry Permit).** Lets a foreigner live in Malaysia without time limit and work, study and run a business without another pass [E0134, Q0033]; it can be cancelled for harm to public order, security, health or morality [Q0037].

**Who can apply.** A Malaysian citizen's wife, husband or biological child under 18, and "fully foreign" nationals [Z0050, Q0016]: investors, experts and professionals in fields recognised by a Malaysian regulating agency, with 5 continuous years on an Employment, Student, Professional Visit or Residence Pass and no break over 6 months [Q0019, Q0020]. Check your points first at [app.imi.gov.my/epc](https://app.imi.gov.my/epc) [E0136]. PLKS, MM2H and PVIP holders can't apply [Q0014, Q0015].

You'll need, as originals with copies [Q0016, Q0096]:

- a passport valid 12 months;
- Form IM.4 (any Immigration office);
- old and new passports (data page and endorsed pages);
- the sponsor's MyKad and declaration;
- 2 white-background passport photos each of you and the sponsor.

Each kind of applicant adds [Q0096]:

- **a spouse:**
  - the information form;
  - the marriage certificate registered in Malaysia;
  - a declaration that the marriage continues;
  - MyKad copies of 2 Malaysian referees;
  - children's birth certificates (and MyKad from 12);
  - proof of address (a tenancy or purchase agreement or a utility bill);
  - the spouse LTSVP deposit receipt;
- **a child under 18:**
  - the foreign parent's passport;
  - the foreign birth certificate;
  - any Malaysian birth registration;
  - the parents' marriage certificates;
  - proof of address;
  - the sponsor's proof of income;
- **a fully foreign applicant:**
  - the information form;
  - 2 referees' MyKad copies.

Copies of Bangladeshi documents (passport, marriage and birth certificates) need attestation by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs or the Bangladesh High Commission, translation into English or Malay, then attestation by a Malaysian mission or Malaysia's Foreign Ministry [Q0035].

1. Download IMI's Entry Permit guideline e-book from [imi.gov.my/index.php/perkhidmatan-utama/permit-masuk](https://www.imi.gov.my/index.php/perkhidmatan-utama/permit-masuk/) [Z0101].
