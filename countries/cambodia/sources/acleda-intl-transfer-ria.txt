International Funds Transfers via Ria Money Transfer

International Funds Transfers
 via Ria Money Transfer

(19 Ratings)

International Funds Transfers via 
Ria Money Transfer
 to serve customers who need to 
send and receive money from abroad
 around the world quickly and securely.

Benefits

Transfer money to pay for imported goods or school fees or families that live in abroad.

Recipient can withdraw money where the 
Ria's logo
 displayed.

Receive money transfer to your account automatically or withdraw at ACLEDA Bank nationwide 
free of charge
.

Have an opportunity to use other services of ACLEDA Bank.

Currency and Transfer Amount

Receive money from abroad

KHR 40 million
 per transaction

USD 10,000
 per transaction

Transfer money abroad

USD 9,999
 per transaction

You can transfer/receive money 
without limiting the number of transactions or the amount
 per day.

To Transfer Money

Go to the nearest ACLEDA Bank nationwide.

Attach valid original ID.

Provide information: sender, receiver, transfer amount, purpose, etc.

To Receive Money

You can 
receive money into your bank account
 automatically.

Or you can withdraw cash at ACLEDA Bank's counter using 
11-digit reference number

Provide information: Sender, Receiver, transfer amount, purpose, etc.

Attach valid original ID.
