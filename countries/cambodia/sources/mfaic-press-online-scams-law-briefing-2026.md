[![](https://opendevelopmentcambodia.net/wp-content/blogs.dir/2/files_mf/177571929520260408MoFAEng.jpg)](https://opendevelopmentcambodia.net/wp-content/blogs.dir/2/files_mf/177571929520260408MoFAEng.jpg)

On 8 April 2026, Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation issued a press release on diplomatic briefing on the enactment of the Cambodian law on combatting online scams.

The ministry hosted a briefing for members of the diplomatic corps and international organizations on Cambodian Law on Combatting Online Scams which came into force on 7 April 2026.
