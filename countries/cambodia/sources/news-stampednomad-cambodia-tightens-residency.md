Cambodia is ramping up enforcement of foreign residency laws through a nationwide sweep led by the General Department of Immigration. The initiative aims to improve national security and curb illegal activities by ensuring every foreign national is properly documented and registered at a verified address.

Under **Sub-Decree No. 03**, which took effect in early **2026**, authorities are conducting regular spot checks of residential areas, apartments, and guesthouses. Property owners are now legally required to verify the status of foreign tenants before allowing them to stay, or they face fines of up to **\$1,000** per undocumented occupant.

## Who is affected

This policy shift primarily impacts expats and digital nomads on long-term stays. While short-term tourists are less likely to be targeted by door-to-door inspections, anyone staying in "collective residential premises" like condos or serviced apartments will find their data being shared more frequently with the police.

If you are living in Cambodia on an **Ordinary (E) visa**, you must ensure your paperwork is impeccable. This includes having a valid work permit if you are listed on a company tax certificate, as officials are increasingly linking residency status to employment documentation.

## What you need to do

Staying compliant requires proactive communication with your landlord and local authorities. Follow these steps to avoid fines or potential deportation:

- **Register your address:** Permanent residents must register within **48 hours** of arrival. All other foreigners must report address changes to local police within **24 hours**.
- **Check your visa and permits:** Ensure your visa extension is current. If you work remotely for a local entity or are on a business extension, confirm your **2025 work permit** application is submitted before the **March 31** deadline.
- **Verify landlord reporting:** Ask your property manager if they have logged your details in the official registry. They are now required to keep entry and exit logs for **one year** and security footage for **90 days**.

## Frequently asked questions

Who is affected by Cambodia's tightened residency checks?+

Expats and digital nomads on long-term stays are the main focus. Short-term tourists are less likely to be targeted, but anyone in collective residential premises like condos or serviced apartments may still be checked.

How soon must permanent residents register their address in Cambodia?+

Permanent residents must register within 48 hours of arrival. Other foreigners must report address changes to local police within 24 hours.

What documents do foreign residents need to stay compliant in Cambodia?+

Foreign residents need compliant visas, work permits, and address registrations. If you are listed on a company tax certificate, officials are increasingly linking residency status to employment documentation.

What penalties can property owners face for undocumented foreign tenants in Cambodia?+

Property owners can be fined up to \$1,000 per undocumented occupant. They are also required to verify the status of foreign tenants before allowing them to stay.

What records must landlords or property managers keep for foreign residents in Cambodia?+

Property managers must keep entry and exit logs for one year and security footage for 90 days. They are also required to log tenant details in the official registry.

Does Cambodia require a work permit for remote workers or business extension holders?+

A valid work permit is required if you are listed on a company tax certificate, and officials are linking residency status to employment documentation. The text also says those working remotely for a local entity or on a business extension should confirm their 2025 work permit application is submitted before the March 31 deadline.
