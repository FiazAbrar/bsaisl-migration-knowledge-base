Credentials of Ambassador of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh

2025-06-16

Royal Palace, Throne Hall: on 16 June 2025, His Majesty King NORODOM SIHAMONI has received the Credentials of H.E. Mr Faiyaz Murshid Kazi, Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh to the Kingdom of Cambodia.
