# Thai cops nab 11 Bangladeshis bound for Malaysia in pickup truck

11-Dec-2023, 22:08

11 Dec 2023, 22:08

[Bernama](/category/author/bernama2016)

## The men, aged 22 to 48, were found in the truck after it overturned following a chase.

![](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/cdn-cgi/image/width=3840,quality=80,format=auto,fit=scale-down,metadata=none,dpr=1,onerror=redirect/https://media.freemalaysiatoday.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Handcuff-Gari-fmt.jpg)

The 11 men had crossed from Bangladesh to Cambodia and were making their way to Malaysia via Thailand, say Thai police.

BANGKOK:

Eleven Bangladeshis believed to be heading to Malaysia were nabbed by Thai police on Saturday after their pickup truck overturned in Sa Kaeo province, on the eastern border of Thailand about 245km from Bangkok.

Thailand’s Central Investigation Bureau (CIB) said the 11 men were aged between 22 and 48 years.

It said the Thai highway police chased after the speeding pickup truck at about 5am before it lost control and overturned.

“The 35-year-old driver, a Thai man, fled the scene. The highway police found the 11 Bangladeshis in the damaged vehicle.

“The initial investigation revealed that the group had travelled from Bangladesh to Cambodia before illegally crossing into Thailand via the pickup truck en route to Malaysia,” it said.
