ACLEDA mobile

 around the world with high security and 
low fees

You can transfer money abroad through 
ACLEDA mobile
 anytime and anywhere worldwide.

Currency in 
US Dollar

You can transfer money up to 
US$10,000

Number of transactions up to 4 times per day.

Enter the transfer information correctly.

To receive money
