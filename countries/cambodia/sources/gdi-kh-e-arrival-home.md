### Frequently asked questions.

What is the Cambodia e-Arrival?

The Cambodia e-Arrival is a new system designed to make the process easier for your arrival to the Kingdom of Cambodia. It replaces the paper Immigration Form, Health Form, and Customs Declaration by providing a single portal.

What is the official website of the Cambodia e-Arrival?

The https://arrival.gov.kh is the only official website of the Cambodia e-Arrival Card.

Is Cambodia e-Arrival a visa?

Please note that Cambodia e-Arrival is not a visa.

Do I need to fill in the Cambodia e-Arrival if I already have a VISA?

All travelers are required to fill in the Cambodia e-Arrival even if they have been granted VISA.

Do I need to pay to update or register with the e-Arrival?

No, it is completely free for all travelers to fill in the e-Arrival Card by the official website or mobile application.

Do I need an internet connection to use the Cambodia e-Arrival?

Yes, you need to have an active connection in order to submit the Cambodia e-Arrival.

When can I submit the Cambodia e-Arrival?

You may register and submit application within 7 days prior to your arrival into the Kingdom of Cambodia.

What do I need to do after I register in the e-Arrival?

Take a screenshot or download your QR code before closing or exiting the e-Arrival Card website. If you use the mobile application, the QR Code will be available after successful submission even without internet connection.

Can I print my e-Arrival QR code instead of screenshot?

Yes. You may print your e-Arrival QR code as well.

I made a mistake. Can I still edit my information after I have successfully registered?

Yes, you may edit your information and resubmit your e-Arrival Card as long as you have not been processed by Immigration Officers.
