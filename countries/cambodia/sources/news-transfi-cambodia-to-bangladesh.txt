bKash
bKash is Bangladesh's dominant mobile financial service with over 65 million registered accounts and the widest agent network in the country. Users rely on bKash for P2P transfers, merchant QR payments, utility bills, school fees, and e-commerce checkout. For businesses, bKash is often the single highest-impact payment method to integrate—its brand is synonymous with mobile money in Bangladesh, and absence at checkout is a direct conversion penalty. International send and receive products terminating into bKash match the UX expectations of Bangladesh's largest financial user base.
Minutes
Nagad
Nagad is Bangladesh Post Office's mobile financial service and bKash's primary competitor, with rapid adoption driven by government disbursements, social safety net payments, and competitive merchant incentives. Nagad excels at reaching users in semi-urban and rural areas where post office brand trust matters—particularly for remittance receive in Sylhet, Rangpur, and coastal districts. Platforms supporting both bKash and Nagad maximize addressable market coverage across Bangladesh's 172 million population and avoid alienating users loyal to either wallet ecosystem.
Minutes
Rocket (Dutch-Bangla Bank)
Rocket, operated by Dutch-Bangla Bank, is one of Bangladesh's pioneering MFS platforms with deep roots in bank-linked mobile banking across 172 million population. It remains popular among users who prefer bank-backed wallets and among certain merchant categories in Dhaka and Chattogram. Rocket supports cash-in and cash-out through DBBL agents and ATMs, making it a reliable rail for RMG payroll-adjacent flows and users transitioning between cash and digital money in Bangladesh's export economy.
Minutes to hours
Bank Transfer (BRAC, City, Islami & Major Banks)
Commercial bank transfers remain essential for B2B settlement, property transactions, and higher-value consumer payments. Leading institutions including BRAC Bank, City Bank, and Islami Bank Bangladesh serve corporate treasuries, exporters, and middle-class account holders. For international platforms, supporting bank transfer collection and payout in BDT provides a critical backstop for transactions that exceed MFS limits or require formal banking records.
Same business day
Upay
Upay is a growing mobile financial service backed by United Commercial Bank, targeting digital-native users and merchant partnerships across Bangladesh's $4.8 billion e-commerce market. While smaller than bKash and Nagad, Upay adds incremental coverage among UCB customers and urban professionals in Dhaka. Including Upay in a multi-rail strategy future-proofs payment acceptance as Bangladesh's MFS market continues to diversify beyond two dominant players serving the seventh-largest remittance receiver nation.

Bank transfers through BRAC Bank, City Bank, and Islami Bank suit higher amounts, RMG business recipients, and export-related payments. TransFi routes sends to the appropriate local rail based on recipient preference, KYC tier, and transaction profile to maximize delivery success.

MFS credits to bKash or Nagad can arrive within minutes when Gulf, Malaysian, or European corridors and compliance checks are complete—matching the speed Bangladeshi users expect from domestic wallet transfers. Bank transfers through BRAC Bank, City Bank, and Islami Bank may take slightly longer depending on institution, amount, and recipient verification status.
