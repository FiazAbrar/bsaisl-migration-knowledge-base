Important Guidance:
  • General Services: Standard Tourist and Business Visas can typically be requested from any Royal Embassy of Cambodia, regardless of your current location.
  • Jurisdictional Matters: Specific legal documents—such as certifying foreign residency, confirming or renouncing nationality, and passport renewals—must be handled by the specific Embassy assigned to your country of residence (listed under the "Jurisdiction" column).
  • Consulates General & Honorary Consulates: Regional offices are available for immediate consular assistance. You can view the full list of Consulates General and Honorary Consulates for more details.
  • If you are not sure, you can always reach out to each Embassy via a quick phone call or email for further guidance.
