# Cambodia | UNHCR

(Saved via Firecrawl, 11 November 2026, from https://www.unhcr.org/where-we-work/countries/cambodia. Strategy text for 2027 planning; relevant parts only.)

UNHCR's engagement in Cambodia has been focused on advocacy with government officials on issues of concern to refugees, asylum-seekers, and stateless individuals/at risk of statelessness residing within the country, including prevention of refoulement and support to refugee recognition. UNHCR provides technical advice and support to the Department of Immigration, Refugee Department and other State officials concerned (including further training and capacity-building).

The operations in Cambodia are coordinated by the Thailand multi-country office.

In Cambodia, UNHCR will maintain technical support to the national asylum system under the General Department of Immigration.

UNHCR also supports protection efforts in neighbouring countries. In Cambodia, UNHCR provides technical support to the General Department of Immigration, which oversees 26 refugees and 11 asylum-seekers of various nationalities. Although Cambodia has a legal framework and is party to the 1951 Convention relating to the Status of Refugees, challenges remain in ensuring access to asylum and efficient case processing.

MCO Thailand contributes to the UNCTs in Cambodia and Viet Nam primarily through its technical expertise on refugee and statelessness protection as UNHCR neither has programming nor staffing in place.
