International Funds Transfers via Thunes 

(11 Ratings)

International Fund Transfer via Thunes is an instant fund transfer service within intermediaries' banks of Thunes.

You can send money abroad on ACLEDA mobile or instant receive money sent from abroad around the world with high security and low fees.

To send money

  • You can transfer money abroad through ACLEDA mobile anytime and anywhere worldwide.
  • Currency in US Dollar. In case of source account in Khmer Riel, it will be automatically converted into US Dollar.
  • You can transfer money up to US$10,000 (China up to US$1,300) per transaction.
  • Number of transactions up to 4 times per day.
  • Enter the transfer information correctly.

Fee for sending money abroad

warningThe fees in our website are quoted for indication purpose only and subject to variation. Please contact our nearest branch or our Call Center for our services requirements and more information.

To receive money

  • You can receive money sent from worldwide directly to your bank account in ACLEDA Bank Plc. instantly with no extra charges.
  • You can receive money instantly in USD or KHR, depending on the account currency.
  • You can receive money up to US$2,500 or equivalent per transaction and US$5,000 or equivalent per day.
  • Number of transactions up to 4 times per day.

Correction Transfer Information and Refund

  • For any successful fund transfer, we are not able to modify/cancel the transaction or refund.
  • For any fund transfer to recipient in cash, you can modify the transfer information or cancel by refund back in case the recipient has not yet withdrawn the money.
