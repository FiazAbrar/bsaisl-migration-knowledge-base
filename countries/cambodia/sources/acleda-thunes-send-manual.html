How to send money abroad via Thunes

  1. 1. Open "ACLEDA mobile" app
  2. 2. Select "TRANSFERS" menu
3. Use Touch ID, Facial, or enter PIN to login
4. Select "INTERNATIONAL TRANSFER"
5. Choose "Thunes"
  1. 6. Fill in your transfer information correctly
  2. 7. Choose "NEXT" button
  1. 8. Fill in the information of overseas recipient correctly
  2. 9. Choose "I have read and agree to the Terms and Conditions"
10. Select "OK" button
  1. 11. Verify your transfer information
  2. 12. Choose "CONFIRM" button
  1. 13. Overseas money transfer was completed
  2. Please provide this transfer information to the recipient including withdrawal code if you send in cash
