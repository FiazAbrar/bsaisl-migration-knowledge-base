      78 Bangladeshis return from Cambodia    

      The flight is scheduled to arrive at Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport today afternoon at 4.45pm    

      30 September, 2020, 05:25 pm    

      Last modified: 30 September, 2020, 06:12 pm    

          File photo of a Biman Bangladesh aircraft. Picture: Salahuddin Ahmed/TBS        

Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok facilitated the repatriation of the second group of 78 Bangladeshis stranded in Cambodia including the mortal remains of a deceased Bangladeshi by a special flight of Biman Bangladesh.

The flight is scheduled to arrive at Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport today afternoon at 4.45pm, said a press release.

The ministry of foreign affairs of Bangladesh arranged this special flight on self-payment basis to repatriate stranded Bangladeshi nationals in Cambodia.

            Keep updated, follow The Business Standard's Google news channel

Earlier on July 7, Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok facilitated the repatriation of the first group of Bangladesh nationals.

They have also arranged for six special flights from Bangkok to Dhaka on April 17, May 14, May 23, June 26, July 15, and September 2 to send 242 Bangladeshis back home.
