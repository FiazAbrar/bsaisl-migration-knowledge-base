## Why Do I Need to Register/Update My Profile?

You should register your new SIM or provide missing profile information to verify your ownership, secure your phone number, and prevent unauthorized usage.

- 1

  Secure your Phone number

  Protect your phone number from unauthorized access and keep your account safe with quick verification.

- 2

  Comply with government rules and regulations

  The process ensures compliance with current government rules and regulations.

- 3

  Receive personalized offers

  Receive exclusive offers and promotions tailored to your preferences, interests, and usage.
