Travelers heading to Cambodia can prepay visa-on-arrival fees online, which began on 
Sept. 1
, ending the requirement to carry exact cash in foreign currency to airport counters.
Paperless airport processing
The prepayment option expands Cambodia's digital border network, which shifted from physical passport entry stamps to the paperless e-Arrival at international airports and seaports in July 2025. The General Department of Immigration extended the v-Pass system on Jan. 1, issuing digital visitor passes directly via email, the official mobile app and the government portal at arrival.gov.kh.
From Sept. 1, travelers can settle their entry fees ahead of departure while submitting the mandatory e-Arrival Card, which combined immigration, customs and health declarations into a single electronic filing in July 2024. The digital payment covers arrivals landing at Techo International Airport, Siem Reap Angkor International Airport and Sihanoukville International Airport, Ministry of Interior travel updates confirmed.
Ongoing overland border closures
While air hubs transition to cashless processing, overland routes from Thailand remain blocked for inbound travelers. All 
seven
 official land checkpoints connecting Thailand to Cambodia have remained closed for standard foreign crossings since 
June 2025
, with e-Visa entry formally suspended at 
Poi Pet
 and 
Cham Yeam
, official immigration advisories reported.
Nomads and long-term expats who historically used overland border runs from Bangkok to reset their stay must reroute through commercial flights or cross through open land checkpoints with Vietnam at Bavet or Laos at Tropaeng Kreal. 
Remote workers organizing regional itineraries must budget for airfare rather than bus transfers, as border authorities continue to turn back foreigners attempting overland entry from Thailand. Anyone planning an upcoming stay should review 
Cambodia's entry requirements
 to confirm approved arrival points before booking transit.
Frequently asked questions
Can I prepay Cambodia visa-on-arrival fees online?
+
Yes. Cambodia began allowing online prepayment of visa-on-arrival fees on Sept. 1, so travelers no longer need to carry exact cash in foreign currency to airport counters.
Which Cambodia airports accept online visa fee prepayment?
+
Techo International Airport, Siem Reap Angkor International Airport and Sihanoukville International Airport accept the online fee payment option.
What is Cambodia's e-Arrival Card?
+
It is a mandatory electronic filing that combines immigration, customs and health declarations into one form. Travelers submit it when settling entry fees ahead of departure.
What is the digital v-Pass in Cambodia?
+
The v-Pass is a digital visitor pass issued at arrival. Cambodia sends it directly by email, through the official mobile app and via the government portal at arrival.gov.kh.
Can I enter Cambodia overland from Thailand right now?
+
Standard foreign crossings from Thailand are blocked. All seven official land checkpoints have remained closed since June 2025.
Is e-Visa entry open at Cambodia's Thailand border checkpoints?
+
No. e-Visa entry is formally suspended at Poi Pet and Cham Yeam.
What should travelers use instead of Thailand land crossings into Cambodia?
+
Travelers should reroute through commercial flights or use open land checkpoints with Vietnam at Bavet or Laos at Tropaeng Kreal.
