International Transfers

I need to send money overseas quickly and safely

Transfer funds abroad with confidence using secure and reliable channels. Send money to your family, partners, or businesses worldwide with ease.

Whether you’re supporting loved ones, running a business, or receiving funds from abroad, Wing Bank makes international transfers simple, fast, and secure. With access to over 200 countries and a wide global partner network, staying connected financially has never been easier.  

Stay Connected Across Borders

 

 

Wing Bank connects Cambodia to the world through trusted international partners and a strong nationwide agent network. Every transfer is designed to give you confidence, convenience, and control—no matter where the money is going or coming from

Why Choose Wing Bank International Transfers

Ways to Send Money

Sending money internationally is simple and flexible with Wing Bank World Transfer.

Sending Options

Cash Pick Up

Send money to the receiver
without a bank account.

Bank Deposit

Send money to the
receiver's bank account.

Home delivery

Send money to the
receiver's home.

How to Send Money via Wing Bank App

 

Sending money takes just a few steps: 

 

  1. Open the Wing Bank App 
  2. Tap World Transfer 
  3. Select the destination country and partner 
  4. Enter receiver details and transfer amount 
  5. Confirm with your 4-digit PIN — transfer completed 

Ways to Receive Money

Receiving money from overseas is just as easy. Whether you’re a migrant worker, business owner, or trader, Wing Bank helps you access your funds quickly and securely.

Receiving Options

Cash Pick Up

Collect your money at any
Wing agent nationwide.

Code to Wing

Redeem your transfer instantly through
the Wing Bank App.

Bank Deposit

Receive funds directly into your
Wing Bank account.

How to Receive Money through the Wing Bank App 

 

  1. Open the Wing Bank App 
  2. Tap Code to Wing 
  3. Select the partner 
  4. Enter required details 
  5. Confirm with your 4-digit PIN — money received.
