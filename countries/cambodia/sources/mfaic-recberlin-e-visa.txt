Cambodia e-Visa Information 

The Cambodia e-Visa, or Electronic Visa, was introduced by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation in April 2006. This convenient online platform allows travelers worldwide to apply for a Cambodia visa (
available for Diplomatic (A), Official (B), Courtesy (C),

Tourist (T) and Ordinary/Business (E) visas
) from anywhere with internet access, simplifying their travel planning and preparation. 
The e-Visa service is exclusively operated by the E-Visa Office within the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation of Cambodia and is not handled by Cambodian embassies abroad, including the Royal Embassy of Cambodia in Berlin. 
Any questions or concerns regarding the e-Visa should be directed to the E-Visa Office.
Please also note that not all entry points accept e-Visas. You can check the entry points 
here
.
