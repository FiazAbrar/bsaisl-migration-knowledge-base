# Cambodia doc: coverage audit

Audits of CLAUDE.md step 9 on doc.md, using only facts already in facts.csv. 1,408 facts; 1,028 cited in doc.md; 380 uncited (reviewed at the end; 61 of them, K0001-K0061, were added by the K pass while this audit ran).

Summary of the three audits:
- **Migrant's questions:** stay, earnings, rights, jail, bank and SIM, pension, family and carry-home are all covered. Gaps: what customs allows a worker to bring into Cambodia (facts exist, uncited); the 2019 official breakdown of yearly visa and permit costs against pay (facts exist, uncited).
- **Aim per visa:** nothing says what happens when an E-class e-visa or a work permit is refused, or when a worker is refused entry at the airport (no facts: Need to know added). Asylum does not say Cambodia is party to the 1951 Refugee Convention (fact exists). Corridor: the Laos ban on Bangladeshi workers and the Tier 3 group's share of the 12-country clearances are uncited; growth beyond construction (service, hospitality, banking in 2022) is uncited.
- **Insider test:** the big names, scandals, crackdowns, costs and scams are in. Missing: two agencies named by returnees (Mashallah Overseas, Alif International Agency); women going outside BMET clearance; the US 2025 estimate of 150,000 people in about 350 compounds; Hun Manet's April 2026 deadline; Nepal's embassy model for travel documents; the survivors' group's leader. One error found: the Labour First Secretary is spelt two ways (Zahir Imam, Jahir Emam).
