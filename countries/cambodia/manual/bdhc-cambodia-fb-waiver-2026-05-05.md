Post 1 (5 May 2026, 09:26):
Important Notice for Bangladeshi Citizens in Cambodia
Dear Bangladeshi expatriates living in Cambodia,
This is to inform you that the visa overstay waiver application process has successfully been completed for approximately 1,500–1,600 Bangladeshi citizens who were stranded in Cambodia. The Embassy of Bangladesh in Bangkok, together with the Honorary Consulate of Cambodia, worked closely with all affected citizens to provide assistance, ensuring their safe return home.
At present, the new application process has been partially closed. In case any citizen requires urgent assistance, he or she may contact the Honorary Consulate of Cambodia or reach out directly to the Embassy of Bangladesh in Bangkok.

Post 2 (5 May 2026, 16:15, with 9 photos of name lists):
In response to the applications for the waiver of visa overstay fines, the attached list (Memo- 332, 333, 334) has been forwarded to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation in Phnom Penh. Upon approval, you may contact Phnom Penh Immigration Office with the necessary documents within 2–3 weeks.
If you wish to submit a new application, please send your passport and visa copies via email to sophear@sophiyacorp.com, or to the official emails of the Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok at mission.bangkok@mofa.gov.bd or fsbangkok@probashi.gov.bd. For any advice or support, you may visit or contact the Office of the Honorary Consulate of Bangladesh in Phnom Penh.
