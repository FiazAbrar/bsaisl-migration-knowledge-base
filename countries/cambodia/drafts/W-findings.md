### Step 2: The job offer and contract

A legal job in Cambodia rests on two papers the employer makes: an employment contract certified by MLVT (the Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training) and, later, a work permit issued through FWCMS (the Foreign Workers Centralized Management System, MLVT's online system for foreign workers) [W0033, W0017]. Since November 2026 the Bangladesh side also needs Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare approval of the demand letter before BMET clears anyone for Cambodia (see Step 1: Find a real job) [W0096, W0097].

1. **Read the contract before you pay anyone.** A fixed-duration contract must be in writing, give an exact start and end date, and last at most 2 years; renewals cannot take it past 4 years in total [W0044, W0045]. It should state the salary, working hours, duties and other conditions [W0047]. Many employers add a probation period, usually 3 months [W0048].
   **Check:**
   1. The job title on the contract is the job you were promised, and it is the same title the employer will put on the visa and work permit (see Step 5: The visa, and checking it is real) [W0090, W0094].
   2. The job is not one closed to foreigners: driver, street vendor, masseur, hairdresser or beautician, shoe shiner, tailor, mechanic or tyre repairer, or maker of Khmer souvenirs, musical instruments or Buddhist statues [W0038].
   3. Nothing in it lets the employer take a placement fee or the cost of your medical check from your wages; both deductions are banned [W0042, W0043].
2. **Make sure the employer registers the contract with MLVT.** The employer must register a foreign worker's contract with MLVT [W0046]. The contract has to be in Khmer and certified by MLVT, which takes about 15 working days [W0053]. To check, call the MLVT employment-contract desk listed on FWCMS (8:00-12:00 and 13:00-17:00): Mr Chuon Ansangwar 085 778 888 or Mr Neth Sophorn 012 282 250 [W0020, W0023].
   **Scams:** A broker may say the job title on your papers is "temporary" and will change once you prove yourself; workers promised computer jobs have arrived with visas naming them construction workers [W0090, W0091].

### Step 3: The employer gets your work permit

In Cambodia the employer, not the worker, gets the work permit and the employment card through MLVT; they are two documents applied for together in one application on FWCMS [W0066, W0077]. The Labour Law (1997), Article 261, says no foreigner may work without both [W0076].

1. **Ask whether the employer has a foreign-worker quota.** An approved quota is the first condition for any work permit [W0028]. The employer registers on FWCMS (fwcms.mlvt.gov.kh) and requests the quota there [W0017]. Quotas for 2027 are requested from 1 September to 30 November 2026; companies set up later can apply as soon as they start operating [W0025, W0029, W0104]. Foreigners may be at most 10% of a company's Cambodian workforce: 3% office staff, 6% skilled and 1% unskilled workers [W0030]. A company that cannot find enough qualified Cambodians can ask MLVT to go above 10% under Prakas 277/20 (a ministerial order) [W0031]. An employer that misses the quota deadline faces fines of KHR 12.2 million to 18 million (about USD 3,050 to 4,500) [W0027].
2. **Give the employer your papers.** You'll need:
   - your passport, valid, with your E-class visa (see Step 5: The visa, and checking it is real) [W0068, W0034];
   - three 4 cm x 6 cm photos [W0068, W0035];
   - a health certificate (see Step 7: After arrival, item 2) [W0037];
   - a residence certificate from the Sangkat (commune) chief where you live, or a rental lease certified by the chief [W0036, W0054];
   - the date of your last Cambodian exit stamp, if you have been in Cambodia before [W0103].
   The employer adds its own papers: incorporation certificate, registered address, tax patent, quota approval, Ministry of Commerce approval and articles of incorporation [W0067].
3. **Make sure it is filed in time.** MLVT's 2026 notice says a foreigner must get the work permit through FWCMS within 90 calendar days of first entering Cambodia [W0026]. In practice MLVT has asked for the permit before the first E-class visa (usually 30 days) runs out [W0050]. Processing takes about 15 to 20 working days once all documents are in and paid for [W0073]. The fee is set in MLVT Guideline 003-15 (2015) [W0110]; one agent quoted about USD 200 for the permit [W0074], and the FWCMS operator, E-Solution (Cambodia) Co., Ltd., adds USD 30 per new or renewed permit, paid through a bank [W0105, W0107].
4. **Renew it every year.** A work permit is valid for one year and is renewed between 1 January and 31 March [W0032]. The deadline for 2026 permits was 31 March 2026; in 2025 MLVT extended the deadline twice, to 31 May 2025 [W0056, W0061].
   **Check it is real:** Ask the employer for your work permit and employment card, and keep a copy with you at all times [W0075]. To ask MLVT about a permit, call its foreign-worker-permit desk: Mr Sim Hong 099 555 339 or Mr Phorn Sovann 012 583 933; for the online system, 085 936 834 or 011 566 686, or email online.fwcms@gmail.com [W0019, W0022, W0024].
   **If it fails:** Without a permit the employer can be fined up to KHR 12.6 million (about USD 3,150) per worker, and the Law on Immigration allows up to 3 months' prison and/or deportation for the foreign worker [W0057, W0059]. Authorities have been enforcing permits more strictly [W0060]. (See What can get you jailed or deported.)

### Step 5: The visa, and checking it is real

To work, you enter on the E-class (ordinary or "business") visa, not the T-class tourist visa: only the E-class visa can be extended inside Cambodia as an EB (work) extension [W0069, W0078]. Turning a tourist visa into an E-class visa after arrival is not an official route [W0079]. (Visa on arrival: see Visit visa.)

1. **Apply for the E-class e-visa online.** The Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation runs the e-visa portal, www.evisa.gov.kh. The E-class e-visa costs USD 35, is single entry, is valid for 3 months from the date of issue, and lets you stay 1 month [W0001]. You'll need:
   - a passport valid for more than 6 months on the day you enter [W0002];
   - a digital passport photo (JPEG or PNG) [W0002];
   - a card to pay: Visa, MasterCard, JCB, Diners Club, UnionPay, Alipay or WeChat [W0002, W0009].
   The approval comes within 3 business days, by email, or you download it from the "Check & Change" page [W0007, W0008]. You show the e-visa certificate to immigration on arrival [W0008].
   **Scams:** A visa obtained with false information or forged documents can be refused, cancelled or revoked, and you stay responsible for every detail even when an agent applies for you [W0010, W0012]. Fees are not refunded once processing starts [W0011].
2. **Check it is real, before you pay the agency the balance.**
   1. Go to www.evisa.gov.kh, open "Check Certificate" (also called Check & Change), and enter the application reference number; there is a link if you have lost it [W0015, W0016].
   2. Check the visa type says E (Business), not T (Tourist), and that the passport number is yours; you must travel on the same passport the e-visa was issued for [W0001, W0013].
   3. Cambodian embassies cannot check an e-visa's status for you; the online check is the only one [W0014].
   **Scams:** Bangladeshis promised work visas have arrived with one-month visit visas or tourist visas, some after getting BMET clearance [W0092, W0093]. Many trafficked Bangladeshis travelled on visas that did not match the job promised [W0094]. (See Scam compounds and trafficking.)
3. **Enter at a port that takes e-visas.** E-visas are accepted at Techo International Airport (Phnom Penh), Siem Reap Angkor and Sihanoukville airports, and the Bavet and Tropaeng Kreal land borders [W0004]. Cham Yeam (Koh Kong) and Poipet (Banteay Meanchey) are closed to e-visa entry until further notice [W0005]. Every traveller must also fill in the e-Arrival form at arrival.gov.kh, ideally within 7 days before arrival [W0006].

### Step 7: After arrival

1. **Fill in the e-Arrival form before you land.** Every traveller fills it in at arrival.gov.kh; your first E-class stay is 30 days [W0006, W0064].
2. **Get the health check.** The health certificate comes from MLVT's Labour Medical Department, or from another clinic with the certificate certified by MLVT; it takes 3 to 7 working days [W0052]. Certification of a private clinic's certificate cost USD 5 (KHR 20,400) [W0108]. Your employer may not deduct the cost of this check from your wages [W0043].
3. **Make sure you are registered at your address.** FPCS (the Foreigners Presence in Cambodia System) is the General Department of Immigration's (GDI) online register of where foreigners live [W0081, W0085]. Your landlord, owner or guesthouse manager is usually the one who must register you, through the "FPCS – GDI" app or website [W0082, W0085]. Ask your landlord directly whether they use FPCS and whether you are registered [W0086]. If you are not, your visa extension can run into problems; some landlords avoid registering tenants to hide rental income from tax [W0083, W0084]. For the work permit you also need a residence certificate from the Sangkat chief (Step 3: The employer gets your work permit, item 2) [W0036].
4. **Get a SIM card.** At a Smart shop or in the Smart app, register the SIM with a scan of your passport and a face scan; approval is usually instant [W0099, W0100, W0101]. Smart's help line is (+855) 10 200 888 or 1204 [W0102].
5. **Open a bank or wallet account.** (See Sending money home.)
6. **Collect your work permit and employment card.** Keep a copy with you at all times [W0075]. (See Step 3: The employer gets your work permit.)
7. **Extend your visa to an EB extension before the 30 days run out.** The GDI (the Ministry of Interior's immigration department) extends the E-class visa for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months at a time [W0064]. 6- and 12-month extensions allow multiple entries; 1- and 3-month extensions are single entry [W0065]. Through an agent an EB extension cost USD 50 for 1 month, USD 80 for 3 months, USD 160 for 6 months and USD 290 for 1 year [W0070]. Going to the Immigration Department directly is supposed to be cheaper, but many foreigners use agents because it is slow [W0072]. Your first 6- or 12-month EB extension can be had without a work permit; the second and every later long-term EB extension needs proof of a valid work permit [W0071]. In 2026 extensions increasingly depend on supporting documents, accommodation records and, in some cases, work-permit or business proof [W0080].

### How long you can stay

- **First entry:** 30 days on the E-class visa [W0064].
- **Visa extensions:** EB extensions of 1, 3, 6 or 12 months, renewed again and again while you work; from the second long-term extension you need a valid work permit [W0064, W0071]. Other E-class extensions exist for job-seekers (EG), retirees (ER) and students (ES) [W0078].
- **Work permit:** one year, renewed each year between 1 January and 31 March; to renew it you need an extension with at least 6 months left, such as an EB [W0032, W0055].
- **Contract:** a fixed-duration contract lasts at most 2 years, and at most 4 years with renewals [W0044, W0045].
- **Citizenship:** From 1 December 2025 the amended Nationality Law offers citizenship to investors of at least USD 1 million (KHR 4 billion) or donors of USD 3 million (KHR 12 billion) to the national budget, and tightens the residence needed for ordinary naturalisation [W0087, W0088, W0089].

### Your residence permit

Cambodia gives working foreigners three papers, which together are your right to live and work there: the EB extension in your passport from the GDI, and the work permit and employment card from MLVT [W0064, W0077]. Keep a copy of the permit and card with you at all times [W0075]. Your address must also be on FPCS (Step 7: After arrival, item 3) [W0081]. Overstaying or working without a permit can mean fines, prison or deportation (see What can get you jailed or deported) [W0059].

> **Need to know**
> - Does the GDI issue any separate residence card to EB holders, or is the passport sticker the only proof?

## Other work routes

### Self-employed and business owners

Self-employed foreigners and foreign owners named on a company's patent tax certificate need their own work permit and employment card, under MLVT Notification 110/23 (28 December 2023) [W0062, W0063]. They apply on FWCMS through the "Self-Employed" or "Employer" login [W0018, W0017]. A foreigner doing business without a work permit, including the self-employed, can be fined KHR 50.4 million (about USD 12,600) under Joint Prakas 498 (31 July 2023) [W0058]. The jobs closed to foreigners apply to them too: driving, street vending, massage, hairdressing, shoe shining, tailoring, mechanic work and making Khmer souvenirs [W0038].
