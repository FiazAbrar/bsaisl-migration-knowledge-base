## Other work routes

Besides a job with one employer (see Work visa: company worker), a foreigner can work on his own permit as a self-employed person or as an owner named on a company's tax registration [W0062, W0063].

### Self-employed and business owners

A self-employed foreigner needs his own work permit and employment card from MLVT (the Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training), under MLVT Notification 110/23 of 28 December 2023; the work permit and employment card are the two papers that together let a foreigner work in Cambodia [W0062, W0076, W0077]. A foreign owner named on a company's patent tax certificate (the company's yearly business-tax registration) needs a work permit too [W0063].

1. **Apply on FWCMS** (the Foreign Workers Centralized Management System, fwcms.mlvt.gov.kh, MLVT's online system for foreign workers). A self-employed person uses its "Self-Employed" function [W0018]. A company uses its "Enterprise" function to register, request a quota to use foreign workers and monitor them [W0017]. The work permit and employment card are applied for together in one application (Work visa: company worker, Step 3) [W0077].
2. **Stay on the EB extension.** The EB (business) extension of the E-class (ordinary) visa covers employees and freelancers, for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months; it does not by itself give the right to work [V0030, V0032]. How to get it is in Work visa: company worker, Step 7 [W0064].
3. **Keep out of the closed trades.** Foreigners may not work as drivers, street vendors, masseurs, hairdressers or beauticians, shoe shiners or tailors, mechanics or tyre repairers, or makers of Khmer souvenirs, musical instruments or Buddhist statues [W0038].

**If it fails:** A foreigner working or doing business without a work permit, the self-employed included, can be fined KHR 50.4 million (riel, Cambodia's currency; about Tk 15.4 lakh) under Joint Prakas 498 of 31 July 2023 (a joint ministerial order) [W0058, G0003].

## Student visa

Cambodia has no student entry visa of its own: a student enters on the E-class (ordinary) visa, which also covers trainees, interns and students, and then extends it inside Cambodia as an ES (student) extension [V0012, V0071, V0034]. The E-class e-visa costs USD 35 (about Tk 4,400) [W0001, G0002].

1. **Get an acceptance letter** from a registered Cambodian school or university, and proof that you can pay for your living costs and fees [V0072, V0035].
2. **Get the E-class e-visa** on www.evisa.gov.kh, as in Work visa: company worker, Step 5; the E-class visa needs a letter of invitation, job offer or contract from a company or organisation in Cambodia [V0012, V0013, W0001].
3. **Apply for the ES extension before your first month ends,** at the immigration office or through a visa agent [V0038, V0014]. You'll need the enrolment letter from a registered school and proof of funds [V0035]. It runs for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months; 1- and 3-month extensions are single entry, and 6- and 12-month ones let you leave and come back [V0034, V0036]. Through an agent, a 6-month extension costs around USD 160 to 170 (about Tk 20,000 to 21,200) [V0073, G0002].
4. **Renew your passport at the reduced student fee.** Bangladeshi students in Cambodia pay 1,190 baht (Thai currency) for a regular passport at the Embassy of Bangladesh in Bangkok, which covers Cambodia because Bangladesh has no embassy there (see The Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok) [V0105, V0110, B0197].

### Can you work?

No foreigner may work without an MLVT work permit and employment card, and the ES extension is issued for study [W0076, V0034].

## Visit visa

The tourist visa (T-class) is for holidays, and working on it is prohibited [V0009]. It costs USD 30 (about Tk 3,700), is single entry, is valid for 3 months from issue and lets you stay 1 month; it can be extended once [V0001, V0002, V0010, G0002]. Bangladesh's Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare tells people not to go abroad for work on a tourist or visit visa on a broker's promise [N0093]. A job needs the E-class visa and a work permit (see Work visa: company worker) [W0069, W0076].

> **Need to know**
> - In November 2026, can a Bangladeshi passport holder apply for the tourist (T) e-visa or get a visa on arrival, or only the E-class e-visa?

### Step 1: Choose how to apply

1. **Online:** www.evisa.gov.kh is run only by the E-Visa Office of Cambodia's MFAIC (Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation); embassies don't run it [V0027]. Step 2 gives the steps.
2. **Through an embassy, by email:** Cambodia's embassy for Bangladesh is the Royal Embassy of Cambodia in New Delhi (A-1/309, Safdarjung Enclave, New Delhi 110029; +91 11 41717136 or 41717143; camemb.ind@mfaic.gov.kh) [V0021, V0052]. Tourist and business visas can typically be requested from any Cambodian embassy, wherever the applicant lives [V0044].
3. **On arrival:** a visa on arrival is offered at international airports and border checkpoints, but not every nationality is eligible [V0024, V0028]. It is now processed electronically, with a digital visitor pass sent by email, and since 1 September 2026 its fee can be paid online in advance [V0056, V0066].

> **Need to know**
> - Does the New Delhi embassy accept visa applications by email from people living in Bangladesh, and what does it charge?

### Step 2: Apply for the e-visa

You'll need:
- a passport valid for more than 6 months on the day you arrive [V0003];
- a digital passport photo (JPEG or PNG) [V0003];
- a Visa or MasterCard credit card [V0003];
- a flight, bus or boat ticket and a hotel or other accommodation booking [V0011].

1. **Open www.evisa.gov.kh** and click "Application" [V0006].
2. **Type the security code** shown in the box [V0006].
3. **Fill in your personal, passport and travel details,** then review them [V0006].
4. **Pay by card** [V0006]. The fee is not refunded once processing has started [W0011].
5. **Save the reference number.** You need it to track the application, change pending details or download the visa under "Check and Change" [V0023].
6. **Wait about 3 business days** for the visa by email; there is no express service [V0022, V0002].
7. **If your photo was wrong,** email the new one with your reference number to info@evisa.gov.kh [V0007]. The e-visa office answers on (855) 23 224 973 and (855) 99 334 973, in Khmer and English [V0008].

**Check it is real:** On www.evisa.gov.kh, open "Check Certificate" (Check & Change) and enter the reference number; there is a link if you have lost it [W0015, W0016]. Check that the visa type is T (Tourist) and that the passport is the one you will travel on [V0001, W0013]. Cambodian embassies can't check an e-visa for you [W0014]. Since 3 October 2026 every Cambodian visa is digital, so no sticker goes in the passport [V0020].

**If it fails:** The Ministry may refuse, cancel or revoke a visa given on false information or forged documents, and you stay responsible for every detail even when an agent applies for you [W0010, W0012].

**What agencies charge:** A Dhaka travel agency charges Tk 7,500 for a Cambodia e-visa, non-refundable, delivered in 5 to 7 working days, against the official USD 30 (about Tk 3,700) [V0080, V0121, V0001, G0002]. It asks for 6 months' bank statements and a bank solvency certificate showing at least Tk 60,000 per applicant [V0081].

> **Need to know**
> - What do Dhaka agencies charge in 2026 for a Cambodia tourist visa sold with a promise of a job after arrival?

### Step 3: Fill in the e-Arrival before you fly

1. **Within 7 days before you arrive,** fill in the e-Arrival (Cambodia's online immigration, health and customs form) at https://arrival.gov.kh, the only official site [V0047, V0049, W0006]. Every traveller must submit it, even with a visa; it is free, and it is not a visa [V0045, V0046, V0029].
2. **Screenshot or download the QR code** before you close the page [V0048].

### Step 4: At the border

1. **Enter at a port that accepts e-visas:** Techo International Airport (Phnom Penh), Siem Reap Angkor or Sihanoukville airport, Bavet on the Vietnam border or Tropaeng Kreal on the Laos border [V0040, V0116, V0117, V0041, V0118]. All land crossings from Thailand, Poipet, Cham Yeam and O Smach included, are closed until further notice [V0042].
2. **Be ready to show why you came.** Officers may refuse entry to anyone without a clear travel history and purpose, money to support themselves and a guarantor in Cambodia [V0061]. The GDI said in July 2025 that it has no policy of refusing any nationality, but applies stricter checks [V0060].

> **Need to know**

### Step 5: Extend once, or leave

1. **Register your address.** FPCS (Foreigners Presence in Cambodia System) is the GDI's online record of where foreigners stay; hotels usually register guests, and someone not registered may be unable to extend a visa [V0058, V0059]. Report a change of address to the local police within 24 hours [V0068].
2. **To extend,** go to the immigration office in Phnom Penh or use a licensed visa agent; it can't be done online [V0064]. A tourist visa can be extended once, for one month, for about USD 45 (about Tk 5,600) [V0077, V0010, G0002].
3. **Leave after about 60 days in all** (30 on entry and one 30-day extension) [V0063]. Overstaying costs USD 10 (about Tk 1,250) a day (see What can get you jailed or deported) [V0078, G0002].

### Can you work?

No: employment on a tourist visa is prohibited [V0009]. Turning a tourist visa into an E-class visa after arrival is not an official route (see Changing visa type) [W0079].

### Scams

- **Tourist visa sold as a work visa:** in July 2026 the State Minister said many victims travel on one- or two-month tourist visas while brokers tell them they have two-year work visas [S0074]. A returnee promised a computer-operator job for Tk 5.3 lakh got BMET (Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training) clearance, then found on arrival he had only a one-month visit visa [W0092]. Many trafficked Bangladeshis travelled on visas that did not match the job promised (see Scam compounds and trafficking) [W0094].
- **The Thailand detour:** after Bangladesh tightened checks on Cambodia, some recruiters allegedly sent workers to Thailand as tourists before moving them to Cambodia [V0115].
- **Look-alike websites:** the GDI has disowned sites such as "Cambodia-e-arrival.online"; the only official sites are www.evisa.gov.kh and arrival.gov.kh [V0050, V0027, V0049]. Guides and agents still quote the old USD 36 tourist e-visa fee, now USD 30 [V0054].

## Family

A worker's husband, wife or child stays on an EB dependant extension: EB extensions cover dependants of EB holders, for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months [V0030]. Only the E-class visa can be extended as EB, so the family member enters on it [W0069, W0078].

1. **Get your marriage and relationship papers certified** at the Embassy of Bangladesh in Bangkok, which covers Cambodia: it attests a marriage certificate for 275 baht and issues a relationship certificate for 205 baht [V0110, V0109, V0106]. The Thai land border is closed, so a worker in Cambodia can't reach Bangkok overland [V0042].
2. **The family member gets the E-class e-visa** on www.evisa.gov.kh, as in Work visa: company worker, Step 5 [W0001, W0069].
3. **After arrival, apply for the dependant EB extension** at the immigration office or through a visa agent, with proof of the relationship and a letter from you, the EB holder [V0038, V0031]. Agents' EB prices are in Work visa: company worker, Step 7 [W0070].
4. **Register the family's address on FPCS** (Work visa: company worker, Step 7) [W0081].

A family member on an EB extension may not work without his or her own work permit [V0032, W0076].

### Marrying in Cambodia

A marriage is legal only if registered before the civil registrar at the commune or sangkat (urban ward) office [V0095]. Two foreigners living legally in Cambodia may marry there under Cambodian law [V0094]. Marriage between a Cambodian and a foreigner follows Sub-Decree 183 of 3 November 2008 [V0096]. The Bangkok embassy issues a marital status certificate for 275 baht [V0107].

Marrying a Cambodian gives no Khmer visa (K-class) and no automatic nationality: the foreign spouse still needs a T or E visa, and may apply for nationality only after living together for three years from registration of the marriage [V0018, V0039, V0084].

## Changing visa type

- **Tourist to E-class:** Not an official route inside Cambodia [W0079, V0057]. After the one tourist extension, a T-class holder must leave; anyone staying more than 60 days is told to enter on the E-class visa [V0063, V0122].
- **E-class to EB (work or business):** The extension a worker lives on (Work visa: company worker, Step 7) [W0064, W0078]. It does not give the right to work; a work permit is still needed [V0032].
- **E-class to EG (job-seeker):** For people looking for work, for 1, 3 or 6 months, generally single entry [V0033].
- **E-class to ES (student):** See Student visa [V0034].
- **EB to residence or citizenship:** See Permanent residence and citizenship [W0087, V0085].

All E-class extensions are applied for inside Cambodia at the immigration office or through a visa agent [V0038]. To leave and come back on a new visa, the Thai land crossings are closed; Bavet (Vietnam) and Tropaeng Kreal (Laos) are open and accept e-visas [V0042, V0041, V0118].

**Scams:** Brokers promised one Bangladeshi a one-year visa and work permit after arrival; after he escaped a scam centre he never got them and became undocumented [V0113]. A broker's promise that the job title on your visa will change later is covered in Work visa: company worker, Step 2 [W0091].

## Having a child in Cambodia

A child born in Cambodia to two Bangladeshi parents is not Cambodian: a child born there to foreign parents gets Khmer nationality only if both parents were themselves born in Cambodia and live there legally, and a child with one Khmer parent is Khmer wherever born [V0082, V0083]. The child is Bangladeshi by descent if the father or mother is a Bangladeshi citizen at the birth [V0111].

1. **Register the birth within 30 days** at the commune or sangkat office; births to foreign parents living in Cambodia are registered the same way, and the birth certificate is free [V0090, V0092, V0091].
2. **Have the Cambodian birth certificate translated into English and attested** by Cambodia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs [V0100].
3. **Register the birth with Bangladesh.** Apply online at bdris.gov.bd, then give the printout and papers to the Embassy of Bangladesh in Bangkok, which registers births in Cambodia and issues the Birth Registration Certificate [V0098, V0099, V0097]. You'll need:
   - the attested Cambodian birth certificate [V0100];
   - both parents' Bangladeshi birth registration certificates [V0102];
   - the parents' work permit and visa copies [V0101].

   It is free within 45 days of the birth and 36 baht after [V0103]. If a parent is Bangladeshi by descent only (born abroad), the child is a citizen only if the birth is registered at a Bangladesh mission [V0112].
4. **Get the child a passport** at the Bangkok embassy: 3,960 baht regular (40 to 45 days) or 7,920 baht express (21 to 28 days) [V0104]. The Thai land border is closed, so a parent in Cambodia can't reach Bangkok overland [V0042].
