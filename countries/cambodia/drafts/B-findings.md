### Background inserts

#### Status (insert for "Is it open now, and who can go?")

On 4 November 2026 the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare told BMET not to issue emigration clearance for a list of 12 countries without the Ministry's own recruitment permission; Prothom Alo and Jugantor list Cambodia among them [B0069, B0229]. In July 2026 the State Minister for Expatriates' Welfare, Nurul Hoque, said that for Cambodia, Laos and Brazil the exemption from embassy attestation of job papers ends: a proper work visa and full verification are needed before BMET clearance, but there is no blanket ban [B0215]. BMET cleared no workers for Cambodia in October 2026 or from 1 to 10 November 2026 [B0107, B0108].

> **Need to know**
> - Is Cambodia on the Ministry's 4 November 2026 list of 12 countries needing Ministry approval (Prothom Alo and Jugantor say yes; Bangla Tribune lists Croatia instead), and what does an agency now submit to get that approval?
> - Since July 2026, who attests a Cambodian employer's demand letter: the Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok's labour wing, and does it visit the employer in Phnom Penh first?

#### The channels (insert)

The only legal way to go to Cambodia for work is with BMET emigration clearance, obtained either by a licensed recruiting agency holding a BMET permission for a named Cambodian employer, or by the worker on a single visa (see Work visa: company worker, Step 6) [B0059, B0010]. Bangladesh signed a labour memorandum of understanding (MoU) with Cambodia on 4 December 2017 for five years, and Cambodia's labour ministry said in May 2022 that the two countries had renewed it [B0194, B0195, B0257]. BMET's permission letters for Cambodia require the agency to choose workers from BMET's database of registered job-seekers, under section 19(3) of the Overseas Employment and Migrants Act 2013 [B0144].

> **Need to know**
> - Does BOESL, the government's own recruiting company, send any workers to Cambodia, and has the 2017 MoU been renewed after 2022?

#### Who qualifies (insert)

BMET's 2025 permission letters for Cambodia are for male workers aged 20 to 45 on 2-year contracts [B0142, B0152, B0161]. BMET has cleared almost no women for Cambodia: none in 2022, one each in 2023, 2024 and 2025, and two in 2026 to 10 November [B0081, B0082, B0083, B0084, B0085].

#### Size (insert)

BMET clearances for Cambodia, from the OEP country-clearance report:

- 2022: 368 [B0076];
- 2023: 1,378 [B0077];
- 2024: 929 [B0078];
- 2025: 12,263, Bangladesh's 8th destination that year [B0079, B0226];
- 2026, 1 January to 10 November: 3,700 [B0080].

The rise came in late 2025: 116 in January 2025, 512 in August, 1,469 in September, 3,235 in October (the highest month ever), 2,469 in November and 2,550 in December [B0092, B0093, B0094, B0095, B0096, B0097]. The fall came in 2026: 2,223 in January, 674 in February, 607 in March, 102 in April, 52 in May, 2 in June, 17 in July, 16 in August, 7 in September and none in October [B0098, B0099, B0100, B0101, B0102, B0103, B0104, B0105, B0106, B0107]. From January 2025 to September 2026, 15,963 Bangladeshi workers went to Cambodia, more than to any other country on the US State Department's Tier 3 list (the worst rating for fighting human trafficking); Russia had 7,752 and Laos 7,532 [B0258, B0259].

The clearances are mostly building jobs on paper. In 2025 the largest job titles were Construction Worker (3,428), Construction Labour (2,849), A Builder (1,456), Building / Construction Helper (1,081), Cleaner (678), A Casual Worker (429), Production Manager (388) and Quality Controller (300), out of 35 titles [B0115, B0116, B0117, B0118, B0119, B0120, B0121, B0122, B0139]. In 2026 the largest were A Casual Worker (736), Farm Worker (580), A Builder (390), Building / Construction Helper (349) and Pipe Fitting Labour (335) [B0128, B0129, B0130, B0131, B0132]. About 400 Bangladeshis held Cambodian work permits in 2022 [B0196].

> **Need to know**
> - How many of the 12,263 workers cleared for Cambodia in 2025 actually received a Cambodian work permit (MLVT's FWCMS count of Bangladeshi permit holders for 2025 and 2026)?

#### What closed it before (insert)

Laos, the other new market of 2025, suspended recruitment, work permits and entry visas for Bangladeshi workers from 1 September to 31 December 2026 [B0230]. In June 2026, 221 Bangladeshis rescued from Cambodian scam compounds came home in four days, and BRAC said about 1,800 more were waiting in September [B0217, B0221]. A TBS investigation (July 2026) found that more than 1,000 BMET-cleared Bangladeshis were still trapped in Cambodian scam compounds, by BRAC's count [B0209].

#### How a job reaches you (insert: the paper trail)

Normally a foreign employer's demand letter must be attested by the Bangladesh embassy before an agency recruits, and the agency then gets BMET clearance [B0213]. Until July 2026 a small-batch exemption let up to five workers go without embassy attestation; syndicates split orders to use it, and 70-75% of workers were going under it, according to TBS [B0214]. Industry insiders told Prothom Alo that Ministry approval was not needed for demand letters of up to seven workers and that this was misused to send people on fake job offers [B0072]. The migration expert Asif Munir told bdnews24 (October 2026) that giving clearances wholesale for countries with no Bangladesh mission, without checking the real situation, is government negligence [B0260].

#### The cap (insert for "What you pay")

BMET's permission letters set the most the agency may take from each worker: Tk 80,770 (2025 letters for Sams Company, Puspita Overseas and SAM International), Tk 1,05,000 (Fahim Recruiting Agency, June 2025) and Tk 1,55,450 (Mongol Global Services, August 2025) [B0143, B0153, B0161, B0155, B0157]. People actually paid far more: up to Tk 5.3 lakh for promised IT and computer-operator jobs, Tk 4.8 lakh through a middleman and Fahim Recruiting Agency, and Tk 5.5 lakh to brokers [B0210, B0212, B0219]. The January 2025 letter says the employer bears all costs until arrival in Cambodia, including levy and airfare [B0143]. Two 2025 letters let the employer pay the visa and work-permit fees and then deduct them from the worker's salary [B0156, B0158].

> **Need to know**
> - How is the BMET cap for Cambodia calculated, and why did it rise from Tk 80,770 to Tk 1,55,450 within 2025?

### Work visa: company worker

### Step 1: Find a real job

1. **Register on OEP,** BMET's website for job-seekers (the Overseas Employment Platform) [B0002]. Recruiting agencies must pick Cambodia workers from this database [B0144].

   You'll need: your NID (national identity card), a mobile phone and Tk 200 [B0001, B0002].

   1. Go to [employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration](https://employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration) [B0001].
   2. Type your name in English exactly as on the NID, your mobile number, NID number and date of birth, and choose your division and district as on the NID [B0001].
   3. Type the captcha, accept the terms and click **Verify** [B0001].
   4. Print 2 copies of the registration slip and take them to your District Employment and Manpower Office (DEMO, BMET's district office) to give your fingerprints; the government fee is Tk 200 [B0002, B0003].

2. **Find out which agency has a real BMET permission for Cambodia.** BMET posts every permission for a Cambodia job order on its list at [bmet.gov.bd/pages/miscellaneous-infos](https://bmet.gov.bd/pages/miscellaneous-infos); search the page for "কম্বোডিয়া" (Cambodia) [B0234]. Each letter names the agency and its licence number (RL), the Cambodian employer, the job, the number of workers, the salary and the most the agency may take from you [B0140, B0143]. The letters seen so far, all for male construction workers in Phnom Penh at USD 420 a month on 2-year contracts:

   - **T.K. Enterprise (RL-1527),** 20 November 2023: 33 workers for Rafa Construction Co. Ltd. [B0167, B0168];
   - **Sams Company (RL-0696),** January 2025: 18 workers for North Asian Construction Co. Ltd.; cap Tk 80,770 [B0140, B0141, B0143];
   - **Puspita Overseas (RL-2648),** 24 March 2025: 144 workers for six employers including Chip Hong Insee Ltd., Sim Sia Cambodia, Sok Hang Group, Vain SR Construction and Phanimex Group; cap Tk 80,770 [B0151, B0153];
   - **Fahim Recruiting Agency (RL-2594),** 25 June 2025: 96 workers for China State Construction (Cambodia) Co. Ltd. and Asbic Construction Company Ltd.; cap Tk 1,05,000 [B0155];
   - **Mongol Global Services (RL-2527),** 31 August 2025: 144 workers for three firms including Xin Caifu (Cambodia) Co., Ltd.; cap Tk 1,55,450 [B0157, B0160];
   - **SAM International (RL-1203),** 10 September 2025: 100 workers for four firms including Royal Bengol Engineering and Constructors Co. Ltd.; cap Tk 80,770 [B0161, B0162].

   A permission is not proof the job is safe: a Chapainawabganj worker who went through Fahim Recruiting Agency paid Tk 4.8 lakh through a local middleman, had a BMET clearance card and a valid work visa, and endured 10 months of hardship in Cambodia (TBS, July 2026) [B0212]. The agency's owner told TBS that some workers leave their assigned employers for better pay or become unreachable, and that only three workers had complained against the agency at BMET [B0216].

   **Check it is real:** check the agency's licence on OEP's agency list, which shows each licence as Active, Expired or Cancelled with its validity date [B0074, B0075]. Then check that your name, the employer and the job match a permission letter on BMET's list [B0234]. Have your visa and papers checked at your DEMO or at BMET in Dhaka; BMET checks job visas free within 3 working days, but cannot check Cambodian visas online (only Saudi, Qatar, Bahrain, Singapore and UAE visas) [B0007, B0011].

   **What the letter promises you:** food, housing, transport and medical care free; an 8-hour day, 6 days a week; and return airfare for leave paid by the employer [B0142]. The agency must have someone meet every flight at the Cambodian airport, or fly with you [B0148, B0165]. If anything goes wrong, the agency must pay your food and lodging, the airfare home and compensation, under an undertaking on Tk 300 stamp paper [B0147]. Within 3 months of departure the agency must give BMET your Cambodian work permit, and after you start work it must report your E-class (business) visa extension and regular salary payment to BMET and the Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok [B0159, B0163].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Has BMET issued any permission for Cambodia since September 2025, and are any of the 2025 permissions still open for recruitment?
   > - Has any of the six agencies above been investigated or suspended over Cambodia scam-compound cases?

3. **Pay only the cap, only by cheque, bank draft or pay order, and only once your visa and flight are confirmed** [B0143, B0166]. The agency must send proof of payment to BMET's Director General [B0166]. Pay only through the agency's bank account and keep every receipt [B0006].

   **Scams:** a broker who takes Tk 4-5.5 lakh for an "IT", "computer operator", "call centre" or "restaurant" job in Cambodia is the pattern in the 2026 scam-compound cases (see Scam compounds and trafficking) [B0210, B0227, B0204]. One returnee paid Tk 5.5 lakh, left with BMET clearance, but was let into Cambodia on a visit visa that was never renewed, found no company and was sold to a scam compound [B0219]. Never go on a tourist or visit visa on a broker's promise, and never go without BMET clearance [B0005].

4. **If you need a loan,** Probashi Kallyan Bank (PKB, the government bank for migrants) lends up to Tk 3 lakh at 9% simple interest over up to 3 years, decided within 7 working days [B0055].

   You'll need [B0053, B0054]:

   - the bank's free form, 3 recent photos and an NID copy;
   - copies of your passport, visa and BMET card, and the contract if you have one;
   - 3 signed cheque leaves (MICR) of an active account in your own name, with a statement;
   - a PKB savings account opened with at least Tk 500 [B0037];
   - at least 2 guarantors: one financially solvent (3 signed cheques, statement, proof of income) and one family member or close relative.

   Fees are 0.5% processing plus VAT, 0.5% documentation plus VAT and Tk 900 per lakh risk cover [B0055]. You must be aged 18 to 60, and you and your guarantors must live in the branch's area [B0052]. A woman can borrow on the same terms with one guarantor and only the documentation fee and risk cover, and must show proof of the migration cost [B0056]. PKB helpline: 16238; from abroad +880 9677-787878 [B0055].

5. **If an agency cheats you,** complain to BMET's Director General in writing with your evidence; it is free and should be settled within 30 working days [B0016]. BMET's complaint form asks for the agency's RL number, name and address, the employer, and the problem (for example job not provided, salary not paid, stranded at the airport, company does not exist), and you get an acknowledgement receipt with a complaint ID [B0255, B0256]. If BMET does not act, the Ministry's grievance officer is the Joint Secretary (Admin and Finance), 02-41030224, 01712992086, adsadmin@probashi.gov.bd, 30 working days [B0051].

### Step 4: Passport, police clearance and certificates

1. **Get an e-passport.** Fill in the form online and submit it at the divisional or regional passport office for your present address, with your original NID (compulsory over age 20) [B0064, B0065, B0025]. The 48-page passport costs Tk 4,025 for 5 years or Tk 5,750 for 10 years at regular delivery (15 working days), Tk 6,325 / Tk 8,050 express (7 days) and Tk 8,625 / Tk 10,350 super express (2 days) [B0022, B0023]. Pay online through A-Challan, DGePay or ShurjoPay, or at any bank through A-Challan [B0024]. Since 18 February 2025 new passports are issued on an online-verified NID without police verification [B0027]. Passport helpline: 16445 (09666716445 from abroad), 24 hours [B0028].

   **If it fails:** if the passport is lost or stolen in Bangladesh, file a GD (general diary) at the nearest police station and re-apply with a copy of the old passport and the GD [B0026].

2. **Get a police clearance certificate** at [pcc.police.gov.bd](https://pcc.police.gov.bd) [B0029].

   You'll need: a passport valid at least 3 more months, with your present address one of the passport's addresses, and Tk 1,500 [B0029, B0030].

   1. Register on the site, log in and click **Apply** [B0061].
   2. Step 1: personal details; step 2: present and permanent address (the police check is done where you live now); step 3: upload scans of your documents [B0062].
   3. Pay Tk 1,500 by treasury challan, code 1-7301-0001-2681, at Bangladesh Bank or Sonali Bank, or online by card with a service charge [B0030].
   4. Track it by SMS: `PCC S <reference>` to 26969 [B0031]. Collect it with your passport or application copy; someone else can collect it with an attested authorisation letter giving their name and NID number [B0067].

   **If it fails:** PCC helpdesk, Sunday to Thursday 9 am to 5 pm: 01320001824, 01320001825, help.pcc@police.gov.bd [B0032].

3. **Attest certificates if the employer asks for them.** Education certificates are attested (apostilled) by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) online on mygov.bd at Tk 200 a page, in 5 working days, after the Education Board and Ministry of Education verify SSC and HSC certificates [B0033, B0035]. Register on mygov.bd, choose the service for your document type, fill in the form and upload the document; an SMS tells you when it is ready to download [B0034]. Experience certificates are attested free after BMET or the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare attests them and they are notarised [B0060]. For countries outside the online Apostille arrangement, you then take the Apostille and documents to MoFA's consular counter between 10 am and 1 pm for a consular sticker [B0232, B0233].

### Step 6: Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight

1. **Do the compulsory 3-day pre-departure orientation (PDO)** at one of BMET's 70 training institutes; the institute sets the fee, paid against a money receipt [B0013, B0004]. Feni TTC (Technical Training Centre) says its fee is only Tk 200 and to pay no more [B0068]. Download your certificate at [training.oep.gov.bd/pdo-certificate](https://training.oep.gov.bd/pdo-certificate) with your passport or NID number and the captcha [B0018]. Get your contract from the agency at least two days before you go and check the job, employer, workplace, length, pay, leave and airfare [B0058]. The contract must be signed with you before clearance, and you must get an attested Bangla translation [B0146].

2. **Get BMET clearance (the smart card).**

   - **Through an agency (group visa):** the agency applies to BMET's emigration branch with its application, the visa copy and the attested contract; BMET checks the papers and clears within 3 working days once the visa is issued [B0059].
   - **On your own (single visa):** apply at BMET's emigration branch in Dhaka or the Chattogram DEMO with an application, the visa copy, the contract if you have one, an attested copy of your OEP registration, an undertaking on Tk 300 non-judicial stamp paper and the original pay order for the welfare fee and insurance; it takes 3 working days [B0010]. A woman on a single visa also needs a no-objection letter from her legal guardian on Tk 150 stamp paper [B0019].

   Your costs:

   - welfare fee to the Wage Earners' Welfare Board (WEWB, the government's welfare board for migrant workers): Tk 5,500 from 1 October 2026 [B0017];
   - insurance: Tk 1,000 once, 5 years' cover, Tk 10 lakh [B0009];
   - clearance (smart card) fee: Tk 250 [B0073];
   - source tax: Tk 1,200 (skilled) or Tk 800 (unskilled) on a group visa; Tk 400 on an unattested single visa, Tk 250 on an attested one [B0073].

   **Check it is real** at [portal.wewb.gov.bd/bmet-wewbm-check](https://portal.wewb.gov.bd/bmet-wewbm-check) with your passport number or membership ID and the captcha [B0020]. The Welfare Board's desks at Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet airports check departing workers' smart cards [B0014]. BMET clearance does not prove you will get a Cambodian work permit: a Lakshmipur worker paid Tk 5.3 lakh, got clearance, received no work permit and was sold to a scam compound [B0211]. Before you fly, check that your visa is the E-class (business) visa, not a tourist visa (see Work visa: company worker, Step 5) [B0163, B0219].

   **If it fails:** BMET's Director (Emigration), +8802222222415, 01711-135901, diremi@bmet.gov.bd; next, the Additional Director General (Employment), 02-48321949, 01720105492, adgemp@bmet.gov.bd [B0012].

3. **The flight.** Once flights are confirmed, the agency must send the flight schedule and worker list to the Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok and to BMET, and report to the Ministry and BMET within 14 days of completing the recruitment [B0149, B0164]. The last group of rescued workers in June 2026 flew home on Thai Airways flight TG-339 [B0217].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which airlines and transit points do Cambodia-bound workers use in 2026 (Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur, Singapore, Guangzhou?), does any transit airport need a visa or extra papers, and what does a one-way Dhaka-Phnom Penh ticket cost?

4. **Before you fly,** keep one set of copies at home and at least 3 with you, open at least 2 bank accounts (one yours, one a family member's), and never carry a packet whose contents you don't know [B0008]. Bangladesh has no embassy in Cambodia; save the Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok's welfare hotline for Bangladeshis in Thailand and Cambodia, +66-818-70-84-43 (see The Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok) [B0197, B0175].

### If a worker dies (Bangladesh-side insert for "If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job")

**Bringing the body home.** The family applies to the Wage Earners' Welfare Board, which records the family's wishes, sends them to the embassy and follows up until the body arrives [B0247]. For a death in Cambodia, the Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok handles the case; the next of kin (spouse or children) applies on the Embassy's form [B0189]. You'll need [B0188]:

- the deceased's passport and visa copy;
- the hospital and district death certificates, with English translations;
- the receiver's NID copy and proof of relationship;
- the application by the next of kin.

**At the airport.** A free ambulance takes the body from Dhaka or Chattogram airport; Dhaka airport welfare desk +8802 8901040, 01404 413388 [B0043]. The family gets a Tk 35,000 cheque for transport and burial on the spot when the body is handed over, and loses it if not collected within 3 months [B0245, B0244, B0014]. Dhaka desk officer in charge: +8801971 430203 [B0246].

**The Tk 3 lakh grant.** The Welfare Board pays the family Tk 3 lakh if the worker had BMET clearance or Board membership [B0235]. It is paid only if the worker dies in the country the clearance was for [B0236]. If the family took the Tk 35,000 cheque at the airport, no separate application is needed; if the body was buried abroad, apply within 1 year of the death to the Board's Director General or through the district Probashi Kallyan Centre [B0237, B0238]. The money goes to the spouse, children and parents, or to full brothers and sisters if the worker was unmarried and both parents are dead [B0239]. You'll need [B0240, B0241, B0242]:

- a family-members certificate from the Union Parishad chairman, mayor or councillor, countersigned by the Upazila Nirbahi Officer;
- a release, undertaking and authorisation on Tk 400 non-judicial stamp paper, in the Board's sample format;
- a copy of the Embassy's certificate (NOC) or the death certificate;
- the bank's certificate or statement for the receiver's account.

The money is paid by bank transfer (BEFTN) into that account [B0243]. Death compensation, unpaid wages and insurance recovered abroad are sent by the embassy to the Board, which pays the heirs [B0248]. Migrant-worker insurance (Tk 1,000 premium at clearance) covers Tk 10 lakh for 5 years [B0009]. The death of a Bangladeshi in Cambodia can be registered at the Embassy in Bangkok by immediate family, with the reporting family member's passport copy, the local death certificate in English and the deceased's passport copy [B0251, B0252].

> **Need to know**
> - Has the Welfare Board paid the Tk 3 lakh grant for any Bangladeshi who died in a Cambodian scam compound, and does it require the death to be at the employer named in the clearance?

### Going home without valid papers (Bangladesh-side insert: the Travel Permit)

A Travel Permit is a one-way document the Embassy issues to a Bangladeshi without a passport who wants to go home [B0179]. For Cambodia it is issued by the Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok [B0197]. You'll need [B0180]:

- an interview in person at the Embassy;
- copies of the old passport and visa page, the air ticket, and your birth certificate or NID;
- a police report (GD) if the passport was lost;
- 2 photos and the filled-in form.

The fee is 550 Baht (ordinary) or 1,100 Baht (urgent) [B0181, B0182]. Consular applications are taken 9:30 am to 12:00 noon and delivered 3:30 to 4:30 pm, Monday to Friday [B0178]. In September-October 2026, 30 Bangladeshis arrested at Phnom Penh airport while trying to fly home were held in immigration detention and released after the Welfare Board wrote to the Embassy's First Secretary (Labour) on an application by the Anirban Survivor Voice Foundation [B0200, B0199, B0205].

> **Need to know**
> - The Embassy's travel-permit page says 550 Baht takes two working days and 1,100 Baht is same-day; its 2025 fees page says the opposite. Which is right?
> - Can a worker stuck in Cambodia get a Travel Permit without going to Bangkok (by post, through a visiting Embassy team in Phnom Penh, or through an honorary consul), and how long does it take?

### Coming home

1. **At the airport,** the Welfare Board's Probashi Kallyan desks at Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet help with customs clearance and lost luggage, take complaints from workers returning after abuse or fraud, take sick returnees to hospital, and give Tk 5,000 to women returning within a year after abuse or fraud [B0041, B0042, B0014]. Dhaka desk: +8802 8901040, 01404 413388; Chattogram 01913631797; Sylhet 01558277904 [B0044, B0015]. BRAC's Migration Programme met returnees from Cambodian scam compounds at Dhaka airport in 2026 with emergency help, counselling and money to reach home [B0206, B0218, B0223].
2. **If you need a bed,** the Wage Earners' Centre near Shahjalal airport charges Tk 100 admission and Tk 200 a night; book at wec.wewb.gov.bd [B0045]. The Probashi Lounge in the airport has free wifi, discounted food and a free phone for anyone showing BMET clearance or Board membership [B0046].
3. **Complain about the agency** at BMET (Step 1, item 5) [B0016]. Some returnees say Bangladeshis involved in trafficking in Cambodia intimidate victims who try to go home [B0224].
4. **To start a business,** PKB's rehabilitation loan for returnees goes up to Tk 50 lakh, up to Tk 3 lakh without collateral, and needs an up-to-date trade licence [B0039, B0040].

> **Need to know**
> - Does the Welfare Board or BMET compensate workers who went to Cambodia with BMET clearance and ended up in a scam compound, and has any agency been made to repay them?
