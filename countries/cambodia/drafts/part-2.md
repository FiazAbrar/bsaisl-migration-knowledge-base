## Work visa: company worker

A Cambodian employer with an approved foreign-worker quota hires you; you enter on an E-class (ordinary or "business") visa, the employer gets your work permit and employment card from MLVT (the Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training) through FWCMS (the Foreign Workers Centralized Management System, MLVT's online system), and you stay on by extending the visa as an EB (work or business) extension from the GDI (General Department of Immigration) [W0028, W0069, W0066, W0017, W0064, W0085]. On the Bangladesh side the job must come through a licensed recruiting agency holding a BMET (Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training) permission for a named Cambodian employer, and you need BMET clearance to leave [B0059, B0144, B0005]. BMET cleared no workers for Cambodia in October 2026 or from 1 to 10 November 2026 (see Status) [B0107, B0108].

### Step 1: Find a real job

The legal jobs on BMET's permission letters for Cambodia are for men aged 20 to 45, as construction workers for firms in Phnom Penh, at USD 420 a month (about Tk 52,400) on 2-year contracts [B0141, B0142, B0151, B0157, B0161, G0002]. How a job order reaches a village, and who sells it, is in Background (see How a job reaches you, and who takes a cut).

1. **Register on the OEP (Overseas Employment Platform),** BMET's website for job-seekers [B0002]. Agencies must pick workers for Cambodia from this database [B0144].

   You'll need [B0001, B0002]:

   - your NID (national identity card);
   - a mobile number;
   - Tk 200 for the government fee.

   1. Open [employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration](https://employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration) and type your name in English, your mobile and NID numbers, and your date of birth, division and district exactly as on the NID; type the captcha, accept the terms and press Verify [B0001].
   2. Print 2 copies of the registration slip and take them to your DEMO (District Employment and Manpower Office, BMET's district office) to give your fingerprints; the DEMO list is on BMET's website [B0002, B0003].

   **If it fails:** call the Probashi call centre, 16135 (free), or +8809610102030 from abroad [B0021].

2. **Know what the legal job looks like, and what it doesn't.** BMET's permission letters for Cambodia are for construction workers in Phnom Penh at USD 420 a month [B0140, B0141, B0151, B0155, B0157, B0161, B0168]. The letters promise free food, housing, transport and medical care, an 8-hour day, 6 days a week, and return airfare for leave paid by the employer [B0142, B0152]. Foreigners may not work in Cambodia as drivers, street vendors, masseurs, hairdressers or beauticians, shoe shiners, tailors, mechanics or tyre repairers, or makers of Khmer souvenirs, musical instruments or Buddhist statues [W0038]. Offers of computer-operator, data-entry or customer-service jobs at Tk 50,000 to over Tk 1 lakh a month are the offers behind the scam-compound cases (see Scam compounds and trafficking) [N0150, B0210, S0008].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does BMET ask for a TTC (Technical Training Centre) construction skill certificate for Cambodia clearances, and do Cambodian employers test workers before hiring?

3. **Find the agency's BMET permission letter.** BMET posts every permission for a Cambodia job order on [bmet.gov.bd/pages/miscellaneous-infos](https://bmet.gov.bd/pages/miscellaneous-infos); search the page for "কম্বোডিয়া" (Cambodia) [B0234]. Each letter names the agency and its RL number (the recruiting licence number every licensed agency has), the Cambodian employer, the job, the number of workers, the salary and the most the agency may take from you [B0140, B0143]. The letters, all for construction workers:

   - **T.K. Enterprise (RL-1527),** 20 November 2023: 33 workers for Rafa Construction Co. Ltd., Phnom Penh [B0167, B0168].
   - **Sams Company (RL-0696),** January 2025: 18 workers for North Asian Construction Co. Ltd.; cap Tk 80,770; OEP licence status Suspended [B0140, B0143, N0090].
   - **Puspita Overseas (RL-2648),** Fakirapool, Dhaka, 24 March 2025: 144 workers for Chip Hong Insee Ltd., Sim Sia Cambodia, Sok Hang Group, Vain SR Construction and Phanimex Group; cap Tk 80,770 [B0151, B0153, N0076].
   - **Al-Grand Sultan Overseas (RL-1582),** Savar, 20 April 2025: 96 workers for Vain SR, Sok Hang, Sim Sia and Phanimex; cap Tk 80,520 [N0081].
   - **Fahim Recruiting Agency (RL-2594),** Naya Paltan, 25 June 2025: 96 workers for China State Construction (Cambodia) Co. Ltd. and Asbic Construction Company Ltd.; cap Tk 1,05,000; OEP licence status Suspended [B0155, N0083, N0087].
   - **Mongol Global Services (RL-2527),** 31 August 2025: 144 workers for Xin Caifu (Cambodia) Co., Ltd., Maliha Jamman M.S Co., Ltd. and Golden Cresent Development Co., Ltd.; cap Tk 1,55,450; OEP licence status Suspended [B0157, B0160, N0071, N0088].
   - **SAM International (RL-1203),** 10 September 2025: 100 workers for Royal Bengol Engineering and Constructors, Xin Cai Fu (Cambodia), Abrar Pvt (Cambodia) and Yong Sheng Global Trading, 25 each; cap Tk 80,770 [B0161, N0074].

   **Check it is real:**

   1. On [oep.gov.bd/agencies](https://www.oep.gov.bd/agencies), find the RL number and check that the status says Active, not Expired or Cancelled, and that the validity date has not passed [B0074, B0075].
   2. Check that the employer and job in your offer are on the agency's permission letter [B0234, B0140].
   3. Ask the agency for the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare's approval of the demand letter (the employer's written job order): under the Ministry's directive of 4 November 2026, BMET may not clear workers for Cambodia without it (see Status) [W0096, W0097, N0106].

   A permission letter is not proof the job is safe: a Chapainawabganj worker told TBS (July 2026) that he paid Tk 4.8 lakh through a local middleman and Fahim Recruiting Agency, left with a BMET clearance card and a valid work visa, and endured 10 months of hardship in Cambodia [B0212, N0110]. Fahim's proprietor told TBS that some workers leave their assigned employers for better pay or become unreachable, and that only three workers had complained against the agency at BMET [B0216].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Has BMET issued any permission for Cambodia since September 2025, and are any 2025 permissions still open for recruitment?
   > - Can a worker see the Ministry's approval of his own job order, and where?

4. **Have the papers checked before you pay.** BMET checks job visas free within 3 working days at its DEMOs or at BMET, 89/2 Kakrail, Dhaka, but it cannot check Cambodian visas online (its online check covers only Saudi, Qatari, Bahraini, Singapore and UAE visas) [B0007, B0011, B0048]. The Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare tells people going to Cambodia to verify job offers through the Ministry, BMET or the embassy [S0115, S0116]. Bangladesh has no embassy in Cambodia; the Embassy in Bangkok covers it, and its labour wing's email is fsbangkok@probashi.gov.bd [B0169, B0197, B0173]. Check the visa yourself as in Step 5 [W0015].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Can a worker in Bangladesh check that a Cambodian employer is a registered company (Ministry of Commerce online register), and does the Embassy's labour wing check the employer before attesting a demand letter?

5. **Never go on a tourist or visit visa on a broker's promise of a job, and never without BMET clearance** [B0005, N0093]. Turning a tourist visa into an E-class visa after arrival is not an official route [W0079]. What happened to people who went this way is in Scams (see Done to workers) [B0219, N0119].

6. **Know what you should pay.** The employer bears all costs until you arrive in Cambodia, including levy and airfare [B0143, N0068]. Once your visa and flight are confirmed, the agency may take no more than the cap in its permission letter, only by cheque, bank draft or pay order, and must send BMET's Director General proof of the payment [B0143, B0166]. Pay only into the agency's bank account and keep every receipt [B0006]. Two 2025 letters let the employer pay your visa and work-permit fees and then deduct them from your salary [B0156, B0158]. The official costs on your side, each in the step that owns it:

   - OEP registration: Tk 200 (Step 1, item 1) [B0002];
   - passport: Tk 4,025 to Tk 10,350 (Step 4, item 1) [B0022];
   - police clearance, if asked: Tk 1,500 (Step 4, item 2) [B0030];
   - pre-departure orientation: set by the training centre, Tk 200 at Feni TTC (Step 6, item 1) [B0013, B0068];
   - BMET clearance: Tk 5,500 welfare fee, Tk 1,000 insurance, Tk 250 clearance fee and source tax of Tk 250 to Tk 1,200 (Step 6, item 2) [B0017, B0009, B0073].

   What people really pay, and to whom, is in Background (see What you pay, and what you earn and keep).

7. **If you need to borrow,** PKB (Probashi Kallyan Bank, the government bank for migrant workers) lends up to Tk 3 lakh at 9% simple interest, repaid monthly over up to 3 years, and decides within 7 working days [B0036, B0055].

   You'll need [B0052, B0053, B0054]:

   - to be 18 to 60, with you and your guarantors living in the branch's area;
   - a PKB savings account opened with at least Tk 500;
   - the free bank form, 3 recent photos and an NID copy;
   - copies of your passport, visa and BMET card, and the contract if you have one;
   - 3 signed machine-readable (MICR) cheque leaves of an active account in your own name, with a statement;
   - two guarantors: one financially solvent, one a family member or close relative.

   Fees are 0.5% processing plus VAT, 0.5% documentation plus VAT, and Tk 900 per lakh for risk cover [B0055]. A woman can borrow on the same terms with one guarantor, paying only the documentation fee and risk cover, and must show proof of the migration cost [B0056]. PKB helpline: 16238, or +880 9677-787878 from abroad [B0038].

8. **If an agency or broker takes your money,** complain in writing to BMET's Director General with your evidence (receipts, passport copy, messages); it is free and meant to be settled within 30 working days [B0016]. BMET's complaint form asks for the agency's RL number, name and address, the employer and the problem (for example job not provided, salary not paid, stranded at the airport, company does not exist), and you get a receipt with a complaint ID [B0255, B0256]. Hand it in at BMET, 89/2 Kakrail, Dhaka-1000 [B0048]. Then, in this order:

   1. BMET's Additional Director General (Employment): 02-48321949, 01720105492, adgemp@bmet.gov.bd [B0012].
   2. The Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare's grievance officer, the Joint Secretary (Admin and Finance): 02-41030224, 01712992086, adsadmin@probashi.gov.bd (30 working days) [B0051].
   3. The Probashi call centre, 16135 [B0021].
   4. The police: in October 2026 a case filed at Paltan Police Station under the Human Trafficking and Migrant Smuggling Prevention and Suppression Act, 2026 led the CID (Criminal Investigation Department) to arrest two men accused of sending two young men to Cambodian scam centres [S0114, S0108].

### Step 2: The job offer and contract

A legal job in Cambodia rests on an employment contract certified by MLVT; the work permit cannot be applied for without it [W0033, W0053].

1. **Get the contract before you pay, and read it.** Under BMET's permission conditions the contract is signed with you before clearance, and you get an attested Bangla translation [B0146]. Get it from the agency at least 2 days before you go and check the job, employer, workplace, length, pay, leave and airfare [B0058]. A fixed-duration contract must be in writing, give exact start and end dates and last at most 2 years, and renewals cannot take it past 4 years in total [W0044, W0045]. It should state the salary, working hours, duties and other conditions [W0047]. Many employers add a probation period, typically 3 months [W0048]. Check in particular:

   - **The job title:** the same job you were promised, and the same one that will appear on your visa; a worker promised a computer-operator job told the Daily Star (July 2026) that his visa named him a construction worker with North Asian Construction Company, and the broker said the title was temporary [W0090, W0091].
   - **The pay and conditions:** USD 420 a month with free food, housing, transport and medical care, as in the permission letters (Step 1, item 3) [B0141, B0142].
   - **Deductions:** an employer may not deduct a placement fee or the cost of your compulsory medical check from your wages [W0042, W0043]; deductions for the visa and work permit are allowed under two 2025 letters (Step 1, item 6) [B0158].

2. **Make sure the employer registers the contract with MLVT.** The employer must register a foreign worker's contract with MLVT; the contract has to be in Khmer and certified by MLVT, which takes about 15 working days [W0046, W0053]. To ask about it, call the MLVT employment-contract officers listed on FWCMS, 8:00 to 12:00 and 13:00 to 17:00: Mr Chuon Ansangwar 085 778 888 or Mr Neth Sophorn 012 282 250 [W0020, W0023].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does BMET or the Embassy in Bangkok ask to see the MLVT-certified Khmer contract before clearance, or only the demand letter and an English contract?
   > - Who pays MLVT's contract certification fee, and how much is it?

### Step 3: The employer gets your work permit

No foreigner may work in Cambodia without both a work permit and an employment card from the labour ministry (Labour Law 1997, Article 261) [W0076, L0038]. Getting them is the employer's job: they are two documents applied for together, in one application on FWCMS [W0066, W0077, W0017].

1. **The employer must have a foreign-worker quota.** An approved quota is the first condition for any work permit; the employer registers on FWCMS (fwcms.mlvt.gov.kh) and requests it there [W0028, W0017]. Foreigners may be at most 10% of a company's Cambodian workforce: 3% office staff, 6% skilled and 1% unskilled workers [W0030]. A company that cannot find enough qualified Cambodians can ask MLVT to go above 10% under Prakas 277/20 (a ministerial order) [W0031]. Quotas for 2027 are requested from 1 September to 30 November 2026 [W0025].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Do the Phnom Penh construction firms hiring Bangladeshis hold enough unskilled-worker quota (1%), or do they apply under Prakas 277/20?

2. **Give the employer your papers.** You'll need [W0068, W0034, W0035, W0037, W0036, W0103]:

   - your passport, with a valid E-class visa (Step 5);
   - three 4 cm x 6 cm photos;
   - a health certificate (Step 7, item 3);
   - a residence certificate from the Sangkat (commune) chief where you live, or a rental lease certified by the chief;
   - the date of your last Cambodian exit stamp, if you have been in Cambodia before.

   The employer adds its incorporation certificate, registered address, tax patent, quota approval, Ministry of Commerce approval and articles of incorporation [W0067].

3. **The permit is filed and paid for.** MLVT's 2026 notice says a foreigner must get the work permit through FWCMS within 90 calendar days of first entering Cambodia [W0026]. Processing takes about 15 to 20 working days once all documents are in and paid for [W0073]. The fees are set in MLVT Guideline 003-15, in force since 26 January 2015 [W0110]. The permit cost about USD 200 (about Tk 24,900) in 2021, and the FWCMS operator, E-Solution (Cambodia) Co., Ltd., adds USD 30 (about Tk 3,700) per new or renewed permit, paid through a bank [W0074, W0105, W0107, G0002]. Under two 2025 permission letters the employer pays the visa and work-permit fees and deducts them from your wage [B0156, B0158, N0066].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What do the work permit and employment card cost under Guideline 003-15 in 2026?
   > - Must the first permit be in hand before the first 30-day E-class visa runs out, as MLVT required in early 2025, or within 90 days of entry?

4. **The agency must report it.** Within 3 months of your departure the agency must submit your Cambodian work permit to BMET [B0159]. The permit lasts one year and is renewed each year between 1 January and 31 March (see Renewing) [W0032].

**What you get back:** a work permit and an employment card; carry a copy of them at all times [W0075].

**Check it is real:**

1. **Ask MLVT.** Call its foreign-worker-permit officers, Mr Sim Hong 099 555 339 or Mr Phorn Sovann 012 583 933; for the online system, 085 936 834 or 011 566 686, or email online.fwcms@gmail.com [W0019, W0022, W0024].
2. **Ask BMET** whether the agency has submitted your work permit, which it must do within 3 months (Step 6, item 2 has BMET's emigration contacts) [B0159, B0012].

> **Need to know**
> - Can a worker check that a work permit number is genuine (an FWCMS lookup, a QR code on the card, or an MLVT phone line that answers in English)?

**If it fails:** without a permit the employer can be fined up to KHR 12.6 million (riel, Cambodia's currency; about Tk 3.8 lakh) per worker, and you face up to 3 months in prison and/or deportation (see What can get you jailed or deported) [W0057, W0059, G0003]. A Lakshmipur worker who paid Tk 5.3 lakh and got BMET clearance received no work permit and was sold to a scam compound (TBS, July 2026) [B0211].

### Step 4: Passport, police clearance and certificates

1. **Get an e-passport.** Over age 20 you need your NID, and you fill in the form exactly as on it [B0025].

   1. Fill in the form online and choose the divisional or regional passport office for your present address [B0064, B0065].
   2. Pay online through A-Challan, DGePay or ShurjoPay, or at any bank through A-Challan [B0024]. A 48-page passport costs Tk 4,025, Tk 6,325 or Tk 8,625 for 5 years and Tk 5,750, Tk 8,050 or Tk 10,350 for 10 years (regular, express, super express) [B0022].
   3. Go to the office with your original NID and any technical certificate [B0066]. With an online-verified NID there is no police verification, and delivery is within 15, 7 or 2 working days of enrolment [B0027, B0023].

   The e-visa needs a passport valid for more than 6 months on the day you enter Cambodia (Step 5, item 1) [W0002].

   **If it is lost or stolen:** in Bangladesh, file a GD (general diary, a police report) at the nearest police station quickly, then re-apply with a copy of the old passport and the GD [B0026]. Passport helpline: 16445 (09666716445 from abroad), 24 hours [B0028]. In Cambodia, passports are issued by the Embassy in Bangkok (see The Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok); the land border between Cambodia and Thailand is closed, so a worker in Cambodia cannot reach Bangkok overland [B0183, V0042].

2. **Get a police clearance if the employer or agency asks for one.**

   You'll need [B0029, B0030]:

   - a passport valid at least 3 more months;
   - a present address that is one of the addresses in your passport;
   - Tk 1,500 by treasury challan, code 1-7301-0001-2681, at Bangladesh Bank or Sonali Bank, or online by card with a service charge.

   1. Register at [pcc.police.gov.bd](https://pcc.police.gov.bd/), log in and click Apply [B0061].
   2. Fill in your personal details and your present and permanent addresses; the police check is done where you live now [B0062].
   3. Upload scans of your documents [B0063].
   4. Track it by texting "PCC S <reference>" to 26969, and collect it with your passport or application copy; someone else can collect it with an attested authorisation letter giving their name and NID number [B0031, B0067].

   **If it fails:** helpdesk Sunday to Thursday, 9 am to 5 pm, 01320001824, 01320001825, help.pcc@police.gov.bd [B0032].

3. **Attest certificates if the employer asks for them.** MoFA (the Ministry of Foreign Affairs) attests (apostilles) education certificates online on mygov.bd at Tk 200 a page in 5 working days, after the Education Board and the Ministry of Education have verified SSC and HSC (secondary and higher secondary) certificates [B0033, B0035]. Register on mygov.bd, choose the service for your type of document, fill in the form and upload the document; an SMS tells you when to download the apostille [B0034]. Experience certificates are attested free once BMET or the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare has attested them and they are notarised [B0060]. For countries outside the online apostille arrangement, you then take the apostille and documents to MoFA's consular counter between 10 am and 1 pm for a consular sticker [B0232, B0233].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does FWCMS ask a Bangladeshi construction worker for any attested certificate or a police clearance, or only the passport, photos and health certificate?
   > - Does Cambodia accept MoFA's online apostille, or is the consular sticker needed?

### Step 5: The visa, and checking it is real

To work you enter on the E-class visa, which is for people who have accepted a job offer from a registered Cambodian company, not the T-class tourist visa: only the E-class visa can be extended inside Cambodia as an EB extension [V0012, W0069, W0078]. Since April 2025 the E-class e-visa is open to applicants from all countries [V0005]. (Tourist visas: see Visit visa.)

1. **Apply for the E-class e-visa online.** The e-visa is run only by the E-Visa Office of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation, at [evisa.gov.kh](https://www.evisa.gov.kh) [V0027, W0001]. It costs USD 35 (about Tk 4,400), is single entry, is valid for 3 months from issue and lets you stay 1 month [W0001, G0002].

   You'll need [W0002, W0009]:

   - a passport valid for more than 6 months on the day you enter;
   - a digital passport photo (JPEG or PNG);
   - a card to pay: Visa, MasterCard, JCB, Diners Club, UnionPay, Alipay or WeChat.

   1. Open the Application menu, type the security code and fill in your personal, passport and travel details [V0006].
   2. Review the form and pay [V0006].
   3. The approval comes within 3 business days by email, or download it from the "Check & Change" page with your reference number [W0008, W0007, V0023].

   You show the e-visa certificate to immigration on arrival [W0008]. A visa got with false information or forged documents can be refused, cancelled or revoked, and you stay responsible for every detail even when an agent applies for you [W0010, W0012]. Fees are not refunded once processing starts [W0011].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Do Cambodian employers or Bangladeshi agencies apply for the E-class e-visa for the worker, and what do agencies charge on top of USD 35?
   > - Does BMET's clearance check that the visa is E-class and not T-class?

2. **Check it is real before you pay the agency the balance or fly.**

   1. **On the portal.** On [evisa.gov.kh](https://www.evisa.gov.kh) open "Check Certificate" (Check & Change) and enter the application reference number; there is a link if you have lost it [W0015, W0016].
   2. **The visa type and passport.** The visa type must say E (Business), not T (Tourist), and the passport number must be yours; you must travel on the passport the e-visa was issued for [W0001, W0013].
   3. **The job.** The employer and job must match your contract and the permission letter (Step 2, item 1) [W0090, B0140].
   4. **Ask the e-visa office.** Cambodian embassies cannot check an e-visa's status; the E-Visa Office in Phnom Penh answers in Khmer and English on (855) 23 224 973 and (855) 99 334 973 [W0014, V0008].

   **If any check fails:** don't travel; complain as in Step 1, item 8 [B0016].

3. **Enter at a port that takes e-visas.** As of 3 October 2026 e-visas were accepted at Techo International Airport (KTI, Phnom Penh), Siem Reap Angkor and Sihanoukville airports, and at the Bavet (Vietnam border) and Tropaeng Kreal (Laos border) land crossings [V0040, V0116, V0117, V0041, V0118]. Cham Yeam (Koh Kong) and Poipet (Banteay Meanchey) are closed to e-visa entry [W0005].

4. **Fill in the e-Arrival form.** Every traveller, even with a visa, fills it in, free, at [arrival.gov.kh](https://arrival.gov.kh), the only official site, within 7 days before arrival; keep a screenshot or download of its QR code [V0045, V0046, V0049, V0047, V0048]. The GDI has disowned look-alike sites such as "Cambodia-e-arrival.online" [V0050].

   Immigration officers may refuse entry to anyone without a clear travel history and purpose, the means to support themselves and a guarantor, the GDI said in July 2025 [V0061].

**Scams:** workers promised work visas have arrived with one-month visit visas or tourist visas, some after getting BMET clearance (see Done to workers) [W0092, W0093].

### Step 6: Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight

1. **Do the compulsory 3-day pre-departure orientation (PDO)** at one of BMET's 70 training institutes; the institute sets the fee, paid against a money receipt, and Feni TTC says its fee is Tk 200 and to pay no more [B0013, B0068]. Download your certificate at [training.oep.gov.bd/pdo-certificate](https://training.oep.gov.bd/pdo-certificate) with your passport or NID number and the captcha [B0018].

2. **Get BMET clearance (the smart card).**

   - **Through an agency (group visa):** the agency applies to BMET's emigration branch with the visa copy, the attested contract, your attested registration, a Tk 300 stamp-paper undertaking that the job is certain, and the pay orders; BMET clears within 3 working days once the visa is issued [B0059].
   - **On your own (single visa):** apply at BMET's emigration branch in Dhaka or the Chattogram DEMO with an application, the visa copy, the contract if you have one, an attested copy of your OEP registration, an undertaking on Tk 300 non-judicial stamp paper and the original pay order for the welfare fee and insurance; it takes 3 working days [B0010]. A woman on a single visa also needs a no-objection letter from her legal guardian on Tk 150 stamp paper [B0019].

   Your costs:

   - welfare fee to the WEWB (Wage Earners' Welfare Board, the government's welfare board for migrant workers): Tk 5,500 from 1 October 2026 [B0017];
   - insurance: Tk 1,000 once, 5 years' cover, Tk 10 lakh [B0009];
   - clearance (smart card) fee: Tk 250 [B0073];
   - source tax: Tk 1,200 (skilled) or Tk 800 (unskilled) on a group visa; Tk 400 on an unattested single visa, Tk 250 on an attested one [B0073].

   **Check it is real** at [portal.wewb.gov.bd/bmet-wewbm-check](https://portal.wewb.gov.bd/bmet-wewbm-check) with your passport number or membership ID and the captcha [B0020]. The Welfare Board's desks at Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet airports check departing workers' smart cards [B0014]. Before you fly, check again that your visa is the E-class (business) visa, not a visit or tourist visa (Step 5): a returnee told the Dhaka Tribune (June 2026) that he left with BMET clearance but was let into Cambodia on a visit visa that was never renewed [W0069, B0219].

   **If it fails:** BMET's Director (Emigration), +8802222222415, 01711-135901, diremi@bmet.gov.bd; next, the Additional Director General (Employment), 02-48321949, 01720105492, adgemp@bmet.gov.bd [B0012].

3. **The flight.** Once flights are confirmed, the agency must send the flight schedule and worker list to the Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok and to BMET, and an agency representative must fly with you or meet every flight at the Cambodian airport [B0149, B0148, B0165]. In the scam-compound cases reported in 2026, brokers routed people through Thailand, or via China when there was no direct flight [S0063, S0030, N0132].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which airlines and transit points do Cambodia-bound workers use in 2026, does any transit airport need a visa or extra papers, and what does a one-way Dhaka-Phnom Penh ticket cost?

4. **Before you fly,** keep one set of copies at home and at least 3 with you, open at least 2 bank accounts (one yours, one a family member's), and never carry a packet whose contents you don't know [B0008]. Save the Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok's welfare hotline for Bangladeshis in Thailand and Cambodia, +66-818-70-84-43 (see The Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok) [B0175].

### Step 7: After arrival

Your first stay on the E-class visa is 30 days [W0064].

1. **Know who should meet you.** Under its permission letter the agency's representative must meet you at the airport or fly with you [B0148, B0165]. In the scam cases, Bangladeshi handlers met workers, kept them in a hotel for a few days and then drove them to a compound "for an interview" (see Scam compounds and trafficking) [S0039, S0020, N0135].

2. **Make sure you are registered at your address.** FPCS (the Foreigners Presence in Cambodia System) is the GDI's online register of where foreigners live, run through the "FPCS - GDI" app and website [W0081, W0085]. Your landlord, owner or guesthouse manager is usually the one who must register you; hotels usually do it automatically [W0082, V0058]. Ask your landlord directly whether they use FPCS and whether you are registered [W0086]. Without FPCS registration you may be unable to extend your visa, and some landlords avoid it to hide rental income from tax [V0059, W0084]. For the work permit you also need a residence certificate from the Sangkat chief (Step 3, item 2) [W0036].

3. **Get the health check.** The health certificate comes from MLVT's Labour Medical Department, or from another clinic with the certificate certified by MLVT; it takes 3 to 7 working days [W0052]. Certifying a private clinic's certificate cost USD 5 (KHR 20,400, about Tk 620) in 2016 [W0108, G0003]. Your employer may not deduct the cost of this check from your wages [W0043].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What does the MLVT medical check cost in 2026, where in Phnom Penh is the Labour Medical Department, and who pays in practice?

4. **Extend your visa as an EB extension before the 30 days run out.** The GDI extends the E-class visa for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months at a time; 6- and 12-month extensions allow multiple entries, and 1- and 3-month extensions are single entry [W0064, W0065]. You apply inside Cambodia at the immigration office or through a visa agent [V0038]. Through an agent an EB extension cost USD 50 for 1 month (about Tk 6,200), USD 80 for 3 months (about Tk 10,000), USD 160 for 6 months (about Tk 19,900) and USD 290 for 1 year (about Tk 36,100) in 2024; going to the Immigration Department directly is supposed to be cheaper, but many foreigners use agents because it is slow [W0070, W0072, G0002]. Your first 6- or 12-month EB extension can be had without a work permit; the second and every later long-term extension needs proof of a valid work permit [W0071]. An EB extension does not by itself give the right to work: you still need the work permit [V0032]. After you start work the agency must report your EB extension and regular salary payment to BMET and the Embassy in Bangkok's labour wing [B0163, N0069]. Later renewals are in Your residence permit (see Renewing) [W0080].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What does an EB extension cost at the GDI counter itself in 2026, and where is the counter in Phnom Penh?
   > - Does the employer arrange the EB extension, or must the worker go alone or through a visa agent?

5. **Collect your work permit and employment card,** and keep a copy with you at all times (Step 3) [W0075].

6. **Check you are registered with the NSSF** (National Social Security Fund, Cambodia's social insurance) [L0071]. Every employer must register its workers, Cambodian and foreign, within 3 days of hiring, using a foreign worker's passport [L0071, L0090]. The membership card is free, and you can check on the NSSF smartphone app that your employer pays contributions every month [L0166, L0171]. What it covers is in Your rights at work [L0072].

7. **Get a SIM card.** At a shop of Smart, a Cambodian mobile operator, or in the Smart app, register the SIM with a scan of your passport and a face scan; approval is usually instant [W0099, W0100, W0101]. Smart's help line: (+855) 10 200 888 or 1204 [W0102].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which mobile operator do Bangladeshi workers in Cambodia use, and what do a SIM and a monthly package cost?

8. **Open a bank account.** It lets you be paid without cash and send money home from your phone (see Sending money home) [G0035, G0043, G0077].

   - **ACLEDA Bank:** the widest network, 263 branches in all provinces and towns [G0034]. You'll need a passport valid at least 3 more months and a phone, and to be 15 or over [G0032].

     1. Go to any branch, or register in the ACLEDA mobile app [G0032].
     2. Ask for a savings account: no opening deposit, no minimum balance and no fee to open or close (closing within 3 months may be charged), in riel, US dollars, baht or euro [G0033].

     An account with no deposit, withdrawal or transfer for more than 12 months becomes dormant and is charged fees [G0081].
   - **ABA Bank (Advanced Bank of Asia):** its app-based Instant Account is for Cambodian nationals only, so a foreigner opens a standard account at an ABA branch [G0042]. You'll need to be 18 or over, your original passport with a valid visa, the application form, and proof of residence: a multiple-entry visa with at least 182 days a year in Cambodia, a rental contract or address certificate of at least 6 months, or an employment contract or certificate showing at least 6 months' work [G0087, G0088]. A current account needs USD 100 (about Tk 12,500) to open and as its minimum balance, has no monthly fee and is free to close [G0089, G0002]. ABA has an interest-free mode for Muslim customers [G0092]. Hotline: 1 800 203 203 or 098 203 203 (24 hours) [G0091, G0040].
   - **Wing Bank:** open the account in the Wing Bank App by scanning your passport; some services need a "Full KYC" (full identity check) upgrade at a Wing branch or agent shop with the same passport [G0047].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Will ACLEDA or Wing open an account for a Bangladeshi on a passport and E-class visa alone, or do they ask for the work permit?
   > - Do employers of Bangladeshi workers pay wages into a bank account or in cash, and through which bank?

9. **Keep your papers valid and on you.** Working without a permit can bring up to 3 months in prison and/or deportation (see What can get you jailed or deported) [W0059].

### Scams

#### Done to workers

- **The office job on a construction visa.** A Sirajganj man told the Daily Star (July 2026) that a broker asked Tk 7 lakh for a computer-operator job paying Tk 80,000 a month; he paid Tk 1 lakh to the broker and the rest to an agency he named as Pushpita Overseas in Fakirapool [N0126, N0127]. His visa named him a construction worker with North Asian Construction Company, and the broker said the title was temporary [N0128, N0129]. Another man told the Daily Star (October 2026) that he paid Tk 7.70 lakh for a data-entry job and that his invitation letter named construction work [N0152, N0153].
- **A visit or tourist visa instead of a work visa.** A Lakshmipur man paid Tk 5.3 lakh for a computer-operator job, got BMET clearance and found on arrival he had a one-month visit visa (Daily Star, July 2026) [N0136, W0092]. A Rajbari man told TBS (May 2026) that he paid Tk 10 lakh, was promised a work visa and was sent on a tourist visa [N0118, N0119]. In 2022 an agent told a man work visas were not available and sent him on a business visa for Tk 4 lakh (TBS, 2022) [N0165, N0166].
- **The visa and work permit that never come.** Recruiters promised a one-year visa and work permit that never came, leaving the worker without legal status (Daily Star, October 2026) [N0154, S0007].
- **The licensed agency's Facebook ad.** In June 2022 M/S Right Job, a licensed agency in Dhanmondi, advertised a Cambodia tiles-installer job on Facebook at USD 600 a month (about Tk 74,800) and agreed a Tk 3.5 lakh deal (Daily Star Bangla, January 2023) [N0175, G0002]. In January 2023 the Daily Star counted 30 Facebook groups and pages offering Cambodia jobs [N0176].
- **Unpaid construction work.** A Pirojpur man paid a village broker Tk 3.5 lakh, went as a construction worker and worked almost unpaid for 1.5 years while brokers held the workers' passports and gave them USD 50 (about Tk 6,200) a month for upkeep (Daily Star Bangla, 2023) [N0171, N0172, N0173, G0002]. The US State Department reported in 2025 that brokers fraudulently recruit Bangladeshis to Cambodian construction sites, where some are indebted and have their passports confiscated [S0174].
- **Passports handed over and workers sold.** Agents handed one group's passports to a company and took USD 3,600 (about Tk 4.5 lakh) a head (TBS, 2022) [N0167, G0002]. The airport handover and sale into scam compounds are in Scam compounds and trafficking [S0039].
- **Checks waved through.** A victim said his fingerprint did not match at the airport Probashi desk, and an official took him aside, called his agency and let him board (Daily Star Bangla, 2023) [N0178]. Since checks tightened, the CID says some recruiters use fake BMET cards, or send workers to Thailand as tourists and move them on to Cambodia [N0138].

Complain in Bangladesh as in Step 1, item 8 [B0016].

> **Need to know**
> - What does a broker in Dhaka charge for a "Cambodia construction visa" in November 2026, after the Ministry's approval rule?

#### Tried by workers

- **Working on a tourist visa, or "converting" it.** Turning a tourist visa into an E-class visa after arrival is not an official route [W0079]. A foreigner working without a work permit can be fined KHR 50.4 million (about Tk 15.4 lakh) under Joint Prakas 498 of 31 July 2023, and faces up to 3 months in prison and/or deportation [W0058, W0059, G0003].
- **False information or forged documents** in a visa application get the visa refused, cancelled or revoked [W0010].
- **Leaving the assigned employer for better pay.** Fahim Recruiting Agency's proprietor told TBS (July 2026) that some workers leave their employers for better-paid jobs and the employers will not take them back [N0117]. MLVT can revoke a work permit when the holder is hired by another employer (see Changing employer) [L0040].
- **Moving on to Thailand or Malaysia without papers.** In July 2026 seven of 11 people caught crossing from Cambodia into Thailand were Bangladeshis carrying no passport or ID, and in 2024 Bangladeshis in Cambodia had fake Thai entry stamps put in 19 men's passports for the trip to Malaysia (see Irregular routes and undocumented workers) [U0082, U0072, U0074].
