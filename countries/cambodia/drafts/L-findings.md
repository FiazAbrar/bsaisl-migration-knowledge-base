### Your rights at work

Cambodia's Labour Law (1997) covers every job done in Cambodia, whatever the nationality of the worker or the employer and wherever the contract was signed [L0174, L0175]. A Bangladeshi with a work permit has the same rights at work as a Cambodian, but the law also says no foreigner may work without a work permit and an employment card from MLVT, the Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training (see Step 3: The employer gets your work permit) [L0038]. Before you take your first job, the employer must tell you clearly what you will be paid, and must tell you again whenever the pay changes [L0177].

#### Your contract

- **Written or spoken:** a contract can be written or spoken; registering it costs nothing [L0001].
- **Fixed-term contract:** a contract for a fixed period must be in writing, or it counts as a permanent ("unlimited-duration") contract [L0003]. A fixed-term contract, including renewals, can last at most two years; beyond that it becomes permanent [L0002].
- **Probation:** at most 3 months for regular staff, 2 months for specialised workers and 1 month for unskilled workers [L0004]. Probation does not count toward seniority pay [L0058].
- **Keep a copy:** at the end of any contract you can ask for an employment certificate, and an employer who refuses must pay damages [L0009, L0019].

#### Wages and the minimum wage

- **Minimum wage:** Cambodia has a legal minimum wage only for garment, textile, footwear, travel-goods and bag factories; there is none for construction, casinos, restaurants or other work [L0052].
  - In 2026 it is USD 210 a month for regular garment workers and USD 208 for those on probation (Prakas 214/25) [L0050].
  - From 1 January 2027 it is USD 212 a month for regular workers and USD 210 on probation (Prakas 226/26 of 30 September 2026) [L0046, L0047].
  - Garment workers also get USD 7 a month for transport or housing, a USD 10 attendance bonus, USD 0.50 a day for meals on overtime (or a free meal), and a seniority bonus of USD 2 to 11 [L0051]. Piece-rate workers whose output falls short are topped up to the minimum [L0048].
- **Payday:** wages are paid twice a month: about half in the second week and the rest, with benefits, in the fourth week (Prakas 442, 2018) [L0055].
- **How:** wages must be paid to you directly, in legal currency, and the employer may not tell you how to spend them [L0020, L0021].
- **Deductions:** no one may deduct a recruitment or placement fee from your wages, whether for the employer, its agent or a labour recruiter [L0026]. Other deductions are limited to unreturned tools, advances and company-shop debts, and may never leave you without enough to live on [L0027].
- **Proof:** if you say you were not paid, the employer must prove it paid, with your signature in its payroll book (or two witnesses' signatures if you cannot write) [L0023]. Signing "all wages paid", or taking your pay without complaining, does not cancel your right to wages still owed [L0024].

#### Hours, overtime and rest

- **Hours:** at most 8 hours a day and 48 a week [L0028].
- **Rest:** at most six working days a week; the weekly rest is at least 24 hours in a row, normally Sunday [L0029].
- **Overtime:** overtime must be voluntary; the employer may not force it by threats or discipline (Prakas 112/25 of 6 May 2025) [L0059]. Total hours, with overtime, may not pass 10 a day except for disaster work [L0060].
- **Overtime pay:** 150% of normal pay in the daytime on working days; 200% at night (10 pm to 5 am) and on your weekly day off [L0061, L0045]. After night overtime the employer must give you a place to sleep or transport [L0063].
- **Check it:** the employer must file overtime on MLVT's online system (LACMS) at least three working days ahead, with the agreement of more than half the workers concerned or two-thirds of staff representatives [L0064].

#### Leave and public holidays

- **Annual leave:** 1.5 working days for each month worked (18 days a year), plus one day for every three years of service [L0030, L0031, L0067]. You can take it after one year; if the contract ends first, you are paid for the leave you have earned [L0032, L0033].
- **Public holidays:** work on a public holiday is voluntary [L0062]. The 2026 holidays include Khmer New Year (14-16 April), Pchum Ben (10-12 October) and the Water Festival (23-25 November) [L0065]. A holiday that falls on a Sunday gives no extra day off [L0066].

#### Sick leave and maternity

- **Sickness:** a doctor-certified illness suspends your contract (you cannot be dismissed for it) for up to six months [L0037]. The Labour Law itself gives no sick pay; MLVT policy is full pay for the first month, 60% for the second and third, then unpaid to the sixth [L0068].
- **NSSF sickness pay:** if a doctor signs you off for more than 7 days in a row, NSSF (the National Social Security Fund) pays 70% of your contributory wage for up to 180 days in 12 months [L0073]. You need 2 months of health contributions in a row, or 6 in the last 12 [L0074].
- **Maternity:** women get 90 days' maternity leave and cannot be dismissed during it [L0034]. With at least one year's service, the employer pays half wages, in full before the leave starts [L0035, L0069]. NSSF adds 70% of earnings for the 90 days if you have 9 months' contributions in the last 12 [L0070].

#### Seniority pay and severance

- **Permanent contract:** you get a seniority payment of 7.5 days' wages in June and 7.5 days in December (15 days a year), paid with the second wage payment of those months [L0053, L0057]. If you resign before the end of June or December, you lose that half-year's payment; nothing is due if you are dismissed for serious misconduct [L0056].
- **Fixed-term contract:** no seniority payment, but at the end you get severance of 5% of all the wages paid over the contract [L0054].

#### Social security (NSSF)

Every employer with one or more workers must register with NSSF, and all paid workers, Cambodian and foreign, are covered [L0071]. The employer must register you within 3 days of hiring, using your passport number [L0090]. The NSSF card is free [L0166].

1. **Check you are registered.** Download the NSSF app and check every month that your employer is paying contributions [L0171]. If your passport is renewed, go to an NSSF office and register the new number with your fingerprints [L0170].
2. **Use the card for treatment.** NSSF members get free care at health facilities with an NSSF agreement, marked with the NSSF logo [L0072]. If a facility asks you to pay, show the card and call 1286; for an ambulance in an emergency, call 1286 [L0085].
3. **Keep copies.** NSSF keeps original documents, so keep a copy of everything you hand in [L0087].

**NSSF office:** Building 3, Russian Federation Boulevard, Tuek Laak I, Toul Kork, Phnom Penh (inside the MLVT compound); info@nssf.gov.kh; hotline 1286 [L0088].

#### Pension

- **Cambodian pension:** NSSF's pension scheme started on 1 October 2022 and applies to foreign workers the same as Cambodians [L0167, L0168]. You and the employer each pay 2% of your contributory wage (4% in all) for the first five years, rising to 8% later [L0162, L0163]. A pension is paid from age 60 after at least 12 months of contributions; a member who does not qualify gets an old-age allowance based on contributions paid, claimed within a month of turning 60 [L0164, L0165]. Benefits are paid to those living in Cambodia, and in 2022 there was no confirmed way for a foreign worker to take the money as a lump sum on leaving [L0167, L0169].
- **Bangladesh pension:** the Probash scheme of Bangladesh's Universal Pension is open to Bangladeshis aged 18 to 50 working abroad, paid in foreign currency [L0156, L0154].
  1. Register online at upension.gov.bd in your own name; no agent is needed [L0159].
  2. Choose Tk 1,000, 2,000, 5,000, 7,500 or 10,000 a month [L0154]. Contributions sent from abroad in foreign currency earn a 2.5% bonus, added to your account [L0157].
  3. Pay by debit or credit card into the scheme's Sonali Bank account, 3, 9 or 12 months at a time if you like [L0161].
  4. After 10 years of unbroken payments, the pension goes into your bank account automatically from age 60 [L0158]. Ten years at Tk 1,000 a month gives an estimated Tk 1,530 a month; at Tk 10,000, about Tk 15,302 [L0155]. If you die, your nominee gets everything paid in, with profit [L0160].

> **Need to know**

#### Unions and help

Workers in Cambodia have the right, without distinction, to form and join unions of their choice [L0178]. Forced labour, and work to pay off a debt, are banned outright [L0036]. CENTRAL (Center for Alliance of Labor and Human Rights) gives legal aid on labour rights: #487 St. 53BT, Sangkat Boeng Tumpun, Khan Meanchey, Phnom Penh; phone +855 93 556 603 [L0135, L0136].

### What can get you jailed or deported

Most of what follows is about papers. Keep your passport, work permit, employment card and visa valid at all times (see Your residence permit) [L0038, L0176].

#### Working without a permit

- No foreigner may work without a work permit and an employment card from MLVT [L0038]. The work permit lasts one year [L0039].
- **For you:** working without a permit can bring up to three months' prison and/or deportation under the Law on Immigration [L0100]. The Minister of Interior may expel any foreigner working without a work card [L0093].
- **For the employer:** a labour inspector can fine the employer KHR 12.6 million (about USD 3,150) per foreign worker without a permit, up to KHR 63 million when five or more are involved (MLVT Guideline 517, 2023) [L0098, L0099]. Keeping a foreigner without an employment card is also an offence under the Labour Law [L0044].
- **If you lose the job:** MLVT can cancel your work permit if you are out of work for more than a month or are hired by another employer (see Changing employer or leaving) [L0040].

#### Overstaying your visa

- **The fine:** an overstay costs USD 10 a day, usually paid at the airport when you leave [L0110, L0112].
- **Arrest:** according to the Nepali Embassy in Bangkok (May 2026), Cambodia's newer rules allow arrest, a fine and prison for a foreigner who does not leave within one month [L0114]. Deportation through the courts can take more than six months [L0110].
- **Waivers:** in 2026 Cambodia waived overstay fines for groups of foreigners who registered to leave, through their embassies: 4,677 Indonesians, 602 Nepalis, and Bangladeshis whose waiver was due to end on 31 October 2026 (see Going home without valid papers) [L0111, L0114, L0123, L0126].
- **Fake notices:** a notice shared online in May 2026 claiming two years' prison and an USD 8,000 fine for foreigners from 1 June 2026 was a forgery, rejected by the Ministry of Interior on 29 May 2026 [L0113].

#### Fake papers and entering illegally

- Entering Cambodia secretly, by fraud or any other illegal way carries 3 to 6 months' prison, followed by expulsion [L0091].
- Altering, lending or using a resident card, work card or employment contract in another name, or applying with false papers, carries 5 to 15 years' prison; anyone who helps gets the same [L0092].
- Giving false documents when signing a job contract is a serious offence that lets the employer dismiss you without notice [L0016, L0015].

#### Online scams

Cambodia's Law on Combating Online Scams came into force by Royal Decree on 6 April 2026 [L0104].

- Carrying out an online scam: 2 to 5 years' prison and a fine of KHR 200 to 500 million; 5 to 10 years if done by a group or against many victims [L0105, L0106].
- Recruiting or training people for scam work: 2 to 5 years [L0107].
- Collecting or using other people's ID cards or passports for scams: 1 to 3 years [L0108].
- Running a scam centre that uses violence, detention or exploitation: 10 to 20 years [L0109].

People forced into scam work are screened by the Cambodian authorities; those identified as trafficking victims have been allowed to go home (see Scam compounds and trafficking) [L0119].

> **Need to know**
> - Does the 2026 anti-scam law protect trafficking victims who were forced to scam from prosecution, and has any Bangladeshi been charged under it?

#### Drugs

- Possessing, carrying or selling drugs such as heroin, methamphetamine or cannabis carries 2 to 5 years' prison and a fine of 4 to 10 million riels [L0101]. Larger amounts (for example 15 to 80 grams of pure methamphetamine) carry 5 to 10 years [L0102].
- Using drugs carries 1 to 6 months' prison and a fine of 100,000 to 1,000,000 riels [L0103].

#### Where you live

- The owner of any home where a foreigner stays must report it to the authorities within 24 hours [L0096].
- Since January 2026, guesthouses, apartment blocks, dormitories, factories and similar premises must ask every foreigner for papers proving the right to stay, and are fined 4 million riels (USD 1,000) for each foreigner staying illegally [L0116, L0115].

#### Detention and deportation

1. **The order:** the Minister of Interior orders expulsion; it takes effect at once and is carried out within 7 days unless a court suspends it [L0094, L0095]. Expulsion can be added to any penalty under the Law on Immigration [L0097].
2. **Appeal:** you can complain to the court within two months of the order [L0094].
3. **In practice:** on 3 September 2026 immigration police detained 32 Bangladeshis at Phnom Penh airport as they tried to fly home; 30 were held in an immigration detention centre for more than six weeks and reached Dhaka on 20 October 2026 [L0118, L0124]. They paid for their own tickets [L0120].
4. **Who helps:** Bangladesh has no embassy in Cambodia; the Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok handles Cambodia (see Contacts) [L0117]. In Bangladesh, the Wage Earners' Welfare Board takes applications to bring home a worker jailed abroad, and for legal aid abroad [L0144].
5. **At Dhaka airport:** returnees from scam compounds have been checked by the CID (Criminal Investigation Department), including their phones [L0125].

### If you aren't paid

Your employer must pay you at least twice a month (see Your rights at work) [L0055]. A wage claim stays valid for three years from the day the wage was due [L0025]. Refusing to pay all or part of your wages, or paying late again and again, is a serious offence by the employer [L0014]. If unpaid wages push you to quit, you can still claim your notice pay, indemnity and damages [L0017].

If you aren't paid, in this order:

1. **Keep proof.** Photograph your contract, work permit, payslips and any messages about pay. Don't sign a paper saying you have been paid in full: the employer has to prove each payment with your signature in its payroll book [L0023, L0024].
2. **Ask the employer in writing.** Send a dated message (WhatsApp or letter) and keep a copy:
   "I, [name], passport [number], have worked for [company] as [job] since [date]. My wages for [months] (USD [amount]) have not been paid. Please pay them by [date]."
3. **Complain to the labour inspectorate.** File a complaint at MLVT's Labour Dispute Department in Phnom Penh, or at the Department of Labour of your province (for example in Sihanoukville) [L0130, L0041]. The rules are in Prakas 073/25 of 4 March 2025 [L0129]. Call MLVT's hotline 1297 or 023 884 375 first; MLVT's website also has an anonymous complaint box [L0127, L0128].
   - The labour inspector must call you and the employer to a joint conciliation meeting within three weeks of the complaint [L0131].
   - An agreement signed before the inspector is legally binding; you can bring someone to help or represent you [L0042].
4. **Ask for a second try, or the Arbitration Council.** If conciliation fails, you and the employer can jointly ask MLVT for one more conciliation within two working days [L0132]. During conciliation either side can ask for the case to go to the Arbitration Council, an independent national body set up under the Labour Law [L0133, L0134]. Conciliation and arbitration are free [L0179].
5. **Go to court within two months.** If conciliation fails, you have two months to file in court, or you lose the claim [L0043].
6. **Get help.** For legal aid, contact CENTRAL (see Your rights at work) [L0135, L0136]. Tell the Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok's labour wing (see Contacts) [L0117]. If an agent or "boss" says you get no salary until you meet a target or find customers, you are in a scam operation (see Scam compounds and trafficking) [L0172].

### Changing employer or leaving

Your work permit belongs to your current job: MLVT can cancel it if you are hired by another employer or stay out of work for more than a month [L0040]. A new employer must get you a new permit (see Step 3: The employer gets your work permit).

- **Ending a fixed-term contract early:** only by written agreement of both sides in front of a labour inspector, or for serious misconduct or force majeure [L0005]. If you walk out without good reason, the employer can claim damages [L0007]. If the employer ends it early without such a reason, it owes you at least the wages up to the contract's end date [L0006].
- **Leaving a permanent contract:** give notice: 7 days with under 6 months' service, 15 days for 6 months to 2 years, 1 month for 2 to 5 years, 2 months for 5 to 10 years, 3 months above 10 [L0010]. During notice you get two paid days off a week to look for work [L0013].
- **If the employer dismisses you:** it needs a valid reason linked to your ability, behaviour or the business's needs [L0012]. Without proper notice it must pay your wages and benefits for the notice period [L0011]. If the business closes or goes bankrupt, it still owes all this [L0018].
- **If the employer cheats or abuses you:** fraud to get you to sign, unpaid or repeatedly late wages, abuse, threats or violence are serious offences; you can leave without notice and still claim indemnity and damages [L0014, L0015, L0017].
- **Seniority pay:** resigning before the end of June or December loses that half-year's payment [L0056].
- **Social security:** you stay covered for work injury only if the new employer registers you with NSSF (see Your rights at work) [L0089].

### Leaving at the end of the job

1. **Get the notice.** On a fixed-term contract of over 6 months, the employer must tell you 10 days ahead that it won't renew (15 days if the contract is over a year); if it doesn't, the contract is extended [L0008].
2. **Collect everything owed within 48 hours.** When the contract ends, wages and any indemnity must be paid within 48 hours [L0022]. Check you get:
   - unpaid wages and overtime [L0022];
   - pay for annual leave not taken [L0033];
   - on a fixed-term contract, severance of 5% of all wages paid [L0054];
   - on a permanent contract, any seniority payment due for the half-year [L0053].
3. **Ask for your employment certificate.** It shows your job and dates, and helps with the next job or with BMET [L0009].
4. **Leave before your visa ends.** Your work permit lapses after a month without work, and every day of overstay costs USD 10 (see What can get you jailed or deported) [L0040, L0110].

### Going home on leave

You earn 18 days' paid annual leave a year and can take it after one year of service [L0067, L0032]. Before you fly, make sure your visa and work permit are valid for your return (see Your residence permit) [L0038].

## If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job

### If you are injured at work

A work injury is covered by NSSF if your employer has registered you (see Your rights at work) [L0071, L0090].

1. **Get treatment.** Go to a health facility with the NSSF logo and show your NSSF card; treatment is free [L0072]. In an emergency call 1286 for an ambulance [L0085].
2. **Make sure the employer reports it.** The employer must tell NSSF at once on 1286 and send the work-injury form within 48 hours, directly or to benefit@nssf.gov.kh [L0081, L0082]. Ask for a copy.
3. **Claim your pay while off work.** The employer pays the first day; from the second day NSSF pays 70% of your contributory wage until you recover [L0075]. A person caring for you gets 50% of your daily allowance [L0076].
4. **Claim for lasting injury.** Permanent disability under 20% is paid as a lump sum; 20% or more as a monthly pension [L0077].
5. **Keep to the deadlines.** Claims for daily allowance and funeral grants must be made within 1 year; for permanent disability, within 5 years [L0083]. Attach your passport and NSSF card [L0084]. NSSF phones you within 20 to 30 working days to collect a cheque [L0086].

> **Need to know**
> - Are injured Bangladeshi construction workers in practice registered with NSSF, or do employers pay hospital bills directly and send them home?

### If a worker dies

**In Cambodia.** If a work injury kills a worker, NSSF pays to transport the body and pays 4 million riels to whoever holds the funeral [L0078, L0079]. Survivors claiming NSSF benefits need a family record book, residence certificate or marriage certificate [L0080]. The bodies of three Bangladeshis were brought home from Cambodia on 8 October 2026 [L0121].

**In Bangladesh.** The family applies to the Wage Earners' Welfare Board (WEWB) to bring the body home, receives Tk 35,000 at the airport for transport and burial, and can claim a Tk 3 lakh grant if the worker had BMET clearance for Cambodia [L0150, L0146, L0137, L0138]. If compensation may be due in Cambodia, WEWB asks the family for a power of attorney so the embassy can run the case; WEWB's legal cell helps prepare it [L0151, L0152]. Compensation, unpaid wages and insurance the embassy recovers are sent to WEWB and paid to the heirs [L0153]. WEWB call centre: 16135 from Bangladesh (free), +880 9610 102030 from abroad [L0145].

> **Need to know**
> - Who pays to fly a Bangladeshi's body from Phnom Penh to Dhaka when the employer will not, and how long does it take through the Embassy in Bangkok?
> - Has NSSF paid a work-injury death benefit to the family of any Bangladeshi in Cambodia?

### If you lose the job

- MLVT can cancel your work permit after a month without work [L0040]. Look for a new employer quickly (see Changing employer or leaving), or leave before your visa ends (see Leaving at the end of the job).
- If you were dismissed without proper notice, claim notice pay; if the business closed, it still owes you (see Changing employer or leaving) [L0011, L0018].
- If you were not paid, follow If you aren't paid.
