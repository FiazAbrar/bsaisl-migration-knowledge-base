### Your rights at work

Cambodia's Labour Law (1997) covers every job done in Cambodia, whatever the nationality of the worker or the employer and wherever the contract was signed [L0174, L0175]. A foreigner may work only with a work permit and an employment card from MLVT (the Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training), after entering legally, with a valid passport and a valid residence permit (Work visa: company worker, Step 3) [L0038, L0176]. Forced labour, and work to pay off a debt, are banned [L0036]. For any broken right, use the routes under If you aren't paid [L0130].

#### Your contract

- **Written or spoken.** A contract can be either; registering it costs nothing [L0001]. A foreign worker's contract must be registered with MLVT, in Khmer, and certified by it (Work visa: company worker, Step 2) [W0046, W0053].
- **Fixed-term.** A contract for a set period must be in writing with exact start and end dates, or it counts as permanent ("unlimited-duration"); one contract runs at most 2 years [L0003, W0044, L0002]. BMET's (the Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training's) permission letters for Cambodian jobs set 2-year contracts [B0152, N0064].
- **Your Bangla copy.** Under BMET's permission conditions the contract is signed with each worker before clearance, and each worker gets an attested Bangla translation [B0146].
- **Probation.** At most 3 months for regular staff, 2 for specialised workers and 1 for unskilled workers; it doesn't count toward seniority pay [L0004, L0058].
- **Pay terms.** The employer must tell you clearly what you will be paid before you start, and again whenever it changes [L0177].
- **Employment certificate.** At the end of any contract you can ask for one, and an employer who refuses must pay damages [L0009, L0019].

#### Wages and the minimum wage

- **Minimum wage.** There is a legal minimum only in garment, textile, footwear, travel-goods and bag factories; construction and every other job have none [L0052].
  - In 2026: USD 210 a month (about Tk 26,200) for regular workers and USD 208 (about Tk 25,900) on probation (Prakas 214/25; a prakas is a ministerial order) [L0050, G0002].
  - From 1 January 2027: USD 212 (about Tk 26,400) for regular workers and USD 210 on probation (Prakas 226/26) [L0046, L0047, G0002].
  - On top, garment workers get USD 7 (about Tk 870) a month for transport or housing, a USD 10 (about Tk 1,250) attendance bonus, USD 0.50 (about Tk 60) a day for meals on overtime or a free meal, and a seniority bonus of USD 2 to 11 (about Tk 250 to 1,370) [L0051, G0002]. Piece-rate workers who fall short are topped up to the minimum [L0048].
- **Your contract wage.** BMET's permission letters for Cambodian construction jobs set USD 420 a month (about Tk 52,400) (see What the job pays) [B0141, N0062, G0002].
- **Payday.** Twice a month: about half in the second week, the rest with benefits in the fourth (Prakas 442, 2018) [L0055].
- **How.** Directly to you, in legal currency, and the employer may not tell you how to spend it [L0020, L0021].
- **Proof.** In a dispute the employer must prove each payment with your signature in its payroll book, or two witnesses' if you can't write [L0023]. Signing "all wages paid", or taking your pay without protest, doesn't cancel your claim to wages still owed [L0024].

#### Tax and deductions from your pay

**Salary tax.** Cambodia has no income tax as such; employees pay a monthly salary tax that the employer deducts and pays to the tax authorities within 20 days after the month, and you file no return [G0051, G0056]. The rate depends on whether you are a tax resident: someone whose home is in Cambodia, or who is there more than 182 days in 12 months [G0055].

- **Residents (since 1 January 2023):** 0% on monthly salary up to KHR 1,500,000 (riel, Cambodia's currency; about USD 367 or Tk 45,800); 5% up to KHR 2,000,000 (about Tk 61,000); 10% up to KHR 8,500,000 (about Tk 2.59 lakh); 15% up to KHR 12,500,000 (about Tk 3.81 lakh); 20% above [G0052, G0083, G0001, G0003]. Each dependent child or spouse cuts taxable salary by KHR 150,000 (about Tk 4,600) a month [G0057, G0003].
- **Non-residents:** a flat 20% on all salary, as a final tax [G0053].
- **Benefits in kind** (such as housing the employer provides): 20% of their value [G0054].

**What may be deducted.** No one may take a recruitment or placement fee out of your wages, for the employer, its agent or a labour recruiter, nor the cost of your medical check [L0026, W0042, W0043]. Other deductions are limited to unreturned tools, advances and company-shop debts, and may never leave you without enough to live on [L0027]. BMET's permission letters for Cambodia let the employer pay your work permit and visa fees up front and take them back from your wages [B0158, N0066, B0156]. Your pension contribution to NSSF (the National Social Security Fund), 2% of your contributory wage, also comes out of your pay (Pension) [L0162].

#### Hours, overtime and rest

- **Hours.** At most 8 a day and 48 a week; BMET's permission letters set 8 hours a day, Monday to Saturday [L0028, B0152, N0075].
- **Rest.** At most six working days a week; the weekly rest is at least 24 hours in a row, normally Sunday [L0029].
- **Overtime.** Voluntary: the employer may not force it by threats or discipline (Prakas 112/25) [L0059]. With overtime, hours may not pass 10 a day except for disaster work [L0060].
- **Overtime pay.** 150% of normal pay in the daytime on working days; 200% at night (10 pm to 5 am) and on your weekly day off [L0061, L0045]. After night overtime the employer must give you a place to sleep or transport [L0063].
- **Check it.** The employer must file overtime at least three working days ahead on LACMS, MLVT's online system, with the agreement of over half the workers concerned or two-thirds of staff representatives [L0064].

#### Leave and public holidays

- **Annual leave.** 1.5 working days for each month worked, 18 a year, plus a day for every three years of service [L0030, L0067, L0031]. You can take it after one year; if the contract ends first, you are paid for the leave you earned [L0032, L0033].
- **Public holidays.** Work on a paid public holiday is voluntary [L0062]. The 2026 holidays include Khmer New Year (14-16 April), Pchum Ben (10-12 October) and the Water Festival (23-25 November) [L0065]. A holiday on a Sunday gives no extra day off [L0066].

#### Sick leave and maternity

- **Sickness.** A doctor-certified illness suspends your contract, so you can't be dismissed for it, for up to six months [L0037]. The Labour Law gives no sick pay; MLVT policy is full pay in the first month, 60% in the second and third, then unpaid to the sixth [L0068].
- **NSSF sickness pay.** If a doctor signs you off for more than 7 days in a row, NSSF pays 70% of your contributory wage for up to 180 days in 12 months, once you have 2 months of health contributions in a row or 6 in the last 12 [L0073, L0074].
- **Maternity.** 90 days' leave, with no dismissal during it [L0034]. With a year's service the employer pays half wages, in full before the leave starts; NSSF pays 70% for the 90 days if you have 9 months' contributions in the last 12 [L0035, L0069, L0070].

#### Seniority pay and severance

- **Permanent contract.** A seniority payment of 7.5 days' wages in June and 7.5 in December, with the second wage payment of those months [L0053, L0057]. Resign before the end of June or December and you lose that half-year's payment; nothing is due if you are dismissed for serious misconduct [L0056].
- **Fixed-term contract.** No seniority payment, but at the end, severance of 5% of all wages paid over the contract [L0054].

#### Social security (NSSF)

NSSF covers every paid worker, Cambodian or foreign, of any employer with one or more workers [L0071]. In 2022 about 400 Bangladeshis held Cambodian work permits with NSSF health and work-injury cover [B0196].

1. **Make sure the employer registers you** within 3 days of hiring, with your passport number [L0090]. The NSSF card is free [L0166].
   **Check it is real:** download the NSSF app and check each month that your employer is paying your contributions [L0171].
2. **Use the card for treatment.** Care is free at health facilities with an NSSF agreement, marked with the NSSF logo [L0072]. If a facility asks you to pay, show the card and call 1286, which is also the number for an ambulance [L0085]. Injured at work: see If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job [L0075].
3. **Re-register a new passport.** Go to an NSSF office and register the new number with your fingerprints [L0170].
4. **Keep copies.** NSSF keeps the original documents you hand in [L0087].

**NSSF office:** Building 3, Russian Federation Boulevard, Tuek Laak I, Toul Kork, Phnom Penh (inside the MLVT compound); info@nssf.gov.kh; hotline 1286 [L0088].

#### Pension

- **Cambodia's NSSF pension.** Started on 1 October 2022, and covers foreign workers the same as Cambodians [L0167, L0168]. You and the employer each pay 2% of your contributory wage for the first five years, rising to 8% in all later [L0162, L0163]. It pays from age 60 after at least 12 months of contributions, to people living in Cambodia; a member who doesn't qualify gets an old-age allowance based on what was paid, claimed within a month of turning 60 [L0164, L0167, L0165]. In 2022 there was no confirmed way for a foreign worker to take the money as a lump sum on leaving [L0169].
- **Bangladesh's Probash pension,** part of the Universal Pension, for Bangladeshis aged 18 to 50 working abroad [L0156, L0154]:
  1. **Register online in your own name** at upension.gov.bd; no third person is involved [L0159].
  2. **Pay Tk 1,000, 2,000, 5,000, 7,500 or 10,000 a month** by debit or credit card into the scheme's Sonali Bank account, 3, 9 or 12 months at a time; 2.5% is added when you pay from abroad in foreign currency [L0154, L0161, L0157].

  What you get back: after 10 years of unbroken payments, a pension into your bank account from age 60, estimated at Tk 1,530 a month on Tk 1,000 contributions and Tk 15,302 on Tk 10,000; if you die, all you paid plus profit goes to your nominee [L0158, L0155, L0160].

### Your residence permit

Your right to stay is the EB (work or business) extension of your E-class (ordinary, or "business") visa, given by the GDI (General Department of Immigration, part of the Ministry of Interior); your right to work is the work permit and employment card from MLVT [W0064, W0078, V0032, W0077]. An EB extension alone doesn't let you work [V0032]. Your address must also be on FPCS (the Foreigners Presence in Cambodia System, the GDI's register of where foreigners live) (Work visa: company worker, Step 7) [W0081, W0085].

#### How long you can stay

- **First entry on the E-class visa:** 30 days [W0064].
- **EB extension:** 1, 3, 6 or 12 months at a time; 6- and 12-month extensions allow you to leave and come back, 1- and 3-month ones are single entry [W0064, V0030, W0065, V0036].
- **Work permit:** one year, and it can be extended only within the validity of your stay [L0039, W0032].
- **Contract:** 2 years under BMET's permission letters [B0152, N0064].

> **Need to know**
> - Is there a limit on how many years a worker can keep renewing EB extensions and work permits?

#### Renewing

1. **Extend the EB before it runs out.** It is done inside Cambodia at the immigration office or through a visa agent (prices: Work visa: company worker, Step 7) [V0038, W0070]. The second and every later 6- or 12-month EB needs proof of a valid work permit [W0071]. In 2026 extensions increasingly depend on supporting documents and accommodation records, and a worker missing from FPCS may be refused [W0080, W0083, V0059].
2. **Renew the work permit every year, between 1 January and 31 March** [W0032]. The employer files it on FWCMS (the Foreign Workers Centralized Management System, MLVT's online system) (Work visa: company worker, Step 3) [W0066, W0105]. To renew you need an EB extension with at least 6 months left [W0055].
3. **Keep your passport valid.** Without a valid passport you may not work [L0176]. The Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok, which covers Cambodia, issues a regular passport for 3,960 baht (Thai currency) in 40 to 45 days, or express for 7,920 baht in 21 to 28 days [L0117, B0183]. The Thai land border is closed, so a worker in Cambodia can't reach the Embassy overland (see The Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok) [V0042, U0136]. After a new passport, re-register it with NSSF (Social security (NSSF)) [L0170].

**Check it is real:** under BMET's permission conditions the agency must send your work permit to BMET within 3 months of departure, and report your EB extension and regular salary to BMET and the Embassy's labour wing in Bangkok [B0159, B0163]. The labour wing's email is fsbangkok@probashi.gov.bd [B0173].

**If it fails:** without a valid extension you are an overstayer, and without a permit you work illegally (What can get you jailed or deported) [U0044, L0100].

#### If you lose your job

**Your permit can go with the job.** MLVT can cancel your work permit once you have been out of work for more than a month, or are hired by another employer [L0040]. Without a valid permit you can't get a second long EB extension (Renewing) [W0071].

**If you are dismissed:**

- The employer needs a valid reason linked to your ability, behaviour or the business's needs [L0012]. Serious misconduct by you, such as giving false documents when hired, lets the employer dismiss you without notice [L0016, L0015].
- Without proper notice the employer owes your wages and benefits for the notice period (Changing employer) [L0011]. During notice you get two paid days off a week to look for work [L0013].
- If the employer ends a fixed-term contract early without serious misconduct or an act of God, it owes you at least the wages up to the contract's end date [L0006].
- Closure or bankruptcy doesn't cancel what the employer owes [L0018].

#### Permanent residence and citizenship

- **Naturalisation:** needs seven years' continuous residence after receiving a residence card, and may be refused at will [V0085, V0086]. An amended Nationality Law in force from 1 December 2025 tightens the residence needed, and offers citizenship to investors of at least USD 1 million (about Tk 12.5 crore) or donors of USD 3 million (about Tk 37.4 crore) to the national budget [W0089, W0087, W0088, G0002].
- **Marriage to a Cambodian:** gives no automatic nationality (Family) [V0039].

### What can get you jailed or deported

#### Working without a permit

- **You:** up to 3 months' prison and/or deportation under the Law on Immigration; the Minister of Interior may expel any foreigner working without a work card [L0100, W0059, L0093].
- **The employer:** a labour inspector can fine it KHR 12.6 million (about Tk 3.84 lakh) per foreign worker without a permit, up to KHR 63 million (about Tk 19.2 lakh) where five or more are involved (MLVT Guideline 517, 2023) [L0098, L0099, G0003].

#### Overstaying your visa

- **The fine:** USD 10 (about Tk 1,250) a day, settled at the airport when you leave; it can add up to thousands [U0044, L0112, G0002]. Breaking the registration and residence rules of the Law on Immigration is fined KHR 50,000 to 1,000,000 (about Tk 1,500 to 30,500), and up to KHR 2,000,000 (about Tk 61,000) for a repeat [U0101, G0003].
- **Arrest:** Cambodia's new provisions allow foreigners who fail to leave within one month to be arrested, fined and jailed [L0114, U0133]. Deportation through the normal procedure can take more than six months [L0110].
- **The waiver:** in 2026 Cambodia let overstayers who registered leave without the fine; for Bangladeshis that waiver was due to end on 31 October 2026 (Going home without valid papers) [U0005, L0126, L0123].

#### False papers and illegal entry

- **Entering secretly or by fraud:** 3 to 6 months' prison, then expulsion; anyone who helps gets the same [L0091, U0095].
- **Faking, altering, lending or using in another name** a resident card, work card or employment contract, or applying with false papers: 5 to 15 years' prison [L0092].
- **Holding or using a Khmer identity card or Cambodian passport:** 5 to 10 years' prison [V0087].

#### Online scams

Cambodia's Law on Combating Online Scams was promulgated by Royal Decree on 6 April 2026 [L0104]:

- carrying out an online scam: 2 to 5 years' prison and KHR 200 to 500 million (about Tk 61 lakh to 1.53 crore); 5 to 10 years and KHR 500 million to 1 billion (about Tk 1.53 to 3.05 crore) when done by a group or against many victims [L0105, L0106, G0003];
- recruiting or training people for scam work: 2 to 5 years [L0107];
- collecting or using other people's identity cards or passports for scams: 1 to 3 years and KHR 100 to 300 million (about Tk 30.5 to 91.5 lakh) [L0108, G0003];
- running a scam centre with violence, detention or exploitation: 10 to 20 years; 15 years to life if someone dies [L0109].

Cambodian officers interview Bangladeshis applying to go home to screen for trafficking victims, and those found to be victims have been let go home (see Scam compounds and trafficking) [L0119, U0024, U0025].

#### Drugs

- Keeping, carrying or trafficking drugs such as heroin, methamphetamine or cannabis: 2 to 5 years' prison and KHR 4 to 10 million (about Tk 1.22 to 3.05 lakh); larger amounts, for example 15 to 80 grams of methamphetamine, 5 to 10 years [L0101, L0102, G0003].
- Using drugs: 1 to 6 months' prison and KHR 100,000 to 1,000,000 (about Tk 3,050 to 30,500) [L0103, G0003].

#### Where you live

- Whoever houses a foreigner must report it to the authorities within 24 hours, and you must report a change of address to the local police within 24 hours [L0096, V0068].
- Since January 2026, guesthouses, apartment blocks, dormitories, factories and similar places must see papers proving your right to stay before letting you stay, and are fined KHR 4 million (about Tk 1.22 lakh) for each foreigner staying illegally [L0116, L0115, G0003].

#### Detention and deportation

1. **The order.** The Minister of Interior orders expulsion; it takes effect at once and is carried out within 7 days unless a court suspends it, and it can be added to any penalty under the Law on Immigration [L0094, L0095, L0097].
2. **Appeal** to a court within two months of the order [L0094].
3. **Detention.** Cambodia runs immigration detention centres in Phnom Penh and Sihanoukville, and in 2026 moved foreigners suspected of scam work, Bangladeshis among them, to the Bati Pre-Deportation Centre in Takeo province [U0049, U0052, U0053, U0054]. How long you stay there depends on your embassy issuing travel papers if you have no passport [U0055]. Detainees have reported officers demanding bribes for phones, beds, food deliveries and release (2023) [U0050].
4. **The ban.** Deportees for illegal entry and work are blacklisted for at least three to five years, and those who refused the 2026 voluntary-departure window and were detained are barred for life once deported [U0043, U0040].
5. **Who helps.** Bangladesh has no embassy in Cambodia; the Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok handles it (The Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok) [L0117]. In Bangladesh the family can apply to the Wage Earners' Welfare Board (WEWB) to bring home a worker jailed abroad, and for legal aid abroad, through the DC office's expatriate welfare branch, the DEMO (District Employment and Manpower Office) or the Board directly [L0144, B0249].

**In practice:** on 3 September 2026 immigration police detained 32 Bangladeshis at Phnom Penh airport as they tried to fly home under the fine waiver; held without phones, 30 reached Dhaka on 20 October 2026 after more than six weeks, on tickets they paid for themselves [L0118, U0020, U0008, L0124, L0120]. In December 2025 Bangladeshi construction workers fleeing the Thai border fighting were detained though most said they had valid visas and work permits, and were asked for USD 800 (about Tk 1 lakh) each to be sent home [N0185, N0186, N0187, G0002].

### If you aren't paid

**Deadlines:** a wage claim lapses three years after the wage was due; once conciliation fails you have two months to go to court [L0025, L0043].

**Your position:** refusing to pay all or part of your wages, or paying late again and again, is a serious offence by the employer; you can then leave without notice and still claim notice pay, indemnity and damages [L0014, L0015, L0017]. The employer must prove every payment with your signature in its payroll book, so don't sign a paper saying you have been paid in full [L0023, L0024].

**Act in this order:**

1. **Keep proof:** your contract and its attested Bangla copy, your work permit, payslips and messages about pay [L0023, B0146].
2. **Complain to MLVT.** File at MLVT's Labour Dispute Department in Phnom Penh or at the Department of Labour of your province, under Prakas 073/25 [L0130, L0129]. MLVT's compound is on Russian Federation Boulevard, Toul Kork, Phnom Penh, where the NSSF office also is [L0088]. Call MLVT first on 1297 or 023 884 375; its website, mlvt.gov.kh, also has an anonymous complaint box [L0127, L0128].
   - The labour inspector must call you and the employer to a joint conciliation meeting within three weeks [L0131].
   - An agreement signed before the inspector is legally binding, and you may bring someone to help or represent you [L0042]. Conciliation and arbitration under the Labour Law are free [L0179].
3. **Ask for a second conciliation, or the Arbitration Council.** If conciliation fails, you and the employer can jointly ask MLVT for one more within two working days [L0132]. During conciliation either side can ask for the case to go to the Arbitration Council, an independent body set up under the Labour Law [L0133, L0134].
4. **Go to court** within two months of failed conciliation [L0043].
5. **Get legal aid** from CENTRAL (Unions and help) [L0135, L0136].
6. **Tell the Bangladesh side.**
   1. The Embassy's labour wing in Bangkok: fsbangkok@probashi.gov.bd, welfare hotline +66-818-70-84-43 (The Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok) [B0173, B0175]. The agency must report your regular salary to it and to BMET [B0163].
   2. BMET, against the agency: a written complaint with evidence to BMET's Director General, free, answered in 30 working days; BMET's complaint form lists non-payment of salary and non-existence of the company, and you get a receipt with a complaint ID [B0016, B0255, B0256].
   3. The Probashi call centre: 16135 (free in Bangladesh) or +8809610102030 from abroad [B0021].
   4. Your family can ask WEWB for legal aid abroad (Detention and deportation) [B0249].

If an agent or boss says there is no salary until you meet a target or find customers, you are in a scam operation (see Scam compounds and trafficking) [L0172].

**In practice:** six Bangladeshis who said they had worked about five months as labourers without pay crawled under the Thai border fence from Poipet in May 2026, trying to reach the Embassy in Bangkok, and were handed to Thai police for prosecution [U0087, U0088, U0089].

### Changing employer or leaving

#### Changing employer

**The permit doesn't move with you.** MLVT can cancel your work permit when another employer hires you, and a new employer needs an approved foreign-worker quota and a new permit for you (Work visa: company worker, Step 3) [L0040, W0028]. A recruiting agency owner said in July 2026 that some workers leave for better-paid jobs and their first employers then refuse to take them back [U0067].

**Leaving a permanent contract:** give notice of 7 days with under 6 months' service, 15 days for 6 months to 2 years, 1 month for 2 to 5 years, 2 months for 5 to 10 years, 3 months above 10 [L0010]. Resigning before the end of June or December loses that half-year's seniority payment [L0056].

**Leaving a fixed-term contract early:** only by written agreement of both sides in front of a labour inspector, or for serious misconduct or an act of God [L0005]. Walk out without good reason and the employer can claim damages [L0007].

**If the employer cheats or abuses you:** fraud to get you to sign, unpaid or repeatedly late wages, abuse, threats or violence let you leave without notice and still claim indemnity and damages [L0014, L0015, L0017].

**Social security:** you stay covered for work injury only if the new employer registers you with NSSF [L0089].

#### Going home on leave

You earn 18 days' paid leave a year and can take it after one year [L0067, L0032]. A 6- or 12-month EB extension allows multiple entries; 1- and 3-month extensions are single entry [W0065, V0036].

#### Leaving at the end of the job

1. **Watch for the notice.** On a fixed-term contract over 6 months the employer must tell you 10 days ahead that it won't renew (15 days if over a year), or the contract is extended [L0008].
2. **Collect what you are owed within 48 hours** of the end [L0022]:
   - unpaid wages and overtime [L0022];
   - pay for leave not taken [L0033];
   - on a fixed-term contract, severance of 5% of all wages paid [L0054];
   - on a permanent contract, any seniority payment due [L0053].
3. **Ask for your employment certificate** [L0009].
4. **Settle your bank account.** An ACLEDA account with no activity for over 12 months becomes dormant and is charged fees; ABA charges USD 50 (about Tk 6,200) after 4 years without a transaction, and closing an ABA current account is free [G0081, G0090, G0089, G0002].
5. **Leave before your EB extension ends;** each day over costs USD 10 (about Tk 1,250) (What can get you jailed or deported) [U0044, G0002].
6. **Cash and goods.** What you may take out of Cambodia and bring into Bangladesh: Coming home [G0058].

### Bringing your family

Cambodia has no family visa: your husband, wife or child enters on the E-class visa and gets an EB dependant extension, with proof of the relationship and a letter from you [V0030, V0031]. They can't work without their own work permit [V0032]. They apply for the extension inside Cambodia, at the immigration office or through a visa agent; the papers and steps are in Family [V0038].
