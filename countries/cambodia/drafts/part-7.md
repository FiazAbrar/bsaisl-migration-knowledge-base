## Sending money home

**Send through a bank or a licensed money-transfer company, never hundi** (sending money through an unlicensed private agent outside the banking system), which is illegal in Bangladesh [G0012]. Money sent legally earns the family the government's 2.5% cash incentive, kept in the FY2026-27 budget, and at some banks a further 2.5% [G0009, G0010]. bKash, a Bangladeshi mobile-money wallet, does not list Cambodia among the countries it receives money from (checked 11 November 2026) [G0004]. Bangladesh Bank publishes no separate figure for money sent from Cambodia; it counts it under "Others" [G0011]. Opening a Cambodian bank account is under Work visa: company worker, Step 7: After arrival [G0032, G0087].

### How to send

**The channels:**
- **ACLEDA Bank** (263 branches in every province and town) sends abroad through Thunes (its international transfer service in the ACLEDA app), Western Union, MoneyGram, Ria and SWIFT (the international bank-to-bank network) [G0034, G0077, G0025, G0028, G0030, G0031].
- **ABA Bank** (Advanced Bank of Asia) sends from its app through Ria, MoneyGram, Western Union, SWIFT, Visa Direct and Mastercard Send [G0035, G0041].
- **Wing Bank** sends from its app through more than 30 partners, including Western Union, MoneyGram and Ria [G0043, G0044].

You'll need:
- your passport or other valid original ID [G0025, G0030];
- at an ABA branch without an ABA account, your passport and a valid visa [G0038];
- for a bKash wallet, if the service you use pays into bKash, the receiver's bKash number and full name exactly as registered with bKash [G0006].

1. **Send the money.** Pick one:
   1. **ACLEDA app, Thunes:** open the app, select TRANSFERS and log in with fingerprint, face or PIN; select INTERNATIONAL TRANSFER, then Thunes; enter the amount in US dollars (a riel account is converted automatically), tap NEXT and enter the receiver's details; tick "I have read and agree to the Terms and Conditions", tap OK, check and tap CONFIRM [G0079, G0077]. Up to 4 times a day [G0018].
   2. **ACLEDA branch, Western Union:** at a branch that serves Western Union, fill in the "To Send Money" form and hand it over with the money, the fee and your ID [G0025]. Western Union and MoneyGram also go from the ACLEDA app, up to 4 times a day and USD 9,999 (about Tk 12.5 lakh) a transfer; Ria at any ACLEDA branch, up to USD 9,999 a transfer [G0027, G0028, G0030, G0002].
   3. **ABA app, Ria:** go to Transfers, then International Transfer - Ria, then Send Money; choose "Pick up at Agent" (cash) or "Bank Deposit"; enter the receiver and the amount, from USD 10 to USD 9,999, and at most USD 9,999 in 30 days; the fee shows after you enter the amount [G0035, G0036].
   4. **Wing Bank app:** tap World Transfer, choose the country and partner, enter the receiver and amount, and confirm with your 4-digit PIN [G0043]. MoneyGram also goes from a Wing outlet [G0046].
2. **Keep the code and give it only to the receiver.** Western Union's receipt carries the MTCN (Money Transfer Control Number), which goes to the receiver with your name, the amount and "Cambodia" as the sending country [G0026]. ABA's Ria code has 11 digits, lasts 21 days and tracks the transfer on riamoneytransfer.com [G0037]. For a Thunes cash pickup, give the receiver the transfer details and the withdrawal code [G0080].
3. **The family collects.** A bKash wallet takes up to Tk 2.5 lakh (about USD 2,000) a transfer, any number of times, and cashes out at Tk 7 per thousand at BRAC Bank, City Bank and Q-Cash ATMs [G0007, G0008, G0002].

**What it costs.** ACLEDA's Thunes fees to Asia-Pacific countries [G0019, G0020, G0002]:
- **to a mobile wallet:** USD 3.70 (about Tk 460) up to USD 2,500 (about Tk 3.1 lakh), USD 6.20 up to USD 5,000, USD 10.20 up to USD 10,000;
- **to a bank account:** USD 4.50 (about Tk 560), USD 7.00 and USD 11.00 for the same bands;
- **for cash pickup:** USD 4.60 (about Tk 570), USD 7.10 and USD 11.10.

ACLEDA says these fees are indicative only [G0021]. A MoneyGram fee at ACLEDA is charged to the sender only [G0029].

**If it fails:** a completed Thunes transfer can't be changed, cancelled or refunded; a Thunes cash-pickup transfer can, until it is collected [G0022]. Changing or cancelling an ABA Ria transfer costs USD 5 (about Tk 620), and a bank-deposit transfer can't be changed [G0039, G0002].

### Hundi

Hundi brokers bring cash to the family's door, and in 2022-2024 paid more taka per dollar than banks (for example Tk 115 against about Tk 108) [G0013]. Workers without valid papers often have no other way to send money [G0014]. Brokers settle through bKash and Nagad (another Bangladeshi mobile wallet) accounts [G0015]. By June 2026 Bangladesh's BFIU (Financial Intelligence Unit) had suspended or frozen about 55,000 mobile-money accounts linked to online gambling and hundi, and in August 2026 it decided to freeze about 14,000 more [G0016, G0017]. Money sent by hundi gets no 2.5% incentive [G0009].

> **Need to know**

### Saving at home

- **Wage Earner Development Bond.** A 5-year Bangladesh government bond for workers who send money legally, sold at authorised bank branches in Bangladesh and at Bangladeshi banks' branches and exchange houses abroad, from Tk 25,000 to Tk 50 lakh, with no purchase limit [G0073]. It pays up to 12% simple profit at maturity [G0074].
- **Probash pension.** Bangladesh's pension scheme for migrants takes monthly payments from abroad and pays a pension from age 60 (see Your rights at work, Pension) [L0154, L0156, L0158].

> **Need to know**

## If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job

In Cambodia, NSSF (the National Social Security Fund) covers work injuries for all paid workers, Cambodian and foreign, and the employer must register you within 3 days of hiring, using your passport number [L0071, L0090]. In Bangladesh the WEWB (Wage Earners' Welfare Board, the government board for migrants' welfare) pays grants to dead workers' families, and its Probashi call centre is 16135 (free), or +8809610102030 from abroad [B0235, B0021]. Its Tk 3 lakh death grant goes only to workers who left with BMET (Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training) clearance or are Board members [B0235]. Bangladesh has no embassy in Cambodia; the Embassy in Bangkok handles it (see The Bangladesh Embassy in Bangkok) [B0169, B0197].

### If you are injured or fall ill

1. **Get treated.** Go to a health facility with the NSSF logo and show your NSSF card; treatment is free [L0072]. If the facility asks you to pay, show the card and call 1286; for an ambulance in an emergency, call 1286 [L0085].
2. **Make sure the employer reports it.** The employer must tell NSSF at once on 1286 and send the work-injury form within 48 hours, to NSSF or to benefit@nssf.gov.kh [L0081, L0082].
3. **Claim pay while off work.** The employer pays the first day; from the second day NSSF pays 70% of your contributory wage until you recover, and a person caring for you gets 50% of your daily allowance [L0075, L0076]. Sickness that isn't from work is under Your rights at work, Sick leave and maternity [L0073].
4. **Claim for lasting injury.** Permanent disability under 20% is paid as a lump sum, 20% or more as a monthly pension [L0077].
5. **Keep to the deadlines.** Claim the daily allowance within 1 year and permanent disability within 5 years, with your passport and NSSF card attached [L0083, L0084]. NSSF keeps originals, so keep copies; it phones you within 20 to 30 working days to collect a cheque [L0087, L0086].
6. **Going home sick.** The WEWB's airport desks take sick returnees to hospital, with a free ambulance from Dhaka or Chattogram airport [B0014, B0043].

### If a worker dies

1. **Ask the WEWB to bring the body home.** The family applies to the WEWB, which records the family's wishes, sends them to the embassy and follows up until the body arrives; call 16135 (free) or +8809610102030 from abroad [B0247, B0021]. For Cambodia, the Embassy in Bangkok handles it, and the next of kin (spouse or children) applies on the Embassy's form [B0189].

   You'll need [B0188]:
   - the deceased's passport and visa copy;
   - the hospital and district death certificates, with English translations;
   - the receiver's NID (national identity card) copy and proof of relationship;
   - the next of kin's application.

   The bodies of three Bangladeshis were brought home from Cambodia on 8 October 2026 [L0121].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Who pays to fly a body from Phnom Penh to Dhaka when the employer won't, what does it cost, and how many days does it take through the Embassy in Bangkok?
   > - Which Cambodian office issues the death certificate for a foreigner, and how does a family arrange burial in Cambodia instead?

2. **Claim in Cambodia.** If a work injury kills the worker, NSSF pays to transport the body and pays 4 million riel (about Tk 1.2 lakh) to whoever holds the funeral [L0078, L0079, G0003]. Survivors claiming NSSF benefits need a family record book, residence certificate or marriage certificate [L0080]. When the Embassy reports that compensation may be due, the WEWB asks the family for a power of attorney so the case can be run in Cambodia, and its legal cell helps prepare it [L0151, L0152]. Compensation, unpaid wages and insurance the Embassy recovers go to the WEWB, which pays the heirs [B0248].
3. **At Dhaka airport,** the WEWB's desk gives the family a Tk 35,000 cheque for transport and burial on the spot when the body is handed over, against an heirship certificate from the Union Parishad chairman, mayor or councillor, plus a photo and NID or passport copy of the nominated person [B0014, B0245, L0148]. The cheque is lost if not collected within 3 months [B0244]. A free ambulance carries the body from Dhaka or Chattogram airport [B0043]. Dhaka desk officer in charge: +8801971 430203 [B0246].
4. **The Tk 3 lakh grant.** The WEWB pays it only if the worker died in the country his clearance was for [B0236]. If the family took the Tk 35,000 cheque at the airport, no separate application is needed; if the body was buried abroad, apply within 1 year of the death to the WEWB's Director General or through the district Probashi Kallyan Centre [B0237, B0238]. It goes to the spouse, children and parents, or to full brothers and sisters if the worker was unmarried and both parents are dead, by BEFTN (Bangladesh Electronic Funds Transfer Network) transfer to a bank account [B0239, B0243].

   You'll need:
   - a family-members certificate from the Union Parishad chairman, mayor or councillor, countersigned by the UNO (Upazila Nirbahi Officer, the subdistrict's chief officer) [B0240];
   - a release, undertaking and authorisation on Tk 400 non-judicial stamp paper, in the WEWB's sample format [B0241];
   - a copy of the Embassy's NOC (no-objection certificate) or the death certificate [B0242];
   - a bank certificate or original statement for the receiver's account, with the routing number [L0143].

5. **The insurance.** Workers who left with BMET clearance since 12 March 2023 are insured for Tk 10 lakh for 5 years, bought with a one-time Tk 1,000 premium at clearance (see Work visa: company worker, Step 6: Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight) [B0009].
6. **Register the death** at the Embassy in Bangkok: immediate family applies with their own passport copy, the local death certificate in English and the deceased's passport copy; it is free within 45 days, 36 Baht after [B0251, B0252, B0185].

### If you lose the job

1. **Your work permit can go.** MLVT (the Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training, which issues work permits) can cancel it if you are out of work for more than a month (see Your residence permit, If you lose your job) [L0040].
2. **Get what you are owed.** Dismissal without proper notice means pay for the notice period, and a business that closes still owes it; wages and any indemnity are due within 48 hours of the contract ending (see Changing employer or leaving) [L0011, L0018, L0022].
3. **If you aren't paid,** follow If you aren't paid; a wage claim lapses three years after the wage was due [L0025].
4. **Hold the agency to its undertaking.** BMET's permission conditions for Cambodia say approved workers must get the job, and if anything goes wrong the agency pays the worker's food and lodging, return airfare and compensation, under an undertaking on Tk 300 stamp paper [B0147]. BMET's complaint form against an agency lists "job not provided", "non-payment of salary" and "non-existence of company" [B0255]. Send the complaint in writing, with evidence, to BMET's Director General; it is free, should be settled in 30 working days, and you get a receipt with a complaint ID [B0016, B0256].
5. **Don't overstay.** An overstay costs USD 10 (about Tk 1,250) a day (see What can get you jailed or deported) [L0110, G0002].

## Coming home

- **Airport help.** The WEWB's Probashi Kallyan desks at Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet airports help with customs clearance and lost luggage, and take complaints from workers returning after abuse or fraud [B0041, B0042]. Women returning within a year after abuse or fraud get Tk 5,000, and sick returnees are taken to hospital [B0014]. Dhaka desk: +8802 8901040, 01404 413388; Chattogram: 01913631797; Sylhet: 01558277904 [B0044, B0015].
- **BRAC's help.** BRAC's Migration Programme gives returnees from Cambodia emergency help, counselling and money to reach home at Dhaka airport [B0223, B0218]. Survivors of scam compounds have sought help, including legal support, at BRAC's Migration Service Centres [S0073].
- **The CID at the airport.** The CID (Criminal Investigation Department, Bangladesh Police) has run forensic checks on returnees from Cambodia and their phones on arrival [L0125]. A trafficking case against a broker is under Scam compounds and trafficking [S0114].
- **A place to stay.** The Wage Earners' Centre near Shahjalal airport costs Tk 100 to enter and Tk 200 a night; book at wec.wewb.gov.bd [B0045]. The Probashi Lounge at Shahjalal airport gives free wifi and telephone and cheaper food to workers who show BMET clearance or WEWB membership [B0046].
- **A complaint against the agency** goes in writing to BMET's Director General (see If you lose the job, item 4) [B0016].
- **Legal help for your family in Bangladesh:** apply to the WEWB directly, or through the expatriate welfare branch of the DC (Deputy Commissioner, the district administration) office or the DEMO (District Employment and Manpower Office) [B0047].
- **A business loan.** PKB (Probashi Kallyan Bank, the government bank for migrants) lends returnees up to Tk 50 lakh, up to Tk 3 lakh without collateral, against an up-to-date trade licence; helpline 16238, or +880 9677-787878 from abroad [B0039, B0040, B0038].
- **If you are coming home without a valid visa or passport,** see Going home without valid papers [B0179].

### What you can carry home

- **Your Cambodian bank account.** An ACLEDA account with no deposit, withdrawal or transfer for more than 12 months becomes dormant and is charged fees; ABA charges USD 50 (about Tk 6,200) after 4 years without a transaction and USD 100 (about Tk 12,500) a year after that [G0081, G0090, G0002]. Closing an ABA current account is free; ACLEDA may charge for closing a savings account within 3 months of opening [G0089, G0033].
- **Leaving Cambodia:**
  - **Cash:** declare USD 10,000 (about Tk 12.5 lakh) or more, or the same in riel, to customs; not declaring is fined 10% of the amount [G0058, G0059, G0002]. Taking out more than USD 10,000 in foreign currency that you did not declare on arrival needs a National Bank of Cambodia permit and customs approval [G0063, G0060].
  - **Gold:** raw gold or precious stones worth USD 10,000 or more must be declared to the National Bank in advance [G0061].
  - **Drugs:** trafficking can be punished with life imprisonment [G0066].
- **Arriving in Bangladesh:**
  - **Cash:** any amount of foreign currency, but more than USD 10,000 must be declared on the FMJ form (the customs currency declaration) [G0067].
  - **Phones:** 2 used phones, plus 1 new phone once a year, without duty or tax [G0068].
  - **Duty-free:** one item from a list that includes a laptop, and 100 g of gold and 200 g of silver jewellery (no more than 12 pieces of one type) [G0071].
  - **Gold bar:** once a year, one declared bar of up to 117 g (10 tola), at Tk 5,000 duty per 11.664 g (about Tk 50,000 for 117 g); undeclared gold means detention, arrest and a criminal case, and gold above 117 g is confiscated [G0069, G0070].
  - **Alcohol:** none for Bangladeshi passport holders [G0072].
