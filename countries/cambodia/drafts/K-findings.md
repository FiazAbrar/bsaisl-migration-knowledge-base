## a. Draft text inserts

### Step 3: The employer gets your work permit (insert, agent W's section)

Your employer, not you, applies for the work permit and the employment card (the foreign worker's "employment book") on FWCMS, MLVT's online foreign-worker system at fwcms.mlvt.gov.kh [K0006]. Before that, the employer must hold MLVT's annual permission to use foreign workers (the quota) [K0002], have a written contract with you [K0004] and register that contract with MLVT [K0005]. MLVT allows foreign workers up to 10% of a company's Cambodian workforce, and only 1% for non-specialised workers such as labourers [K0056, K0057]; companies send next year's request by the end of November [K0058]. The permit fee is the one set by Joint Prakas No. 335 of 27 March 2020 [K0017, K0060].

**Check it is real:**
- The employer must show you legal proof of its business licence [K0003].
- You must receive the registered contract, the work permit and the employment card yourself [K0007, K0009].
- When you start or leave, the employer must write your job, wage and dates in your employment card within 15 working days [K0008].

### What can get you jailed or deported (insert, agent L's section)

A labour inspector can take away your work permit if you entered Cambodia illegally, use an improper passport or have no proper residence visa [K0011, K0012, K0013], if you are unfit for the job or have a contagious disease [K0014], if you have had no work for more than a month or have gone to work for another employer [K0015], or if you gave false information about yourself, your job, workplace, home or documents [K0016]. Using foreign workers without MLVT permission (including self-employed foreigners), or without employment cards, is fined 12,600,000 riel (200,000 x 63) per the inter-ministerial fines prakas [K0019, K0020], which is Joint Prakas No. 498 of 31 July 2023 [K0018].

### Step 7: After arrival (insert, agent W/L)

Your employer must register you with the NSSF (National Social Security Fund) within 3 days of starting work, using your passport details [K0034]; the NSSF card is free [K0035]. For the pension you pay 2% of your wage and the employer 2% in the first five years [K0036, K0037], rising to 8% in total in the next five [K0038]. A pension needs age 60 and at least 12 months of contributions; with less you get an old-age allowance from what you paid in [K0039, K0040]. The employer also pays 0.8% for work-injury cover and 2.6% for health care [K0041, K0042].

Cambodia is replacing passport stamps with the Cambodia v-Pass (Visitor Pass) at the three international airports and international ports; you get it by email, at arrival.gov.kh or in the Cambodia e-Arrival app [K0033]. Fill in the arrival card only at www.arrival.gov.kh or in the app [K0031].

### Scams (insert, agent N/V)

- **Fake visa sites:** the GDI says cambodiaivisa.com is not a government website [K0030]. Use only the GDI's site www.immigration.gov.kh and its hotline (+855) 78 38 66 99 [K0030, K0027].

- Puspita Overseas (RL-2648), 13 Mar 2025: 68 workers for L.Y.P. Group, Soma Group and Sinohydro (Cambodia) United; USD 420; Tk 80,770 [K0045, K0046, K0047, K0048].

- Shams Company (RL-0696), 6 Feb 2025: 24 workers for Koh Kong Construction Co. Ltd.; USD 420, renewable 2-year contract; Tk 80,770 [K0053, K0054, K0055].
