# V findings: visit visa, student visa, closed visas, changing visa type, family, children (11 November 2026)

Facts: `countries/cambodia/work/V/facts.csv` (V0001-V0123). Sources: `work/V/sources.csv` (36 registered). W ids cited are in `work/W/facts.csv`.

## a. Draft doc text

## Visas not open to Bangladeshis

- **Visa-free entry:** only for ordinary passports of the other ASEAN countries (Laos, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia, Brunei, Myanmar) plus the Maldives and Seychelles; Bangladeshis always need a visa [V0004, V0065].
- **Diplomatic visa (A) and Official visa (B):** only for diplomatic or official/service passports on government missions [V0015].
- **Courtesy visa (C):** only for members of international NGOs registered with Cambodia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs [V0016].
- **Khmer visa (K):** only for people of Khmer origin or with a Cambodian parent; marrying a Cambodian does not qualify you [V0017, V0018].
- **Retirement extension (ER):** only for people aged 55 or over [V0037].
- **Citizenship by naturalisation:** not a route for workers: it needs seven years' continuous residence after receiving a residence card, and the government can refuse it at will [V0085, V0086].

## Visit visa

The tourist visa (T-class) is for holidays; working on it is prohibited [V0009]. It costs USD 30 online, allows one entry within 3 months of issue, and gives a stay of one month [V0001, V0002]. It can be extended once (see Step 5: Extend once, or leave) [V0010]. Bangladeshis promised office jobs in Cambodia have arrived to find they held only a one-month visit or tourist visa, and were taken from the airport to scam compounds (see Scam compounds and trafficking) [W0092, W0093]. A job in Cambodia needs the ordinary visa (E-class) and a work permit (see Work visa: company worker, Step 5: The visa, and checking it is real) [V0012, V0079].

### Step 1: Choose how to apply

1. **Apply online** at www.evisa.gov.kh, the only official e-visa site, run by the E-Visa Office of Cambodia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation (MFAIC) [V0027].
2. **Or apply through an embassy:** Bangladesh is covered by the Royal Embassy of Cambodia in New Delhi (A-1/309, Safdarjung Enclave, New Delhi 110029; +91 11 41717136 / 41717143; camemb.ind@mfaic.gov.kh) [V0043, V0052]. Any Cambodian embassy can usually take tourist and business visa applications [V0044].
3. **Visa on arrival** is sold at international airports and land borders, but not to every nationality [V0024, V0028].

> **Need to know**

(Travel guides still list Bangladesh among nationalities that must arrange a visa in advance; the e-visa office said in April 2025 that the E-class e-visa is open to all countries.)
> - Does the New Delhi embassy accept applications by email from people living in Bangladesh, and what does it charge?

### Step 2: Apply for the e-visa

You'll need: a passport valid for more than six months on the day you arrive, a digital passport photo (JPEG or PNG), a Visa or MasterCard credit card [V0003], a flight ticket and a hotel booking [V0011].

1. **Open www.evisa.gov.kh** and click "Application" [V0006].
2. **Type the security code** shown in the box, then fill in your personal details, passport details and travel details [V0006].
3. **Review everything, then pay** by card [V0006]. The fee is not refunded once processing has started [W0011].
4. **Save the reference number** at once; you need it to track the application, change pending details or download the visa under "Check and Change" [V0023].
5. **Wait about 3 business days** for the visa by email; there is no express service [V0022, W0008]. Cambodian embassies cannot check an e-visa's status; check it online [W0014].
6. **If your photo is wrong,** email the new photo with your reference number to info@evisa.gov.kh [V0007]. The e-visa office phones are (855) 23 224 973 and (855) 99 334 973 (Khmer and English) [V0008].

**Check it is real:** since 3 October 2026 every Cambodian visa is digital; no visa sticker goes in the passport [V0020]. Travel on the same passport the e-visa was issued for [W0013].

**What agencies charge:** a Dhaka travel agency charges Tk 7,500 for a Cambodia e-visa, non-refundable, in 5 to 7 working days, against the official USD 30 [V0080, V0121, V0001]. It asks for 6 months' bank statements and a bank solvency certificate showing at least Tk 60,000 per person [V0081].

**Scams:** guides and agents still quote the old USD 36 fee, cut to USD 30 [V0054]. The government has disowned look-alike sites such as "Cambodia-e-arrival.online" [V0050].

> **Need to know**
> - What do Dhaka agencies charge in 2026 for a Cambodia tourist visa with a "job-seeker" promise attached?

### Step 3: Fill in the e-Arrival before you fly

1. **Within 7 days before you fly,** fill in the e-Arrival (immigration, health and customs form) at https://arrival.gov.kh, the only official site [V0047, V0049]. It is free, and every traveller must submit it even with a visa [V0045, V0046]. It is not a visa [V0029].
2. **Screenshot or download the QR code** before closing the page [V0048].

Since 1 September 2026, visa-on-arrival fees can be paid online in advance [V0066].

### Step 4: Enter at an airport or an open border

1. **Fly in, or cross from Vietnam or Laos.** E-visas are accepted at Techo International Airport (Phnom Penh), Siem Reap Angkor and Sihanoukville airports, at Bavet on the Vietnam border and at Tropaeng Kreal on the Laos border [V0040, V0116, V0117, V0041, V0118]. All land crossings from Thailand, including Poipet, Cham Yeam and O Smach, are closed until further notice [V0042, V0025].
2. **Be ready to explain your trip.** Officers may refuse entry to anyone without a clear travel history and purpose, money to support themselves and a guarantor in Cambodia [V0061]. The immigration department says no nationality is barred [V0060].

> **Need to know**
> - What do immigration officers at Techo airport ask Bangladeshi tourists (return ticket, cash, hotel), and how many are refused entry?

### Step 5: Extend once, or leave

1. **To extend, go to** the immigration office in Phnom Penh or through a licensed visa agent; it cannot be done online [V0064]. A tourist visa can be extended once, for one month, for about USD 45 [V0010, V0077].
2. **Make sure your address is registered** in FPCS (Foreigners Presence in Cambodia System), the police record of where foreigners stay; hotels usually do it, and without it an extension may be refused [V0058, V0059]. Report a change of address to the local police within 24 hours [V0068].
3. **Leave after about 60 days in total** [V0063]. Overstaying costs USD 10 a day [V0078] (see What can get you jailed or deported).

> **Need to know**
> - What is the official General Department of Immigration fee for a one-month tourist extension in 2026, and what do agents in Phnom Penh charge Bangladeshis?

## Student visa

Cambodia has no separate student entry visa: students enter on the ordinary visa (E-class), which also covers trainees, interns and students, then extend it inside Cambodia as a student extension (ES) [V0012, V0071]. The E-class e-visa costs USD 35 [W0001].

1. **Get an acceptance letter** from a registered Cambodian school or university, and proof of money for living costs and fees [V0072, V0035].
2. **Enter on the E-class visa** (see Visit visa, Step 2: Apply for the e-visa) [V0012].
3. **Apply for the ES extension** at the immigration office or through a visa agent [V0038]. It runs for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months [V0034]; 1- and 3-month extensions are single entry, 6- and 12-month ones allow you to leave and come back [V0036]. A 6-month extension costs around USD 160-170 through an agent [V0073].

Bangladeshi students in Cambodia renew passports through the Embassy of Bangladesh in Bangkok at a reduced fee (1,190 baht, regular) [V0105].

> - Can an ES holder work part-time with a work permit, or is any work illegal?
> - Are Dhaka agents selling Cambodian "student visas" as a route to jobs?

## Changing visa type

A tourist visa cannot normally be turned into an ordinary (E-class) visa inside Cambodia; claims that it can are not the official route [V0057, W0079]. Expats reported that converting tourist visas into E-class extensions was suspended [V0069]. After the one tourist extension, a person must leave and come back on a new visa, and anyone planning to stay more than 60 days is told to enter on the E-class visa [V0070, V0122].

The E-class visa is extended inside Cambodia into one of these [V0038]:
- **EB (business):** employees, freelancers and their dependants, for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months [V0030]. It does not give the right to work: a work permit is still needed (see Work visa: company worker, Step 3: The employer gets your work permit) [V0032].
- **EG (job-seeker):** for people looking for work, for 1, 3 or 6 months, generally single entry [V0033].
- **ES (student)** (see Student visa) [V0034].
- **ER (retirement):** age 55 and over [V0037].

Because the Thai land borders are closed, leaving to get a new visa means a flight or a crossing into Vietnam (Bavet) or Laos (Tropaeng Kreal) [V0042, V0041, V0118].

**Scams:** brokers promise a one-year visa and work permit after arrival; one Bangladeshi who escaped a scam centre never got them and became undocumented [V0113]. A broker told another worker whose visa named a different job that the job title was temporary and would change [W0090, W0091]. After Bangladesh tightened checks on Cambodia, some recruiters allegedly sent workers to Thailand as tourists and moved them on to Cambodia [V0115].

> - What does a Phnom Penh agent charge a Bangladeshi for a first EG (job-seeker) extension?

## Family

Cambodia has no family visa. A worker's husband, wife or child enters on the ordinary visa (E-class) and gets an EB dependant extension, which needs proof of relationship and a letter from the EB holder [V0030, V0031].

1. **Have your marriage and relationship papers certified:** the Embassy of Bangladesh in Bangkok, which covers Cambodia, attests a marriage certificate for 275 baht and issues a relationship certificate for 205 baht [V0110, V0109, V0106].
2. **Bring the family in on the E-class visa** and apply for the dependant extension at the immigration office or through an agent [V0038, V0030].

> **Need to know**
> - Does the immigration department accept a Bangladeshi marriage certificate attested by the Bangkok embassy, or does it also need MFAIC legalisation?
> - Do any Bangladeshi workers in Cambodia bring their families, and is there a minimum salary for it?

### Marrying in Cambodia

A marriage is legal only if registered before the civil registrar at the commune or sangkat (municipal ward) office [V0095]. Two foreigners living legally in Cambodia may marry there under Cambodian law [V0094]. Marriage between a Cambodian and a foreigner still follows Sub-Decree 183 of 3 November 2008 [V0096]. Marrying a Cambodian gives no Khmer visa and no automatic nationality: the foreign spouse still needs a tourist or E-class visa, and can apply for nationality only after living together for three years from registration of the marriage [V0018, V0039, V0084]. The Bangkok embassy issues a marital status certificate for 275 baht [V0107].

> **Need to know**
> - What documents does a commune office ask a Bangladeshi to show before registering a marriage (single-status certificate, police clearance, MFAIC approval)?

## Having a child in Cambodia

A child of two Bangladeshi parents born in Cambodia does not become Cambodian; that happens only if both foreign parents were themselves born in Cambodia and live there legally, or if one parent is Cambodian [V0082, V0083, V0088]. The child is Bangladeshi by descent if the father or mother is a Bangladeshi citizen [V0111].

1. **Register the birth in Cambodia within 30 days** at the commune or sangkat (municipal ward) office; foreign parents use the same procedure and the birth certificate is free [V0090, V0092, V0091].
2. **Have the Cambodian birth certificate translated into English and attested** by Cambodia's Ministry of Foreign Affairs [V0100].
3. **Apply online for Bangladeshi birth registration** at http://bdris.gov.bd, then submit the printout to the Embassy of Bangladesh in Bangkok, which registers births in Cambodia [V0098, V0099, V0097]. You'll need: the attested birth certificate, both parents' passport copies, both parents' Bangladeshi birth registration certificates, and the parents' work permit and visa copies [V0100, V0102, V0101]. It is free within 45 days of birth and 36 baht after [V0103].
4. **Do not skip registration if a parent is a citizen by descent** (born abroad): the child is Bangladeshi only if the birth is registered at a Bangladesh mission [V0112].
5. **Apply for the child's passport** at the Bangkok embassy: 3,960 baht regular (40-45 days) or 7,920 baht express (21-28 days) [V0104].
