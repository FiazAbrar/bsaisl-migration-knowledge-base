### Background insert: What you pay, and what you earn and keep

#### Money sent home

Bangladesh Bank publishes remittances for 18 named countries and counts Cambodia under "Others", so there is no official figure for money sent from Cambodia [G0011]. bKash, a Bangladeshi mobile-money service, does not list Cambodia among the countries it receives remittances from (checked 11 November 2026) [G0004]. How the money actually goes is under Sending money home.

#### What it costs to live (insert under "What the job pays")

A 2025 study cited by Cambodian unions and NGOs found garment workers took home USD 287 a month on average, while median household spending was USD 391 [G0075]. A garment worker in Phnom Penh reported needing overtime to reach about USD 320 a month including transport, housing and food allowances [G0076]. In Phnom Penh in 2026, food for one person cooking mostly local food costs about USD 150 to 250 a month [G0084], rent in basic local buildings about USD 7 per square metre a month [G0085], and electricity USD 30 to 60 a month [G0086].

#### Salary tax (insert under "What the job pays", or under Your rights at work)

Cambodia has no general income tax for employees; they pay a monthly salary tax that the employer deducts from pay and pays to the tax department within 20 days after the month, and the employee files no annual return [G0051, G0056]. The rate depends on whether you are a tax resident: someone whose home is in Cambodia or who is there more than 182 days in 12 months [G0055].

- **Residents:** 0% on monthly salary up to KHR 1,500,000 (about USD 367), 5% up to KHR 2,000,000, 10% up to KHR 8,500,000, 15% up to KHR 12,500,000 and 20% above [G0052, G0083, G0001]. Each dependent child or spouse reduces taxable salary by KHR 150,000 a month [G0057].
- **Non-residents:** a flat 20% on all salary, as a final tax [G0053].
- **Benefits in kind** (such as housing or a car given by the employer): 20% of their value [G0054].

> **Need to know**

> - Does the payslip show the salary tax deducted, and can a worker check it was paid to the General Department of Taxation?

### Insert for Work visa: company worker, Step 7: After arrival

#### Opening a bank account

A bank account lets you be paid without cash and send money home from your phone (see Sending money home) [G0035, G0043, G0077]. Three are used most:

1. **ACLEDA Bank:** the widest network, with 263 branches in all provinces and towns [G0034].
   You'll need: a passport valid at least 3 more months and a phone, and to be 15 or over [G0032].
   1. Go to any branch, or register in the ACLEDA mobile app [G0032].
   2. Ask for a savings account: no opening deposit, no minimum balance and no fee to open or close (closing within 3 months may be charged), in riel, US dollars, baht or euro [G0033].
   **Watch out:** an ACLEDA account with no deposit, withdrawal or transfer for more than 12 months becomes dormant and the bank charges fees on it [G0081].
2. **ABA Bank (Advanced Bank of Asia):** the app-based Instant Account is for Cambodian nationals only; a foreigner opens a standard account at an ABA branch [G0042].
   You'll need: to be 18 or over; your original passport with a valid visa; the application form; and proof of residence, which can be a multiple-entry visa with at least 182 days a year in Cambodia, a rental contract or address certificate of at least 6 months, or an employment contract or certificate of employment showing at least 6 months' work [G0087, G0088].
   A current account needs USD 100 (or KHR 400,000) to open and kept as the minimum balance, has no monthly fee and is free to close [G0089]. ABA has an interest-free mode for Muslim customers [G0092]. After 4 years with no transaction ABA charges USD 50, then USD 100 a year [G0090]. Hotline: 1 800 203 203 or 098 203 203 (24 hours) [G0091, G0040].
3. **Wing Bank:** open the account in the Wing Bank App by scanning your passport; some services need a "Full KYC" upgrade at a Wing branch or Wing agent shop with the same passport [G0047].

### Section: Sending money home

## Sending money home

**Send through a bank or a licensed money-transfer company, never hundi** (sending through an unlicensed private agent outside the banking system), which is illegal in Bangladesh [G0012]. A family that receives money sent legally gets the government's 2.5% cash incentive, and some banks add a further 2.5% [G0009]; the 2026-27 budget keeps the 2.5% incentive [G0010].

bKash does not list Cambodia as a country it receives money from [G0004]. Cambodian banks send abroad by Western Union, MoneyGram, Ria, Thunes and SWIFT (bank-to-bank) transfer [G0025, G0028, G0030, G0018, G0031, G0041, G0044].

You'll need:
- your passport (with a valid visa if you send at an ABA branch without an ABA account) [G0030, G0038];
- for a bKash wallet, the receiver's bKash number and full name exactly as registered with bKash [G0006].

### Send from the ACLEDA mobile app (Thunes)

1. **Open** the ACLEDA mobile app, select TRANSFERS and log in with fingerprint, face or PIN [G0079].
2. **Select** INTERNATIONAL TRANSFER, then Thunes [G0079].
3. **Fill in** the amount in US dollars (a riel account is converted automatically), tap NEXT, then the receiver's details [G0077, G0079].
4. **Tick** "I have read and agree to the Terms and Conditions", tap OK, check the details and tap CONFIRM [G0079].
5. **If you sent it for cash pickup,** give the receiver the transfer details and the withdrawal code [G0080].

Fees to Asia-Pacific countries: to a mobile wallet USD 3.70 up to USD 2,500, USD 6.20 up to USD 5,000, USD 10.20 up to USD 10,000; to a bank account USD 4.50, 7.00 and 11.00; for cash pickup USD 4.60, 7.10 and 11.10 [G0019, G0020]. ACLEDA says these fees are only indicative [G0021]. You can send up to 4 times a day [G0018].

**If it fails:** a completed Thunes transfer cannot be changed, cancelled or refunded; a cash-pickup transfer can, until it is collected [G0022].

### Send by Western Union, MoneyGram or Ria at ACLEDA

1. **Go** to an ACLEDA branch that serves Western Union, MoneyGram or Ria, with your passport [G0025, G0028, G0030].
2. **For Western Union,** fill in the "To Send Money" form and hand it over with the money, the fee and your passport [G0025].
3. **Keep the receipt.** For Western Union it carries the Money Transfer Control Number (MTCN); tell it only to the receiver, with your name, the amount and "Cambodia" as the sending country [G0026].

Ria at ACLEDA: up to USD 9,999 per transfer [G0030]. Western Union and MoneyGram can also be sent from the ACLEDA app, up to 4 times a day and USD 9,999 per transfer [G0027, G0028]. The MoneyGram fee is charged to the sender only [G0029].

### Send from the ABA app (Ria)

1. **Open** the ABA app and go to Transfers, then International Transfer - Ria, then Send Money [G0035].
2. **Choose** "Pick up at Agent" (cash) or "Bank Deposit", and enter the receiver and the amount (USD 10 to USD 9,999; at most USD 9,999 in 30 days) [G0035, G0036]. The fee in USD shows after you enter the amount [G0036].
3. **Send the receiver the 11-digit code** the app shows; it is valid 21 days, and you can track the transfer with it on riamoneytransfer.com [G0037].

Without an ABA account you can send by Ria at an ABA branch with your passport and a valid visa [G0038]. The ABA app also sends by MoneyGram, Western Union, SWIFT, Visa Direct and Mastercard Send [G0041].

**If it fails:** changing or cancelling costs USD 5; a bank-deposit transfer cannot be changed [G0039].

### Send from the Wing Bank app

1. **Open** the Wing Bank App and tap World Transfer [G0043].
2. **Choose** the country and the partner (Wing works with more than 30, including Western Union, MoneyGram and Ria), enter the receiver and the amount, and confirm with your 4-digit PIN [G0043, G0044].

MoneyGram can also be sent at a Wing outlet [G0046]. Wing advertises SWIFT bank transfers at 0.12% plus a USD 15 fee [G0048].

### When the money arrives in Bangladesh

- **bKash:** up to Tk 2.5 lakh per transfer, any number of times [G0007]. The family can cash out at BRAC Bank, City Bank and Q-Cash ATMs for Tk 7 per thousand [G0008].
- **The incentive:** 2.5% from the government, sometimes 2.5% more from the bank, on money sent legally [G0009, G0010].

### If the money doesn't arrive

Complain in this order:
1. **The bank in Cambodia:** ACLEDA call centre +855 23 994 444 or +855 15 999 233 (24 hours), then its Complaint Management Centre, 015 888 654, cmc@acledabank.com.kh (working days 7:30 to 16:30) [G0023, G0024]; ABA 098 203 203 (24 hours) [G0040]; Wing (+855) 23 999 989, care.centre@wingbank.com.kh [G0045]. Have your receipt and the transfer code ready [G0026, G0037].
2. **The National Bank of Cambodia (NBC),** which takes consumers' complaints about financial services: 085 600 002, 098 220 002, 085 600 003, 097 278 3030 or 098 220 001; Phnom Penh branch 016 568 433; Preah Sihanouk branch 016 687 194 [G0049, G0050].
3. **bKash** in Bangladesh, if the money was sent to a bKash wallet: +88-02-55663001, support@bkash.com, its Facebook page or live chat [G0005].

### Hundi

Hundi brokers bring cash to the family's door and in 2022-2024 paid more taka per dollar than banks (for example Tk 115 against about Tk 108) [G0013]. Workers without valid papers often have no other way to send money [G0014]. Brokers settle payments through bKash and Nagad accounts [G0015]. By June 2026 Bangladesh's Financial Intelligence Unit (BFIU) had suspended or frozen about 55,000 mobile-money accounts linked to online gambling and hundi, and in August 2026 it decided to freeze about 14,000 more [G0016, G0017]. Money sent by hundi gets no 2.5% incentive (see When the money arrives in Bangladesh) [G0009].

> **Need to know**
> - Is there a hundi network for Bangladeshis in Phnom Penh, and what rate does it pay against the banks?

### Saving in Bangladesh

The Wage Earner Development Bond is a 5-year Bangladesh government bond for workers abroad who send money legally, sold at authorised bank branches in Bangladesh and at Bangladeshi banks' branches and exchange houses abroad, from Tk 25,000 to Tk 50 lakh with no purchase limit [G0073]. It pays up to 12% simple profit at maturity [G0074].

### Insert for Leaving at the end of the job (and Coming home): money and what you can bring

#### Your bank account

An ACLEDA account with no activity for over 12 months becomes dormant and is charged fees [G0081]; ABA charges USD 50 after 4 years without a transaction and USD 100 a year after that [G0090]. Closing an ABA account at any branch is free [G0089]; ACLEDA charges only if you close within 3 months of opening [G0033].

#### Leaving Cambodia

- **Cash:** declare USD 10,000 or more (or the same in riel) to customs when you leave or arrive; not declaring is fined 10% of the amount [G0058, G0059, G0062]. Taking out more than USD 10,000 that you did not declare on arrival needs a National Bank of Cambodia permit and customs approval [G0063, G0060].
- **Gold:** raw gold or precious stones worth USD 10,000 or more must be declared to the National Bank in advance [G0061].
- **Drugs:** trafficking can be punished with life imprisonment [G0066].

On arrival in Cambodia every passenger fills in the Passenger's Declaration Form and gives it to customs [G0064]. The duty-free allowance is up to 2 litres of alcohol, 200 cigarettes, 350 ml of perfume and your personal belongings; goods worth USD 100 or more pay duty [G0065].

#### Arriving in Bangladesh

- **Cash:** bring any amount of foreign currency, but declare more than USD 10,000 on the FMJ form (the foreign-currency declaration form) [G0067].
- **Phones:** 2 used phones and 1 new phone a year, free of duty and tax [G0068].
- **Gold:** at most 117 g (10 tola), as one bar, once a year, declared, paying Tk 5,000 per 11.664 g (about Tk 50,000 for 117 g) [G0069]. Undeclared gold means detention, arrest and a criminal case; gold above 117 g is confiscated [G0070].
- **Gold and silver jewellery, and one item:** 100 g of gold and 200 g of silver ornaments (no more than 12 of one kind), and one item from a duty-free list that includes a laptop [G0071].
- **Alcohol:** a Bangladeshi passport holder may bring none [G0072].
