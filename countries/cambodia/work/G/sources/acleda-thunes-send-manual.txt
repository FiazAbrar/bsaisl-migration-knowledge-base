How to send money abroad via Thunes

1. Open "
ACLEDA mobile
" app

2. Select "
TRANSFERS
" menu

3. Use Touch ID, Facial, or enter PIN to login

4. Select "
INTERNATIONAL TRANSFER
"

5. Choose "
Thunes
"

6. Fill in your transfer information correctly

7. Choose "
NEXT
" button

8. Fill in the information of overseas recipient correctly

9. Choose "I have read and agree to the Terms and Conditions"

10. Select "
OK
" button

11. Verify your transfer information

12. Choose "
CONFIRM
" button

13. Overseas money transfer was completed

Please provide this transfer information to the recipient including withdrawal code if you send in cash
