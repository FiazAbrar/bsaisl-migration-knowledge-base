# International Funds Transfers via Swift 

[ (40 Ratings) ](../rate/view_rate?v_ser=true&referer=ps_ftintltransfer_swift&lan=eng&s=41)

- [](../assets/image/Swift-Go.jpg)

  ![](../assets/layout/loading-img.svg)

  Swift Go

  **FREE!!!** Receive money transferred to your bank account in ACLEDA Bank faster and free of charge with **Swift Go** through our correspondent bank (Bank of New York Mellon).

  In just 4 hours or less (transaction during working hours), the money will be credited to your bank account automatically. The amount received up to US\$10,000 per transaction.

- [](../assets/image/Zero-Fee-for-Inbound-Transfer-via-SWIFT.jpg)

  ![](../assets/layout/loading-img.svg)

  **FREE!!!** Receive money transfer in all currencies sent from worldwide via **Swift** and **free of charge**. This offer is valid until the end of 2026. Terms & conditions apply.

Since your business is growing from day to day, and fund transfer has played an important role in your global business, we have innovated a new way to enhance our [products and services](product) quality delivering to you.

Recognising the need for its customers to lower the cost of transferring money from end to end, ACLEDA Bank registered with an international level financial message transfer service — Swift.

Now you can transfer money worldwide via Swift on **ACLEDA mobile** conveniently. Please see how to transfer [here](../../qr/toanchet-manual/swift-send?l=eng&h=0).

Now we have many partners abroad to be our [correspondents](bp_correspondent) which enable our customers to transfer their money in many currencies throughout the world. Regarding how to transfer funds to a beneficiary customer through our bank, please choose one of the Remittance Instructions available below.
