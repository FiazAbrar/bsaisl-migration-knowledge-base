### Keep Your PIN Code Safe

 

- Your PIN code is made up of 4 digits. You will need it to access your Wing Bank account and use services that involve your money. 
- Your PIN code allows you to log in again on a new device, transfer money, make payments, and buy things with your Wing Bank account. 
- Do not share your PIN code with anyone. If someone knows your PIN, they may be able to access your account and use your money. 

 

Always keep your PIN code safe and secret.

## 

##### 

![](https://www.wingbank.com.kh/storage/uploads/2026-06/security21_1780558832.png)

### Use Your Primary Phone Number

 

- Your phone number plays an important role in your Wing Bank account, especially when you change your device or need to access your account again. 
- Always use your primary phone number when registering your account. This helps keep your account safer and makes it easier to access your banking services anytime.

 

Do not share your Wing Bank OTP code with anyone.
