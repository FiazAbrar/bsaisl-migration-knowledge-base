International Money Transfers With Wing Bank

By B2B Asia News
on Mar, 13 2025
Sponsored

Wing Bank allows users to send money to 200+ countries and territories through its extensive partner network. Whether supporting family, paying for education, or covering other expenses, Wing Bank customers can make use of a fast, reliable, and cost-effective money transfer service.

Wing Bank’s international money transfer service is designed to make sending and receiving money a seamless experience, allowing you to send funds anytime, anywhere, directly from the Wing Bank App via your smartphone, or at any Wing outlets located across the country.

Receiving Money Instantly Anywhere, Anytime​ With Wing Bank

If you need to receive money from abroad or are a Cambodian migrant worker who needs to send money back to Cambodia, the process is easy with Wing Bank. 

Beneficiaries in Cambodia can choose to cash out at any of the 12,000 agents nationwide, or receive funds through a ‘Code To Wing’ function through the Wing Bank App and pick up the money or have it sent directly into a Wing Bank account.

These are fast and highly secure international financial transactions, which often can be near-instant or real-time, with multiple transfer options:

  • Cash Pick Up: Recipients can pick up cash without needing a bank account from the extensive Wing network.
  • Bank Deposit: Direct transfers can be made to the recipient's bank account, which, of course, includes Wing Bank.
  • Direct to Wallet: Money can be sent directly to the receiver's mobile wallet on the Wing Bank app.

Why Using Wing Bank Is The Best Option To Send And Receive Money From Overseas To Cambodia

See more here about sending and receiving money abroad to and from Cambodia with Wing Bank.

Available to 200 countries and territories, the service’s speed, security, and convenience of up to 30 global partners means sending and receiving money from overseas has never been easier.

  • Transfer Fees: These vary depending on the partner, amount, and service type, however the bank advertises international SWIFT transfers at just 0.12 per cent + a USD 15 cable fee.
  • Inward Remittance allows for a swift transfer of money from abroad through Wing Banks’s network of correspondent banks in USD.
  • Senders must have filled in a full KYC (Know-Your-Customer) with Wing Bank – transaction limits may also be in place.

Until May 2025, international money transfers with Wing Bank via Moneygram are a flat fee of USD 5 for partner countries through the Wing Bank App.

For more information, contact Wing Bank’s care team via Wingchat on the Wing Bank App, or call them at 023 999 989.
