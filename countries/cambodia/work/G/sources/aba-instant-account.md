# Instant Account

Introducing ABA Instant Account in ABA Mobile for new-to-bank customers! Instant Account replaces the traditional method of opening a bank account that usually requires traveling to a bank branch and passing through lengthy formal procedures.

From now on, any Cambodian with a valid ID card or Passport who wishes to access modern digital banking services can open their first ABA account right through ABA Mobile in a few minutes!

### Conditions

| Eligibility | - Cambodian National only - At least 15 years of age or above on the date of subscription to the Instant Account - Owns a valid and original identification document: Cambodian National ID or Passport - A new customer to ABA - Applicable for ABA Mobile app user only - Applicable for single accountholder only |
| Opening channel | ABA Mobile app only |
| Minimum opening balance | USD 0.00 or KHR 0.00 |
| Restricted Service12 | International Inward Transfer | International Outward Transfer | Card to Card Transfer |
| Conversion to Enjoy Full Service | - Can be converted at any ABA branch - Account will have full access immediately13 |

[Is it eligible for foreigners?]

Only someone with a Cambodian National ID can activated ABA Instant Account.

[Is it possible to send or receive international transfers using Instant Account?]

Instant Account is available for use within Cambodia only.

[What should I do if I wish to get full banking services on my Instant Account?]

Please visit any ABA Bank branch and request for an upgrade to a standard bank account.

Contact us instantly without leaving the ABA Mobile app by tapping the Contact Us button on the app's main screen. Additionally, you can chat with us via Facebook Messenger, on our website or call us 24/7 hotline at **098 203 203**.
