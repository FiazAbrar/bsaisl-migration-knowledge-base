International Fund Transfer via MoneyGram

International Fund Transfer
 via MoneyGram

(9 Ratings)

You can send & receive money instantly around the world via MoneyGram conveniently.

Benefits

You can transfer money abroad on 
ACLEDA mobile
 in your phone or at ACLEDA Bank's counter nationwide to pay for imported goods, tuition fees or send to family around the world at any time.

Receive money transferred to your account in ACLEDA Bank in minutes from abroad all over the world with no extra charges.

Receive money transferred using 8-digit number with ID card to withdraw money at ACLEDA Bank's counter with no extra charges.

Special Features

You can receive money in 
USD
 or 
KHR
 according to the account's currency and withdraw money from 
ACLEDA Bank's ATM
 anytime.

Transfer money abroad via 
ACLEDA mobile
 in US dollars through intermediary banks with MoneyGram's logo worldwide. In case, the currency of the transfer account is not US dollar, the system will convert it automatically.

You also can transfer money abroad at ACLEDA Bank's branches nationwide.

Transfer fee will charge from the sender only, for the receiver has no extra charges.

To send money

You can send money abroad by yourself in 
ACLEDA mobile
 at any time by entering the correct transfer information and recipient information.

You can transfer up to 
4 times
 per day and 
USD9,999
 per transaction on ACLEDA mobile.

Or you can transfer money abroad at ACLEDA Bank's counter by debit from your account or using cash transfer attaching ID card without limitation.

To receive money

You can receive money transferred to your account automatically.

You can receive money up to 
USD10,000
 or 
KHR40 million
 per day on ACLEDA mobile.

Or you can withdrawal cash at ACLEDA Bank's counter by showing the 8-digit reference number and attaching ID card without limitation.

Receiving money with no extra charges.
