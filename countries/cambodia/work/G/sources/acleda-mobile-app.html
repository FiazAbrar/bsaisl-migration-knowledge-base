ACLEDA mobile Super App 

The bank in your hand!
(1041 Ratings)

ACLEDA mobile (AC Super App) is a multi-functional mobile application that provides services to support daily life activities. This multi-functional mobile application integrates a variety of financial and non-financial services in one place, easy to use, responsive to all needs, modern, fast, secure, and highly reliable.

Download ACLEDA mobile from:
    

Benefits 

  • Be convenient, fast and highly secure in making banking transactions via ACLEDA mobile.
  • Reduce cost and save time by not going to the Bank.
  • Be able to view account information and make fund transfer, payment, mobile top up or payment of goods and services via QR Code anytime and anywhere by using personal mobile.
  • Decrease risk of carrying physical cash along for making payment as well as risk of cash surplus, shortage, counterfeit, etc., while counting money.
  • Be able to view some information about ACLEDA Bank Plc. such as Locations, Exchange Rates, Promotion, FAQs, Security Tip, Help, News, etc.
  • Have an opportunity to use the other products and services of ACLEDA Bank Plc.
  • Get special offers from our partners.

Customer's Qualification

  • Age 15 years or older.
  • Have a mobile phone running iOS, Android or Huawei AppGallery and phone number.
  • Have an identification document:
    • Cambodian
      • Khmer Identity Card (in validity)
      • Passport (at least 3 months validity)
      • Travel Letter for Cambodian Worker Working Abroad (at least 3 months validity)
      • Other similar identification documents
    • Foreigner
      • Passport or permanent resident card for immigrant alien (at least 3 months validity)

Self-Registration

  • You can register for ACLEDA mobile by downloading and installing it from App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) or AppGallery (Huawei).
  • After installing, open the application and follow step-by-step instructions. Read more...

Registration at ACLEDA Bank's branches

  • If you cannot download and install ACLEDA mobile application, please go to ACLEDA Bank.
  • Bring the requirements above.

Register ACLEDA mobile for Minor

Empower your children with safe and smart digital banking!

Guardians and parents can register ACLEDA Super App for your children from 10 to under 15 years old, allowing them to enjoy essential app features while you easily monitor and manage all transactions of your children.

  • To register ACLEDA Super App for minor, guardians or parents must have core bank account and linked on ACLEDA Super App.
  • Wallet account balance for minor of KHR2,000,000 and USD500.
  • Transaction amount of KHR400,000 or USD100 per day.

Cash Deposit at Counter/ATM

Please bring your smart phone and phone number connected ACLEDA mobile to the Bank or Cash Deposit ATM to deposit cash into wallet account.

Cash Withdrawal at Counter/ATM

You can withdraw cash from wallet account or withdraw cash transferred by phone number either at the ACLEDA Bank's office or ATM by providing:

  • Mobile phone number or wallet account number
  • Withdrawal amount
  • Gold code or ACLEDA mobile's PIN (gold code is used to withdraw cash transferred by mobile phone number and PIN is used to withdraw cash form wallet account.)
  • One Time Password (OTP) will be sent to your mobile phone to confirm the transaction.
