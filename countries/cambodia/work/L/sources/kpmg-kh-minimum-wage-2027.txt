November 2026

Implementation of the New Minimum Wage for 2027 in the
Textile, Garment, Footwear, Travel Goods, and Bag Industries

The Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training ("MLVT"): Prakas No. 226/26 dated 30
September 2026 on the Determination of Minimum Wage for Workers/Employees in the
Textile, Garment, Footwear, Travel Goods, and Bag Industries for 2027

On 30 September 2026, the MLVT issued the above Prakas, which sets the 2027 minimum wage for
workers/employees in textiles, garments, footwear, travel goods, and bags.

Key Provisions

Effective Date: 1 January 2027

Minimum Monthly Wage:

· Regular workers/employees: USD 212 per month
· Probationary workers/employees: USD 210 per month

Piece - Rate Workers/Employees:

· Piece-rate workers/employees are paid based on their actual output. If their output-based earnings

   exceed the applicable minimum wage, they are entitled to a higher amount.

· If their output-based earnings fall below the applicable minimum wage, the employer must pay the

   difference so that each worker/employee receives at least:

    USD 210 for probationary workers/employees

    USD 212 for regular workers/employees

Additional Allowance: A temporary monthly allowance of USD 2.50 will continue to apply in 2027, in
addition to the existing transportation and accommodation allowances.

Other Benefits: All other existing benefits and entitlements of workers/employees remain unchanged.

  KPMG Commentary

  This wage adjustment reflects the Royal Government of Cambodia's continued efforts to
  strengthen labour standards and improve living conditions for workers in key manufacturing
  industries. Employers in the affected industries should review their payroll arrangements and
  make the necessary adjustments to ensure compliance with the revised minimum wage by 1
  January 2027.
