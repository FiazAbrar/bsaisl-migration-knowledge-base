Visa Information

Types of Visas

Below are the available visa types. Each has specific characteristics, including Entry Type, Validity, Length of Stay, Extension Status, Processing Time, and required supporting documents. Please review carefully before applying.

Diplomatic Visa (A)
Entry Type Multiple
Validity Three (3) months from the date of issue
Length of Stay Three (3) months from the date of entry
Extension Status Yes, extendable
Processing Time 15 days
Who is eligible? This visa is issued to accredited diplomats and their family members holding Diplomatic Passports and/or EU Laissez-Passers, and officials of the United Nations holding Red Laissez-Passers.
Requirement
  • Diplomatic passport/UN Red Laissez-Passer
  • A Note Verbale or an official letter from the sending government or UN agency
Official Visa (B)
Entry Type Single
Validity Three (3) months from the date of issue
Length of Stay Three (3) months from the date of entry
Extension Status Yes, extendable
Processing Time 15 days
Who is eligible? This visa is issued to non-immigrant foreigners holding Official/Service Passports who are undertaking official government missions or missions with an international organization that has a valid memorandum of understanding with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation, including officials of the United Nations holding Blue Laissez-Passers.
Requirement
  • Official (Service) passport/United Nations Blue Laissez-Passer
  • A Note Verbale or an official letter from the sending government or UN agency
Courtesy Visa (C)
Entry Type Single
Validity Three (3) months from the date of issue
Length of Stay Three (3) months from the date of entry
Extension Status Yes, extendable
Processing Time 15 days
Who needs this visa? Members of registered international NGOs.
Supporting Documents
  • A valid MoU between the NGO and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation of Cambodia
  • An invitation letter from the NGO, job offer, or supporting letter from one of the government ministries of your country
Business Visa (E)
Entry Type Single
Validity Three (3) months from the date of issue
Length of Stay One month from the date of entry
Extension Status Yes, extendable (multiple options)
Processing Time 3 working days
Who needs this visa? Those who have accepted an employment offer in Cambodia, received an invitation from a registered company or organization in Cambodia, or are being sent to Cambodia for business purposes by a registered company or organization, also for trainees, interns and students. 

This visa type is also suitable for retired foreigners who wish to stay in Cambodia for an extended period. Read More
Supporting Documents
  • Letter of invitation or job offer/contract from a company or organization in Cambodia
  • Letter from the sending company
  • Pension/retirement document (for retired applicant only)
Tourist Visa (T)
Entry Type Single
Validity Three (3) months from the date of issue
Length of Stay One (calendar) from the date of entry
Extension Status Yes, extendable (once)
Processing Time 3 working days
Who needs this visa? General travelers to Cambodia for leisure activities; employment is prohibited.
Supporting Documents Flight/bus/boat ticket, accommodation booking, and proof of residence or travel history if applying out of your passport country (for non-EU citizens)
Khmer Visa (K)
Entry Type Multiple
Validity Valid until the passport expires
Length of Stay Unlimited stay
Extension Status No. This visa expires only when the passport expires. Once you obtain a new passport, you must apply for a new K Visa, as it is not transferable.
Processing Time 3 working days
Who needs this visa? Those who are of Khmer origin or have Cambodian ancestry, including children born to Cambodian citizens (by birth or naturalization) who now hold a foreign passport.
Supporting Documents
  • Proof of Cambodian origin, such as a birth certificate, national ID, or an old passport with a Khmer visa. Since details such as your parents' names, places of birth, and current nationality are also required, a birth certificate works best because it includes all of these details.
  • Note: Foreign nationals married to Cambodian citizens are not eligible for a K Visa and should apply for a T or E-class visa instead
Apply for a Visa
Please note the difference between visa validity and length of stay. All our visa types are valid for three months, meaning you can enter Cambodia on any day within that three-month period starting from the issue date. Entry is limited to a single visit, except for Type A and K visas, which permit multiple entries.
Your permitted length of stay begins the moment you pass through immigration at any entry point in Cambodia and is counted by the (calendar) month.
