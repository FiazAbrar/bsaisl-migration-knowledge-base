## Entry requirements & Visa

## Cambodia Student Visa Procedure

### Visa Category: E-class (ES) Student Visa

Foreign students enrolled in Cambodian educational institutions must obtain an E-class (ordinary) Student Visa, often referred to as ES. This visa permits legal study and residence in Cambodia, with options for multiple extensions while the course is ongoing.

### Duration & Extensions

- **Initial validity:** 30 days (Apply for tourist E-visa)
- **Extension options:** Inside Cambodia, you can extend it in increments of 1, 3, 6, or 12 months, depending on your study duration.

### Application Requirements for Initial Visa

Applicants should prepare and submit the following:

- Valid passport (min. 6 months validity).
- Passport-sized photos. (White Background)
- Letter of acceptance from CamTech or equivalent institution.
- Proof of financial capability to support living and tuition.
- Medical insurance documentation.

### How to Apply

- Visit the [official eVisa website](https://www.evisa.gov.kh/) for Cambodia.
- Click on "[Apply for eVisa](https://www.evisa.gov.kh/)" and select "Tourism (ordinary)" as the purpose of your visit.
- Fill out the application form with your personal information and upload the required documents, including your passport and a passport-style photo.
- Pay the visa fee online using a credit card or PayPal.
- Submit your application and wait for the approval notification via email.
- Print out your eVisa and bring it with you to Cambodia along with your passport and other supporting documents.

### Cost & Processing

- **Online visa (e-Visa):** USD \$35, valid for 30 days, single-entry, processed in ~3 business days.
- **In-country visa extensions (ES):** additional fees apply—amounts may vary, but typical extension processing is through immigration services.

### Extension

A 6-month visa extension fee in Cambodia generally costs around \$160 to \$170 USD when obtained through an agent for an "Extension Of Stay" (EOS), or it can be around \$180 if applying directly to the Ministry, though fees are subject to change and may vary depending on your nationality and the specific E-type visa you hold.

### Agency Fee for Visa extension

- 1 year : **295\$**
- 6 months : **175\$**
- 3 months: **85\$**

## Application Process:

### Admission Requirements

- Scanned copy of valid passport (and visa if available)
- A letter of recommendation from the host institution or lecturer
- An official transcript from the host institution
- A valid certificate of English language proficiency (IELTS = 5.5 or equivalent)
- Current photo of 4 x 6 with a white background
- A health certificate issued within the last 6 months
- Passing the CamTech panel interview

### Application Process

- Apply online through this link: [https://forms.gle/XoCfPEbMtrxymaij9](https://forms.gle/XoCfPEbMtrxymaij9)
- Submit required documents, including academic transcripts and proof of English language proficiency
- Receive an offer letter on your application within 3 weeks.

### Application Period

First term:

- Receiving an application in Mid-November to late December
- Starting academic term in early January

Second term:

- Receiving an application in Mid-March to late April
- Starting academic term in early January

Third term:

- Receiving an application in Mid-July to late August
- Starting academic term in early October.
