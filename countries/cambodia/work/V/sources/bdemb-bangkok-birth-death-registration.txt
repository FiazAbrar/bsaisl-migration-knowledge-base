 Birth Registration 

Content: Pages

Children of Bangladeshi nationals (father or mother or both) born in Thailand or Cambodia are eligible to register their birth at the Embassy of Bangladesh in Bangkok. Parents/legal guardians of the newborn are expected to apply for the child’s birth registration.

The death of a Bangladeshi national who passed away in Thailand / Cambodia could be registered through the Embassy of Bangladesh in Bangkok. The immediate family members of the deceased are expected to apply for the death registration.

For Birth Registration:

Step 1: 
Apply online through 
http://bdris.gov.bd/

Step 2:
 Pay the fees, if applicable (Could be paid by bank transfer or at the Embassy by debit/credit card)

Type of Service

Fee in Thai Baht

Birth Registration (
if registered within 45 days of birth
)

Free

Birth Registration (
after 45 days of birth
)

36

Step 3:
 Submit the online application printout and other documents to the Embassy

Step 4:
 Collect the Birth Registration Certificate from Embassy

Required Documents:

1. Birth certificate from local authorities of Thailand/Cambodia translated into English and attested by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

2. Passport copies of Father and Mother

3. Birth registration certificate of Father and Mother.

3. Work permit and visa copy of parents. 

* The Embassy may ask for additional documents if required.

For Death Registration:

*for death registration, one must have his / her birth registration

Step 1:
 Apply online through 
http://bdris.gov.bd/

Step 2:
 Pay the fees, if applicable (Could be paid by bank transfer or at the Embassy by debit/credit card)

Type of Service

Fee in Thai Baht

Death Registration (
if registered within 45 days of death
)

Free

Death Registration (
after 45 days of death
)

36

Step 3:
 Submit an online application printout (with the signature of the reporting family member) and other documents to the Embassy

Step 4:
 Collect the Death Registration Certificate from Embassy

Required Documents:

Death certificate from local authorities of Thailand/Cambodia and translated into English by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs. 

Passport copy of the deceased

Passport copy of the reporting family member

* The Embassy may ask for additional documents if required.
