# Important Update on Cambodia Visas

In an effort to make your visa application as smooth as possible, allowing you to apply 24/7 from anywhere, the Royal Government of Cambodia is transitioning away from physical visa stickers, effective 3 October  2026. All visa applications submitted (online or through the Embassy) after this date will be processed and issued digitally. Visa stickers will no longer be placed in passport books. This applies to all types of visas.

To enter Cambodia, most foreign visitors require a visa unless they hold a passport from a [visa-exempt ](https://recberlin.mfaic.gov.kh/en-us/visa-exemption)country. For a complete list of visa categories, processing times and required documents, please check [here](https://recberlin.mfaic.gov.kh/en-us/types-of-visas). There are three options to obtain a visa:

### OPTION 1: Apply Through the Embassy (E-mail Only)

Applying for a visa at the Embassy, in-person and by post, is no longer available. Instead, please send everything via email to **consular.recberlin@gmail.com**, and we will assist you with submitting it into the electronic portal.  All applications are now processed online through the electronic portal. Once approved, we will send it to you by e-mail. 

Please attach (T type):

- ■ **Application Form:** Downloaded, completed (digitally or by hand), and signed. Download the form [HERE](https://recberlin.mfaic.gov.kh/admin/UploadFiles/Original/6945c387-3cd5-479d-8248-eff7713e31d0.pdf).
- ■ **Passport Biometric Page:** A clear scanned copy or photo of your passport biometric page. The Machine Readable Zone (MRZ) lines at the bottom must be fully visible and clear. See sample  [here](https://recberlin.mfaic.gov.kh/admin/UploadFiles/Original/jbu0pudm.45f.jpg). 
- **■ ****A photo with white background**: optional, but recommended if you want a cleaner look on the visa, as by default, the photo is pulled directly from your passport page. 
- ■ Travel Itinerary: Round-trip booking or ticket (flight, train, bus, or vessel ticket).
- ■ **Proof of Accommodation:** Hotel booking or proof of residence in Cambodia.
- ■ Travel / Entry History (for non-EU citizens residing in the EU): Proof of legal entry or residence in the EU or the Schengen zone (e.g., residence permit copy, entry stamp, or equivalent registration document).
- ■ **Proof of payment for the visa: **please make a direct transfer of payment to the following IBAN or scan the QR Code from your bank app and attach the proof of payment. 

![Payment QR Code](https://recberlin.mfaic.gov.kh/admin/UploadFiles/Original/fccooasp.02z.jpg)

The following fees apply for applications submitted by email:

- ■ **Tourist Visa (T):** €35 (€30 visa fee + €5 uploading fee)
- ■ **Business/Ordinary Visa (E):** €40 (€35 visa fee + €5 uploading fee)

As the electronic Visa will be sent to you by email, please make sure to use an e-mail address that you check regularly.

**IMPORTANT NOTICE ON PROCESSING TIMES:** Express or rush processing is unavailable. Although processing is usually within 3 business days, we recommend submitting your application at least one week before arrival. Applications submitted more than one month in advance will remain pending until the one-month arrival window opens, at which point your visa will be processed and issued. 

### OPTION 2: Apply Online via the Official e-Visa Portal

If you wish to do everything by yourself, you may apply directly through the official Cambodian e-Visa portal.

Apply for an e-Visa: [https://www.evisa.gov.kh/](https://www.evisa.gov.kh/)

Your e-Visa is issued electronically and sent straight to your email. You can also track your application status, update pending details, or download your e-Visa directly on the portal under the "Check and Change" section. Please save your reference number immediately after submitting your application, as some applicants report having trouble finding it in their emails later.

***Since the e-Visa portal is accessible to anyone, why should I still apply through the Embassy?***

*While the direct e-Visa portal is accessible to anyone, it comes with limitations. Visas obtained directly online have restricted entry coverage—for instance, certain small border checkpoints and cruise ship arrivals do not accept them. In contrast, the electronic visa obtained through the Embassy provides the full versatility of a traditional sticker visa. It allows you to enter Cambodia through **any** international checkpoint, including all land borders and cruise ship arrivals, ensuring complete flexibility for your travel plans. Also, you will receive full support from our embassy in your local time zone.*

Please check the entry points that currently accept e-Visas [here](https://recberlin.mfaic.gov.kh/en-us/international-check-points).

### OPTION 3: Obtain a Visa on Arrival (VoA)

If you prefer obtaining your visa upon reaching Cambodia, Visa on Arrival (VoA) remains available at all international airports and international border checkpoints. Please note that land border crossings between Cambodia and Thailand remain closed until further notice.

\
Regardless of the visa option you choose, we wish you a safe journey and a wonderful stay in the Kingdom of Cambodia.
