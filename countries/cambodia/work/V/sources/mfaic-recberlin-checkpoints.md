The following table lists the available border crossings and e-Visa acceptance for Cambodia. This refers to the e-Visa you receive when applying directly on the e-Visa portal, rather than through the Embassy. ***The electronic visa obtained through the Embassy (via email) is valid at any open entry point because it simply replaces the traditional sticker visa***.\
