## Key takeaways

- Cambodia has no digital nomad visa; long stays generally run on the ordinary (E class) visa, extended inside the country, and its current terms should be confirmed with the immigration department.
- Visa exemption for ordinary passports covers only the other ASEAN states, the Maldives and Seychelles; others need a tourist visa for a stay of 30 days.
- Citizenship for investors and donors is discretionary naturalization by Royal Decree; the 2025 thresholds await an official text.
- A stay of more than 182 days in any 12-month period makes a person tax resident, and residents are taxed on foreign income too.
- Numbeo budgets for one person in September 2026 ran from about \$790 a month in Siem Reap to \$1,250 in central Phnom Penh.

Cambodia has no visa built for remote workers. Short visits run on visa exemptions or the tourist visa, longer stays on the ordinary visa system, and the one route with a full page here is naturalization for investors and donors.

### Short stays

No general visa-free entry. Ordinary passports of the 9 other ASEAN states (14 or 30 days), the Maldives (30 days) and Seychelles (15 days) are exempt; everyone else needs a Tourist Visa (T class), by e-Visa or on arrival, for a 30-day stay.

The [visa exemption list](https://recberlin.mfaic.gov.kh/en-us/visa-exemption) can change, and temporary schemes for single nationalities come and go, so it is worth checking before travel.

The tourist visa (T class) is single entry and [valid for three months](https://www.cambodiaembassyuk.org/faq/), for a stay of 30 days. It is sold online through the official [e-Visa portal](https://www.evisa.gov.kh/) and on arrival.

### Longer stays

A business visa (E class) comes on the same basic terms: single entry, three months' validity and a 30-day stay. Long stays generally run on this ordinary visa, extended inside Cambodia by the General Department of Immigration.

Extension types, lengths and fees change from time to time, so anyone planning a long stay should confirm the current terms with the General Department of Immigration, or with the agent handling the extension, before relying on them.

### Investors and donors

The [citizenship route](/visas/cambodia-citizenship-by-investment) for investors and donors is naturalization under the [Law on Nationality](https://www.cdc.gov.kh/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/LAW-96-Nationality-E.pdf), decided by Royal Decree at the government's discretion. Ordinary naturalization requires 7 years of continuous residence from the issue of a residence card; qualifying investors are exempt from that period.

A sub-decree of Dec. 1, 2025 revised the investment and donation thresholds. Its official text has not appeared on a government site yet, so anyone weighing this route should have a Phnom Penh lawyer who handles nationality cases confirm the current amounts. The route suits few nomads: it is a change of nationality, not a residence permit, and the 1996 law asks applicants to speak Khmer.

### How to choose

- **A trip of a few weeks:** a visa exemption, if the passport has one, or the tourist visa.
- **A base for months:** the E class visa with extensions, after confirming current terms.
- **A permanent move with capital:** the naturalization route, once the 2025 rules are published in full.

Prices in Cambodia are widely quoted in US dollars. Each range adds monthly spending for one person before rent to a one-bedroom apartment, from outside the center to the center. The figures are crowd-sourced and were last updated in September 2026.

### Monthly budgets for one person

- **Phnom Penh:** about \$980 to \$1,250 a month, combining [\$622 in living costs](https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/in/Phnom-Penh) with \$361 to \$630 in rent.
- **Siem Reap:** about \$790 to \$880, combining [\$550 in living costs](https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/in/Siem-Reap) with \$235 to \$334 in rent.
- **Sihanoukville:** rent data only, \$300 to \$400 for a [one-bedroom](https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/in/Sihanoukville), with too few prices for a monthly living-cost estimate.

Rent explains most of the gap between the capital and Siem Reap: a central one-bedroom in Phnom Penh costs nearly twice the Siem Reap figure, while living costs before rent differ by about \$70.

### What the figures leave out

The living-cost estimate covers everyday spending such as food, transport and utilities. Visa extension fees, health insurance, coworking and flights come on top, and none of them is in these ranges.

Three places cover most long stays, with very different costs.

### Phnom Penh

The capital suits people who want coworking, services and the most flights. It is also the most expensive: a central one-bedroom averaged \$630 a month in September 2026.

### Siem Reap

The gateway to Angkor suits people who want a smaller, slower base. Central rent averaged \$334 a month, a little over half the Phnom Penh figure, with living costs before rent around \$550.

### Sihanoukville

The coastal city suits people who want the sea. One-bedroom rents run \$300 to \$400, but too few prices are reported for a full budget, so costs there are harder to pin down.

Kampot is a smaller, slower riverside town near the coast, popular with long-stay visitors who want a quiet base within reach of Kep and the sea.

The border town of Poipet sits inside the zone near Thailand where the UK government advises against travel that isn't strictly necessary; see the safety notes below.

Mobile data is Cambodia's strong point. The median mobile download speed was [207.43 Mbps](https://www.speedtest.net/global-index/cambodia) in August 2026, well ahead of the fixed broadband median of 81.84 Mbps.

That gap makes a local SIM or eSIM a sensible backup, and sometimes the main connection, for video calls. Fixed-line speeds vary by building, so checking the connection before signing a lease pays off.

### Coworking

Phnom Penh has the most choice. [Impact Hub Phnom Penh](https://phnompenh.impacthub.net/) offers coworking space in the capital.

Cambodia decides tax residency by home or days. Under Article 5 of the 2023 [Law on Taxation](https://embindpp.gov.in/pdf/menu/Tax-Law-Cambodia-2023.pdf), a person is a resident if they live in or have their principal place of abode in Cambodia, or are present for more than 182 days in any 12-month period ending in the tax year.

The 12-month window rolls. Older descriptions of a calendar-year test describe the previous law.

### What residents are taxed on

Residents are taxed on income from Cambodian and foreign sources; non-residents only on Cambodian-source income (Article 4). For residents, salary tax covers foreign salary at progressive monthly rates from 0% to 20%, with a credit for tax already paid abroad when proof is provided (Articles 41, 47 and 50). Non-residents' Cambodian salary is taxed at a flat 20% withheld by the payer (Article 49).

### No special regime

No official source gives remote workers, retirees or naturalized investors a special tax regime.

### What to check

- Whether a stay will pass the day count in any 12-month window, or whether a Cambodian home makes a person resident sooner.
- How foreign salary or other foreign income would be declared, and what proof of foreign tax the credit needs.
- Whether the home country has a tax treaty with Cambodia.

The English text cited here is an unofficial translation hosted by India's embassy in Phnom Penh; the Khmer text governs, and a local tax adviser can confirm how it applies to a specific case.

### Arrival

All travelers can submit the [Cambodia e-Arrival form](https://immigration.gov.kh/) within seven days before arrival or on arrival, free of charge. Tourist visas are sold through the official e-Visa portal at evisa.gov.kh.

### Customs

Visitors should remove shoes and hats before entering a pagoda or the Royal Palace, dress modestly at sacred sites and leave shoes at the door of a home. The traditional greeting, the sampeah, presses the palms together with a slight bow.

### Safety

As of Sept. 23, 2026, UK travel advice [warns against all travel](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/cambodia) that isn't strictly necessary within 20 km of the land border with Thailand. Heavy fighting took place along parts of the border in 2025; a ceasefire was signed on Dec. 27, but the situation remains tense.

Land crossings between Cambodia and Thailand remain suspended, and unexploded ordnance, including landmines, remains in the border area. Overland trips to Thailand aren't possible for now.

## Frequently asked questions

Does Cambodia have a digital nomad visa?+

No. Long stays generally run on the ordinary (E class) visa, extended inside Cambodia by the General Department of Immigration. Extension lengths and fees change, so confirm them with the department before a long stay.

Who can enter Cambodia without a visa?+

No general visa-free entry. Ordinary passports of the 9 other ASEAN states (14 or 30 days), the Maldives (30 days) and Seychelles (15 days) are exempt; everyone else needs a Tourist Visa (T class), by e-Visa or on arrival, for a 30-day stay.

When does a remote worker become a Cambodian tax resident?+

On more than 182 days in Cambodia in any 12-month period, or sooner with a home or principal place of abode there. Residents are taxed on Cambodian and foreign income.

Can foreigners get Cambodian citizenship through investment?+

Investors and donors can apply for naturalization, which is granted by Royal Decree at the government's discretion. The 2025 thresholds haven't been published in an official text yet.

Is it safe to travel near the Thai border?+

The UK government advises against travel that isn't strictly necessary within 20 km of the land border with Thailand after fighting in 2025, and land crossings remain suspended.
