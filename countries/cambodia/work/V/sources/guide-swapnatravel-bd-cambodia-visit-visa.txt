Basic Document

01.

Scan copy of Passport (7 months validity) good quality
02.

Photo (Lab print and good quality scan copy)
03.

Personal / Company Bank statement of last 06 months with Bank Solvency Certificate (Minimum balance BDT 60,000 for each applicant.

Forwarding letter.
05.

Air ticket booking (Confirmed)
06.

Hotel booking (Confirmed)

Vaccine certificate (double dose)
09.

NID card scan copy (both sides)
