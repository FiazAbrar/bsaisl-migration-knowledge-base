Frequently Asked Questions

- VISAS-

Do I Need a Visa to Enter Cambodia? ▼
If you are not from one of the visa-exempt countries, you will need a Cambodia visa to enter Cambodia. You can check the list of visa-exempt countries here to see if your country qualifies.
How Can I Apply for a Cambodia Visa? ▼
You can apply for a Cambodia visa through the Royal Embassy of Cambodia in Berlin (via e-mail only) or apply directly on e-Visa portal (evisa.gov.kh), which is processed directly by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation. For more details, visit this link. The last option is Visa on Arrival at all international entry points (not every country is eligible). 
I heard there are two types of e-Visa. Is it true? ▼
Technically yes. Although both are electronic visas, there are a few key differences.

The e-Visa applied directly online is managed by the e-Visa office of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation in Phnom Penh. While it offers the convenience of applying yourself, it has limitations regarding entry points (Check this link). Furthermore, if you encounter any issues, you must contact their support team directly because the Embassy does not have access to or control over those applications, compounded by the time zone difference.

In contrast, the electronic visa obtained through the Embassy (via email) is valid at all entry points, just like a traditional sticker visa. Moreover, you will receive full support from our embassy in your local time zone.

- e-ARRIVAL -

What Is Cambodia e-Arrival? ▼
The Cambodia e-Arrival is a digital platform that replaces traditional paper forms for immigration, health declarations, and customs. For now, it applies only to airport arrival. 
When Can I Complete and Submit the Cambodia e-Arrival? ▼
You can complete the Cambodia e-Arrival not longer than 7 days before your arrival in Cambodia.
After Submitting the Cambodia e-Arrival, What Should I Do? ▼
Once you’ve completed and submitted the form, you’ll receive a QR code.
  • Save the QR code: Screenshot or save it. It remains accessible offline.
  • Print (optional): You can print it if you are worried that your phone battery may die.

Keep it ready to present upon request by the immigration officer or even by some airlines staff during check in.  

Do I Still Need to Submit the Cambodia e-Arrival if I Already Have a Valid Cambodia Visa? ▼
Yes. Cambodia e-Arrival is not a visa; it collects information about immigration, health and customs.
Is There a Fee for the Cambodia e-Arrival? ▼
No. The e-Arrival system is free via the official website or mobile application.
Do I Need an Internet Connection to Use the Cambodia e-Arrival System? ▼
Yes, you need an active connection to submit the form. After your QR code is generated, you can store and present it offline.
Can I Edit My Information After Submitting the Cambodia e-Arrival? ▼
Yes, you can edit and resubmit as long as your QR code hasn’t been scanned at the airport. Once it’s processed, no further changes can be made.
