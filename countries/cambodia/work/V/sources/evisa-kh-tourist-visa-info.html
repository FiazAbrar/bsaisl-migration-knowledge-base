Visa T
Enter Type Single entry only
Fee USD 30
Validity 3 months(starting from the date of issue)
Length of Stay 1 month
Processing time 3 business days
Requirement Passport validity of more than six months balance at the time of entry, a recent passport-size photo in digital format (JPEG or PNG format), a valid credit card (Visa/MasterCard).
