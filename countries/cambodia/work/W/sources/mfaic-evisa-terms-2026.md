# eVisa Kingdom of Cambodia (Official Government Website): Terms & Conditions Information
Source: https://www.evisa.gov.kh/information/terms_&_conditions/6 (Firecrawl scrape, 11 Nov 2026)

Last Updated: Jul 14, 2026

**1. Acceptance of Terms**

These Terms and Conditions govern the access to and use of the Official Cambodia e-Visa System ("the System"), administered by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation of the Kingdom of Cambodia ("the Ministry").

**2. Purpose of the System**

The System is established to facilitate the electronic submission, processing, issuance, modification, extension, and administration of visas under the authority of the Ministry.

**3. User Responsibilities**

Users shall:
- provide complete, accurate, and truthful information;
- upload valid and genuine supporting documents;
- ensure that all information remains accurate throughout the application process;

Users remain responsible for the accuracy of all information submitted, including applications submitted by authorized representatives.

**4. Visa Applications**

Submission of an application does not constitute approval of a visa.

The Ministry reserves the right to:
- approve or refuse any application;
- request additional information or documents;
- verify information with competent authorities;
- cancel or revoke a visa where justified under the laws and regulations of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

**5. Processing Time**

Processing times are estimates only and may vary depending on application completeness, verification requirements, public holidays, technical maintenance, or other relevant factors.

**6. Fees**

Visa fees apply only to categories prescribed by the Ministry.
- For fee-paying visa categories, the fees will be published on the System and, unless otherwise determined by the Ministry, are non-refundable once processing has commenced.
- The Ministry reserves the right to revise visa fees or exemptions at any time.

**7. Fraudulent Information**

The Ministry may refuse, cancel, or revoke a visa if an applicant submits false information or forged documents, impersonates another person, or misuses the System. Such cases may be referred to the competent authorities.
