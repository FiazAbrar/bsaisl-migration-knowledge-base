# Get Your e-Visa in 3 Easy Steps

**Apply Online** Filling out application forms is so easy with passport reader technology
**Payment** Pay safely using our wide range of secure payment options.
**Download** Receive your approved e-Visa via email or download from "Check & Change".

- Fast processing time within 3 business days.

# Get e-Visa Approval

1. Complete the secure online e-Visa application form.
2. Confirm your details and pay the visa fee.
3. Receive your approval letter within 3 business days.
4. Present your e-Visa certificate to immigration upon arrival.
5. Submit the Cambodia e-Arrival form online
    (required for all travelers, including visa holders) within 7 days before arrival to expedite entry.

# Submit e-Arrival

All travelers are required to fill in the Cambodia e-Arrival even if they have been granted VISA, to avoid waiting for submitting at immigration counter, we recommend to submit e-Arrival online within 7 days before their arrival to ensure smooth entry.

[Submit e-Arrival](https://www.arrival.gov.kh/)
