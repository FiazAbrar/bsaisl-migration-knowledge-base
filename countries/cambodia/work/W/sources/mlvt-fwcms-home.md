### Enterprise

This function is for company to register and request quota to use foreign worker and monitoring foreign workers.

[Login](https://fwcms.mlvt.gov.kh/authorization/login?type=employer) [Register](https://fwcms.mlvt.gov.kh/company/new_register)

### Employer

This function is only for foreign employers or those who represent the company named in patent tax.

[Login](https://fwcms.mlvt.gov.kh/authorization/login?type=self_register) [Register](https://fwcms.mlvt.gov.kh/workers/self/register?request_type=owner)

### Self-Employed

This Function is for self-employed business

[Login](https://fwcms.mlvt.gov.kh/authorization/login?type=self_register) [Register](https://fwcms.mlvt.gov.kh/workers/self/register)
