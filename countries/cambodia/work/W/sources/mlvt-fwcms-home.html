Welcome to Cambodia FWCMS

Welcome and thank you for visiting our new online Foreign Workers Centralized Management System (FWCMS). FWCMS is a web-based online management system of Ministry of Labour and Vocational Training, Cambodia. We are very pleased to provide you with all the services related online application, quota, foreigner work permit and one stop window services. We invite you to use all of our services and to explore ways that we can help you for convenience and easy to apply foreign worker quota as well as to apply the foreign work permit anywhere and anytime.

Announcement


1. Foreign Workers need to renew the 2024 foreigner work permit and foreign employment card for 2025 before the end of May 2025. For those who have NOT renewed their work permit for 2025 due to the date, it is subjected to a penalty or fine by Labour Law of Cambodia.

2. Foreign workers staying and working in the Kingdom of Cambodia in 2025 must urgently apply for a foreigner work permit and foreign employment card not later than 90 days from the first day of entry into the Kingdom of Cambodia.
