# Kyrgyzstan doc: coverage audit

Audits of CLAUDE.md step 9 on doc.md, using only facts already in facts.csv. 1,099 facts; 913 cited in doc.md; 186 uncited (reviewed at the end).

Summary of the three audits:
- **Migrant's questions:** stay, earnings, rights, jail, bank, pension, family and carry-home are all covered. Gaps: SIM operators and cost; late or impossible registration (facts exist, uncited); the employer's duty to explain rights (fact exists, uncited).
- **Aim per visa:** the transit visa (type TR) is missing from the doc although three facts cover it; no fee is given for the study (S) or family (FF) visa; nothing says whether a refused visit or study visa can be appealed; the security-service call-back on doubtful documents is uncited. Corridor growth: the October 2026 sector list and the April 2024 Kyrgyz recruiting visit to Dhaka are uncited. Comparison with Pakistan's government channel is a gap.
- **Insider test:** the big names, scandals, crackdowns, costs and scams are in. Missing: the US Tier 2 Watch List rating, the employer's fine for illegal hiring and the rise in illegal-hiring cases, the Kyrgyz company named in the 2022 Al-Jabeen case, the skills mismatch the GKNB reported in 2024, and the 2023 OEP job titles.

new: Kyrgyzstan has two short visit visas: the tourist visa (type TS, up to 30 days) and the business visa (type B, up to 90 days), which also covers medical treatment [V0013, V0015, V0018]. A transit visa (type TR), for passing through Kyrgyzstan to a third country, needs a ticket to your final destination or that country's visa or permit, is valid up to 3 calendar days, and costs 1,783 som or USD 25 (about Tk 2,500 to 3,100) [V0035, V0036, V0076, G0003, G0002].
