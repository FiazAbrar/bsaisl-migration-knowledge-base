# 180 Bangladeshis to be sent back from Kyrgyzstan

They will be flown back to Dhaka on Tuesday

File image of Ministry of Foreign Affairs. Photo: Collected

[![Tribune Desk](https://www.dhakatribune.com/profile/133/picture/)](https://www.dhakatribune.com/author/tribune-desk-1)[Tribune Desk](https://www.dhakatribune.com/author/tribune-desk-1)

Publish : 08 Sep 2025, 11:38 PMUpdate : 08 Sep 2025, 11:38 PM

The government of Kyrgyzstan is set to repatriate 180 Bangladeshi nationals who became irregular after travelling to the Central Asian country.

They will be flown back to Dhaka on Tuesday, said the Ministry of Foreign Affairs on Monday.

According to the Bangladesh Embassy in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, the flight had initially been scheduled to depart on Monday but was later cancelled.
