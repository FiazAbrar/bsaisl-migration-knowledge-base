## What can you do with Western Union?

### Send money abroad

With Western Union you can send money from Kyrgyzstan at [agent location](/global-services/find-locations?WUCountry=kg&WULanguage=en) to over 200 countries and territories worldwide.

### Pick up Cash

When you are expecting to receive money in Kyrgyzstan you can pick it up in cash at any Western Union location.

#### Always near you

Whether you are in Bishkek, Osh or Jalal-Abad – you will always find a Western Union location near you. Got to the nearest agent location to pick up cash.

## Frequently asked questions

### What do I need to bring to pick up money at an agent location?

Bring a valid government-issued ID and the tracking number (MTCN).

### How can I find out if a money transfer is ready for me to pick up?

Visit our website and select [Track Transfer](/global-service/track-transfer). You’ll need the name of the person who sent you the money and the tracking number (MTCN).

### How can I exchange US-Dollars to Kyrgyzstani Som?

If you are traveling to Kyrgyzstan, you can send money with Western Union to pick up Kyrgyzstani Som at [any agent location](/global-services/find-locations?WUCountry=kg&WULanguage=en) in Kyrgyzstan.

¹ Transfer speed and fund availability may be affected by factors such as service choice, amount, destination, currency, regulations, ID requirements, and system availability of our banking partners. See Terms for details.
