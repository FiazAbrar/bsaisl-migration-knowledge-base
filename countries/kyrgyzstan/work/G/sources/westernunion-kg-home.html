Frequently asked questions

What do I need to bring to pick up money at an agent location?

Bring a valid government-issued ID and the tracking number (MTCN).

How can I find out if a money transfer is ready for me to pick up?

Visit our website and select Track Transfer. You’ll need the name of the person who sent you the money and the tracking number (MTCN).

How can I exchange US-Dollars to Kyrgyzstani Som?

If you are traveling to Kyrgyzstan, you can send money with Western Union to pick up Kyrgyzstani Som at any agent location in Kyrgyzstan.
