Events

[edit]
  • 16 February – President Japarov dismisses his ministers for transportation, environment and emergency situations.[1]
  • 8 March – Two people are killed and 19 people are injured following a mining incident in Samarkandek, Batken Region.[2]
  • 27 March – President Japarov announces that the country has started printing out their national currency.[3][4][5]
  • 3 April – The Kyrgyz Ministry of Emergency Situations says that Kyrgyzstan has dispatched over 100 tons of humanitarian aid to Iran. The shipment is ordered by President Japarov.[6]
  • 12 May – Former security chief Kamchybek Tashiev and seven others are arrested on charges of planning a coup.[7]
  • 3 June – Kyrgyzstan is elected to a rotating seat at the United Nations Security Council.[8]
  • 4 August – President Japarov signs a law banning the use of cremation for deceased human bodies.[9][10]
