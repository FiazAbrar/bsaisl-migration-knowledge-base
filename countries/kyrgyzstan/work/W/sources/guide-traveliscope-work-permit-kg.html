Documents

What documents are required

We prepare the petition and manage the filing. Here is what is needed — for a new permit and for a one-year extension.

New permit — 2 months

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months
  • White-background photo
  • Company invitation letter
  • Company guarantee letter
  • Company tax letter

Extension — 1 year

  • Valid 2-month visa
  • Passport
  • White-background photo
  • One-year invitation letter
  • Employment contract
  • Guarantee letter
  • Medical certificate (form 086)
  • HIV/AIDS certificate
  • Company tax letter
  • Protocol (if applicable)
  • Protocol payment receipt (if applicable)

How do I apply for a Kyrgyzstan work permit for a foreign employee?+
Through the Unified Work Permit on the evisa.e-gov.kg portal. You grant us a power of attorney, we prepare the petition and draft employment contract, file the application and follow it through to issuance. You provide the employee passport, a photo and basic details.
How much does a Kyrgyzstan work permit cost?+
It depends on the permit term (2-month or 1-year) and the number of employees. Send a request and we will prepare a transparent quote for your case, with no hidden fees.
How long does it take to get a Kyrgyzstan work permit?+
It depends on the permit type and current portal workload — the short-term permit is faster than the annual one. We give you exact timing for your case when we quote.
What is the unified work permit in Kyrgyzstan?+
A single electronic document in force since 1 July 2025. It grants a foreign national entry, stay and the right to work at once, issued for up to 60 days or for 1 year via evisa.e-gov.kg.
Do EAEU citizens need a work permit in Kyrgyzstan?+
Citizens of EAEU countries do not need a work permit. Workers from Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka and other non-EAEU countries do — and we process it for them.
What documents are required for a Kyrgyzstan work permit?+
A power of attorney to us, the employee passport and a photo, and details for the draft employment contract. The medical (HIV) certificate is provided by the employee or arranged on request.
Can a company or agency apply for a work permit on behalf of a worker?+
Yes. Under power of attorney we process the permit for an employer or for a recruitment agency — you keep the client while we handle the filing and follow-through.
