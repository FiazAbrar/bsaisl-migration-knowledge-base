Work visa — what you need to know
Intended for all foreigners planning to work in Kyrgyzstan as employees or as individual entrepreneurs.

It is obtained online through the official e-visa portal. The employer or the applicant themselves (if they have individual entrepreneur status) registers in their 'Personal Account' and submits the application.

For citizens of visa-exempt countries, the legislation provides the possibility to enter without a visa and then legally remain by obtaining the unified permit after arrival.
Features
For employees, the process is initiated by the employer; for sole proprietors, by the applicant themselves
Annual quotas apply
The visa combines permission for entry, stay, and work
Family members must apply for separate documents
Benefits
Issued online
Allows working for multiple employers simultaneously (up to 3)
Can be renewed annually
Path to permanent residency after 5 years of residence

What additional documents may be required?
Documents required vary depending on individual circumstances, specific visa conditions, the place, and the method of application.
*Foreign official documents must be apostilled and legalized in accordance with the regulations of the country whose consular or diplomatic mission they are being submitted to. **Prior to submitting the documents, it is strongly advised to verify the specific procedures and requirements with the relevant mission.
