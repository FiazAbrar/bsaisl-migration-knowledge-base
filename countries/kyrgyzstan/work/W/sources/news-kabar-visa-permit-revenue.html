Revenues from visas and unified permits to Kyrgyzstan amount 5 billion soms

Society 666 17 December 2025 17:14
Kabar Agency

Bishkek, Dec. 17, 2025. /Kabar/. Overall, revenues from visas and unified permits exceeded 5 billion soms, making a significant contribution to the state budget. This was announced today at briefing by Seitek Zhumakadyr uulu, Director of the consular department of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

According to him, a total of 48,439 unified permits have been issued through the "Electronic Visa" portal to date. "Foreign workers are primarily employed in industry, construction, transportation, and communications. According to monitoring results, their share is 56.2%. In trade and services, 32.6%.

Looking specifically at financial and economic indicators, the following revenues were received for the first 11 months of this year:

from unified permits – 1.19 billion soms;

from guarantee payments – 234.3 million soms," Zhumakadyr uulu noted.
