The Kyrgyz Republic is a State Party to the Refugee Convention and its Protocol, and has an established Refugee
Law and national asylum procedures.

In 2019 the Kyrgyz Republic become the first country in the world to resolve all its known cases of statelessness, but
has not yet acceded to the UN Statelessness Conventions. In 2024, the Kyrgyz Republic joined Kazakhstan,
Tajikistan, Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan in adopting the Ashgabat Declaration on Ending Statelessness in Centra
Asia.

Reaffirming its commitment to protecting forcibly displaced and stateless people, authorities, academia, NGOs and
the UN in the Kyrgyz Republic have made 14 pledges to the objectives of the Global Compact on Refugees since
2019 - four of which have already been fully implemented.

The UNHCR National Office the Kyrgyz Republic is based in Bishkek.
