# V findings: non-work visas, family, children (Kyrgyzstan)

Agent V, 10 November 2026. Facts V0001-V0167 in `countries/kyrgyzstan/work/V/facts.csv`; sources in `work/V/sources.csv` and `work/V/sources/`.

**The legal frame (read first).** Since 30 April 2026 visas, the Unified Permit (единое разрешение, the single stay-and-work permit) and the resident card (резидент-карта) are governed by a Temporary Regulation (Временное положение) approved by Cabinet decree No. 312, valid until 31 December 2026 [V0010]. It suspends the 2017 visa Instruction (decree No. 155) until that date [V0011], so the 2017 text (saved as `refworld-kg-visa-instruction-2017-155-ru`) is NOT cited for current rules. ## a. Draft doc text

## Visas not open to Bangladeshis

- **Visa-free entry:** Bangladesh is not on the visa-free list; it is on the list of countries that get short-term visas in a simplified way [V0155, V0001, V0003].
- **Simplified tourist visa without invitation, for Bangladeshis living in Bangladesh:** The simplified route covers only Bangladeshis holding a valid resident card of the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Brunei, Oman or Bahrain (see Visit visa, Step 1) [V0002].
- **7-day visa-free stay with a long-term US, UK or Schengen visa:** Chinese and Indian citizens only [V0005].
- **Investment visa (type I):** Needs a contribution of at least 10 million som to the Kyrgyz economy [V0040].
- **Sapar visa (type J):** Only on a reasoned request from a Kyrgyz state or local government body [V0039].
- **Digital nomad visa (type DN):** Only for software and ICT professionals, on the request of the digitalisation authority [V0041].
- **Meken visa (type M):** Ethnic Kyrgyz and people born in Kyrgyzstan or the Kyrgyz SSR only [V0168].
- **Religious visa (type R):** Missionaries with a permit from the state religious affairs body only [V0042].
- **Driver's visa (type T):** Truck drivers on international freight only [V0043].
- **Diplomatic (D) and official (O) visas:** Diplomatic and service passports only [V0044].


## Visit visa

Kyrgyzstan has two short visit visas: the tourist visa (type TS, up to 30 days) and the business visa (type B, up to 90 days, also used for medical treatment) [V0013, V0015, V0018]. Neither lets you work or earn money, and a tourist visa cannot be switched to a work status inside the country (see Changing visa type) [V0016, V0045]. Visas are issued by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MFA) in Bishkek, Kyrgyz embassies and consulates, and online through the MFA's e-visa portal, evisa.e-gov.kg [V0012, V0093]. Bangladesh has no Kyrgyz visa-free deal: every Bangladeshi needs a visa [V0155, V0003].

### Step 1: Check which route you are on

1. **If you hold a resident card (iqama, Emirates ID residence) of the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Brunei, Oman or Bahrain,** you can get a tourist visa of up to 1 month in the simplified way: on your own application, with no invitation and no security vetting [V0002, V0022, V0023]. The MFA has said this also works on arrival at Manas airport in Bishkek [V0100].

   > **Need to know**
   > - In 2026, does the MFA visa point at Manas airport actually issue tourist visas on arrival to Bangladeshis with a Gulf resident card, and at what fee?

2. **If you live in Bangladesh,** you cannot simply apply for a tourist visa yourself in practice. In September 2021 the MFA stopped self-applications for tourist visas from Bangladesh, India, Pakistan and Nepal and required an invitation from a host organisation instead, as for business visas [V0094, V0095]. A Bishkek tour operator said people from these countries were coming as "tourists" in transit to work visas in the UAE or Saudi Arabia [V0097]. The 2026 rules still let a tourist visa be based either on a Kyrgyz tour company's letter or on your own request [V0014].

   > **Need to know**
   > - On evisa.e-gov.kg in November 2026, what does "Do I need visa?" show for Bangladesh: is the tourist e-visa open to self-application, or only with a tour company's letter?
   > - What does a Bishkek tour company charge a Bangladeshi for a tourist-visa letter, and does it require a booked tour?

3. **For business or medical visits,** a company registered in Kyrgyzstan (business) or a Kyrgyz clinic (treatment) must send a written request, with its founding and registration documents or treatment papers [V0017, V0018].
4. **If you are going to look for work, don't use a visit visa.** A business visa gives no right to work or run a business [V0016], and a tourist visa can't be changed to another visa inside Kyrgyzstan [V0045]. Work needs a Unified Permit arranged by the employer (see Work visa: company worker, Step 3) [V0048].

### Step 2: Apply on the e-visa portal

You'll need:
- a passport valid at least 6 months after the visa ends, with at least 2 free pages [V0083];
- a colour photo on a white background (35x45 mm for an embassy application) [V0034];
- scans of your documents; documents not in Kyrgyz or Russian must be translated [V0089];
- for the simplified route, a scan of your Gulf or Brunei resident card [V0002];
- the inviting company's or clinic's letter, or the tour company's letter, if you have one [V0014, V0017];
- a Visa or MasterCard debit or credit card open for international payments; it need not be in your name [V0081, V0093].

1. On evisa.e-gov.kg, open "Get information", then "Do I need visa?", and choose Bangladesh to see the current rule [V0090].
2. Press "Apply for a visa", choose your country, passport type and visa type, and fill in the form in English [V0080].
3. Open the verification email within 72 hours, or the application is cancelled [V0088].
4. Pay by card [V0093].
5. Wait: an e-visa takes up to 5 working days; urgent processing up to 2 working days [V0019]. Ordinary applications also go to the national security body for up to 4 working days (1 day at the urgent rate) [V0021].
6. The e-visa arrives by email; print it [V0031].

**Fees (Cabinet decree No. 839):**
- single-entry visa of up to 1 month: 3,589 som or USD 50 [V0156];
- multiple-entry visa of up to 1 month: 4,315 som or USD 60 [V0157];
- extending a single-entry visa: 704 som or USD 10 per 30 days [V0158];
- an e-visa issued within two working days: 200% more [V0078].

> **Need to know**
> - What does the e-visa portal actually charge a Bangladeshi for a 30-day single-entry tourist or business e-visa in November 2026 (the portal says only that the fee "depends on visa category, type and period")?

**If it fails:** The reasons for a refusal are not given, and the fee is not refunded [V0025, V0026, V0082]. If the portal returns your application for corrections, you get an email: go to evisa.e-gov.kg, Services, "Check the status", enter the reference number, correct the lines marked in red and press Submit [V0087]. Fix it within 30 calendar days or it is cancelled [V0027]. If you chose the wrong visa category, you can ask for a refund within 15 working days [V0028]. After a refusal you may apply again once your documents are in order [V0007]. The e-visa helpline is +996 703 428 865 or +996 999 312 002 [V0086].

**Check it is real:** On evisa.e-gov.kg, Services, "Check status", enter your reference number [V0091]. At the border the officer checks the visa in the migration database, so show the same passport the visa was issued on [V0030]. Don't buy your air ticket until the e-visa is approved [V0085].

> **Need to know**
> - Can a third party (relative, employer in Bangladesh) verify a Kyrgyz e-visa by its number or QR code without the applicant's reference number?

### Step 3: At the border and after arrival

1. **Tell the border officer the true purpose of your visit:** it is recorded in the migration database [V0009].
2. **Register your address within 5 working days of arrival** (see Work visa: company worker, Step 7) [V0084].
3. **Leave before the visa ends.** An overstayer can leave only on an exit visa, which carries a fee that rises with the overstay (see What can get you jailed or deported) [V0056].

**Scams:**
- Agents sell "Kyrgyz tourist visas" as a route to jobs; a visit visa gives no right to work [V0016, V0097].
- If the agent applies for you, insist on your own reference number and check the status yourself (Visit visa, Step 2, item 6) [V0091].

## Student visa

Foreign students enter on a study visa (type S), valid up to 90 days, and then switch to a resident card of up to one year, renewed through the university [V0049, V0058, V0057]. Most foreign students in Kyrgyzstan study medicine: in 2022, 28,239 foreigners made up 77% of students at 22 medical universities [V0123]. In 2024 there were at least 800, and by one student's count over 1,000, Bangladeshi medical students [V0126, V0135].

### Step 1: Choose an accredited university, and plan for BMDC

1. **Check the university passed state accreditation in 2026.** A 2025 presidential decree gave the state a monopoly on medical education; private medical schools had to pass state accreditation by 1 June 2026, and only accreditation gives the right to issue diplomas [V0115, V0116]. In May 2026:
   - accredited for six years: Osh State University; International University of Kyrgyzstan (which runs the International School of Medicine); International Higher School of Medicine; Asian International University (named after S. Tentishev) [V0105];
   - accredited for one year only: Kyrgyz National University; Jalal-Abad State University; Kyrgyz-Uzbek International University; Issyk-Kul State University; Jalal-Abad International University; University of South Asia; International European University [V0106];
   - not subject to accreditation: Kyrgyz State Medical Academy (KSMA, Akhunbaev); Kyrgyz-Russian Slavic University; Kyrgyz-Turkish Manas University [V0104];
   - failed: Avicenna; ADAM; Royal Metropolitan; Altamimi; Ala-Too; International Medical University; Kyrgyz Medical-Dental Institute; Salymbekov; Eurasian International; Bishkek International Medical Institute; International University of Medicine and Science; Central Asian International Medical University; Osh International Medical University [V0107].

   Schools that failed may only run pre-university courses or clinical bases [V0117]. The study visa itself requires proof of the university's state accreditation, and for medical schools the medical accreditation [V0051].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Where is the Ministry of Health's official list of accredited medical universities published, and is the one-year accreditation for the seven universities above being renewed in 2027?

2. **If you are already enrolled at a school that failed,** the Ministry of Health (order No. 912 of 3 August 2026) moved students free of charge to accredited schools: Royal Metropolitan to the University of South Asia; Salymbekov and ADAM to the International Higher School of Medicine; Avicenna and Altamimi to the International European University; Eurasian and International Medical University to the International University of Kyrgyzstan; Bishkek International Medical Institute and the International University of Medicine and Science to Asian International University; Osh International Medical University to Osh State University [V0108, V0110, V0111, V0164, V0112, V0165, V0113, V0114]. Applications closed on 10 August 2026 [V0109].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What happened to Bangladeshi students who missed the 10 August 2026 transfer deadline, and does BMDC accept a degree finished at the receiving university?

3. **Apply only from the first year.** A December 2025 draft rule would let foreigners study medicine only from the first year, with no transfer into senior years [V0121, V0122].
4. **Apply through the state channel, not only an agent.** The state enterprise "Centre for admission of foreign students" (ГП «Центр по приему иностранных студентов») and a single online platform built by Tunduk take foreign applications, list universities, fees and teaching language, and sell insurance, in English and Russian [V0166, V0118, V0119, V0120].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What is the web address of the single admission platform for foreign applicants, and is it the only channel for medical admission in 2026-27?

5. **Get BMDC's Eligibility Certificate before you go.** The Bangladesh Medical and Dental Council (BMDC) asks for its Eligibility Certificate when you later sit its qualifying exam [V0140]. To practise in Bangladesh you must pass BMDC's Registration Qualifying Examination (RQE), open only if your degree is considered equivalent to a Bangladeshi MBBS or BDS [V0137, V0138]. After passing you get provisional registration for internship, and full registration after it [V0139]. No college is obliged to give you an internship; some colleges outside Dhaka have demanded Tk 80,000 to 1 lakh [V0148, V0147].

   > **Need to know**
   > - How do you apply for the BMDC Eligibility Certificate (form, fee, documents, office), and for which session years is it compulsory?

**Scams:** Agents take most of the gap between real tuition (USD 2,000-3,000 a year in 2022) and what students pay [V0124, V0125]. Agents still sell places at schools that failed accreditation or promise transfers into senior years [V0107, V0121].

> **Need to know**
> - What do Dhaka agents charge in 2026 for a Kyrgyz MBBS place (tuition, hostel, "processing")?

### Step 2: The visa

You'll need:
- a scan of your passport [V0050];
- a written request from the university's head, with its founding and registration documents [V0050];
- proof of the university's state (and medical) accreditation [V0051].

The university applies, or you apply on evisa.e-gov.kg as in Visit visa, Step 2 [V0062, V0093]. The study visa is valid up to 90 days [V0049]. Documents issued in Bangladesh must be apostilled or legalised, with a notarised translation into Kyrgyz or Russian [V0029].

### Step 3: Resident card after arrival

Within the 90 days, the university or you apply for a resident card, which lets you stay up to one year [V0062, V0057, V0059]. You'll need:
- a certified copy of your study contract stating the study period [V0052];
- an HIV-negative certificate from the Republican Centre for HIV control in Kyrgyzstan [V0053];
- medical certificate form 086/U and compulsory medical insurance [V0054];
- consent to open a special bank account; the card itself is a bank debit card with a QR code, whose fees follow the bank's tariffs [V0063, V0161, V0162];
- a phone number in your own name and your actual address; report any change of address in your personal account [V0163].

A decision takes 10 working days; a refusal can be appealed in court [V0064, V0065]. Extending a single-entry student visa costs 352 som (USD 5) per 30 days, and a multiple-entry one 704 som (USD 10) [V0159, V0160].

> **Need to know**
> - What do students pay in 2026 for the resident card itself (the Temporary Regulation's general fee annex is not published in the saved text)?
> - Can a foreign student work part time in Kyrgyzstan, with or without a permit?

### When studies end

If you finish, stop studying or are expelled, you get an exit visa valid up to 10 days to leave [V0055, V0056].

### Safety

In May 2024 mobs in Bishkek attacked foreign students, entering medical hostels and using social media apps to find them; at least 28 people were hurt on the night of 17-18 May [V0128, V0129, V0131]. Bangladeshi students at Royal Metropolitan and the International School of Medicine were caught up, and Bangladeshi, Indian and Pakistani students often share hostels [V0130, V0134, V0136]. Bangladesh has no embassy in Kyrgyzstan; the embassy in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, is accredited to it and opened a 24-hour emergency line, +998 93 000 9780, in May 2024 (see Contacts) [V0127, V0132, V0133].

## Family

A worker or student can bring a spouse, children under 18 and dependent parents on a foreigner's family visa (type FF), valid up to 90 days, then a resident card [V0066, V0067, V0068, V0060]. The family member may not work [V0068].

1. **Check you qualify as the sponsor:** you must be in Kyrgyzstan on a Unified Permit, resident card, residence permit, or a study, special work, relatives, official, investment or digital nomad visa [V0067].
2. **Prepare the papers:** your family member's passport scan; your application; your employer's request (if a company sponsors you); proof of the relationship (marriage and birth certificates); proof of your own legal stay [V0069]. Bangladeshi certificates need apostille or legalisation and a notarised translation into Kyrgyz or Russian [V0029].
3. **Apply on evisa.e-gov.kg** (the employer through its personal account, or you) [V0062].
4. **After arrival, apply for the family member's resident card** with the HIV certificate, form 086/U and medical insurance [V0060, V0053, V0054].

> **Need to know**
> - Is there a minimum salary or housing requirement for a worker sponsoring family, and what does the family resident card cost?
> - Have any Bangladeshi workers in Kyrgyzstan actually brought family on an FF visa?

**Spouse of a Kyrgyz citizen:** the relatives visa (type RL, up to 90 days) needs the Kyrgyz spouse's request with passport scan, the marriage certificate and proof of means, followed by a resident card [V0070, V0071, V0072, V0073, V0061]. Marriage does not change either spouse's citizenship [V0152]. A foreign spouse or child of a Kyrgyz citizen who overstayed gets a one-time entry to regularise within 30 working days [V0008].

## Changing visa type

Inside Kyrgyzstan you can be issued any visa that matches your real purpose, including after a visa-free entry, but not a tourist, transit or religious visa [V0045, V0006]. In practice the person or company that invited you files the request at least 5 working days before your current visa ends [V0046, V0047]. A worker needs a Unified Permit from the employer, not a visa change on their own (see Work visa: company worker, Step 3) [V0048].

> **Need to know**
> - Can a Bangladeshi on a tourist or business visa get a Unified Permit inside Kyrgyzstan without leaving, and do agents sell this "conversion"?

## Having a child in Kyrgyzstan

1. **The child is Bangladeshi, not Kyrgyz.** Kyrgyz law gives citizenship by birth in Kyrgyzstan to children of Kyrgyz citizens, stateless parents or ethnic-Kyrgyz parents, not to children of two Bangladeshi parents [V0149, V0150, V0151]. The child is Bangladeshi by descent if the father or mother is Bangladeshi [V0153].
2. **Register the birth in Kyrgyzstan** and get the Kyrgyz birth certificate.

   > **Need to know**
   > - Where and within what deadline do foreign parents register a birth in Kyrgyzstan (civil registry office or public service centre), and what documents and fee are needed?

3. **Get the child a visa within 180 days of birth.** Apply for a visa matching your own status, without leaving the country; the parents sign the form [V0074, V0075].
4. **Register the birth with the Bangladesh Embassy in Tashkent** and apply for the child's passport (see Contacts) [V0127]. If the Bangladeshi parent is a citizen by descent only, the birth must be registered at the Bangladesh mission accredited to Kyrgyzstan for the child to be Bangladeshi [V0154].
