## Sending money home

**Send through a bank or a money-transfer company on the register of the NBKR (National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic, the central bank), never hundi** [G0004]. Hundi (sending money through unlicensed private agents outside the banking system) is illegal in Bangladesh, and hundi brokers settle their deals through bKash and Nagad, Bangladesh's mobile-money wallets [G0022, G0023]. By June 2026 Bangladesh's Financial Intelligence Unit (BFIU) had frozen or suspended about 55,000 mobile-money accounts linked to digital hundi and online gambling, and in August 2026 it decided to freeze about 14,000 more [G0024]. Kyrgyzstan prosecutes informal channels too: in May 2025 the GKNB (State Committee for National Security, Kyrgyzstan's security service) detained two men who had moved more than USD 20 million (about Tk 249 crore) through Bishkek exchange points outside official records and kept some clients' money, including USD 2 million (about Tk 24.9 crore) handed over by one man [G0025, G0002]. Money sent legally earns the family Bangladesh's 2.5% cash incentive, kept in the FY2026-27 budget, and in some cases a further 2.5% from the bank [G0021, G0017]. Bangladesh Bank publishes no separate figure for money sent from Kyrgyzstan; it counts it under "Others" [G0020].

### Your salary and a bank account

Companies, and since 1 January 2026 individual entrepreneurs, must pay a foreign worker's wages by bank transfer to a card, not in cash [L0185, W0105, L0129]. The resident card (the plastic permit that follows the first Unified Permit) works as a bank card, a special bank account is opened for it, and your wages are paid onto it (Work visa: company worker, Step 7: After arrival) [W0075, W0083]. A bank can also open a current account of your own for a foreigner who doesn't live permanently in Kyrgyzstan (a "non-resident"); Bakai Bank, for example, does [G0045].

You'll need [G0045]:
- your passport and a copy;
- where the bank asks for it, the document that gives you the right to stay in Kyrgyzstan (the Unified Permit or resident card) and a copy.

1. **Fill in the bank's papers:** a client form, an application, a signature card and the account agreement [G0045].
2. **Show the originals** of the documents you copied, for comparison [G0045].

### How to send

**The channels:**
- **Transfer systems licensed in Kyrgyzstan.** The NBKR's register lists the operators allowed to send money; Western Union (registered 2018), MoneyGram (2014) and Ria Money Transfer (2017) are on it, among 20 international systems in July 2026, with Zolotaya Korona, Unistream and Hello [G0004, G0005, G0006, G0007, G0008].
- **Banks.** Optima Bank, for example, sends money without a bank account through Western Union, Zolotaya Korona, MoneyGram, Astrasend and Ria, and to bank accounts abroad through SWIFT (the international bank-transfer network), at all its branches [G0009]. Western Union has agents in Bishkek, Osh and Jalal-Abad [G0012].
- **Bank apps.** MBANK's app sends money to 160 countries, to phone numbers, mobile wallets and bank accounts; the fee depends on the bank's partner [G0018].
- **Straight to bKash: no Kyrgyz partner.** bKash's list of countries with partners that pay into a bKash wallet does not include Kyrgyzstan (checked 10 November 2026) [G0013]. Profee, a transfer app, advertises card payments from Kyrgyzstan to bKash, Nagad and Rocket wallets, but it is not among the 20 international operators on the NBKR register [G0019, G0004, G0008].

You'll need:
- the receiver's surname, first name and patronymic, and his bank account number, or if he has no account, his identity document details [G0010];
- for a bKash wallet, if your channel pays into bKash, the receiver's bKash number and full name exactly as registered when the account was opened [G0015].

1. **Check the company is licensed.** On [nbkr.kg](https://www.nbkr.kg/index1.jsp?item=2665&lang=RUS), open "Реестр международных и локальных систем денежных переводов" (register of international and local money transfer systems) and look under "Операторы международных систем денежных переводов" (operators of international money transfer systems) [G0004].
2. **Change som into dollars only at a bank or licensed exchange bureau.** In February 2026 the NBKR fined 14 people in Bishkek 17,500 som (about Tk 24,900) each for changing cash currency without a licence [G0026, G0003].
3. **Send.**
   1. At a bank branch, write the receiver's exact details on the bank's application form, hand over the money and pay the commission at the bank's tariff [G0010, G0011].
   2. In the MBANK app, tap Payments, then Transfers, then "В другую страну" (to another country), then choose the transfer type and payment service [G0018].
4. **The family collects.** A bKash wallet takes up to Tk 2.5 lakh (about USD 2,000) per transfer, any number of times, and cashes out at Tk 7 per thousand at BRAC Bank, City Bank and Q-Cash ATMs; the 2.5% government incentive is added [G0016, G0017, G0002].

### Saving at home

- **Wage Earner Development Bond.** A 5-year Bangladesh government bond for workers abroad, sold at authorised-dealer bank branches in Bangladesh and at Bangladeshi banks' branches and exchange houses abroad, from Tk 25,000 to Tk 50 lakh, with no limit on how much you buy [G0027]. It pays 12% simple profit on holdings up to Tk 15 lakh (11% for Tk 15-30 lakh, 10% for Tk 30-50 lakh, 9% above), every six months; the profit is income-tax free and the principal can be taken in US dollars [G0028].
- **Probash pension.** Bangladesh's pension scheme for migrants takes monthly payments from abroad and pays a monthly pension from age 60 (see Your rights at work, Probash pension (Bangladesh)) [L0153, L0157].

## If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job

In Kyrgyzstan the employer is liable for work injuries, and a claim for them goes straight to court [L0056, L0070]. In Bangladesh the Wage Earners' Welfare Board (the Welfare Board, the government board for migrants' welfare) pays grants to injured workers and to dead workers' families, and runs the Probashi call centre, 16135 [L0140, L0149, B0023]. Its grants go only to workers who left with BMET (Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training) clearance or are Board members, and the Tk 10 lakh migrant-worker insurance only to those who left with BMET clearance [L0140, L0150, B0010]. Bangladesh has no embassy in Kyrgyzstan; the Embassy in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, covers it (see The Bangladesh Embassy in Tashkent) [B0120].

> **Need to know**
> - How can a Bangladeshi in Kyrgyzstan who left without BMET clearance become a Welfare Board member, and does the Embassy in Tashkent enrol members?

### If you are injured or fall ill

1. **Get treated.** The employer is responsible for work injuries, including on transport it provides to and from work [L0056]. Unless agreed otherwise in writing, the side that invited you bears your upkeep and medical care [L0099]. Sick pay is under Your rights at work [L0054].
2. **Ask the employer for a work-accident report.** If it refuses, or you disagree with what it says, take it to court [L0071].
3. **Claim compensation in court.** It covers lost earnings, extra costs, a one-off sum set by the Cabinet of Ministers for disability, and moral damage, and can't be refused entirely even if you were careless [L0057, L0059, L0058, L0070]. A Kushtia welder injured at work, without a contract, got no help from his employer, went four months unpaid, surrendered to the police and spent over three months in jail [U0015, L0177, L0175].
4. **Too ill to travel alone?** A free emergency exit visa (type E) is issued on a medical opinion and a petition from the health authority [L0126, W0032].
5. **Going home.** The Welfare Board's airport desks take sick returnees to hospital, with a free ambulance from Dhaka or Chattogram airport [B0016, B0047].
6. **Claim the treatment grant in Bangladesh.** The Welfare Board pays up to Tk 2 lakh for treatment to workers who come home disabled or critically ill, if they had BMET clearance or Board membership [L0149, L0150].

   You'll need [L0151]:
   - medical reports and prescriptions;
   - a bank statement;
   - a copy of the arrival stamp in your passport, and a passport copy.

   Apply within 1 year of coming home; a committee sees you on a WhatsApp video call [L0151, L0152].

### If a worker dies

These steps are for a relative or friend in Kyrgyzstan and the family in Bangladesh.

1. **Ask the Welfare Board to bring the body home.** The family applies to the Welfare Board, which takes the family's wishes and sends them to the Bangladesh mission; call 16135 (free in Bangladesh) or +880 9610102030 from abroad [L0147, L0148]. The Embassy in Tashkent's website lists "Repatriation of Dead Body" as a service, but the page is empty [B0133].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Who pays to fly a body from Bishkek to Dhaka, and what does it cost in 2026?
   > - Does the Embassy in Tashkent issue the NOC (no-objection certificate) to send a body home from Kyrgyzstan, and how many days does it take?
   > - Which Kyrgyz office issues the death certificate for a foreigner, and how does a family arrange burial in Kyrgyzstan instead?

2. **Claim from the employer.** The employer pays the family a funeral benefit, and the wages the worker had not received, within a week of the family giving it the documents [L0055, L0049, L0050]. For a death at work, his dependants get his average monthly earnings minus his own share, plus the Cabinet's one-off compensation; children under 18 count as dependants without proof [L0060, L0059, L0061].
3. **At Dhaka airport,** the Welfare Board gives a Tk 35,000 cheque for transport and burial, against an heirs' certificate (warish sanad) from the Union Parishad chairman, mayor or councillor, and its ambulance carries the body free from Dhaka or Chattogram airport [L0145, L0146, B0047].
4. **The Tk 3 lakh grant.** The Welfare Board pays the family Tk 3 lakh if the worker had BMET clearance or Board membership, and only if he died in the country he was cleared for, so a worker cleared for Kyrgyzstan who dies in Russia does not qualify [L0140, L0141].

   You'll need [L0142, L0143, L0144]:
   - a family-members certificate on official pad from the Union Parishad chairman, mayor or councillor, countersigned by the UNO (Upazila Nirbahi Officer, the subdistrict's chief officer);
   - an indemnity, undertaking and power of attorney on Tk 400 non-judicial stamp paper;
   - a copy of the Bangladesh embassy's NOC or the death certificate.

5. **The insurance.** Workers who left with BMET clearance since 12 March 2023 are insured for Tk 10 lakh for 5 years, bought with a one-time Tk 1,000 premium at clearance (Work visa: company worker, Step 6: Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight) [B0010].

### If you lose the job

1. **Your permit ends with the job.** The Unified Permit (Единое разрешение, the combined work visa and work permit) lapses when the job ends, early or on time, or the employer closes, and can't be moved to another employer; you have 30 days to leave on it, then it is cancelled automatically (see Your residence permit, If you lose your job) [L0107, L0108, L0109, L0110].
2. **Get everything owed by the last working day:** all wages and pay for unused leave, and, if the employer closes or cuts staff, severance of at least 2 months' average pay (see Changing employer or leaving) [L0047, L0039, L0023].
3. **The employer pays your ticket home;** one that doesn't goes on the Register of Bad-Faith Employers, where it stays for a year (see Changing employer or leaving, Leaving at the end of the job) [L0111, L0113, L0118].
4. **If you were dismissed unfairly or not paid,** bring the dispute within 2 months of the dismissal order (see If you aren't paid) [L0066].
5. **Staying on without a permit** brings a fine and expulsion (see What can get you jailed or deported) [L0074, L0091].

### What you can carry home

- **Leaving Kyrgyzstan with cash.** A foreigner may not take more than USD 5,000 (about Tk 6.2 lakh) in cash foreign currency out of Kyrgyzstan, under a Cabinet decree of 13 January 2023 that stays in force until the Cabinet decides otherwise; the GKNB's Border Service and the State Customs Service enforce it [G0029, G0031, G0002]. The Border Service reminded travellers of the ban in September 2024, after stopping an Uzbek citizen with USD 30,000 (about Tk 37.3 lakh) [G0032, G0002]. In July 2026 customs at Manas airport (Bishkek) stopped an Uzbek citizen taking out USD 28,100 (about Tk 35 lakh) undeclared, and opened a criminal case [G0033, G0002]. Banks send larger sums by SWIFT bank transfer (see How to send) [G0009].
- **Arriving in Bangladesh:**
  - **Cash:** any amount of foreign currency, but more than USD 10,000 (about Tk 12.4 lakh) must be declared on the FMJ form (the customs currency declaration) [G0034, G0002].
  - **Phones:** 2 used phones, plus 1 new phone once a year, without duty or tax [G0035].
  - **Duty-free:** one item from the duty-free list, such as a laptop, a television up to 29 inches, a microwave, a sewing machine, or 100 g of gold and 200 g of silver jewellery (no more than 12 pieces of one type) [G0038].
  - **Gold bar:** once a year, one declared bar of up to 117 g (10 tola), at Tk 5,000 duty per 11.664 g; undeclared gold means arrest and a criminal case [G0036, G0037].
  - **Alcohol:** none for Bangladeshi passport holders [G0039].
