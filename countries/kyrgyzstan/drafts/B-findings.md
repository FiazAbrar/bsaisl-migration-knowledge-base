## a. Draft doc text

### For Background, "Size" and "Status" (numbers for N and the lead)

BMET (the Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training) cleared no workers for Kyrgyzstan in 2022, 1,713 in 2023, 12,747 in 2024 and 6,652 in 2025 [B0073, B0074, B0076, B0078]. From 1 January to 9 November 2026 it cleared 11 [B0080].

- **2023:** clearances peaked at 920 in November [B0082].
- **2024:** none in February or March, then 5,236 in April, 3,358 in May and 1,547 in June, the biggest months the corridor has had [B0083, B0084, B0085, B0086, B0087]; it fell to 172 by September [B0088].
- **2025:** it climbed to 1,499 in May and 1,410 in July, then fell to 57 in August, 1 in September and none in October [B0089, B0090, B0091, B0092, B0093].
- **Since then:** 13 in November 2025, 1 in December, 5 in January 2026, 5 in February, 1 in April, and none from May to 9 November 2026 [B0094, B0095, B0096, B0097, B0098, B0099, B0100, B0101].

Almost all are men: 55 women in 2023, 39 in 2024, 56 in 2025 and none in 2026 [B0075, B0077, B0079, B0081]. BMET records most under the job title "Worker" (9,384 of 12,747 in 2024; 2,356 of 6,652 in 2025); the named jobs are mostly garment work [B0102, B0076, B0106, B0078]:

- 2024: garments worker 1,213, swing line checker 892, construction worker 182 [B0103, B0104, B0105];
- 2025: sewing equipment operator 874, casual worker 649, garments worker 646, general labour 555, swing line checker 522 [B0107, B0108, B0109, B0110, B0111];
- 2026: general labour 7, casual worker 3, worker 1 [B0112].

Kyrgyzstan is not one of the 12 countries for which the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare and Overseas Employment told BMET, on 4 November 2026, to give no clearance without its approval [B0114, B0115]. There is no labour agreement: on 14 October 2026 Bangladesh's Foreign Secretary and Kyrgyzstan's First Deputy Minister of Labour, Social Security and Migration, Arlen Aliyasov, only explored signing an MoU (memorandum of understanding) [B0139]. The Kyrgyz side said it plans to recruit more foreign workers, in construction, textiles, pharmaceuticals, digital services, agriculture and teaching [B0140].

> **Need to know**
> - Why did BMET clearances for Kyrgyzstan stop in August 2025: a BMET or Ministry instruction, a Kyrgyz halt on work permits or visas for Bangladeshis, or agencies stopping?
> - How are Bangladeshis reaching Kyrgyzstan in 2026 without BMET clearance: on Kyrgyz work visas cleared nowhere, on tourist visas, or through Dubai?

### Step 1: Find a real job

On 10 November 2026 there is almost no legal flow: BMET cleared 11 workers for Kyrgyzstan in 2026, none since April [B0080, B0098, B0099, B0100, B0101]. Bangladesh and Kyrgyzstan have no labour agreement; their governments only discussed one in October 2026 [B0139].

> **Need to know**
> - Has BOESL (Bangladesh Overseas Employment and Services Limited, the government recruiting agency) ever posted a Kyrgyzstan circular?
> - Does BMET issue permission letters for Kyrgyzstan job orders, and where are they published? None appear on BMET's list of posted permission letters.
> - Is there a government cap on what an agency may take for Kyrgyzstan? The Ministry's 2017 cap table does not list Kyrgyzstan.

1. **Register on OEP (the Overseas Employment Platform),** BMET's website for job-seekers [B0002, B0003].

   You'll need [B0002, B0003]:
   - your NID (national ID card);
   - a mobile number;
   - Tk 200 for the government fee.

   1. Open [employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration](https://employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration) [B0003].
   2. Type your name in English, your mobile and NID numbers, date of birth, division and district exactly as on the NID, then the captcha; accept the terms and press "যাচাই করুন" (Verify) [B0002].
   3. Print 2 copies of the registration slip and give your fingerprints at the DEMO (District Employment and Manpower Office, BMET's district office); the DEMO list is on BMET's website [B0003, B0004].

   **If it fails:** call the Probashi call centre, 16135 (free), or +8809610102030 from abroad [B0023].

2. **Learn a trade if you can.** BMET lists its TTCs (Technical Training Centres) with contacts on its website [B0005]. The jobs BMET cleared for Kyrgyzstan in 2024 and 2025 were mostly garment jobs: garments worker, sewing equipment operator, swing line checker [B0103, B0104, B0107, B0109, B0111].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Do Kyrgyz garment employers test sewing skills before hiring Bangladeshis, and where (in Dhaka, by video, or on arrival)?

3. **Through a recruiting agency: check the licence and the approval.** You'll need the agency's name and RL number (the recruiting licence number every licensed agency has) [B0154].

   1. On [oep.gov.bd/agencies](https://www.oep.gov.bd/agencies), find the RL number and check the status says Active (not Expired or Cancelled) and the validity date has not passed [B0154, B0155].
   2. Go to the address on that list, not a middleman's office [B0154].
   3. Ask whether the job order has BMET's permission or the Ministry's approval. Industry insiders say that, until November 2026, demand letters for up to seven workers needed no Ministry approval, and that this was used to send workers on fake job offers [B0117].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which licensed agencies sent workers to Kyrgyzstan in 2024-2025, and through which Kyrgyz employers?
   > - Were Kyrgyzstan visas mostly cleared as group visas through agencies, or as single visas the worker brought himself?

4. **Never go on a tourist visa on a broker's promise of a job, and never go without BMET clearance** [B0006]. Bangladesh's CID (Criminal Investigation Department) arrested the owner of Jannat Trade International, which advertised car-wash jobs in Kyrgyzstan on YouTube at Tk 60,000-65,000 a month and took Tk 5 lakh for a visa, ticket and BMET card [B0156, B0152]. The firm had no manpower licence, only a tours and travel licence [B0143]. Immigration at Dhaka airport found the papers fake; six others it sent on 59-day tourist visas got no jobs [B0142, B0144]. CID says the syndicate sends people to Dubai on a visit visa, then on to Kyrgyzstan [B0145].

5. **Have the visa and papers checked before you pay.** Take them to your DEMO or to BMET, 89/2 Kakrail, Dhaka; it is free and takes 3 working days [B0008, B0013, B0052]. BMET checks only Saudi, Qatari, Bahraini, Singapore and UAE visas online, not Kyrgyz ones [B0013]. How to check a Kyrgyz visa and work permit yourself is in Step 5: The work visa, and checking it is real.

6. **Pay only through the bank, into the agency's account, and keep every receipt** [B0007].

   **If an agency or broker takes your money,** complain in this order:
   1. To BMET's Director General: a written application with your evidence (receipts, passport copy, messages); it is free and should be settled within 30 working days [B0018].
   2. The Probashi call centre, 16135 [B0023].
   3. The Ministry's grievance officer, Joint Secretary (Admin & Finance): 02-41030224, 01712992086, adsadmin@probashi.gov.bd [B0055].
   4. For a fake agency like Jannat Trade, the police: CID's counter-trafficking unit made the arrests in that case [B0156].

7. **Borrow from PKB (Probashi Kallyan Bank, the government bank for migrants) once you have a visa,** not from moneylenders: up to Tk 3 lakh at 9% simple interest, repaid monthly over up to 3 years, decided within 7 working days [B0038, B0059].

   You'll need [B0056, B0057, B0058, B0040]:
   - to be 18 to 60, with you and your guarantors living in the branch's area;
   - a PKB savings account opened with at least Tk 500;
   - the free bank form, 3 photos and an NID copy;
   - copies of your passport, visa, BMET card and contract;
   - two guarantors: one financially solvent, one a family member or close relative.

   Women can take PKB's women's migration loan, with one guarantor [B0060]. Apply at a branch or on mygov.bd, which sends application and tracking numbers by SMS; helpline 16238, or +880 9677-787878 from abroad [B0061, B0042].

### Step 4: Passport, police clearance and certificates

1. **Get an e-passport.** You'll need your NID (compulsory over age 20); fill in the form exactly as on it [B0027].

   1. Fill in the form at [epassport.gov.bd](https://www.epassport.gov.bd/) and choose the passport office for your present address [B0068, B0069].
   2. Pay online through A-Challan, DGePay or ShurjoPay, or at a bank through A-Challan [B0026]. A 48-page passport costs Tk 4,025 / 6,325 / 8,625 for 5 years and Tk 5,750 / 8,050 / 10,350 for 10 years (regular / express / super express) [B0024].
   3. Go to the office with your original NID and any technical certificate [B0070]. There is no police verification with an online-verified NID, and delivery is within 15, 7 or 2 working days of enrolment [B0029, B0025].

   **If it fails:** passport helpline 16445 (09666716445 from abroad), 24 hours [B0030]. If a passport is lost in Bangladesh, file a GD (General Diary, a police report) at the nearest police station and re-apply with a copy of the old passport and the GD [B0028]. For a passport lost or expiring in Kyrgyzstan, see The Bangladesh Embassy in Tashkent [B0127].

2. **Get a police clearance if the employer or agency asks for one.**

   You'll need [B0031, B0032]:
   - a passport valid at least 3 months;
   - a present address that is one of the addresses in your passport;
   - Tk 1,500 by treasury challan, code 1-7301-0001-2681, at any Bangladesh Bank or Sonali Bank branch, or online by card with a service charge.

   1. Register at [pcc.police.gov.bd](https://pcc.police.gov.bd/), log in and click "Apply" [B0065].
   2. Fill in your personal details and your present and permanent addresses; the police verify you at the present address [B0066].
   3. Upload the scanned documents [B0067].
   4. Track it by texting "PCC S <reference>" to 26969, and collect it with your passport or application copy [B0033, B0071].

   **If it fails:** helpdesk Sunday to Thursday, 9 am to 5 pm, 01320001824, 01320001825, help.pcc@police.gov.bd [B0034].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does the Kyrgyz work permit or work visa application require a Bangladeshi police clearance, and must it be apostilled and translated into Russian?

3. **Apostille any certificate the Kyrgyz side asks for.** Kyrgyzstan accepts the Apostille issued online by Bangladesh's Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) [B0148, B0150]. For Kyrgyzstan the online Apostille is enough; no stamp at MoFA's consular counter is needed [B0149].

   1. Have the document verified by its issuer first: SSC and HSC certificates by the Education Board, then the Ministry of Education [B0037]. Experience certificates must be attested by BMET or the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare and notarised [B0064].
   2. On [mygov.bd](https://www.mygov.bd/), register, choose the attestation service for your type of document, fill in the form and upload the document; it costs Tk 200 a page, paid online, and takes 5 working days [B0036, B0035].
   3. An SMS tells you when the Apostille is issued; download it from your mygov.bd account [B0036].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which documents does the Kyrgyz side ask Bangladeshi workers for (diploma, skill certificate, police clearance, medical certificate), and must they be translated into Russian and notarised in Bishkek?

### Step 6: Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight

1. **Do the 3-day pre-departure orientation (PDO).** It is compulsory, at one of BMET's 70 training institutes; pay against a money receipt (Tk 200 at Feni TTC, which says to pay no more) [B0015, B0072]. Download the certificate at [training.oep.gov.bd/pdo-certificate](https://training.oep.gov.bd/pdo-certificate) with your passport or NID number and the captcha [B0020].

2. **Get the contract at least 2 days before you go** and check the job, employer, workplace, length, pay, leave and airfare against what you were promised [B0062]. The contract terms and your rights in Kyrgyzstan are in Step 2: The job offer and contract and Your rights at work.

3. **Get BMET clearance (the smart card).** On a group visa the agency applies to BMET's emigration branch with the visa copy, the contract, your registration, a Tk 300 stamp undertaking and the pay orders; BMET clears within 3 working days once the visa is in hand [B0063].

   Your costs:
   - welfare fee Tk 5,500 to the Wage Earners' Welfare Board, from 1 October 2026 [B0019];
   - insurance Tk 1,000 once (5 years' cover, Tk 10 lakh) [B0010];
   - clearance fee Tk 250 [B0153];
   - source tax Tk 1,200 (skilled) or Tk 800 (unskilled) on a group visa; Tk 250 on an attested single visa, Tk 400 on an unattested one [B0153].

   **Single visa:** apply yourself at BMET's emigration branch in Dhaka or the Chattogram DEMO with your application, the visa copy, any contract, an attested OEP registration copy, a Tk 300 stamp undertaking and the pay order for the welfare fee and insurance [B0011]. A woman on a single visa also needs a no-objection letter from her legal guardian on stamp paper [B0021].

   **Check it is real:** at [portal.wewb.gov.bd/bmet-wewbm-check](https://portal.wewb.gov.bd/bmet-wewbm-check) type your passport number or membership ID and the captcha [B0022]. The welfare desks at Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet airports check smart cards, and in the Jannat Trade case immigration at Dhaka airport found the visa, ticket and BMET card fake [B0016, B0142].

   **If it fails:** BMET's Director (Emigration), +8802222222415, 01711-135901, diremi@bmet.gov.bd [B0014].

4. **The flight.** Never route through Dubai on a visit visa at a broker's suggestion: CID describes that as the trafficking route to Kyrgyzstan [B0145].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which airlines and transit points do Kyrgyzstan-bound workers use in 2026 (Dubai, Sharjah, Delhi, Almaty, Tashkent, Istanbul), and what does a one-way ticket cost?
   > - Who pays the airfare for Kyrgyzstan jobs in practice: the employer or the worker?
   > - Does an agency representative fly with or meet Kyrgyzstan-bound groups?

5. **Before you fly:** keep one set of copies at home and 3 with you, open 2 bank accounts (one yours, one a family member's), and never carry a packet whose contents you don't know [B0009]. Save the Bangladesh Embassy in Tashkent's numbers (see Contacts) [B0124].

### The Bangladesh Embassy in Tashkent

Bangladesh has no embassy in Kyrgyzstan; its Embassy in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, covers Kyrgyzstan, and Ambassador Mohammad Monirul Islam is accredited to the Kyrgyz Republic from there [B0120, B0122]. When Kyrgyzstan sent home 180 Bangladeshis who had become irregular, in September 2025, the Embassy gave out the flight details [B0118, B0119]. In May 2024, after mob attacks on foreign students in Bishkek, the Ambassador was sent to Bishkek to look after Bangladeshis [B0121].

**E-passport.** The Embassy has issued e-passports since 30 September 2025, and in December 2025 extended the service to Bangladeshis living in Kyrgyzstan during the Ambassador's visit to Bishkek on 13-14 December [B0129, B0127, B0128]. Fees from 14 October 2025 [B0130, B0131, B0132]:

### Coming home

- **Airport help.** The Welfare Board's desks at Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet help with customs clearance and lost luggage, and take complaints from workers returning after abuse or fraud [B0045, B0046]. Dhaka desk: +8802 8901040, 01404 413388; Chattogram: 01913631797; Sylhet: 01558277904 [B0048, B0017]. Women returning within a year after abuse or fraud get Tk 5,000, and sick or injured workers get a free ambulance from Dhaka or Chattogram airport [B0016, B0047].
- **A place to stay.** The Wage Earners' Centre near Shahjalal airport costs Tk 100 to enter and Tk 200 a night; book at wec.wewb.gov.bd [B0049]. The Probashi Lounge at Shahjalal airport gives free wifi and telephone and cheaper food to workers who show BMET clearance or Welfare Board membership [B0050].
- **Legal help for your family in Bangladesh:** apply to the Welfare Board directly, or through the expatriate welfare branch of the Deputy Commissioner's office or your DEMO [B0051].
- **A business loan.** PKB lends returnees up to Tk 50 lakh, up to Tk 3 lakh without collateral; you need an up-to-date trade licence [B0043, B0044]. Helpline 16238 [B0042].
- **If you were sent home by Kyrgyzstan** (as 180 Bangladeshis were in September 2025), see Going home without valid papers [B0118].
