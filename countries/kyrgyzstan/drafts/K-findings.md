## a. Draft doc text (inserts)

### Insert 1. Background, "Is it open now, and who can go?" > Status (and Work visa: company worker, Step 3)

Since 28 September 2026 the Single Permit (Единое разрешение, the combined work visa and work permit) and the resident card (резидент-карта) are no longer the job of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs: a presidential decree moved them to the Ministry of Labour, Social Security and Migration [K0013, K0014, K0015, K0022]. The same decree moved residence permits, citizenship matters and the registration of foreigners there from Kyzmat, a state agency under the Presidential Administration [K0017, K0018, K0019]. The ministry has a new Department for Migration Affairs to do this work and is to hand the Single Permit and resident card service to an outside contractor [K0020, K0016]. The Cabinet had two months to issue the new rules [K0021].

> **Need to know**
> - Since October 2026, does the employer still apply for a Single Permit on the e-Visa portal (evisa.e-gov.kg), or through a new Ministry of Labour portal or contractor, and who is the contractor?

### Insert 2. Work visa: company worker, Step 3 (fees)

Until 1 November 2026, garment companies that were members of the Chamber of Commerce and Industry paid a special rate, and had to show the Chamber's letter confirming membership [K0001, K0008]:

- Single Permit: USD 75, paid in som at the National Bank rate on the day, decided within 7 working days [K0002];
- state duty for the permit to hire a foreign worker and the work permit: 20,000 som [K0003];
- guarantee deposit (гарантийный взнос): 5,000 som [K0004];
- Single Permit for an individual entrepreneur (ИП): 50,000 som [K0005];
- resident card: USD 225, decided within 10 working days, plus 5,000 som for the electronic work permit [K0006, K0007].

> **Need to know**
> - What are the standard (annex 5) Single Permit and resident card fees since 1 November 2026, for garment and other employers, and was the garment rate extended?

### Insert 3. Work visa: company worker, Step 5 or Step 7 (medical papers)

For a long-term visa or resident card on the special work visa (types SW1 and SW2), the worker submits [K0009, K0010, K0011]:

- a certificate that he has no HIV antibodies, issued by the Republican Centre for control of blood-borne hepatitis and HIV;
- a medical certificate on form 086/U (форма 086/У);
- a compulsory medical insurance policy.

### Insert 4. Work visa: company worker, Step 7 (registration)

Cabinet Resolution No. 600 of 4 September 2026 set new registration rules, in force about 23 September 2026 [K0026, K0027]. Bangladesh is not on the list of 92 countries whose citizens get a registration-free period; India's citizens, for example, have 30 days [K0029, K0028].

1. **Make sure your host registers you.** An employer that houses you in company housing must register you; you do not have to go to the office yourself unless the system has no record of your entry [K0036, K0037]. Registration needs a record of your border crossing, and is made on the passport you entered with [K0032].
   You'll need [K0038, K0039]:
   - the host's request (ходатайство);
   - your valid passport with visa;
   - your Single Permit or resident card;
   - the host's Kyrgyz passport or permanent residence permit;
   - the owner's notarised consent if the host does not own the flat or hostel [K0033];
   - the receipt for the state duty and the registration fee.
   1. The host applies at any office of the population-registration service, on the Online Registration of Foreign Citizens website, or in the mobile app, through the Unified External Migration Accounting System (ЕСУВМ) [K0030].
   2. The host pays within 3 working days of getting the payment code, or the application is deleted [K0035].
   3. An office registers you in 1 working day; an online application takes 3 working days, or 1 if urgent [K0031, K0049].
   4. You get a registration slip (регистрационный талон) on paper or electronically, with a QR code [K0050].
   Registration lasts as long as your Single Permit or resident card [K0041]. At most one person per 12 square metres can be registered in a house or flat [K0034]. Workers who move from site to site or work in the mountains can be registered at the company's legal address [K0042].
   **If you move, change passport or purpose:** re-register within 10 working days [K0043]. Registration ends if you are out of Kyrgyzstan for 30 days or more [K0044].
   **If it fails:** a refused registration fee is not refunded [K0046]. Without a valid visa you cannot be registered [K0047].

### Insert 5. What can get you jailed or deported

If you come late for registration, you are registered only after the police have recorded your offence; otherwise you bring the offence report and the fine receipt [K0045]. Someone who has stayed more than 6 months without registration and visa cannot be registered at all [K0048].

Since 26 October 2026 a presidential decree gives the labour inspection, the Service for Control and Supervision of Labour Legislation under the Ministry of Labour, migration-control powers over foreign workers, to be divided with the Ministry of Internal Affairs (the police) [K0023, K0025]. The Cabinet has three months, to late January 2027, to issue the rules [K0024].

### Insert 6. Your rights at work (minimum wage)

The legal minimum wage is 3,280 som a month in 2026: the monthly pay for unskilled work over a full month of normal hours [K0051, K0053]. The 2026 budget law, Law No. 9 of 27 January 2026, sets 3,608 som for 2027 and 3,968.8 som for 2028 [K0054, K0052].
