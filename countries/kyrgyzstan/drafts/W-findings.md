## a. Draft doc text

### (For the intro or Background: the documents in one paragraph)

A Bangladeshi needs a visa to enter Kyrgyzstan; there is no visa-free entry on an ordinary Bangladeshi passport [W0028]. To work, the document is the Unified Permit (Единое разрешение, also called the Single Permit), an electronic document with a QR code that combines the work visa, the employer's permit to hire foreign labour and the work permit [W0027]. It replaced the separate work permit and work visa on 1 January 2025 [W0052]. The employer applies for it on the e-Visa portal (evisa.e-gov.kg), run by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MFA) [W0091, W0001]. Since 30 April 2026 the rules for visas, the Unified Permit and the resident card (резидент-карта, the plastic card that lets a foreigner live and work for up to a year) sit in one Temporary Regulation set by Cabinet Resolution No. 312, which runs until 31 December 2026 [W0003, W0002, W0079]. A presidential decree of September 2026 moves the Unified Permit and resident card from the MFA to the Ministry of Labour, Social Security and Migration (Минсоцтруда), which is to set up a migration department [W0127, W0128].

> **Need to know**
> - From what date does the Ministry of Labour, not the MFA, issue Unified Permits, and does the e-Visa portal stay the same?
> - What replaces Resolution No. 312 after 31 December 2026?

### Step 2: The job offer and contract

The job reaches you as a named place on a Kyrgyz employer's Unified Permit application: the employer, not you, applies, and only a company or individual entrepreneur registered in Kyrgyzstan can do so [W0053, W0100]. Any individual or legal entity may bring a foreign worker [W0100]. Construction and light industry (garments above all) are the sectors that hire most [W0013]; most foreign workers are in industry, construction, transport and communications [W0102], and 46% of all foreign workers are in Jalal-Abad region in the south, not Bishkek [W0104].

1. **Get the employer's details in writing before you pay anyone.** You need the company's full legal name, its address in Kyrgyzstan, the job title, the work address (city and region) and the monthly wage in som (KGS) [W0049, W0090]. The employer's application to the MFA is a statement letter naming you, your passport number, the purpose and the period, signed by the head of the company, who undertakes to keep your stay legal [W0049, W0050]. Ask the agency for a copy of that letter.

   **Check it is real:** the permit is tied to you, the named employer, the type of work, the position and the period, so a job offer from one company and a permit from another is not the same job [W0141]. Minimum pay for foreign workers is set by region and sector [W0090].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What is the minimum monthly wage a Unified Permit application must show for a garment or construction worker in 2026, and where is it published?
   > - Do Kyrgyz employers give Bangladeshi workers a copy of the statement letter or the Unified Permit before departure?

2. **Get the employment contract, in Russian with a Bengali or English translation, before the permit is filed.** The employer uploads the employment contract with the application, so it exists before the permit is issued [W0092]. Documents on the portal must be in Kyrgyz or Russian or translated into one of them [W0035]. Make sure the contract states who pays for housing, food and medical care: under the Law on External Migration the inviting party bears your financial support and medical care unless the parties agree otherwise in writing [W0019]. Your wages must go to a bank card, not cash (see Step 7, item 4) [W0068, W0075]. What the contract must contain under the Labour Code is in Your rights at work.

   **For comparison:** a 2024 job order approved by Pakistan's emigration bureau for garment workers in Bishkek paid 35,000 to 37,000 som a month, with free housing, food, medical care and transport but no free air ticket [W0143].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What wage and conditions do BMET permission letters for Kyrgyzstan state, and do workers receive the same contract in Bishkek?
   > - Is the contract signed in Dhaka, or only on arrival in Kyrgyzstan?

**Scams:** a business visa (type B) or tourist visa does not let you work, so an offer of "a visa now, the work permit after you arrive" leaves you working illegally, which can end in expulsion [W0029, W0016]. A valid visa or registration alone does not allow anyone to hire you [W0142].

### Step 3: The employer gets your work permit

You do nothing at this step except send documents; the employer files everything online and pays [W0064, W0066]. There is no longer a commission that shares out quota places, and no separate quota letter, work permit and work visa: one application for a named worker gives one Unified Permit [W0053, W0052].

**The quota.** Each year the Cabinet of Ministers sets the maximum number of foreign workers [W0015]. For 2026 it was set at 52,000, then raised to 100,000 by order of 30 April 2026 [W0011, W0010]. In 2025 it was raised to 42,000 [W0014]. Since 2025 the quota no longer lists types of work [W0012]. Unified Permits are issued only within the quota [W0095].

1. **The employer applies on the e-Visa portal.** In its personal account (Личный кабинет) on [evisa.e-gov.kg](https://www.evisa.e-gov.kg/) the employer [W0064, W0065, W0066]:

   1. creates the company account;
   2. fills in the online quota form;
   3. fills in the form for you by name and uploads scans of the documents.

   The documents include the employer's statement letter (petition), copies of documents, the employment contract, medical certificates and any licence the work needs [W0092]. Your part is your passport scan (valid at least 6 months beyond the permit, with 2 free pages) and photo [W0036, W0037].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What exact documents does the e-Visa portal ask of a Bangladeshi worker for a Unified Permit in 2026 (police clearance, education certificate, medical certificate, HIV test)?
   > - Must any Bangladeshi document be attested by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Dhaka or legalised by a Kyrgyz embassy?

2. **The employer pays.** The fee is paid when the application is filed and covers the work visa and the work permit [W0054]. The employer must also pay a non-refundable guarantee deposit [W0055]. Under the 2025 rules (Resolution No. 367) the costs were [W0057, W0058, W0059, W0060, W0061, W0062]:

   - permit to employ foreign labour: 20,000 som;
   - guarantee deposit: 5,000 som;
   - electronic work permit: 5,000 som;
   - Unified Permit for entry and the first 60 days: USD 75;
   - extension to 360 days: USD 225;
   - in total, 30,000 som plus USD 300 per worker.

   They are paid in two stages: first for two months, then for 12 months [W0063]. Resolution No. 312 replaced these rules on 30 April 2026 [W0137]; a July 2026 amendment gave garment companies the same amounts (USD 75 plus 20,000 som state duty plus 5,000 som deposit for the permit; USD 225 plus 5,000 som for the resident card) as a special rate until 1 November 2026 [W0069, W0070, W0071, W0072]. Fees paid on the portal are not refunded if the application is refused [W0038]. These are the employer's costs: the Law on External Migration puts the duty to arrange your stay documents on whoever invites you [W0018].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What are the standard (non-garment) Unified Permit and resident card fees under Resolution No. 312 since 1 November 2026?
   > - How much of the permit cost do agencies or brokers charge Bangladeshi workers, and in what name?

3. **The MFA decides.** The MFA's Consular Department issues the permit [W0099, W0135]. Under the 2025 rules a decision took 7 to 10 working days [W0087]; since 2026 the permit can be issued urgently for an extra fee [W0007]. If the portal returns the application for corrections, the 5-working-day review starts again once they are made [W0042]. The approved permit, with its QR code, appears in the employer's account [W0067].

   **If it is refused:** a refusal can be challenged in court [W0094]. The permit is refused or annulled for false information, a crime, a dangerous disease, a security threat or terrorist links [W0093].

> **Need to know**
> - Does a refusal come with written reasons that the employer can show the worker?

### Step 5: The work visa, and checking it is real

There is no separate work visa sticker to collect: the Unified Permit is itself the electronic work visa [W0027, W0052].

1. **Get a copy of the Unified Permit and its reference number from the agency.** Print the permit with its QR code and carry it with your passport. The application's reference number is an 8-character code of letters and digits [W0044]. Don't buy a ticket before the permit is approved [W0039].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Do Manas airport border guards in Bishkek ask Bangladeshis for a printed Unified Permit, or check it online?
   > - Do Bangladeshi workers on a Unified Permit fly direct, or through Dubai, Istanbul or Almaty, and does the transit airport ask for anything?

2. **Check it on the portal.** On [evisa.e-gov.kg](https://www.evisa.e-gov.kg/) open Services, then Check status, and enter the reference number: it shows the status of the application [W0043].
3. **Scan the QR code.** The permit carries a QR code that can be read [W0027].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What does a genuine Unified Permit's QR code open (which website, which details)?

4. **Ask the MFA directly if in doubt.** Visa Center hotline (WhatsApp): +996703428865; Consular Department 24/7 hotline: +996999312002; e-visa@mfa.gov.kg; 10 Togolok-Moldo street, Bishkek; Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm [W0046, W0047, W0045, W0048]. Ready-to-use message: "I am [name], Bangladeshi passport [number]. My employer [company name] applied for a Unified Permit, reference [code]. Please confirm it is approved and valid until [date]."

**Scams:** a "visa" that is a tourist or business visa, a permit in another company's name, or a permit with no reference number is not a work permit (Step 2, Scams) [W0029, W0141]. The old ework.e-gov.kg portal closed for quota and permit applications on 25 December 2024, so a quota letter from it is out of date [W0138].

**If it fails:** report the agency and broker as in Scams; do not travel [W0039].

### Step 7: After arrival

1. **Get registered at your address within 5 working days.** Bangladeshis must register at their place of stay within 5 working days of entry [W0034]. The host does it: an employer that gives you company housing must register you [W0118, W0018].

   You'll need [W0119, W0120, W0115, W0116]:

   - your passport (the one you entered on);
   - the Unified Permit or resident card;
   - the host's petition naming your actual address;
   - if the host does not own the home, the owner's notarised consent;
   - a receipt for the state duty and registration fee.

   1. The host files online on the Online Registration of Foreign Citizens site or app, or at any office of the population registration service, wherever you live [W0113, W0112]. In Bishkek the Service Centre for Foreign Citizens is at 121 Jumabek Street (Wedding Palace building); in Osh, 211 Kurmanjan Datka Street; Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 [W0131, W0132].
   2. Pay the state duty and fee within 3 working days of getting the payment code, or the application is deleted [W0121].
   3. Registration takes 1 working day [W0114]. You get a registration slip, on paper or electronic, with a QR code; keep it on your phone [W0124].

   Registration lasts as long as your Unified Permit or resident card [W0122]. A home or flat needs at least 12 square metres per registered person [W0117]. Workers who move from site to site or work in the mountains can be registered at the employer's legal address [W0125]. Registration functions are moving to the Ministry of Labour (see the paragraph at the top of this section) [W0127].

   **If you move or get a new passport:** re-register within 10 working days [W0123]. If you leave Kyrgyzstan for 30 days or more, the registration lapses and must be done again [W0126].

   > **Need to know**
   > - How much are the registration state duty and fee for a worker in 2026?
   > - Are Bangladeshis on the exempt list with a longer period, or is it 5 working days?
   > - Do employers in practice register Bangladeshi workers, or leave it to them?

2. **Medical tests.** Under the pre-2025 system the work permit file needed an HIV test from the Republican AIDS Center, 8 Logvinenko Street, Bishkek [W0148]. Since July 2026 an 086/U medical certificate, compulsory health insurance and an HIV test are required for SW1 and SW2 resident cards [W0073]; the employer uploads medical certificates with the permit application [W0092].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does a Bangladeshi on a Unified Permit need an HIV test or 086/U certificate in Kyrgyzstan, where, and at what cost?

3. **Before day 60, the employer turns the permit into a resident card.** The first Unified Permit lasts up to 60 days; the employer then applies for a resident card (see Your residence permit) [W0004, W0074].
4. **Bank account and wages.** The resident card is also a bank (debit) card, and your wages must be paid onto it [W0075, W0081]. A special bank account is opened for it [W0083]. Since 1 January 2026 individual entrepreneurs must pay foreign workers through a bank [W0105].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which bank issues the resident card, and can the worker withdraw cash and send money home from it?

5. **Taxpayer number (INN).** The resident card holder is given a taxpayer number (ИНН) [W0076].
6. **SIM card.** Buy it with your passport; register the number to your passport in the operator's app, or at the operator's office with your passport if you can't [W0145, W0147]. Until it is registered the SIM only calls the operator and downloads its app [W0146].
7. **Keep your documents valid.** Working without a valid permit can end in expulsion (see What can get you jailed or deported) [W0016]. In June 2026, raids on construction sites and sewing workshops produced 922 offence reports against foreigners in ten days [W0106, W0107].

### Your residence permit

#### The resident card

The resident card is a temporary stay permit for up to a year, renewable [W0079]. It lets a foreigner live and work legally for up to 360 days [W0080]. It is a plastic debit card with a QR code holding your stay dates, address, personal, biometric and medical data, and your job and pay [W0081]. The QR code shows how long you may stay [W0078].

1. **The employer applies before your first permit runs out.** It is paid online and you need not appear in person [W0077]. Under the 2025 rules, apply at least 10 working days before the current document expires [W0086]. A decision takes up to 10 working days [W0082]. The 2025 fee was USD 225 plus 5,000 som for the electronic work permit (Step 3, item 2) [W0061, W0059].
2. **Check it:** scan the QR code and check the end date [W0078].

**If it is refused or annulled:** the decision can be challenged in court [W0084]. The police and national security bodies can shorten its validity [W0085]. You then get a Leave Visa (type L), valid up to 10 days, to leave [W0030, W0031].

> **Need to know**
> - What is the resident card fee for a worker since 1 November 2026?

#### How long you can stay

- **Unified Permit:** up to 60 days [W0004].
- **Resident card:** up to 1 year, renewed by the employer [W0079].
- **Temporary residence permit (вид на жительство):** for foreigners staying over six months with a legal income, including work under a contract with a work permit; up to one year, renewable yearly for up to five years [W0020, W0021].
- **Permanent stay (immigrant status):** decided within a yearly immigration quota of 400 people for 2026, within nine months, and needs a permanent income [W0022, W0025, W0023, W0024]. April 2026 draft amendments proposed dropping this stage [W0026].
- **Labour migrants** must keep a permanent home outside Kyrgyzstan [W0017].

**If the employer closes:** under the 2025 rules the permit lapses and you get 30 days to leave [W0088]. Employers must also see that a foreign worker leaves when the contract ends [W0005].

**If you overstay:** an exit visa costs 17,500 som after more than three months and 192,500 som after more than a year, plus USD 1,100 for each further year [W0008, W0009]. A free Emergency Leave Visa (type E) is issued when severe illness stops someone leaving alone (see Going home without valid papers) [W0032].

> **Need to know**
> - Has any Bangladeshi worker been given a temporary residence permit or immigrant status in Kyrgyzstan?
> - Does a worker whose employer closes get the 30 days under Resolution No. 312 too?

#### Family

Family of a Unified Permit or resident card holder can get a family visa (type FF) for up to 90 days, with no right to work (see Family) [W0033].

## Other work routes

### Working for more than one employer

Under the 2025 rules a foreign worker could work for up to three organisations at once, with one as the main employer [W0089].

> **Need to know**
> - Does Resolution No. 312 keep the three-employer rule, and how is a second employer added?

### Self-employed (individual entrepreneur)

A foreigner registered as an individual entrepreneur applies for a Unified Permit himself, by statement letter [W0051]. For a non-EAEU (Eurasian Economic Union) national, registering as an individual entrepreneur does not by itself give the right to work [W0136].

### The patent: not open to Bangladeshis

The Tax Service's patent (800 som a month) lets a foreigner work only for private individuals, and only during visa-free stay; Bangladeshis have no visa-free stay [W0133, W0134, W0028].

## b. Notes (for notes.md)

**The system on 10 Nov 2026 (W side).**
- 1 Jan 2025: Single/Unified Permit pilot under Resolution No. 721 (2 Dec 2024) replaced the employer quota permit + personal work permit + work visa (W1); applications moved from ework.e-gov.kg (closed 25 Dec 2024) to evisa.e-gov.kg; the interdepartmental quota commission was abolished [W0052, W0053, W0138, W0099].
- 1 Jul 2025: Resolution No. 367 (27 Jun 2025) added the resident card and set the Annex 2 fees (30,000 som + USD 300) [W0057-W0062]. The 7 to 10 day decision time, 3-employer rule, 30 days to leave and 10-day extension rule are from #367 summaries (Caravan-Info, 4 Jul 2025) [W0086-W0089].
- 30 Apr 2026: Resolution No. 312 replaced #721/#367 with one Temporary Regulation (visas + Unified Permit + resident card), MFA in charge, until 31 Dec 2026 [W0001-W0003, W0137]. Amended by No. 506 (23 Jul 2026) [W0069].
- Sept 2026: presidential decree moving Unified Permit, resident card, ВНЖ and registration to the Ministry of Labour (new migration department, services outsourced; Cabinet has two months) [W0127-W0130].
- 4 Sept 2026: new registration procedure, Cabinet Resolution No. 600 [W0111].

**Quota:** 2025: 25,000, then 42,000 (order No. 710, 18 Aug 2025) [W0014]; 2026: 52,000, then 100,000 (30 Apr 2026) [W0011, W0010]. Traveliscope (agency) claims 120,000 for 2027 by an order of 7 Sept 2026 [W0140]; no news or official source confirms it (searched Russian news: квота 2027 трудовая миграция). The 2027 immigration quota (400, order No. 780-т, 13 Oct 2026) is a different quota.

**Bangladeshis in the numbers (for N):** by 30 Apr 2025, 2,573 of 12,232 60-day Single Permits went to Bangladeshis, second after China (2,844) [W0097, W0096]; IOM DTM (via Economist.kg): Bangladeshis 24% of ~48,000 permit holders by Dec 2025, second after China 40%, Pakistan 17%, India 5% [W0103]; 48,439 unified permits issued by 17 Dec 2025 [W0101]. 17,989 foreigners got work permits in 2024 (logged in source, not as a fact).
