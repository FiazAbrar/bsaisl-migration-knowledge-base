# L findings: law and work (Kyrgyzstan)

Facts: `countries/kyrgyzstan/work/L/facts.csv` (L0001-L0189). Sources: `work/L/sources.csv` (22 sources: 11 official, 11 news). The Kyrgyz laws are the current consolidated editions from the Ministry of Justice's legal database (cbd.minjust.gov.kg), saved with Firecrawl.

## a. Draft doc text

### Your rights at work

The Labour Code of the Kyrgyz Republic (Code No. 23 of 23 January 2025, in force since 2025) covers foreign workers employed by organisations in Kyrgyzstan, unless a law or treaty says otherwise [L0001]. Forced labour, meaning making someone work under threat of violence, is banned [L0002]. For any broken right, use the routes under If you aren't paid.

#### Contract

- **Your copy.** The contract is made in two copies signed by both sides, and one is yours [L0006]. The employer may not make you do work that isn't in it [L0009].
- **What it must say.** The workplace, and the pay terms: the wage rate or salary, extra payments and bonuses [L0004, L0003]. Terms can be changed only by agreement of both sides, in writing [L0005].
- **Length.** A fixed-term contract lasts at most 5 years; if it ends, neither side ends it and you keep working, it becomes open-ended [L0007, L0008].
- **Probation.** Only if it is written in the contract, and at most 3 months [L0010, L0011].
- **Papers about your job.** Ask in writing; within 5 working days the employer must give you certified copies of documents about your job, even after you leave [L0012]. Ask for them before you need to complain.

> **Need to know**
> - In what language are Bangladeshi workers' Kyrgyz contracts written, and do they get a Bangla or English copy?
> - Does the Single Permit file include the contract the worker signed in Bangladesh, or a different one signed in Bishkek?

#### Pay and deductions

**Pay**

- At least the legal minimum wage [L0042], which is 3,280 som a month in 2026 (2,865 som in 2025) [L0162]. A rise to 9,800 som from 2027 was proposed in October 2026 and needs parliament's approval [L0163].
- In som, in money: paying in IOUs, receipts or food or goods coupons is banned [L0043, L0044].
- At least once a month, by bank transfer to a bank card, not cash [L0046, L0185, L0045, L0129].
- With each payment the employer must tell you in writing each part of your wage and the size and reason of every deduction (a payslip) [L0053].
- If pay, holiday pay or final pay is late, the employer must add 0.25% of the unpaid amount for every calendar day of delay [L0048].

**Deductions**

Only in cases the Labour Code or another law allows, and all together at most 20% of each payment (50% where a law allows) [L0051, L0052]. Employers have deducted workers' "broker money": one Bangladeshi was paid Tk 10,000 in his first month, of which the employer kept Tk 7,000, saying he had paid the broker to bring them [L0179]. Garment workers promised a fixed Tk 52,000 a month were paid by the number of pieces sewn [L0187, L0181].

> **Need to know**
> - What do Bangladeshi garment and construction workers in Kyrgyzstan actually earn a month in 2026, after deductions?
> - Do employers deduct food, housing or "visa cost" from Bangladeshi workers' wages, and how much?

#### Hours, rest and leave

- **Hours.** At most 40 hours a week; 36 for heavy, harmful or dangerous work [L0024, L0025]. A break of 30 minutes to 1 hour a day for rest and meals, unpaid [L0031].
- **Overtime.** Only with your written consent, at most 4 hours over any 2 days in a row [L0026, L0027]. Paid at least 1.5 times for the first 2 hours and double after that [L0029]. The employer must record your overtime and tell you the hours if you ask [L0028]. Night hours are paid at least 1.5 times [L0030].
- **Rest days.** 2 a week on a 5-day week, 1 on a 6-day week; Sunday is the usual rest day [L0032, L0033]. Work on a rest day or a non-working holiday pays at least double, or single pay plus another day off if you choose [L0034, L0035]. Eid al-Fitr (Orozo Ait) and Eid al-Adha (Kurman Ait) are non-working holidays [L0036].
- **Annual leave.** 28 calendar days paid, usable after 11 months' work for the first year [L0037, L0038]. On leaving you are paid for unused days, once you have worked at least a month [L0039, L0040].
- **Sick leave.** The employer pays a sickness benefit [L0054].
- **Maternity.** 70 days before and 56 after the birth (70 after a complicated birth or twins) [L0041].

> **Need to know**

#### Housing, food and health care

Unless agreed otherwise in writing, the hosting side bears the foreigner's financial support and medical care [L0099].

> **Need to know**

#### Your passport

Taking a citizen's passport as security, or otherwise unlawfully, is fined 30 calculation units (RP; 1 RP = 100 som) for a person or 130 RP for a company [L0089, L0090]. If yours is taken, complain through the routes under If you aren't paid.

> **Need to know**
> - Does the passport-confiscation fine (Code of Offences, Article 137) cover a foreigner's passport, or only a Kyrgyz citizen's?

#### Probash pension (Bangladesh)

Kyrgyzstan's own pension rules for foreign workers are unclear (see Need to know under Hours, rest and leave). Bangladesh's Probash pension, part of the Universal Pension Scheme, is open to Bangladeshis aged 18 to 50 [L0153].

1. **Register online in your own name** at upension.gov.bd, under Probash; no one else is involved [L0154].
2. **Choose and pay a monthly amount:** Tk 1,000, 2,000, 5,000, 7,500 or 10,000, by debit or credit card into a Sonali Bank account, 3, 9 or 12 months at a time; 2.5% is added when you pay from abroad in foreign currency [L0160, L0155, L0159, L0156].

What you get back: after 10 years' unbroken payments, a monthly pension into your bank from age 60 (Tk 1,000 a month for 20 years gives about Tk 4,927 a month); if you die, everything paid plus profit goes to your nominee [L0157, L0161, L0158].

> **Need to know**
> - How can a worker in Kyrgyzstan pay the Probash pension if he has only a Kyrgyz som bank card?

### What can get you jailed or deported

Fines are in calculation units (RP, raschetnyi pokazatel); 1 RP is 100 som [L0073, L0090].

#### No valid papers, or overstay

**The fine.** Being in Kyrgyzstan without a valid visa, registration, residence permit or work permit, with expired ones, or staying after your legal stay ends [L0074]:

- up to 1 month: 55 RP (5,500 som) [L0074, L0090];
- 1 to 3 months: 100 RP (10,000 som) [L0075, L0090];
- 3 to 6 months: 150 RP (15,000 som) [L0076, L0090];
- 6 months to a year: 175 RP (17,500 som) [L0077, L0090];
- over a year: 200 RP (20,000 som) [L0078, L0090].

Staying for a purpose other than the one declared on entry (for example working on a visit visa), or breaking transit rules: 100 RP [L0079]. Working without a permit makes you liable to expulsion [L0091].

**The order and the 30 days.** With the fine comes a written order to fix the violation; if it isn't fixed within 30 days, a court orders expulsion [L0080, L0092].

**Leaving after an overstay.** You need an exit visa (type L), valid up to 10 days [L0123]. It costs 17,500 som (USD 200) for an overstay up to 3 months, 96,250 som (USD 1,100) for 3 months to a year, 192,500 som (USD 2,200) for 1 to 2 years, and USD 1,100 more for each further year [L0102, L0103, L0104, L0105]. People who leave this way are put in a database of violators [L0124]. Going home with no passport: see Going home without valid papers.

**In practice:** a Bangladeshi welder whose visa expired the day after he reached Bishkek worked seven months unpaid, was caught and served three months in jail [L0189, L0174].

> **Need to know**
> - Is the exit visa fee charged on top of the Code of Offences fine?
> - Where is the exit visa issued (Ministry of Foreign Affairs consular department in Bishkek, or the airport)?

#### Expulsion and entry bans

- Expulsion is ordered by a court; it is done by handing you to your country's authorities or by your own controlled departure [L0081, L0083].
- If you don't leave by the court's deadline, you are held in a special centre for up to 30 days until expulsion [L0082].
- Unpaid fines do not stop the expulsion [L0084]. You pay its cost; if you can't, the employer or host who invited you does, and only exceptionally the state [L0096]. Employers pay a guarantee fee of 50 RP per worker that funds this when the worker has no money [L0127, L0128].
- Expulsion for an offence brings a 2-year entry ban; after a prison sentence the ban is 5 years for less serious crimes, 10 for serious ones, life for especially serious ones [L0093, L0094, L0095]. No visa is issued until the ban ends [L0097].

In detention, the Ombudsman's free 115 hotline is meant for people in closed institutions facing torture, violence or cruel treatment [L0139, L0184]. A returnee who spent over three months in detention said detainees were made to stand for hours in the sun [L0178].

> **Need to know**
> - Where are the special centres for people awaiting expulsion, and can the Bangladesh mission visit them?
> - Who pays the air ticket of a Bangladeshi expelled with no money and no traceable employer?

#### False papers

Using a document you know is forged: a fine of 500 to 1,000 RP or 2 to 5 years in prison [L0136]. False information in a visa, registration or work permit application gets the visa refused [L0098]; forged documents at hiring are grounds for dismissal [L0019].

#### Crossing the border illegally

Crossing the state border illegally, for example into Kazakhstan without papers, is 1 to 3 years' corrective labour, a fine of 500 to 1,000 RP, or up to 2 years in prison [L0135]. (See Irregular routes and undocumented workers.)

#### Drugs

- Making, buying, keeping or carrying drugs for sale, or selling them: 5 to 8 years in prison with confiscation of property [L0132]; up to 12 to 15 years for especially large amounts or sale to a child [L0133].
- Keeping or carrying a large amount without intent to sell: a fine of 1,000 to 2,000 RP or up to 5 years [L0134].

> **Need to know**
> - What happens to a foreigner caught with a small amount of drugs (below "large"), and is he expelled?

### If you aren't paid

**The routes:** the labour inspection, the labour disputes commission at your workplace, or a court (after compulsory mediation); the prosecutor's office also supervises labour law [L0062, L0069]. If the employer has no disputes commission, go to the inspection or the court [L0063]. From Bangladesh and through the Bangladesh mission: see Contacts.

**Deadlines:** within 3 months of learning of the breach, 2 months for a dismissal; unpaid wages can be claimed for 3 years; the same deadlines apply to complaints to the prosecutor and the inspection [L0066, L0067, L0068].

**Keep evidence even without a contract:** WhatsApp or Telegram messages, photos of documents, payment records, colleagues' statements [L0169]. Ask the employer in writing for certified copies of your papers (Your rights at work) [L0012]. An employer that uses workers without a written contract is fined 75 RP (person) or 230 RP (company) [L0088].

1. **Complain to the labour inspection,** the Service for Control and Supervision of Labour Legislation under the Ministry of Labour, Social Security and Migration [L0165].
   1. Write a complaint to its local office where the employer is, or go through a public service centre (TsON) in the regions [L0166, L0167].
   2. State the breach, for example: "I, [name], passport [number], work for [employer, address] as [job] since [date]. My wages for [months] have not been paid. I ask for an inspection." [L0170]
   3. It can be anonymous, but that may limit the check [L0168].

   What you get back: an order to the employer to fix the breach [L0171]. In the first half of 2026 inspectors recovered 4.4 million som of unpaid wages for all workers [L0164]. From 26 October 2026 the Service also takes over migration control of foreign labour [L0165], so a worker without valid papers who complains may face migration checks.

   > **Need to know**
   > - What are the address and phone of the labour inspection in Bishkek, and does it take complaints from foreign workers?
   > - Does the inspection take a complaint in English, or must it be in Russian or Kyrgyz?
2. **Labour disputes commission** at the workplace, if the employer has one; it must decide within 10 days [L0064].
3. **Unpaid wages already calculated** (on your payslip) are recovered through a notary since 1 January 2026 [L0065].

   > **Need to know**
   > - How does a worker get a notary's enforcement order for unpaid wages, and what does it cost?
4. **Court,** for disputes the other routes don't settle, after compulsory mediation [L0062]. Work-injury compensation and a refused accident report go straight to court [L0070, L0071]. Money claims found justified are paid in full [L0072].
5. **The Ombudsman (Akyikatchy):** free hotline 115, WhatsApp (0999) 000-115, in writing at the office, or at ombudsman.kg [L0184, L0137, L0138].

**Late pay** earns 0.25% a day (Your rights at work) [L0048]. If the employer broke the law or contract, you can resign and the employer must release you on the date you ask, once the inspection, a union or a court confirms the breach [L0014, L0015].

**Employers on the bad list.** The Ministry of Foreign Affairs keeps a Register of Bad-Faith Employers, for unpaid wages, worse working conditions or not paying a worker's way home; a listed employer stays on it for a year [L0116, L0117, L0113, L0118].

**In practice:** Bangladeshis unpaid in Kyrgyzstan have ended up undocumented and jailed rather than paid. A welder injured on the job, working without a contract, went four months unpaid, surrendered to the police and spent over three months in jail [L0177, L0175]. In 2022 a Kyrgyz human rights organisation helped rescue unpaid garment workers, while their rights group said BMET stalled although they had gone with BMET clearance [L0182, L0183]. BRAC said many of the 180 people who came home on 10 September 2025 had no work, no wages and no papers, and some families paid ransom [L0172, L0188, L0173].

> **Need to know**
> - Has any Bangladeshi in Kyrgyzstan recovered unpaid wages through the inspection, a court or the Bangladesh mission?
> - Which NGO or legal clinic in Bishkek takes foreign workers' wage cases free?
> - Does the employer's own permit lapse (and the worker become undocumented) if the employer stops paying taxes, as the rules imply [L0120]?

### Changing employer or leaving

#### If you want to change employer

**You can't move your permit.** The Single Permit (the combined work visa and work permit) cannot be passed to another employer, and you cannot be transferred [L0108]. It lapses when the job ends, early or on time, or the employer closes [L0107]. A new employer would have to get you a new permit.

**Running away.** If you leave without notice and the employer reports you to the police, no other employer may hire you, nor may the same employer again [L0114, L0115]. With no valid permit you fall under What can get you jailed or deported.

**A second job.** A resident card can list up to three organisations you work for [L0130].

> **Need to know**
> - Can a worker whose permit lapsed get a new Single Permit from a new employer without leaving Kyrgyzstan?
> - The Temporary Regulation runs to 31 December 2026 [L0106]: what replaces it in 2027?

#### If you resign or are dismissed

- **Resigning:** one month's written notice [L0013]. If you leave a fixed-term contract early without a valid reason, the employer may claim up to one month's average pay [L0016]. You can also end it by agreement; the other side must answer a written proposal within 3 working days [L0017].
- **Dismissal:** for example for absence of over 3 hours in a row without good reason, or for forged documents at hiring [L0018, L0019]. Not while on sick leave or holiday, unless the business closes [L0022].
- **Closure or staff cuts:** 1 month's written notice, one paid day a week to look for work, and severance of at least 2 months' average pay [L0020, L0021, L0023].

#### Leaving at the end of the job

1. **The 30 days.** When the job ends you have 30 days to leave on your Single Permit; then it is cancelled automatically [L0109, L0110]. The employer must cancel it within 3 calendar days [L0112]. Your residence card is also cancelled when work stops [L0119].
2. **The ticket.** The employer must pay for your departure; if it doesn't, it goes on the Register of Bad-Faith Employers [L0111, L0113].
3. **What you are owed by the last working day:** all wages, and pay for unused leave [L0047, L0039].
4. **Overstayed?** You need the paid exit visa (What can get you jailed or deported) [L0123, L0102].
5. **Too ill to travel alone?** A free emergency exit visa (type E) [L0126].

> **Need to know**
> - In practice, do Kyrgyz employers pay Bangladeshi workers' tickets home?

#### Going home on leave

Annual leave is 28 days (Your rights at work) [L0037].

### If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job

#### Injury at work

1. **Get treated.** The employer is responsible for work injuries, including on its transport to and from work [L0056].
2. **Ask for an accident report.** If the employer refuses or you disagree with it, go to court [L0071].
3. **Claim compensation:** lost earnings, extra costs, a one-off sum set by the Cabinet of Ministers for disability, moral damage [L0057, L0059]. It can't be refused entirely even if you were careless [L0058]. The claim goes straight to court [L0070].
4. **Back in Bangladesh:** the Wage Earners' Welfare Board pays up to Tk 2 lakh for treatment of workers who return disabled or critically ill, if they had BMET clearance or Board membership [L0149, L0150]. Apply within a year of return with medical reports and prescriptions, a bank statement, the arrival stamp and passport copies; a committee sees you on a WhatsApp video call [L0151, L0152].

> **Need to know**
> - What is the Cabinet's one-off compensation for a work injury or death in 2026?

#### If a worker dies

- **In Kyrgyzstan:** the employer pays the family a funeral benefit, and unpaid wages within a week of the documents [L0055, L0049, L0050]. For a death at work, dependants get his average monthly earnings minus his own share, plus the one-off compensation [L0060, L0059]; children under 18 count as dependants without proof [L0061].
- **Bringing the body home:** the family applies to the Wage Earners' Welfare Board, which sends their wishes to the Bangladesh mission [L0147]. Call 16135 (free in Bangladesh) or +880 9610102030 from abroad [L0148].
- **At the airport:** a Tk 35,000 cheque for transport and burial, with an heirs' certificate from the Union Parishad chairman, mayor or councillor [L0145, L0146].
- **Tk 3 lakh grant** to the family, only if the worker had BMET clearance or Board membership and died in the country he was cleared for [L0140, L0141]. A worker cleared for Kyrgyzstan who dies in Russia or Europe gets nothing. Send [L0142, L0143, L0144]:
  - a family-members certificate from the Union Parishad chairman, mayor or councillor, countersigned by the Upazila Nirbahi Officer;
  - an indemnity, undertaking and power of attorney on Tk 400 stamp paper;
  - a copy of the embassy's NOC or the death certificate.

> **Need to know**
> - Who pays to fly a body from Bishkek to Dhaka, and what does it cost?
> - Which Bangladesh mission issues the NOC for a death in Kyrgyzstan?

- Kyrgyz law ties the worker hard to the employer: the Single Permit is not transferable and dies with the job, leaving 30 days to leave [L0107-L0110].
- 2025 returnee stories show unpaid workers ending up undocumented and jailed, not compensated [L0174, L0175].

- No report of a Bangladeshi death in Kyrgyzstan was found.
