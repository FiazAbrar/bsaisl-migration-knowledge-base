## a. Draft doc text

### For the intro (rates line)

On 10 November 2026, USD 1 = KGS 87.45 (Kyrgyz som) at the National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic (NBKR) and Tk 124.42 at Bangladesh Bank, so KGS 1 = about Tk 1.42 and KGS 1,000 = about Tk 1,423 [G0001, G0002, G0003].

### For Background, "What you pay, and what you earn and keep" (topic: What it costs to live in Bishkek)

(Suggested H4 "What it costs to live" inside "What you pay, and what you earn and keep", or a short part of Step 7: After arrival.)

The government's subsistence minimum for Bishkek was 9,569.55 som (about Tk 13,600) per person a month in early 2026, 10,722 som (about Tk 15,250) for a working-age adult, and food alone was 6,220 som (about Tk 8,850) [G0046, G0003]. A worker who pays his own housing and food spends far more than that:
- **A room or a shared place:** 8,000 to 20,000 som a month (about Tk 11,400 to 28,500) in June 2026 listings; a studio or one-room flat starts at 20,000 to 25,000 som, 30,000 to 45,000 som furnished [G0047, G0003].
- **Food, cooked at home:** about 10,000 to 13,000 som a month (about Tk 14,200 to 18,500) [G0048, G0003].
- **Bus:** 17 som a ride paid by card, 20 som in cash; a monthly transport card costs 1,020 som (about Tk 1,450) [G0049, G0003].
- **In all, living as cheaply as possible:** about 27,000 to 42,000 som a month (about Tk 38,400 to 59,800) for one person renting a room or shared place [G0050, G0003].

For comparison, the average wage was 46,300 som (about Tk 65,900) across Kyrgyzstan in the first quarter of 2026 and about 60,000 som (about Tk 85,400) in Bishkek [G0051, G0003].

### For "Your rights at work" (or Background, "What the job pays"): Tax and deductions from your pay

(Suggested H3 or H4 "Tax and deductions from your pay"; agent L may place it next to social insurance.)

Kyrgyzstan taxes income at a basic rate of 10%, under the Tax Code of 18 January 2022 [G0040]. When a company or a registered individual entrepreneur employs a foreign worker, the employer is the tax agent: it must withhold the income tax from the pay and pay it, with all other mandatory payments, to the State Tax Service [G0042]. Contributions to the Social Fund (state social insurance) are also charged on the pay of foreign employees, at main rates of 27.25% or 12.25%, of which 10% is taken from the worker's pay and the rest paid by the employer [G0041]. In the garment industry, the State Tax Service said in March 2026 that it had cut the mandatory payments for each worker (income tax and social insurance together) from 5,500 to 9,500 som to a single 2,500 som (about Tk 3,560) [G0044, G0003].

A different system applies to migrants from neighbouring countries hired by private Kyrgyz individuals for construction, farm or household work: they pay a fixed tax through a patent, after getting a personal identification number (PIN) at a Public Service Center and the first paper patent from the local tax office (passport, notarised translation, PIN certificate and temporary registration card), and renew it online at cabinet.salyk.kg [G0043].

> **Need to know**
> - How much income tax and Social Fund contribution is actually deducted each month from a Bangladeshi garment or construction worker's pay, and does the payslip show it?
> - Can a Bangladeshi working for a private individual (not a company) get a patent, or is the patent only for citizens of neighbouring countries?
> - Is there a Bangladesh-Kyrgyzstan double taxation agreement?

## Sending money home

**Send through a bank or a money transfer system on the National Bank of the Kyrgyz Republic's register, never hundi** [G0004]. Hundi (sending money through unlicensed private agents, outside the banking system) is illegal in Bangladesh, and hundi brokers pay out through bKash and Nagad mobile-money wallets [G0022, G0023]. Bangladesh's Financial Intelligence Unit (BFIU) had frozen or suspended about 55,000 mobile-money accounts linked to digital hundi and online gambling by June 2026, and decided in August 2026 to freeze about 14,000 more [G0024]. Kyrgyzstan also prosecutes informal money channels: in May 2025 the State Committee for National Security (GKNB) detained two men who had moved more than USD 20 million through Bishkek exchange points outside official records and kept some clients' money, including USD 2 million from one man [G0025]. Money sent legally earns the family Bangladesh's 2.5% cash incentive, kept in the FY2026-27 budget, and sometimes a further 2.5% from the bank; hundi gets neither [G0021, G0017].

Bangladesh Bank does not publish a separate figure for remittances from Kyrgyzstan: its country table counts them under "Others" [G0020].

### Which channels work from Kyrgyzstan

- **Transfer systems licensed in Kyrgyzstan.** The NBKR's register lists the operators allowed to provide money transfers in the country; Western Union (registered 2018), MoneyGram (2014) and Ria Money Transfer (2017) are on it, among 20 international systems in July 2026 [G0004, G0005, G0006, G0007, G0008]. To check a name, open nbkr.kg, go to the page "Реестр международных и локальных систем денежных переводов" (register of international and local money transfer systems; nbkr.kg/index1.jsp?item=2665&lang=RUS) and look under "Операторы международных систем денежных переводов" (operators of international systems) [G0004].
- **Banks.** Optima Bank, for example, sends money without a bank account through Western Union, Zolotaya Korona, MoneyGram, Astrasend and Ria, and to bank accounts abroad through SWIFT (the international bank transfer network), at all its branches [G0009]. Western Union has agent locations in Bishkek, Osh and Jalal-Abad [G0012].
- **Bank apps.** MBANK's app sends money abroad to 160 countries, to phone numbers, mobile wallets and bank accounts: Payments, then Transfers, then "В другую страну" (to another country), then the transfer type and payment service; the fee depends on the bank's partner [G0018].
- **Not available: sending straight to bKash from a listed Kyrgyz partner.** bKash's list of countries with partners that pay into a bKash wallet does not include Kyrgyzstan (checked 10 November 2026) [G0013]. bKash tells people whose country is not listed to call +88-02-55663001 or e-mail support@bkash.com [G0014]. Profee, a transfer app, advertises payments from Kyrgyzstan to bKash, Nagad and Rocket wallets by Visa or Mastercard card, but it is not on the NBKR register [G0019, G0004].

### Step by step: sending through a bank or transfer company

You'll need:
- the receiver's surname, first name and bank account number, or if he has no account, the details of his identity document [G0010];
- for a bKash wallet, if your channel pays into bKash, the receiver's bKash number and full name exactly as registered when the account was opened [G0015].

1. **Check the company is licensed.** Find it in the NBKR register (see Which channels work from Kyrgyzstan) [G0004]. Change som into dollars only at a bank or licensed exchange bureau: in February 2026 the NBKR fined 14 people in Bishkek 17,500 som each for changing currency without a licence [G0026].
2. **Fill in the form at the branch.** Write the receiver's exact details on the bank's application form, hand over the money and pay the commission at the bank's tariff [G0011].
3. **The family collects.** A remittance into bKash can be up to Tk 2.5 lakh (about USD 2,000) per transfer, any number of times, and cashed out at Tk 7 per thousand at BRAC Bank, City Bank and Q-Cash ATMs; the 2.5% government incentive is added on receipt [G0016, G0017, G0002].

> **Need to know**
> - Which documents does a Kyrgyz bank ask a foreign worker for at the counter to send money abroad (passport only, or also registration, work permit or PIN)?
> - What is the receipt's reference called in Ria and MoneyGram, and how does the family check the transfer from Bangladesh?

**If it fails, complain in this order:**
1. **The bank or transfer company.** Complain in writing (by letter or electronically) with your full name, address, phone numbers, what happened and copies of the receipt; the answer must be signed by the head of the bank or branch and give its reasons [G0052].
2. **The NBKR.** If you disagree with the answer or get none, complain to the National Bank by letter or e-mail, mail@nbkr.kg, with your full name, phone, address, what happened and copies of documents; public reception (0312) 61 04 86 (+996 312 61 04 86), also 0555 85 57 48 and 0770 73 73 49; Osh office (03222) 48434 [G0053, G0054].
3. **bKash**, for a wallet transfer: 16247 in Bangladesh, +88-02-55663001 from abroad, support@bkash.com [G0014].

> **Need to know**
> - Does the NBKR take complaints in English, and how long does it take to answer?

### Your salary and a bank account

A bank can open a current account for a foreigner who does not live permanently in Kyrgyzstan (a "non-resident"). At Bakai Bank, for example, you fill in a client form, an application, a signature card and the account agreement, and give a copy of your passport and, where required, of the document that gives you the right to stay in Kyrgyzstan, showing the originals (for the papers that give you the right to stay, see Work visa: company worker, Step 7: After arrival) [G0045].

### Saving at home: the Wage Earner Development Bond

The Wage Earner Development Bond is a 5-year Bangladesh government bond for workers abroad, bought at authorised-dealer branches of banks in Bangladesh and at Bangladeshi banks' branches and exchange houses abroad, in sizes from Tk 25,000 to Tk 50 lakh, with no limit on how much you buy [G0027]. It pays 12% simple profit on holdings up to Tk 15 lakh (11% for Tk 15-30 lakh, 10% for Tk 30-50 lakh, 9% above), every six months; the profit is income-tax free and the principal can be taken in US dollars [G0028].

> **Need to know**
> - Can a worker in Kyrgyzstan, where no Bangladeshi bank has a branch or exchange house, buy the bond through a family member in Bangladesh?

### For "Coming home" (or "Leaving at the end of the job"): What you can carry home

(Suggested H3 "What you can carry home" under Coming home, with a pointer from Leaving at the end of the job.)

- **Leaving Kyrgyzstan with cash.** A foreigner may not take more than USD 5,000 (about Tk 6.2 lakh) in cash foreign currency out of Kyrgyzstan, under a Cabinet decree of 13 January 2023 that stays in force until the Cabinet lifts it; the Border Service and Customs enforce it [G0029, G0031, G0002]. The Border Service reminded travellers of the ban in September 2024, after stopping an Uzbek citizen with USD 30,000 [G0032]. In July 2026 customs at Manas airport (Bishkek) stopped an Uzbek citizen carrying USD 28,100 without declaring it, and opened a criminal case [G0033]. Banks send larger sums abroad by bank transfer through SWIFT (see Step by step: sending through a bank or transfer company) [G0009].
- **Arriving in Bangladesh:**
  - Cash: any amount of foreign currency may be brought, but more than USD 10,000 (about Tk 12.4 lakh) must be declared on the FMJ form [G0034, G0002].
  - Phones: 2 used phones, plus 1 new phone once a year, without duty [G0035].
  - Duty-free: one item from the duty-free list, such as a laptop, a television up to 29 inches, a microwave, a sewing machine, or 100 g of gold and 200 g of silver jewellery (no more than 12 pieces of one type) [G0038].
  - Gold bar: once a year, one declared bar of up to 117 g (10 tola), at Tk 5,000 duty per 11.664 g; undeclared gold means arrest and a criminal case [G0036, G0037].
  - Alcohol: none for Bangladeshi passport holders [G0039].

> **Need to know**
> - Is the USD 5,000 cash limit for foreigners leaving Kyrgyzstan still enforced at Manas airport in 2026, and does it cover som and roubles as well as dollars?
> - Can a foreign worker take a National Bank gold bar bought in Bishkek out of Kyrgyzstan, and with what papers?

**Conflicts and caveats**
- Cash export: the official decree text (edition 1 Aug 2024, latest on cbd.minjust.gov.kg) still says "до принятия соответствующего решения Кабинета Министров" (until the Cabinet decides); no repeal found.
