## Work visa: company worker

A Kyrgyz employer applies online for a Unified Permit (Единое разрешение, Yedinoye razresheniye), one electronic document that combines the work visa, the permit to employ foreign labour and the work permit; you enter on it for up to 60 days, and the employer then turns it into a resident card (резидент-карта), a plastic card that lets you live and work for up to a year [W0027, V0048, W0079]. These rules sit in a Temporary Regulation approved by Cabinet Resolution No. 312 and valid only until 31 December 2026, and presidential decree No. 337 of 28 September 2026 moved the Unified Permit and resident card from the MFA (Ministry of Foreign Affairs) to a new Department for Migration Affairs in the Ministry of Labour, Social Security and Migration, which is to hand the service to an outside contractor [W0001, W0003, L0106, K0013, K0014, K0015, K0020, K0016]. The Cabinet was given two months to bring its rules into line [K0021]. BMET (the Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training) cleared 11 workers for Kyrgyzstan from 1 January to 9 November 2026 (see Status) [B0080].

> **Need to know**
> - Since October 2026, does the employer still apply for a Unified Permit on the e-Visa portal (evisa.e-gov.kg), or through a new Ministry of Labour portal or contractor, and who is the contractor?
> - What replaces the Temporary Regulation after 31 December 2026?

### Step 1: Find a real job

A company worker's Unified Permit is applied for by the employer, for a worker it names [W0049, W0053]. Bangladesh and Kyrgyzstan have no labour agreement: on 14 October 2026 their officials only explored signing an MoU (memorandum of understanding) [B0139]. How names reach employers, and who sells them, is in Background (see How a job reaches you, and who takes a cut).

1. **Register on the OEP (Overseas Employment Platform),** BMET's website for job-seekers [B0003]. The Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare warns never to go without BMET clearance (Step 6, item 2) [B0006].

   You'll need [B0002, B0003]:

   - your NID (national ID card);
   - a mobile number;
   - Tk 200 for the government fee.

   1. Open [employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration](https://employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration) and type your name in English, your mobile and NID numbers, and your date of birth, division and district exactly as on the NID; type the captcha, accept the terms and press "যাচাই করুন" (Verify) [B0002].
   2. Finish the form and pay the Tk 200 fee [B0003].
   3. Take 2 printed copies of the registration slip to your DEMO (District Employment and Manpower Office, BMET's district office) to give your fingerprints; the DEMO list is on BMET's website [B0003, B0004].

   **If it fails:** call the Probashi call centre, 16135 (free), or +8809610102030 from abroad [B0023].

2. **Know which jobs exist.** The Kyrgyz Ministry of Labour names construction and light industry as the sectors most in need of foreign workers, and says Bangladeshis usually work in garments [W0013, N0057]. Beyond the plain "Worker" title, the jobs BMET cleared for Kyrgyzstan were mostly garment jobs: garments worker, sewing equipment operator and swing line checker (sewing line checker) [B0102, B0103, B0104, B0107, B0109, B0111]. Garment shops keep the workers who can sew and leave the rest without shelter or money, a Bishkek shop owner said in 2024 [N0078]. Nearly half of all foreign workers (46%) are in Jalal-Abad region in the south, not in Bishkek [W0104]. BMET's TTCs (Technical Training Centres) are listed with contacts on its website [B0005].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Do Kyrgyz garment employers test sewing skill before hiring Bangladeshis, and where (in Dhaka, by video, or on arrival)?
   > - Has BOESL (Bangladesh Overseas Employment and Services Limited, the government recruiting agency) ever posted a Kyrgyzstan circular?

3. **Through a Bangladeshi recruiting agency: check the licence and the approval.** You'll need the agency's name and RL number (the recruiting licence number every licensed agency has) [B0154].

   1. On [oep.gov.bd/agencies](https://www.oep.gov.bd/agencies), find the RL number and check the status says Active, not Expired or Cancelled, and that the validity date has not passed [B0154, B0155].
   2. Go to the address on that list, not a middleman's office [B0154].
   3. Ask whether the job order has BMET's permission or the Ministry's approval. Industry insiders said in November 2026 that demand letters for up to seven workers had needed no Ministry approval, and that this was used to send workers on fake job offers [B0117].

   A licence and a BMET smart card are no guarantee: in 2022, garment workers sent with valid visas, work permits and BMET smart cards by Makkah Madina Trade International (RL 810) and Al-Jabeen Establishment Ltd (RL 884) said they had been sold to a Bishkek factory owner for USD 8,300 (about Tk 10.3 lakh) [N0126, N0128, N0125, G0002]. Al-Jabeen's managing director said the returnees could not do the work [N0130].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which licensed agencies sent workers to Kyrgyzstan in 2024-2025, and for which Kyrgyz employers?
   > - Does BMET issue permission letters for Kyrgyzstan job orders, and where are they published? None appear on BMET's list of posted letters.

4. **If the job comes through a Kyrgyz recruiting firm or a Bangladeshi already in Kyrgyzstan,** know the rules they work under. Bishkek recruiting firms bring workers mainly from Bangladesh through a partner there who finds the people, and Bangladeshis already working in Kyrgyzstan offer to invite friends and relatives [N0069]. Kyrgyz law lets a private employment agency charge a worker a one-time fee of at most half of one month's wage, and the government keeps a public database of licensed agencies [N0145, N0147]. A Bishkek recruiter said in 2024 that 70-80% of intermediaries take large sums and then arrange no visa or job, or a job at lower pay than promised, and that most deceived Bangladeshis were brought by fellow Bangladeshis [N0070, N0076].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Where is the Kyrgyz database of licensed private employment agencies, and can a worker in Bangladesh search it by firm name?

5. **Never go on a tourist or business visa on a broker's promise of a job.** The Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare warns against it [B0006]. A business visa (type B) gives no right to work or earn income in Kyrgyzstan, and a valid visa or registration alone does not let anyone hire you [V0016, W0142]. Working on one is fined (see Tried by workers) [L0079].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Can a Bangladeshi already in Kyrgyzstan on a tourist or business visa get a Unified Permit without leaving, and do agents sell this "conversion"?

6. **Have the papers checked before you pay.** BMET checks job visas free within 3 working days at its DEMOs or at BMET, 89/2 Kakrail, Dhaka; its online check covers only Saudi, Qatari, Bahraini, Singapore and UAE visas, not Kyrgyz ones [B0008, B0013, B0052]. Check the Unified Permit yourself as in Step 5 [W0043].

7. **Know what you should pay.** Pay any Bangladeshi agency only into its own bank account, and keep every receipt [B0007]. Under Kyrgyz law whoever invites you must arrange your stay documents, and the employer files and pays the permit fees (Step 3, item 2) [W0018, W0064, W0055]. The official costs on your side, each in the step that owns it:

   - OEP registration: Tk 200 (Step 1, item 1) [B0003];
   - passport: Tk 4,025 to Tk 10,350 (Step 4, item 1) [B0024];
   - apostille of certificates: Tk 200 a page (Step 4, item 3) [B0035];
   - police clearance, if asked: Tk 1,500 (Step 4, item 2) [B0032];
   - BMET clearance: Tk 5,500 welfare fee, Tk 1,000 insurance, Tk 250 clearance fee and source tax of Tk 250 to Tk 1,200 (Step 6, item 2) [B0019, B0010, B0153].

   What people really pay, and to whom, is in Background (see What you pay, and what you earn and keep).

   > **Need to know**
   > - Has BMET or the Ministry set a maximum migration cost for Kyrgyzstan? The Ministry's cost table does not list it.
   > - Who pays the flight to Bishkek in practice: the employer or the worker?

8. **If you need to borrow,** Probashi Kallyan Bank (PKB), the government bank for migrant workers, lends up to Tk 3 lakh at 9% simple interest, repaid monthly over up to 3 years, and decides within 7 working days [B0038, B0059].

   You'll need [B0056, B0057, B0058, B0040]:

   - to be 18 to 60, with you and your guarantors living in the branch's area;
   - a PKB savings account opened with at least Tk 500;
   - the free bank form, 3 photos and an NID copy;
   - copies of your passport, visa and BMET card, and the contract if you have one;
   - two guarantors: one financially solvent, one a family member or close relative.

   Women can take PKB's women's migration loan with one guarantor [B0060]. Apply at a branch or on mygov.bd, which sends application and tracking numbers by SMS; helpline 16238, or +880 9677-787878 from abroad [B0061, B0042].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does PKB lend against a Kyrgyz Unified Permit, which has no visa sticker?

9. **If an agency or broker takes your money,** complain in writing to BMET's Director General with your evidence (receipts, passport copy, messages); it is free and meant to be settled within 30 working days [B0018]. Hand it in at BMET, 89/2 Kakrail, Dhaka-1000 [B0052]. Then, in this order:

   1. BMET's Additional Director General (Employment): 02-48321949, 01720105492, adgemp@bmet.gov.bd [B0014].
   2. The Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare's grievance officer: 02-41030224, 01712992086, adsadmin@probashi.gov.bd (30 working days) [B0055].
   3. The Probashi call centre, 16135 [B0023].
   4. For an unlicensed office, the police: in the Jannat Trade International case a victim filed a case under the human trafficking law at Paltan police station, and the CID (Criminal Investigation Department) arrested the owner (see Done to workers) [N0119, B0156].

### Step 2: The job offer and contract

1. **Get the employer's details in writing before you pay anyone.** The employer's application is a statement letter to the Director of the MFA's Consular Department naming you, your passport number, the purpose and the period, in which the head of the company undertakes to keep your stay legal [W0049, W0050]. The permit ties you to that employer, the type of work, the position and the period, and it cannot be passed to another employer [W0141, L0108]. Ask whoever brings the job for:

   - the employer's full legal name and its address in Kyrgyzstan [W0049];
   - the job title and the town or region of the workplace [W0141, W0090];
   - the monthly wage in som (KGS, the Kyrgyz currency), since minimum pay for foreign workers is set by region and sector [W0090];
   - a copy of the statement letter [W0049].

   **For comparison:** a 2024 job order approved by Pakistan's emigration bureau for garment workers in Bishkek paid 35,000 to 37,000 som a month (about Tk 49,700 to Tk 52,500), with free housing, food, medical care and transport, but no free air ticket [W0143, G0003].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What minimum monthly wage must a Unified Permit application show for garment or construction work in 2026, and where is it published?
   > - Do Kyrgyz employers give Bangladeshi workers a copy of the statement letter before departure?

2. **Get the employment contract before the permit is filed, and read it.** The employer uploads the employment contract with its application, so the contract exists before the permit is issued [W0092]. Documents on the portal must be in Kyrgyz or Russian or translated into one of them [W0035]. Get the contract from the agency at least 2 days before you go and check the job, employer, workplace, length, pay, leave and airfare against what you were promised [B0062]. Check in particular:

   - **Housing, food and medical care:** the inviting side pays for your keep and medical care unless you agree otherwise in writing [W0019]. The garment association Legprom's director said in January 2026 that some employers break their contracts by not providing housing [N0091].
   - **How you are paid:** the employer must pay wages to a bank card, not in cash (Step 7, item 4) [W0068, L0129].
   - **Your copy:** the contract is made in two copies, and one is yours [L0006].

   What a contract must contain under the Labour Code is in Your rights at work [L0001].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What wage and conditions do BMET permission letters for Kyrgyzstan state, and do workers get the same contract in Bishkek?
   > - Is the contract signed in Dhaka, or only on arrival in Kyrgyzstan, and do workers get a Bangla or English copy?

3. **Give the employer exactly what is in your passport.** The passport must be valid at least 6 months beyond the permit and have at least 2 free pages, and at the border you must show the same passport the visa was issued on [W0036, W0037, V0030]. Get the passport first (Step 4, item 1) [W0036].

**Scams:** an offer of "a visa now, the work permit after you arrive" leaves you on a visa that does not allow work (Step 1, item 5); many South Asians brought on such promises never got the work visa and moved from workshop to workshop without wages [V0016, U0045, U0046].

### Step 3: The employer gets your work permit

You do nothing at this step except send documents; the employer files everything on the e-Visa portal, evisa.e-gov.kg, and pays [W0064, W0066, W0054, W0055]. There is no longer a commission that shares out quota places: since 1 January 2025 the employer applies directly for a named worker, and one application gives one Unified Permit [W0052, W0053]. The MFA's Consular Department issued the permit under Resolution No. 312 until decree No. 337 of 28 September 2026 moved this to the Ministry of Labour [W0001, W0135, K0013, K0014].

**The quota.** Each year the Cabinet of Ministers sets the maximum number of foreign workers, and Unified Permits are issued only within it [W0015, W0095]. The 2026 limit was set at 52,000 and raised to 100,000 by an order of 30 April 2026 [W0011, W0010]. Since 2025 the quota no longer lists types of work [W0012]. When Kyrgyzstan stopped quotas for Bangladeshis before is in Background (see What closed it before).

1. **The employer applies on the e-Visa portal.** In its personal account (Личный кабинет, Lichnyy kabinet) on [evisa.e-gov.kg](https://www.evisa.e-gov.kg/) the employer [W0091, W0064, W0065, W0066]:

   1. creates the company account;
   2. fills in the online quota form;
   3. fills in the form for you by name and uploads scans of the documents.

   The documents include the employer's statement letter, copies of documents, the employment contract, medical certificates and any licence the work needs [W0092]. Your part is a scan of your passport (Step 2, item 3) and any certificate the employer asks for, apostilled and translated (Step 4, item 3) [W0036, V0029].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What exact documents does the portal ask of a Bangladeshi worker for a Unified Permit in 2026 (police clearance, education certificate, medical certificate)?

2. **The employer pays.** The fee is paid when the application is filed and covers the work visa and the work permit; the employer also pays a non-refundable guarantee deposit [W0054, W0055]. The guarantee fee, 50 RP (calculation units) per worker, pays for the departure or expulsion of a worker who has no money of his own [L0127, L0128]. Under the 2025 rules (Resolution No. 367) the costs per worker were [W0057, W0058, W0059, W0060, W0061, W0062, G0002, G0003]:

   - permit to employ foreign labour: 20,000 som (about Tk 28,400);
   - guarantee deposit: 5,000 som (about Tk 7,100);
   - electronic work permit: 5,000 som (about Tk 7,100);
   - Unified Permit for entry and the first 60 days: USD 75 (about Tk 9,300);
   - extension to 360 days: USD 225 (about Tk 28,000);
   - in total, 30,000 som plus USD 300 (about Tk 80,000).

   They are paid in two stages: first for two months, then for 12 months [W0063]. Resolution No. 312 replaced these rules on 30 April 2026 [W0137]. Resolution No. 506 of 23 July 2026 gave garment companies a special rate until 1 November 2026, on a letter from the Chamber of Commerce and Industry confirming membership [K0001, K0008]: USD 75 for the Unified Permit, paid in som at the National Bank rate, plus 20,000 som state duty and a 5,000 som guarantee deposit; and USD 225 for the resident card plus 5,000 som for the electronic work permit [K0002, K0003, K0004, K0006, K0007]. These are the same amounts as the 2025 rates, and the special rate lapsed on 1 November 2026 [W0057, W0058, W0060, W0061, K0001]. Fees paid on the portal are not refunded if the application is refused [W0038].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What are the standard Unified Permit and resident card fees under Resolution No. 312 (Annex 5) since 1 November 2026, and was the garment rate extended?
   > - How much of the permit cost do agencies or brokers charge Bangladeshi workers, and under what name?

3. **The permit is decided.** Under the 2025 rules a decision took 7 to 10 working days, and the 2026 garment rate set up to 7 working days [W0087, K0002]. Since 2026 the permit can be issued urgently for an extra fee [W0007]. If the portal returns the application for corrections, the 5-working-day review starts again once they are made [W0042]. The approved permit, with its QR code, appears in the employer's account [W0067].

   **If it is refused:** the reasons are not explained, and the refusal can be challenged in court [L0122]. A permit is refused or annulled for false information, a crime, a dangerous disease, a security threat or terrorist links [W0093].

**What you get back:** a copy of the Unified Permit with its QR code, and the 8-character reference number of the application (Step 5) [W0067, W0044].

### Step 4: Passport, police clearance and certificates

1. **Get an e-passport** before the employer files, so the permit carries your exact details (Step 2, item 3) [W0049]. Over age 20 you need your NID, and you fill in the form exactly as on it [B0027].

   1. Fill in the form at [epassport.gov.bd](https://www.epassport.gov.bd/) and choose the passport office for your present address [B0068, B0069].
   2. Pay online through A-Challan, DGePay or ShurjoPay, or at any bank through A-Challan [B0026]. A 48-page passport costs Tk 4,025, Tk 6,325 or Tk 8,625 for 5 years and Tk 5,750, Tk 8,050 or Tk 10,350 for 10 years (regular, express, super express) [B0024].
   3. Go to the office with your original NID and any technical certificate [B0070]. With an online-verified NID there is no police verification, and delivery is within 15, 7 or 2 working days of enrolment [B0029, B0025].

   **If it is lost or stolen:** file a GD (general diary, a police report) at the nearest police station quickly, then re-apply with a copy of the old passport and the GD [B0028]. Passport helpline: 16445 (09666716445 from abroad), 24 hours [B0030]. For a passport lost or expiring in Kyrgyzstan, see The Bangladesh Embassy in Tashkent [B0127].

2. **Get a police clearance if the employer or agency asks for one.**

   You'll need [B0031, B0032]:

   - a passport valid at least 3 months;
   - a present address that is one of the addresses in your passport;
   - Tk 1,500 by treasury challan, code 1-7301-0001-2681, at any Bangladesh Bank or Sonali Bank branch, or online by card with a service charge.

   1. Register at [pcc.police.gov.bd](https://pcc.police.gov.bd/), log in and click "Apply" [B0065].
   2. Fill in your personal details and your present and permanent addresses; the police verify you at the present address [B0066].
   3. Upload the scanned documents [B0067].
   4. Track it by texting "PCC S <reference>" to 26969, and collect it with your passport or application copy [B0033, B0071].

   **If it fails:** helpdesk Sunday to Thursday, 9 am to 5 pm, 01320001824, 01320001825, help.pcc@police.gov.bd [B0034].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does the Unified Permit application require a Bangladeshi police clearance?

3. **Apostille and translate any certificate the Kyrgyz side asks for.** Documents issued abroad must carry an apostille (the international certificate under the Hague Apostille Convention) or consular legalisation, with a notarised translation into Kyrgyz or Russian [V0029]. Kyrgyzstan accepts the apostille that Bangladesh's MoFA (Ministry of Foreign Affairs) issues online, and for Kyrgyzstan no stamp at MoFA's consular counter is needed [B0150, B0148, B0149].

   1. Have the document verified by its issuer first: SSC and HSC (secondary and higher secondary school) certificates by the Education Board, then the Ministry of Education [B0037]. Experience certificates must first be attested by BMET or the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare and notarised [B0064].
   2. On [mygov.bd](https://www.mygov.bd/), register, choose the attestation service for your type of document, fill in the form and upload the document; it costs Tk 200 a page, paid online, and takes 5 working days [B0036, B0035].
   3. An SMS tells you when the apostille is issued; download it from your mygov.bd account [B0036].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which documents does the Kyrgyz side ask Bangladeshi workers for (diploma, skill certificate, police clearance), and is the Russian translation notarised in Dhaka or in Bishkek?

### Step 5: The work visa, and checking it is real

There is no visa sticker to collect: the Unified Permit is itself the electronic work visa [W0027, W0052]. The portal tells applicants not to buy an air ticket before approval [W0039].

1. **Get a copy of the Unified Permit and its reference number from the employer or agency.** The reference number is an 8-character code of letters and digits [W0044]. Print the permit with its QR code and carry it with your passport [W0027].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Do Manas airport (Bishkek) border guards ask Bangladeshis for a printed Unified Permit, or check it online?

**Check it is real:**

1. **On the portal.** On [evisa.e-gov.kg](https://www.evisa.e-gov.kg/) open Services, then Check status, and enter the reference number: it shows the status of the application [W0043, V0091].
2. **The QR code.** The permit carries a QR code that can be read [W0027].
3. **The details.** Your name and passport number, the employer, the type of work, the position and the period must match what you were promised; a permit from a different company is not the same job, and it cannot be moved to another employer [W0141, L0108].
4. **Ask the MFA directly.** Visa Center hotline (WhatsApp): +996703428865; Consular Department 24-hour hotline: +996999312002; e-visa@mfa.gov.kg; 10 Togolok-Moldo Street, Bishkek; Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm [W0046, W0047, W0045, W0048]. You can send this message [W0044, W0049]:

   "I am [name], Bangladeshi passport [number]. My employer [company name] applied for a Unified Permit for me, reference [code]. Please confirm it is approved, for which employer and position, and until what date."

> **Need to know**
> - What does a genuine Unified Permit's QR code open (which website, which details)?
> - Does the MFA hotline answer workers' questions in English?

**If any check fails:** don't travel; complain as in Step 1, item 9 [W0039, B0018].

**Scams:** the GKNB (State Committee for National Security, Kyrgyzstan's security service) tells foreigners and employers to apply for permits only through the MFA's portal [N0099]. The old ework.e-gov.kg portal closed for quota and permit applications on 25 December 2024, so a quota letter from it is out of date [W0138]. In 2024 Jannat Trade International handed out e-visa copies of which Dhaka airport immigration found no online record (see Done to workers) [N0116].

### Step 6: Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight

1. **Do the compulsory 3-day pre-departure orientation (PDO)** at one of BMET's 70 training institutes; the institute sets the fee, paid against a money receipt, and Feni TTC says its fee is Tk 200 and to pay no more [B0015, B0072]. Download your certificate at [training.oep.gov.bd/pdo-certificate](https://training.oep.gov.bd/pdo-certificate) with your passport or NID number and the captcha [B0020].

2. **Get BMET clearance (the smart card).**

   - **Through an agency (group visa):** the agency applies to BMET's emigration branch with the visa copy, the attested contract, your attested registration, a Tk 300 stamp-paper undertaking that the job is certain, and the pay orders; BMET clears within 3 working days once the visa is issued [B0063].
   - **On your own (single visa):** apply at BMET's emigration branch in Dhaka or the Chattogram DEMO [B0011]. You'll need an application, the visa copy, the contract if you have one, an attested copy of your OEP registration, an undertaking on Tk 300 non-judicial stamp paper and the original pay order for the welfare fee and insurance; it takes 3 working days [B0011]. A woman on a single visa also needs a no-objection letter from her legal guardian on Tk 150 non-judicial stamp paper [B0021].

   Your costs:

   - welfare fee to the Wage Earners' Welfare Board (the government's welfare board for migrant workers): Tk 5,500, from 1 October 2026 [B0019];
   - insurance: Tk 1,000 once, 5 years' cover, Tk 10 lakh [B0010];
   - clearance (smart card) fee: Tk 250 [B0153];
   - source tax: Tk 1,200 (skilled) or Tk 800 (unskilled) on a group visa; Tk 400 on an unattested single visa, Tk 250 on an attested one [B0153].

   **Check it is real** at [portal.wewb.gov.bd/bmet-wewbm-check](https://portal.wewb.gov.bd/bmet-wewbm-check) with your passport number or membership ID and the captcha [B0022]. The Welfare Board's desks at Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet airports check departing workers' smart cards [B0016]. In the Jannat Trade case, immigration at Dhaka airport found the visa, ticket and BMET card fake [B0142].

   **If it fails:** BMET's Director (Emigration), +8802222222415, 01711-135901, diremi@bmet.gov.bd [B0014].

   > **Need to know**
   > - In 2026, does BMET's emigration branch clear a worker who shows a printed Kyrgyz Unified Permit, and does it treat it as a group or single visa?

3. **The flight.** Never route through Dubai on a visit visa at a broker's suggestion: the CID describes that as the trafficking syndicates' route to Kyrgyzstan [B0145]. A 2022 group of workers flew Dhaka-Dubai-Bishkek [N0134].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which airlines and transit points do Kyrgyzstan-bound workers use in 2026, does the transit airport ask for anything, and what does a one-way ticket cost?

4. **Before you fly,** keep one set of copies at home and at least 3 with you, open at least 2 bank accounts (one yours, one a family member's), and never carry a packet whose contents you don't know [B0009]. Bangladesh has no embassy in Kyrgyzstan; save the numbers of the Embassy in Tashkent, Uzbekistan, which covers it: +99871 2556520, +99871 2551971, +99878 1209101 (see The Bangladesh Embassy in Tashkent) [B0120, B0124].

### Step 7: After arrival

The first Unified Permit lasts up to 60 days; get registered at your address as soon as you arrive, and before the permit ends the employer applies for your resident card [W0004, K0029, V0048].

1. **Get registered at your address at once.** Bangladesh is not on the list of 92 countries whose citizens get a registration-free period, and the e-Visa portal says to register within 5 working days of entry [K0029, W0034]. Whoever invites you must arrange your registration, and an employer that gives you company housing must register you [W0018, K0036]. You need not go to the office yourself unless the system has no record of your border crossing; registration is made on the passport you entered with [K0037, K0032]. The rules are in Cabinet Resolution No. 600 of 4 September 2026, and decree No. 337 moved registration from Kyzmat, the state service agency, to the Ministry of Labour [K0026, K0019].

   You'll need [K0038, K0039]:

   - the host's petition (ходатайство, khodataystvo) naming the actual address where you live;
   - your valid passport with its visa;
   - the Unified Permit or resident card;
   - the host's Kyrgyz passport or permanent residence permit;
   - if the host does not own the flat or hostel, the owner's notarised consent;
   - a receipt for the state duty and registration fee.

   1. The host applies through the ESUVM (Unified External Migration Accounting System) at any office of the population registration service, on the Online Registration of Foreign Citizens website, or in its mobile app [K0030]. Kyzmat ran Service Centres for Foreign Citizens in Bishkek at 121 Jumabek Street (Wedding Palace building) and in Osh at 211 Kurmanjan Datka Street, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 [W0131, W0132].
   2. The host pays the state duty and fee within 3 working days of getting the payment code, or the application is deleted; a fee is not refunded if registration is refused [K0035, K0046].

### Scams

#### Done to workers

- **The visa with no job.** A Bangladeshi who came in July 2025 on a work visa bought from intermediaries for USD 5,000 (about Tk 6.2 lakh) found no work and had no money for a ticket home [N0101, N0165, G0002]. In 2025, Facebook pages, WhatsApp groups and YouTube channels drew recruits with Central Asian jobs, naming employers who did not know their names were used; arrivals found no jobs, contracts or sponsors [N0110, N0111]. Check the permit yourself (Step 5) [W0043].
- **The unlicensed office with tourist visas.** In 2024 Jannat Trade International, an office in Naya Paltan, Dhaka, with only a tours and travel licence, advertised car-wash jobs in Kyrgyzstan on YouTube at Tk 60,000-65,000 a month and took Tk 5 lakh for a visa, ticket and BMET card [N0112, B0143, B0152]. It filmed people receiving visas at its office for its YouTube channel [N0114]. At Dhaka airport the papers proved fake, and the six people it did send went on 59-day tourist visas and got no jobs [B0142, B0144, U0033]. The CID's counter-trafficking unit arrested the owner, Ibrahim Mollik Nahid, and two associates [N0120, B0156].
- **The visa that ends on arrival.** A Lalmonirhat welder who reached Bishkek on 3 June 2024 found his visa expired the next day; he worked seven months unpaid and served three months in jail [U0009, U0010].
- **Sold to an employer.** In 2022 workers with valid visas, work permits and BMET cards said their agencies sold them to a garment factory owner, who would let them leave only if they paid him back (Step 1, item 3) [N0125, N0127]. One was paid Tk 10,000 in his first month, of which the employer kept Tk 7,000, saying he had paid the broker [N0129].
- **A different job, and the passport taken.** A 2022 group sent to sew was made to work on a building site and tend livestock, and the shop owner took their passports "for registration" and kept them [N0164, N0132]. In June 2024 a sewing-shop owner near Bishkek and a district police officer took workers' passports, withheld wages and threatened them with prison for illegal stay; both were detained [U0049, U0050].
- **"Legalisation" in Bishkek.** In 2025 the GKNB arrested a Bangladeshi who took USD 66,500 (about Tk 82.7 lakh) from fellow Bangladeshis for papers to stay legally and gave them forged permits; more than 120 passports with expired documents were seized [U0063, U0064, G0002].
- **"Europe through Kyrgyzstan."** See What people believe, and the facts [N0105, N0140].

Complain in Bangladesh as in Step 1, item 9 [B0018].

#### Tried by workers

- **Working on a tourist or business visa.** Staying for a purpose other than the one declared on entry is fined 100 RP (10,000 som, about Tk 14,200); in May 2025, 46 foreigners found working on tourist visas on a Bishkek building site were each fined 10,000 som [L0079, U0091, U0061, G0003].
- **Forged or bought papers.** Using a document you know is forged is punished with a fine of 500 to 1,000 RP or 2 to 5 years in prison (1 RP is 100 som) [L0136, L0073, L0090]. False information in an application gets a visa refused and a Unified Permit annulled [L0098, W0093].
- **Walking out for taxi or delivery work.** No work visa has been issued for taxi or delivery work, so every foreigner doing it works illegally [N0061, N0163]. Once an employer reports to the police that you left without notice, no other employer may hire you [L0114]. What follows is in What can get you jailed or deported [L0091].
- **Crossing into Kazakhstan without papers.** Illegal border crossing is punished with 1 to 3 years' corrective labour, a fine of 500 to 1,000 RP or up to 2 years in prison (see Irregular routes and undocumented workers) [L0135].
