Kyrgyzstan set 52,000 foreign labor quota for 2026 amid domestic workforce shortages

![](https://cdn-1.aki.kg/cdn-st-0/qi1/O/en-893073.1777030444.b.jpg)

Deputy Minister of Labor Mirlan Baigonchokov

**AKIPRESS.COM** - The national quota for foreign labor has been set at 52,000 workers for 2026, a representative from the Ministry of Labor, Social Security and Migration announced during a meeting of the Ishenim parliamentary group...
