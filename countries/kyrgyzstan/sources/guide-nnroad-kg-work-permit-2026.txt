Kyrgyzstan Work Permit for Employers: The Single Permit and Quota
Insights · By Chen ChunJie · Published 7 November 2026
Since January 2025, a foreign national working in Kyrgyzstan needs a Single Permit, applied for by an employer in Kyrgyzstan. This guide covers who needs it, how the quota works, the steps and timing, and what applies once the person starts.
Last reviewed in November 2026 against National Investment Agency guidance and published government announcements. Fees and terms change often, so we confirm them for each engagement.

How the Single Permit works
The Single Permit replaced the separate employer permit, individual work permit and work visa from 1 January 2025. Applications go through the e-government Single Window, and processing takes about 7 working days once the file is complete. In 2026 a temporary regulation (Cabinet Resolution No. 312) sets the procedure, and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs issues the permits until the end of 2026.
The 2026 quota
Foreign workers are admitted within an annual quota. For 2026 it was raised in steps to 52,000 people, and it can be increased further.

Tax and social contributions
Income tax is 10%, withheld from pay, or 5% for High Technology Park staff. Social contributions total 27.25%, or 12.25% for qualifying businesses, including the employee’s 10%, on pay up to 720,940 som a month in 2026. Wages are paid in som.
Penalties
A foreigner working without a permit and the company that brought them in can both be fined, and employers with repeated violations are placed on a register of unreliable employers.
FAQ: Kyrgyzstan work permit

What is the Single Permit?
One permit, introduced in January 2025, that replaces the employer permit, the individual work permit and the work visa.

How long does it take?
About 7 working days once the file is complete.

Do EAEU citizens need a permit?
No. They can work on an employment contract without it.

Can a foreign company apply?
No. An employer in Kyrgyzstan applies. Companies without one use a local employer, such as
