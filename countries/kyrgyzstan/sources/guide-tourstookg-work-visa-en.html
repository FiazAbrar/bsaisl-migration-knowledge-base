Kyrgyzstan Work Visa & Permit 2025: Price, Rules & Apply Online

How to Get a Work Permit in Kyrgyzstan (2025 Guide)
New Unified Permit System

Major Update (July 1, 2025): The old W1 and W2 work visas have been abolished. They are replaced by the electronic "Unified Permit". This single document now grants the right to both enter the country and work legally.

If you are looking for information on how can I apply for a work permit visa under the new rules, this guide covers everything from the official government fees to the step-by-step online process.

Kyrgyzstan Work Visa Price 1 Year 2025

The working visa Kyrgyzstan fee structure has changed. It is now a combination of local government fees (paid in SOM) and the Unified Permit fee (paid in USD). The price depends on your citizenship.

Payment Stages

The kyrgyzstan work visa price is not paid in a single lump sum. The government has divided the process into two stages:

Stage 1: Primary Application

Duration: 2 months.

You apply for the initial "Unified Permit" to enter the country. This serves as your entry visa. You pay the first part of the fees at this stage.

Stage 2: Extension

Duration: Up to 1 year.

After you arrive and begin working, the permit is extended for the main period (12 months). You pay the remaining balance here.

Special Rule for Uzbekistan: Citizens of Uzbekistan living in border regions who commute daily for work receive a permit issued immediately for 6 months.

Who is eligible for a work permit?

To obtain a Unified Permit, you must have a legal basis for your stay. There are two options:

  • With an Employer: You have a job offer from a registered Kyrgyz company (OsOO/LLC). The company acts as the applicant.
  • Self-Employed (Individual Entrepreneur): You register as an Individual Entrepreneur (IP/Patent) and apply for the Unified Permit yourself.

How to get a work permit in Kyrgyzstan? (The Process)

The entire process is now digital via the government portal evisa.e-gov.kg. Here is the workflow:

  • Registration: The Employer (or you, if Self-Employed/IP) registers in the "Personal Cabinet" on the portal.
  • Quota Application: Submit an electronic request for a foreign labor quota.
  • Employee Application: Fill out the profile and upload scanned documents (Passport, Contract).
  • Online Payment: Pay the fees using a bank card directly on the portal.
  • Issuance: Download the electronic "Unified Permit" with a QR code.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to check Kyrgyzstan work permit online?

The new Unified Permit contains a unique QR code. You can verify the document by scanning this code or by logging into the dashboard on the evisa.e-gov.kg website.

How can I apply for a work permit visa?

You cannot apply as a private individual without legal status. You must either have an Employer who applies for you, OR you must register as an Individual Entrepreneur (IP/Patent) to submit the application.

What is the difference between the old visa and the Unified Permit?

Previously, you needed a Work Permit (paper) AND a Work Visa (sticker in passport). Now, these are combined into one electronic document called the "Unified Permit." It is faster and cheaper.

Source: Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 367
