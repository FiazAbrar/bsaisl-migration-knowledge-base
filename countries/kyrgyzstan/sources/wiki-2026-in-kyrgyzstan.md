## Events

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=2026_in_Kyrgyzstan&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Events")\]

- 16 February – President Japarov dismisses his ministers for transportation, environment and emergency situations.^([\[1\]](#cite_note-1))
- 8 March – Two people are killed and 19 people are injured following a mining incident in [Samarkandek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samarkandek "Samarkandek"), [Batken Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Batken_Region "Batken Region").^([\[2\]](#cite_note-2))
- 27 March – President Japarov announces that the country has started printing out their [national currency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyrgyz_som "Kyrgyz som").^([\[3\]](#cite_note-3)[\[4\]](#cite_note-4)[\[5\]](#cite_note-5))
- 3 April – The [Kyrgyz Ministry of Emergency Situations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Emergency_Situations_(Kyrgyzstan) "Ministry of Emergency Situations (Kyrgyzstan)") says that Kyrgyzstan has dispatched over 100 tons of humanitarian aid to Iran. The shipment is ordered by President Japarov.^([\[6\]](#cite_note-6))
- 12 May – Former security chief [Kamchybek Tashiev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamchybek_Tashiev "Kamchybek Tashiev") and seven others are arrested on charges of planning a coup.^([\[7\]](#cite_note-7))
- 3 June – Kyrgyzstan is elected to a rotating seat at the [United Nations Security Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council "United Nations Security Council").^([\[8\]](#cite_note-8))
- 4 August – President Japarov signs a law banning the use of [cremation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cremation "Cremation") for deceased human bodies.^([\[9\]](#cite_note-9)[\[10\]](#cite_note-10))
