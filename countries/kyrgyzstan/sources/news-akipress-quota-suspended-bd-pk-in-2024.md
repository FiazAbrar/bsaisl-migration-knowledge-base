Kyrgyzstan suspended distribution of quotas for inviting workers from Pakistan, Bangladesh and India since April

![](https://cdn-1.aki.kg/cdn-st-0/qer/K/en-774450.1716200095.b.jpg)

**AKIPRESS.COM** - Since April 2024, Kyrgyzstan has temporarily suspended the allocation of quotas for inviting workers from Pakistan, Bangladesh, and India, said Arlen Aliyasov, head of the department for working with foreign citizens of the Ministry of Labor...
