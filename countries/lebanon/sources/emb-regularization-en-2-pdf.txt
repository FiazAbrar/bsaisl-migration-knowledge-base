Banglaldesh Embassy
Beirut, Lebanon

Date: 29 January 2024

The concerned authority of Lebanon has decided to restart the Regularisation (Settlement)
programme for the Irregular Exaprtiate Workers of Bangladesh in Lebanon. Expatriate Workers of
Bangladesh must follow the following General Terms and Guidelines prescribed by the Office of
the General Security, Lebanon:

1. An irregular worker will be allowed to be regularised (settled) with only a genuine and
qualified sponsor.

2. Regularisation (Settlement) programme will be applicable for both male and female workers
of Bangladesh.

3. Female workers (for the time being) will be allowed to be regularised (settled) only as
Domestic Worker/Housemaid.

4. No worker will be allowed to be settled (regularised) with the sponsorship of any cleaning
agency/company.

5. An irregular worker of a cleaning agency/ company may be allowed to be regularised
(settled) with other types of sponsorship except cleaninig agency/company.

6. Settlement (Legalisation) programme will continue untill the Office of the General Security
makes new decision.

7. The Embassy will make a written communication regarding the message of clearence for
Regularisation (Settlement) with the worker and the sponsor .

8. Exception: An irregular worker will not be allowed to be regularised (settled)-

a) if the worker is found involved in any kind of court case;

b) if the new sponsor of the worker is fake;

c) if the new agency/company of the worker is blacklisted by the Lebanese Labour
Ministry/ by the Office of the General security, Lebanon/ by any other Authority of
Lebanese Government.

Steps to be Followed for Regularisation (Settlement) Programme for an Irregular Worker
of Bangladesh in Lebanon
Step-1
An irregular worker of Bangladesh in Lebanon who wishes to be regular (settled) will come
in person at the Embassy of Bangladesh along with his/her new sponsor and fill-in an Settlement
Application Form (prescribed by the Office of the General Security,Lebanon). Assistance will be
given by the Embassy. The Form will be submitted at the Embassy with the following attachments:
Essential Papers:
(a) 04(Four) Copies of Colour Photos (Passport Size)
(b) Clear Copy of Worker’s Latest Passport-(2 sets)
(c) Clear Copy of New Kafil/Sponsor’s ID-(2 sets)
(d) For a Company- Clear Copy of Commercial Licence Certificate-(2 sets)
(e) For a Concierge- Land/Building Certificat/Notarised Building Committee Agreement
Certificate-(2 sets)
(f) For a Domestic Woker/ a Housemaid-Salary Certificate/ Income Certificate (2 Sets)
(g) For a Farmer- Clear Copy of Land Certificate-(2 Sets)

Optional Papers
(h) A copy of worker’s visa if available with the worker

(i) A copy of latest Akama(Residence Permit) if available with the worker

N.B. No Application will be received without Essential Papers

Step-2
The Embassy will make an investigation with the worker and the sponsor separately. The embassy
will follow its own procedures for investigation.
Step-3
e After primary investigation if the application is found appropriate, the Embassy will send
the file with a serial number to the Office of the General Security, Lebanon along with the
documents.
e Alternative copy of the all files will be preserved at the Embassy for next action.
e After primary investigation if the application is found inappropriate, the Embassy will
inform the applicant/ the Sponsor.

Step-4

a) If the Office of the General Security rejects the submitted file (for settlement) for whatever
reason, they will inform the Embassy. The Embassy will communicate the decision with the
worker and the sponsor and the file will be closed at this stage.

b) Ifthe Office of the General Security accepts the submitted file, they will provide a Clearance
to the Embassy for next action. ,
