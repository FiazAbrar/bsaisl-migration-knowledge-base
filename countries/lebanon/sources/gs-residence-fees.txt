Important Notes:

Urgent Paid Service Fee (processed by the Public Relations Department) : LBP 4,900,000 per residence permit (in addition to the prescribed legal fee).

Overstay penalties and visa fees shall be collected according to the fee applicable to each at the time they become due. The previous fee schedule shall continue to apply until the end of the fiscal year, while the new fee schedule shall apply to any fees that become due after the effective date of this circular.

The departure status regularization fee for persons who entered the country illegally shall be collected in accordance with the new fee schedule (covering two years), in addition to the visa conversion fee, where applicable.

In cases where a three-year residence permit may be granted, a fee equal to three times the annual residence permit fee shall be charged.
