JORDAN PASS:
A sightseeing ticket package which grants visitors access to over 40 of Jordan's top and most beautiful tourist attraction sites.
This pass helps save the visitor time, money, and effort while granting them access to the best sites the Hashemite Kingdom of
Jordan has to offer. Jordan pass must be purchased prior to visiting these attractions and before arrival to Jordan to be exempt
of entry visa fees. To buy the Jordan pass

Kindly note:
· Tourists are exempt of entry visa fees to the Group or Individuals if a Jordan pass is purchased before arrival to Jordan And a

   proof of stay a minimum of (3) three nights is provided.
· The Jordan Pass that covers all of Jordan's tourist attractions comes in (3)categories:

   (Jordan Wonderer) (Jordan Explorer) (Jordan Expert) to choose a prepaid bundle of the chosen trip, you will find Free
   downloadable digital brochures.
· Jordan pass: will be scanned at any entrance of attractions in Jordan by the special readers of the officials in charge.
· Choose your preferred Jordan Pass package out of the three available options.
   Once the Jordan Pass is purchased according to regulations, a prior single-entry visa could be issued at the Embassy which
    would waive the entry visa fee to the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan.

To obtain the exempted visa through the Embassy please provide:

 Visa application form must be filled out entirely.

 A passport valid for at least (6) months from the expected date of arrival.
 The original passport must have a full blank page to accommodate the visa stamp.
 One recent photo (2X2) Inch.
 A copy of Jordan Pass ticket.
 Visa application consular fee is ($ 7.50) per passport. Payment must be paid by money order only with your name, address

    and phone number, payable to the Embassy of Jordan).
