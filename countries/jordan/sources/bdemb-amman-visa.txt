APPLY FOR VISA

Embassy of Bangladesh in Amman is accredited to The Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan and also concurrently accredited to the Syrian Arab Republic and the State of Palestine. So the Jordanian national and nationals of Syria and the Palestine residing in their respective country may apply to this Embassy for Bangladesh visa. 
Nationals of other countries may apply for visa at the Embassy subject to holding valid residence permit of Jordan.

Visa Application Process:
Step One:
Apply online through the web portal: 
visa.gov.bd 
.
Get a print-out of the duly filled-in online visa application form (with barcode).
Step Two:
Send an email attaching the copy of passport, printed application form and required documents to the Embassy (
mission.amman@mofa.gov.bd).
Or
You can visit the Embassy and present the above documents at the counter.
Step Three:
Once you receive confirmation from the Embassy (by email / at the Embassy counter), please deposit the required visa fees and submit the original passport, the required documents, two copies of passport size photographs, and the bank deposit slip (original copy). Application submission time is between 09:00 am to 12:00 pm every working day.

Requirements / Eligibility Criteria:

Diplomatic / Official Visa:
Passport with a validity of minimum 06 months (minimum two blank pages in the passport)
Copy of passport.
Copy of ID/Iqama
Two copies of passport size photograph
Note verbale / letter from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the applicant
Business Visa:

Passport with a validity of minimum 06 months (minimum two blank pages in the passport)
Copy of passport
Copy of ID / Iqama
Two copies of passport size photograph (with white back ground).
Invitation / recommendation letter from a registered company / business entity in Bangladesh (clearly mentioning the name, passport number and nationality of the applicant)
Valid business license / registration of the company / entity in Bangladesh.
Recommendation letter from the company / business entity in Jordan / home country (clearly mentioning the name, passport number and nationality of the applicant) and its valid business license copy.

Tourist Visa:
Passport with a validity of minimum 06 months (minimum two blank pages in the passport)
Copy of passport.
Copy of ID/Iqama.
Two copies of passport size photograph
Copy of hotel reservation (preferred).
Copy of return air-ticket (preferred)
Student Visa:
Passport with a validity of minimum 01 year (minimum two blank pages in the passport)
Copy of passport.
Copy of ID/Iqama
Two copies of passport size photograph
Offer letter from the university/college
Proof of scholarship or Bank statement or Certificate of financial guarantee
Original and attested/stamped copy of all relevant academic certificates and transcripts. Attestation should be done by the respective Ministry/Embassy of the applicant in Amman. (Original documents will be returned back and attested documents will be kept by the Embassy).
Visa for Military Personnel (For Course/Training/Conference):
Passport with a validity of minimum 06 months (minimum two blank pages in the passport)
Copy of passport
Copy of Military ID/Security ID
Two copies of passport size photograph
Letter from the Armed Forces Division (AFD) of Bangladesh
Letter from the Armed Forces Division / Headquarters of the officer concerned / applicant

Ijtema /Tabligh Visa:
Passport with a validity of minimum 06 months (Minimum two blank pages in the passport)
Copy of passport
Copy of ID/Iqama
Two copies of passport size photograph
A recommendation letter from the Kakrail Markaz Masjid
A recommendation letter from the Markaz in Jordan/respective country

Work/Employment Visa: 
Passport with a validity of minimum 06 months (Minimum two blank pages in the passport)
Copy of passport
Copy of ID/Iqama
Two copies of passport size photograph
Work permit from Bangladesh Investment Development Authority (BIDA)/ Bangladesh Export Processing Zone Authority (BEPZA)
Appointment letter from the Organization in Bangladesh (clearly mentioning the name, passport number and nationality of the applicant)
Copy of agreement / contract between the applicant and employers in Bangladesh detailing the terms of employment

NGO Visa (other than UN Agencies):
Passport with a validity of minimum 06 months (Minimum two blank pages in the passport)
Copy of passport.
Copy of ID/Iqama
Two copies of passport size photograph
NOC/Recommendation from NGO Bureau of Bangladesh.
Appointment letter from the Organization in Bangladesh. (Clearly mentioning the name, passport number and nationality of the applicant).
Copy of agreement/contract between the applicant and employers in Bangladesh.
NOC/Recommendation letter from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Bangladesh Only for those who are travelling Bangladesh to work with any NGO/INGO working in Chittagong Hill Tracts or in Cox’s Bazar District.

Work/Employment Visa (UN agencies in Bangladesh):
Passport / United Nations laissez-passer (UNLP) with a validity of minimum 06 months (minimum two blank pages in the passport)
Copy of passport
Copy of ID/Iqama
Two copies of passport size photograph
Appointment letter from the Organization in Bangladesh (clearly mentioning the name, passport number and nationality of the applicant)
Copy of agreement/contract between the applicant and employers in Bangladesh
NOC/Recommendation letter from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Bangladesh

Family visa: For him/her who got married with a Bangladesh national and he/she is staying in Bangladesh.
Passport with a validity of minimum 06 months (minimum two blank pages in the passport)
Copy of passport.
Copy of ID/Iqama
Two copies of passport size photograph
Proof of his/her marriage to a Bangladeshi male or female (marriage document should be attested/stamped by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and embassy of the applicant)
NID copy of his/her spouse living in Bangladesh.
Passport copy of his/her spouse living in Bangladesh.

Important Considerations:
Complete Application:
 Without required documents application will not be processed or will be considered as an incomplete application and no visa will be endorsed against incomplete application. 
Online application alone is not processed.
Embassy has the full rights to refuse any application without any explanation.
Criterion: 
Depending on the nationality and the category of the visa, requirements / eligibility criteria may vary. 
Interview for Visa: 
Only for tourist visa, personal interview is mandatory. Otherwise applicant is assessed on the basis of the documents he/she submits with the application. If the submitted documents need any clarification, then Embassy may ask the applicant to come for personal interview.
Authorization for submission and passport collection:
 For collection of passport, applicant has to bring the original copy of the money receipt. It is preferable that applicant himself will collect the passport. If it is not possible, a person authorized by the applicant through an authorization letter can collect the passport.
Visa Fee Payment:
 Please be aware that once you deposit any amount it can’t be refunded. If you deposited for multiple entry visa but you are not eligible for multiple entry as per the criterion, instead of rejecting your application, the Embassy may consider you for single entry visa without any refund/adjustment of your payment. Visa fees can be submitted in Jordanian Dinar (JOD). Please make sure that you deposit the exact amount mentioned in the list after deduction of any bank charges or commission related to your transaction.
