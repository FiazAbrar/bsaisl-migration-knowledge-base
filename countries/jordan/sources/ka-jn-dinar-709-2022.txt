                    Jordanian dinar exchange rate rises against some currencies in 2022

Jordan News

                        last updated:

Aug 23,2023

(File photo: Jordan News)

+

-

AMMAN 
— The annual report of the Jordanian Central Bank for
the past year 2022 revealed an increase in the exchange rate of the Jordanian
Dinar against major global currencies.
اضافة اعلان

The exchange rate of the Jordanian Dinar against the Euro by
6.9 percent, the British Pound by 9.1 percent, and the Japanese Yen by 18.6
percent, compared to their prevailing rates in 2021, Al-Saa News reported.

The Central Bank indicated that it has maintained a fixed
exchange rate policy for the Jordanian Dinar against the US Dollar since 1995,
at an average rate of 709 fils per dollar.

According to the Central Bank, this policy is considered the
cornerstone of the monetary policy and one of the most important pillars of
overall economic stability.
