Unified e-portal launched to admit foreign students to study in Jordan

+

-

                        Amman - Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research announced it launched the unified admission applications for international students (non-Jordanians) wishing to enroll in Jordan's public universities in various specializations and academic programs.
اضافة اعلان

In a statement Sunday, the ministry said online applications for overseas students will be available for the second semester of the 2024-2025 academic year, starting December 29, until the end of Thursday, February 27, 2025.

The ministry added that prospective students must obtain an equivalency of their high school certificate from the Jordanian Ministry of Education and a preliminary, conditional, or final admission from the target university.

The ministry indicated that students can fill out up to ten options in the application, including one or more universities, via the e-link studyinjordan.jo

The applicant must provide the required documents and pay the application fee of JD35 through the e-payment service, eFAWATEERcom.

The admission process is carried out exclusively via the e-portal without the need for any intermediaries or additional fees, which ensures "transparency and easy procedures."

To facilitate the application process, the e-platform provides comprehensive details about the specializations offered in Jordan's official universities.

For more details, the applicant is advised to visit the following link: studyinjordan.jo to fill out the application.
