# Three arrested over Jordan recruitment scam

Staff Correspondent \

The Criminal Investigation Department of the police has arrested three members of an alleged fraud gang for using forged documents and false promises of jobs in Jordan.

Manjur Pradhania, 35, Osman Gani, 25, both from Chandpur, and Kazi Md Ata-e-Rabbi, 28, from Laxmipur, were arrested at about 4:00pm on Sunday, said a CID press release issued on Monday.

Acting on a tip-off, a team of CID’s Human Trafficking Monitoring Cell conducted a drive at the office of Surah Travels and Traders at Rupayan Millennium Square in the capital’s North Badda and arrested them.

During the raid, 55 Bangladeshi passports, 16 overseas employment agreements on non-judicial stamps, two CPUs and one DVR were seized from the office, said the release.

Of the seized passports, 18 were found to belong to individuals seeking employment in Jordan, while the remaining passports were linked to prospective workers intending to travel to Saudi Arabia, according to the preliminary investigation.

According to CID officials, Surah Travels and Traders were using the name, logo and seal of Jordan-based Jerash Garments and Fashion Manufacturing Company Limited, located in Al-Tajamouat Industrial Estate in Amman, without authorisation to recruit workers for Jordan.

The agency collected about Tk two lakh from each aspiring worker by offering employment visas and job opportunities in Jordan.

CID in its release said that the alleged fraud came to light after the Jordanian company lodged a complaint through the Bangladesh Embassy in Jordan.

The complaint was later forwarded to the relevant authorities through the Ministry of Expatriates’ Welfare and Overseas Employment, prompting the CID to launch an investigation.

According to investigators, the accused were collecting passports under the pretext of foreign employment without a valid recruiting license or necessary approvals, an act believed to be tied to migrant smuggling.

The investigation was ongoing to identify and arrest other individuals and associates involved, the press release added.
