## Jordanian Citizenship: Security for You, Your Children, and Your Wealth (Updated for 2025)

 

Are you looking for a safe haven for your wealth and your family’s future? Jordan is an attractive investment destination, not only for its political stability, growing economy, strategic location, and rich culture, but also as a safe haven for wealthy individuals seeking to preserve and grow their investments in a stable environment.

Whether you are looking for a safe place to preserve your wealth or planning for retirement, Jordan offers an ideal opportunity through dedicated programs that allow you to obtain Jordanian citizenship or five-year residency when you invest in the Kingdom. The Jordanian government has refined these opportunities for foreign investors, opening up a wide range of benefits based on **Cabinet Decision No. (3657) dated 13/9/2021** and subsequent updates, including **Cabinet Decision No. (6404) dated 30/2/2022 and 3/4/2022**.

 

### Jordanian Citizenship by Investment: A Secure Opportunity for You, Your Children, and Your Wealth

 

Jordan offers a clear and structured path to citizenship by investment, allowing foreign investors and their families to integrate into a stable and prosperous environment. The program provides one of the most effective ways to obtain a second passport, which can be processed upon fulfilling the investment criteria. A Jordanian passport offers visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to a significant number of countries, facilitating business expansion, international travel, and educational opportunities.

Beyond global mobility, investing in Jordan offers significant advantages for wealth preservation and growth. The country provides a favorable tax environment and acts as a safe haven for assets in uncertain times. A Jordanian passport can also open doors to global markets, enabling business expansion into new territories.

For those concerned about political instability in their home country, Jordanian citizenship provides an invaluable escape route and a secure place to live for you and your family. The benefits of citizenship can also be passed on to future generations, ensuring access to top-tier education, healthcare, and a better quality of life in a stable and welcoming society.

In short, investing in Jordan to obtain citizenship is an investment in your future and the future of your family’s security and prosperity.

 

### Why Invest in Jordan?

 

- **Funds are Guaranteed:** For specific investment routes, such as deposits with the Central Bank, funds are guaranteed by the state, enhancing security for investors.
- **Jordanian Citizenship has a Good Reputation:** Jordanian citizenship is well-regarded internationally, easing travel and visa applications. Jordan is also considered a safe country, mitigating concerns about financial misconduct.
- **Strategic Location:** Jordan’s position at the crossroads of Asia, Africa, and Europe provides unique access to diverse markets and business opportunities.
- **Flexibility in Fund Transfer:** Funds for investment can be transferred from the investor’s personal account or from their companies’ bank accounts, provided there is a board resolution from the company showing that they have shares or profits equal to the transferred amount.
- **Refined & Clear Regulations:** The program is transparent, with clearly defined pathways, investment thresholds, and requirements, ensuring a smooth process for applicants.

 

### How to Obtain Jordanian Citizenship or Residency by Investment

 

Jordanian citizenship or five-year residency is granted to foreign investors who meet specific conditions and make qualified investments in the Kingdom. The program is structured into several distinct pathways, each with varying financial commitments and benefits.

**Eligible Economic Sectors (for most citizenship/residency paths):**

- Industry (all branches)
- Agriculture (plant and animal, with modern technologies)
- Tourism (classified per ISIC4, excluding certain food service establishments)
- Information and Communication Technology
- Education (recognized institutions)
- Health (hospitals, specialized medical centers)
- Media Sector (including film production)
- **Recently Added Commercial Sectors (by Cabinet Decision No. 6404, 30/2/2022):**
  - Warehouses for medicines and medical supplies
  - Car and heavy machinery agencies/showrooms
  - Food logistics services (storage)
  - Navigation and maritime transport
  - Housing for existing operating companies
  - Tourist restaurants

**Main Investment Pathways and Their Costs/Conditions (as of 2025):**

1.  **Deposit or Purchase of Treasury Bonds (Citizenship):** Investors can obtain citizenship through **one of the following two options**:
    - **Option A: Deposit:** Transfer **USD 1,000,000** or its equivalent in Jordanian Dinars as a deposit in favor of the Ministry of Investment at the Central Bank for **three years** without interest. This deposit cannot be broken or borrowed against.
    - **Option B: Treasury Bonds:** Transfer the equivalent of **USD 1,000,000** in Jordanian Dinars to the Central Bank for investment in treasury bonds for **six years**, with interest determined by the Central Bank. These bonds cannot be disposed of during this period.
2.  **Purchase of Shares and/or Stakes in Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs) (Citizenship):** This path requires the purchase of shares and/or stakes in active Jordanian companies in productive economic sectors for an amount not less than **USD 1,500,000**. These stakes must not be disposed of for at least **3 years**, with a lien placed on them by the Companies Control Department or the Securities Depository Center. The process includes review of commercial register, financial statements, and employment records, along with a deed of assignment and a company commitment to use funds for development.
3.  **Establishing and Registering a New Investment Project (Citizenship):** This path involves creating a new project with specific capital and job creation requirements:

    - **Within Amman Governorate:** Total paid-up capital of at least **USD 1,000,000** and providing **20 real job opportunities** for Jordanians (verified by Social Security statements).
    - **Outside Amman Governorate:** Total paid-up capital of at least **USD 750,000** and providing **10 real job opportunities** for Jordanians (verified by Social Security statements).
    - *Investors initially receive residency until the project is operational and employment targets are met, then a temporary Jordanian passport for 3 years, followed by full citizenship.*
4.  **Existing Investments (Citizenship):** This path is for investors with existing businesses in Jordan:
    - **Within Amman Governorate:** Investor’s average share of total fixed, non-current, and tangible assets not less than **USD 1,000,000** over the last **3 years**, and providing **20 job opportunities** for Jordanians over those **3 years**.
    - **Outside Amman Governorate:** Investor’s average share of total fixed, non-current, and tangible assets not less than **USD 500,000** over the last **3 years**, and providing **10 job opportunities** for Jordanians over those **3 years**.
    - *Note: Citizenship is not granted to any investor who acquired stakes from another investor who previously obtained Jordanian citizenship with those specific stakes.*
5.  **Five-Year Residency Through Real Estate Purchase:** Investors or ordinary individuals are granted **5-year residency** upon purchasing one or more properties with a value of not less than **JOD 200,000** (as estimated by the Department of Lands and Survey). The property must be retained for at least **5 years** without disposal or mortgage. Ownership must be for residential purposes with an independent title deed. Ownership from first and second-degree relatives is not allowed. The purchase must be subsequent to the Cabinet decision, and the application submitted within a maximum of 3 months from the purchase date.
6.  **Employment of Jordanian Workers (Citizenship – based on Cabinet Decision No. 6404, 3/4/2022):** Jordanian citizenship can be granted to an investor who employs **150 Jordanian citizens** in Amman Governorate, or **100 Jordanian citizens** in other governorates. These employees must have been registered with the Social Security Corporation for the preceding year, with a commitment to retain them for two consecutive years after obtaining citizenship.

**Family Inclusion:** An investor who has obtained citizenship or five-year residency can apply for their family members to obtain citizenship or residency. This includes the investor’s wife, unmarried daughters, widowed and divorced daughters living under their care, male children under 18 years of age, and dependent parents. If the investment size exceeds **USD 3 million**, citizenship can be granted to the investor’s male children who are under 30 years of age at the time of application, their wives, and children.

 

### General Conditions for Obtaining Jordanian Citizenship/Residency:

 

- **Security Approval:** A mandatory security clearance is required for all applicants before any preliminary or final approval for granting Jordanian citizenship.
- **Good Conduct:** The investor must be of good conduct and not have been convicted of any felony or misdemeanor involving moral turpitude.
- **Legal Source of Income:** Applicants must demonstrate a stable and legal source of income.
- **Medical Examination:** A medical examination proving freedom from infectious diseases is required.
- **Compliance:** Failure to adhere to any of the stated conditions will result in the withdrawal of Jordanian citizenship or cancellation of residency, as applicable.

 

### Benefits of Obtaining a Jordanian Passport by Investment:

 

- **Freedom of Travel:** A Jordanian passport allows its holder visa-free or visa-on-arrival travel to a significant number of countries.
- **Permanent Residence:** Citizenship grants the right to reside permanently in the Kingdom and enjoy full citizen rights.
- **Ease of Investment:** Citizens can invest in Jordan with greater ease and convenience.
- **Access to Government Services:** Access to all government services provided to Jordanian citizens.
- **Education and Healthcare:** Access to quality education and healthcare services in Jordan for the passport holder and their family members.

### Conclusion

In conclusion, Jordanian citizenship by investment, along with the option for five-year residency, presents a compelling opportunity for foreign investors seeking stability, security, and a promising future for themselves and their families. With its well-defined, updated programs, attractive investment environment, and strategic location, Jordan stands out as an ideal choice for those looking to expand their global horizons and secure a safe haven for their wealth.
