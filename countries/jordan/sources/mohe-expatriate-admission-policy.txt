General policy for admission of expatriate students at the Jordanian universities for
   the academic year 2023/2024 issued based on the provisions of the article (6)

clause (6) of higher education and scientific research law number (17) of 2018 and
   its amendments pursuant to the resolution of higher education council number
                                  (322/2023) on 06/07/2023.

First: Admission at bachelor degree:

Expatriate students are admitted at Jordanian universities at bachelor degree
according to the following conditions:

A. 1. The student should be passed in the Jordanian certificate of general secondary,
    or equivalent, he should present the certificate authenticated by the Jordanian
    ministry of education.
    2. The student should be passed in the Arab or foreign certificate of general
    secondary study; he should present one of the following the documents:
     An equivalent document of the general secondary certificate issued by the
       Jordanian ministry of education.
     An equivalent document of the general secondary certificate issued by the
       concerned entity at his country.
     An official document from the liable entity of higher education at his country
       advised that a certificate of general secondary which he bears enable his to
       enroll at the required university major at the universities of his country.

B. It is allowed for the student who gains the Jordanian certificate of general
   secondary study to enroll at the different specializations at the university in
   consistent with the available specializations for the general secondary steams
   according to the general policy to admit the Jordanian students at the Jordanian
   universities.

C. In the event that the stream of the general secondary study certificate is not of the

   enrollment at each college/ major, and the student should be passed with an
   average not less than (50%).
   2. It is allowed to admit the students who fulfill the conditions of this policy
   increase the capacity, where not more than (25%) at each major at the Jordanian
   universities, provided fulfill the accreditation criteria of the major.

Second: Admission at diploma degree:

A. The students are admitted at the middle university colleges provided they pass at
   the Jordanian certificate of general secondary or equivalent with an average of

Third: Admission at bridging phase:

A. The student who pass the exam of Jordanian middle university certificate
    (comprehensive exam) considers as entitled for the competitive admission at the
    normal program through unified admission coordinating unit provided that he
    should be within the highest (5%) of the passed students, his average at this exam
    (two years system and three years system) is not less than (68%) excluding the
    student who wants for bridging to engineering, medicine, pharmacy majors
    provided that his average is not less than (70%).

B. In consideration what is contained at article (A), The student who pass the exam
   of Jordanian middle university certificate (comprehensive exam) at previous
   years to be compete for (25%) for the students who gained the Jordanian middle
   university certificate (comprehensive exam) at previous years through unified
   admission coordinating unit.

C. The university may admit the students who gained Jordanian middle university
   certificate (comprehensive exam) at the Jordanian university through bridging
   excluding the minimum limits of the admission averages contained at the general

Forth: Admission at postgraduate programs:

A. The applicant for enrollment at master program shall have bachelor degree with
   a merit of not less than good or equivalent.

B. Student who has bachelor degree with a merit of passed or equivalent can apply
   a request for enrollment at master programs provided the following:
   1. Study (3) subjects of master degree (during the first year of enrollment)
       determined by targeted department, and they will be unified for this group of
       students.
   2. Pass in each subject with an average not less than 70% or equivalent to be an
       official student, the subjects which he finished will be calculated at his plan,
       and otherwise his admission will be canceled.

C. Student who has higher diploma degree with a merit not less than very good can
   apply for enrollment at master programs regardless his merit of bachelor.

D. Student who has master degree with a merit not less than good or equivalent can
   apply for enrollment at Ph.D. programs.

E. Student who has bachelor degree with a merit of passed or equivalent and master
   degree with a merit of not less than good or equivalent can apply for enrollment
   at Ph.D. programs.

F. Student who wants to enroll at postgraduate programs at Jordanian universities
   should present a proof which that he passed one of the foreign languages exams
   (TOEFl, IELTS, French language, German language) or exam of English
   language level equivalent for the international exams hold at the university, as
   follows:
   1. TOEFL (iBT): Scientific majors (medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, Ph.D. in
       pharmacy, veterinary, all engineering departments, all English language
       majors (literature, translation, methodology and styles of English language
       studying, ...etc.) with a mark not less than (90), rest of scientific majors, MBA
       with a mark of not less than (69), and humanitarian, economic, administrative
       majors with a mark of not less than (59), from AMIDEAST center, Jordan
       branch or from the accredited centers at the state which the student lives
       provided he should present a document proves that the center is accredited at
       it.
   2. (IELTS) exam: Scientific majors (medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, Ph.D. in
       pharmacy, veterinary, all engineering departments, all English language
       majors (literature, translation, methodology and styles of English language
       learning, ...etc.) with a mark not less than (6.5), rest of scientific majors, MBA
       with a mark of not less than (5.5), and humanitarian, economic, administrative
       majors with a mark of not less than (5) from the accredited centers at Jordan
       (UniHouse - for consultations and Studies, Oval International Academy,
       British Council - Amman) or from the accredited centers at the state which

4. They exclude from the above clause (f) the following:

First: Students not speaking Arabic who want to enroll at postgraduate programs at
       Arabic language majors and Sharia college majors at the Jordanian
       universities from the condition of foreign language in case they are fluent in
       Arabic language.

Second: Students who passed the exam of the condition of foreign language for
        admission at postgraduate programs at the Jordanian universities, when they
        enroll at a new master degree program or PhD program.

Third: Exclude students who gained a scientific degree from the states speaking
        English language.

  register any subjects of his study plan for the next semester in case that he failed

J. It is not allowed for the students who have occupational and technical diploma
  (because they are not a higher diploma) to enroll at postgraduate programs.
K. It is allowed for the students who fulfill the conditions of this policy to increase
  the capacity, where it will be (15%) for master degree, (10%) for Ph.D., provided
  fulfill the criteria of accreditation for each major.
Fifth: Miscellaneous provisions:

                                                               7
A. The priority will be given to achieve the condition of English language other than
   the following conditions which enable the student to enroll at higher studies
   programs.

B. It is allowed to equal the subjects which the student studied them at diploma and
   he did not gain through them a diploma degree for enrollment at bachelor
   program at the Jordanian universities with maximum of 20 accredited hours.

C. It is not allowed for the student who gained the middle university certificate from
   outside the Kingdom by bridging pass at medicine and dentistry majors at the
   Jordanian universities.

D. It is not allowed to calculate subjects from higher diploma program when
   enrollment the student at master program unless he gains the higher diploma as a
   result of his failure in gaining master degree, in this case it is allowed for the
   university to calculate not more than (9) accredited hours when he enrolls a new
   master program.

E. It is allowed to calculate subjects from master program which the student gained
   for enrollment at a new master program provided the following:
   1. The content of study plan is the same with a percentage of not less than (70%).
   2. The calculated hours are not more than (6) accredited study hours.

Sixth: It is excluding from this policy the admitted students for implementing the
        cultural agreements at the Jordanian official universities by a decision from
        minister of higher education and scientific research/ chairman of higher
        education council, and as the texts of the agreements.

Seventh: The Jordanian student who bears a nationality of foreign state can apply
           for the international program at the Jordanian universities provided:
