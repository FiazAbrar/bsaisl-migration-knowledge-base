                         Terms of Reference (ToR)

         Development of Strategic Plan (including Advocacy Plan) for OKUP

1. Background
Ovibashi Karmi Unnayan Program (OKUP) is a grassroots migrant-led organization in Bangladesh. It
was formed in 2004 by a group of returnee migrants and their families. Later, in 2008, OKUP was
officially registered with the NGO Affairs Bureau, the Government of Bangladesh.
The formation of OKUP was driven by a simple yet profound motivation: to create a safe and trusted
space for vulnerable returnee migrants. The goal was to provide 'peer-to-peer' support for individual
migrants who face unique and often isolating challenges. These challenges include social and economic
reintegration of returnee migrants, particularly dealing with social stigma, managing the psychological
impact of their experiences abroad, and accessing support and services.
Over time, while working closely with migrant communities at the grassroots level, OKUP also
recognized how deeply climate change affects migration, both internally and internationally. This
understanding led OKUP to expand its work to support and stand with the communities promoting rights-
based and community-driven solutions to enhance their adaptation and resilience.

Despite the considerable growth, like many NGOs, OKUP is dependent on short term project funding to
continue to operate each year. Recently funding crisis become a major issue of non-continuation of the
program or project in the community. Staff are also demotivated to the development field.

Given the pace of OKUP's growth and the challenges to the sustainability of the organization, SCCF
and CAFOD (Caritas member organizations from the UK and France and long-term mutual partners of
OKUP) agreed to provide funding and support to develop the organizational strategic planning with an
operational plan for the next five years to ensure the long-term sustainability of OKUP in the years to
come.
In view to respond effectively to these evolving challenges, OKUP seeks to develop a 5-year
Strategic Plan (2027 - 2031) which includes a separate advocacy plan and an operational plan.

2. Goal of the Assignment
The main goal is to develop a visionary and evidence-based strategic plan that will include advocacy
strategy and an operational plan. It will focus:

    · Strengthen the organization's role in labor migration and climate-induced migration

    · Enhance program impact for migrant communities and climate vulnerable people

    · Align with national laws, acts, policies and guidelines (e.g. OEMA 2013, Overseas Employment
         Policy 2016, Environment Policy 2019, BCCSAP 2009, NAP 2023 - 2050, BCCP 2022 - 2041
         etc.) and global frameworks (e.g., SDGs, Global Compact for Migration, UNFCCC etc.)

    · Boost institutional sustainability and resource mobilization.

3. Objectives
The specific objectives are to:

    · Develop a comprehensive strategic plan (2027 - 2031) with a clear vision and roadmap for the
         future sustainability of the organization.

    · Develop a results-based strategic framework with measurable indicators.

    · Develop an advocacy strategy that provides strategic direction, effective
         resource management, leadership continuity and operational efficiency.

    · Develop a costed operational plan.
4. Scope of Work and Deliverables
A consultant(s) will be hired to undertake this work which will involve working closely with OKUP
senior management to carry out a comprehensive strategic plan of OKUP and then work jointly to
develop an operational plan. Consultant(s) need to focus on the following issues:

4.1 Foundational Framework Review: Enable participatory sessions to review, refine, or validate the
organization's core identity, establishing a clear Vision, Mission, Values and Principles that align with
current global and national development contexts including human mobility, climate change,
development fund etc.

Deliverables: Inception Report (methodology, detailed work plan)

4.2 Programmatic Directions and Goal Setting: Frame overarching programmatic goals and specific
objectives that define the organization's thematic focus areas, outlining clear pathways for sustainable
community impact aligned with present development fund scenario.

Deliverables: Context & Organizational Assessment Report - Strategic plan.

4.3 Organizational Priorities: Identify critical internal operations and management issues through
environmental analysis (e.g., Stakeholder Analysis/ SWOT/PESTEL), and establish strategic objectives
to enhance institutional efficiency, governance, and capacity.

Deliverables: Context & Organizational Assessment Report - Strategic plan.

4.4 Advocacy Strategy: Design strategic directions to elevate the organization's public profile and
advocacy influence, corresponding with a robust resource mobilization framework to ensure sustainable
funding for the plan's execution.

Deliverables: Five years strategic plan including advocacy plan (maximum 40 pages). A four to six
pager (brief and key version) with presentable graphic design of this strategy.

4.5 Costed Operational Plan: Develop a timebound and budgeted operational plan on this strategy
which includes a pragmatic and visionary directions for the next five years.

Deliverables: A costed Operational plan of the strategy.

Validation: Need to organize and facilitate several workshops/ meetings with OKUP and its strategic
partners to validate the plan. Consultant(s) need to finalize the strategic plan with including the
feedback, recommendations, thinking from OKUP and its strategic partners. Finally, they will present
the final strategic plan to the OKUP management.
