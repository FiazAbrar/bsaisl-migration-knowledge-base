                    Interior Ministry changes foreign student residency permit renewal process

Jordan News

                        last updated:

Mar 06,2023

Interior Minister Mazen Al-Faraya. (File photo: Ameer Khalifeh/Jordan News)

+

-

AMMAN

— The 
Ministry of Interior
 announced changes to the procedures for renewing
annual resident permits for Arab and foreign students studying in Jordanian
colleges, according to the Jordan News Agency, Petra.
اضافة اعلان

According
to a press statement on Monday, Interior Minister 
Mazen Al-Faraya
 delegated the
authority to renew annual residency permits for foreign students to the
director of the Residency and Borders Department and directors of the
department’s branches in the Public Security Department.

Students can now renew their residency without visiting the ministry.
This
means students can now renew their residency without visiting the ministry.

The new
procedures will benefit students at both public and private colleges in Jordan.
