# The Ministry of Interior launches E-Visa Services for Visa and Residence Permits

[ Print](javascript:window.print(); "Print")

[ Share ](# "Share")

- [](#)
- [](#)
- [](#)
- [](#)
- [](#)

##### View all the latest GES news and insights

[cta](/middle-east/en/services/tax/perspectives/ges-alerts.html)

 

On 15 July 2023, the [Ministry of Interior](https://petra.gov.jo/Include/InnerPage.jsp?ID=51282&lang=en&name=en_news&cat=en_news) in Jordan introduced a number of electronic services for visa and residence permits. \

The Ministry stated that the electronic services include applications for the following visas and residence permits:  

- Visit visa
- Transit visa
- Exit-entry visa
- Study visa
- Work visa
- Family/Follower residency
- Workless residency with bank deposit
- Study residency
- Work residency
- Transfer residency
- Urgent travel document
- Temporary extended residence permit
- Request for separation of a companion
- Request of adding a companion
- Residence cancellation
- Annual residence for five years

As of 23 July 2023, the Ministry will stop receiving any paper applications concerning these services and any new applications must now be submitted online.

For more information, please visit: [MOI E Services - Ministry of Interior](https://moi.gov.jo/EN/Pages/E_Applications_for_Visa_and_Residence_Permits)

##### Deloitte’s view

#####  

The introduction of electronic visa application services aims to have a positive impact on the application timelines and should make this process more efficient, less time-consuming, and reduce costs. Employers, foreign nationals, and residents should take this new process into consideration since manual applications will no longer be accepted and applications must be submitted online. Considering this is a new process, we will be monitoring for any further information and keep you updated on any developments.

### Pre-entry visa exemption for Armenian citizens 

The United Arab Emirates (UAE) and Armenia have signed a protocol amending a Memorandum of Understanding for visa-free travel for citizens of both countries. The amendment waives the requirement of pre-entry visas between the two countries for stays of up to 90 days.

### Bahrain have launched a new service for renewing the residence and work permits for expatriates 

The Ministry of Interior for Nationality, Passports and Residence Affairs (NPRA), in coordination with the Labour Market Regularly Authority (LMRA) in the Kingdom of Bahrain have launched a new service for renewing the residence and work permits for expatriates who are outside the country.

### A Visit Visa can be renewed a week before its expiry 

The Kingdom of Saudi Arabia’s General Directorate of Passports (Jawazat) has requested visit visa holders, staying in the Kingdom, to renew its validity, seven days prior to the expiry of the visa.
