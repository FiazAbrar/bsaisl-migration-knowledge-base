Important note:

Effective March 19th 2023, E-Visa application services will be implemented.
This is applicable to all applicants, including Arab and Foreign nationals,
seeking to visit the Hashemite kingdom of Jordan. This electronic service will
substitute the visa issuance process conducted through the Embassy.

E-visa applications will now be processed and issued directly and exclusively
through the Ministry of Interior via the following
link: https://eservices.moi.gov.jo

Please note that the registration for the QR code for visit Jordan Is no longer
required.
