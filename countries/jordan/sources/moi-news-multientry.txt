The Minister of Interior decides to introduce a multi-entry visa for a period of one year

The Minister of Interior, Mazen Al-Faraih, decided to introduce a multi-entry visa, for a period of one full year, so that its holder can enter and exit the Kingdom during this period without the need to obtain prior approvals on each trip.

The Ministry of Interior said in a press statement today, Sunday, that this measure comes as a continuation of the decisions taken by the Ministry for the purposes of providing facilities to investors and visitors to the Kingdom, and keeping abreast of all developments, in a manner that is beneficial to the national economy and ensures the flow of capital movement and the creation of more job opportunities.

It is noteworthy that the visas that were previously granted, with a maximum limit, are limited to a period of six months and for several trips only.
