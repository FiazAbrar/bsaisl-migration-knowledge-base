## Migrant workers demand their rights

We have demand-driven interventions for the migrant workers especially women at different stages of migration including safe migration awareness, need based supports and assistance to vulnerable and survivor returnee migrant workers. 

 

- 

  ### Rights

  Protecting and upholding the rights of migrant workers across all stages of migration.

- 

  ### Dignity

  Restoring dignity to migrant workers through access to justice, rehabilitation and sustainable reintegration.

- 

  ### Oppurtunities

  Providing opportunities for safe migration through informed decision-making and access to legal pathways.
