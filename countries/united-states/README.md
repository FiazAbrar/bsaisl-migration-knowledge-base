## 1 Background

<a id="in-one-minute"></a>

### 1.1 In one minute

Bangladeshis go to the United States as students, exchange scholars, visitors, family immigrants, or irregularly overland from South America <sup>[N2 (2024)](#n2), [N3 (2024)](#n3), [N4](#n4), [N5](#n5)</sup>. More than 17,000 Bangladeshi students were in US higher education in 2023-24, a record <sup>[N2 (2024)](#n2)</sup>. Since 21 January 2026 a Bangladeshi found eligible for a business or tourist visa must post a bond of USD 5,000 to 15,000 and fly in and out through Boston, New York JFK or Washington Dulles, and from the same date the US issues no immigrant (green card) visas to Bangladeshis, so family members of US citizens cannot join them for now <sup>[N1](#n1), [N6](#n6), [N4](#n4)</sup>. Brokers in Bangladesh sell the irregular route through Brazil and Mexico, and in one case a young man paid a broker ring Tk 25 lakh and never left <sup>[N5](#n5), [N7](#n7)</sup>. People without valid papers are being sent back: about 500 by March 2026, with about 5,000 more in process <sup>[N8](#n8)</sup>.

<a id="is-it-open-now-and-who-can-go"></a>

### 1.2 Is it open now, and who can go?

Open for students, exchange visitors and visitors who pass an in-person interview, with a bond for visitors; closed for now for immigrant visas, as of the latest reporting (January 2026) <sup>[N1](#n1), [N6](#n6)</sup>.

<a id="status"></a>

#### 1.2.1 Status

- **February 2025:** the US Embassy in Dhaka told the Bangladesh government by diplomatic note that the US would send back Bangladeshis who had become irregular (see [Deportation to Bangladesh](#deportation-to-bangladesh)) <sup>[N9](#n9)</sup>.
- **June 2025:** the US imposed a full travel ban on 12 countries; Bangladesh is not one of them <sup>[N6](#n6)</sup>.
- **September 2025:** the H-1B work visa application fee was raised to USD 100,000 (see [Work visa: H-1B](#work-visa-h-1b)) <sup>[N6](#n6)</sup>.
- **6 January 2026:** Bangladesh was added to the visa bond list, effective 21 January 2026 (see [Visit visa and the visa bond](#visit-visa-and-the-visa-bond)) <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>.
- **21 January 2026:** the US suspended immigrant visas for citizens of 75 countries including Bangladesh; applications can be submitted but none are issued, and no end date has been given (see [Family immigrant visa: the green card](#family-immigrant-visa-the-green-card)) <sup>[N6](#n6)</sup>.
- **March 2026:** Bangladesh planned to ask for B-1 business travellers to be exempted from the bond <sup>[N8](#n8)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Is the immigrant visa pause for Bangladeshis still in force in November 2026, and has any court ruled on it?
> - Was the visa bond pilot, which began on 20 August 2025 for 12 months, extended past August 2026, and did Bangladesh get any exemption for B-1 business travellers?

<a id="the-channels"></a>

#### 1.2.2 The channels

- **Student visa:** the US Embassy urges students to apply early, and the US Ambassador says the US encourages Bangladeshi students (see [Student visa](#student-visa)) <sup>[N10](#n10), [N11](#n11)</sup>.
- **Exchange visitor (J-1) through Fulbright:** for secondary school teachers and for experienced academics and professionals (see [Exchange visitor visa: Fulbright](#exchange-visitor-visa-fulbright)) <sup>[N3 (2024)](#n3), [N12](#n12)</sup>.
- **Visit visa (B1/B2):** business and tourism, now with a bond (see [Visit visa and the visa bond](#visit-visa-and-the-visa-bond)) <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>.
- **Family immigrant visa: the green card:** for relatives of US citizens and residents; paused since 21 January 2026 (see [Family immigrant visa: the green card](#family-immigrant-visa-the-green-card)) <sup>[N4](#n4), [N6](#n6)</sup>.
- **Work visa: H-1B:** used by US companies to hire foreign workers; its application fee is now USD 100,000 <sup>[N6](#n6)</sup>.
- **Not a legal route:** the overland route through South America and Mexico (see [Irregular routes and undocumented stay](#irregular-routes-and-undocumented-stay)) <sup>[N5](#n5)</sup>.

<a id="size"></a>

#### 1.2.3 Size

- **US visas issued to Bangladeshis, US fiscal 2023 (October 2022 to September 2023):** about 59,254, of them 44,674 non-immigrant and 14,580 immigrant <sup>[N4](#n4)</sup>.
- **Bangladeshis granted US permanent residence:** 14,890 in 2019, 9,010 in 2020, 6,180 in 2021, 10,140 in 2022 and 18,240 in 2023 <sup>[N4](#n4)</sup>.
- **Students:** 4,802 in 2013-14 and 17,099 in 2023-24, up 26% in a year, making Bangladesh the 8th-largest sender of students to the US (13th a year before) <sup>[N2 (2024)](#n2)</sup>.
- **Waiting family:** some Bangladeshi families have 15 to 16 immigration applications pending, and US immigration lawyers put those waiting at several lakh <sup>[N4](#n4)</sup>.
- **Remittance:** see Sending money home <sup>[N13](#n13)</sup>.

<a id="how-a-job-reaches-you-and-who-takes-a-cut"></a>

### 1.3 How a job reaches you, and who takes a cut

In this reporting no job reaches a worker in Bangladesh through a recruiting agency: the way in is a university's admission, a Fulbright grant, or a relative's immigrant petition, and the cut is taken by brokers selling the irregular route or a fake promise (see [Irregular routes and undocumented stay](#irregular-routes-and-undocumented-stay)) <sup>[N2 (2024)](#n2), [N12](#n12), [N4](#n4), [N5](#n5)</sup>. In a US Justice Department case, migrants paid large sums to brokers in Bangladesh, and a Bangladeshi ring then housed them in São Paulo, Brazil, and in Tapachula and Monterrey, Mexico, before telling them how to cross the Rio Grande river into the US <sup>[N5](#n5)</sup>. A young man from Chatkhil, Noakhali, paid Tk 25 lakh to a broker ring from Lakshmipur and Feni to go to the US and had not gone two and a half years later <sup>[N7](#n7)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Do any Bangladeshis go to the US on employer-sponsored work visas (H-1B, H-2A, H-2B, EB-3) through BMET (the Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training, which clears Bangladeshis going abroad for work), and does BMET clear them?

<a id="what-you-pay-and-what-you-earn-and-keep"></a>

### 1.4 What you pay, and what you earn and keep

- **The visa bond:** USD 5,000, 10,000 or 15,000 for a visit visa, set at the interview and returned if the visa is refused or its conditions are met (see [Visit visa and the visa bond](#visit-visa-and-the-visa-bond)) <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>.
- **The H-1B fee:** USD 100,000 per application since September 2025 <sup>[N6](#n6)</sup>.
- **The irregular route:** Tk 25 lakh paid to a broker ring in one 2025 case <sup>[N7](#n7)</sup>.
- **Health care:** a US-based Bangladeshi wrote that a two-day hospital stay for a birth billed over USD 50,000, of which he paid a USD 6,000 insurance deductible (see [Health care and its cost](#health-care-and-its-cost)) <sup>[N14](#n14)</sup>.
- **Sending money home in cash:** 1% tax since 1 January 2026 (see [Sending money home](#sending-money-home)) <sup>[N15](#n15)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - What do Bangladeshi students and their families typically pay in total (tuition, living costs, agency fees) for a US degree, and what share have full scholarships?

<a id="what-ties-you-to-the-employer"></a>

### 1.5 What ties you to the employer

- **Students:** a US-based Bangladeshi columnist wrote that a student is not legally allowed to work off campus, with an opportunity at some institutions (see [Student visa](#student-visa)) <sup>[N14](#n14)</sup>.
- **Visitors:** the bond is returned only if all visa conditions are met <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>.
- **H-1B:** the visa is used by US companies to hire foreign workers <sup>[N6](#n6)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Can an H-1B worker change employer, and what happens to the visa when the job ends?

<a id="what-goes-wrong-most-and-what-people-wrongly-believe"></a>

### 1.6 What goes wrong most, and what people wrongly believe

The reported problems are removal of people who lost their immigration cases, student visas cancelled over the holder's conduct, money lost to brokers, and family petitions frozen by the January 2026 pause <sup>[N16](#n16), [N14](#n14), [N7](#n7), [N4](#n4)</sup>.

<a id="what-people-believe-and-the-facts"></a>

#### 1.6.1 What people believe, and the facts

- **"Come on a visitor visa and switch to asylum."** Bangladeshis post this on Facebook; a US-based columnist warned that if US immigration sees such posts, visas will get harder for ordinary Bangladeshis, and US immigration officials have suspended asylum applications (see [Asylum](#asylum)) <sup>[N14](#n14), [N6](#n6)</sup>.
- **"A student can openly find restaurant or gas-station work."** A student's visa was cancelled after he asked on Facebook for help finding such work <sup>[N14](#n14)</sup>.
- **"Paying the bond gets me the visa."** It does not; the money is returned if the visa is refused <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>.
- **"The immigrant visa pause stops all US visas."** It does not apply to non-immigrant, temporary, tourist or business visas, and one US immigration lawyer said student, travel and business visas may now be issued faster <sup>[N6](#n6), [N4](#n4)</sup>.
- **"Blank boxes on a form must be filled with something."** A US-based columnist reported parents whose immigration form had a date entered under "date of death", who then had to prove they were alive <sup>[N14](#n14)</sup>.

<a id="irregular-routes-and-where-an-irregular-stay-ends"></a>

#### 1.6.2 Irregular routes, and where an irregular stay ends

- **Overland through Brazil and Mexico: for the smugglers it has ended in US prison.** Two Bangladeshi smugglers were each sentenced to 46 months, and the alleged organiser, extradited from Brazil, faces 5 to 15 years (see [Irregular routes and undocumented stay](#irregular-routes-and-undocumented-stay)) <sup>[N5](#n5)</sup>.
- **Staying without papers: it ends in removal to Dhaka**, sometimes in handcuffs on the plane (see [Deportation to Bangladesh](#deportation-to-bangladesh)) <sup>[N17](#n17)</sup>.

<a id="visas-not-open-to-bangladeshis"></a>

## 2 Visas not open to Bangladeshis

- **Immigrant visas (green cards) for now:** since 21 January 2026 no immigrant visa is approved or issued to a Bangladeshi citizen, although applications can still be submitted; a dual national applying on a valid passport of a country not on the list is exempt <sup>[N6](#n6)</sup>.
- **Visa-free or bond-free visit visas:** a Bangladeshi passport holder found eligible for a B1/B2 visa must post the bond <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>.

<a id="student-visa"></a>

## 3 Student visa

In July 2026 the US Ambassador, Brent Christensen, said the US encourages Bangladeshi students in US higher education <sup>[N11](#n11)</sup>. Some go on full scholarships for a PhD, as a Bangladeshi student did to the University of South Florida <sup>[N18](#n18)</sup>.

<a id="step-1-get-admission-and-information"></a>

### 3.1 Step 1: Get admission and information

1. Write to EducationUSA, the US Embassy's education advising service, at EducationUSA-Bangla@state.gov for information on applying, programmes and funding <sup>[N2 (2024)](#n2)</sup>.
2. Use only the Embassy's official website and the education advising service for information, as the Embassy advises <sup>[N10](#n10)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Where is the EducationUSA advising centre in Dhaka, what are its hours, and is advising free?
> - What document does the university send (the I-20), and what is the SEVIS fee and visa fee in 2026?

<a id="step-2-apply-for-the-visa-early"></a>

### 3.2 Step 2: Apply for the visa early

1. Apply for the student visa as soon as you have admission from an approved institution: the Embassy said on 31 July 2025 that qualified students should apply quickly so there is enough time for processing <sup>[N10](#n10)</sup>.
2. Attend the interview in person: since January 2026 every US visa applicant must <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - How long is the wait for a student visa interview in Dhaka in late 2026, and what share of Bangladeshi student applicants are refused?
> - Are student visa applicants' social media accounts checked, and what do officers look for?

<a id="can-you-work"></a>

### 3.3 Can you work?

A US-based Bangladeshi columnist wrote that a student is not legally supposed to work off campus, with an opportunity at some institutions <sup>[N14](#n14)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - What on-campus work, CPT (Curricular Practical Training) and OPT (Optional Practical Training) are open to students, and for how many hours?

<a id="scams-and-mistakes"></a>

### 3.4 Scams and mistakes

<a id="done-by-students"></a>

#### 3.4.1 Done by students

- A student's visa was cancelled after he posted on Facebook, on getting it, asking for help finding restaurant or gas-station work in the US <sup>[N14](#n14)</sup>.
- A student was sent home with his visa cancelled after allegedly trying to forcibly kiss a woman at the gas station where he worked, a columnist reported <sup>[N14](#n14)</sup>.

<a id="exchange-visitor-visa-fulbright"></a>

## 4 Exchange visitor visa: Fulbright

Fulbright grants take Bangladeshi teachers, academics and professionals to the US on exchange; the TEA programme gives J-1 visa support <sup>[N3 (2024)](#n3), [N12](#n12)</sup>.

<a id="fulbright-teaching-excellence-and-achievement-tea"></a>

### 4.1 Fulbright Teaching Excellence and Achievement (TEA)

A six-week exchange in the US for secondary school teachers, run through the US Embassy's American Center in Dhaka <sup>[N3 (2024)](#n3)</sup>.

**Who qualifies:** full-time secondary teachers of English, EFL (English as a foreign language), maths, science, foreign languages, social studies, journalism or special education, with a bachelor's degree and at least five years of full-time teaching <sup>[N3 (2024)](#n3)</sup>. Ministry officials, education administrators, full-time teacher trainers, university teachers and private English tutors cannot apply <sup>[N3 (2024)](#n3)</sup>.

**What it covers:** J-1 visa support, a pre-departure orientation in Dhaka, return and domestic US airfare, programme fees, housing (usually shared) and meals, and accident and health insurance <sup>[N3 (2024)](#n3)</sup>.

**How to apply:**
1. Apply online with an institutional support and reference form (ISRF) filled in by your supervisor and a leave approval form (LAF) from https://fulbright.irex.org/ <sup>[N3 (2024)](#n3)</sup>.
2. If shortlisted, take a paper-based IELTS (score 6.0 or more, or equivalent), which the American Center arranges, and an interview in English <sup>[N3 (2024)](#n3)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - When does the TEA call open in 2027, and does it run every year?

<a id="fulbright-visiting-scholar"></a>

### 4.2 Fulbright Visiting Scholar

A fully funded grant for one academic year of research or teaching at a US institution <sup>[N12](#n12)</sup>.

**Who qualifies:** Bangladeshi public and private professionals, university teachers and researchers with 10 or more years of professional experience, holding a PhD or equivalent professional degree and proposing research and teaching in their field, with English good enough to work professionally at a US university <sup>[N12](#n12), [N19](#n19)</sup>.

**What it covers:** return airfare, academic fees, a monthly stipend for room and living costs, a book allowance, health and accident insurance, and travel and excess-baggage allowances <sup>[N12](#n12)</sup>.

**How to apply:** online, after creating a login account; questions go to dhakausexchanges@state.gov <sup>[N12](#n12)</sup>. For the round starting in September 2027 the deadline was extended to 31 October 2026, 10:59 am Bangladesh time <sup>[N19](#n19)</sup>. More than 200 Bangladeshis have taken part over 52 years <sup>[N19](#n19)</sup>.

<a id="visit-visa-and-the-visa-bond"></a>

## 5 Visit visa and the visa bond

The B1/B2 visa is for business and tourism <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>. Since 21 January 2026 Bangladesh is on the US visa bond list of 38 countries, under a 12-month pilot that began on 20 August 2025 <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>. Diplomatic sources said the US had signalled to Bangladesh that it was taking a hard line on overstaying, and the US Department of Homeland Security told Congress that of about 39 million visa holders due to leave in fiscal 2023, about 400,000 stayed past expiry <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>. The embassy's own payment instructions are covered elsewhere (see "The visitor visa bond" in the Illegal routes doc).

<a id="step-1-the-interview"></a>

### 5.1 Step 1: The interview

Attend the visa interview in person; every applicant must <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>.

<a id="step-2-post-the-bond"></a>

### 5.2 Step 2: Post the bond

1. If the officer finds you eligible, the officer sets the bond at USD 5,000, 10,000 or 15,000 <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>.
2. Submit DHS (Department of Homeland Security) form I-352 and agree to the bond terms on Pay.gov, the US Treasury's online payment platform <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>.
3. Paying the bond does not guarantee a visa; the money comes back if the visa is refused <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>.

<a id="step-3-travel-through-the-named-airports"></a>

### 5.3 Step 3: Travel through the named airports

Enter and leave the US only through Boston Logan (BOS), New York JFK or Washington Dulles (IAD) airports; otherwise you may be refused entry, or your departure may not be recorded <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>.

<a id="getting-the-bond-back"></a>

### 5.4 Getting the bond back

The money is returned if the visa holder complies with all the visa's conditions <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - How long after coming home is the bond refunded, and to which account?

<a id="business-travellers"></a>

### 5.5 Business travellers

Bangladesh's National Security Adviser asked the US Trade Representative on 8 January 2026 to ease travel for Bangladeshi businesspeople, and Bangladesh planned to ask in March 2026 for the B-1 business category to be exempted <sup>[N20](#n20), [N8](#n8)</sup>. The Foreign Adviser said Bangladesh would seek an exemption through the normal process <sup>[N21](#n21)</sup>.

### 5.6 Scams

- **Tried by travellers:** Facebook posts showing how to come on a visitor visa and switch to asylum (see [Asylum](#asylum)) <sup>[N14](#n14)</sup>.
- **Done to travellers:** (see "A visit or tourist visa sold as a job" in the Dalal scams doc).

<a id="family-immigrant-visa-the-green-card"></a>

## 6 Family immigrant visa: the green card

An immigrant visa gives a green card on entry and permanent residence; it covers family-based green cards (spouse, parents, children, siblings) and employment-based permanent residence <sup>[N4](#n4)</sup>.

<a id="the-pause-from-21-january-2026"></a>

### 6.1 The pause from 21 January 2026

- **What it does:** citizens of 75 countries including Bangladesh can still submit immigrant visa applications, but none will be approved or issued, and no end date has been given <sup>[N6](#n6)</sup>.
- **Why:** the State Department said it follows a November 2025 order to tighten screening of would-be immigrants who might become a financial burden <sup>[N6](#n6)</sup>. On a list of 120 countries ranked by immigrant welfare use, Bangladesh was 19th, with 54.8% of Bangladeshi immigrant households in the US taking US government benefits <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>.
- **Who is hit:** relatives of US citizens with petitions already filed cannot go for now; new petitions can be filed but get no visa; a US citizen who newly marries cannot bring the spouse for now; three US immigration lawyers said lakhs of Bangladeshis would be harmed <sup>[N4](#n4)</sup>. Lawyer Moin Chowdhury said a child who passes 21 meanwhile cannot come <sup>[N4](#n4)</sup>. Lawyer Dilli Raj Bhatta said it includes people whose interviews were already scheduled, and that legal challenges were expected <sup>[N4](#n4)</sup>.
- **Screening:** USCIS (US Citizenship and Immigration Services), which decides immigration applications, updated its policy in August 2025 to extend social media vetting of applications to searches for "anti-American activity" <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>.
- **Who is not hit:** non-immigrant, temporary, tourist and business visa applicants, and dual nationals applying on a passport of a country not on the list <sup>[N6](#n6)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Does a child who turns 21 during the pause lose the place in the queue, or does the Child Status Protection Act protect them?

<a id="filling-in-the-forms"></a>

### 6.2 Filling in the forms

A US-based columnist reported parents whose immigration form had a date entered in the "date of death" field; at the interview they were told they had died in 2002 and had to prove they were alive <sup>[N14](#n14)</sup>.

<a id="scams-1"></a>

### 6.3 Scams

A Narail man posing on Facebook as a US-based pilot of Bangladeshi origin promised to marry a divorced woman and take her to the US, and took Tk 1 crore 9 lakh from her for a passport, visa and other pretexts <sup>[N22 (2024)](#n22)</sup>. She sent the money to 13 different mobile numbers, sold 37 bhori of gold and took a bank loan <sup>[N22 (2024)](#n22)</sup>. When she found his Khulna address was false he threatened to post private videos of her <sup>[N22 (2024)](#n22)</sup>. She filed a case at Wari police station and police arrested him, but under pressure to repay her debts she settled for Tk 60 lakh <sup>[N22 (2024)](#n22)</sup>. (see "Marriage, family-visa and fake-nikahnama schemes" in the Dalal scams doc).

<a id="work-visa-h-1b"></a>

## 7 Work visa: H-1B

US companies use the H-1B visa to hire foreign workers, and its application fee was raised to USD 100,000 in September 2025 <sup>[N6](#n6)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Who pays the USD 100,000 fee (the employer or the worker), does it apply to every new H-1B petition, and how many Bangladeshis got H-1B visas in 2025?

<a id="asylum"></a>

## 8 Asylum

The US has suspended asylum applications, and set its refugee ceiling for fiscal 2026 at 7,500, the lowest in its history, mainly for white South Africans <sup>[N6](#n6)</sup>. Bangladeshis who post on Facebook how to switch from a visitor visa to asylum risk making visas harder for everyone, a US-based columnist wrote <sup>[N14](#n14)</sup>. (see "Asylum claims built on a false story" in the Illegal routes doc).

> **Need to know**
> - Is the suspension of asylum applications for all nationalities or only the travel-ban countries, and is it still in force?

<a id="irregular-routes-and-undocumented-stay"></a>

## 9 Irregular routes and undocumented stay

<a id="overland-through-south-america-and-mexico"></a>

### 9.1 Overland through South America and Mexico

In the case of Saifullah Al-Mamun, a Bangladeshi extradited from Brazil on 8 July 2026 and charged in Laredo, Texas, with conspiring to smuggle people from Bangladesh into the US, the charges describe this route <sup>[N5](#n5)</sup>:
1. People paid large sums to brokers in Bangladesh <sup>[N5](#n5)</sup>.
2. Saifullah housed them in São Paulo, Brazil, and arranged their travel <sup>[N5](#n5)</sup>.
3. Mohammad Milon Hossain kept them in Tapachula, Mexico, and sent them to Monterrey <sup>[N5](#n5)</sup>.
4. Moktar Hossain kept them in Monterrey and told them how to cross the Rio Grande into the US, which many found hard to cross safely <sup>[N5](#n5)</sup>.

Milon and Moktar pleaded guilty and were each sentenced to 46 months; Saifullah faces 5 to 15 years if convicted of smuggling for financial gain <sup>[N5](#n5)</sup>. The Justice Department's Joint Task Force Alpha has arrested more than 464 smugglers and associates, and more than 414 have been convicted in the US <sup>[N5](#n5)</sup>. (see "The United States through South America and Mexico" in the Illegal routes doc).

<a id="money-paid-to-brokers-who-never-send-you"></a>

### 9.2 Money paid to brokers who never send you

Zakir Hossain, 24, of Chatkhil, Noakhali, paid Tk 25 lakh to a Lakshmipur and Feni broker ring to go to the US, and two and a half years later had not gone; his family holds papers for Tk 19 lakh of it <sup>[N7](#n7)</sup>. When he asked for his money back the brokers threatened him, and in August 2025 he and a friend were found dead in his car in a Dhaka hospital car park; his family suspects the ring, and police had not yet found the cause of death <sup>[N7](#n7)</sup>.

<a id="where-undocumented-bangladeshis-live"></a>

### 9.3 Where undocumented Bangladeshis live

Bangladesh's Foreign Ministry has no figure, but officials said irregular Bangladeshis are likely concentrated in New York, New Jersey and Connecticut <sup>[N9](#n9)</sup>.

<a id="deportation-to-bangladesh"></a>

### 9.4 Deportation to Bangladesh

- **Who is sent back:** people who stayed after losing their immigration cases, including some convicted of crimes in the US; Bangladesh's Foreign Ministry is told about each one in advance <sup>[N16](#n16)</sup>.
- **Bangladesh's terms:** it takes people back once their citizenship is confirmed, and asked the US not to send them in handcuffs <sup>[N9](#n9)</sup>.
- **How they arrive:** 34 people from 6 March to 21 April 2025; 39 by chartered plane on 2 August 2025; 30 more (29 men and one woman) on 5 September 2025, put on the plane in handcuffs that were removed on landing in Dhaka <sup>[N16](#n16), [N23](#n23), [N17](#n17)</sup>.
- **Totals:** 187 by September 2025 and about 500 by March 2026, with about 5,000 more in process <sup>[N17](#n17), [N8](#n8)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - What help do returnees get at Dhaka airport, and does removal bring a ban on returning to the US, for how long?

## 10 Health care and its cost

A US-based Bangladeshi wrote in March 2025 that each of his children's two-day hospital births billed over USD 50,000, of which he paid a USD 6,000 insurance deductible, and that a vlogger in New York, being poor, got the city's insurance and paid nothing <sup>[N14](#n14)</sup>.

<a id="sending-money-home"></a>

## 11 Sending money home

<a id="the-1-tax-on-cash"></a>

### 11.1 The 1% tax on cash

Since 1 January 2026, money sent from the US in cash, money order, cashier's cheque or similar physical means carries a federal excise tax of USD 1 per USD 100; sending from a bank account, by debit card or online carries no extra tax <sup>[N15](#n15)</sup>. It is part of the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, signed on 4 July 2025 <sup>[N15](#n15)</sup>. Experts said it hits hardest irregular migrants without bank accounts and low-income migrants <sup>[N15](#n15)</sup>.

<a id="who-sends-it"></a>

### 11.2 Who sends it

At the April 2025 remittance fair in Jackson Heights, New York, Sonali Bank, Janata Bank and Islami Bank Bangladesh were ranked first to third for remittance from the US; Mastercard, MoneyGram and Ria first to third among non-banks; and Sonali Exchange, BA Express and Standard Express were awarded for service to the Bangladeshi community <sup>[N24](#n24), [N25](#n25)</sup>.

<a id="the-numbers"></a>

### 11.3 The numbers

- USD 3.52 billion in fiscal 2022-23, second after Saudi Arabia <sup>[N26 (2024)](#n26)</sup>.
- USD 1.4449 billion in January-March 2025, about 18% of the total and the most from any country, ahead of the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia; USD 4.271 billion in July 2024 to April 2025 <sup>[N13](#n13), [N27](#n27)</sup>.
- Fourth in 2025 with 11% of the total, and fifth by January 2026, according to Standard Express's chief executive <sup>[N4](#n4), [N15](#n15)</sup>.

The US became the top source after August 2024 <sup>[N13](#n13)</sup>. Large firms such as Western Union and MoneyGram collect remittances in an aggregated way, and some in the sector say exchange houses collect from various countries and send from the US <sup>[N27](#n27), [N13](#n13)</sup>. (see "Ways to send money and what each costs" in the Sending money home doc).

<a id="if-a-student-or-worker-dies"></a>

## 12 If a student or worker dies

In April 2026 two Bangladeshi PhD students at the University of South Florida went missing; a family friend reported them the next day, police found one body and arrested his roommate, and the other student's body had not been found two days later <sup>[N28](#n28), [N29](#n29)</sup>.
1. The Bangladesh Embassy in Washington, the Foreign Ministry and the local police kept in touch with the family <sup>[N29](#n29)</sup>.
2. The district commissioner said the family must submit an application, which the district sends to the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare to bring the body home <sup>[N29](#n29)</sup>.
3. The upazila officer said the embassy does the main work and the local administration gives the embassy any local information it needs <sup>[N18](#n18)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Who pays to bring a body home from the US for a student not registered with BMET, and is the Wage Earners' Welfare Board grant available?
