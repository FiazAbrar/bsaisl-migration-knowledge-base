# United States open questions

These are the questions the United States doc can't yet answer, grouped by the doc's sections. Each is a "Need to know" in `doc.md`.

## Background

- What do Bangladeshi students and their families typically pay in total (tuition, living costs, agency fees) for a US degree, and what share have full scholarships?
- Is the US Embassy in Dhaka again issuing immigrant visas to Bangladeshis after the 21 August 2026 ruling, or are interviews still held up by the global appointment pause the plaintiffs reported on 26 August?
- How many Bangladeshis did BMET (the Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training, which clears Bangladeshis going abroad for work) clear for the United States in 2024, 2025 and 2026, and on which visas (H-1B, H-2A, H-2B, EB-3)?

## Student visa

- Where is the EducationUSA advising centre in Dhaka, what are its hours, and is advising free?
- How long is the wait for a student visa interview in Dhaka in late 2026, and what share of Bangladeshi student applicants are refused?

## Exchange visitor visa: Fulbright

- When does the TEA call open in 2027, and does it run every year?

## Visit visa and the visa bond

- How many weeks after departure does the bond money arrive, and is it paid back to the card or account it came from?

## Work visa: H-1B

- How many Bangladeshis got H-1B visas in 2025?
- How many days may an H-1B worker stay in the US after losing the job, to find a new employer or leave?

## Joining a husband in the US

- What can a wife do about her own status if her abusive husband is in the US on a student or H-1B visa rather than as a citizen or green card holder?

## Irregular routes and undocumented stay

- What help do returnees from the US get at Dhaka airport, and how many years must a person removed from the US wait before a new US visa?

## Health care and its cost

- What health insurance do US universities require of F-1 students, and what does a typical student plan cost a year?

## If a student or worker dies

- Who pays the funeral home and airfare to bring home the body of a student or visitor not registered with BMET, and can the family claim the Wage Earners' Welfare Board's death grant?
