# United States open questions

These are the questions the United States doc can't yet answer, grouped by the doc's sections. Each is a "Need to know" in `doc.md`.

## Background

- Is the immigrant visa pause for Bangladeshis still in force in November 2026, and has any court ruled on it?
- Has the permanent visa bond rule taken effect, from what date, with what amounts for Bangladeshis, and did Bangladesh get any exemption for B-1 business travellers?
- Do any Bangladeshis go to the US on employer-sponsored work visas (H-1B, H-2A, H-2B, EB-3) through BMET (the Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training, which clears Bangladeshis going abroad for work), and does BMET clear them?
- What do Bangladeshi students and their families typically pay in total (tuition, living costs, agency fees) for a US degree, and what share have full scholarships?
- Can an H-1B worker change employer, and what happens to the visa when the job ends?

## Student visa

- Where is the EducationUSA advising centre in Dhaka, what are its hours, and is advising free?
- What document does the university send (the I-20), and what is the SEVIS fee and visa fee in 2026?
- How long is the wait for a student visa interview in Dhaka in late 2026, and what share of Bangladeshi student applicants are refused?
- What on-campus work, CPT (Curricular Practical Training) and OPT (Optional Practical Training) are open to students, and for how many hours?

## Exchange visitor visa: Fulbright

- When does the TEA call open in 2027, and does it run every year?

## Visit visa and the visa bond

- How long after coming home is the bond refunded, and to which account?

## Family immigrant visa: the green card

- Does a child who turns 21 during the pause lose the place in the queue, or does the Child Status Protection Act protect them?

## Work visa: H-1B

- How many Bangladeshis got H-1B visas in 2025, and is the salary-based selection in force for the 2027 round?
- Can an H-1B worker change employer, and how long can they stay after losing the job?

## Joining a husband in the US

- Can a wife whose status depends on an abusive husband apply for her own immigration status, and what is the national domestic violence hotline number?

## Asylum

- Has USCIS resumed asylum decisions since the November 2025 halt?

## Irregular routes and undocumented stay

- What help do returnees get at Dhaka airport, and does removal bring a ban on returning to the US, for how long?

## Health care and its cost

- What health insurance must students and visitors carry, and what does a student plan cost?

## If a student or worker dies

- Who pays to bring a body home from the US for a student not registered with BMET, and is the Wage Earners' Welfare Board grant available?

## Contacts

- The address, phone and consular helpline of the Bangladesh Embassy in Washington and the Consulates in New York and Los Angeles.
