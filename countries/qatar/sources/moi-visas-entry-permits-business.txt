On-arrival tourist visa for Pakistanis

Terms & Conditions:

•The applicant should hold the amount of (5000 QR) or the equivalent of a valid credit card

•The return ticket must be confirmed

•The passport must be valid for at least 6 months

•Duration of the visa will be 30 days and can be extended for a similar period. A return ticket should be confirmed before extension.

•Those coming directly from Pakistan should receive a certificate of vaccination against polio.

•The business visit is granted though only accredited companies.
