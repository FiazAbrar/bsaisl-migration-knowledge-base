 Travel Permit 

Content: Pages

A Travel Permit is issued to a Bangladeshi citizen who does not possess a valid Bangladeshi Passport but intends to return to Bangladesh.
Requirements for Travel Permit:
Duly filled in Travel Permit application form (please 
click here
 to find the form).
Copy of previous passport.
Three passport size photographs.
Physical presence of the applicant at the Embassy for an interview.
Fee (non-refundable):
 QR 50.00 for ordinary delivery and QR 98. for urgent delivery.
Receiving of application:
 The application is received at the counter between 08:30 AM -4:00 PM (Sunday-Thursday)
Delivery time:
 Between 8:30AM - 04:30 PM (Sunday – Thursday).
Note:

A Travel Permit is valid for 3 months only.
