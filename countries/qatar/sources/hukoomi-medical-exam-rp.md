# Medical Examination for Residence Permit

Ministry of Public Health

Service Type

Information service

Last updated

11 Jan 2021

## Service Description

People who wish to obtain a residence permit in Qatar shall visit the Medical Commission with all the required documents to undergo a medical examination.

## Steps

- Submit original passport.
- Attach clear copy of your residence permit with a cell phone number to call for emergency.
- Provide medical certificate issued by an accredited center in the country of the foreign labor.

## Fees

A fee of QR100 applies, and it is paid by payment cards.

## Additional Information

- Companies must register their employees on Qatar e-Government Portal (Hukoomi).
- Registration and medical examination may be carried out in the accredited private hospitals (Al-Ahli Hospital, Doha Clinic Hospital, Al-Emadi Hospital) for VIP employees and families only in accordance with applicable procedures of these hospitals.
- The result of the medical examination shall be sent electronically to the residency system of the Ministry of Interior.
