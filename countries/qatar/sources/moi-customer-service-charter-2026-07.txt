    
Public Service Charter of the Ministry of Interior

         
         2030     

We are committed to serving you with e ciency, transparency,
   and respect, in a manner that enhances security, protects
        society, and achieves Qatar National Vision 2030.

                                 
                                Rights of the Service User

                   .    
                   Receiving fair and excellent service.
   .     
   Knowing the requirements and procedures of the service clearly.

                 .  
                 Submitting complaints and suggestions.

     .   
     Protecting the privacy and confidentiality of personal data.

         .     
         Receiving the service within the specified timeframe.

                                 
                                Duties of the Service User

             .   
             Providing accurate and complete information.
            .      
            Adhering to the queuing system and respecting

                       the priority of other visitors.
             .     
            Complying with approved laws and procedures.
            .     
             Treating employees with respect and following

                      regulations during interactions.
               .    
                Preserving public property and facilities.
