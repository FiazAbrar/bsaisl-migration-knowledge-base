# Request to Issue Labor Recruitment Approval

Provided by Ministry of Labour

Service Type

E-Service

Last updated

11 Apr 2023

## Service Description

This service allows companies to submit a request to issue labor recruitment approval online through the website of the Ministry of Labour (MOL). The request is then reviewed by MOL employees to make a decision about it. If the application is approved (in terms of gender, number, and profession of workers), it is then automatically transferred to the nationalities selection system. After the application is approved, its information is exported to the Ministry of Interior.

## Steps

- Visit the service information page (https://www.mol.gov.qa/En/services/Pages/ServiceDetails.aspx?itemid=96) on the MOL website.
- Click on "Apply", then select "Establishments Portal", available as one of the options on the login page (https://esrv.mol.gov.qa/services/login).
- Login to the National Authentication System (NAS) using the smart card, if you are one of the authorized signatories.
- Choose the Establishment ID number for the establishment's main branch or one of its branches for which a labor recruitment request is to be submitted.
- Select "Request to Issue a Labour Recruitment Approval" service.
- Fill out the required details and submit your request.

## Fees

No fees are required for this service.

## Additional Information

- The Establishment ID must be active.
- There should be no ban on the Establishment ID.
