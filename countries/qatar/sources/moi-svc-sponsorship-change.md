##### Terms & Conditions

1.  The sponsored person shall have completed two years inside the country with the same sponsor .
2.  No Objection Certificate (NOC) from present sponsor to transfer the sponsorship to the new sponsor .
3.  Approval of the new sponsor to transfer in to his sponsorship .
4.  The present sponsor, the new sponsor and the employee shall sign the sponsorship transfer form. The signatures of present and new sponsors will be verified.
