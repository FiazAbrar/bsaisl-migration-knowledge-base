# Council of Ministers Decision No. (38) of 2026 Amending Certain Provisions of the Executive Regulations of the Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Combating the Financing of Terrorism Law Issued by Council of Ministers Decision No. (41) of 2019

Cabinet / Council of Ministers,Having reviewed the Constitution,And Law / Act No. (22) of 2017 on the Regulation of Real Estate Mediation Activities, as amended by Law / Act No. (13) of 2026,And the Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Terrorism Financing Law issued by Law / Act No. (20) of 2019, and the laws amending it,And Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (29) of 1996 concerning Cabinet / Council of Ministers decisions submitted to the Amir for ratification and issuance,And Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (28) of 2023 establishing the General Authority for Regulating the Real Estate Sector, as amended by Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (34) of 2026,And the Executive Regulations of the Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Terrorism Financing Law issued by Cabinet / Council of Ministers Decision / Resolution No. (41) of 2019, and the decisions amending them,And upon the proposal of the Chairman of the National Committee for Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT),Has decided as follows:

### Article 1

The text of Article (59) of the Executive Regulations of the aforementioned Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Terrorism Financing Law shall be replaced with the following text: Article (59):«The following supervisory authorities shall be competent to monitor, follow up on, and supervise the compliance of the sectors indicated opposite each of them with the requirements of Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Terrorism Financing:No.Supervisory AuthoritySector1The BankBanks and exchangeProviders of Transfer of Funds or value servicesInsurance and reinsuranceFinance and investment companies2Qatar Financial Markets AuthorityFinancial Mediation companiesQatar Stock ExchangeQatar Central Securities Depository Company3Ministry of JusticeLawyersAuthorized notaries4Ministry of Commerce and IndustryChartered accountantsDealers in Precious metals or GemstonesTrust and company service providers5Qatar Financial Centre Regulatory AuthorityEntities established in the Centre: financial institutions and Designated Non-Financial Businesses or Professions (DNFBPs).6General Authority for Regulating the Real Estate SectorReal estate brokers7Charitable Activities Regulatory AuthorityNon-profit organizationsThe supervisory authorities include any other entity that legally has the power to regulate or supervise, or is responsible for Financial Institutions Oversight, or for the oversight of non-profit organizations and Designated Non-Financial Businesses or Professions (DNFBPs).».

### Article 2

All competent authorities, each within its respective jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall come into force as of 1/1/2027. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.
