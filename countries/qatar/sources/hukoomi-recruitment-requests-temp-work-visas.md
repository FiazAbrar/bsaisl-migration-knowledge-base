# Submit Recruitment Requests - Approvals for Temporary Work Visas

Provided by Ministry of Labour

Service Type

E-Service

Last updated

16 Aug 2022

## Service Description

This service enables establishments to request the issuance of a recruitment approval for temporary work visas. These work visas are approvals specially for workers to be recruited temporarily to work in the State of Qatar.

## Steps

- Open "Request for Temporary Work Visa" service, then click on "Apply".
- Select "Establishments Portal" from the "Login Method" list.
- Log in to the National Authentication System "Tawtheeq" using a smart card.
- Enter all the required details.
- Confirm the details and submit the application.

## Fees

No fees are required for this service.

## Additional Information

Service Conditions and Requirements:

- Conditions: The establishment must be one of the below entities:
  - Governmental and semi-government entities.
  - Industry and energy companies’ sector.
  - Factories that have government contracts.
  - Companies that have government contracts with the government or energy companies.
  - Any establishment that has been granted the authority to benefit from the service by the Ministry of Labour.
- Requirements:
  - A smart card to verify the identity of the person authorized in the establishment to use the service through the National Authentication System.
  - The establishment has the authority to submit the application.
  - The establishment's registration must be valid.
  - There is no ban on the establishment.
- Service Submission Timings: Sunday to Thursday, from 7:00 am to 2:00 pm.
