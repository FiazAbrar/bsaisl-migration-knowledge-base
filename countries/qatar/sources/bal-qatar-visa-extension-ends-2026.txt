                            Qatar | Temporary entry visa extension policy ends                        

                                June 8, 2026                            

Qatar’s Ministry of Interior 
announced
 that the temporary extension policy for entry visas officially ended, effective June 7, 2026.

Key takeaways:

The policy was introduced due to airspace disruptions caused by tensions with Iran, stranding travelers within the country. 
It granted automatic rolling one-month extensions
 for all entry visas, free of charge.

Effective June 7, standard immigration procedures have been fully restored.
 Visa holders must comply with the original validity periods and fee structures applicable to their visa type.

Individuals whose visas expire on or after June 7 should initiate renewal procedures, apply for an extension if eligible or arrange timely departure.
