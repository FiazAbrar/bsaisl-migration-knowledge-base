Service Details

This visa is issued on-arrival at the airport as per the following.

1 - For those coming on Qatar Airways with the intention of continuing their journey to their destination. 

2 - For those coming on Qatar Airways with the intention of Tourist travel in Doha.

Fees

			Free of Charge

Terms & Conditions

1 - For those coming on Qatar Airways with the intention of continuing their journey to their destination. 

- Qatar should be falling on the way to his final destination. 

- The visitor should hold return ticket.

- The duration of stay in the country should be not less than 12 hours and not more than 48 hours. 

2 - For those coming on Qatar Airways with the intention of Tourist travel in Doha.

-  The form should be filled by the officer of Qatar Airways. 

- The duration of stay in the country should be not less than 4 hours and not more than 12 hours. 

- Qatar Airways will be responsible for them from thier time of entry until they leave the country.
