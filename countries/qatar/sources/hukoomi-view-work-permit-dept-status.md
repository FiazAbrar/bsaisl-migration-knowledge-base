# View Work Permit Department Applications Status

Ministry of Labour

Service Type

E-Service

Last updated

03 Jan 2022

## Service Description

Employers who have filed an application to hire foreign workers can check the status of their license requests online.

## Steps

- Enter application number, year of application, and establishment ID.
- Click on "Search".

## Fees

No fees are required for this service.

## Additional Information

For more information, you can call MOL on 16505 or 44068979.
