                                                        Nine arrested for running fake companies, visa trade in Qatar

Published:
                                                        19 Jun 2023 - 09:07 pm
                                                        | Last Updated:
                                                        19 Jun 2023 - 09:21 pm

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                The Peninsula Online

Doha, Qatar: The Search and Follow-up Department of the Ministry of Interior (MoI) has apprehended nine (9) suspects over fraudulent activities, specifically setting up fake companies with the intention of engaging in visa trading. 

According to MoI, the 9 expatriate suspects of Arab and Asian nationalities were arrested for their involvement in fraudulent activities through deceiving citizens by making false promises of profits through the creation of fake companies with the intention of engaging in visa trading.

With the approval of the Public Prosecution, several suspects were apprehended after their illicit activities were monitored on social media platforms and through specific Document Clearance Offices.

The ministry said that subsequent searches conducted at their residences and workplaces led to the discovery of significant amounts of money, totaling over QR 190,000. 

Additionally, counterfeit company records, rental contracts, Qatari citizens' ID cards, prepaid bank cards, and bank cards belonging to the expatriate workers to whom the visas were sold were seized.

The suspects are currently being referred to the Public Prosecution to initiate the necessary legal proceedings in this matter.
