# Apply to Replace Employer (Before Getting Residency Permit)

Provided by Ministry of Interior

Service Type

E-Service

Last updated

11 Jan 2021

## Service Description

You can submit a request to the Expatriate Affairs & Unified Services Centers at the Ministry of Interior (MOI) to replace an employer with another employer before getting the residency permit after meeting the required conditions.

## Steps

**Applying Online:**

- Log in with your smartcard to the [e-services portal](https://eservices.moi.gov.qa/eservices-portal/) of MOI.
- Select required transaction type (personal for smart card owner or company name for authorized person).
- Select "Residency" then "Consume RP Change Sponsorship Approvals".
- Fill out the required information and attach any necessary documents.
- Click on "Confirm" and pay appropriate fees.

**Applying Offline:**

- Download and fill out the [application form](https://portal.moi.gov.qa/wps/wcm/connect/dd501fca-a10f-4bc5-8819-090a95488d8c/Employer%2BTransfer%2BApplication%2BForm.pdf?MOD=AJPERES).
- Submit the form alongside with the No Objection Certificate to the Expatriate Affairs & Unified Services Dept.

## Fees

A fee of QR300 applies.

## Additional Information

- The expatriate should exceed the legal term (three months) since his entry to the country.
- The new employer should also get approval from the concerned body to recruit staff with the same nationality and profession.
