@Ahnaf Shamim Hi. This may take an hour or upto 15 working days to further process it. It means your medical reports are now with MoPH Qatar for the validation and they are the ones who declare the applicant fit/unfit and share the status with MoI Qatar. I would suggest you to call QVC Qatar (+97444069999) as it helped me as well.


If after 15 working days (Friday/Saturday is weekend) the status is still not changed, your sponsor then needs to raise the ticket.


Good luck!!
