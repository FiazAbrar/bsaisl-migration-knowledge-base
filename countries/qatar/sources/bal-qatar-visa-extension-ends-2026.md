# Qatar \| Temporary entry visa extension policy ends

June 8, 2026

Qatar’s Ministry of Interior [announced](https://www.futuregate-qa.com/news-detail.html?id=aea7dba6-b6c8-458f-94ae-0d9ae6b61bfd&country=qatar) that the temporary extension policy for entry visas officially ended, effective June 7, 2026.

**Key takeaways:**

The policy was introduced due to airspace disruptions caused by tensions with Iran, stranding travelers within the country. **It granted automatic rolling one-month extensions** for all entry visas, free of charge.

**Effective June 7, standard immigration procedures have been fully restored.** Visa holders must comply with the original validity periods and fee structures applicable to their visa type.

Individuals whose visas expire on or after June 7 should initiate renewal procedures, apply for an extension if eligible or arrange timely departure.
