#### Free on-Arrival Tourist Visa for a maximum of (30) days (Pakistani, Indian and Thai nationalities)

A free on-arrival tourist visa is granted to Pakistani, Indian and Thai nationals for maximum period of (30) days, or according to hotel reservation.

**Terms and requirements:**

1-  Passport valid for at least three months

2- Confirmed round-trip ticket.

3- Confirmed hotel reservation through the [www.discoverqatar.qa](https://www.discoverqatar.qa/) site only. Hotel reservations from other sites are not accepted.

4-  Health insurance from an accredited [insurance company](https://www.moph.gov.qa/arabic/derpartments/policyaffairs/hfid/hirs/insurancecompanies/Pages/default.aspx)
