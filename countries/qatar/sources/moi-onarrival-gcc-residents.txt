Visa for residents of the GCC countries – On-arrival visa for single trip, for a fee of (QR 100 - for period of (30) days, which can be extended

Single Entry on-Arrival Visa for GCC Residents for a fee of (QR 100) for period of (30) days, extendable

On arrival visa for residents of the GCC countries is granted for single trip, for a fee of (QR 100), for a period of (30) days, which can be extended.

Terms and requirements:

1- Passport valid for at least three months.

2- Confirmed round-trip ticket.

3- Hotel reservation in one of the country's hotels during the visit period or a clear address of a friend or relative.

4- Health insurance from one of the accredited 
insurance companies
.

5- The resident’s profession must be one of the 
approved professions
 (206 professions).
