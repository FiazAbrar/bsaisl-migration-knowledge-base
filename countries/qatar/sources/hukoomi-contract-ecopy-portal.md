# Portal for Printing Employment Contracts

Provided by Ministry of Labour

Service Type

E-Service

Last updated

11 Aug 2026

## Service Description

Employees residing in Qatar who are subject to the country's Labor Law may submit an online request to the "Labour Relations" Department at the Ministry of Labour (MOL), through the portal for printing employment contracts, to obtain an e-copy of their employment contracts after they are authenticated.

## Steps

- Enter your QID card or visa number, as well as mobile number.
- Enter the code sent to your mobile.
- Click on "Login".

## Fees

No fees are required for this service.

## Additional Information

For more information, you may contact the Ministry of Labour through the hotline number: 16505 - 44068979
