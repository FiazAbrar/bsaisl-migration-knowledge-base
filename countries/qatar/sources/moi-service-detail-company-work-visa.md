##### Terms & Conditions

 

- Attested educational qualification certificates to prove his education credentials for the profession
- Good conduct certificate (Police clearance certificate) issues by concerned authorities in the home country of the expatriate, attested by Qatar Embassy in his country of expatriate or the representing mission/.
- Employment contract between worker and employer.
- Pass the medical test outside the country as per the norms laid down \
