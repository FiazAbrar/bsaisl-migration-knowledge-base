    MOPH Explores Mechanism for Introducing Medical Visas in Qatar

    10 April 20262 Mins Read
    Copy Link WhatsApp Twitter Facebook Email LinkedIn
    Follow Us
    Instagram Facebook X (Twitter) TikTok Snapchat LinkedIn WhatsApp
    Share
    Facebook Twitter LinkedIn WhatsApp Email Copy Link

    According to Al Sharq, the Ministry of Public Health (MOPH) is exploring mechanisms for introducing medical visas that would allow patients and visitors to receive medical treatment at healthcare institutions in Qatar, as part of broader efforts to support medical tourism and attract patients from outside the country.

    According to information obtained from informed sources, the Ministry has held meetings with private sector representatives to discuss the proposed framework for applying for medical visas. The mechanism under consideration involves an electronic portal through which patients would submit medical reports evidencing their health conditions from outside Qatar.

    The proposed visa would enable patients to access advanced medical procedures at accredited healthcare institutions across the country. In addition to clinical treatment, visa holders would be able to benefit from preventive care programmes, specialised health services and tailored treatment plans.

    The framework also allows for recovery and rest periods at resorts and tourist facilities, offering patients suitable environments to complete their treatment journeys. These facilities would support both physical and mental wellbeing during post‑treatment recovery.

    Healthcare institutions in Qatar are equipped with advanced capabilities covering a wide range of services, including mental and physical rehabilitation, physiotherapy, rehabilitative care, precise diagnostic services, intensive care, laboratory testing and vaccination programmes. These services are delivered through a comprehensive healthcare system aligned with international standards.

    The introduction of a medical visa would place Qatar among more than 40 countries that have adopted similar visa schemes to support medical tourism. The initiative is expected to strengthen Qatar’s healthcare sector and contribute to its growing reputation as a destination for specialised medical treatment.

    Qatar has continued to enhance its position in global healthcare rankings, achieving first place in the Middle East and North Africa region and ranking eighteenth worldwide in the latest international health indices. The proposed medical visa is anticipated to further support the country’s healthcare ecosystem while attracting regional and international patients.

    Source: Al Sharq
