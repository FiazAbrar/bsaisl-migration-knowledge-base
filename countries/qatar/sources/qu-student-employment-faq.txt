                                  

      The Frequently Asked Questions about the Student Employment Program

     .                   
In this document we have compiled the most frequently asked questions from students about
the Student Employment Program.

                                                                     
     When can I apply to join the Student Employment Program?

                       
                                                 .        

         The current Student Employment System allows all students who meet the program's
         eligibility requirements to register through the electronic system for student employment
         throughout the semester.

                                                                     
     What are the eligibility requirements to join the Student Employment Program?

          2.5        24    
          .)          (   
         The student must complete 24 credit hours with a GPA of 2.5 and above. The student
         must also complete the requirements of the career excellence workshop (Excluded from
         this the students enrolled in the postgraduate program and the Arabic language program
         for non-native speakers).

                                                                     
     Can the student have more than one job at the same time?

                                                                     .      
         The student can hold two jobs at the same time.

                                                             
     Can my student employment salary that is previously determined change?

  .                   

Yes, the student employment salary rate is subject to change according to any change that may
occur in the student employment payment policy.
                                                                      
     How can I register in the Career Excellence Workshop?

                     
                                                    .       

         During each semester, an announcement is sent to students enrolled in the Student
         Employment Program that includes information on when and how to register in the
         training workshop.
