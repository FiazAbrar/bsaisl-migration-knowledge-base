Permanent Residency

Introduction

**Introduction**

The Law No. 10 of 2018 on Permanent Residence stipulates the establishment of a committee in the Ministry of the Interior called the "Permanent Residence Card Granting Committee", whose task is to carry out all the procedures related to the granting of the card. The committee will be formed by a decision of the Council of Ministers to form the committee, who will nominate its members and determine the duration of their membership and its working system.

The Committee shall decide on all tasks and procedures related to the requirements for granting permanent residence to eligible categories in accordance with its working mechanisms. The Committee shall submit its recommendations to the Minister of the Interior, in turn to issue the appropriate decision regarding the applications referred to.

Terms and Conditions

**Terms and Conditions:**

**Terms and Conditions to obtain permanent residency permit in accordance with the Law No. 10 of the year 2018**

- The applicant should complete 20 years in Qatar on a legal residence permit if he/she is born abroad and 10 years if born in Qatar.
- He/she should have enough income to cover expenditures of him and his family.
- He/she should be of good behaviour and reputation and should not have been convicted of a final verdict on any dishonorable offence inside Qatar or abroad.
- He/she should have enough knowledge of the Arabic language.

**Those exempted from the conditions and requirements:**

- Children of Qatari woman married to non-Qatari
- Non-Qatari husband of Qatari woman
- The non-Qatari wife of Qatari
- Children of Qatari by naturalization
- Those who performed great services to the state
- Persons with special capabilities that the state needs
