## About Sahem

The “Sahem” program provides a set of integrated solutions and services that aim to empower various categories into the Qatari labour market. The program targets students enrolled in accredited universities in Qatar, by providing part-time job opportunities. In addition to provide appropriate opportunities for people with disabilities with the aim of integrating them into Qatari labour market. 

**Program Benefits for Individuals (University Students and People with Disabilities)**

- Work opportunities to gain exceptional professional experience in Qatar.
- Opportunity to secure a full-time job in the future.
- The flexibility in recruitment procedures with companies registered on the Sahem program platform.
- Beneficiaries receive an experience certificate upon completing the part-time job period.
- Individuals recieves the "Sahem" card upon signing their contract. The card offers them a variety of discounts.

 

**Program Benefits** **for Employers**

- **Support for Nationalization Efforts**\
  The employment of Qatari students and children of Qatari women is counted within the company’s approved localization (Qatarization) quota, contributing to the implementation of Law No. 12 of 2024 regarding the localization of jobs in the private sector
- **Flexible, Approved Training Contracts**\
  The program offers flexible training contracts that are officially approved by Qatar’s Ministry of Labour, enabling employers to legally and efficiently engage young local talent.
- **Short-Term Employment Options**\
  Employers can hire students for short durations based on specific operational needs. This flexibility supports seasonal or project-based work without long-term commitments.
- **Efficient and Reliable Hiring Process**\
  The program ensures a smooth recruitment journey, providing access to verified candidate data and streamlining the process of identifying suitable applicants.

##### [University Students](#collapse-0)

The program supports university students enrolled in accredited universities in Qatar, by providing part-time job opportunities.

**Program participants will be eligible to work under the following conditions:**

- The applicant must be a current student at a university or higher education institution in Qatar.
- The student must be 17 years of age or older.
- The student must hold a valid Qatari ID card.

##### **Note**

##### **Exchange students from universities outside Qatar are not eligible to join the program.**

##### **Required Personal Documents**

- Copy of a valid Qatari ID
- High-quality personal passport sized photo (size 6x4)
- Copy of last updated CV

&nbsp;

- A copy of the birth certificate certified by the Ministry of Public Health (for both children of Qatari Mothers and those born in Qatar)

##### 

##### **University Enrollment Documentation**

- Letter of registration/enrolment at a university or higher education institution within Qatar mentioning the semester currently registered.

#### **Terms and Conditions**

Applicants must adhere to the following conditions regarding part-time employment:

- Working hours should comply with the following criteria: a maximum of 20 hours per week during the semester and a maximum of 40 hours per week during the summer vacation with taking into consideration of the Student academic calendar.
- The part-time employment contract is only valid if the student continues their studies and expires upon graduation.
- Qatar Manpower Solutions & Co. (Jusour) will verify that part-time employees are students.
- According to the contract signed between the parties, Jusour is also responsible for taking appropriate action at the event of the student’s graduation.

##### [People with Disability](#collapse-1)

### Part-time/full-time job opportunities for people with disabilities

The Program is dedicated to enhancing the capabilities of people with disabilities, fostering their potential and promoting their independence. Alongside with providing them with a flexible working schedule depending on their health condition.

This program offers job opportunities for Qatari, non-Qatari with Qatari mothers and residents with disabilities in Qatar, aiming to empower them in the private sector.

**Eligibility**

Candidates for this program must meet the following criteria:

- The applicant must be a person with disability
- Submission of an approved medical report from Hamad Medical Corporation

#### **Required Documents**

- Copy of a valid Qatari ID
- Medical report from Hamad Medical Corporation
- High-quality personal passport-sized photo (Size 6x4)
- Copy of last updated CV
- A copy of the birth certificate certified by the Ministry of Public Health (for both children of Qatari Mother and those born in Qatar)
- Emergency contact details:\
  - Name
  - Relationship
  - Telephone number
