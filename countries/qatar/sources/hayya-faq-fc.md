## Is there a deadline for completing the payment?

Yes, you must complete the required payment within 14 days of receiving the notification email. Failure to make the payment within this timeframe will result in cancellation of your Hayya application, and you will need to submit a new one again.

## What does it mean if my application is marked as “Pending Accommodation Confirmation”?

If your application status is “Pending Accommodation Confirmation”, you must provide your accommodation details within 30 days of receiving the notification email. Failure to do so will result in cancellation of your Hayya application, and you will need to submit a new one again.

## Do Indian applicants for the Hayya E-Visa need to book their accommodation through Discover Qatar only?

No. Indian applicants for the Hayya E-Visa have the flexibility to choose their accommodation before arriving in the State of Qatar. They are not restricted to using only Discover Qatar and may book through any platform of their choice, or select the "Host Family and Friends" option available on the Hayya platform or app.

## I have been waiting for a long time and my application is still pending. Can I start another application?

It is not allowed to start another application while your current application status is still "Pending."The applicant is kindly requested to wait for approval.

## What are the requirements for entry into the State of Qatar through Hayya?

1. Minimum of 3 months passport validity from the date of arrival to the State of Qatar
2. Proof of accommodation (Hotel booking or Hosted by Family and Friends)
3. Return flight ticket
4. Hayya E-Visa

## Am I able to extend my E-Visa?

If you would like to extend your E-Visa prior to its expiry, you will need to proceed to the Directorate of Passport at Hamad Internation Airport, where all visa extensions are processed, and apply there for an extension.

## If I choose to book a hotel as my accommodation, does it have to cover the entire duration of my stay?

Yes, your booked accommodation should align with the dates on your flight ticket, covering your entire stay from arrival to departure without any gaps. If you decide to extend your stay, you should rebook or find an alternative accommodation.

## I have a Hayya E-Visa, but I have just changed my passport. Can I update my passport number?

If you have renewed or replaced your passport after receiving the Hayya E-Visa, it is imperative to update your Hayya application with the new passport details and upload a copy. An updated e-visa will then be issued and sent to you. Please ensure that the information on your e-visa aligns with the details on your new passport to avoid being denied boarding or entry. Therefore, we recommend traveling with your new passport and updated e-visa to maintain consistency in your documentation.

## Can I work in Qatar while on a Hayya E-Visa?

A visitor on Hayya E-Visa is not permitted to work in the State of Qatar by law.

## If my application gets rejected, will I be able to know the reason?

In case of rejection, your application will display the reason under your dashboard. In certain cases, the reason for rejection may not be provided. Please note that the Hayya E-Visa approval or rejection is granted at the discretion of the authorities.

## How can I contact Hayya for support?

For support, you can contact Hayya by dialing 2022 locally or +974 4441 2022 internationally. You can also email info@hayya.qa.

## If I already have an approved Hayya E-Visa, will I still need a proof of accommodation at the time of travel?

Yes, we recommend that you have a confirmed accommodation booking or be hosted through the "Host Family & Friends" accommodation option. This can be checked at any point during your travel. Please refer to your Hayya E-Visa approval email for more information.

## What are the accommodation options that Hayya offers?

Currently, you have two accommodation options:

1. Host Family and Friends: where your host needs to apply to host you inside his/her owned or rented property (Please refer to the section "Hosting Family & Friends / Guest & Host" for more details).
2. Booked Accommodation: where the guest books accommodation and uploads the details.

Please note that the hotel booking through Discover Qatar requires initial approval, and accommodation requirements may vary based on your Hayya E-Visa type.

## When should I book my flight?

You should consider booking your flight after getting your Hayya application approved, receiving your E-Visa, and meeting all the other application requirements. Any losses an applicant may incur due to traveling prior to the Hayya E-Visa issuance will be at their sole responsibility.

## My application is pending. Can I travel to Qatar?

To travel or enter the State of Qatar, your application must be fully approved. Once approved, you will be issued a Single-Entry E-Visa, which will be sent to your registered email address. Without it, you will not be allowed to enter into Qatar.

## Can I update or edit my application after receiving approval and Hayya E-Visa?

Yes, you can update or edit your approved Hayya application as long as the "Last Entry Date" on your Hayya E-Visa has not passed and you are not currently inside the State of Qatar. However, no changes can be made once you have entered Qatar. While updates are free of charge, modifying your application will revert its status to "Pending," and it will be subject to the approval process again. Please also be aware that any changes might require you to update your accommodation booking at your own cost.

## Is the Hayya printed card still available and can it be used it to enter Qatar?

The printed Hayya Card is no longer available. The Hayya E-Visa is the official document for entry to the State of Qatar. The printed HAYYA Card cannot be used to enter the State of Qatar.

## Is there a Hayya E-Visa fee, and is it refundable?

Visa charges may apply. The E-Visa fee is QAR 100, which is non-refundable. Please note that this fee is non-refundable regardless of your travel status or arrangements. The payment should be done within 14 days from the time the status is changed to "Pending Payment". Otherwise, the application will be cancelled due to non-payment

## Can I apply for a Hayya Visit Visa?

Yes, applicants can apply for any of the available Hayya Visit types (Single-Entry):

1. Tourist Visa - (A1)
2. GCC Resident Visa (A2)
3. Visa with ETA (A3)
4. Visa for Companion of GCC Citizen (A4)
5. Visa-Free for US Citizens (F1)

Please make sure to review the eligibility criteria for each visa type before submitting your application.

## What’s the difference between Hayya Business and Hayya Personal?

Hayya Business is designed for governmental and semi-governmental organizations, providing tailored solutions for large-scale events and visa services. Unlike Hayya Personal, which is for individual visitors, Hayya Business provides specialized services. For more information about these services, please visit: https://business.hayya.qa/en.

Our experts will guide you through the onboarding process, evaluate your business or event needs, and offer a customized solution tailored to your requirements.

The onboarding process takes up to 5 business days after submitting the completed application for the Group Visas and Accreditations Service (GVA). For other services, the timeline may vary depending on the assessment of your requirements.

We provide clear milestones and regular updates throughout the process. You will also have direct access to our stakeholder management team, who will keep you informed and address any concerns along the way.

## How do I get support after onboarding?

After onboarding, you’ll have access to Hayya’s support services. Our team is available to assist with any operational questions or issues, either through remote assistance or on-site support, depending on your needs.

## Can an organization be issued two or more types of E-visas?

Yes, an organization can be issued multiple types of E-visas, depending on the approvals granted by the relevant authorities. For instance, if an organization engages in various activities, such as hosting events, business meetings, and tourism operations, they may qualify for different visas based on the nature of these activities and the corresponding approvals received.

## What are the different types of visas and what is the eligibility for each?

The available visa types are:

**Official Visa:**

The Hayya Official Visa is intended for governmental or semi-governmental organizations inviting individuals for official or business purposes, such as meetings, conferences, or business-related activities. This visa is available exclusively to these organizations and requires approval from the relevant authorities for eligibility.

**Validity:** 60 Days

**Entry Type:** Single Entry

**Eligibility**: Governmental or semi-governmental organizations.

**Conference & Events Visa (CNE):**

The Conference & Events Visa is designed for organizations hosting or organizing events such as international conferences, tournaments, and exhibitions. This visa allows both workforce members and visitors to enter the country for the duration of the event . It is available for governmental and semi-governmental entities, as well as private sector organizations with the required permit from the relevant authority to host or organize the event.

**Validity:** Customized based on the event.

**Entry Type:** Multiple Entry

**Eligibility:** Governmental, Semi-governmental organizations or private sector organizations with the required permit from the relevant authority to host or organize the event

**Transit Visa:**

The Hayya Transit Visa is a short-term visa tailored specifically for travelers passing through Qatar for brief stays or layovers. It allows visitors to explore the country during their transit period. Currently, this visa is exclusively available through the aviation industry for transit passengers.

**Validity:** 4 Days

**Entry Type:** Single entry

**Eligibility:** Currently, this visa is exclusively available through the aviation industry for transit passengers.

**Tourist Visas (A1, A2, A3 & F1):**

These visas are available to organizations engaged with tourism operations, allowing individuals to enter Qatar for tourism-related purposes. Eligibility is restricted to those governmental and semi- governmental organizations associated with recognized tourism operations. For more information on the different types of tourist visas, please visit hayya.qa . **Validity:** 30 Days

**Entry Type:** Single Entry

**Eligibility:** Restricted to those governmental and semi- governmental organizations associated with **recognized** tourism operations.

**Are there any specific requirements for the passport photo?**

The passport photo should meet the standard requirements set by the immigration authorities, including dimensions, background, colour, and facial expression. Ensure the photo adheres to the guidelines to avoid delays in processing.

**How long does it take to process each visa type?**
The processing time varies depending on the visa type and the volume of requests received. All visa applications are subject to review and approval by the relevant authorities, and there is no fixed SLA (Service Level Agreement) for processing. We recommend submitting applications well in advance to allow sufficient time for review.

## I am a private organization; can I avail of Hayya Business Services?

Currently, Hayya Business Services are only available to governmental and semi-governmental
organizations, unless you are authorized by a government entity to access these services.

## If I’m a private organization, can I apply for a Conference & Events Visa account?

Yes, you can apply as a private organization, provided you have a valid permit from the relevant governmental authority that authorizes you to host or organize the event or the conference.

## Who is eligible to apply for an Official Visa?

Official visas are granted only to governmental and semi-governmental organizations, and access is provided upon approval from the relevant authorities. This type of visa can be used exclusively for official purposes.

## What are the requirements for Tourist Visa (A1)?

1. Minimum of 3 months passport validity from the date of arrival to the State of Qatar
2. Proof of accommodation (Hotel booking or Hosted by Family and Friends)
3. Return flight ticket
4. Hayya E-Visa

## Do I have to provide a proof of accommodation for a Tourist Visa (A1)?

Yes, you are required to provide a proof of booked accommodation by uploading a copy of your booking and providing the hotel address or to be hosted by family and friends. In some cases, you may be exempted from providing a proof of accommodation or requested to have your booked accommodation through Discover Qatar (www.discoverqatar.qa).

## Can I bring or add family members (below 18)?

Yes, you can add dependents below 18 years old by applying for Hayya with their personal information on the app or portal and going through the travel requirements list.

## Who is eligible for the Tourist Visa (A1)?

The Hayya Tourist Visa (A1) is for anyone who wishes to travel to Qatar for tourism or visit friends and family.

## Can I still apply for a regular visit visa to enter Qatar, or do I have to get a Tourist Visa (A1)?

Yes, there are still multiple visa options available!
Whether you should apply for a regular visit visa issued by the Ministry of Interior, or a Hayya Tourist Visa (A1), depends on your profile and the purpose of your visit.

## Can I bring along my spouse or children with a Tourist Visa (A1)?

Each visitor shall have their own Hayya E-Visa. If you wish to bring along your spouse or children, please also have them apply. If your children are below 18 years old, you can add them as a dependent on your application.

## Is there an age limit for applying for a Tourist Visa (A1)?

There is no age limit for applying for a Tourist Visa (A1). As long as you meet the listed requirements, you can apply for the Tourist Visa (A1).

## How long can I stay in Qatar with my GCC Resident Visa (A2)?

A2 is single-entry visa that allows its holder to remain in Qatar for up to 30 days from the date of entry.

## What are the requirements for GCC Resident Visa (A2)?

- Minimum of 3 months passport validity from the date of arrival to the State of Qatar
- A GCC ID copy valid at least for three months from the date of arrival to the State of Qatar
- A return flight ticket (required for check-in for those travelling by air)
- Hayya E-Visa
- Fee of QAR 100

## Can I add family members below 18 with a GCC Resident Visa (A2)?

Yes, you can add dependents below 18 to your application and apply for the appropriate visa for them: Tourist Visa (A1) or Visa with ETA (A3) if the individual is not a GCC Resident. However, if the individual is a GCC resident, you can apply for a GCC Resident Visa (A2) for them.

## Can I bring along my spouse with a GCC Resident Visa (A2)?

Each GCC Resident applicant needs to apply for a separate visa. If you wish to bring along your spouse with a valid GCC residence permit, please have them apply as well.

## Is on-arrival visa still applicable for me if I’m a GCC resident?

Yes. On-arrival visas are still available for GCC residents.

## Can I bring along dependents with a GCC Resident Visa (A2)?

Yes, you can add your dependent to your application. If your dependent is not a GCC resident, then a separate Tourist Visa (A1) application must be submitted.

## Is my profession eligible for a GCC Resident Visa (A2)?

Once you start the application process, you will be able to select your job title from the drop-down list. The job title you select must be identical to the job title on your valid Residence Permit. In case you didn't find your job title listed, please select "others" and manually enter it.

## Am I eligible for a GCC Resident Visa (A2)?

If you are a GCC resident holding a valid GCC residence permit (valid for 3 months from the date of the application), you can apply for a GCC Resident Visa. You should be traveling to Qatar from your country of residency.

## Will the validity of my UK, Schengen or US Visa or Residency affect my application? Can I enter Qatar if it has expired?

To apply for a Visa with ETA, your US, UK, Canadian, Schengen, Australian or New Zealand visa or residency must be valid when you receive your E-Visa. Your passport should have a 3 months validity from your date of arrival in Qatar.

## Can I bring along family members with a Visa with ETA (A3)?

Yes. If they are below 18, you can either add them as dependents to your application or apply for a **Tourist Visa (A1)**, **GCC Resident Visa (A2)**, or **Visa with ETA (A3)** for them. However, if they are above 18, they must apply for a Hayya E-Visa separately based on their profile.

## What are the requirements of a Visa with ETA (A3)?

1. A valid visa or residency for the US, UK, Canada, Schengen, Australia, or New Zealand at the time of the application
2. Return flight ticket
3. Fee of QAR 100

## Am I eligible for a Visa with ETA (A3)?

If you hold a valid visa or residency of the US, the UK, Canada, Schengen, Australia, or New Zealand, you can apply for a Visa with ETA (A3).

## What are the requirements for Visa for Companion of GCC Citizen (A4)?

- A valid passport at the time of applying for the companion
- A minimum of 3-month passport validity from the date of arrival in the State of Qatar
- A GCC ID copy valid at least for three months from the date of arrival to the State of Qatar
- A return flight ticket (required for check-in for those travelling by air)
- As a GCC citizen applying for **Visa for Companion of GCC Citizen** **(** A4 **)**, please note that your companion must accompany you when you (the sponsor) travel to Qatar, as they will not be allowed to enter the State of Qatar alone.
- Fee of QAR 100

## Can I bring more than one companion with me?

There is no limit on how many companions you can bring. However, you will have to submit individual applications with supporting documents for each one of them.

## Does my companion require proof of accommodation?

No, they don't require a proof of accommodation since they are considered your companion. Please note that they are only authorized to travel with you and will not be allowed to enter the State of Qatar alone.

## What are the requirements for Visa for Companion of GCC Citizen (A4)?

1. A valid passport at the time of applying for the companion
2. A minimum of 3-month passport validity from the date of arrival in the State of Qatar
3. A return flight ticket (required for check-in for those travelling by air)
4. As a GCC citizen applying for **Visa for Companion of GCC Citizen** **(A4)**, please note that your companion must accompany you when you (the sponsor) travel to Qatar, as they will not be allowed to enter the State of Qatar alone.

## I am a GCC Citizen. How can I bring a companion with me to Qatar?

• Regardless of age, you’ll need to apply for your companion’s visa through a separate Hayya account.
• If your companion is traveling with you, we recommend applying for an A4 visa, as it does not require proof of accommodation. If not eligible, the A2 visa would be an alternative, which requires accommodation.

• Important Note: Your companion has two options for applying:
1\. They can create their own Hayya account and apply themselves.
2\. Or you, as a GCC citizen, can create an account using their details and apply on their behalf.

## Am I eligible for a Visa-Free for US Citizens (F1)?

If you hold the US nationality/citizenship, you can apply for a Visa-Free for US Citizens (F1).

## What are the requirements for a Visa-Free for US Citizens (F1)?

1. US Nationality/Citizenship at the time of the application
2. Accommodation details
3. A return flight ticket
4. A fee of QAR 76.

## Can I bring along family members with a Visa-Free for US Citizens (F1)?

Yes. If they are US citizens and below 18, then you can add them as dependents to your application and apply for a Visa-Free for US Citizens (F1) for them. However, if they are above 18, they must apply for a separate Visa-Free for US Citizens (F1).

## Do I have to provide a proof of accommodation for a Visa-Free for US Citizens (F1)?

No. However, if you are being hosted by a family member or friend, then they should provide all the required details to successfully host you.

## When can I travel to Qatar once my Visa-Free for US Citizens (F1) is approved?

US citizens may enter Qatar within 2 years from the date of their Visa-Free (F1) approval.

## How long can I stay in Qatar with my Visa-Free for US Citizens (F1)?

F1 is a multiple-entry visa. US citizens can stay for up to 90 days upon each visit to Qatar.

## Can I remove or add a property?

You can add only one property and remove a property only if your guest list is empty.

## Can family members be hosted?

Yes, the host can add family members as guests regardless of their age.

## I added a guest, but they did not receive approval for a Hayya E-Visa.

Each entry application is an individual case. Hosting a guest is not a guarantee for them to receive a Hayya E-Visa approval.

## Should my guest apply for a Hayya E-Visa before I add them or afterwards?

We recommend your guest applies for the Hayya E-Visa after you have added them to your "Family & Friends" list (invitees). However, if the guest applies for the Hayya E-Visa before you add them to the list, you can still add them easily, provided that the guest updates their residence information in their e-visa application accordingly.

## Can I remove an added guest?

You can still remove added guests from your "Family & Friends" list if their e-visa has expired or if they have already used their e-visa to enter and exit Qatar. However, if their e-visas are still valid or they are still present in the State of Qatar, you cannot remove them from the guest list.

## Who can sign up as a host under “Host Family & Friends” option?

Any Qatar resident with a valid QID and proof of address registered on the "Metrash" app can sign up as a host on Hayya and add 1 property. The maximum guest capacity that can be added to the registered property is 5.

## My conference or event has been cancelled or postponed. Is my Conference and Events Visa still valid?

In case of changes to the event, you must contact the event organizer who applied on your behalf for further details and support.

## What is the validity of a Conference and Events Visa?

The validity of the Conference and Event Visa depends on the duration of the event.

## What is the fee for the Conference and Events Visa?

The Conference and Events Visa is free of charge.

## What are the requirements for the Conference and Events Visa?

An individual will have to provide the following to their organizer:

1\. A personal Photo

2\. A passport with a minimum validity of three months from the date of arrival in the State of Qatar.

3\. Additional documents or information, if requested by the event organizer.

## Who is eligible for a Conference and Events Visa?

This visa is designed for invitees invited by event organizers to the State of Qatar for the purpose of attending or participating in a conference, event, or exhibition.
