Steps

Prepare Your Documents

Kindly visit the “Documents required at QVC “section in this website to ensure you bring along all the required documents mentioned to the location of Qatar Visa Centre at the time of your appointment.

Book appointment

Kindly visit the “Book appointment“ section of this website to book your appointment to visit the Qatar Visa Centre . The appointment letter will provide you details of your appointment, the Visa Service Center address, fees to be paid and other important guidelines of the process.

Guidelines

  • Allow approximately 2 hours for your medical test to take place
  • Bring all  the required documents and information to the Medical Center at QVC
  • Before your appointment, do not fast for your medical tests. You can eat and drink as usual
  • Bring prescription glasses or contact lenses if there are any
  • Bring any specialist’s report/s about your medical condition
  • If you have been previously diagnosed or treated for TB, please bring previous related records such as medical reports and x-rays
  • After you finish your biometric enrollment and in most normal cases, medical tests will take an estimated period of one hour. It is recommended to show up 15 minutes earlier before your scheduled appointment.
  • You are recommended not to bring along any personal belongings like valuable jewelry to the examination

  • Biometric visa fee is collected by Ministry of Interior, State of Qatar
  • The Biometric visa fees is not collected by SQ Services Ltd., the above published fee is for the information to the applicants

  • Value Added Fees such as Lounge Fees which is paid by applicant directly at QVC is collected by SQ Services Ltd. and is inclusive of VAT
  • All fees once paid are non-refundable, please refer to “Disclaimer & Privacy Policy” of the website for service conditions & further details
  • “The Qatar Visa Center is operated by Biomet Services Pte. Ltd., through a local operator SQ Services Ltd.”
  • Fees for Value Added Services such as Lounge, Insurance etc., can be made through UPI, Credit or Debit Card by the applicant directly at QVC center.
  • All payments made through UPI, credit or debit card will be charged in local currency.
  • All Value-Added Services are optional.
  • Applicants have an option to pay using any of the available mode of payments, when visiting the QVC in their country.
