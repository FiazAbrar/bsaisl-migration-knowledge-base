The Metrash app is Qatar's official Ministry of Interior (MOI) mobile application, giving citizens and residents convenient access to essential government services. From checking visa applications and renewing vehicle registration to managing residency information, reporting traffic accidents and obtaining official certificates, the app allows users to complete many procedures without visiting government service centres.

Introduced in December 2024, the updated Metrash app replaced the former Metrash2 application, which was discontinued in March 2025. This guide explains how to access Metrash, its main features, the services available under each category, and how residents can use the application to manage everyday government transactions in Qatar.

## What Is the Metrash App in Qatar?

Metrash is a digital government service application operated by Qatar's Ministry of Interior. It brings together a wide range of MOI services in one platform, allowing eligible users to submit applications, check records, request documents and manage official transactions.

The app covers several important service categories:

- Traffic: Vehicle registration, driving licences, ownership transfers and accident reports.

- Travel: Return permits, border pre-registration and travel-related inquiries.

- Residency: Residence permit information, passport updates and lost-document reports.

- Visa: Visa applications, amendments, cancellations and status inquiries.

- Delegation: Authorizing another person to perform specified tasks.

- Certificates: Requesting and accessing official government certificates.

- Some services depend on the user's residency status, sponsorship arrangements, vehicle ownership or other eligibility conditions.

## Who Can Use the Metrash App?

Metrash is primarily available to Qatari citizens and residents with a valid Qatar ID (QID).

Users generally need:

- A valid Qatar ID.

- An active mobile number suitable for MOI account verification.

- A compatible smartphone with internet access.

- Access to the mobile number registered for the relevant MOI services.

 

Access to individual services may depend on the user's identity, legal status and authorization.

## Traffic Services: Vehicle Registration, Driving Licences and Accident Reports

The Traffic section allows vehicle owners and drivers in Qatar to access important information and complete selected procedures electronically.

### Vehicle Registration and Ownership Services

Depending on eligibility, users can access services involving:

### How to Check Traffic Violations Through Metrash

- Open Metrash and sign in.

- Select the Traffic section.

- Open the traffic violations inquiry service.

- Choose the relevant vehicle or available identification option.

- Review the recorded violations and their details.

- If payment is available, follow the instructions and confirm the amount before completing the transaction.

 

Users should check whether a violation qualifies for any officially announced discount or special payment arrangement rather than assuming a reduction applies.

### How to Report a Minor Traffic Accident

- Metrash supports electronic accident-reporting procedures for eligible incidents.

- Ensure everyone involved is safe and move away from immediate danger where possible.

- Open the relevant traffic accident reporting service.

- Enter the requested vehicle and accident information.

- Take clear photographs of the damage and other details requested by the application.

- Review the information and submit the report.

- Follow any additional instructions issued by the Traffic Department.

 

Accidents involving injuries, serious danger or circumstances requiring immediate police assistance should be reported through emergency services rather than relying solely on an electronic submission.

## Travel Services: Return Permits and Border Procedures

The Travel section helps eligible residents manage travel-related procedures, including return permits and border services.

### Qatar Return Permit for Residents

Residents who remain outside Qatar for more than six months may require a return permit, subject to their residency status and applicable immigration rules.

Where the service is available to the relevant sponsor or resident, a return permit request can be initiated through Metrash.

Applicants should check the validity of their Qatar ID, passport and residence status before arranging travel. A return permit does not automatically resolve an expired or cancelled residence permit.

### Abu Samra Border Pre-Registration

Travellers entering or leaving Qatar through the Abu Samra land border can use the available pre-registration services to prepare travel and vehicle information.

Pre-registration may help streamline border procedures, but it does not replace passport checks, immigration requirements, vehicle documentation or any required permission to enter another country.

Travellers should confirm the applicable Saudi Arabia entry requirements before departure.

### Travel Inquiries

The app also provides access to selected travel-related records and inquiries, including information relevant to sponsored individuals and applicable travel restrictions.

## Residency Services: Qatar ID, Passport Updates and Lost Documents

The Residency section is particularly useful for expatriates and sponsors who need to manage residence permits and related records.

### Qatar ID and Residence Permit Services

Available functions can include residence permit inquiries, renewal-related procedures, passport information updates and applications requiring MOI approval.

### How to Submit a Residence Permit Application

Qatar's Hukoomi government portal documents the following procedure for certain residence permit applications requiring approval from the Expatriate Affairs Department:

- Log in to Metrash.

- Select Residency.

- Open Residency Procedures.

- Choose Residence Permit.

- Select the applicable Personal or Company service.

- Choose the sponsored individual.

- Review and submit the application.

 

This particular application service is listed as free of charge, although other residence permit issuance or renewal transactions may carry separate fees.

### Updating Passport Information

Residents who renew their passports may need to update the corresponding details in MOI records.

Where the passport update service is available, users can follow the instructions in the Residency section and provide the required information.

Make sure the passport number, nationality, expiry date and personal details match the new document.

### Reporting a Lost or Damaged Qatar ID

Metrash provides access to relevant lost-document and replacement services, depending on the user's status and the document involved.

Users should report lost documents promptly and follow MOI instructions regarding replacement, identity verification and any applicable charges.

- Checking visa application status.

- Managing applications where amendments or cancellations are permitted.

- Accessing visa inquiries and printing services.

### Qatar Visa Center Services

Certain recruitment and immigration procedures may involve the Qatar Visa Center (QVC), including employment-related documentation, medical examinations and biometric appointments.

The precise services available through Metrash depend on the employer's authorization and the applicant's circumstances.

Some procedures can be managed electronically, while medical examinations and biometric collection may still require attendance at an authorized centre.

## Delegation: How to Authorize Another Person

One of the important features of the updated Metrash application is the ability to authorize another person to complete specified government procedures.

The MOI introduced personal and family authorization features with the redesigned application.

 

### Personal and Family Authorization

Personal authorization allows eligible users to assign particular tasks to another person within a specified period.

Family authorization enables eligible relatives to assist with selected services, subject to the available relationship and authorization requirements.

### Vehicle Delegation

Vehicle owners can also access applicable authorization services involving the use, transportation or release of vehicles.

Where permitted, an owner may authorize another person to receive a vehicle after its release from impoundment.

### How to Use Delegation Services

- Sign in to Metrash.

- Open the Delegation or Authorization section.

- Select the relevant authorization category.

- Enter the authorized person's requested details.

- Choose the permitted services and authorization period.

- Review and confirm the request.

 

Users should grant only the permissions necessary for the intended transaction and review or revoke authorizations when they are no longer required.

## Common Metrash Problems and How to Resolve Them

Registration or Verification Problems

- If account activation fails, check that the Qatar ID details and registered mobile number are correct. Follow the verification instructions and use official MOI support channels if the problem continues.

Service Not Appearing

- Not every service is available to every user. Certain functions require sponsorship authority, vehicle ownership or another specific eligibility condition.

- Check the appropriate service category and confirm that the application is updated.

Payment or Application Failure

- Avoid repeatedly submitting payments without checking whether the previous transaction was recorded.

- Review the application's transaction history or relevant inquiry service before trying again.

Incorrect Personal Information

- If passport, residency or vehicle information is inaccurate, use the available update service or contact the competent MOI department.

- Do not submit conflicting information merely to complete an application.
