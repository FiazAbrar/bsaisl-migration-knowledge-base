Updates at QVC

Nepal

Qatar Visa Center, Nepal has started “Express Service ” from 06th Apr 2025. Express Service at the Qatar Visa Center, Kathmandu is designed for patrons who wish to complete their biometric enrolment and medical test on priority. Please visit our Amenities section for more information.

Bangladesh

The center is now accepting appointments,

Appointments can be booked at Qatar Visa Center , Sylhet and the center operates Sunday to Thursday, between 8:30 am to 4:30 pm.

Information on appointment scheduling, requirements, steps and guidelines for the visit at the visa center can be accessed in the English, and the regional languages at www.qatarvisacenter.com

Multi-lingual information services support is also available through dedicated email info.bgd@qatarvisacenter.com or by calling the helpline number +880 9666 777 101.


Qatar Visa Centers in India, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Nepal and Philippines

W.e.f from 9th of September 2023, Qatar Visa Centers will now only accept passports with more than 195 days of validity / date of expiration for booking an appointment at the QVCs. Applicants are requested to renew their passports and inform their sponsors accordingly and then book an appointment by visiting the website www.qatarvisacenter.com.


Philippines

Qatar Visa Center, Manila has started accepting “Domestic Worker” Visa Category from 17th November 2022. These category applicants must enroll their biometrics, sign their work contract, and undergo medical tests as mandated by Ministry of Interior, The State of Qatar.


India

Applicants/sponsors are hereby notified that as per the new regulations from the MOI, Qatar all Indian passports to be processed correctly at QVC should consist of minimum 3 Names – 1. First or Given Name, 2. Father’s Name, and 3. Family or Last Name.
