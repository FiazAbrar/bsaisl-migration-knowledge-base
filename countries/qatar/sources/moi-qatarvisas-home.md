A valid health insurance policy from [insurance companies](https://www.moph.gov.qa/english/derpartments/policyaffairs/hfid/hirs/insurancecompanies/Pages/default.aspx) registered with the Ministry of Public Health is required to enter Qatar.

Visitors coming to Qatar by car via the Abu Samra Border are required to complete the required insurance policies electronically ([Qatar Unified Bureau](https://online.qubinsurance.com/)). They can also [pre-register](https://move.hayya.qa/ar/trip) through Hayya.
