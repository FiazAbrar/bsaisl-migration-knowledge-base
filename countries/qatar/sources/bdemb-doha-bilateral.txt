Bangladesh–Qatar Bilateral Relations
Bangladesh and the State of Qatar enjoy excellent and brotherly relations founded on mutual respect, shared values, and strong people-to-people connectivity. Almost half a million Bangladeshi nationals residing in Qatar form the backbone of these relations, contributing significantly to Qatar’s development while strengthening bilateral ties.
Diplomatic relations were established in 1974. Bangladesh opened its Embassy in Doha in 1975, followed by Qatar’s Embassy in Dhaka in 1982. In recent years, bilateral relations have gained renewed momentum, particularly following the State Visit of His Highness the Amir of Qatar to Bangladesh in April 2024.
Cooperation between the two countries spans a wide range of areas, including manpower, trade and investment, energy, defence, education, culture, health, and humanitarian assistance. Qatar has been a key destination for Bangladeshi workers since 1976. In trade, Bangladesh exports food products, ready-made garments, pharmaceuticals, and agricultural items, while Qatar is a major supplier of liquefied natural gas (LNG) and urea fertilizer to Bangladesh. Long-term LNG agreements signed in 2017 and 2023 will ensure energy supply to Bangladesh from 2026 for a period of 15 years.
Trade and investment relations have been further strengthened through high-level business engagements, investment summits, and exhibitions organized in Doha, enhancing awareness among Qatari investors about Bangladesh’s growing economic potential.
Defence cooperation has expanded steadily with the establishment of Bangladesh’s Defence Wing in Doha in 2021 and the signing of several defence agreements. A landmark agreement in 2025 opened new avenues for the deployment of Bangladeshi personnel to the Qatar Armed Forces, further elevating defence ties.
Cultural relations between Bangladesh and Qatar remain vibrant, marked by regular participation in art events and the organization of cultural festivals. A bilateral agreement on cultural cooperation was signed in 2023. Collaboration in education, health, and humanitarian fields continues to grow, in line with Qatar National Vision 2030 and Bangladesh’s development priorities.
Overall, Bangladesh and Qatar continue to deepen their partnership with a shared commitment to peace, development, and mutual prosperity.
