Metrash Application: Metrash Application offer Citizens & Residents of Qatar to access more than (400) services provided by MOI through different mobile devices. Services like Traffic, Citizens, Resident, Visa holders etc. Metrash is free application to use for individuals that provides them with secure and encrypted services, Available in several languages: Arabic, English, French, Spanish, Hindi and Urdu. Metrash able the users to access E-wallet to reach for the official documents. In quick easy steps user will be able to register through Metrash.
