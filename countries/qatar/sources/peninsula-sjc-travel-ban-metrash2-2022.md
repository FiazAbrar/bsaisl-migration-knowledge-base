# Check travel ban order through Metrash2: Supreme Judiciary Council

Published: 08 Mar 2022 - 12:19 pm \| Last Updated: 08 Mar 2022 - 12:24 pm

![Peninsula](/get/maximage/20220308_1646731299-639.jpg?1646731299)

##### The Peninsula Online

Doha: The Supreme Judiciary Council announced that citizens and residents can review the circulars of travel ban orders online before leaving the country.

On its official Twitter account, the Council explained that "Citizens and residents can, through Metrash2 application, view the circulars of travel ban orders before leaving the country.”

Citizens and residents can also inquire about travel ban order through the following numbers: 55788372, 44597769, and 44597777.
