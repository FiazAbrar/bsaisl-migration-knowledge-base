# Inquire about the Expiry Date of Official Documents

Provided by Ministry of Interior

Service Type

E-Service

Last updated

11 Jan 2021

## Service Description

Individuals may use this service to inquire about the expiry date of official documents, including QID, passport, residence permit, and driving license.

## Steps

- Select "ID Number" then enter it in its respective field, or select "Passport Number" then enter it in the allotted field and select the nationality.
- Enter verification code, then click on "Search".

## Fees

No fees are required for this service.

## Additional Information

For more information about the services provided by the Ministry of Interior (MOI), please visit MOI website (https://portal.moi.gov.qa/wps/portal).
