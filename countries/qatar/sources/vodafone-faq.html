    Digital Vouchers

    1. What is a digital voucher?
      A digital voucher is an electronic voucher that can be purchased through Vodafone Qatar and redeemed with the designated merchant for eligible products or services.

    2. How will I receive my digital voucher?
      Your voucher will be delivered electronically through My Vodaone App, after your payment has been successfully processed and any required verification has been completed. Delivery times may vary depending on system processing and merchant requirements.

    3. Where can I use my digital voucher?
      Digital vouchers can only be redeemed with the merchant specified at the time of purchase and must be used in accordance with that merchant's redemption instructions and terms.

    4. Can I use my voucher more than once?
      Unless otherwise stated by the merchant, each digital voucher may only be redeemed once.

    5. How long is my voucher valid?
      The validity period is displayed before you complete your purchase[PK5.1][AE5.2]. Please ensure you redeem your voucher before its expiry date, as expired vouchers cannot be renewed, extended, or refunded unless required by applicable law.

    6. Can I cancel my purchase or get a refund?
      No. All digital voucher purchases and successfully delivered are final. Once a voucher has been issued, it cannot be cancelled, refunded, exchanged, transferred, or redeemed for cash, except where required by applicable law.

    7. Can I transfer or sell my voucher?
      Digital vouchers are intended for personal use only and may not be resold or used for commercial purposes.

    8. What should I do if I accidentally delete or lose my voucher?
      Vodafone Qatar will make reasonable efforts to assist where possible where customer can refer to My Voucher section on My Vodafone App to view earlier purchased vouchers and voucher codes; however, Vodafone Qatar is not responsible for lost, stolen, deleted, disclosed, or unauthorized use of voucher codes after successful delivery.

    9. What happens if my payment is successful but I do not receive my voucher?
      If your voucher is not delivered due to a technical issue or system failure, Vodafone Qatar will investigate the issue and, where appropriate, reissue the voucher or process a refund.

    10. Who should I contact if my voucher does not work?
      Any issues relating to the voucher, redemption, or the merchant's products or services must be addressed directly with the respective merchant. Vodafone[PK6.1][AE6.2] Qatar will assist customers with complaints relating to the purchase, delivery or redemption of digital vouchers purchased through Vodafone Qatar channels. Where required by applicable law or regulation, Vodafone Qatar will investigate and address customer complaints and facilitate an appropriate resolution. However, if a complaint is not resolved satisfactorily, customers may contact Vodafone Qatar Customer Care through Vodafone Qatar's official customer service channels.

    11. What if the merchant refuses to accept my voucher?
      Please contact the merchant first to verify the redemption conditions. If you believe the issue is unrelated to the merchant's redemption policy, you may contact Vodafone Qatar Customer Care for assistance.

    12. Can a merchant change the voucher terms after I purchase it?
      Merchants may modify or discontinue the acceptance of digital vouchers in accordance with their own policies but will honor all paid and delivered vouchers. Vodafone Qatar is not responsible for changes made by the merchant.

    13. Are there limits on how many vouchers I can purchase?
      Yes. Vodafone Qatar may apply daily, weekly, or monthly purchase limits [PK7.1][AE7.2]and reserves the right to decline transactions that exceed those limits.

    14. Why was my transaction declined or cancelled?
      Transactions may be declined, delayed, suspended, or cancelled for several reasons, including payment verification requirements, suspected fraud, purchase limit restrictions, pricing errors, technical issues, or system malfunctions.

    15. What happens if there is a pricing or technical error?
      Vodafone Qatar reserves the right to cancel or reverse transactions resulting from pricing errors, technical errors, or system malfunctions. Where applicable, any amount paid will be refunded.

    16. Does Vodafone Qatar guarantee the merchant's products or services?
      No. Vodafone Qatar only distributes digital vouchers and is not responsible for the quality, availability, or performance of the merchant's products or services

    17. Can Vodafone Qatar change these Terms & Conditions?
      Yes. Vodafone Qatar may update these Terms & Conditions from time to time. The latest version will be available through Vodafone Qatar's official channels and will take effect upon publication unless otherwise stated.

    18. Who can I contact if I need help?
      For questions relating to your purchase or voucher delivery, please contact Vodafone Qatar Customer Care. For questions relating to voucher redemption or the merchant's products or services, please contact the respective merchant directly.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    1. What is the Instant SIM?

    The Instant SIM is an innovative product by Vodafone Qatar that allows customers to self-activate a new Prepaid or Postpaid connection instantly, anywhere and anytime in Qatar, without needing internet access.

    1. How does the Instant SIM work?

    To activate the Instant SIM, customers need to:

    1.      Insert the SIM into a smartphone with a camera.
    2.      Scan the QR code on the SIM pack or visit www.vodafone.qa/activate
    3.      Choose their line type: Prepaid or Postpaid.
    4.      Provide the necessary eKYC documentation (Passport for Prepaid or Qatar ID for Postpaid).
    5.      Follow the online steps to activate the SIM instantly.
    1. What makes the Instant SIM unique?

    The Instant SIM is unique because:

    - It allows customers to self-activate without needing Wi-Fi or any internet connection.

    - There’s no need to wait in queues or visit a Vodafone store.

    - Customers can choose between a Physical SIM or an eSIM.

    - It does not require a credit or debit card for activation.

    1. Do I need an internet connection to activate the Instant SIM?

    No, an internet connection is not required to activate the Instant SIM. As long as the Instant SIM is inserted in the phone and data is switched on, the process is designed to work with special dedicated data for the process of eKYC.

    1. Can I choose between a Physical SIM and an eSIM?

    Yes, customers have the option to choose between a Physical SIM and an eSIM when using the Instant SIM.

    1. What happens to the Physical Instant SIM if I choose eSIM?

    The physical SIM will no longer be valid

    1. What documents do I need to activate my Instant SIM?

    For a Prepaid connection, you will need your Passport or a valid Qatari ID. For a Postpaid connection, you will need your Qatar ID. These documents are required for the eKYC (electronic Know Your Customer) process during activation.

    1. How long does it take to activate the Instant SIM?

    The activation process is instant and can be completed within a few seconds, depending on how quickly you can follow the steps. However in case of prepaid it may take up to 2 minutes and for Postpaid activation it can take up to 15 minutes after your complete activation steps.

    1. Can I use the Instant SIM with any smartphone?

    Yes, the Instant SIM can be used with any smartphone that has a camera for scanning the QR code and supports SIM or eSIM functionality.

    1. What happens if I encounter an issue during activation?

    If you encounter any issues during activation, you can visit a Vodafone store to continue with the activation. In that case, you will be provided with a new SIM free of charge and activation will be performed by one of our sales agents.

    1. Is the Instant SIM available only in Qatar?

    Yes, the Instant SIM is currently available exclusively in Qatar, and it is the first of its kind in the GCC region.

    1. Can I activate the Instant SIM for both Prepaid and Postpaid services?

    Yes, the Instant SIM can be activated for either Prepaid or Postpaid services, depending on your preference.

    1. What are the benefits of using the Instant SIM compared to traditional SIM activation methods?

    The benefits include:

    - Instant activation without needing internet access.

    - No need to visit a store or wait in queues.

    - Flexibility to choose between a Physical SIM or an eSIM.

    - No requirement for a credit or debit card.

    - Convenience of purchasing from various retail locations or ordering online.

    1. Can I choose a number during activation

    For Prepaid activation customer is assigned automatically a free number.

    In case of postpaid, customer is can choose a Free Number or a star number which comes for free with the plan or can select a higher value star number. However in that case, customer will need to make a payment for the star number for which customer will need to connect to a WiFi network.

    1. Can I buy multiple Instant SIMs at once?

    Yes, you can purchase multiple Instant SIMs however remember you can not have more 2 postpaid and a maximum for 5 connections under your ID.

    1. Is there a validity period for the Instant SIM after purchase?

    While the SIM itself may not expire immediately, it’s recommended to activate the SIM soon after purchase to ensure it functions as intended. Specific validity terms may apply.

    1. How do I know if my activation was successful?

    After completing the activation process, you will receive a confirmation message on your smartphone, an SMS and an email indicating that your line is active and ready for use.

    1. What should I do if I lose my Instant SIM before activation?

    If you lose your Instant SIM before activation, you will need to purchase a new one. It’s essential to keep your SIM card secure until activation is complete.

    1. Can the Instant SIM be used for international roaming?

    Yes, once activated and used locally, the Instant SIM can be used for international roaming. However you may need to activate a roaming add-on after activation to use Voice and Data services.

    1. What should I do if my smartphone doesn’t support eSIM?

    If your smartphone doesn’t support eSIM, you can opt for the Physical SIM version of the Instant SIM during the activation process.

    1. Can I transfer my existing Vodafone number to an Instant SIM?

    Transferring an existing Vodafone number to an Instant SIM requires additional steps. It’s recommended to contact Vodafone customer support for detailed instructions.

    1. In case of Prepaid activation; can I recharge online after activation?

    After the activation, Vodafone gives you free data to make an online recharge and pay for it using your debit or credit card?

    1. In case of Prepaid activation; can I recharge from any retail store?

    Yes, you can Vodafone’s prepaid cards or request a digital voucher from more than 2000 locations in Qatar.

    1. Can I activate the Instant SIM on MyVodafone App

    Yes, if you already have the MyVodafone App downloaded and have internet access, you can continue with Self Activation on MyVodafone App. However, remember that you can continue your activation on Web even if you don’t have internet access.

    1. Can I recharge with any product or only selected products will work on Instant SIM

    Yes, you can recharge with any existing Prepaid recharge product. However, we do recommend 6 products as part of the activation journey which are:

    -          Combo 40

    -          Combo 65

    -          Combo 100

    -          Credit 20

    -          Combo 15

    -          Ahlan 65

    1. Is there an Offer for Postpaid customers

    Yes, customers will continue to get existing Digital offer for these Postpaid plans

    1. Can I still activate a Postpaid connection from Website?

    Yes, you can activate a Postpaid connection on eSIM or Instant SIM (if you have one) once you start the purchase journey from Vodafone website

    In case of Postpaid, customers have the option to request Home delivery of the SIMs?

    1. Can I request Home delivery of a SIM through online activation of prepaid plan?

    No, the home delivery is only available for Postpaid customers.

    1. Can I start self-activation without having an Instant SIM?

    Yes, you can visit Vodafone website or MyVodafone App to start purchasing a Prepaid or Postpaid connection. You can activate an eSIM for Prepaid or Postpaid connection or you can request home delivery of SIM for a Postpaid connection.

    1. Do I need to do anything extra to start using my Instant SIM after activation?

    Prepaid customers, will need to recharge to have the allowances to make calls or use internet. They can start receiving calls immediately.

    Postpaid customers, will need to “restart the phone” or “Switch on and Switch off airplane mode” to be able to use the line for the first time.

    Vodafone Ahli Plans

    1. What are Vodafone Ahli Plans?

      Vodafone Ahli Plans are postpaid family plans that let customers manage multiple mobile lines under one tariff plan and one monthly bill.

    2. How many primary lines are included with Ahli Plans?

      Each Ahli Plan includes two primary lines as part of the monthly plan fee.

    3. Can I add more primary lines to my Ahli Plan?

      Yes. Additional primary lines can be added with Ahli Plus at QR 199 per line per month, up to a maximum of two additional primary lines, at the applicable monthly charge.

    4. What is the full-speed local data allowance for each Ahli Plan?
      • Ahli includes 50 GB
      • Ahli Pro includes 125 GB
      • Ahli Premium includes 250 GB
      • Ahli Platinum includes 500 GB
    5. What happens after I use my full-speed data allowance?

      After the full-speed allowance is used, data continues at a reduced speed:

      • Up to 1 Mbps on Ahli
      • Up to 10 Mbps on Ahli Pro
      • Up to 15 Mbps on Ahli Premium and Ahli Platinum
    6. Are local calls included in Ahli Plans?

      Yes. Local calls are unlimited for included primary lines.

    7. Are international minutes included?

      Yes. International minutes are included depending on the selected plan: Ahli includes up to 300 country-specific minutes, Ahli Pro includes 150 minutes, Ahli Premium includes 300 minutes and Ahli Platinum includes 400 minutes.

    8. Do Ahli Plans include roaming benefits?

      Yes. Selected Ahli Plans include roaming benefits. Ahli Pro includes 10 GB global roaming data, Ahli Premium includes GCC roaming benefits plus 15 GB global roaming data and 100 global roaming minutes, and Ahli Platinum includes roaming benefits in GCC and 30 eligible countries, plus 30 GB global roaming data and 150 global roaming minutes.

    9. What is Call+?

      Call+ is a service that allows eligible customers to make unlimited roaming minutes to the world through the MyVodafone App only, subject to a fair usage policy of 1,000 minutes where applicable.

    10. Can I add more lines to the Ahli Plans other than Ahli Plus?

      Yes. Customers can add Ahli add-on plans, including Ahli Lite and Ahli Net, for an additional monthly charge on top of the main Ahli Plan fee.

    11. What is included in Ahli Lite?

      Ahli Lite is ideal for kids and other members in the family who generally would use a prepaid line. It includes 10 GB local data, endless data @ 256 Kbps after the included allowance, 200 local minutes and free calls to other Ahli lines.

    12. What is included in Ahli Net?

      Ahli Net includes 75 GB local data and unlimited data @ 256 Kbps after the included allowance. It does not include local minutes.

    13. Do Ahli Plans include lifestyle benefits?

      Yes. Depending on the selected plan, customers may receive benefits such as Vodafone MyBook, Vodafone World benefit, Bonus iPoints, star number value, Family Hub features and valet parking.

    14. Who is eligible for valet parking benefits?

      New Ahli Premium customers receive four regular valet parking usages per month, while new Ahli Platinum customers receive four VIP valet parking usages per month, subject to eligibility and venue availability.

    15. Do unused Ahli allowances carry over to next month?

      No. Ahli Plan allowances refresh at the start of each billing cycle, and unused allowances do not carry over.

    16. What is Family Hub?

      Family Hub is a dedicated place in the My Vodafone App, designed to allow you to manage your family’s mobile lines more easily. In one place, you can check the bills, usages and balances, set spending limits, and buy or approve add-ons.

    17. Who controls the Family Hub?

      The Main Line Owner controls Family Hub by default and manages all linked members, benefits, and permissions.

    18. Can Family Hub control be delegated to another family member?

      Yes. The Main Line Owner can assign Family Hub administration rights to a linked member while maintaining ownership of the account.

    19. If a linked member's mobile number is registered under a different QID, who controls the Family Plan account?

      The Main Line Owner maintains full ownership and control of the Family Plan account regardless of the QID under which linked members' numbers are registered.

    20. Who can perform a SIM swap for a linked member?

      SIM swap requests require the presence of both the Main Line Owner and the linked member line owner at a Vodafone store.

    21. Who can perform a Transfer of Ownership (TON) for a linked member number?

      Transfer of Ownership (TON) requests require the presence of both the Main Line Owner and the linked member line owner at a Vodafone store.

    22. Who can disconnect a linked member's number?

      Disconnection requests can only be performed by the Main Line Owner.

    23. Can I add my line in Ahli plan while maintaining my QID link associated with the number?

      Yes. This is one of the innovative features of Ahli Plans. It allows a linked member to enjoy Ahli Plan benefits while continuing to use government apps, such as Metrash, on the same number linked to their QID.

    24. Does the Main Line Owner lose control if family members belong to different QIDs?

      No. The Main Line Owner continues to manage the Family Plan, benefits, and Family Hub settings regardless of the QID of linked members.

    25. Does Ahli Plus include all the same benefits as the main Ahli Plan?

      Ahli Plus includes the same local and roaming benefits as the main Ahli Plan, but excludes additional iPoints Welcome Bonus, Vodafone World benefits and valet parking benefits.

    26. Is Park Your Number available with Ahli Plans?

      No. Park Your Number is not available with Ahli Plans.

    27. How can someone be added to an Ahli Family Plan?

      Eligible family lines can be added or linked to the Ahli Family Plan through Vodafone retail stores, subject to the approved activation process and required consent or documentation.

      Yes. A family member can join the Ahli Family Plan while keeping their mobile number registered under their own QID, so they can continue using government applications linked to that number, such as Metrash.

    28. How can someone be removed from an Ahli Family Plan?

      The Main Line Owner can request to remove or unlink family lines from the Ahli Family Plan through Vodafone retail stores, subject to Vodafone’s approved process and applicable rules.

    29. Is eSIM swap available for Ahli Family Plan members through My Vodafone App?

      No. eSIM swap is not available for Ahli Family Plan members through the My Vodafone App. It must be handled through Vodafone retail stores where applicable.

    30. What happens if the Family Plan Owner’s QID is cancelled?

      If the Family Plan Owner’s QID is cancelled, Vodafone’s standard process applies, and all numbers under the account may be subject to the applicable account treatment and disconnection process.

    31. What happens if a Family Plan Member’s QID is cancelled?

      If a Family Plan Member’s QID is cancelled while their ID is linked to a Family Plan, the relevant team may unlink the QID according to the approved process.

    32. Do prorated charges apply when activating Ahli Plans?

      Yes. Prorated charges apply when activating the Ahli Family package.

    33. What is the lock-in period for Ahli Plans?

      A 3-month lock-in period applies to Ahli Plans. If both main lines are disconnected before the 3-month lock-in period is completed, the Main Line Owner must pay the applicable penalty calculated based on the remaining period.

    34. How does Vodafone World work with Ahli Plans?

      Vodafone World gives eligible Ahli customers monthly voucher benefits through the My Vodafone App. Ahli customers can receive up to QR 10 per line per month, Ahli Pro up to QR 20 per line per month, Ahli Premium up to QR 40 per line per month and Ahli Platinum up to QR 80 per line per month.

    35. Can Vodafone World benefits be changed during the billing period?

      Eligible customers can redeem one Vodafone World benefit per bill period. If they want to switch to another benefit, they must unsubscribe from the existing benefit and subscribe to another benefit before the bill renewal date. The newly selected benefit becomes available after the bill renewal date.

    36. Do Vodafone World benefits renew automatically?

      Yes. Unless the customer unsubscribes from the selected Vodafone World benefit, it will renew automatically after the bill renewal date.

    37. What are the rules for using valet parking benefits?

      Eligible Ahli Premium customers receive four regular valet parking usages per month, and eligible Ahli Platinum customers receive four VIP valet parking usages per month. Customers must use the Digital Valet Parking service through the My Vodafone App, and the barcode can only be used once per hour and cannot be shared with other users or cars.

    38. Is Multi-SIM available with Ahli Plans?

      No. Multi-SIM service is not available with Ahli Plans.

    39. Can Call+ be used in Qatar?

      No. Call+ is designed for use while roaming only and is accessed through the My Vodafone App.

    40. What does a customer need in order to use Call+ while roaming?

      The customer must be on an eligible Ahli Plan, must use the My Vodafone App, and must be connected to the internet through roaming data or Wi-Fi. If the customer has no internet connection, Call+ will not work.

    41. Will Call+ work if VPN is enabled?

      No. If a VPN connection is detected, Call+ will not work and the VPN should be disabled.

    42. Are Call+ minutes charged separately?

      No. Call+ minutes are not charged separately when used according to the plan rules, because they are covered under the eligible plan allowance. Standard roaming charges may apply if data or fair usage limits are exceeded.

    2. Who is eligible to use Call+?
      • New Unlimited+ Plans customers get Call+ free of charge.
      • Postpaid+ Plans customers can access Call+ by subscribing to the Call+ Add-on for QR 100/month.
    3. How do I activate Call+?
      • Call+ is only accessible through My Vodafone App (MVA).
      • New Unlimited+ customers get automatic access while roaming.

    Caller Name Presentation

    1. What is the Caller Name Presentation (CNAP)?
      The Caller Name Presentation (CNAP) is a feature that displays the name associated with a phone number when someone calls, similar to how the caller ID displays the number. CNAP is mandatory for all local corporate telephone numbers in Qatar as mandated by the Communication Regulatory Authority (CRA). This allows the recipient of a phone call to see the name of the calling organization, specifically when the call is made by a local corporate entity. This name appears on the recipient’s mobile screen before the call is answered. The feature aims to improve transparency by helping users identify callers and decide whether to answer the call, which can potentially reduce unwanted calls and help consumers avoid fraudulent or scam calls.

    2. What is the purpose of the CNAP initiative?
      The main objectives are to:
      • Combat scams and fraud calls
      • Enhance transparency by reducing the number of unwanted calls
      • Build trust between consumers and organizations
      • Improve the overall communication experience

    3. How does CNAP Work?
      The telecom provider maintains a database of subscriber names linked to corporate telephone numbers which is typically populated with information from the Customer Official Certificates issued by local authorities (e.g. Establishment Card, Company Registration Certificate, Commercial License etc.) submitted during registration and updated as needed. When a call is made, the system will fetch the caller’s registered name and display it on the recipient’s screen.

    4. What is the scope of CNAP?
      CNAP applies to calls that:
      • Originate from corporate fixed-lines and mobile numbers in Qatar.
      • Terminate to local mobile numbers within Qatar
      • Are made over VoLTE, 4G, 5G, or future advanced network technologies

    5. Who does CNAP apply to?
      CNAP applies to calls originating from corporate mobile and fixed line numbers of all legally registered local corporate entities operating in Qatar. These include:
      • Government ministries and agencies
      • Private sector companies across all industries
      • Non-profit organizations and associations
      • Educational and healthcare institutions
      • Banks and financial institutions

    6. How will the caller's name be displayed on my phone?
      When you receive a call from a registered local corporate entity, the caller’s name (limited to 25 English characters). The name displayed is based on the corporate entity’s official registration documents with the service providers.

    7. Will consumers be charged CNAP?
      No. CNAP is free of charge for both callers and recipients.

    8. What if the displayed caller name is incorrect or outdated?
      Corporate entities should ensure their registered information and official registration documents with the service provider are accurate and up to date. Discrepancies should be reported to the respective telecom providers.

    9. Does CNAP work while I’m traveling or roaming?
      No. The CNAP feature is only active for calls made and received within Qatar. It does not function while roaming outside Qatar.

    10. What happens if I have already saved the caller’s number on my phone?
      If the number is already saved in your contacts, the name you saved will appear on your screen instead of the CNAP-registered name. CNAP only displays the caller's name when the number is not saved on your device.

    11. What types of numbers are excluded from CNAP?
      CNAP does not apply to:
      • Personal or individual mobile numbers and residential fixed line numbers.
      • International or roaming calls
      • Residential-use fixed lines under business accounts
      • Non-local numbers

    12. Will CNAP work if I’m using a 2G or 3G network while receiving calls?
      No. CNAP only works on VoLTE, 4G, 5G, or advanced networks. It will not function on 2G or 3G networks when receiving calls.

    13. What happens if my device does not support CNAP?
      CNAP will not work on 2G and 3G only devices. If your mobile device does not support CNAP, the caller’s name will not be displayed. You may need to update your device

    14. Is CNAP available for both on-net and off-net calls (Ooredoo to Vodafone and Vodafone to Ooredoo)?
      Yes. CNAP works for both on-net (same provider) and off-net (different provider) domestic calls within Qatar.

    15. What language will the caller’s name appear in?
      Caller names will appear in English only, using ASCII characters. Arabic or other scripts are not supported at this stage

    16. How long can the caller’s name be?
      The caller’s name is limited to a maximum of 25 English (ASCII) characters.

    17. What is the difference between Phase 1 and Phase 2 of CNAP?
      • Phase 1: Applies to all calls from corporate local fixed-line numbers across all sectors.
      • Phase 2: Expands to include calls from all corporate local mobile numbers, with an option for organizations to opt out specific numbers used for non-public calling purposes.

    18. Can organizations opt out of CNAP?
      Yes. In Phase 2 of the CNAP initiative, corporate entities will have the option to opt out specific mobile numbers, particularly those that are used for personal or non-official purposes and will not be used for calling the public.

    19. Can CNAP be used for internal calls within an organization (Internal phone extension to internal extension)?
      CNAP is designed for calls made over public telephony networks. Internal calls within an organization’s private network are not subject to CNAP, though internal configurations may vary by organization.

    20. Do corporate entities need to opt in to use CNAP?
      No. All legally registered corporate and government entities in Qatar are automatically enrolled in CNAP. This includes all their corporate fixed-line and mobile numbers. CNAP is a mandatory service and not optional. However, in Phase 2, organizations will have the ability to opt out specific mobile numbers that are used for personal or non-public calling purposes.

    21. What should I do if I need support or have issues with the displayed caller name?
      If a corporate entity notices that the displayed caller name is incorrect, outdated, not appearing as expected, or not work at all, they should contact their telecom service provider directly. The service provider is responsible for managing CNAP records and can assist with updating or correcting the registered name based on the updated official registration documents.

    22. How is CNAP different from third-party caller ID apps?
      Unlike third-party apps, CNAP relies on official subscriber data provided to the telecom operator as per official certificates issued by local authorities.

    23. Can I change the name that is displayed for my number?
      The name displayed through CNAP is based on the information provided during customer registration via official documents. Changes to this information will require updating your subscriber details with your Telecom operator as per updated official documents issued by local authorities.

      PayLater is Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) solution, allowing shoppers to split their payments for handset/devices (“Device”) into four equal monthly instalments which is interest free with participating merchants including Vodafone Qatar.       PayLater allows shoppers to make purchases upfront and divide the amount into four equal monthly instalments. At checkout, shoppers can select PayLater as their payment option.
