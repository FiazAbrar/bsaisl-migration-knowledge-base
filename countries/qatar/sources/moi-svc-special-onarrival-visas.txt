On Arrival Visas for the Citizens of Some Countries

Service Details

The visa is given for the citizens of following countries upon arrival in the airport.

Republic of Turkey

Republic of Belarus

Republic of Iran

Republic of Poland

Fees

			For fee details please check Terms & Conditions

Terms & Conditions

Republic of Turkey:

 It includes two types of visas:

    Business Visa.

    Tourist Visa.

Requirements:

    The applicant should hold a credit card with available limit of USD 1000 or its equivalent amount in cash.

    To hold two way ticket.

    The applicant should have a hotel reservation for the period of stay or he should have address of a relative living in the State.

    The visa is valid for two weeks and can be extended for maximum period of one month by paying extension fee of QR 100.

Republic of Belarus:

It includes two types of visas:

    Business Visa.

    Tourist Visa.

Requirements:

    The applicant should hold a credit card with available limit of USD 1000 or its equivalent amount in cash.

    To hold two way ticket.

    The applicant should have a hotel reservation for the period of stay or he should have address of a relative living in the State.

    The validity of tourist visa is one month and it is non-extendable as per ministerial decree 19/2007. The visa issuance fee is QR 100.

    The business visa is valid for one month and can be extended for another three months. The visa extension fee is QR 200 for each month.

Republic of Iran:

It includes only business visas.

Requirements:

    The applicant should hold a credit card with available limit of QR 5000 or its equivalent amount in cash.

    To hold two way ticket.

    The visa is valid for 72 hours and is extendable for similar period for a fee of QR 50.

    The applicant should have a hotel reservation for the period of stay.

    The applicant should be coming for visiting an accredited company in the country.

Republic of Poland:

Requirements:

The visa is given for the citizens of Republic of Poland holding normal and diplomatic passports.

Republic of Hungary:

Requirements:

The visa is given for the citizens of Republic of Hungary holding normal and diplomatic passports.
