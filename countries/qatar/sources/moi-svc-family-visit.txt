Service Details

This visa is granted for one month and can be extended for another five months for first degree relatives.

Fees

			Visa issuance fee: QR 200

Visa Extension fee: QR 200 for every month 

Terms & Conditions

Requirements: 

Submit the application electronically through Metrash2

Upload the Passport copy of the visitor valid for at least six months.

Upload the document that proves the relationship.

Upload the proof of family accommodation in the name of the host, accredited by relevant authorities.

Conditions for family visit approval

:

The profession of the host should not be of Labour category.

He/She should have a monthly salary of QAR 5000/- with accredited employment contract.

The visitor shall have a health insurance for the period of his/her visit.

Family Members that can be hosted 
:

First Degree : Father, Mother - Wife, Children

Second Degree: Brother, Sister - Grand Father, Grand Mother - Grand Children

Third Degree: Children or brother or sister - Paternal Uncle or Aunt (Brother & Sister of Father) - Maternal Uncle or Aunt (Brother & Sister of Mother)
