HAYYA E-VISA & VISA ON ARRIVAL – APPROVED HOTELS

HAYYA E-VISAS

	The Hayya E-Visa application can be submitted at the 
Hayya website
, or by downloading the Hayya App from one of the following App stores:

Google Play

Apple App Store 

Huawei App Gallery

	Further information about the Hayya E-Visa process can be found in the 
FAQ
 and the 
How To
 sections of the Hayya website.

	Booking accommodation through Discover Qatar is mandatory for certain nationalities when applying for a Hayya Entry Permit. As an official and trusted accommodation provider for the Hayya E-Visa process, Discover Qatar can help streamline and expedite your application.

Hayya Application Process:

		The applicant applies for a Hayya E-Visa – the status will state “Pending” until the authorities process the application

		Once the authorities approve the application, the applicant will be notified that the application is “Pending Accommodation Verification”

		The applicant uploads their accommodation voucher

		The authorities will approve the accommodation and change application to “Pending Payment”

		If required, the applicant will be requested to make QAR 100 payment (E-Visa Fee)

		Once a payment is made, the application enters the approval stage

VISA ON ARRIVAL – MANDATED HOTEL REQUIREMENT

	The MOI currently require travelers with passports issued by the following countries to make a Discover Qatar VOA hotel booking before travelling:

India, Pakistan, Thailand, Iran. 

	Other nationalities may be added to this requirement at the discretion of the State of Qatar. For more information on Visa requirements please visit the 
Visit Qatar
 website.
