##### Service Details

This visa is given for government entities, semi-government organizationsand private companies and establishments for bringing expatriate workers in rare professions. The visa is issued for for one month and can be extended to six months as a maximum.

##### Terms & Conditions

*Requirements:*

- Obtain the approval from RARC
- Copy of establishment ID of the sponsor
- Passport copy of the arriving person
- Submit the documents that prove profession or educational qualification of the person
- Shall pass medical test in case in case he wants to stay in the country more than 30 days.
