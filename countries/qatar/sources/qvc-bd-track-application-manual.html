Results Available with Employer

  • Subject to Ministry of Interior requirement, Qatar Visa Center may call the applicant back for re-enrollment of biometric data

  • Subject to Ministry of Health requirement, Qatar Visa Center may call the applicant back for Medical Re-examination or referral the applicant to local healthcare providers for additional test

  • Completion of Biometric Enrolment or Medical Tests does NOT mean the issuance of visa, applicants are requested to contact their employers for their Visa decision

Appointment Conﬁrmed

This status means that Appointment is confirmed at the Qatar Visa Center and the applicant needs to visit the center for Enrolling biometrics and complete the medical tests along with the required documents. For the list of complete documents, please refer to the appointment letter.

Biometric Completed

This status means that the Applicant's biometric enrolment is complete which includes enrolment of fingerprint and capturing of IRIS and facial image. Subject to request from the Ministry of Interior, the applicant may be called for Re-Enrolment if required.

Medical Complete

This Status means that the Applicant has completed the Medical tests at the Qatar Visa Center and the reports have been sent to MoH for validation. Subject to request from the Ministry of Health, the request from the Ministry of Health, the applicant may be called back for re-test or, in cases of medical referral, in the country of origin.

Result Available

This Status means that the Applicant's Biometrics Enrolment and Medical tests are processed by the Ministry of Interior and that the visa issuance result can now be accessed by the Employer. The applicant can now contact the employer for the result.

Appointment No show

This status means that the Applicant has did not visit the Qatar Visa Center, at the scheduled appointment and is required to book a fresh appointment to visit the center.

Appointment Reschedule

This status means that the user has successfully rescheduled the Appointment to visit the Qatar Visa Center.

Re-Enrolment Required

This Status means that the Applicant is required to revisit the Qatar Visa Center to complete the Re- Enrolment of the Biometrics data and must contact the Qatar Visa Center immediately.
