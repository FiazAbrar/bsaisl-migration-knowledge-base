# Supervision Management of Other Financial Institutions

The Other Financial Institutions Supervision Department is responsible for supervising exchange houses, finance companies, investment companies, investment funds, and financial and investment consulting companies operating in the country. That is through conducting regular off-site and on-site assessments and inspections to comprehensively address the risks associated with these financial institutions (FIs).
