Required Attachments
1. Photocopy of the host's I.D card.
2. Photocopy of valid passport of the person to be brought in.
3. A certified copy of marriage contract & birth certificates for children.
4. A certified copy of the employment contract emphasizing Job & Salary.
5. A certificate of good conduct (Police Clearance) which is certified by the relevant authorities for adults.
6. A health insurance with a company that is accepted in the state, for the whole duration of the visitor's stay in the country.
7. Pass the medical examination abroad in accordance with the adopted mechanism adapted of the relevant authorities.
8. Any other conditions deemed necessary by the relevant authority in this regard.

Important Notes
1. This form should be typed in Arabic.
2. Name & data should be typed as written in the passport.
3. A valid residency permit for the foreign spouse of the gulf citizen issued in his country or a marriage approval certified by the relevant authorities.
4. All the documents must be in serial as mentioned above.
5. Original documents are required at the time of registration.
