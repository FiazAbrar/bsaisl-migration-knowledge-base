Issue Residence Permit Application through Metrash

Provided by Ministry of Interior

Service Type                                                                              E-Service
Last updated                                                                              12 Jun 2022

Service Description

This service allows you to apply for issuing residency permits that require approval from the Expatriate Affairs Department at the Ministry of
Interior (MOI) for various reasons, such as passport expiry, fingerprint issues, medical test issues, etc.

Steps

 · Log in to Metrash mobile app, by entering QID number and password.
 · Click on "Residency" then "Residency procedures".
 · Select "Residence Permit" then click on "Continue".
 · Click on "Next" after selecting the type of service (Personal or Company).
 · Select the sponsored person and click on "Next" and then "Yes" to submit the application.

Fees

No fees are required for this service.

Additional Information

You need to have a registered account in Metrash mobile app.
