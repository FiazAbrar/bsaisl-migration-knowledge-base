About Us

Embassy of the State of Qatar- This Embassy is counted as a bilateral mission in Dhaka, which ensures the interests of the State of Qatar in the People’s Republic of Bangladesh, and also plays a vital role in developing bilateral relations between the two countries in various fields.

Consular Services: This section provides services in general to the local people who are interested in visiting the State of Qatar as well as it helps and assists Qatari Nationals during their visit to the People’s Republic of Bangladesh.
