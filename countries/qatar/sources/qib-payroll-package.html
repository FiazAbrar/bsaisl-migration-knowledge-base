    Payroll Package details

    Secure registration: Your employer completes a quick verification and validation process through the QIB WPS Portal. This confirms employer sponsorship and ensures safe and compliant access to the service.

    No minimum balance: Open and maintain a Current Account without keeping any minimum balance and get full flexibility in managing your money.

    Free Himyan Debit Card: Receive a free Himyan Debit Card that allows you to withdraw cash from any ATM in Qatar and use it for local online and in‑store payments.

    Visa Debit Card option: Apply for a Visa Debit Card for international use, which helps you make purchases and withdraw cash outside Qatar.

    Instant salary advance: Get up to 80% of your net salary before payday whenever you need extra support (subject to terms and conditions based on QIB listing approval of company). Apply through the QIB Lite App and receive the advance at a profit rate of just 1% per month.

    Free access to Lite App: Get complimentary access to the QIB Lite App, where you can make local and international transfers and pay your bills easily.

    WPS Family Shield option: You can instantly apply for the WPS Family Shield policy, which offers financial protection to your family in case of death or disability of the insured person.

    Please visit this page if you want to know about the terms of conditions of QIB Payroll Package

    Employer must be a WPS Registered customer with QIB

    Worker must be under employer’s sponsorship

    Employer must initiate the request to open an account for the worker

    Worker must have a Valid QID and Passport

    Worker must have a valid local mobile number
