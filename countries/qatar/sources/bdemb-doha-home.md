# [Embassy of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh, Doha, Qatar](/)

![quote](/site-assets/images/quote.svg)

![H. E. Mr. Mohammad Hazrat Ali Khan](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-doha-mofa/2024/12/f3661c1516794e6697679aa4aed9df4c.png)

Ambassador's Desk

[](/pages/files/6952663fa31054345f102de6)

H. E. Mr. Mohammad Hazrat Ali Khan

Dear Visitors and fellow expatriate Bangladeshi nationals in Qatar, Assalamualaikum and Greetings from the Bangladesh Embassy. We are delighted to welcome you to the official website of the Embassy of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh in Doha. This platform provides comprehensive information on Bangladesh’s rich cultural heritage, dynamic present, vision for the future, and the history of a resilient and progressive nation of 180 million people. It also highlights Bangladesh’s more than 14 million expatriates and migrant workers worldwide, including the vibrant community of around half a million Bangladeshi nationals in Qatar. Bangladesh and Qatar enjoy excellent bilateral relations, founded on shared history, religion, vibrant people-to-people connections, as well as growing cooperation in trade, investment, and energy. The Embassy invites you to learn more about our work, meet our dedicated team, and connect with the broader Bangladesh we proudly represent—forward-looking, dynamic, and committed to fostering peace and prosperity with our friends and partners. Long live Bangladesh-Qatar friendship.

With warm regards,

Ambassador's Desk
