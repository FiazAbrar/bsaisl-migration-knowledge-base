Planning a trip to Qatar for leisure or sightseeing? Depending on your nationality, you may need to obtain a tourist visa before arriving. The good news is that the process has become much simpler since the relaunch of the Hayya platform in April 2023.

This guide walks you through everything you need to know about applying for a 
Qatar Tourist Visa (A1) online using the Hayya platform
. We’ll cover 
eligibility criteria, the step-by-step application process, visa fees
, and answers to common questions—so you can prepare for your visit with confidence.

Qatar Tourist Visa (A1) Updates

Tourist Visa (A1) 
Entry Permit is now valid for 90 days
 from the date of issue (earlier it was 30 days). This means once your visa is approved, you have 90 days to enter Qatar. If you are planning to travel in the next 3 months it is a good idea to apply now (especially considering the delay in approvals).

Qatar Tourist Visa Eligibility Requirements

The Hayya Tourist Visa (A1) is for anyone who wishes to travel to Qatar for tourism or visit friends and family. It is available to 
nationals of all countries
, except GCC citizens, who can avail of visa-free entry to Qatar.

These are the
 requirements to apply 
for Hayya Tourist Visa (A1):

The visitor’s 
passport
 should be valid for 
at least three months
 from the date of entry to Qatar. 

The visitor should have 
proof of accommodation
. This can be “
Hosted by Family and Friends
” or “Other Accommodation” (hotel booking or rented apartment). In some cases, you may be exempted from providing a proof of accommodation or requested to have your booked accommodation through 
Discover Qatar
.

If arriving by air, the visitor should have a 
return ticket
.

Please note that nationals of more than 100 countries are 
eligible for visa-free entry to Qatar
. If your nationality is eligible for visa-free entry, you don’t have to apply for a tourist visa.

Qatar Tourist Visa Cost

Qatar Tourist Visa (A1) costs
 QAR 100 (USD 27)
 per visitor. Some nationalities are exempt from this requirement.

Qatar Tourist Visa Validity

The Tourist Visa you receive through the Hayya platform is valid for 
30 days from entry date.
 Many visitors were able to renew it for 30 more days after entering Qatar.

Entry Permits are 
valid for 90 days from the date of issue
 so visitors must enter Qatar within those days.

How To Apply For Qatar Tourist Visa Online

If you are interested in applying for a Tourist Visa (A1) through the Hayya Portal, follow these steps:

Step 1: Visit the Hayya Portal

Go to the website address: 
www.hayya.qa

Step 2: Click on “Login”

This is located on the top right of the page.

Hayya portal home page

Step 3: Sign Up and Login

If you are a first-time user of the Hayya portal, click on “
Sign Up
” and create an account using your email address. You will be required to enter a verification code sent to your email.

Once you have created an account, login using your email and password. If you are a registered user, you may have to verify your email using the OTP sent to your email.

Hayya Portal Sign up and Login forms

Step 4: Click on “My Hayya”

You can find this on the top right (click on your name for the drop-down). 

Step 5: Click on “Apply for Hayya”

You can see this button in the centre of the page, under My Hayya.

Step 6: See the eligible visa categories

On the Hayya journey pop-up, select “Flying to Qatar” or “Driving to Qatar” (if using land border).

Select your nationality.

Answer the next questions. Click confirm.

The system will show you
 the eligible visa categories
 based on your answers.

If it shows Tourist Visa (A1), click on that.

Read the information and click on “Apply for A1 visa”.

Step 7: Click on “Start the Application”

Read the information on the next page. The application autosaves as you enter your details, so any completed sections will be stored automatically. You can 
finish the form in one sitting or return to it later
. However, each section must be completed in the order shown — you cannot skip ahead to the next part until the previous one is finished. 

You will need the following: 

Copy of your personal photo, ID or passport

Hotel booking or Host information

Once these are ready, click on “Start the Application”.

Step 8: Select Your Purpose of Visit 

You can choose from a range of purposes including business meetings, convention participation, event participation, friend visit, heritage tourism, leisure, shopping, sports event, wedding party, etc. or select “Others” and specify the purpose.

Before proceeding to next step, please keep this information at hand:

Digital photograph of yourself

Valid ID (passport or Qatar ID). For passport holder the validity should be at least 3 months

Details of your onward travel

Hotel booking for the duration of your stay*

in case someone is hosting you, please be sure they 
register it on the portal
. Then click “Next”.

Step 9: Upload Personal Photo

Click on “Browse for picture”. You can see a PDF guide showing the guidelines for your photo. Read the guide and select “I acknowledge the instructions for uploading my personal photo.” check box. Then upload your photo from your device. If it is accepted, you can see a message “The image is OK”.

Ensure that the photo meets the requirements such as a light background and sharpness. Only image file (.jpg or .jpeg) is allowed. 
Maximum file size is 2MB.

Step 10: Upload Your Passport

Click on “Browse for passport picture”. You can see a PDF guide showing the guidelines for your country. Read them and select “Continue” check box. Select the Passport type. For most people this will be “Normal”.

Your 
data will be extracted automatically
. If the data is unreadable, you can enter it manually. If there is any missing field, ensure you fill them.

Re-check to make sure the auto-filled fields before proceeding, otherwise it might get rejected.

Enter your contact information – such as your phone number, emergency contact name and their phone number.

Click on “Validate your ID”. If the “Validate your ID” option is inactive after automatic data extraction, select. the “Passport Type” as “Normal”.

The first and last names are mandatory requirements. Middle names, third Latin names, and fourth Latin names are optional. If your 
last name is not on your passport
, you can add your father’s name as your last name. If you are not sure, refer to the PDF that was shown during passport upload.

Now you can see a message that says “Your ID has been validated!”. Click “Next”.

Step 11: Provide Your Residency Information

Select your country of birth and your country of residence. Declare if you have a previous nationality. If you have, select the country.

Step 12: Select Your Accommodation Type

If you are staying with family or friends, your details should have already been added by your host in Qatar. 
Here is how Qatar residents can invite to host Hayya visitors
. 

Otherwise, you can select “Other Accommodation” and enter the accommodation name. Finally review and submit.

Step 13: Pay the Visa Fee

Once your application has been reviewed and approved, you will receive an 
email with a payment link.
 The visa fee of QR 100 should be paid using a credit/debit card. Visa or Mastercard are usually accepted. 
If your nationality is exempt 
you will not receive the payment request.

Once the visa application is processed, the 
Hayya Entry Permit
 will be sent to your email. You can also access your Entry Permit by downloading the “Hayya To Qatar” mobile app.

Hayya Tourist Visa Processing Time

As of 2025, the average approval time is less than a week. However there may be delays in some cases. It is recommended that you 
do not book your flight tickets
 until your visa is fully approved.

Additional information

The passport should be 
valid for at least three (3) months
 from the date of entry in Qatar.

Since the initial visa is issued for 30 days, your 
return flight ticket should be within 30 days of entry
, even if you plan to extend your stay. As the visa extension is conditional, your airline may not allow you to board. You can book a ticket with minimal or zero change fees.

For each adult family member, 
a separate application
 is required. So, if you are travelling as a family, your spouse should apply through their own Hayya account.

You can 
add children below 18 years as dependents
 to your Hayya account, but all details should be filled in. You can also apply separately for children.

Visa holders are 
not allowed to work
 in Qatar during their stay.

Documents Needed For Travel Using Tourist Visa

While travelling using the Hayya Tourist Visa, you will need the following:

1
Passport
Valid for a minimum of 3 months from entry.
2
Hayya Entry Permit
You will receive this once your visa is approved.
3
Return Flight Tickets
Airlines require that return tickets should be within 30 days.
4
Accommodation Details
Clear address in Qatar. Invitation email and host details if “host family and friends” option. Booking details if hotel.
5
Visitor’s Health Insurance
Recommended. Only mandatory if planning to stay for more than 30 days.

Currently, COVID-19 vaccination and PCR testing are not required for visitors to Qatar.

latest offers from Qatar Airways
, Qatar’s 5-star airline, rated as 
one of the best in the world
. With its reputation for luxury, comfort, and a wide-reaching global network, booking with Qatar Airways ensures an extraordinary travel experience.

How to Extend Qatar Tourist Visa (A1)

Step 1: Purchase a health insurance via MOPH website

To extend your Hayya Tourist Visa (A1) beyond the first 30 days, you should 
purchase a mandatory health insurance
 from any MOPH registered insurance company. You can find the 
list of approved insurance companies here
. 

Click on your preferred company and buy the insurance from the company website. The insurance fee is
 QR 50 for 30 days 
– for all companies. Once the purchase is completed it will be updated in the syetem (through passport number). Note that in some cases updation can take up to 24 hours.

Step 2: Go to the 
Visit Visa Extension Service page
 on the MOI website

This is available under Visa Services –> Visit Visa Extension.

Step 3: Enter the visa number or passport number

You can find the visa number on the sticker on your passport.

Step 4: Select nationality from the drop-down list

Step 5: Enter the verification code from the picture or audio

Step 6: Click on the “Extend” button

If you are eligible, the visa will be renewed for another 30 days.

FAQ on Qatar Tourist Visa Procedure

How many days is a Qatar Tourist Visa valid for?

Qatar Tourist Visa holders can stay in Qatar for up to 30 days. In some cases, this can be extended for 30 more days.

How much does a Qatar Tourist Visa cost?

Qatar Tourist Visa costs QAR 100 (USD 27). 

How much is the fine after the Qatar Tourist Visa expires?

The fine for an expired tourist visa in Qatar is QAR 200 (USD 55) per day.

Should I use only Qatar Airways for my travel?

You can use any airline to Doha. There are no restrictions on travel modes.

Can I bring a dependent along with me?

Yes, you can apply for dependents below 18 years old by adding their information on the Hayya application portal. Note that each visitor should have their own Hayya Entry Permit.

Is there any age limit to apply for a Tourist Visa (A1(?

There is no age limit to apply for a Tourist Visa (A1). As long as you meet the listed requirements, you can apply for the Tourist Visa (A1).

Can I still apply for a regular visit visa or do I have to get a Tourist Visa (A1)?

Yes, several visa options are still available. Whether you should apply for a regular visit visa issued by the Ministry of Interior or a Hayya Tourist Visa (A1) depends on your nationality, travel profile, and the purpose of your visit.
