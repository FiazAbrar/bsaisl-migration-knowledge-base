## Hospital Visit Repatriation of sick and poor expatriates

Content: Pages

\

The Embassy regularly visits the hospitals and provides assistance to the sick person. On request from the hospital authorities or relatives of the sick person, the Embassy facilitates the repatriation process of a poor and sick Bangladeshi expatriate.
