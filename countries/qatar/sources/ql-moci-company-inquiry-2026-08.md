Businesses and individuals considering a new partnership in Qatar can check important information about a company before making a decision through the [**Ministry of Commerce and Industry’s Company Inquiry Service**](https://ucs.moci.gov.qa/Services/ServiceDetails/09e109a8-f551-f111-bec7-7ced8d2c0c9b).

The Ministry of Commerce and Industry (MOCI) is highlighting the digital service as a way for users to review a company’s **regulatory record and general information from an official and trusted source**.

The service is intended to help potential partners make more informed decisions before entering into a commercial relationship.

### Why check a company before partnering?

Choosing the right business partner can have a significant impact on any commercial venture. Having access to official company information gives businesses an additional way to carry out basic checks before committing to a partnership.

Rather than relying solely on information supplied by a prospective partner, users can consult the MOCI service to obtain information directly from an official source.

This can help individuals and companies better understand who they may be doing business with and support more informed decision-making.

### What does the Company Inquiry Service provide?

According to MOCI, the service enables users to review a company’s **regulatory record and general information**.

The Ministry describes the platform as a tool for obtaining the information needed to assess potential business partners with greater confidence.

It can be particularly useful for entrepreneurs, investors and companies evaluating new commercial relationships in Qatar.

### Supporting informed business decisions

The service forms part of MOCI’s efforts to make official business information more accessible and help users base commercial decisions on verified information.

For anyone considering partnering with a company in Qatar, checking its available official information can serve as a practical first step before moving forward with an agreement.

Further details about the Company Inquiry Service can be accessed through the **Ministry of Commerce and Industry’s official channels** or through the QR code shared in its announcement.
