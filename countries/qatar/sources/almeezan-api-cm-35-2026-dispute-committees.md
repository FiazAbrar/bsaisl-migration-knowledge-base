# Council of Ministers Decision No. (35) of 2026 amending certain provisions of Decision / Resolution No. (6) of 2018 on the formation of labor dispute resolution committees, the rules and procedures to be followed before them, the mechanism for implementing their decisions, and determining their remuneration.

Cabinet / Council of Ministers,
After reviewing the Constitution,
And Labour Law promulgated by Law No. (14) of 2004, and the laws amending it,
And Amiri Decision / Resolution No. (29) of 1996 regarding Cabinet / Council of Ministers decisions submitted to the Amir for ratification and issuance,
And Council of Ministers Decision No. (6) of 2018 forming Labour Dispute Settlement Committees and the rules and procedures to be followed before them, the mechanism for enforcing their decisions, and determining their remuneration, as amended by Council of Ministers Decision No. (17) of 2022,
And upon the proposal of the Minister of Labour,
Has decided as follows:

## Almawad (1-2)


### Article 1

The texts of Articles (1), (2/first Paragraph), (5), and (13) of the Council of Ministers Decision No. (6) of 2018 referred to shall be replaced with the following texts:

Article (1):
« In applying the provisions of this Decision / Resolution, the following words shall have the meanings assigned to each of them, unless the context requires otherwise:
Ministry: Ministry of Labour.
Minister: Minister of Labour.
Committee: Labour Dispute Settlement Committee.».
Article (2/first Paragraph):
« One or more committees shall be formed at the Ministry for the settlement of labour disputes, each chaired by a judge of the Court of First Instance selected by the Supreme Judiciary Council, and with the membership of a representative of the Ministry of Labour and an expert in the field of accounting selected by the Minister.».
Article (5):
« Each party to the dispute may appear before the Committee in person, or authorize a representative to appear on his behalf, even if such representative is not his spouse, relative, or in-law. The Chairman of the Committee may verify the capacities of the attending Litigants.».
Article (13):
« The Decision / Resolution of the Committee by which the dispute before it is concluded shall be signed by the Chairman of the Committee and the secretary, and shall briefly include the following:
1. The names of the Chairman and the two members of the Committee that issued the Decision / Resolution, and the date and place of its issuance.
2. The names and capacities of the Litigants, and their attendance or absence.
3. The requests of the Litigants.
4. The basis of the relationship and the Articles of the Labour Law applicable to the requests of the Litigants, without prejudice to ruling on defenses related to jurisdiction and form or substantive defenses.
The provisions of the Civil and Commercial Procedure Law shall apply to the procedures before the Labour Dispute Settlement Committee and the decisions it issues, in matters for which no special provision is provided in the aforementioned Labour Law or in this Decision / Resolution.».

### Article 2

All competent authorities, each within its respective jurisdiction, shall implement this Decision / Resolution. It shall come into force on the day following the date of its publication in the Official Gazette.
