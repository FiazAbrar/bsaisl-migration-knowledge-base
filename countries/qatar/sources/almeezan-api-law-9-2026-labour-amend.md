# Law No. (9) of 2026 amending certain provisions of the Labour Law promulgated by Law No. (14) of 2004

We, Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani               Emir of the State of Qatar,

Having reviewed the Constitution,

And Labour Law promulgated by Law No. (14) of 2004, and the laws amending it,

And Law No. (17) of 2018 establishing the Workers' Support and Insurance Fund,

Upon the proposal of the Council of Ministers,

And upon the approval of the Shura Council,

We have decided to ratify the following Law:

## Almawad (1-5)


### Article 1

The following text shall be substituted for the text of Article (2) of Law / Act No. (14) of 2004 promulgating the Labour Law:
«The Minister of Labour, in coordination with the competent authorities, shall issue the decisions necessary to implement the provisions of the attached Law / Act. Until these decisions are issued, the decisions in force shall continue to be applied, provided that they do not conflict with the provisions of the attached Law / Act».

### Article 2

The texts of Articles (1/Items 1, 2, 3), (3), (36), (43/second Paragraph), (78/Item 3), (115 bis), (115 bis/4), (120), (124), (144), (145 bis) of the aforementioned Labour Law shall be replaced by the following texts:

Article (1/ Items 1, 2, 3):

“1. Ministry: Ministry of Labour.

2. Minister: Minister of Labour.

3. Department / Directorate: the competent administrative Unit at the Ministry, as the case may be.”.

Article (3):

“Except where a special provision is made, the provisions of this Law / Act shall not apply to the following categories:

1.  Employees and workers in ministries, other government agencies, and public authorities and institutions.

2. Officers and members of the armed forces, police, and other military entities, and persons working at sea.

3. Employees and workers in companies established by the government or in whose establishment it participates and which operate in the fields of petroleum and in the marketing and sale of Petroleum Products, chemicals, petrochemicals, and their derivatives; companies established by QatarEnergy or in whose establishment it participates, or in which it contributes; workers in companies engaged in implementing exploration and production-sharing agreements, field development and production-sharing agreements, and joint venture agreements in the field of petroleum operations and petrochemical industries; as well as those whose employment affairs are regulated by special laws.

4. Persons employed in casual work.

5. Domestic employees such as drivers, nannies, cooks, gardeners, and the like.

6. Members of the Employer’s family, namely his spouse, ascendants, and descendants from among the workers residing with him in his dwelling and fully supported by him.

7. Workers engaged in agriculture and grazing, except for persons working in agricultural establishments that manufacture or market their products, or those who permanently operate or repair the mechanical machinery necessary for agriculture.

8. Workers under the part-time work or freelance work system.

By a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, upon the proposal of the Minister, all or some of the provisions of this Law / Act may be applied to the categories mentioned in Items (4, 5, 6, 7, 8) referred to, or a special work system may be issued for either of the two categories referred to in Item (8) of this Article.”.

Article (36):

“The procedures and controls for the License of recruitment, the rules and conditions governing the work of offices recruiting workers from abroad for the account of others, and the administrative penalties imposed in the event of violation of any of these provisions shall be determined by a decision of the Minister.”.

Article (43/second Paragraph):

“If the nature of the work allows the Employee / Worker to know the Employer’s clients or to have access to the secrets of the establishment’s work, the Employer may stipulate in the employment Contract that, after the end of the Contract, the Employee / Worker shall not compete with him or participate in or work on any project competing with him, provided that the duration of this condition shall not in any case exceed two years. The condition shall not be enforced except after the approval of the Department / Directorate, and if the Contract is terminated during the probation period, the condition shall be deemed null and void.”.

Article (78/ Item 3):

“3. One working day on the occasion of the National Day of the State.”.

 Article (115/bis):

“Each of the Employee / Worker and the Employer, if a dispute arises between them relating to the application of this Law / Act or the employment Contract, must submit the dispute to the Department / Directorate for amicable settlement.

The Department / Directorate shall take the necessary procedures to settle the dispute amicably within a period not exceeding seven days from the date it is submitted to it, and shall present the result of the settlement to the two parties within the following seven days. If the two parties accept it, Evidence / Proof of what has been agreed upon shall be made in a Report / Official Record signed by them and approved by the Department / Directorate, and it shall have the force of an executory instrument.

If the dispute is not settled within the period referred to in the previous Paragraph, or if either party to the dispute rejects the settlement of the Department / Directorate, or if the period expires without the two parties or either of them expressing an opinion of acceptance or rejection, the Department / Directorate must refer the dispute within the following three working days to the Labour Dispute Settlement Committee. The referral shall be accompanied by a memorandum containing a summary of the subject matter of the dispute, the arguments of the two parties, their documents, and the observations of the Department / Directorate. The secretariat of the Committee shall, within three working days from the date the dispute is referred to it, set a hearing to consider the dispute on a date not exceeding seven working days from the date of referral. Each of the Employee / Worker and the Employer shall be notified of the date of the hearing at least three days before it is held.
The submission of a request for settlement of the dispute to the Department / Directorate shall result in the suspension of the period legally prescribed for the forfeiture of the Case/Lawsuit claiming those rights, until the expiry of the time limits set out in this Article.”.

Article (115 bis/4):

“The decision of the Committee by which the dispute before it is concluded shall have the force of an executory instrument in accordance with the Law / Act. The decision of the Committee must be reasoned and must include the data to be determined by a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers.

The Committee may use the necessary electronic systems after verifying the identity of the Litigant / Adversary or the witness, as the case may be.”.

Article (120):

“Workers may carry out a peaceful Strike from work if the Employer breaches his obligations prescribed under the employment Contract or under the provisions of the Law / Act, if an amicable solution, conciliation, or Arbitration between them and the Employer is impossible In accordance with the provisions of this Law, subject to the following controls:

1. Approval of three quarters of the general Committee of the workers of the profession or industry.

2. The workers or their representative shall notify the Employer and the Ministry in writing of the workers’ desire to Strike, provided that the notice includes the reasons for the Strike, the workers’ demands, and the specified duration thereof.
3. The Employer shall be granted a period of not less than two weeks before commencing the Strike, and the approval of the Ministry shall be obtained, after coordination with the Ministry of Interior regarding the time and place of the Strike.

4. The place of the Strike shall not exceed the workers’ place of residence or the workplace.

5. The Strike shall be temporary, not exceeding six working days, and shall not cause the Employer’s facilities to be disrupted.

6. No prejudice shall be caused to the funds of the State, or to the property, security, or safety of individuals.

7. The Strike shall not take place in vital facilities, such as petroleum and gas and the industries associated with them, electricity and water, ports, airports and transportation, hospitals, and other facilities of a vital nature to be determined by a decision of the Minister.

8. The Strike shall be suspended upon the commencement of the procedures for settling the collective labour dispute.

Employers may, after the approval of the Ministry, employ substitute workers under temporary contracts during Strikes.

The Strike from work stipulated in this Article shall result in its duration being counted as leave for the Employee / Worker without pay.”.

Article (124):

“Every establishment employing one hundred workers or more shall be obliged to form a ‘joint Committee’ comprising representatives in equal numbers from the Employer and the workers. The number of members of the joint Committee shall be as follows:

1. Four if the number of the establishment’s workers is two hundred workers or fewer.

2. Six if the number of workers is more than two hundred workers and fewer than five hundred workers.

3. Eight if the number of workers is five hundred or more.”.

Article (144):

“A fine of not less than (2,000) two thousand riyals and not more than (5,000) five thousand riyals shall be imposed on anyone who violates any of the provisions of Articles (7), (12), (19),(21),(22),(23),(27),(28),(35),(39 second Paragraph),(46),(47),(48),(52 bis),(57),(58),(68),(70),(73),(74),(75),(77),(91),(92),(95),(97),(99),(100),(115),(124),(139) of this Law / Act.”.

Article (145 bis):

“Imprisonment for a period not exceeding one year, and a fine of not less than (2,000) two thousand riyals and not more than ten thousand (10,000) riyals, or either of these two penalties, shall be imposed on anyone who violates any of the provisions of Articles (66), (120 Item/7) of this Law / Act.”.

### Article 3

The following texts shall be added to the referenced Labour Law:
Article (23 bis):
“Without prejudice to the legislation regulating any technical and craft professions, workers may be employed in certain professions only after obtaining the training and testing Testimony required for that from the training centers accredited by the Ministry.

The professions referred to shall be determined pursuant to a list prepared by the Department / Directorate, after coordination with the concerned authorities, approved by the Minister, and published on the website of the Ministry. This shall apply to every amendment or update to the said list.
A decision shall be issued by the Minister concerning the controls for the License to practice the professions referred to in the first paragraph of this Article, the training required to practice them, and the cases of suspension and cancellation of the License.”.
Article (61/ Item 11):

“11- If the Employee / Worker incites the remaining workers to Strike for unlawful reasons and this results in disrupting the business of the Employer’s establishments.”.

Article (146 bis):
“Without prejudice to the penalties stipulated in this Law / Act, the Minister may, in the event that the Employer violates any of its provisions, decide to suspend all or some of the requests and transactions of the violating establishment with the Ministry.

In the event that the violation is repeated in the same establishment during the same year, or collective disputes occur due to delay in payment of wages, the Minister may suspend all or some of the requests and transactions of the establishments Reporting to the violating Employer with the Ministry, whenever they are connected to the violation or it is proven that they were used to circumvent the implementation of the provisions of this Law / Act.
In all cases, the suspension shall not extend to depriving other parties connected to the violator of services or affecting their rights, nor shall the suspension include the attestation of Employment Contracts, or the renewal of Work Permits and residences of workers Reporting to those establishments.
The suspension shall not be lifted until the Employer corrects the violation and removes its causes within the period specified for him by the Department / Directorate pursuant to notifying him at his national address or by any means that proves knowledge.
The Employee / Worker harmed by the Employer’s violation of the provision of Article (66) of this Law / Act may submit a request to change his employer or a request to terminate the contractual relationship with the violating establishment, and in this case the violating establishment shall be obligated to bear the expenses of his return to his country.”.
Article (146 bis/1):
“By a decision of the Minister or his delegate, in the event of repeated violation of any of the provisions of Articles (29), (33), (45), (66), (108), (133) of this Law / Act, or the provisions of its implementing decisions, the name of the violating establishment may be published on the website of the Ministry.

The Minister may also, in the case of violations related to recruiting workers from abroad for the account of others, and after notifying the violator at his national address or by any means that proves knowledge to correct the violation within a period he specifies, impose a financial penalty in accordance with the table attached to this Law / Act, while obligating the violator to remove the causes of the violation within three months from the date of his notification, other than the Crimes and penalties stipulated in this Law / Act.”.

### Article 4

A Schedule / Table of financial penalties for violations of recruiting workers from abroad for the account of others shall be attached to the aforementioned Labor Law. By a decision of the Cabinet / Council of Ministers, based on the proposal of the Minister and in accordance with the requirements of the public interest, this Schedule / Table may be amended by addition or deletion, or by amending the amount of the financial penalty stated therein, as the case may be.

### Article 5

All competent authorities, each within its respective jurisdiction, shall implement this Law / Act. It shall be published in the Official Gazette.
