Ministry of Labour Launches First Phase of Moawen Domestic Worker Recruitment Platform
1 min read
•
Sep 9, 2026
Government Services
Tech
Jobs & Careers
Share this article
The Ministry of Labour has launched the first phase of "Moawen", a government digital platform for domestic worker recruitment.
The platform brings the different parties involved in the recruitment process together through one unified digital system, with the aim of making the recruitment journey easier and clearer.
Moawen is designed around unified procedures and the protection of rights throughout the domestic worker recruitment process. Users can visit the platform via 
https://moawen.mol.gov.qa
.
