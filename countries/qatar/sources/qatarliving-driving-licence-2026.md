Want to drive legally in Qatar? Residents generally need a Qatar driving licence issued by the General Directorate of Traffic under the Ministry of Interior.

The application rules are national, so the process is the same whether you live in West Bay, Al Wakra, Lusail or elsewhere in Qatar.

This guide to getting a Qatar driving licence in 2026 covers eligibility, documents, driving-school enrolment, the testing process and official government fees.

For most first-time applicants, the process involves confirming eligibility, obtaining sponsor approval, passing an eyesight test, enrolling with an approved driving school, receiving a learner’s permit, completing the required training and passing the Traffic Department’s tests.

Driving-school tuition is separate from the Ministry of Interior fees listed below.

## Who can apply for a Qatar driving licence in 2026?

The main legal requirements are:

- Meeting the minimum age
- Being medically fit to drive
- Passing the required driving tests
- Holding legal residence in Qatar if you are a non-Qatari applicant

The General Directorate of Traffic states that non-Qatari and GCC-national applicants seeking a Qatar licence must hold legal residence in the country.

### Minimum age requirements

The current minimum ages are:

- Light vehicles: 18 years
- Motorcycles: 18 years
- Heavy vehicles: 21 years
- Constructional and agricultural equipment: 21 years

The Ministry of Interior’s published requirements do not use one separate age category for every type of public-transport vehicle, so applicants for buses, taxis and other professional driving categories should check the conditions attached to the specific licence or permit.

## Profession, sponsorship and residency rules

A valid residence permit and Qatar ID are generally required for a resident applying for a Qatar driving licence.

For non-Qataris, the Ministry of Interior’s published application procedure also requires a letter from the sponsor approving the application and identifying the type of licence requested. If the applicant is under personal sponsorship, a copy of the sponsor’s QID may also be required.

In practice, an applicant’s occupation, sponsorship category and employer approval can affect whether an application is accepted. However, there is no dependable current public list covering every occupation that is permitted or restricted.

Do not rely on an old profession list circulating online. Before buying a training package, ask the driving school to check your QID and application eligibility through the Traffic Department’s current system.

You must also meet the physical-fitness requirements and pass an eyesight test. A complete general medical examination is not automatically required for every standard light-vehicle application. More detailed medical assessment may be required where the applicant has a disability, medical condition or another issue affecting fitness to drive.

## Documents you need to apply

The exact requirements can vary by nationality, sponsorship type and licence category.

For a standard non-Qatari application, prepare:

- Valid Qatar ID
- Original passport and a copy
- Sponsor or employer approval letter identifying the requested licence type
- Copy of the personal sponsor’s QID, where applicable
- Recent colour photographs if requested
- Registered eyesight-test result
- Original foreign driving licence, if you already hold one

The MOI’s published procedure refers to three recent colour photographs, although digital applications and driving-school systems may retrieve or capture some information electronically. Follow the checklist generated for your application rather than printing photographs unnecessarily.

If a foreign licence is not in Arabic or English, an approved translation may be requested.

## The Qatar driving-test process, step by step

For most new drivers, the licensing process is handled through a driving school approved by the General Directorate of Traffic.

Applicants who already hold a foreign licence may follow a different testing route depending on the issuing country and the Traffic Department’s decision.

## Step 1 — Enrol at an approved driving school

New drivers are generally referred to an approved driving school.

The school helps open the learner’s file, register the eyesight-test result, provide theory and practical training and arrange Traffic Department test appointments.

Before paying, ask the school for an itemised quotation showing:

- Full or half-course training
- Number of theory lessons
- Number of practical lessons
- Automatic or manual vehicle
- Learner-permit fee
- Test fees
- Transport, if offered
- Internal assessment charges
- Retest or additional-lesson costs
- Refund and transfer conditions

Driving-school package prices are not government fees and vary considerably by provider.

An applicant who already holds a valid foreign driving licence may be sent to an approved school for a direct driving test rather than automatically completing the full course. The Ministry of Interior says that foreign-licence holders coming to Qatar for work may be tested through a driving school. If they pass, they can be granted the relevant licence. If they fail, they may receive another test opportunity before being directed to complete a full training course.

## Step 2 — Eyesight test and fitness assessment

Applicants must satisfy the physical-fitness requirement and complete an eyesight test.

The eyesight result can be entered electronically into the Ministry of Interior licensing system by an authorised provider.

If you need glasses or contact lenses to meet the required vision standard, this may be recorded as a condition on the licence.

A full medical examination should not be described as mandatory for every ordinary applicant. Applicants with disabilities or medical issues affecting driving may be referred to the MOI Medical Services Department for assessment and licence conditions.

## Step 3 — Get your learner’s permit

A learner’s driving permit is required before completing authorised practical road training.

The current official fee is:

- Learner’s permit: QAR 150
- Validity: three months
- Renewal: QAR 150 for another three-month period

The same fee applies to learner permits for the different vehicle categories listed by the Ministry of Interior.

The learner’s permit does not allow unrestricted independent driving. Training must take place under the approved learning arrangement and in the authorised vehicle.

## Step 4 — Theory test

The theory examination tests knowledge of areas such as:

- Traffic signs
- Road markings
- Traffic signals
- Right-of-way rules
- Safe driving behaviour
- Lane discipline
- Speed limits
- Emergency situations
- General Qatar traffic rules

The General Directorate of Traffic is responsible for conducting theoretical and road tests for licence applicants.

The language options and test format should be confirmed with the driving school, as the available interface can depend on the current testing system.

Passing the theory stage allows the learner to proceed with the remaining practical training and assessments required for the chosen licence.

## Step 5 — Practical driving test

The practical assessment may involve more than one component, depending on the licence category and current Traffic Department process.

For a light vehicle, preparation commonly covers:

- Vehicle control
- Parking and manoeuvres
- Starting and stopping safely
- Lane use
- Junctions and roundabouts
- Observation and mirror use
- Speed control
- Road driving

The Ministry of Interior’s official fee for a driving test is:

- QAR 50 for one test attempt

This means the test fee is not necessarily a single lifetime or application charge. Another QAR 50 government fee may apply when a new test attempt is booked.

Driving schools may separately charge for:

- Additional practical lessons
- Vehicle use
- Internal assessments
- Rescheduling
- Transport
- Administrative processing

Do not present QAR 300–400 as a standard retest fee for every school. Ask the school to separate its own charges from the MOI’s QAR 50 test fee.

Once you satisfy all required test stages, you pay the licence-issuance fee and receive the Qatar driving licence.

## Qatar driving licence costs in 2026

Official Ministry of Interior fees are separate from the driving school’s tuition and service charges.

The current published fees are as follows.

### Learner’s permit

- QAR 150
- Valid for three months
- Renewal: QAR 150

### Temporary driving licence

- QAR 150
- Valid for three months

This is a specific temporary licence issued to eligible tourists, visitors and foreigners. It should not be confused with driving under an international driving permit or a valid GCC licence.

### Driving test

- QAR 50 for one test attempt

### Private light-vehicle licence

**Qatari citizens**

- QAR 500
- Valid for ten years

**Non-Qatari residents**

- QAR 250
- Valid for five years

The official fee table does not list a separate QAR 250 five-year light-vehicle option for Qatari citizens.

### Heavy-vehicle licence

**Qatari citizens**

- QAR 600
- Valid for ten years

**Non-Qatari residents**

- QAR 300
- Valid for five years

### Constructional, agricultural or industrial vehicle licence

**Qatari citizens**

- QAR 600
- Valid for ten years

**Non-Qatari residents**

- QAR 300
- Valid for five years

### Motorcycle licence

**Qatari citizens**

- QAR 300
- Valid for ten years

**Non-Qatari residents**

- QAR 150
- Valid for five years

### Indicative government-fee total for a resident

A non-Qatari applying for a standard light-vehicle licence may pay at least:

- QAR 150 for the learner’s permit
- QAR 50 for one test attempt
- QAR 250 for the five-year licence

That produces an indicative minimum of **QAR 450 in MOI fees**, excluding:

- Driving-school tuition
- Eyesight test
- Additional test attempts
- Additional lessons
- Translation
- Delivery or service charges

The final amount depends on the applicant’s route and number of test attempts.

## Converting or using a foreign driving licence

Holding a foreign licence does not guarantee automatic conversion.

The Ministry of Interior’s published process says foreign-licence holders who have come to Qatar for work and hold residence permits may be referred to an approved driving school for a competency test.

If the applicant passes, the Qatar licence may be issued. If the applicant fails, another test opportunity may be provided before the person is transferred to a full driving course.

Some applicants may receive a different route depending on the country that issued the licence, nationality, reciprocity rules and current Traffic Department policy.

Bring:

- Original foreign driving licence
- Qatar ID
- Passport
- Sponsor approval
- Eyesight-test result
- Translation where requested

Do not pay a school for a full course until it has checked whether you qualify for a direct test or another reduced-training route.

## Visitors and tourists

The rules for visitors differ from those for Qatar residents.

The Ministry of Interior states that:

- Visitors and foreign tourists holding a valid international driving permit may drive in Qatar for up to six months from entry.
- Foreign residents of GCC countries visiting Qatar may use a valid GCC-country driving licence for up to three months.
- The date of entry may be checked through the visitor’s passport.
- An eligible visitor may also apply for a temporary Qatar driving licence valid for three months at a fee of QAR 150.

A national foreign licence alone should not automatically be assumed valid for a tourist. Confirm the requirements with the rental company and the Traffic Department before driving.

Once a person becomes a Qatar resident, visitor-driving rules should not be relied upon as a substitute for obtaining the appropriate Qatar licence.

## Validity and renewal

Current licence validity is generally:

- Qatari light-vehicle licence: ten years
- Non-Qatari resident light-vehicle licence: five years
- Qatari heavy or equipment licence: ten years
- Non-Qatari heavy or equipment licence: five years
- Learner’s permit: three months
- Temporary visitor licence: three months

Civilian light-vehicle licences can be renewed electronically through the MOI e-services portal or the Metrash app.

The official online renewal service allows renewal only within six months before the current licence expires or after its expiry date.

Current light-vehicle renewal fees are:

- QAR 500 for Qataris for ten years
- QAR 250 for residents for five years
- QAR 20 delivery charge where applicable

A fresh eyesight test is not listed as a standard step in the published online renewal process. It may still be requested in an individual case because of age, medical circumstances, licence type or a Traffic Department instruction.

Residents should keep their QID and immigration status valid. Driving with an expired or otherwise invalid licence can expose the driver to penalties and insurance problems.

## FAQs

### How long does it take to get a driving licence in Qatar?

There is no guaranteed standard timeframe.

The process depends on:

- Application eligibility
- Sponsor approval
- Driving-school schedule
- Course type
- Lesson availability
- Test appointments
- Whether the applicant passes each stage
- Whether additional training is required

A straightforward application may take several weeks, while limited test availability or failed attempts can extend the process to several months.

### How much does a Qatar driving licence cost in 2026?

For a non-Qatari resident applying for a light-vehicle licence, the published MOI fees include:

- QAR 150 for a three-month learner’s permit
- QAR 50 for one driving-test attempt
- QAR 250 for the five-year licence

The indicative minimum is therefore QAR 450 before the eyesight test, driving-school package and any repeat tests or additional services.

### Can I drive in Qatar with my foreign licence?

It depends on your status.

Visitors with a valid international driving permit may drive for up to six months. Eligible GCC residents visiting Qatar may use their valid GCC licence for up to three months.

Qatar residents generally need a Qatar driving licence. A resident holding a foreign licence may be eligible for a test-based or other approved route, depending on the licence and current Traffic Department decision.

### What is the minimum age to drive in Qatar?

The minimum age is:

- 18 for light vehicles and motorcycles
- 21 for heavy vehicles and constructional or agricultural equipment

### Do I have to go through a driving school?

Most new non-Qatari applicants are referred to an approved driving school.

Foreign-licence holders may be sent to a school for a direct competency test rather than a complete course. Applicants who do not pass the permitted tests may be required to complete full training.

### Does my profession affect my eligibility?

It can affect how the application is assessed, particularly for expatriates.

However, no dependable current public list covers every eligible or restricted occupation. Ask an approved school to check the profession shown on your QID through the current Traffic Department system before paying for training.

### Do I need an employer NOC?

The MOI’s published application requirements for non-Qataris include a sponsor letter approving the application and specifying the licence type.

The exact format may be handled electronically or through the driving school, depending on the current system and sponsorship arrangement.

### Do I need a full medical examination?

Not normally for an ordinary light-vehicle application.

Applicants must meet the physical-fitness standard and pass an eyesight test. A more detailed medical report may be required for disabilities or health conditions that could affect safe driving.

### Do I need another eye test when renewing?

The published online renewal steps do not list a new eye test as a universal requirement for every light-vehicle renewal.

Follow any additional requirement displayed in Metrash or issued by the Traffic Department for your individual case.
