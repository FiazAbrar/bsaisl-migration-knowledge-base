## Visa Attestation

Content: Pages

|                    |
|--------------------|
| **Housemaid Visa** |

The Sponsor needs to submit the housemaid visa through enlisted agents to the Embassy for attestation. Without attestation from the Embassy, the housemaid will not get clearance from Bangladesh authority to travel to Qatar. For Attestation of housemaid visa, the sponsor needs to submit the following documents through enlisted agent:

1.  Copy of the agreement between the sponsor and the housemaid;
2.  Copy of the visa;
3.  Copy of the insurance;
4.  Copy of the passport of the housemaid;
5.  Qatari ID and contact details of the Sponsor

The fees for housemaid visa attestation is QR 50.00 each. The Embassy receives documents from 7:30 AM to 2:00 PM from Sunday to Thursday and deliver the documents after three working days.

|                 |
|-----------------|
| **Family Visa** |

Husband/wife living in Qatar can submit visa copy of his/her spouse and children for attestation. The other required documents are as follows:

1.  Copy of the visa;
2.  Copy of the passports (husband, wife, children)
3.  Copy of the Nikahnama,

The Sponsor can submit the documents to the Embassy directly. The fees for family visa attestation is QR 50.00 each. The Embassy receives documents from 7:30 AM to 2:00 PM from Sunday to Thursday and deliver the documents after three working days.
