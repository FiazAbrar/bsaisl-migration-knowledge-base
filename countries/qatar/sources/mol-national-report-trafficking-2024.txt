       ­ Suspicion exclusion: Suspicion exclusion means that the Public Prosecution has conducted a judicial
           investigation into the case and changed the description of the offence to exclude the crime of human
           trafficking.

       ­ Save the case and copy: It means that the Public Prosecution investigated the case file, saved the case
           and ordered a copy of the case for referral to the competent prosecution or the competent authority.

       ­ Complaints and related cases: The term complaints and linked cases means that the prosecution re-
           ceived the case as a case that is not a human trafficking case, but after the public prosecution>s investiga-
           tions, the prosecution categorised it as a human trafficking case.
