## What is Transit Visa?

Generally, a transit visa is a **temporary travel authorization** that lets passengers pass through a foreign country to get to another. For example, if you are travelling from country A to country C through country B, you may need a transit visa from country B. However, a **Transit Visa is not required for most transit passengers** travelling through Qatar (if you are not planning to leave Doha Airport).

There may be certain situations when a visa is required (for example, you have booked separate tickets with two different airlines and need to collect your baggage to check in to the connecting flight).

#### Do I need a Transit Visa to travel via Qatar?

**A Transit Visa is not required** for transit travellers who are not planning to leave Doha Airport’s transit area.

You will need **a visa in two situations**:\
\(1\) You want to visit the city of Doha for sightseeing.\
\(2\) You have to collect your own baggage from one airline and check in with another airline.

If you are wondering if you need a transit visa for your layover in Qatar, [please check this article](https://www.dohaguides.com/transit-visa-for-qatar/). We have explained the different scenarios in detail.

## What is a Qatar Transit Visa?

**Qatar Transit Visa is an optional visa** which is only required if you want to leave the airport transit area for baggage collection or visit Doha city for sightseeing.

A Qatar Transit Visa allows travellers who are not eligible for [visa-free entry](https://www.dohaguides.com/qatar-visa-on-arrival-requirements-eligibility/) to leave Hamad International Airport during their layover. These visas are **valid for up to 96 hours (4 days)**, making them ideal for travellers with long stopovers who want to explore Doha.

### Key Details

- **Visa Fee:** QAR 100 per applicant
- **Processing Time:** Minimum 3 working days (excluding weekends and public holidays)
- **Validity:** Maximum 96 hours (up to 3 nights)
- **Eligibility:** Travellers from countries not eligible for visa-free entry ([check your nationality on Visit Qatar](https://www.visitqatar.qa/))

## How to Apply for a Qatar Transit Visa

### Required Documents

To apply for a Qatar Transit Visa, you must prepare the following:

- **Colour copy of your passport** showing your full name, nationality, date and place of birth, passport number, issue and expiry dates. Include pages with parents’ names if listed.
- **Valid passport** with at least **three months validity** at the time of travel
- **6×6 cm colour photograph** (minimum 600×600 pixels) with a white background, showing at least 80% of your face
- All documents must be **JPEG or PDF format**, and **not exceed 1 MB**

### Application Process

1.  If you want to apply online, go to the [**Discover Qatar website**.](https://www.discoverqatar.qa/transit-visa-application-form/overview)
2.  Select your **arrival date**.
3.  Submit the required documents online or at the **Discover Qatar Transit Desk** at HIA.
4.  Pay the **QAR 100 visa fee**.
5.  Receive visa approval (minimum 3 working days for online applications).

**Note:** The transit visa **cannot be extended beyond 96 hours**.

Important: All visas are approved and issued at the sole discretion of Qatar’s Ministry of Interior. If your visa application is rejected, Discover Qatar offers a full refund minus a QAR 25 service fee.

## Transit vs Stopover: What’s the Difference?

| Type | Duration | Requirements | Point of Sale |
|----|----|----|----|
| Transit | Up to 12 hours | Confirmed Discover Qatar transit tour | HIA Transit Desk |
| Stopover | 12–96 hours | Confirmed QR/Code-share airline ticket and hotel booking | Online via Discover Qatar |

Both options allow travelers to leave the airport and explore Doha, but the **stopover visa** is ideal for longer layovers and allows you to stay overnight at a hotel.

## Fees, Refunds, and Terms

- **Transit Visa Fee:** QAR 100 per applicant
- **Refunds:** Full refund minus QAR 25 service fee if rejected
- **Visa Extension:** Not possible beyond 96 hours
- **Approval:** All visas issued at the **discretion of Qatar’s Ministry of Interior**

## FAQ On Qatar Transit Visa

------------------------------------------------------------------------

#### Do I need a visa for transiting via Doha?

No, you do not need a visa to transit in Doha at Hamad International Airport. A visa is only required if you want to leave the airport and enter Qatar.

#### How much does Qatar Transit Visa cost?

Qatar Transit Visa costs QAR 100 per applicant.

#### Can I extend my Qatar Transit Visa?

No. The visa cannot be extended for more than 96 hours.

#### Do I get a complimentary hotel stay with a Qatar Transit Visa?

No. Any hotel or accommodation must be arranged and paid for by the passenger(s).

#### Should I collect my baggage if I have a Transit Visa?

If your stopover is more than 24 hours, baggage must be collected in Doha. If less than 24 hours, you have the option to collect at your final destination.

#### Can I leave Doha Airport in transit?

You can leave Doha Airport only if you have a valid Qatar visa (visa on arrival, transit visa, etc.)
