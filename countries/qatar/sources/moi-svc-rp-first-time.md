##### Fees

1\. Personal Sponsorship Work permit fee: QR 100 annually . Residence permit fee: QR 300 annually + QR 100 for ID card (as per the validity of residence permit). Fee for obtaining residence permit or renewing it for those who are on parent sponsorship, accompanying persons, those who got separate residences permits and new born babies including additions and deletion in passports: QR 400 2. Company Sponsorship Work permit fee: QR 100 annually Residence permit fee: QR 1000 annually + QR 100 for ID card (for the period of residence permit.

##### Terms & Conditions

Conditions & Requirements:

A\) Requirements for RP on Personal Sponsorship

    Print the residence permit application form and sign it by the sponsor

    Submit the original passport of the employee

    Photo of the employee

    Blood group certificate

 

B\) Requirements for RP on Company Sponsorship

\

    Labour contract attested by Labour Department

    Print the residence permit application form and sign by the sponsor

    Submit original passport of the employee

    Copy of company's establishment ID

    Photo of the employee

    Blood group certificate
