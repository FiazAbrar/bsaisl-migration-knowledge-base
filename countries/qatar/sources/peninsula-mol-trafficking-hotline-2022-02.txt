                                                        Ministry of Labour launches hotline to report human trafficking crimes

Published:
                                                        20 Feb 2022 - 03:09 pm
                                                        | Last Updated:
                                                        20 Feb 2022 - 03:10 pm

 File photo

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                QNA

Doha: The Ministry of Labour, represented by the National Committee to Combat Human Trafficking, announced the launch of direct means of communication to report complaints regarding abuses related to human trafficking cases and crimes.

In a statement, the ministry urged the public to report any violations or complaints related to human trafficking crimes via the hotline 16044, and the e-mail: 
[email protected]
.

The ministry's allocation of direct means of communication comes as part of Qatar's efforts to combat human trafficking, based on the state's commitment to human rights, and within the framework of its National Vision 2030.
