Steps at QVC
Submit your appointment letter, and original and valid copy of your passport and other documents as per the checklist for verification
Receive a token and be seated in the waiting area for your number to be called
Upon token being called, please head to the designated biometric booth
Enroll your fingerprints, iris scan, facial capture and sign the employment contract – if applicable
Steps at Medical Facility at QVC
Head to the Medical Facility reception counter with same token
Register at the reception
Receive a token and be seated in the waiting area for your number to be called
Upon token being called, please head to the nurse’s station for standard medical parameter measurement
You are then redirected to the Nurse room where blood sample is collected, vaccination administered and eyesight recorded
This is followed by X-Ray imaging
Lastly, the visit to the medical facility is concluded with the visit to the Doctor for a general physical examination, after finishing this step, the applicant may leave QVC
Security Regulations at QVC
Access to the Qatar Visa Center is restricted to applicants only. A guardian must accompany applicants below the age of 16. Exceptions can be made whereby an applicant over the age of 60 is allowed to be accompanied by a caretaker. This exception also applies to applicants with a physical disability and interpreters for the hearing impaired.
As per our security regulations, applicants are prohibited from possessing the below items when entering QVC
All battery operated or electronic gadgets such as cameras, compact discs, laptops, or portable music players or any other electronic storage devices such as USBs
All bags such as travel bags, back packs, briefcases, suitcases, leather, jute or cloth bags and zip folders. Only a plastic bag containing your application related papers would be permitted
Sealed envelopes or packages
Any flammable item such as matchboxes, lighters, fuel etc
Any sharp objects such as scissors, pen knives or nail filers
Weapon or weapon like objects or explosive material of any kind
Other items may be prohibited based on the discretion of the security and or QVC officials
There is no facility at the Qatar Visa Centre to store prohibited items. Applicants are requested to make alternate arrangements to safeguard their belongings.
For security reasons applicants may be subjected to physical frisking. Handheld / archway metal detectors are safe for all including pregnant women and heart patients with cardiac pacemakers.
Applicants are requested to cooperate with QVC officials & employees. In case of having any serious concerns, contact the Qatar Visa Center Manager.
