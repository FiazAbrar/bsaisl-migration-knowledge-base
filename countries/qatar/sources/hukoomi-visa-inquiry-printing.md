# Visa Inquiry and Printing

Ministry of Interior

Service Type

E-Service

Last updated

18 Sep 2022

## Service Description

You can check the latest status on a visa application. Once an application has been approved, you may pay online to proceed.

If the application has been rejected, you might click on the Remarks icon to view the reasons for rejection. If your application is still under processing, you can only view remarks provided by the Ministry of Interior (MOI).

## Steps

- Enter visa number or passport number.
- Select nationality.
- Click on "Submit".
- Pay fees and print the visa once it is issued.

## Fees

Print fees vary according to visa type.

## Additional Information

For more information and details, please check the contact details page on MOI website.
