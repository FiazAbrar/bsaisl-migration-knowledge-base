### FACTSHEET

## Qatar

March 2026

While a small country with a population of about 3 million, mostly expatriates, Qatar is one of the wealthiest nations due to its vast natural gas reserves.

##### UNHCR PRESENCE

##### Staff:

##### Office:

UNHCR’s office in Qatar is hosted inside the UN House in **Lusail,** offered by MOFA and inaugurated in March 2023.

This followed the signing of a Host Country Agreement between UNHCR and the State of Qatar in December 2018, which established the framework for UNHCR’s presence in the country.

#### Partnerships and Resource Mobilisation

■ In December 2025, UNHCR and the **Ministry of Interior** signed a Memorandum of Understanding to support the implementation of Qatar’s Political Asylum Law, passed in 2018, by providing legal and technical expertise to develop and enforce national asylum procedures. As the first country to enact asylum legislation in the region, the aim is a to lay the foundation for positioning Qatar as a center of excellence in protection.
