Tourist visas by hotels and approved travel agencies

This visa is granted to the hotels and travel agencies accredited by Qatar National Tourism Council.

Terms of submission:

Fill the application form and submitting it after signature by the concerned hotel.

Attach a clear copy of the passport of the coming person and give the required details (name - date of birth - passport number - expiry date - sex - nationality).

A copy of valid registration of the company.

A copy of the hotel reservation request.

A fee (QR 100) by credit card.

The application form (attached copy) shall be submitted by the hotel manager or his representative.

Online Tourist Visa

Individuals not eligible for visa-free entry or ETA can apply for a Tourist Visa online.  Visitors to Qatar travelling on board any airline may apply for a Tourist Visa through the 
Qatar Visa Service
.  The cost of the Qatar Tourist Visa is QAR 100.  Tourist Visa holders are allowed to stay in Qatar for up to 30 days on a single trip.

To submit a request for a Qatar Tourist Visa, visitors are required to:

Complete an online form.

Upload required documents (including passport scans & personal photographs).

Provide an airline booking reservation.

Make an online payment using a valid Visa or Mastercard.

Once the application is complete, visitors will be contacted via e-mail with a response to their visa application(
All visas are approved and issued at the sole discretion of Qatar's Ministry of Interior
