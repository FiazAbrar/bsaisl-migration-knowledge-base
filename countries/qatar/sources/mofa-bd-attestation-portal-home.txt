Bangladesh e-Apostille
The e-Apostille initiative is a digital platform introduced by the Government of Bangladesh to streamline and modernize the authentication and legalization of public documents for international use. This initiative aligns with the Hague Apostille Convention (1961), which simplifies the process of document authentication by eliminating the need for further legalization by embassies or other foreign authorities...

Our Mission, Vision and Values
Our Mission
To provide a fast, secure, and globally recognized document authentication system through e-Apostille, reducing time, cost, and bureaucratic hurdles for Bangladeshi citizens seeking authentication for education, employment, migration, and legal documents.
Our Vision
To make Bangladesh a forerunner in digital authentication by ensuring seamless international recognition of public documents, eliminating manual verification, and integrating with global best practices for a hassle-free attestation process.
Our Values
Efficiency – Quick and fully digital attestation via myGov
Transparency – Secure QR-coded authentication, no intermediaries.
Cost Savings – No need for Embassy visits, saving substantive amount of money yearly
Global Compliance – Accepted in 114 Apostille Convention countries.
User-Centric – 24/7 access, mobile updates, and easy document tracking.
Innovation – Paperless, and automated verification
