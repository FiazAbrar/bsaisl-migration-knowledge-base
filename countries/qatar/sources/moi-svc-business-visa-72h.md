##### Terms & Conditions

- To produce the necessary document that proves his profession as businessman
- The visitor should be coming for visiting an accredited company in the country.
- The visitor should hold a credit card with QR 5000 balance or its equivalent amount in cash.
- The visitor should have a hotel reservation for the period of stay.
- He should hold a return ticket.
