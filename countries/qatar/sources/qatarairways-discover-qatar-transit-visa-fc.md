## Discover Qatar Transit Visa

Transit and Stopover visitors who do not qualify for 
visa-on-arrival can now apply for a Transit Visa with 
Discover Qatar for only QAR 100.

| Passenger type | Transit (up to 12 hours transit) | Stopover (12 – 96 hours transit) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Point of Sale | Discover Qatar Transit Desk at HIA | Online with Stopover booking flow |
| Processing time | Immediate | 4 working days |
| Requirements | Confirmed DQ transit tour booking. Confirmed QR/Code-share airline ticket, with a maximum transit in Doha of 96 hours. | Confirmed stopover DQ/QRH hotel booking. Confirmed QR/Code-share airline ticket, with a maximum transit in Doha of 96 hours. |

### Terms & Conditions

• In case of visa rejection customers will receive a full refund less a nonrefundable QAR 25 service fee.

• Visa validity cannot be extended beyond 96 hours.

• Visa fee is non-refundable once the visa is approved.

• If the Discover Qatar booking is used to apply for a visa, it becomes nonrefundable.

• All visa rules and policies for international visitors are subject to change 
without notice and are approved and issued at the sole discretion of Qatar's 
Ministry of Interior.
