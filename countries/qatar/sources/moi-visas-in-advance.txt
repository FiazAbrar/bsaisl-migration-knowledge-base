Visas in advance

Visas in advance
:

Visas in advance are the normal visas that travelers to Qatar are given prior to their travel to enter the country. This visa should be presented at the arrival port .

Visas issued in advance: 

Business visa (90) days

Tourist visas through hotels and approved travel agencies

Family visas (Personal visit visas)

Transit visa through Qatar Airways (96 hours)
