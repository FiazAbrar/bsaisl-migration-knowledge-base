##### Terms & Conditions

- The applying company or establishment should have prior approval by concerned departments.
- Fill in the application form and submit through company representative or owner.
- Submit the documents that prove the visitor is a businessman.
- He should have return air ticket. 
- Clear passport copy of the visitor.
