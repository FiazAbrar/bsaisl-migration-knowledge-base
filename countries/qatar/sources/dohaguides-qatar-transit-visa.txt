What is Transit Visa?

Generally, a transit visa is a 
temporary travel authorization
 that lets passengers pass through a foreign country to get to another. For example, if you are travelling from country A to country C through country B, you may need a transit visa from country B. However, a 
Transit Visa is not required for most transit passengers
 travelling through Qatar (if you are not planning to leave Doha Airport).

There may be certain situations when a visa is required (for example, you have booked separate tickets with two different airlines and need to collect your baggage to check in to the connecting flight). 

Do I need a Transit Visa to travel via Qatar?

A Transit Visa is not required
 for transit travellers who are not planning to leave Doha Airport’s transit area.

You will need 
a visa in two situations
: 
(1) You want to visit the city of Doha for sightseeing.
(2) You have to collect your own baggage from one airline and check in with another airline.

If you are wondering if you need a transit visa for your layover in Qatar, 
please check this article
. We have explained the different scenarios in detail.

What is a Qatar Transit Visa?

Qatar Transit Visa is an optional visa
 which is only required if you want to leave the airport transit area for baggage collection or visit Doha city for sightseeing.

A Qatar Transit Visa allows travellers who are not eligible for 
visa-free entry
 to leave Hamad International Airport during their layover. These visas are 
valid for up to 96 hours (4 days)
, making them ideal for travellers with long stopovers who want to explore Doha.

Key Details

Visa Fee:
 QAR 100 per applicant

Processing Time:
 Minimum 3 working days (excluding weekends and public holidays)

Validity:
 Maximum 96 hours (up to 3 nights)

Eligibility:
 Travellers from countries not eligible for visa-free entry (
check your nationality on Visit Qatar
)

Discover Qatar Transit Tours

Waiting at the airport doesn’t have to be boring. If your transit is 6 hours or more, you can 
join a city tour of Doha
, visit architectural landmarks, explore the cultural treasures, or even take a desert adventure to the Inland Sea. For shorter transit times of 3 to 6 hours, you can enjoy the Discover Relaxation at the Airport program with massages and wellness treatments.

Discover Qatar handles all arrangements, ensuring you return to the airport on time for your connecting flight.

Transit Tour Booking Locations at HIA

Main Desk – Duty Free Plaza South:

Located behind the escalators

Handles transit tour bookings and transit visa assistance

Serves as the meeting point for all tours

North Desk – North Node, Concourse C:

Located between the HIA Information Desk and Joe & The Juice

Handles last-minute bookings and real-time availability

If your departure is imminent or you are already at the airport, you can book your transit tour at either of the desks for immediate availability.

How to Apply for a Qatar Transit Visa

Required Documents

To apply for a Qatar Transit Visa, you must prepare the following:

Colour copy of your passport
 showing your full name, nationality, date and place of birth, passport number, issue and expiry dates. Include pages with parents’ names if listed.

Valid passport
 with at least 
three months validity
 at the time of travel

6×6 cm colour photograph
 (minimum 600×600 pixels) with a white background, showing at least 80% of your face

All documents must be 
JPEG or PDF format
, and 
not exceed 1 MB

Application Process

If you want to apply online, go to the 
Discover Qatar website
.

Select your 
arrival date
.

Submit the required documents online or at the 
Discover Qatar Transit Desk
 at HIA.

Pay the 
QAR 100 visa fee
.

Receive visa approval (minimum 3 working days for online applications).

Note:
 The transit visa 
cannot be extended beyond 96 hours
.

Important: All visas are approved and issued at the sole discretion of Qatar’s Ministry of Interior. If your visa application is rejected, Discover Qatar offers a full refund minus a QAR 25 service fee.

FAQ On Qatar Transit Visa

Do I need a visa for transiting via Doha?

No, you do not need a visa to transit in Doha at Hamad International Airport. A visa is only required if you want to leave the airport and enter Qatar.

How much does Qatar Transit Visa cost?

Qatar Transit Visa costs QAR 100 per applicant.

Can I extend my Qatar Transit Visa?

No. The visa cannot be extended for more than 96 hours.

Do I get a complimentary hotel stay with a Qatar Transit Visa?

No. Any hotel or accommodation must be arranged and paid for by the passenger(s).

Should I collect my baggage if I have a Transit Visa?

If your stopover is more than 24 hours, baggage must be collected in Doha. If less than 24 hours, you have the option to collect at your final destination.

Can I leave Doha Airport in transit?

You can leave Doha Airport only if you have a valid Qatar visa (visa on arrival, transit visa, etc.)
