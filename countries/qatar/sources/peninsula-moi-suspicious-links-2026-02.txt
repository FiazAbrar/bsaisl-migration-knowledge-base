                                                        Ministry of Interior warns against suspicious links to prevent cybercrimes

Published:
                                                        01 Feb 2026 - 10:07 am
                                                        | Last Updated:
                                                        01 Feb 2026 - 10:09 am

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                The Peninsula 

Doha, Qatar: The Ministry of Interior has urged the public to remain vigilant against cybercrimes by avoiding interaction with suspicious electronic links that falsely claim affiliation with government institutions or ministries within the country.

In a public awareness message, the Ministry emphasised that public awareness is a key line of defence against cyber threats, calling on residents to verify the authenticity of digital messages before responding to them.
