# قرار وزير العمل رقم (50) لسنة 2026 بتعديل بعض أحكام القرار رقم (4) لسنة 2015 بضوابط نظام حماية أجور العمال  الخاضعين لقانون العمل

قرار وزير العمل رقم (50) لسنة 2026 بتعديل بعض أحكام القرار رقم (4) لسنة 2015 بضوابط نظام حماية أجور العمال  الخاضعين لقانون العمل

## المواد (1-3)


### Article 1

The phrase "Ministry of Labour" shall be substituted for the phrase "Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs" as stated in Articles (1), (3) of Minister of Labour and Social Affairs Decision No. (4) of 2015 referred to above.

### Article 2

The text of Article (2) of Decision of the Minister of Labour and Social Affairs No. (4) of 2015 referred to above is replaced with the following text:

	Article (2):

	« Employers are required to transfer the wages of employees hired on an annual or monthly wage to the employee's account in financial institutions within the State, within seven days from the date such wages are due, through the Wage Protection System (WPS). The wage of the employee is due on the first day of every Gregorian month. 

	As for the wages of other employees, they are due on the first day of every two weeks.»

### Article 3

All relevant authorities, each within its own competence, must implement this Decision / Resolution, and it shall be effective from the day following its publication in the Official Gazette.
