#### Single Entry on-Arrival Visa for those accompanying GCC Citizens for a fee of (QR100) for (30) days, extendable

On-arrival visa f for the accompaniers of the citizens of the GCC countries, single trip, for a fee of (100) riyals, for (30) days, which can be extended.

This visa is granted to servants or the like, who enter with the accompanying citizen sponsor or with any of the first degree relatives of the sponsor. The citizen is not allowed to leave the country being the accompanier inside the country.

**Terms and requirements:**

1- Passport valid for at least three months

2 - Valid residence permit of not less than 6 months

3- Confirmed round-trip ticket

4- Health insurance from one of the accredited [insurance companies](https://www.moph.gov.qa/arabic/derpartments/policyaffairs/hfid/hirs/insurancecompanies/Pages/default.aspx).
