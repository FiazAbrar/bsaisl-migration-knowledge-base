                            About Jusour

Jusour is a private joint-stock company owned by the Government of Qatar, with a mission to transform and elevate Qatar’s labor market. By fostering innovation and providing diverse opportunities, Jusour plays a vital role in supporting the goals of Qatar National Vision 2030 and the Third National Development Strategy.

                            Explore Our Programs

Sahem  "Empowering Your Future"

Sahem

"Sahem" Program offers part-time job opportunities specifically designed for students studying in accredited university in Qatar and people with disabilities . By offering practical work experience,"Sahem" program helps beneficiaries build their career paths and supports the country's advancement toward a knowledge-based economy.

Tawzeef "Your Gateway to Opportunity"

Tawzeef

It offers a comprehensive platform that connects job seekers with employers and recruitment agencies in Qatar, one of the world's leading economies and an ideal place to live and work. Utilizing advanced employment solutions powered by artificial intelligence, the portal is designed to meet the needs of job seekers and employers at all levels, ensuring a seamless and efficient recruitment process.

Tadweer "Your Alternative Path"

Tadweer

"Tadweer" is Jusour’s dedicated workforce-sustainability flagship program. Through Tadweer, companies facing financial challenges can reduce their workforce, allowing eligible companies to retrain and rehire these employees to meet changing needs. Companies can reskill and redeploy workers to meet changing demands, while expatriate employees are provided with opportunities to reintegrate into the labor market.

Right to Access Information – Jusour

In adherence to the principles of transparency and in order to strengthen trust between Qatar Manpower Solutions (Jusour) and the community, Jusour is committed to implementing the provisions of Law No. (9) of 2022 on the Regulation of the Right to Access Information. This law guarantees to individuals the right to request information and access data that is not classified as confidential, in accordance with the rules and procedures stipulated by law.

In this context, any individual may submit a request for information related to Jusour through the official channels designated for this purpose. Requests will be processed in accordance with the legally defined timeframes and procedures, ensuring transparency and the protection of legal rights for all parties.

Scope of the Right to Access Information:

Individuals have the right to submit a formal request to access available information, in accordance with legal provisions, via email at - 
[email protected]

Please ensure that the subject line of your email states “Request for Access to Information” to facilitate and expedite the processing of your request.

Requests will be handled with integrity and transparency, ensuring compliance with the standards set by Law No. (9) of 2022.

Jusour is committed to providing responses within the legally defined timeframes. Please note that some information may be subject to legal restrictions or exemptions.

For more information on how to submit requests for access to information, please contact us through the official communication channels. At Jusour, we believe that transparency is the foundation for building trust and achieving excellence in workforce solutions.
