# Test Registration Process

## Before making a reservation for the test

1.  ①Access [Prometric's registration website (hereinafter called the registration website)![Open in new window](/jft-basic/common/img/common/icon_blank.png)](https://www.prometric-jp.com/en/ssw/test_list/archives/1) and obtain a Prometric ID.
2.  ②Check the test date and test venue for the country where you will take the test at the registration website.
3.  ③Please make a reservation on the registration website by three business days before you wish to take the test (four business days before if taking the test on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday). You can also make changes to your reserved test date, time, or venue through the registration website.

## Test Registration Process

1\. If you are paying the test fee by credit card or e-wallet

1.  ![](/jft-basic/schedule/img/ico_other_login.png)Log in to the registration website with your Prometric ID
2.  ![](/jft-basic/schedule/img/ico_other_select.png)Select the test date, test venue and test start time
3.  ![](/jft-basic/schedule/img/ico_other_pay.png)Enter your payment information and pay the test fee
4.  ![](/jft-basic/schedule/img/ico_other_finish.png)Reservation complete

2\. If you are purchasing a voucher

- \*To find out which countries vouchers can be used in and how to purchase them, check [Payment methods by country![Open in new window](/jft-basic/common/img/common/icon_blank.png)](https://www.prometric-jp.com/en/ssw/exam/payment/) and [Voucher![Open in new window](/jft-basic/common/img/common/icon_blank.png)](https://www.prometric-jp.com/en/ssw/exam/voucher/).

1.  ![](/jft-basic/schedule/img/ico_other_voucher.png)Purchase via [Voucher Express![Open in new window](/jft-basic/common/img/common/icon_blank.png)](https://vouchersglobal.prometric.com/my.policy) (for companies and organizations only)\
    or\
    pay in cash at a [voucher sales counter![Open in new window](/jft-basic/common/img/common/icon_blank.png)](https://www.prometric-jp.com/en/ssw/exam/voucher/sales/)
2.  ![](/jft-basic/schedule/img/ico_other_login.png)Log in to the registration website with your Prometric ID
3.  ![](/jft-basic/schedule/img/ico_other_select.png)Select the test date, test venue and test start time
4.  ![](/jft-basic/schedule/img/ico_other_input.png)Enter the voucher number and expiration date
5.  ![](/jft-basic/schedule/img/ico_other_finish.png)Reservation complete

- \*Payment methods for the test fee differ depending on the country. For details, check the [Payment methods by country![Open in new window](/jft-basic/common/img/common/icon_blank.png)](https://www.prometric-jp.com/en/ssw/exam/payment/).

Before taking the test, review [Steps for Taking the Test![Open in new window](/jft-basic/common/img/common/icon_blank.png)](https://www.prometric-jp.com/en/ssw/exam/flow/) and [supplementary materials for test navigation![Open in new window](/jft-basic/common/img/common/icon_blank.png)](https://www.prometric-jp.com/en/ssw/test_sub/jft/operation/)

Take the test

After the test is over, the test results (total score and assessment results) will be displayed on the computer screen

Within 5 business days after taking the test, [the notification of assessment results](../about/index.html#se06) will be issued on the registration website
