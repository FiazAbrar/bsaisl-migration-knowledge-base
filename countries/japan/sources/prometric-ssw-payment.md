# Payment methods by country

1.  [TOP](https://www.prometric-jp.com/en/ssw/)
2.  [Examination information](https://www.prometric-jp.com/en/ssw/exam/)
3.  Payment Methods by Country

**【Notes】**

**When using a credit card, debit card, or prepaid card**

- Please prepare a card that supports 3D Secure.

**When using e-wallet or online banking**

- The e-wallets and online banking services available may change without notice.

**When using Voucher**

- For information on purchasing Voucher, please see [the "Examination Information Voucher](/en/ssw/exam/voucher/)" page.

The payment methods available in the country where you take the test vary. Please select and confirm the country where you will take the test.

![](/files/bgeditor/img/1323__aW1nX3Nzdy1leGFtLXBheW1lbnRfMDE-d-.jpg)

- Mastercard, VISA, JCB, and AMEX are only available.
- Please use a 3D secure card when making a reservation.

- For detailed payment methods, please see "[Paying with Online Services](https://paypay.ne.jp/guide/net-service/)" on the PayPay website.

## India

- Mastercard and VISA are only available.

**online banking**

- When making reservations on a PC, please have your smartphone handy. When making payment, you will need to scan the QR code with your smartphone.
- When using Online Banking, the transfer destination may show a name other than Prometric, and the name may change depending on the bank and time of the transfer.
- Due to the nature of the payment system, the exam fee displayed on the payment screen may fluctuate by less than ±1 THB, but the actual amount deducted will be the exam fee (excluding VAT).
