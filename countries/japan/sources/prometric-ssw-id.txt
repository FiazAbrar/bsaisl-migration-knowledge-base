Country-Identification Document

【Notes】

Identification Document must be originals (copies are invalid) and within their validity period. Electronic data such as smartphone data cannot be used for the exam.

If the facial photograph on Identification Document has been altered or edited, and your identity cannot be verified, including through AI authentication, you will not be able to take the exam.

If it is determined that the authenticity of Identification Document cannot be verified at Test Center, you will not be allowed to take the exam.

The name, passport number (or NIK number), and date of birth listed on Identification Document must exactly match the information registered with Prometric ID.

Even if you are unable to take the exam due to incomplete Identification Document, the exam fee will not be refunded.
