Test Registration Process

Before making a reservation for the test

①Access 
Prometric's registration website (hereinafter called the registration website)
 and obtain a Prometric ID.

②Check the test date and test venue for the country where you will take the test at the registration website.

③Please make a reservation on the registration website by three business days before you wish to take the test (four business days before if taking the test on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday). You can also make changes to your reserved test date, time, or venue through the registration website.

Test Registration Process

1. If you are paying the test fee by credit card or e-wallet

Log in to the registration website with your Prometric ID

Select the test date, test venue and test start time

Enter your payment information and pay the test fee

Reservation complete

2. If you are purchasing a voucher

*To find out which countries vouchers can be used in and how to purchase them, check
