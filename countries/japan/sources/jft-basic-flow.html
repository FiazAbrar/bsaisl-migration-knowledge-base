Test Registration Process

Before making a reservation for the test

  1. ①Access Prometric's registration website (hereinafter called the registration website) and obtain a Prometric ID.
  2. ②Check the test date and test venue for the country where you will take the test at the registration website.
  3. ③Please make a reservation on the registration website by three business days before you wish to take the test (four business days before if taking the test on a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday). You can also make changes to your reserved test date, time, or venue through the registration website.

Test Registration Process

1. If you are paying the test fee by credit card or e-wallet

  1. Log in to the registration website with your Prometric ID
  2. Select the test date, test venue and test start time
  3. Enter your payment information and pay the test fee
  4. Reservation complete

2. If you are purchasing a voucher

  • *To find out which countries vouchers can be used in and how to purchase them, check Payment methods by country and Voucher.
  1. Purchase via Voucher Express (for companies and organizations only)
    or
    pay in cash at a voucher sales counter
  2. Log in to the registration website with your Prometric ID
  3. Select the test date, test venue and test start time
  4. Enter the voucher number and expiration date
  5. Reservation complete
  • *Payment methods for the test fee differ depending on the country. For details, check the Payment methods by country.

Before taking the test, review Steps for Taking the Test and supplementary materials for test navigation

Take the test

After the test is over, the test results (total score and assessment results) will be displayed on the computer screen

Within 5 business days after taking the test, the notification of assessment results will be issued on the registration website
