Registration Guide

How to Confirm Registration for JLPT

Complete your registration in just four simple steps.

Create Profile

Create your JLPT account by completing the registration process.

Complete Profile

  • Sign in to your account.
  • Complete all required profile information.
  • Once your profile reaches 100% completion, your JLPT Code will be generated.
  • Save your JLPT Code and wait for the confirm registration period to open.

Confirm Registration

  • Click the "Confirm Registration" button.
  • You will be redirected to the JLPT Confirm Registration Portal.
  • Enter your JLPT Code and registered email address.
  • Select your desired JLPT proficiency level.
  • Click "Confirm Registration" to complete your registration.

Complete Payment

  • Complete your payment within 10 minutes.
  • Your registration will not be confirmed if payment is not completed within the allotted time.
