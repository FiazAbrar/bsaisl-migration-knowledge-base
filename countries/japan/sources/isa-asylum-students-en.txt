If you are an international student and you are considering submitting an application for refugee
recognition, please read the following.

                                                                                                       February 2018
                                                                       Immigration Bureau, Ministry of Justice

    Previously, in principle, the operations of the Ministry of Justice permitted foreign nationals
residing legitimately in Japan, who had submitted applications for refugee recognition, to work in
Japan after 6 months had passed from the time of the submission of their applications, but from
January 15 of this year, the management of the status of residence for applicants, who are
undergoing the refugee recognition procedures, has been revised.

    Specifically, in the case of an international student, if you applied for refugee recognition
after being expelled or dropping out from your educational institution, or graduating from your
educational institution, in principle, you will not be permitted to work in Japan even after 6
months have passed from the time of the submission of your application.
(Note 1. It is possible for you to apply for refugee recognition during the period of your studies in

         Japan.)
(Note 2. You will be permitted to work only if there is a high likelihood that you are a refugee as

         defined under the 1951 Refugee Convention, or if there is a high likelihood that
         humanitarian consideration needs to be given due to the situation in your home country.)

    If you feel like the educational institution where you are a student has committed an act
violating any laws or regulations, please contact your nearest regional immigration bureau.
