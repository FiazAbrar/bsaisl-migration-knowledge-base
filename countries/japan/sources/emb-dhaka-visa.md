# Visa

2024/10/24

### General Information

During the busy periods of mid-March to early June and mid-September to late October each year, we are receiving so many visa applications, mainly from international students. As a result you may have to wait more than one month to get your desired appointment at VFS.

To apply for a visa through VFS, you must make an online reservation on their website. Please note that reservations for the month after next are generally released available on the 15th of each month.

Furthermore, in principle, visa applications through VFS must be made in advance by the company's website, however there are cases where reservations are cancelled on the same day, If you are in a hurry, please visit VFS directly to check whether it is possible to apply on the same day (first-come, first-served) by waiting for cancellations.

Please note that acceptance of applications from the waiting list depends on the situation on the day, and we cannot guarantee that you will be able to apply via the waiting list.

Please contact VFS for more details regarding appointment reservations.

### Application for Japan visa after 3rd November, 2024

From 3rd November, 2024 onwards, the Embassy of Japan will entrust VFS Global with the facilitation of the Japan visa application process. The Embassy of Japan will no longer accept visa applications directly from the visa applicants after 3rd November, 2024.

For all inquiries related to visa applications, we kindly request that you reach out directly to the VFS Global.

2 Visa examination period

The standard processing time is a minimum of 7 working days from the date of application at VFS Global to the return of your passport. However, any day on which either the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan or the Embassy of Japan in Bangladesh is closed for a public holiday will not be counted as a working day. Please note that processing may take longer if additional documents are requested or if the submitted documents require verification. We therefore recommend that you apply well in advance.
