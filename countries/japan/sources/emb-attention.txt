Attention

“Beware of Deceptive Japanese Visa/Job Ads"

Dhaka: 03 December 2014

Beware of Deceptive Japanese Visa/Job Ads.  Embassy of Japan provides free education counseling service at the Embassy and JUAAB Office (26 Indira Road, Farmgate).  The Embassy does not source out visa processing to any private companies.  It is a total fraud about full time job availability in Japan.
