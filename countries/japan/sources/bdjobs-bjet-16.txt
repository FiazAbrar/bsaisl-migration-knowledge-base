Overview:

B-JET: “Bangladesh-Japan ICT  Engineers’ Training Program” implemented within the JICA technical cooperation  project, established by the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA) and  the Bangladesh Computer Council (BCC) in 2017. The program has produced over  400 graduates, with approximately 250 alumni successfully hired by more than 80  leading Japanese companies.

The program is now jointly operated  by Shinko Shuppansha Keirinkan — a leading Japanese educational publisher — and  North South University (NSU), the premier university in Bangladesh. Training is  conducted at the dedicated B-JET Centre within the NSU campus. Graduates from  all universities across Bangladesh are welcome to apply.

The 16th Batch of  B-JET offers passionate engineers a unique opportunity to learn Japanese  language, culture, and business etiquette alongside advanced IT training —  opening doors to career opportunities in Japan’s thriving tech industry.

Requirements:

CSE/IT  graduates, especially the class of 2025 or 2026, are encouraged to apply.

Graduates  from all universities across Bangladesh are welcome.

Bachelor’s  Degree in CSE, IT, or ICT-related subjects is required.

Candidates  with practical working experience in programming, software development, AI, or  Machine Learning are preferable.

Good  knowledge of modern programming languages, object-oriented programming, and  strong problem-solving ability is preferable.

Strong  interest in learning the Japanese language.

Self-learning  mindset, good communication skills in English, and willingness to work in the  Japanese ICT market.

Genuine desire to build a  long-term career in Japan’s IT sector.

Class Contents:

Nihongo (Japanese Language)  Class: 
The Nihongo classes are designed to  teach trainees basic Japanese and help them effectively communicate with  Japanese people for working in Japan’s IT sector. Trainees receive guidance to  attempt JLPT N5 and N4 level exams, aiming for N3 level, conducted by  experienced Japanese language instructors.

Selection Process:

Step 1 – Application Submission: 
Submit your online application via the registration form.

Step 2 – Initial Application  Review: 
Submitted applications are  reviewed; shortlisted candidates will be notified by email.

Step 3 – Coding Test: 
Shortlisted candidates will be invited to an online coding  test (anti-cheating tab/screen restrictions apply).

Step 4 – AI Skills Interview: 
Candidates who pass the coding test will proceed to an  AI-powered skills interview.

Step 5 – Japanese Language Test  & General Interview: 
Candidates who  pass the AI interview will be invited to a Japanese Language Test and General  Interview.

Step 6 – Ideathon: 
Final-stage candidates will be required to submit an  innovative project idea with a demo video.
