Implementation of consultation services for technical intern trainee

Handling consultations of technical intern trainee

Technical Intern Trainee can consult the Organization for Technical Intern Training on various matters.
Regarding the principal language, decide on the day of the week then call or e-mail and consult in your
native language. Also, even local offices prepare an interpreter during the time of consultation.

Concerning declaration in Article 49 Item 1 of Technical Intern Training Act

The Technical Intern Training Act Article 49 Item 1 states that when a technical
 intern trainee experience unlawful act from the supervising organization or from the company where the
training is conducted , you can report what was done to you.
If the technical intern trainee contacts the organization ( OTIT ), you can file a report in the language of
your country.

Pertaining to the implemented day and time of consultation in your native
language

If you are a technical intern trainee , anyone can report or consult by phone, e-mail or letter. The phone
call charge is zero yen.
