Test information

Exam Name Japan Foundation Test for Basic Japanese(JFT-Basic)
Eligibility
  • Must be a person whose native language is not Japanese.
  • If you are an Indonesian national, you must be at least 18 years old on the test date.
  • If you are a Myanmar national, you must be at least 17 years old on the test date.
  • Those with Japanese nationality are not eligible to take the test.
*If you are taking the exam in Japan, you must have a Japanese residence status (short-term stay is also possible)
Retake Policy You cannot take the same exam again for 45 days starting from the day after the exam date.
Each exam has a designated reservation period and exam period. During the reservation period, please make a reservation for an exam date that is 45 days or more after the day following your exam date, within the exam period.
Once you have achieved a total score of 200 points or more, you are not eligible to retake the exam.
Implementation format CBT(Computer Based Testing)
Question content 4 sections below
  • letters and vocabulary
  • conversation and expression
  • listening comprehension
  • reading comprehension
The number of questions Approximately 50 questions
Test time 60分

Notes

Eligibility

  • If you are taking the exam in Japan, you must have a status of residence and a passport issued by a competent authority of a foreign government or region designated by the Minister of Justice in a public notice as one that will cooperate in the smooth execution of deportation orders.
  • Even if you pass the exam, this does not guarantee that you will be granted a "Specified Skilled Worker" or "Training Worker" residence status, or that your training work plan will be approved. Even if an application for a Certificate of Eligibility for Residence Status, an application for permission to change residence status, or an application for approval of a training work plan is submitted for those who have passed the exam, it does not necessarily mean that you will receive a Certificate of Eligibility for Residence Status or permission to change residence status. Furthermore, even if you receive a Certificate of Eligibility for Residence Status, your visa application will be reviewed separately by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and it does not necessarily mean that you will receive a visa.
  • If you have obtained Japanese Language Proficiency Test (JLPT) N4 or higher, you do not need to take Japan Foundation Test for Basic Japanese(JFT-Basic) to apply for the "Specified Skilled Worker" residence status. Similarly, if you have obtained Japanese Language Proficiency Test (JLPT) N5 or higher, you do not need to take Japan Foundation Test for Basic Japanese(JFT-Basic) to apply for the "Training and Employment" residence status.

ID creation

  • If you do not have Prometric ID, please create one first.
  • It is prohibited for one person to obtain multiple Prometric IDs.
  • When registering your passport or e-KTP information with Prometric ID, please be sure to enter it exactly as it appears on Identification Document. The registered information will be displayed exactly as it is on Confirmation Letter and result notices.
    • In the case of a passport
      • If your passport's MRZ is single-name, your full name will be automatically registered in the First Name field, and a hyphen ("-") will be displayed in the Last Name field.
      • Even if the order of names displayed on your MRZ and Confirmation Letter differs, you can still take the exam as long as you registered according to the MRZ.
    • In the case of e-KTP
      • e-KTP: Please enter your name exactly as it appears on the ticket (including your degree, etc.). Your name will be consolidated into the First Name field, and a hyphen ("-") will be automatically entered in the Last Name field.
      • A hyphen ("-") will appear in the blank space for your name on Confirmation Letter and result notification documents, but this is not an error and does not require any correction.
      • Symbols cannot be entered. If your name contains symbols, please remove them before entering your name.
      • Since name information can only be registered up to 39 characters, please register only the first 39 characters if your name exceeds 40 characters.
  • If there are errors in your registration information, you may not be able to take the exam.
  • If unauthorized access is detected, your account will be deleted.
  • If you book an exam via a Vietnamese sending organization, please register the email address specified by the sending organization when creating your Prometric ID.

Scheduling

  • Reservations and changes can be made until 23:59 (Japan time) three business days before the test date. If the test date falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or Japanese holiday, you can apply until 23:59 (Japan time) four business days before the test date.
  • Business days are days excluding Saturdays, Sundays, Japanese holidays, and New Year's holidays.
  • The dates vary depending on Test Center. Please note that Test Center may not hold exams depending on the date and time.
  • Seating is limited. Please complete Scheduling as soon as possible.
  • If there are errors in your registration information, you may not be able to take the exam.
  • If unauthorized access is detected, your account will be deleted.

Rescheduling

  • Cancellations are not possible.
  • No changes can be made after the reservation deadline has passed.
  • Scheduling cannot be changed unless there are vacant seats.
  • Regardless of attendance or absence On the test day, test fees and Voucher will not be refunded or Voucher will be reissued.

On the day of exam date

  • Entry to Test Center will begin 45 minutes before the start of the exam.
  • Please arrive at Test Center 30 minutes before the start of the exam. If you are late, you will not be able to take the exam.
  • You will be required to present Identification Document and Confirmation Letter on On the test day. If you cannot present it, you will not be able to take the test.
  • Even if you are unable to take the test due to being late or Any lack of or mistake in your identification document(s), Test Fee will not be refunded.
  • The following types of clothing are prohibited at Ateneo University in the Philippines.
    Dirty clothes, shorts and shirts for room use, shorts and shirts for sports such as gym and cycling, sleeveless clothes (for men), clothes with deep necklines, clothes with wide openings in the back, clothes without shoulder straps, clothes that show your navel, extreme clothes. short skirts or dresses, extremely low-rise jeans, and slippers.

Identification Document

  • The "name, passport number (or NIK number), and date of birth on Identification Document" must be the same as the "name, passport number (NIK number), and date of birth registered in your Prometric ID."
  • Must be an original.
  • Copying is invalid.
  • It cannot be presented on a smartphone screen or other electronic media.
  • The photo of Identification Document must be clear and can be verified as the person who came to the test site on On the test day.
  • Must be within the expiry date and include a photo.
