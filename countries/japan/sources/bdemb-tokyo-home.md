# [Embassy of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh, Tokyo](/)

![quote](/site-assets/images/quote.svg)

![H.E. Mr. Md. Daud Ali](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-tokyo-mofa/2026/8/e1ad6607-a7df-49ae-afb6-c6bb5eb8dfd4.jpg)

Ambassador

[](#)

H.E. Mr. Md. Daud Ali

It is my great pleasure to welcome you to the official website of the Embassy of Bangladesh in Japan. Since the establishment of diplomatic relations in 1972, our relations have continued to grow and expand. Japan has been a steadfast partner in Bangladesh’s development journey, contributing significantly to our infrastructure development, economic growth, human resource development and social progress. Today, our cooperation covers many new areas, including development cooperation, trade and investment, science and technology, ICT, education, skills development, and people-to-people exchanges. The Embassy of Bangladesh in Tokyo is committed to further strengthening this partnership and serving as a bridge between the peoples of Bangladesh and Japan. We greatly acknowledge the contribution of the Bangladeshi community in Japan in strengthening the friendship between our two countries. We are committed to providing efficient and accessible services to the Bangladeshi nationals living here. We continue to work to make the consular services more convenient, quicker, and responsive to the needs of our citizens. The Embassy also promotes Bangladesh’s huge potentials in trade, investment, tourism, education, and culture in Japan. We work to connect businesses, investors, industries, universities, research institutions and other organizations in both countries for greater collaboration. I invite you to explore the website and learn more about Bangladesh and the many opportunities for closer engagement between our two countries. I look forward to working with our Japanese friends, the Bangladeshi community, and all our partners to further strengthen the bonds of friendship and cooperation between Bangladesh and Japan.

With warm regards,

Ambassador
