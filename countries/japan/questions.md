# Japan open questions

These are the questions the Japan migration guide can't yet answer, grouped by the guide's sections. Each is a "Need to know" in `doc.md`; an answer from someone who knows (a recruiting agency, a returnee, the Bangladesh Embassy in Tokyo, BMET, OTIT or ISA) can close it.
