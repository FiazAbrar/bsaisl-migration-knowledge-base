## Irregular status, regularisation and deportation (prefix U)

**Searched.** immigration.gov.mv (work visa, FAQs, downloads, press announcements, news 8556); xpat.egov.mv (homepage, announcements list, public verify pages, PDF guides 81, 159, 164, 166, 167, 168); one.gov.mv (Work Permit Missing Report service, needs login); gazette.gov.mv (2025/R-120 not found in English); male.mofa.gov.bd (menus are JavaScript only); English news: Atoll Times, Edition, MMTV, PSM, Raajje, Plus.mv, Corporate Maldives, Maldives Independent, Adhadhu, AsiaNews, Daily FT; Bangla: Khobor Sangjog, bdnews24, Daily Star Bangla, Jago News (blocked), BSS, TBS. Searches: "Operation Kurangi regularisation fee", "Mass Legalisation Programme", "missing report MVR 1,000 10,000", "2025/R-120", "Immigration Act deportation blacklist", "overstay fine work visa", "voluntary repatriation Hulhumale detention", "মালদ্বীপ অবৈধ বাংলাদেশি বৈধকরণ ২০২৬", "Human Rights Watch / Amnesty Maldives Kurangi".

**Key findings.**
- Mass Legalisation Programme: announced 18 Dec 2025 (U0023), opened 1 Jan, deadline 2 Apr extended to 2 May 2026 and declared final (U0024, U0025). No fee for the worker is published; the employer pays the work permit deposit (U0020). Final enforcement phase 2 Apr 2026 to 1 May 2027 (U0028); deportations of violators targeted before 17 Nov 2026 (U0035).
- Missing-report system fully documented in the Xpat guides of 10 Sep 2026 (U0066-U0084): MVR 1,000 to file, MVR 2,000 negligence fine, MVR 10,000 to withdraw outside the grace period; the LRA investigates. The Oct 2024 news (MVR 1,000 / 2,000 / 10,000, 30-day free withdrawal 6 Oct-4 Nov 2024) matches.
- Immigration publishes a list of 8,576 passports of absconding or missing workers held at Immigration, 8,543 Bangladeshi (U0109). A strong insider detail: workers can look up whether their passport is there.
- Work visas linked to expired permit fees show as invalid (U0097): the mechanism by which an employer's non-payment makes a worker undocumented.
- Regulation 2025/R-120 (gazetted c. 14-16 Dec 2025) replaced 2023/R-111; standing legalisation chapter removed (U0091), employer change after one month of unpaid fees with an NOC (U0088), employer change for proven negligence (U0089). Official text not saved (Dhivehi on gazette.gov.mv).

**Conflicts.**
- Raajje 185843 says immediate deportation "starting from January 2025"; MMTV 10640 (6 Jul 2026, U0034) says "from next year", meaning 2027. Raajje is evidently a typo; I used MMTV.
- Deportation totals vary with the counting period: 2,013 (Nov 2023-Jul 2024, U0134), 4,054 (to 17 Sep 2024), 9,021 (two years to Nov 2025, U0048), 9,697 (to Dec 2025, plus.mv), 12,552 (to 13 Jun 2026, U0037). These are not conflicts; I used the latest.
- Number without biometrics: 2,933 (minister, Apr 2026, U0031) vs 2,895 phase-3 target (U0038). Probably updated counts; both cited.
- Kurangi start: 2 May 2024 (Atoll Times, Maldives Independent) vs "2 May 2025" in MMTV 6894. MMTV is wrong; not used.
- Ban length: 10 years (2013, U0118) vs "will not be allowed to return" (2024, U0117) vs "varies by offence" (PILC, U0061). No current official figure, so it stays a Need to know.

**Practice.**
- PILC 2024 survey (847 Bangladeshis): 60% undocumented, 56% of them became undocumented after legal entry (U0058, U0059). Workers are told their visa became an "open visa" (U0060).
- UN CERD (Nov 2025): no judicial review, release negotiated between employers and Immigration (U0114, U0115).
- Bangladesh High Commission counsellor (labour) Md Sohel Parvez (TBS, Jan 2025): withdrawal by the employer, or an LRA complaint leading to a letter for a new employer (U0055, U0057).
- bdnews24 (25 Oct 2026): 45 Bangladeshi deaths in the first half of 2026, 10 of them undocumented; bodies repatriated with WEWB funding (U0121). For L/B's death section.

U0087 (MVR 50,000 employer negligence fine): for L. U0093 (absconding appendix). U0125: an old High Commission phone and Viber from 2022, for B to confirm. U0126 (2019 programme, 16,351 approved). - The visa on arrival is open to Bangladeshis; no 2024-2026 suspension for tourists found. The friction is at Velana (fake bookings, no address) [V0084-V0086] and in the High Commission's repeated warnings that tourist entry cannot become a work visa (17 Aug 2025 notice [V0079-V0081]; 31 Oct 2026 "no free visa" notice [V0082-V0083]).

- Visit sponsorship: the professional-category restriction [V0054] means ordinary Bangladeshi workers cannot bring relatives on a sponsored visit; relatives must come as tourists with paid hotels. F may want this for Bringing your family.

**What happens in practice.**
- The single biggest problem for Bangladeshi workers is getting dollars, not the transfer itself. Wages are in rufiyaa, banks and exchange houses won't take rufiyaa or cap monthly dollar sales, so workers buy dollars on the curb market at MVR 21-22.5 (official 15.42) or use hundi [G0005, G0029, G0031]. This is the reason formal remittances fell 16.4% in FY2025-26 [G0002].
- The monthly caps are bank-by-bank and keep falling: SBI USD 700 -> 500 -> 400 -> 150 (Oct 2025) [G0010, G0008]; Habib USD 1,000 -> 300 (by Sept 2026); Bank of Ceylon stopped new remittance accounts [G0007]; 2024 ranges USD 250-300 [G0013]. No cap was found for BML, MIB or CBM.
- The salary-into-bank rule (gazetted 25 Aug 2026, effective about 24 Sept 2026) matters because a 2025 op-ed and a 2023 study say most workers have no bank account [G0051, G0004]; undocumented workers cannot open one, which is why the 2016 version of the rule stopped being enforced (Raajje, not logged as fact). Agent W/U may want this.
- Bangladesh-side efforts are all proposals as of 8 Nov 2026: BB letter of 8 Oct 2026 (ACU rufiyaa settlement, taka cards, swap), branches of BRAC Bank / National Bank, the HC's June 2026 talks with Bank of Maldives about direct rufiyaa-to-taka transfers (BSS 23 June 2026, page not saved, see c.) and Sept 2026 requests to SBI/BoC/Habib [G0054, G0055, G0009]. Sonali Bank planned an exchange house in Malé in 2023 [news-tbs-sonali-exchange-house-maldives]; no evidence it opened.
- Crackdown on informal dollar trading: First Amendment to the Foreign Currency Act (in force 1 Sept 2026) adds fines for selling above the MMA rate; police/immigration task force detained 4 Bangladeshis + 2 Indians on 30-31 Aug 2026 [G0027, G0034]. The Home Minister has blamed the rate on "criminal networks" (Adhadhu headline seen, not saved).
