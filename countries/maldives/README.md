# Maldives: migration guide

Sources checked up to 9 Nov 2026.

How to go to the Maldives on a Bangladeshi passport, visa by visa, step by step. A raised number such as 3 is an official source; N3 is a news, NGO, research or guide source, listed under "Needs an official source"; a year, like N3 (2022), marks a source older than 2025. "Need to know" marks a point no source has yet confirmed. "You'll need:" lists what to have before starting a procedure.

Tk is the Bangladeshi taka (1 lakh is Tk 100,000); MVR is the Maldivian rufiyaa, converted throughout at MVR1 = about Tk 8.06, from the Maldives Monetary Authority's (MMA, the central bank) rate of MVR 15.42 per US dollar and Bangladesh Bank's Tk 124.35 per dollar on 8 November 2026 <sup>[1](#o1), [2](#o2)</sup>. Dollars bought on the street in Malé cost far more (see Sending money home) <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>. BMET (Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training) is the Bangladesh government agency that registers people who want to work abroad and gives them emigration clearance <sup>[3](#o3), [4](#o4)</sup>. In the Maldives, the Ministry of Homeland Security, Labour and Technology issues work quotas, work permits and the Work Permit Entry Pass a new worker must hold on arrival, through its online system Xpat (xpat.egov.mv) <sup>[5](#o5), [6](#o6), [N2](#n2), [7](#o7)</sup>. Maldives Immigration issues visas and runs IMUGA (imuga.immigration.gov.mv), its online portal <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>. Every Bangladeshi worker needs the permit and Entry Pass before flying; a Bangladeshi visitor gets a free 30-day tourist visa on arrival, which never allows work <sup>[9 (2011)](#o9), [6](#o6), [10](#o10)</sup>.

## 1 Background

<a id="in-one-minute"></a>

### 1.1 In one minute

A Maldives job starts as a quota: a Maldivian employer pays the government MVR 2,000 (about Tk 16,100) a year for each foreign-worker slot plus MVR 350 (about Tk 2,800) a month for the permit, and quota holders sell slots to agents <sup>[N3](#n3), [N4 (2023)](#n4), [1](#o1), [2](#o2)</sup>. Almost no one goes through a licensed agency: in 2023, 59% went through a sub-agent or dalal (দালাল, broker) and 36% through a relative <sup>[N4 (2023)](#n4)</sup>. Workers paid about Tk 2 lakh on average in 2023 and Tk 3 to 4 lakh in 2024, against caps of Tk 95,000 to 1,15,000 in the government's 2024 permission letters <sup>[N4 (2023)](#n4), [N5 (2024)](#n5), [11 (2024)](#o11), [12 (2024)](#o12)</sup>. There is no minimum wage for foreigners; average pay was MVR 6,760 (about Tk 54,500) a month in 2024, but wages are in rufiyaa and a dollar to send home cost up to 46% more on the street than at the official rate in 2026 <sup>[13 (2021)](#o13), [N6 (2024)](#n6), [N1](#n1)</sup>. The permit ties you to one employer: if it stops paying the permit fees, or reports you missing, you become illegal through no fault of your own, and since May 2026 anyone found without valid papers is deported <sup>[N7](#n7), [14](#o14), [N8](#n8)</sup>. The last legalisation window closed on 2 May 2026 <sup>[N9](#n9), [N10](#n10)</sup>. The corridor shut in 2019 and again in March 2024, but is now busy: 32,408 workers were cleared from 1 January to 7 November 2026, making the Maldives Bangladesh's fourth-largest destination <sup>[N4 (2023)](#n4), [N11](#n11), [15](#o15)</sup>.

<a id="is-it-open-now-and-who-can-go"></a>

### 1.2 Is it open now, and who can go?

Open, and busy: the Maldives was Bangladesh's fourth-largest destination in 2026, with 32,408 workers cleared by BMET from 1 January to 7 November, after Saudi Arabia, Singapore and Qatar <sup>[15](#o15)</sup>. The Maldives has shut Bangladeshi recruitment twice since 2019, first over undocumented workers, then in 2024 after a surge of arrivals, trafficking flags and forged company papers (see [What closed it before](#what-closed-it-before)) <sup>[N4 (2023)](#n4), [N11](#n11), [N12 (2024)](#n12)</sup>.

<a id="status"></a>

#### 1.2.1 Status

- **New work visas:** being issued. In October 2026 the Bangladesh High Commission in Malé told people coming on a new work visa to collect their BMET smart card (the emigration clearance card) before travelling ([Step 6: Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight](#step-6-orientation-bmet-clearance-and-the-flight): Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight) <sup>[N13](#n13)</sup>.
- **Fraud warning, 23 October 2026:** the High Commission warned of fraud, false promises and illegal money collection in the name of jobs, visas and work permits, and asked anyone cheated to report it rather than stay silent <sup>[N13](#n13), [16](#o16)</sup>. It told people not to pay, without checking first, anyone who promises a guaranteed job, a quick work permit or to "regularise" a visa, and never to pay more than the government-set cost (see [Done to workers](#done-to-workers)) <sup>[N13](#n13)</sup>.
- **Quotas closed for some jobs:** in December 2025 the Ministry of Homeland Security, Labour and Technology stopped issuing quotas at once for taxi drivers, co-pilots, ship captains, entertainment workers and shop cashiers, with more jobs phased out over two to five years <sup>[N2](#n2)</sup>.
- **Demand:** in July 2026 the Maldives High Commissioner in Dhaka said the government is regularising undocumented workers and wants more skilled Bangladeshis, such as nurses and accountants, but Regulation 2025/R-120 ends quotas for accountants two years, and for nurses three years, after 14 December 2025 (see [How long you can stay](#how-long-you-can-stay)) <sup>[N14](#n14), [N15](#n15), [5](#o5), [N2](#n2)</sup>. Construction demand comes from land reclamation at RasMale, Gulhi Falhu, Thilafushi and Uthuru Thila Falhu, the new Velana airport terminal and new resorts <sup>[N16 (2024)](#n16)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - What changed in 2025 to let 40,159 Bangladeshis go, when in March 2025 private employers were limited to 10 Bangladeshi workers each and the rest went only to government projects: was the 10-worker limit lifted, or did a few large employers and state projects take most of them?
> - Is the 2021 MoU still the framework for recruitment in 2026?
> - Why did monthly clearances jump from 878 in April 2025 to 5,411 in May 2025: new demand letters, or workers already in the Maldives being cleared after regularisation?
> - Are workers who get a Welfare Board membership card at the High Commission in Malé counted in BMET's clearance numbers?
> - Is any restriction on Bangladeshi unskilled quotas in force on 8 November 2026 (a per-employer limit, a sector list, a ceiling set by the Cabinet under the 2024 amendment)?

<a id="the-channels"></a>

#### 1.2.2 The channels

- **A relative or acquaintance already in the Maldives, or a village broker:** how almost everyone goes. In a 2023 survey of 370 current and returnee workers, 59% went through sub-agents or dalals (দালাল, brokers), 36% through relatives, and only 4% through a licensed recruiting agency <sup>[N4 (2023)](#n4)</sup>.
- **A licensed agency with a BMET permission letter:** rare; an example is ZM Tours and Travels' May 2024 permission for 100 construction labourers for Sanken Overseas Maldives (Pvt) Ltd ([Step 1: Find a real job](#step-1-find-a-real-job): Find a real job, item 4) <sup>[17 (2024)](#o17)</sup>.
- **Professionals:** nurses, doctors, teachers and engineers go on the same work permit with recognised qualifications (see Work visa: professional (teachers, doctors, nurses, engineers)) <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>.
- **No legal route:** coming on a tourist visa to find work. Brokers sell it as an "open visa"; it is a tourist visa that does not allow work (see [What people believe](#what-people-believe-and-the-facts), and the facts) <sup>[N17 (2023)](#n17), [N18 (2024)](#n18)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - In 2025-26, what share of BMET clearances for the Maldives were individual ("single visa") clearances against agency permission letters?

<a id="who-qualifies"></a>

#### 1.2.3 Who qualifies

Anyone aged 18 or over whom a Maldivian employer with a valid quota names on a work permit; the employer must register the workplace, hold a quota and get the permit before the worker can work (see [Step 3: The employer gets your work permit and entry pass](#step-3-the-employer-gets-your-work-permit-and-entry-pass): The employer gets your work permit and entry pass) <sup>[18 (2024)](#o18), [7](#o7)</sup>. The job must not be one closed to foreigners: taxi and other fare-collecting drivers, co-pilots, vessel captains, photography and videography, entertainment, and cashiers in food shops <sup>[N15](#n15)</sup>. Professional jobs need an MQA (Maldives Qualifications Authority) level 7 qualification, or level 5 with 5 years' experience (see Work visa: professional (teachers, doctors, nurses, engineers)) <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>. Women are few: 148 of the 32,408 cleared in 2026 <sup>[19](#o19), [15](#o15)</sup>. In 2025 a private employer could bring at most 10 Bangladeshi workers, and only if it was paying its fees and was not blacklisted <sup>[N11](#n11)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Is there an upper age limit or a skill test for a Maldives unskilled work permit, and does BMET demand a skill certificate for the Maldives?

<a id="size"></a>

#### 1.2.4 Size

- **People:** about 100,000 Bangladeshis live and work in the Maldives, about a fifth of its roughly 5 lakh population <sup>[N14](#n14), [N19](#n19)</sup>. Immigration's border data showed 90,624 Bangladeshi workers in the country in December 2023, against 139,220 Bangladeshis with "active" work permits, because a permit stays active after the worker runs away or leaves unless the employer cancels it <sup>[N20 (2023)](#n20), [N21 (2023)](#n21)</sup>.
- **Undocumented:** estimates run from at least 60,000 foreigners of all nationalities (US State Department, 2025) and 63,000 (Maldives Immigration, 2023) to the High Commission's late-2024 estimate of 10,000 to 15,000 Bangladeshis (see [Undocumented workers: regularisation](#undocumented-workers-regularisation)) <sup>[N22](#n22), [N20 (2023)](#n20), [N19](#n19)</sup>.
- **Departures:** BMET cleared 7,020 workers for the Maldives in 2024, 40,159 in 2025 (5th destination) and 32,408 in 2026 to 7 November (4th), about 5% of the 626,601 it cleared for all countries <sup>[20](#o20), [21](#o21), [15](#o15)</sup>. Monthly clearances jumped from 878 in April 2025 to 5,411 in May 2025 and have run at about 1,000 to 5,500 a month since <sup>[22](#o22), [23](#o23)</sup>. About 104,000 went from 2004 to 2018 and 4,411 in 2019, before the ban <sup>[N23 (2024)](#n23)</sup>.
- **Other nationalities:** Indians, Sri Lankans and Bangladeshis make up most of the 62,000 foreign workers who entered in 2023, and the 2019 ban on Bangladeshis brought a surge of Indian workers <sup>[N24](#n24), [N25 (2023)](#n25)</sup>. From 17 November 2023 to 30 January 2025, 75,542 foreigners arrived on work permits, and 17,039 work permits were issued to Bangladeshis <sup>[N11](#n11)</sup>.
- **Jobs:** 33% of Bangladeshis work in restaurants, hotels and resorts, 25% in construction, 7% in day labour, 2% in domestic work and 2% in fishing <sup>[N17 (2023)](#n17)</sup>.

<a id="what-closed-it-before"></a>

#### 1.2.5 What closed it before

- **18 September 2019:** the Ministry of Economic Development banned new unskilled Bangladeshi workers to tackle undocumented workers <sup>[N4 (2023)](#n4)</sup>.
- **After the ban:** the sixth amendment to the Employment Act, under President Solih, capped workers from any one country at 100,000 <sup>[N26](#n26), [N20 (2023)](#n20)</sup>.
- **17 December 2023:** the new government reopened unskilled hiring from Bangladesh, and paused unskilled hiring from other countries <sup>[N27 (2023)](#n27)</sup>. 14,689 permits went to 3,174 employers almost at once <sup>[N11](#n11)</sup>.
- **30 March 2024:** recruitment from Bangladesh stopped after employers brought people in large numbers and some transactions were flagged for trafficking <sup>[N11](#n11)</sup>. On 22 May 2024 the High Commission announced that the Maldives had stopped new work visas for Bangladeshis because its law allowed 100,000 workers from one country <sup>[24 (2024)](#o24)</sup>. The minister said companies had submitted forged documents <sup>[N12 (2024)](#n12)</sup>. The High Commission's labour wing said some companies brought workers in on false job offers and left them jobless, possibly to extort them, and a Bangladeshi syndicate was implicated; the workers had proper visas <sup>[N28 (2024)](#n28)</sup>. 6,436 had BMET clearance by 22 May 2024, and many could not fly <sup>[N23 (2024)](#n23)</sup>.
- **9 February 2021:** Bangladesh and the Maldives signed an MoU (memorandum of understanding) on recruitment, including the regularisation of undocumented workers <sup>[N29 (2021)](#n29)</sup>.
- **29 June 2024:** Bangladeshis allowed again, but only for government projects; later, private employers paying their fees could bring up to 10 each <sup>[N11](#n11)</sup>.
- **September 2024:** the eighth amendment replaced the fixed 100,000 cap with numbers and sectors set by regulation on the Cabinet's advice; the minister said the total would follow demand, "whether it be 100,000, 110,000 or 120,000" <sup>[18 (2024)](#o18), [N30 (2024)](#n30), [N16 (2024)](#n16)</sup>.

<a id="how-a-job-reaches-you-and-who-takes-a-cut"></a>

### 1.3 How a job reaches you, and who takes a cut

The quota is the root: a Maldivian employer pays the state for each foreign-worker slot <sup>[N3](#n3)</sup>. Researchers found corruption running from agents in Bangladesh and the Maldives up to "quota providers who sell their yearly quotas" to the agents <sup>[N4 (2023)](#n4)</sup>. The choke point is Xpat, the ministry's expatriate system, where quotas and permits are issued; the Malaysian company Bestinet is taking it over <sup>[N3](#n3), [N31](#n31), [N32](#n32)</sup>.

<a id="the-quota"></a>

#### 1.3.1 The quota

- **What it costs the employer:** MVR 6,200 (about Tk 50,000) a year per worker, made up of a MVR 2,000 quota fee and a MVR 350 monthly work permit fee (fees and deposit: see [Step 3: The employer gets your work permit and entry pass](#step-3-the-employer-gets-your-work-permit-and-entry-pass): The employer gets your work permit and entry pass) <sup>[N3](#n3)</sup>.
- **Paper workers:** of 221,231 active work permits, only about half are regularly paid; in December 2023 only 39,004 of 139,220 Bangladeshi permits were <sup>[N33 (2024)](#n33), [N28 (2024)](#n28)</sup>.
- **Quota fraud under investigation:** since 2019 Maldives Police and the Anti-Corruption Commission have investigated 22 recruitment agencies, including current or former immigration officers, for manipulating the quota system; 20 cases were still pending in 2025 <sup>[N22](#n22)</sup>.
- **"Unnecessary workers":** a Malé-based Bangladeshi journalist describes syndicates among employers, Bangladeshi agencies and middlemen that profit from hiring workers no one needs <sup>[N28 (2024)](#n28)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - What does a Maldivian employer or "quota provider" charge per Bangladeshi slot in 2026, and is it paid once or yearly?

<a id="who-controls-the-system-bestinet"></a>

#### 1.3.2 Who controls the system: Bestinet

- **The deal:** the Malaysian company Bestinet signed a 15-year agreement in October 2016 to run the Maldives' expatriate system; it lapsed in 2019 when Bangladeshi recruitment stopped <sup>[N3](#n3), [N32](#n32)</sup>. In 2024 the government chose to give Bestinet a new contract rather than pay USD 13.7 million in compensation plus further claims <sup>[N33 (2024)](#n33)</sup>.
- **The money:** Bestinet takes MVR 1,542 of each worker's yearly MVR 6,200, about MVR 6.9 billion over 15 years, and workers pay USD 50 (MVR 771) each for medical insurance under the deal <sup>[N3](#n3), [N31](#n31)</sup>.
- **The system:** it adds biometrics, face recognition and ten-fingerprint capture; in February 2026 it was being tested with two state-owned companies for launch on 1 April 2026 <sup>[N33 (2024)](#n33), [N31](#n31)</sup>.
- **Allegations:** Adhadhu reports that Bestinet faces allegations of corruption, fraud and deception in Malaysia, Bangladesh and Nepal, and former Home Minister Umar Naseer has called for an investigation of the deal <sup>[N32](#n32)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Did the Bestinet system go live in April 2026, and does it replace Xpat for work permits and permit checks?

<a id="company-visa-and-free-visa"></a>

#### 1.3.3 Company visa and "free visa"

A Maldives work permit ties you to the company that applied for it <sup>[24 (2024)](#o24)</sup>. A "free visa" is a permit from one company used to work for someone else; the High Commission says there is no such thing, and breaking the rule can mean arrest at any time and deportation (see [If your employer reports you absconded](#if-your-employer-reports-you-absconded)) <sup>[24 (2024)](#o24), [N34](#n34)</sup>. Workers who leave the employer named on the permit lose their legal status, because the employer files a "missing" report <sup>[N19](#n19)</sup>. Some do it because the promised job was never there <sup>[N35 (2024)](#n35)</sup>.

<a id="the-broker-and-the-village"></a>

#### 1.3.4 The broker and the village

The dalal is the main link: he processed 59% of migrations in 2023 <sup>[N4 (2023)](#n4)</sup>. Only about 10% of workers had any contact afterwards with whoever sent them, so the broker answers to no one when the job fails <sup>[N4 (2023)](#n4)</sup>.

<a id="what-you-pay-and-what-you-earn-and-keep"></a>

### 1.4 What you pay, and what you earn and keep

<a id="the-cap"></a>

#### 1.4.1 The cap

Each BMET permission letter sets the most the agency may take: the 2024 Maldives letters allowed Tk 95,000 where the employer paid the airfare both ways and Tk 1,15,000 where it paid one way ([Step 1: Find a real job](#step-1-find-a-real-job): Find a real job, item 4) <sup>[11 (2024)](#o11), [12 (2024)](#o12)</sup>. Maldivian rules ban agencies from charging workers recruitment fees or holding their passports, with fines of MVR 5,000 to 50,000 <sup>[N22](#n22)</sup>.

<a id="what-people-really-pay"></a>

#### 1.4.2 What people really pay

- **2023 survey:** an average of Tk 205,997, from Tk 32,000 to Tk 3,75,000 <sup>[N4 (2023)](#n4)</sup>.
- **2024 press:** Tk 3 to 4 lakh, and one Chattogram man's deal was Tk 4.6 lakh (Tk 2.6 lakh paid, Tk 2 lakh more due on the visa) <sup>[N5 (2024)](#n5), [N18 (2024)](#n18)</sup>.
- **Maldives-side survey, 2024:** over 94% of 847 Bangladeshi workers had paid recruitment fees of up to USD 2,200 <sup>[N6 (2024)](#n6)</sup>.
- **New arrivals, April 2024:** workers flying to Malé told a reporter they had paid Tk 2.5 to 3.5 lakh each, on promises of Tk 40,000 to 60,000 a month with food and housing <sup>[N36 (2024)](#n36)</sup>.

<a id="how-it-is-paid"></a>

#### 1.4.3 How it is paid

In parts: one 2024 deal took Tk 2.6 lakh first, with Tk 2 lakh more due when the visa came <sup>[N18 (2024)](#n18)</sup>. Workers who had paid when recruitment stopped in 2024 were left waiting with no visa; how to get money back is in [Step 1: Find a real job](#step-1-find-a-real-job): Find a real job <sup>[N23 (2024)](#n23), [N18 (2024)](#n18)</sup>.

<a id="how-they-borrow"></a>

#### 1.4.4 How they borrow

- **2023:** 53% borrowed from friends and relatives, 37% used family savings, 19% sold assets, 10% took NGO or microcredit loans, and only 4% a bank loan <sup>[N4 (2023)](#n4)</sup>. A legal-aid organisation's research cited in 2023 found work tied to debt in over 75% of cases <sup>[N17 (2023)](#n17)</sup>.
- **2024:** more than half of the workers in the Maldives-side survey had borrowed to pay <sup>[N6 (2024)](#n6)</sup>. The Chattogram man borrowed Tk 1 lakh at monthly interest <sup>[N18 (2024)](#n18)</sup>.

<a id="what-the-job-pays"></a>

#### 1.4.5 What the job pays

- **Average pay:** MVR 6,760 (about Tk 54,500) a month, with over 70% working more than 54 hours a week (2024) <sup>[N6 (2024)](#n6)</sup>. In 2023 Bangladeshi workers earned an average of Tk 35,147 a month: Tk 37,066 documented, Tk 25,650 undocumented <sup>[N4 (2023)](#n4)</sup>.
- **No minimum wage for foreigners:** the Minimum Wage Order excludes expatriate workers (see [Your rights at work](#your-rights-at-work)) <sup>[13 (2021)](#o13)</sup>.
- **Skilled and general workers:** carpenters, electricians and general labourers in hotels, restaurants and companies earned on average USD 400-500 (about Tk 50,000 to 62,000) a month in 2024, and most employers gave food and housing <sup>[N36 (2024)](#n36), [2](#o2)</sup>.
- **Undercutting:** a water-sports guide says undocumented workers who work for food and a bed push pay down, so people who "deserve 12,000-14,000 MVR" work for MVR 5,000 (about Tk 40,300) <sup>[N19](#n19)</sup>.
- **Housing:** undocumented workers spend about 35% of their earnings on accommodation <sup>[N17 (2023)](#n17)</sup>.

<a id="what-goes-unpaid"></a>

#### 1.4.6 What goes unpaid

- **Less than promised:** 47% got less than promised, Tk 18,203 a month on average against Tk 29,743 promised <sup>[N4 (2023)](#n4)</sup>.
- **Overtime:** 36% were not paid for overtime <sup>[N17 (2023)](#n17)</sup>.
- **Months unpaid:** a Bangladeshi crew building a resort on an island 75 km from Malé went unpaid for two months <sup>[N37](#n37)</sup>.

The 2024 amendment fines employers MVR 50,000 for each case of neglecting a foreign worker, including non-payment; how to claim is in If you aren't paid <sup>[N38 (2024)](#n38)</sup>.

<a id="money-sent-home"></a>

#### 1.4.7 Money sent home

The Maldives sent USD 38.05 million to Bangladesh in July-September 2026, 21st among sending countries <sup>[25](#o25)</sup>. Bangladesh Bank figures reported in the press put the year to June 2026 at about USD 118 million, down 16.4% from USD 141.1 million a year earlier <sup>[N39](#n39)</sup>. On the Maldives side, Bangladeshi workers sent over USD 112 million through formal channels in 2024, 72% of all money sent out by migrant workers <sup>[N40](#n40)</sup>. Much of it goes informally: in a 2023 study, 66% of Bangladeshi workers in the Maldives sent money through hundi (an unlicensed agent who pays the family in taka) and 64% had no bank account <sup>[N41 (2023)](#n41)</sup>. The cause is that wages are paid in rufiyaa and dollars are scarce (see [Sending money home](#sending-money-home)) <sup>[N42](#n42), [N43](#n43)</sup>.

<a id="what-ties-you-to-the-employer"></a>

### 1.5 What ties you to the employer

Completely: your work permit names one employer, and working for anyone else, or moving without the ministry's procedure, makes you illegal <sup>[24 (2024)](#o24), [N44 (2024)](#n44), [N45](#n45)</sup>.

- **To enter:** only an employer's permit and Entry Pass get a Bangladeshi worker in; the employer gets the work e-Visa within 15 days of arrival, and a tourist cannot switch (see [Step 3: The employer gets your work permit and entry pass](#step-3-the-employer-gets-your-work-permit-and-entry-pass): The employer gets your work permit and entry pass) <sup>[6](#o6), [26](#o26)</sup>.
- **To stay legal:** the employer pays the permit fees; if it stops, your permit is cancelled and your visa shows as invalid, through no fault of yours <sup>[N7](#n7), [27](#o27)</sup>. In December 2023 only 39,004 of 139,220 Bangladeshi permits were being paid <sup>[N28 (2024)](#n28)</sup>.
- **To quit:** the High Commission's model contract asks 2 months' notice or 2 months' pay, and you buy your own ticket home <sup>[28](#o28)</sup>.
- **To change employer:** you need a no-objection letter from the current employer, with narrow exceptions when the employer stops paying fees or is found negligent (see [If you want to change employer](#if-you-want-to-change-employer)) <sup>[29](#o29), [N46](#n46)</sup>.
- **To leave:** no exit permit is needed; the employer records your departure in Xpat, which cancels the permit (see [Leaving at the end of the job](#leaving-at-the-end-of-the-job)) <sup>[N47 (2024)](#n47), [N46](#n46)</sup>.
- **Which law:** the Employment Act covers every private-sector worker, but the minimum wage excludes foreigners (see [Your rights at work](#your-rights-at-work)) <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30), [13 (2021)](#o13)</sup>.
- **Your passport:** the employer may not keep it, yet about 70% of Bangladeshi workers don't hold their own <sup>[N47 (2024)](#n47), [N48](#n48)</sup>.
- **If you run away or the permit lapses:** the employer can report you missing in Xpat for MVR 1,000, and a worker found without a valid permit is detained and deported (see [If your employer reports you absconded](#if-your-employer-reports-you-absconded)) <sup>[14](#o14), [N17 (2023)](#n17), [N8](#n8)</sup>.

<a id="what-goes-wrong-most-and-what-people-wrongly-believe"></a>

### 1.6 What goes wrong most, and what people wrongly believe

The Maldives government names withheld passports and unpaid or underpaid wages as the commonest signs of trafficking, and in 2024 companies brought workers in for jobs that did not exist <sup>[N22](#n22), [N28 (2024)](#n28)</sup>. In 2024, 70% of workers surveyed did not hold their own passports, and 40% said an agency or agent had taken them <sup>[N6 (2024)](#n6)</sup>. Most arrive legally: over 70% entered through legal channels, but most fell into irregular status afterwards <sup>[N6 (2024)](#n6)</sup>.

<a id="what-people-believe-and-the-facts"></a>

#### 1.6.1 What people believe, and the facts

- **"A free visa or open visa lets you work anywhere."** No visa does; the permit is for one company, and the "open visa" is a tourist visa <sup>[24 (2024)](#o24), [N17 (2023)](#n17)</sup>.
- **"I've got a real visa, so a job is waiting."** In 2024 workers with proper visas arrived to find no job <sup>[N28 (2024)](#n28)</sup>. The High Commission tells workers to check the visa and the employer before coming ([Step 5: Check your permit and visa are real](#step-5-check-your-permit-and-visa-are-real): Check your permit and visa are real) <sup>[N13](#n13)</sup>.
- **"Come as a tourist; the permit gets done there."** One Maldivian employer brought a returnee back on a tourist visa planning to arrange a permit, and failed <sup>[N19](#n19)</sup>. Bangladeshis suspected of coming to work are refused entry at the airport (see [Step 3: The employer gets your work permit and entry pass](#step-3-the-employer-gets-your-work-permit-and-entry-pass): At the airports) <sup>[N19](#n19)</sup>.
- **"Once there I can run a shop."** Business is illegal for migrant workers; Bangladeshi shop owners have been selling up to Maldivians <sup>[N19](#n19)</sup>.

<a id="irregular-routes-and-how-they-end"></a>

#### 1.6.2 Irregular routes and how they end

- **Tourist visa to work:** brokers use the visa on arrival for Bangladeshi tourists to send workers without the High Commission's attestation; those workers are being identified <sup>[N18 (2024)](#n18)</sup>. Some land via Kuala Lumpur, Chennai or Malé holding what they think is a 12-month work visa but is a 3-month tourist visa <sup>[N4 (2023)](#n4)</sup>.
- **Leaving the permit employer:** the employer files a missing report; the worker can be detained and deported with little chance to put his case <sup>[N17 (2023)](#n17), [N19](#n19)</sup>. The way back is the company withdrawing the report or a Labour Relations Authority letter (see [If your employer reports you absconded](#if-your-employer-reports-you-absconded)) <sup>[N19](#n19)</sup>.
- **How it ends:** 12,552 people were deported between 17 November 2023 and 13 June 2026, and the government aims to remove the rest by 17 November 2026 (see [Going home without valid papers](#going-home-without-valid-papers)) <sup>[N49](#n49), [N50](#n50)</sup>.

<a id="work-visa-company-worker-construction-resort-shop"></a>

## 2 Work visa: company worker (construction, resort, shop)

Every Bangladeshi worker goes the same way: a Maldivian employer with a quota gets a work permit and a Work Permit Entry Pass for you on Xpat, you fly on the entry pass within 90 days, and the employer gets your work e-Visa within 15 days of arrival <sup>[7](#o7), [6](#o6)</sup>. The employer pays the quota (MVR 2,000, about Tk 16,100, a year), the permit fee (MVR 350, about Tk 2,800, a month) and the deposit, and Regulation 2025/R-120, the rules in force since December 2025, puts every permit fee and fine on the employer <sup>[N15](#n15), [31](#o31), [7](#o7), [N51](#n51), [5](#o5), [1](#o1), [2](#o2)</sup>. Visa stickers are no longer valid; only an e-Visa counts <sup>[6](#o6)</sup>. Whether new permits are being issued for Bangladeshis now is under Is it open now, and who can go? <sup>[N13](#n13)</sup>.

<a id="step-1-find-a-real-job"></a>

### 2.1 Step 1: Find a real job

1. <a id="step-1-find-a-real-job-item-1"></a>**Register on the Overseas Employment Platform (OEP),** BMET's website for job-seekers <sup>[3](#o3)</sup>.

   You'll need <sup>[32](#o32), [3](#o3)</sup>:

   - your NID (national ID card);
   - a mobile number;
   - Tk 200 for the government fee.

   1. Open [employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration](https://employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration) <sup>[3](#o3)</sup>.
   2. Type your name in English, mobile and NID numbers, date of birth, division and district exactly as on the NID, the captcha, tick the terms and press "যাচাই করুন" (Verify) <sup>[32](#o32)</sup>.
   3. Print 2 copies of the registration slip and give your fingerprints at your DEMO (District Employment and Manpower Office, BMET's district office) <sup>[3](#o3)</sup>. DEMO addresses are listed on BMET's website <sup>[33](#o33)</sup>.

2. <a id="step-1-find-a-real-job-item-2"></a>**Learn a trade if you need one.** BMET lists every TTC (Technical Training Centre) and IMT (Institute of Marine Technology) on its website <sup>[35](#o35)</sup>. In BMET's Maldives job orders, skilled trades (mason, electrician, carpenter, plumber, plasterer) pay USD 325 to 350 (about Tk 40,400 to 43,500) a month against USD 250 to 300 (about Tk 31,100 to 37,300) for labourers <sup>[12 (2024)](#o12), [36 (2024)](#o36), [37 (2024)](#o37), [2](#o2)</sup>. For skilled jobs BMET issues the smart card only once the skill is confirmed <sup>[11 (2024)](#o11)</sup>.

   > **Need to know**

3. <a id="step-1-find-a-real-job-item-3"></a>**Government jobs (BOESL).** BOESL (Bangladesh Overseas Employment and Services Limited, the state recruiting agency) got permission in June 2024 to send 76 doctors (medical officers and specialists) to the Maldives Ministry of Health on 2-year contracts; their route is under Work visa: professional (teachers, doctors, nurses, engineers) <sup>[38 (2024)](#o38)</sup>.

4. <a id="step-1-find-a-real-job-item-4"></a>**Through a recruiting agency: find and check the permission letter.** For each Maldives job order the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare and Overseas Employment (Bangladesh's ministry for migrant workers) issues a permission letter naming the agency, the employer, the jobs and pay, the conditions and the most the agency may take from you <sup>[12 (2024)](#o12)</sup>. BMET's system copy of the letter carries the High Commission's demand attestation number as the "Block visa number", in the form BHC-MALE(L)D-01/2015/39 <sup>[12 (2024)](#o12), [36 (2024)](#o36), [37 (2024)](#o37)</sup>. Examples:

   - Siam Recruiting Agency (RL1531), 25 March 2024: 100 workers for Rasheed Carpentry and Construction Pvt Ltd (now on Xpat's Employer Negligence list with 12 confirmed cases, [Step 5, item 2: Check the employer is not on the negligence list](#step-5-check-your-permit-and-visa-are-real-item-2)) <sup>[39](#o39)</sup>; labourers USD 250, electricians, masons, painters, bar benders and carpenters USD 325 a month; 2-year contract, 8 hours a day, 6 days a week; housing, food, medical care, resident permit and transport by the company; one-way airfare free; the agency may take at most Tk 1,15,000 <sup>[12 (2024)](#o12)</sup>.
   - ZM Tours and Travels (RL1287), May 2024: 100 construction labourers for Sanken Overseas Maldives (Pvt) Ltd at USD 250 a month; 8 hours a day, 48 a week; the same benefits; airfare both ways free; the agency may take at most Tk 95,000 <sup>[11 (2024)](#o11)</sup>.
   - The Ifthi Overseas (RL0894), 8 May 2024: FEDO Private Limited, 150 visas; construction labourers USD 300, masons and plumbers USD 350 <sup>[36 (2024)](#o36)</sup>. FEDO now has 58 confirmed negligence cases on Xpat's Employer Negligence list, the most of any employer <sup>[39](#o39)</sup>. In July 2026 a Bangladeshi newspaper reported that the Anti-Corruption Commission (ACC) had sought The Ifthi Overseas' records as one of 57 agencies in its inquiry into women sent abroad as domestic workers on fake "returnee" passports; it is an inquiry, not a charge <sup>[N52](#n52)</sup>.
   - North East Overseas (RL2199), 23 May 2024: 30 workers for Duplex Private Limited; labourers USD 300, carpenters, masons and bar benders USD 350 <sup>[37 (2024)](#o37)</sup>.

   You'll need: the agency's name and RL number (the recruiting licence number of every licensed Bangladeshi agency) <sup>[12 (2024)](#o12)</sup>.

   1. Open [bmet.gov.bd/pages/miscellaneous-infos](https://bmet.gov.bd/pages/miscellaneous-infos) and type the agency's RL number (for example RL1531) in the search box; the agency's permission letters are listed <sup>[40](#o40)</sup>.
   2. Open the PDF; check the employer, your job, the pay and the maximum fee against what you were told <sup>[12 (2024)](#o12), [11 (2024)](#o11)</sup>.

5. <a id="step-1-find-a-real-job-item-5"></a>**Check that the demand letter is attested by the High Commission in Malé.** A Maldives demand letter is attested by the Labour Welfare Wing of the Bangladesh High Commission in Malé <sup>[43](#o43)</sup>. The employer first registers at [employer.oep.gov.bd/employer/registration](https://employer.oep.gov.bd/employer/registration) <sup>[44](#o44)</sup>. It then brings the Labour Wing <sup>[43](#o43)</sup>:

   - a covering letter to the High Commissioner on company letterhead;
   - the demand letter;
   - its agreement with the Bangladeshi agency, and the power of attorney to it;
   - the agency's recruiting licence;
   - a specimen employment contract;
   - the company's registration certificate;
   - the quota approval from the Maldives authority;

- **Know what the agency promised.** Under the Maldives permission conditions the agency files a stamped undertaking that you will get the job, pay and benefits in the letter, and that it will pay your housing, food, ticket home and compensation if you don't <sup>[11 (2024)](#o11)</sup>.

- **Get the contract and its Bangla translation before clearance.** The agency must sign the contract with you before BMET clearance and give you a Bangla translation attested by BMET <sup>[11 (2024)](#o11)</sup>.
- **Never go on a tourist visa to work.** The Ministry warns that brokers send people abroad on tourist or visit visas with false job promises (see [Tourist visa](#tourist-visa)) <sup>[51](#o51)</sup>.
- **Check the company.** The High Commission asks workers to check the visa and the company properly before coming on a work visa <sup>[52](#o52)</sup>.
- **Borrow from PKB** (Probashi Kallyan Bank, the government bank for migrants) once you have a visa: up to Tk 3 lakh at 9% simple interest, repaid monthly over up to 3 years, decided within 7 working days <sup>[53](#o53)</sup>.

  - the free bank form, 3 photos and an NID copy;
  - passport, visa and BMET card copies;
  - 3 signed MICR cheque leaves from your own account, with a statement;
  - a PKB savings account opened with at least Tk 500;
  - two guarantors: one financially solvent person (cheques, statement, proof of income) and one family member.

  You and the guarantors must live in the branch's area and you must be 18 to 60 <sup>[53](#o53)</sup>. Fees: 0.5% processing and 0.5% documentation, plus VAT, and Tk 900 per lakh risk cover <sup>[53](#o53)</sup>. Women can take PKB's women's migration loan, which needs one guarantor <sup>[54](#o54)</sup>. Apply at the branch or on mygov.bd, which sends application and tracking numbers by SMS <sup>[55 (2019)](#o55)</sup>. Helpline 16238, or +880 9677-787878 from abroad <sup>[53](#o53)</sup>.

<a id="step-2-the-job-offer-and-contract"></a>

### 2.2 Step 2: The job offer and contract

In the Maldives the job offer is the letter of appointment that the employer uploads to Xpat with the work permit application <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>. The written employment contract is signed later, within 45 days of arrival <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>.

1. <a id="step-2-the-job-offer-and-contract-item-1"></a>**Get the letter of appointment before you pay anything or sign at the agency.** The employer must use the ministry's template <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>. It must list <sup>[59 (2021)](#o59)</sup>:

   - the employer's name, address, contact details and registration number;
   - your name, permanent address, nationality, date of birth, passport number and an emergency contact (name and phone);
   - job title and the quota work type, which is the job category the employer's quota was issued for;
   - basic salary in US dollars and in rufiyaa (MVR), and the salary payment date;
   - work site, start date, job description and working hours;
   - whether the job is permanent or on contract, and the contract length.

   **Check what it says:**

   - The salary must be at least what the employer advertised on the National Job Center, because the employer may hire abroad only after advertising there for at least 7 days, and a quota worker is owed the advertised salary and benefits <sup>[N53](#n53)</sup>.
   - The job must not be one the Maldives gives no quota for: taxi and other fare-collecting drivers, co-pilots, vessel captains, photography and videography, entertainment, and cashiers in fruit, vegetable and food shops <sup>[N15](#n15)</sup>. For jobs being closed to foreigners, check the date (see [How long you can stay](#how-long-you-can-stay)) <sup>[N15](#n15)</sup>.
   - The work site must be named: a foreign worker may not work at a site that is not registered with the ministry <sup>[N15](#n15)</sup>.

   **Ask for it in writing.** Send the employer or agent: "Please send me a copy of the letter of appointment you uploaded in Xpat for my work permit, showing my basic salary in USD and MVR, work site, working hours and contract length, before I pay anything." <sup>[59 (2021)](#o59)</sup>

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does a worker on a new work permit sign the letter of appointment, as the employer-change template has required since 7 January 2026 <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>?
   > - Do Bangladeshi workers usually see the letter of appointment before leaving Dhaka, or only the entry pass?

2. <a id="step-2-the-job-offer-and-contract-item-2"></a>**Know what you must not pay for.** The employer pays the quota fee (MVR 2,000 per worker for 12 months), the work permit fee (MVR 350 a month) and the security deposit, which the ministry last published as MVR 8,000 (about Tk 64,500) for a Bangladeshi <sup>[N15](#n15), [31](#o31), [N51](#n51), [7](#o7), [60 (2021)](#o60), [1](#o1), [2](#o2)</sup>. Regulation 2025/R-120, the Regulation on Expatriate Employment, puts every permit fee, service fee and fine on the employer <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>. Under the Employment Act an employment agency may charge the employer, but no fee may be taken directly or indirectly from a prospective employee <sup>[61 (2022)](#o61)</sup>. Quota and work permit fees are not refunded <sup>[N53](#n53)</sup>. What workers really pay in practice is under What people really pay <sup>[N5 (2024)](#n5)</sup>.

   **If someone in the Maldives asks you for these fees:** report it to the Labour Relations Authority (LRA) on 332 8282 or through its online complaint form <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>. The Bangladesh-side complaint routes are in [Step 1: Find a real job](#step-1-find-a-real-job): Find a real job.

3. <a id="step-2-the-job-offer-and-contract-item-3"></a>**Sign the written contract within 45 days of arrival and keep your copy.** The regulation gives the employer 45 days from your arrival to sign a contract with you and give you a copy <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>. The Employment Act requires a written agreement that states your name, passport number and emergency contact, whether the job is permanent or temporary, the start date, pay and benefits, how salary is calculated, pay day, leave days, disciplinary rules, appraisal and how dismissal works <sup>[61 (2022)](#o61)</sup>. The employer must give you a signed copy, and an employer who does not is fined MVR 2,000 to MVR 20,000 <sup>[61 (2022)](#o61)</sup>. A fixed-term contract may last at most two years, and probation at most three months <sup>[61 (2022)](#o61)</sup>. You must also get a written job description within 20 days of starting <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>. Before the employer can make the first work permit fee payment, it uploads a notarised agreement between itself and you <sup>[31](#o31)</sup>.

   **Check it is real:** the salary in USD and MVR, the work site and the working hours must match the letter of appointment ([Step 2, item 1: Get the letter of appointment before you pay anything or sign at the agency](#step-2-the-job-offer-and-contract-item-1)) <sup>[59 (2021)](#o59)</sup>.

   **If it fails:** complain to LRA (see [If you aren't paid](#if-you-arent-paid)) <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>.

   > **Need to know**
   > - If the contract signed in the Maldives pays less than the letter of appointment uploaded in Xpat, which one does the LRA enforce?
   > - Is the contract given only in English or Dhivehi, or also in Bengali?

**Scams:** since 15 September 2026 nobody who enters on a business visa or a special visa can get a work permit without leaving; a real job needs a Work Permit Entry Pass issued before you fly <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>. A "business visa" or "special visa" sold as a work visa is therefore not a route to a work permit <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>.

<a id="step-3-the-employer-gets-your-work-permit-and-entry-pass"></a>

### 2.3 Step 3: The employer gets your work permit and entry pass

The employer does the paperwork in this step on Xpat, logged in with a verified eFaas account (the Maldives government digital ID) <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>. The office in charge is the Department of Foreign Labour at the Ministry of Homeland Security, Labour and Technology, on 1485 and xpat@home.gov.mv <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>. The rules are Regulation 2025/R-120, published on 14 December 2025 <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>.

1. <a id="step-3-the-employer-gets-your-work-permit-and-entry-pass-item-1"></a>**The employer registers the work site and gets a quota.** Before bringing a worker it must register the work site, obtain a quota and obtain a work permit; bringing a worker without a quota, or moving a worker to another business without telling the ministry, is illegal <sup>[N15](#n15)</sup>. It first advertises the job on the National Job Center for at least 7 days and may use a quota only if no suitable Maldivian applied <sup>[N53](#n53)</sup>. The ministry checks the employer's finances against its tax records <sup>[N53](#n53)</sup>. The quota costs MVR 2,000 per worker for 12 months, except that micro, small and medium businesses get up to 20 quota slots free <sup>[N15](#n15), [5](#o5)</sup>. The quota slot must still have at least 145 days of validity when the work permit is requested <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>.

2. <a id="step-3-the-employer-gets-your-work-permit-and-entry-pass-item-2"></a>**You send the employer your documents; it applies for the work permit.** You must be 18 or older <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>.

   You'll need:
   - passport bio-data page, valid at least 12 months (and at least one year on arrival) <sup>[7](#o7), [N51](#n51)</sup>;
   - a digital passport photo to Maldives Immigration standards <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>;
   - a police report from Bangladesh less than 6 months old (see [Step 4: Passport, police clearance and certificates](#step-4-passport-police-clearance-and-certificates): Passport, police clearance and certificates) <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>;
   - proof of the vaccinations the Ministry of Health requires <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>;
   - for a professional job, your certificates with certified English translations, and any registration the job needs (see Work visa: professional (teachers, doctors, nurses, engineers)) <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>.

   The employer adds the letter of appointment and the Maldives Immigration Visa Applicant Information Form (IM30) <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>. You must travel on the same passport used in the application; if you get a new passport after the entry pass is issued, the employer must cancel it and apply again <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>. A completed application is processed in 3 working days, and the employer is notified by email and SMS and tracks it under "My Applications" <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>.

3. <a id="step-3-the-employer-gets-your-work-permit-and-entry-pass-item-3"></a>**The employer pays the deposit; the ministry issues your Work Permit Entry Pass.** Once approved, the application moves to "Pending Payment"; after payment the Work Permit Entry Pass is issued <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>. The deposit is set for each nationality and is no more than the cost of your ticket home <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>. The ministry's last published table puts it at MVR 8,000 for Bangladesh, against MVR 3,500 for India, MVR 3,800 for Pakistan, MVR 4,000 for Sri Lanka and MVR 8,000 for Nepal, and says rates can change without notice <sup>[60 (2021)](#o60)</sup>. The employer pays it online by BML (Bank of Maldives) Gateway or its Xpat wallet; government employers are exempt <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>. When the deposit is refunded it goes back to the employer's Xpat wallet <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>. After payment the permit shows "Issued (Pending Arrival)" <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>.

   > **Need to know**
   > - What deposit does Schedule 5 of Regulation 2025/R-120 set for a Bangladeshi worker in 2026, and is the 2021 table (MVR 8,000) still applied?
   > - Do agents in Dhaka or Malé charge workers for the deposit, and under what name?

4. <a id="step-3-the-employer-gets-your-work-permit-and-entry-pass-item-4"></a>**Pass the pre-departure medical, if it runs in Bangladesh.** The Maldives Ministry of Health requires everyone seeking a work permit to take a Pre-Departure Health Assessment (PDHA) in their home country, where the PDHA system is running <sup>[62](#o62)</sup>. It covers your medical history, a physical examination, a chest X-ray, and tests for HIV, hepatitis B, hepatitis C and syphilis, plus filariasis and malaria tests for people from areas where they are common <sup>[62](#o62)</sup>. You also get a measles-rubella (MR) vaccination <sup>[62](#o62)</sup>. The clinic must be designated by the Ministry of Health Maldives <sup>[62](#o62)</sup>. At registration the clinic checks your face against your passport and takes your photo and fingerprints <sup>[62](#o62)</sup>. You are counselled and sign a consent form in a language you understand <sup>[62](#o62)</sup>. Grade A means you are cleared to work in the Maldives; Grade B means a significant abnormal finding and you are not eligible <sup>[62](#o62)</sup>. The clinic uploads your fingerprints and result within 2 days, and the result is valid for 3 months from the examination <sup>[62](#o62)</sup>. Pre-departure screening of Bangladeshi workers, at clinics accredited by the Health Protection Agency, began in October 2017 but was later halted; since 4 January 2024 a permit is issued only with the medical done after arrival <sup>[N54 (2017)](#n54), [N55 (2023)](#n55), [N56 (2024)](#n56)</sup>.

   > **Need to know**
   > - Is the PDHA running in Bangladesh in November 2026; if so, which Dhaka clinics are designated and what do they charge?
   > - Until a PDHA clinic exists in Bangladesh, is the 15-day medical after arrival ([Step 7: After arrival](#step-7-after-arrival): After arrival, item 2) the only one a Bangladeshi worker needs?

5. <a id="step-3-the-employer-gets-your-work-permit-and-entry-pass-item-5"></a>**Fly within 90 days.** You must enter the Maldives within 90 days of the entry pass being issued, and you must hold it on arrival <sup>[7](#o7), [6](#o6)</sup>. The flight and BMET clearance are in [Step 6: Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight](#step-6-orientation-bmet-clearance-and-the-flight): Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight.

**How long, in order:**

1. Job Center advert: at least 7 days <sup>[N53](#n53)</sup>.
2. Work permit decision: 3 working days once complete <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>.
3. Deposit paid: entry pass issued <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>.
4. You fly within 90 days <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>.
5. On landing you hold a 30-day conditional work permit <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>.
6. Medical within 15 days of arrival, and the work e-Visa within 15 days of arrival ([Step 7: After arrival](#step-7-after-arrival): After arrival) <sup>[5](#o5), [6](#o6)</sup>.
7. Full work permit within 30 days, extendable by 15; if no permit is issued within 45 days you may move to another employer without your employer's consent <sup>[N53](#n53), [N51](#n51)</sup>.
8. Contract within 45 days of arrival <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>.

The regulation counts these days without government holidays <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>.

**What you get back:** a Work Permit Entry Pass and a work permit number in the form WP00000000, which you need for the work permit check in [Step 5: Check your permit and visa are real](#step-5-check-your-permit-and-visa-are-real) <sup>[7](#o7), [63](#o63)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Can a refused quota or work permit be appealed, and by whom?
> - What does the entry pass document look like (PDF, QR code, fields), and does BMET check it before clearance?

<a id="step-4-passport-police-clearance-and-certificates"></a>

### 2.4 Step 4: Passport, police clearance and certificates

1. <a id="step-4-passport-police-clearance-and-certificates-item-1"></a>**Get an e-passport.** You fill the form online and submit it at the divisional or regional passport office for your present address <sup>[64](#o64)</sup>.

   You'll need <sup>[64](#o64)</sup>:
   - your NID (compulsory over 20); fill the form exactly as on it; nothing needs attesting;
   - technical certificates such as a driving licence, uploaded with the form and shown in original at the office.

   1. Fill in the form online at epassport.gov.bd <sup>[64](#o64)</sup>.
   2. Pay through A-Challan, DGePay or ShurjoPay, or at a bank <sup>[65](#o65)</sup>. A 48-page passport costs Tk 4,025 / 6,325 / 8,625 for 5 years and Tk 5,750 / 8,050 / 10,350 for 10 years (regular / express / super express) <sup>[65](#o65)</sup>.
   3. Go to the passport office with the original NID and certificates <sup>[64](#o64)</sup>. Delivery is within 15, 7 or 2 working days of biometric enrolment <sup>[65](#o65)</sup>. No police verification is needed with an online-verified NID <sup>[66](#o66)</sup>.

   **If it fails:** passport helpline 16445 (09666716445 from abroad), 24 hours <sup>[67 (2023)](#o67)</sup>. If the passport is lost, file a GD (General Diary, a police report) at the nearest police station and re-apply with a copy of the old passport and the GD <sup>[64](#o64)</sup>.

2. <a id="step-4-passport-police-clearance-and-certificates-item-2"></a>**Get a police clearance.** The work permit application needs a police report from Bangladesh less than 6 months old <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>.

   You'll need <sup>[68](#o68)</sup>:
   - a passport valid at least 3 months;
   - a present address that is the permanent or emergency address in the passport, where you live;
   - a scan of the passport data page attested by a first-class gazetted officer;
   - Tk 1,500 by treasury challan, code 1-7301-0001-2681, at any Bangladesh Bank or Sonali Bank branch, or online by card with a service charge.

   1. Register at [pcc.police.gov.bd](https://pcc.police.gov.bd/), log in and click "Apply" <sup>[68](#o68)</sup>.
   2. Fill in your personal details, then your present and permanent addresses; police verify you at the present address <sup>[68](#o68)</sup>.
   3. Upload the scanned documents <sup>[68](#o68)</sup>.
   4. Track it by SMS: "PCC S <reference>" to 26969. Collect it with your passport or application copy, or send someone with an attested authorisation letter <sup>[68](#o68)</sup>.

   **If it fails:** helpdesk Sunday-Thursday 9 am-5 pm, 01320001824, 01320001825, help.pcc@police.gov.bd <sup>[68](#o68)</sup>.

   > **Need to know**
   > - Must the police clearance for a Maldives work permit be attested, and by whom?

3. <a id="step-4-passport-police-clearance-and-certificates-item-3"></a>**Certificates for a skilled or professional job.** On mygov.bd, register, choose the attestation service for the document, upload it and pay Tk 200 a page; MoFA (Ministry of Foreign Affairs) attests within 5 working days and sends an SMS <sup>[69](#o69)</sup>. SSC and HSC (Secondary and Higher Secondary School Certificate) certificates go first to the education board and the Ministry of Education <sup>[70](#o70)</sup>. Experience certificates must first be attested by BMET or the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare and notarised; MoFA then attests them free <sup>[69](#o69)</sup>. The Maldives side (MQA recognition) is in Work visa: professional (teachers, doctors, nurses, engineers) <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>.

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does the Maldives accept Bangladesh's e-Apostille, or must certificates also be attested by a Maldivian mission?

<a id="step-5-check-your-permit-and-visa-are-real"></a>

### 2.5 Step 5: Check your permit and visa are real

1. <a id="step-5-check-your-permit-and-visa-are-real-item-1"></a>**Check the work permit on Xpat before you pay the rest or fly.**

   You'll need: your work permit number (WP followed by 8 digits) and your first name as on the permit, or your passport number <sup>[63](#o63)</sup>.

   1. Open [xpat.egov.mv/Home/WorkPermitVerify](https://xpat.egov.mv/Home/WorkPermitVerify), or click "Work Permit Verification" on the xpat.egov.mv home page <sup>[71](#o71)</sup>.
   2. Type the number in the "Work Permit Number" box <sup>[63](#o63)</sup>.
   3. Type your first name or your passport number in the "Name or Passport Number" box <sup>[63](#o63)</sup>.
   4. Press "Verify" <sup>[63](#o63)</sup>.

   **Check it is real:** the result shows your permit details with your photo <sup>[48 (2022)](#o48)</sup>. Once the deposit is paid, the employer's Xpat record shows "Issued (Pending Arrival)" <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>.

   **If it fails:** "Employee not found. Please Enter correct details" means no match; check the number for typing errors and try your passport number instead of your name <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>. If it still fails, call the Department of Foreign Labour on 1485, email xpat@home.gov.mv or use Xpat's live chat (weekdays 8.30 am to 2 pm) <sup>[5](#o5), [72](#o72)</sup>.

2. <a id="step-5-check-your-permit-and-visa-are-real-item-2"></a>**Check the employer is not on the negligence list.** Xpat publishes the names of employers with confirmed negligence cases since 15 September 2025, with their registration number and number of cases <sup>[71](#o71), [39](#o39)</sup>. Open [xpat.egov.mv/EmploymentApproval/EmploymentApprovalMissingPerson/MissingNegligenceEmployerList](https://xpat.egov.mv/EmploymentApproval/EmploymentApprovalMissingPerson/MissingNegligenceEmployerList) and type the company name or registration number in "Search" <sup>[39](#o39)</sup>. Since 15 September 2025 an employer that abandons or neglects foreign workers it brought in is fined MVR 50,000 per worker <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>.

3. <a id="step-5-check-your-permit-and-visa-are-real-item-3"></a>**After arrival, check the e-Visa.** Once approved, an e-Visa logo appears in the Visa section of Xpat and the e-Visa can be viewed or downloaded at any time <sup>[6](#o6)</sup>. The employer holds the Xpat account, so ask it for a copy <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>. The e-Visa is valid as long as the work permit, and it shows as invalid if the work permit fee has not been paid <sup>[6](#o6)</sup>.

4. <a id="step-5-check-your-permit-and-visa-are-real-item-4"></a>**Use the Xpat MV app as a second check.** The regulation provides no physical work permit card; the official Xpat MV app holds a digital card, added with your work permit number and passport number, and its QR code checks the permit against Xpat <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>. The app was last updated in 2023 <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>.

5. <a id="step-5-check-your-permit-and-visa-are-real-item-5"></a>**Check whether you must give fingerprints.** The Fingerprint Requirement page on xpat.egov.mv needs only your passport number and country, and tells you only whether you must enrol fingerprints <sup>[73](#o73), [71](#o71)</sup>.

**If a check fails, in this order:**

1. Department of Foreign Labour, Ministry of Homeland Security, Labour and Technology: 1485, xpat@home.gov.mv, H. Pamelia, Waterfront Building, Boduthakurufaanu Magu, Malé <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>.

<a id="step-6-orientation-bmet-clearance-and-the-flight"></a>

### 2.6 Step 6: Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight

1. <a id="step-6-orientation-bmet-clearance-and-the-flight-item-1"></a>**Do the pre-departure orientation (PDO), 3 days.** It is compulsory, at one of 70 BMET institutes; pay against a money receipt (Tk 200 at Feni TTC; pay no more) <sup>[4](#o4), [74](#o74)</sup>. Download the certificate at training.oep.gov.bd/pdo-certificate: type your passport or NID number and the captcha <sup>[75](#o75)</sup>.
2. <a id="step-6-orientation-bmet-clearance-and-the-flight-item-2"></a>**Check the contract** at least 2 days before you go: job, employer, workplace, length, pay, leave and airfare <sup>[76 (2021)](#o76)</sup>, against the permission letter ([Step 1, item 4: Through a recruiting agency: find and check the permission letter](#step-1-find-a-real-job-item-4)). The High Commission's model contract says <sup>[28](#o28)</sup>:
   - pay monthly, by the 7th of the next month, into a bank account in your name;
   - deductions for advances no more than 50% of a month's pay;
   - the employer renews your employment approval at least 2 months before it expires and pays any fine if it doesn't;
   - the employer pays your flight out and your flight home at the end;
   - if you quit early you give 2 months' notice or 2 months' pay and buy your own ticket;
   - the employer may not keep your passport;
   - you may not change employer during the contract without the employer's permission.
3. <a id="step-6-orientation-bmet-clearance-and-the-flight-item-3"></a>**BMET clearance (the smart card).** The High Commission says workers on a new Maldives work visa must take the BMET smart card before coming <sup>[52](#o52)</sup>. For a group visa the agency applies, with the visa copy, attested contract, your attested OEP registration, a Tk 300 stamp undertaking and the pay order for the fees; BMET clears within 3 working days of the visa <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>. Your costs:
   - welfare fee Tk 5,500 to the Wage Earners' Welfare Board from 1 October 2026 <sup>[77](#o77)</sup>;
   - insurance Tk 1,000 once (5 years, Tk 10 lakh cover) <sup>[46](#o46)</sup>;
   - source tax Tk 1,200 (skilled) or Tk 800 (unskilled) on a group visa, Tk 250 (attested) or Tk 400 (unattested) on a single visa <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>;
   - clearance fee Tk 250 <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>.

   > **Need to know**
   > - Is the Tk 250 clearance fee still charged, given the Ministry reportedly abolished the smart card fee in December 2024 <sup>[N57 (2024)](#n57)</sup>?

   **Individual visa:** apply at BMET's emigration branch in Dhaka or the Chattogram DEMO with your application, visa copy, contract if any, attested OEP registration, a Tk 300 stamp undertaking and the pay order for the welfare fee and insurance; 3 working days <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>. Women on a single visa need a guardian's no-objection letter <sup>[76 (2021)](#o76)</sup>.

   **Check it is real** at portal.wewb.gov.bd/bmet-wewbm-check: type your passport number (or membership ID) and the captcha <sup>[78](#o78)</sup>; the airport welfare desks check smart cards <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>. **If it fails:** BMET's Director (Emigration), +8802222222415, 01711-135901, diremi@bmet.gov.bd, or the OEP hotline 09614445271 <sup>[4](#o4), [34](#o34)</sup>.

   **If you are already in the Maldives without a smart card,** get a Welfare Board membership card ([High Commission services, item 2: Get a Welfare Board membership card if you came without BMET clearance](#high-commission-services-item-2)) <sup>[52](#o52), [79](#o79)</sup>.
4. <a id="step-6-orientation-bmet-clearance-and-the-flight-item-4"></a>**The flight.** Under the 2024 permission letters the employer paid the airfare (one way or both ways, as the letter says), and an agency representative must fly with each group or meet it at the Maldives airport <sup>[12 (2024)](#o12), [11 (2024)](#o11)</sup>. After each group arrives, the agency must report to the Ministry, BMET and the High Commission in Malé <sup>[11 (2024)](#o11)</sup>.

   US-Bangla Airlines (flight BS 337, about 4 hours) and Maldivian (Q2 503) fly Dhaka-Malé nonstop <sup>[N58](#n58)</sup>. A one-way US-Bangla economy ticket for 10 December 2026 cost Tk 36,699 when checked on 9 November 2026, without a checked bag; via Colombo, FitsAir was Tk 42,351 and SriLankan Tk 54,519 <sup>[N58](#n58)</sup>.

   > **Need to know**
   > - Is there any flight from Chattogram to Malé in 2026, or do Chattogram workers connect through Dhaka?
   > - Do agencies actually send a representative with Maldives groups, and do they report arrivals to the High Commission?

5. <a id="step-6-orientation-bmet-clearance-and-the-flight-item-5"></a>**Before you fly:** keep one set of copies at home and 3 with you, open 2 bank accounts, and never carry a packet you don't know <sup>[46](#o46)</sup>. Don't pack idols for worship, pork, alcohol, vapes or e-cigarettes, pornography or anything "contrary to Islam": Maldives Customs bans them <sup>[80 (2024)](#o80)</sup>. Save the Labour Wing hotline, +960 7648006 (WhatsApp) <sup>[52](#o52)</sup>.

<a id="step-7-after-arrival"></a>

### 2.7 Step 7: After arrival

1. <a id="step-7-after-arrival-item-1"></a>**Let the employer meet you at the airport.** Employers must receive their workers on arrival in person or send someone <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>. Immigration lets you in on the entry pass, which must be for the passport you travel on <sup>[6](#o6), [7](#o7)</sup>. From landing you hold a 30-day conditional work permit (see [Step 3: The employer gets your work permit and entry pass](#step-3-the-employer-gets-your-work-permit-and-entry-pass): The employer gets your work permit and entry pass) <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>.

2. <a id="step-7-after-arrival-item-2"></a>**Do the work permit medical within 15 days.** New arrivals must submit a work permit medical within 15 days of arrival <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>. It must come from a provider registered with the ministry <sup>[N53](#n53)</sup>. Registered providers include government hospitals such as Indira Gandhi Memorial Hospital and Hulhumalé Hospital and the atoll and regional hospitals, and private ones such as ADK Hospital, Medica Hospital, Tree Top Hospital and Jibon Clinic; the list was last updated on 18 August 2026 <sup>[72](#o72)</sup>. The employer uploads the report in Xpat, choosing the provider from a list and giving the issue and expiry dates <sup>[81 (2022)](#o81)</sup>. After approval your work permit card shows "fit", "unfit" or "needs isolation" <sup>[81 (2022)](#o81)</sup>. If you are unfit or need isolation the employer contacts the Health Protection Agency (HPA) <sup>[81 (2022)](#o81)</sup>. If you are found unfit no permit is issued and the employer must arrange your return; a treatable condition that is not contagious may get a conditional permit <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>. The medical is valid for 1 year from the doctor's final approval and must be renewed <sup>[5](#o5), [82 (2024)](#o82)</sup>. If a permit is cancelled while the medical has lapsed, a fine of MVR 2,000 per year applies, which is the employer's to pay <sup>[82 (2024)](#o82), [N51](#n51)</sup>.

   > **Need to know**
   > - Is the Maldives medical due within 15 days of arrival (Xpat) or 30 days (Ministry of Health guideline) <sup>[5](#o5), [62](#o62)</sup>?
   > - Which results make a worker unfit in the Maldives medical (HIV, hepatitis B, TB), and who pays the flight home?

The ministry's minimum policy <sup>[83 (2021)](#o83)</sup>:

Since 23 November 2025 visa stickers in passports are not valid; only an approved e-Visa counts, and you need it to leave and re-enter <sup>[6](#o6)</sup>. Ask the employer for a copy once it is approved ([Step 5, item 3: After arrival, check the e-Visa](#step-5-check-your-permit-and-visa-are-real-item-3)) <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>. In Greater Malé the employer books a slot at [xpatappointment.home.gov.mv](https://xpatappointment.home.gov.mv/), 2pm to 4pm on working days; elsewhere it arranges this with the island council office <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>.

6. <a id="step-7-after-arrival-item-6"></a>**Live where the employer houses you.** The employer must provide housing for the whole job, to the standard in the regulation, and gives its details in the permit application <sup>[N53](#n53)</sup>. Your housing rights are under Housing, health and safety <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.

In an LRA wage case, salary counts as paid only if it went into that account <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>.

   You'll need (Bank of Maldives, BML) <sup>[N60](#n60), [N61](#n61), [N62](#n62)</sup>:
   - your passport, original shown for checking;

   Apply for your first account online at [app.bankofmaldives.com.mv/join](https://app.bankofmaldives.com.mv/join/) <sup>[N60](#n60)</sup>. The BML expatriate account in rufiyaa needs a minimum deposit of MVR 200 and costs MVR 75 a month <sup>[N60](#n60)</sup>. How to send money is under Sending money home <sup>[N63](#n63)</sup>.

<a id="your-rights-at-work"></a>

### 2.8 Your rights at work

The Employment Act (Law 2/2008) covers every worker in the state and private sector except the military and police <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. The Labour Relations Authority (LRA) enforces it <sup>[N47 (2024)](#n47)</sup>. Foreign workers can take any breach of their contract to the Employment Tribunal <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. Any clause in a contract or regulation that takes away a right in the Act is void <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. Forced work is banned <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. Since the 8th amendment (1 September 2024), a foreigner may work only with authorisation under the Act and its regulations <sup>[85 (2024)](#o85)</sup>. The 9th amendment, in force since 14 March 2026, strengthened the procedures for ending a job and reviewed the quota fees employers pay to recruit foreign workers <sup>[86](#o86)</sup>.

Since mid-December 2025 the Ministry of Homeland Security, Labour and Technology's Regulation 2025/R-120 (the Regulation on Expatriate Employment, also reported as the Regulation for Immigrant Workers) has set out the rights and duties of foreign workers and their employers; it replaced the 2023 expatriate regulation <sup>[87](#o87), [5](#o5), [N45](#n45)</sup>. Its appendices cover accommodation standards and what happens when a worker is reported missing ("absconding") <sup>[N45](#n45)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Is there an English text of Regulation 2025/R-120, and which of its articles cover wages, passports, housing and the ticket home?
> - What exactly did the 9th amendment (March 2026) change in notice periods and dismissal procedure?

<a id="contract-and-probation"></a>

#### 2.8.1 Contract and probation

- Probation is at most 3 months <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. During it either side can end the job without notice <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>, but the rules on hours, leave and pay still apply to you <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- A disciplinary suspension may last at most 14 days <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- The employer must give you a written job description within one month of starting work under the Act, and within 20 days under Regulation 2025/R-120 <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30), [N51](#n51)</sup>. If it doesn't, you can still enforce the terms and any verbal promises made to you <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- The employer must sign the contract with you and give you a copy within 45 days of arrival (see [Step 2: The job offer and contract](#step-2-the-job-offer-and-contract): The job offer and contract) <sup>[N51](#n51)</sup>.
- A recruitment agency may not take any fee from you, directly or indirectly <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Must the contract be in a language the worker understands, such as Bengali?

<a id="pay-service-charge-and-deductions"></a>

#### 2.8.2 Pay, service charge and deductions

- **Minimum wage: none for foreigners.** The minimum wage, in force since 1 January 2022, covers every worker except expatriates <sup>[13 (2021)](#o13)</sup>. The law leaves it to the Minister to announce when it will apply to foreigners, and no minimum has been set for migrant workers <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30), [N47 (2024)](#n47)</sup>.
- **When and how.** You must be paid at least monthly <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. Since 25 September 2026 wages must go into a bank account in your own name at an MMA-licensed bank, and cash wages are no longer allowed ([Step 7: After arrival](#step-7-after-arrival): After arrival, item 7) <sup>[5](#o5), [N59](#n59), [N68](#n68)</sup>.
- **Payslip.** With every payment the employer must give a statement of total pay, each deduction with its reason, and net pay <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. If it doesn't, you can complain to the Employment Tribunal <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- **Banned.** The employer may not make you pay wages back, sign a receipt for more than you got, or buy from its shop <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- **Deductions allowed** <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30), [N47 (2024)](#n47)</sup>:
  - by law or court order;
  - with your written consent, for housing, goods, a loan or an advance, never more than one third of your wage;
  - for leave, medical costs, or insurance or provident-fund payments.
- **Absence.** For time off work the employer may deduct pay for that time only and impose no other penalty <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- **Service charge (resorts, hotels, guesthouses, tourist restaurants).** Tourism businesses must add at least 10% service charge to every bill <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. It must be shared equally among all staff, and last month's share must be paid before the end of this month <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. The employer may keep only 1% <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. LRA can fine an employer up to MVR 100,000 for not collecting or not sharing it <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- **Ramadan allowance.** For Muslim foreign workers it is at the employer's discretion <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- **Fines on bad employers.** An employer that fails to pay wages, puts you in a job that isn't on your permit, or otherwise exploits you faces a MVR 50,000 (about Tk 4 lakh) fine <sup>[N69](#n69), [1](#o1), [2](#o2)</sup>. Regulation 2025/R-120 fines breaches MVR 50,000 the first time, MVR 75,000 the second and MVR 100,000 the third <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>. For repeated wage breaches the Minister can tell government offices to stop serving the employer <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - In practice, do resort and guesthouse employers pay service charge to Bangladeshi kitchen, cleaning and maintenance staff at the same rate as Maldivian staff?

<a id="hours-rest-and-overtime"></a>

#### 2.8.3 Hours, rest and overtime

- **48 hours a week** at most, not counting overtime <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- **A day off.** After 6 days in a row you must get 24 hours off in a row <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. On resorts, tourist boats and industrial islands, the contract may let these days off build up and be taken together <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- **Overtime** only if your contract allows it <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. It is paid at 1.25 times the hourly wage on normal days and 1.5 times on Fridays and public holidays <sup>[N47 (2024)](#n47)</sup>. On resorts, boats and industrial islands, 2 extra hours a day are allowed, paid as overtime <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- **Breaks:** at least 30 minutes for a meal after 5 hours in a row, and 15 minutes for each prayer time <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- **Records.** The employer must keep records of your normal hours, overtime and overtime pay <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- **Free to leave after work.** The employer may not keep you at the worksite, on the island or on the boat after working hours <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. If you need a vehicle or boat to come and go, the employer or site owner must allow it <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Do private-sector employers have to shorten working hours in Ramadan, or does the government's Ramadan timetable apply only to state offices?

<a id="leave-and-public-holidays"></a>

#### 2.8.4 Leave and public holidays

- **Annual leave:** 30 paid days after one year <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. The employer sets the start date after talking to you <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. If you ask, leave pay must be paid by your last working day before the leave <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. Any agreement to give up your leave is void, and unused leave must be paid before the job ends <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- **Sick leave:** at least 30 paid days a year <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. You need a certificate from a licensed doctor, handed in on your first day back <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. 15 of the 30 days can be taken without a certificate, at most 2 days in a row <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. You cannot be dismissed for being temporarily off work through illness or injury <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- **Other paid leave:** 10 days a year for family responsibilities, such as caring for a sick relative <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>; 3 days for fathers when a child is born <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>; 60 days' maternity leave <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. Since March 2026, a mother returning from maternity leave gets time off for childcare until the child turns two <sup>[86](#o86)</sup>.
- **Public holidays:** every Friday, the Prophet's birthday, the day the Maldives embraced Islam, Independence Day, National Day, the first day of Ramadan, Eid ul-Fitr, Victory Day, Republic Day, Hajj Day and Eid ul-Adha <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. Since 2026, 1 May (Labour Day) too <sup>[86](#o86)</sup>. Working normal hours on a public holiday earns at least an extra half-day's wage on top of overtime <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.

<a id="your-passport"></a>

#### 2.8.5 Your passport

Employers may not take your passport or identity documents <sup>[N47 (2024)](#n47)</sup>. Under the Anti-Human Trafficking Act (12/2013), taking or holding someone's identity or travel document as part of trafficking carries 5 years in prison <sup>[N70 (2013)](#n70)</sup>. Even so, employers holding passports is one of the most common abuses reported <sup>[N22](#n22), [N71 (2024)](#n71)</sup>.

**If your passport is taken,** in this order:

1. Ask for it back in writing (WhatsApp or a letter) and keep a copy: "I ask you to return my passport no. [number], which you have kept since [date]. The law does not allow an employer to keep a worker's passport. [Name, work permit no., date]" <sup>[N47 (2024)](#n47)</sup>.
2. Complain to LRA on its online form under "Noncompliance of fundamental rights" (see [If you aren't paid, item 2: Complain to LRA](#if-you-arent-paid-item-2)) <sup>[88 (2019)](#o88)</sup>.
3. Tell the Bangladesh High Commission's labour wing in Malé (hotline +960 7648006, WhatsApp) <sup>[52](#o52)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Have the Maldives Police or Immigration ever recovered a passport for a Bangladeshi worker, or charged an employer for keeping one?

<a id="housing-health-and-safety"></a>

#### 2.8.6 Housing, health and safety

- The employer must provide housing for the whole of your employment, to the standards set by regulation <sup>[N53](#n53), [30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- You must have medical insurance from an approved insurer before your work permit is issued <sup>[N47 (2024)](#n47)</sup>. The employer may deduct insurance payments from your wage <sup>[N47 (2024)](#n47)</sup>.
- In the worst fire, on 10 November 2022, a blaze in a garage at M. Nirufehi in Malé spread into the workers' quarters above it and killed 10, two of them Bangladeshi; the quarters held at least 38 people, with a gas cylinder beside each bed, and the President ordered inspections of foreign workers' housing <sup>[N72 (2022)](#n72), [N73 (2022)](#n73), [N74 (2022)](#n74), [N75 (2022)](#n75)</sup>.
- The employer must provide safety measures and safety equipment free of charge <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. You may refuse work you reasonably believe is a serious danger to your life or health <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- LRA employment officers can inspect workplaces without warning <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. The employer must let you talk to them freely <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>, and LRA may not tell the employer who complained <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - What do the December 2025 accommodation standards require (space per person, toilets, cooking), and who inspects labour quarters?
> - Who pays the insurance premium in practice: the employer, or the worker through a wage deduction?

<a id="pension"></a>

#### 2.8.7 Pension

Foreign workers do not have to join the Maldives Retirement Pension Scheme. You can join only if your employer agrees to pay the employer's share and handle the paperwork <sup>[N76 (2023)](#n76)</sup>. If you join, you can withdraw your savings once you decide to leave the Maldives. The money is paid in rufiyaa into an account at a Maldivian bank <sup>[N76 (2023)](#n76)</sup>. The law gives migrant workers no other old-age, disability or death benefit <sup>[N47 (2024)](#n47)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - How many Bangladeshi workers are members of the pension scheme, and how long does a withdrawal take before departure?

<a id="unions"></a>

#### 2.8.8 Unions

You may form or join a union without prior approval (Industrial Relations Act 1/2024) <sup>[N47 (2024)](#n47)</sup>. Being a union member, or taking part in its lawful activities, is not a reasonable cause for dismissal <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. Protests are a different matter (see [What can get you jailed](#what-can-get-you-jailed)).

<a id="how-long-you-can-stay"></a>

### 2.9 How long you can stay

**As long as the fee is paid, one year at a time.** Your employer pays the work permit fee of MVR 350 a month, for at least one month at a time, and the permit lasts as long as it is paid <sup>[31](#o31), [6](#o6)</sup>. Your work e-Visa lasts as long as the permit, renewed for at most one year at a time, and it shows as invalid once the fee runs out <sup>[6](#o6)</sup>. If the fee goes unpaid you may move to another employer without consent, and after 6 months unpaid the ministry can cancel the permit and send you home <sup>[N53](#n53)</sup>. The permit also lapses if your yearly medical or your insurance expires, and it cannot be extended until your biometrics are updated <sup>[N51](#n51), [5](#o5)</sup>.

**The upper limit.** You may hold only one work permit at a time <sup>[N45](#n45)</sup>. A non-professional or unskilled work permit can be issued for up to 10 years in total, up from 5 years before December 2025 <sup>[N15](#n15), [N77](#n77)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Is there a maximum total stay for professional-category work permits?
> - Can a worker who reaches 10 years return on a new permit after leaving?

**Jobs closing to foreigners.** Regulation 2025/R-120 cancels quotas for some jobs a set time after 14 December 2025 <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>:

- after 2 years: hairdressers, beauticians, personal trainers, electricians, English-speaking guest relations officers, tour guides, accountants and imams (babysitters were taken off this list on 17 December 2025) <sup>[N15](#n15), [N53](#n53)</sup>;
- after 3 years: divers, nurses, building inspectors, surveyors, site supervisors, pilots and photographers <sup>[N15](#n15)</sup>;
- after 4 years: electrical engineers and dive instructors <sup>[N15](#n15)</sup>;
- after 5 years: housekeeping, human resources and front office managers, and primary and secondary teachers <sup>[N15](#n15)</sup>.

**Time abroad.** A work permit is voided if you stay outside the Maldives for more than four months while it is valid <sup>[N77](#n77)</sup>. If your permit expired while you were abroad, the employer can get a temporary 15-day approval with a medical done abroad, and you then need a Maldives medical within 15 days of return <sup>[82 (2024)](#o82)</sup>. See also Going home on leave <sup>[N46](#n46)</sup>.

**Other ways a permit ends.** A permit can be cancelled for a change of employer, breaking the regulation, a court order, long absence, or missing or false documents <sup>[N15](#n15)</sup>. It is voided if you are told to leave for health reasons and do not leave in time, and revoked if you do not cooperate with an investigation for 10 days <sup>[N77](#n77)</sup>. When the job ends, see Leaving at the end of the job <sup>[N46](#n46)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - After a work permit is cancelled, how many days does a worker have to leave or change employer before counting as overstaying?

<a id="what-can-get-you-jailed"></a>

### 2.10 What can get you jailed

**Drugs.** Since March 2026 the amended Drugs Act punishes even small amounts more harshly <sup>[N78](#n78)</sup>. Trafficking a Schedule 1 drug (which includes cannabis and heroin) brings 20 years and a fine of MVR 75,000 to 7.5 million <sup>[89](#o89)</sup>. Bringing in more than 350 g of cannabis, 250 g of heroin or 100 g of another Schedule 1 drug into the country on arrival can bring the death penalty <sup>[89](#o89)</sup>. Even very small amounts can bring arrest, long prison terms and in some cases whipping <sup>[N79](#n79)</sup>. Owners, tenants and occupants of a room used for drug-taking can be liable if they knew or had reason to know <sup>[89](#o89)</sup>. Never carry a package or bag for anyone else <sup>[N78](#n78)</sup>.

**Alcohol.** It is sold only on resort islands. Having it, drinking it or being drunk anywhere else can mean arrest and deportation <sup>[N79](#n79)</sup>.

**Religion and Ramadan.** Practising any religion other than Islam in public is illegal and can mean prison <sup>[N79](#n79)</sup>. In Ramadan, outside resorts, do not eat, drink or smoke in public during the day <sup>[N79](#n79)</sup>.

**Sex.** Same-sex relations are illegal <sup>[N79](#n79)</sup>.

**Protests and politics.** A visa condition bans migrants from "political activities" <sup>[N71 (2024)](#n71)</sup>. In July 2024 the government said it would arrest and deport the Bangladeshis who organised a peaceful protest in G.Dh. Thinadhoo <sup>[N71 (2024)](#n71)</sup>. In December 2024 Immigration detained 55 workers, 17 of them Bangladeshi, who had protested near Muliaage in Malé over unpaid wages. Immigration said it would act under the Immigration Act once the investigation was finished <sup>[N80 (2024)](#n80), [N81 (2024)](#n81)</sup>.

**Papers.** You may work only with authorisation under the Employment Act <sup>[85 (2024)](#o85)</sup>. You may not work at a worksite that is unregistered or cancelled <sup>[N45](#n45)</sup>, and you may not be moved to another business without the ministry being told <sup>[N45](#n45)</sup>. Raids, detention and deportation are under Going home without valid papers and If your employer reports you absconded <sup>[N8](#n8)</sup>.

**Running a business, trading dollars, gambling.** Running your own business on a work visa is the main reason Bangladeshis are detained in raids (see [The crackdown](#the-crackdown)) <sup>[N82 (2024)](#n82), [N19](#n19)</sup>. Selling dollars above the official rate, or letting someone else use your bank account for it, can bring criminal charges (see [Sending money home](#sending-money-home)) <sup>[N83](#n83), [N84](#n84)</sup>. In April 2026 police handed 38 Bangladeshis to Immigration for deportation over drugs and gambling <sup>[N85](#n85)</sup>.

**Any law.** Immigration can revoke the visa of anyone who breaks any Maldivian law or regulation <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - What does the Drugs Act now give for a first offence of using or possessing a small amount of cannabis, and are foreigners deported after serving the sentence?
> - When a Bangladeshi is arrested, is the High Commission told, and how does the family find out where he is held?

<a id="if-you-arent-paid"></a>

### 2.11 If you aren't paid

The employer must pay at least monthly <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. Complaining against your employer is not a lawful reason to dismiss you <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. When a job ends, everything owed must be paid within 7 days <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. In a dispute over dismissal it is the employer who must prove the reason <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.

**Collect proof first.** Keep your payslips (the employer must give one with every payment) and ask for a copy of the hours and overtime records the employer must keep <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. The LRA form asks whether you have a written contract and job description <sup>[88 (2019)](#o88)</sup>.

Complain in this order:

1. <a id="if-you-arent-paid-item-1"></a>**Write to the employer.** If the employer doesn't fix a breach of the Act or your contract within the time your notice gives, you can resign after giving the notice your contract requires, and the law treats that as dismissal without reasonable cause <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. Send it by WhatsApp or letter and keep a copy: "To [employer]: I have not been paid my wages for [months], [amount]. The Employment Act requires wages to be paid at least monthly. Please pay by [date]. [Name, passport no., work permit no., date]" <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
2. <a id="if-you-arent-paid-item-2"></a>**Complain to LRA,** online or on 332 8282; LRA may not tell the employer who complained <sup>[N51](#n51), [30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.

   You'll need <sup>[88 (2019)](#o88)</sup>:
   - your name, email, phone and address in the Maldives;
   - your passport number;
   - your job title and start date;
   - the employer's name, address and phone, and your supervisor's name and phone.

   1. Open lra.gov.mv/submit-a-complaint-form <sup>[88 (2019)](#o88)</sup>.
   2. Fill in "Personal Information" (choose "Bangladeshi" under Nationality) <sup>[88 (2019)](#o88)</sup>.
   3. Under "Employment Information" give your designation and start date, and tick whether you have a written contract and job description <sup>[88 (2019)](#o88)</sup>.
   4. Under "Employers Information" give the employer's details and say whether it is still in business <sup>[88 (2019)](#o88)</sup>.
   5. Under "Employment Disputes" choose "Non Payment of Wages", "Non Payment of Overtime/ allowances", "Breach of Contract", "Failure to grant leaves", "Working Environment Issue", "Noncompliance of fundamental rights" or "Others", and write the details: months unpaid, amounts, names <sup>[88 (2019)](#o88)</sup>.

   **In practice:** in a 2022-23 case of 49 workers unpaid for months in Hulhumalé, LRA wrote that it could not make progress because the company would not cooperate, and told them to go to the Tribunal <sup>[N86 (2023)](#n86)</sup>.
3. <a id="if-you-arent-paid-item-3"></a>**File a case at the Employment Tribunal.** You can file yourself, or someone can file for you, about any breach of your employment agreement <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. On an island outside Malé, file through the island's court <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. The Tribunal can hold hearings by audio or video if travel costs or distance would delay the case <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. A dismissal complaint must be filed within three months of the dismissal <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. Tribunal decisions are final, though either side can appeal to the High Court within 60 days <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. If the employer ignores a decision, the Tribunal sends it to court for enforcement <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>, through the Civil Court <sup>[N47 (2024)](#n47)</sup>.
4. <a id="if-you-arent-paid-item-4"></a>**Get legal help.** The Public Interest Law Centre (PILC) filed the Tribunal case for the 49 Hulhumalé workers <sup>[N86 (2023)](#n86)</sup>.
5. <a id="if-you-arent-paid-item-5"></a>**Tell the Bangladesh High Commission's labour wing in Malé:** hotline +960 7648006 (WhatsApp), labour.male@mofa.gov.bd <sup>[52](#o52), [90](#o90)</sup>.

**If the company goes bankrupt or closes:** the contract ends within a month <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. Workers are paid before the government and other creditors, starting with wages, overtime and service charge for the last 27 weeks <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.

**Protests.** Workers who protested over unpaid wages in December 2024 were detained by Immigration. LRA looked into their claims at the Immigration Detention Centre <sup>[N80 (2024)](#n80), [N81 (2024)](#n81)</sup>.

<a id="if-you-want-to-change-employer"></a>

### 2.12 If you want to change employer

You have the right to change employer, but only through the ministry's procedure <sup>[N47 (2024)](#n47)</sup>. Working for another business without that procedure is illegal <sup>[N45](#n45)</sup>.

**When you can move:**
- **With a no objection letter** (NOC) from your current employer, issued within the last 3 months <sup>[29](#o29)</sup>.
- **A new work permit holder** can apply straight away if the permit has at least 8 days left <sup>[29](#o29)</sup>.
- **Under the December 2025 rules,** a change is possible once the work permit fee has gone unpaid for a month, together with a no objection letter <sup>[N46](#n46)</sup>. A worker reported missing during a period the ministry announces can change employer if it is proven the employer neglected him <sup>[N46](#n46)</sup>.

**Why it matters:** if your employer doesn't pay its foreign worker fees, the ministry cancels the permits, and they come back only after full payment <sup>[N7](#n7)</sup>. In October 2025 only 7,900 of the 68,000 employers registered on the Expat Portal were paying regularly, and more than 8,000 had been suspended <sup>[N7](#n7)</sup>.

**How the move happens:**

1. <a id="if-you-want-to-change-employer-item-1"></a>**Get the NOC.** It must give the old employer's details and your details, and may give your work permit number <sup>[29](#o29)</sup>. Ready wording for the old employer: "No Objection Letter. [Company name, registration no., address, phone] has no objection to [worker's name], [nationality], passport no. [ ], work permit no. [ ], changing employer to [new employer]. [Date, name and signature of authorised person, company stamp]" <sup>[29](#o29)</sup>.
2. <a id="if-you-want-to-change-employer-item-2"></a>**Get a medical report no older than 45 days** <sup>[29](#o29)</sup>.
3. <a id="if-you-want-to-change-employer-item-3"></a>**Apply through Change Employer.** It is on oneGov (one.gov.mv), which redirects to the Xpat Portal <sup>[29](#o29)</sup>. The new employer needs a quota slot valid for at least 45 days, linked to an active worksite <sup>[29](#o29)</sup>.

   You'll need <sup>[29](#o29)</sup>:
   - your passport bio-data page and a passport-size digital photo;
   - a letter of appointment from the new employer, following the template on the Xpat home page;
   - the Immigration IM30 form (visa applicant information);
   - the medical report and the NOC;
   - certificates, for professional jobs.
4. <a id="if-you-want-to-change-employer-item-4"></a>**Wait for approval.** It takes 3 working days once the application is complete <sup>[29](#o29)</sup>. After approval, the new employer pays the work permit deposit and the new permit is issued <sup>[29](#o29)</sup>. If the new employer's quota slot came from regularisation and doesn't show, it should call the ministry (1500 in its guide; the Department of Foreign Labour is now on 1485) <sup>[29](#o29), [5](#o5)</sup>.
5. <a id="if-you-want-to-change-employer-item-5"></a>**Don't start at the new site** until the new permit is issued and the site is registered <sup>[N45](#n45)</sup>.

<a id="if-your-employer-reports-you-absconded"></a>

### 2.13 If your employer reports you absconded

**What it is.** An employer reports a worker missing in Xpat and pays a non-refundable MVR 1,000 within 3 days, and the work permit status becomes "Reported Missing" <sup>[14](#o14)</sup>. The employer types the date you went missing and remarks on how you left, and can file without any supporting documents by declaring it cannot provide them <sup>[14](#o14)</sup>. It can file long after you left <sup>[14](#o14)</sup>. Working for anyone other than the employer named on your permit is illegal, and switching without approval leads the employer to file the report <sup>[N44 (2024)](#n44), [N87](#n87)</sup>.

**What follows.**
- After a 3-day hold in which the employer can withdraw, the Labour Relations Authority (LRA) investigates whether you absconded or the employer was negligent, looking at the employer's violation history and earlier missing reports <sup>[14](#o14)</sup>.
- If the LRA finds you absconded, the report is approved and your permit becomes "Missing Abscond" <sup>[14](#o14), [91](#o91)</sup>.
- If it finds employer negligence, the employer is fined (MVR 2,000 in Xpat's missing-report process; MVR 50,000 per worker under Regulation 2025/R-120) and you can get a new permit with another employer through the employer-change process ("Missing Negligence") <sup>[14](#o14), [5](#o5), [91](#o91), [N88](#n88)</sup>.
- After 30 days the employer may cancel your permit and free its quota slot while the investigation goes on <sup>[14](#o14)</sup>.
- If you do not cooperate with the investigation for 10 days, your permit is revoked <sup>[N88](#n88)</sup>.
- Your passport, if the employer hands it in, is kept by Immigration, which publishes the list of passports of absconding or missing workers it holds (8,576 entries in November 2026, 8,543 of them Bangladeshi) <sup>[92](#o92), [93 (2020)](#o93)</sup>.
- Without a valid permit you can be detained and deported at any check (see [Going home without valid papers](#going-home-without-valid-papers)) <sup>[N8](#n8), [N89 (2024)](#n89)</sup>.

**What you can do, in this order:**
1. <a id="if-your-employer-reports-you-absconded-item-1"></a>**Check your status** on Xpat (see [Step 1: Find a real job](#step-1-find-a-real-job): Check your status) <sup>[94](#o94)</sup>.
2. <a id="if-your-employer-reports-you-absconded-item-2"></a>**Ask the employer to withdraw the report** if you are willing to go back <sup>[95](#o95)</sup>. The employer does it in Xpat: Work Permit, View Work Permit, Details, "Missing" tab, "New Request" in the Missing Withdrawal table, then (if the report was already approved) a "Re-Issue Work Permit" application and deposit payment, after which the permit shows "Issued (Arrived)" <sup>[95](#o95)</sup>. Within the grace period it is free; outside it the employer pays MVR 10,000 <sup>[95](#o95)</sup>. There is no time limit for now, but a blacklisted worker's report cannot be withdrawn <sup>[95](#o95)</sup>. A "Missing Negligence" report can be withdrawn only if you agree to return and have a valid contract under the Employment Act (2/2008) <sup>[95](#o95)</sup>. The Bangladesh High Commission's labour wing says companies that filed reports must withdraw them and take the worker back <sup>[N87](#n87)</sup>.
3. <a id="if-your-employer-reports-you-absconded-item-3"></a>**If the employer refuses, go to the LRA** and explain why you left; if the LRA accepts your reasons it gives a letter on which another company can get you a work permit <sup>[N87](#n87)</sup>. Say, in writing:
   > "I am [full name], passport [number], work permit [number]. My employer [company name] reported me missing on [date]. I left because [my salary was not paid from (month) / my passport was taken / I was not given food or housing]. I ask the LRA to find that my absence was caused by the employer's negligence so that I can change employer." <sup>[14](#o14), [N88](#n88)</sup>
4. <a id="if-your-employer-reports-you-absconded-item-4"></a>**Appeal** a missing-report decision to the LRA if it goes against you <sup>[14](#o14)</sup>.
5. <a id="if-your-employer-reports-you-absconded-item-5"></a>**If you left over unpaid wages or mistreatment,** as many construction workers do, file the wage complaint too (see [If you aren't paid](#if-you-arent-paid)) <sup>[N90 (2024)](#n90)</sup>.

> **Need to know**

<a id="leaving-at-the-end-of-the-job"></a>

### 2.14 Leaving at the end of the job

1. <a id="leaving-at-the-end-of-the-job-item-1"></a>**Get notice in writing** <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. An employer ending a contract with no fixed end date must give at least <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>:
   - two weeks after more than six months' service;
   - one month after more than one year;
   - two months after more than five years.

   The employer can skip notice by paying the wages and benefits for the notice period, or without pay only where your conduct is unacceptable <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. If you resign, give the notice your contract requires <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. For redundancy the notice is one month under one year of service, two months for 1 to 4 years and three months over 4 years <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
2. <a id="leaving-at-the-end-of-the-job-item-2"></a>**Collect everything owed within 7 days** <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. That includes unused annual leave <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup> and your share of service charge <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. If it isn't paid, see [If you aren't paid](#if-you-arent-paid).
3. <a id="leaving-at-the-end-of-the-job-item-3"></a>**Ask for a service record** within 6 months of leaving <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. It must give the employer, your dates of service, workplace, job title, salary and benefits, and no reason for leaving unless you ask <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. Wording: "Please give me a written performance record under section 24 of the Employment Act. [Name, passport no., dates of employment]" <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
4. <a id="leaving-at-the-end-of-the-job-item-4"></a>**Ticket home.** The 2021 regulation made the employer arrange the return trip when the job or permit ends, and if the government ordered a worker sent home and the employer did not act, the government sent him home using the employer's deposit <sup>[96 (2021)](#o96)</sup>. Deportations are still paid from employers' deposits held by the state <sup>[N92 (2024)](#n92)</sup>.
5. <a id="leaving-at-the-end-of-the-job-item-5"></a>**Pension savings,** if you joined: apply to withdraw them before you go <sup>[N76 (2023)](#n76)</sup>.
6. <a id="leaving-at-the-end-of-the-job-item-6"></a>**Permit cancellation.** When the employer records on Xpat that you have left the country, your work permit is cancelled <sup>[N46](#n46)</sup>. No exit permit is needed <sup>[N47 (2024)](#n47)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Does the December 2025 regulation still make the employer pay the ticket home, and what can a worker do if the employer refuses?
> - How can a worker check on Xpat or IMUGA that his permit was cancelled properly, so he is not later listed as absconded?

<a id="going-home-on-leave"></a>

### 2.15 Going home on leave

1. <a id="going-home-on-leave-item-1"></a>**Agree the dates.** After a year you are owed 30 days' paid leave, and the employer sets the dates after talking to you <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
2. <a id="going-home-on-leave-item-2"></a>**Ask for your leave pay before you fly.** If you ask, it must be paid by your last working day <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. No one can make you give up your leave <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
3. <a id="going-home-on-leave-item-3"></a>**Come back within four months.** A work permit is cancelled if you stay outside the Maldives more than four months while it is valid <sup>[N46](#n46)</sup>.
4. <a id="going-home-on-leave-item-4"></a>**Check your permit's expiry date before you book.** If it expires while you are abroad, the employer can get a temporary 15-day approval with a medical done abroad, and you need a Maldives medical within 15 days of return <sup>[82 (2024)](#o82)</sup>.

<a id="bringing-your-family"></a>

### 2.16 Bringing your family

A company worker cannot bring a spouse, children or parents to live with him: only a Maldivian citizen or a foreigner on a professional-category work visa may sponsor a family member, and the Dependent Visa is only for the family of professional-category work visa holders <sup>[97](#o97), [98](#o98)</sup>. The same rule covers a sponsored family visit, because a sponsor of a visit must also be a Maldivian or a professional-category work visa holder <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>. Which jobs count as professional is under Work visa: professional (teachers, doctors, nurses, engineers); the steps are under Dependent visa <sup>[97](#o97)</sup>. Without a sponsor, a relative can come only as a visitor for up to 30 days, and may not work (see [Tourist visa](#tourist-visa)) <sup>[99](#o99)</sup>.

<a id="scams"></a>

### 2.17 Scams

<a id="done-to-workers"></a>

#### 2.17.1 Done to workers

Report it to the High Commission's labour wing in Malé (hotline +960 7648006, WhatsApp), which asks cheated workers to come forward; you can also report a broker to the Maldives Police on 119 or 332-2111 (investigations@police.gov.mv), and in Bangladesh as in [Step 1: Find a real job](#step-1-find-a-real-job): Find a real job <sup>[16](#o16), [52](#o52)</sup>.

- **Jobs that don't exist.** Companies brought workers in on real visas, left them jobless, possibly to extort them; a Bangladeshi syndicate was implicated <sup>[N28 (2024)](#n28)</sup>.
- **"Free visa" and "open visa".** Sold as a right to work anywhere; it can mean arrest and deportation <sup>[24 (2024)](#o24), [N17 (2023)](#n17)</sup>.
- **Paying to "regularise" or for a fast permit.** The High Commission warns against paying anyone who promises this; the last legalisation window closed on 2 May 2026 (see [Undocumented workers: regularisation](#undocumented-workers-regularisation)) <sup>[N13](#n13), [N9](#n9)</sup>.
- **A "business visa" or "special visa" sold as a work visa.** Since 15 September 2026 nobody who entered on one can get a work permit without leaving the Maldives <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>.
- **Employers who bring workers and abandon them.** Check the employer against Xpat's negligence list before paying ([Step 5, item 2: Check the employer is not on the negligence list](#step-5-check-your-permit-and-visa-are-real-item-2)) <sup>[71](#o71), [5](#o5)</sup>.
- **Passports taken by the agent.** 40% of workers surveyed said agencies or agents took their passports; agencies are banned from doing this <sup>[N6 (2024)](#n6), [N22](#n22)</sup>.
- **Pay promised in dollars, paid in fewer rufiyaa.** Workers promised USD 350 a month were paid MVR 4,000 (about USD 260) after arrival, with their passports held; the letter of appointment must show pay in both currencies ([Step 2: The job offer and contract](#step-2-the-job-offer-and-contract): The job offer and contract) <sup>[N86 (2023)](#n86), [59 (2021)](#o59), [1](#o1)</sup>.

<a id="tried-by-workers"></a>

#### 2.17.2 Tried by workers

- **Forged papers.** Traffickers target Bangladeshis entering on falsified passports, and forged company documents led to the 2024 halt <sup>[N22](#n22), [N12 (2024)](#n12)</sup>. An employer that submits forged certificates for a professional job takes full responsibility for them (Work visa: professional (teachers, doctors, nurses, engineers)) <sup>[100](#o100)</sup>.
- **Working for someone else on a "free visa".** It can mean arrest and deportation <sup>[24 (2024)](#o24)</sup>.
- **Running a business on a work visa.** It is illegal, and it is the main reason Bangladeshis are detained in raids <sup>[N19](#n19), [N82 (2024)](#n82)</sup>.
- **Marrying to stay legal.** The Family Court now asks for a valid visa and passport before a marriage (see Marriage visa (spouse of a Maldivian)) <sup>[N93 (2024)](#n93)</sup>.

## 3 Work visa: professional (teachers, doctors, nurses, engineers)

A professional uses the same route as a company worker: the employer gets a quota, the work permit and the entry pass on Xpat, and then the work e-Visa (see [Step 3: Register with your professional council](#step-3-register-with-your-professional-council): The employer gets your work permit and entry pass) <sup>[7](#o7), [6](#o6)</sup>. Unlike a company worker, a professional can bring a spouse, children under 18 or parents (see [Dependent visa](#dependent-visa)) <sup>[97](#o97)</sup>. The rights and problem procedures under Work visa: company worker (construction, resort, shop) apply too <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. In June 2024 BOESL, the state recruiting agency, got BMET permission to send 76 doctors (medical officers and specialists) to the Maldives Ministry of Health on 2-year contracts <sup>[38 (2024)](#o38)</sup>. Xpat classes each occupation as Professional or Non-Professional: registered nurses, medical officers, primary school teachers and civil and electrical engineers are Professional, while carpenters, masons and waiters are Non-Professional <sup>[101](#o101)</sup>. A professional or managerial permit needs an MQA level 7 qualification in the field, or MQA level 5 with 5 years' relevant experience and an MQA recognition report <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>. On the MQA scale, level 7 is a bachelor's degree and level 5 a diploma <sup>[100](#o100)</sup>. The employer also uploads your certificates, with certified English translations, and any registration the occupation needs <sup>[7](#o7)</sup>. Under Regulation 2025/R-120, quotas for nurses must be cancelled after three years, for electrical engineers after four and for primary and secondary teachers after five (see [How long you can stay](#how-long-you-can-stay)) <sup>[N15](#n15), [5](#o5)</sup>.

## 4 Work visa: domestic worker

### 4.2 Step 2: Go only through an approved agency

1. <a id="step-2-go-only-through-an-approved-agency-item-1"></a>**Use one of the agencies the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare has approved to send women domestic workers** <sup>[108 (2024)](#o108)</sup>. Each has lodged a Tk 15 lakh security deposit with BMET, must have at least one woman on its office staff, and may take no migration cost from you; it signs an undertaking that its licence may be cancelled if you are abused at work <sup>[108 (2024)](#o108), [4](#o4)</sup>. The ministry's order approved 21 agencies <sup>[108 (2024)](#o108)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Do any of the 21 approved women-domestic-worker agencies send women to the Maldives, and through which Maldivian agents?

<a id="step-3-the-employer-gets-your-permit-maldives-side"></a>

### 4.3 Step 3: The employer gets your permit (Maldives side)

1. <a id="step-3-the-employer-gets-your-permit-maldives-side-item-1"></a>**Only a Maldivian citizen can employ you in a home,** and one person may register up to five foreign workers under Regulation 2025/R-120 <sup>[N97](#n97), [5](#o5)</sup>. The household is registered as a worksite on Xpat, which only verified eFaas users can use, and you may not be put to work at an unregistered worksite <sup>[N97](#n97)</sup>. The employer pays a quota fee of MVR 2,000 per worker for 12 months <sup>[N97](#n97)</sup>.
2. <a id="step-3-the-employer-gets-your-permit-maldives-side-item-2"></a>**Check the permit and entry pass are real** before you fly (see [Step 5: Check your permit and visa are real](#step-5-check-your-permit-and-visa-are-real): Check your permit and visa are real); the permit must be for the registered household where you will work <sup>[N97](#n97)</sup>.

<a id="scams-1"></a>

### 4.4 Scams

- **Spa, massage and "salon" jobs.** Traffickers advertise spa and similar jobs on social media and bring South Asian women on tourist visas or as massage therapists, then force them into commercial sex <sup>[N22](#n22)</sup>. Police have reported a rise in Bangladeshi women brought in on tourist visas for this <sup>[N98 (2022)](#n98)</sup>. A tourist visa does not allow any paid work (see [Tourist visa](#tourist-visa)) <sup>[99](#o99), [N22](#n22)</sup>.
- **If you are found at such a venue,** the police arrest and immediately deport foreigners rather than treat them as victims <sup>[N22](#n22)</sup>.
- **Housemaid on paper, thrown out in practice.** Immigration has said many undocumented people came as housemaids and were thrown out by their employers, and in 2014 it froze housemaid permits for three months for that reason <sup>[N99 (2014)](#n99)</sup>.

<a id="tourist-visa"></a>

## 5 Tourist visa

Bangladeshis, like all other nationalities except Israeli passport holders, get a tourist visa on arrival at the airport with no application in advance <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>. It is free and allows up to 30 days from entry <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>. It is only for visitors with no intention to work or earn money in the Maldives (see [Can you work on a tourist visa?](#can-you-work-on-a-tourist-visa)) <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>. The Bangladesh High Commission in Malé asks Bangladeshis travelling as tourists to follow the visa-on-arrival conditions strictly <sup>[109](#o109)</sup>.

**Status for Bangladeshis (8 November 2026):** visa on arrival open; no suspension or quota for Bangladeshi tourists is published <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>. Bangladeshis are nonetheless turned back at Velana International Airport for fake hotel bookings and for not knowing where they will stay (see [Step 3: At the airports](#step-3-at-the-airports): At the airports) <sup>[N100](#n100)</sup>.

<a id="step-1-prepare"></a>

### 5.1 Step 1: Prepare

You'll need:

- a machine-readable passport (with the two coded lines at the bottom of the photo page) valid at least 1 month; a passport whose validity has been extended is refused <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>; airlines in Dhaka often ask for 6 months' validity at boarding <sup>[N101](#n101)</sup>;
- a confirmed return ticket <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>;
- a prepaid, confirmed booking at a registered hotel, guesthouse or resort for the whole stay, or an Immigration-approved sponsorship instead (see Visiting a relative (sponsored visit)) <sup>[8](#o8), [N100](#n100)</sup>;
- enough money for the stay: there is no published minimum, and the officer judges it against your days and hotel cost <sup>[8](#o8), [N101](#n101)</sup>;
- for a child, the child's own passport and own declaration <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>;
- a yellow fever vaccination certificate only if arriving from, or recently through, a yellow-fever-risk country (children under one exempt) <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>;
- what your next destination requires, if you are flying on <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>.

1. <a id="step-1-prepare-item-1"></a>**Carry the printed confirmation of a prepaid, confirmed booking at a registered facility;** many Bangladeshis are sent back for carrying fake booking copies <sup>[8](#o8), [N100](#n100)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - How much cash or bank balance do Velana officers expect a Bangladeshi tourist to show per day in 2026?
> - Do officers phone the hotel to check a booking, or check it on a system?
> - Which Dhaka travel agencies sell "Maldives package" bookings that turn out to be unpaid or fake?

<a id="step-2-fill-in-the-traveller-declaration-on-imuga"></a>

### 5.2 Step 2: Fill in the Traveller Declaration on IMUGA

1. <a id="step-2-fill-in-the-traveller-declaration-on-imuga-item-1"></a>**Submit the Traveller Declaration within 96 hours (4 days) before your flight lands,** only at [imuga.immigration.gov.mv](https://imuga.immigration.gov.mv/); it is free and needs no agent <sup>[8](#o8), [110](#o110)</sup>. A declaration is also required within 96 hours of departure <sup>[111](#o111)</sup>.

   You'll need: your passport, an email address you can open on your phone, your flight details and the address where you will stay <sup>[112](#o112)</sup>.

   1. Open the portal and choose Traveller Declaration <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>.
   2. Type your email and press "Send verification code"; enter the 6-digit code sent to you <sup>[112](#o112)</sup>.
   3. Fill in the six steps: Verify email, Purpose, Personal, Travel, Health, Customs <sup>[112](#o112)</sup>.
   4. Add family or group members at the end; every traveller, child or adult, needs a separate declaration <sup>[112](#o112), [8](#o8)</sup>.
   5. Submit; the confirmation, with a QR code, comes by email; keep a screenshot and a print-out <sup>[112](#o112), [N101](#n101)</sup>.

2. <a id="step-2-fill-in-the-traveller-declaration-on-imuga-item-2"></a>**Show the declaration with your passport at immigration on arrival** <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>.

**Scams:** websites and people charge fees to submit the declaration; the real one is free and only on imuga.immigration.gov.mv (see [Done to travellers](#done-to-travellers)) <sup>[110](#o110), [8](#o8)</sup>.

<a id="step-3-at-the-airports"></a>

### 5.3 Step 3: At the airports

**At Dhaka airport:** airlines often ask for 6 months' passport validity at boarding <sup>[N101](#n101)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Does Bangladesh immigration at Shahjalal airport stop ("offload") Maldives-bound tourists, and on what grounds?
> - Must a Bangladeshi tourist going to the Maldives show anything at Dhaka beyond passport, ticket and booking?

**At Velana International Airport, Malé:** the officer checks your passport, declaration, return ticket, booking and money, and may ask why you came, where you will stay and when you will leave <sup>[8](#o8), [N101](#n101)</sup>. You must answer truthfully and completely <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>. A complete itinerary does not guarantee entry: the officer decides under sections 7 and 8 of the Immigration Act 2007 <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>. Anyone who cannot prove enough money for living and accommodation for the stay is refused <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>. Bangladeshis are turned back for fake hotel bookings or for being unable to say where they will stay <sup>[N100](#n100)</sup>.

**If refused entry:** you are sent back on the airline you came on, and may be held at a place Immigration chooses until the flight; the carrier bears the cost of detention and removal <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>.

<a id="extending-your-stay"></a>

### 5.4 Extending your stay

The Controller of Immigration may let a tourist stay up to 90 days from entry, after paying the fee; it is a decision, not a right <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>. The fee in the Immigration Act is MVR 750 (about Tk 6,000) for each three months or part of three months <sup>[10](#o10), [1](#o1), [2](#o2)</sup>.

1. <a id="extending-your-stay-item-1"></a>**Apply on IMUGA before your 30 days end** <sup>[113](#o113), [10](#o10)</sup>.

   You'll need: your passport number (or a scan of the photo page if the passport was renewed or details don't match), a passport-size photo taken within the last 12 months, your current hotel details, and money for the fee <sup>[113](#o113), [10](#o10)</sup>. Extensions are usually approved when the traveller shows enough money and a resort or hotel stay, or a letter from a local sponsor <sup>[N102](#n102)</sup>.

   1. Log in to IMUGA <sup>[113](#o113)</sup>.
   2. Enter your passport number, or upload the photo page; the system fills in your details and hotel record <sup>[113](#o113)</sup>.
   3. Update the hotel if you moved <sup>[113](#o113)</sup>.
   4. Upload the photo and submit <sup>[113](#o113)</sup>.

2. <a id="extending-your-stay-item-2"></a>**Wait about 48 hours** for the decision <sup>[113](#o113)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Is the extension fee still MVR 750 after the Immigration Act's 2020 amendment, and is it paid online in IMUGA?
> - Can a tourist without an IMUGA account (no Maldivian eFaas login) open one from abroad, and with which ID?

<a id="visiting-a-relative-sponsored-visit"></a>

### 5.5 Visiting a relative (sponsored visit)

A relative or friend in the Maldives can sponsor your visit through a Sponsorship Declaration, which replaces the hotel booking <sup>[8](#o8), [98](#o98)</sup>. Only a Maldivian citizen or a foreigner on a valid professional-category work visa can sponsor; a Bangladeshi construction, resort or shop worker cannot (see [Visas not open to Bangladeshis](#visas-not-open-to-bangladeshis)) <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>.

1. <a id="visiting-a-relative-sponsored-visit-item-1"></a>**Send the sponsor a copy of your passport, and proof of relationship if any** (marriage or birth certificate) <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>.
2. <a id="visiting-a-relative-sponsored-visit-item-2"></a>**The sponsor applies on IMUGA while you are still outside the Maldives** <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>.

   You'll need (the sponsor): form IM20, your passport copy, the sponsor's National Identity Card (Maldivian) or Work Permit Card or Employment Approval (foreigner), proof of relationship, and the company seal if a company sponsors <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>.

   1. Log in to IMUGA, go to Visa, then Apply for a Visa <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>.
   2. Confirm the visitor is outside the Maldives, choose Sponsorship and the category (Visiting) <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>.
   3. Fill in the form, upload the documents and submit <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>.

3. <a id="visiting-a-relative-sponsored-visit-item-3"></a>**Wait about 5 working days;** the sponsor downloads the Sponsorship Approval Slip from IMUGA <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>.
4. <a id="visiting-a-relative-sponsored-visit-item-4"></a>**Buy your ticket only after approval, and enter within 30 days of it** <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>. Fill in the Traveller Declaration as in [Step 2: Fill in the Traveller Declaration on IMUGA](#step-2-fill-in-the-traveller-declaration-on-imuga): Fill in the Traveller Declaration on IMUGA <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Is there a fee for a visiting Sponsorship Declaration?
> - Does a visitor on a sponsorship still get 30 days, or the length the sponsor asked for?

<a id="can-you-work-on-a-tourist-visa"></a>

### 5.6 Can you work on a tourist visa?

No. A tourist visa is for people with no intention to work or earn <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>. Every foreigner must stay within the conditions of the visa, and an employer may not hire anyone without a work visa <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>. Breaking the Immigration Act costs a foreigner a fine of MVR 15,000 to 50,000 (about Tk 1.2 to 4 lakh), and Immigration may deport the person and bar re-entry for a set period <sup>[10](#o10), [1](#o1), [2](#o2)</sup>. In 2021 Immigration took legal action against sixty foreigners after complaints that resorts and other businesses used foreigners as musicians and photographers without proper visas <sup>[114 (2021)](#o114)</sup>. The High Commission warns that people who try to work on a tourist visa face jail and a fine, and that anyone who enters on one visa and works elsewhere is arrested and sent back to Bangladesh <sup>[26](#o26), [N34](#n34)</sup>.

**Overstaying:** a foreigner must leave when the visa ends <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>. Staying without a valid visa is prohibited and punished as above <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>; staying over 60 days without permission has brought fines of MVR 15,000 or deportation <sup>[N102](#n102)</sup>. For what happens after arrest, see [Going home without valid papers](#going-home-without-valid-papers).

<a id="scams-2"></a>

### 5.7 Scams

<a id="done-to-travellers"></a>

#### 5.7.1 Done to travellers

- **Paid "IMUGA" or "Maldives visa" sites:** the visa on arrival and the declaration are free; sites and people charging for them are scams <sup>[8](#o8), [110](#o110), [N102](#n102)</sup>.
- **Fake hotel bookings sold with "packages":** travellers carrying fake bookings are sent back from Velana <sup>[N100](#n100)</sup>.
- **A "job" or "free visa" on a tourist visa:** no free visa exists, a tourist can never switch to a work visa, and working on the wrong visa ends in arrest and removal <sup>[N34](#n34), [26](#o26)</sup>.

<a id="tried-by-travellers"></a>

#### 5.7.2 Tried by travellers

Giving false information or false documents can get the visa revoked, with or without notice <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>; after revocation without notice you must leave within one day <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>.

**If someone sold you a "job on a tourist visa", or you are stranded in the Maldives,** in this order:

1. <a id="tried-by-travellers-item-1"></a>**Bangladesh High Commission, Malé, labour welfare wing:** emergency hotline +960 7648006 (WhatsApp) <sup>[109](#o109)</sup>. (Address and consular contacts: see Contacts.)
2. <a id="tried-by-travellers-item-2"></a>**Report the broker to Maldives Immigration:** call centre 1434 (8.30 am to 2 pm, Sunday to Thursday), or anonymously through Immigration Watch on IMUGA <sup>[111](#o111)</sup>.
3. <a id="tried-by-travellers-item-3"></a>**In Bangladesh,** complain about the broker or agency to the Probashi call centre, 16135, or BMET (see [Step 1: Prepare](#step-1-prepare): Find a real job) <sup>[56](#o56), [4](#o4)</sup>.

Sample to the High Commission: "I, [name, passport no.], paid [broker, address, phone] Tk [amount] on [date] for a job in the Maldives. I was sent on a tourist visa on [date] and [what happened]. I am now at [place], phone [number]." <sup>[109](#o109)</sup>

<a id="business-visa"></a>

## 6 Business visa

A business visa is for a visitor doing approved business, not for working as an employee <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>. It allows multiple entries and a total stay of up to 90 days in any 12 months <sup>[115](#o115)</sup>. A locally registered Maldivian business or organisation must invite you and apply for you <sup>[115](#o115)</sup>.

<a id="step-1-the-maldivian-company-applies"></a>

### 6.1 Step 1: The Maldivian company applies

You'll need (to send the company): a clear colour scan of your passport photo page, the passport valid at least 6 months, and a photo taken within the last 3 months that meets Immigration's photo standard <sup>[115](#o115)</sup>.

1. <a id="step-1-the-maldivian-company-applies-item-1"></a>**The company submits the application on the Business Portal of the Ministry of Economic Development and Trade,** [business.egov.mv](http://business.egov.mv) <sup>[115](#o115)</sup>.
2. <a id="step-1-the-maldivian-company-applies-item-2"></a>**A General Service Request (GSR) number is generated** and the status shows "Submitted" <sup>[115](#o115)</sup>.
3. <a id="step-1-the-maldivian-company-applies-item-3"></a>**The Ministry approves, then Immigration verifies the application** <sup>[115](#o115)</sup>.
4. <a id="step-1-the-maldivian-company-applies-item-4"></a>**The approved eVisa is downloaded from the Business Portal** <sup>[115](#o115)</sup>.

Do not travel before approval <sup>[115](#o115)</sup>. Approval does not guarantee entry <sup>[115](#o115)</sup>. The Immigration Act sets the business visa fee at MVR 750 (about Tk 6,000) for each three months or part <sup>[99](#o99), [1](#o1), [2](#o2)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - What does the Business Portal actually charge for a business visa in 2026, who pays it, and how many working days does approval take?
> - Can the visitor check the eVisa's validity himself, on IMUGA or elsewhere?

<a id="can-you-work"></a>

### 6.2 Can you work?

No: a business visa covers business or work approved by the government, not employment in the Maldives <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>. Working as an employee breaks the visa (see [Tourist visa](#tourist-visa)) <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>.

<a id="scams-3"></a>

### 6.3 Scams

A "business visa" sold as a work visa: since 15 September 2026 nobody who enters on a business visa can get a work permit without leaving the Maldives <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>.

<a id="meeting-visa"></a>

## 7 Meeting visa

The Meeting Visa is a 14-day visa on arrival for attending a meeting, conference or convention <sup>[116](#o116)</sup>. The event must be for a business registered with the Registrar of Businesses, for government use, or approved by the relevant government authority <sup>[116](#o116)</sup>. You'll need what a tourist needs: a confirmed return ticket, a prepaid hotel booking, proof of money, a machine-readable passport valid 1 month, and the Traveller Declaration within 96 hours before the flight (see [Step 2: The job offer and contract](#step-2-the-job-offer-and-contract): Fill in the Traveller Declaration on IMUGA) <sup>[116](#o116)</sup>. It cannot be extended, and you cannot switch to a business visa inside the Maldives <sup>[116](#o116)</sup>.

<a id="dependent-visa"></a>

## 8 Dependent visa

The Dependent Visa lets the spouse, children under 18 or parents of a foreigner working on a professional-category work visa live in the Maldives with him or her <sup>[97](#o97)</sup>. It is not open to the family of company workers on ordinary work permits (see [Bringing your family](#bringing-your-family)) <sup>[97](#o97), [98](#o98)</sup>. The visa costs MVR 750 (about Tk 6,000) <sup>[97](#o97), [1](#o1), [2](#o2)</sup>. The worker in the Maldives does every step on IMUGA; the family's part is to send the papers and travel <sup>[97](#o97)</sup>.

### 8.1 Step 1: Check you qualify

1. <a id="step-1-check-you-qualify-item-1"></a>**Check your own visa category.** You can sponsor only if you hold a valid professional-category work visa; your Work Permit Card or Employment Approval is attached to the sponsorship <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>.
2. <a id="step-1-check-you-qualify-item-2"></a>**Check who you can bring.** A spouse, children below 18, or parents <sup>[97](#o97)</sup>.

<a id="step-2-get-the-familys-papers-ready-in-bangladesh"></a>

### 8.2 Step 2: Get the family's papers ready in Bangladesh

1. <a id="step-2-get-the-familys-papers-ready-in-bangladesh-item-1"></a>**Collect the documents for each family member** <sup>[97](#o97)</sup>:
   - passport copy (biodata page) and a passport-size photo to the Immigration photo standard;

<a id="step-3-submit-the-sponsorship-declaration-on-imuga"></a>

### 8.3 Step 3: Submit the Sponsorship Declaration on IMUGA

1. <a id="step-3-submit-the-sponsorship-declaration-on-imuga-item-1"></a>**Apply at least 7 working days before the family's arrival,** while they are still outside the Maldives; the option disappears once they are in the country <sup>[97](#o97), [98](#o98)</sup>. Processing normally takes 5 working days <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>.
   You'll need: form IM20 signed by you, the family member's passport copy, your Work Permit Card or Employment Approval, and the marriage or birth certificate <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>.
   1. Log in to IMUGA, go to **Visa** and select **Apply for a Visa** <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>.
   2. Confirm the person is outside the Maldives, select **Sponsorship** and choose the category <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>.
   3. State the purpose as bringing in a dependent: a sponsorship filed for another purpose (such as a visit) does not qualify the person for a Dependent Visa later <sup>[97](#o97)</sup>.
   4. Fill in the form, upload the documents, review and submit <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>.
   **What you sign:** on form IM20 the sponsor takes full responsibility for the person's whole stay, including accommodation, food and medical costs <sup>[117](#o117)</sup>.
2. <a id="step-3-submit-the-sponsorship-declaration-on-imuga-item-2"></a>**Download the Sponsorship Approval Slip** from IMUGA once approved <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>.
3. <a id="step-3-submit-the-sponsorship-declaration-on-imuga-item-3"></a>**Book the flight only after approval,** and make sure the family enters the Maldives within 30 days of the approval date <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>.

<a id="step-4-apply-for-the-dependent-visa-after-arrival"></a>

### 8.4 Step 4: Apply for the Dependent Visa after arrival

1. <a id="step-4-apply-for-the-dependent-visa-after-arrival-item-1"></a>**Apply on IMUGA once the family has arrived on the approved sponsorship** <sup>[97](#o97)</sup>.
   You'll need: application form IM20, photo, passport biodata page, a copy of your work permit or work visa, the family member's last arrival stamp (new applicants) or last visa page (renewals), proof of relationship in English, medical report and expatriate health insurance; fee MVR 750 <sup>[97](#o97)</sup>.
   1. Go to imuga.immigration.gov.mv, select **Visa**, sign in and click **Apply for a Visa** <sup>[97](#o97)</sup>.
   2. In the prerequisite field confirm that the person is in the Maldives <sup>[97](#o97)</sup>.
   3. Choose visa type **Dependent Visa** and the right reason/subtype, fill in the details, attach the documents and submit <sup>[97](#o97)</sup>.
2. <a id="step-4-apply-for-the-dependent-visa-after-arrival-item-2"></a>**Download the eVisa** from IMUGA when it is approved <sup>[97](#o97)</sup>.

**If it fails:** call the Immigration hotline 9199157 or 3330487, or email permits@immigration.gov.mv <sup>[97](#o97), [118](#o118)</sup>.

<a id="marriage-visa-spouse-of-a-maldivian"></a>

## 9 Marriage visa (spouse of a Maldivian)

The Marriage Visa is a resident visa for a foreigner legally married to a Maldivian citizen <sup>[119](#o119)</sup>. The marriage must first be lawful under the Family Act (Act No. 4/2000) and registered with the Family Court <sup>[119](#o119), [120 (2004)](#o120)</sup>. The first visa is for one year, then renewals of up to five years at a time <sup>[119](#o119)</sup>. Marriage gives residence, not citizenship: under the Constitution a foreigner becomes a citizen only in accordance with the law, and a non-Muslim never can <sup>[119](#o119), [121](#o121)</sup>.

<a id="step-1-check-the-marriage-is-allowed"></a>

### 9.1 Step 1: Check the marriage is allowed

1. <a id="step-1-check-the-marriage-is-allowed-item-1"></a>**Religion.** A Maldivian woman may not marry a non-Muslim man <sup>[120 (2004)](#o120)</sup>.
2. <a id="step-1-check-the-marriage-is-allowed-item-2"></a>**Money (foreign men only).** A foreign man marrying a Maldivian woman must be able, as the court judges, to maintain a family, and signs a declaration accepting the Family Act's obligations before the marriage <sup>[120 (2004)](#o120)</sup>. The Family Court's 2012 regulation set this at MVR 15,000 a month, shown by a salary letter (government job) or six months of bank statements (private job); the rule does not apply to foreign women marrying Maldivian men <sup>[N103 (2012)](#n103)</sup>.
3. <a id="step-1-check-the-marriage-is-allowed-item-3"></a>**Legal status.** Since September 2024 the Family Court wants a copy of the foreigner's valid visa and valid passport with the marriage application, so an undocumented worker cannot marry until his status is regular <sup>[N93 (2024)](#n93)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - What monthly income does the Family Court require of a foreign man in 2026 (still MVR 15,000?), and how does a resort or construction worker prove it?
> - Does the Family Court ask a Bangladeshi for a no-objection or single-status certificate from the Bangladesh High Commission in Malé?

<a id="step-2-apply-to-the-family-court-to-marry"></a>

### 9.2 Step 2: Apply to the Family Court to marry

1. <a id="step-2-apply-to-the-family-court-to-marry-item-1"></a>**File the marriage application with the Registrar of Marriages at the Family Court, together with your Maldivian partner, at least 7 days before the wedding date** <sup>[120 (2004)](#o120)</sup>.
   You'll need: a copy of your valid passport and valid visa, and proof of income if you are a man <sup>[N93 (2024)](#n93), [N103 (2012)](#n103)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Where exactly is the Family Court's marriage counter in Malé, what form is used, what does registration cost, and how long does approval take?

**Marrying abroad (for example in Bangladesh).** It is allowed, but the couple must follow the Family Court's procedure and get prior approval <sup>[N104](#n104)</sup>. Where the country has a Maldivian embassy, the embassy must stamp a document confirming the marriage was performed by someone authorised to conduct Muslim marriages <sup>[N103 (2012)](#n103)</sup>.

<a id="step-3-get-the-sponsorship-declaration-or-change-visa-inside-the-maldives"></a>

### 9.3 Step 3: Get the Sponsorship Declaration, or change visa inside the Maldives

**If the marriage ends.** A foreign spouse divorces or is divorced under the Family Act, and a Maldivian husband who divorces a foreign wife must pay her travel home <sup>[120 (2004)](#o120)</sup>. After divorce you may keep residence only if you have a Maldivian child; after your spouse's death, only if you still meet the rules <sup>[122](#o122)</sup>. **Scams:** Marriage applications between Maldivians and foreigners rose in 2024 as Immigration started tracing and deporting undocumented workers, and the Family Court responded by checking that the foreigner is legally resident <sup>[N93 (2024)](#n93)</sup>. When the income rule was announced, the Family Court's registrar cited a Bangladeshi husband who fled the country with his Maldivian wife's jewellery <sup>[N103 (2012)](#n103)</sup>.

<a id="resident-visa"></a>

## 10 Resident visa

A Resident Visa is long-term residence; the groups Immigration lists are a foreigner married to a Maldivian, a foreigner divorced from a Maldivian who has a Maldivian child, a widow or widower of a Maldivian who still meets the rules, and a large investor <sup>[122](#o122)</sup>. Spouses use the Marriage visa route (see Marriage visa (spouse of a Maldivian)) <sup>[119](#o119)</sup>. The investor route, the Corporate Resident Visa, needs one of the following and is applied for on ONEGov, the government's online services portal <sup>[122](#o122)</sup>:

- at least USD 1,000,000 invested in the Maldives in the past three financial years;
- a USD 250,000 fixed deposit in a Maldives-licensed bank for five years;
- at least USD 250,000 invested in a government-approved project.

Years of work in the Maldives are not among the grounds for a Resident Visa <sup>[122](#o122)</sup>.

<a id="transit-and-medical-treatment"></a>

## 12 Transit and medical treatment

**Transit:** a transit traveller is one flying on to another country through Malé without passing immigration <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>. Anyone who passes immigration, even for one night, enters on a tourist visa and needs everything in [Step 1: Find a real job](#step-1-find-a-real-job): Prepare <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>.

**Medical treatment:** there is no separate medical visa; a patient comes on a tourist visa <sup>[10](#o10), [8](#o8)</sup>. Advanced care is only in Malé (ADK Hospital, Tree Top Hospital and the government's IGMH), public hospitals ask for payment or proof of insurance up front, and unpaid bills can bar future travel <sup>[N102](#n102)</sup>. If treatment is still going on when the visa ends, a special visa can be granted to continue it, on form IM22 signed by a sponsor who takes full responsibility; it costs MVR 750 for each three months or part <sup>[10](#o10), [124](#o124)</sup>. Immigration's permits section: permits@immigration.gov.mv, hotline 9199157, phone 3330487 <sup>[124](#o124)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Does Immigration accept a hospital as sponsor on form IM22 for a patient on a tourist visa?
> - Do Bangladeshi workers' relatives come to Malé to care for an injured worker, and on which visa?

<a id="special-visa"></a>

## 13 Special visa

A special visa lets a foreigner stay for humanitarian work, to continue medical treatment that was under way when the visa expired, to finish a court case to claim a right (such as unpaid wages), or where a government authority finds it imperative <sup>[99](#o99), [10](#o10)</sup>. It is applied for on form IM22, signed by a sponsor that takes full responsibility for the person; to continue medical treatment or finish a court case it costs MVR 750 (about Tk 6,000) for each three months or part <sup>[124](#o124), [10](#o10), [1](#o1), [2](#o2)</sup>. Trafficking victims could get one to stay and work while their case is investigated and prosecuted <sup>[N22](#n22)</sup>. Since 15 September 2026 nobody who entered on a special visa can get a work permit without leaving the Maldives <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>. Immigration's permits section: permits@immigration.gov.mv, hotline 9199157, phone 3330487 <sup>[124](#o124)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Who signs form IM22 as sponsor for a worker with a wage case at the Employment Tribunal, and has any Bangladeshi worker been given this visa?

<a id="visas-not-open-to-bangladeshis"></a>

## 14 Visas not open to Bangladeshis

- **A work visa on arrival, or a switch from a tourist visa to a work visa:** since a 2011 Cabinet decision a Bangladeshi worker must hold the work visa before arriving, and a new worker is let in only on an Entry Pass (work permit) issued by the Ministry of Homeland Security, Labour and Technology <sup>[9 (2011)](#o9), [6](#o6)</sup>; the Bangladesh High Commission in Malé says a person who comes as a tourist can never get a work visa (see [Changing visa type](#changing-visa-type)) <sup>[26](#o26)</sup>.
- **"Free visa":** no such visa category exists in the Maldives <sup>[N34](#n34)</sup>.
- **A visit sponsored by a relative who is a company worker:** only a Maldivian citizen or a foreigner on a professional-category work visa can sponsor a visitor (see Visiting a relative (sponsored visit)) <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>.
- **A multiple-entry tourist visa:** the Immigration Act allows multiple-entry permits for every visa type except the tourist and special visas <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>.
- **Diplomatic visa:** only for people the Maldives government considers entitled to diplomatic immunity <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>.

<a id="changing-visa-type"></a>

## 15 Changing visa type

- **Tourist to work:** not possible; a Bangladeshi worker must have the work visa before arrival and enter on a work-permit Entry Pass (see [Step 3: The employer gets your work permit and entry pass](#step-3-the-employer-gets-your-work-permit-and-entry-pass): The employer gets your work permit and entry pass) <sup>[9 (2011)](#o9), [6](#o6), [26](#o26)</sup>.
- **Meeting to business:** not possible inside the Maldives <sup>[116](#o116)</sup>.
- **To a sponsored visit or dependent visa:** the Sponsorship Declaration can be filed only while the person is outside the Maldives <sup>[98](#o98)</sup>.
- **Business or special visa to work:** not possible without leaving; a worker needs a Work Permit Entry Pass issued before travel <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>.
- **Another valid visa to marriage visa:** possible inside the Maldives once the marriage is legally registered (see [Step 3: The employer gets your work permit and entry pass](#step-3-the-employer-gets-your-work-permit-and-entry-pass): Get the Sponsorship Declaration, or change visa inside the Maldives) <sup>[119](#o119)</sup>.
- **Sponsor Declaration to student visa:** a student enters on the approved Sponsor Declaration and applies for the student visa within 30 days (see [Step 3: The employer gets your work permit and entry pass](#step-3-the-employer-gets-your-work-permit-and-entry-pass): Student visa after arrival) <sup>[123](#o123)</sup>.
- **Extending the same visa:** possible at Immigration's discretion (see [Extending your stay](#extending-your-stay)) <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Has Immigration ever allowed a tourist-to-work switch for Bangladeshis, for example during a regularisation drive?

**If a visa is refused or revoked.** Immigration can revoke any visa, with or without notice, for false information or documents, if the reason for the visa no longer exists, or for breaking any Maldivian law <sup>[99](#o99)</sup>. Revoked without notice, you must leave within one day <sup>[99](#o99)</sup>. The Immigration Act gives no right to appeal or to ask for a review <sup>[99](#o99)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Does Immigration accept a written request to reconsider a refused or revoked visa in practice, and at which counter or email?

<a id="asylum"></a>

## 16 Asylum

The Maldives offers no asylum: it has not signed the 1951 Refugee Convention or its 1967 Protocol, and it has no asylum system or refugee protection body <sup>[N105 (2021)](#n105)</sup>. A foreigner whose visa has expired must leave the country <sup>[99](#o99)</sup>. The Immigration Controller can hold people in immigration detention, potentially indefinitely, without a judge reviewing it, without a lawyer and without an interpreter <sup>[N105 (2021)](#n105)</sup>. Claiming asylum is not a way to stay or to avoid deportation <sup>[N105 (2021)](#n105), [99](#o99)</sup>.

What does exist is narrower:

- **Trafficking victims** could get a special visa to stay and work while their case is investigated and prosecuted, or where there was evidence of forced labour by a previous employer <sup>[N22](#n22)</sup>. To report forced labour or unpaid wages, see If you aren't paid <sup>[N22](#n22)</sup>.
- **A worker with a case in court** to claim a right (for example unpaid wages) can be given a special visa to stay until the case ends <sup>[99](#o99)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - How does a worker apply for the trafficking-victim special visa (form IM22? through the police or the Labour Relations Authority?), and have any Bangladeshis received one?

<a id="having-a-child-in-the-maldives"></a>

## 17 Having a child in the Maldives

A child born in the Maldives to two Bangladeshi parents does not become Maldivian: citizenship comes only from a Maldivian parent <sup>[121](#o121)</sup>. The child must be registered both in the Maldives and with Bangladesh <sup>[N106 (2022)](#n106), [125](#o125)</sup>.

1. <a id="having-a-child-in-the-maldives-item-1"></a>**Register the birth with the island or city council within 7 days** <sup>[N106 (2022)](#n106)</sup>. The health worker or attendant who delivered the baby fills in the birth form, and the council must register the birth within a week of the application <sup>[N107 (2022)](#n107)</sup>. The duty is on the parents, and missing the 7 days brings a MVR 10,000 fine <sup>[N107 (2022)](#n107), [N106 (2022)](#n106)</sup>. The law is the Birth and Death Registration Act (Law No. 23/2022) <sup>[126 (2022)](#o126)</sup>.
2. <a id="having-a-child-in-the-maldives-item-2"></a>**Register the child as a Bangladeshi at the High Commission in Malé.** Bangladesh's Births and Deaths Registration Act 2004 requires a Bangladeshi born abroad to be registered with a Bangladesh mission <sup>[125](#o125)</sup>. At the High Commission in Kuala Lumpur the child and both parents must come in person with the local birth certificate attested by that country's foreign ministry, both parents' passport copies, the attested marriage certificate, the online application form and two photos <sup>[125](#o125)</sup>. Then apply for the child's passport at the High Commission ([High Commission services, item 1: Renew or replace your passport](#high-commission-services-item-1)) <sup>[125](#o125), [127 (2024)](#o127)</sup>.
3. <a id="having-a-child-in-the-maldives-item-3"></a>**Get the child a visa.** A child under 18 can get a Dependent Visa only if a parent holds a professional-category work visa (see [Dependent visa](#dependent-visa)) <sup>[97](#o97)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - What does the Bangladesh High Commission in Malé ask for to register a baby born in the Maldives, and must the Maldivian birth certificate first be attested by the Maldives Ministry of Foreign Affairs?
> - What happens to a baby born to a company worker or an undocumented mother, who cannot get a Dependent Visa: is the baby given any visa, or are mother and child sent home?
> - Do councils (Malé City Council) register births to foreign parents on the same form and fee as Maldivians?

<a id="sending-money-home"></a>

## 18 Sending money home

**Send through a bank or a licensed money-transfer company, never hundi** (an unlicensed agent who pays your family in taka) <sup>[46](#o46)</sup>. Three things make it hard from the Maldives <sup>[N42](#n42), [N1](#n1), [N108 (2024)](#n108)</sup>:

- **You are paid in rufiyaa, but money leaves the Maldives only in US dollars.** Banks and exchange houses are reluctant to take rufiyaa, so many workers buy dollars on the street first <sup>[N109](#n109), [N43](#n43), [N42](#n42)</sup>.
- **The street price of a dollar is far above the official one.** The official rate is MVR 15.42 per dollar <sup>[1](#o1)</sup>; on the black market a dollar cost a record MVR 22.45 on 13 August 2026, 45.59% more <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>. In September 2026 an MP calculated that workers buying dollars at MVR 21-22 lose 35-40% before sending, and that a worker sending MVR 5,000 (about Tk 40,300) a month loses about Tk 12,500 a month <sup>[N110](#n110), [1](#o1), [2](#o2)</sup>.
- **Banks cap what a foreign worker can send each month,** because the MMA gives each bank only a limited monthly dollar allocation <sup>[N108 (2024)](#n108)</sup>. In September 2026 Habib Bank had cut its remittance ceiling to USD 300, Bank of Ceylon had stopped opening new remittance accounts, and State Bank of India allowed Indian workers USD 150 a month <sup>[N43](#n43)</sup>.

**Your wages now go into a bank account.** Since 25 September 2026, employers must pay all foreign workers' salaries and benefits into a bank account in the worker's own name at a bank licensed by the MMA (how to open one: [Step 7: After arrival](#step-7-after-arrival): After arrival, item 7) <sup>[N59](#n59), [N111](#n111), [N68](#n68)</sup>. Paying wages in cash is no longer allowed; an employer who breaks the rule risks losing work-permit quota, a fine, blacklisting on Xpat and referral to investigators <sup>[N68](#n68)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Is any tax taken in 2026 when a foreign worker sends money abroad? (A 3% remittance tax was repealed from 1 January 2020, but an August 2026 report says a 3% tax applies to transfers, and MIRA's website still shows the 2016 Remittance Tax Regulation without a repealed mark <sup>[N112 (2019)](#n112), [N113](#n113), [128 (2017)](#o128)</sup>.)
> - What is the monthly cap for a foreign worker paid in rufiyaa at Bank of Maldives, Maldives Islamic Bank and Commercial Bank of Maldives in November 2026?
> - What does a worker do if the employer keeps paying cash after the new rule: where and how to report it?

You'll need:
- the receiver's bKash number (a Bangladeshi mobile-money account) and full name exactly as given when the account was opened <sup>[N63](#n63)</sup>.

1. <a id="sending-money-home-item-1"></a>**Choose a channel bKash lists for the Maldives:** NBL Money Transfer Maldives, Commercial Bank of Maldives, Western Union or MoneyGram <sup>[N63](#n63)</sup>.
   - **NBL Money Transfer (Maldives)** is owned by Bangladesh's National Bank PLC and licensed by the MMA; it has operated since December 2011 at Gadhamoo, Boduthakurufaanu Magu, Malé <sup>[N114](#n114)</sup>. In 2023 it was the only Bangladeshi exchange house in the Maldives <sup>[N115 (2023)](#n115)</sup>.
   - **Commercial Bank of Maldives** sends to bKash instantly through its "Bangla eRemit" link with Commercial Bank of Ceylon in Bangladesh <sup>[N116 (2022)](#n116)</sup>.
   - Workers on outer islands have to travel to an exchange house in a town to send money formally <sup>[N115 (2023)](#n115)</sup>.
2. <a id="sending-money-home-item-2"></a>**Send.** At the counter, give the bKash number and the registered name and hand over the money; the limit is Tk 2.5 lakh per transfer, as many times as you like <sup>[N63](#n63)</sup>.
3. <a id="sending-money-home-item-3"></a>**The family collects.** Money sent legally gets the government's 2.5% cash incentive, kept in the FY2026-27 budget, and sometimes a 2.5% bank incentive too; it can be cashed out at Tk 7 per thousand at BRAC Bank, City Bank and Q-Cash ATMs <sup>[129](#o129), [N63](#n63)</sup>. Money sent by hundi gets no incentive <sup>[N110](#n110)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Does NBL Money Transfer Maldives accept rufiyaa in November 2026, at what rate, and what fee does it charge to send USD 100 to bKash?
> - What ID does a Maldives remitter ask for (passport, work visa card, work permit), and can a worker whose visa has expired still send money legally?
> - What reference number is on the receipt, and how does the family trace a transfer that hasn't arrived?
> - Where does a worker complain when a bank or remitter in Malé loses or delays a transfer: does the MMA take consumer complaints, and how?
> - Do Bank of Maldives or Maldives Islamic Bank send directly to Bangladeshi bank accounts or bKash, and at what cost?

**Hundi and street dollars: the risks.**
- In Bangladesh, the Financial Intelligence Unit (BFIU) froze about 55,000 mobile-money accounts linked to digital hundi and online gambling in 2026 and decided in August 2026 to freeze about 14,000 more <sup>[N117](#n117)</sup>.
- In the Maldives, since 1 September 2026 selling or offering dollars above the MMA rate is fined MVR 25,000 to MVR 1,000,000, advertising such rates MVR 25,000 to MVR 500,000, and criminal charges can follow <sup>[N83](#n83)</sup>. Money changers now need an MMA licence <sup>[N83](#n83)</sup>.
- In August 2026 police and immigration detained four Bangladeshi and two Indian workers for trading currency; MVR 54.4 million and USD 1.62 million had passed through their accounts in three years <sup>[N84](#n84)</sup>. Police warned that anyone who knowingly lets someone else use their bank account for illegal transactions can be charged as an accomplice <sup>[N84](#n84)</sup>.

**What is being done.** The Bangladesh High Commission in Malé has asked banks to let Bangladeshi workers send at least USD 50-100 <sup>[N43](#n43)</sup>. On 8 October 2026 Bangladesh Bank proposed to the MMA a rufiyaa settlement through the Asian Clearing Union (a regional payment system), taka cards and a currency swap, and is exploring branches of Bangladeshi banks such as BRAC Bank and National Bank in the Maldives; none had been agreed by late October 2026 <sup>[N42](#n42), [N39](#n39)</sup>.

<a id="saving-at-home"></a>

### 18.1 Saving at home

**The Wage Earner Development Bond** is a 5-year government bond for workers abroad, bought in taka or foreign currency in your own name or a named beneficiary's in Bangladesh, at authorised-dealer bank branches in Bangladesh or Bangladeshi banks' branches and exchange houses abroad, in certificates of Tk 25,000 to Tk 50 lakh, with no limit on how much you buy <sup>[130](#o130)</sup>. It pays 12% simple profit on holdings up to Tk 15 lakh (11% for Tk 15-30 lakh, 10% for Tk 30-50 lakh, 9% above), paid every six months, tax-free, and the principal can be taken back in US dollars <sup>[130](#o130)</sup>.

> - Does NBL Money Transfer Maldives sell the Wage Earner Development Bond?

<a id="undocumented-workers-regularisation"></a>

## 19 Undocumented workers: regularisation

**Status on 8 November 2026: no legalisation window is open.** The Mass Legalisation Programme ran from 1 January 2026, was extended once from 2 April to 2 May 2026, and the Ministry called that extension the final opportunity <sup>[N118](#n118), [N9](#n9), [N10](#n10)</sup>. The final phase of Operation Kurangi (the government's registration and enforcement drive) runs from 2 April 2026 to 1 May 2027 and is for deportation <sup>[N119](#n119)</sup>. Since 2 May 2026 any worker found with unpaid fees is to be deported immediately, and the government says that from 2027 anyone found in breach will be deported with no further chance to regularise <sup>[N8](#n8), [N50](#n50)</sup>. The standing legalisation chapter was removed from the expatriate employment rules in December 2025; the rules now in force are Regulation 2025/R-120 (Regulation on Expatriate Employment) <sup>[N88](#n88), [87](#o87)</sup>.

<a id="how-workers-become-undocumented"></a>

### 19.1 How workers become undocumented

Most undocumented Bangladeshis entered legally and lost their status after arrival <sup>[N48](#n48), [N92 (2024)](#n92)</sup>. The main causes are an employer not paying the work permit fees, a visa not renewed, and a missing report filed by the employer <sup>[N48](#n48), [N92 (2024)](#n92)</sup>. A work visa whose permit fees have lapsed shows as invalid in Immigration's system <sup>[27](#o27)</sup>. Workers are sometimes told their work visa has become an "open" visa, which is in fact a tourist visa, and so do not seek a remedy <sup>[N92 (2024)](#n92)</sup>. About 70 percent of Bangladeshi workers do not hold their own passports <sup>[N48](#n48)</sup>. Many learn their status only when they try to fly home <sup>[N92 (2024)](#n92)</sup>.

<a id="step-1-check-your-status"></a>

### 19.2 Step 1: Check your status

1. <a id="step-1-check-your-status-item-1"></a>**Check your work permit** on Xpat's Work Permit Verification page, as in [Step 5, item 1: Check the work permit on Xpat before you pay the rest or fly](#step-5-check-your-permit-and-visa-are-real-item-1) <sup>[94](#o94)</sup>.
2. <a id="step-1-check-your-status-item-2"></a>**Check your fingerprints are on record** at xpat.egov.mv/Home/RequiresFingerprint: type your passport number, choose "Bangladesh", then "Check" <sup>[131](#o131), [73](#o73)</sup>. Workers without fingerprints taken under Operation Kurangi were the target of the 2026 raids <sup>[N120](#n120)</sup>.
3. <a id="step-1-check-your-status-item-3"></a>**If you were regularised before,** check at xpat.egov.mv/Home/RegularizationVerify with your regularisation number (it looks like RGZ/2026/...) and first name <sup>[132](#o132), [91](#o91)</sup>.
4. <a id="step-1-check-your-status-item-4"></a>**Ask your employer** to show your permit in Xpat, which lists your status and the due dates of every fee <sup>[N121](#n121)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - What status words does the public Work Permit Verification page show (for example "Reported Missing", "Missing Abscond", fees overdue), and can a worker without his permit number check at all?

<a id="step-2-give-your-fingerprints-operation-kurangi"></a>

### 19.3 Step 2: Give your fingerprints (Operation Kurangi)

Operation Kurangi recorded the biometrics of every foreign worker, without checking legal status at that stage <sup>[133](#o133)</sup>. By April 2026, 2,933 of about 206,000 foreigners had not given them <sup>[N8](#n8)</sup>. Phase three, launched by Immigration on 9 May 2026, hunts down those people, about 2,895 of them, for deportation <sup>[134](#o134), [N120](#n120), [N122](#n122)</sup>.

You'll need: yourself (no one can go for you); your passport or work permit number and accommodation address if you have them, though you can register without them <sup>[133](#o133)</sup>.

1. <a id="step-2-give-your-fingerprints-operation-kurangi-item-1"></a>**Go without an appointment** to the Operation Kurangi Joint Operation Center, NCIT Building, Malé, Saturday to Thursday, 8 am to 10 pm except public holidays, or outside Greater Malé to your island council office in government hours <sup>[133](#o133)</sup>.
2. <a id="step-2-give-your-fingerprints-operation-kurangi-item-2"></a>**If it fails,** call 7690228 or email operation.kurangi@mohst.gov.mv <sup>[133](#o133)</sup>.

<a id="the-2026-mass-legalisation-programme-closed"></a>

### 19.4 The 2026 Mass Legalisation Programme (closed)

It was open to workers registered under Operation Kurangi who were living illegally, including those in a dispute with their employer and those with no employer <sup>[N119](#n119)</sup>. Who qualified <sup>[91](#o91)</sup>:
- entered legally on a work permit or Work Permit Entry Pass;
- physically in the Maldives;
- work permit expired, or marked "Missing (Abscond)" before 31 December 2025;
- not blacklisted for criminal or security reasons;
- fingerprints given under Operation Kurangi;
- not legalised in this programme before (one chance only).

Only a new employer, never the last one, could apply, through Xpat <sup>[91](#o91), [N10](#n10)</sup>. The employer clicked "+ New Regularized Work Permit", fetched the worker by first name, passport country and number, chose the quota slot, entered occupation and salary in USD, uploaded documents and paid the deposit; an unpaid deposit cancelled the application <sup>[91](#o91)</sup>. Workers without all ten fingerprints first went through a regularisation request and got an RGZ number <sup>[91](#o91)</sup>. Workers marked "Missing Negligence" were not legalised but could change employer instead <sup>[91](#o91)</sup>. The Bangladesh High Commission told workers only those who had given Kurangi fingerprints could apply <sup>[N123](#n123)</sup>. Under Operation Kurangi the fully documented share of foreign workers rose from 28 to 62 percent <sup>[N121](#n121)</sup>.

<a id="routes-still-open"></a>

### 19.5 Routes still open

1. <a id="routes-still-open-item-1"></a>**Employer withdraws a missing report** and re-issues your permit (see [If your employer reports you absconded](#if-your-employer-reports-you-absconded)) <sup>[95](#o95), [N88](#n88)</sup>.
2. <a id="routes-still-open-item-2"></a>**Employer negligence found by the LRA:** a "Missing Negligence" worker can move to a new employer <sup>[14](#o14), [91](#o91), [N88](#n88)</sup>.
3. <a id="routes-still-open-item-3"></a>**Permit fees unpaid for over a month:** you can change employer with a no-objection letter from the current one <sup>[N88](#n88)</sup>.

> **Need to know**

> - What do brokers in Malé charge to "fix papers" for an undocumented worker in 2026, and what do they actually do?

<a id="scams-4"></a>

### 19.6 Scams

Legalisation was done only by the new employer through Xpat, and a detained worker loses the chance to legalise <sup>[91](#o91), [N89 (2024)](#n89)</sup>. An "open visa" is in fact a tourist visa, and working for anyone but the employer on your permit is illegal <sup>[N92 (2024)](#n92), [N44 (2024)](#n44)</sup>.

<a id="the-crackdown"></a>

### 19.7 The crackdown

- **Who is caught.** Raids target businesses, food courts and markets; Bangladeshis are mainly detained for running businesses on a work visa <sup>[N82 (2024)](#n82), [N50](#n50), [N49](#n49)</sup>. Locals can report workers anonymously on the "Immigration Watch" website <sup>[N48](#n48)</sup>. In April 2026 police handed 38 Bangladeshi men to Immigration for deportation over drugs and gambling <sup>[N85](#n85)</sup>.
- **Phase three, 9 May 2026.** More than 200 Immigration and 100 police officers swept 26 zones of Malé, detained 254 people and gave notices to 44 Maldivian employers <sup>[N124](#n124), [N125](#n125)</sup>. Those detained are deported at the earliest opportunity <sup>[N124](#n124)</sup>.
- **Since then.** Raids detained 27 of 88 workers checked at the Malé West Harbour food courts, 12 of 20 in Hulhumalé in June, 20 of 55 at Hulhumalé's Rashu Market and 13 of 426 in Greater Malé in September 2026 <sup>[N50](#n50), [N126](#n126), [N49](#n49), [N127](#n127)</sup>.
- **Numbers.** 12,552 people were deported between 17 November 2023 and 13 June 2026 <sup>[N49](#n49)</sup>. The government aims to finish removing violators by 17 November 2026 <sup>[N50](#n50)</sup>.

<a id="going-home-without-valid-papers"></a>

## 20 Going home without valid papers

Staying in the Maldives without a valid permit is prohibited, and a foreigner must leave when the permit expires or is cancelled <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>. The Immigration Act lets the authorities fine a foreigner MVR 15,000 to 50,000 for breaking it, deport him and ban him from returning for a set period <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>. In 2026 people found without papers are detained and deported (see [The crackdown](#the-crackdown)) <sup>[N8](#n8), [N124](#n124)</sup>.

### 20.2 Step 2: Leave

<a id="if-you-are-detained"></a>

### 20.3 If you are detained

People awaiting deportation are held in the immigration detention centre in Hulhumalé, which takes about 200 <sup>[N130 (2019)](#n130), [N131](#n131)</sup>. They are sent home as their documents and tickets are ready, paid from the deposit the employer lodged with the state <sup>[N131](#n131), [N92 (2024)](#n92)</sup>. A worker with no record in the system must get identity papers from his embassy before he is deported <sup>[N44 (2024)](#n44)</sup>. Once detained, a worker can no longer legalise <sup>[N89 (2024)](#n89)</sup>. The UN anti-racism committee reported in 2025 that detainees are often not brought before a judge, lack lawyers and interpreters, and that release sometimes depends on deals between employers and Immigration <sup>[N132](#n132)</sup>.

**What family or a lawyer can do:**
1. <a id="if-you-are-detained-item-1"></a>**Ask to visit:** fill in form IM11 (Detainee Visitation Request Form, immigration.gov.mv Downloads) with the detainee's name, nationality, date of birth and passport number, attach your ID (a spouse adds a marriage certificate copy), choose in-person or video call, and email it to ecdadmin@immigration.gov.mv <sup>[136](#o136)</sup>.
2. <a id="if-you-are-detained-item-2"></a>**Send a lawyer:** the lawyer fills in form IM10 (Legal Representation Request Form) with a copy of his licence <sup>[137](#o137), [136](#o136)</sup>.
3. <a id="if-you-are-detained-item-3"></a>**Call** Immigration's call centre, +960 1434 <sup>[134](#o134)</sup>.
4. <a id="if-you-are-detained-item-4"></a>**Tell the Bangladesh High Commission's labour wing:** hotline +960 7648006 (WhatsApp) <sup>[52](#o52)</sup>.

### 20.4 Bans after you leave

The Immigration Act lets Immigration ban a deported foreigner for a set period, and the length depends on the offence <sup>[10](#o10), [N92 (2024)](#n92)</sup>. In 2024 Immigration said deportees were sent "with the condition that they will not be allowed to return" <sup>[N133 (2024)](#n133)</sup>. In 2013 the ban for deported undocumented workers was 10 years, against six months for those who went home at their own expense <sup>[N134 (2013)](#n134)</sup>. A blacklisted worker can't be legalised or have a missing report withdrawn <sup>[91](#o91), [95](#o95)</sup>.

<a id="if-a-worker-dies-is-injured-or-loses-the-job"></a>

## 21 If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job

Under the Occupational Safety and Health Act (in force since July 2024), a worker injured at work, or the heirs of a worker killed at work, are owed by the employer: medical costs, wages lost during treatment, compensation for disability, and compensation for death <sup>[N135](#n135), [N47 (2024)](#n47)</sup>. The Bangladesh side (Wage Earners' Welfare Board grants, the body at the airport) is under Coming home <sup>[4](#o4), [138](#o138)</sup>.

<a id="if-you-are-injured-or-fall-ill-at-work"></a>

### 21.1 If you are injured or fall ill at work

1. <a id="if-you-are-injured-or-fall-ill-at-work-item-1"></a>**Get treatment.** The employer must provide or arrange medical care for a work injury, at no cost to you <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30), [N47 (2024)](#n47)</sup>. You have medical insurance from your work permit <sup>[N47 (2024)](#n47)</sup>.
2. <a id="if-you-are-injured-or-fall-ill-at-work-item-2"></a>**Make sure it is reported.** The Minister must be told within 48 hours of an injury needing more than first aid <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
3. <a id="if-you-are-injured-or-fall-ill-at-work-item-3"></a>**Sick leave and your job.** You have 30 paid sick days a year <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>, and you cannot be dismissed for being temporarily off through illness or injury <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
4. <a id="if-you-are-injured-or-fall-ill-at-work-item-4"></a>**Compensation.** LRA's Workplace Safety and Health Protection Regulation (October 2024) covers all medical costs, wages for the days off for treatment, and damages for temporary or permanent disability <sup>[N136 (2024)](#n136)</sup>. Permanent disability compensation runs for ten years, from MVR 1,260,000 to MVR 19 million depending on age and degree of disability <sup>[N136 (2024)](#n136)</sup>.
5. <a id="if-you-are-injured-or-fall-ill-at-work-item-5"></a>**If the employer won't pay,** complain to LRA, then the Employment Tribunal (see [If you aren't paid](#if-you-arent-paid)) <sup>[N47 (2024)](#n47), [30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Has any Bangladeshi worker received disability compensation under the 2024 regulation, and through whom was it claimed?

<a id="if-a-worker-dies"></a>

### 21.2 If a worker dies

**In the Maldives.**

1. <a id="if-a-worker-dies-item-1"></a>**The employer's duties.** The Minister must be notified within 48 hours <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. The 2021 regulation made the employer tell the family and the Bangladesh High Commission and pay for burial, or send the body home if the family wished, and the 2023 regulation also made it pay for burial; both have since been replaced by Regulation 2025/R-120 <sup>[96 (2021)](#o96), [N47 (2024)](#n47), [87](#o87)</sup>. The High Commission attests a demand letter only if the employer agrees to send a dead worker's body home at its own cost, and the work permit insurance must cover repatriation of the body or burial in the Maldives <sup>[43](#o43), [83 (2021)](#o83)</sup>.
2. <a id="if-a-worker-dies-item-2"></a>**The High Commission** works with Maldivian authorities to send bodies home <sup>[N137](#n137)</sup>. Eight Bangladeshis died in 10 days in July and August 2026, and five died in a fire on Dhigurah Island in March 2026; 45 Bangladeshis died in the first half of 2026, 10 of them undocumented <sup>[N137](#n137), [N138](#n138), [N139](#n139)</sup>.
3. <a id="if-a-worker-dies-item-3"></a>**Compensation for a death at work:** at least MVR 50,000 (about Tk 4 lakh) even when the employer was not negligent. Where the employer was negligent, from MVR 1.46 million to MVR 15.4 million depending on age, wage and degree of negligence <sup>[N136 (2024)](#n136), [1](#o1), [2](#o2)</sup>.
4. <a id="if-a-worker-dies-item-4"></a>**Wages and dues:** all money owed must be paid within 7 days of the job ending <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
5. <a id="if-a-worker-dies-item-5"></a>**Pension savings,** if the worker was a member: the heirs claim from the Maldives Pension Administration Office with a court judgment or order from Bangladesh naming the heirs <sup>[N76 (2023)](#n76)</sup>.

**In practice:** when Bangladeshi WAMCO worker Ameen Mia, 24, went missing on Thilafushi landfill in January 2026, no missing person report was filed for two days, and he was found dead under garbage <sup>[N135](#n135)</sup>.

<a id="if-you-lose-the-job"></a>

### 21.3 If you lose the job

- **No unemployment benefit** exists for migrant workers <sup>[N47 (2024)](#n47)</sup>.
- **Dismissal without notice** is allowed only where your conduct is unacceptable <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- **Notice or pay in lieu** (see [Leaving at the end of the job, item 1: Get notice in writing](#leaving-at-the-end-of-the-job-item-1)) <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- **Unfair dismissal:** complain to the Employment Tribunal within three months <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. The employer must prove the cause <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. The Tribunal can order reinstatement or compensation <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>. Workers dismissed during probation cannot use this route <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.
- **A new employer can take you over** through Change Employer on oneGov and Xpat (see [If you want to change employer](#if-you-want-to-change-employer)) <sup>[29](#o29)</sup>.
- **If the company closed or went bankrupt:** workers are paid first (see [If you aren't paid](#if-you-arent-paid)) <sup>[30 (2022)](#o30)</sup>.

<a id="coming-home"></a>

## 22 Coming home

- **Leaving the Maldives with cash.** Declare cash or cheques worth USD 10,000 or more in any currency to Maldives Customs, online at [customs.gov.mv/CashDeclaration/Index](https://www.customs.gov.mv/CashDeclaration/Index) <sup>[80 (2024)](#o80)</sup>. The form asks for your passport details, flight, address in the Maldives, purpose, each currency and amount, and a passport copy, and you declare it is true <sup>[139](#o139)</sup>. Above USD 10,000 you must also show where the money came from, or it can be confiscated <sup>[N140 (2024)](#n140)</sup>. Bags are searched: in one case Velana airport security found USD 81,030 hidden in a cornflakes box in a Bangladeshi passenger's bag for Dhaka, which he said someone at a guest house had given him <sup>[N141](#n141)</sup>.
- **Souvenirs you may not take out** without a permit: black coral and other coral, triton shells, pearl oysters, lobsters, turtles and turtle shells, conch shells, giant clams and shark fins <sup>[80 (2024)](#o80)</sup>.
- **Customs in Bangladesh.**
  - Duty-free: 65 kg of baggage, 2 used phones and 1 new phone a year, and once a year, if declared, 100 g of gold or 200 g of silver jewellery (no more than 12 pieces of one type) <sup>[140](#o140)</sup>.
  - Once a year you may bring a declared gold bar up to 117 g (10 tola), paying Tk 5,000 duty per tola; more is confiscated, and an undeclared bar means arrest <sup>[140](#o140), [141](#o141)</sup>.
  - No alcohol on a Bangladeshi passport <sup>[140](#o140)</sup>.
  - Declare foreign currency over USD 10,000 on the FMJ form <sup>[141](#o141)</sup>.
  - Customs emergency line at Dhaka airport: +88 01319-056591 <sup>[141](#o141)</sup>.

  > **Need to know**

- **Airport help.** The Welfare Board's desks at Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet airports give food and water to people returning from jail or on an outpass (travel permit), money to workers coming back from war, disaster or lay-off, help with customs and lost luggage, and take complaints of abuse or cheating <sup>[142](#o142)</sup>. Women returning within a year after abuse or fraud get Tk 5,000 cash <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>. A free ambulance takes injured, sick or dead workers from Dhaka and Chattogram airports; Dhaka desk +8802 8901040, 01404 413388; Chattogram 01913631797 <sup>[143](#o143)</sup>. Sylhet desk 01558277904 <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>.
- **A place to stay.** The Wage Earners' Centre (Lonjonipara, Borua, Khilkhet, Dhaka-1229, near Shahjalal airport): Tk 100 admission, Tk 200 a night; book at wec.wewb.gov.bd or in person with proof of clearance or membership, a passport copy and the ticket; 01310 350555, 01754 715720 (24 hours) <sup>[144](#o144)</sup>. The Probashi Lounge at Shahjalal airport gives free wifi and phone calls and food at up to 30% off with proof of clearance or membership <sup>[145](#o145)</sup>.
- **If your family needs legal help in Bangladesh, such as protecting its property,** apply to the Welfare Board (directly, through the Deputy Commissioner's expatriate welfare branch or the DEMO) <sup>[146](#o146)</sup>.
- **Business loan:** PKB's rehabilitation loan, up to Tk 50 lakh (Tk 3 lakh without collateral), 9% over up to 10 years, at the branch for the project's area with a trade licence and return papers <sup>[147](#o147)</sup>; fees 0.5% processing (max Tk 15,000) and 0.5% documentation <sup>[147](#o147)</sup>.
- **BRAC** gives returnees airport help, counselling, money or materials to restart earning, and legal help <sup>[148](#o148)</sup>.
- **If a worker dies,** the family claims compensation from the employer through the Ministry (aswelfare@probashi.gov.bd), which writes to the High Commission and the Welfare Board within 7 working days <sup>[149](#o149)</sup>. The airport desk hands over the body and a Tk 35,000 cheque for transport and burial <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>. The employer must send the body home at its own cost under the High Commission's attestation conditions (see [If a worker dies](#if-a-worker-dies), is injured or loses the job) <sup>[43](#o43)</sup>.
