### L754 FAIL
- sentence: "The employer can skip notice only by paying the wages and benefits for the notice period [L0181]."
- cited: L0181
- evidence says: s.25: "Employment can be terminated without notice provided that the employee's salary and other benefits for the required notice period ... have been paid in lieu of notice." But s.23 also allows dismissal without notice "where their work ethic is deemed unacceptable" (L0015). "only" is wrong.
- fix: The employer can skip notice by paying the wages and benefits for the notice period, or without pay only where your conduct is unacceptable [L0181, L0015].

### L757 OUTDATED
- sentence: "If the government orders a worker sent home and the employer doesn't act, the government sends him home and acts against the employer, using the employer's deposit [L0129, L0135]."
- cited: L0129, L0135
- evidence says: both facts are from Regulation 2021/R-16 (trade-regulation-foreign-employment-2021.txt), which facts.csv notes is superseded (2023, then December 2025). The sentence states it in the present tense; the preceding sentence correctly dates the return-trip rule to 2021. A newer source, the 2024 PILC report (U0062), says "Such return is facilitated by the deposit fees accrued by the State from employers, which are kept explicitly for such purposes."
- fix: Under the 2021 regulation, if the government ordered a worker sent home and the employer did not act, the government sent him home and acted against the employer, using the employer's deposit [L0129, L0135]; deportations are still paid from employers' deposits held by the state [U0062].

### L797 UNSUPPORTED
- sentence: "**Check your permit and visa before you book.** They must stay valid while you are away (see Step 5: Check your permit and visa are real) [L0108]."
- cited: L0108
- evidence says: "if an expatriate remains out of Maldives for over four months while the work permit is valid" (the permit is voided). Nothing says the permit and visa must stay valid during leave. W0063 (Xpat medical requirement) actually provides for a permit that expires while the worker is abroad: "Submit this report via the Xpat system for a temporary 15-day approval."
- fix: **Check your permit's expiry date before you book.** If it expires while you are abroad, the employer can get a temporary 15-day approval with a medical done abroad, and you need a Maldives medical within 15 days of return [W0063].
