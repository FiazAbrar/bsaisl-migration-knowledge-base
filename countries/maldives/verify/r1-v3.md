### L493 UNSUPPORTED

- cited: W0002, W0008
- evidence says: W0008 sits under "Entry with a New Passport: Holders of Work, Business, or Corporate Resident Visas must ensure their visa passport number matches ..." and is about holders of an existing visa. It does not cover first arrivals on an entry pass. For a new arrival the rule is W0017: "Employees must enter the Maldives with the same passport submitted in the work permit application. - fix: Immigration lets you in on the entry pass, which must be for the passport you travel on [W0002, W0017].
