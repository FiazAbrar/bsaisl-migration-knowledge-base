Sponsorship\
Declaration
============

A Sponsorship Declaration is required when a Maldivian citizen or a foreign national holding a valid professional category Work Visa intends to sponsor the entry of a foreign national into the Maldives for an approved purpose. The Sponsorship Declaration must be submitted to and approved by Immigration before the arrival of the sponsored individual.

[](https://imuga.immigration.gov.mv/)

Apply on IMUGA

![Sponsorship Declaration Illustration](/svg/hero_images/sponsor.svg)

## Sponsorship Requirements and Process

Quick Navigation

### Purpose of Sponsorship

A Sponsorship Declaration may be submitted for purposes as follows:

- Visiting
- Dependent Visa
- Marriage Visa

### Eligibility

The sponsor must be either:

- A Maldivian citizen; or
- A foreign national holding a valid Professional category Work Visa.

### Required Documents

- Completed Application Form **(IM20)**.
- Passport copy of the sponsored individual.
- National Identity Card of the sponsor (for Maldivian sponsors).
- Work Permit Card or Employment Approval (for foreign sponsors).
- Proof of relationship, where applicable (e.g. marriage certificate or birth certificate).
- Company seal on the application form (where the sponsor is a company).
- Any additional supporting documents, If required.

### Application Process

1.  Log in to the IMUGA Portal.
2.  Navigate to **Visa** and select **Apply for a Visa**.
3.  Confirm that the sponsored individual is currently outside the Maldives.
4.  Select **Sponsorship** and choose the appropriate sponsorship category.
5.  Complete the application form.
6.  Upload the required supporting documents.
7.  Review the application and submit.

### Processing Time

Applications are normally processed within five (5) working days. Applicants are advised to submit their applications well in advance of the intended travel date.

Important Information

- The Sponsorship Declaration option is available only if the sponsored individual is outside the Maldives at the time of application.
- Once approved, the Sponsorship Approval Slip may be accessed and downloaded through the IMUGA Portal.
- The sponsored individual must enter the Maldives within 30 days from the date of approval.
- Sponsors should ensure that the purpose of travel, intended duration of stay, and proposed travel dates provided in the application are accurate.
- Applicants are advised to purchase airline tickets only after the Sponsorship Declaration has been approved.

updated: 10/09/2026
