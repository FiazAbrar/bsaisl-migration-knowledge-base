Traveller Declaration\
FREE Self-Declaration Form
==========================

The Traveller Declaration is a self-declaration form provided by Maldives Immigration and is completely FREE of charge. Submit it yourself via the official IMUGA portal.

[](https://imuga.immigration.gov.mv/)

Fill Traveller Declaration (FREE)

![Traveller Declaration illustration](/images/traveller-declaration.png)

## Traveller Declaration Requirements and Process

Quick Navigation

### Overview

We would like to inform all travelers that the Traveller Declaration is a **self-declaration form** provided by Maldives Immigration and is **completely FREE of charge**.

### What is the Traveller Declaration?

- A simple **self-declaration** you can submit on your own—no agents or middlemen are required.
- Accessible directly at the official [IMUGA portal](https://imuga.immigration.gov.mv/).
- There are **no fees** for submitting the form.

### How to Submit (Free)

1.  Visit the official IMUGA portal: [imuga.immigration.gov.mv](https://imuga.immigration.gov.mv/).
2.  Open the Traveller Declaration and fill in your details.
3.  Submit the form electronically—no payment is required.

### Beware of Misleading Websites

- There have been reports of websites or individuals **charging a fee** to submit the Traveller Declaration.
- Always use the **official IMUGA portal** to avoid fees and misinformation.
- Do not share personal information or make payments on **unofficial** websites.

### Important Notes

- The Traveller Declaration is a **FREE** service from Maldives Immigration.
- Submit only via [imuga.immigration.gov.mv](https://imuga.immigration.gov.mv/).
- We wish you safe travels.

updated: 17/08/2026
