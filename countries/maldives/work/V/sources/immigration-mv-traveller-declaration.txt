Overview
We would like to inform all travelers that the Traveller Declaration is a 
self-declaration form
 provided by Maldives Immigration and is 
completely FREE of charge
.
What is the Traveller Declaration?
A simple 
self-declaration
 you can submit on your own—no agents or middlemen are required.
Accessible directly at the official 
IMUGA portal
.
There are 
no fees
 for submitting the form.
How to Submit (Free)
Visit the official IMUGA portal: 
imuga.immigration.gov.mv
.
Open the Traveller Declaration and fill in your details.
Submit the form electronically—no payment is required.
Beware of Misleading Websites
There have been reports of websites or individuals 
charging a fee
 to submit the Traveller Declaration.
Always use the 
official IMUGA portal
 to avoid fees and misinformation.
Do not share personal information or make payments on 
unofficial
 websites.
Important Notes
The Traveller Declaration is a 
FREE
 service from Maldives Immigration.
Submit only via 
imuga.immigration.gov.mv
.
We wish you safe travels.
