PASSPORT CONFISCATION
                       Employment agencies may charge a fee as agreed upon with
                       employers; however, no fee shall be collected directly or indirectly
                       from any prospective employees.3

                       Employers are prohibited from confiscating employees' passports or
                       any identification documents.4 Employers found liable for the
                       offense may face imprisonment for a term not exceeding five
                       years.5

                       There must be a written employment contract between the

                       essential details to be included in the contract.6

                         (1) contracts for a definite term;
                         (2) contracts for an indefinite term; and
                         (3) contracts specific to certain work.7

                        LEGAL ACCESS TO SOCIAL PROTECTION

                           Insurance coverage is not specified by law at present.

                           However, it is a legal requirement that employees applying for
                           work permits must secure medical insurance coverage from an
                           insurance provider approved by the Ministry of Economic
                           Development and Trade.27

                           Employers can deduct payments to an insurance fund from the
                           employee's salary.28

                           Employees are entitled to medical services for workplace
                           injuries, as well as regular medical tests and checkups for those
                           working in hazardous environments or with dangerous
                           chemicals.29

                           The employer must grant 30 days of paid sick leave annually. A
                           medical certificate specifying the illness and recommended
                           duration must be submitted upon returning to work.

                           However, 15 of these days may be taken without a certificate,
                           provided no single period exceeds 2 consecutive days.30

LODGING COMPLAINTS/          GRIEVANCES AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
RESOLVING DISPUTES
                                  Labor Relations Authority
                                  The Labour Relations Authority is mandated to ensure compliance
                                  with the Employment Act and its regulations by implementing
                                  necessary administrative measures and conducting periodic
                                  workplace inspections.40

                                  The Authority handles complaints related to non-compliance with
                                  employment laws, regulations and employment contracts.

                                  The Employment Tribunal
                                  The Employment Tribunal in the Maldives adjudicates disputes
                                  between employers and employees related to wrongful dismissal,
                                  unfair treatment, and violations of employment laws and
                                  contracts.41

                                  As an impartial body, it resolves conflicts and enforces legal rights
                                  under employment legislation. The decisions made by the Tribunal
                                  can be appealed to the appellate courts42 and are enforced through
                                  the Civil Court of the Maldives.43
