Maldives work permit and work visa: fees, medical and permit check (2026) · CareersMV

Checked on 7 Oct 2026.

Your employer applies and pays: MVR 2,000 a year for a quota slot and MVR 350 a month for your work permit. You supply documents and pass a medical. You should never pay anyone for the paperwork.

The rules are in Regulation 2025/R-120, in force since 14 December 2025, and in the Employment Act. The regulation is published in Dhivehi only. Older websites still quote the 2021 and 2023 regulations it replaced, so their fees and deadlines may be out of date.

## Who does what

Almost every step is your employer's job, done on the government's Xpat system. The office in charge is the Department of Foreign Labour at the Ministry of Homeland Security, Labour and Technology. Older pages call it the Ministry of Homeland Security and Technology.

For the application you provide these documents (R-120 Annex 4, section 11):

- a colour copy of your passport data page and a digital passport photo
- a police report from your home country, less than 6 months old
- proof of the vaccinations the Ministry of Health requires
- certificates accredited by the Maldives Qualifications Authority, if the job needs a qualification

Your passport must be valid for at least one year if you arrive on an entry pass (Annex 4, section 10).

When you land you hold a 30-day conditional work permit. Your employer has 30 days to complete the full permit, plus 15 more if needed (Articles 34 and 36). If no permit is issued within those 45 days, you may move to another employer without your employer's consent.

The regulation counts its deadlines without government holidays (Article 83), so they run longer than calendar days.

Within 45 days of arrival you and your employer must sign an employment contract, and you get a copy (Article 58). You must also get a written job description within 20 days of starting (Article 59).

## How much a work permit costs

A work permit costs your employer MVR 350 a month plus MVR 2,000 a year for the quota slot: MVR 6,200 a year.

Your work permit stays valid only for the months the fee has been paid (R-120 Article 38). It also lapses if a required document, such as your medical or insurance, expires.

## The medical test

You take two medicals: one before you travel and one in the Maldives after you arrive. The first is done in your home country if the Maldives pre-departure scheme runs there.

The medical must be done at a provider registered with the ministry, using the Ministry of Health's tests (R-120 Annex 4, section 13). If you are found unfit, no permit is issued and your employer must arrange your return home. For a condition that is not contagious and can be treated, the ministry may issue a conditional permit.

## The work permit card

Regulation 2025/R-120 does not mention a physical work permit card. The official Xpat MV app, published by the Ministry of Economic Development, holds a digital card instead.

- You add a card by entering the work permit number and passport number.
- The card's data comes from the Xpat portal, and its QR code checks the permit against the portal in real time.
- The app was last updated in 2023. If it stops working, use the verification page below.

Your work e-visa can be viewed and downloaded in the Xpat system once it is approved. Your employer holds the account, so ask them for a copy.

## How to check your work permit, step by step

You can check a permit on the official Xpat portal, but you need the work permit number as well as your name or passport number. There is no official check by passport number alone.

7. If you see "Employee not found. Please Enter correct details", check the number for typing errors and try your passport number instead of your name.

The only public page on the portal that takes a passport number alone is the Fingerprint Requirement check. It needs your passport number and country, and only tells you whether you must enrol fingerprints.

A permit may not be extended until your biometrics are updated (R-120 Annex 4, section 10).

## Changing employer

You can change employer only through a new work permit taken out by the new employer (R-120 Article 43). Normally your current employer must agree.

You do not need your current employer's no-objection letter if your permit fee has gone unpaid. Nor do you need it if the Labour Relations Authority, the Employment Tribunal or a court finds your employer negligent. The same goes if a missing report against you is proved false.

## What you should never pay for

You should never pay the government fees for your own permit. Article 77 of Regulation 2025/R-120 says the employer pays all permit fees, service fees and fines under it.

- The quota fee, the work permit fee or the security deposit.
- A recruitment fee. An employment agency may charge the employer, but "no fee shall be taken directly or indirectly from a prospective employee" (Employment Act, section 70).
- Money back out of your salary. An employer may not make you pay back salary or sign a receipt for more than you were paid (section 55).

If anyone asks you for these payments, report it through the Labour Relations Authority complaint form or on 332 8282.
