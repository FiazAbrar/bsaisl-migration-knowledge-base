## Expatriate Account

Expatriate Account is fully Shariah-Compliant and is based on the Islamic principle of Wadi'ah Yad al-Dhamanah (guaranteed safe custody). For expatriate customers our Current Account is the ideal choice.

## Key features

- Shari'ah concept: Wadi'ah Yad al-Dhamanah (guaranteed safe custody)
- Deposit or withdraw your money whenever you want and keep track of your spending with Internet and Mobile Banking.

| Description | MVR Accounts |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum Deposit | MVR 200 |
| Maintenance | MVR 75 per month |

Indian Rupee (INR), Chinese Yuan (CNY) and Euro (EUR) accounts can be opened online. Fees and charges are applicable as per the Bank's Schedule of Charges and Fees.

**Eligibility**

- **Expatriates with work Visa:** Work permit with at least 1 month validity remaining at the time of onboarding.
- **Other Expatriates:** Customer must hold a valid visa at the time of onboarding.
