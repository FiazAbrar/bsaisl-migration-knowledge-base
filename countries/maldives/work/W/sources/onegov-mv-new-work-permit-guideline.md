# Request New Work Permit

This service allows employers to apply for a new work permit, which is required for expatriates to work in the Maldives for a specified duration.

Last updated on

April 12, 2025

Table of Contents

# **Before You Apply**

- Ensure the business has a valid quota slot for the relevant occupation.

- The utilised quota pool must be linked to an active site.

- Employees must enter the Maldives with the same passport submitted in the work permit application. If a new passport is issued after the entry pass, the employer must cancel the existing entry pass and apply for a new work permit.


# **Request a New Work Permit**

## **Description**

This service can be used to request for a new work permit. A work permit is a government-issued permit that allows expatriates to work in the Maldives for a specified duration.

### **Eligibility**

- Domestic Work Permit: The applicant must be a Maldivian citizen.

- Commercial Work Permit: The applicant must be a registered business with an existing quota.

- The quota slot must have at least 145 days of validity.

- The prospective work permit holder must be 18 years or older.

- For professional and managerial occupations:

  - MQA level 7 certificate in the relevant field OR

  - MQA level 5 certificate with 5 years of relevant experience and an MQA qualification recognition report.
- Work permits for domestic household work are only issued to female employees.

- A new work permit holder may apply for an employer change without a time restriction, provided their permit has at least 8 days of validity.


### **How to Apply**

1. Select the **Request New Work Permit** service on oneGov platform.

2. Click "Proceed"and you will be redirected to the service on Xpat Portal.


#### **Required Documents**

- Passport bio-data page (minimum validity of 12 months).

- New passport page showing the old passport number (if applicable).

- Passport-size digital photo (per [Maldives Immigration standards](https://immigration.gov.mv/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/VISA-PHOTO-STANDARD_V2_2.pdf)).

- Letter of appointment. (including the fields specified on the [template](https://xpat.egov.mv/Home/ViewAnnouncement/82) on the Xpat home page)

- Maldives Immigration Visa Applicant Information Form ( [IM30 form](https://immigration.gov.mv/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/emp-bus-spc-airports.pdf)).

- Educational certificates (for professional occupations) with certified translations if not in English.

- Relevant registration certificates (if required by the occupation).

- Medical report (only for domestic workers, not older than 45 days).

- Police report (only for domestic workers, not older than 6 months).


### **After You Apply**

- You will receive a notification via email and SMS.

- Check the status of your application under 'My Applications'.

- If marked 'Incomplete', a notification with remarks will be sent.

- Once approved, the application will be updated to 'Pending Payment'.

- After payment, the Work Permit Entry Pass will be issued.

- Employees must enter the Maldives within 90 days of receiving the entry pass.

- Employers must personally receive their employees or arrange for someone to do so upon their arrival.


#### **Service Fee**

- Work permit deposit payment details are available on the Xpat homepage.


#### **Payment Notification**

- Once the application reaches 'Pending Payment' status, an email and SMS notification will be sent.

- Payment can be made via BandeyriPay.


#### **Processing Time**

- Completed applications are processed within 3 working days.

- Additional verification may extend processing time.


# **Change Employer**

#### **Description**

This service allows an existing work permit holder to switch employers.

### **Eligibility**

- The new employer must have a quota slot valid for at least 45 days.

- The utilised quota pool must have at least one active site.

- If a regularisation quota slot was used, the quota slot may not be visible for employer change applications. In such cases, employers should contact 1500 for assistance.


### **How to Apply**

1. Select the **Change Employer** service on oneGov platform.

2. Click "Proceed" and you will be redirected to the service on Xpat Portal.


#### **Required Documents**

- Passport bio-data page.

- Passport-size digital photo. (according to [Maldives Immigration standards](https://immigration.gov.mv/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/VISA-PHOTO-STANDARD_V2_2.pdf))

- Letter of appointment. (including the fields specified on the [template](https://xpat.egov.mv/Home/ViewAnnouncement/82) on the Xpat home page)

- Maldives Immigration Visa Applicant Information Form ( [IM30 form](https://immigration.gov.mv/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/emp-bus-spc-airports.pdf)).

- Educational certificates (for professional occupations).

- Medical report (not older than 45 days).

- No objection letter from the current employer (issued within the last 3 months) with employer details, employee details, and optional work permit number.


### **After You Apply**

- Notification will be sent via email and SMS.

- Check the application status under 'My Applications'.

- Once approved, the status changes to 'Pending Payment'.

- After payment, the new work permit will be issued.


#### **Service Fee**

- Work permit deposit details are available on the Xpat homepage.


#### **Payment Notification**

- Payment instructions will be sent via email and SMS.


#### **Processing Time**

- Completed applications are processed within 3 working days.


# **Deposit Payment**

#### **Description**

This service allows employers to pay the work permit deposit before acquiring a work permit.

### **Eligibility**

- Employers with a pending work permit payment can make the deposit.

- Government entities are exempt from work permit deposits.


### **How to Apply**

1. Select the **Deposit Payment** service on the oneGov platform.

2. Click "Proceed" and you will be redirected to the service on Xpat Portal.

3. Choose a payment method:

   - Online via BML Gateway.

   - Xpat Digital Wallet.

   - Partial payment from Xpat Wallet and remaining from BML Gateway.
4. Complete the payment process.


### **After You Apply**

- You will receive an email and SMS notification confirming the deposit payment.

- The work permit request status will update to 'Issued (Pending Arrival)' or 'Issued (Arrived)'.

- If a reserved amount remains in the Digital Wallet due to an unsuccessful payment attempt, the Ministry will resolve the issue automatically.

- If the Digital Wallet balance is insufficient, employers may opt for partial payment via BML Gateway.


#### **Processing Time**

- Payments are processed in real time.
