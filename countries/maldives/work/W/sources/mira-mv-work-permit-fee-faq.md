# Work Permit Fee

## What documents do I have to submit when making a Work Permit payment for the first time?

- Passport Biodata Page (One-year validity) color scanned copy
- Letter of appointment
- Medical Report
- Passport size digital photo of employee
- Notarized Agreement between a Maldivian or a company registered in Maldives and the Employee

## Will Work Permit fee be accepted from atoll councils?

No, only online payment is currently accepted.

## What is the minimum period for which Work Permit Fee can be paid?

Work Permit Fee can be paid up to a minimum of 1 month. Maximum up to expiry of passport or employee contract.

## What is the rate of Work Permit fee?

MVR 350 per month.

## Who is liable to pay Work Permit Fee?

The employer of the expatriate.

## How can I get the deposit kept at the Ministry of Economic Development refunded?

Please consult with Ministry of Economic Development.

## How to get a copy of last paid receipt?

Payment receipt can be generated online.

## Can we get Work Permit fee related information via phone?

Yes. Please contact 1415.
