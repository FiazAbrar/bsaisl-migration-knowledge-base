Hiring foreign workers in the Maldives: quota, work permit and fees (2026) · CareersMV

Checked on 7 Oct 2026.

Hiring a foreign worker in the Maldives costs MVR 2,000 a year for a quota and MVR 350 a month for the work permit. First you register the worksite and advertise the job to Maldivians for at least 7 days. The rules are in Regulation 2025/R-120, in force since 14 December 2025, and every fee is yours.

## Which rules apply

It has been amended three times:

- **17 December 2025** (2025/R-122): babysitter taken off the list of jobs being reserved for Maldivians.
- **1 April 2026** (2026/R-19): no quota fee for micro, small and medium businesses with 20 quotas or fewer.
- **25 August 2026** (2026/R-69): foreign employees must be paid into a bank account in their own name, from 30 days after publication.

## The steps, in order

1. 01 **Register on Xpat.** Since 1 September 2025 every user logs in with a verified eFaas account.
2. 02 **Register the worksite.** A foreigner may work only at a worksite the ministry has registered and approved (Articles 14 to 16). Worksites include business premises on any island, vessels and households (Article 15).
3. 03 **Advertise the job to Maldivians.** Post it on the National Job Center for at least 7 days. You can use a quota only if no suitable Maldivian applied (Article 56 and Schedule 2, Article 18).
4. 04 **Get the quota.** Apply through Xpat and pay the quota fee before you use it (Articles 25 to 28).
5. 05 **Apply for the work permit.** Upload the documents listed below and pay the security deposit. The ministry then issues a Work Permit Entry Pass (Article 34 and Schedule 4).
6. 06 **Receive the worker at the port.** You must meet the worker on arrival and arrange their accommodation (Article 60).
7. 07 **Complete the work permit within 30 days.** Medical, insurance, accommodation, verification of the worker and the first month's fee (Article 36). A further 15 days can be given, with penalties.
8. 08 **Get the work visa.** Immigration asks the employer to apply within 15 days of the worker's arrival on the entry pass.

The ministry weighs your request against the Maldivians you employ, the Maldivians available for the job and your finances (Article 21). It checks your finances against your tax records.

## Fees and deposits

The regulation puts every fee for these permits on the employer (Article 77).

| Security deposit | Set by the ministry for each nationality, no more than the air fare home; refundable under Schedule 5 | Article 51; Schedule 5, Article 4 |

Quota and work permit fees are not refunded.

## Documents for the work permit

Schedule 4, Article 11 of the Regulation lists what to upload:

- a colour copy of the passport data page, and of any previous passport
- a digital passport photo
- a letter of appointment with the details in the ministry's template (Schedule 9)
- education certificates accredited by the Maldives Qualifications Authority, if the job needs them
- a licence or permit, if the job needs one from a government office
- a work permit medical
- a police report from the worker's home country, less than 6 months old
- details of the accommodation you have arranged
- proof of the vaccinations set by the Ministry of Health

The medical and the health insurance must come from providers registered with the ministry (Schedule 4, Articles 13 and 14).

## What you owe the worker

- **Accommodation:** for the whole employment, to the standard in Schedule 6 (Article 60). Professional-category staff may arrange their own.
- **A written contract:** within 45 days of arrival, signed by both sides, with a copy to the worker (Article 58).
- **A job description:** in writing, within 20 days of the worker starting (Article 59).
- **The advertised pay:** a quota worker gets the salary and benefits your National Job Center advert offered (Schedule 2, Article 18).

If the work permit fee goes unpaid, the worker gains the right to move to another employer without your consent. After 6 months unpaid, the ministry can cancel the permit and send the worker home (Schedule 4, Article 18).
