Update

### Important Notice: Restriction on Issuing Work Permits to Individuals on Business and Special Visas

Effective from **15 September 2026**, foreign nationals who enter the Maldives on a **Business Visa** or **Special Visa** will not be eligible to obtain a Work Permit while remaining in the Maldives.

Accordingly, Work Permit applications submitted through the Xpat System for individuals who entered the Maldives on a Business Visa or Special Visa will not be accepted.

Employers are advised to ensure that a **Work Permit Entry Pass** is obtained prior to the individual's entry into the Maldives for employment purposes.

Important Notice

### Important Notice: Salary Payments to Expatriate Employees

With reference to **Amendment No. 3 to the Regulation on Expatriate Employment (R-120/2025)**, salaries payable to expatriate employees working in the Maldives under their employment agreements must be deposited into a bank account opened in the respective employee's name at a bank registered with or authorized by the **Maldives Monetary Authority (MMA)**.

Accordingly, all employers are advised to ensure that bank accounts are opened for their expatriate employees at the earliest opportunity, and that salaries are deposited directly into the employees' personal bank accounts.

Furthermore, in investigations conducted by the **Labour Relations Authority** concerning wage disputes involving expatriate employees, salary payments will only be recognized as settled if the funds have been deposited into the employee's personal bank account at a bank registered with or authorized by the MMA.

[Announcement](https://gazette.gov.mv/iulaan/412237)

Important Notice

### Important Notice: Enforcement of Penalties Under Regulation 2025/R-120

The Ministry wishes to remind all employers, employees, employer representatives, employment agencies, accommodation service providers, work permit insurance service providers, and work permit medical service providers of their obligations under the Regulation on Expatriate Employment **(Regulation No. 2025/R-120)**.

The following penalties may be imposed in accordance with the **Regulation No. 2025/R-120, Chapter 7**:

**Article 5**

Where a violation is identified:

- **Article 5(a):** A fine of MVR 50,000 shall be imposed for a **first violation**.
- **Article 5(b):** A fine of MVR 75,000 shall be imposed for a **second violation**.
- **Article 5(c):** A fine of MVR 100,000 shall be imposed for a **third or subsequent violation**.

**Article 10**

- **Article 10(b):** Where an employer is determined to have been negligent towards an employee, a fine of **MVR 50,000 per affected employee** shall be imposed.

**Article 11**

- **Article 11(c):** For violations or circumstances not specifically prescribed elsewhere in the Regulation, the Ministry may impose a fine of **up to MVR 100,000**, proportionate to the nature and severity of the violation.

### Important Notice

**Important Notice on Employer Fines – Effective 15 September 2025**

In accordance with the Eighth Amendment to Law No. 2/2008 (Employment Act):

- As stipulated in **Article 65-7 (a)**, employers who abandon or neglect foreign employees after bringing them to the Maldives for employment will be subject to a fine of **MVR 50,000 (Fifty Thousand Rufiyaa).**
- As per **Article 65-8 (a)**, in cases not covered under Article 65-7 (b), a fine of up to **MVR 100,000 (One Hundred Thousand Rufiyaa)** will be imposed.

Effective **September 15, 2025**, these penalties will be enforced through the **Xpat System.**
Please note that **no further services will be provided through the Xpat System unless** **the imposed fines are fully paid and cleared.**

### Summary of Maldives Work Permit Medical Requirements Update (Effective 4th January 2024):

- **New Arrivals:** Submit medical report within 15 days of arrival.

- **Abroad with Expired Permit:** Temporary 15-day approval with foreign medical report; must update in Maldives within 15 days of return.

- **Departure Penalties:** Cancelling a permit with an expired medical report after 4th July 2024 incurs a MVR 2,000 fine per year of invalid report.

- **Work Permit Medical Validity Period Adjustment:** The period for work permit medical validity, necessitating renewal, is now set to 1 year.
