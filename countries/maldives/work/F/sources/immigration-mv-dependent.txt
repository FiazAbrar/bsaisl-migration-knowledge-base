Dependent 
Visa
A Dependent Visa is granted to immediate family members of foreign nationals working in the Maldives under a professional category of Work Visa.
It allows spouses, children (below 18 years old), or parents to stay in the Maldives legally as dependents of the main visa holder.

Dependent  Requirements and Process
Quick Navigation
Before Arrival (First-Time Entry)
Before your family travels, you need to submit a 
Sponsorship Declaration
 through the 
IMUGA portal
. 
Make sure to apply at least 
7 working days before arrival
.
Once they arrive on approved sponsorship, you can apply for the 
Dependent Visa
.
Documents Required
• Application Form (
IM20
)
• Passport-size photo (as per the standard)
• Passport copy (biodata page)
• Copy of your Work Permit or Work Visa
• Last arrival stamp 
(for new applicants)
• Last visa page 
(for renewals)
• Proof of relationship, such as birth or marriage certificate in English
• Medical report
• Expatriate health insurance &
• Any other documents, if needed
How to Apply
Visit 
imuga.immigration.gov.mv
Select 
"Visa"
Sign in to the IMUGA portal
Click 
"Apply for a Visa"
Complete the 
prerequisite
 field to confirm whether you are currently in the Maldives
Select visa type: 
"Dependent Visa"
 and choose the appropriate 
reason/subtype
Fill in the application details
Attach required documents
Review and submit the application
Once the application is approved, the eVisa will be viewed and downloaded through the IMUGA Portal.
Fees
Visa fee: 
MVR 750
Important Notes
When applying for Sponsorship Declaration, the 
stated purpose must be to bring in a dependent
 in order to qualify for a Dependent Visa.
