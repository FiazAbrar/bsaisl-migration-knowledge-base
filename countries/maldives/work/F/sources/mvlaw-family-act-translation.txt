                                  FAMILY ACT

                               A TRANSLATION

                                        Translator's Note
   This is a reasonably accurate translation of the Maldives Family Law originally
 found in the Maldivian Language, Dhivehi. Every effort has been made to maintain

      the style and format of the Dhivehi original. The reader may expect certain
 inconvenient defects in this Translation which may be attributable to corresponding

     defects in text, format and style of the Dhivehi original. However, where it is
     absolutely essential or justifiable for the purposes of this Translation, certain
departures from the Dhivehi text and style have been made without compromising the

                   substance or spirit of the relevant Dhivehi provision.
