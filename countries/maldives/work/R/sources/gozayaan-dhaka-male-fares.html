About Maldives International Airport

Male Velana International Airport (IATA code: MLE, ICAO: VRMM)

The main international airport in the Maldives is the Male Velana International Airport. It is situated on Hulhule Island in the North Male Atoll, near the capital, Male. Tourists enter the Maldives through this airport, which is the main gateway. Right now, the airport is only functional internationally. Many major airports are interconnected with Male International Airport. 

The departure gates are located on the left side of the arrival gate. There are four departure gates named by numbers starting from 1 to 4. You must enter here with your passport, undergo the security screening, and finish the baggage scanners. 

Again, there is the airline check-in counter. After you pass through the scanners, you won't be able to come out from the departure terminal. So, remember to double-check whether you have all your things with you before crossing it. After finishing the check-in at the airline counter, you must go to the electronic staircase or lift on the 1st floor. Here, you will see the boarding gate. 

Facilities and services you will get in the airports are an ATM, Free Wi-Fi, a Pharmacy, a Luggage store, a Lounge, a SIM card, an Information desk, a Food Court, a Tourist police, a Prayer room, a smoking area, a layover hotel.

About Maldives

Malé Atoll

It is located on the southern edge of Kaafu Atoll. North Malé Atoll is the largest city in the Maldives. It is also the most famous destination among many other beautiful places you will want to see in Maldives. You can do Scuba diving and snorkeling here. Also, there are places like the Grand Friday Mosque, Malé's National Museum, Male Fish Market, Dive Club Maldives, Underwater Scooters, and the Tsunami Monument.

Sun Island

Sun Island is one of the most famous tourist spots in the Maldives. Here are a few fantastic beaches, eye-soothing green nature, and beautiful tropical flowers. Again, the blue waters will give you a peaceful feeling. You will have things to do here, such as Diving, snorkeling, and windsurfing. Also, there are relaxing options at the sauna and spa. You will find beautiful resorts and spas if you are worried about the accommodation.

Banana Reef

You will find the location of the Banana Reef in the North Male- Atoll. If you love diving, it is the most suitable place for you. You can attach to activities here, like scuba diving, jet skiing, and snorkeling. The other beautiful places are Alimatha Island, Maldive Victory, Manta Point, and Biyadhoo Island. Also, some fantastic places to stay here are Paradise Island Resort & Spa, Coco Bodhu Hithi, Adaaran Prestige Ocean Villas, etc.

Alimatha Island

Alimatha Island is situated in Vaavu Atoll on the eastern corner of Maldives. The crystal-clear water will give you a surreal feeling. You can go Diving, boating, and snorkeling. So, you can pick this place for vacation or honeymoon. Also, you can go canoeing at Fotte Kandu and Miyaru Kandu. There are resorts like Alimatha Aquatic Resort, VOI Dhiggiri Resort, Fun Island Resort, Holiday Inn Resort Kandooma Maldives etc.

Frequently Asked Questions

How Long Does It Take To Fly From Bangladesh To Maldives?

The flight duration from Dhaka, Bangladesh, to Maldives is typically 4 hours and 35 minutes. Also, add another 30 minutes for it to take off and land. Due to some reasons, the time may vary.

How Can I Go from Bangladesh To the Maldives?

You will get six airlines from Bangladesh to the Maldives. The most well-known route from Bangladesh is Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport in Dhaka to Male International Airport in Male City.

Do I Need A Visa For Maldives From Bangladesh?

You will need a tourist visa to go to Maldives. If a foreigner travels to Maldive as a tourist, he will not need pre-approval for the visa. So, if you plan to go to the Maldives, please remember them.

Which Things You Can't Bring To The Maldives?

Tourists can't bring things to Maldives, such as alcoholic beverages, firearms, pornography, pork products, and idols of worship. Also, it is strictly prohibited to bring any illegal drugs into Maldives.

Does the Backpack Count As a Carry-On?

If you carry a backpack, it is considered a personal item until it fits under the seat in front of you. Also, you must confirm whether it is according to the airline's size requirement.
