# V findings: visits and short visas (Maldives)

Agent V, 8 November 2026. Facts V0001 to V0124 in `work/V/facts.csv`; 23 sources in `work/V/sources.csv` (16 official, 7 news, guide or third party). Nothing committed.

## a. Draft doc text

## Visas not open to Bangladeshis

- **A work visa on arrival, or a switch from a tourist visa to a work visa:** since a 2011 Cabinet decision a Bangladeshi worker must hold the work visa before arriving, and a new worker is let in only on an Entry Pass (work permit) issued by the Ministry of Homeland Security and Technology [V0075, V0074]; the Bangladesh High Commission in Malé says a person who comes as a tourist can never get a work visa (see Changing visa type) [V0079].
- **"Free visa":** no such visa category exists in the Maldives [V0082].
- **A visit sponsored by a relative who is a company worker:** only a Maldivian citizen or a foreigner on a professional-category work visa can sponsor a visitor (see Tourist visa, Visiting a relative) [V0054].
- **A multiple-entry tourist visa:** the Immigration Act allows multiple-entry permits for every visa type except the tourist and special visas [V0115].
- **Diplomatic visa:** only for people the Maldives government considers entitled to diplomatic immunity [V0034].
- **Entry on an Israeli passport:** refused since 15 April 2025 (not a Bangladeshi passport issue, listed for completeness) [V0012].

## Tourist visa

Bangladeshis, like all other nationalities except Israeli passport holders, get a tourist visa on arrival at the airport with no application in advance [V0001, V0012]. It is free and allows up to 30 days from entry [V0025]. It is only for visitors with no intention to work or earn money in the Maldives (see Can you work?) [V0114]. The Bangladesh High Commission in Malé asks Bangladeshis travelling as tourists to follow the visa-on-arrival conditions strictly [V0076].

**Status for Bangladeshis (8 November 2026):** visa on arrival open; no suspension or quota for Bangladeshi tourists is published [V0001, V0012]. Bangladeshis are nonetheless turned back at Velana International Airport for fake hotel bookings and for not knowing where they will stay (see Step 3: At the airports) [V0084, V0085, V0086].

### Step 1: Prepare

You'll need:

- a machine-readable passport (with the two coded lines at the bottom of the photo page) valid at least 1 month; a passport whose validity was extended by hand is refused [V0002, V0003]; airlines in Dhaka often ask for 6 months' validity at boarding [V0098];
- a confirmed return ticket [V0004];
- a prepaid, confirmed booking at a registered hotel, guesthouse or resort for the whole stay, or an Immigration-approved sponsorship instead (see Visiting a relative) [V0004, V0085];
- enough money for the stay: there is no published minimum, and the officer judges it against your days and hotel cost [V0004, V0096];
- for a child, the child's own passport and own declaration [V0008, V0007];
- a yellow fever vaccination certificate only if arriving from, or recently through, a yellow-fever-risk country (children under one exempt) [V0009];
- what your next destination requires, if you are flying on [V0010].

1. **Carry the printed confirmation of a prepaid, confirmed booking at a registered facility;** many Bangladeshis are sent back for carrying fake booking copies [V0004, V0085].

### Step 2: Fill in the Traveller Declaration on IMUGA

1. **Submit the Traveller Declaration within 96 hours (4 days) before your flight lands,** only at [imuga.immigration.gov.mv](https://imuga.immigration.gov.mv/), IMUGA being Maldives Immigration's official online portal; it is free and needs no agent [V0005, V0006, V0014, V0113]. A declaration is also required within 96 hours of departure [V0020].

   You'll need: your passport, an email address you can open on your phone, your flight details and the address where you will stay [V0016, V0018].

   1. Open the portal and choose Traveller Declaration [V0005].
   2. Type your email and press "Send verification code"; enter the 6-digit code sent to you [V0016].
   3. Fill in the six steps: Verify email, Purpose, Personal, Travel, Health, Customs [V0017, V0018].
   4. Add family or group members at the end; every traveller, child or adult, needs a separate declaration [V0019, V0007].
   5. Submit; the confirmation, with a QR code, comes by email; keep a screenshot and a print-out [V0016, V0097].

2. **Show the declaration with your passport at immigration on arrival** [V0005].

**Scams:** websites and people charge fees to submit the declaration; the real one is free and only on imuga.immigration.gov.mv (see Scams) [V0015, V0006].

### Step 3: At the airports

**At Dhaka airport:** airlines often ask for 6 months' passport validity at boarding [V0098].

> **Need to know**

**At Velana International Airport, Malé:** the officer checks your passport, declaration, return ticket, booking and money, and may ask why you came, where you will stay and when you will leave [V0005, V0004, V0099]. You must answer truthfully and completely [V0030]. A complete itinerary does not guarantee entry: the officer decides under sections 7 and 8 of the Immigration Act 2007 [V0011]. Anyone who cannot prove enough money for living and accommodation for the stay is refused [V0029]. Bangladeshis are regularly turned back, mostly for fake hotel bookings or for being unable to say where they will stay [V0084, V0085, V0086].

**If refused entry:** you are sent back on the airline you came on, and may be held at a place Immigration chooses until the flight; the carrier bears the cost of detention and removal [V0031].

### Extending your stay

The Controller of Immigration may let a tourist stay up to 90 days from entry, after paying the fee; it is a decision, not a right [V0026, V0038]. The fee in the Immigration Act is MVR 750 for each three months or part of three months [V0027].

1. **Apply on IMUGA before your 30 days end** [V0047, V0043].

   You'll need: your passport number (or a scan of the photo page if the passport was renewed or details don't match), a passport-size photo taken within the last 12 months, your current hotel details, and money for the fee [V0047, V0048, V0027]. Extensions are usually approved when the traveller shows enough money and a resort or hotel stay, or a letter from a local sponsor [V0090].

   1. Log in to IMUGA [V0047].
   2. Enter your passport number, or upload the photo page; the system fills in your details and hotel record [V0047, V0046].
   3. Update the hotel if you moved [V0048].
   4. Upload the photo and submit [V0047].

2. **Wait about 48 hours** for the decision [V0049].

### Visiting a relative (sponsored visit)

A relative or friend in the Maldives can sponsor your visit through a Sponsorship Declaration, which replaces the hotel booking [V0004, V0055]. Only a Maldivian citizen or a foreigner on a valid professional-category work visa can sponsor; a Bangladeshi construction, resort or shop worker cannot (see Visas not open to Bangladeshis) [V0054].

1. **Send the sponsor a copy of your passport, and proof of relationship if any** (marriage or birth certificate) [V0056].
2. **The sponsor applies on IMUGA while you are still outside the Maldives** [V0062, V0057].

   You'll need (the sponsor): form IM20, your passport copy, the sponsor's National Identity Card (Maldivian) or Work Permit Card or Employment Approval (foreigner), proof of relationship, and the company seal if a company sponsors [V0056].

   1. Log in to IMUGA, go to Visa, then Apply for a Visa [V0057].
   2. Confirm the visitor is outside the Maldives, choose Sponsorship and the category (Visiting) [V0057, V0055].
   3. Fill in the form, upload the documents and submit [V0057].

3. **Wait about 5 working days;** the sponsor downloads the Sponsorship Approval Slip from IMUGA [V0058, V0059].
4. **Buy your ticket only after approval, and enter within 30 days of it** [V0061, V0060]. Fill in the Traveller Declaration as in Step 2: Fill in the Traveller Declaration on IMUGA [V0005].

### Can you work?

No. A tourist visa is for people with no intention to work or earn [V0114]. Every foreigner must stay within the conditions of the visa, and an employer may not hire anyone without a work visa [V0042, V0041]. Breaking the Immigration Act costs a foreigner a fine of MVR 15,000 to 50,000, and Immigration may deport the person and bar re-entry for a set period [V0044, V0045]. Immigration has prosecuted foreigners who worked at resorts on the wrong visa, such as sixty musicians and photographers in 2021 [V0089]. The High Commission warns that people who try to work on a tourist visa face jail and a fine, and that anyone who enters on one visa and works elsewhere is arrested and sent back to Bangladesh [V0080, V0083].

**Overstaying:** a foreigner must leave when the visa ends [V0043]. Staying without a valid visa is prohibited and punished as above [V0040, V0044]; staying over 60 days without permission has brought fines of MVR 15,000 or deportation [V0091]. For what happens after arrest, see Going home without valid papers.

### Scams

#### Done to travellers

- **Paid "IMUGA" or "Maldives visa" sites:** the visa on arrival and the declaration are free; sites and people charging for them are scams [V0006, V0015, V0092].
- **Fake hotel bookings sold with "packages":** travellers carrying fake bookings are sent back from Velana [V0085].
- **A "job" or "free visa" on a tourist visa:** no free visa exists, a tourist can never switch to a work visa, and working on the wrong visa ends in arrest and removal [V0082, V0079, V0083].

#### Tried by travellers

Giving false information or false documents gets the visa revoked, with or without notice [V0039]; after revocation without notice you must leave within one day [V0117].

**If someone sold you a "job on a tourist visa", or you are stranded in the Maldives,** in this order:

1. **Bangladesh High Commission, Malé, labour welfare wing:** emergency hotline +960 7648006 (WhatsApp) [V0077]. (Address and consular contacts: see Contacts.)
2. **Report the broker to Maldives Immigration:** call centre 1434 (8.30 am to 2 pm, Sunday to Thursday), or anonymously through Immigration Watch on IMUGA [V0021, V0023].
3. **In Bangladesh,** complain about the broker or agency (see Work visa: company worker (construction, resort, shop), Scams).

Sample to the High Commission: "I, [name, passport no.], paid [broker, address, phone] Tk [amount] on [date] for a job in the Maldives. I was sent on a tourist visa on [date] and [what happened]. I am now at [place], phone [number]."

## Business visa

A business visa is for a visitor doing approved business, not for working as an employee [V0032]. It allows multiple entries and a total stay of up to 90 days in any 12 months [V0063]. A locally registered Maldivian business or organisation must invite you and apply for you [V0064].

### Step 1: The Maldivian company applies

You'll need (to send the company): a clear colour scan of your passport photo page, the passport valid at least 6 months, and a photo taken within the last 3 months that meets Immigration's photo standard [V0067, V0068].

1. **The company submits the application on the Business Portal of the Ministry of Economic Development and Trade,** [business.egov.mv](http://business.egov.mv) [V0119].
2. **A General Service Request (GSR) number is generated** and the status shows "Submitted" [V0065].
3. **The Ministry approves, then Immigration verifies the application** [V0066].
4. **The approved eVisa is downloaded from the Business Portal** [V0066].

Do not travel before approval [V0069]. Approval does not guarantee entry [V0070].

> **Need to know**

### Can you work?

No: a business visa covers business or work approved by the government, not employment in the Maldives [V0032]. Working as an employee breaks the visa (see Tourist visa, Can you work?) [V0042, V0044].

## Meeting visa

The Meeting Visa is a 14-day visa on arrival for attending a meeting, conference or convention [V0071]. The event must be for a business registered with the Registrar of Businesses, for government use, or approved by the relevant government authority [V0072]. You'll need what a tourist needs: a confirmed return ticket, a prepaid hotel booking, proof of money, a machine-readable passport valid 1 month, and the Traveller Declaration within 96 hours before the flight (see Tourist visa, Step 2: Fill in the Traveller Declaration on IMUGA) [V0120, V0005, V0002]. It cannot be extended, and you cannot switch to a business visa inside the Maldives [V0073, V0121].

## Student visa

International students apply directly to the Maldives National University (MNU), where teaching is in English [V0100, V0103].

### Step 1: Get admission

1. **Apply online to MNU** [V0100].

   You'll need: an English transcript, the passport front page signed and stamped by an authorising body, proof of English, attested copies of certificates, and a passport-size photo with a blue background [V0101].

2. **Check your status online about three weeks later** [V0102]. If admitted, MNU's Registrar issues an offer letter [V0106].

### Step 2: Sponsor Declaration before travel

1. **Register on IMUGA** ([imuga.immigration.gov.mv/register](https://imuga.immigration.gov.mv/register)), then under Visa choose New Application, "Sponsor Declaration (IM21)" [V0104].
2. **Fill in the form;** if over 18, fill the sponsor part yourself: your name as signee, "Student" as designation, date and signature, and upload it as a non-editable file [V0105].

   Attach: a white-background photo (45 x 35 mm, at least 200 dpi), form IM21, passport photo page, birth certificate, marriage certificate if married, and MNU's offer letter [V0106].

3. **Wait for approval on the portal; do not travel before it** [V0107].

### Step 3: Student visa after arrival

1. **Within 30 days of arrival, apply on IMUGA** under New Application, Student Visa (form IM23) [V0108].

   You'll need: the white-background photo, form IM23, health insurance from Allied Insurance (MVR 800 a year, form from studentsupport@mnu.edu.mv), a medical report from any hospital in Malé, the passport photo page, MNU's letter and the page with your last arrival stamp [V0109, V0122].

2. The Immigration Act sets the student visa fee at MVR 300 for each three months or part [V0028].

Insurance must cover the whole visa period [V0122]. Campus housing applications open in January/February and June/July, and need your visa papers [V0110]. MNU estimates food at about USD 120 a week and a phone at about USD 25 a month [V0123, V0124].

**Contacts:** MNU Student Administrative Services, Rahdhebai Higun, Malé, +960 3345155, studentservices@mnu.edu.mv; MNU call centre 1445 [V0111, V0112].

## Transit and medical treatment

**Transit:** a transit traveller is one flying on to another country through Malé without passing immigration [V0013]. Anyone who passes immigration, even for one night, enters on a tourist visa and needs everything in Tourist visa, Step 1: Prepare [V0001, V0004].

**Medical treatment:** there is no separate medical visa; a patient comes on a tourist visa (or a meeting, business or sponsored visit) [V0024, V0001]. Advanced care is only in Malé (ADK Hospital, Tree Top Hospital and the government's IGMH), public hospitals ask for payment or proof of insurance up front, and unpaid bills can bar future travel [V0093, V0094, V0095]. If treatment is still going on when the visa ends, a special visa can be granted to continue it, on form IM22 signed by a sponsor who takes full responsibility; it costs MVR 750 for each three months or part [V0035, V0052, V0037]. Immigration's permits section: permits@immigration.gov.mv, hotline 9199157, phone 3330487 [V0053].

> **Need to know**
> - Does Immigration accept a hospital as sponsor on form IM22 for a patient on a tourist visa?
> - Do Bangladeshi workers' relatives come to Malé to care for an injured worker, and on which visa?

## Changing visa type

- **Tourist to work:** not possible; a Bangladeshi worker must have the work visa before arrival and enter on a work-permit Entry Pass (see Work visa: company worker (construction, resort, shop), Step 3: The employer gets your work permit and entry pass) [V0075, V0074, V0079].
- **Meeting to business:** not possible inside the Maldives [V0121].
- **To a sponsored visit, dependent or marriage visa:** the Sponsorship Declaration can be filed only while the person is outside the Maldives [V0062, V0055].
- **Sponsor Declaration to student visa:** the one change built into the system; a student enters on the approved Sponsor Declaration and applies for the student visa within 30 days (see Student visa, Step 3: Student visa after arrival) [V0104, V0108].
- **Extending the same visa:** possible at Immigration's discretion (see Tourist visa, Extending your stay) [V0038].

> **Need to know**
> - Has Immigration ever allowed a tourist-to-work switch for Bangladeshis, for example during a regularisation drive?
