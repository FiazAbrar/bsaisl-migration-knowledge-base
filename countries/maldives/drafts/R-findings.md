### 2. Pre-Departure Health Assessment (PDHA) in Bangladesh

Context for Step 3, item 4 (W0085 etc. already describe the PDHA guideline). History, all news:

- Maldives Immigration started pre-departure medical and security screening (MIPSS, the Maldives Immigration Pre-Departure Screening System) in October 2017, first for Bangladeshi workers only, accepting medicals only from facilities accredited by the Health Protection Agency (HPA) [R0035].
- Pre-arrival screening was then halted; in December 2023 the HPA said it had been stopped for two years and asked for it back after 19 filariasis cases among migrant workers in Kulhudhuffushi [R0036].
- In December 2023 the Cabinet decided pre-arrival medicals would be done at places designated by the Ministry of Health, and cut the after-arrival medical deadline from 30 to 15 days [R0037].
- Since 4 January 2024 a work permit is issued or renewed only with a medical report from a registered facility, uploaded on Xpat; a worker arriving on a Work Permit Entry Pass has 15 days to submit it [R0038]. A worker who leaves without a medical report is fined MVR 2,000 for each year [R0039].

### 3. Bangladesh universal pension: Probash scheme

Suggested place: Your rights at work, #### Pension (after the Maldives pension paragraph) or Sending money home.

Bangladesh's National Pension Authority runs a Probash scheme for any Bangladeshi working or living abroad, paid in foreign currency; after coming home you can keep paying in taka or switch schemes, and the pension is paid in taka [R0010]. Monthly contributions are Tk 1,000, 2,000, 5,000, 7,500 or 10,000 [R0011]. People who join between 18 and 50 pay until age 60; those over 50 pay for 10 years [R0022]. The pension starts at 60 and is paid monthly into the bank account or mobile wallet given at registration [R0017]. The amount depends on investment returns, so the figures below are projections [R0024]: after 10 years of paying, Tk 1,530 a month (on Tk 1,000) up to Tk 15,302 (on Tk 10,000); after 20 years, Tk 4,927 up to Tk 49,268 [R0012, R0013].

1. **Register at www.upension.gov.bd** and choose Probash; you get a pension ID and password to check your deposits [R0021].
2. **Pay from abroad** in foreign currency by debit or credit card or another legal banking channel [R0015]. Contributions sent in foreign currency earn the government's remittance incentive on top, credited to the pension account [R0016]. Back in Bangladesh you can also pay at any Sonali or Agrani Bank branch counter [R0021].
3. **Know the limits.** You cannot take the money out before pension age; you can only borrow up to 50% of your deposits, with the Authority's approval, and repay in at most 24 instalments [R0018]. If you stop paying you still get a pension on what you paid in; if you die before 60 your nominee gets the whole amount back in one sum [R0019]. If you die while drawing the pension, your nominee gets the same monthly pension until the date you would have turned 75 [R0020].
4. **For family at home:** you can open a Surokkha scheme account for a family member aged 18 or over (spouse, parent, sibling) and pay in foreign currency, using their NID, bank account number and nominee details [R0014].

Help: National Pension Authority helpline 16131 or +88 09610 900800 (9am-5pm Bangladesh time), support@upension.gov.bd [R0023] (R0023's quote proves the email; the phone numbers are in Bangla digits in the same page footer: re-log with a Bangla quote if the lead wants the phone cited separately).

### 4. The Nirufehi fire (November 2022)

Suggested place: Background, What goes wrong most, or Your rights at work (housing).

Shortly after midnight on Thursday 10 November 2022, a fire in a car repair garage at M. Nirufehi, near the Maaveyo Mosque in Malé, spread into the migrant living quarters above it [R0025, R0033]. It killed 10 workers, eight Indians and two Bangladeshis: Asia Begum of Dhanbari, Tangail, and Uzzal of Panchbibi, Joypurhat [R0030, R0027]. A third Bangladeshi was treated for severe burns at IGMH (Indira Gandhi Memorial Hospital) [R0027]. The quarters had housed at least 38 people, with tiny rooms for around 25, a single window for ventilation and a gas cylinder beside each bed [R0026, R0029]. It was the deadliest fire in the Maldives [R0033]. Police had not stated a cause by 20 November 2022 [R0031]. President Solih ordered stepped-up inspections of foreign workers' housing and a plan to move garages and workshops out of residential areas [R0032]. In July 2024 the Homeland Security Minister said some of the dead were still unidentified and that a Malaysian company's proposal to build a dedicated foreign-worker accommodation block was being considered [R0034]. All bodies, including the two Bangladeshis, had been sent home by 19 November 2022 [R0030].

### 6. Appeal or review of a refusal or revocation

For Changing visa type, Going home without valid papers, or each visa's "If refused":

The Controller of Immigration and Emigration can revoke any visa, with or without notice, if the application had false information or documents, the holder falls in a barred category, the reason for the visa no longer exists, or the holder broke any Maldivian law or regulation [R0001, R0002, R0003]. If a visa is revoked without notice you must leave within one day; if revoked with notice, you may not stay from the date of revocation [R0004, R0005]. The Immigration Act gives no right to appeal or ask Immigration to review a refusal or a revocation [R0006]. A person in court proceedings to claim a right can be given a special visa to stay until the case ends [R0007]. Breaking the Act costs a foreigner a fine of MVR 15,000 to 50,000, and the Controller may deport and ban them for a set period [R0008].

### 8. MQA recognition

For Work visa: professional (adds to W0105-W0112):

MQA gives foreigners a qualification recognition report [R0040]. The employer downloads MQA's "Application Form for Qualification Recognition/Validation (for Foreigners)" from mqa.gov.mv; no online application is offered [R0041]. The fee is MVR 500, plus MVR 250 for each extra copy [R0042]. The form is handed in at MQA between 9am and 12 noon on working days [R0043]. MQA is now at Umar Zahir Office Building, 5th Floor, Orchid Maa Hingun, Hulhumalé (phone 3041831, 3041829) [R0044]. A course on MQA's recognised foreign list is still judged case by case on the holder's eligibility [R0045].
