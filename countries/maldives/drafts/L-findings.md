## a. Draft doc text

(These are H3 sections under "## Work visa: company worker (construction, resort, shop)", plus the H2 "If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job". The H4s inside "Your rights at work" follow the Malaysia pattern.)

### Your rights at work

The Employment Act (Law 2/2008) covers every worker in the state and private sector except the military and police [L0001]. The Labour Relations Authority (LRA) enforces it [L0093]. Foreign workers can take any breach of their contract to the Employment Tribunal [L0071]. Any clause in a contract or regulation that takes away a right in the Act is void [L0002]. Forced work is banned [L0003]. Since the 8th amendment (1 September 2024), a foreigner may work only with authorisation under the Act and its regulations [L0085]. The 9th amendment, in force since 14 March 2026, tightened the rules on ending a job and on quota fees [L0037, L0038].

Since 15 December 2025 the Ministry of Homeland Security and Technology's Regulation for Immigrant Workers has set out the rights and duties of foreign workers and their employers. It replaced the 2023 expatriate regulation [L0099, L0100]. Its appendices cover accommodation standards and what happens when a worker is reported missing ("absconding") [L0104].

#### Contract and probation

- Probation is at most 3 months [L0004]. During it either side can end the job without notice [L0005], but the rules on hours, leave and pay still apply to you [L0184].
- The employer must give you a written job description within one month of starting work [L0006]. If it doesn't, you can still enforce the terms and any verbal promises made to you [L0007].
- Under the 2023 regulation the contract had to be signed within 45 days of arrival [L0098].
- A recruitment agency may not take any fee from you, directly or indirectly [L0072].

> **Need to know**
> - Under the December 2025 regulation, how many days after arrival must the contract be signed, and must it be in a language the worker understands?

#### Pay, service charge and deductions

- **Minimum wage: none for foreigners.** The minimum wage, in force since 1 January 2022, covers every worker except expatriates [L0068]. The law leaves it to the Minister to announce when it will apply to foreigners, and no minimum has been set for migrant workers [L0069, L0070].
- **When and how.** You must be paid at least monthly, in cash or by cheque on a Maldivian bank, to you or someone you name [L0051, L0052].
- **Payslip.** With every payment the employer must give a statement of total pay, each deduction with its reason, and net pay [L0059]. If it doesn't, you can complain to the Employment Tribunal [L0060].
- **Banned.** The employer may not make you pay wages back, sign a receipt for more than you got, or buy from its shop [L0061, L0062, L0064].
- **Deductions allowed** [L0063, L0186]:
  - by law or court order;
  - with your written consent, for housing, goods, a loan or an advance, never more than one third of your wage;
  - for leave, medical costs, or insurance or provident-fund payments.
- **Absence.** For time off work the employer may deduct pay for that time only and impose no other penalty [L0009].
- **Service charge (resorts, hotels, guesthouses, tourist restaurants).** Tourism businesses must add at least 10% service charge to every bill [L0054]. It must be shared equally among all staff, and last month's share must be paid before the end of this month [L0055, L0056]. The employer may keep only 1% [L0057]. LRA can fine an employer up to MVR 100,000 for not collecting or not sharing it [L0067].
- **Ramadan allowance.** For Muslim foreign workers it is at the employer's discretion [L0053].
- **Fines on bad employers.** An employer that fails to pay wages, puts you in a job that isn't on your permit, or otherwise exploits you faces a MVR 50,000 fine [L0112]. For repeated wage breaches the Minister can tell government offices to stop serving the employer [L0066].

#### Hours, rest and overtime

- **48 hours a week** at most, not counting overtime [L0024].
- **A day off.** After 6 days in a row you must get 24 hours off in a row [L0025]. On resorts, tourist boats and industrial islands, the contract may let these days off build up and be taken together [L0026].
- **Overtime** only if your contract allows it [L0032]. It is paid at 1.25 times the hourly wage on normal days and 1.5 times on Fridays and public holidays [L0033]. On resorts, boats and industrial islands, 2 extra hours a day are allowed, paid as overtime [L0027].
- **Breaks:** at least 30 minutes for a meal after 5 hours in a row, and 15 minutes for each prayer time [L0030, L0031].
- **Records.** The employer must keep records of your normal hours, overtime and overtime pay [L0028].
- **Free to leave after work.** The employer may not keep you at the worksite, on the island or on the boat after working hours [L0029]. If you need a vehicle or boat to come and go, the employer or site owner must allow it [L0185].

> **Need to know**
> - Do private-sector employers have to shorten working hours in Ramadan, or does the government's Ramadan timetable apply only to state offices?

#### Leave and public holidays

- **Annual leave:** 30 paid days after one year [L0040]. The employer sets the start date after talking to you [L0041]. If you ask, leave pay must be paid by your last working day before the leave [L0042]. Any agreement to give up your leave is void, and unused leave must be paid before the job ends [L0044, L0043].
- **Sick leave:** at least 30 paid days a year [L0045]. You need a certificate from a licensed doctor, handed in on your first day back [L0046]. 15 of the 30 days can be taken without a certificate, at most 2 days in a row [L0047]. You cannot be dismissed for being temporarily off work through illness or injury [L0180].
- **Other paid leave:** 10 days a year for family responsibilities, such as caring for a sick relative [L0049]; 3 days for fathers when a child is born [L0050]; 60 days' maternity leave [L0048]. Since March 2026, parents also get time off for childcare until the child turns two [L0039].
- **Public holidays:** every Friday, the Prophet's birthday, the day the Maldives embraced Islam, Independence Day, National Day, the first day of Ramadan, Eid ul-Fitr, Victory Day, Republic Day, Hajj Day and Eid ul-Adha [L0035]. Since 2026, 1 May (Labour Day) too [L0036]. Working normal hours on a public holiday earns at least an extra half-day's wage on top of overtime [L0034].

#### Your passport

Employers may not take your passport or identity documents [L0087]. Under the Anti-Human Trafficking Act (12/2013), taking or holding someone's identity or travel document as part of trafficking carries 5 years in prison [L0088, L0089]. Even so, employers holding passports is one of the most common abuses reported [L0143, L0150].

**If your passport is taken,** in this order:

1. Ask for it back in writing (WhatsApp or a letter) and keep a copy: "I ask you to return my passport no. [number], which you have kept since [date]. The law does not allow an employer to keep a worker's passport. [Name, work permit no., date]" [L0087].
2. Complain to LRA on its online form under "Noncompliance of fundamental rights" (see If you aren't paid, item 2) [L0127].
3. Tell the Bangladesh High Commission's labour wing in Malé (see Contacts) [lead: cite B's labour-wing fact].

#### Housing, health and safety

- The employer must provide housing [L0136], and it must meet the standards set by regulation [L0075]. The 2021 regulation required board as well as lodging [L0132].
- You must have medical insurance from an approved insurer before your work permit is issued [L0090]. The employer may deduct insurance payments from your wage [L0186].
- The employer must provide safety measures and safety equipment free of charge [L0073]. You may refuse work you reasonably believe is a serious danger to your life or health [L0076].
- LRA employment officers can inspect workplaces without warning [L0078]. The employer must let you talk to them freely [L0080], and LRA may not tell the employer who complained [L0079].

> **Need to know**
> - What do the December 2025 accommodation standards require (space per person, toilets, cooking), and who inspects labour quarters?

#### Pension

Foreign workers do not have to join the Maldives Retirement Pension Scheme. You can join only if your employer agrees to pay the employer's share and handle the paperwork [L0173]. If you join, you can withdraw your savings once you decide to leave the Maldives. The money is paid in rufiyaa into an account at a Maldivian bank [L0174, L0175]. The law gives migrant workers no other old-age, disability or death benefit [L0137].

> **Need to know**
> - How many Bangladeshi workers are members of the pension scheme, and how long does a withdrawal take before departure?

#### Unions

You may form or join a union without prior approval (Industrial Relations Act 1/2024) [L0092]. Being a union member, or taking part in its lawful activities, is not a reasonable cause for dismissal [L0188]. Protests are a different matter (see What can get you jailed).

### What can get you jailed

**Drugs.** Since March 2026 the amended Drugs Act punishes even small amounts more harshly [L0161]. Trafficking a Schedule 1 drug (which includes cannabis and heroin) brings 20 years and a fine of MVR 75,000 to 7.5 million [L0156]. Bringing in more than 350 g of cannabis, 250 g of heroin or 100 g of another Schedule 1 drug at the airport can bring the death penalty [L0157]. Owners, tenants and occupants of a room used for drug-taking can be liable if they knew or had reason to know [L0158]. Never carry a package or bag for anyone else [L0160].

**Alcohol.** It is sold only on resort islands. Having it, drinking it or being drunk anywhere else can mean arrest and deportation [L0153].

**Religion and Ramadan.** Practising any religion other than Islam in public is illegal and can mean prison [L0151]. In Ramadan, outside resorts, do not eat, drink or smoke in public during the day [L0152].

**Sex.** Same-sex relations are illegal [L0155].

**Protests and politics.** A visa condition bans migrants from "political activities" [L0141]. In July 2024 the government said it would arrest and deport the Bangladeshis who organised a peaceful protest in G.Dh. Thinadhoo [L0140]. In December 2024 Immigration detained 55 workers, 17 of them Bangladeshi, who had protested near Muliaage in Malé over unpaid wages. Immigration said it would act under the Immigration Act once the investigation was finished [L0144, L0146].

**Papers.** You may work only with authorisation under the Employment Act [L0085]. You may not work at a worksite that is unregistered or cancelled [L0105], and you may not be moved to another business without the ministry being told [L0102]. More than 2,000 migrants were deported between November 2023 and July 2024 [L0142]. Overstaying, raids and detention are covered in "Going home without valid papers" and "If your employer reports you absconded".

> **Need to know**
> - What does the Drugs Act now give for a first offence of using or possessing a small amount of cannabis, and are foreigners deported after serving the sentence?
> - When a Bangladeshi is arrested, is the High Commission told, and how does the family find out where he is held?

### If you aren't paid

The employer must pay at least monthly [L0051]. When a job ends, everything owed must be paid within 7 days [L0065]. In a dispute over dismissal it is the employer who must prove the reason [L0018].

**Collect proof first.** Photos of your passport, work permit, letter of appointment or contract, job description, payslips or wage register, and a daily record of your hours [L0059, L0028]. The LRA form asks whether you have a written contract and job description [L0126].

Complain in this order:

1. **Write to the employer.** If the employer doesn't fix a breach of the Act or your contract within the time your notice gives, you can resign, and the law treats that as dismissal without reasonable cause [L0017]. Send it by WhatsApp or letter and keep a copy: "To [employer]: I have not been paid my wages for [months], [amount]. The Employment Act requires wages to be paid at least monthly. Please pay by [date]. [Name, passport no., work permit no., date]" [L0051, L0017].
2. **Complain to LRA.** It is free, and LRA may not tell the employer who complained [L0079].

   You'll need [L0125, L0126]:
   - your name, email, phone and address in the Maldives;
   - your passport number;
   - your job title and start date;
   - the employer's name, address and phone, and your supervisor's name and phone.

   1. Open lra.gov.mv/submit-a-complaint-form [L0125].
   2. Fill in "Personal Information" (choose "Bangladeshi" under Nationality), then click "Next" [L0125].
   3. Under "Employment Information" give your designation and start date, and tick whether you have a written contract and job description [L0126].
   4. Under "Employers Information" give the employer's details and say whether it is still in business [L0126].
   5. Under "Employment Disputes" choose "Non Payment of Wages", "Non Payment of Overtime/ allowances", "Breach of Contract", "Failure to grant leaves", "Working Environment Issue", "Noncompliance of fundamental rights" or "Others", and write the details: months unpaid, amounts, names [L0127].

   **In practice:** in a 2022-23 case of 49 workers unpaid for months in Hulhumalé, LRA wrote that it could not make progress because the company would not cooperate, and told them to go to the Tribunal [L0148].
3. **File a case at the Employment Tribunal.** You can file yourself, or someone can file for you, about any breach of your employment agreement [L0071]. On an island outside Malé, file through the island's court [L0081]. The Tribunal can hold hearings by audio or video if travel costs or distance would delay the case [L0084]. A dismissal complaint must be filed within three months of the dismissal [L0019]. Tribunal decisions are final, though either side can appeal to the High Court within 60 days [L0082]. If the employer ignores a decision, the Tribunal sends it to court for enforcement [L0083], through the Civil Court [L0094].
4. **Get free legal help.** The Public Interest Law Centre (PILC) filed the Tribunal case for the 49 Hulhumalé workers [L0147].
5. **Tell the Bangladesh High Commission's labour wing in Malé** (see Contacts) [lead: cite B's labour-wing fact].

**If the company goes bankrupt or closes:** the contract ends within a month [L0183]. Workers are paid before the government and other creditors, starting with wages, overtime and service charge for the last 27 weeks [L0022, L0023].

**Protests.** Workers who protested over unpaid wages in December 2024 were detained by Immigration. LRA looked into their claims at the Immigration Detention Centre [L0144, L0145].

### If you want to change employer

You have the right to change employer, but only through the ministry's procedure [L0095]. Working for another business without that procedure is illegal [L0102].

**When you can move:**
- **With a no objection letter** (NOC) from your current employer, issued within the last 3 months [L0118].
- **A new work permit holder** can apply straight away if the permit has at least 8 days left [L0123].
- **Under the December 2025 rules,** a change is possible once the work permit fee has gone unpaid for a month, together with a no objection letter [L0106]. A worker reported missing during a period the ministry announces can change employer if it is proven the employer neglected him [L0107].

**Why it matters:** if your employer doesn't pay its foreign worker fees, the ministry cancels the permits, and they come back only after full payment [L0114]. In October 2025 only 7,900 of the 68,000 employers registered on the Expat Portal were paying regularly, and more than 8,000 had been suspended [L0115].

**How the move happens:**

1. **Get the NOC.** It must give the old employer's details and your details, and may give your work permit number [L0118]. Ready wording for the old employer: "No Objection Letter. [Company name, registration no., address, phone] has no objection to [worker's name], [nationality], passport no. [ ], work permit no. [ ], changing employer to [new employer]. [Date, name and signature of authorised person, company stamp]" [L0118].
2. **Get a medical report no older than 45 days** [L0119].
3. **The new employer applies.** It uses "Change Employer" on oneGov (one.gov.mv), which redirects to the Xpat Portal [L0116]. The new employer needs a quota slot valid for at least 45 days, linked to an active worksite [L0117].

   You'll need [L0120, L0119, L0118]:
   - your passport bio-data page and a passport-size digital photo;
   - a letter of appointment from the new employer, following the template on the Xpat home page;
   - the Immigration IM30 form (visa applicant information);
   - the medical report and the NOC;
   - certificates, for professional jobs.
4. **Wait for approval.** It takes 3 working days once the application is complete [L0121]. After approval, the new employer pays the work permit deposit and the new permit is issued [L0122]. If the new employer's quota slot came from regularisation and doesn't show, it should call 1500 [L0124].
5. **Don't start at the new site** until the new permit is issued and the site is registered [L0105, L0102].

> **Need to know**

### Leaving at the end of the job

1. **Give or get notice in writing** [L0014]. For a contract with no fixed end date, the notice is [L0011, L0012, L0013]:
   - two weeks after more than six months' service;
   - one month after more than one year;
   - two months after more than five years.

   The employer can skip notice only by paying the wages and benefits for the notice period [L0181]. For redundancy the notice is one month under one year of service, two months for 1 to 4 years and three months over 4 years [L0182].
2. **Collect everything owed within 7 days** [L0065]. That includes unused annual leave [L0043] and your share of service charge [L0056]. If it isn't paid, see If you aren't paid.
3. **Ask for a service record** within 6 months of leaving [L0016]. It must give the employer, your dates of service, workplace, job title, salary and benefits, and no reason for leaving unless you ask [L0016]. Wording: "Please give me a written performance record under section 24 of the Employment Act. [Name, passport no., dates of employment]" [L0016].
4. **Ticket home.** The 2021 regulation made the employer arrange the return trip when the job or permit ends [L0128]. If the government orders a worker sent home and the employer doesn't act, the government sends him home and acts against the employer, using the employer's deposit [L0129, L0135].
5. **Pension savings,** if you joined: apply to withdraw them before you go [L0174].
6. **Permit cancellation.** When the employer records on Xpat that you have left the country, your work permit is cancelled [L0109]. No exit permit is needed [L0097].

> **Need to know**
> - Does the December 2025 regulation still make the employer pay the ticket home, and what can a worker do if the employer refuses?
> - How can a worker check on Xpat or IMUGA that his permit was cancelled properly, so he is not later listed as absconded?

### Going home on leave

1. **Agree the dates.** After a year you are owed 30 days' paid leave, and the employer sets the dates after talking to you [L0040, L0041].
2. **Ask for your leave pay before you fly.** If you ask, it must be paid by your last working day [L0042]. No one can make you give up your leave [L0044].
3. **Come back within four months.** A work permit is cancelled if you stay outside the Maldives more than four months while it is valid [L0108].
4. **Check your permit and visa before you book.** They must stay valid while you are away (see Step 5: Check your permit and visa are real) [L0108].

## If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job

Under the Occupational Safety and Health Act (in force since July 2024), a worker injured at work, or the heirs of a worker killed at work, are owed by the employer: medical costs, wages lost during treatment, compensation for disability, and compensation for death [L0169, L0091]. The Bangladesh side (Wage Earners' Welfare Board grants, the BMET insurance, the body at the airport) is in Coming home [lead: cite B's Welfare Board facts].

### If you are injured or fall ill at work

1. **Get treatment.** The employer must provide or arrange medical care for a work injury, at no cost to you [L0074, L0139]. You have medical insurance from your work permit [L0090].
2. **Make sure it is reported.** The employer must tell the Minister within 48 hours of an injury needing more than first aid [L0077].
3. **Sick leave and your job.** You have 30 paid sick days a year [L0045], and you cannot be dismissed for being temporarily off through illness or injury [L0180].
4. **Compensation.** LRA's Workplace Safety and Health Protection Regulation (October 2024) covers all medical costs, wages for the days off for treatment, and damages for temporary or permanent disability [L0162, L0163]. Permanent disability compensation runs for ten years, from MVR 1,260,000 to MVR 19 million depending on age and degree of disability [L0164].
5. **If the employer won't pay,** follow "If you aren't paid" (LRA, then the Tribunal).

> **Need to know**
> - Has any Bangladeshi worker received disability compensation under the 2024 regulation, and through whom was it claimed?
> - Who pays for an injured worker's flight home with an escort: the employer, the insurer or the High Commission?

### If a worker dies

**In the Maldives.**

1. **The employer's duties.** The employer must notify the Minister within 48 hours [L0077]. Under the 2021 regulation it must also tell the family and the Bangladesh High Commission and pay for burial, or send the body home if the family wishes, arranged with the family [L0130, L0131]. The 2023 regulation also made the employer pay burial costs [L0138].
2. **The High Commission** works with Maldivian authorities to send bodies home [L0171]. Eight Bangladeshis died in 10 days in July and August 2026, and five died in a fire on Dhigurah Island in March 2026 [L0170, L0172].
3. **Compensation for a death at work:** at least MVR 50,000 even when the employer was not negligent. Where the employer was negligent, from MVR 1.46 million to MVR 15.4 million depending on age, wage and degree of negligence [L0165, L0166].
4. **Wages and dues:** all money owed must be paid within 7 days of the job ending [L0065].
5. **Pension savings,** if the worker was a member: the heirs claim from the Maldives Pension Administration Office with a court judgment or order from Bangladesh naming the heirs [L0176].

**In practice:** when Bangladeshi WAMCO worker Ameen Mia, 24, went missing on Thilafushi landfill in January 2026, no missing person report was filed for two days, and he was found dead under garbage [L0167, L0168].

> **Need to know**
> - What papers does the High Commission in Malé need to issue the NOC for a body, and how long does repatriation take?
> - Who pays for sending the body home in practice (employer, work permit insurance, Welfare Board), and what happens when the worker was undocumented?
> - Has any Bangladeshi family received the MVR 50,000 or higher compensation, and how was it claimed from Bangladesh?

### If you lose the job

- **No unemployment benefit** exists for migrant workers [L0187].
- **Notice or pay in lieu** (see Leaving at the end of the job, item 1) [L0181, L0182].
- **Unfair dismissal:** complain to the Employment Tribunal within three months [L0019]. The employer must prove the cause [L0018]. The Tribunal can order reinstatement or compensation [L0021]. Workers dismissed during probation cannot use this route [L0020].
- **A new employer can take you over** through Change Employer on oneGov and Xpat (see If you want to change employer) [L0116].
- **If the company closed or went bankrupt:** workers are paid first (see If you aren't paid) [L0022].

- The Employment Act English text (LRA) is consolidated only to the 7th amendment (2022). The 8th (2024) and 9th (2026) amendments are known only from presidency press releases. Every Act fact is therefore "possibly-outdated".
- The overtime fractions in s.37(b) are garbled in the LRA PDF text layer ("1 � times"), so L0033 uses the ILO reading (1.25 and 1.5).
- The expatriate regulation changed twice: 2021/R-16 (English, trade.gov.mv), then 2023/R-111 (Dhivehi only), then the December 2025 Regulation for Immigrant Workers (Dhivehi only; known from Edition and Corporate Maldives). The 2023/R-111 PDF was fetched, but its Thaana text layer is unreadable. Facts on the return ticket, body repatriation and board come from the 2021 English text (L0128-L0135) and are marked as such. The ILO brief cites 2023/R-111 for housing (s.57), burial (s.63) and absconding (s.60). A Dhivehi reader should confirm these against the December 2025 text.
- **Conflict:** the Edition (L0106) says "Employer changes can be brought after one month passes without paying work permit fee, and a no objection letter". It is unclear whether this means both conditions or two separate routes. oneGov (April 2025, L0118) requires an NOC in every case. The draft keeps the NOC and adds a Need to know.
- **Superseded:** the 2021 rule that a worker who cancels his own permit can get a new one only after 6 months with an NOC from the old employer is out of date (oneGov L0123 says no waiting time). It is not used in the doc.
- **Minimum wage:** the 7th amendment (2022) removed the fixed date for foreigners; the Order excludes them (L0068). A search found no 2025-2026 order for foreigners.
- **Passport:** a see.mv report (403, not saved) says an Employment Tribunal refused an employer's request to hold a Sri Lankan worker's passport because only a court can order that. Worth saving for the passport section.

Unpaid employer fees lead to cancelled permits, which pushes workers into irregular status (L0114, L0115). Agent U should link this to "If your employer reports you absconded".
