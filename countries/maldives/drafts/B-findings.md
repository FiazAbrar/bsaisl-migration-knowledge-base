### For Background, "Size" (hand to N; official numbers)

BMET (Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training) cleared 40,159 workers for the Maldives in 2025 and 32,408 from 1 January to 7 November 2026, against 7,020 in 2024 [B0145, B0146, B0144]. In 2026 the Maldives is Bangladesh's fourth-largest destination, after Saudi Arabia, Singapore and Qatar [B0148]. Only 148 of the 2026 workers were women [B0147]. Clearances jumped from 878 in April 2025 to 5,411 in May 2025 and have run at 1,000 to 5,000 a month since; September 2026 was the highest month, at 5,015 [B0149, B0150].

> **Need to know**
> - What caused the jump in Maldives clearances from 878 in April 2025 to 5,411 in May 2025: new demand letters, or workers already in the Maldives being cleared after regularisation?
> - Are workers who get a Welfare Board membership card at the High Commission in Malé counted in BMET's clearance numbers?

### Work visa: company worker, Step 1: Find a real job

1. **Register on the Overseas Employment Platform (OEP),** BMET's website for job-seekers [B0002].

   You'll need [B0001, B0002]:

   - your NID (national ID card);
   - a mobile number;
   - Tk 200 for the government fee.

   1. Open [employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration](https://employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration) [B0002].
   2. Type your name in English, mobile and NID numbers, date of birth, division and district exactly as on the NID, the captcha, tick the terms and press "যাচাই করুন" (Verify) [B0001].
   3. Print 2 copies of the registration slip and give your fingerprints at your DEMO (District Employment and Manpower Office, BMET's district office) [B0002]. DEMO addresses are listed on BMET's website [B0003].

   **If it fails:** call the OEP hotline, 09614445271 [B0004].

2. **Learn a trade if you need one.** BMET lists every TTC (Technical Training Centre) and IMT (Institute of Marine Technology) with contacts on its website [B0005]. In BMET's Maldives job orders, skilled trades (mason, electrician, carpenter, plumber, plasterer) pay USD 300 to 350 a month against USD 250 to 300 for labourers [B0151, B0153, B0154]. For skilled jobs BMET issues the smart card only once the skill is confirmed [B0165].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does BMET or the Maldives ask for a skill test or NSDA certificate before a Maldives work visa in a skilled trade?

3. **Government jobs (BOESL).** BOESL (Bangladesh Overseas Employment and Services Limited, the state recruiting agency) recruited 76 doctors, medical officers and specialists, for the Maldives Ministry of Health in 2024, on 2-year contracts; their route is under Work visa: professional [B0156].

4. **Through a recruiting agency: find and check the permission letter.** For each Maldives job order the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare issues a permission letter naming the agency, the employer, the jobs and pay, the conditions and the most the agency may take from you [B0157, B0158]. BMET's system copy of the letter carries the High Commission's demand attestation number as the "Block visa number", in the form BHC-MALE(L)D-01/2015/39 [B0152, B0153, B0154]. Examples:

   - Siam Recruiting Agency (RL1531), 25 March 2024: 100 workers for Rasheed Carpentry and Construction Pvt Ltd; labourers USD 250, electricians, masons, painters, bar benders and carpenters USD 325 a month; 2-year contract, 8 hours a day, 6 days a week; housing, food, medical care, resident permit and transport by the company; one-way airfare free; the agency may take at most Tk 1,15,000 [B0151, B0158, B0157].
   - ZM Tours and Travels (RL1287), May 2024: 100 construction labourers for Sanken Overseas Maldives (Pvt) Ltd at USD 250 a month; 8 hours a day, 48 a week; the same benefits; airfare both ways free; the agency may take at most Tk 95,000 [B0155, B0160, B0159].
   - The Ifthi Overseas (RL0894), 8 May 2024: FEDO Private Limited, 150 visas; construction labourers USD 300, masons and plumbers USD 350 [B0153].
   - North East Overseas (RL2199), 23 May 2024: 30 workers for Duplex Private Limited; labourers USD 300, carpenters, masons and bar benders USD 350 [B0154].

   You'll need: the agency's name and RL number (the recruiting licence number of every licensed Bangladeshi agency) [B0151].

   1. Open [bmet.gov.bd/pages/miscellaneous-infos](https://bmet.gov.bd/pages/miscellaneous-infos) and search "Maldives", "মালদ্বীপ" or the agency's RL number [B0151].
   2. Open the PDF; check the employer, your job, the pay and the maximum fee against what you were told [B0157, B0159].
   3. Check the "Block visa number" (BHC-MALE...) matches the attestation number on your demand letter [B0152].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Where does BMET publish the Maldives permission letters for the 40,159 workers cleared in 2025 and the 32,408 in 2026, given the latest on its list date from June 2024 [B0145, B0146, B0156]?
   > - Do individual (single) Maldives work visas get a permission letter at all?

5. **Check that the demand letter is attested by the High Commission in Malé.** A Maldives demand letter is attested by the Labour Welfare Wing of the Bangladesh High Commission in Malé [B0118]. The employer first registers at [employer.oep.gov.bd/employer/registration](https://employer.oep.gov.bd/employer/registration) [B0086]. It then brings the Labour Wing [B0118, B0119]:

   - a covering letter to the High Commissioner on company letterhead;
   - the demand letter;
   - its agreement with the Bangladeshi agency, and the power of attorney to it;
   - the agency's recruiting licence;
   - a specimen employment contract;
   - the company's registration certificate;
   - the quota approval from the Maldives authority;
   - photos of the workers' accommodation;
   - USD 125 per demand letter.

   The High Commission attests only if the employer accepts that it will not keep workers' passports, will send home a worker's body at its own cost, will renew the work visa card in time, and pays a basic salary of at least USD 250 a month with food and housing on top [B0120, B0121, B0122, B0124]. The demand letter must state the agent's address, job, number of posts, basic salary, contract length, probation, hours, housing, food, transport, air tickets both ways, overtime and medical and other benefits [B0123]. In the employer's covering letter it takes responsibility for workers who have an accident, are injured or die [B0128].

   **Check it is real:** a demand letter offering less than USD 250 basic, or charging you for food or housing, is below the High Commission's minimum [B0124]. BMET checks demand letters and job visas free within 3 working days at your DEMO or at BMET, 89/2 Kakrail, Dhaka; bring the visa and, if stamped, a copy of the passport page [B0006, B0007, B0008]. BMET's online visa check does not cover the Maldives [B0006]; check the work permit itself on Xpat Online (Step 5) [B0087]. The Labour Wing answers attestation questions on +960 3320859, +960 3027960, +960 3027961 and +960 3027962 [B0125].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Can a worker in Bangladesh ask the Labour Wing in Malé to confirm a demand letter's attestation number, and how (email, WhatsApp)?

6. **Through a relative or friend in the Maldives (individual visa).** His employer applies for your work permit and entry pass in the Maldives (Work visa: company worker, Step 3) [B0137]. The High Commission then attests the individual entry pass (Employment Approval) [B0137]. The employer fills in an online form, then brings the Labour Wing [B0141, B0137, B0138]:

   - a covering letter to the High Commissioner;
   - the Employment Approval or entry pass copy;
   - an appointment letter and a contract signed by the employer;
   - the employer's passport or ID card copy and the company's registration;
   - the quota approval;
   - your passport copy;
   - photos of the worksite and accommodation;
   - USD 20 cash per entry pass.

   The same conditions apply, plus a contract of at least 2 years for unskilled workers, signed when you arrive, with a copy for you [B0139, B0140]. The appointment letter must show your job title, monthly basic pay, the Employment Approval number and dates, the work site, contract length, weekly hours, food and housing [B0142]. With your own visa, you apply for BMET clearance yourself (Step 6, item 3) [B0009].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does the High Commission still attest individual entry passes, and does BMET refuse clearance on an unattested Maldives entry pass?
   > - What does a relative's employer charge, in practice, for an individual Maldives work visa ("own visa")?

**Before paying:**

- **Know the cap.** Each Maldives permission letter sets its own maximum; the 2024 letters set Tk 95,000 with airfare both ways paid by the employer, and Tk 1,15,000 with only one-way airfare paid [B0159, B0160, B0157, B0158].
- **Pay late, by bank.** The agency may take its money only once your visa and flight are confirmed, by cheque, bank draft or pay order [B0157, B0159]. Pay into the agency's bank account and keep receipts [B0011].
- **Get the contract and its Bangla translation before clearance.** The agency must sign the contract with you before BMET clearance and give you a Bangla translation attested by BMET [B0163].
- **Never go on a tourist visa to work.** The Ministry warns that brokers send people abroad on tourist or visit visas with false job promises (see Tourist visa) [B0057].
- **Check the company.** The High Commission asks workers to check the visa and the company properly before coming on a work visa [B0079].
- **Borrow from PKB** (Probashi Kallyan Bank, the government bank for migrants) once you have a visa: up to Tk 3 lakh at 9% simple interest, repaid monthly over up to 3 years, decided within 7 working days [B0012].

  You'll need [B0013, B0014]:
  - the free bank form, 3 photos and an NID copy;
  - passport, visa and BMET card copies;
  - 3 signed MICR cheque leaves from your own account, with a statement;
  - a PKB savings account opened with at least Tk 500;
  - two guarantors: one financially solvent person (cheques, statement, proof of income) and one family member.

  You and the guarantors must live in the branch's area and you must be 18 to 60 [B0015]. Fees: 0.5% processing and 0.5% documentation, plus VAT, and Tk 900 per lakh risk cover [B0012]. Women can take PKB's women's migration loan, which needs one guarantor [B0016]. Apply at the branch or on mygov.bd, which sends application and tracking numbers by SMS [B0017]. Helpline 16238, or +880 9677-787878 from abroad [B0018].

**If an agency or broker takes your money,** in this order:

1. Call the Probashi call centre, 16135 (toll free, 24 hours; +8809610102030 from abroad), run by the Wage Earners' Welfare Board [B0060].
2. Complain in writing, with evidence, to BMET's Director General: free, decided within 30 working days; Director (Employment) 01711111544, diremi@bmet.gov.bd [B0072].
3. Free legal advice: 16699 [B0021]. Taking money on a false job promise, or holding a migrant's passport, is a crime punishable by up to 5 years' jail and a fine of at least Tk 1 lakh [B0019].

If you are already in the Maldives, call the Labour Welfare Wing's hotline, +960 7648006 (WhatsApp) [B0081].

### Work visa: company worker, Step 4: Passport, police clearance and certificates

1. **Get an e-passport.** You fill the form online and submit it at the divisional or regional passport office for your present address [B0022, B0023].

   You'll need [B0024, B0025, B0026]:
   - your NID (compulsory over 20); fill the form exactly as on it; nothing needs attesting;
   - technical certificates such as a driving licence, uploaded with the form and shown in original at the office.

   1. Fill in the form online at epassport.gov.bd [B0022].
   2. Pay through A-Challan, DGePay or ShurjoPay, or at a bank [B0027]. A 48-page passport costs Tk 4,025 / 6,325 / 8,625 for 5 years and Tk 5,750 / 8,050 / 10,350 for 10 years (regular / express / super express) [B0028].
   3. Go to the passport office with the original NID and certificates [B0023, B0026]. Delivery is within 15, 7 or 2 working days of biometric enrolment [B0029]. No police verification is needed with an online-verified NID [B0030].

   **If it fails:** passport helpline 16445 (09666716445 from abroad), 24 hours [B0031]. If the passport is lost, file a GD (General Diary, a police report) at the nearest police station and re-apply with a copy of the old passport and the GD [B0032].

2. **Get a police clearance if the employer asks for one.**

   You'll need [B0033, B0034]:
   - a passport valid at least 3 months;
   - a present address that is the permanent or emergency address in the passport, where you live;
   - a scan of the passport data page attested by a first-class gazetted officer;
   - Tk 1,500 by treasury challan, code 1-7301-0001-2681, at any Bangladesh Bank or Sonali Bank branch, or online by card with a service charge.

   1. Register at [pcc.police.gov.bd](https://pcc.police.gov.bd/), log in and click "Apply" [B0035].
   2. Fill in your personal details, then your present and permanent addresses; police verify you at the present address [B0036].
   3. Upload the scanned documents [B0037].
   4. Track it by SMS: "PCC S <reference>" to 26969. Collect it with your passport or application copy, or send someone with an attested authorisation letter [B0038].

   **If it fails:** helpdesk Sunday-Thursday 9 am-5 pm, 01320001824, 01320001825, help.pcc@police.gov.bd [B0039].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does Maldives Immigration or Xpat Online ask Bangladeshi workers for a police clearance for a work permit, and must it be attested?

3. **Certificates for a skilled or professional job.** On mygov.bd, register, choose the attestation service for the document, upload it and pay Tk 200 a page; MoFA (Ministry of Foreign Affairs) attests within 5 working days and sends an SMS [B0053, B0054]. SSC and HSC certificates go first to the education board and the Ministry of Education [B0056]. Experience certificates must first be attested by BMET or the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare and notarised; MoFA then attests them free [B0055]. The Maldives side (MQA recognition) is in Work visa: professional.

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does the Maldives accept Bangladesh's e-Apostille, or must certificates also be attested by the Maldives (which has no embassy in Dhaka)?

### Work visa: company worker, Step 6: Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight

1. **Do the pre-departure orientation (PDO), 3 days.** It is compulsory, at one of 70 BMET institutes; pay against a money receipt (Tk 200 at Feni TTC; pay no more) [B0040, B0041]. Download the certificate at training.oep.gov.bd/pdo-certificate: type your passport or NID number and the captcha [B0042].
2. **Check the contract** at least 2 days before you go: job, employer, workplace, length, pay, leave and airfare [B0043], against the permission letter (Step 1, item 4). The High Commission's model contract says [B0129, B0130, B0131, B0132, B0133, B0134, B0135, B0136]:
   - pay monthly, by the 7th of the next month, into a bank account in your name;
   - deductions for advances no more than 50% of a month's pay;
   - the employer renews your employment approval at least 2 months before it expires and pays any fine if it doesn't;
   - the employer pays your flight out and your flight home at the end;
   - if you quit early you give 2 months' notice or 2 months' pay and buy your own ticket;
   - the employer may not keep your passport;
   - you may not change employer during the contract without the employer's permission.
3. **BMET clearance (the smart card).** The High Commission says workers on a new Maldives work visa must take the BMET smart card before coming [B0078]. For a group visa the agency applies, with the visa copy, attested contract, your attested OEP registration, a Tk 300 stamp undertaking and the pay order for the fees; BMET clears within 3 working days of the visa [B0044]. Your costs:
   - welfare fee Tk 5,500 to the Wage Earners' Welfare Board from 1 October 2026 [B0045];
   - insurance Tk 1,000 once (5 years, Tk 10 lakh cover) [B0046];
   - source tax Tk 1,200 (skilled) or Tk 800 (unskilled) on a group visa, Tk 250 (attested) or Tk 400 (unattested) on a single visa [B0010];
   - clearance fee Tk 250 [B0010].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Is the Tk 250 clearance fee still charged, given the Ministry reportedly abolished the smart card fee in December 2024 [B0047]?

   **Individual visa:** apply at BMET's emigration branch in Dhaka or the Chattogram DEMO with your application, visa copy, contract if any, attested OEP registration, a Tk 300 stamp undertaking and the pay order for the welfare fee and insurance; 3 working days [B0009]. Women on a single visa need a guardian's no-objection letter [B0048].

   **Check it is real** at portal.wewb.gov.bd/bmet-wewbm-check: type your passport number (or membership ID) and the captcha [B0049]; the airport welfare desks check smart cards [B0050]. **If it fails:** BMET's Director (Emigration), +8802222222415, 01711-135901, diremi@bmet.gov.bd, or the OEP hotline 09614445271 [B0051, B0004].

   **If you are already in the Maldives without a smart card,** get a Welfare Board membership card (High Commission services, item 2) [B0080, B0088].
4. **The flight.** Under the 2024 permission letters the employer paid the airfare (one way or both ways, as the letter says), and an agency representative must fly with each group or meet it at the Maldives airport [B0158, B0160, B0161]. After each group arrives, the agency must report to the Ministry, BMET and the High Commission in Malé [B0162].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which airlines fly Dhaka or Chattogram to Malé in 2026, and what does a one-way ticket cost?
   > - Do agencies actually send a representative with Maldives groups, and do they report arrivals to the High Commission?

5. **Before you fly:** keep one set of copies at home and 3 with you, open 2 bank accounts, and never carry a packet you don't know [B0052]. Save the Labour Wing hotline, +960 7648006 (WhatsApp) [B0081].

### High Commission services (new section under Contacts or after Coming home; lead to decide placement)

The Bangladesh High Commission is at Plot 10982, Street 16, Nirolhu Magu, Hulhumalé [B0095]. It opens Sunday to Thursday, 9 am to 5 pm, and 10 am to 4 pm in Ramadan [B0098, B0097].

1. **Renew or replace your passport.** Passports are not printed in Malé; they come from Bangladesh, and the High Commission posts the list of arrived passports on its Facebook page [B0106, B0101].

   You'll need [B0104]:
   - the filled online form;
   - your current passport and a copy;
   - your NID or birth registration copy;
   - 1 photo;
   - USD 110.

   1. Fill in the form at [epassport.gov.bd/onboarding](https://www.epassport.gov.bd/onboarding) [B0100].
   2. Bring the printout and papers to the High Commission; dress well, as your photo is taken there [B0099, B0103].
   3. Watch the High Commission's Facebook page for your name, then collect it [B0106]. Someone else can collect it with your signed authorisation, the delivery slip, your old passport if any and their own ID [B0108].

   Apply 6 months before the passport expires; an expired passport can still be renewed [B0107]. A correction of data needs your NID and often a police check, which adds time [B0116].

   **If the passport is lost or the company holds it and won't return it:** get a report from a Maldives police station (yourself or through the company); the police officer emails it to the High Commission, which checks it before issuing a new passport [B0105]. The High Commission also has a sample application for collecting a passport deposited with Maldives Immigration [B0085].

   > **Need to know**
   > - How long does a passport applied for in Malé take to arrive in 2026?
   > - What is the e-passport fee in Malé now (USD 110 was the 2024 figure for the old passport)?

2. **Get a Welfare Board membership card if you came without BMET clearance.** It gives the Welfare Board's help (body repatriation, Tk 3 lakh to a dead worker's family, Tk 35,000 for burial, scholarships, PKB loans) to workers who have no BMET smart card [B0166, B0088]. You don't need it if you had a BMET smart card for your Maldives job [B0089].

   You'll need [B0088, B0166, B0168]:
   - a valid Maldives work permit, ID card or visa copy;
   - passport copy;
   - 2 passport photos;
   - your nominee's ID copy;
   - USD 40.

   1. Fill in the form at [portal.wewb.gov.bd](https://portal.wewb.gov.bd/) [B0090].
   2. Bring the printout, copies and fee to the Labour Welfare Wing, yourself or through someone [B0088].
   3. You usually get the card's soft copy within a week and the card later [B0114].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Is the membership fee USD 40 (High Commission page, December 2025) or USD 35 (FAQ and poster) [B0088, B0168]?

3. **Go home without a valid passport: the travel permit.** The travel document (a 3-page passport) is for going home in an emergency when your passport has expired or is lost; it is valid once, only to Bangladesh, for 3 months [B0109, B0110].

   You'll need [B0113, B0167]:
   - the application (sample on the High Commission's forms page) [B0117];
   - a passport, NID or birth certificate copy;
   - 1 passport-size and 1 stamp-size photo;
   - Maldives Immigration's voluntary return request form ("token") or immigration clearance copy;
   - USD 13 cash [B0111].

   It is ready in 5 to 7 days [B0112]. Sample wording: "I came to the Maldives in [year] for work. I have no valid passport. I need to return to Bangladesh urgently for [family/health] reasons. I attach the papers and fee and request a travel permit." [B0167]. How to get the Immigration token is under Going home without valid papers.

4. **Driving licence verification** (for a Maldives licence): apply with your Bangladesh licence, passport and visa copies and USD 11 cash; the letter usually comes within a week [B0115].

5. **Universal pension.** The High Commission asks Bangladeshis abroad to register in the government's universal pension scheme [B0083].

### Coming home (Bangladesh side)

- **Airport help.** The Welfare Board's desks at Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet airports give food and water to people returning from jail or on an outpass (travel permit), money to workers coming back from war, disaster or lay-off, help with customs and lost luggage, and take complaints of abuse or cheating [B0061, B0062]. Women returning within a year after abuse or fraud get Tk 5,000 cash [B0075]. A free ambulance takes injured, sick or dead workers from Dhaka and Chattogram airports; Dhaka desk +8802 8901040, 01404 413388; Chattogram 01913631797 [B0059]. Sylhet desk 01558277904 [B0076].
- **A place to stay.** The Wage Earners' Centre (Lonjonipara, Borua, Khilkhet, Dhaka-1229, near Shahjalal airport): Tk 100 admission, Tk 200 a night; book at wec.wewb.gov.bd or in person with proof of clearance or membership, a passport copy and the ticket; 01310 350555, 01754 715720 (24 hours) [B0063]. The Probashi Lounge at Shahjalal airport gives free wifi and phone calls and food at up to 30% off with proof of clearance or membership [B0064].
- **If your family needs legal help in Bangladesh, such as protecting its property,** apply to the Welfare Board (directly, through the Deputy Commissioner's expatriate welfare branch or the DEMO) [B0065].
- **Business loan:** PKB's rehabilitation loan, up to Tk 50 lakh (Tk 3 lakh without collateral), 9% over up to 10 years, at the branch for the project's area with a trade licence and return papers [B0066, B0067]; fees 0.5% processing (max Tk 15,000) and 0.5% documentation [B0068].
- **BRAC** gives returnees airport help, counselling, money or materials to restart earning, and legal help [B0069].
- **If a worker dies,** the family claims compensation from the employer through the Ministry (aswelfare@probashi.gov.bd), which writes to the High Commission and the Welfare Board within 7 working days [B0073]. The airport desk hands over the body and a Tk 35,000 cheque for transport and burial [B0075]. The employer must send the body home at its own cost under the High Commission's attestation conditions (see If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job) [B0121].
