## a. Draft doc text

### Step 2: The job offer and contract

In the Maldives the job offer is the letter of appointment that the employer uploads to Xpat (xpat.egov.mv, the expatriate-worker system of the Ministry of Homeland Security and Technology) with the work permit application [W0014]. The written employment contract is signed later, within 45 days of arrival [W0151].

1. **Get the letter of appointment before you pay anything or sign at the agency.** The employer must use the ministry's template [W0014]. It must list [W0057, W0058, W0059, W0060]:

   - the employer's name, address, contact details and registration number;
   - your name, permanent address, nationality, date of birth, passport number and an emergency contact (name and phone);
   - job title and the quota work type, which is the job category the employer's quota was issued for;
   - basic salary in US dollars and in rufiyaa (MVR), and the salary payment date;
   - work site, start date, job description and working hours;
   - whether the job is permanent or on contract, and the contract length.

   **Check what it says:**

   - The salary must be at least what the employer advertised on the National Job Center, because the employer may hire abroad only after advertising there for at least 7 days, and a quota worker is owed the advertised salary and benefits [W0168, W0170].
   - The job must not be one the Maldives gives no quota for: taxi and other fare-collecting drivers, co-pilots, vessel captains, photography and videography, entertainment, and cashiers in fruit, vegetable and food shops [W0138]. For jobs being closed to foreigners, check the date (see How long you can stay) [W0139, W0140, W0141].
   - The work site must be named: a foreign worker may not work at a site that is not registered with the ministry [W0133].

   **Ask for it in writing.** Send the employer or agent: "Please send me a copy of the letter of appointment you uploaded in Xpat for my work permit, showing my basic salary in USD and MVR, work site, working hours and contract length, before I pay anything." [W0057, W0060]

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does a worker on a new work permit sign the letter of appointment, as the employer-change template has required since 7 January 2026 [W0183]?
   > - Do Bangladeshi workers usually see the letter of appointment before leaving Dhaka, or only the entry pass?

2. **Know what you must not pay for.** The employer pays the quota fee (MVR 2,000 per worker for 12 months), the work permit fee (MVR 350 a month) and the security deposit, which the ministry last published as MVR 8,000 for a Bangladeshi [W0135, W0024, W0025, W0064]. Regulation 2025/R-120, the Regulation on Expatriate Employment, puts every permit fee, service fee and fine on the employer [W0162]. Under the Employment Act an employment agency may charge the employer, but no fee may be taken directly or indirectly from a prospective employee [W0125]. Quota and work permit fees are not refunded [W0177]. What workers really pay in Bangladesh is in What you pay, and what you earn and keep (see What people really pay).

   **If someone in the Maldives asks you for these fees:** report it to the Labour Relations Authority (LRA) on 332 8282 or through its online complaint form [W0163]. The Bangladesh-side complaint routes are in Step 1: Find a real job.

3. **Sign the written contract within 45 days of arrival and keep your copy.** The regulation gives the employer 45 days from your arrival to sign a contract with you and give you a copy [W0151]. The Employment Act requires a written agreement that states your name, passport number and emergency contact, whether the job is permanent or temporary, the start date, pay and benefits, how salary is calculated, pay day, leave days, disciplinary rules, appraisal and how dismissal works [W0126, W0127]. The employer must give you a signed copy, and an employer who does not is fined MVR 2,000 to MVR 20,000 [W0128, W0129]. A fixed-term contract may last at most two years, and probation at most three months [W0130, W0131]. You must also get a written job description within 20 days of starting [W0152]. Before the employer can make the first work permit fee payment, it uploads a notarised agreement between itself and you [W0026].

   **Check it is real:** the salary in USD and MVR, the work site and the working hours must match the letter of appointment (item 1) [W0057].

   **If it fails:** a lower salary or no contract is a wage complaint (see If you aren't paid); your rights under the contract are in Your rights at work.

   > **Need to know**
   > - If the contract signed in the Maldives pays less than the letter of appointment uploaded in Xpat, which one does the LRA enforce?
   > - Is the contract given only in English or Dhivehi, or also in Bengali?

**Scams:** since 15 September 2026 nobody who enters on a business visa or a special visa can get a work permit without leaving; a real job needs a Work Permit Entry Pass issued before you fly [W0032]. A "business visa" or "special visa" sold as a work visa is therefore not a route to a work permit [W0032].

### Step 3: The employer gets your work permit and entry pass

Everything in this step is done by the employer on Xpat, logged in with a verified eFaas account (the Maldives government digital ID) [W0049]. The office in charge is the Department of Foreign Labour at the Ministry of Homeland Security and Technology, on 1485 and xpat@home.gov.mv [W0040, W0041]. The rules are Regulation 2025/R-120, published on 14 December 2025 [W0047]. Whether new permits are being issued for Bangladeshis at all is in Is it open now, and who can go? (see Status).

1. **The employer registers the work site and gets a quota.** Before bringing a worker it must register the work site, obtain a quota and obtain a work permit; bringing a worker without a quota, or moving a worker to another business without telling the ministry, is illegal [W0132, W0134]. It first advertises the job on the National Job Center for at least 7 days and may use a quota only if no suitable Maldivian applied [W0168]. The ministry checks the employer's finances against its tax records [W0176]. The quota costs MVR 2,000 per worker for 12 months, except that micro, small and medium businesses get up to 20 quota slots free [W0135, W0048]. The quota slot must still have at least 145 days of validity when the work permit is requested [W0010].

2. **You send the employer your documents; it applies for the work permit.** You must be 18 or older [W0011].

   You'll need:
   - passport bio-data page, valid at least 12 months (and at least one year on arrival) [W0013, W0166];
   - a digital passport photo to Maldives Immigration standards [W0004];
   - a police report from Bangladesh less than 6 months old (see Step 4: Passport, police clearance and certificates) [W0164];
   - proof of the vaccinations the Ministry of Health requires [W0165];
   - for a professional job, your certificates with certified English translations, and any registration the job needs (see Work visa: professional (teachers, doctors, nurses, engineers)) [W0016].

   The employer adds the letter of appointment and the Maldives Immigration Visa Applicant Information Form (IM30) [W0014, W0015]. You must travel on the same passport used in the application; if you get a new passport after the entry pass is issued, the employer must cancel it and apply again [W0017]. A completed application is processed in 3 working days, and the employer is notified by email and SMS and tracks it under "My Applications" [W0021, W0023].

3. **The employer pays the deposit; the ministry issues your Work Permit Entry Pass.** Once approved, the application moves to "Pending Payment"; after payment the Work Permit Entry Pass is issued [W0018]. The deposit is set for each nationality and is no more than the cost of your ticket home [W0153]. The ministry's last published table puts it at MVR 8,000 for Bangladesh, against MVR 3,500 for India, MVR 3,800 for Pakistan, MVR 4,000 for Sri Lanka and MVR 8,000 for Nepal, and says rates can change without notice [W0064, W0065, W0066]. The employer pays it online by BML (Bank of Maldives) Gateway or its Xpat wallet; government employers are exempt [W0181, W0182]. When the deposit is refunded it goes back to the employer's Xpat wallet [W0045]. After payment the permit shows "Issued (Pending Arrival)" [W0180].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What deposit does Schedule 5 of Regulation 2025/R-120 set for a Bangladeshi worker in 2026, and is the 2021 table (MVR 8,000) still applied?
   > - Do agents in Dhaka or Malé charge workers for the deposit, and under what name?

4. **Pass the pre-departure medical, if it runs in Bangladesh.** The Maldives Ministry of Health requires everyone seeking a work permit to take a Pre-Departure Health Assessment (PDHA) in their home country, where the PDHA system is running [W0085]. It covers your medical history, a physical examination, a chest X-ray, and tests for HIV, hepatitis B, hepatitis C and syphilis, plus filariasis and malaria tests for people from areas where they are common [W0087]. You also get a measles-rubella (MR) vaccination [W0088]. The clinic must be designated by the Ministry of Health Maldives [W0096]. At registration the clinic checks your face against your passport and takes your photo and fingerprints [W0095, W0092]. You are counselled and sign a consent form in a language you understand [W0094]. Grade A means you are cleared to work in the Maldives; Grade B means a significant abnormal finding and you are not eligible [W0089]. The clinic uploads your fingerprints and result within 2 days, and the result is valid for 3 months from the examination [W0093, W0090].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Is the PDHA running in Bangladesh in November 2026; if so, which Dhaka clinics are designated and what do they charge?
   > - If it is not running, what medical (if any) do Maldives employers require before a Bangladeshi worker flies?

5. **Fly within 90 days.** You must enter the Maldives within 90 days of the entry pass being issued, and you must hold it on arrival [W0019, W0009]. The flight and BMET clearance are in Step 6: Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight.

**How long, in order:**

1. Job Center advert: at least 7 days [W0168].
2. Work permit decision: 3 working days once complete [W0021].
3. Deposit paid: entry pass issued [W0018].
4. You fly within 90 days [W0019].
5. On landing you hold a 30-day conditional work permit [W0148].
6. Medical within 15 days of arrival, and the work e-Visa within 15 days of arrival (Step 7) [W0043, W0003].
7. Full work permit within 30 days, extendable by 15; if no permit is issued within 45 days you may move to another employer without your employer's consent [W0169, W0149].
8. Contract within 45 days of arrival [W0151].

The regulation counts these days without government holidays [W0150].

**What you get back:** a Work Permit Entry Pass and a work permit number in the form WP00000000, which you need for every check in Step 5 [W0018, W0055].

> **Need to know**
> - Can a refused quota or work permit be appealed, and by whom?
> - What does the entry pass document look like (PDF, QR code, fields), and does BMET check it before clearance?

### Step 5: Check your permit and visa are real

1. **Check the work permit on Xpat before you pay the rest or fly.**

   You'll need: your work permit number (WP followed by 8 digits) and your first name as on the permit, or your passport number [W0055, W0054].

   1. Open [xpat.egov.mv/Home/WorkPermitVerify](https://xpat.egov.mv/Home/WorkPermitVerify), or click "Work Permit Verification" on the xpat.egov.mv home page [W0051].
   2. Type the number in the "Work Permit Number" box [W0055].
   3. Type your first name or your passport number in the "Name or Passport Number" box [W0054].
   4. Press "Verify" [W0054].

   **Check it is real:** the employer and job must match your letter of appointment [W0057]. Once the deposit is paid, the employer's Xpat record shows "Issued (Pending Arrival)"; ask the employer for a screenshot of it before you fly [W0180].

   **If it fails:** "Employee not found. Please Enter correct details" means no match; check the number for typing errors and try your passport number instead of your name [W0158]. If it still fails, call the Department of Foreign Labour on 1485 or email xpat@home.gov.mv [W0040, W0041].

2. **Check the employer is not on the negligence list.** Xpat publishes the names of employers with confirmed negligence cases since 15 September 2025, with their registration number and number of cases [W0052, W0056]. Open [xpat.egov.mv/EmploymentApproval/EmploymentApprovalMissingPerson/MissingNegligenceEmployerList](https://xpat.egov.mv/EmploymentApproval/EmploymentApprovalMissingPerson/MissingNegligenceEmployerList) and type the company name or registration number in "Search" [W0056]. Since 15 September 2025 an employer that abandons or neglects foreign workers it brought in is fined MVR 50,000 per worker [W0046, W0035].

3. **After arrival, check the e-Visa.** Once approved, an e-Visa logo appears in the Visa section of Xpat and the e-Visa can be viewed or downloaded at any time [W0005]. The employer holds the Xpat account, so ask it for a copy [W0160]. The e-Visa is valid as long as the work permit, and it shows as invalid if the work permit fee has not been paid [W0030, W0006].

4. **Use the Xpat MV app as a second check.** There is no physical work permit card; the official Xpat MV app holds a digital card, added with your work permit number and passport number, and its QR code checks the permit against Xpat [W0156]. The app was last updated in 2023, so use the verification page in item 1 if it fails [W0157].

5. **Check whether you must give fingerprints.** The Fingerprint Requirement page on xpat.egov.mv needs only your passport number and country, and tells you only whether you must enrol fingerprints [W0159, W0051].

> **Need to know**
> - What does the Work Permit Verification result show (employer, occupation, expiry date, status)?
> - Does Maldives Immigration have a public check for a work e-Visa or entry pass by passport number?

**If a check fails, in this order:**

1. Department of Foreign Labour, Ministry of Homeland Security and Technology: 1485, xpat@home.gov.mv, H. Pamelia, Waterfront Building, Boduthakurufaanu Magu, Malé [W0040, W0041, W0042].
2. Labour Relations Authority: 332 8282 or its online complaint form [W0163].
3. The Bangladesh-side routes in Step 1: Find a real job.

### Step 7: After arrival

1. **Let the employer meet you at the airport.** Employers must receive their workers on arrival in person or send someone [W0020]. Immigration lets you in on the entry pass; if the passport number on your papers does not match your passport, your passport can be held for checks before entry [W0002, W0008]. From landing you hold a 30-day conditional work permit (Step 3, How long) [W0148].

2. **Do the work permit medical within 15 days.** New arrivals must submit a work permit medical within 15 days of arrival [W0043]. It must come from a provider registered with the ministry [W0172]. Registered providers include government hospitals such as Indira Gandhi Memorial Hospital and Hulhumalé Hospital and the atoll and regional hospitals, and private ones such as ADK Hospital, Medica Hospital, Tree Top Hospital and Jibon Clinic; the list was last updated on 18 August 2026 [W0076, W0077, W0078, W0079]. The employer uploads the report in Xpat, choosing the provider from a list and giving the issue and expiry dates [W0081, W0082]. After approval your work permit card shows "fit", "unfit" or "needs isolation" [W0083]. If you are unfit or need isolation the employer contacts the Health Protection Agency (HPA) [W0084]. If you are found unfit no permit is issued and the employer must arrange your return; a treatable condition that is not contagious may get a conditional permit [W0155]. The medical is valid for 1 year from the doctor's final approval and must be renewed [W0044, W0062]. If a permit is cancelled while the medical has lapsed, a fine of MVR 2,000 per year applies, which is the employer's to pay [W0061, W0162].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Is the Maldives medical due within 15 days of arrival (Xpat) or 30 days (Ministry of Health guideline) [W0043, W0086]?
   > - Which results make a worker unfit in the Maldives medical (HIV, hepatitis B, TB), and who pays the flight home?
   > - What do registered clinics charge for the work permit medical in 2026, and do employers deduct it from wages?

3. **Get the health insurance from day one.** You must be insured from the date of arrival [W0091]. The insurance must come from a registered provider, and your permit lapses if the insurance or medical expires [W0172, W0154]. The ministry's minimum policy [W0067, W0068, W0069, W0070, W0071, W0072, W0073, W0074]:

   - at least MVR 100,000 cover for 12 months, inside the Maldives, for people aged 65 and below;
   - outpatient care MVR 2,000 a year;
   - pre-existing conditions covered;
   - repatriation of your body home, or burial in the Maldives, if you die there;
   - cashless claims at all government hospitals and health facilities, ADK Hospital and Medica Hospital; no co-payment for inpatient care at government centres;
   - premium at most MVR 800 a person.

   The registered insurers in 2021 were Amana Takaful, Dhivehi Insurance Company, Solarelle Insurance and Allied Insurance Company of the Maldives [W0075].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Are the MVR 100,000 minimum and MVR 800 premium cap still in force under Regulation 2025/R-120, and which insurers are registered in 2026?
   > - Do workers receive their own policy card or number?

4. **Make sure the employer applies for your work e-Visa within 15 days.** The employer must apply for and obtain your work e-Visa within 15 days of your arrival on the entry pass, through Xpat [W0003, W0029]. It needs your photo and a colour copy of your passport bio page [W0004]. Since 23 November 2025 visa stickers in passports are not valid; only an approved e-Visa counts, and you need it to leave and re-enter [W0001, W0002]. Ask the employer for a copy once it is approved (Step 5, item 3) [W0160]. If you get a new passport, make sure the visa shows the new passport number before you travel [W0007].

5. **Give your fingerprints if Xpat says you must.** A permit may not be extended until your biometrics are updated [W0161]. People whose permit was issued while they were already in the country (for example on another employer's permit) must enrol, because biometrics are not taken at the border for them [W0039]. In Greater Malé the employer books a slot at [xpatappointment.home.gov.mv](https://xpatappointment.home.gov.mv/), 2pm to 4pm on working days; elsewhere it arranges this with the island council office [W0037, W0038].

6. **Live where the employer houses you.** The employer must provide housing for the whole job, to the standard in the regulation, and gives its details in the permit application [W0174, W0171]. Housing complaints are in Your rights at work.

7. **Open a bank account in your own name.** Since 25 September 2026 your salary must be paid into an account in your own name at a bank licensed by the Maldives Monetary Authority (MMA) [W0033, W0147]. In an LRA wage case, salary counts as paid only if it went into that account [W0034].

   You'll need (Bank of Maldives, BML) [W0097, W0102, W0100]:
   - your passport, original shown for checking;
   - a work permit with at least one month left;
   - an "Employment Verification Letter" from your employer stating your permanent and present address, gross salary, length of service and work permit number [W0101].

   Apply for your first account online at [app.bankofmaldives.com.mv/join](https://app.bankofmaldives.com.mv/join/) [W0099]. The BML expatriate account in rufiyaa needs a minimum deposit of MVR 200 and costs MVR 75 a month [W0098]. Sending money home is in Sending money home.

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which banks do employers of Bangladeshi construction and resort workers use, and do employers open the accounts themselves?
   > - Does the employer keep the worker's bank card or PIN in practice?

8. **Get a SIM card.** Dhiraagu needs a valid passport or work permit from a foreigner [W0104]. Foreign workers may hold at most three mobile numbers with each operator [W0103].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What does Ooredoo Maldives ask from a work-permit holder, and what does a basic prepaid SIM cost?

9. **Keep your passport.** Whether the employer may keep it, and what to do if it does, is in Your rights at work.

### How long you can stay

**As long as the fee is paid, one year at a time.** Your work permit lasts for the months your employer has paid at MVR 350 a month, paid at least one month ahead [W0024, W0027]. Your work e-Visa lasts as long as the permit, renewed for at most one year at a time, and it shows as invalid once the fee runs out [W0030, W0031, W0006]. If the fee goes unpaid you may move to another employer without consent, and after 6 months unpaid the ministry can cancel the permit and send you home [W0173]. The permit also lapses if your yearly medical or your insurance expires, and it cannot be extended until your biometrics are updated [W0154, W0044, W0161].

**The upper limit.** A non-professional or unskilled work permit can be issued for up to 10 years in total, up from 5 years before December 2025 [W0136, W0145].

> **Need to know**
> - Is there a maximum total stay for professional-category work permits?
> - Can a worker who reaches 10 years return on a new permit after leaving?

**Jobs closing to foreigners.** Regulation 2025/R-120 cancels quotas for some jobs a set time after 14 December 2025 [W0047]:

- after 2 years: hairdressers, beauticians, personal trainers, electricians, English-speaking guest relations officers, tour guides, accountants and imams (babysitters were taken off this list on 17 December 2025) [W0139, W0175];
- after 3 years: divers, nurses, building inspectors, surveyors, site supervisors, pilots and photographers [W0140];
- after 4 years: electrical engineers and dive instructors [W0141];
- after 5 years: housekeeping, human resources and front office managers, and primary and secondary teachers [W0141].

**Time abroad.** A work permit is voided if you stay outside the Maldives for more than four months while it is valid [W0143]. If your permit expired while you were abroad, the employer can get a temporary 15-day approval with a medical done abroad, and you then need a Maldives medical within 15 days of return [W0063]. Going home on leave is in Going home on leave.

**Other ways a permit ends.** A permit can be cancelled for a change of employer, breaking the regulation, a court order, long absence, or missing or false documents [W0137]. It is voided if you are told to leave for health reasons and do not leave in time, and revoked if you do not cooperate with an investigation for 10 days [W0144, W0146]. What happens when the job ends is in Leaving at the end of the job.

> **Need to know**
> - After a work permit is cancelled, how many days does a worker have to leave or change employer before counting as overstaying?

## Work visa: professional (teachers, doctors, nurses, engineers)

A professional uses the same route as a company worker: the employer gets a quota, the work permit and the entry pass on Xpat, and then the work e-Visa (see Work visa: company worker (construction, resort, shop), Step 3) [W0018, W0003]. Xpat classes each occupation as Professional or Non-Professional: registered nurses, medical officers, primary school teachers and civil and electrical engineers are Professional, while carpenters, masons and waiters are Non-Professional [W0184, W0185, W0186, W0187, W0188]. A professional or managerial permit needs an MQA (Maldives Qualifications Authority) level 7 qualification in the field, or MQA level 5 with 5 years' relevant experience and an MQA recognition report [W0012]. On the MQA scale, level 7 is a bachelor's degree and level 5 a diploma [W0189, W0190]. The employer also uploads your certificates, with certified English translations, and any registration the occupation needs [W0016]. Quotas for nurses end three years after 14 December 2025, for electrical engineers four years after, and for primary and secondary teachers five years after (see How long you can stay) [W0140, W0141, W0047].

### Step 1: Get your certificates attested in Bangladesh

1. **Attest every certificate at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.** MQA recognition needs your degree, transcript or mark sheet, earlier qualifications, and SSC, HSC or school-leaving certificates, all attested by the foreign ministry of your country [W0106, W0107]. A provisional certificate is not accepted [W0108]. How to get Bangladeshi certificates attested is in Step 4: Passport, police clearance and certificates.

   **Scams:** the employer signs a declaration that the certificates are genuine and takes full responsibility if forged ones are submitted [W0112].

### Step 2: The employer gets your qualification recognised by MQA

1. **The employer applies to MQA, one form per certificate.** The form is the "Application Form for Qualification Recognition/Validation (for Foreigners)" [W0105].

   You'll need (the employer submits): the attested copies from Step 1, an English translation of any certificate not in English, your passport bio page, and the employer's company registration [W0106, W0107, W0192, W0193, W0191].

   1. The employer hands in the form at MQA, Velaanaage, 2nd Floor, Malé, between 9am and 12 noon on working days [W0110, W0111].
   2. It pays MVR 500 per validation and MVR 250 for each extra copy [W0109].
   3. MQA's phone is +960 3344077 and email info@mqa.gov.mv [W0111].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Is the MQA foreigner form now submitted online, what does it cost in 2026, and how long does recognition take?

### Step 3: Register with your professional council

1. **Doctors: register with the Maldives Medical and Dental Council (MMDC).** The MMDC's published fees are MVR 500 for two years' temporary basic registration for a foreign doctor, MVR 500 for a licence valid two years, and MVR 3,000 for each attempt at its licensing exam [W0113, W0114, W0115].

   You'll need (as BOESL, the Bangladesh government's recruiting company, listed for Ministry of Health jobs): passport, your Bangladesh medical council registration, undergraduate and postgraduate degrees, internship certificate, certificate of good standing, experience certificates and the MMDC pre-registration form [W0123, W0124].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Must a Bangladeshi MBBS pass the MMDC licensing exam, and are all BMDC-recognised Bangladeshi medical colleges on the MMDC list?
   > - What are the MMDC fees in 2026 (the published table dates from 2015)?

2. **Nurses: register with the Maldives Nursing and Midwifery Council (MNMC).** Foreign nurses get a temporary registration and licence for two years, in line with their contract, and renew every two years [W0116, W0121].

   You'll need [W0117, W0118, W0119, W0122]:
   - two years' clinical experience within the last five years, with references that describe your clinical area and a hospital of at least 150 beds;
   - IELTS 5.5 or an equivalent English certificate;
   - police clearance;
   - copies notarised by the institution that issued the certificate, an embassy or consulate of the issuing country, or a government office of that country [W0120].

3. **Teachers: get a teaching licence.** Since July 2023 all local and foreign teachers must hold a licence from the Teacher Registration Board at the Ministry of Education [W0178, W0179].

   > **Need to know**
   > - How does a foreign teacher apply for the teaching licence (portal, documents, fee), and can the licence be obtained before arrival?
