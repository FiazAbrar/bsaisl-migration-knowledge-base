                    Qualifications Recognition service
  is given for 3 stages of a qualification as following:

Recognition of an intended programme

Qualification assessment report

Validation/Recognition of the certificate

Recognition of an intended programme

                    This service is given for students who apply to MQA ensure if an academic programme they intend to study or currently pursuing is recognized by MQA.

Qualification Assessment Report

                    Qualification assessment report is issued for those who have
  completed their academic programmes but their certificates are not awarded.

Validation/Recognition of the certificate

                    This service is given when the certificates are submitted for recognition.
