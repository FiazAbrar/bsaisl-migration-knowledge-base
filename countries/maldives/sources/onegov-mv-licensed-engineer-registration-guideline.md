# Construction Industry Licensed Architect / Engineer Registration

This service allows applicants to register as licensed architects or engineers, specify the responsibilities of the registered individual, and carry out the classification of practitioners. The registration is based on academic qualifications and experience, as specified in the Construction Industry Practitioners Regulation (2019/R-1010).

Last updated on

November 05, 2025

# **Before You Start**

To proceed with this application, ensure you:

- Meet the academic qualification and experience criteria specified in the Construction Industry Practitioners Regulation (2019/R-1010).

- Review the registration categories and grades, which include:

  - **Category 1**: [Licensed Architect](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1NWr8TPLlqQtOmCgONONbgLU09AUzdARN/view?usp=sharing)

  - **Category 2**: [Licensed Engineer](https://drive.google.com/file/d/15-jd-nWHxQu3yFMEG0tBknRzfJ4HrSVj/view?usp=sharing)

  - Detailed requirements for these categories and grades are outlined in the regulation document.

# **Service Fee**

You’ll need to pay a registration fee of MVR 500 for each application.

# **Apply for a new registration**

Register as a licensed architect or engineer to legally practise within the construction industry in the Maldives.

## **Eligibility**

- Your academic qualification and experience must meet the criteria specified in the Construction Industry Practitioners Regulation (2019/R-1010).

## **Documents You Will Need**

- [Experience form](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pZQBz7Y6JZyJmHXuOK5t5VNqYqPrjGDP/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=109087087705810177584&rtpof=true&sd=true)

- Accredited copy of educational certificates

- Support documents for experience, such as construction permits, drawing approval permits, and letters from Building Consent Authorities, listing you as a designer or checker

**Important Notes:**

- **Foreigners may apply only if they are spouses of a Maldivian, as per the Construction Industry Practitioners Regulation (2019/R-1010).**

- **Overseas work experience is considered but at half the original consideration level.**

# **Update the information on the current registration**

## **Eligibility**

- You must be a registered architect or engineer or authorised to submit updates for your registration.

## **Documents You Will Need**

- Accredited copy of educational certificates (if qualifications have changed)

- Support documents for experience, if applicable

**Important Note: Any changes must be reported to the Ministry within 21 days to avoid penalties.**

# **Renew the current registration**

## **Eligibility**

- Your academic qualification and experience must meet the criteria specified in the Construction Industry Practitioners Regulation (2019/R-1010).

## **Documents You Will Need**

- Accredited copy of educational certificates

- Support documents for experience, including construction permits, drawing approval permits, and letters from Building Consent Authorities

# **How to Apply**

- **Search and select** the Construction Industry Licensed Architect / Engineer Registration service on the oneGov platform. Choose the relevant application type:

  - Apply for new registration

  - Change registration details

  - Renew registration
- **Complete the application form** with the required details and **attach the necessary documents**.

- **Submit your application** after reviewing all details.

# **Payment Notification**

- Once your application is approved, you will receive a notification via email and SMS to make the payment.

- Access the payment prompt under **My Applications** on the oneGov portal.

- Clicking this will redirect you to the BandeyriPay portal, where you can complete the payment.

# **After You Apply**

After submitting your application:

- You will receive a notification via email and SMS.

- Track the status of your application under the My Applications tab.

- Once processed, your architect or engineer registration will be sent via email.

# **How Long Will it Take?**

Completed applications will be processed within 10 working days from the date of document verification completion.
