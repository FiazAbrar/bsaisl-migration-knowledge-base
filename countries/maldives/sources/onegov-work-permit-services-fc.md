# Request New Work Permit (oneGov guideline)

Source: https://one.gov.mv/support/guidelines/new-work-permit-services-130 (Government of Maldives, oneGov; last updated on April 12, 2025; scraped with Firecrawl 2026-11-08)

## Request a New Work Permit - Eligibility (extract)

- The prospective work permit holder must be 18 years or older.
- Work permits for domestic household work are only issued to female employees.
- A new work permit holder may apply for an employer change without a time restriction, provided their permit has at least 8 days of validity.

## After You Apply (extract)

- After payment, the Work Permit Entry Pass will be issued.
- Employees must enter the Maldives within 90 days of receiving the entry pass.
- Employers must personally receive their employees or arrange for someone to do so upon their arrival.

# Change Employer

#### Description

This service allows an existing work permit holder to switch employers.

### Eligibility

- The new employer must have a quota slot valid for at least 45 days.
- The utilised quota pool must have at least one active site.
- If a regularisation quota slot was used, the quota slot may not be visible for employer change applications. In such cases, employers should contact 1500 for assistance.

### How to Apply

1. Select the Change Employer service on oneGov platform.
2. Click "Proceed" and you will be redirected to the service on Xpat Portal.

#### Required Documents

- Passport bio-data page.
- Passport-size digital photo. (according to Maldives Immigration standards)
- Letter of appointment. (including the fields specified on the template on the Xpat home page)
- Maldives Immigration Visa Applicant Information Form (IM30 form).
- Educational certificates (for professional occupations).
- Medical report (not older than 45 days).
- No objection letter from the current employer (issued within the last 3 months) with employer details, employee details, and optional work permit number.

### After You Apply

- Notification will be sent via email and SMS.
- Check the application status under 'My Applications'.
- Once approved, the status changes to 'Pending Payment'.
- After payment, the new work permit will be issued.

#### Service Fee

- Work permit deposit details are available on the Xpat homepage.

#### Processing Time

- Completed applications are processed within 3 working days.
