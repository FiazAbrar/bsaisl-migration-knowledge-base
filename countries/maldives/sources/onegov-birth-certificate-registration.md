## Birth Certificate Registration

This service can be used to apply for services related to registering the birth certificate of an individual. (އުފަންވުން ރަޖިސްޓްރީކޮށް އުފަންދުވަހުގެ ސެޓްފިކެޓް، އުފަންދުވަހުގެ ރަޖިސްޓްރީ ހެއްދުން)

This service is provided by [Fuvahmulah City Council](/entities/FVM)
