Meeting \
Visa
=========

Effective from 14th May 2023, an on-arrival 14 (fourteen) days short-stay visa ‘Meeting Visa’ will be granted for any foreign national visiting the Maldives for the purpose of attending a meeting, conference or convention held in the Maldives provided that the individual satisfies the entry requirements to the Maldives.

[](https://imuga.immigration.gov.mv/)

Fill Traveller Declaration on IMUGA

![Meeting Visa illustration](/svg/hero_images/meeting_visa.svg)

## Meeting Requirements and Process

Quick Navigation

### Entry Prerequisites

1.  The primary purpose of the visit is to **attend/participate in a meeting, conference, or convention** held in the Maldives:

- For permissible businesses accepted by the Registrar of Businesses; or
- For government/state use; or
- Arranged by other entities with **approval from the concerned government authority**.

1.  A complete travel itinerary including **confirmed return tickets**, a **prepaid confirmed booking** at a registered hotel, and **proof of sufficient funds** for the stay.
2.  **Traveller Declaration** filled and submitted electronically via [IMUGA](https://imuga.immigration.gov.mv/) within **96 hours** prior to flight time to Maldives.
3.  A passport or travel document with **Machine Readable Zone (MRZ)** and at least **1 month validity**. (Passengers with extended validity in their passports are not allowed to enter.)
4.  Entry requirements to onward destinations (e.g., visa and passport validity).

### Important Notes

- **Meeting Visa is non-extendable.**
- A Meeting Visa holder is **not eligible** to obtain a **Business Visa while in the Maldives**.

Entry to the Maldives is **not guaranteed** even with a comprehensive travel plan (e.g., confirmed return tickets, prepaid confirmed accommodation, proof of sufficient funds). Under **Sections 7 and 8** of **The Maldives Immigration Act 2007**, entry is permitted at the port of entry following an immigration officer's determination of eligibility.
