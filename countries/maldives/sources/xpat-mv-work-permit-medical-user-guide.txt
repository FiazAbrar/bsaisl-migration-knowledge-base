   work permit medical record within the Xpat system in order to

3. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

         1. How do I register as a medical service provider?
              Contact us at xpat@1500help.mv regarding the registration. Attach the relevant documents
              along with the email

         2. Do I have to update work permit medical for existing work permits?
              Employers must submit a work permit medical in order to pay the work permit fee from 15th July
              2022 onwards

         3. Will work permit medical be mandatory after this update?
              Work permit medical will be mandatory starting 15th July 2022

         4. Is work permit medical validity required when paying work permit fee?
              Work permit medical can limit the work permit fee payment duration. You can renew the work
              permit medical to extend the fee payment duration if it is the limiting factor

         5. What happens if the employee is unfit or requires isolation?
              If the employee is unfit or requires isolation, please contact HPA

         6. Can I update an expired work permit medical?
              Yes. An expired work permit can be updated by submitting a new work permit medical request
              within the Xpat System
