#### Updates & Notices

Latest Alert

##### Announcement: Expatriate Biometrics Update Services

Expatriate Biometrics Update services in the Greater Male' Area are available exclusively through the Appointment Booking Portal. Biometric collection services will only be provided to individuals with a confirmed appointment.

**Appointment Booking Portal:** https://xpatappointment.home.gov.mv/

**Service Hours:** 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM on working days.

Employers are required to book time slots through the portal and ensure that employees attend at the scheduled appointment time.

Employers whose employees are accommodated outside the Greater Malé Area are advised to coordinate directly with the respective Local Council Offices for biometric collection services.

**Important Notice**

Failure to complete the required biometric updates are subject to service restrictions on the Xpat System. Employers are urged to ensure that all pending biometric updates are completed at the earliest.
