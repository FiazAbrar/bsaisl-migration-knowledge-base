Dependent \
Visa
===========

A Dependent Visa is granted to immediate family members of foreign nationals working in the Maldives under a professional category of Work Visa. It allows spouses, children (below 18 years old), or parents to stay in the Maldives legally as dependents of the main visa holder.

[](https://imuga.immigration.gov.mv/)

Open IMUGA

![Dependent Visa illustration](/svg/hero_images/dependent_visa.svg)

## Dependent Requirements and Process

Quick Navigation

### Before Arrival (First-Time Entry)

- Before your family travels, you need to submit a **Sponsorship Declaration** through the [IMUGA portal](https://imuga.immigration.gov.mv/).
- Make sure to apply at least **7 working days before arrival**.
- Once they arrive on approved sponsorship, you can apply for the **Dependent Visa**.

### Documents Required

• Application Form (**IM20**)\
• Passport-size photo (as per the standard)\
• Passport copy (biodata page)\
• Copy of your Work Permit or Work Visa\
• Last arrival stamp *(for new applicants)*\
• Last visa page *(for renewals)*\
• Proof of relationship, such as birth or marriage certificate in English\
• Medical report\
• Expatriate health insurance &\
• Any other documents, if needed

\

### How to Apply

1.  Visit [imuga.immigration.gov.mv](https://imuga.immigration.gov.mv/)
2.  Select **"Visa"**
3.  Sign in to the IMUGA portal
4.  Click **"Apply for a Visa"**
5.  Complete the **prerequisite** field to confirm whether you are currently in the Maldives
6.  Select visa type: **"Dependent Visa"** and choose the appropriate **reason/subtype**
7.  Fill in the application details
8.  Attach required documents
9.  Review and submit the application\
    \
    Once the application is approved, the eVisa will be viewed and downloaded through the IMUGA Portal.

### Fees

- Visa fee: **MVR 750**

### Important Notes

\

- When applying for Sponsorship Declaration, the **stated purpose must be to bring in a dependent** in order to qualify for a Dependent Visa.
