                CONTRACT OF EMPLOYMENT

This CONTRACT OF EMPLOYMENT is made on this day........ month of ...........................

20........ between................................................................................... .......(here in after referred
to as ''the Employer'') of the one part and ...................................................................... Passport
No............................................................................................... (here in after referred to as ''the
Employee'') of the other part.

WHEREAS the Employer shall employ the Employee in accordance with the terms and
conditions of this Contract of Employment and subject to the laws, rules, regulations, national
policies and directives in Maldives.

IT IS HEREBY AGREED as follows:
1. Duration of this Contract of Employment

         The duration of this Contract of Employment shall be for a period of..............year(s)
         commencing on the day of arrival of the Employee in Maldives until such time this
         Contract of Employment is terminated in accordance with the terms and conditions of
         this Contract of Employment.
2. Wages
         2.1 The Employee shall receive a basic wage of USD............ (Excluding allowances

                  and overtime). This basic wage shall comply with the minimum wages to be
                  determined according to the Laws of Maldives.
         2.2 Wages shall be paid by the Employer on a monthly basis no later than seventh day
                  of the following month.
         2.3 The payment of the monthly wages shall be made through a bank account in the
                  Employee's name.
         2.4. Free food or Food allowance..............will be paid by the company
3. Working Hours
         Working hours shall be eight (8) hours per day in accordance with the labour laws in
         Maldives.

4. Overtime
         ln the event the Employee, upon the request by the Employer, agrees to work in excess of
         his normal working hours, the Employee shall be paid in accordance with the labour laws
         in Maldives.

5. Rest Day

         5.1 The Employee shall be entitle to ........... rest day in each week.
         5.2 In the event the Employee upon the request by the Employer, agrees to work on

                  such rest day, the Employee shall be paid in accordance with the labour laws in
                  Maldives.
6. Public Holiday

         6.1 The Employee shall be entitled public holidays in accordance with the labor laws
                  in Maldives.

         6.2 In the event the Employee, upon the request by the Employer, agrees to work on
                  such public holiday, the Employee shall be paid in accordance with the labour
                  laws in Maldives.

7. Annual Leave
         The Employee shall be entitled to annual leave in accordance with the labor laws in
         Maldives.

8. Levy
         The payment of levy is subject to the relevant laws, rules, regulations, national policies
         and directives applicable in Maldives from time to time in force.

9. Medical and Accident Insurance
         The Employee shall be insured under the medical and accidental insurance scheme. In
         any case if the insurance is not available the employer will take responsibility. In case of
         death the employer will be responsible to repatriate the dead body.

10. Deductions
         The Employer is entitled to make deduction for not more than 50% in a month from the
         Employee wages in the event of any monetary advance in accordance with the labour
         laws in Maldives.

                                                                                                                              2
11. Accommodation
         The Employer shall provide the Employee with reasonable accommodation with basic
         amenities in accordance with the laws, rules and regulations in Maldives.

12. Sick Leave
         The Employee shall be entitled to sick leave in accordance with the labour laws in
         Maldives.

13 Renewal of Employment approval
         13.1 The Employer shall renew the Employment approval at least two (2) months
         before the expiry date.
         13.2 Any penalty or compound due to the failure of the Employer to do so shall be
         borne by the Employer.

14. Air Passage
         The first travelling expenses from Bangladesh to any agreed point of entry in Maldives
         shall be borne by the Employer and the expenses from any agreed point of exit in
         Maldives to Bangladesh shall be borne by the Employer upon completion of this Contract
         of Employment.

15. Repatriation
         15.1 The repatriation cost of the Employees from their place of work to their original
                  exit point in Bangladesh shall be borne by the Employer under the following
                  circumstances:
                  (i) at the completion of this Contract of Employment;
                  (ii) termination of this Contract of Employment by the Employer other than
                           non-compliance of the terms and conditions of this Contract of
                           Employment by the Employee; or
                  (iii) termination of this Contract of Employment by the Employee due to non-
                           compliance of the terms and conditions of this Contract of Employment by
                           the Employer.
         The term "original exit point' in this Contract of Employment shall mean Dhaka,
         Bangladesh.

16. Termination:
         16.1 ln the event the Employer intends to terminate, this Contract of Employment, the
                  Employer shall give two (2) months' notice of his intention to terminate such

                                                                                                                              3
                  contract or two (2) months wages in lieu of notice to the Employee, and arrange
                  repatriation to Bangladesh for the Employee.
         16.2 In the event the Employee intends to terminate this "Contract of Employment" the
                  Employee shall give two (2) months' notice on-indemnify two (2) months wages
                  in lieu thereof to the Employer and the Employee shall bear the cost of air fare to
                  Bangladesh.
17. Restrictions
         17.1 The Employee shall not participate in any political activities or activities of
                  connected with political organizations in Maldives.
         17.2 The Employee shall not change employment during this contract Employment
                  period without the permission from the Employer
18. Extension
         The Employer and the Employee may agree that this Contract of Employment may be
         extended, subject to relevant laws, rules, regulations, national policies and directives
         relating to Employment applicable in Maldives.
19. Safekeeping of the Passport
         19.1 The Employer shall not keep the passport of the Employee in his/her custody.
         19.2 The Employee passport is lost or damaged the Employee shall bear related
                  expenses.
         19.3 The Employee shall carry the passport to the Employer for medical screening and
         other VISA related issue.
20. Foreign Employee Identity Card or work permit card
         The Employer shall ensure that each employee receives the ID card or work permit card
         Authentic in the event of any divergence of between any of the texts, the English text
         shall prevail.
21. Outstanding Wages
         In the event the Employee is to be repatriated before the expiry of this Contract of
         Employment, the Employer shall pay all outstanding basic wages and all other payments
         owed to the Employee subject to the relevant laws, rules, regulations, national policies
         and directives relating to employment applicable in Maldives.
22. Amendment

                                                                                                                              4
The Employer and the Employee may amend this Contract of Employment to incorporate any
other terms and conditions which shall be more favorable to the Employee.
23. Time is of the Essence
Time whenever mentioned shall be of the essence of this Contract of Employment.
24. Laws
This Contract of Employment shall be subjected to the law of Maldives.
25. Succession
This Contract of Employment shall be binding on the successor in title, assigns, personnel or
representatives of the parties hereto.
26. Language of this Contract of Employment
This Contract of Employment shall be prepared in 6 original texts, two each in English, Dhivehi
and Bangla language, all text being equally authentic. In the event of any divergence of
interpretation between any of the texts, the English text shall prevail.
