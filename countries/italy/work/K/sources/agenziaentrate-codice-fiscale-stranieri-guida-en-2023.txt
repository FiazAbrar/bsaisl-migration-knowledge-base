PURPOSE

The tax code identifies a citizen in dealings with the
public administration and is needed to register with
the Local Health Authority (ASL) and choose the
general practitioner.

The tax code is valid only when issued and certified
by the Revenue Agency and does not expire.

It is delivered by ordinary mail:

 to the address of residence, for resident

    citizens

 to the domicile for tax purposes communicated

    by the citizen at the time of the request, for non-
    resident citizens.
