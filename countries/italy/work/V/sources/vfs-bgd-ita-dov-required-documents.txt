                                                    DHAKA

Guidelines for prospective Bangladeshi students wishing to enroll at Italian universities, academic year
2019-2020.
PROSPECTIVE BANGLADESHI STUDENTS WILLING TO ENROLL AT ANY ITALIAN UNIVERSITY ARE REQUIRED
TO SUBMIT CERTIFICATION OF ENGLISH LANGUAGE IELTS ACADEMIC (INTERNATIONAL ENGLISH
LANGUAGE TESTING SYSTEM, HTTP: //WWW.IELTS.ORG) WITH A MINIMUM OVERALL BAND SCORE OF 6.0
REQUEST OF DECLARATIONS OF VALUE (DOV) AND REQUEST OF ENROLLMENT TO ITALIAN UNIVERSITIES
WITHOUT IELTS CERTICATE WITH THE ABOVE MENTIONED MINIMUM SCORE WILL NOT BE CONSIDERED.
THE CRITERIA WILL BE APPLIED TO STUDENTS WILLING TO ENROLL TO ANY:

    A) 3-YEAR UNDERGRADUATE DEGREE (LAUREA DI DURATA TRIENNALE);
    B) 5-YEAR UNDERGRADUATE DEGREE (LAUREA MAGISTRALE A CICLO UNICO DI DURATA

         QUINQUENNALE);
    C) 2-YEAR GRADUATE DEGREE (LAUREA MAGISTRALE DELLA DURATA DI UN BIENNIO SUCCESSIVO AL

         CORSO DI LAUREA);
    D) PHD ( DOTTORATO DI RICERCA);
    E) GRADUATE AND POSTGRADUATE DEGREE.

         DHAKA, 9 APRIL 2019
