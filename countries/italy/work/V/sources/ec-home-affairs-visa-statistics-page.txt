Short-stay visas issued by Schengen countries
28 May 2026

These statistics show the number of visa applications and visas issued for every country that is a member of the 
Schengen area
 – as from 2009.
You can also view this data by the
 country from which the applications were made
 (i.e., by consulate).
The data on visas applied for at the consulates of 
Cyprus
 concern national visas and they are shown on a different sheet in the Excel file.
Ireland
 is not covered, as it maintains an independent visa policy from that of the Schengen area.
