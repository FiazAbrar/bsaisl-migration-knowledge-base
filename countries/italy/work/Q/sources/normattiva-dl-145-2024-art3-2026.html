Art. 3

Suspension of proceedings relating to citizens of countries at particular risk
1. In relation to applications for work permits for workers who are citizens of states and territories characterised by a high risk of applications being submitted
((accompanied by))
counterfeit documentation or in the absence of the legal requirements, Article 22, paragraph 5.01, of the consolidated text of the provisions concerning the regulation of immigration and rules on the status of foreigners, pursuant to Legislative Decree 25 July 1998, n. 286 , does not apply and the work permit can be issued after verification
((,))
by the National Labor Inspectorate
((, of respect))
of the requirements and procedures referred to in Article 24-bis of the same consolidated law.
2. Unless, on the date of entry into force of this decree, an entry visa for Italy has already been issued, the validity of work permits already issued pursuant to Article 22 of the Consolidated Law referred to in Legislative Decree No. 286 of 1998 for the workers referred to in paragraph 1 is suspended until the One-Stop Shop for Immigration expressly confirms the successful completion of the checks required by the same paragraph.
Pending receipt by the consular office of the confirmation referred to in the first sentence,
((which is sent))
exclusively through the specific IT application, the procedures for issuing entry visas to Italy following the authorizations referred to in the first sentence, pending on the date of entry into force of this decree, are suspended.
3. The states and territories referred to in paragraph 1 are identified by decree of the Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation. Until December 31, 2025, pending the adoption of the decree referred to in the first sentence, paragraphs 1 and 2 apply to applications for authorizations and authorizations for workers who are citizens of Bangladesh, Pakistan, and Sri Lanka.
