##### 31 March 2025

# Important Notice for the work visa application

This is to inform all applicants that work visa applications will start on 3rd April 2025, through the new webform system for Italy in Bangladesh.

To streamline the application process, applicants are required to register on the new webform system. Applicants in possession of Nulla Osta issued on or after 22 October 2024 are eligible to submit their applications through this portal

Please note that successful registration on the portal will suspend the expiration of the Nulla Osta.

**Important Information:**

Nulla Osta issued before 22 October 2024 will not be accepted on the web platform. Applicants with Nulla Osta issued before this date can still request an appointment via email at [work.appointment@vfsglobal.com](mailto:work.appointment@vfsglobal.com). duly filled up in all its parts; First Name/Family Name/Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY)/Passport No./ Mobile No./E-mail ID/ Nulla Osta Number.

Nulla Osta issued before 22 October 2024 are subject to review by Italian Immigration Authorities as per Italian Law Decree 145 of 11 October 2024. Applicants who sent an email will be called to apply by VFS once their Nulla Osta has been verified.

Please visit Book an appointment \| vfsglobal page for more details on the guidelines section.
