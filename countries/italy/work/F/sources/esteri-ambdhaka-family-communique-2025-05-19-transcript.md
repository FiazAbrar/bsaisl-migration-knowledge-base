Embassy of Italy Dhaka

High incidence of fraudulent documents in Family Reunion Visa applications: advice for applicants

In processing family reunion visa applications, the Embassy is detecting increasing numbers of fake Bangladeshi and Italian civil status documents.

The public is advised that the Embassy disposes of advanced verification systems, making it impossible to bypass or deceive the necessary controls.

The Embassy urges all applicants to scrupulously verify the authenticity of each and every document within the application they submit. Applicants are reminded that they are responsible for the authenticity and veracity of each submitted document.

They are also reminded that submitting a forged document, or a document containing false information, will not only lead to the rejection of the visa, but will also cause the applicant to be flagged within the common system shared by Schengen Countries, with permanent impact on the possibilities of future legal travel to the European Union.

May 19, 2025

Advice on family reunion visa applications

In response to the query of some applicants, the Embassy of Italy wishes to communicate that its Visa Office is working continuously to process family reunion visa applications.

The processing of current 2025 family files, whenever possible within the stipulated timeframes, is a priority for the Visa Office in accordance with applicable laws.

At the same time, the Embassy is working to clear the 2024 backlog by implementing chronological and thematic priority criteria, including the processing of demonstrable urgent cases, such as those involving minors or humanitarian situations. Some applications are also processed with priority because of judicial decisions and legal considerations.

The Embassy of Italy calls on applicants to be patient and wait for updates on their visa process from VFS Global.

13 May 2025
