# Family Reunion: Italian Embassy urges visa applicants to be patient

- May 19, 2025, 03:07 PM
- by [UNB NEWS](/author/17)

&nbsp;

- UNB News
- Publish- May 19, 2025, 03:07 PM
-  [UNB NEWS](/author/17)

\

\

The Italian Embassy in Dhaka has said the processing of current 2025 family files, whenever possible within the stipulated timeframes, is a priority for the Visa Office in accordance with applicable laws.

In response to the query of some applicants, the Embassy of Italy said its Visa Office is working continuously to process family reunion visa applications.

[Bangladesh, Italy for making arrangements to prevent transnational crimes](https://unb.com.bd/category/Bangladesh/bangladesh-italy-for-making-arrangements-to-prevent-transnational-crimes/159074)

At the same time, the Embassy said, it is working to clear the 2024 backlog by implementing chronological and thematic priority criteria, including the processing of demonstrable urgent cases, such as those involving minors or humanitarian situations.

Some applications are also processed with priority because of judicial decisions and legal considerations.

The Embassy of Italy called on applicants to be patient and wait for updates on their visa process from VFS Global.
