Nationality of arrivals

In August, Bangladeshis accounted for 21 per cent of all sea arrivals (down from 33% in July), followed by Egyptians (17%, up
from 15% in July), and Eritreans and Sudanese (12% each, compared to 11% and 6% respectively in July). Other nationalities
arriving in significant numbers included Tunisians (7%), Guineans, Iranians, and Somalis (4% each), Algerians (3%), and
Ethiopians, Pakistanis, and Malians (2% each). In August, the main nationalities of those departing from Libya were Bangladeshi
(26%, down from 37% in July), Egyptian (21%, up from 17% in July), and Eritrean (15%, up from 12% in July). From Tunisia, the
main nationalities were Tunisian (45%, up from 26% in July), Guinean (25%, unchanged from July), and Burkinabe (6%, up from
2% in July). Among those departing from Türkiye in August, the main nationalities were Afghan (44%, up from 34% in July),
Iranian (41%, down from 47% in July), and Iraqi (13%, down from 17% in July). Almost all persons departing from Algeria in
August were Algerian nationals (97%, same as in July), with a smaller proportion (7%) of unknown nationalities.

Country of embarkation

In the first eight months of 2025, 88 per cent of people reaching Italy via sea departed from Libya (37,582 persons in 705
landings), followed by 8 per cent from Tunisia (3,287 persons in 135 landings), 2 per cent from Türkiye (1,011 persons in
13 landings), and 2 per cent from Algeria (813 people in 76 landings). In August, Libya was the main country of departure
(80% of all people landed in Italy), followed by Tunisia (15%), Algeria and Türkiye (3% each).
