Notice to work visa applicants
In response to some queries on how to get an appointment for work visa applications, the Embassy wishes to recall the following.
- Those who are in possession of a work "Nulla Osta" issued in 2025 or re-confirmed in 2025 must register in the dedicated VFS Global web portal at the following link: https://vfseu.mioot.com/forms/ITABDE/ .
It is necessary to upload copy of the 2025 Nulla Osta, that can be provided by the employer in Italy.
The Embassy will verify the validity of Nulla Osta and provide an appointment in the next few days after the registration.
- Those who are in possession of a Nulla Osta issued in 2023-2024 and submitted a visa application already, must wait until a review of their Nulla Osta is completed by the Immigration Office in Italy. Once the review is done, they will be contacted by VFS Global.
05 December 2025
