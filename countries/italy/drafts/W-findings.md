## Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal)

The Decreto Flussi ("flows decree") is Italy's yearly quota of foreign workers; an employer in Italy applies online for a named worker on a fixed "click day", the Sportello Unico per l'Immigrazione (SUI, the immigration desk at each province's Prefettura) issues a nulla osta (work authorisation), and only then can the worker apply for a visa in Dhaka [W0030, W0031, W0011, W0036, W0040]. Bangladesh is one of the countries with a cooperation agreement that share a reserved quota of 25,000 non-seasonal workers a year in 2026, 2027 and 2028, inside a national non-seasonal quota of 76,200 [W0023, W0024, W0022]. About 10,000 Bangladeshis got Italian work visas in 2025, and the Embassy of Italy in Dhaka expects a similar number in 2026 [W0091].

### Step 2: The job offer and contract

1. **Get a named offer from the employer, not a "visa".** The employer's application to the SUI names you and must include [W0031, W0032, W0068]:

   - a proposed contratto di soggiorno (contract of stay: the employer's guarantee of housing and promise to pay your journey home) with the job's conditions [W0031, W0067, W0068];
   - documents on where you will live, signed digitally; a hotel, or a fixed dormitory on a building site with the employer's own declaration, is accepted [W0031];
   - an asseverazione: a certificate from a labour consultant or an employer organisation that the employer follows the national collective contract (CCNL) and can afford the number of workers it asks for, judged on its assets, turnover and staff [W0051, W0052].

   The asseverazione checks that the employer follows the CCNL for the sector, which sets your pay and hours (see Your rights at work) [W0051].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Do Italian employers send the worker the proposed contratto di soggiorno before click day, or does the worker first see it after arrival?
   > - What job titles and monthly pay appear on the offers Bangladeshis actually receive in 2026 (construction, food industry, logistics, hotels)?

2. **Give the employer exactly what is in your passport.** The visa is delayed if the name, surname, sex or date of birth on the nulla osta do not match your passport, so send a clear scan of the passport data page and check the spelling the employer types [W0113].

   **Ready-to-use message to the employer:**
   "Dear [employer name], please send me (1) a copy of the nulla osta with its number and the SUI (Prefettura) that issued it, and (2) every email you receive from the Sportello Unico about my application. My name must be exactly as in my passport: [SURNAME], [Given names], born [DD/MM/YYYY], passport [number]. Thank you, [your name], [WhatsApp number]."
   The employer is required by law to tell you promptly about every communication on your nulla osta [W0018].

3. **Know who may file.** A private employer may file no more than three nulla osta requests a year; larger numbers must go through an employer organisation, a labour consultant or similar professional, or a temporary-work agency, who must keep requests in line with the firm's turnover and staff [W0034]. An employer cannot apply if it got a nulla osta in the last three years and then did not sign the contratto di soggiorno, unless that was not its fault [W0035]. The nulla osta is refused if the employer was convicted in the last five years of people smuggling, caporalato (illegal gangmaster labour) or employing undocumented workers [W0050].

   **Scams:** a nulla osta obtained with forged or fraudulent documents is refused or revoked, and the visa, contract and permit fall with it [W0038]. Forging a visa, permit or contratto di soggiorno, or using one, carries 1 to 6 years' prison in Italy [W0065]. What people pay for a nulla osta and who sells it: see How a job reaches you, and who takes a cut.

### Step 3: The employer gets your nulla osta

The employer (or its labour consultant or employer organisation) does every step below on the Interior Ministry's ALI portal; you wait in Bangladesh [W0016, W0030]. The dates are for the 2027 round, the next one [W0005, W0011].

1. **The employer checks the Italian labour market first.** It asks the Centro per l'Impiego (public job centre) whether a worker already in Italy is available; if the centre does not answer within 8 days, the check counts as negative and the employer may hire from abroad [W0029, W0033].

2. **The employer pre-fills the application** on the Portale Servizi ALI ([portaleservizi.dlci.interno.it](https://portaleservizi.dlci.interno.it/AliSportello/ali/home.htm)) from 9:00 on 23 October 2026 to 20:00 on 7 December 2026, using form B2020 for non-seasonal work [W0005, W0006, W0015, W0016]. From 9 to 13 December 2026 the portal reopens only to complete and save forms already started; no new applications can be added [W0007, W0008]. The labour inspectorate (Ispettorato Nazionale del Lavoro) can also run random checks on employers with the Agenzia delle Entrate (tax agency) [W0053].

3. **The employer clicks "send" on click day.** For 2027 [W0009, W0010, W0011, W0012, W0013]:

   - non-seasonal work (form B2020): 16 February 2027, from 9:00;
   - domestic and care work (form A-bis): 18 February 2027 (see Work visa: domestic and care worker);
   - seasonal agriculture: 12 January 2027; seasonal tourism: 9 February 2027 (see Work visa: seasonal worker).

   Applications are handled mostly in order of arrival, so the first minutes count [W0142]. They can be sent until 31 December 2027 while quota remains [W0014]. The 2027 national figures are 76,200 non-seasonal places (14,000 of them for domestic and care work), 89,000 seasonal and 650 self-employed, 165,850 in total [W0001, W0002, W0003, W0004].

4. **The quota is shared out, then the SUI decides.** The Ministry of Labour splits the quota by province and sector within 10 days of click day [W0028, W0140]. From that allocation the SUI has at most 60 days to issue the nulla osta, after the Questura (provincial police immigration office) has checked the employer and you [W0036, W0140]. Applications that have not entered the quota six months after 31 December of the year lapse and are archived [W0027]. A nulla osta is valid for up to six months from issue [W0037].

   > - How long after click day did Bangladeshis' 2026 nulla osta actually arrive?

5. **Latest status for Bangladeshis.** In October 2024 Italy suspended every nulla osta already issued to Bangladeshis (and Pakistanis and Sri Lankans) that had no visa yet, because of a high number of false documents, and made new ones depend on prior checks by the labour inspectorate [W0088, W0054, W0055]. That special regime was set until 31 December 2025, pending a Foreign Ministry decree naming the high-risk countries [W0054, W0056]. Old suspended nulla osta were eventually rejected in large numbers, while new ones are, in the Ambassador's words in September 2026, processed in a relatively short time with very few refusals [W0094, W0092, W0093].

   > **Need to know**

### Step 5: The visa at the Italian Embassy in Dhaka, and checking it is real

All Italian visa applications in Bangladesh go through VFS Global, the Embassy's visa application centre, never to the Embassy directly [W0099]. VFS centres take applications Sunday to Thursday, 9:00 to 12:00 [W0103]:

- Dhaka: Nafi Tower (4th and 5th floor), 53 South Gulshan Avenue, Gulshan 1 [W0100];
- Chattogram: World Trade Center, 102/103 Agrabad CA, Commerce College Road [W0101];
- Sylhet: Nirvana Inn Complex (4th floor), Ramer Dighir Par, Mirzajungle Road [W0102];
- help: info.itbd@vfshelpline.com, +880 9606777666 [W0099].

1. **Register on VFS's work-visa webform** as soon as you have the nulla osta [W0126].

   1. Open [vfseu.mioot.com/forms/ITABDE](https://vfseu.mioot.com/forms/ITABDE/), enter your email and the captcha, and press "Get One Time Password" [W0129].
   2. Enter the code from your email and fill in the form; the same login later shows the status of your application [W0129].
   3. Registering stops the nulla osta from expiring while you wait for an appointment [W0127].

   A nulla osta issued before 22 October 2024 cannot go on the webform; its holder emails work.appointment@vfsglobal.com with first name, family name, date of birth (DD/MM/YYYY), passport number, mobile, email and nulla osta number, and waits to be called [W0128]. A nulla osta under code L/N (Article 27, outside the quota) needs no booking: take a printed copy and a copy of the passport data page to a VFS centre, the Embassy checks it, and you get an email with your submission date [W0130, W0131].

2. **Submit the application at VFS.** You'll need [W0110, W0111, W0112, W0113, W0114, W0117]:

   - the original national ("D") visa form, filled in in English or Italian and signed;
   - one photo, 3.5 x 4.0 cm, colour, white background, taken in the last 6 months;
   - your passport, valid at least 3 months beyond the visa's end, with two blank visa pages, and a copy of the data page;
   - a copy of the nulla osta (check name, surname, sex and date of birth);
   - documents or certificates for the job sector you will work in (experience letters, training certificates);
   - anything else the Embassy asks for.

   You may file only one visa application at a time, even with two passports [W0116]. The visa fee is EUR 116, paid in cash in taka [W0107, W0108].

   > - Must experience letters be apostilled for a Decreto Flussi visa, or only for the Blue Card (see Step 4)?

3. **The employer must confirm within 15 days.** When the Embassy finishes its checks, the SUI emails the employer by PEC (certified email) asking it to confirm it still wants to hire you; if it does not confirm within 15 days the request counts as refused and the nulla osta is revoked [W0039, W0115, W0139, W0017]. Only after this confirmation does the Embassy issue the visa [W0040]. Tell the employer to watch its PEC inbox once you have applied [W0018].

4. **Waiting time.** The Ambassador said in September 2026 that new work authorisations are processed quickly and few are refused [W0092, W0093].

   > **Need to know**
   > - How many working days from VFS submission to visa in 2026 for a Decreto Flussi visa?

**Check it is real:**

1. **Your details.** The nulla osta must carry your exact name, sex and date of birth, the issuing SUI and a number [W0113].
2. **Ask the employer for the SUI's emails.** Verification of a nulla osta is done in Italy, and the Embassy tells holders to ask their employer for updates [W0090, W0018].
3. **Ask the Embassy in writing** at visti.dhaka@esteri.it, attaching the nulla osta [W0106].
4. **The webform status** on vfseu.mioot.com shows your application's progress [W0129].

**Scams:** forging documents, signatures and seals is a crime, and the Embassy says so on its visa page [W0105]. In August 2025 the Embassy said it had received hundreds of applications with an "L/N" (Blue Card) nulla osta, that very few succeeded because the applicants were not highly qualified, and warned against brokers selling an "L/N code carta blu" nulla osta [W0146, W0147]. A valid L/N nulla osta does not guarantee a visa (see Work visa: highly skilled and out-of-quota (EU Blue Card, Article 27)) [W0147, W0119].

### Step 7: After arrival

1. **Sign the contratto di soggiorno within 15 days of entering Italy.** You and the employer sign it, digitally or you by hand, and the employer uploads it to the SUI on the ALI portal within the same 15 days [W0041, W0042, W0141, W0019]. You may work on the nulla osta while it is being signed [W0043]. If it is not sent in time the nulla osta is revoked, unless the delay was not your fault [W0038].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Do SUIs still call workers in person after arrival (for the integration agreement or fingerprints), or is everything now done online by the employer?

2. **Apply for the permesso di soggiorno (residence permit) within 8 working days of entry,** through the "kit" (application envelope) sold at post offices [W0060, W0133]. Put in the kit [W0133, W0138]:

   - the signed contratto di soggiorno;
   - a copy of your codice fiscale (tax code);
   - a declaration of hospitality and rental contract, with a copy of the host's ID;
   - a copy of every passport page with your data, visas and stamps;
   - a copy of the UNILAV (the employer's hiring notice to the job centre).

   Pay a EUR 16 revenue stamp (marca da bollo) and a postal slip of EUR 70.46 for a permit up to 1 year or EUR 80.46 for over 1 and up to 2 years [W0134, W0135, W0136]. Keep the post-office receipt: with it you are lawfully present and may work while you wait [W0143]. The permit should be issued within 90 days [W0063, W0064].

3. **Codice fiscale.** The SUI can send your documents, including the codice fiscale, to the consulate before you travel [W0044].

4. **Residence registration, health service, bank account and SIM.** For a bank account see Sending money home.

### Your residence permit

#### How long you can stay and renewing

The work permit lasts as long as the contratto di soggiorno, up to 1 year for a fixed-term contract and 2 years for a permanent one; each renewal can last up to 3 years [W0057, W0058, W0059]. Ask the Questura to renew at least 90 days before it expires, through the same post-office kit; you are fingerprinted again [W0061, W0133, W0062].

#### If you lose your job

Losing the job does not cancel the permit: register as available for work at the job centre and you may stay for the rest of the permit and at least one year [W0045, W0046]. For unemployment benefit see If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job.

#### The long-term EU permit

After 5 years with a valid permit, a worker earning at least the yearly assegno sociale (the state's minimum social allowance) can ask the Questura for the EU long-term residence permit [W0069, W0070]. It needs an Italian language test, a housing-suitability certificate and proof of income; the postal slip is EUR 130.46 [W0072, W0137]. It is issued within 90 days, valid 10 years and renewed automatically [W0071]. For citizenship after 10 years see Citizenship.

## Work visa: highly skilled and out-of-quota (EU Blue Card, Article 27)

Article 27 of the Testo Unico Immigrazione (TUI, Italy's immigration law) lets some workers enter outside the yearly quota: managers and highly specialised staff, university teachers, translators, seafarers, posted workers, artists, athletes, journalists, digital nomads and nurses hired by public or private health facilities [W0074, W0075]. The EU Blue Card is the main one for graduates; the Embassy and VFS call these nulla osta "L/N" [W0076, W0119, W0130].

**Who qualifies for the Blue Card** (one of these) [W0077, W0078, W0079]:

- a degree from a course of at least three years;
- at least five years' experience at a comparable level in the job's field;
- for ICT managers and specialists, three years' experience in the last seven.

The employer needs a contract or binding offer of at least six months, at pay no lower than the CCNL and the ISTAT (national statistics office) average gross annual pay [W0080, W0081]. The SUI decides within 30 days [W0082].

1. **The employer applies to the SUI;** the rest follows Step 3 to Step 7 of the Decreto Flussi route (see Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal)) [W0076, W0082].
2. **Take the L/N nulla osta to VFS on a walk-in basis** (Step 5, item 1) [W0130].
3. **Submit** [W0110, W0149, W0113, W0118]:

   - the national ("D") visa form and photos;
   - a passport valid at least 15 months from the application date, with two blank pages;
   - the SUI's notice of the nulla osta to the employer;
   - your degree with a dichiarazione di valore (statement of value) from the Italian Embassy or a CIMEA certificate, or contracts or payslips proving five years in the sector, translated and apostilled (one apostille per document).

4. **First 12 months:** you may work only in the job you were admitted for, and a change of employer needs prior approval from the labour office [W0083]. You may bring family whatever the length of your permit (see Family reunification) [W0084].

**The Article 23 route (training in Bangladesh first).** Workers who complete vocational and civic-language training in Bangladesh under programmes approved by Italian ministries can enter with a nulla osta issued within 30 days, and may request the visa up to 12 months after the course (until the end of 2027) [W0120, W0144]. Several hundred Bangladeshis a year now go through such pre-departure training projects [W0096].

**Scams:** see Step 5, "Scams" (the "L/N code carta blu" nulla osta) [W0146, W0147].

## Self-employment visa

The quota for self-employed workers is 650 a year for all countries [W0004]. You must show adequate resources, the qualifications Italian law requires for the activity, a certificate from the competent Italian authority no older than three months, suitable housing and lawful yearly income above the health-charge exemption level [W0085, W0086]. The Embassy decides within 120 days and the visa must be used within 180 days [W0087]. The permit lasts up to 2 years; each renewal up to 3 [W0066].

For a consultant or officer of an existing Italian company, the Embassy asks for [W0121, W0122, W0123, W0124, W0125]:

- a nulla osta from the Questura issued within the 90 days before you apply;
- proof the company has been registered for at least three years;
- the company's guarantee of pay of at least EUR 8,500 a year;
- 6 months of personal bank statements from a bank in Bangladesh;
- if married, the nikahnama translated into English and apostilled.
