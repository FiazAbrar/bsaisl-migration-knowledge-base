## Work visa: seasonal worker

A seasonal permit lets you work in Italy on farms or in tourism and hotels for at most nine months in any twelve, then go home [D0034, D0035, D0039]. Many in Bangladesh call it the কৃষি ভিসা (agriculture visa) [N0149]. Bangladesh is on the list of countries whose citizens can take these jobs, and you can't apply yourself: an employer in Italy, or its trade association, files a named request for you [D0004, D0005, D0124].

For Bangladeshis the nulla osta (the work authorisation issued by the SUI, the Sportello Unico per l'Immigrazione, the immigration desk at each province's Prefettura, the state's office in the province) is never issued by silent consent: the Questura (provincial police headquarters) must approve it, and the INL (Ispettorato Nazionale del Lavoro, the national labour inspectorate) first checks the employer, together with AGEA (the state agency for farm payments) for farm jobs [D0024, D0025, D0026].

### How the seasonal quota works

Italy allows 88,000 seasonal entries in 2026, 89,000 in 2027 and 90,000 in 2028, for all countries together [D0001, P0003, D0003]. Most places are reserved for applications filed through employers' organisations, so who files decides whether you can be brought in [D0006, D0009, D0010]:

- **Farm jobs through farmers' organisations:** 47,000 places a year, for applications filed by Coldiretti (the national confederation of direct farmers), CIA (the Italian farmers' confederation), Confagricoltura (the general confederation of Italian agriculture) and the farm cooperative federations [D0006, D0007, D0008].
- **Tourism jobs through tourism employers' organisations:** 13,000 places in 2026 and 14,000 in 2027 [D0009].
- **Returning seasonal workers:** 5,000 places in 2026 for workers who have done seasonal work in Italy at least once in the last five years and whose employer asks for a multi-year nulla osta [D0010].

A private employer filing alone may send at most three nulla osta requests a year; the limit does not apply to employers' organisations, labour consultants and staffing agencies [D0023, P0038, P0039].

**Dates for the 2027 round:** employers pre-fill applications on the ALI portal (the Interior Ministry's website for nulla osta requests) from 9:00 on 23 October 2026 to 20:00 on 7 December 2026 [P0007, P0008, P0036]. The click day (the moment the online quota opens) is 12 January 2027 from 9:00 for farm jobs (form C-Stag agricolo) and 9 February 2027 for tourism jobs (form C-Stag turistico); applications are handled mostly in order of arrival [P0016, P0017, P0020, P0011, W0142]. Applications stay open until 31 December 2027 while places remain [P0021]. A 2026 application that had not won a place by 30 June 2027, or a 2026 nulla osta not followed by a visa by then, lapses [D0028].

### Step 1: Find a real seasonal employer

1. **Know who can hire you.** Only an employer in Italy, or its trade association on its behalf, can ask for you by name [D0124]. The nulla osta is refused or revoked if the employer has been sanctioned for undeclared work or has no real business [D0046]. An employer that did not sign the contratto di soggiorno (the contract of stay signed after you arrive) for workers it asked for in the last three years cannot apply, nor can one sent to trial for slavery, trafficking or caporalato (illegal gangmaster labour) [D0029, D0030]. How to check an employer: see Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Step 1.

   **Scams:** in Bangladesh, agencies sell an "employer" and a nulla osta: one man paid over EUR 7,000 (about Tk 9.8 lakh) for an employer contact and expected to pay as much again when the visa came [D0090, G0001]. Networks have filed applications in the names of employers who did not know their names were used [D0098, D0100]. One, broken up in Grosseto in September 2025, brought in nearly 200 Bangladeshis with fake employers, charging EUR 2,000-4,000 (about Tk 2.8-5.6 lakh) per application, collected in Bangladesh and Italy; some families mortgaged land to pay [D0098, D0099, D0128, G0001]. Around Latina in 2014, about 40 farm employers filed false hiring requests for money, and migrants paid the network EUR 6,000-8,000 (about Tk 8.4-11.2 lakh) each [D0104, D0105, G0001]. A worker who arrives for a company that does not exist falls into irregular status [D0091]. More: Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Scams.

2. **If you have worked a season in Italy before, ask the same employer to call you back.** A seasonal worker admitted at least once in the last five years, who kept to the permit's conditions and went home when it expired, has priority over first-timers, with the same or another employer [D0031]. The employer can ask for a three-year nulla osta for you (see Staying on: another season or a non-seasonal permit) [D0010, D0041].

### Step 2: The employer applies on click day

The employer does this step; you can only check it was done.

1. **The employer pre-fills and sends form C-Stag on the ALI portal,** C-Stag agricolo for farm jobs and C-Stag turistico for tourism, from 9:00 on click day, after pre-filling it [P0011, P0016, P0017, P0020, P0036]. Seasonal jobs skip the check at the Centro per l'Impiego (public job centre) that non-seasonal and domestic jobs need [D0017]. The portal and the employer's documents: see Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Step 3.
2. **The SUI issues the nulla osta** within 20 days of the application winning a quota place [D0027]. For a Bangladeshi it first waits for the Questura's approval and the INL's check of the employer [D0024, D0025, D0026].

**Check it is real:** as for any nulla osta (see Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Step 5).

### Step 3: Visa, documents and the flight

You apply at VFS Global, the company that takes all Italian visa applications in Bangladesh, and give fingerprints, as every work visa applicant must [W0099, M0021]. The fees, documents and Bangladesh-side steps are the same as for the non-seasonal worker (see Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Step 4; Step 5; Step 6).

### Step 4: After arrival

1. **Start work, and sign the contratto di soggiorno.** You can start work as soon as you arrive with the nulla osta and visa, before the contract is signed [D0032]. Signing, the permesso di soggiorno (residence permit) and the codice fiscale (tax code): see Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Step 7. In practice, workers have waited up to a year for a Prefettura appointment to sign, and employers often disappear [D0095]. One Bangladeshi farm worker who arrived in October 2022 was paid and housed, never got the appointment, and three years later had his nulla osta revoked [D0096]. Another, who entered legally in 2023, was refused a permit by the Latina Questura, ordered expelled and held at Ponte Galeria in Rome, a CPR (Centro di permanenza per il rimpatrio, a detention centre for people being deported) [D0097]. Bangladeshis who entered on the Decreto Flussi and found no job, because the employer could not be found or was a scam, have formed a committee in Rome, Tikase [D0094].

2. **Find your SIISL account.** Once the contract is signed, INPS (Istituto Nazionale della Previdenza Sociale, the national social security institute) enrols you in SIISL (the Information System for Social and Labour Inclusion, the government's job-matching platform) [D0038]. Through SIISL you can take a seasonal job with another employer while your nulla osta is valid, without going home for a new visa [D0037].

### Your rights as a seasonal worker

Farm and hotel pay follow their own national collective contracts (CCNL, contratto collettivo nazionale di lavoro) [L0030, L0040] (see Your rights at work). What is different for seasonal workers:

- **Hotel pay:** seasonal hotel workers get the same minimums as permanent staff; their 13th and 14th month's pay and unused leave build up month by month and are paid at the end with the TFR (trattamento di fine rapporto, end-of-service pay) [L0044, L0045].
- **Housing:** if the employer gives you a place to live, the rent can't be more than one third of your pay and can't be taken automatically from your wages [D0043]. An employer who gives unfit housing, overcharges or deducts rent from pay is fined EUR 350-5,500 (about Tk 49,000-7.7 lakh) per worker [D0044, G0001].
- **If the nulla osta is revoked through the employer's fault:** the employer must pay you an indemnity based on the CCNL wages it owes you [D0045].
- **If you lose the job:** the rule that lets a worker who loses a job stay at least a year to look for another does not apply on a seasonal permit [L0013]; NASpI (unemployment pay) is not paid on a seasonal permit [L0064].
- **Pension after you go home:** the rule that keeps a worker's Italian pension rights after going home for good, without a social security agreement, does not cover seasonal workers [L0002, L0011].

### Staying on: another season or a non-seasonal permit

1. **Take a second seasonal job.** Within the nine months, your permit can be extended for a new seasonal job with the same or another employer, if it starts within 60 days of the end of the last contract [D0036, D0035].
2. **Come back on a multi-year nulla osta.** With a three-year seasonal nulla osta, the Embassy issues the visa for the second and third years when you show the employer's contract proposal [D0041]. In those later years a different employer can make the request [D0042].
3. **Convert to a non-seasonal permit.** After at least three months of regular work, if an employer offers you a fixed-term or permanent contract, you can ask the SUI to convert your seasonal permit into a work permit [D0040, W0073] (see Changing visa type).
4. **Otherwise, go home** at the end of the nine months, unless you hold a permit for another reason [D0039]. Going home on time keeps your priority for the next season [D0031]. Why Italy once closed seasonal work to Bangladeshis: see What closed it before.

### Caporalato: illegal gangmasters

Caporalato is when a caporale (gangmaster) recruits workers to work for others under exploitative conditions [D0053]. Recent cases were around Latina (the Agro Pontino), near Naples and in Calabria [D0106, D0109, D0112].

#### What it looks like

- **Near Naples (Aversa):** prosecutors found farm workers paid about EUR 2.70 (about Tk 380) an hour for 10-14 hours a day, carried packed into the cargo space of vans [D0106, D0107, G0001].
- **Calabria (Sibari plain), September 2026:** 18 Pakistanis, one Bangladeshi and one Indian were arrested in a caporalato inquiry [D0109]. Farms allegedly paid EUR 40 (about Tk 5,600) cash for ten hours, handed to the gangmasters, who kept EUR 5 (about Tk 700) a day for transport and EUR 60 (about Tk 8,400) a month for rent [D0110, D0111, G0001].
- **Latina:** Satnam Singh, an Indian worker employed off the books on a farm at Borgo Santa Maria, lost an arm to a machine on 19 June 2024 [D0112]. On 8 July 2026 the Latina court sentenced the farm's owner to 16 years at first instance [D0113].
- **Fake hiring:** in June 2026, 30 people were arrested across Italy for false Decreto Flussi applications filed on the ALI portal through a CAF (centro di assistenza fiscale, a tax help office) in Taranto, mostly for people from Pakistan, Bangladesh and India, who paid up to EUR 6,500 (about Tk 9.1 lakh) for a nulla osta and visa [D0129, D0101, D0127, D0102, G0001].

#### The law

Law 199/2016 punishes both the recruiter and the employer who uses exploited workers with 1-6 years in prison and a fine of EUR 500-1,000 (about Tk 70,000-1.4 lakh) per worker, or 5-8 years with violence or threats [D0053, D0054, D0055, G0001]. Signs of exploitation include pay repeatedly far below the national contracts, and degrading working conditions, surveillance or housing [D0056, D0057].

#### If you are exploited

Act in this order:

1. **Call the anti-trafficking helpline, 800 290 290:** free, anonymous and open 24 hours a day, every day, for victims of trafficking or exploitation and anyone reporting it [D0114]. Its interpreters cover English, Arabic and ten other languages, not Bengali [D0115].
2. **Report it to the INL or the police** (see If you aren't paid). The INL can pass the case to the prosecutor or the Questore (the head of the Questura) and give its opinion on a permit for you [D0049].
3. **Ask for the special permit for victims.** If police or prosecutors find violence, abuse or exploitation of a foreign worker who helps bring out the facts and identify those responsible, the Questore issues an Article 18-ter permit at once, on the prosecutor's proposal [D0047, D0048]. It is marked "casi speciali" (special cases), lasts one year, can be renewed and lets you work, employed or self-employed [D0125, D0050, D0126]. While you wait, the receipt for your application lets you stay and work [D0052]. When it expires it can be converted into a work permit outside the quotas [D0051].

## Work visa: domestic and care worker

This visa lets a family in Italy hire you as a colf (house worker) or badante (carer) [D0011, D0012]. Italy allows 13,600 domestic and care workers in 2026, 14,000 in 2027 and 14,200 in 2028, inside the non-seasonal quota and from the same list of countries, which includes Bangladesh [D0011, P0002, D0004]. This quota is only for jobs in a household; care jobs in clinics or care homes use the general non-seasonal quota (see Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal)) [D0012]. Separately, up to 10,000 carers a year can enter outside the quota until 2028 (see The out-of-quota care route) [D0058, P0043]. As for seasonal work, a Bangladeshi worker's nulla osta always needs the Questura's approval and the INL's check of the employer [D0024, D0025, D0026].

### Step 1: Find a real family employer

1. **Check the family can afford you.** An employer living alone needs taxable income of at least EUR 20,000 a year (about Tk 27.9 lakh), or EUR 27,000 (about Tk 37.7 lakh) if the household has more members [D0021, G0001]. An employer who is ill or disabled and hires you for their own care needs no minimum income [D0022].

   **Scams:** a domestic job "offer" with no family behind it ends the same way as a fake farm job (see Work visa: seasonal worker, Step 1, item 1).

### Step 2: The family applies on click day

The family does this step; you can only check it was done.

1. **The family asks the job centre first.** It must ask the Centro per l'Impiego whether a worker already in Italy can fill the job; if the centre offers no one within 8 days, it can go ahead [D0019, D0018].
2. **The family gets the job certified.** It needs an asseverazione (a labour consultant's or employers' organisation's certificate that the job meets the rules), unless the employer is not self-sufficient [D0019].
3. **The family files form A-bis on the ALI portal** on click day: 18 February 2027 from 9:00 for the 2027 round [P0011, P0019, P0020]. The pay stated must follow the domestic-work CCNL and be no less than the assegno sociale (the state's minimum social allowance: EUR 7,002.97, about Tk 9.8 lakh, a year in 2025) [D0020, G0001]. ### Your rights as a domestic worker

The domestic-work CCNL (CCNL lavoro domestico) was renewed on 28 October 2025 [D0074].

#### Pay

Minimum pay from 1 January 2026 [D0065]:

- **Live-in, level B** (general home helper): EUR 983.16 (about Tk 1.37 lakh) a month [D0066, G0001].
- **Live-in, level BS** (carer of a person who can look after themselves, or babysitter): EUR 1,053.39 (about Tk 1.47 lakh) a month [D0067, D0075, G0001].
- **Live-in, level CS** (untrained carer of a person who can't look after themselves): EUR 1,193.84 (about Tk 1.67 lakh) a month [D0068, D0076, G0001].
- **Live-in, level DS** (trained carer of a person who can't look after themselves): EUR 1,474.73 (about Tk 2.06 lakh) a month plus a EUR 207.69 (about Tk 29,000) allowance [D0069, D0070, G0001].
- **Not live-in:** EUR 7.45 (about Tk 1,040) an hour at level BS and EUR 8.30 (about Tk 1,160) at level CS [D0071, D0072, G0001].

Food and lodging are valued at EUR 6.66 (about Tk 930) a day [D0073, G0001]. A 13th month's pay (tredicesima) is due in December [D0083].

#### Hours, rest and leave

- **Hours:** live-in, at most 10 non-consecutive hours a day and 54 a week; not live-in, at most 8 a day and 40 a week [D0077, D0116].
- **Rest:** live-in workers get at least 11 consecutive hours' rest a day and 36 hours a week, 24 of them on Sunday [D0079, D0078].
- **Overtime:** +25% between 6:00 and 22:00, +50% at night, +60% on Sundays and public holidays [D0080, D0081, D0117].
- **Leave:** 26 working days a year [D0082].
- **Lodging and food:** a live-in worker must get lodging that protects dignity and privacy, and healthy, sufficient food [D0087].
- **Sickness:** your job is kept for 10 days with up to six months' service, 45 days up to two years, and 180 days after that [D0120, D0085, D0121].

#### Probation and ending the job

- **Probation:** live-in workers have 30 working days' paid probation [D0084].
- **Notice:** for 25 hours a week or more, 15 days up to five years' service and 30 days after; half that if you resign [D0118, D0119, D0086].

For unpaid wages see If you aren't paid.

### The out-of-quota care route

Each year from 2025 to 2028, up to 10,000 workers can enter outside the quota to care for people with disabilities, very old people or children up to six [D0058, D0059, D0060, P0043]. The family can't apply on its own: it applies through a registered employment agency (APL, agenzia per il lavoro) or an employers' association that signed the domestic-work CCNL [D0061]. It can't hire its own spouse or relatives up to the third degree this way [D0062]. For the first 12 months you may only do that care work, and changing employer needs the local labour inspectorate's prior approval [D0063, D0064].

## Work visa: highly skilled and out-of-quota (EU Blue Card, Article 27)

Article 27 of the TUI (Testo Unico Immigrazione, Italy's immigration law) lets some workers enter outside the yearly quota: managers and highly specialised staff, university teachers, translators, seafarers, posted workers, artists, athletes, journalists, digital nomads and nurses hired by health facilities [W0074, W0075]. The EU Blue Card, for highly qualified jobs, is the main one; the Embassy and VFS Global call its nulla osta code "L/N" [W0076, M0013, W0130].

### Who qualifies for the Blue Card

You need one of these:

- a degree from a course of at least three years [W0077];
- at least five years' experience at a comparable level in the job's field [W0078];
- for ICT (information and communications technology) managers and specialists, three years' experience in the last seven [W0079].

The employer needs a contract or binding offer of at least six months, at pay no lower than the CCNL and the average gross annual pay published by ISTAT (Italy's national statistics office) [W0080, W0081]. The visa is strictly for highly qualified workers recruited for highly qualified jobs [W0119].

### Step 1: The employer gets your L/N nulla osta

1. **The employer applies to the SUI,** which issues or refuses the nulla osta within 30 days [W0082]. The employer's declarations are checked for truthfulness [P0035].
2. **Take the L/N nulla osta to a VFS centre, walk-in.** No webform booking is needed: bring a printed copy of the nulla osta and a copy of your passport data page; the Embassy checks the nulla osta and emails you a date to submit the application [W0130, W0131]. VFS addresses and hours: see Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Step 5.

**Scams:** the Embassy has warned against brokers selling a "Nulla Osta L/N code - carta blu" [M0015]. Very few Bangladeshi L/N applicants have succeeded, most because they were not highly qualified, and a valid L/N nulla osta does not guarantee a visa [M0014, M0013].

### Step 2: The visa at VFS

1. **Submit the application at VFS** on the date in the Embassy's email [W0131]. You'll need:

   - the original national ("D") visa form, filled in in English or Italian and signed [W0110];
   - a passport valid at least 15 months from the application date, with two blank pages [W0149];
   - a copy of the nulla osta, with your name, surname, sex and date of birth checked [W0113];
   - your degree with a dichiarazione di valore (the Italian Embassy's statement of the degree's value) or a CIMEA certificate (from Italy's centre for recognising foreign qualifications), or contracts or payslips proving five years in the sector, translated and apostilled [W0118].

   Fees, photos and fingerprints are as in Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Step 5.
2. **Follow the after-arrival steps** in Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Step 7. A permit for managers and specialised workers under Article 27 carries a EUR 100 (about Tk 14,000) contribution [K0049, G0001]. For the first 12 months, changing employer needs the labour office's prior approval [W0083]. You may bring your family whatever the length of your permit (see Family reunification) [W0084].

### The Article 23 route: training in Bangladesh first

Workers who complete vocational and civic-language training in Bangladesh, in programmes approved by Italian ministries, can get a work visa on that basis [W0120]. The nulla osta deadline for them is 30 days, and they may ask for the visa up to 12 months after the course, until the end of 2027 [W0144]. Several hundred Bangladeshis a year now go legally through these pre-departure training projects [W0096].

## Self-employment visa

Italy admits 650 self-employed workers in 2027, from all countries together [P0004]. You must show enough money, the qualifications Italian law requires for the activity, suitable housing and lawful yearly income above the level that exempts people from health charges [W0085, W0086]. The Embassy decides within 120 days, and the visa must be used within 180 days of issue [W0087]. The permit lasts up to 2 years, and each renewal up to 3 [W0066].
