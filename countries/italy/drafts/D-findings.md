## Work visa: seasonal worker

A seasonal permit lets you work in Italy in farming or in tourism and hotels for at most nine months in any twelve, then go home [D0034, D0035, D0039]. Bangladesh is on the list of countries whose citizens can take these jobs [D0004, D0005]. Italy allows 88,000 seasonal entries in 2026, 89,000 in 2027 and 90,000 in 2028 for all countries together [D0001, D0002, D0003]. You can't apply yourself: an employer in Italy, or its trade association, files a named request for you [D0124]. For Bangladeshis the nulla osta (the work authorisation the employer gets from the Prefettura's immigration desk, the SUI) is never issued automatically: the Questura (provincial police headquarters) must approve it and the National Labour Inspectorate (INL) checks the employer first, with AGEA (the state agricultural payments agency) for farm jobs [D0024, D0025, D0026].

### How the seasonal quota works

The quota is split between employers, and the split decides who can bring you in [D0006, D0009, D0010]:

- **Farm jobs through farmers' organisations:** 47,000 places a year are reserved for applications filed by the national farm organisations: Coldiretti (the national confederation of direct farmers), CIA (the Italian farmers' confederation), Confagricoltura (the general confederation of Italian agriculture) and the cooperative federations [D0006, D0007, D0008].
- **Tourism jobs through tourism employers' organisations:** 13,000 places in 2026 and 14,000 in 2027 [D0009].
- **Returning seasonal workers:** 5,000 places in 2026 for workers who have done seasonal work in Italy at least once in the last five years and whose employer asks for a multi-year nulla osta [D0010].

A private employer filing alone can send at most three nulla osta requests a year; the limit does not apply to employers' organisations, labour consultants and staffing agencies [D0023].

**When employers apply:** in 2026 the click day (the date and time the online quota opens, first come first served) was 9:00 on 12 January for farm jobs and 9 February for tourism jobs, and applications stay open until 31 December 2026 while places remain [D0013, D0014, D0016]. For 2027, employers pre-filled applications from 23 October to 7 December 2026, and the click days are 12 January 2027 for farm jobs and 9 February 2027 for tourism [D0088, D0089, D0122]. An application that has not won a place by 30 June 2027, or a nulla osta not followed by a visa by then, lapses [D0028].

### Step 1: Find a real seasonal employer

1. **Know who can hire you.** Only an employer in Italy, or its trade association on its behalf, can ask for you by name [D0124]. A seasonal nulla osta is refused or revoked if the employer has been sanctioned for undeclared work or has no real business activity [D0046]. An employer who did not sign the contratto di soggiorno (the residence contract signed in Italy) for workers it requested in the last three years cannot apply, and nor can one sent to trial for slavery, trafficking or caporalato (illegal gangmaster labour) [D0029, D0030].

   **Scams:** in Bangladesh, workers pay agencies thousands of euros for an "employer" and a nulla osta: one man paid over EUR 7,000 for the contact and expected to pay as much again on getting the visa [D0090]. Italian police have found networks that filed applications in the names of employers who did not know about them [D0098, D0100]. One such network, broken up in Grosseto in September 2025, charged EUR 2,000-4,000 per application, and some families mortgaged land to pay [D0099, D0128]. In 2014, about 40 farm employers around Latina filed false hiring requests for money, and migrants paid the network EUR 6,000-8,000 each [D0104, D0105]. A worker who arrives for a company that does not exist falls into irregular status [D0091] (see Scams).

   > **Need to know**

   > - Do Bangladeshi-owned businesses in Italy (shops, restaurants) file seasonal applications for relatives, and in which provinces?

2. **If you have worked a season in Italy before, ask the same employer to call you back.** A seasonal worker admitted at least once in the last five years, who kept to the permit's conditions and went home when it expired, has priority over first-time applicants, with the same or another employer [D0031]. The employer can ask for a multi-year (three-year) nulla osta for you [D0010, D0041].

### Step 2: The employer applies on click day

The employer does this step; you can only check it was done.

1. **The employer files the C-Stag form on the ALI portal,** the Ministry of the Interior's website for nulla osta applications. Farm employers use form C-Stag agricolo and tourism employers C-Stag turistico, from 9:00 on click day [D0013, D0014]. Seasonal jobs do not need the prior check at the Centro per l'Impiego (public employment office) that non-seasonal and domestic jobs need [D0017]. The ALI portal, pre-filling and the employer's documents are set out under Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker, Step 3.
2. **The SUI issues the nulla osta.** It is issued within 20 days of the application winning a quota place [D0027]. For Bangladeshis it waits for the Questura's approval and the INL's check of the employer, and it is never granted by silence when that time runs out [D0024, D0025, D0026].

   **Check it is real:** see Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker, Step 5.

> **Need to know**

### Step 3: Visa, documents and the flight

The visa application at the Italian Embassy in Dhaka, the documents and the Bangladesh-side steps are the same as for the non-seasonal worker (see Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker, Step 4, Step 5 and Step 6). In November 2025, 34,958 people in Dhaka who had handed their documents and passports to the Embassy had never been called for a visa interview [D0092].

### Step 4: After arrival

1. **Start work, and sign the contratto di soggiorno.** You can start work as soon as you arrive with the nulla osta and visa, before the contratto di soggiorno is signed [D0032]. The contract signing and permit application are in Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker, Step 7. In practice, workers have waited up to a year for a Prefettura appointment, and employers often disappear [D0095]. One Bangladeshi farm worker who arrived in October 2022 was paid and housed, never got the appointment, and three years later had his nulla osta revoked [D0096]. Another Bangladeshi who entered legally in 2023 was refused a permit by the Latina Questura, ordered expelled and held at the Ponte Galeria CPR (detention centre for repatriation) in Rome [D0097]. Bangladeshis who entered on the Decreto Flussi and found no job, because the employer could not be found or was a scam, have formed a committee in Rome, Tikase [D0094].
2. **Find your SIISL account.** Once the contract is signed, INPS (the national social security institute) enrols you in SIISL, the government job-matching platform [D0038]. Through SIISL you can take a seasonal job with another employer while your nulla osta is valid, without going home for a new visa [D0037].

### Your rights as a seasonal worker

Pay, hours and contributions follow the national farm or tourism contract (see Your rights at work). Rules specific to seasonal work:

- **Housing:** if the employer gives you a place to live, the rent can't be more than one third of your pay and can't be taken automatically from your wages [D0043]. An employer who gives unfit housing, charges too much or deducts rent from pay is fined EUR 350-5,500 per worker [D0044].
- **If your permit is cancelled through the employer's fault:** the employer must pay you an indemnity based on the CCNL (national collective agreement) wages it owes you [D0045].

### Staying on: another season, a multi-year permit, or a non-seasonal permit

1. **A second seasonal job.** Within the nine months, your nulla osta is extended and your permit can be renewed for a new seasonal job with the same or another employer, if it starts within 60 days of the end of the last contract [D0036].
2. **A multi-year nulla osta.** With a three-year seasonal nulla osta, the Embassy issues the visa for the second and third years when you show the employer's contract proposal [D0041]. In those later years a different employer can hire you [D0042].
3. **Converting to a non-seasonal permit.** After at least three months of regular work, if an employer offers you a fixed-term or permanent contract, you can ask the SUI to convert your seasonal permit to a work permit [D0040] (see Changing visa type).
4. **Otherwise, go home.** At the end of the nine months you must return to Bangladesh unless you hold a permit for another reason [D0039]. Going home on time protects your priority for the next season [D0031].

### Caporalato: illegal gangmasters

Caporalato means a caporale (gangmaster) recruits workers to work for others under exploitative conditions [D0053]. Recent cases were around Latina (the Agro Pontino), near Naples and in Calabria [D0106, D0109, D0112].

#### What it looks like

- **Near Naples (Aversa), December 2025:** prosecutors found farm workers paid about EUR 2.70 an hour for 10-14 hours a day, carried packed into the cargo space of vans [D0106, D0107].
- **Calabria (Sibari plain), September 2026:** 18 Pakistanis, one Bangladeshi and one Indian were arrested in a caporalato inquiry [D0109]. Farms allegedly paid EUR 40 cash for ten hours, handed to the gangmasters, who kept EUR 5 a day for transport and EUR 60 a month for rent [D0110, D0111].
- **Latina, 2024:** Satnam Singh, an Indian worker employed off the books on a farm at Borgo Santa Maria near Latina, died after a work accident [D0112]. In July 2026 the Latina court sentenced the farm's owner to 16 years at first instance [D0113].
- **Fake hiring:** in June 2026, 30 people were arrested across Italy, including Latina and Ragusa, for false Decreto Flussi applications filed through a CAF (tax assistance centre) in Taranto, mostly for people from Pakistan, Bangladesh and India, who paid up to EUR 6,500 for a nulla osta and visa [D0129, D0127, D0101, D0102].

#### The law

Law 199/2016 made caporalato a crime under Article 603-bis of the Criminal Code [D0053]. The recruiter and the employer who uses exploited workers are both punished: 1-6 years in prison and a fine of EUR 500-1,000 per worker, or 5-8 years with violence or threats [D0053, D0054, D0055]. Signs of exploitation include pay repeatedly far below the national contracts, and degrading working conditions, surveillance or housing [D0056, D0057].

#### If you are exploited, in this order:

1. **Call the anti-trafficking helpline, 800 290 290:** free, anonymous and open 24 hours a day, for victims of trafficking or labour exploitation and anyone reporting it [D0114].
2. **Report it to the Labour Inspectorate or the police** (see If you aren't paid). The INL can pass the case to the prosecutor or the Questura and give its opinion on a permit for you [D0049].

#### The special permit for victims (Article 18-ter)

If police or prosecutors find violence, abuse or exploitation of a foreign worker who helps bring out the facts and identify those responsible, the Questore (head of the Questura) issues a permit at once on the prosecutor's proposal [D0047, D0048]. It is marked "casi speciali", lasts one year, can be renewed, and lets you work [D0125, D0050, D0126]. While you wait, the receipt for your application lets you stay and work [D0052]. When it expires it can be converted into a work permit outside the quotas [D0051].

## Work visa: domestic and care worker

This visa lets a family in Italy hire you as a colf (house worker) or badante (live-in carer) [D0011, D0012]. Italy allows 13,600 domestic and care workers in 2026, 14,000 in 2027 and 14,200 in 2028, from the same list of countries, which includes Bangladesh [D0011, D0004]. From 2026 this quota is only for jobs with a household; care jobs in clinics or care homes use the general non-seasonal quota (see Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker) [D0012]. Separately, up to 10,000 carers a year can enter outside the quota until 2028 (see The out-of-quota care route) [D0058, D0059]. As for seasonal work, a Bangladeshi worker's nulla osta always needs the Questura's approval and the INL's check [D0024, D0025, D0026].

### Step 1: Find a real family employer

1. **Check the employer can afford you.** An employer living alone needs taxable income of at least EUR 20,000 a year, or EUR 27,000 if the household has more members [D0021]. An employer who is ill or disabled and hires you for their own care needs no income [D0022].

   **Scams:** a domestic job "offer" with no family behind it ends the same way as a fake farm job (see Work visa: seasonal worker, Step 1, item 1).

> **Need to know**
> - What does a domestic-work nulla osta sell for in Bangladesh in 2026?

### Step 2: The family applies on click day

1. **The family checks the job office first.** The family must first ask the Centro per l'Impiego whether a worker already in Italy can fill the job [D0019]. If the centre does not offer one within 8 days, the family can go ahead [D0018].
2. **The family gets the contract certified.** An asseverazione (certification by a labour consultant or employers' organisation that the job meets the rules) is needed unless the employer is not self-sufficient [D0019].
3. **The family files form A-bis on the ALI portal.** In 2026 the click day was 18 February; in 2027 it is 18 February 2027 [D0015, D0123]. The pay stated must follow the domestic-work CCNL and be no less than the assegno sociale (the state's minimum social allowance: EUR 7,002.97 a year in 2025) [D0020]. The rest of the procedure is in Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker, Step 3.
4. **On arrival, the family registers you with INPS.** If you start work before the contratto di soggiorno is signed, the family must file the hiring notice with INPS itself [D0032, D0033]. Visa, documents and arrival are as in Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker, Step 4 to Step 7.

### Your rights as a domestic worker

The domestic-work national contract (CCNL lavoro domestico) was renewed on 28 October 2025 [D0074].

#### Pay

Minimum pay from 1 January 2026 [D0065]:

- **Live-in, level B** (general home helper): EUR 983.16 a month [D0066].
- **Live-in, level BS** (carer of a person who can look after themselves, or babysitter): EUR 1,053.39 a month [D0067, D0075].
- **Live-in, level CS** (carer of a person who can't look after themselves, untrained): EUR 1,193.84 a month [D0068, D0076].
- **Live-in, level DS** (trained carer of a person who can't look after themselves): EUR 1,474.73 a month plus a EUR 207.69 allowance [D0069, D0070].
- **Not live-in:** EUR 7.45 an hour at level BS and EUR 8.30 at level CS [D0071, D0072].

Food and lodging are valued at EUR 6.66 a day [D0073]. A 13th month's pay (tredicesima) is due in December [D0083].

#### Hours, rest and leave

- **Hours:** live-in, at most 10 non-consecutive hours a day and 54 a week; not live-in, at most 8 a day and 40 a week [D0077, D0116].
- **Rest:** live-in workers get at least 11 consecutive hours' rest a day and 36 hours a week, 24 of them on Sunday [D0079, D0078].
- **Overtime:** +25% by day, +50% at night, +60% on Sundays and public holidays [D0080, D0081, D0117].
- **Leave:** 26 working days a year [D0082].
- **Lodging and food:** a live-in worker must get lodging that protects dignity and privacy [D0087].
- **Sickness:** your job is kept for 10 days in the first six months, 45 days up to two years, and 180 days after that [D0120, D0085, D0121].

#### Probation and ending the job

- **Probation:** live-in workers have 30 working days' paid probation [D0084].
- **Notice:** for 25 hours a week or more, 15 days up to five years' service and 30 days after; half that if you resign [D0118, D0119, D0086].

For unpaid wages see If you aren't paid.

### The out-of-quota care route

From 2025 to 2028, up to 10,000 workers a year can enter outside the quota to care for people with disabilities, very old people or children under six [D0058, D0059, D0060]. The family applies through a registered employment agency (APL) or an employers' association that signed the domestic-work CCNL, not on its own [D0061]. The family can't hire its own spouse or relatives up to the third degree this way [D0062]. For the first 12 months you may only do that care work, and changing employer needs the local labour inspectorate's prior approval [D0063, D0064].
