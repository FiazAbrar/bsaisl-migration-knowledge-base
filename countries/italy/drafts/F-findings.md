## a. Draft doc text

### Bringing your family

*(H3 under "Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal)". Short pointer; the full procedure is in the Family reunification H2.)*

You can bring your wife and children under 18 once you hold a permit of at least one year, earn above the income line and have a home the comune certifies as suitable; for your wife (but not your children) you must also have lived legally in Italy for two years without a break [F0007, F0008, F0010, F0012]. The whole procedure, from the online application to the visa in Dhaka, is in Family reunification [F0019, F0051]. Your wife can work in Italy once she arrives on her family permit [F0092, F0087].

## Family reunification

Ricongiungimento familiare (family reunification) is how a foreigner living legally in Italy brings a wife, children or parents from Bangladesh [F0001, F0002, F0004]. It runs in two halves: you apply in Italy for a nulla osta (entry clearance) from the SUI (Sportello Unico per l'Immigrazione, the immigration desk at the Prefettura, the central government's office in each province), then your family applies with it for a visa at VFS Global in Dhaka for the Italian Embassy [F0019, F0051]. The Embassy says it issues about 12,000 to 13,000 family visas a year, with a small refusal rate on new applications (September 2026) [F0086, F0088]. Family members can work as soon as they arrive [F0087, F0092].

> **Need to know**
> - How many of the 12,000 to 13,000 family visas a year go to Bangladeshi wives and children, and how many to parents?

### Who can come

- **Your wife or husband,** aged 18 or over and not legally separated [F0001]. If you already live in Italy with one wife, a second wife cannot come [F0006].
- **Children under 18** on the day you apply, unmarried, including your wife's children or children born outside marriage; the other parent, if alive, must consent [F0002, F0005].
- **Adult children** only if they depend on you because of a health condition amounting to total disability [F0003].
- **Parents** if they depend on you and have no other children in Bangladesh, or if they are over 65 and your brothers and sisters cannot support them for documented serious health reasons [F0004, F0079].

No other relatives (brothers, sisters, in-laws) can come on a family visa [F0004, F0079]. A Bangla news site reported in November 2025 that parents had been removed from the list; the law in force in November 2026 and the Embassy's July 2026 checklist still include them [F0090, F0004, F0079].

### Who can sponsor, and the income line

1. **Your permit:** a long-term card (EU long-term permit) or a permit of at least one year for work, self-employment, study, religious or family reasons [F0007]. An asylum seeker still waiting for a final decision cannot apply [F0025].
2. **Two years in Italy (since 11 December 2024):** for a wife, a disabled adult child or a parent, you must have lived legally in Italy without a break for at least two years when you apply; this does not apply to children under 18, or to people with refugee or subsidiary protection [F0008, F0009].
3. **Income:** a lawful annual income of at least the assegno sociale (Italy's state minimum pension, EUR 7,101.12 a year in 2026) plus half of it for each person you bring [F0012, F0016, F0017]. In 2026 that is [F0017, F0018]:
   - 1 person: EUR 10,651.68 a year;
   - 2 people: EUR 14,202.24;
   - 3 people: EUR 17,752.80;
   - each more person: EUR 3,550.56 more;
   - two or more children under 14 (with no one else): at least EUR 14,202.24 [F0013, F0018].

   The income of relatives living with you also counts [F0014].
4. **Housing:** a home that meets health and hygiene standards, certified suitable by the comune (municipality); for a single child under 14, the consent of the home's holder is enough [F0010, F0011].
5. **A parent over 65:** you also need health insurance for them covering all risks in Italy, or their enrolment in the SSN (Servizio Sanitario Nazionale, the national health service) for a fee [F0015, F0048].

You can instead bring your family with you when you first come, if you hold an entry visa for a job contract of at least one year and meet the same housing and income conditions (familiari al seguito) [F0026].

### What it costs

- **Revenue stamps (marca da bollo):** EUR 16 entered in the online application, and a second EUR 16 stamp for the nulla osta [F0039, F0040].
- **Visa fee in Dhaka:** Tk 16,240 (EUR 116) [F0052].
- **VFS service charge:** Tk 5,320 (EUR 38), VAT included, plus a Tk 270 bank draft charge [F0053, F0054].

VFS converts at EUR 1 = Tk 140 [F0055].

### Step 1: Check you qualify

1. **Check your permit and dates:** the permit's length and type (see Who can sponsor, and the income line, item 1), and whether you have two unbroken years of legal residence if you are bringing your wife or a parent [F0007, F0008].
2. **Add up your income** from last year's tax return (or Modello Unico) or CUD (Certificazione Unica, the employer's yearly income statement), or all your payslips if you started work less than a year ago; compare it with the 2026 line for the number of people you are bringing [F0046, F0017].
3. **Check the home** has room for everyone before you pay for a certificate (Step 2) [F0010].

### Step 2: Get the housing suitability certificate

The certificato di idoneità alloggiativa (housing suitability certificate) is issued by the comune where you live [F0044, F0010].

You'll need [F0043, F0044, F0045]:

- a rental contract registered and running at least six months from the date you will apply, or the purchase deed if you own the home;
- if you are not the only holder of the home, form S2 signed by the holder(s), consenting to host your family, with a signed copy of their ID.

**In Rome:**

1. Fill in the request online on the Prefettura di Roma's RIA site, servizi.utgroma.it/ria/richiesta/nuova, and give your email so you are told when the Municipio has replied [F0049].
2. Print the request with the list of attached documents and hand it in at your Municipio (district office) [F0050].
3. Keep the receipt showing the RIA code: you can attach it to the nulla osta application if the certificate has not arrived yet [F0044].

> **Need to know**
> - How long do Rome's Municipi and Milan's comune take to issue the certificate in 2026, and do they send an inspector?
> - What floor area per person do comuni apply (the 1975 health ministry decree), and is a shared Bangladeshi flat with several families ever accepted?

### Step 3: Apply for the nulla osta on the ALI portal

You apply online; the law gives the SUI 150 days to decide (it was 90 days until October 2025) [F0019, F0020, F0091].

You'll need [F0038, F0039, F0042, F0043, F0044, F0045, F0046, F0047]:

- SPID (Sistema Pubblico di Identità Digitale, Italy's free digital ID), opened with an email, a mobile number, a valid ID and your health card with your codice fiscale (tax code);
- a EUR 16 revenue stamp;
- scans of your passport and your family's passports (pages with number and personal details), your valid permit (or the expired one with the renewal receipt), your codice fiscale and your family status certificate from the comune;
- the housing papers from Step 2;
- income papers: last tax return or CUD (all payslips if employed less than a year), your contract or Unilav (the hiring notice), the last three payslips, and form S3 signed by your employer within the last month showing the job continues and your monthly pay, with the employer's ID;
- for a parent over 65, your signed undertaking to take out health insurance [F0048].

1. Log in with SPID at the Ministry of the Interior's portal, nullaostalavoro.dlci.interno.it, and fill in the family reunification form, entering the revenue stamp number [F0027, F0039].
2. Attach the documents as PDFs, with forms S1 (one child under 14), S2 and S3 or S3 bis as needed [F0028].
3. Watch the "communications" section of the ALI portal and your email: the SUI first asks the Questura (provincial police headquarters) to check for criminal records, then handles files in order of arrival [F0029, F0031].
4. **If you get a notice that papers are missing,** go to the SUI on the date it gives with the missing papers and the originals; if they are still incomplete that day, the application is rejected [F0030].
5. Go to the SUI when called with the original revenue stamp, a second EUR 16 stamp, your permit and a copy [F0040].
6. When the nulla osta is issued, the SUI sends it electronically to the Embassy in Dhaka; send the original to your family [F0032, F0041].

**Check it is real:** a genuine nulla osta appears in your own ALI portal account, under communications [F0031, F0032]. Check your family's names, sex and dates of birth on it match their passports exactly, or the visa will be delayed [F0155].

**Time limit:** the nulla osta is valid 6 months and cannot be extended; your family must apply for the visa within that time [F0033, F0034].

> **Need to know**
> - How long does the SUI in Rome, Milan and Venice actually take to issue a family nulla osta in 2026?

### Step 4: Prepare and apostille the papers in Bangladesh

Since 30 March 2025 Bangladesh has been party to the Apostille Convention, and the Embassy now wants every Bangladeshi document in original with an apostille (one apostille per document), instead of the old two-step legalisation by MOFA and the Embassy [F0151, F0068, F0070, F0082]. How to get an apostille: see Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Step 4: Passport, police clearance and certificates.

You'll need [F0068, F0069, F0074, F0075, F0076, F0059, F0060, F0065]:

- **For a wife:** the nikahnama in Bangla and English and the marriage certificate, both original and apostilled; the marriage certificate must give the solemnisation date (nikahnama column 12), the registration date (column 24), and the volume, page and serial number; if either of you married before, the apostilled talaknama (divorce deed);
- **For every applicant:** a family certificate (পারিবারিক সনদ) from the Union Parishad chairman or ward commissioner, original and apostilled;
- **For children:** the original birth certificate and both parents' consent, apostilled; for a guardian, court papers from a Bangladeshi court, apostilled;
- **For parents:** a dependent certificate from the chairman or commissioner on letterhead, or from a lawyer on stamp paper.

1. **Make every name match.** Name, father's name and date of birth must be the same in every document, with no exceptions; your wife's passport must show your name as spouse [F0061, F0064].
2. **Get the family certificate in the Embassy's format:** issued in the name of the living head of the family, listing all immediate family members from senior to junior, dead members marked "late", with real addresses (your Italian address if you live in Italy) [F0060].
3. **If you married by phone or video,** make sure the nikahnama says so and shows the kazi was physically present [F0062].
4. **Do not use correction fluid or overwrite anything** [F0066].

**Check it is real:** the Embassy checks every civil status document; the law lets it refuse the visa until it has verified the marriage, birth and relationship papers [F0021]. If it doubts them, it can certify the relationship with a DNA test, paid for by the family [F0022, F0023].

**Scams:** in May 2025 the Embassy said it was finding more and more fake Bangladeshi and Italian civil status documents in family applications; a forged paper means refusal and a flag in the Schengen system that permanently affects future travel to the EU [F0152, F0153].

> **Need to know**
> - Does the Embassy in Dhaka ask Bangladeshi families for DNA tests in practice, which laboratory does it use, and what does the test cost?
> - Does the Embassy accept MOFA's electronic apostille (e-APP with QR code), or only the paper apostille?

### Step 5: The visa at VFS Dhaka

You'll need [F0071, F0072, F0073, F0057, F0058, F0077, F0078]:

- the D-type (long-stay) visa form, signed (both parents sign for a child);
- a recent ICAO photo (3.5 x 4.0 cm, white background);
- a passport valid at least three months beyond the visa, with two blank pages, plus copies of all passports (VFS asks for validity of 1 year and 6 months);
- a copy of the nulla osta;
- a one-way flight booking with the booking reference;
- copies of all your passports, past and present, all pages, and your Italian permit and identity card;
- the documents from Step 4;
- recommended for a wife: certified copies of all pages of your passports since the wedding, the ID of all wedding witnesses, and for a phone or video marriage the ID and Italian permits of the witnesses in Italy;
- the fees (see What it costs).

1. **Book and submit at VFS Global's Italy visa centre** with all originals; long-stay applicants give no fingerprints [F0066, F0067].
2. **For a wife, bring your passport stamps showing you were in Bangladesh for the wedding** [F0063].
3. **File only one application per person:** the same person cannot have two applications at once, even with two passports [F0080].
4. **Track the application on VFS's passport tracker** and keep the phone number and email you gave working: if the Embassy wants more documents, the tracker shows it with a deadline, and missing it is your fault [F0083].
5. **Collect the passport within 90 days,** or it goes back to the Embassy and on to Bangladesh's Ministry of Home Affairs [F0084, F0085].

VFS states a minimum of 21 calendar days [F0056]. In May 2025 the Embassy was still clearing a 2024 backlog of family files, with priority for minors and humanitarian cases [F0154].

> **Need to know**
> - How long did a family visa take at VFS Dhaka in 2026, from submission to passport return?

### Step 6: After arrival

1. **Within 48 hours,** you or the host give a written declaration of hospitality to the police or the comune [F0035].
2. **Within 8 days,** book an SUI appointment on the ALI portal (section "book an appointment") with the SPID used for the application; the passport entry stamp proves the date [F0036].
3. **At the SUI,** the entry is registered and the residence permit papers are completed; the Questura then issues the permesso di soggiorno per motivi familiari (family permit) [F0037, F0092]. Codice fiscale, residence registration, SSN enrolment and fingerprints: as for workers (see Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Step 7: After arrival).

### Keeping the family permit

- It lasts as long as your own permit and is renewed with it [F0093].
- It allows work as an employee or self-employed, study and training, and registering at job centres [F0092].
- A child under 18 living with you gets a family permit valid until 18, or the EU long-term permit if you hold one [F0109].
- If you die, or you separate legally or divorce, your wife can convert her permit into a work, self-employment or study permit [F0094].
- A permit given on marriage is revoked at once if the couple do not actually live together, unless they have a child [F0097]. A marriage made only to get a permit gets the application refused [F0024].

### If the nulla osta or visa is refused

You can challenge a refused nulla osta or family permit in the ordinary court (tribunale) [F0095].

> **Need to know**
> - What is the deadline to appeal a refused family nulla osta or visa, and which ASGI lawyers or patronati in Rome and Milan take these cases?

### Scams

- **Fake civil status papers:** fake nikahnamas, birth and family certificates get the visa refused and the person flagged in the Schengen system (see Step 4: Prepare and apostille the papers in Bangladesh) [F0152, F0153].
- **Duplicate applications:** a second application in another passport is not allowed [F0080].

> **Need to know**
> - Do brokers in Rome or Milan sell fake employer S3 statements, payslips or rental contracts to reach the income and housing line, and what do they charge?
> - Do brokers in Dhaka sell "fast-track" family visa appointments at VFS?

### Family of an Italian or EU citizen

- **Visa:** the spouse, parents, parents-in-law and children under 21 of an Italian or EU citizen apply at VFS for the EU family member visa, free for dependants who prove it [F0100, F0101]. They need an invitation letter from the citizen stating the intention to exercise the right to family unity, with the citizen's ID; a spouse needs the marriage extract from the Italian comune plus the apostilled nikahnama and marriage certificate [F0102, F0103].
- **Residence card:** after three months the family member applies to the Questura for the residence card of a family member of an EU citizen, valid five years and free apart from the cost of the forms [F0104, F0105, F0106].
- **Marrying in Italy:** a foreigner legally resident at least a year who marries an Italian, an EU citizen or a legal resident in Italy can get a family permit [F0096]. A legal resident can also convert his or her own permit into a family permit within a year of its expiry [F0098].
- **Parent of an Italian child:** a foreign parent of an Italian child living in Italy gets a family permit even without a valid permit [F0099].
- **Citizenship by marriage:** two years' legal residence in Italy after marrying an Italian (three if living abroad), halved if you have children [F0147, F0148].

## Having a child in Italy

A child born in Italy to Bangladeshi parents is not Italian at birth: Italian citizenship passes by blood [F0111]. The child takes your legal status and needs a permit, a Bangladeshi birth registration and a Bangladeshi passport [F0109, F0114].

### Step 1: Register the birth in Italy

1. **Declare the birth** within 3 days at the hospital's office (direzione sanitaria), or within 10 days at the comune where the baby was born or at your own comune of residence [F0107, F0108].
2. **Get the atto di nascita (Italian birth record)** naming the child and both parents; you need it for every later step [F0115].

### Step 2: Get the child's residence permit

A child under 18 living with a legally resident parent gets a family permit valid until 18 (or the EU long-term permit if the parent holds one) [F0109].

> **Need to know**
> - Is a newborn's permit requested by post-office kit or directly at the Questura, and what does it cost?

### Step 3: Register the birth with the Bangladesh Embassy in Rome

1. **Apply online at bdris.gov.bd,** choosing the Embassy in Rome as the office; the online application is deleted after 15 days if you do not submit it [F0114, F0116].
2. **Submit the printed application at the Embassy** with the Italian birth record naming the child and both parents and copies of both parents' passports; the baby does not need to come [F0115, F0118, F0127].
3. **Collect the certificate:** it usually takes 5 working days [F0117].

Register a birth only once: a second registration for the same person is an offence [F0119].

### Step 4: Get the child a Bangladeshi passport

1. **Fill in the passport form on passport.gov.bd;** at the payment page click "Skip Payment", because the Embassy enters the fee [F0121].
2. **Book one appointment for the whole family** on the Embassy's website [F0126].
3. **Bring** the Bangladeshi birth registration certificate, a copy of the Italian birth record, copies of both parents' passports and photos (2 of the child, 1 of each parent) [F0120].
4. **Pay EUR 100 by bank card** (no cash) [F0122, F0123].

A child under 2 getting a first passport does not need to come; an older child must [F0124]. The passport is printed in Dhaka and usually reaches the Embassy within 2 months; there is no urgent service [F0125].

> **Need to know**
> - Does the Embassy in Rome now issue e-passports for children, and has the EUR 100 fee changed?

### Can the child become Italian?

- **At 18:** a child born in Italy who has lived there legally without a break becomes Italian by declaring it within one year of turning 18 [F0112].
- **Earlier, through a parent:** a child born in Italy can apply after three years' legal residence; otherwise the child follows the parents' citizenship (see Becoming an Italian citizen) [F0113].

**Travelling to Bangladesh with an Italian passport:** a child with an Italian passport needs a No Visa Required seal: fill in the form on visa.gov.bd, email it with the passports to bdvisa.rome@gmail.com, wait for approval, then book an appointment; it costs EUR 50 per passport and takes 5 working days [F0128, F0130, F0131]. A child without a Bangladeshi passport needs the Bangladeshi birth certificate and the parents' Bangladeshi passports for it [F0129].

> **Need to know**
> - How long does the comune take to record the birth so the atto di nascita can be printed, and does a missing residence registration of the parents delay it?

### Becoming an Italian citizen

*(Placement for the lead to decide: an H3 under "How long you can stay" in Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), or its own H2.)*

A non-EU foreigner can be granted Italian citizenship after ten years' uninterrupted legal residence [F0132, F0133]. A June 2025 referendum to cut this to five years failed for lack of quorum, so ten years still applies [F0149, F0150, F0133].

**Who qualifies:**

- **Ten years' legal residence:** registered as resident at the comune and in order with permit rules for the whole period [F0133, F0134].
- **Income:** enough to support yourself [F0135].
- **No criminal record** and no security bar [F0135].
- **Italian at B1 or above,** proved by an Italian school diploma, a certificate from a recognised body, or a CPIA (Centro Provinciale per l'Istruzione degli Adulti, the public adult school) attestation; holders of the EU long-term permit are exempt [F0136, F0137, F0138].

**How to apply:**

1. **Get your Bangladeshi birth certificate and police clearance;** the police clearance is valid six months from issue, the birth certificate does not expire [F0142].
2. **Make sure names match** in every Italian and Bangladeshi document; any difference needs an attestation from the Bangladesh Embassy, legalised by the Prefettura [F0143].
3. **Pay EUR 250** by post-office bollettino, bank transfer or PagoPA [F0139, F0140].
4. **Apply online** at portaleservizi.dlci.interno.it [F0141].
5. **Wait:** the law gives 24 months, extendable to 36 [F0146].
6. **Read the decision** on the SEND digital notifications platform, which also tells you how to take the oath [F0144].

Whether you keep Bangladeshi citizenship depends on Bangladesh's law [F0145].
