## a. Draft doc text

## Visas not open to Bangladeshis

- **Visa-free visit:** Bangladeshis need a visa even to change planes in an Italian airport (an airport transit visa), so any visit needs a Schengen visa [V0052].
- **Airport transit without a visa:** Closed unless you already hold a valid Schengen or national visa or a residence permit, or another listed exemption [V0052].
- **Working holiday visa (vacanze-lavoro: up to 6 months' work in a year):** Only for citizens of New Zealand, Canada, Australia, Hong Kong, South Korea and Japan, the countries with an agreement with Italy [V0133].
- **Turning a tourist visa into a stay or a job:** A tourist visa does not allow work, study or applying to stay in Italy (see Changing visa type) [V0044].

## Student visa

Italy issued about 500 study visas to Bangladeshis in 2025 and expects to issue more in 2026, but the Italian ambassador has said many more would not be sustainable because universities have few places for foreign students [V0064, V0065]. The student visa is a type D (national, long-stay) visa called "Immatricolazione Università" (university enrolment), valid 365 days with multiple entries [V0083]. The embassy alone decides: a university's acceptance does not guarantee an appointment or a visa, and the embassy must also judge "migration risk" from the family's money, past studies and how well the course fits them [V0075, V0076].

The 2026/27 deadline: visa applications for bachelor's and master's courses must reach the embassy by 30 November 2026, and universities may set earlier dates for each course [V0071, V0072, V0073]. For 2027/28 the deadline is 31 October 2027 [V0072].

### Step 1: Choose a course and check you qualify

1. **Check the entry level.** A bachelor's course needs a school-leaving certificate after at least 12 years of schooling (in Bangladesh, the HSC) [V0080].
2. **Check the language.** Courses taught in Italian need Italian at least at level B2, tested by the university [V0081]. For courses in English, the Dhaka embassy accepts only IELTS, with a score of at least 6, issued within the last two years [V0105, V0106]. The certificate must come from a recognised institute or university and show all four skills (listening, reading, speaking, writing) [V0069, V0082].
3. **Check the money.** You must show EUR 10,179.85 a year, from lawful, traceable sources, preferably your own or your family's [V0085]. The embassy accepts funds of the student or of parents, grandparents, siblings, uncles and aunts, or cousins, including relatives living in Italy or Italian citizens [V0068]. It checks where the money came from, the account's movements and whether the sum fits the family's income and assets [V0086]. Bank statements must cover the last 12 months, and the sponsor's money must be explained (salary, property, savings held for some time) [V0107, V0108].

### Step 2: Get your certificates checked (CIMEA or Declaration of Value)

The visa needs your school (or bachelor's) certificate backed either by statements from CIMEA (Italy's official centre for comparing foreign qualifications) or by a Declaration of Value (Dichiarazione di Valore, DoV: the Italian Embassy's statement of what your certificate is worth in Italy) [V0090, V0070]. Whatever the university uses, the embassy needs a DoV or a CIMEA statement that also confirms the certificate is authentic [V0103]. From 2026/27 a university can no longer accept you "with reservation" while these papers are missing [V0102].

1. **Apostille your certificates first.** Since June 2025 the Italian Embassy no longer legalises Bangladeshi documents: they need an apostille from Bangladesh's Ministry of Foreign Affairs through the MyGov portal (see Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker, Step 4) [V0010].
2. **Either: apply to CIMEA yourself** on [cimea-diplome.it](https://cimea-diplome.it/), the only official channel [V0118]. Bangladesh is not in the Lisbon Recognition Convention, so you need the Comparability & Verification service [V0114]:
   - cost: EUR 300 per qualification (EUR 245.90 plus VAT) [V0113];
   - time: 60 working days; urgent comparability in 30 working days for EUR 100 more, but the verification part always takes the ordinary time [V0115, V0116];
   - only the certificate holder can apply; CIMEA processes nothing through agents and prohibits brokerage [V0117, V0120].
3. **Or: ask for a Declaration of Value** at the Italian Embassy in Dhaka, through VFS (see Step 4 for VFS addresses) [V0070, V0001]. The embassy's 2026 fee list prices a DoV at EUR 50 (Tk 7,000) and marks it free for study in Italy [V0017].

**Check it is real:** a genuine CIMEA statement carries a QR code and verification link, recorded on a blockchain; CIMEA has had fake CIMEA certificates submitted to Italian universities [V0118, V0119].

### Step 3: Pre-enrol on Universitaly

1. **Apply to the university first,** on its own portal, by its own deadline; each university checks your documents before it validates you [V0073, V0078].
2. **Pre-enrol on [universitaly.it](https://www.universitaly.it/),** the Ministry of University and Research's free, official portal; no other channel is accepted [V0074].
3. **Wait for the university to validate your pre-enrolment.** Only validated students can apply for the visa [V0078]. From 2026/27 each university may validate only up to its places for foreign students plus 20% [V0077]. Uni-Italia, a non-profit association, gives guidance on Universitaly through its centres abroad [V0079].
4. **Do not pay tuition yet:** university fees are normally paid only after the visa is issued [V0091].

### Step 4: Apply for the visa at VFS

1. **Wait for VFS's email.** Students registered on Universitaly are contacted by VFS Global by email, at the address used on Universitaly, to book the appointment [V0067]. Appointments are always free, also through VFS [V0009].
2. **You'll need** (the embassy's checklist):
   - the type D visa form, one ICAO photo (3.5 x 4.0 cm, white background), and your passport (original and one copy), valid at least 3 months beyond the visa, with two blank pages [V0141, V0142];
   - the Universitaly pre-enrolment receipt showing tuition fees [V0104];
   - the DoV (or its application receipt) or CIMEA statements [V0070];
   - the IELTS (6 or more) or B2 certificate [V0105];
   - 12 months' bank statements of the student and sponsor, and the sponsor's birth-certificate link to you, apostilled by Bangladesh's MoFA [V0107, V0109];
   - accommodation: a booking of at least 30 days, a lease, or a hosting declaration by a legal resident of Italy with ID [V0110];
   - a one-way flight booking, and health insurance valid across Schengen for the whole stay [V0111];
   - nikahnama, talaqnama or spouse's death certificate if relevant, in English and apostilled [V0027].

   A scholarship does not replace the proof of money [V0112]. Applying for a university residence place is not proof of accommodation [V0088].
3. **Give fingerprints:** since 11 January 2025 they are taken for national visas too [V0089].
4. **Pay** EUR 50 for the study visa, in cash in Taka [V0013, V0015].
5. **If your admission or language test is in Italy,** you get a type D visa with a conventional validity of 100 days, and you do not need to come home for a new visa after passing [V0084].

### Step 5: After you arrive

1. **Apply for the study permit within 8 working days,** at the Questura (the police immigration office) of the city where you will live, with the kit from a post office or a desk at the university [V0093]. The post office receipt proves you are legally present until the permit is ready [V0094]. Patronati (free advice offices run by trade unions) and some municipalities help fill it in free [V0095]. Information: poliziadistato.it, portaleimmigrazione.it, toll-free 803160 [V0096].
2. **Enrol at the university by 31 January.** From 31 January of the academic year the visas of students not yet enrolled are revoked and the Questura is told [V0092].
3. For the codice fiscale (tax code), health service and bank account, see Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker, Step 7.

### Can you work?

A study permit allows a job of up to 20 hours a week, and at most 1,040 hours a year [V0123]. The Labour Inspectorate (INL) says no contract may go over 20 hours a week, not even in summer, and that working more needs a work permit (see Changing visa type) [V0124, V0125].

### Scholarships (DSU)

Bangladesh is on Italy's 2026/27 list of "particularly poor countries" [V0131]. For students from these countries, the regional grants for university students (DSU, diritto allo studio) judge need by a certificate from the Italian Embassy that the student does not come from a notably high-income, high-status family [V0132]. Being eligible for a grant does not count as money for the visa until the grant is formally awarded [V0087].

### How long you can stay

1. **Renew the permit** at the Questura at least 60 days before it expires [V0097].
2. **Pass exams:** renewal needs one exam passed in the first year and at least two in each later year [V0098]. Permits are not issued for more than three years beyond the course's length [V0099].
3. **Changing course:** you may move to another university degree with approval, but not to private courses [V0101]. Formally withdrawing from studies ends your right to stay, and you must leave Italy [V0100].
4. **After graduating,** see Changing visa type.

### If the visa is refused

A refusal must give reasons, and you can appeal to the Regional Administrative Court (TAR) of Lazio within 60 days [V0049, V0050]. There is no right to a visa, only a "legitimate interest" [V0051].

### Scams

#### Done to students

Pre-enrolment on Universitaly is free, CIMEA takes no requests through agents, and visa appointments are free, so you can do all three yourself [V0074, V0117, V0009]. In 2019 the embassy itself warned students not to use intermediaries or brokers [V0121]. Fake CIMEA certificates have been submitted to Italian universities; check the QR code (see Step 2) [V0119, V0118].

#### Tried by applicants

Fake documents lead to immediate refusal, and in 2019 the embassy said applicants with fake papers would be banned and reported to Italian and Bangladeshi police [V0135, V0122]. A student visa used only to enter Italy is revoked if you do not enrol by 31 January [V0092].

## Visit visa (Schengen)

A Schengen visa (type C) allows up to 90 days in any 180 days in Italy and the other Schengen countries [V0036]. It covers tourism, visiting family or friends, business and medical treatment [V0021, V0029, V0032]. It never allows work or study, or applying to stay (see Changing visa type) [V0044].

Most Bangladeshi applicants are refused. In 2025 the Italian consulate in Dhaka received 6,521 Schengen applications and refused 3,948 (60%), after refusing 65% in 2024 [V0053, V0140]. Italy's refusal rate worldwide in 2025 was 11%, and its consulates in New Delhi, Karachi and Colombo refused 17%, 40% and 39% [V0055, V0062, V0060, V0061]. In Dhaka, Germany refused 30%, Spain 40%, France 47% and Sweden 74% in 2025 [V0056, V0057, V0058, V0059]. The Italian ambassador says the rate is high because many people who try to enter Italy illegally through these visas submit fake documents [V0063].

### Step 1: Prepare the papers

You'll need (the embassy's tourism checklist):

- the Schengen visa form, filled in English or Italian and signed [V0019];
- one ICAO photo, 3.5 x 4.0 cm, colour, white background, under 6 months old [V0020];
- a passport valid at least three months beyond the visa, with two blank visa pages [V0019];
- your personal bank statement from a bank in Bangladesh for the last 6 months [V0024];
- if employed: the employer's original letter with your salary, years of service, leave approval and confirmation of your job on return [V0022];
- if self-employed: trade licence, incorporation certificate, tax return and TIN, and 6 months' company bank statements [V0023];
- a return flight booking with a PNR (reservation number) [V0025];
- a hotel booking, or other proof of where you will stay, for the whole trip [V0143];
- travel insurance for all Schengen countries and the whole stay, covering at least EUR 30,000 [V0026];
- if married, divorced or widowed: nikahnama, talaqnama or death certificate, in English and apostilled, one apostille per document [V0027].

The embassy may ask for more [V0028]. Italy's minimum money for a visit is set by a 2000 Interior Ministry table: for one person, EUR 269.60 for 1 to 5 days; EUR 44.93 a day for 6 to 10 days; EUR 51.64 plus EUR 36.67 a day for 11 to 20 days; and EUR 206.58 plus EUR 27.89 a day beyond 20 days [V0045, V0046, V0047, V0048].

**Visiting family or friends in Italy:** add the invitation letter in the embassy's format, proof of the relationship, the host's ID and, if the host is not Italian, their residence permit [V0021].

**Business:** add the Italian company's invitation in the embassy's format, its Chamber of Commerce registration (Visura Camerale) no older than 6 months, and proof of recent trade such as bills of lading [V0029, V0030, V0031].

**Medical treatment:** add the hospital's appointment confirmation, the expected length of treatment, and proof you can pay; for treatment paid by the Italian state, the Region's act or a Ministry of Health authorisation [V0034, V0032, V0033].

### Step 4: At the border

1. **A visa does not guarantee entry:** border police can refuse you if you can't show the purpose of the trip and the conditions are not met [V0037]. Carry the return ticket, hotel booking or invitation, money, and the EUR 30,000 insurance [V0043].
2. **Get your passport stamped** on entering the Schengen area from Bangladesh: the stamp is your "declaration of presence" [V0038]. If you arrive through another Schengen country, declare your presence at the Questura within 8 days; a hotel's registration counts [V0039, V0040].
3. **Emergency number in Italy:** 112 [V0042].

**Changing planes in Italy without entering:** you need an airport transit visa unless you hold a valid Schengen or national visa or residence permit, or another listed exemption [V0052].

### Can you work?

No: a tourist visa does not allow work, study or applying to stay in Italy [V0044]. Overstaying can lead to an expulsion order (see What can get you jailed or deported) [V0041].

### If the visa is refused

The refusal letter must give reasons; you can appeal to the TAR of Lazio within 60 days, or reapply and pay again [V0049, V0050].

### Scams

#### Done to travellers

A syndicate including three Italian Embassy officials in Dhaka allegedly sold tourist visas for EUR 7,000 and work visas for EUR 15,000; Rome police arrested them in February 2025 [V0136]. Anyone charging for a VFS appointment is cheating you: appointments are free [V0009].

#### Tried by travellers

Fake or altered documents lead to immediate refusal, and other Schengen embassies in Dhaka have issued the same warning [V0135]. In June 2026 the CID arrested a Biman ground officer who allegedly helped three passengers fly to Italy on fake Schengen visas; two were caught at Rome airport and sent back [V0134]. Immigration police describe a "page substitution" ring that cuts a genuine Italian visa page out of one passport and puts it into a look-alike's passport [V0139]. Bangladesh's Special Branch stopped 15,516 passengers at airports in 2025, and Italy is among the countries it links to visa fraud [V0137, V0138].

## Changing visa type

- **Tourist or business visa to anything else:** not allowed; a tourist visa does not allow work, study or applying to stay [V0044].
- **Study permit to work permit:** allowed outside the Decreto Flussi quotas, before the study permit expires, once a job contract is signed at the SUI (contratto di soggiorno) or, for self-employment, after the SUI's certification [V0126]. People admitted for a training course or traineeship can convert only after finishing it [V0128].
- **After graduating:** a graduate with a degree, master's, PhD or ITS (higher technical) diploma from Italy can get a permit of 9 to 12 months to look for a job or start a business linked to the studies, then convert it to a work permit [V0129]. This needs the minimum income set for family reunification, health cover and registering as available for work at a Centro per l'Impiego (public job centre) [V0130].
- **Working more than 20 hours a week while studying:** convert to a work permit first, before the study permit expires [V0125].
