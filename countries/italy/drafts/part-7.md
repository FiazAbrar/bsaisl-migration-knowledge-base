## Sending money home

**Send through a licensed money-transfer company, Poste Italiane (the Italian post office) or a bank, never hundi** (also called hawala: money passed through private agents outside the banking system). Hundi is illegal in Bangladesh, and in Italy running a banking or payment business without authorisation is a crime [G0045, G0080]. By June 2026 Bangladesh's Financial Intelligence Unit (BFIU) had frozen or suspended about 55,000 mobile-money accounts linked to digital hundi and online gambling [G0044]. Money sent legally earns the family the government's 2.5% cash incentive, kept in the FY2026-27 budget, and in some cases another 2.5% from the bank [G0043, G0030]. Italy charges no tax on money sent home: its 2018 tax of 1.5% on transfers above EUR 10 (about Tk 1,400) to non-EU countries was repealed by the 2021 Budget Law [G0026, G0025, G0001].

### Your salary and bank account

Since 1 July 2018 every employer in Italy must pay wages, and any advance, through a bank or post office by a traceable method, never in cash; an employer who pays cash is fined EUR 1,000 to 5,000 (about Tk 1.4 lakh to Tk 7 lakh) [G0057, G0058, G0001]. Payment onto a prepaid card in your name counts, even a card without an IBAN (international bank account number) [G0060]. Your signature on the payslip (busta paga) does not prove you were paid, so keep your bank statements as proof (see If you aren't paid) [G0059].

1. **Open a conto di base (basic bank account).** Banks and Poste Italiane must offer it to everyone staying legally in the EU, including asylum seekers and refugees [G0050]. It gives transfers, salary credit, a bank card, cash withdrawals and deposits, and automatic bill payment [G0048]. It is free if your ISEE (indicatore della situazione economica equivalente, the official family means figure) is below EUR 11,600 (about Tk 16.2 lakh) a year; above that it still costs much less than an ordinary account [G0047, G0049, G0001]. You can't open one if you already have another current account in Italy, unless you close it, or use it for a business [G0049].

   You'll need:
   - your codice fiscale (Italian tax code), even a numeric provisional one [G0053];
   - your permesso di soggiorno (residence permit), or the post-office receipt for your permit application or renewal with your photo on it; an Italian identity card is not needed [G0054, G0055].

   1. Go to a bank branch or post office and say: "Vorrei aprire un conto di base. Il mio ISEE è sotto 11.600 euro, quindi deve essere gratuito." ("I want to open a basic account. My ISEE is below EUR 11,600, so it must be free.") [G0047]
   2. Show the codice fiscale and the permit or receipt [G0053, G0054].

   **If it fails:** in 2026 Banca d'Italia (Italy's central bank) found that banks failed to offer or hid basic accounts, and that their computer systems shut out migrants with numeric provisional tax codes or permit receipts; only 180,000 basic accounts existed at the end of 2025 against 7 million people eligible [G0051, G0052]. If a bank refuses your numeric codice fiscale, permit or receipt, go to a patronato (a free welfare advice office, most run by trade unions), a trade union or a migrants' association such as ASGI (Associazione per gli Studi Giuridici sull'Immigrazione, a migration lawyers' association) [G0056]. Then complain in writing (see If the money doesn't arrive, which gives the same complaint route for banks) [G0061].

### How to send

You'll need:
- for a bKash wallet (a Bangladeshi mobile-money account): the receiver's bKash number and full name exactly as registered with bKash [G0031];

1. **Check the company is licensed.** Banks and payment institutions are on Banca d'Italia's public registers at [infostat.bancaditalia.it/GIAVAInquiry-public/ng/](https://infostat.bancaditalia.it/GIAVAInquiry-public/ng/); type the company's name [G0079]. In 2020 the Guardia di Finanza (Italy's financial police) seized a payment institution and six money-transfer shops in Rome run by Bangladeshis, accused of moving over EUR 20 million to Bangladesh without identifying the senders [G0041]. The case grew out of a 2019 investigation into a Bangladeshi network that, for profit, helped compatriots stay in Italy illegally [G0042].
2. **Choose the channel.** bKash lists these partners for sending from Italy straight to a bKash wallet: Ria, Remitly, National Exchange, Placid Express, WorldRemit, Wise, NEC Money, Taptap Send, Western Union, MoneyGram and ACE Money Transfer [G0028]. In the World Bank's survey of August-September 2025, sending EUR 140 (about Tk 19,500) cost, fee and exchange-rate margin together [G0033, G0001]:
   - 2.98% (fee EUR 0.99, about Tk 140) online with a debit card through Poste Italiane's Western Union service, for cash pickup, the cheapest [G0035];
   - 4.57% (fee EUR 2.50, about Tk 350) in cash at a National Exchange Company agent, to a bank account [G0036];
   - 4.81% (fee EUR 3, about Tk 420) in cash at a Janata Exchange Company branch, to a bank account [G0037];
   - 5.46% (fee EUR 5, about Tk 700) in cash at a post office through Poste Italiane's MoneyGram service [G0038];
   - 22.24% at Extrabanca (fee EUR 25, about Tk 3,500) and 33.57% by bank transfer from Intesa Sanpaolo (fee EUR 47, about Tk 6,600) [G0039, G0040, G0001].
3. **Compare the rate, not only the fee.** The total cost is the fee plus the gap between the market rate and the rate the company gives you [G0035]. On 10 November 2026 the market rate was about Tk 139.6 per euro [G0001].
4. **Send.** At the counter, give the bKash number and registered name (or the bank details) and hand over the cash; or send from the company's app with your card [G0031, G0035]. A bKash wallet takes up to Tk 2.5 lakh per transfer, any number of times [G0029].
5. **The family collects.** bKash remittance can be cashed out for Tk 7 per thousand at BRAC Bank, City Bank and Q-Cash ATMs, and the 2.5% government incentive is added [G0029, G0030].

### Tax on your pay

IRPEF (imposta sul reddito delle persone fisiche, Italy's income tax) is 23% on income up to EUR 28,000 a year (about Tk 39 lakh) and 33% from EUR 28,000 to 50,000 (about Tk 70 lakh), after the 2026 Budget Law cut the middle rate from 35% [G0066, G0067, G0065, G0001]. Since 2025 a worker who is not an Italian, EU or EEA (European Economic Area) citizen gets no tax deduction for family members living abroad, such as a wife, children or parents in Bangladesh [G0068]. The deduction for parents applies only to parents living with you, up to EUR 750 (about Tk 1 lakh) [G0069, G0001]. The article of the income tax code that sets these family deductions (TUIR article 12) is repealed from 1 January 2027 [M0016].

## If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job

In Italy, INAIL (Istituto nazionale per l'assicurazione contro gli infortuni sul lavoro, the national work-accident insurer) pays for work injuries, and INPS pays unemployment benefit [L0061, L0063, L0072]. In Bangladesh the Wage Earners' Welfare Board (the Welfare Board, the government board for migrants' welfare) pays grants to members' families and runs the Probashi call centre, 16135 [B0107, B0144]. The Bangladesh Embassy in Rome and Consulate General in Milan issue the papers to send a body home [B0091, B0110].

### If you are injured

1. **Call 112 in an emergency,** Italy's emergency number [V0042].
2. **Tell the employer at once,** even for a small injury; if you don't and the employer doesn't know, the days before the employer learns of it are not paid [L0059, L0060].
3. **Know what INAIL pays while you can't work:** see Injury at work [L0061, L0062].
4. **If the employer won't report the injury or you worked off the books,** a patronato serves workers working legally in Italy, and the national anti-trafficking helpline, 800 290 290, takes calls from victims of exploitation, anonymously, 24 hours a day [L0012, D0114].
5. **Going home injured.** Welfare Board membership covers bringing home workers in distress, and the Board's ambulance takes injured or sick workers free from Dhaka or Chattogram airport [B0107, B0169].

### If a worker dies

These steps are for a relative or friend in Italy and the family in Bangladesh.

1. **Get the death certificate** from the Italian hospital or the comune (town hall) [B0111].
2. **Ask the Embassy or Consulate for the nulla osta (no-objection letter) to send the body home.** It is free; the mission pays the air freight if the worker was a Welfare Board member, or on application if the family is poor [B0091, B0092, B0110]. Email Rome's labour welfare wing, welfare.rome@gmail.com, or in Milan fslbcgmilan@gmail.com and mission.milan@mofa.gov.bd [B0093, B0112].

   You'll need [B0093, B0111]:
   - the death certificate;
   - the original passport and a copy;
   - the name, phone number and ID of a Bangladeshi in Italy who handles the transport;
   - the name, phone number and ID of the person receiving the body in Bangladesh;
   - a copy of the Welfare Board membership card, if any.

   Sample email: "My [relation], [name], passport [number], worked for [employer, phone] in [town], Italy, and died on [date] at [hospital or place]. ### If you lose the job

1. **Your permit stays valid.** Losing the job, even by resigning, does not cancel your permit or your family's (see Your residence permit, If you lose your job) [L0014, L0015].
2. **If you were dismissed,** challenge it in writing within 60 days (see If you aren't paid) [L0093].
3. **Claim NASpI (Nuova Assicurazione Sociale per l'Impiego, unemployment pay).**

   Who gets it: employees who lost the job against their will, with at least 13 weeks of contributions in the last four years [L0063, L0066]. Not people who resigned, unless for just cause (forced out by the employer's conduct); not holders of a seasonal permit; not fixed-term farm workers [L0070, L0071, L0064, L0065].

   How much and how long: 75% of average monthly pay up to a yearly reference amount, falling 3% a month from the sixth month, for half the weeks you contributed in the last four years [L0068, L0069, L0067].

   1. Apply online on inps.it within 68 days of the job ending, or lose the right; apply within 8 days to be paid from the eighth day [L0072, L0075].
   2. If you can't apply online, call the INPS contact centre, 803 164 (free from a landline) or 06 164 164 (from a mobile), or go to a patronato [L0073].
   3. Give a bank or postal account in your own name or joint name (see Your salary and bank account) [L0074].

   A trip to Bangladesh for reasons other than looking for work keeps NASpI only while you keep its conditions [L0076].
4. **Check your final pay,** including the TFR (see Changing employer or leaving, Leaving at the end of the job) [L0028].

## Coming home

- **Leaving Italy with cash.** Declare EUR 10,000 (about Tk 14 lakh) or more in cash, or the same in other currencies, to Italian customs on the EU cash declaration form when you leave the EU [G0070, G0073, G0001]. Gold bars of at least 99.5% gold and coins of at least 90% gold count as cash [G0071]. Customs can act below EUR 10,000 if they suspect the money is linked to crime, and not declaring is penalised [G0072].
- **Customs in Dhaka.**
  - Up to 2 used mobile phones and 1 new phone once a year, without duty or tax [G0074].
  - At most 117 g (10 tola) of gold, one bar, once a year, declared at customs [G0075].
  - Declare foreign currency over USD 10,000 on the FMJ form [G0076].
- **Airport help.** The Welfare Board's desks at Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet help with customs clearance and lost luggage, and take complaints from workers coming home after abuse or fraud [B0167, B0168, B0137]. Dhaka desk: +8802 8901040, 01404 413388; Chattogram: 01913631797; Sylhet: 01558277904 [B0170, B0138]. Women returning within a year after abuse or fraud get Tk 5,000, and sick or injured workers get a free ambulance from Dhaka or Chattogram airport [B0137, B0169].
- **A place to stay.** The Wage Earners' Centre near Shahjalal airport costs Tk 100 to enter and Tk 200 a night; book at wec.wewb.gov.bd [B0171]. The Probashi Lounge at Shahjalal airport gives free wifi and telephone and cheaper food to workers who show BMET clearance or Welfare Board membership [B0172].
- **If your family needs legal help in Bangladesh,** apply to the Welfare Board directly, or through the expatriate welfare branch of the Deputy Commissioner's office (the district administration) or the DEMO (District Employment and Manpower Office, BMET's district office) [B0173].
- **A business loan.** Probashi Kallyan Bank (PKB, the government bank for migrants) lends returnees up to Tk 50 lakh, up to Tk 3 lakh without collateral; you need an up-to-date trade licence [B0165, B0166]. Helpline 16238, or +880 9677-787878 from abroad [B0164].
- **BRAC's help.** At Dhaka airport BRAC's Migration Programme has given returnees from Italy emergency help, counselling and money to get home, followed by counselling, training and financial help [U0061, U0062].
- **Children.** Welfare Board membership gives members' children scholarships and a migrant quota for school admission [B0107].

### Pension after you go home

A worker on a non-seasonal contract who goes home for good keeps the pension rights earned in Italy, even if Italy has no social security agreement with the home country [L0001, L0002, L0011]. Seasonal workers are excluded [L0002, L0011].

- **Hired after 1 January 1996:** you get a pension at Italy's pensionable age, given as 67 for 2023-2024 and rising with life expectancy, even with fewer than 20 years of contributions [L0003, L0004].
- **Hired before 1996:** you need 20 years of contributions [L0005].

At pensionable age, apply in one of these ways [L0006, L0007, L0009, L0008]:
- online, if you have INPS login details, attaching documents authenticated by the Italian consulate, then posting them to INPS, Direzione provinciale di Perugia, via Canali 5, 06122 Perugia;
- at the Italian consulate where you live, if you have no login;
- through a patronato.
