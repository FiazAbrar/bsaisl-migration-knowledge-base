## a. Draft doc text

### Your rights at work

#### Equal treatment and your contract

A worker with a valid permit has, by law, the same treatment and rights at work as an Italian worker [L0019]. Minimum pay and overtime premiums are set by the national collective contract (CCNL, contratto collettivo nazionale di lavoro) for your sector [L0023, L0034, L0035].

> **Need to know**

#### Pay: the minimum in your sector

Minimums in the sectors where most Bangladeshi workers are hired, gross before tax and contributions:

- **Farm work (operai agricoli):** national floor for Area 3 (common workers) from 1 June 2026 is EUR 1,042.43 a month, and for nursery and flower workers EUR 7.99 an hour [L0032, L0033]. Real farm pay is set in each province's contract, which may not go below these floors and rose 3.4% on 1 June 2026, with another 1.7% from 1 January 2027 [L0034, L0030, L0031].
- **Construction (Edilizia Industria):** a common labourer (operaio comune) earns at least EUR 9.48 an hour nationally from 1 March 2026, plus provincial extras; in Ragusa, for example, EUR 10.45 an hour or EUR 1,807.23 a month for 40 hours a week over 5 days [L0035, L0039, L0038]. Holiday pay and the Christmas bonus are set aside at 18.5% of pay and paid to you by the Cassa Edile (the builders' fund), not by the employer [L0036, L0037].
- **Hotels (CCNL Turismo):** from May 2026 at least EUR 1,412.60 a month at level 7 and EUR 1,507.45 at level 6 (EUR 8.76 an hour), paid 14 times a year, with the 13th month in December and the 14th in July [L0040, L0041, L0042]. Seasonal hotel workers get the same minimums, and their 13th, 14th and unused leave are paid at the end with the TFR [L0044, L0045].
- **Bars, restaurants, pizzerias, canteens and catering:** these use a separate contract, the FIPE CCNL, not the hotel one [L0043].
- **Cleaning (pulizie e multiservizi):** a level-2 cleaner earns at least EUR 1,413.50 a month full-time from 1 October 2026, or EUR 8.17 an hour; much cleaning work is part-time, and pay is cut in proportion to hours [L0046, L0047, L0048].

#### Your payslip, and the "paga globale" trick

Check every payslip (busta paga) against your own record: the Labour Inspectorate takes claims for wages whether or not they appear on the payslip [L0077]. A known trick in subcontracting is the "paga globale": a flat sum that folds in holiday pay, leave and TFR, while the payslip carries invented items that are never paid [L0049, L0050]. Among Fincantieri shipyard subcontractors, whose workers included Bangladeshis, the Guardia di Finanza (financial police) found 1,951 workers paid irregularly, about EUR 6 million paid off the books, and 383 workers on under EUR 7 gross an hour who accepted it because they needed the job to renew their permit [L0049, L0052, L0053, L0051].

Keep your own record of days and hours worked and every payment received; the Labour Inspectorate asks for exactly this (see If you aren't paid, item 2) [L0084].

#### Hours, rest and leave

Normal hours are 40 a week [L0020]. With overtime, the average may not exceed 48 hours a week, counted over up to four months [L0021]. Overtime is counted separately and paid with the premium set in your CCNL; where the CCNL says nothing, it needs your agreement and is capped at 250 hours a year [L0023, L0022]. You are entitled to 11 hours' rest in a row every 24 hours and at least 24 hours in a row every 7 days, normally Sunday [L0024, L0025]. Paid leave is at least four weeks a year, and it can't be swapped for money except when the job ends [L0026, L0027].

#### Sick pay

1. **Go to your family doctor (medico di base);** the doctor sends the sickness certificate online to INPS (the national social security institute), so you don't have to send it to the employer yourself [L0054].
2. **Stay at home during check hours,** 10:00 to 12:00 and 17:00 to 19:00 every day on the certificate, weekends and holidays included, because INPS sends doctors to check [L0058].

What you get: INPS pays from day 4, at 50% of average daily pay up to day 20 and 66.66% from day 21 to day 180; the first three days are paid by the employer only if your CCNL says so [L0055, L0056, L0057].

#### Injury at work

**Tell the employer at once,** even for a small injury; if you don't and the employer doesn't know, the days before the employer learns of it are not paid [L0059, L0060].

What you get: INAIL (the national work-accident insurer) pays 60% of daily pay from the fourth day after the accident while you can't work, and 75% from day 91 [L0061, L0062]. For long-term disability, death and what to do if the employer hides the accident, see If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job.

> **Need to know**
> - Who issues the INAIL injury certificate to a worker (pronto soccorso or family doctor), and must the worker keep or send a copy?

#### End-of-service pay (TFR)

Every employee gets the TFR (trattamento di fine rapporto, end-of-service payment) whenever the job ends, however it ends [L0028]. Each year adds the year's pay divided by 13.5, roughly one month's pay [L0029]. A missing TFR can be claimed through the Labour Inspectorate (If you aren't paid, item 2) [L0079].

### What can get you jailed or deported

#### No valid permit

Entering or staying in Italy without a valid permit is a crime punished by a fine of EUR 5,000 to 10,000 [L0098]. If your permit expired more than 60 days ago and you did not apply to renew it, the Prefect (Prefetto, the government's representative in each province) orders expulsion [L0099]. Renewal is requested from the Questore (head of the Questura, the provincial police immigration office) of the province where you live, at least 90 days before expiry (see Your residence permit) [L0109]. If police ask, you must show your passport and permit; refusing without good reason can mean arrest of up to a year and a fine of up to EUR 2,000 [L0113].

#### Expulsion and CPR detention

You can ask for 7 to 30 days to leave by yourself, including through an assisted voluntary return programme [L0100, L0101]. If the police cannot remove you at once, the Questore can hold you in a CPR (Centro di Permanenza per i Rimpatri, repatriation detention centre): three months at first, extendable by three more and then up to twelve more months in total [L0106, L0107, L0108]. At the court hearing that confirms removal, you have a right to a lawyer, free legal aid and an interpreter [L0105]. An expulsion brings a 3 to 5 year ban recorded in the Schengen Information System, which closes every EU and Schengen country, not only Italy; coming back without authorisation is punished by 1 to 4 years in prison [L0103, L0104, L0102]. For CPRs, returns to Bangladesh and the Albania centres, see Irregular routes and undocumented workers.

#### Fake documents

Forging a visa, permit or contratto di soggiorno, using one, or forging papers to obtain them, is punished by 1 to 6 years in prison [L0112]. For how fake papers are sold, see Scams.

#### Drugs and crime

Selling or carrying drugs to sell is punished by 6 to 20 years and a fine of EUR 26,000 to 260,000, or 6 months to 5 years for small amounts [L0117, L0118]. Even possession for your own use brings administrative penalties, including suspension of your passport [L0119]. A conviction for a serious crime, even before appeal, bars a permit [L0115]. A judge can replace a prison sentence of up to two years with expulsion [L0116].

#### Working without a permit

An employer who hires someone without a valid permit faces 6 months to 3 years in prison and a EUR 5,000 fine per worker [L0017]. A permit for employment lets you do other legal work too [L0114].

### If you aren't paid

**If you are not paid, paid less than your CCNL, or your overtime, 13th month or TFR is missing, in this order:**

1. **Write to the employer by registered letter with return receipt (raccomandata A/R)** asking for the money; this formal notice (messa in mora) protects your claim from expiring, and the Inspectorate later asks for it [L0086].
   Sample wording: "Io sottoscritto [name], nato a [place] il [date], codice fiscale [number], dipendente dal [start date] con mansione di [job], chiedo il pagamento delle retribuzioni non pagate per i mesi di [months], pari a euro [amount], oltre a [straordinari / tredicesima / TFR]. In mancanza, mi rivolgerò all'Ispettorato del Lavoro e al giudice." ("I, [name] ... ask for payment of unpaid wages for [months] ... Otherwise I will go to the Labour Inspectorate and the court.")
2. **File a request for inspection (Modulo INL 31) with the Labour Inspectorate (Ispettorato Territoriale del Lavoro, ITL)** of the province where you work [L0082].
   You'll need [L0083, L0084]:
   - a copy of your passport or permit (without it the complaint is not accepted);
   - copies of payslips, bank transfers or receipts, and your own sheet of days and hours worked;
   - the names of witnesses, if any.
   1. Download Modulo INL 31 from ispettorato.gov.it; it exists in English and in Bangla [L0089].
   2. Tick what is owed: unpaid wages (on or off the payslip), 13th/14th month, TFR, unpaid overtime, or work "in nero" (off the books) [L0077, L0078, L0079, L0080].
   3. Write the facts in Bangla if you need to, but add Italian too [L0085].
   4. Send it by email or PEC to your province's ITL (addresses at ispettorato.gov.it/contatti) or hand it in [L0088].
   What happens: the ITL may call you and the employer to a conciliation, where a union or a professional can assist you [L0087, L0090]. If inspectors find money owed, they order the employer to pay (diffida accertativa), and the employer has 30 days to seek conciliation [L0091, L0092]. Form 31 is not for dismissals (item 4) [L0081].
3. **Go to a union or a patronato** (welfare advice offices, many run by the unions), which serve workers with a permit, can assist you at the conciliation and can send resignation forms and NASpI claims for you [L0012, L0090, L0097, L0073].
4. **If you are dismissed,** challenge the dismissal in writing within 60 days of receiving it (a union letter counts), then file in the labour court (tribunale, giudice del lavoro) or ask for conciliation within 180 days, or you lose the right [L0093, L0094].

### Changing employer or leaving

#### If you want to change employer

A permit for employment can be used for other legal work, and losing or leaving a job does not cancel it [L0114, L0014].

#### If you resign

Resign only online, on the Ministry of Labour form at lavoro.gov.it; a paper letter has no effect [L0095]. A patronato, union or the ITL can send it for you, and you can withdraw it within seven days [L0097, L0096]. Resigning loses NASpI unemployment pay, unless you resign for just cause, for example because wages are not paid (If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job) [L0070, L0071].

#### If you lose the job

Losing the job, even by resigning, does not cancel your permit or your family's [L0014, L0015]. Register as available for work (dichiarazione di immediata disponibilità, DID); the NASpI claim counts as this [L0013, L0063]. You may then stay for the rest of the permit, and at least one year (not on a seasonal permit), or as long as NASpI lasts if longer; after that, a new permit depends on income [L0013, L0016].

### Leaving at the end of the job

1. **Check your last payslip** for unused leave, the 13th (and 14th) month pro rata, and the TFR [L0027, L0045, L0028].
2. **Claim NASpI within 68 days** if the job ended involuntarily (see If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job) [L0072].
3. **If you leave Italy for good,** your INPS contributions stay yours for a pension (see Pension after you go home) [L0001].

### Going home on leave

**With only the renewal receipt** (permit expired, renewal filed at the post office), the Polizia di Stato allows you to leave and come back showing your valid passport, the post-office receipt and the expired permit (original or copy) [L0120, L0121]. For people waiting for their first permit, the police require the same border crossing both ways and no transit through another Schengen country, and the border officer stamps both the passport and the receipt [L0122, L0123, L0124].

**If you are stuck in Bangladesh** (permit expired, lost or stolen), apply for a re-entry visa (visto di reingresso) at the Italian Embassy in Dhaka; it is issued only after the Questura in Italy clears it [L0130].
You'll need [L0131, L0127, L0125, L0126, L0128, L0129]:
- the national (type D) visa form and one ICAO photo (3.5 x 4 cm, white background, taken in the last 6 months);
- a passport valid at least 15 more months, with two blank visa pages;
- if the permit expired no more than 60 days before you apply: a copy of the expired permit and the renewal receipt;
- if it was lost or stolen: an apostilled police report from the country where it happened;
- proof of the date you left Italy (exit stamp, or the ticket if the passport was lost);
- a letter in English or Italian explaining why you need to return;
- a flight booking.

### If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job

#### Lost the job: NASpI unemployment pay

**Who gets it:** employees who lost the job against their will, with at least 13 weeks of contributions in the last four years; not people who resigned (except for just cause), not seasonal-permit holders, and not fixed-term farm workers [L0063, L0066, L0070, L0071, L0064, L0065].

**How much and how long:** 75% of average monthly pay (above a yearly reference amount, less), falling 3% a month from the sixth month, for half the weeks you contributed in the last four years [L0068, L0069, L0067].

1. **Apply online on inps.it within 68 days** of the job ending; apply within 8 days to be paid from the eighth day [L0072, L0075].
   Or through the INPS contact centre, 803 164 (free from a landline) or 06 164 164 (mobile), or a patronato [L0073].
2. **Give a bank or postal account in your own name** [L0074].

A trip to Bangladesh while on NASpI keeps the payment only if you keep to the Centre for Employment's obligations [L0076].

#### If a worker dies

For bringing the body home, the death certificate and the Wage Earners' Welfare Board grant, see Bangladesh Embassy services and Coming home. If the worker dies after pensionable age, the family can get a survivors' pension under the general rules [L0010].

### Pension after you go home

The law keeps your Italian pension rights when you return home for good, even if there is no social security agreement with your home country [L0001, L0011].

- **Hired after 1 January 1996:** you get a pension at Italy's pensionable age, given as 67 for 2023-2024 and rising with life expectancy, even with fewer than 20 years of contributions [L0003, L0004].
- **Hired before 1996:** you need 20 years of contributions [L0005].
- **Seasonal workers** are excluded from this rule [L0002, L0011].

**At pensionable age, apply** in one of these ways [L0006, L0007, L0009, L0008]:
- online, if you have INPS login credentials, attaching documents authenticated by the Italian consulate, then posting them to INPS, Direzione provinciale di Perugia, via Canali 5, 06122 Perugia;
- at the Italian consulate where you live, if you have no credentials;
- through a patronato.
