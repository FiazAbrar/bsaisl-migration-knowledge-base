## Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal)

The Decreto Flussi ("flows decree") is Italy's yearly quota of foreign workers: an employer in Italy applies online for one named worker on a fixed "click day", the SUI (Sportello Unico per l'Immigrazione, the immigration desk at the Prefettura, the central government's office in each province) issues a nulla osta (work authorisation), and only then does the worker apply for a visa in Dhaka [W0030, W0031, W0011, W0036, W0040]. Bangladesh is one of the countries with a cooperation agreement that share 25,000 reserved non-seasonal places a year in 2026, 2027 and 2028, inside a national non-seasonal quota of 76,200 [W0023, W0024, W0022]. About 10,000 Bangladeshis got Italian work visas in 2025, and the Embassy of Italy in Dhaka expects a similar number in 2026 [W0091].

### Step 1: Find a real job

A worker in Bangladesh can't apply for a nulla osta; only an Italian employer can, for a worker it names at its own choice, so every legal job starts with an employer who knows your name [N0145, N0113]. There is no public list of workers and no list of certified agencies an employer must use [N0113, N0120]. How names reach employers, and who sells them, is in Background (see How a job reaches you, and who takes a cut).

1. **Register on the Overseas Employment Platform (OEP),** the website of BMET (the Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training) for job-seekers [B0124]. BMET clears no one for work abroad without it, and the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare warns never to go without BMET clearance (Step 6, item 2) [B0127, B0132].

   You'll need [B0123, B0124]:

   - your NID (national ID card);
   - a phone that can receive codes;
   - Tk 200 for the government fee.

   1. Open [employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration](https://employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration) and type your name in English exactly as on your NID, your mobile and NID numbers, and your date of birth, division and district as on the NID; type the captcha, accept the terms and press "যাচাই করুন" (Verify) [B0123].
   2. Finish the form and pay the Tk 200 fee [B0124].
   3. Take 2 printed copies of the registration slip to your DEMO (District Employment and Manpower Office, BMET's district office) to give your fingerprints [B0124]. DEMO addresses and phones are on BMET's list at [bmet.gov.bd](https://bmet.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922e139933eb65569e2af6c) [B0125].

   **If it fails:** call the government's migrant call centre, 16135 (+880 9610102030 from abroad) [B0144].

2. **Know there is no government recruiter for Italy.** Bangladesh and Italy signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on Migration and Mobility in Dhaka in May 2025; it covers legal migration and the return of people with no right to stay, and sets no number of workers [B0027, B0030]. Bangladesh asked Italy for larger quotas for skilled and semi-skilled workers in May 2025, and for Italian language and skills training in August 2026 [B0028, B0039]. A job in Italy still comes through an Italian employer's nulla osta (Step 3) [W0031, W0036].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Has BOESL (Bangladesh Overseas Employment and Services Limited, the state recruiter) ever posted an Italy job circular?
   > - Has the 2025 MoU's joint committee met, and has it given Bangladesh a share of the 25,000 reserved places?

3. **Or train for a place outside the quota.** Under Article 23 of Italy's immigration law, a worker who completes a pre-departure course approved by Italy's Ministry of Labour (basic Italian, civic education and job skills) can enter outside the quota; the course can be done in Bangladesh, and applications run all year with no click day [N0029, N0030, N0031, W0120]. Several hundred Bangladeshis a year now go this way [W0096]. The visa steps for this route are in Work visa: highly skilled and out-of-quota (EU Blue Card, Article 27) [W0144].

   - **The first projects in Bangladesh** were set up by Italian private firms: Frontiere Skills and Format, with about 300 students enrolled in November 2026 [N0024, N0025]. Frontiere Skills' TASTE project trains restaurant and hotel workers at Hotel Regency, Dhaka; Format trains textile workers and says its training is free [N0036, N0032].
   - **The EU Talent Partnership,** an EU-funded project (EUR 3 million, about Tk 41.9 crore, 2024 to 2027) run by BMET with the ILO (International Labour Organization), trains workers for EU jobs [B0040, G0001]. By July 2026, 698 trainees held NSDA (National Skills Development Authority) certificates, mainly for Italy, and many were waiting for employer interviews [B0041, B0042]. Its next phase adds Italian language training and a 10-day pre-departure orientation [B0043].
   - **The Italian Embassy** said in October 2026 that it would hold a special event in Dhaka in November 2026 to recruit workers through language and skills training [B0046].

   > **Need to know**
   > - How does a worker get a place on a Frontiere Skills or Format course: where to apply, what selection, and what is paid at each stage?
   > - How does a worker apply for a Talent Partnership course, at which TTCs (Technical Training Centres), and is it free?
   > - How many Talent Partnership graduates have received an Italian nulla osta?
   > - Where and when is the Embassy's November 2026 recruitment event, and who can attend?

4. **If a relative, broker or agency brings you an employer, check the employer can legally hire you.** The nulla osta is refused or revoked if:

   - the employer has no real business activity or was sanctioned for undeclared work [D0046];
   - it got a nulla osta in the last three years and then did not sign the contratto di soggiorno (contract of stay), unless that was not its fault [W0035];
   - it was convicted in the last five years of people smuggling, caporalato (illegal gangmaster labour) or employing undocumented workers [W0050];
   - the documents were forged or obtained by fraud [W0038].

   A private employer may file no more than three nulla osta requests a year; larger numbers must go through an employer organisation, a labour consultant or similar professional, or a temporary-work agency, who must keep them in line with the firm's turnover and staff [W0034, P0038]. Networks have filed applications in the names of employers who did not know their names were used, and through a CAF (tax assistance centre) in Taranto with compliant employers [D0098, D0100, D0101, D0127]. A worker who arrives for a company that does not exist falls into irregular status [D0091].

   Ask whoever brings the job for:

   - the employer's full name and address, and the job on offer, which go into its application (Step 2, item 1) [W0031];
   - the name of the labour consultant or employer organisation filing for it, if any [W0034, W0051].

   > **Need to know**
   > - How can a worker in Bangladesh check that an Italian employer exists and is active (for example a visura camerale, the Chamber of Commerce registration extract, from registroimprese.it), and what does it cost?
   > - Can a worker ask a labour consultant or employer organisation in Italy to confirm it filed an application in the worker's name?

5. **Know what you should pay.** Bangladesh's migration cost caps cover 14 countries, and Italy is not one of them; Italy sets no migration cost for the worker beyond the fees [B0049, N0164]. Pay any Bangladeshi agency only into its own bank account, against a receipt [B0128]. The Embassy tells anyone who uses an agency to check its reputation, take a receipt for every payment and know every document presented in their name [M0008]. The official costs, each in the step that owns it:

   - OEP registration: Tk 200 (Step 1, item 1) [B0124];
   - passport: Tk 4,025 to Tk 10,350 (Step 4, item 1) [B0145];
   - apostille of certificates: Tk 200 a page (Step 4, item 3) [B0156];
   - police clearance, if asked: Tk 1,500 (Step 4, item 2) [B0153];
   - the visa at VFS Global, the Embassy's visa application centre: Tk 21,830 (Step 5, item 2) [M0018, M0019, M0020, W0099];
   - BMET clearance: Tk 5,500 welfare fee, Tk 1,000 insurance, Tk 250 clearance fee and Tk 250 or Tk 400 source tax (Step 6, item 2) [B0140, B0131, B0133];
   - in Italy, the residence permit application: EUR 116.46 or EUR 126.46 (about Tk 16,260 or Tk 17,650) (Step 7, item 3) [K0021, K0022, W0135, W0136, G0001].

   What people really pay, and to whom, is in Background (see What you pay, and what you earn and keep).

6. **If you need to borrow,** Probashi Kallyan Bank (PKB), the government bank for migrant workers, lends up to Tk 3 lakh to people aged 18 to 60 [B0160, B0161]. You first open a PKB savings account with at least Tk 500, and one of your guarantors must be a family member or close relative [B0162, B0163]. PKB helpline: 16238, or +880 9677-787878 from abroad [B0164].

7. **If a broker or agency takes your money,** complain in writing to BMET's Director General with your evidence; it is free and meant to be settled within 30 working days [B0139]. Hand it in at BMET, 89/2 Kakrail, Dhaka-1000 [B0174]. Then, in this order:

   1. BMET's Additional Director General (Employment): 02-48321949, 01720105492, adgemp@bmet.gov.bd [B0135].
   2. The Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare's grievance officer: 02-41030224, 01712992086, adsadmin@probashi.gov.bd (30 working days) [B0177].
   3. Call 16135 for advice at any stage [B0144].

   The Embassy says anyone asking for money beyond the published fees should be reported to the authorities [M0007].

### Step 2: The job offer and contract

1. **Get a named offer from the employer, not a "visa".** The employer's application to the SUI names you and must include [W0031, W0032, W0068]:

   - a proposed contratto di soggiorno (contract of stay): the job's conditions, the employer's guarantee of housing and its promise to pay your journey home [W0031, W0067, W0068];
   - documents on where you will live, signed digitally; a hotel, or a fixed dormitory on a building site with the employer's own declaration, is accepted [W0031];
   - an asseverazione: a certificate from a labour consultant or employer organisation that the employer follows the CCNL (contratto collettivo nazionale di lavoro, the national collective contract for its sector, which sets pay and hours) and can afford the number of workers it asks for, judged on its assets, turnover and staff [W0051, W0052].

   Minimum pay and hours under each CCNL are in Your rights at work [W0051].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Do Italian employers send the worker the proposed contratto di soggiorno before click day, or does the worker first see it after arrival?
   > - What job titles and monthly pay appear on the offers Bangladeshis actually receive in 2026 (construction, food industry, logistics, hotels)?

2. **Give the employer exactly what is in your passport.** The visa is delayed if the name, surname, sex or date of birth on the nulla osta do not match your passport, so send a clear scan of the passport data page and check the spelling the employer types [W0113]. Get the passport first (Step 4, item 1) [W0113].

3. **Ask the employer to forward everything.** The employer must promptly tell you of every communication it receives about your nulla osta [W0018, P0026]. You can send this message [W0018, W0113]:

   "Dear [employer name], please send me (1) a copy of the nulla osta with its number and the SUI (Prefettura) that issued it, and (2) every email you receive from the Sportello Unico about my application. My name must be exactly as in my passport: [SURNAME], [Given names], born [DD/MM/YYYY], passport [number]. Thank you, [your name], [WhatsApp number]."

   **If it fails:** checks on a nulla osta are made in Italy, so without the employer's copies you can check little yourself (Step 5, "Check it is real") [W0090, W0018]. The Embassy tells applicants to know every document presented in their name [M0008].

### Step 3: The employer gets your nulla osta

The employer, or its labour consultant or employer organisation, does every step below on the Interior Ministry's ALI portal; you wait in Bangladesh [W0016, W0030, W0034]. The dates are for the 2027 round, the next one [W0005, W0011].

1. **The employer checks the Italian labour market first.** It asks the Centro per l'Impiego (public job centre) whether a worker already in Italy is available; if the centre does not answer within 8 days, the check counts as negative and the employer may hire from abroad [W0029, W0033].

2. **The employer pre-fills the application** on the Portale Servizi ALI ([portaleservizi.dlci.interno.it](https://portaleservizi.dlci.interno.it/AliSportello/ali/home.htm)) from 9:00 on 23 October 2026 to 20:00 on 7 December 2026, on form B2020 for non-seasonal work [W0005, W0006, W0015, W0016]. Pre-filling is compulsory, and the authorities check the truth of the employer's declarations when it logs in [P0036, P0037]. From 9 to 13 December 2026 the portal reopens only to complete and save forms already started; no new applications can be added [W0007, W0008]. The INL (Ispettorato Nazionale del Lavoro, the labour inspectorate) may check pre-filled applications in advance, and can run random checks on employers with the Agenzia delle Entrate (tax agency) [P0010, W0053].

3. **The employer clicks "send" on click day.** For 2027 [W0009, W0010, W0011, W0012, W0013]:

   - non-seasonal work (form B2020): 16 February 2027, from 9:00;
   - domestic and care work (form A-bis): 18 February 2027 (see Work visa: domestic and care worker);
   - seasonal agriculture: 12 January 2027; seasonal tourism: 9 February 2027 (see Work visa: seasonal worker).

   Applications are handled mostly in order of arrival, and the quota is usually gone within the first ten minutes [W0142, N0150]. They can be sent until 31 December 2027 while quota remains [W0014]. The 2027 national figures are 76,200 non-seasonal places (14,000 of them for domestic and care work), 89,000 seasonal and 650 self-employed, 165,850 in all [W0001, W0002, W0003, W0004].

4. **The quota is shared out, then the SUI decides.** The Ministry of Labour splits the quota by province within 10 days of click day [W0028, W0140]. From that allocation the SUI has at most 60 days to issue the nulla osta [W0036, W0140]. Applications that have not entered the quota six months after 31 December of the year lapse and are archived [W0027]. A nulla osta is valid for up to six months from issue [W0037].

5. **Know the extra checks on Bangladeshis.** For the 2026-2028 rounds, a nulla osta for a worker from Bangladesh, Pakistan, Sri Lanka or Morocco is never issued automatically when the time runs out: it always needs a favourable opinion from the Questura (the provincial police immigration office), and the INL first checks the employer [D0024, D0025, D0026]. The earlier rule that singled out Bangladesh, Pakistan and Sri Lanka ran until 31 December 2025, pending a Foreign Ministry decree naming high-risk countries [W0054, W0056]. Why, and what happened to the nulla osta frozen in October 2024, is in Background (see Status) [M0001].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Has the Foreign Ministry named high-risk countries, and is Bangladesh on the list in 2026?
   > - Do all Prefetture apply the Questura opinion and INL check to Bangladeshi non-seasonal nulla osta in 2026, as the Cosenza Prefettura's circular does?
   > - How does the employer see on ALI that its application "entered the quota", and what does the screen say?
   > - In 2026, how many B2020 applications for Bangladeshis were sent on click day, and how many got a nulla osta?
   > - How long after click day did Bangladeshis' 2026 nulla osta actually arrive?

**What you get back:** a copy of the nulla osta, which the employer gives you; you upload it when you register for the visa (Step 5, item 1) [M0030].

### Step 4: Passport, police clearance and certificates

1. **Get an e-passport** before the employer files, so the nulla osta carries your exact details (Step 2, item 2) [W0113]. Over age 20 you need your NID, and you fill in the form exactly as on it [B0148]. A 48-page passport costs [B0145]:

   - 5 years: Tk 4,025 (regular), Tk 6,325 (express), Tk 8,625 (super express);
   - 10 years: Tk 5,750, Tk 8,050 or Tk 10,350.

   1. Pay online through A-Challan, DGePay or ShurjoPay, or at any bank through A-Challan [B0147].
   2. Enrol at the passport office; with an online-verified NID there is no police verification [B0150].
   3. Delivery takes up to 15, 7 or 2 working days from enrolment (regular, express, super express) [B0146].

   The visa needs a passport valid at least 3 months beyond the visa's end, with two blank visa pages [W0112].

   **If it is lost or stolen:** file a GD (general diary, a police report) at the nearest police station quickly, then re-apply with a copy of the old passport and the GD [B0149]. Passport helpline: 16445 (09666716445 from abroad), 24 hours [B0151].

2. **Police clearance, if the Embassy or employer asks for it.** You'll need a passport valid at least 3 months, and your present address must be one of your passport's addresses [B0152].

   1. Apply at [pcc.police.gov.bd](https://pcc.police.gov.bd/) [B0152].
   2. Pay Tk 1,500 by treasury challan, code 1-7301-0001-2681, at Bangladesh Bank or Sonali Bank, or online by card with a service charge [B0153].
   3. Track it by texting PCC S and your reference number to 26969 [B0154].

   Helpdesk: Sunday to Thursday, 9 am to 5 pm, 01320001824, 01320001825, help.pcc@police.gov.bd [B0155].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does the Italian Embassy in Dhaka require a police clearance for a Decreto Flussi work visa?

3. **Apostille your certificates in Bangladesh, not at the Italian Embassy.** The visa needs documents or certificates for the job sector you will work in (Step 5, item 2) [W0114]. Since June 2025 the Italian Embassy in Dhaka no longer legalises Bangladeshi public documents; they need an apostille (the international certificate under the Hague Apostille Convention) from Bangladesh's Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) through MyGov [B0051]. Italy accepts Bangladesh's apostille [B0072, W0095].

   You'll need [B0156, B0158]:

   - a scan of each certificate (JPEG, JPG or PNG);
   - Tk 200 per page;
   - for SSC (Secondary School Certificate) and HSC (Higher Secondary Certificate): verification by your Education Board and the Ministry of Education first.

   1. Register on [mygov.bd](https://www.mygov.bd/), choose the attestation service for your document type, fill in the form and upload the document [B0157].
   2. Pay Tk 200 per page online; it takes 5 working days [B0156].
   3. An SMS comes when the apostille is issued; download it from your MyGov account [B0157].

   A birth certificate is first notarised, then attested by the Law Ministry, then by MoFA [B0159]. The Embassy accepts only one apostille per document [P0110, W0125].

   > **Need to know**

4. **Translate into Italian with an Embassy-authorised translator, if asked.** VFS Global takes all translation and legalisation requests, and legalising an e-apostille is free [B0052, B0066, B0058]. The Embassy's authorised translators are [B0055, B0071, B0056]:

   - Tradusoft.com, 136 Link Road, Middle Badda, Dhaka; +8801776015230;
   - EXINSO Ltd., House 63/4 (1st floor), Progoti Sharani, North Badda, Dhaka 1212; +8801797-412712;
   - Prof. Stefania Chiapello; +8801755596360.

   The Embassy's 2026 fee list, at EUR 1 = Tk 140, charges EUR 37 (Tk 5,180) each for a police clearance or a study certificate [B0059, B0060, B0061]. A dichiarazione di valore (the Embassy's statement of what a foreign qualification is worth in Italy) is EUR 50 (Tk 7,000), free for study in Italy [B0062].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What does the Embassy's EUR 37 fee for a police clearance or study certificate pay for: translation, legalisation or a certificate?
   > - Does a Decreto Flussi worker ever need a dichiarazione di valore, or only for the Blue Card and study?

   **Scams:** Embassy appointments are always free, including through VFS; anyone selling a slot is not the Embassy [B0053]. Forging documents, signatures or seals is prosecuted [B0054].

### Step 5: The visa at the Italian Embassy in Dhaka, and checking it is real

All Italian visa applications in Bangladesh go through VFS Global, never to the Embassy directly; VFS handles only the logistics and has no say in decisions or document checks [W0099, M0006]. VFS centres take applications Sunday to Thursday, 9:00 to 12:00 [W0103]:

- Dhaka: Nafi Tower (4th and 5th floor), 53 South Gulshan Avenue, Gulshan 1 [W0100];
- Chattogram: World Trade Center, 102/103 Agrabad CA, Commerce College Road [W0101];
- Sylhet: Nirvana Inn Complex (4th floor), Ramer Dighir Par, Mirzajungle Road [W0102];
- help: info.itbd@vfshelpline.com, +880 9606777666 [W0099].

1. **Register on VFS's work-visa webform** as soon as you have the nulla osta [W0126, M0029].

   You'll need [M0029, M0030, W0129]:

   - your own email address;
   - a copy of the nulla osta, which the employer in Italy can provide.

   1. Open [vfseu.mioot.com/forms/ITABDE](https://vfseu.mioot.com/forms/ITABDE/), enter your email and the captcha, and press "Get One Time Password" [W0129, M0012].
   2. Enter the code from your email, fill in the form and upload the nulla osta; the same login later shows the status of your application [W0129, M0030].
   3. Registering stops the nulla osta from expiring while you wait for an appointment [W0127].
   4. The Embassy checks the nulla osta and then gives you an appointment, within a few days for a 2025 nulla osta [M0031]. Holders of a 2023 or 2024 nulla osta who already applied wait until the immigration office in Italy finishes its review, after which VFS contacts them [M0032].

   Give your own phone and email, so VFS reaches you directly and not through an intermediary [M0009]. An L/N nulla osta (Article 27, outside the quota) follows a walk-in route instead (see Work visa: highly skilled and out-of-quota (EU Blue Card, Article 27)) [W0130].

   > **Need to know**

2. **Submit the application at VFS.** You'll need [W0110, W0111, W0112, W0113, W0114, W0117]:

   - the original national ("D") visa form, filled in in English or Italian and signed;
   - one photo, 3.5 x 4.0 cm, colour, white background, taken in the last 6 months;
   - your passport, valid at least 3 months beyond the visa's end, with two blank visa pages, and a copy of the data page;
   - a copy of the nulla osta (check name, surname, sex and date of birth);
   - documents or certificates for the job sector you will work in, such as experience letters or training certificates (Step 4, item 3);
   - anything else the Embassy asks for.

   You give fingerprints at VFS; they are compulsory for every work visa applicant [M0021]. You may file only one visa application at a time, even with two passports [W0116]. Pay in cash, in taka, at the Embassy's rate of EUR 1 = Tk 140 [M0022]:

   - the visa fee: Tk 16,240 (EUR 116) [M0018];
   - the VFS service charge: Tk 5,320 (EUR 38), VAT included [M0019];
   - a bank draft charge: Tk 270 [M0020].

   That is Tk 21,830 in all [M0018, M0019, M0020]. The VFS charge already covers SMS and email notices, photocopies, the photo booth and help filling in the form [M0023].

   > - What do Dhaka agents charge in 2026 to fill in the form or "manage" the file, on top of the Tk 21,830?

3. **The employer must confirm within 15 days.** When the Embassy finishes its checks, the SUI emails the employer by PEC (posta elettronica certificata, certified email) asking it to confirm it still wants to hire you; if it does not confirm within 15 days the request counts as refused and the nulla osta is revoked [W0039, W0115, W0139, W0017]. Only after this confirmation does the Embassy issue the visa [W0040]. Tell the employer to watch its PEC inbox once you have applied [W0018].

4. **Follow the application and collect the passport.** If the Embassy wants more documents, VFS's passport tracker shows it with a deadline, and a wrong phone number or email is your loss [F0083]. Collect the passport within 90 days, or it goes back to the Embassy and on to Bangladesh's Ministry of Home Affairs [F0084, F0085]. To take your passport back before a decision, ask VFS in person with the signed withdrawal form, a copy of a valid ID and the VFS receipt [B0068].

   > - How many working days from VFS submission to visa in 2026 for a Decreto Flussi visa?

**Check it is real:**

1. **Your details.** The nulla osta must carry your exact name, sex and date of birth, the issuing SUI and a number [W0113].
2. **The Embassy's check.** The Embassy verifies every nulla osta registered on the webform before it gives an appointment; in May 2025 it said over 70% of webform entries carried fake or invalid nulla osta [M0031, M0005].
3. **Ask the employer for the SUI's emails.** Verification of a nulla osta is done in Italy, and the Embassy tells holders to ask their employer for updates [W0090, W0018].
4. **Ask the Embassy in writing** at visti.dhaka@esteri.it, attaching the nulla osta [W0106].
5. **The webform status** on vfseu.mioot.com shows your application's progress, and VFS notices come by SMS and email [W0129, M0023].

**If any check fails:** complain as in Step 1, item 7 [B0139]. Neither the Embassy nor VFS asks for money beyond the published fees or holds a passport against your will, and the Embassy says such cases should be reported to the authorities [M0007].

**Scams:** the Embassy and VFS take only the published fees, and paying an intermediary risks cancellation of the visa and legal action [M0007, N0043]. The Embassy has warned against brokers who sell a "Nulla Osta L/N code - carta blu": very few Bangladeshis with one got the visa, because most were not highly qualified, and a valid L/N nulla osta does not guarantee a visa [M0015, M0014, M0013]. Forging documents, signatures and seals is a crime [W0105].

### Step 6: Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight

Institute list: [bmet.gov.bd](https://bmet.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922dd2c933eb65569e13cd6) [B0126]. Download your certificate at [training.oep.gov.bd/pdo-certificate](https://training.oep.gov.bd/pdo-certificate) with your passport or NID number and the captcha [B0141].

   > **Need to know**

2. **Get BMET clearance (the smart card) yourself.** A worker who got the visa on their own applies at BMET's emigration branch in Dhaka or the Chattogram DEMO; it takes 3 working days [B0132].

   You'll need [B0132]:

   - an application;
   - a copy of the visa;
   - the contract, if you have one;
   - an attested copy of your OEP registration;
   - an undertaking on Tk 300 non-judicial stamp paper;
   - the original pay order for the welfare fee and insurance.

   Your costs:

   - welfare fee to the Wage Earners' Welfare Board (the government's welfare board for migrant workers): Tk 5,500 [B0140];
   - insurance: a one-time Tk 1,000 premium, 5 years' cover, Tk 10 lakh [B0131];
   - clearance fee: Tk 250 [B0133];
   - source tax on a single visa: Tk 400 (unattested visa) or Tk 250 (attested visa) [B0133].

   A woman going on a single visa also needs a no-objection letter from her legal guardian on Tk 150 non-judicial stamp paper [B0142].

   **Check it is real** at [portal.wewb.gov.bd/bmet-wewbm-check](https://portal.wewb.gov.bd/bmet-wewbm-check) with your passport number and the captcha [B0143]; the airport welfare desks at Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet check every departing worker's smart card [B0137]. **If it fails:** BMET's Director (Emigration), +8802222222415, 01711-135901, diremi@bmet.gov.bd [B0135].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does BMET check an Italian nulla osta or visa before clearance, and how? BMET's online visa check covers only Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Singapore and the UAE [B0134].
   > - Is an Italy worker always cleared as a "single visa", or must an agency file it?
   > - Is the source tax for an Italian visa Tk 400 or Tk 250 (does a stamped D visa count as "attested")?

3. **Before you fly,** keep one set of copies at home and at least 3 with you, open at least 2 bank accounts (one yours, one a family member's), and never carry a packet whose contents you don't know [B0130]. Carry the contacts of the Embassy of Bangladesh in Rome: Via dell'Antartide 5-7, 00144 Rome; +39 06 808 3595; emergency +39 3511 766 537 [B0073, B0074]. The contratto di soggiorno includes the employer's promise to pay your journey home (Step 2, item 1) [W0067].

   > **Need to know**

### Step 7: After arrival

You have 8 working days from entry to apply for the permesso di soggiorno (residence permit), and 15 days to sign the contratto di soggiorno [W0060, K0040, W0041].

1. **Sign the contratto di soggiorno within 15 days of entering Italy.** You and the employer sign it, digitally or you by hand, and the employer uploads it to the SUI on the ALI portal within the same 15 days [W0041, W0042, W0141, W0019]. You may work on the nulla osta while it is being signed [W0043]. If it is not sent in time the nulla osta is revoked, unless the delay was not your fault [W0038].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Do SUIs still call workers in person after arrival (for the integration agreement, the codice fiscale and the kit), or is everything now done online by the employer?

2. **Get your codice fiscale (tax code).** The codice fiscale is a 16-character code that identifies you to every public office; you need it to enrol with the health service and choose a doctor [K0054]. For a worker coming on a nulla osta, the SUI gives it [K0051].

   1. The SUI first gives a provisional code made only of numbers [K0052].
   2. The permanent code, of letters and numbers, is assigned when your permit is issued [K0053]. It never expires [K0055].
   3. If your details produce the same code as someone else's, the SUI sends you to any Agenzia delle Entrate office for a permanent one [K0056].

   If you have no codice fiscale at all, go to any Agenzia delle Entrate office with form AA4/8, filled in and signed, and your passport with visa or your permit [K0057]. The Agenzia publishes its guide to the codice fiscale for foreigners in Bengali [K0058].

   > **Need to know**
   > - With only the provisional numeric code from the SUI, do banks, phone shops and the comune accept it, or do they ask for the permanent code?

3. **Apply for the permesso di soggiorno through the SUI and the post office.** If you have a nulla osta, the SUI fills in the permit application and hands it to you in an envelope; you then take it to a post office with a Sportello Amico (the post-office counter that accepts permit applications) [K0015, K0016]. Comuni (town councils) and patronati (union-linked advice offices) can also fill in the yellow-band kit free of charge [K0018, K0017]. Only you can hand it in, not a lawyer or agent [K0032].

   You'll need [W0133, W0138, K0034, K0035, K0036, K0020]:

   - the application from the SUI (or the yellow-band kit, one per person);
   - a copy of the nulla osta and of the signed contratto di soggiorno;
   - proof of where you live: a residence certificate, or a dichiarazione di ospitalità (the host's declaration that you live with them) with the rental contract and a copy of the host's ID;
   - a copy of your codice fiscale, if you already have it;
   - A4 copies of every passport page with your details, visas and stamps;
   - a copy of the UNILAV (the employer's hiring notice to the job centre);
   - the money below.

   **What it costs** [K0021, K0022, K0023, K0047, K0048, K0033, W0135, W0136, G0001]:

   - EUR 16 (about Tk 2,230) revenue stamp (marca da bollo), bought at a tabaccheria (tobacconist);
   - EUR 30 (about Tk 4,190) paid to the post office clerk when you hand in the envelope;
   - one postal payment slip (bollettino) for EUR 30.46 (the permit card) plus a contribution: EUR 40 for a permit up to 1 year (slip total EUR 70.46, about Tk 9,840) or EUR 50 for a permit over 1 and up to 2 years (slip total EUR 80.46, about Tk 11,230).

   A permanent contract gives a permit of up to 2 years and a fixed-term one up to 1 year, so expect EUR 126.46 (about Tk 17,650) or EUR 116.46 (about Tk 16,260) in all [W0057, W0058, K0021, K0022, W0136, W0135, G0001].

   1. **Book a slot** at a Sportello Amico post office on poste.it or the Poste Italiane app (optional, saves queueing) [K0031]. The list of Sportello Amico offices is on poste.it, or call 800.200.309 free, Monday to Saturday, 8:00 to 20:00 [K0030].
   2. **Fill in the bollettino** for the card plus contribution; slips are at every post office [K0024].
   3. **Hand in the envelope open,** with your passport for identification and all copies [K0019, K0020].
   4. **Keep the two papers the clerk gives you** [K0025, K0027]:
      - the ricevuta (receipt), with codes to follow your application on portaleimmigrazione.it;
      - the letter calling you to the Questura, with date, time and place; if no date can be set yet, the letter says it will come by registered post to the Italian address you gave [K0028].
   5. **Go to the Questura on the date** for fotosegnalamento (photo and fingerprints) [K0041, K0029]. Bring the original passport and originals of every document copied into the kit, the post-office receipt, the appointment letter and 4 passport-size photos [K0117, K0038, K0037].
   6. **Check when the card is ready** on poliziadistato.it after the fingerprint appointment [K0029]. The card shows your photo, fingerprints, codice fiscale and reason for stay [K0041, K0042].

   **What the receipt allows:** with the receipt of a first work-permit application you have the same rights as a permit holder, you are lawfully present and you may work while you wait, unless the police notify you and your employer of a reason to refuse [K0044, W0143, K0045]. Keep it with your passport: it lets you move around in Italy [K0026].

   **How long it takes:** by law the permit is issued within 90 days [W0063, W0064]. In practice some Questure are far slower: in March 2024 Florence organisations reported first appointments being set for September 2025, and in February 2025 protesters there put the average wait to collect a permit at 1 year, 6 months and 15 days [K0108, K0109]. In January 2025 a man died of cold outside the Rome Questura's immigration office, where people queue overnight for appointments [K0110]. Renewing the permit is in Your residence permit [W0061].

   > **Need to know**
   > - How long after the post-office kit are Questura fingerprint appointments being set in Rome, Milan, Venice, Bologna and Naples in November 2026?
   > - How long after fingerprints does a first work permit actually arrive in those cities in 2026?
   > - Can a worker holding only the first-issue receipt fly to Bangladesh and back, and what do Questure tell Bangladeshi workers in 2026?

4. **Sign the Accordo di integrazione (integration agreement).** Every newcomer over 16 asking for a permit of at least one year signs it with the state, at the SUI for a work permit; without it the permit is not issued [K0092, K0046, K0039, K0090]. You get a copy in a language you choose, or in English [K0093].

   - **How it works:** you start with 16 credits and must reach 30 credits within two years, including spoken Italian at level A2 and basic knowledge of the Constitution and civil life (health, school, work, tax) [K0095, K0099, K0105, K0096, K0097]. If you have children, you commit to send them to school [K0098].
   - **The civic course:** a free session of 5 to 10 hours, within three months of signing; missing it costs 15 of your 16 credits [K0100, K0101, K0102].
   - **Earning credits:** without certificates, the SUI can test you, also at a CPIA (public adult-education centre) [K0103]. Enrolling with the health service and choosing a doctor gives 4 credits (Step 7, item 6) [K0082].
   - **The check:** one month before the two years end, the SUI asks you to send documents within 15 days and offers a free test [K0104]. 30 credits or more closes the agreement; 1 to 29 extends it one year; zero or less means the permit is revoked or not renewed and you are expelled [K0105, K0106, K0107, K0091].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Do Prefetture in 2026 actually call Bangladeshi workers to the civic session, and is it in person or online?
   > - Has any Bangladeshi worker had a permit refused for failing the integration agreement check?

5. **Register your residence at the comune (iscrizione anagrafica).** Every non-EU citizen with a regular permit who lives in a comune must register in its anagrafe (population register), on the same terms as Italians [K0059, K0073]. You can register before the permit card arrives [K0062, K0071].

   You'll need (Rome and Milan lists for a worker awaiting the first work permit) [K0061, K0062, K0112, K0113, K0070, K0114, K0115, K0116]:

   - a valid passport;
   - the post-office receipt of the permit application;
   - the SUI nulla osta, the contratto di soggiorno and the permit application filed at the SUI;
   - your codice fiscale (Milan asks for original and copy);
   - if others already live in the flat, a declaration from the resident accepting you, with a copy of their ID [K0063].

   1. **Rome:** fill in the ministerial residence form, sign it, and hand it to the anagrafe office of your Municipio (city district) in person, by PEC or ordinary email, or by registered letter, with a copy of your ID [K0064]. It is free [K0065]. The office registers you within two working days, and registration counts from the day you declared [K0060].
   2. **Milan:** apply online on the comune's form "Cambio di residenza per persone straniere provenienti dall'estero" and upload the documents; files sent by email are ignored, and each file may be up to 7MB [K0068, K0069]. If you live as a guest, upload the host's consent declaration with the host's details and the rental or ownership references [K0072].

   After every permit renewal, renew your declaration of residence at the anagrafe within 60 days, or you are removed from the register one year after the permit expires [K0066, K0067].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Do comuni with many Bangladeshis (Rome Municipio V and VI, Milan, Venice-Mestre, Monfalcone, Palermo) accept a residence at a shared flat where the landlord refuses to sign, and what do brokers charge for a residence address?

6. **Enrol with the health service (SSN) and choose a doctor.** As an employed worker you must enrol in the SSN (Servizio Sanitario Nazionale, the national health service) and you get the same care as Italians; your dependent family members are covered too [K0078, K0079]. Go to the ASL (Azienda Sanitaria Locale, local health authority) of your comune of residence or, if you are not yet resident, of the address on your permit [K0081, K0080].

   You'll need [K0085, K0086, K0083, K0054]:

   - your permit or the post-office receipt of the application;
   - with only the receipt: your employment contract or last payslip;
   - your codice fiscale;
   - a signed undertaking to report any change in your status.

   1. **Choose your medico di base** (family doctor) at the ASL counter; this also earns 4 integration credits [K0082].
   2. **Collect the tessera sanitaria** (health card). With only the receipt, it is given on showing your passport and visa, and enrolment lasts up to 3 months, extendable [K0087, K0088]. Enrolment ends when the permit expires or is revoked [K0084].

   > **Need to know**
   > - In Rome and Milan in 2026, does the ASL enrol a newly arrived worker with only the post-office receipt and the provisional numeric codice fiscale?

7. **Get an identity card (carta d'identità), if you want one.** Once registered as a resident, you can ask the comune for an electronic identity card (CIE); for non-EU residents it is valid only as ID in Italy, not for travel abroad [K0074]. In Rome, show a valid ID and your permit, book on prenotazionicie.interno.gov.it, and pay EUR 22.21 (about Tk 3,100); the card comes by post within 6 days [K0075, K0077, K0111, G0001, K0076].

8. **SIM card and bank account.** Phone operators ask for your name, surname and codice fiscale and check your ID document when you activate a SIM [K0089]. Opening a bank or post-office account is in Sending money home [G0053].

### Scams

#### Done to workers

The main scams, each covered where it happens:

- **The fake or empty nulla osta.** Firms in Italy have filed hiring requests only to sell the nulla osta, with no job behind it, and networks have used employers' names without their knowledge (Step 1, item 4) [N0109, N0103, D0100]. In May 2025 the Embassy found fake or invalid nulla osta in over 70% of webform entries; check yours as in Step 5, "Check it is real" [M0005, M0031].
- **"I know someone at the Embassy."** Italian Foreign Ministry staff and two Bangladeshi middlemen were arrested in 2025 over exactly this offer (see How a job reaches you, and who takes a cut) [N0072, N0073]. The Embassy and VFS take only the published fees, and paying an intermediary risks cancellation of the visa and legal action [M0007, N0043].
- **Paid appointments.** Embassy appointments are free, including through VFS [B0053].
- **The "L/N carta blu" nulla osta,** sold as a sure route; it is only for highly qualified workers (Step 5, "Scams") [M0015, M0013].
- **The passport held as security.** Brokers keep the passport until the last instalment; a ring working from a Gulshan-1 travel agency in Dhaka took a woman's passport and returned it only after she paid Tk 4.4 lakh into their bank accounts [N0059, N0162, N0095]. Neither the Embassy nor VFS holds a passport against your will [M0007].
- **Someone else flies on your visa.** In July 2026 the CID (Criminal Investigation Department) arrested seven members of the same ring, who had sent another woman to Italy on a woman's family visa; she learned at Dhaka airport that the other woman had flown on 24 April [N0093, N0096]. The broker approached her as she collected her passport from the Embassy [N0094]. Its alleged leader, Belayet Hossain, had sent about 30 people this way since 2016 [N0097].

Complain as in Step 1, item 7 [B0139].

#### Tried by workers

- **Fake Schengen visas.** In May 2026 three men flew to Rome on fake Schengen visas with a Biman ground officer's help; two were caught at Rome airport and sent back, after agreeing Tk 30 lakh each and paying Tk 20 lakh up front, and the CID arrested the officer [N0090, N0091, N0092]. The Embassy warns that entering with a fraudulent visa leads to immediate repatriation [M0035].
- **Two applications with two passports.** Only one visa application per person is allowed at a time [W0116, P0113].
- **Forged papers.** A nulla osta obtained with forged or fraudulent documents is refused or revoked, and forging or using a forged visa, permit or contratto di soggiorno carries 1 to 6 years' prison in Italy (see What can get you jailed or deported) [W0038, W0065].
