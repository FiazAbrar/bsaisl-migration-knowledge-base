### Your rights at work

With a valid permit you have, by law, the same treatment and rights at work as an Italian worker [L0019]. Minimum pay and overtime premiums are set by the CCNL (contratto collettivo nazionale di lavoro, the national collective contract) for your sector; before your nulla osta (work authorisation) was issued, a labour consultant or employer organisation had to certify that your employer follows it (Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Step 2) [L0023, L0034, L0035, W0051]. For any right that is broken, use the steps under If you aren't paid [L0077].

#### Pay: the minimum in your sector

Monthly or hourly minimums, gross (before tax and contributions), in the sectors where most Bangladeshis are hired, at EUR 1 = about Tk 139.6 [G0001]:

- **Farm work (operai agricoli):** the national floor for Area 3 (common workers) from 1 June 2026 is EUR 1,042.43 a month (about Tk 1,45,500), and for nursery and flower workers EUR 7.99 an hour (about Tk 1,115) [L0032, L0033, G0001]. Real farm pay is set in each province's contract, which may not go below these floors; provincial wages rose 3.4% on 1 June 2026 and rise another 1.7% on 1 January 2027 [L0034, L0030, L0031].
- **Construction (Edilizia Industria):** a common labourer (operaio comune) earns at least EUR 9.48 an hour (about Tk 1,323) nationally from 1 March 2026, plus provincial extras; in Ragusa, for example, EUR 10.45 an hour or EUR 1,807.23 a month (about Tk 2,52,300) for 40 hours a week over 5 days [L0035, L0039, L0038, G0001]. Holiday pay and the Christmas bonus are set aside at 18.5% of gross pay and paid to you by the Cassa Edile (the construction industry's fund), not by the employer [L0036, L0037].
- **Hotels (CCNL Turismo):** from May 2026 at least EUR 1,412.60 a month at level 7 (about Tk 1,97,200) and EUR 1,507.45 at level 6 (EUR 8.76 an hour, about Tk 1,223), paid 14 times a year, with the 13th month in December and the 14th in July [L0040, L0041, L0042, G0001]. Seasonal hotel workers get the same minimums, and their 13th, 14th and unused leave are paid at the end of the job with the TFR (trattamento di fine rapporto, end-of-service pay; see End-of-service pay (TFR)) [L0044, L0045].
- **Bars, restaurants, pizzerias, canteens and catering:** a separate contract, the FIPE CCNL, not the hotel one [L0043].
- **Cleaning (pulizie e multiservizi):** a level-2 cleaner earns at least EUR 1,413.50 a month full-time from 1 October 2026 (about Tk 1,97,300), or EUR 8.17 an hour (about Tk 1,141); much cleaning work is part-time, and pay is cut in proportion to the hours [L0046, L0047, L0048, G0001].

#### Your payslip, and the "paga globale" trick

Your employer must pay wages through a bank or post office (transfer, cheque or card), never in cash, and is fined EUR 1,000 to 5,000 (about Tk 1,39,600 to 6,98,000) for paying cash; opening an account is in Sending money home [G0057, G0058, G0001]. Your signature on the busta paga (payslip) does not prove you were paid, so keep your bank statements [G0059]. The Labour Inspectorate takes claims for wages whether or not they appear on the payslip [L0077].

A known trick in subcontracting is the "paga globale": a flat sum that folds in holiday pay, leave and TFR, while the payslip carries invented items that are never paid [L0049, L0050]. Among subcontractors at the Fincantieri shipyards, whose workers included Bangladeshis, the Guardia di Finanza (Italy's financial police) found 1,951 workers paid irregularly and about EUR 6 million (about Tk 84 crore) paid off the books; 383 workers took under EUR 7 gross an hour (about Tk 977) because they needed the job to renew their permit [L0049, L0052, L0053, L0051, G0001].

Keep your own sheet of the days and hours you work and every payment you receive; the Labour Inspectorate asks for exactly this (If you aren't paid, item 2) [L0084].

#### Hours, rest and leave

Normal hours are 40 a week [L0020]. With overtime, the average may not exceed 48 hours a week [L0021]. Overtime is counted separately and paid with the premium set in your CCNL; where the CCNL says nothing, it needs your agreement and is capped at 250 hours a year [L0023, L0022]. You are entitled to 11 hours' rest in a row every 24 hours, and at least 24 hours in a row every 7 days, normally on Sunday [L0024, L0025]. Paid leave is at least four weeks a year, and it can't be swapped for money except when the job ends [L0026, L0027].

#### Sick pay

1. **Go to your medico di base (family doctor),** chosen when you enrol with the health service (Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Step 7); the doctor sends the sickness certificate online to INPS (Istituto Nazionale della Previdenza Sociale, the national social security institute), so you don't send it yourself [K0082, L0054].
2. **Stay at home during check hours,** 10:00 to 12:00 and 17:00 to 19:00, every day on the certificate, weekends and holidays included [L0058].

What you get: INPS pays from day 4, at 50% of average daily pay up to day 20 and 66.66% from day 21 to day 180; the first three days are paid by the employer only if your CCNL says so [L0055, L0056, L0057].

#### Injury at work

Tell the employer at once, even about a small injury; if you don't and the employer doesn't know, the days before the employer learns of it are not paid [L0059, L0060].

What you get: INAIL (Istituto Nazionale Assicurazione Infortuni sul Lavoro, the national work-accident insurer) pays 60% of daily pay from the fourth day after the accident while you can't work, and 75% from day 91 [L0061, L0062]. For long-term disability, death and an employer who hides the accident, see If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job [L0061].

#### End-of-service pay (TFR)

Every employee gets the TFR whenever the job ends, however it ends [L0028]. Each year adds that year's pay divided by 13.5, roughly one month's pay [L0029]. A missing TFR can be claimed through the Labour Inspectorate (If you aren't paid, item 2) [L0079].

#### Your Italian pension

The INPS contributions taken from your pay stay yours: a non-seasonal worker who goes home for good keeps the pension rights earned in Italy, even if Italy has no social security agreement with the home country [L0001, L0011]. Seasonal workers are excluded from this rule [L0002, L0011].

- **Hired after 1 January 1996:** you get a pension at Italy's pensionable age (67 in 2023-2024, adjusted for life expectancy), even with fewer than 20 years of contributions [L0003, L0004].
- **Hired before 1996:** you need 20 years of contributions [L0005].

**At pensionable age, apply** in one of these ways [L0006, L0007, L0009, L0008]:

- online, if you have INPS login credentials, attaching documents authenticated by the Italian consulate, then posting them to INPS, Direzione provinciale di Perugia, via Canali 5, 06122 Perugia [L0006];
- at the Italian consulate where you live, if you have no credentials [L0007];
- through a patronato (a free welfare advice office that serves workers with a permit) [L0009, W0049, L0012].

If you die after pensionable age, your family can get a survivors' pension under the general rules [L0010].

### Your residence permit

#### How long you can stay

The permesso di soggiorno (residence permit) for work lasts as long as your contratto di soggiorno (contract of stay), up to 1 year on a fixed-term contract and 2 years on a permanent one [W0057, W0058]. Each renewal can last up to 3 years [W0059, K0050]. While a permit is being issued, renewed or converted, the post-office receipt lets you stay and work, even after the 60-day limit, unless the police notify you of a reason to refuse [P0040, P0041].

#### Renewing

Ask the Questore (head of the Questura, the provincial police immigration office) of the province where you live to renew your permit at least 90 days before it expires [L0109, W0061]. If it expired more than 60 days ago and you did not ask to renew it, the Prefetto (the government's representative in the province) orders your expulsion [L0099].

You'll need [K0017, K0019, K0020, W0138]:

- the yellow-band kit (one per person), free at every post office [K0017];
- your passport, and A4 copies of every passport page with your details, visas and stamps [K0019, K0020];
- copies of your UNILAV (the employer's hiring notice to the job centre) and your last tax return or CUD (Certificazione Unica, the employer's yearly income statement) [W0138];
- the money below.

**What it costs** [K0021, K0022, K0023, K0047, K0048, W0135, G0001]:

- EUR 16 (about Tk 2,234) revenue stamp (marca da bollo), from a tabaccheria (tobacconist) [K0021, G0001];
- EUR 30 (about Tk 4,188) to the post-office clerk when you hand in the kit [K0022, G0001];
- one postal slip (bollettino): EUR 70.46 (about Tk 9,836) for a permit of up to 1 year, or EUR 80.46 (about Tk 11,232) for over 1 and up to 2 years [K0023, K0047, K0048, W0135, G0001].

1. **Have the kit filled in.** Some comuni (town councils) and patronati fill it in free of charge [K0018].
2. **Hand it in, yourself, at a Sportello Amico post office** (the counters that take permit applications), open, with your passport and the copies; no agent or lawyer can do it for you [K0016, K0019, K0032]. Find the offices on poste.it or on 800.200.309, free, Monday to Saturday, 8:00 to 20:00 [K0030].
3. **Keep the receipt and the Questura appointment letter** the clerk gives you; the receipt lets you stay and work while you wait (How long you can stay) [K0025, K0027, P0040].
4. **Go to the Questura on the date** to be fingerprinted again [W0062, L0111]. Then check when the card is ready on poliziadistato.it [K0029].
5. **Renew your residence registration at the comune within 60 days of every renewal,** or you are struck off the register one year after the permit expires [K0066, K0067].

What you get back: a permit, issued or renewed by law within 90 days [W0063, L0110]. A permit is also revoked or not renewed if your integration agreement (accordo di integrazione, signed on arrival) ends with zero credits (Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Step 7) [K0107].

#### If you lose your job

Losing the job, even by resigning, does not cancel your permit or your family's [L0014, L0015]. Register as available for work, and you may stay for the rest of the permit and at least one year (not on a seasonal permit), or as long as your unemployment pay lasts if that is longer; after that, a new permit depends on your income [L0013, W0046, L0016]. NASpI unemployment pay and how to claim it: If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job [L0063].

#### Long-term EU permit

After 5 years with a valid permit, you can ask the Questura for the EU long-term residence permit (permesso di soggiorno UE per soggiornanti di lungo periodo) [W0069].

You'll need [W0070, W0072, W0137]:

- lawful income of at least the yearly assegno sociale (the state's minimum social allowance), EUR 7,101.12 (about Tk 9,91,300) in 2026 [F0017, G0001];
- a pass in an Italian language test [W0072];
- a housing suitability certificate from your comune (how to get one: Family reunification, Step 2) [W0137];
- the post-office kit, with a slip of EUR 130.46 (about Tk 18,212), which includes a EUR 100 contribution [W0137, K0049, G0001].

What you get back: a permit issued within 90 days, valid 10 years and renewed automatically [W0071]. Your children under 18 living with you get the long-term permit too, you no longer have to prove Italian at B1 for citizenship, and losing all integration-agreement credits no longer costs you the permit [F0109, F0138, K0091].

#### Becoming an Italian citizen

You can be granted Italian citizenship after ten years' uninterrupted legal residence; a June 2025 referendum to cut this to five years failed for lack of quorum [F0132, F0133, F0149, F0150].

**Who qualifies:**

- **Ten years' legal residence:** registered as a resident at the comune and in order with permit rules for the whole period [F0133, F0134].
- **Income:** enough to support yourself [F0135].
- **No criminal record** and no security bar [F0135].
- **Italian at B1 or above,** proved by an Italian school diploma, a certificate from a recognised body, or an attestation from a CPIA (Centro Provinciale per l'Istruzione degli Adulti, the public adult school); EU long-term permit holders are exempt [F0136, F0137, F0138].

1. **Get your Bangladeshi birth certificate and police clearance;** the police clearance is valid six months from issue, the birth certificate does not expire [F0142].
2. **Make every name match** across your Italian and Bangladeshi documents; any difference needs an attestation from the Bangladesh Embassy, legalised by the Prefettura (the central government's office in the province) [F0143].
3. **Pay EUR 250** (about Tk 34,900) by post-office bollettino, bank transfer or PagoPA (Italy's online payment system for public bodies) [F0139, F0140, G0001].
4. **Apply online** at portaleservizi.dlci.interno.it [F0141].
5. **Wait:** the law gives the Ministry 24 months to decide, extendable to 36 [F0146].
6. **Read the decision** on SEND, the state's digital notifications platform, which also tells you how to take the oath [F0144].

Whether you keep Bangladeshi citizenship depends on Bangladesh's law [F0145]. Children: see Having a child in Italy [F0112].

### What can get you jailed or deported

#### No valid permit

Entering or staying in Italy without a valid permit is a crime, fined EUR 5,000 to 10,000 (about Tk 6.98 lakh to 13.96 lakh) [L0098, G0001]. A permit that expired more than 60 days ago without a renewal request leads to expulsion (Renewing) [L0099]. If the police ask, you must show your passport and permit; refusing without good reason can mean arrest of up to a year and a fine of up to EUR 2,000 (about Tk 2.79 lakh) [L0113, G0001].

#### Expulsion and CPR detention

You can ask for 7 to 30 days to leave by yourself, including through an assisted voluntary return programme (Going home without valid papers) [L0100, L0101]. If you can't be removed at once, the Questore can hold you in a CPR (Centro di Permanenza per i Rimpatri, repatriation detention centre): three months at first, extendable by three months and then by up to twelve more months in total [L0106, L0107, L0108]. At the court hearing that confirms your removal you have the right to free legal aid and an interpreter [L0105]. An expulsion brings a 3 to 5 year entry ban, recorded in the Schengen Information System, which closes every EU and Schengen country, not only Italy; coming back without authorisation is punished by 1 to 4 years in prison [L0103, L0104, L0102]. Where irregular stays end, and the CPRs: Irregular routes and undocumented workers [L0106].

**Help from the Bangladesh side:** the Consulate General in Milan visits Bangladeshis held in Italian jails, and gives a certificate of financial hardship so a person who can't pay gets a free lawyer from the Italian state (Help from the Bangladesh Embassy in Rome and Consulate in Milan) [B0103, B0101].

#### Fake documents

Forging a visa, permit or contratto di soggiorno, using one, or forging papers to obtain them, is punished by 1 to 6 years in prison [L0112]. How fake papers are sold: Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Scams [L0112].

#### Drugs and crime

Selling drugs, or holding them to sell, is punished by 6 to 20 years and a fine of EUR 26,000 to 260,000 (about Tk 36.3 lakh to 3.63 crore), or 6 months to 5 years for minor cases [L0117, L0118, G0001]. Even possession for your own use brings administrative penalties, including suspension of your passport [L0119]. A conviction for a serious crime, even before appeal, bars a permit [L0115]. A judge can replace a prison sentence of up to two years with expulsion [L0116].

#### Working without papers

An employer who employs someone without a valid permit faces 6 months to 3 years in prison and a EUR 5,000 (about Tk 6.98 lakh) fine per worker [L0017, G0001]. If you are exploited by a caporale (an illegal gangmaster), a special permit protects you when you report it (Caporalato: illegal gangmasters) [D0047, D0050].

### If you aren't paid

**If you are not paid, paid less than your CCNL, or your overtime, 13th month or TFR is missing, act in this order:**

1. **Write to the employer by registered letter with return receipt (raccomandata A/R)** asking for the money; this formal notice (messa in mora) stops your claim from expiring [L0086].
   Sample wording: "Io sottoscritto [name], nato a [place] il [date], codice fiscale [number], dipendente dal [start date] con mansione di [job], chiedo il pagamento delle retribuzioni non pagate per i mesi di [months], pari a euro [amount], oltre a [straordinari / tredicesima / TFR]. In mancanza, mi rivolgerò all'Ispettorato del Lavoro e al giudice." ("I, [name], born in [place] on [date], tax code [number], employed since [start date] as [job], ask for payment of unpaid wages for [months], EUR [amount], plus [overtime / 13th month / TFR]. Otherwise I will go to the Labour Inspectorate and the court.") [L0086]
2. **File a request for inspection (Modulo INL 31) with the Ispettorato Territoriale del Lavoro (ITL, the Labour Inspectorate) of the province where you work** [L0082].
   You'll need [L0083, L0084]:
   - a copy of your passport or permit (without it the complaint is not accepted) [L0083];
   - copies of payslips, bank statements or receipts, and your own sheet of days and hours worked [L0084].
   1. Download Modulo INL 31 from ispettorato.gov.it; it exists in English and in Bangla [L0089].
   2. Tick what is owed: unpaid wages (on or off the payslip), the 13th or 14th month, TFR, unpaid overtime, or work "in nero" (off the books) [L0077, L0078, L0079, L0080].
   3. Write the facts in Bangla if you need to, but add Italian too [L0085].
   4. Send it by email or PEC (posta elettronica certificata, certified email) to your province's ITL, whose addresses are at ispettorato.gov.it/contatti, or hand it in [L0088].

   What happens: the ITL may call you and the employer to a conciliation, where a union or a professional can assist you [L0087, L0090]. If inspectors find money owed, they order the employer to pay (diffida accertativa), and the employer has 30 days to seek conciliation [L0091, L0092]. Form 31 can't be used against a dismissal (Changing employer) [L0081].
3. **Go to a union or a patronato,** which serve workers with a permit, can assist you at the conciliation, and can send resignation forms and NASpI claims for you [L0012, L0090, L0097, L0073].
4. **Tell the Bangladesh Embassy's labour welfare wing** in Rome, welfare.rome@gmail.com, which visits workplaces to check conditions and pay; Milan's is fslbcgmilan@gmail.com [B0094, B0109].

If the pay is far below the CCNL and comes with degrading work or housing, it may be caporalato (illegal gangmaster labour), a crime: see Caporalato: illegal gangmasters [D0056, D0057, D0049].

### Changing employer or leaving

#### Changing employer

A permit for employment can be used for other legal work, and losing or leaving a job does not cancel it (If you lose your job) [L0114, L0014]. The exceptions are EU Blue Card holders (the permit for highly qualified workers) and out-of-quota care workers, who need the labour office's prior approval to change employer in their first 12 months [W0083, D0064]. In August 2026 Bangladesh raised with Italy the case of several hundred workers who entered regularly in 2022-23 but cannot change employer or extend their permits because their sponsors can't be reached [B0038].

**If you resign:** resign only online, on the Ministry of Labour form at lavoro.gov.it; a paper letter has no effect [L0095]. A patronato, union or the ITL can send it for you, and you can withdraw it within seven days [L0097, L0096]. Resigning loses NASpI unemployment pay, unless you resign for just cause, for example because wages are not paid [L0070, L0071].

**If you are dismissed:** challenge the dismissal in writing within 60 days of receiving it (a union letter counts), then file at the labour court (giudice del lavoro) or ask for conciliation within 180 days, or you lose the right [L0093, L0094].

#### Going home on leave

**With only the renewal receipt** (permit expired, renewal filed at the post office), the Polizia di Stato lets you leave and come back showing your valid passport, the post-office receipt and the expired permit, original or copy [L0120, L0121]. If you are waiting for your first permit, you must leave and come back through the same border crossing, with no transit through another Schengen country, and the border officer stamps both the passport and the receipt [L0122, L0123, L0124].

**If you are stuck in Bangladesh** (permit expired, lost or stolen), apply for a re-entry visa (visto di reingresso) at the Italian Embassy in Dhaka; it is issued only after the Questura in Italy clears it [L0130].

You'll need [L0131, L0127, L0125, L0126, L0128, L0129]:

- the national (type D) visa form and one passport-standard photo to ICAO (International Civil Aviation Organization) rules (3.5 x 4 cm, white background, taken in the last 6 months) [L0131];
- a passport valid at least 15 more months, with two blank visa pages [L0127];
- if the permit expired no more than 60 days before you apply: a copy of the expired permit and the renewal receipt [L0125];
- if it was lost or stolen: an apostilled police report from the country where it happened [L0126];
- proof of the date you left Italy (the exit stamp, or the ticket if the passport was lost) [L0128];
- a letter in English or Italian explaining why you need to return [L0129];
- a flight booking [L0131].

#### Leaving at the end of the job

1. **Check your last payslip** for unused leave, the 13th (and 14th) month pro rata, and the TFR; claim anything missing through If you aren't paid [L0027, L0045, L0028].

### Bringing your family

You can bring your wife and children under 18 once you hold a permit of at least one year, earn above the income line and have a home the comune certifies as suitable; for your wife (not your children) you must also have lived legally in Italy for two years without a break [F0007, F0008, F0010, F0012]. With a contract of at least one year, your family can instead come with you on your first entry, if you meet the same housing and income conditions [F0026]. Blue Card holders can bring family whatever the length of their permit [W0084]. The whole procedure, from the online application to the visa in Dhaka, is in Family reunification [F0019, F0051]. Your wife can work in Italy once she arrives on her family permit [F0092, F0087].
