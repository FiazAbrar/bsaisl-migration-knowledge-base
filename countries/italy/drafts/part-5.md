## Student visa

The student visa is a type D (national, long-stay) visa called "Immatricolazione Università" (university enrolment), valid 365 days with multiple entries [V0083]. Italy issued about 500 study visas to Bangladeshis in 2025 and expects to issue more in 2026, but the Italian ambassador says many more would not be sustainable because universities have few places for foreign students [V0064, V0065]. A university's acceptance does not guarantee a visa appointment or a visa: the Italian Embassy in Dhaka also judges the "migration risk" from the family's money, your past studies and how well the course fits them [V0075, V0076].

For courses starting in 2026/27, visa applications for bachelor's and master's courses must reach the Embassy by 30 November 2026, and universities may set earlier dates for each course [V0071, V0072, V0073]. For 2027/28 the deadline is 31 October 2027 [V0072].

### Step 1: Choose a course and check you qualify

1. **Check the entry level.** A bachelor's course needs a school-leaving certificate after at least 12 years of schooling (in Bangladesh, the HSC, Higher Secondary Certificate) [V0080].
2. **Check the language.** Courses taught in Italian need Italian at level B2 or above, tested by the university [V0081]. For courses in English, the Dhaka Embassy accepts only IELTS, with a score of at least 6, issued within the two years before you apply [V0105, V0106]. The certificate must come from a recognised institute or university and cover all four skills (listening, reading, speaking, writing) [V0069, V0082].
3. **Check the money.** You must show EUR 10,179.85 (about Tk 14.2 lakh) a year, from lawful, traceable sources, preferably your own or your family's [V0085, G0001]. The Embassy accepts money held by you or by your parents, grandparents, brothers and sisters, uncles and aunts, or cousins [V0068]. It checks where the money came from, the account's movements, and whether the sum fits the family's income and assets [V0086]. The bank statements must cover the last 12 months, and the sponsor's money must be explained by salary, property or savings held for some time [V0107, V0108]. A scholarship does not replace this proof [V0112].

### Step 2: Get your certificates checked

The visa needs your HSC (or bachelor's) certificate backed either by statements from CIMEA (Italy's official centre for comparing foreign qualifications) or by a Declaration of Value (Dichiarazione di Valore, DoV: the Italian Embassy's statement of what your certificate is worth in Italy) [V0090, V0070]. Whichever the university uses, the Embassy needs a DoV or a CIMEA statement that also confirms the certificate is genuine [V0103]. From 2026/27 a university can no longer accept you "with reservation" while these papers are missing [V0102].

1. **Apostille your certificates first,** following Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Step 4. The Italian Embassy no longer legalises Bangladeshi documents; they need an apostille (the international stamp of authenticity) from Bangladesh's Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) through the MyGov portal, at Tk 200 a page, in 5 working days, after your Education Board and the Ministry of Education verify the SSC (Secondary School Certificate) and HSC [V0010, B0156, B0158].
2. **Either: apply to CIMEA yourself** on [cimea-diplome.it](https://cimea-diplome.it/), its only official channel [V0118]. Use the Comparability & Verification service, meant for qualifications from countries outside the Lisbon Recognition Convention (the European treaty on recognising qualifications) [V0114]:
   - **Cost:** EUR 300 (about Tk 41,900) per qualification, which is EUR 245.90 plus VAT [V0113, G0001].
   - **Time:** 60 working days; the comparability part can be done in 30 working days for EUR 100 (about Tk 14,000) more, but the verification part always takes the ordinary time [V0115, V0116, G0001].
   - **Who applies:** only the certificate holder; CIMEA takes no requests through agents and prohibits brokerage [V0117, V0120].
3. **Or: ask for a DoV** from the Italian Embassy, through VFS Global (the private company that takes applications for the Embassy; addresses in Visit visa (Schengen), Step 2) [V0070, V0001, M0006]. The Embassy's 2026 fee list prices a DoV at EUR 50 (Tk 7,000) and marks it free for study in Italy [V0017].

**Check it is real:** a genuine CIMEA statement carries a QR code and a verification link recorded on a blockchain; fake CIMEA certificates have been submitted to Italian universities [V0118, V0119].

### Step 3: Pre-enrol on Universitaly

1. **Apply to the university first,** on its own portal and by its own deadline; the university checks your documents before it validates you [V0073, V0078].
2. **Pre-enrol on [universitaly.it](https://www.universitaly.it/),** the free official portal of Italy's Ministry of University and Research; no other channel is accepted [V0074].
3. **Wait for the university to validate your pre-enrolment.** Only validated students can apply for the visa [V0078]. From 2026/27 each university may validate only up to its places for foreign students plus 20% [V0077]. Uni-Italia, a non-profit association, gives guidance on Universitaly through its centres abroad [V0079].
4. **Do not pay tuition yet:** university fees are normally paid only after the visa is issued [V0091].

### Step 4: Apply for the visa at VFS

1. **Wait for VFS's email.** VFS Global emails students registered on Universitaly, at the address used there, to book the appointment [V0067]. Appointments are always free, also through VFS [V0009].
2. **You'll need** (the Embassy's checklist):
   - the type D visa form, one ICAO photo (the international passport-photo standard: 3.5 x 4.0 cm, white background), and your passport (original and one copy), valid at least 3 months beyond the visa, with two blank pages [V0141, V0142];
   - the Universitaly pre-enrolment receipt showing the tuition fees [V0104];
   - the DoV (or its application receipt) or the CIMEA statements [V0070];
   - the IELTS (6 or more) or Italian B2 certificate [V0105, V0081];
   - 12 months' bank statements of the student and the sponsor, and the birth certificate linking the sponsor to you, apostilled by MoFA [V0107, V0109];
   - proof of accommodation: a booking of at least 30 days, a lease, or a hosting declaration by a legal resident of Italy with a copy of their ID [V0110];
   - a one-way flight booking, and health insurance valid across the Schengen area for the whole stay [V0111];
   - if married, divorced or widowed: the nikahnama (Muslim marriage contract), talaknama (divorce deed) or spouse's death certificate, in English and apostilled [V0027].

   Applying for a university residence place is not proof of accommodation [V0088].
3. **Give your fingerprints:** since 11 January 2025 they are taken for national visas too [V0089].
4. **Pay in cash, in Taka** [M0022]:
   - visa fee: Tk 7,000 (EUR 50) [M0026];
   - VFS service charge: Tk 5,320 (EUR 38) [M0026];
   - bank draft charge: Tk 270 [M0020].

   Depending on the course's length the Embassy may grant the visa free, and then the VFS charge is waived too [M0026, M0027].
5. **If your admission or language test is in Italy,** you get a type D visa with a fixed validity of 100 days, and you do not need to come home for a new visa after passing [V0084].

**Check it is real:** VFS only handles the logistics and has no say in the decision; neither VFS nor the Embassy asks for money beyond the published fees [M0006, M0007].

### Step 5: After you arrive

1. **Apply for the study permit within 8 working days,** at the Questura (the provincial police headquarters that issues residence permits) of the city where you will live, with the kit from a post office or a desk at the university [V0093]. The post office receipt proves you are in Italy legally until the permit is ready [V0094]. Patronati (free advice offices run by trade unions) and some municipalities help fill in the kit free [V0095]. Information: poliziadistato.it, portaleimmigrazione.it, toll-free 803160 [V0096].
2. **Enrol at the university by 31 January.** From 31 January of the academic year the visas of students not yet enrolled are revoked and the Questura is told [V0092].
3. **For the codice fiscale (tax code), the health service and a bank account,** follow Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Step 7.

### How long you can stay

1. **Renew the permit** at the Questura at least 60 days before it expires [V0097].
2. **Pass your exams:** renewal needs one exam passed in the first year and at least two in each later year [V0098]. Permits are not issued for more than three years beyond the course's length [V0099].
3. **Changing course:** you may move to another university degree with approval, but not to a private course [V0101]. Formally withdrawing from your studies ends your right to stay, and you must leave Italy [V0100].
4. **After graduating:** see Changing visa type [V0129].

### Can you work?

A study permit allows a job of up to 20 hours a week, and at most 1,040 hours a year [V0123]. The National Labour Inspectorate (INL) says no contract may go over 20 hours a week, not even in summer, and that working more needs a work permit first (see Changing visa type) [V0124, V0125].

### Grants for students from poor countries

Bangladesh is on Italy's 2026/27 list of "particularly poor countries" [V0131]. For students from these countries, the regional grants for university students (DSU, diritto allo studio, the right to study) judge need by a certificate from the Italian Embassy that the student does not come from a notably high-income, high-status family [V0132]. Being eligible for a grant does not count as money for the visa until the grant is formally awarded [V0087].

> **Need to know**
> - How does a Bangladeshi student get the Italian Embassy's "not from a high-income family" certificate for DSU in 2026, and how long does it take?

### Bringing your family

A student whose permit lasts at least one year can bring children under 18 once the income and housing rules are met, and a wife or husband after two years' legal residence in Italy (see Family reunification) [F0007, F0008].

### If the visa is refused

The refusal must give reasons, and you can appeal to the Regional Administrative Court (TAR, Tribunale Amministrativo Regionale) of Lazio within 60 days [V0049, V0050]. Nobody has a right to a visa, only a "legitimate interest" the court can protect [V0051].

### Scams

#### Done to students

Pre-enrolment on Universitaly is free, CIMEA takes no requests through agents, and visa appointments are free, so you can do all three yourself [V0074, V0117, V0009]. The Embassy itself has warned students not to use intermediaries or brokers [V0121]. Fake CIMEA certificates have been submitted to Italian universities; check the QR code (see Student visa, Step 2) [V0119, V0118]. If an agent asks for money beyond the published fees or holds your passport, report it (see Contacts in Bangladesh) [M0007].

#### Tried by applicants

Forged or altered documents lead to immediate refusal, and the Embassy has said applicants with fake papers are banned from applying and reported to the Italian and Bangladeshi police [V0135, V0122]. A student visa used only to get into Italy is revoked if you do not enrol by 31 January [V0092].

## Visit visa (Schengen)

A Schengen visa (type C, short stay) allows up to 90 days in any 180 days in Italy and the other Schengen countries [V0036]. It covers tourism, visiting family or friends, business and medical treatment [V0021, V0029, V0032]. It never allows work, study or applying to stay in Italy (see Changing visa type) [V0044]. Bangladesh's Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare warns people not to go to work on a tourist or visit visa on a broker's promise [B0127].

Most Bangladeshi applicants are refused. In 2025 Italy's consulate in Dhaka received 6,521 Schengen applications and refused 3,948 (60%), after refusing 65% in 2024 [V0053, V0140]. Italy refused 11% worldwide, and its consulates in New Delhi, Karachi and Colombo refused 17%, 40% and 39% [V0055, V0062, V0060, V0061]. In Dhaka, Germany refused 30%, Spain 40%, France 47% and Sweden 74% [V0056, V0057, V0058, V0059]. The Italian ambassador says the rate is high because many people who try to enter Italy illegally through these visas submit fake documents [V0063].

### Step 1: Prepare the papers

You'll need (the Embassy's tourism checklist):

- the Schengen visa form, filled in in English or Italian and signed [V0019];
- one ICAO photo (the international passport-photo standard): 3.5 x 4.0 cm, colour, white background, under 6 months old [V0020];
- a passport valid at least three months beyond the visa, with two blank visa pages [V0019];
- your own bank statement from a bank in Bangladesh for the last 6 months [V0024];
- if employed: your employer's original letter giving your salary, years of service, leave approval and confirmation that your job is kept for your return [V0022];
- if self-employed: trade licence, incorporation certificate, tax return and TIN (Taxpayer Identification Number), and 6 months' company bank statements [V0023];
- a return flight booking with a PNR (the booking reference) [V0025];
- a hotel booking, or other proof of where you will stay, for the whole trip [V0143];
- travel insurance for all Schengen countries and the whole stay, covering at least EUR 30,000 (about Tk 41.9 lakh) [V0026, G0001];
- if married, divorced or widowed: nikahnama (marriage contract), talaknama (divorce deed) or death certificate, in English and apostilled, one apostille per document [V0027].

The Embassy may ask for more [V0028]. The money you must show for a visit is set by an Interior Ministry table [V0045, V0046, V0047, V0048, G0001]. For one person it is:

- 1 to 5 days: EUR 269.60 in all (about Tk 37,600) [V0045, G0001];
- 6 to 10 days: EUR 44.93 a day (about Tk 6,300) [V0046, G0001];
- 11 to 20 days: EUR 51.64 (about Tk 7,200) plus EUR 36.67 a day (about Tk 5,100) [V0047, G0001];
- over 20 days: EUR 206.58 (about Tk 28,800) plus EUR 27.89 a day (about Tk 3,900) [V0048, G0001].

**Visiting family or friends in Italy:** add the invitation letter in the Embassy's format, proof of the relationship, the host's ID and, if the host is not Italian, their residence permit [V0021].

**Business:** add the Italian company's invitation in the Embassy's format on company paper, its Chamber of Commerce registration (Visura Camerale) no older than 6 months, and proof of recent trade such as bills of lading and receipts [V0029, V0030, V0031].

**Medical treatment:** add the hospital's appointment confirmation, the expected length of treatment, and proof you can pay for it [V0034, V0033]. For treatment paid by the Italian state, add the Region's act or a Ministry of Health authorisation [V0032].

### Step 3: At the border

1. **A visa does not guarantee entry:** border police can refuse you if you cannot show the purpose of the trip and that the conditions are met [V0037]. Carry the return ticket, the hotel booking or invitation, the money, and the EUR 30,000 insurance [V0043].
2. **Get your passport stamped** when you enter the Schengen area from Bangladesh: the stamp is your "declaration of presence" [V0038]. 3. **The emergency number in Italy is 112** [V0042].

### Can you work?

No: a visit visa does not allow work, study or applying to stay in Italy [V0044]. A visitor who overstays can be expelled (see What can get you jailed or deported) [V0041].

### If the visa is refused

The refusal letter must give reasons; you can appeal to the TAR (Regional Administrative Court) of Lazio within 60 days, or apply again and pay again [V0049, V0050].

## Family reunification

Ricongiungimento familiare (family reunification) is how a foreigner living legally in Italy brings a wife or husband, children or parents from Bangladesh [F0001, F0002, F0004]. It runs in two halves: you apply in Italy for a nulla osta (entry clearance) from the SUI (Sportello Unico per l'Immigrazione, the immigration desk at the Prefettura, the central government's office in each province), then your family applies with it for a visa at VFS Global in Dhaka [F0019, F0051]. The Embassy says it issues about 12,000 to 13,000 family visas a year, with few refusals on new applications [F0086, F0088]. Family members can work as soon as they arrive [F0087, F0092].

### Who can come

- **Your wife or husband,** aged 18 or over and not legally separated [F0001]. If you already live in Italy with one wife, a second wife cannot come [F0006].
- **Children under 18** on the day you apply, unmarried, including your wife's children and children born outside marriage; the other parent, if alive, must consent [F0002, F0005].
- **Adult children** only if they depend on you because of a health condition amounting to total disability [F0003].
- **Parents** who depend on you and have no other children in Bangladesh, or parents over 65 whose other children cannot support them for documented serious health reasons [F0004, F0079].

No other relatives, such as brothers, sisters or in-laws, can come on a family visa [F0079].

### Who can sponsor, and the income line

1. **Your permit:** an EU long-term permit, or a permit of at least one year for work, self-employment, international protection, study, religious or family reasons [F0007]. An EU Blue Card holder can bring family whatever the length of the permit [W0084]. An asylum seeker still waiting for a final decision cannot apply [F0025].
2. **Two years in Italy:** to bring a wife or husband, a disabled adult child or a parent, you must have lived legally in Italy without a break for at least two years when you apply [F0008]. This does not apply to children under 18, or to people with international protection [F0008].
3. **Income:** a lawful yearly income of at least the assegno sociale (Italy's state minimum pension, EUR 7,101.12 a year in 2026) plus half of it for each person you bring [F0012, F0017]. Count it from last year's tax return or CUD (Certificazione Unica, the employer's yearly income statement), or from all your payslips if you started work less than a year ago [F0046]. The income of relatives living with you also counts [F0014]. The 2026 lines are [F0017, F0018, G0001]:
   - 1 person: EUR 10,651.68 a year (about Tk 14.9 lakh) [F0017, G0001];
   - 2 people: EUR 14,202.24 (about Tk 19.8 lakh) [F0017, G0001];
   - 3 people: EUR 17,752.80 (about Tk 24.8 lakh) [F0017, G0001];
   - each more person: EUR 3,550.56 (about Tk 5 lakh) more [F0017, G0001];
   - two or more children under 14: at least EUR 14,202.24 in any case [F0013, F0018].
4. **Housing:** a home that meets health and hygiene standards, certified suitable by the comune (municipality) (see Family reunification, Step 1); for a single child under 14, the consent of the home's holder is enough [F0010, F0011].
5. **A parent over 65:** you also need health insurance for them covering all risks in Italy, or their enrolment in the SSN (Servizio Sanitario Nazionale, the national health service) for a fee [F0015].

**Coming together:** a worker who holds an entry visa for a job contract of at least one year can bring the family at the same time, under the same housing and income rules (familiari al seguito) [F0026].

### Step 2: Apply for the nulla osta on the ALI portal

You apply online, on the Interior Ministry's immigration portal (ALI); the law gives the SUI 150 days to decide [F0027, F0020, P0044].

You'll need:

- SPID (Sistema Pubblico di Identità Digitale, Italy's free digital ID), opened with an email, a mobile number, a valid ID and your health card with your codice fiscale (tax code) [F0038];
- a EUR 16 revenue stamp [F0039];
- scans of your passport and your family's passports (the pages with number and personal details), your valid permit (or the expired one with the renewal receipt), your codice fiscale and your family status certificate from the comune [F0042];
- the housing certificate (Family reunification, Step 1), or the receipt of its request [F0044];
- income papers: last tax return or CUD (all payslips if employed under a year), your contract or Unilav (the employer's hiring notice), the last three payslips, and form S3 signed by your employer within the last month showing the job continues and your monthly pay, with the employer's ID [F0046, F0047];
- for a parent over 65, your signed undertaking to take out health insurance [F0048].

1. **Log in with SPID** at nullaostalavoro.dlci.interno.it, fill in the family reunification form, and enter the revenue stamp's number [F0027, F0039].
2. **Attach the documents as PDFs,** with forms S1 (one child under 14), S2 and S3 or S3 bis as needed [F0028].
3. **Watch the "communications" section** of the ALI portal and your email: the SUI first asks the Questura (provincial police headquarters) to check for criminal records, then handles files in order of arrival [F0029, F0031].
4. **If you get a notice that papers are missing,** go to the SUI on the date it gives with the missing papers and the originals; if they are still incomplete that day, the application is rejected [F0030].
5. **Go to the SUI when called** with the original revenue stamp, a second EUR 16 stamp, your permit and a copy [F0040].
6. **When the nulla osta is issued,** the SUI sends it electronically to the Embassy in Dhaka; send the original to your family [F0032, F0041].

**Check it is real:** a genuine nulla osta appears in your own ALI portal account, under communications [F0032]. Check that your family's names, sex and dates of birth on it match their passports exactly, or the visa will be badly delayed [F0155, P0094].

**Time limit:** the nulla osta is valid 6 months and cannot be extended; your family must apply for the visa within that time [F0033, F0034].

Bangladesh joined the Apostille Convention on 30 March 2025, and the Embassy now wants every Bangladeshi document in original with an apostille from MoFA (Bangladesh's Ministry of Foreign Affairs), one apostille per document [F0151, F0068, F0070]. How to get an apostille: see Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Step 4 [B0157].

You'll need:

- **For a wife or husband:** the nikahnama (Muslim marriage contract) in Bangla and English and the marriage certificate, both original and apostilled; the marriage certificate must give the solemnisation date (nikahnama column 12), the registration date (column 24), and the volume, page and serial number; if either of you married before, the apostilled talaknama (divorce deed); Hindu and other-faith couples bring the marriage certificate or a true copy of the register, translated into English and apostilled [F0068, F0074, F0075, P0107];
- **For every applicant:** a family certificate (paribarik sanad) from the Union Parishad (rural council) chairman or the ward councillor, original and apostilled [F0059, F0069];
- **For children:** the original birth certificate and both parents' consent, apostilled; for a guardian, court papers from a Bangladeshi court, apostilled [F0076, P0109];
- **For parents:** a dependent certificate from the chairman or councillor on letterhead, or from a lawyer on stamp paper [F0065].

1. **Make every name match.** Name, father's name and date of birth must be identical in every document, with no exceptions; your wife's passport must show your name as her husband [F0061, F0064].
2. **Get the family certificate in the Embassy's format:** issued in the name of the living head of the family, listing all immediate family members from senior to junior, with dead members marked "late" and real addresses (your Italian address if you live in Italy) [F0060].
3. **If you married by phone or video,** make sure the nikahnama says so and shows the kazi (marriage registrar) was physically present [F0062].
4. **Do not use correction fluid or overwrite anything** [F0066].

**Check it is real:** the Embassy verifies every civil status document, and the law lets it hold the visa until it has [F0021]. If it doubts the papers, it can confirm a parent or child relationship by a DNA test the family pays for [F0022, F0023].

> **Need to know**
> - Does the Embassy in Dhaka ask Bangladeshi families for DNA tests in practice, which laboratory does it use, and what does the test cost?

### Step 4: The visa at VFS Dhaka

You'll need:

- the D-type (long-stay) visa form, signed (both parents sign for a child) [F0071, P0091];
- a recent ICAO photo (the international passport-photo standard: 3.5 x 4.0 cm, white background) [F0071];
- a passport valid at least three months beyond the visa, with two blank pages [F0072];
- a copy of the nulla osta [F0071];
- a one-way flight booking with the booking reference [F0071];
- copies of all the sponsor's passports, past and present, all pages, and the sponsor's Italian permit and identity card [F0073, F0058];
- the documents from Family reunification, Step 3;
- recommended for a wife: certified copies of all pages of the husband's passports since the wedding and the ID of all wedding witnesses, and for a phone or video marriage the ID and Italian permits of the witnesses in Italy [F0077, F0078];
- the fees (see What it costs).

1. **Book and submit at a VFS centre** with all originals (addresses and booking in Visit visa (Schengen), Step 2) [F0051, F0066, V0001].
2. **Give fingerprints,** except children aged 12 or under [V0089, M0025].
3. **For a wife, bring passport stamps showing the husband was in Bangladesh for the wedding** [F0063].
4. **File only one application per person:** the same person cannot have two applications at once, even with two passports [F0080].
5. **Track the application on VFS's passport tracker** and keep the phone number and email you gave working: if the Embassy wants more documents, the tracker shows it with a deadline, and missed messages are your own responsibility [F0083].
6. **Collect the passport within 90 days,** or it goes back to the Embassy and on to Bangladesh's Ministry of Home Affairs [F0084, F0085].

VFS states a minimum of 21 calendar days [F0056].

### Step 5: After arrival

1. **Within 48 hours,** you or the host give a written declaration of hospitality to the police or the comune [F0035].
2. **Within 8 days,** book an SUI appointment on the ALI portal ("book an appointment"), with the SPID used for the application; the passport entry stamp proves the date [F0036].
3. **At the SUI,** the entry is registered, the residence permit papers are completed and the family member gets a codice fiscale; the Questura then issues the permesso di soggiorno per motivi familiari (family residence permit) [F0037, K0051, F0092].
4. **For health service enrolment, residence registration and fingerprints,** follow Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker (non-seasonal), Step 7; health cover extends to dependent family members staying legally in Italy [K0079].

### Keeping the family permit

- It lasts as long as the sponsor's permit and is renewed with it [F0093].
- It allows work as an employee or self-employed, study, and registering at job centres [F0092].
- A child under 18 living with you gets a family permit valid until 18, or the EU long-term permit if you hold one [F0109].
- If the sponsor dies, or the couple legally separate or divorce, the family permit can be converted into a work or study permit [F0094].
- A permit given on marriage is revoked at once if the couple do not actually live together, unless they have a child [F0097]. A marriage made only to get a permit gets the application refused [F0024].

### If the nulla osta or visa is refused

You can challenge a refused nulla osta or family permit in the ordinary court (tribunale) [F0095].

> **Need to know**
> - What is the deadline to appeal a refused family nulla osta or visa, and which lawyers or patronati in Rome and Milan take these cases?

### Scams

- **Fake civil status papers:** in May 2025 the Embassy said it was finding more and more fake Bangladeshi and Italian civil status documents in family applications [F0152]. A forged paper means refusal and a flag in the Schengen system that permanently affects future travel to the EU [F0153]. Each applicant is personally responsible for every document filed in their name [P0111].
- **Duplicate applications:** a second application in another passport is not allowed [F0080, P0113].

### Family of an Italian or EU citizen

- **Visa:** the wife or husband, parents, parents-in-law and children under 21 of an Italian or EU citizen apply at VFS for the EU family member visa [F0100]. The visa fee and VFS charge are waived for those who prove they depend on the citizen [F0101]. They need an invitation letter from the citizen stating the intention to exercise the right to family unity, with the citizen's ID; a wife or husband also needs the marriage extract from the Italian comune plus the apostilled nikahnama and marriage certificate [F0102, F0103].
- **Residence card:** after three months the family member applies to the Questura for the residence card of a family member of an EU citizen, valid five years and free apart from the cost of the forms [F0104, F0105, F0106].
- **Marrying in Italy:** a foreigner legally resident for at least a year who marries an Italian, an EU citizen or a legal resident of Italy can get a family permit [F0096].
- **Parent of an Italian child:** a foreign parent of an Italian child living in Italy gets a family permit even without a valid permit [F0099].
- **Citizenship by marriage:** the spouse of an Italian can apply after two years' legal residence in Italy after the marriage (three years if living abroad), halved if the couple have children [F0147, F0148].

## Changing visa type

- **Visit visa to anything else:** Not allowed. A tourist or business visa does not allow work, study or applying to stay [V0044].
- **Study permit to work permit:** Allowed, outside the Decreto Flussi quotas, before the study permit expires, once a contratto di soggiorno (contract of stay) is signed at the SUI or, for self-employment, after the SUI's certification [V0126]. People admitted for a training course or traineeship can convert only after finishing it [V0128]. A student who wants to work more than 20 hours a week must convert first [V0125].
- **Study to job search, after graduating:** Allowed. A graduate with a degree, master's, PhD or ITS (Istituto Tecnologico Superiore, higher technical) diploma from Italy can get a permit of 9 to 12 months to look for a job or start a business linked to the studies, then convert it to a work permit [V0129]. This needs the minimum income set for family reunification, health cover, and registering as available for work at a Centro per l'Impiego (public job centre) [V0130].
- **Seasonal to non-seasonal work:** Allowed after at least three months of regular work, if an employer offers a fixed-term or permanent contract; ask the SUI (see Work visa: seasonal worker) [W0073].
- **Family permit to work or study:** Not needed to work, since the family permit already allows it [F0092]. If the sponsor dies, or the couple separate or divorce, it can be converted into a work or study permit [F0094].
- **Any permit to family permit:** A foreigner legally resident at least a year who marries an Italian, an EU citizen or a legal resident can get a family permit [F0096]. A family member already living legally in Italy can convert their own permit into a family permit within a year of its expiry, if the sponsor meets the requirements [F0098].
- **Asylum seeker's or special protection permit to work permit:** Not allowed. An asylum seeker's permit cannot be converted into a work permit even after working, and special protection permits cannot be converted since 2023 (see Asylum) [A0030, A0085].

A permit must be issued, renewed or converted within 90 days of the application [W0063]. While waiting with the post office receipt, you may stay and work until the police notify a reason to refuse [P0040].

## Having a child in Italy

A child born in Italy to Bangladeshi parents is not Italian at birth, because Italian citizenship passes by blood [F0111]. The child follows the legal status of the parent it lives with, and needs an Italian permit, a Bangladeshi birth registration and a Bangladeshi passport [F0109, F0114].

### Step 1: Register the birth in Italy

1. **Declare the birth** within 3 days at the hospital's office (direzione sanitaria), or within 10 days at the comune where the baby was born or at your own comune of residence [F0107, F0108].
2. **Get the atto di nascita (Italian birth record)** naming the child and both parents; every later step needs it [F0115].

> **Need to know**
> - How long does the comune take to record the birth so the atto di nascita can be printed, and does parents' missing residence registration delay it?

### Step 2: Get the child's residence permit

A child under 18 living with a legally resident parent gets a family permit valid until 18, or the EU long-term permit if the parent holds one [F0109].

> **Need to know**
> - Is a newborn's permit requested with the post office kit or directly at the Questura, and what does it cost?

### Step 3: Register the birth with the Bangladesh Embassy in Rome

1. **Apply online at [bdris.gov.bd](https://bdris.gov.bd/),** Bangladesh's birth and death registration system, choosing the Embassy in Rome as the office; an application not submitted within 15 days is deleted [F0114, F0116].
2. **Submit the printed application at the Embassy** with the Italian birth record naming the child and both parents and copies of both parents' passports; the baby does not need to come [F0115, F0118]. The fee is EUR 1 (about Tk 140) [B0082, G0001].
3. **Collect the certificate:** it usually takes 5 working days [F0117].

Register a birth only once: registering the same person twice is an offence [F0119].

### Step 4: Get the child a Bangladeshi passport

1. **Fill in the passport form on passport.gov.bd;** at the payment page click "Skip Payment", because the Embassy enters the fee [F0121].
2. **Book one appointment for the whole family** on the Embassy's website [F0126].
3. **Bring** the Bangladeshi birth registration certificate, a copy of the Italian birth record, copies of both parents' passports, and photos (2 of the child, 1 of each parent) [F0120].
4. **Pay by bank card,** not cash: the Embassy's FAQ gives EUR 100 (about Tk 14,000) for a child's passport [F0122, F0123, G0001].

A child under 2 getting a first passport does not need to come; an older child must [F0124]. The passport is printed in Dhaka and usually reaches the Embassy within 2 months; there is no urgent service [F0125].

> **Need to know**
> - Does the Rome Embassy charge EUR 100 for a child's first passport, as its FAQ says, or EUR 105 or 130 as on its 2026 fee list?

### Can the child become Italian?

- **At 18:** a child born in Italy who has lived there legally without a break becomes Italian by declaring it within one year of turning 18 [F0112].
- **Earlier:** a child born in Italy can apply after three years' legal residence [F0113].

For the parents' own citizenship, see Becoming an Italian citizen.

**Travelling to Bangladesh on an Italian passport:** the child needs a No Visa Required (NVR) seal from the Bangladesh Embassy [F0128]:

1. Fill in the form on visa.gov.bd [F0128].
2. Email it with the passports to bdvisa.rome@gmail.com and wait for approval [F0128].
3. Book an appointment and pay EUR 50 (about Tk 7,000) per passport; the seal takes 5 working days [F0130, F0131, G0001].

A child without a Bangladeshi passport also needs the Bangladeshi birth certificate and the parents' Bangladeshi passports for the seal [F0129].

### If a parent has no permit

The juvenile court can authorise a parent or other family member to enter or stay in Italy for a set time, for serious reasons linked to a child living in Italy [F0110]. A foreign parent of an Italian child living in Italy gets a family permit even without a valid permit [F0099].
