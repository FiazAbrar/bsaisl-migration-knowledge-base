### For Background, "Is it open now, and who can go?" (hand to N)

#### Size

BMET (the Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training) gave emigration clearance for Italy to 556 workers in 2022, 16,846 in 2023, 1,162 in 2024, 9,365 in 2025 and 8,614 from 1 January to 9 November 2026 [B0001, B0002, B0003, B0004, B0005]. Italy was the 8th-largest destination by clearances in 2023, 9th in 2025 and 8th in 2026 so far [B0024, B0025, B0026]. Few women go: 152 in 2023, 12 in 2024, 286 in 2025 and 261 in 2026 to 9 November [B0006, B0007, B0008, B0009].

The flow follows Italy's visa decisions, not Bangladesh's: clearances peaked at 4,043 in July 2023, fell to 59 in December 2023 and stayed between 11 and 234 a month through 2024 [B0010, B0011, B0012, B0013]. They recovered to 1,644 in August 2025 and have run at 450 to 1,310 a month in 2026 (690 in October) [B0014, B0015, B0016]. On OEP, most 2025-2026 clearances carry vague job titles such as "A Casual Worker" (2,842 in 2026) and "General Labour" (2,427); 453 in 2025 were "Agriculture Labour" [B0022, B0023, B0020].

> **Need to know**
> - What does the OEP job title "Agent" (616 Italy clearances in 2025) mean in practice?
> - Does "A Casual Worker" on OEP mean a seasonal (stagionale) nulla osta?

#### What closed it before

Italy revoked Bangladesh's place in its worker quota in 2012, and formal labour migration stopped in 2013 [B0032]. A 2020 bilateral agreement restarted it, and workers began going again in 2021 [B0033]. Visa processing at the Italian Embassy in Dhaka then took over a year in some cases, and Bangladesh's Foreign Adviser asked Italy in May 2025 to clear the pending applications [B0034, B0029]. In December 2025 the Embassy said holders of a 2023 or 2024 nulla osta must wait until the Italian immigration office finishes reviewing it [B0048].

### Work visa: Decreto Flussi worker, Step 1: Find a real job (Bangladesh-side items)

1. **Register on the Overseas Employment Platform (OEP),** BMET's website for job-seekers [B0124]. BMET clears no one for work abroad without it, and the Ministry warns never to go without BMET clearance [B0127].

   You'll need [B0123, B0124]:

   - your NID (national ID card);
   - a phone that can receive codes;
   - Tk 200 for the government fee.

   1. Open [employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration](https://employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration) and type your name in English exactly as on your NID, your mobile and NID numbers, and your date of birth, division and district as on the NID; type the captcha, accept the terms and press "যাচাই করুন" (Verify) [B0123].
   2. Finish the form and pay the Tk 200 fee [B0124].
   3. Take 2 printed copies of the registration slip to your DEMO (District Employment and Manpower Office, BMET's district office) to give your fingerprints [B0124]. DEMO addresses and phones are on BMET's list at [bmet.gov.bd](https://bmet.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922e139933eb65569e2af6c) [B0125].

   **If it fails:** call the government's migrant call centre, 16135 (+8809610102030 from abroad) [B0144].

2. **Know that there is no government (G2G) or BOESL route to Italy.** Bangladesh and Italy signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on Migration and Mobility in Dhaka on 6 May 2025; it covers legal migration and the return of people without the right to stay, and sets no number of workers [B0027, B0030]. Bangladesh asked Italy for larger quotas for skilled and semi-skilled workers at that meeting, and in August 2026 asked again for Italian language and skills training [B0028, B0039]. A job in Italy still comes through an Italian employer's nulla osta (work authorisation) under the quota (see Step 3: The employer gets your nulla osta).

   > **Need to know**
   > - Has the 2025 MoU's joint committee met, and did it give Bangladesh a reserved quota in the 2026-2028 decree?
   > - Has BOESL (Bangladesh Overseas Employment and Services Limited, the state recruiter) ever posted an Italy circular?

3. **The EU Talent Partnership, the only government-run training route.** The EU-funded Talent Partnership project (EUR 3 million, 2024 to 2027), run by BMET with the ILO (International Labour Organization), trains workers for EU jobs [B0040]. By July 2026, 698 trainees had NSDA (National Skills Development Authority) certificates, mainly for Italy, and many were waiting for employer interviews [B0041, B0042]. Its next phase adds Italian language training and a 10-day pre-departure orientation [B0043]. Italy's Ambassador said in October 2026 that the Embassy would hold a special event in Dhaka in November 2026 to recruit workers through language and skills training [B0046].

   > **Need to know**
   > - How does a worker apply for a Talent Partnership course, at which TTCs, and is it free?
   > - How many Talent Partnership graduates have actually received an Italian nulla osta?
   > - Where and when is the Italian Embassy's November 2026 recruitment event, and who can attend?

4. **Know what you should pay.** The Ministry's migration cost caps (2017) cover 14 countries; Italy is not one of them, so no legal maximum exists for Italy [B0049]. The Italian Embassy's migration expert said in September 2026 that the Italian visa fee is only EUR 65 while the migration cost in Bangladesh is very high, and that young people pay traffickers Tk 20 to 30 lakh for illegal routes [B0044, B0045]. Real prices and who takes them are in Background (see What you pay, and what you earn and keep). Pay any Bangladeshi agency only into its own bank account, against a receipt [B0128].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Do BMET permission letters exist for any Italy job order? None appear among the 3,162 entries on BMET's list up to December 2025.

5. **If you need to borrow,** Probashi Kallyan Bank (PKB), the government bank for migrant workers, lends up to Tk 3 lakh to people aged 18 to 60 [B0160, B0161]. You open a PKB savings account with at least Tk 500 first, and one of your guarantors must be a family member or close relative [B0162, B0163]. PKB helpline: 16238, or +880 9677-787878 from abroad [B0164].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does PKB lend against an Italian nulla osta, or only once the visa is stamped?

6. **If a broker or agency takes your money,** complain in writing to BMET's Director General with your evidence; it is free and meant to be settled within 30 working days [B0139]. Hand it in at BMET, 89/2 Kakrail, Dhaka-1000 [B0174]. Then, in this order:

   1. BMET's Additional Director General (Employment): 02-48321949, 01720105492, adgemp@bmet.gov.bd [B0135].
   2. The Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare's grievance officer: 02-41030224, 01712992086, adsadmin@probashi.gov.bd (30 working days) [B0177].
   3. Call 16135 for advice at any stage [B0144].

### Step 4: Passport, police clearance and certificates

1. **Get an e-passport.** Over age 20 you need your NID, and you fill in the form exactly as on it [B0148]. A 48-page passport costs [B0145]:

   - 5 years: Tk 4,025 (regular), Tk 6,325 (express), Tk 8,625 (super express);
   - 10 years: Tk 5,750, Tk 8,050 or Tk 10,350.

   1. Pay online through A-Challan, DGePay or ShurjoPay, or at any bank through A-Challan [B0147].
   2. Enrol at the passport office; with an online-verified NID there is no police verification [B0150].
   3. Delivery takes up to 15, 7 or 2 working days from enrolment (regular, express, super express) [B0146].

   **If it is lost or stolen:** file a GD (general diary) at the nearest police station quickly, then re-apply with a copy of the old passport and the GD [B0149]. Passport helpline: 16445 (09666716445 from abroad), 24 hours [B0151].

2. **Police clearance, if the Embassy or employer asks for it.** You'll need a passport valid at least 3 months, and your present address must be one of your passport's addresses [B0152].

   1. Apply at [pcc.police.gov.bd](https://pcc.police.gov.bd/) [B0152].
   2. Pay Tk 1,500 by treasury challan, code 1-7301-0001-2681, at Bangladesh Bank or Sonali Bank, or online by card with a service charge [B0153].
   3. Track it by texting PCC S and your reference number to 26969 [B0154].

   Helpdesk: Sunday to Thursday, 9 am to 5 pm, 01320001824, 01320001825, help.pcc@police.gov.bd [B0155].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does the Italian Embassy in Dhaka require a police clearance for a Decreto Flussi work visa, or only for family and study visas?

3. **Apostille your certificates in Bangladesh, not at the Italian Embassy.** Since June 2025 the Italian Embassy in Dhaka no longer legalises Bangladeshi public documents; they need an apostille (the international certificate under the Hague Convention) from Bangladesh's Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) through MyGov [B0051]. Italy is among the 114 countries that accept Bangladesh's apostille [B0072, B0070].

   You'll need [B0156, B0158]:

   - a scan of each certificate (JPEG, JPG or PNG);
   - Tk 200 per page;
   - for SSC (Secondary School Certificate) and HSC (Higher Secondary Certificate): verification by your Education Board and the Ministry of Education first.

   1. Register on [mygov.bd](https://www.mygov.bd/), choose the attestation service for your document type, fill in the form and upload the document [B0157].
   2. Pay Tk 200 per page online; it takes 5 working days [B0156].
   3. An SMS comes when the apostille is issued; download it from your MyGov account [B0157].

   A birth certificate is first notarised, then attested by the Law Ministry, then by MoFA [B0159].

4. **Translate into Italian with an Embassy-authorised translator, through VFS.** All translation and legalisation requests go through VFS, the Embassy's visa agency, and legalisation of an e-apostille is free [B0052, B0058]. The Embassy's authorised translators are [B0055, B0057, B0071, B0056]:

   - Tradusoft.com, 136 Link Road, Middle Badda, Dhaka; +8801776015230;
   - EXINSO Ltd., House 63/4 (1st floor), Progoti Sharani, North Badda, Dhaka 1212; +8801797-412712;
   - Prof. Stefania Chiapello; +8801755596360.

   The Embassy's 2026 fee list (at EUR 1 = Tk 140) charges EUR 37 (Tk 5,180) each for a police clearance or a study certificate [B0059, B0060, B0061]. VFS centres take these requests in Dhaka (Nafi Tower, 4th and 5th floors, 53 South Gulshan Avenue, Gulshan 1), Chattogram (World Trade Center, 5th floor, 102/103 Agrabad) and Sylhet (Nirvana Inn Complex, Ramer Dighir Par, Mirzajungle Road) [B0064, B0067, B0066]. VFS: +880 9606777666, info.itbd@vfshelpline.com [B0065].

   **Scams:** Embassy appointments are always free, including through VFS; anyone selling a slot is not the Embassy [B0053]. Forging documents, signatures or seals is prosecuted [B0054].

5. **A declaration of value (dichiarazione di valore) for a degree,** the Embassy's statement of what a foreign qualification is worth in Italy, is EUR 50 (Tk 7,000) on the fee list and free for study in Italy [B0062].

### Step 6: Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight

1. **Do the compulsory 3-day pre-departure orientation (PDO)** at one of BMET's 70 training institutes; the institute sets the fee, paid against a money receipt [B0136]. Institute list: [bmet.gov.bd](https://bmet.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922dd2c933eb65569e13cd6) [B0126]. Download your certificate at [training.oep.gov.bd/pdo-certificate](https://training.oep.gov.bd/pdo-certificate) with your passport or NID number and the captcha [B0141].

2. **Get BMET clearance (the smart card) yourself.** A worker who got the visa on their own applies at BMET's emigration branch in Dhaka or the Chattogram DEMO; it takes 3 working days [B0132].

   You'll need [B0132]:

   - an application;
   - a copy of the visa;
   - the contract, if you have one;
   - an attested copy of your OEP registration;
   - an undertaking on Tk 300 non-judicial stamp paper;
   - the original pay order for the welfare fee and insurance.

   Your costs:

   - welfare fee to the Wage Earners' Welfare Board: Tk 5,500 from 1 October 2026 (it was Tk 3,500) [B0140, B0133];
   - insurance: a one-time Tk 1,000 premium, 5 years' cover, Tk 10 lakh [B0131];
   - clearance fee: Tk 250 [B0133];
   - source tax on a single visa: Tk 400 (unattested visa) or Tk 250 (attested visa) [B0133].

   A woman going on a single visa also needs a no-objection letter from her legal guardian on Tk 150 non-judicial stamp paper [B0142].

   **Check it is real** at [portal.wewb.gov.bd/bmet-wewbm-check](https://portal.wewb.gov.bd/bmet-wewbm-check) with your passport number and the captcha [B0143]; the airport welfare desks at Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet check every departing worker's smart card [B0137]. **If it fails:** BMET's Director (Emigration), +8802222222415, 01711-135901, diremi@bmet.gov.bd [B0135].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does BMET check an Italian nulla osta or visa before clearance, and how? BMET's online visa check covers only Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Singapore and the UAE [B0134].
   > - Is an Italy worker always cleared as a "single visa", or must an agency file it?
   > - Is the source tax for an Italian visa Tk 400 or Tk 250 (is a stamped Schengen D visa "attested")?

3. **Before you fly,** keep one set of copies at home and at least 3 with you, open at least 2 bank accounts (one yours, one a family member's), and never carry a packet whose contents you don't know [B0130].

   > **Need to know**

### Coming home

1. **At the airport,** the Probashi Kallyan (expatriate welfare) desk helps with customs and lost luggage and takes complaints from workers returning after abuse or fraud [B0167, B0168]. Dhaka desk: +8802 8901040, 01404 413388; Chattogram: 01913631797; Sylhet: 01558277904 [B0170, B0138].
2. **If you are sick or injured,** the Welfare Board's ambulance takes you free from Dhaka or Chattogram airport [B0169].
3. **If you need a bed,** the Wage Earners' Centre near Shahjalal airport costs Tk 100 to enter and Tk 200 a night; apply at wec.wewb.gov.bd [B0171]. The Probashi Lounge at Shahjalal airport has free wifi and telephone for workers who show their BMET clearance or Board membership [B0172].
4. **To start a business,** PKB's rehabilitation loan lends returnees up to Tk 50 lakh, up to Tk 3 lakh without collateral, with an up-to-date trade licence [B0165, B0166].
5. **If your family needs legal help in Bangladesh,** apply to the Wage Earners' Welfare Board directly or through the DC office's expatriate welfare branch or the DEMO [B0173].

### Help from the Bangladesh Embassy in Rome and the Consulate in Milan

Bangladesh has an Embassy in Rome and a Consulate General in Milan, plus honorary consuls in Catania, Florence, Palermo, Genoa, Cagliari, Naples, Venice (Marghera) and Bari [B0073, B0100, B0096]. The Milan Consulate also runs two-day mobile services in other northern cities, such as Udine in September 2026 [B0119].

**Passport.** Apply online at [epassport.gov.bd/onboarding](https://www.epassport.gov.bd/onboarding), choosing country Italy and mission Rome or Milan; choosing a Bangladesh district sends the file to that district's office instead [B0117]. Then:

1. Book an appointment: Rome at [bdembassyrome.com](https://www.bdembassyrome.com); Milan at [passport.bcgmilan.it](https://passport.bcgmilan.it/), which opens every Thursday and confirms by email within 72 hours [B0085, B0115, B0116].
2. Pay by bank card; Rome takes no cash [B0084]. Rome's 2026 fees: EUR 130 for 10 years, EUR 105 for 5 years [B0078].
3. The passport is printed in Dhaka and usually arrives within 2 months; there is no emergency service [B0086]. Check with the ITA100000... number on your enrolment slip; DHL return costs about EUR 10 to 20 [B0088, B0089].

You can renew when 6 months or less are left [B0087]. If your passport is lost in Italy, file a denuncia (police report) at the Questura or Carabinieri and bring the original [B0118, B0120]. Without an NID, a birth certificate and a valid permesso di soggiorno (residence permit) let you correct your name, parents' names or age (by up to 8 years) [B0121].

> **Need to know**
> - The Rome FAQ says EUR 100 for any passport [B0083]; which fee does the Embassy charge at the counter in November 2026?

**NID.** Apply at the Rome Embassy with the online form, an English birth registration and its online-verified copy, your valid passport and a copy, and one photo [B0095].

**Power of attorney** (to let someone in Bangladesh act for you, for example on land or a court case). Bring a written power of attorney with the full names, dates of birth and document numbers of everyone giving and receiving it, 2 photos of each, and copies of your valid passport and the holder's passport or NID; everyone giving it must come in person [B0113, B0114]. Rome charges EUR 50 for the first 5 pages, then EUR 5 a page [B0081].

**Wage Earners' Welfare Board membership.** Workers who came to Italy on their own can join at the mission, aged 18 to 65 [B0122]. Fill in the form at [portal.wewb.gov.bd/register](https://portal.wewb.gov.bd/register), then hand in the printout with copies of your passport and permesso, a colour photo, a return envelope and EUR 32; Milan needs no appointment and takes applications daily until 1 pm [B0104, B0105, B0106]. Members' families get, among other things, Tk 35,000 for transport and burial and a Tk 3 lakh grant after a death abroad [B0108, B0107].

**If a Bangladeshi dies in Italy.** The mission issues the nulla osta to send the body home free, and pays the air freight if the person was a Welfare Board member, or on application if the family is poor [B0110, B0091, B0092]. Email the labour welfare wing (Rome: welfare.rome@gmail.com; Milan: fslbcgmilan@gmail.com and mission.milan@mofa.gov.bd) with [B0093, B0111, B0112]:

- the death certificate from the Italian hospital or comune (town hall);
- the original passport and a copy;
- name, phone and ID of a Bangladeshi in Italy handling the transport;
- name, phone and ID of the person receiving the body in Bangladesh;
- the Welfare Board membership copy, if any.

**Legal help.** If you face a case and cannot pay a lawyer, the Milan Consulate issues a certificate of financial hardship so you can get a free lawyer from the Italian state [B0101]. Bring your original passport and a copy and the case papers from the police, Carabinieri or Guardia di Finanza, and come for an interview [B0102]. The Consulate also visits Bangladeshis in Italian jails [B0103]. The Rome Embassy's labour welfare wing visits workplaces to check conditions and pay (welfare.rome@gmail.com) [B0094].
