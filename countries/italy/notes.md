- 2027 round: pre-fill 23 Oct-7 Dec 2026 (live now), click day non-seasonal 16 Feb 2027.
- Ambassador interview (16 Sep 2026, Embassy site): ~10,000 work visas 2025, similar 2026; ~12-13k family visas/yr; ~250,000 Bangladeshis in Italy; ~USD 1.7 bn remittances; ~500 study visas; old suspended nulla osta "rejected in large numbers"; several hundred via pre-departure training.
