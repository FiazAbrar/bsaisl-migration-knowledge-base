Last updated on: 06/07/2026

All relevant information on visas for Italy can be found at the following address:

Visto per l’Italia

Thanks to the Visa Database it is possible to obtain practical information to apply for an entry visa to Italy:

IMPORTANT NOTICE

The forgery of documents, signatures and seals is a crime that is prosecuted under current regulations.

VISA APPLICATION:

Visas for Italy can only be requested at the VFS Agency, Italy Visa and Legalization Application Centre

Link: 
VFS Global

Web portal for work visa (Decreto Flussi)

Work visa applicants in Decreto Flussi quotas, holders of a valid Nulla Osta issued after 22 October 2024, will be able to begin their application process through the VFS webform portal.

The portal is accessible through the following link: 
https://vfseu.mioot.com/forms/ITABDE/

Attention: Italian Law Decree 145/2024 introduced the requirement for 
confirmation of the job offer by the employer in Italy
. Such confirmation will be requested by the SUI (Sportello Unico per l’Immigrazione) to the employer once the visa application has been processed, and 
must be provided within 15 days
.

CITIZENSHIP

For information on the rules regarding acquisition of Italian citizenship. Refer to the Consular Services Guide of the Ministry of External Affairs and International Cooperation:

Citizenship – Ministero degli Affari Esteri e della Cooperazione Internazionale

Translations and legalizations:

Sunday to Thursday, from 2 pm to 4 pm. Visa applications: Sunday to Thursday, 9 a.m. to 12 p.m.

Return documents and passports: from Sunday to Thursday, from 4 p.m.to 5 p.m.

Applicants are advised that passport withdrawal requests will be accepted by VFS Global only under the following conditions:

The applicant must be present in person at the Visa Application Centre.

The applicant must sign the official withdrawal request form.

A copy of the applicant’s valid ID and VFS receipt must be attached to the request.

Please note that any withdrawal request not fulfilling the above conditions would not be accepted.
