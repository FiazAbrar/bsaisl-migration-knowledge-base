 Embassy of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh, Rome 

Ambassador and Permanent Representative to FAO, WFP & IFAD

H.E. Mr. A. T. M. Rokebul Haque

 I welcome you to the official website of the Embassy of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh to Italy. I extend my warm greetings to the People of Italy, Montenegro & Serbia and the entire expatriate Bangladesh nationals living within the jurisdiction of this Mission. Bangladesh-Italy enjoys excellent bilateral relations since establishment of their diplomatic relations in 1973. Italy is one of the first countries in Europe which recognized Bangladesh (12 February 1972) within months of its glorious victory on 16th December 1971. Since then the relationship between Bangladesh and Italy has grown many fold. The Mission appreciates the contribution of the Bangladesh community the largest in European Union to the economy of Bangladesh and Italy. The Embassy is committed to provide all necessary consular and welfare services efficiently to the expatriate community of Bangladesh. This Mission is also concurrently accredited to Serbia and Montenegro. The Embassy is also committed to further consolidate and promote Bangladesh’s relations with Montenegro & Serbia. Besides, on the multilateral front Bangladesh Mission in Rome actually participates all the meetings and negotiations of Rome-based UN agencies like WFP, IFAD and FAO. Bangladesh is now a Member of FAO Council and WFP Executive Board along with its regular contribution to different forums of those agencies. This is a manifestation of trust and respect that Bangladesh enjoys among the international community. On this website and also in our verified Facebookpage, people will get information and updates on services extended by the Embassy for our expatriate community. Thank you very much for visiting our website. A.T.M Rokebul Haque Ambassador 

 With warm regards, 

Ambassador and Permanent Representative to FAO, WFP & IFAD
