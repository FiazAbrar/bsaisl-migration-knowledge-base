##### 1 August 2026

# Update on L/N Nulla Osta submission process

Please note that applicants holding a Nulla Osta issued under L/N (Work Visa category) are not required to book an online appointment.

Applicants may visit the Visa Application Centre on a walk-in basis and submit:

- A hard copy of the approved Nulla Osta; and

- A copy of the passport biodata page.

These documents will be forwarded to the Embassy for verification.

Once the Embassy has validated the Nulla Osta, applicants will receive an email confirming the date and time for submission of their visa application.
