Embassy of Italy Dhaka
Information for work visa applicants
Yesterday, 26 May, a group of representatives of work visa applicants demonstrating in front of VFS were received at the Embassy.
The Embassy expressed understanding for the distress of applicants whose "Nulla Osta" is still suspended by effect of Italian Law Decree 145/2024, pending re-examination.
The Embassy disclosed that, since the introduction of "Nulla Osta" re-examination, over 7000 work visas have already been either issued or rejected, and hundreds of applicants are being called through VFS, in order to either re-submit their passport for receiving a work visa, or to submit their application, in case of applicants who requested an appointment via email in previous months and were kept on hold.
It was reiterated that the "Nulla Osta" are being reviewed by Provincial Immigration Authorities in Italy (SUI – Sportello Unico Immigrazione). However, the backlog is significant and the process requires time: concerned applicants are invited to be patient.
It was also underlined that any "Nulla Osta" that has not yet been reviewed by Immigration Authorities in Italy cannot be processed by the Embassy. During the process of re-examination, the expiration of suspended "Nulla Osta" is considered frozen and each document will eventually be processed.
Through VFS, the Embassy has launched a webform portal to streamline 2025 Flow Decree quota applications. Regrettably, over 70% of the entries contain fake or invalid "Nulla Osta" documents, hampering the speed at which genuine applicants can be identified and called in to present their application.
The Embassy clarified that VFS Global is tasked only with the logistical process of visa applications, and holds no authority over the decision-making and over checks to assess the authenticity and veracity of documents.
It was recalled that, to meet previous requests, in late 2024 and early 2025 all passports have been returned to applicants pending their "Nulla Osta" verification process.
Applicants have also been recommended to monitor the web pages of the Embassy and VFS, distrusting second-hand information, particularly when shared by middle men. They have been warned that entrusting middle men with their livelihood constitutes a serious risk.
The Embassy is actively working on improving outlets of communication with applicants, to minimise second-hand information. If the service of an agency is necessary, applicants should be thoroughly informed about the agent's reputation, ask for a receipt for every sum they pay, and be aware of each and every document that is being presented in their name.
While providing contacts in their application, applicants are invited to make sure that VFS is always able to contact them directly and not through an intermediary.
Neither the Embassy, nor VFS will ever ask applicants for money except for publicly listed fees, or hold an applicant's passport against their will. Were any such facts to occur, applicants should immediately report to the authorities.
The Embassy of Italy assures all concerned applicants of its commitment to support legal channels of migration from Bangladesh to Italy and provide the fastest and most orderly service possible, according to Italian laws and regulations, the abuse of which will not be allowed.
27 May 2025
