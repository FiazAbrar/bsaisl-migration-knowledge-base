Consular Services 

General information on the website of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation.

Consular services are provided in accordance with principles of equality, impartiality, efficiency and transparency, according to the chronological order in which files are submitted, except for service requirements.

Appointments, where scheduled, are always free of charge, also when booked through VFS
. The Embassy has continuous checks on the integrity of the entire process.

The counterfeiting of documents, signatures and seals is an offence that is prosecuted under the law
.

 Consular Desk Hours: 

All legalization procedures must be submitted to the VFS agency. The other types of application for which the signature is required in front of an official of the Embassy will be received on Tuesdays and Thursdays from 9.30 to 12.30.

From June 2025, public documents of Bangladesh do not need to be legalized at the Consular Office of the Embassy of Italy in Dhaka, but they need to receive the “apostille” from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Bangladesh through the 
“MyGov”
 web portal.

Italian citizens can make an appointment during office hours, using telephone numbers and e-mail addresses.

The hours are as follows:

Visa Submission: 8.30 AM – 01.00 PM

Passport Delivery: 8.30 AM – 01.00 PM

Legalization Submission: 02.00 PM – 03.30 PM

Legalization Delivery: 02.00 PM to 03.30 PM

Information: 03.30 PM to 04.30 PM

Call Center: 08.30 AM – 04.30 PM
