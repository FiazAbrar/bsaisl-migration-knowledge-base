Nationality of arrivals

In December, Bangladeshi nationals accounted for 31 per cent of all sea arrivals (30% in November), followed by Egyptians (12%,
down from 15% in November) and Sudanese (10%, up from 9% in November). Other nationalities arriving in significant numbers
included Algerians (8%), Tunisians (6%), Somalis (5%), Malians and Pakistanis (4%), and Eritreans and Nigerians (3%). In December,
the main nationalities of individuals departing from Libya were Bangladeshi nationals at 39 per cent (up from 33% in November),
followed by Egyptians (15%, compared to 16% in November) and Sudanese (13%, up from 10% in November). From Tunisia, the
main nationalities were Tunisians (43%, down sharply from 90% in November), followed by Malians (12%) and Guineans (11%);
neither nationality had been recorded among departures in November. Almost all individuals departing from Algeria were
Algerian nationals (88%, compared to 91% in November), with a smaller proportion of persons of unknown nationality (12%).
Departures from Türkiye comprised exclusively Iranian nationals (100%), with no such departures recorded in the previous
month.

Country of embarkation

In 2025, 88 per cent of people reaching Italy via sea departed from Libya (58,408 persons in 1,103 landings), followed by
7 per cent from Tunisia (4,861 persons in 213 landings), 3 per cent from Algeria (1,812 persons in 176 landings), 2 per
cent from Türkiye (1,224 people in 17 landings), and less than 1 per cent from Greece (11 people in one landing). In
December, Libya was the main country of departure (78% of all people landed in Italy), followed by Tunisia (13%), Algeria
(6%) and Türkiye (less than 1%).
