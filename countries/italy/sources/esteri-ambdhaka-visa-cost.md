# Cost for a visa and how to pay

Last updated on: 30/06/2026

**For Schengen Visas (Type “C”):**

Adults: 90 euros

Children between 6 and 12 years of age: 45 euros

**National Visas (Type “D”):** 116 euros

**National Visas for Study Purposes (Type “D”):** 50 euros

For all visas the payment is processed in cash in local currency (BDT).
