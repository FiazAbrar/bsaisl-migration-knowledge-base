The right to obtain or to acquire family reunite by a foreign citizen regularly residing in Italy is recognized by articles 28, 29 and 29 bis of legislative law 286/98 and subsequently amended (consolidated text of immigration laws). Family reunification is allowed to foreign family members in possession of a special entry visa issued by the italian embassy of the country of origin. To obtain a visa, the foreign citizen residing in Italy have to submit at the proper immigration office a request for the authorization permit that will enable him/her to reunify the family. On the presentation of which the embassy grants the foreign citizen an entry visa for family reasons. The family reunification is therefore divided in two stages: The first stage is the verification of the requirements presented by the foreign citizen at the immigration office and to verify that the foreign citizen have a clean record to demostrate that he/she have no any impediments to public safety for the issue of the authorization permit for family reuinfication. The second stage is strictly connected to the first and is the verification of the requirements for the issue of the entry visa. Who can apply for family reuinification. They can apply for family reuinification, foreigners residing in Italy who are holders of UE Residence card according to law 30/2007, Blue UE card, Stay permit UE for long-term residence, or a residence permit with a duration of not less than one year in which it was issued:
- Asylum permit
- Subsidiary Protection permit
- Subordinate work permit
- Autonomy work permit
- Family reasons permit
- Religious reasons permit
- Stay permit ICT (regardless of the duration)
- Scientific research permit (regardless of the duration)
- Study permit

How to submit the applications: The application for authorization permit for family reunification, accompanied by all the documents required can only be submitted exclusively by computerized procedure through the dedicated wed site of the Ministry of internal affairs: https://nullaostalavoro.dlci.interno.it. # EXAMINATION OF THE APPLICATION AND CONCLUSION OF THE PROCEEDING

The Unified Immigration Desk (SUI) under the The Territorial Government Office download the application for family reunification and the documentation attached electronically. Then the SUI will proceed with the required verification through the Police Headquarters to ensure regarding any impediments to the application (e.g. criminal convictions). Once the Police Headquarters gives a positive response, the Territorial Government Office will examine family reunification applications in a chronological order of arrival. We inform that all communications will be accessible and consultable exclusively in your reserved area of the Ministry of the Interior's services portal,in the communications section.

# INCOMPLETE DOCUMENTATION / NON-CONFORMING DOCUMENTATION

In case the documentation attached to the reunification application is incomplete or all the requirements are not fully satisfied, the person concerned receives a notification of the rejection of the application on the “ALI portal”, according to Article 10 bis of Law 241/90, as amended. The rejection notice will indicate the date of the convocation at the immigration office (“sportello unico”): on that occasion, it will be necessary to submit the documentation to supplement and overcome the impediments communicated in the notice letter, it will be necessary to produce the original documentation produced at the time of submission of the application and necessary for the clearance. It should be noted that according to Article 10 bis of Law 241/1990, in case that on the day and time indicated in the appointment notice, the required documents are incomplete, the application will be rejected.

# COMPLETE AND COMPLIANT SUPPLEMENTARY DOCUMENTATION

If the documents are complete and compliant, the Immigration Office “S.U.I.” (Sportello Unico Immigrazione) will electronically send the “entry clearance” to the relevant embassy. This will be contextually visible on the ALI Portal, in the communications section.

# POST-CLEARANCE REQUIREMENTS

Please note that the notification of “entry clearance” will be given only through the ALI Portal. The Entry Clearance is valid for a period of 6 months from the date of the application previously submitted online. This period cannot be extended and on the expiry date it will not be renewed.

The family member to be reunified therefore has 6 months to book an appointment at the Italian Embassy in the country of origin and apply for a visa relating to the Entry Clearance issued in Italy. It will be necessary, at that appointment, to provide documents proving the family relationship. After appropriate assessments, the Embassy will issue the visa, which it will be valid for a period of 365 days (12 months). The family members to be reunited have 12 months time to enter Italy. The applicant or the host has to present a written declaration of hospitality, within 48 hours of arrival of the family member in Italy, to the local authority for public security (Police headquarters or Police Office, The Municipality). Within 8 days of the arrival of the family member in Italy (the stamp on the passport will be used as proof), the applicant has to book an appointment on the Territorial Government Office’s website “portale ALI”, in the section “book an appointment”, by logging in with the SPID credentials used at the time of application submission. In case the applicant does not have the spid credentials used to submit the application available, it will be possible to book an appointment on the "ZeroCoda" platform. The applicant must present himself at the immigration office (Sportello Unico) of Reggio Emilia, on the established date, to register the entry into Italy and complete the issuing of the residence permit documents.
