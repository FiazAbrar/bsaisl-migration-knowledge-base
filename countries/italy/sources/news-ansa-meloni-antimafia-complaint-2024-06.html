Meloni says filed anti-mafia complaint on migrant flows

Regular flows being used for irregular immigration says PM

Ansa English Desk ROME - 04 June 2024 - News

(ANSA) - ROME, JUN 4 - Premier Giorgia Meloni said Tuesday she had gone to Italy's national anti-mafia and anti-terrorism prosecutor Giovanni Melillo earlier in the day to deliver a complaint on the entry flows into Italy of foreign workers over the last few years benefitting from the so-called 'Flow Decree' passed by a previous government.
    She aid "regular migrant flows for work reasons are being used as a further channel of irregular immigration". (ANSA).
