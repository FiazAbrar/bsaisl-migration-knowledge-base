Where to apply for a visa

Last updated on: 30/06/2026

All relevant information on visas for Italy are available at the link: 
https://www.esteri.it/en/servizi-consolari-e-visti/ingressosoggiornoinitalia/

The 
Visa Database
 contains all relevant practical information to request a visa for Italy: 
https://www.esteri.it/en/servizi-consolari-e-visti/ingressosoggiornoinitalia/

Visa Applications for Italy can be filed exclusively at the VFS Global, e-mail: 
info.itbd@vfshelpline.com
 ; Phone: +880 9606777666 / +880 966611384 (Mobile Phones only), or through the “contact us” section at the link: 
https://visa.vfsglobal.com/bgd/en/ita/contact-us

All the necessary information on all types of visas to Italy are available on the VFS Global website 
https://visa.vfsglobal.com/bgd/en/ita/
