## Page 1

পারিবারিক সনদপত্র (paribarik sanadpatra)
Family Certificate
Documents to submit to obtain it (পাওয়ার জন্য যেসকল কাগজাদি দাখিল করতে হবে):
- Original valid passport and a photocopy of the passport
- Copy (English/Bangla) of the nikahnama (নিকাহনামা, Muslim marriage contract) issued by the Nikah Registry Office, attested by the Ministry of Law and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Dhaka, and apostilled
- Photocopy of the birth registration certificate (জন্মনিবন্ধন সনদ) of every family member, or photocopy of the e-passport/machine-readable passport of every family member
The applicant must be present in person. (আবেদনকারীকে সশরীরে উপস্থিত থাকতে হবে।)
[Side panel on every page: Bangladesh Consulate General, Milan, Italy (বাংলাদেশ কনস্যুলেট জেনারেল মিলান, ইতালি), with the Government of Bangladesh seal.]

নির্ভরশীল সনদপত্র (nirbharshil sanadpatra)

  or
  Photocopy of the e-passport/machine-readable passport of every family member, or photocopy of the National ID card (জাতীয় পরিচয়পত্র)
The applicant must be present in person.

## Page 5

অবিবাহিত সনদপত্র (abibahito sanadpatra)
Unmarried Certificate
Documents to submit to obtain it:
- Original valid passport and a photocopy of the passport
- Original copy/photocopy of the certificate stating the applicant is unmarried, issued by the Deputy Commissioner (জেলা প্রশাসক, District Administrator) of the applicant's own district, attested by the Ministry of Law and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Dhaka, and apostilled
Special note (বিশেষ দ্রষ্টব্য):
1. The application must be made at the Consulate General within 6 months of the date the certificate was issued.
2. The certificate stating the applicant is unmarried needs verification by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Dhaka, so the applicant must submit it with all the documents above 7 working days in advance.
The applicant must be present in person.

বিবাহের সনদপত্র (bibaher sanadpatra)
Marriage Certificate
Documents to submit to obtain it:

- Copy of the husband's/wife's passport/National ID card

## Page 7

টেলিফোনে বিবাহের সনদপত্র (telephone-e bibaher sanadpatra)
Telephonic Marriage Certificate
Documents to submit to obtain it:
- The applicant's original valid passport and a photocopy of the passport, and a photocopy of the passport/NID of the husband/wife living in the home country (Bangladesh)
- Copy and photocopy of the original nikahnama (with mention of the marriage by telephone) issued by the Nikah Registry Office, attested by the Ministry of Law and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and apostilled
- Photocopy of the passport, La Carta di Identitá and Permesso Di Soggiorno of the witnesses of both parties
- The address of the place in Italy where the marriage was performed at the time of registering the marriage, and the telephone number in Italy over which the marriage was performed
The applicant must be present in person.

Documents to submit to obtain it:
- Copy of the birth registration form filled in online (https://www.bdrisgovbd.org/application)

## Page 23

পাসপোর্ট এনরোলমেন্ট-এর এপয়েন্টমেন্ট নিতে নিম্নবর্ণিত পদ্ধতি অনুসরণ করতে হবে
To take an appointment for passport enrolment, the following procedure must be followed:
- To submit an application for a new passport at the Consulate, an appointment must be taken from the link https://passport.bcgmilan.it/. [This appointment link is opened every Thursday, and the opening time is announced in advance on social media.]
- After registering with the correct OID number and email ID, an appointment request must be sent from the link above. After the information is checked for accuracy, the appointment will be confirmed by return email within 72 hours.
- To get an OID number, go online to https://www.epassport.gov.bd/onboarding.
- Alternative online link for urgent appointments (জরুরি এপয়েন্টমেন্ট): if information is given at https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1QZ_aqFhPqt6wrLZXHddZrQerC25w_AA1VdCaY0aIZfo/viewform, an appointment date will be given.
- No appointment is needed for a newborn's first passport.

## Page 24

ইতালিতে জন্মগ্রহণকারী বাংলাদেশি শিশুদের নতুন মেশিন রিডেবল পাসপোর্ট আবেদনের জন্য নিম্নবর্ণিত নিয়ম অনুসরণ করতে হবে
For a new machine-readable passport application for Bangladeshi children born in Italy, the following rules must be followed:
1. Go online to the link (http://www.passport.gov.bd/Application-1.aspx) and fill in the new machine-readable passport form (a 4-page form with a barcode).
2. Submit the following documents with the completed form at the Consulate's counter:
- The child's birth registration certificate issued by the Consulate or by a Union/Pourashava/City Corporation in Bangladesh (the place of birth must be in Italy)
- The child's Italian birth registration certificate issued by the Italian local authority (Comune)
- Photocopy of the father's and mother's passports (pages 2 and 3)
- Two recent passport-size colour photos of the child (a clear photo on a white background with the face straight and both ears equally visible)
- One passport-size colour photo each of the father and mother
3. To submit the new passport application at the Consulate, an appointment must be taken from the following link: https://passport.bcgmilan.it/

## Page 25

মেশিন রিডেবল পাসপোর্ট নবায়নের জন্য নিম্নবর্ণিত নিয়ম অনুসরণ করতে হবে
For renewal (নবায়ন) of a machine-readable passport, the following rules must be followed:
1. The passport renewal (reissue) form can be collected from the mission (দূতাবাস) or downloaded from the following link (http://www.passport.gov.bd/Reports/MRP_Information_Alteration_Correction.pdf).
2. Submit the following documents with the completed form at the mission's counter:
- Original passport and one photocopy of it (pages 2 and 3)
- If the passport is lost, a G.D. (Denuncia) from the relevant police station (Questura) stating the passport number
- One recent passport-size colour photo
3. To submit the passport renewal (reissue) application at the mission, an appointment must be taken from the following link: https://passport.bcgmilan.it/
[N.B.: In line with the decision taken by the Government of Bangladesh and as directed by the Security Services Division, Ministry of Home Affairs, and the Department of Immigration and Passports, Dhaka, machine-readable passport (MRP) work will stop completely from 31 December 2025. Under these circumstances, applying for a machine-readable passport except in very urgent need is discouraged.]

## Page 26

ই-পাসপোর্ট আবেদনের জন্য যে নিয়মাবলী অনুসরণ করতে হবে (১)
Rules to follow to apply for an e-passport (1):
1. The e-passport application must be filled in online by going to the link https://www.epassport.gov.bd/onboarding. Those who want to submit their e-passport application at the Milan Consulate must choose country - Italy and mission - Milan at the start of the online application. If a district of Bangladesh is mentioned, the application will go to that district's passport office.
2. Submit the following documents with the completed form at the Consulate's counter:
- Only expatriate Bangladeshis living abroad can apply for an e-passport with a birth registration as well as with an NID. The birth registration must be online in English. But if someone has earlier made their MRP with an NID, they will need the NID for their e-passport.
3. If a previous handwritten passport or MRP or e-passport is lost, a GD (denuncia, দেনুনশা) must be made at the local Questura/Carabinieri (কস্তুরা/কারাবিনিয়েরি) and its original copy must be submitted at the Consulate.
4. For passport correction:
- To correct information in adults' passports an NID and an undertaking (অঙ্গীকারনামা) will be needed. Without an NID, an application to correct age and information by up to a maximum of 8 years can be submitted with the birth registration and Italian residence card. Correction applications with a birth registration will be accepted until 30 June 2026.
- To correct information in minors' (under 18) passports, the birth registration and an undertaking will be needed.

## Page 27

ই-পাসপোর্ট আবেদনের জন্য যে নিয়মাবলী অনুসরণ করতে হবে (২)
Rules to follow to apply for an e-passport (2):
5. For the student discount, students will need a student certificate (ছাত্রত্ব সনদ) from the relevant educational institution or the Comune.
6. To make a passport for children:
- For children's first passport, a printed copy of the application form filled in online (with barcode), a copy of the father's and mother's passports, the Bangladeshi and Italian birth certificates, and a 4R-size photo will be needed.
- The 4R-size photo of children under 6 years old must be clear, with the face straight, both ears equally visible, without a cap or band on the head, on a white background. It will be scanned and uploaded to the system. If the photo is not correct, the application will not be accepted. Photos of everyone aged 6 and over will be taken directly with a camera in the enrolment room at the Consulate.
- At enrolment, children under 6 years old do not have to give fingerprints or an iris scan.
- Children aged 6 to 11 must give fingerprints.
- All applicants aged 12 and over must give fingerprints and an iris scan.
7. Every time an applicant applies for an e-passport (new or renewal), the applicant's fingerprints and iris must be scanned.
8. When printing the application, it must be filled in with Acrobat Reader, and it must be saved and then opened offline with Acrobat Reader and printed. Then the complete barcode (small in size) will show. The enrolment process cannot be started with an application form carrying an incomplete barcode (a large barcode). Applicants are asked to be careful about this.
To submit a new passport application at the Consulate, an appointment must be taken from the set link.

## Page 28

প্রবাসী বাংলাদেশির মৃতদেহ বাংলাদেশে প্রেরণের জন্য যে নিয়মাবলী অনুসরণ করতে হবে
Rules to follow to send the body of an expatriate Bangladeshi to Bangladesh:
If a Bangladeshi living in Italy dies in Italy, a no-objection letter (অনাপত্তি পত্র, Nulla Osta) is issued free of charge (বিনা ফি-তে) by the Consulate General for repatriation of the body to Bangladesh.
- If the deceased was a member of the Wage Earners' Welfare Board (ওয়েজ আর্নার্স কল্যাণ বোর্ড), the Consulate General bears the air fare for transporting the body.
- Besides this, if the deceased's family is financially needy (আর্থিকভাবে অস্বচ্ছল), the air fare for transporting the body is given on application even if the deceased was not a member of the Wage Earners' Welfare Board.
Documents needed to issue the no-objection letter (Nulla Osta):
1. Death certificate (issued by the Italian hospital/Comune)
2. The deceased's original passport and a copy of the passport
3. The name, phone number and document of a Bangladeshi living in Italy to contact about transporting the body,
4. The name, phone number and document of the person receiving the body in Bangladesh
5. If the deceased was a member of the Wage Earners' Welfare Board, a copy of the membership registration.
To get the no-objection letter (Nulla Osta), the documents and information above must be sent by email to the mission's (দূতাবাসের) labour welfare wing (শ্রম কল্যাণ উইং) (fslbcgmilan@gmail.com) and to mission.milan@mofa.gov.bd.

## Page 29

ওয়েজ আর্নার্স কল্যাণ বোর্ডের সদস্যপদ গ্রহণ করার জন্য প্রয়োজনীয় তথ্যাবলী-১
Information needed to take membership of the Wage Earners' Welfare Board - 1
1. Expatriates up to a maximum age of 65 years can become members.
2. Go to the Wage Earners' Welfare Board website link (http://www.wewb.gov.bd) and fill in the "membership registration form" (সদস্যপদ নিবন্ধন ফর্ম) online.
3. With a printed copy of the form filled in online, attach (a) a passport copy (b) a photocopy of the visa copy/ soggiorno copy/ residence permit, one colour photo (passport size), and submit it with a return envelope (DHL, TNT etc.) and a fee of 32 euro at the Consulate's designated counter. Only citizens of Bangladesh living in Milan city can come directly to the Consulate General to collect their card.
4. After the application is received, at the end of verification and processing the membership card will be sent by postal service outside Milan city.
5. No appointment is needed to submit the membership registration application at the Consulate General. Applications are accepted every day until 1:00 pm.

## Page 30

ওয়েজ আর্নার্স কল্যাণ বোর্ডের সদস্যপদ গ্রহণ করার জন্য প্রয়োজনীয় তথ্যাবলী-২
Information needed to take membership of the Wage Earners' Welfare Board - 2
The benefits an expatriate worker who takes Wage Earners' Welfare Board membership will get:
1. Education scholarship every year for meritorious children.
2. Opportunity for children's admission under the expatriate quota (প্রবাসী কোটা) in various educational institutions in Bangladesh.
3. Arrangement to repatriate an expatriate worker in distress from abroad to the home country.
4. Ambulance service from the airport for a sick expatriate worker.
5. Payment of the transport cost to send a body to Bangladesh.
6. Financial help of 35 (thirty-five) thousand Taka for carrying the body within the country and burial costs, from the Probashi Kallyan Desk set up at the airport, when the body is handed over to the family.
7. A financial grant of 3 (three) lakh Taka to the family of a worker who dies abroad.
8. Besides this, other welfare services given from time to time by the Wage Earners' Welfare Board.
For details, contact the Consulate's labour welfare wing by email at fslbcgmilan@gmail.com.
