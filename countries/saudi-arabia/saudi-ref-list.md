# Saudi Arabia: migration knowledge base

Researched 26 Oct 2026. Linked terms open their entry in the Glossary below. Numbers like \[4\] cite official sources. Numbers like \[N4\] cite news or third-party sites and still need an official source.

## Work visa: company worker

- **Who:** anyone with a Saudi company job who passes the [skill test](#mw5zj3a13sz.56914) for their trade \[1\]. The Saudi Embassy in Dhaka has required it since June 2025, even for labourers \[N1\].
- **How:** register on [OEP](#mw5zj3a13sz.55804) \[4\]. A [licensed agency](#mw5zj3a13sz.56534) processes the visa. Book the [medical](#mw5zj3a13sz.57329) yourself \[5\], get [police clearance](#mw5zj3a13sz.58162) if the agency asks, give fingerprints at the [Tasheer visa centre](#mw5zj3a13sz.57787), attend the [pre-departure orientation](#mw5zj3a13sz.58860), and get [BMET clearance](#mw5zj3a13sz.56162) \[4, 14\].
- **Cost and time:** the government cap is Tk 1,65,000 \[N2\], but workers typically pay Tk 4-5 lakh \[N3\]. You pay the skill test (USD 85) \[3\], the [Welfare Board fee](#mw5zj3a13sz.59231) (Tk 5,500) \[10\] and insurance (Tk 1,000) \[11\]. The visa is issued within 10 business days \[7\].
- **Watch out:** check the agency \[4\] and the visa on the [KSA Visa platform](#mw5zj3a13sz.60353) \[8, 9\] yourself. A "house driver" visa for a factory job is a [free visa](#mw5zj3a13sz.60608) \[N4\]. Ask for the [attested job demand](#mw5zj3a13sz.58460) \[12\].
- **Once there:** work only for that employer. Your contract is registered on [Qiwa](#mw5zj3a13sz.59485) and you get an [iqama](#mw5zj3a13sz.60135). You can change employer without consent when the contract ends \[13\].

## Work visa: domestic worker

- **Who:** housemaids, house drivers, cooks and gardeners. Women must be 25 to 45 (50 for returnees) and complete one month of housekeeping training at a [BMET training centre](#mw5zj3a13sz.59002) \[14, 15\].
- **How:** the household hires through [Musaned](#mw5zj3a13sz.59737) and a [licensed agency](#mw5zj3a13sz.56534). The medical, visa and BMET clearance are the same as above. Accept your contract on Musaned within 5 days \[16\].
- **Cost:** women pay nothing. Agencies have been barred from charging them since Sep 2025 \[17\]. There is no skill test.
- **Watch out:** agents faking ages \[N6\], and anyone charging a woman money \[17\].
- **Once there:** fewer rights than company workers: a weekly day off and at most 10 hours' work a day, but no minimum wage \[18, 19\]. You can't change employer freely, and a [huroob](#mw5zj3a13sz.60938) report in the first 2 years means leaving within 60 days \[N7\]. If abused, go to the embassy rather than run.

## Temporary and seasonal work visas

- **Who:** short contracts or seasonal jobs such as harvests or the Hajj season, requested by a Saudi employer \[8\].
- **How and cost:** not documented for Bangladesh.
- **Once there:** work for that employer for the contract or season, then leave.

## Family residence

- **Who:** a worker's spouse, unmarried daughters and sons. Saudi Arabia publishes no salary floor. Guides say about SR3,500-6,000 a month \[N9\]. Since Oct 2026 workers with a "skilled" work permit can bring family together with their own work visa \[20, 21\]. Most Bangladeshi labourers hold "basic" permits.
- **How:** attest the marriage and birth certificates in Dhaka. The worker applies on [Absher](#mw5zj3a13sz.59941), and the family gets visas through the [Tasheer visa centre](#mw5zj3a13sz.57787) \[N9\].
- **Cost:** about SR2,000 per visa, then SR400 per dependant per month \[N9\].
- **Once there:** dependants can't work without separate permission \[N9\]. Register a baby born there on Absher within 30 days \[N10\] and at the Bangladesh Embassy \[22\].

## Family visit visa

- **Who:** relatives invited by someone living in Saudi Arabia \[23\].
- **How:** the relative applies on the [KSA Visa platform](#mw5zj3a13sz.60353), then you apply at the [Tasheer visa centre](#mw5zj3a13sz.57787) \[23\].
- **Cost and time:** USD 101 plus insurance \[23\]. Bangladeshis reportedly get single entry for up to 30 days \[N11\].
- **Watch out:** a visit visa can never become a job or an [iqama](#mw5zj3a13sz.60135) \[N12\].
- **Once there:** no work \[23\].

## Umrah visa

- **Who:** Muslims aged 18 or over, or with a guardian, outside the Hajj season. Women don't need a mahram \[24\].
- **How:** through an approved Umrah agency on [Nusuk](#mw5zj3a13sz.60265) \[24\].
- **Cost:** USD 101 plus insurance plus a Nusuk package \[24\]. A one-year multiple-entry option started in July 2026 \[N13\].
- **Watch out:** "stay on and work after Umrah" is illegal work. Saudi Arabia can cut Umrah visas without warning \[N14\].
- **Once there:** Umrah only. No work, no Hajj \[24\].

## Hajj visa

- **Who:** Muslims within Bangladesh's quota, 127,198 in 2026 \[N15\].
- **How:** register at the Islamic Foundation for the government package, or with an approved Hajj agency \[N15\].
- **Cost:** in 2026 the government package was Tk 4.67 lakh, and private packages Tk 5.10-7.50 lakh \[N15\].
- **Watch out:** Hajj on any other visa brings a fine of up to SR20,000, deportation and a ban of up to 10 years \[N16\].
- **Once there:** Hajj only.

## Tourist visa

- **Who:** no instant e-visa on a Bangladeshi passport alone, unless you hold a used US, UK or Schengen visa \[25\]. The Package Visa is open to Bangladeshis \[26\].
- **How:** buy an approved package (flight plus 4-star hotel) for a visa in 48 hours \[26\], or apply at the [Tasheer visa centre](#mw5zj3a13sz.57787) \[25\].
- **Cost:** USD 101 plus insurance through the visa centre \[25\].
- **Watch out:** "tourist visa plus job" is illegal work \[N12\].
- **Once there:** tourism and Umrah. No work, no Hajj \[25\].

## Business visit visa

- **Who:** people invited by a Saudi business \[27\].
- **How:** the business requests it on the [KSA Visa platform](#mw5zj3a13sz.60353), then you apply at the [Tasheer visa centre](#mw5zj3a13sz.57787) \[27\].
- **Cost and time:** USD 101 plus insurance, 1-2 working days \[27\].
- **Watch out:** the visit must not be linked to a work contract, so a "business visa" for a factory job is illegal work \[27\].
- **Once there:** meetings, events and Umrah. No work \[27\].

## Student visa

- **Who:** students admitted to a licensed Saudi institution \[28\].
- **How:** the institution requests the visa \[28\].
- **Once there:** study. Long-term students may work part-time, but the rules aren't published \[N17\].

## Transit visa

- **Who:** travellers on a stopover of up to 96 hours. It's unclear whether Bangladeshis still qualify \[N18\].
- **How and cost:** book with Saudia or flynas, about SR53 in fees \[N19\].
- **Once there:** up to 96 hours. No work \[N19\].

## Premium Residency

- **Who:** high earners and investors only, through the Premium Residency Center \[29\]. It is out of reach for nearly all Bangladeshi workers.
- **Cost:** SR4,000 plus USD 170 for most categories \[N20\].
- **Once there:** work and change employer without a sponsor \[N20\].

## Other visas

- Medical, event, government and escort visas exist \[8\]. We found no Bangladesh-specific detail. None allow work.
- Not open to Bangladeshis: the UK-only Electronic Travel Authorization \[38\], diplomatic visas and goods-transport visas \[8\].

## Corridor

- Saudi Arabia took two-thirds of Bangladeshi workers in 2025, about 755,000. In 2026 up to 25 Oct it took 321,872, down 46% \[30\].
- Bangladeshis are the largest foreign group in Saudi Arabia's private sector: 1.75 million in 2022, ahead of Indians (1.40 million) and Pakistanis (1.21 million) \[31\].
- Since Feb 2026 low-skilled visas are being rejected \[N21\]. Skill-certified trades are in demand for 2034 World Cup construction \[N22\].
- Dhaka-Riyadh flights were suspended after the 10 Oct 2026 attack on Riyadh airport \[N23, N24\].
- Short visas were paused before Hajj 2025 \[N25\].

## Overstaying and asylum

- Overstaying any visa can bring a fine of up to SR50,000, up to 6 months in jail, deportation and a re-entry ban \[N26\].
- Saudi Arabia has no asylum system and hasn't signed the Refugee Convention \[32\]. UNHCR recognition gives no right to stay or work \[33\]. There is no realistic asylum route for Bangladeshis.
- A worker whose status breaks should contact the Bangladesh Embassy rather than hide.

## Glossary

### OEP

- **What:** the Overseas Employment Platform, BMET's online system for everyone going abroad to work \[4\].
- **Who runs it:** BMET (Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training), under the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare.
- **Where:** [oep.gov.bd](https://www.oep.gov.bd/). Hotline 09614445271.
- **Use it to:** register with your NID, check an agency's licence, and download your clearance card \[4\].

### BMET clearance

- **What:** permission to leave Bangladesh for a job abroad, checked at the airport. It comes as an emigration clearance (smart) card \[4, 14\].
- **How:** the agency applies after the visa is issued. You give fingerprints at the district BMET office, then download the card from [OEP](https://www.oep.gov.bd/employee-card).
- **Fees paid at this step:** the Welfare Board fee and the insurance premium \[10, 11\].

### Recruiting agency

- **What:** a Bangladeshi agency licensed by the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare to send workers abroad. Each has an RL (recruiting licence) number.
- **How to check:** search the RL number or name on [OEP's agency list](https://www.oep.gov.bd/agencies). The Status must say "Active" \[4\].
- **Warning sign:** a real RL number with a different address or phone number means a broker is using the agency's name.

### Skill test

- **What:** Saudi Arabia's Skill Verification Program (SVP). Work visas for listed trades are linked to passing it \[1\].
- **Who runs it:** Takamol, for the Saudi labour ministry. In Bangladesh the tests are held at BMET training centres \[N1\].
- **Cost:** USD 85 per attempt since 9 Sep 2026. A retake costs USD 85 again \[3\].
- **How to check:** certificate check by passport number and serial number on the [SVP portal](https://svp-international.qiwa.sa/) \[2\].

### Wafid medical check

- **What:** the health check every worker needs, at a centre approved by the Gulf Health Council (formerly GAMCA).
- **How:** book a slip on [Wafid](https://wafid.com/en/book-appointment/) yourself (USD 10). The system assigns the centre. You can't choose it \[5\].
- **How to check:** [Wafid status search](https://wafid.com/en/medical-status-search/) by passport or slip number. Results can take up to 26 days \[5\]. The Saudi Foreign Ministry also has a check \[6\].
- **Warning sign:** an agent who says he can choose the centre or fix the result.

### Tasheer visa centre

- **What:** the Saudi visa application centre in Bangladesh. Applicants give fingerprints (biometrics) there, mandatory for work visas since Oct 2023 \[N28\].
- **Who runs it:** Tasheer, the Saudi visa services company, with VFS Global \[N28\].
- **Where:** Jamuna Future Park, Dhaka, and a centre in Chattogram \[N28\]. Appointments at [vc.tasheer.com](https://vc.tasheer.com/appointment).
- **Cost:** not published.

### Police clearance

- **What:** a certificate showing you have no criminal record. No official checklist requires it for Saudi Arabia, but agencies may ask for it.
- **Where:** apply online at [pcc.police.gov.bd](https://pcc.police.gov.bd/ords/f?p=500), run by Bangladesh Police \[37\].
- **Cost and time:** Tk 500, 7 working days in Dhaka, 10 outside \[N29\].

### Attested job demand

- **What:** the employer's job demand letter, checked and attested by the Labour Welfare Wing of the Bangladesh Embassy in Riyadh, confirming the employer and job are real \[12\].
- **Why it matters:** BMET requires it for Saudi clearance, but batches of up to five workers can skip it \[N5\].
- **How to check:** ask the agency for the attested letter and call the labour wing on 8001000125 \[34\].

### Pre-departure orientation (PDO)

- **What:** a briefing every worker attends before BMET clearance. You get a PDO card and certificate on [OEP](https://www.oep.gov.bd/pdo-certificate) \[4\].

### BMET training centres

- **What:** Technical Training Centres (TTCs) run by BMET across the country. They host the Saudi skill test and the compulsory one-month housekeeping course for women domestic workers, offered at 41 TTCs \[14\].

### Welfare Board fee

- **What:** a fee every worker pays to the Wage Earners' Welfare Board, which funds support for migrants and their families. It is Tk 5,500 from 1 Oct 2026, up from Tk 3,500 \[10\]. A one-time Tk 1,000 insurance premium is paid with it \[11\].

### Qiwa

- **What:** the Saudi labour ministry's platform for company workers' contracts, work permits and job transfers \[13\].
- **Use it to:** compare your registered contract (salary, job title) with the one you signed in Bangladesh, and change employer. [qiwa.sa](https://qiwa.sa/)

### Musaned

- **What:** the Saudi platform for hiring domestic workers and registering their contracts \[16, 19\].
- **Use it to:** accept your contract through the SMS link within 5 days \[16\]. Helpline 920002866 \[35\].

### Absher

- **What:** the Saudi Interior Ministry's online portal for residents \[36\].
- **Use it to:** check your iqama, apply for family visas, register births, and check for overstay amnesties. [absher.sa](https://www.absher.sa/)

### Iqama

- **What:** the Saudi residence card. The employer gets it for you after arrival and must renew it. Check its expiry on Absher.

### Nusuk

- **What:** the official Saudi platform for Umrah and Hajj bookings and permits \[24\].

### KSA Visa platform

- **What:** the Saudi Foreign Ministry's visa website, with every visa type, fee and application \[8\]. The older portal visa.mofa.gov.sa also checks visas \[9\].
- **Use it to:** check a visa by visa number and passport number on [Track Application](https://ksavisa.sa/track-application).

### Free visa

- **What:** a visa bought from a Saudi sponsor who has no job for the worker (also called "azad visa"). The worker must find work alone, which is illegal, and often pays the sponsor a monthly cut. It ends in deportation \[N4\].
- **Warning sign:** the job title on the visa (such as "house driver") doesn't match the promised job.

### Huroob

- **What:** an absconding report an employer files when a worker leaves. It makes the worker illegal. Some employers file false reports to punish workers who complain \[N8\]. Contact the embassy.
