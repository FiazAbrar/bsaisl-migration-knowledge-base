## What Is Tasheer and Who Needs an Appointment

Tasheer is the company that runs the Saudi visa service centres for the Saudi missions abroad. It checks documents, fills in the visa form on the Visa Platform (Enjaz) for you, takes biometrics, collects the fees and sends the passport to the embassy or consulate. [Tasheer](https://www.tasheer.com/en/solutions/visa-solutions) lists 126 centres in 74 countries.

You need a Tasheer appointment for almost every visa that starts outside the Kingdom: family visit, personal visit, resident (family or dependant iqama), work, temporary work, business and others on the booking form. The booking site lists the visa types, and the Saudi embassy decides the validity, number of entries and length of stay when it processes the file.

For Pakistani applicants, the embassy part of that processing is now done on the same day at the Saudi Embassy in Islamabad, announced on 30 October 2026. Read [same-day Saudi visa processing for Pakistanis](https://saudilifeguide.com/saudi-same-day-visa-processing-pakistan/) for what is known so far.

**In Pakistan, Tasheer and Etimad are the same thing.** The local centres are still widely called Etimad, but booking for all six Pakistan centres is on vc.tasheer.com. Third-party sites that offer “Etimad appointments” for a fee are not needed.

## How to Book a Tasheer Appointment Online

The booking takes about ten minutes once the MOFA visa application has been approved. Keep the approval (pre-approval or invitation), the sponsor’s iqama copy and each applicant’s passport next to you.

1.  Open [vc.tasheer.com](https://vc.tasheer.com/) and select your country and language. The menus and centres change with the country you pick.
2.  Go to **Appointment**, then **Schedule** (direct link: [vc.tasheer.com/appointment](https://vc.tasheer.com/appointment)).
3.  Choose the applicant’s **nationality**, the **visa type** (for example Family Visit, Resident or Work), the **number of entries** and the **visa validity**.
4.  A table shows the earliest **Normal** and **Lounge** date for each city. Pick the centre that suits you and tap Continue.
5.  Enter the applicant details exactly as they appear in the passport and on the MOFA approval. A spelling difference here causes trouble at the counter.
6.  Choose the date and time, confirm, and print the **appointment letter**. Book one slot per applicant.

On 12 October 2026 the Pakistan table showed normal family visit slots for the very next day in all six cities, while lounge slots ran from 13 to 27 October depending on the city. Tasheer itself says most centres are booked 3 to 5 days ahead, so check the table on the day you book rather than relying on someone’s WhatsApp forward.

**Normal or Lounge?** Normal is the standard service. Lounge (premium) costs extra, has its own slots and accepts walk-ins while it has capacity. If you are not blocked and the dates suit you, normal is enough.

## Reschedule, Cancel or Reprint a Tasheer Appointment Letter

All three are in the Appointment menu on vc.tasheer.com: **Reprint Letter**, **Cancel Appointment** and **Reschedule Appointment**. Tasheer notes that cancelling or rescheduling is not offered in a few countries.

The rules from the [Tasheer FAQ](https://vc.tasheer.com/static/faq) are strict, so read them before you move a slot:

- You can reschedule or cancel only **up to the day before** the appointment.
- A change made **within 3 days** of the appointment blocks normal booking for **7 days**; only Lounge is open to you in that time.
- A **no-show** also blocks normal booking for 7 days.
- More than **5 changes** blocks normal booking for **14 days**.
- Arrive 15 minutes early. A late arrival can mean the appointment is cancelled, upgraded to Lounge at extra cost, or a 7-day wait.

If you started an application and did not finish it, choose **Retrieve Application** while scheduling; Tasheer sends a verification code. This is also not available in every country.

## Documents Required for a Tasheer Appointment in Pakistan

These lists are from Tasheer’s official [documents required page](https://vc.tasheer.com/static/documentrequired) for a Pakistani national at the Islamabad centre. Select your own centre and visa type on that page before you go, because each mission can add items.

### Family visit visa

- Original passport valid for at least 6 months, with 2 blank pages side by side.
- 2 photos, 2 x 2 inch (51 x 51 mm), white background, not older than 3 months.
- Copy of the MOFA family visit approval. The name and passport number must match the passport.
- Copy of the sponsor’s passport and iqama (a Jawazat or [Muqeem print](https://saudilifeguide.com/muqeem-print/) if the iqama shows no expiry date).
- Proof of relationship attested by Pakistan’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs, for example the NADRA Family Registration Certificate. Marriage or birth certificates issued in Saudi Arabia need Saudi MOFA attestation.
- Unmarried certificate on e-stamp paper for a female applicant over 15.
- Copies of the applicant’s passport and CNIC.

**Timing:** submit at least one week before the MOFA approval expires. Tasheer does not accept later applications, and many families lose the approval this way.

### Resident visa (family joining on a dependant iqama)

- The same passport, photo, sponsor and relationship documents as above, plus a copy of the pre-approval (the “yellow slip”).
- You can submit only **14 days after** the pre-approval was issued.
- Original medical report for applicants over 18 from a centre approved by the Saudi mission (booked through [Wafid](https://saudilifeguide.com/wafid-medical/)).
- Vaccination cards for children under 18 from a government hospital, signed and stamped by the DHO.
- Unmarried certificate for females over 15 from the Union Council, attested by Pakistan MOFA.

The sponsor’s side of this visa (Absher request, Muqeem approval, SAR 379 MOFA fee) is in our [permanent family visa guide](https://saudilifeguide.com/permanent-family-visa-saudi-arabia/).

### Work visa

- Original passport and a copy (6 months validity, 2 blank pages).
- Copy of the [E-Number](https://saudilifeguide.com/saudi-work-visa-from-pakistan/).
- Original CNIC.
- Medical report: Tasheer requires one for work, temporary work, resident and educational visas, and a medical is valid for 2 months.

## What Happens on the Day at the Tasheer Centre

1.  Arrive 15 minutes early with the printed appointment letter and the original documents.
2.  Reception checks the documents and gives you a token.
3.  At the counter you pay the fees and the staff fill in the visa form on the Visa Platform (Enjaz) for you. You do not register on Enjaz yourself.
4.  You give biometrics: fingerprints and a face photo.
5.  Tasheer sends the passport and file to the Saudi embassy or consulate. Keep the original invoice-cum-receipt; you need it to collect the passport.

**Who gives biometrics:** every applicant, except children under 6, people over 80 and diplomatic passport holders. For an applicant under 6 or over 80, a first-degree relative can submit the application on their behalf. Everyone else must attend in person, so a father cannot submit for his 10-year-old without the child.

The Saudi Visa Bio app lets some travellers enrol biometrics on their phone, but its store listing covers Umrah. Do not skip the centre for a family visit, resident or work visa on the strength of the app.

## No More Visa Sticker: What You Travel With

Tasheer no longer puts a Saudi visa sticker in the passport for these countries. The Saudi foreign ministry moved work, residence and visit visas to an electronic visa with a QR code: India, Bangladesh and the Philippines from May 2023, then Pakistan from 24 July 2023, Nepal from 14 August 2023 and Sri Lanka from 21 August 2023 (Gulf News and The National reported both phases). Stickers issued earlier stay valid.

So “visa stamping” now means submitting at Tasheer and receiving the e-visa. Once it is issued you can check and print it by passport number; our [Saudi visa check guide](https://saudilifeguide.com/saudi-visa-check/) shows how, and MOFA’s [visa platform](https://visa.mofa.gov.sa) has an application inquiry.

## After the Appointment: Tracking and Passport Collection

Tasheer does not publish an official processing time, and it cannot speed up a file: the embassy decides. Track progress with **Track Status** on vc.tasheer.com, or pay for SMS updates at the counter.

- Collect the passport with the **original receipt**. Someone else can collect with a signed authorisation letter (the form is under Download Forms).
- Courier delivery is optional and costs extra.
- A passport not collected within **30 days**, or after 2 failed courier attempts, is sent back to the mission.

Once your family has the visa, the next steps are on the Saudi side: arrival, then either an [Absher visit visa extension](https://saudilifeguide.com/extend-your-saudi-arabia-visit-visa/) or, for dependants, the iqama within 90 days.

## Common Tasheer Problems and How to Avoid Them

- **Name or passport number mismatch.** The MOFA approval must match the passport exactly. Fix the approval before booking, not at the counter.
- **Approval about to expire.** For a family visit visa, submit at least a week before the approval expires. For a resident visa, wait until 14 days after the pre-approval.
- **Booking blocked.** A no-show or a late change locks you out of normal slots for 7 days, and more than 5 changes for 14 days. Pick a date you can keep.
- **Unattested relationship documents.** Get the Family Registration Certificate or marriage and birth certificates attested by Pakistan MOFA before the appointment.
- **Budget surprises.** The fee table excludes insurance. Carry enough for insurance and optional extras such as photos or courier.
- **Expired medical.** A medical report is valid for 2 months, so do not book the medical too early for work or resident visas.

## Before the Appointment: What the Sponsor in Saudi Arabia Does

Tasheer is the last step. The sponsor in the Kingdom first applies for the visa online with Nafath on [KSA Visa](https://ksavisa.sa), pays the SAR 300 visa fee for a family visit and sends the approval home. Our [family visit visa guide](https://saudilifeguide.com/how-to-apply-family-visit-visa-in-saudi-arabia/) walks through that part, and the [cost of living calculator](https://saudilifeguide.com/cost-of-living-calculator/) helps if you are planning a longer family stay.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I book a Tasheer appointment?

Book a Tasheer appointment on vc.tasheer.com: choose your country, open Appointment, then Schedule, select your nationality and visa type, pick a centre, date and time, and print the appointment letter. Booking is free; the visa and service fees are paid at the centre.

### How much are Tasheer fees in Pakistan for a family visit visa?

At the Islamabad centre, a family visit visa costs PKR 34,619 before medical insurance: PKR 23,220 visa fee (SAR 300), PKR 3,050 application fee and a PKR 7,260 service fee plus PKR 1,089 tax. Medical insurance is priced at the counter by age and validity, and is added on top of this total.

### How do I reprint my Tasheer appointment letter?

Go to vc.tasheer.com, choose your country, then open Appointment and select Reprint Letter. Enter the details you used when booking to download the letter again. Bring the printed letter, your original passport and all the listed documents to the centre on the day.

### Can I reschedule or cancel a Tasheer appointment?

Yes, through Appointment, then Cancel Appointment or Reschedule Appointment on vc.tasheer.com, up to the day before your slot. A change within 3 days of the appointment, or a no-show, blocks normal bookings for 7 days, and more than 5 changes blocks them for 14 days.

### Do children need to go to Tasheer for biometrics?

Children under 6 and people over 80 do not give biometrics, and a first-degree relative can submit their application for them. Everyone aged 6 to 80 must attend the Tasheer centre in person for fingerprints and a face photo, so book a slot for each family member.

### Does Tasheer still stamp the Saudi visa in the passport?

No. Saudi Arabia replaced passport visa stickers with an electronic visa carrying a QR code, from May 2023 for India and from 24 July 2023 for Pakistan. You still visit Tasheer to submit documents and give biometrics, then travel with the printed e-visa, which you can check by passport number.
