## Questions

Can a domestic worker issue their own final exit visa?

No. It is issued by the employer or sponsor through Absher, and the independent application route open to some other resident categories does not cover domestic workers.

Can it be cancelled during the probation period?

No. The passports directorate has confirmed that a final exit visa issued during probation cannot be cancelled electronically on Absher, and it cannot be turned into an iqama afterwards.

What is huroob, and why does it block a final exit visa?

Huroob is the status given to a worker an employer has reported as absent from work. It fails the second of the four conditions, so no visa can be issued while it stands. You can [check your absence from work status](https://blog.wego.com/huroob-check/) yourself before assuming anything.

How long is a final exit visa valid?

Up to 60 days from the date it is issued. Where the holder already has an iqama with between 30 and 60 days left, the visa is shortened to match what remains on the iqama.

What happens if you do not leave before it expires?

A fine of SAR 1,000 applies. The employer can only cancel the expired visa once that fine is paid, and only while the iqama is still valid.

- Fares are a one way economy sample for one adult, departing a month after the sample date, because a final exit visa gives most people about two months to leave. Among what remained, each row takes the lowest price after adding SAR 25 for every hour slower than the fastest option on that route, so a 32 hour routing does not headline a page written for people on a deadline. Dammam to Delhi is the clearest case: the raw cheapest is SAR 492 over 32h 10m, against SAR 586 in 4h 35m.

The widget follows the first, which is the issuance rule. Confirm the exact number on Absher.
