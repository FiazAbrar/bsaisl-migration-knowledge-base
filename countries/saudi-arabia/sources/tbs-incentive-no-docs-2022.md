# No document needed to avail cash incentives for remittance over Tk5 lakh

**TBS Report**

23 May, 2022, 04:30 pm

Last modified: 23 May, 2022, 08:49 pm

![](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2Zz48dXNlIHhsaW5rOmhyZWY9Ii9zaXRlcy9hbGwvdGhlbWVzL3Nsb3RoL2ltYWdlcy9pY29ucy5zdmcjb3Blbi1wb3B1cCIgLz48L3N2Zz4=)

Representational image. Photo: Reuters.

**The Bangladesh Bank (BB) has relaxed the requirement for availing 2.5% cash incentive for inward remittances over Tk5 lakh.**

From now on migrant workers can get the incentives without submitting any documents, said a circular of the banking regulator today.

Earlier remitters needed to submit remittance related documents.

#### [![The Business Standard Google News](/sites/all/themes/sloth/images/google_news.svg) Keep updated, follow The Business Standard's Google news channel](https://news.google.com/publications/CAAqBwgKMIivlQsw2ZKrAw?ceid=US:en&oc=3)

This new facility will be available until further notice, the circular adds.

Bangladesh received \$2.09 billion in remittances in April, the highest amount in a single month of the current fiscal year.
