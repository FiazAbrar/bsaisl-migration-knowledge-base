## Tasheer Visa Service Center Directory — How to Use This Page

Every Tasheer Visa Service Center handles Saudi visa applications and biometric enrollment as part of the Tasheer/Nusuk process. NewHaj built this directory to help you find the nearest Tasheer Visa Service Center faster. After all, the official guide these locations come from is a long PDF, and PDFs are hard to search on a phone.

First, try the search box to find your country or city directly. Or, browse by region using the filters (APAC, Europe, Africa, Middle East, Egypt, or Partners). Each card shows the full address and a direct link to open the location in Google Maps. Each card also carries an honest status label. "Corrected" means our team fixed a broken link or a truncated address during review. "Unverified" means the entry matches the source guide exactly, and we haven't individually re-confirmed it yet. Neither label claims that NewHaj has physically visited the location — that distinction matters, so we keep it clear.

This directory covers visa service centers only. So, if you're also planning the rest of your trip, check two more NewHaj resources: the [Makkah and Madinah transport directory](https://newhaj.com/transport/) for ground transport, and the [Hajj and Umrah tour operator listings](https://newhaj.com/operator/mihman-turizm-hac-hajj-umrah-packages-contacts/) for full-package operators.

For the visa application itself — appointment booking and official document requirements — always use the official platforms directly. Visit [Tasheer's official visa center portal](https://vc.tasheer.com/) for appointments, or [Nusuk](https://www.nusuk.sa/), the official Saudi platform for Hajj and Umrah pilgrims. NewHaj has no affiliation with Tasheer or Nusuk. This page is simply an independent, community-maintained directory that saves you time finding a physical location — not a substitute for the official booking process.

## Tasheer Visa Service Centers — Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Tasheer Visa Service Center?

A Tasheer Visa Service Center (VSC) is a physical location where applicants submit Saudi visa applications and biometric data as part of the Tasheer/Nusuk visa process. This NewHaj directory lists Tasheer VSC locations worldwide, based on information from the Tasheer platform's user guide.

How many Tasheer Visa Service Centers are listed here?

140 unique locations across 88 countries. The source guide's own header states 141 total locations — the difference is a confirmed duplicate: the guide lists the Copenhagen, Denmark center twice, under two different sections, with two different map links. No location was dropped; this page lists each physical center once.

Is NewHaj an official Tasheer or Nusuk service?

No. NewHaj is an independent Hajj and Umrah information hub. We maintain this directory using information from the official Tasheer user guide — NewHaj itself is not an official Tasheer or Nusuk channel. Always confirm hours, required documents, and payment methods directly with the center before visiting.

What do the "Corrected" and "Unverified" labels mean?

"Corrected" means our team fixed the address text or map link during review — for example, we replaced a broken map link, or completed a truncated address. "Unverified" means the entry matches the source guide exactly, and we haven't individually re-confirmed it against the physical location yet. Neither label means NewHaj has personally visited or verified the location on the ground. That distinction matters, so we don't want to overstate what we've actually checked.

I found an incorrect address or map link — how do I report it?

Every listing has a "Report issue" button that opens a pre-filled email to NewHaj with the country, city, address, and map link already included — just add what's wrong or what it should say instead.
