Saudi authorities clarify fine for delayed residency issuance for domestic workers

The General Directorate of Passports says a SAR 500 fine applies if a domestic worker’s residency permit is not issued within 90 days of arrival.

Ajel News69 days ago · 1 min read
Xf

Key Takeaways

AI

A query was submitted to the General Directorate of Passports by a follower, asking: "I have a domestic worker who entered on a trial period, and I never issued her a residency permit. She has now been with me for four years. How much do I need to pay, including fines, to issue her a one-year residency permit?"

The Passports Directorate, via its account on X, clarified that a fine of 500 riyals (SAR) is imposed for delaying the issuance of a residency permit (Iqama) beyond 90 days from the worker’s date of entry.

View the post on X

It added that the residency permit can be issued through the Absher or Muqeem platforms after the required fees are paid by the employer.

The directorate also confirmed that a medical examination is required to complete the process.
