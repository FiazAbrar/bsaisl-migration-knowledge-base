# Visa Status Inquiry (Musaned), Firecrawl scrape 2026-11-08
Source: https://musaned.gov.sa/en/services/visa-status-inquiry  (Last modified: 04/12/2025 - 4:13 PM Saudi time)

Check the status of your domestic labor visa application easily and securely through Musaned's online platform

Service Steps

1. Navigate to the "Services" page on the Musaned platform.
2. Select the "Visa Status Inquiry" service from the list of available services.
3. Enter your National ID or Iqama Number in the designated field.
4. Enter your Request Number.
5. Click on the "Verify" button.
6. The system will display the current status of your visa.

Alternative Visa: An alternative visa is issued to the employer without government fees if the domestic worker exits the Kingdom permanently within 90 days of their entry.
