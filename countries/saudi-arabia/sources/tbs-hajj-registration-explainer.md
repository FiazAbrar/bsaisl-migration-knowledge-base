# Explainer: How to register for Hajj 2026

## Deadline for initial registration is 12 October

**TBS Report**

05 October, 2025, 07:00 pm

Last modified: 05 October, 2025, 07:10 pm

**The Ministry of Religious Affairs has announced that the initial registration for Bangladeshis intending to perform Hajj in 2026 will close on 12 October.**

As of today (5 October), 1,419 pilgrims have registered under the government quota and 2,417 under the private sector. A total of 127,198 pilgrims from Bangladesh will be allowed to perform Hajj next year.

[Deadline for primary Hajj registration set for 12 Oct won’t be extended: Ministry](/bangladesh/deadline-hajj-primary-registration-set-12-october-wont-be-extended-ministry-1231406)

To complete the initial registration, each applicant must deposit Tk3.5 lakh.

**Registration Process**

**For government quota pilgrims:**

Aspirant pilgrims must complete both the initial and final registration through any of the following options.

- The e-Hajj system: [www.hajj.gov.bd](http://www.hajj.gov.bd)
- The Labbaik mobile app
- Union Digital Centres
- Offices of the Islamic Foundation in divisional and district headquarters
- Baitul Mukarram and the Ashkona Hajj Office in Dhaka

**For private quota pilgrims:**

They can register through an approved Hajj agency listed by the ministry.

**Private Hajj Packages**

The Hajj Agencies Association of Bangladesh (HAAB) has announced three packages for pilgrims under private management, with Tk5.10 lakh set as the minimum cost.

[Govt announces Hajj package at Tk4.67 lakh, reducing Tk12,000 airfare](/bangladesh/govt-announces-hajj-package-tk467-lakh-reducing-tk12000-airfare-1248171)

HAAB Secretary General Farid Ahmed Majumder announced the packages in Dhaka on 30 September, stating that the maximum cost per pilgrim will be Tk7.50 lakh, while the general package will cost Tk5.50 lakh.

Unlike the government-managed packages, food expenses are included in private packages, offering enhanced services.

The package costs are based on an exchange rate of Tk32.85 per Saudi Riyal, and any change in the rate will be reflected in the final price.

[Saudi Arabia launches new licensing system for Hajis' accommodation](/world/saudi-arabia-launches-new-licensing-system-hajis-accommodation-1252021)

The HAAB has made the general and economy packages mandatory for all private agencies, though customised versions of the special package may be offered with slight variations in pricing and amenities.

**Government Hajj Packages**

On 28 September, Religious Affairs Adviser Dr AFM Khalid Hossain announced the government-managed Hajj packages for 2026.

Package-1: Tk6.90 lakh\
Package-2: Tk5.58 lakh\
Package-3: Tk4.67 lakh (minimum cost)

**Key dates and deadlines**

Initial registration closes: 12 October 

Hajj contract signing: 9 November 

Final payment deadline: 31 December

Rental and transport agreements deadline: 1 February 2026

Visa issuance begins: 8 February 2026

Hajj flights begin: 18 April 2026

Hajj expected: 26 May 2026 (subject to moon sighting)

**Eligibility:**

Physically and mentally healthy individuals

Age: 12 years and above
