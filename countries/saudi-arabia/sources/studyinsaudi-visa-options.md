# Study Visa & Pathways
Full Scholarships: Tuition, housing & living — all covered. Partial Scholarships. Paid Study Programs (Self-Funded).

## Visas
Short-term Visa
### Eligibility
Study, training, or participation in short-term programs in Saudi Arabia for a period not exceeding six months, suitable for language study, short-term programs, and student exchange programs
Applicant must be 16 years of age or older
A valid passport with at least 6 months validity from the intended date of entry to the Kingdom.
Acceptance from a licensed and accredited educational institution in the Kingdom.
Proof of financial ability to cover tuition and living expenses or a financial guarantee.
Written consent of the guardian and companions for visa holders under 18 years of age.
Duration: Up to 6 months (extendable)Extendable for an additional 6 months

Long-term Visa
### Eligibility
Study, work, or research in Saudi Arabia for a period exceeding one year, suitable for academic degrees such as Bachelor's, Master's, and Doctorate
Applicant must be 16 years of age or older
A valid passport with at least 6 months validity from the intended date of entry to the Kingdom.
Acceptance from a licensed and accredited educational institution in the Kingdom.
Proof of financial ability to cover tuition and living expenses or a financial guarantee.
Written consent of the guardian and companions for visa holders under 18 years of age.
A medical report confirming freedom from infectious diseases
Medical insurance document for the visa holder and dependents
Proof of proficiency in the language of study, except for those wishing to study Arabic
Duration: 1 year (renewable annually)Renewable annually

01 Full Scholarships
These fully funded scholarships allow non-Saudi students to study at Saudi universities, colleges, academies, and institutes in various disciplines without incurring tuition fees. The scholarships cover all study-related expenses according to established regulations.
Applicants must meet the required conditions and submit necessary documents such as academic certificates, transcripts, letters of recommendation, and other supporting documents.
