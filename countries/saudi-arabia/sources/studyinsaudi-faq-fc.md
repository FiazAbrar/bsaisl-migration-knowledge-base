# Frequently Asked Questions

What stages does a scholarship application go through?
After submission, the application is forwarded to the selected educational institution for review. Once approved by the institution, it is processed by both the institution and the Ministry of Education to complete the final admission procedures.

I made an error in my application data. How can I edit it?
Contact information can be updated at any time. However, other application data cannot be modified after submission.

What are the requirements to study in Saudi Arabia?
The following are required:
- The applicant must be 16 years of age or older.
- Submit a valid passport.
- Submit a medical report proving freedom from infectious diseases.
- Attested certificates and required documents per the educational institution's requirements.

How can I get a scholarship?
You can apply for a scholarship through the educational institution where you wish to study by submitting an admission application. The institution will review your application and issue an admission decision accordingly.

What does an "Under Process" application status mean?
This status indicates that your application is currently under review by the relevant educational institution and is being processed according to the approved procedures. Please continue to monitor your application status on the platform until a final decision (acceptance or rejection) is issued.

Why was my study application rejected?
The application was rejected because one or more of the admission requirements for the program or educational institution were not met.

Can I request the complete deletion of my profile from the platform?
A student can submit a support ticket to request the deletion of their profile from the system.
