# Transfer of services from another employer (Freedom of movement)

[Service Link](https://sso.qiwa.sa/ar/sign-in "Service Link")

Sector

business sector

An electronic service that allows a resident employee to transfer to work for a new employer without needing the approval of the current employer, subject to the regulations and conditions.

[Service Level Agreement](https://www.qiwa.sa/ar/frequently-asked-questions)
