# Saudi Arabia surges ahead as Bangladesh’s top remittance source

**TBS Report**

24 November, 2025, 01:45 pm

Last modified: 24 November, 2025, 02:35 pm

![](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2Zz48dXNlIHhsaW5rOmhyZWY9Ii9zaXRlcy9hbGwvdGhlbWVzL3Nsb3RoL2ltYWdlcy9pY29ucy5zdmcjb3Blbi1wb3B1cCIgLz48L3N2Zz4=)

An illustration depicts the flags of Bangladesh and Saudi Arabia, symbolizing the growing financial ties as Saudi Arabia becomes Bangladesh's top remittance source in Q1 of FY26. \| Illustration: Ashrafun Naher Ananna/TBS Creative

Saudi Arabia has surged ahead as Bangladesh's top remittance source in the current fiscal year **(FY2025-26)**, with inflows jumping **43.7% to \$1.2 billion** in the first quarter (Q1), up from **\$859 million** a year earlier.

By contrast, the United States, which was last year's overall leader with **\$4.7 billion** for the entire **FY2024-25**, has slipped behind in the **Q1 FY26** rankings, even as overall remittances rose **16%** year-on-year to **\$7.6 billion** during the July-September period of **FY26.**
