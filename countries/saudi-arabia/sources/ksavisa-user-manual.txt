4.1 Companies outside the kingdom



Authorized offices can login through selecting “companies outside the
kingdom”, where the login information is obtained after the reviewing of the
representative of the Custodian of the Tow Holy Mosques in the host country

The data of the electronic wallet number and the IBAN number of the

be fed through bank transfer to make payments for the requests, where the
wallet owner can benefit from:

  • Operations account statement
  • Entering a visa
  • Pay the fees
  • Medical insurance payment
