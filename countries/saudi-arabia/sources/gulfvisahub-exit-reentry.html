Saudi Exit Re-Entry Visa 2026: Fees, Validity & Rules

Saudi exit re-entry visa guide for 2026. Check SAR 200 single and SAR 500 multiple visa fees, validity, Iqama rules, extensions, Muqeem checks and common problems.

Editorial policy

A Saudi exit and re-entry visa allows an eligible Iqama holder to leave Saudi Arabia temporarily and return without ending the residency relationship.

For 2026, the official fee is SAR 200 for a single exit and re-entry visa for up to two months and SAR 500 for a multiple exit and re-entry visa for up to three months. When the resident is inside Saudi Arabia, each additional month costs SAR 100 for a single visa or SAR 200 for a multiple visa. If an additional month is added while the resident is outside Saudi Arabia, the official fee rule doubles the additional-month charge.

Before travelling, check the live visa record in Absher or Muqeem. Confirm the visa type, return-before date, Iqama validity, passport validity, and whether any employer, sponsor, traffic, passport, or immigration issue is blocking travel.

An exit and re-entry visa is not a final exit visa. It is for temporary travel when you intend to return to Saudi Arabia.

What Is a Saudi Exit and Re-Entry Visa?

An exit and re-entry visa is a travel authorisation connected to Saudi residency. It is used when an Iqama holder leaves Saudi Arabia for a temporary trip and plans to return without ending the residency relationship.

People commonly use it for:

  • Annual leave
  • Family visits
  • Medical travel
  • Business travel
  • Emergency travel
  • Study or family arrangements for dependants
  • Repeated regional or international travel under a multiple visa

The visa does not replace your passport, Iqama, airline booking, destination-country visa, or employment leave approval. Each of those is a separate requirement.

If you are still learning how Saudi residency works, read our Saudi Iqama explained guide. It explains how the work permit, Iqama, employer, Qiwa, Absher, Muqeem, and travel records connect.

Single Exit and Re-Entry Visa vs Multiple Exit and Re-Entry Visa

Single Exit and Re-Entry Visa

A single visa allows one temporary departure and one return.

It may suit you if:

  • You expect to make only one trip
  • Your travel dates are fixed
  • You do not need repeated cross-border travel
  • You want the lower base fee

Once you return using that visa, it cannot normally be used for another trip.

Multiple Exit and Re-Entry Visa

A multiple visa allows repeated exits and returns during the approved period.

It may suit you if:

  • You travel regularly for work
  • You visit family frequently
  • You have regional business responsibilities
  • You need flexibility for more than one journey

A multiple visa does not override the return-before date. Every trip must remain within the live validity shown in the official system.

Which One Is Better?

Choose according to your real travel plan, not only the initial price.

If you are making one confirmed trip, a single visa may be enough. If you expect several trips during the same approved period, a multiple visa may be more practical.

Do not request a longer visa or a multiple visa simply because an agent says it is "safer." Your Iqama, passport, sponsor record, service conditions, and official portal determine what can actually be issued.

Who Can Apply or Be Included?

The route depends on the resident's category.

Private-Sector Employees

An employer or authorised establishment representative can issue an exit and re-entry visa through the employer-side service when the official conditions are met.

Saudi Arabia's Labour Reform Initiative also allows eligible expatriate private-sector workers to submit an exit and re-entry request without prior employer approval. The employer is notified electronically. This does not mean every worker, contract, occupation, or status automatically qualifies.

Before making travel decisions, check your current employment record. Our Qiwa Saudi Arabia guide explains how contracts, work permits, transfers, and labour-side records connect.

Family Members and Dependants

A resident sponsor may use eligible Absher family services for a spouse, child, or dependant.

The family member's passport, Iqama, sponsor relationship, and other official conditions must be clear before the visa is issued.

If you are planning family sponsorship rather than temporary travel, read our Saudi family visa salary requirement guide.

Domestic Workers

Domestic workers normally follow sponsor-side Absher procedures rather than the standard Qiwa private-sector route.

Service menus, cancellation rules, and "gone out and did not return" handling can differ from the process for a normal private-sector employee.

Investors, Executives, Government Employees, and Special Categories

Some residents use a route connected to their organisation, investment status, government employer, or special residency category.

Follow the service that appears for your actual category instead of assuming the standard private-sector workflow applies.

Do not use an old passport number after renewing your passport. A mismatch between the passport and the Saudi electronic record can cause problems during issuance, airline check-in, or return.

Main Rules You Should Understand

1. The Visa Must Be Issued Before Departure

A resident should not leave Saudi Arabia assuming that return permission can simply be added later.

Before travelling, confirm that the live exit and re-entry visa appears in the official system and that the details are correct.

2. The Person Normally Must Be Inside Saudi Arabia at First Issuance

Official Saudi issuance guidance states that the person for whom the exit and re-entry visa is issued must be inside the Kingdom when the visa is issued.

An outside-Kingdom extension is a different service. It is not the normal first-issuance route.

3. Passport Validity Matters

Saudi service guidance requires the passport to be valid for at least 90 days for exit and re-entry visa issuance.

Your airline, transit country, or destination country may require more. Some travel routes commonly require six months of passport validity, so check those rules separately.

4. Your Iqama Must Support the Travel Period

An exit and re-entry visa does not keep an expired Iqama active.

The official fee rule limits the visa period to the remaining validity of the residency record. If your Iqama is close to expiry, the employer or sponsor may need to solve the renewal issue first.

If your Iqama is near expiry, read our detailed Saudi Iqama renewal guide.

5. Existing Visas and Unresolved Problems Can Block Issuance

Depending on the service and resident category, problems may include:

  • Another active visa
  • Unpaid traffic violations
  • A previous unused visa that was not cancelled correctly
  • An absconding or related status problem
  • Recruitment or Istiqdam-related violations
  • Missing fingerprint where required
  • Insufficient passport validity
  • Incorrect passport details
  • Iqama or sponsorship problems

Use the exact message shown in Absher, Muqeem, or the employer system to identify what needs to be fixed.

6. Employment Leave and Immigration Permission Are Different

A valid exit and re-entry visa is immigration permission. It is not automatically proof that your annual leave, absence from work, or employment obligations are settled.

Check your employment contract, approved leave, duty schedule, and employer communication separately.

7. The Return-Before Date Controls Your Journey

The safest rule is simple: use the live return-before date shown by the official system.

Do not calculate your return date from an old screenshot, an agent's message, the payment date, or your memory.

Recheck the live record:

  • After issuance
  • After any extension
  • Before departure from Saudi Arabia
  • Before your return flight

8. The Live Digital Record Is More Important Than an Old Printout

Keeping a printout is useful, but the live government record controls the current status.

If an old printout and the live record do not match, stop and verify through the official portal, employer, sponsor, or Jawazat before travelling.

  • Pending transfer, termination, or final-exit action

Do not start temporary travel while another major employment or residency transaction is unresolved unless you understand how the systems may interact.

Step 2: Choose Single or Multiple Travel

Decide how many trips you genuinely need.

For one trip, a single visa may be enough. For repeated travel, compare the multiple visa fee with your actual schedule.

Step 3: Confirm Who Will Submit the Request

Depending on your category, the request may be handled by:

  • Employer or authorised establishment representative
  • Eligible private-sector worker under the labour-reform route
  • Family sponsor for dependants
  • Individual sponsor for a domestic worker
  • Relevant organisation for a special resident category

Do not share your personal Absher password or one-time verification code with an agent.

Step 4: Pay Through the Official Channel

Use the government payment route, SADAD, or the approved Saudi bank channel shown by the service.

Before confirming payment, check:

  • Iqama number
  • Visa category
  • Single or multiple type
  • Requested period
  • Payer details

Keep the bank or SADAD reference.

Step 5: Submit the Visa Request

Enter or confirm:

  • Resident identity details
  • Passport details
  • Visa type
  • Required period
  • Return deadline or duration
  • Any acknowledgement shown by the service

Do not accept a return date that is earlier than your planned journey.

Step 6: Check the Live Result

After issuance, verify:

  • Name
  • Iqama number
  • Passport number
  • Single or multiple status
  • Visa number
  • Issue date
  • Return-before date
  • Current status

Save a copy, but recheck the live record close to departure.

Step 7: Check Airline, Transit, and Destination Requirements

The Saudi exit and re-entry visa only covers Saudi departure and return permission.

You may still need to check:

  • Destination visa
  • Transit visa
  • Passport validity
  • Airline document rules
  • Health or vaccination requirements where applicable
  • Return ticket requirements
  • Travel restrictions affecting the route

Step 8: Return Before the Official Deadline

Plan to arrive back in Saudi Arabia before the deadline, not merely depart from the foreign airport before it.

Allow a safety margin for:

  • Flight cancellation
  • Missed connection
  • Weather
  • Passport problems
  • Airline document checks
  • Time-zone differences
  • Unexpected travel restrictions

Returning on the last possible day creates avoidable risk.

The official legal rule also states that when the resident is outside Saudi Arabia, the charge for each additional month is doubled.

All requested periods remain limited by the validity of the Iqama and the conditions shown in the official service.

Who Should Pay for an Employee?

Saudi MHRSD guidance on employment of non-Saudis states that the employer bears exit and re-entry visa fees for the worker as part of the employer's obligations under Saudi labour rules.

This is separate from every possible personal or family expense.

An eligible worker using a worker-initiated service may see payment steps or undertakings on the live platform. Follow the current official screen and keep the payment reference.

Do not hand cash to an unofficial agent or company representative without an official record.

Are Cancellation Fees Refunded?

No. Absher's current cancellation service states that the visa fee cannot be refunded after cancellation.

Are the Fees Always Exactly the Table Amount?

The fee structure above is the official legal structure, but the final amount displayed to you can depend on:

  • Single or multiple visa
  • Requested duration
  • Whether the resident is inside or outside Saudi Arabia
  • Remaining Iqama validity
  • Extension period
  • Current system calculation
  • Any separate official payment item shown by the service

Use the amount generated by the official portal before paying.

Minimum Iqama Validity for Exit Re-Entry Visa and Extensions

There is not one simple Iqama-validity formula that should be applied blindly to every exit and re-entry service.

For normal issuance, the Iqama must be valid and must support the requested travel period under the official system.

For a specific outside-Kingdom Absher extension route, Absher states that when a single exit and re-entry extension is requested using a number of days, the residence permit must be valid for at least 90 days plus the number of days entered for the extension.

Absher also states that when the service uses a Return Date option, the selected date is the last date for returning to Saudi Arabia. Where there is a difference between the Hijri and Gregorian dates in that service, the Gregorian date is used.

This distinction matters. Do not take the 90-days-plus formula and assume it applies identically to every first-time issuance, every worker category, or every visa screen.

Use the eligibility result shown in your live Absher, Muqeem, employer, or sponsor service.

Validity: How Long Can You Stay Outside Saudi Arabia?

There is no safe one-size-fits-all answer.

Your live visa may be based on:

  • A specific period
  • A return-before date
  • The visa type
  • The remaining Iqama validity
  • The service conditions for your category

Use these rules:

  1. Check the exact return-before date in the live record.
  2. Make sure the Iqama supports the travel or extension period.
  3. Return before the deadline with a safety margin.
  4. Recheck the live record after an extension.
  5. Follow the date shown by the official service.
  6. Do not assume a generic "six-month rule" applies to everyone.

For Absher's outside-Kingdom extension service, current official guidance says the extension is subject to the remaining visa and residency conditions and the extension service can allow a maximum period of up to 180 days in eligible cases.

A service maximum does not mean every resident automatically qualifies for the maximum.

Extending an Exit and Re-Entry Visa While Outside Saudi Arabia

An extension may be possible for an eligible resident who is already outside Saudi Arabia.

The correct route can depend on whether the person is:

  • A family member
  • A domestic worker
  • A private-sector employee
  • A government employee
  • An organisation-sponsored resident
  • A person whose return visa has already expired

Absher Extension for Sponsored Family Members or Workers

Absher provides an outside-Kingdom extension service for eligible sponsored people such as family members and domestic workers.

Current official conditions include requirements such as:

  • The person must be outside Saudi Arabia when the extension is requested
  • The extension fee must be paid
  • The person and relevant sponsor record must not have a disqualifying status
  • Iqama validity must meet the service requirement
  • The requested return date must remain within the allowed residency period
  • The service conditions shown in Absher must be met

The official Absher guidance says the extension duration can be up to 180 days, depending on the remaining validity and the service conditions.

When the Normal Electronic Route Does Not Work

Some cases may need another official route, such as:

  • Employer action
  • Sponsor action
  • Tawasul request
  • Jawazat review
  • Ministry of Foreign Affairs return-visa extension service
  • Embassy or consular coordination
  • Correction of passport, Iqama, or sponsor records

Saudi MOFA also provides an official Request for Extending a Return Visa service for certain eligible cases involving expired return visas.

Do not pay anyone who promises a guaranteed extension. Ask for the exact official service name, government reference number, and payment record.

Emergency Situations

Saudi authorities may announce temporary measures during major disruptions such as war, airspace closures, serious regional emergencies, or other exceptional events.

These measures usually have specific dates and eligibility rules.

Do not treat a temporary emergency announcement as a permanent visa rule. Check current Ministry of Interior, Jawazat, Absher, MOFA, and Saudi government announcements when a major disruption affects your trip.

Cancelling an Unused Exit and Re-Entry Visa

An eligible unused exit and re-entry visa can be cancelled through the official route.

For the current Absher cancellation service, the person whose visa is being cancelled must be inside Saudi Arabia.

Absher states that cancellation must be completed:

  • Within 90 days from the visa issuance date, or
  • Before the return date shown on the visa,

whichever comes first.

If the visa is not cancelled within the allowed period, penalties may apply under the applicable rules.

The visa fee is not refunded after cancellation.

Before Cancelling an Exit and Re-Entry Visa

  • Confirm the resident is still inside Saudi Arabia
  • Check that the visa has not already been used for travel
  • Confirm the correct visa number and person
  • Check the cancellation deadline shown by the official service
  • Understand that the government visa fee will not be refunded
  • Save the cancellation confirmation
  • Recheck the live status after cancellation

Common Problems and How to Handle Them

Problem 1: The Employer Refuses to Issue the Visa

First identify why the visa is not being issued.

Possible reasons include:

  • Leave has not been approved
  • Employment dispute
  • Expired or near-expiry Iqama
  • Unpaid traffic violations
  • Passport validity problem
  • Another active visa
  • Pending transfer or termination
  • System error
  • Worker eligibility issue
  • Employer-side action has not been completed

Saudi labour reforms created an exit and re-entry route for eligible expatriate private-sector workers without prior employer approval, with the employer notified electronically.

Do not assume you qualify automatically. Check the current official service and your contract status.

Problem 2: Absher or Muqeem Says the Iqama Is Not Valid Enough

The requested visa or extension period may not fit inside the remaining residency validity.

Ask the employer or sponsor to check:

  • Iqama renewal
  • Work permit status
  • Health insurance
  • Passport update
  • Government payments
  • Traffic fines
  • Linked record errors

Do not shorten your trip blindly until you understand the date and message displayed by the official service.

Problem 3: The Passport Was Renewed but the Old Number Appears

The Saudi passport record may need to be updated.

Depending on the category, the update may involve:

  • Resident
  • Employer
  • Family sponsor
  • Jawazat
  • Absher or Muqeem service

Carrying both the old and new passports does not automatically fix an incorrect electronic record.

Problem 4: The Visa Was Paid but Not Issued

Payment and visa issuance are two different steps.

Check:

  • Payment status
  • Correct Iqama number
  • Visa category
  • Eligibility
  • Existing visa
  • Traffic violations
  • Passport validity
  • Pending request or system message

Keep your SADAD or bank reference.

Problem 5: The Visa Shows the Wrong Return Date

Do not travel until you understand or correct the date.

The employer, sponsor, or applicant should check whether the wrong duration or return date was selected.

Cancellation and re-issuance may sometimes be necessary, but remember that an already-paid visa fee is not refunded after cancellation.

Problem 6: The Resident Is Outside and the Visa Is About to Expire

Start checking the extension route before the final days.

The employer or sponsor should review:

  • Current visa status
  • Iqama validity
  • Passport validity
  • Available Absher extension service
  • Official fee
  • Whether another MOFA, Tawasul, Jawazat, or embassy route applies

Do not rely on the airline to solve an expired Saudi return permission.

Problem 7: The Resident Missed the Return Deadline

Do not attempt to travel on an expired visa without official confirmation.

The employer or sponsor may need to:

  • Check Absher or Muqeem
  • Request an eligible extension
  • Use Tawasul
  • Contact Jawazat
  • Use an official return-extension service
  • Correct employment or sponsorship records

Do not assume there is always a fixed ban or always an easy correction. The outcome depends on the live immigration and employment record.

Problem 8: The System Shows "Gone Out and Did Not Return"

This status can affect employment, sponsorship, and future processing.

The correct handling depends on the resident category.

A domestic worker can have a different route from a private-sector employee. A private-sector worker should have the employer check the connected Muqeem, Qiwa, and immigration records and use the official correction route that applies.

Problem 9: A Family Member's Visa Is Valid but the Sponsor Has a Problem

Family travel can still be affected by:

  • Dependant Iqama expiry
  • Sponsor Iqama problems
  • Passport mismatch
  • Family member linked to an old passport
  • Missing individual travel record
  • Unpaid violations where relevant
  • Other linked record issues

Check each family member separately.

Problem 10: The Traveller Actually Wants to Leave Permanently

Do not use exit and re-entry as a substitute for final exit without understanding the consequences.

Permanent departure involves employment settlement, residency closure, family status, debts, bank accounts, vehicles, utilities, and other records.

If the real plan is to leave Saudi Arabia permanently, use our Saudi final exit and Iqama cancellation guide.

Exit and Re-Entry Visa Mistakes to Avoid

  • Confusing a single visa with a multiple visa
  • Looking only at the issue date and ignoring the return-before date
  • Assuming the visa protects you if your Iqama expires
  • Planning to return on the final day with no safety margin
  • Using an old screenshot instead of checking the live visa status
  • Ignoring a passport-number mismatch after passport renewal
  • Assuming approved annual leave and immigration permission are the same thing
  • Paying an unofficial agent without a government reference
  • Failing to cancel an issued but unused visa
  • Assuming cancellation will refund the visa fee
  • Waiting until the visa expires before checking an outside-Kingdom extension
  • Using private-sector employee rules for a domestic worker or dependant
  • Treating exit and re-entry as a replacement for final exit
  • Sharing an Absher password or verification code with another person

Processing Time

That does not mean every case will be completed immediately.

Delays can happen because of:

  • Iqama or work permit expiry
  • Insufficient passport validity
  • Unpaid traffic violations
  • Another active visa
  • Previous unused-visa problems
  • Employer or sponsor inaction
  • Qiwa contract or transfer status
  • Incorrect passport details
  • Missing fingerprint where required
  • System maintenance
  • Outside-Kingdom extension review
  • Tawasul or Jawazat handling
  • Major travel disruption

Do not book non-refundable travel until the visa and any required employment-side steps are clear.

Exit and re-entry is for temporary travel followed by return to Saudi Arabia. Final exit is for permanent departure under the current residency relationship. If you intend to leave permanently, follow the proper final-exit process instead.

Bottom Line

A Saudi exit and re-entry visa is the main temporary-travel permission for an eligible Iqama holder who plans to return to Saudi Arabia.

For 2026, the official fee structure remains SAR 200 for a single visa for up to two months and SAR 500 for a multiple visa for up to three months, with additional-month charges. When a resident is outside Saudi Arabia, the additional-month charge is doubled under the official legal rule.

Before travelling, check the live visa status, return-before date, passport, Iqama, employment leave, and any employer or sponsor issue. Use Absher or Muqeem rather than an old screenshot.

If you need an extension, cancellation, or correction, use the official route that applies to your category. Do not wait until the final day and do not pay an unofficial agent who cannot show an official service and government reference.

Requirements can change. Confirm important travel and payment decisions through the live Saudi government portal before booking or departure.
