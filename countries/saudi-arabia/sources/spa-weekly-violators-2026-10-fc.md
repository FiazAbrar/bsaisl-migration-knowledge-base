Riyadh, October 17, 2026, SPA -- In inspection campaigns across Saudi Arabia between October 8 and 14 to ensure compliance with residency, labor, and border security laws, the Ministry of Interior recorded 13,822 violations, including 6,547 related to residency, 3,896 to border security, and 3,379 to labor laws.

Border authorities apprehended 2,021 individuals attempting to cross into the Kingdom illegally. They also arrested 28 people for attempting to leave the Kingdom illegally.

A total of 24 people involved in transporting, sheltering, and employing violators were arrested.

A total of 34,834 expatriates, including 32,433 men and 2,401 women, are currently undergoing procedures to enforce regulations.

Meanwhile, 20,190 individuals were detained for violating the regulations and were instructed to contact their countries' embassies or consulates to obtain proper travel documents. In addition, 6,509 were advised to make travel arrangements, and 9,454 were repatriated.

The Ministry of Interior warned that anyone who facilitates the illegal entry of individuals into the Kingdom, transports them within its territory, or provides them with shelter, assistance, or other services may face penalties of up to 15 years in prison and a fine of up to SAR1 million. Vehicles used for transport and properties used for shelter may also be confiscated.

The ministry emphasized that such acts constitute major crimes that warrant arrest and urged the public to report any violations by calling 911 in Makkah, Riyadh, and Eastern regions, or 999 and 996 in other parts of the Kingdom.
