Chapter 1: Initial Concepts of Police Clearance (Police Clearance) Service and Global Migration

To travel to any country in the world for the international labor market, higher education, or permanent residence (PR/Immigration), verification of a citizen's past criminal record is a universal requirement. The Police Clearance service issued by Bangladesh Police is an official government certificate proving that no criminal offense, anti-state activity, or arrest warrant exists against the person concerned at any police station or court in the country. Submitting this character certificate is mandatory as a precondition for visa issuance in countries across the Middle East, Europe, North America, or East Asia.

Previously, the process of obtaining this character certificate was extremely lengthy and troublesome. Ordinary citizens had to appear in person multiple times at the local police station, and suffered from files getting stuck and unwanted mediation by brokers. However, under the Digital Bangladesh vision, Bangladesh Police has launched a self-sufficient online portal, through which one can apply from home and instantly monitor the application's progress. Especially for expatriate brothers and sisters, Probashi App has added direct government server integration, allowing the certificate's authenticity and expiry countdown to be monitored right from the mobile phone with a touch of a finger.

Why is a Police Clearance Certificate essential in the international visa process?

Legal validity of migration: Foreign states demand a clean police record from any arriving foreign national to ensure their geographical security and public order. Work permission and work permit: Employing companies in Malaysia, Qatar, the UAE, Oman, Kuwait, and various EU countries have made a clearance certificate mandatory alongside work permit applications. BMET manpower clearance: Police clearance is also verified for many countries when obtaining an immigration smart card from Bangladesh's Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training (BMET). Student visa and sponsorship: This must be submitted at the visa interview after university admission is confirmed in the UK, Canada, Australia, and Schengen countries.

The Police Clearance Certificate's validity generally remains effective for a maximum of 6 months (180 days) from the date of issue. Therefore, preparing this certificate at the right time, considering the visa submission timing for going abroad, is a primary duty of a wise expatriate.

Chapter 2: The Online Police Clearance Application Portal (pcc.police.gov.bd) and Registration Method

Anyone can very easily complete a police clearance certificate bangladesh online application through Bangladesh Police's official service portal, pcc.police.gov.bd. Before starting the application, the applicant must create a personal account on the portal with a valid mobile number and active email address. A secure dashboard is created for each account, from which tracking and future reference can be maintained.

The 4 main stages of registration and application process

Step 01: Sign up

Go to the portal and provide your name, mobile number, and password, and activate the account through OTP verification.

Step 02: Passport entry

Enter the passport number, issue date, expiry date, and the name of the issuing authority, matching the passport's information page.

Step 03: Address selection

Accurately select the district, thana, and ward from the dropdown menu for the permanent and current addresses. Selecting the wrong thana can cause the application to be rejected.

Step 04: Challan and submit

Upload a copy of the government's 500 taka challan and submit the file. The system will automatically generate a reference number (Ref ID).

The most important thing to be careful about when applying on the portal is spelling accuracy. If there is even the slightest mismatch in the spelling of your name and your father's name between your passport, National ID (NID), and online application, the Special Branch's software will show a mismatch and put the application on hold.

Chapter 3: Government Fee, A-Challan, and MFS (bKash/Nagad) Payment Gateway

According to the rules set by the Ministry of Finance of the Government of Bangladesh, the official fee for the Police Clearance Certificate is exactly 500 taka (BDT 500). This fee can be deposited directly at the treasury branch of Bangladesh Bank or Sonali Bank via challan, or paid instantly from home through the government's online Automated Challan System (A-Challan) using bKash, Nagad, or Rocket.

The specific Economic Code for the treasury challan

When depositing money via manual bank challan, the designated economic code must be filled in with extreme care:

1-2211-0020-2611

The description field must state: "Deposit for Police Clearance Certificate fee." The original copy of the challan (the green page) must be scanned and attached online.

The rule for paying within 5 minutes via the online A-Challan

Enter the Ministry of Finance's automated challan website (ibas2.finance.gov.bd/acs). In the Ministry/Division field, select "Ministry of Home Affairs - Public Security Division." Selecting "Police Clearance Fee" in the service field will automatically fill in the designated code. Provide the applicant's name, NID number, and mobile number, and select MFS (bKash / Nagad / Rocket) as the payment method. Once the fee and gateway charge are paid, a 12-digit challan number and PDF copy will be generated instantly. Uploading that PDF copy directly to the police portal takes no additional time for bank verification.

Chapter 5: Police Clearance Status Check and Real-Time Tracking Pipeline

After submitting an application, every citizen's main curiosity is to know the current state of their file. The system for checking police clearance check online bangladesh and police clearance certificate status online is extremely transparent and timely. Which table in the police headquarters' central database the file is being processed under can be tracked at any time with the reference number and passport number.

The 7 specific stages of the status pipeline and their real meaning

  1. Application Submitted: Your application has been successfully received on the online portal and is awaiting initial system verification.
  2. Payment Verified: The authenticity of the 500 taka deposit has been automatically matched from the Treasury or Sonali Bank server.
  3. Inquiry Assigned: The application has been sent to the relevant local police station or Special Branch (SB/DSB), and an inquiry officer (IO) has been assigned.

  6. Certificate Printed: The certificate has been printed on official security paper with a QR code and digital watermark.
  7. Ready for Delivery / Delivered: The certificate is ready to be handed over at the SP office or DMP counter, or is suitable for sending to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs attestation branch.

Chapter 6: Special Branch (SB) and District Special Branch (DSB) Investigation Protocol

Among Bangladesh Police's intelligence branches, the Special Branch (SB) and, at the district level, the District Special Branch (DSB), carry out the most powerful and central responsibility. A Police Clearance Certificate is not just any ordinary character certificate; it is a deep national security clearance. Therefore, after your application is submitted, it is first automatically screened in the SB and DSB databases.

What matters are examined in the SB and DSB investigation?

CDMS (Crime Data Management System) database search: Whether there is any FIR, chargesheet, or conviction warrant against the applicant's name, father's name, and fingerprints in the Central Crime Data Management System. Anti-terrorism and anti-militancy cell: Whether there is any record of involvement with a banned militant organization, money laundering, or seditious activity. Passport duplication and forgery: Whether more than one passport has been issued in one person's name, or whether there is a prior record of any fake passport. International Red Notice (Interpol): Whether there is a match with any Interpol wanted list or international criminal network.

If there is no allegation against a candidate, the SB system sends the signal "No Adverse Record Found," as a result of which the file is immediately forwarded to the local police station for the next stage of field verification.

Chapter 7: Field-Level Verification by the Local Police Station Inquiry Officer (IO)

The most sensitive and practical step of online police clearance is the police station-level investigation. After the file reaches the police station with jurisdiction over the current or permanent address mentioned in the application, the officer-in-charge (OC) of the station appoints an officer of Sub-Inspector (SI) or Assistant Sub-Inspector (ASI) rank as the inquiry officer (IO).

What you need to have with you when meeting the inquiry officer:

The original passport and a photocopy of the passport (the officer will verify and return it). The original National ID (NID) or online birth registration certificate. The original character certificate from the local ward councilor or UP chairman. The house rent agreement or a copy of the electricity/gas bill (as proof of current address). If the applicant cannot appear personally (if living abroad), proof of authorization to a valid representative and a photocopy of the expatriate's passport.

The inquiry officer usually contacts the applicant by phone and asks them to show documents at the police station on a specified day, or sometimes visits the home and gathers character information from local notable persons or neighbors. If everything is satisfactory in the investigation, the officer uploads a positive report on their digital tablet or the online system.

Chapter 8: The Legal Path to Resolution if There Is a Pending Criminal Case, Police GD, or Warrant

For many job seekers or expatriates, the biggest nightmare is finding out, after applying for police clearance, that there is a pending case, a General Diary (GD), or a harassment case against their name. In such a situation, police headquarters will not issue the character certificate under any circumstances, and the status directly shows "Adverse Report / Rejected." However, this is not a permanent deadlock; it is possible to resolve this problem through the correct legal process.

Situation 1: A pending case with regular bail

If you are on regular bail from the court and there is no court injunction on foreign travel, the certified bail bond and order copy from the honorable court must be submitted to the inquiry officer. However, obtaining the certificate may be complicated until resolution under some sensitive sections.

Situation 2: Acquittal or withdrawal of a case

If you have been acquitted in a case, obtaining a Certified Judgment Copy of the final verdict from the court and submitting an application to the District Superintendent of Police's office will update the police crime database to generate a clear report.

Situation 3: Resolution of a General Diary (GD)

If there is only a GD at the police station over a family or land dispute, submitting a settlement letter from both parties or a GD resolution report in the presence of the inquiry officer locally will open the way for the clearance certificate.

If you run into legal complications, the only 100% reliable and permanent solution is to obtain the relevant court records through a skilled criminal lawyer and submit a legal petition directly to the SP office, without paying any dishonest middleman.

Chapter 10: Method for Collecting the Certificate from the District Superintendent of Police's (SP) Office and DMP

After the online application is finally approved and printed, it must be collected from the designated delivery counter. For metropolitan areas, the original certificate is distributed by the Metropolitan Police Headquarters (e.g., DMP, CMP, KMP); for district level, it is distributed by the Police Clearance Cell of the District Superintendent of Police's (SP) office.

What to bring to collect the certificate:

The application delivery slip printed from the online portal. The applicant's original passport, or, if the holder is abroad, the original National ID copy of the representative nominated by the applicant. The second copy of the challan or the money receipt printout of the A-Challan. Displaying the official SMS received on the mobile.

Chapter 11: Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) Consular Wing Attestation and e-Apostille Guide

The process is not finished after collecting the police clearance certificate from the police department. According to international law and migration agreements, for any domestic document of Bangladesh to be valid internationally, it must carry the official attestation stamp of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) of the Government of Bangladesh.

The step-by-step protocol for attestation at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs:

Digital appointment / serial: A token must be collected by standing in line in the morning or online at the Consular branch of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs at Segunbagicha. Matching the police department's signature: MOFA officials enter the police database and match the certificate's signature with the relevant SP or DC's signature. Attaching a security sticker and seal: If everything is correct, an official security sticker with a barcode and a special ink seal are attached to the reverse side of the certificate. e-Apostille Convention recognition: For countries that are members of the Hague Apostille Convention, this MOFA attestation is directly considered an authoritative international document.

Chapter 12: Foreign Embassy Legalization, Notary Public, and Language Translation Guidance

After the Ministry of Foreign Affairs' attestation is completed, the next step is determined based on the policy of the destination country's embassy. International notary and translation guidance:

Approved translator: The certificate translation must be done by a translation organization approved by the Supreme Court or the embassy. A general translation is not acceptable. Notary public certification: A notary public's red ribbon and wax seal must confirm the attachment of the original English certificate and the translated page. Submission at the visa application center: This legalized copy must be submitted along with other files at VFS Global or the relevant country's visa center.

Chapter 49: Passport Verification vs. Police Clearance: The Fundamental Legal Difference

Many people confuse the police investigation at the time of passport creation with the investigation for a police clearance certificate. But legally, their purpose and scope are completely different.

Comparative outline of the main differences:

Passport police investigation: Its purpose is only to confirm whether the applicant is genuinely a citizen of Bangladesh and whether they or their family exist at the permanent address mentioned on the passport. It does not issue a character certificate. Police Clearance Certificate: This is a specific legal certification of the citizen's freedom from crime. It clearly states that there is no criminal allegation against the applicant at any court or police station in Bangladesh. It is made directly for the international justice system. Applying authority: The passport investigation happens at the request of the Passport Directorate, while the police clearance application is made by the citizen directly to the Inspector General of Police (IGP).

Chapter 53: 25 Essential Technical Questions and Answers on Police Clearance

Below are detailed, documented answers to common and complex questions that arise in the minds of ordinary citizens and expatriates regarding online police clearance application, status tracking, and delivery:

Question 1: How long does it take to get a Police Clearance Certificate?

Answer: In Dhaka and other metropolitan areas, police clearance is usually obtained within 7 to 10 working days, and at the district level within 10 to 15 working days. However, if there is any investigation complication in the background, it may take somewhat longer. Question 2: What is the government fee for police clearance, and where must it be deposited? Answer: The government fee is fixed at exactly 500 taka. It can be deposited at the treasury branch of Sonali Bank or any scheduled bank with code 1-2211-0020-2611, or paid online via the government's A-Challan portal using bKash, Nagad, or Rocket. Question 3: How long is the Police Clearance Certificate's validity? Answer: Its international acceptability remains effective for a maximum of 180 days, or 6 months, from the date of issue of the certificate. A new application must be made again once it expires. Question 4: If the current and permanent address on the passport differ, which address should I apply with? Answer: The application must mainly be made based on the permanent address mentioned in the passport. However, if there is valid proof of long-term residence at the current address (City Corporation certificate and utility bill), investigation is also possible under the current police station. Question 5: If I am working abroad, how can I get police clearance without going to the country? Answer: Get a passport copy attested at the Bangladesh embassy located abroad and apply online, and nominate a valid representative in the country (such as a brother, father, or wife). After the investigation is complete, your representative can collect the certificate from the SP office. Question 6: What information is displayed when the certificate's QR code is scanned? Answer: Scanning the QR code directly opens the pcc.police.gov.bd portal and displays the candidate's name, parents' names, passport number, issue date, and the digital authenticity of the approving police officer. Question 7: I had a case in the past but was acquitted by the court — will I get the certificate? Answer: Yes, absolutely. However, when applying, the certified copy of the court's acquittal order must be submitted to the inquiry officer and the SP office so your name is removed from the crime database. Question 8: Is police clearance delivered directly to the home? Answer: No, police clearance is not sent home by post or courier. The applicant or their nominated representative must personally collect it from the relevant SP office or DMP office with the original slip. Question 9: Is Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) attestation mandatory? Answer: Attestation with a sticker from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs' Consular Wing is 100% mandatory for submission to any international visa, work permit, or foreign embassy. Question 11: How do I correct incorrect information that was submitted in the application? Answer: If the investigation has not started after submission, you can request a correction by raising a ticket at the portal's helpdesk or showing the original documents to the inquiry officer. But if correction is needed after final approval, a fresh application must be made. Question 12: Does police clearance include a blood test or medical report? Answer: No, police clearance contains no medical or health information. It is only a certificate of freedom from criminal offense and character cleanliness. For medical examinations, GAMCA or an approved medical center issues a separate certificate. Question 13: If my passport is an e-Passport, is there any special benefit? Answer: Yes, since e-Passport biometric data is directly linked with the police server, background verification is completed faster than with an MRP passport. Answer: Yes, police clearance can be obtained as many times as needed by submitting a new challan each time. There is no legal limitation on obtaining it multiple times.
