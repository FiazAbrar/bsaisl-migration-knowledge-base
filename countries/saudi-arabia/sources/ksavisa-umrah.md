# Umrah

Visit visa for the purpose of Umrah allows you to enter Saudi Arabia to perform Umrah.

### Eligibility

- The Visit Visa for Umrah is available for Muslims of all nationalities.
- Umrah is available throughout the year except during the Hajj season, which is from the 15th of Dhul Qa'dah to the 15th of Dhul Hijjah.

### Activity Guidelines

- Attending events and touristic activities.
- You can freely move between cities within Saudi Arabia while on an Umrah.
- Travelers are not authorized to undertake the Haj pilgrimage while on this visa.

### Additional support

- Contact the Ministry of Foreign Affairs through communication channels for the most accurate and up-to-date information on the Saudi Arabia visa application process. Avoid unauthorized individuals or companies when seeking assistance with your application.

- Note: Applying for Umrah directly through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (Umrah Al-Mujamala) is available to all nationalities and is for those who have prior approval from the embassy. As for regular Umrah, it is done through Nusuk and authorized agents.

#### Target audience
Citizen, resident, visitor

#### Visa duration and validity
- Single Entry Visa
  - Validity: 90 days
  - Stay Duration: 90 days
- Multiple Entry Visa
  - Validity: 1 year
  - Stay Duration: 90 days
