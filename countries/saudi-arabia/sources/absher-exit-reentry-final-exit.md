# Absher: Issuance of Exit/Re-Entry or Final Exit Visa - Card (Firecrawl scrape 2026-11-07; page "Last Updated:29/10/2026")

This service enables beneficiaries to electronically issue an Exit and Re-Entry Visa or a Final Exit Visa for family members or domestic workers.

Terms
• The beneficiary must be present inside the Kingdom at the time of issuance.
• The "Return Before" date can be set up to 7 days before the residency expiry date (other terms and conditions will also apply).
• For issuing a single-trip Exit and Re-Entry Visa with the duration option in months, the residency validity must be equal to or more than (90 days + the entered duration).
• If issued with a "Return Before" date, that date will be the last day the individual can return to Saudi Arabia.
• A registered fingerprint must exist for the individual (males and females aged 15 years and above).

Requirements
• The beneficiary must have a digital ID on Absher platform.
• Payment of the visa fee.
• Settlement of all traffic violations for the individual, worker, and employer.
• The individual must be present in Saudi Arabia at the time of visa issuance.
• The individual's passport must be valid for at least 60 days for Final Exit and 90 days for Exit and Re-Entry.
• The individual's residency permit must be valid.
• The individual must not have any vehicles registered under their name at the time of issuing a Final Exit Visa.
• A registered fingerprint must exist for the individual (males and females aged 15 years and above).

FAQ: If the residency permit is expired, can I issue an Exit and Re-Entry Visa or a Final Exit Visa? No, having a valid residency permit is a requirement for issuing the visa.
