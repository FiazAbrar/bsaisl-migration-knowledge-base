# Change expatriate Profession

[Service Link](https://qiwa.sa/en "Service Link")

Sector

business sector

An electronic service supported by artificial intelligence enables the establishment to apply to change the profession of the resident employee who works for it to match the work he does, and the establishment can also apply through the service to correct the profession of the employee who works for it according to the Unified Saudi Occupational Classification Guide without visiting the ministry

# কোনও সমস্যা হয়েছে।

জাভাস্ক্রিপ্ট চালানো যায়নি।

- Login to the Qiwa platform
- Choose a career change service
- start serving

- The applicant must be an accredited representative of the facility in the systems of the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development.
- All facility licenses must be valid.
- The entity must be located in the platinum or green range.
- The status of the facility must be current.
- It is not permitted to change a resident’s profession to a profession restricted to Saudis.
- A valid professional practice certificate from the Saudi Council of Engineers for engineering professions.
- A professional practice certificate from the Ministry of Agriculture for veterinary professions.
- A professional practice certificate from the Saudi Authority for Accounting Specialties for those in accounting professions.
- The status of the worker whose profession is requested to be changed must not be “awaiting service transfer.”
- There is no request to change a profession in progress for the same worker.
- The worker must not have been issued a final exit visa

- The service is fully automated and does not require any documents or paperwork.

How can I cancel or modify a job change request?

You can access the requests sent on the Qawia platform, or choose to view the requests from the services page

Is it possible to change the profession in the visa after it is issued?

It is possible to change the profession of the employee after coming or cancel the visa and re-issue it by choosing the required profession

Can I change the profession of an expatriate employee to a profession subject to localization decisions?

It's not possible
