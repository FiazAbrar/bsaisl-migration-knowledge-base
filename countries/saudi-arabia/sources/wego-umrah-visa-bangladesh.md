## Who Is Eligible for an Umrah Visa from Bangladesh?

[The Umrah Visa](https://blog.wego.com/2026-umrah-visa/) is open to all practicing Muslims holding a valid Bangladeshi passport.

### Age and health requirements

- There is no minimum age, but minors under 18 must travel with a parent or legal guardian whose visa is linked to theirs.
- Pilgrims must be in good health and free from contagious diseases.
- A mandatory [meningococcal meningitis (ACWY) vaccination](https://haj.gov.sa/en/FAQ) is required for all pilgrims aged one year and above, taken at least 10 days before arrival in Saudi Arabia. A conjugate MenACWY certificate is valid for up to five years; a polysaccharide certificate, or one that does not state the vaccine type, is valid for three years.

### Mahram rules for women

Women aged 18 and above can now apply for an umrah visa without a male guardian (mahram), provided they travel as part of an authorized group or book through a licensed umrah operator.

Saudi Arabia updated this framework to allow women to perform [Umrah without a mahram](https://blog.wego.com/complete-guide-for-umrah-without-mahram/). Individual operator policies may still vary, so confirm with your agency before booking.

## Required Documents for the Umrah Visa

Your authorized umrah agent will collect the following before submitting your application through the Maqam portal:

- Valid Bangladeshi passport (minimum six months’ validity, at least two blank pages).
- Recent passport-size photograph with a white background.
- Meningococcal meningitis (ACWY) vaccination certificate.
- Completed biometric enrollment via the Saudi Visa Bio app.
- Confirmed hotel and transport booking (new 2026 Nusuk requirement).
- Proof of payment for visa and insurance fees.

### The Saudi Visa Biometric requirement

Bangladesh is among the countries where biometric enrollment is mandatory for umrah e-visa issuance. Download the Saudi Visa Bio app ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=sa.gov.mofa.saudivisabio)/[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/my/app/saudi-visa-bio/id1530028975)) on your smartphone, scan your passport, capture a facial image, and register all ten fingerprints through the camera.

The process takes under 10 minutes, and all data is automatically deleted from the app once enrollment is complete.

## How to Apply for an Umrah Visa from Bangladesh

**Booking online:** Wego is an official partner of the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah, with ministry-approved hotels and the eVisa in partnership with Nusuk. You can [book the whole trip from your phone](https://blog.wego.com/book-umrah-online-from-your-phone/).

Packages are supplied by licensed Umrah operators, so check the agency named on your booking against the [approved Umrah agency list](https://www.hajj.gov.bd/agency-profile) on Bangladesh’s official Hajj Management Portal.

Bangladeshi pilgrims cannot apply directly on the [Nusuk platform](https://umrah.nusuk.sa/) as individual applicants. The application must go through a Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah licensed operator. Here is the step-by-step process:

1.  Choose an authorized umrah travel agency in Bangladesh that is registered on the Maqam portal.
2.  Submit your passport, photographs, vaccination certificate, and preferred travel dates to the agent.
3.  Complete your biometric enrollment through the Saudi Visa Bio app.
4.  The agent uploads your documents to the Maqam/Nusuk portal and books your Nusuk-verified hotel and ground transport.
5.  Pay the visa fee (SAR 300), mandatory medical insurance (SAR 100–180), and the agent’s service fee.
6.  Once approved, your e-visa is issued electronically, with no embassy visit required.

For a broader overview of the application process across nationalities, see our [complete umrah visa guide](https://blog.wego.com/umrah-visa/).

### Processing time

Standard processing takes 3–7 working days after complete submission. During peak seasons such as [Ramadan](https://blog.wego.com/umrah-during-ramadan/) or school holidays, expect 7–10 working days or longer.

## Umrah Visa Fees for Bangladeshi Pilgrims

The cost of an umrah visa from Bangladesh breaks down into the official Saudi government fee and the agent’s service charges:

| Fee Component               | Approximate Cost (SAR) | Approximate Cost (BDT) |
|-----------------------------|------------------------|------------------------|
| Umrah visa fee              | SAR 300                | ~BDT 9,800             |
| Mandatory medical insurance | SAR 100–180            | ~BDT 3,300–5,900       |
| Agent service fee           | Varies                 | BDT 4,000–8,000        |
| Total visa processing       | SAR 450–900            | BDT 17,000–38,000      |

For a detailed breakdown of how umrah visa pricing works globally, check our [umrah visa price 2026 guide](https://blog.wego.com/2026-umrah-visa-price/).

### The 2026 Nusuk booking rule

A major change in 2026 requires every umrah visa application to be tied to a confirmed Nusuk-verified hotel and transport booking. This means your total out-of-pocket cost will be higher than the visa fee alone, so factor in accommodation near the Haram in [Makkah](https://www.wego.com/hotels/saudi-arabia/makkah/cheap-hotels) and [Madinah](https://www.wego.com/hotels/saudi-arabia/al-madinah/cheap-hotels) as part of your visa application budget.

Multiple airlines connect [Dhaka](https://www.wego.com/airports/dac/airports-in-dhaka/hazrat-shahjalal-international-airport-dac) to Saudi Arabia’s two main umrah gateways. [Biman Bangladesh Airlines](https://www.wego.com/airlines/biman-bangladesh-airlines-bg) operates direct flights to both [Jeddah](https://www.wego.com/airports/jed/airports-in-jeddah/king-abdulaziz-international-airport-jed) and [Madinah](https://www.wego.com/airports/med/airports-in-madinah/prince-mohammad-bin-abdulaziz-international-airport-med), with multiple weekly departures.

[Saudia](https://www.wego.com/airlines/saudia-sv), [flynas](https://www.wego.com/airlines/flynas-xy), and [US-Bangla Airlines](https://www.wego.com/airlines/us-bangla-airlines-bs) also operate nonstop flights on this route, while [Air Arabia](https://www.wego.com/airlines/air-arabia-g9) offers connecting flights via Sharjah. Nonstop flights from Dhaka to Jeddah take about 7 hours and 30 minutes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an umrah visa cost from Bangladesh in 2026?

The total visa processing cost ranges from BDT 17,000 to BDT 38,000, depending on insurance tier and agent service fees. The official Saudi visa fee alone is SAR 300.

Is biometric enrollment mandatory for Bangladeshi umrah pilgrims?

Yes. Bangladesh is among the countries where biometric registration through the Saudi Visa Bio app is mandatory before the umrah e-visa can be issued.
