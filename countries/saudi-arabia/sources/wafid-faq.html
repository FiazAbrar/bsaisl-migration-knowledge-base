Frequently Asked Questions

What is Wafid Program?

• Wafid is a program under the umbrella of the Gulf Health Council that enables individuals bound to work or reside in the GCC to book their medical check-up appointments and ensuring that they are fit.

How to book an appointment on the website?

To register for the medical test, please follow these steps:
• Please access the following link: https://wafid.com/
• Tap "Book an Appointment"
• Choose the country, the city, and the travelling country (the medical center will be assigned automatically based on the city)
• Enter all required information and tap "Submit" (please double-check all dates and information before submitting)
• Fill out the payment page — note that the expiry date format is year then month (YY MM) — and ensure all credit card details are entered correctly. Tap "Pay"
• After receiving the medical exam slip, please visit the medical center at your earliest convenience to complete your medical examination.

Notes:
1. If the nationality is Egyptian, the expatriate must enter the national ID number.
2. To continue, you must enter the number shown below and check the box stating: "I agree that all information entered is correct as shown on the page below."

How can the applicant generate his slip?

• From print GCC slip tab, please enter the passport and the nationality then tap generate.

The applicant can’t make the payment.

Please check the following and make sure that:
• there is no bank issue
• all credit card details are correct on the payment confirmation page and
• credit card is valid with enough balance
• the expire date is entered correctly as year then month (YYMM)
• Try to pay again using another card,
• Check the internet network or try another internet browser

In case the issue is still actual please do the following:
• Please send the issue to [email protected] and provide us with passport number and screenshot for the error.

How to know the location of the medical centers in every country & city?

• Please access the following link https://wafid.com/
• From "Medical centers" list tab, please select the country and the city, all medical centers for the determined city will be displayed.
• You can also enter any medical center name, and the medical center details will be displayed.

Can the applicant cancel the slip after generating?

• For any queries regarding slip cancellations, please contact [email protected]

How can the applicant check the status after doing the medical examination?

• From the check candidate status tab , enter that passport number and the Nationality or the slip number

Can the applicant choose specific medical center?

• The applicant can’t choose the medical center since the medical center is assigned automatically as per the selected city and country.

Can the applicant change the city after generating the slip?

• Change of city is possible after the expiry of the current appointment slip, and the validity of the slip is available on the slip.
After that you may generate new slip with desired city.

Within how many days a result of the medical examination appears?

• The medical examination result appears within 26 days.

In case the applicant didn’t find his nationality or country on the system?

• Please contact [email protected] and mention the required nationality.

From any age, the expatriate can register on the site?

• The applicant can book an appointment from any age.

How much is the medical appointment?

• $10 per applicant for an appointment

How can the applicant change the wrong data?

If the applicant enters incorrect information such as passport number, name, nationality, profession, date of birth, visa number and date, passport expiry date, passport issue date, or place of passport issuance, please follow the instructions below:
1. The applicant should contact the medical center and request them to submit a change request, either as a “Change candidate details” or a “Change medical exam request.”
2. Ask the medical center for the change request number and the submission date.
3. The change request is free of charge; the applicant should not pay any fees.

After generating the slip, when should the applicant visit the medical center?

The applicant should visit the medical center as soon as possible, immediately after generating the slip.

If the applicant has paid but the slip has not been generated:

Please contact [email protected] and provide the following information and documents:
(Nationality, date and time of payment, passport number, full name, credit card number, cardholder’s name, email address, a copy of the credit card, and a recent bank statement reflecting the transaction)

If the applicant made two payments and wants to request a refund for one:

Please contact [email protected] and provide the following:
(Nationality / date and time of access /passport number /Name/credit Card number /card name owner/E-mail/copy of the credit card/Bank statement).

Can the applicant book an appointment in a different country from their nationality? For example if he is Indian but he is resident in Sudan, can he submit application to medical center in Sudan?

Yes, the applicant can book the appointment in any country by selecting:
- The country where the examination will take place
- His nationality

If the applicant already has a valid medical report in the system, when can they rebook?

The applicant may rebook a new appointment 2 months after the date of the existing report.

Can the applicant change the examination date?

• No, the examination date cannot be changed or edited. It is automatically calculated based on the approval date of the medical exam.

Can the expiry date of the medical report be changed?

The expiry date is calculated based on the modification date, not the original examination date.

If the system is down or unstable?

Please try another Browser.
• Make sure that you have stable internet network.
• Contact [email protected], mention the country and provide screenshot for the error.

What are the cases that are evaluated as unfit status?

Infectious Diseases:
1. HIV positive (AIDS)
2. Hepatitis (B) Surface Antsgen Poutive
3. Hepatitis C Antibody - postive
4. Microfilaria and Malaria
5. Leprosy
6. Any abnormal chest X-ray manifestations including, but not limited to:
a. Active on past evidence of TB
b. Pulmonary fibrosis and pulmonary calcification
c. Pleural effusion
d. Lymphadenopathy.

Non-infectious diseases
1. Renal failure
2. Liver failure or hepatic insufficency
3. Heart failure
4. Uncontrolled Hypertension
5. Uncontrolled diabetes.
6. Different types of cancer.
7. Psychiatric and neurological disorders
8. Any distortion, amputation or physical disability impeding the applicant's performance.
9. Hemoglobin below 79/100di.
