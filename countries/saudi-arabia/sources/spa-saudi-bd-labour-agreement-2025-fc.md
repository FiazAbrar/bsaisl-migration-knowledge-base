Saudi Arabia, Bangladesh Sign Labor Agreement

Riyadh, October 06, 2025, SPA -- Minister of Human Resources and Social Development Ahmed Al-Rajhi and Bangladesh’s Minister of Expatriates Welfare and Overseas Employment Dr. Asif Nazrul signed a general labor recruitment agreement in the presence of several officials and specialists from both sides.

The agreement establishes a regulatory framework for the recruitment procedures of Bangladeshi workers, protects the rights of both workers and employers, and regulates the contractual relations between them.

This step is part of the ministry’s efforts to strengthen international partnerships, open new labor markets, ensure a balanced labor market, and explore areas of cooperation at all levels to achieve common interests.

During their meeting, Al-Rajhi and Nazrul also discussed ways to enhance bilateral relations in accordance with their shared goals.
