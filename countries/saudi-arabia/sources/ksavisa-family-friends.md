# Family and Friends

Visit visa for family and friends permit entry into Saudi Arabia for the purpose of visiting family members and friends residing in Saudi Arabia.

### Eligibility

First: Electronic Visa (e-Visa)

- This type of visa can be obtained instantly through the KSA Visa platform, and it will be delivered via email. Citizens of eligible countries can obtain this visa, or individuals meeting one of the following requirements:
- Holders of visit visas (Schengen, USA, UK).
- Permanent residents in the USA, EU, or the UK.
- Residents in one of the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) countries.

Second: Visa through Service Centers or Embassies

- Visa through Service Centers: This option is available in certain countries where visa service centers are present.
- Visa through Saudi Embassies: In case visa service centers are not available in the applicant's country, visitors can apply for a visit visa through the KSA Visa platform and then proceed directly to the Saudi Arabian embassy in their country of residence.

Third: Visa on Arrival

- Holders of visit visas (Schengen, USA, UK).
- Permanent residents in the USA, EU, or the UK.

### Activity Guidelines

- Attending events and touristic activities.
- Visit family or friends.
- Performing Umrah is permitted under this visa.
- Travelers are not authorized to undertake the Haj pilgrimage while on this visa.

#### Target audience
Citizen, resident, visitor

1 minute to 3 business days

- Single Entry Visa

  - Stay Duration: 90 days
- Multiple Entry Visa
