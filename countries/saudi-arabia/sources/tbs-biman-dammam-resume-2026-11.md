# Biman to resume Dammam flights on 7 November; Riyadh flights remain suspended

## Dhaka-Dammam flights will operate daily during the daytime until further notice, while Riyadh services remain suspended amid safety concerns and low passenger demand.

**TBS Report**

03 November, 2026, 06:00 pm

Last modified: 03 November, 2026, 06:00 pm

![](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2Zz48dXNlIHhsaW5rOmhyZWY9Ii9zaXRlcy9hbGwvdGhlbWVzL3Nsb3RoL2ltYWdlcy9pY29ucy5zdmcjb3Blbi1wb3B1cCIgLz48L3N2Zz4=)

Photo: TBS

**Biman Bangladesh Airlines will resume regular flights on the Dhaka-Dammam-Dhaka route from 7 November, while flights on the Dhaka-Riyadh-Dhaka route will remain suspended until further notice amid security concerns following recent airstrikes in Saudi Arabia.**

The airline has adjusted its flight schedule to various destinations in Saudi Arabia, taking into account passenger safety and the overall situation, according to a press release issued today (3 November).

Biman said daily flights to Dammam would operate during the day until further instructions, considering passenger safety and the overall situation.

#### [![The Business Standard Google News](/sites/all/themes/sloth/images/google_news.svg) Keep updated, follow The Business Standard's Google news channel](https://news.google.com/publications/CAAqBwgKMIivlQsw2ZKrAw?ceid=US:en&oc=3)

Meanwhile, flights on the Dhaka-Riyadh-Dhaka route will remain suspended until further notice due to insufficient passenger demand. A significant number of passengers travelling to Riyadh are currently reaching Saudi Arabia through alternative routes, the release added.

Passengers have been requested to contact their nearest Biman Bangladesh Airlines sales centre for ticketing and other travel-related information.
