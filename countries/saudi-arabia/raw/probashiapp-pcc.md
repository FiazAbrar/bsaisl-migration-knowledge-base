## Chapter 1: Initial Concepts of Police Clearance (Police Clearance) Service and Global Migration

![পুলিশ ক্লিয়ারেন্স চালান কোড ও ফি ভেরিফিকেশন এ-চালান সোনালী ব্যাংক](/assets/images/police_03_echallan_payment_guide.jpg)

পুলিশ ক্লিয়ারেন্স চালান কোড ও ফি ভেরিফিকেশন এ-চালান সোনালী ব্যাংক

To travel to any country in the world for the international labor market, higher education, or permanent residence (PR/Immigration), verification of a citizen's past criminal record is a universal requirement. The Police Clearance service issued by Bangladesh Police is an official government certificate proving that no criminal offense, anti-state activity, or arrest warrant exists against the person concerned at any police station or court in the country. Submitting this character certificate is mandatory as a precondition for visa issuance in countries across the Middle East, Europe, North America, or East Asia.

Previously, the process of obtaining this character certificate was extremely lengthy and troublesome. Ordinary citizens had to appear in person multiple times at the local police station, and suffered from files getting stuck and unwanted mediation by brokers. However, under the Digital Bangladesh vision, Bangladesh Police has launched a self-sufficient online portal, through which one can apply from home and instantly monitor the application's progress. Especially for expatriate brothers and sisters, Probashi App has added direct government server integration, allowing the certificate's authenticity and expiry countdown to be monitored right from the mobile phone with a touch of a finger.

**Why is a Police Clearance Certificate essential in the international visa process?**

Legal validity of migration: Foreign states demand a clean police record from any arriving foreign national to ensure their geographical security and public order. Work permission and work permit: Employing companies in Malaysia, Qatar, the UAE, Oman, Kuwait, and various EU countries have made a clearance certificate mandatory alongside work permit applications. BMET manpower clearance: Police clearance is also verified for many countries when obtaining an immigration smart card from Bangladesh's Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training (BMET). Student visa and sponsorship: This must be submitted at the visa interview after university admission is confirmed in the UK, Canada, Australia, and Schengen countries.

The Police Clearance Certificate's validity generally remains effective for a maximum of 6 months (180 days) from the date of issue. Therefore, preparing this certificate at the right time, considering the visa submission timing for going abroad, is a primary duty of a wise expatriate.

## Chapter 2: The Online Police Clearance Application Portal (pcc.police.gov.bd) and Registration Method

![পুলিশ ক্লিয়ারেন্সের জন্য প্রয়োজনীয় নথিপত্র চেকলিস্ট পাসপোর্ট এনআইডি চেয়ারম্যান সার্টিফিকেট](/assets/images/police_04_document_checklist_scan.jpg)

পুলিশ ক্লিয়ারেন্সের জন্য প্রয়োজনীয় নথিপত্র চেকলিস্ট পাসপোর্ট এনআইডি চেয়ারম্যান সার্টিফিকেট

Anyone can very easily complete a police clearance certificate bangladesh online application through Bangladesh Police's official service portal, pcc.police.gov.bd. Before starting the application, the applicant must create a personal account on the portal with a valid mobile number and active email address. A secure dashboard is created for each account, from which tracking and future reference can be maintained.

**The 4 main stages of registration and application process**

Step 01: Sign up

Go to the portal and provide your name, mobile number, and password, and activate the account through OTP verification.

Step 02: Passport entry

Enter the passport number, issue date, expiry date, and the name of the issuing authority, matching the passport's information page.

Step 03: Address selection

Accurately select the district, thana, and ward from the dropdown menu for the permanent and current addresses. Selecting the wrong thana can cause the application to be rejected.

Step 04: Challan and submit

Upload a copy of the government's 500 taka challan and submit the file. The system will automatically generate a reference number (Ref ID).

The most important thing to be careful about when applying on the portal is spelling accuracy. If there is even the slightest mismatch in the spelling of your name and your father's name between your passport, National ID (NID), and online application, the Special Branch's software will show a mismatch and put the application on hold.

## Chapter 3: Government Fee, A-Challan, and MFS (bKash/Nagad) Payment Gateway

![police clearance check online bangladesh status tracker pipeline 7 stages](/assets/images/police_05_application_status_tracker.jpg)

police clearance check online bangladesh status tracker pipeline 7 stages

According to the rules set by the Ministry of Finance of the Government of Bangladesh, the official fee for the Police Clearance Certificate is exactly 500 taka (BDT 500). This fee can be deposited directly at the treasury branch of Bangladesh Bank or Sonali Bank via challan, or paid instantly from home through the government's online Automated Challan System (A-Challan) using bKash, Nagad, or Rocket.

**The specific Economic Code for the treasury challan**

When depositing money via manual bank challan, the designated economic code must be filled in with extreme care:

1-2211-0020-2611

The description field must state: "Deposit for Police Clearance Certificate fee." The original copy of the challan (the green page) must be scanned and attached online.

**The rule for paying within 5 minutes via the online A-Challan**

Enter the Ministry of Finance's automated challan website (ibas2.finance.gov.bd/acs). In the Ministry/Division field, select "Ministry of Home Affairs - Public Security Division." Selecting "Police Clearance Fee" in the service field will automatically fill in the designated code. Provide the applicant's name, NID number, and mobile number, and select MFS (bKash / Nagad / Rocket) as the payment method. Once the fee and gateway charge are paid, a 12-digit challan number and PDF copy will be generated instantly. Uploading that PDF copy directly to the police portal takes no additional time for bank verification.

## Chapter 4: Required Documents and Scanning Guidance for the Application Process

![স্পেশাল ব্রাঞ্চ এসবি ও ডিএসবি পুলিশ ক্লিয়ারেন্স ইনভেস্টিগেশন প্রসেস](/assets/images/police_06_sb_dsb_inquiry_process.jpg)

স্পেশাল ব্রাঞ্চ এসবি ও ডিএসবি পুলিশ ক্লিয়ারেন্স ইনভেস্টিগেশন প্রসেস

Around 60% of the reasons an online police clearance application is cancelled or rejected is due to uploading unclear or incorrect documents. The police administration digitally scans the authenticity of every file. Therefore, before applying, it is essential to prepare color scanned copies of all documentary evidence in the specified size and resolution.

| Document name | Approved format and size | Special requirement |
|----|----|----|
| Original passport information page | JPEG / JPG / PDF (max 300 KB) | Must be attested by a first-class gazetted officer or notary public. A minimum of 3 months' validity is recommended. |
| Chairman / Councilor certificate | JPEG / JPG / PDF (max 200 KB) | Specific certification of current and permanent address by a Union Parishad Chairman, municipal mayor, or City Corporation ward councilor. |
| National ID (NID) / birth certificate | JPEG / JPG (max 200 KB) | Clear color copies of both sides. A smart NID card is preferred for those 18 and older. |
| Recently taken passport-size photo | JPEG / JPG (max 100 KB) | A clear facial photo with white background. Photos wearing a cap or sunglasses are not acceptable. |
| Original copy of the bank or A-Challan | JPEG / JPG / PDF (max 200 KB) | The original page with bank seal and signature, or a clear digital copy of the automated A-Challan. |

**⚠️ Avoid common mistakes when taking photos with a mobile camera**

Do not take a crooked or shadowed photo with your mobile and upload it directly. Use CamScanner or a flatbed scanner to prepare a clear file, straight-cropped, at 300 DPI resolution. If the text on the document is unclear, the inquiry officer will reject the file.

## Chapter 5: Police Clearance Status Check and Real-Time Tracking Pipeline

![থানা ইনভেস্টিগেশন অফিসার পুলিশ ক্লিয়ারেন্স স্পট ভেরিফিকেশন](/assets/images/police_07_thana_officer_verification.jpg)

থানা ইনভেস্টিগেশন অফিসার পুলিশ ক্লিয়ারেন্স স্পট ভেরিফিকেশন

After submitting an application, every citizen's main curiosity is to know the current state of their file. The system for checking police clearance check online bangladesh and police clearance certificate status online is extremely transparent and timely. Which table in the police headquarters' central database the file is being processed under can be tracked at any time with the reference number and passport number.

**The 7 specific stages of the status pipeline and their real meaning**

1.  Application Submitted: Your application has been successfully received on the online portal and is awaiting initial system verification.
2.  Payment Verified: The authenticity of the 500 taka deposit has been automatically matched from the Treasury or Sonali Bank server.
3.  Inquiry Assigned: The application has been sent to the relevant local police station or Special Branch (SB/DSB), and an inquiry officer (IO) has been assigned.
4.  Field Verification in Progress: The police officer is verifying the applicant's address and criminal history. The IO may call you at this stage.
5.  Superintendent Approved: Based on the inquiry officer's satisfactory report, the District Superintendent of Police (SP) or DC Traffic has given official approval to issue the clearance.
6.  Certificate Printed: The certificate has been printed on official security paper with a QR code and digital watermark.
7.  Ready for Delivery / Delivered: The certificate is ready to be handed over at the SP office or DMP counter, or is suitable for sending to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs attestation branch.

Although updates for each step are sent via SMS and email, notifications are often delayed due to server jams or network errors. Therefore, it is safest to directly save the reference number in Probashi App's live tracker module, which brings updates every hour directly from the central API.

## Chapter 6: Special Branch (SB) and District Special Branch (DSB) Investigation Protocol

![পেন্ডিং মামলা ও জিডি থাকলে পুলিশ ক্লিয়ারেন্স পাওয়ার আইনি সমাধান ব্লুপ্রিন্ট](/assets/images/police_08_case_gd_remedy_blueprint.jpg)

পেন্ডিং মামলা ও জিডি থাকলে পুলিশ ক্লিয়ারেন্স পাওয়ার আইনি সমাধান ব্লুপ্রিন্ট

Among Bangladesh Police's intelligence branches, the Special Branch (SB) and, at the district level, the District Special Branch (DSB), carry out the most powerful and central responsibility. A Police Clearance Certificate is not just any ordinary character certificate; it is a deep national security clearance. Therefore, after your application is submitted, it is first automatically screened in the SB and DSB databases.

**What matters are examined in the SB and DSB investigation?**

CDMS (Crime Data Management System) database search: Whether there is any FIR, chargesheet, or conviction warrant against the applicant's name, father's name, and fingerprints in the Central Crime Data Management System. Anti-terrorism and anti-militancy cell: Whether there is any record of involvement with a banned militant organization, money laundering, or seditious activity. Passport duplication and forgery: Whether more than one passport has been issued in one person's name, or whether there is a prior record of any fake passport. International Red Notice (Interpol): Whether there is a match with any Interpol wanted list or international criminal network.

If there is no allegation against a candidate, the SB system sends the signal "No Adverse Record Found," as a result of which the file is immediately forwarded to the local police station for the next stage of field verification.

## Chapter 7: Field-Level Verification by the Local Police Station Inquiry Officer (IO)

![পুলিশ ক্লিয়ারেন্স সনদের আসল বনাম নকল কিউআর কোড ফরেনসিক যাচাই](/assets/images/police_09_qr_code_forensic_check.jpg)

পুলিশ ক্লিয়ারেন্স সনদের আসল বনাম নকল কিউআর কোড ফরেনসিক যাচাই

The most sensitive and practical step of online police clearance is the police station-level investigation. After the file reaches the police station with jurisdiction over the current or permanent address mentioned in the application, the officer-in-charge (OC) of the station appoints an officer of Sub-Inspector (SI) or Assistant Sub-Inspector (ASI) rank as the inquiry officer (IO).

**What you need to have with you when meeting the inquiry officer:**

The original passport and a photocopy of the passport (the officer will verify and return it). The original National ID (NID) or online birth registration certificate. The original character certificate from the local ward councilor or UP chairman. The house rent agreement or a copy of the electricity/gas bill (as proof of current address). If the applicant cannot appear personally (if living abroad), proof of authorization to a valid representative and a photocopy of the expatriate's passport.

The inquiry officer usually contacts the applicant by phone and asks them to show documents at the police station on a specified day, or sometimes visits the home and gathers character information from local notable persons or neighbors. Under Article 36 of the Constitution of Bangladesh, every citizen's freedom of movement and the freedom to leave the country are part of fundamental human rights. The honorable court has specifically observed that a citizen cannot be arbitrarily deprived of international opportunity merely because of a harassing case filed for political purposes, or a pending allegation, unless a competent court has issued a formal injunction or a travel ban on their foreign travel.

**Major legal precedents regarding Writ Petitions in the High Court Division:**

1.  Right to clearance while a case remains unresolved: The honorable court has opined that merely because of a pending General Diary (GD) or FIR under investigation, before a chargesheet is filed or charges are framed, the police department cannot indefinitely withhold a candidate's character certificate. In such cases, the police are obligated to issue the certificate while noting the actual legal status in the investigation report.
2.  Writ of Mandamus: If any official deliberately withholds a file or demands an unethical favor, the aggrieved citizen can file a writ petition in the honorable High Court under Article 102 of the Constitution. The court issues a rule to the Home Secretary and the Inspector General of Police to issue the certificate within a specific number of working days.
3.  Preventing damage to a citizen's dignity: There is a court directive that a candidate's social defamation or unreasonable intimidation in the locality must not occur in the name of investigation.

Many people confuse the police investigation at the time of passport creation with the investigation for a police clearance certificate. But legally, their purpose and scope are completely different.

**Comparative outline of the main differences:**

Passport police investigation: Its purpose is only to confirm whether the applicant is genuinely a citizen of Bangladesh and whether they or their family exist at the permanent address mentioned on the passport.
