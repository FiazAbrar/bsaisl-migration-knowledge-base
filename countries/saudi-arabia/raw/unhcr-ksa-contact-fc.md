For emergencies only, you can always contact us via SMS beyond the times specified above.

In case you couldn’t reach the office, please check this website to know if the contact time has been changed.

Please note that this asylum application form is **only** for persons residing in Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Oman and Qatar as these are within UNHCR, Riyadh’s areas of responsibility.

_**Note:**_ The UNHCR premise is in the Diplomatic Quarter (DQ). The diplomatic authorities restrict entry to the DQ for only those with an appointment to UNHCR. The UNHCR therefore encourages you to ensure having a prior appointment before approaching the Office. Due to the high volume of calls, we acknowledge that sometimes it is hard to get through to the UNHCR Office via phone. UNHCR aims to respond to all queries. We do apologize in advance for any delay in responding to your application.

We assure you that all the information you share with us will be strictly confidential and will only be seen by the concerned UNHCR staff.
