Step 1
Account Creation
To create an account for
the GovClick service for
tickets, visit the website
saudia.com, then select
"Book & Manage".
Choose "Government
Ticket (GovClick)".
Select "Sign up" if you do not
already have a membership in
the AlFursan program.




                                      You can sign up now by visiting
                                 saudia.com/en-SA/Govt/GovernmentFares
           Enter your personal
           information and agree to
           the terms and conditions,
           then choose "Join Now".
••••••••
           Note: The mobile number
           entered must match the
           number registered in Absher.
Step 2
Log in
To log in to GovClick,
visit the saudia.com website
and select "Book & Manage".
Choose the "Government
Ticket (GovClick)".
                                        Enter your AlFursan
                                        membership number and
                                        password to "Log in".




      You can log in now by visiting
saudia.com/en-SA/Govt/GovernmentFares
Upon entering the required
information, you will
receive a verification code
via SMS to your registered
mobile number.
Step 3
Account Verification
You will be redirected to the
main page, which includes
your personal information
and your AlFursan
membership number.

Then you will be asked to
authenticate your account
by completing the steps
for "Verify account".
A verification code will be
sent to your previously
registered mobile number.
Enter the 6-digit code, then
press "Verify" to proceed.
You will be redirected to the
main page of GovClick once
you have completed all the
steps to confirm your
information.

Your personal information
will be displayed, and you will
be allowed to fully utilize all
the services of GovClick.
Step 4
Adding Family
Members
Click on "Family List" from
the main page of GovClick
to view and manage your
family members.
Select "Add member" to
add a new member to your
family list.
Fill in all the required
information for adding
the new member, then
press "Add member".
To manage the family list,
click on "Manage".
You can remove a member
from the family list by
choosing "Remove" and then
"Done" to proceed.
Step 5
Cancelling a Trip
To remove or modify a
booking, please choose
"Manage".
Enter the booking reference
and family name, then
select "Continue".
The booking details will
be displayed, please
choose "Cancel trip".
Enter the verification code
sent to confirm the process,
then press "Confirm".
Select the passenger
whose booking you
wish to cancel.
Cancellation details will be
displayed along with the
refund amount.
The booking has been
cancelled and the details
have been sent to your email.
Step 6
Transaction
History
You can view the history of
account transactions from
the main page by selecting
"Transactions", sorting and
categorizing them according
to your preference.
Step 7
Manage
My Booking
Visit "Manage My Booking"
on the Saudia website.

Retrieve your ticket details
using the booking reference
number or E-ticket number
and last name.
To change your flight
booking, select "Change
Flight" and log in with your
AlFursan credentials. After
logging in, you can update
your travel destination, flight
date/time, or cabin class.

To change flights for
accompanying family
members, ensure your
GovClick account is verified,
then easily add them to your
booking.
Enter your AlFursan
membership number and
password to "Log in".
Select the flight(s) you wish to
change.
Enter your new flight(s)
details.
Choose your preferred flight.
Add the required information
and click on “Confirm”.
If your new flight has a higher
fare, conveniently pay the
difference using your credit,
debit, or MADA card.

If your new flight has a lower
fare, you will receive the
eligible refund balance as an
E-document by email.
To cancel your flight, click on
"Cancel trip" and receive the
refund amount in your wallet.
(Please create an account on
GovClick: GovClick Registration)
