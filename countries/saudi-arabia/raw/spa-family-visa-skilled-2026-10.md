Saudi Arabia Allows Skilled Workers to Process Family Visas Alongside Work Permits

Riyadh, October 06, 2026, SPA -- The Ministry of Interior announced today that in line with Saudi Arabia's commitment to enhancing the quality of life for all residents and in coordination with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development, skilled expatriate heads of households are now permitted to bring their family members to the Kingdom.

Eligible dependents include spouses, sons under 25, and unmarried daughters. They may obtain entry visas concurrently with completion of the head of household's work visa. Residency procedures will be finalized after arrival in the Kingdom, in accordance with applicable regulations and guidelines.
