# PACKAGE VISA
A service that enables visitors of eligible nationalities to obtain an electronic tourism visa by purchasing integrated travel packages through authorized travel agents. Each package combines confirmed accommodation, confirmed flight bookings, and the tourism visa in a single, seamless offering.

## What is Package Visa?
This service allows visitors of certain nationalities to obtain an instant electronic tourism visas to Saudi Arabia upon purchasing an integrated tourism package. The package must include confirmed accommodation and flight bookings from a travel agent approved by the Ministry of Tourism.
## Who is eligible for this service?
Citizens of selected countries are eligible to benefit from this service when purchasing an integrated tourism package through a travel agent approved by the Ministry of Tourism.
## What is the processing time for visa issuance?
The visa is issued electronically upon the completion of the tourism package purchase and subsequent data verification. Processing is finalized within a maximum of (48) hours from the time of purchase completion.
## What is the validity period of the visa?
The visa is valid for a duration of three (3) months from the scheduled departure date. This is a single-entry visa.
## Can the visa information be changed after issuance?
Modifications to visa data are strictly prohibited post-issuance. Applicants are urged to thoroughly verify the accuracy of all information prior to finalizing the purchase transaction.
