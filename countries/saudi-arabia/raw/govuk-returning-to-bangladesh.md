## What assistance is provided?

### Pre-departure information

Returning individuals can contact IRARA to discuss reintegration options in Bangladesh.

### Short-term assistance

- Airport reception (upon request)

- Accommodation for up to ﬁve nights

- Transportation to an address in-country

- Care and food provision packs

- Small cash assistance for immediate needs

Services will only be available within 5 days of your return to Bangladesh.

### Medium-term assistance

- Assistance with ﬁnding and reuniting with family

- Support with in-country documentation

- Signposting to local services

- Mental Health support

### Long-term assistance

Access to a Returnee Education and Entrepreneurship Fund for:

- Support with accessing the local job market

- Support with setting up a business

- Support with accessing vocational training or further education

- Assistance with legal migration opportunities

## Who can get support?

If you are a Bangladeshi national who has returned, or will be returned from the United Kingdom, IRARA can help you rebuild your life in Bangladesh.

If you want support, then you must contact BRAC within 3 months following your return. Assistance will then be available for up to 12 months.

## How to access support?

If you are currently in the UK, contact the UK IRARA ofﬁce using the details below.

If you have already returned to Bangladesh, please contact the BRAC ofﬁce in Dhaka using the details below.

## About the programme

The Home Ofﬁce Returns Reintegration Programme assists the return and reintegration of people in the United Kingdom with no legal status or right to remain.

The programme offers a wide range of services, providing assistance on arrival and longer-term support for persons returning to Bangladesh.

## About IRARA and BRAC

IRARA’s trusted partner in Bangladesh is BRAC. BRAC has been creating opportunities for people and driving social and economic transformation across Bangladesh since 1972. Together IRARA and BRAC have extensive experience in resettling citizens and providing advice and support for successful reintegration through counselling, skills training, ﬁnancial support and referrals provided by BRAC.
