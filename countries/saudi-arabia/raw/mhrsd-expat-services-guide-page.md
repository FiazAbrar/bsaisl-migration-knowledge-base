The Guide to Services for Expatriates is a comprehensive reference that provides expatriates in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia with information about their rights and the government services available to them. The guide includes instructions on how to access essential services such as healthcare, education, and housing. It also provides information on legal procedures and important phone numbers for assistance in emergency situations.
