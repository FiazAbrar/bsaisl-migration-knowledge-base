## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

## 1 Am I eligible for assistance under the PROTTASHA project?

Yes, if you are returning from or have recently returned from the European Union, you are primarily eligible for immediate and/or reintegration assistance under the project.

## 1 Upon your arrival

• When possible, IOM and BRAC representatives will welcome you home upon arrival at the airport and provide immediate assistance
• BRAC will arrange temporary accommodation and meal for you if needed
