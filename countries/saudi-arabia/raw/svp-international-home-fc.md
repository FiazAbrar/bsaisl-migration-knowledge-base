# Skills Verification Program

The Skills Verification Program aims at verifying that professional workers possess the qualifications and skills required to perform their jobs in Saudi Arabia, which contributes to raising the quality of skill level in the labor market and raising productivity.

To get a Skill verification program certification and book assessment please visit [NAVTTC - Skills Verification Program](https://svp.navttc.gov.pk/home-page)
