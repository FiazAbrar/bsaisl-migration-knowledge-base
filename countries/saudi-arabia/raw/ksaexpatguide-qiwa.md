# Qiwa Login Guide for Employees: Create Your Individual Account and Check Your Contract Before You Accept It

Qiwa login for employees starts with your iqama number. You register once at [sso.qiwa.sa](https://sso.qiwa.sa/en/registration), confirm your identity through the Nafath app, then sign in with a password or with Nafath. The real reason to do it is your contract: your employer sends it to your Qiwa individual account, and what you approve there becomes the documented record of your salary and terms.

Most guides treat Qiwa as an employer tool and stop at "how to log in". We start from the other side. As an employee, the most important button you will ever press on Qiwa is the one that approves your contract. This page explains what that approval means for your rights, what to check first, and then how to register, log in and find your contract later.

## Why your Qiwa contract matters more than the paper you signed

Qiwa (قوى) is the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (HRSD) platform for labor services. Its [Contract Management service](https://qiwa.sa/en/service-overview/business-owners/hire-employees/contract-management) lets an employer create a contract and send it to you. You can approve it, reject it or ask for changes from your individual account. HRSD says that once both parties agree, the contract is considered documented and approved by the Ministry.

Three things make that documented contract powerful for you.

### Your wage clause can be enforced without a lawsuit

In October 2025, HRSD and the Ministry of Justice launched an initiative that turns the wage clause of a documented Qiwa contract into an enforceable instrument (سند تنفيذي). The clause covers basic salary, housing allowance, transport allowance and other cash allowances, where these apply. Under the [official guideline](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2025-10/aldlyl-ala-rshady-lmbadrt--qd-al-ml-almwthq-sndana-tnfydhyana---en.pdf), if your employer has not paid your full wage 30 days after the due date, or has paid only part of it 90 days after the due date, you can file an enforcement request on the [Najiz platform](https://najiz.sa/). The employer then has five days to object or pay. Your wage history is checked electronically against the Wage Protection data on the Mudad platform.

The guideline says this applies in phases: first to new contracts and updated contracts, then to fixed-term contracts as they are renewed or extended, then to indefinite contracts. It also needs the contract to be on the executive contract template with an execution number from the Ministry of Justice, which the two systems generate automatically.

### The Labor Law backs the written contract

Under the [amended Labor Law](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/BoeLaws/Laws/LawDetails/08381293-6388-48e2-8ad2-a9a700f2aa94/1), in force since 19 February 2025, Article 51 says the contract must be written in two copies, one for each party, and documented according to the regulations. Article 52 says the Ministry's unified contract model must include, at a minimum, your name, nationality and identity details, your salary with allowances and benefits, the type and place of work, the start date and the duration for a fixed-term contract. Article 9 makes Arabic mandatory for contracts and says the Arabic text prevails if another language is used alongside it. Article 8 says any condition that goes against the law is void.

### Employers are under pressure to document

HRSD measures each establishment by the share of its contracts documented on Qiwa. In April 2026 it raised the target to 85% from 30 April and 90% by 30 June 2026, and warned that falling short can affect access to services linked to compliance. This is one reason an employee may suddenly receive a contract request for a job they have held for some time.

Approving a contract with a lower salary than you were promised is the one mistake that is hard to undo. The documented wage is the figure that enforcement and Wage Protection checks rely on. If the numbers are wrong, request a change or reject it, and keep your offer letter.

## What to check before you accept a contract on Qiwa

Open the request and read every field. Use this table as a checklist.

| Field | What to check | Why it matters |
|----|----|----|
| Basic salary | Matches your offer letter, to the riyal | Part of the wage clause that can be enforced |
| Housing and transport allowances | Listed separately if your offer included them | Also part of the enforceable wage clause |
| Job title | Matches the work you actually do | Article 52 requires the type of work to be stated |
| Contract type and duration | Fixed-term or indefinite, with correct dates | Decides notice rules and how the enforceable model is phased in |
| Start date | Your real first working day | Your service length and [end-of-service award](/tools/end-of-service-calculator/) are counted from it |
| Working hours | Daily and weekly hours as agreed | Gives you a written record if you are later pushed to work extra hours |
| Probation | Stated, and within the legal maximum | Qiwa says even probation contracts must be documented before you start |

If hours are a sticking point, our [overtime calculator](/tools/overtime-calculator/) shows what extra hours should pay. For probation limits, see our guide to the [probation period in Saudi Arabia](/probation-period-saudi-arabia/).

### How long do I have to respond?

Qiwa says the request is cancelled automatically if there is no response within 10 days of the date it was created. The employer can send it again, but do not let it lapse by accident. If something is wrong, use the option to request changes or reject it, and tell your employer why.

### Can my employer change the contract after I approve it?

Only with your approval. Qiwa says the employer can edit an approved contract and send the update to you to approve or reject. If you reject it, the last approved contract stays valid.

## What people get wrong about Qiwa contracts

**"The contract I signed in my home country is the one that counts."** That paper is useful evidence of what you were offered, so keep it. In Saudi Arabia, though, the documented Qiwa contract is the one the systems use, and the one whose wage clause can be enforced through Najiz.

**"I am registered with GOSI, so my contract is documented."** Qiwa says the opposite flow is true: contracts are documented on Qiwa and then reflected automatically in GOSI, not the other way round. Check Qiwa itself.

**"If I press reject, I lose my job."** For an update to an existing contract, Qiwa says rejection simply keeps your last approved contract in force. Rejecting is a normal part of the process. It tells your employer the terms need fixing.

## How to register a Qiwa individual account

You need your iqama number, your date of birth as recorded on it, and the Nafath app set up on your phone. If Nafath is not ready yet, our [Nafath app guide](/nafath-app-guide/) covers it.

1.  Open [sso.qiwa.sa](https://sso.qiwa.sa/en/registration) and choose Register (on the login page it reads "Don't have a Qiwa account? Register now").
2.  Enter your iqama or national ID number.
3.  Enter your date of birth. Qiwa shows the calendar type, Hijri or Gregorian, once your ID number is entered.
4.  Press Next and approve the request in the Nafath app. Qiwa says you are authenticated through Nafath using your mobile phone number.
5.  Complete the second registration step on screen to finish creating your account.

## Qiwa login, password reset and finding your contract later

### How to log in

The login page accepts your national ID, iqama number or email address, plus your password. The alternative is the Log in with Nafath button, which skips the password and sends an approval request to your Nafath app.

### Forgot your password?

Click I forgot my password, enter your identity number and press Send verification link. If you have lost access to your registered contact details, the reset page points you to Log in with Nafath instead.

### Check your current contract

1.  Log in to your Qiwa individual account.
2.  Go to Services and select Employment Contracts.
3.  Scroll to Your current contracts.
4.  Click Preview Contract to read the full contract.

The same section handles termination. Qiwa asks you to pick a termination reason, accept the terms and confirm with a 4-digit code sent to your phone. Choose the reason carefully, because some reasons end the contract at once and others require the notice period. Our guide to [resignation rules in Saudi Arabia](/resignation-rules-saudi-arabia/) explains the choices.

KSA Expat Guide is not part of Qiwa or HRSD and cannot see or change contracts. Use qiwa.sa only. Qiwa support: 920000105 or support@qiwa.sa. If your employer stops paying, see our guide on what to do when an [employer is not paying salary](/employer-not-paying-salary/).

## Frequently asked questions

How do I log in to Qiwa as an employee?

Go to the Qiwa login page at sso.qiwa.sa and enter your iqama number, national ID or email address with your password. You can also press Log in with Nafath and approve the request in the Nafath app instead of using a password. If you do not have an account yet, choose Register, enter your iqama number and date of birth, and confirm your identity through Nafath. Qiwa support is on 920000105.

I forgot my Qiwa password. How do I reset it?

On the Qiwa login page, click I forgot my password. Enter your iqama or national ID number and Qiwa sends a verification link to your registered contact details. If you no longer have access to the phone or email on your account, the reset page offers Log in with Nafath instead, so you can get in by approving a request in the Nafath app and then update your details.

How long do I have to accept a contract on Qiwa?

Qiwa says a contract request runs from the moment the employer creates it until the employee approves or rejects it, and that the request is cancelled automatically if there is no response within 10 days of its creation. So check your individual account soon after your employer tells you the contract has been sent. If the request expires, the employer has to send it again.

Where can I check my employment contract on Qiwa?

Log in to your Qiwa individual account, go to Services and select Employment Contracts, then scroll to Your current contracts. Find your contract and click Preview Contract to see the full text, including your employer details. The same section is where you start a termination request and view contracts that ended earlier, so it is worth knowing well before you ever need it.

What happens if I reject a contract update from my employer?

Qiwa says that if an employee rejects an update request, the last approved and authenticated contract stays valid. It remains in force until an update is accepted or one of the parties ends the contract. So rejecting a change you did not agree to does not leave you without a contract. Talk to your employer first, and use the labor office if the disagreement continues.

Is a Qiwa contract the same as GOSI registration?

No. Qiwa explains that contracts are documented on Qiwa first and then reflected automatically in the systems of GOSI, the social insurance body, and not the other way round. Being registered with GOSI does not by itself mean your contract has been documented on Qiwa. Check the Employment Contracts section of your Qiwa account to see whether a documented contract exists.
