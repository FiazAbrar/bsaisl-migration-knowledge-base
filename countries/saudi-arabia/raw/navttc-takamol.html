NAVTTC Takamol Skill Verification for the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia

Takamol provides skills assessment (verification) services only - it is not a training or job provider. Successful candidates receive an internationally recognised certification required for employment in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Why Takamol

Choosing Takamol

The purpose of Takamol is to obtain an internationally recognised certification, valid for employment in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

International Certification

A globally recognised skills certification, required for employment in Saudi Arabia.

Skills Assessment Only

Takamol verifies your existing competency. It does not provide training or jobs.

Employment in KSA

Certified candidates are eligible for employment in KSA, subject to applicable requirements.

Nationwide Test Centers

Take your assessment at an approved Test Center in your city across Pakistan.

How to Apply

Step-by-Step Guide

Candidates register directly on the Takamol SVP portal and pay an assessment fee of USD 85 per test (equivalent to PKR 23,500-24,500, subject to prevailing exchange rates).

  1. 01 Step 1

    Create Your Profile

    Register on the Takamol SVP portal using your valid personal and passport information.

  2. 02 Step 2

    Select Trade & Date

    Choose your preferred assessment city, occupation/trade and an available assessment date.

  3. 03 Step 3

    Pay USD 85 Fee

    Pay the prescribed Takamol assessment fee of USD 85 per test (equivalent to PKR 23,500-24,500, subject to prevailing exchange rates) through the online payment system.

  4. 04 Step 4

    Get Appointment Slip

    Download the appointment / roll-number slip generated by the system.

  5. 05 Step 5

    Auto Center Allocation

    The system automatically allocates you to an approved Test Center based on your city, trade, date and capacity.

  6. 06 Step 6

    Take the Assessment

    Report to the assigned Test Center on the scheduled date with your original documents and complete the assessment.

  7. 07 Step 7

    Receive Certificate

    On success, your result/certificate is uploaded to your SVP portal within 24 hours. If unsuccessful, book a new appointment.

Register on the SVP Portal
Requirements

Required Documents

Only two documents are required for a Takamol test. No additional supporting documents are needed.

Valid Pakistani Passport

Required for registration and identity verification at the Test Center.

CNIC

Computerised National Identity Card.

That is all - no additional supporting documents are needed.

Good to Know

Skill Verification is mandatory only for candidates going to KSA in occupations notified by Saudi Takamol.
The assessment fee is USD 85 per test (equivalent to PKR 23,500-24,500, subject to prevailing exchange rates), effective from 09 September 2026, paid online through the SVP portal.
If unsuccessful, you may re-book a new appointment via the SVP portal.

NAVTTC SVP

The Role of NAVTTC SVP

NAVTTC is the implementing partner in Pakistan. It onboards institutes as Takamol Test Centers and facilitates the implementation of Takamol guidelines through approved Test Centers.

Test Center Onboarding

Onboards and approves institutes as Takamol Test Centers across Pakistan.

Technical Support

Provides technical support to Test Centers for smooth assessment operations.

Complaint Resolution

Handles complaints and maintains high standards across Test Centers.

Capacity Building

Trains Test Center staff - Assessors, Internal Validators and Focal Persons.

What NAVTTC SVP does NOT provide:

Training, curriculum, increased enrolment or financial support. Curriculum, assessment methodology, test patterns and compliance mechanisms are established and managed by Takamol. Candidates register directly through the SVP portal and pay the USD 85 assessment fee per test.

Onboarding

How to Become an SVP Test Center

A structured, compliance-based onboarding process approved by NAVTTC and Saudi Takamol.

  1. 1
    01 Step 1

    EOI Announcement & Application

    NAVTTC publishes an Expression of Interest. Institutions email their registration interest with the NAVTTC registration certificate and a 5-minute video of the full center facility.

  2. 2
    02 Step 2

    Initial Screening & Categorization

    Applications are reviewed for completeness and eligibility, then categorized by trade requirements and geographical priorities from Saudi Takamol.

  3. 3
    03 Step 3

    KAM Assignment

    A dedicated Key Account Manager (KAM) is assigned to guide the institution and act as the primary point of contact throughout onboarding.

  4. 4
    04 Step 4

    Registration with NAVTTC

    Institutions not already registered with NAVTTC complete the prescribed registration before proceeding.

  5. 5
    05 Step 5

    Physical Inspection & Compliance

    A physical inspection verifies infrastructure, equipment, human resources and practical labs against the prescribed tool lists and trade-specific standards.

  6. 6
    06 Step 6

    Documentation & Internal Review

    Required documents, photos and compliance evidence are collected, then reviewed internally; any deficiencies are communicated for rectification.

  7. 7
    07 Step 7

    NAVTTC Approval

    Compliant institutions are approved by the Selection Committee and the Executive Director, NAVTTC.

  8. 8
    08 Step 8

    Saudi Takamol Approval

    The complete profile is forwarded to Saudi Takamol for final technical evaluation and approval.

  9. 9
    09 Step 9

    Onboarding & Orientation

    The center is officially onboarded for the approved trades, and the KAM conducts an orientation before testing begins.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Skill Verification Program (SVP)?
The SVP is a joint initiative of Saudi Takamol and NAVTTC to assess and verify the competency of skilled workers seeking employment in Saudi Arabia, ensuring candidates meet the occupational standards required by the Saudi labour market.
Who is required to undergo Skill Verification?
Candidates intending to work in Saudi Arabia in occupations notified by Saudi Takamol as mandatory must successfully complete the Skill Verification before travelling for employment.
Is Takamol a training or job provider?
No. Takamol provides skills assessment services only. It is not a training provider or a job provider. Successful candidates become eligible for employment in Saudi Arabia, subject to the applicable employment requirements and procedures.
What documents does a candidate need?
Only a valid Pakistani Passport and CNIC are required for a Takamol test. No additional supporting documents are required.
What is the assessment fee?
Candidates register directly through the SVP portal and pay the prescribed Takamol assessment fee of USD 85 per test, equivalent to PKR 23,500-24,500 (subject to prevailing exchange rates), effective from 09 September 2026.
What is the role of NAVTTC?
NAVTTC is the implementing partner in Pakistan. It onboards institutes as Takamol Test Centers and facilitates implementation of Takamol guidelines. Curriculum, assessment methodology, test patterns and compliance mechanisms are established and managed by Takamol.
Who can become an SVP Test Center?
Any institution registered with NAVTTC as a Training Institute or Trade Testing Center that meets the prescribed infrastructure, human-resource and compliance requirements may apply. There is no separate SVP application fee.
How does an institution apply to become a Test Center?
Submit an Expression of Interest (EOI) with a valid NAVTTC registration certificate and a 5-minute video of the complete facility through the prescribed NAVTTC process, announced via the NAVTTC website and other channels.
