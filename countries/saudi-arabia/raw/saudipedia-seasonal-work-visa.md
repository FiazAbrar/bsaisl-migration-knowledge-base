Yes, the seasonal work visa, issued specifically for the [Hajj](https://saudipedia.com/en/article/1253/religion/hajj-and-umrah/hajj) season, may be used for that purpose. An entry fee of SAR1,000 is required. The stay duration for this visa is four months, non-renewable and non-convertible into a permanent work visa. Holders of seasonal work visas may enter the [Kingdom of Saudi Arabia](https://saudipedia.com/en/article/2403/geography/provinces-and-cities/the-kingdom-of-saudi-arabia) starting from the first day of Shawwal, but not before. The last permissible day for entry is the final day of Dhu al-Qidah. The duration of stay must not exceed the end of Muharram. The visa applicant is required to submit a financial guarantee of SAR2,000 for each worker to the [Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development](https://saudipedia.com/en/article/269/government-and-politics/ministries/ministry-of-human-resources-and-social-development). This amount is refundable once the ministry verifies that the worker has left the country, or the visa has been canceled.
