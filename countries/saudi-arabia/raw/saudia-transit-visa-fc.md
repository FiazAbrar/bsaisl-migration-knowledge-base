## How to Book a Stopover with Saudia

Saudia invites you to experience the culture, heritage, and warm hospitality of Saudi Arabia with the new Stopover Transit eVisa, offered in collaboration with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

The visa is valid for 90 days and allows travelers to stay in the Kingdom for up to 4 days (96 hours) during their stopover journey.

Apply for the Stopover Transit Visa Apply for your stopover transit visa directly from the Seats & Extras page during the booking process.

Complete your paymentFinalize your booking and visa payment to instantly receive your issued Visa and travel insurance via email for your stay in Saudi Arabia

#### Key Information:

- The residence period is up to 96 hours, and the visa is valid for 90 days from the date of issuance. (Validity and conditions may change according to MOFA regulations.)
- The stopover transit visa is not extendable.
- It allows one stopover trip per booking only.
- Visa fees, service charges, medical fees, and insurance costs are calculated according to MOFA regulations.
- Issuance of the visa does not guarantee entry into the Kingdom, as final approval is subject to verification by passport control officers upon arrival in Saudi Arabia.

Stopover Visa conditions

- Hold a valid passport with no restrictions or conditions, and with at least six months’ validity remaining (subject to applicable bilateral agreements).
- Possess a confirmed onward travel ticket to the final destination.
- Have a valid entry visa for the destination country, or a residence permit if required (unless exempt from visa requirements).

How to Apply for a Stopover Visa (If Your Country Is Not Eligible)?

1. 1\. Select your international departure and arrival destinations (not point-to-point travel only).
2. 2\. Choose a flight with a suitable connection time, up to 96 hours maximum.
3. 3\. If you do not already have a valid entry document for Saudi Arabia, apply for the stopover transit visa via the Additional Services page on the Saudia website.
4. 4\. Prepare your personal photo in advance (200 × 200 px, max 20 KB) before completing the application.
5. 5\. Pay the flight and visa fees together in a single transaction on the Saudia website.
6. 6\. Once completed, you will receive your visa and insurance details by email for your stay in Saudi Arabia.
