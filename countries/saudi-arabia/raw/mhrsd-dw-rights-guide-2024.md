## Guide to the rights and obligations of domestic workers in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia
# General executive supervision of domestic workers

#### 1. Preamble
The Regulations for Domestic Workers and the Like stipulate the principle of consent based on science and knowledge. It guarantees the awareness and knowledge of the domestic employer and the domestic worker of the terms of the contract, the nature of the work, the place of work, the wage, and the daily and weekly rest period as specified by the regulation. This is required to be done before the employee's recruitment and departure from his home country. The Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development has identified 14 professions that fall within the scope of domestic workers.

#### 3. What professions are available for domestic workers?
14 professions fall within the scope of domestic workers at present, as follows:
o Domestic worker
o Private driver
o Nanny
o House nurse
o House cook
o House tailor
o House waiter
o Butler
o House manager
o Housekeeper
o Personal assistant
o House farmer
o Physiotherapist
o Specialist. private speech and hearing therapist

#### 4. Digital services for domestic workers
The Kingdom of Saudi Arabia provides specialized services for domestic workers. These services include Musaned website, which is an integrated electronic platform that provides individual employers in Saudi Arabia with domestic workers recruitment by agencies and their costs, electronic documentation between local recruitment offices and foreign offices of countries sending domestic workers, as well as electronic contracting between the recruitment office and employers. The Kingdom has also provided a service for filing complaints and settling domestic worker disputes electronically; you can find more digital services on the website of the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development.

#### 7. Rights and obligations of the domestic worker
According to the laws of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, the following shall be prohibited:
o Recruiting or employing a worker under the age of 21 years.
o Assigning the domestic worker to work other than what was agreed upon in the contract, or working for someone else.
o Sexually harassing the worker, whether the harassment is verbal or physical.
o Compulsory work or any work practiced within the framework of trafficking in persons.

According to what is stipulated in the Regulations of Domestic Workers and the like, the domestic worker is entitled to the following:
o The wage shall be as stipulated in the unified contract signed by the employer and the worker.
o One-day weekend as agreed upon by both parties in the contract.
o Continuous daily rest shall not be less than eight hours.
o One month's paid leave if he spends two years and wishes to renew it for a similar period.
o The cost of a travel ticket at the employer's expense shall be once every two years.
o An end-of-service bonus worth one month's wages, if he has served the employer for four consecutive years.
o Sick leave of 30 days per year based on a medical report proving the need for leave.
o Maintaining the worker's identification documents such as passports, IDs, etc.

The worker shall: Not to refuse work or leave service without an acceptable excuse. Not to work for himself.

#### 8. Obligations of the Domestic Employer
o Enabling the worker to communicate with his relatives.
o Completing the medical examination procedures after the worker arrives in the Kingdom, issuing and renewing the residency and paying the fees.
o Completing the requirements for issuing a driving license for the driver's profession, issuing and renewing the license, and paying the fees.
o Medical treatment or health insurance for the domestic worker in accordance with the regulations and instructions approved in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
o Finish body shipment procedures upon death of domestic worker.

#### 9. Obligations of recruitment offices
o Informing the worker of the type and nature of the work and the amount of pay.
o Conducting the necessary medical examinations for the worker within a period not exceeding (30) days before he entered the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
o Receiving and housing the workers whose recruitment is mediated until they are delivered to the employers and provide them with subsistence.
o Providing appropriate housing and support for the domestic workers whose recruitment is mediated in the event of their absence or refusal to work for the employer.
o Educating the worker about the competent authorities to consider his complaints regarding any violation of his rights and freedoms.
o The office shall not, by itself or a third party, request or accept from any domestic worker, whether before or after starting work, any commission in exchange for obtaining the work or collect any amounts from him in exchange for recruitment.

#### 10. Resolving disputes and complaints
In the event of any dispute between the domestic worker or the employer, either of them shall have the option of filing a complaint with the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development. The Ministry may settle the case between the two parties amicably within five working days, starting from the date of submitting the complaint to the Ministry, if the amicable settlement is not possible, the competent committee shall consider the matter and may decide on it in the same session, or a second session within ten working days.

#### 11. Domestic worker penalties
1. A fine not exceeding two thousand riyals, or banning him from working in the Kingdom permanently, or both.
The violating domestic worker bears the cost of returning to his country, if he does not have financial benefits that meet his fines, he is deported to his country at the Kingdom's expense.
