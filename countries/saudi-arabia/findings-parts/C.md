# Findings, part C: company worker: rights, stay, jail, unpaid, change employer, huroob, leaving, leave, family, scams

Researched 7 Nov 2026. Draft: drafts/SA3.md. Ledger rows C0001-C0153 (ledger-parts/C.csv).

## Sources and dates
- Main labour-law source is now MHRSD's "Awareness Guide on the Rights and Duties of Employers and Workers", 1st ed. 2025 (PDF modified 29 Dec 2025), which reflects the Feb 2025 Labour Law amendments. It replaces the Indian Embassy pages for hours, leave, probation, end of service, fees and passport rules.
- Indian Embassy "Saudi labour law: basic points" is partly outdated: probation "90 days, extendable to 180" (now a 180-day cap), maternity "10 weeks" (now 12), sick leave and leave entitlements otherwise match. Its footer "last updated November 05, 2026" is site-wide, so pages logged as undated / possibly-outdated. Still used for: 12-hour daily cap, notice 30/60 days, food allowance, ticket in probation, age 18-60, prohibited items, medicines, parcels.
- Labour Law English text on hrsd.gov.sa is the Feb 2023 upload (pre-amendment). Used only for Art. 88 (pay within 1 week / 2 weeks) and Art. 81.
- Job mobility: Ministerial Decree 51848 (5 Nov 2020, in force 14 Mar 2021) is the official text for "12 months from entry + 90 days' notice" and "after contract expiry". The exceptions list (3 months unpaid, permit/iqama expired, employer absent, commercial cover-up, consent) is from the World Bank 2026 report box 4.1 (citing MHRSD); no official page with the exceptions list was found (MHRSD service page hides the Terms tab behind JavaScript). MHRSD service page "Transfer of services from another employer (Freedom of movement)" dated 8 Sep 2026 gives the steps and "Free".
- Huroob: MHRSD "Procedural Guide for ending the contractual relationship" (Oct 2022) is the only official text: absence report only for workers WITHOUT a Qiwa-documented contract; 30 days/year or 15 consecutive; permit valid 60+ days; report is "non-retractable"; 60 days to final exit or transfer (transfer only if 12+ months in KSA). Gulf News 31 Jul 2025 (citing SPA) on the 2025 Qiwa rule: report only if iqama valid 60+ days and no active contract; 60 days after "disconnected from work"; then automatic "absent". Ajel 4 Aug 2026 and Gulf News / KPMG 30 Sep-Oct 2026 (Qiwa clarification): transfer still possible after the grace period if 12+ months in KSA; new employer pays overdue permit fees. No SPA original fetched.
- Wage insurance: MHRSD guideline (Oct 2024). In force from 6 Oct 2024; policy renewed yearly. Saudi government told the ILO (GB.356, Mar 2026) the scheme still operates.

## Conflicts
- Friendly settlement fee: MHRSD service page "Free Service" (C0036) vs MHRSD Directory of Services Provided to Expatriates 2024 "100 SR" (C0037). Shown in doc as one Need to know. Gulf Visa Hub (Aug 2026) also says free.
- 19911 languages: MHRSD news (2024) says Arabic and English; friendly-settlement service page says Arabic, English, Urdu. Commoner Law (guide site) claims Bengali, Hindi, Malayalam, unsupported. Old draft's India-embassy claim that 19911 takes Bengali was not re-found. Doc states Arabic and English, and points to the embassy line for Bangla.
- Old draft "ask the employer to withdraw huroob within 15-20 days" (from Gulf News / Migrant-Rights 2022 and KSA Expats "cancel within 15 days"): the official 2022 guide says the absence report is non-retractable. Dropped.
- Old draft "employer has 14 days to respond, accept offer within 10 days, transfer fee" (Wego 2024): not in any official source; dropped. Wego also gives employer-side transfer fees SR 2,000 / 4,000 / 6,000; not needed by workers (employer pays, official).
- Old draft "retirement 60 men, 55 women": the Feb 2025 guide gives no ages and the new Social Insurance Law changed retirement ages for Saudis; expats are not in the pension branch (GOSI). Dropped.
- Old draft "File within 12 months; it is free [Clyde & Co]": now cited to MHRSD service page.
- Old draft "probation up to 90 days": now 180 (official 2025).
- Old draft "wage insurance if not paid for over 6 months": also requires 80%+ of the workforce unpaid; added.

## Reality check (what happens to workers)
- HRW, Sep 2025 (Sendan International, Aramco sites): hundreds of workers incl. Bangladeshis unpaid up to 8 months; 8 of 11 followed up didn't claim wage insurance for fear of losing jobs or deportation; 3 claimants still waiting; one Bangladeshi had paid Tk 4.2 lakh recruitment fees and "quietly left", working irregularly. HRW: victims rarely compensated, sometimes years later; 6-month/80% rule excludes most cases; cap SR 17,500 and no end-of-service.
- HRW "Die First" (Dec 2024): employers retaliate against job changes by issuing exit visas or false theft/debt charges; workers lack know-how for Qiwa; not notified of absconding reports when phone not linked; challenging false huroob costly; workers fear complaining to embassies; embassies overstretched; passport confiscation still common; migrant leaders advise never signing blank papers or sharing Absher/Qiwa OTPs.
- World Bank (2026): job switching by low-skilled expats rose 19% after LRI; 65% of switchers change sector; employers interviewed say labourers go to industrial areas and apply at every factory; original employers try to block transfers by issuing final exit; many destination employers still seek the original employer's approval.
- Migrant-Rights (2022): false huroob used against workers; burden of proof on worker; bribes demanded to withdraw.
- Embassy labour wing: Probashir Diganta (Sep 2022) reports the Riyadh labour wing recovered SR 68,017 (six years' wages) for a domestic worker. No public data on how many wage cases the wing handles or wins.
- BMET arbitration (MFA, undated): a Saudi domestic worker's unpaid-wage claim settled for under half after agency threats (relevant to the Bangladesh-side complaint route in another part).
- MHRSD told HRW it took "corrective action" on Sendan via the Wage Protection System; ILO GB.356 Saudi submission claims 88% WPS compliance and 77% friendly settlement resolution (statistics kept out of the doc).
- Weddi user manual (2024): sessions are remote by Webex and there is "no interpretation service for non-Arabic speakers" - a real barrier; doc tells workers to bring an Arabic speaker or the labour wing.

## Outside bodies
- ILO: Article 26 complaints only by member States, Conference delegates or the Governing Body; individuals can't file. A 2025 complaint by Workers' delegates (BWI-backed) against Saudi Arabia on forced labour, wage protection and discrimination is before the Governing Body (GB.356, Mar 2026). Writing to the ILO about one's own case does not open a case.
- IOM: not researched in this part; no evidence found that IOM takes individual wage cases in Saudi Arabia.
- OKUP (Bangladesh NGO): free legal advice and case support for migrants and families, 01842773300.
- BRAC Migration Programme legal help is covered in "Coming home" (other part).

## Fix round after independent check (7 Nov 2026), ledger rows C0154-C0181
- Complaint routes: MHRSD Beneficiary Page (client page, selfservice.hrsd.gov.sa): HRSD or Absher account, Nafath login, Complaints > Add a Request > sector/category/subcategory; free, 5 days (user guide Nov 2024). MHRSD "Reporting Violations of Labor Regulations": MHRSD app or Tawakkalna, Absher registration, workplace location + company data + copy of violation, free, 3 days, status in app. Used as the route for hours/overtime/leave/heat-ban breaches.
- CHI: complaint against employer for no insurance or deducted premium at care.chi.gov.sa (guest or account), 15 working days, confidential. Live page and Firecrawl failed (proxy error; stealth retry denied by the permission classifier, not pursued); Wayback copy May 2025 used. The CHI insurance-check form's input is still unknown (Need to know).
- Weddi manual: portal login is "user name and password"; how a foreign worker first creates it (Nafath/Absher) not stated anywhere official found -> Need to know. Claimant no-show: manual says "dismissed", expat guide says "saved"; both say reopen (guide: within 21 working days). Doc says dismissed, reopen within 21 working days from My Claims.
- Settlement outcome: expat guide 2024: settlement report printed from "My Lawsuits". No official text found saying the settlement report is an executive instrument enforceable on Najiz -> Need to know.
- Embassy labour wing: contact page gives 0114195300 ext. 401-403, direct 011-4550406/011-4550479, emails counselorriyadh@probashi.gov.bd and labourwing.riyadh@yahoo.com (decoded from Cloudflare-obfuscated HTML). No hours or response time found. Weddi has an "embassy attorney" filing option, but nothing shows the Bangladesh labour wing uses it -> Need to know.
- Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare charter p.9 re-checked on a 200 dpi page image: Welfare-1 officer 02-41030251, saswelfare@probashi.gov.bd; reporting officer (Joint Secretary, Mission and Welfare wing) 02-41030219, 01711427769, adsmission@probashi.gov.bd (C0180).
- Wage insurance: insurer is Al-Etihad Cooperative Insurance; labor.aletihad.sa has a Worker Portal (Submit Claim) asking iqama/border number, mobile, whether the worker has left KSA and whether the mobile is on Absher; phone 800 3040 110. The insurer's user manual PDF is image-only (no text). Payout time not found -> Need to know.
- Absher card "Issuance of Exit/Re-Entry or Final Exit Visa" (updated 29 Oct 2026, Firecrawl): conditions (in KSA, valid iqama, passport 60 days final exit / 90 days re-entry, traffic fines paid, fingerprint, no vehicle for final exit, Return Before date, iqama >= 90 days + trip). The card describes issuing for family/domestic workers; the worker's own menu path, final-exit fee and final-exit validity are not on any official page found -> Need to know. Exit/re-entry fee SR 200 from MHRSD expat guide 2024 (official); extra-month and multiple-trip fees only from Gulf Visa Hub (C0169). Three-year non-return ban lifted 16 Jan 2024 (Deloitte, C0170). World Bank: worker declares on Absher understanding of measures for not returning before the employer-approved leave ends (reconciles "notified, not asked" with the employer setting leave dates).
- Job change: World Bank lists a valid attested Qiwa-documented contract as a main-route condition (C0179). No official page lists the exceptions (MHRSD transfer page has only steps; "Immediately", free). A Baker McKenzie alert (gcn-lri-conditions-2020, fetched) lists the same exceptions plus "no documented contract within 3 months of entry"; its table did not convert, so not used.
- Skill grade: shown on the establishment's Qiwa account; worker can request correction (MHRSD guideline 2025). Whether the worker's own Qiwa shows it: not found.
- Huroob: no official or reliable source found that friendly settlement or the labour court can cancel a false absence report; guide sites only assert it. Kept as Need to know; route given is the embassy form (huroob / court-case types).
