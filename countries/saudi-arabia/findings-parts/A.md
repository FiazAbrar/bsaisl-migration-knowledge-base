## Conflicts and changes from the old draft

- **Skill test fee is now USD 85, not USD 50.** TBS (24 Sep 2026, English and Bangla) reports the SVP fee rose from USD 50 to USD 85 in early September 2026 (A0007); Pakistan's NAVTTC official page says USD 85 per test effective 9 Sep 2026 (A0008). BMET's own documents still say USD 50: SVP FAQ Jan 2026 (A0065, status conflict), warning notice Oct 2025, poster Dec 2025 (USD 50, about Tk 6,200). No BMET notice of the new fee found on bmet.gov.bd (Takamol category has 6 entries, newest 15 Dec 2025). Doc uses USD 85. Old draft's "about Tk 6,150" dropped; Tk equivalent of USD 85 left out (only a commercial guide gives Tk 10,440).
- **Welfare fee Tk 5,500** confirmed by the ministry circular of 20 Sep 2026 (scanned image, read visually): raised from Tk 3,500 (fixed 2015) to Tk 5,500, effective 1 Oct 2026 (A0011).
- **SVP portal domain.** svp-international.pacc.sa now redirects to svp-international.qiwa.sa (Firecrawl scrape, saved as raw/svp-international-home-fc.md). From a non-Bangladesh location the page shows Pakistan/NAVTTC contacts. Doc keeps the pacc.sa address that BMET's FAQ and posters give, since it redirects.
- **Saudi CR number replaced.** Under the Commercial Register Law effective 3 April 2026, companies are identified by a unified national number starting with 7 (A0101, from a third-party registry guide; status lead). The old verification page v.mc.gov.sa no longer resolves (DNS failure). The Ministry of Commerce now offers "Query about commercial registration data" (search by name or unified national number, free, Arabic, help line 1900) (A0098-A0100). The embassy's e-demand manual still asks employers for a "10 digit CR number" (A0102); kept as written there.
- **Embassy attestation for single visas.** TBS 17 Jun 2025 (A0087): group visas always needed attestation; it was reinstated for single visas, and the embassy introduced online attestation. TBS Mar 2025: BAIRA protests; relaxed for demand letters issued up to 28 Feb 2025. Dhaka Post 5 Feb 2025 (Firecrawl scrape, not saved as evidence): BAIRA demanded clearance without attestation for up to 24 visas. Bangla Tribune May 2023: ministry had temporarily suspended the attestation requirement for group visas; rule 6 (1)-(2) of the 2017 Overseas Employment and Migrants Rules lets BMET process demand letters only with mission attestation or ministry permission. No 2026 source found that changes the June 2025 position; doc states that single visas need attestation, cited to TBS.
- **Relative's visa and the agency.** Migrant-Rights (Feb 2024) quotes the Cumilla DEMO assistant director: "even if you receive a visa directly, you have to go through a registered agent" (A0077). BMET's citizen charter lists clearance issued directly to workers who obtained a visa "on individual initiative" in 3 working days (A0057). Not a conflict: the agency is needed on the Saudi side (the employer delegates an authorised office on the MOFA visa platform to deal with the Saudi mission, A0076); the BMET clearance itself can be taken by the worker. Both kept.
- **Government cost cap Tk 1,65,000 (2016).** Only in news: Bonik Barta 3 Oct 2026 (A0013), Migrant-Rights 2024 (BMET deputy director quoted, BDT 165,000/USD 1,500), Daily Star 2016. The 2016 ministry order itself is not online. Kept with [N] citation.
- **BMET permission letters.** Search of bmet.gov.bd/pages/miscellaneous-infos: "সৌদি" gives 48 entries (6 Aug 2025 to 3 Nov 2025), "Saudi" 3 (latest 10 Dec 2025, God Gift International RL-0484, 19 workers). The whole agency-permission category (3,102 entries, all countries) has nothing newer than 10 Dec 2025. Saudi sent 755,194 workers in 2025 (Bonik Barta), so posted letters cover a tiny share. Caps seen: Tk 50,000 (RL-1059 Asmacs, 23 kitchen workers, Riyadh, SR 800 + SR 200 food) and Tk 58,610 (RL-0579 Bonanza, 318 workers, Haif Company, heavy equipment, SR 900-1,500 + food SR 200-300). Old draft's "press the green button" could not be verified; dropped.
- **Bonanza Overseas.** The ILO pilot was for Qatar construction (2018-2021), not Saudi Arabia (UN Network on Migration page, saved via Firecrawl, A0096-A0097). Bonanza is Active on OEP, valid to 31 Dec 2028 (A0090) and has a 2025 BMET permission letter for 318 workers to Saudi Arabia (A0019). Doc says this precisely.
- **Recruitment Advisor** has many Bangladeshi agency pages with reviews of Saudi recruitment (searched site: e.g. Bangladesh Export Corporation "paid 600,000 BDT ... cleaner ... fake job contract", Raj Overseas 400,000 BDT, Al Rabeta 420,000 BDT, TM Overseas 29 reviews). Many reviews are from early 2023.
- **Agency counts on OEP list (7 Nov 2026):** 2,204 Active, 264 Expired, 235 Cancelled, 151 Suspended.
- **OEP registration fee.** DEMO Sylhet notice (17 Aug 2025): government fee Tk 200 and 2 copies of the slip for fingerprinting (A0028). TTC Magura notice (Aug 2026): Tk 202 charge (A0029). A Facebook TTC post also says Tk 202.

## Reality check (detail behind the doc's "What happens in practice")

- **Costs.** Bonik Barta 3 Oct 2026: Saudi averages about Tk 5 lakh, up to Tk 9 lakh; initial Saudi visa about SR 2,000 (Tk 70,000), work permit adds Tk 1-1.5 lakh; sponsors take permits in their own name for workers they don't need; BBS survey: 52.3% of cost goes to brokers, 42.9% to agencies, 3.9% to government; TIB: some mission officials attest demand letters without proper checks or for bribes. TBS 24 Sep 2026: migration cost Tk 3.5-5 lakh; low-skilled wages Tk 25,000-30,000; a hospital cleaner paid Tk 3.5 lakh for SR 800.
- **Free visas.** Migrant-Rights Feb 2024: in Cumilla about 80% go on "independent visas" from relatives and friends; workers get a 3-month iqama; the Bangladesh contract is not valid at destination; more than half go on free visas; activists say about 10% go through first-degree relatives. Dhaka Tribune Oct 2020: free-visa worker paid sponsor SR 300 a month out of SR 700 earnings.
- **Skill test.** TBS 24 Sep 2026: 447,572 tested since mid-2023, 122,909 (27%) failed; retake pays again; agencies estimate new charges add Tk 11,064-14,470; 3-day prep course Tk 2,000 vs 3-month TTC courses about Tk 748; seven workers from one agency failed together. BAIRA asked to cancel the welfare fee rise.
- **BMET complaints.** Bonik Barta 26 Apr 2024: 2023: 2,380 complaints, 865 settled (36%), Tk 4.88 crore compensation; 2022: 1,240 complaints, 339 settled; complaints should be settled in a month but some take years; no written receipts means little compensation. Prothom Alo / TBS 30 Jul 2026: BMET settled 500 complaints manually in 2025 and recovered Tk 10 crore; the new OEP Online Complaint Management Module (BMET, RMMRU and Systech Digital) links all DEMOs, lets migrants and families file, upload proof, track and get notifications; 100 officials trained. A LinkedIn search snippet (Helvetas) gives a different 2025 figure, "2,200 complaints ... BDT 100 million"; doc uses the Prothom Alo figure (500). Migrant Forum in Asia: a woman settled after three hearings for less than half her claim, threatened by the agency at hearings. TBS (Jul 2026): state minister receives 40-50 complaints a day; PKB staff accused of Tk 10,000-20,000 bribes per Tk 3 lakh loan.
- **ILO / IOM.** Searched for whether ILO or IOM Bangladesh take individual complaints: found only programme and research pages (ILO notes BMET's online complaints system lacks a strong follow-up process). No individual complaint channel found; left as "Need to know". OKUP offers free legal counselling and case filing (01842773300).
- **Licence cancellations.** DesheBideshe 13 Oct 2026: 52 agency licences cancelled for visa fraud, some offices sealed; travel agencies sending workers though it isn't their job.
