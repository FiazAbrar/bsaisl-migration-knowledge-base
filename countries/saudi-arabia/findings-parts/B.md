## Corrections to the old draft

- **Police clearance fee is Tk 1,500, not Tk 500.** The official portal (pcc.police.gov.bd, B0052) asks for a Tk 1,500 treasury challan under code 1-7301-0001-2681 or card payment. Probashi App (Sep 2026, B0062) still says Tk 500. Rajshahi Metropolitan Police posted a "re-fixed police clearance fee" notice on 23 Oct 2025 (B0059; body is an image, not captured). Official portal kept. Conflict recorded.
- **PCC conditions the old draft missed:** passport valid at least 3 months; current address must be the permanent or emergency address in the passport and you must live there; passport page scan must be attested by a first-class gazetted officer; SMS status (PCC S <ref> to 26969); collection with passport or application copy; helpdesk numbers (B0049-B0058, B0071-B0072). The old draft's "certificate from your chairman or councillor" and "upload NID" only apply when an MRP passport has no address (B0051).
- **Welfare fee is Tk 5,500 from 1 Oct 2026** (ministry circular 20 Sep 2026, transcribed from the scan: B0099-B0101), up from Tk 3,500 set in 2015. It applies to outgoing workers, workers abroad and fees collected at the airport. BMET's citizen charter (Aug 2026) still shows (3500+1000) = Tk 4,500 including insurance (B0096) and WEWB's charter shows Tk 4,500 for membership from abroad (E0020); both predate the circular. Riyal amount now charged abroad: Need to know.
- **Wafid booking now has two tiers:** Standard USD 10 (centre assigned) and Premium USD 25 (choose centre and date) (B0004, wafid-book). The Wafid FAQ still says the centre can't be chosen (B0014): conflict between two pages of the same official site; the booking page is the current product.
- **Wafid result validity is 2 months for visa issuing** (GHC regulations Art. 42, B0017); the old "60 days" was close. The old "set fee about Tk 8,500-10,000" is not an official fee: the GHC does not set the centre's fee (Saudi Life Guide, B0038); Daily Star (Jul 2025) says the total "should not exceed Tk 8,500-9,000" (B0030). The doc now says "should not be more than about Tk 8,500-9,000" citing the news source.
- **Tasheer fee "about Tk 694" removed.** The old citation [N111] was the TBS article on the skill-test fee, not Tasheer. No reliable Tasheer fee for Bangladesh found (only Facebook/YouTube claims of Tk 1,822). Now a Need to know; vc.tasheer.com blocked.
- **Ami Probashi IVR visa check (16768) removed.** It is a private paid service (Bangladesh Monitor, Jan 2026); free official checks exist (BMET 3 working days, BMET app, KSA Visa). Rule: recommend only what has evidence it helps.
- **"Work only for the employer on your visa [13]" removed:** the cited MHRSD LRI page doesn't say it.
- **"Pay: there is no minimum wage" removed** from this chunk: no source fetched in this pass says so for private-sector expatriates; the rights section owns wage rules.
- **BMET clearance fees:** charter (p.1, transcribed from page image, B0096): immigration clearance fee Tk 250 per worker; source income tax: single visa unattested Tk 400, attested Tk 250, "only for Saudi visa Tk 500"; group visa skilled Tk 1,200, unskilled Tk 800 (p.2). Dhaka Tribune 1 Dec 2024 (B0102): ministry abolished the Tk 250 smart card fee and Tk 250 modification fee; clearance with QR codes "continue without any fee". Conflict kept as one Need to know line.
- **PDO certificate valid 3 months** (BMET memo 10 May 2022, forwarded by BAIRA; B0084): if clearance isn't taken within 3 months the course must be redone. PDO can be done at any TTC/IMT in any district (B0085).

## Reality checks (detail behind the doc)

- **Medical (Wafid/GAMCA):** Daily Star (11 Jul 2025): agencies sell a Tk 10,000 "standard" package or a Tk 20,000 "guaranteed fit" package; syndicate charges Tk 25,000-30,000; over 200 centres but about 90% of referrals go to about 20 centres via pre-booked slots; a worker who paid Tk 25,000 for a fit certificate was found unfit in Saudi Arabia and deported; the ministry's monitoring and enforcement joint secretary said they had heard of extra charges but received no formal complaints and had contacted the GCC. TBS Bangla (6 Sep 2025): set fee about Tk 10,000 but most pay double or more; bribes of Tk 30,000-80,000 to turn unfit into fit; ex-BAIRA official: only 2-5% pay the set fee, the rest at least Tk 17,000-20,000; centres declare unfit first then demand money; 186 of 260 centres are in Dhaka; after ministry pressure some got certificates without extra payment. MRRORS (24 May 2026): GHC's revised regulations add a Grievance Committee open to migrants and penalties on centres whose fit workers are found unfit in the destination; workers report arbitrary vaccination and booking add-ons; unfit workers barred from retesting for 3 months; Kuwait implemented the revision on 17 May 2026 (Saudi implementation date not confirmed).
- **Complaint route for medical:** GHC regulations (6th version, 2021) Ch. 11 and annex: complaints/appeals to the Council are final; written; within 2 weeks of the event; anyone may complain; by personal visit with the form, fax, mailbox or email; anonymous or late complaints rejected; sanctions on centres from notice to revocation. No GHC complaint email found in the regulations or the Wafid FAQ (FAQ only gives support@wafid.com, decoded from Cloudflare-protected links). Whether outside bodies (ILO, IOM, BRAC) take individual medical-centre cases: no evidence found; not suggested in the doc.
- **Tasheer biometrics:** made mandatory for Bangladeshi work visa applicants in Oct 2023 (Daily Star); centres at Jamuna Future Park (Dhaka) and Chattogram; online booking at vc.tasheer.com/appointment. Rescheduling rules and "booking is free" are from Saudi Life Guide (12 Oct 2026), written for Pakistan/India; assumed to apply to the same vc.tasheer.com system. Agency/Facebook ads sell "Tasheer appointment" services; Saudi Life Guide says paid booking sites aren't needed.
- **Police clearance forgery:** TBS (17 Aug 2026): Chattogram ring sold forged PCCs with counterfeit QR codes linking to a fake website. No news found on bribes demanded during police verification beyond Facebook group questions; not put in the doc.
- **Embassy "visa fee":** TBS (31 Aug 2022): agencies had paid USD 220-250 extra per application through middlemen; Saudi Embassy said no fees for applying. Older than 12 months; ledger row for the middlemen figure could not be logged (quote check failed on the escaped $ sign).
- **Visas disappearing / cancelled:** TBS Bangla (Mar 2026): MHRSD cancelled several thousand applications made on fake demand; ex-BAIRA official says visas appear in the Saudi system then vanish; issuance rate down to about 5%. Hence the doc tells workers to recheck the visa before final payment and before flying.
- **Fake visa + fake ticket:** Alokito News (31 May 2025): two men paid Tk 12 lakh for Saudi Arabia, given fake visa and ticket, broker turned them back on the way to the airport; RAB-14 arrested him. Newsbangla24 (2020) similar case (30 people, Russia visas) not used.
- **Iqama:** TBS (Oct 2024): workers on employment visas usually get a 3-month temporary permit; employers must pay about SR 11,000 a year (SR 8,600 levy plus insurance and SR 600 to the Interior Ministry) for low-wage sectors; many delay, workers become undocumented, are detained and deported; 80% of BMET's monthly complaints relate to iqama/employment in Saudi Arabia; 1,133 complaints in six months of 2024. BAIRA claimed 60-70% of foreign workers lacked valid iqamas (claim, not used).
- **Passport confiscation:** MHRSD rights guide (Dec 2025) says passport or iqama may not be withheld "under no circumstances" and points to the "Beneficiary Page" platform for complaints; HRW (Dec 2024) still calls on Saudi Arabia to enforce the ban. Where the Beneficiary Page is: not found (Need to know).
