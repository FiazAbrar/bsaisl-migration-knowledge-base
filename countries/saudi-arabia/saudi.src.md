# Saudi Arabia: migration guide

How to go to Saudi Arabia from Bangladesh, visa by visa, step by step. Researched 26-28 Oct 2026. Citations like \[3\] are official sources; \[N3\] are news or other third-party sources still to be confirmed officially; a year after a citation, like \[N3 (2022)\], means the source is older than 2025. "Need to know" marks what we haven't found yet.

Tk is the Bangladeshi taka (1 lakh is Tk 100,000), SR the Saudi riyal and USD the US dollar. BMET is the Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training, Bangladesh's government agency for overseas jobs [14]. In Saudi Arabia a foreign worker's visa and residence are tied to a sponsor, normally the employer [N4].

## Work visa: company worker

For a job with a Saudi company. It takes about 1 to 3 months, and you must enter Saudi Arabia within 90 days of the visa being issued [N219]. The employer pays the recruitment fees, the iqama (residence card), the work permit and the ticket home at the end of the job [100]. You pay the skill test (USD 50), the Welfare Board fee (Tk 5,500) and insurance (Tk 1,000) [141, 10, 11]. Agencies usually ask Tk 3-5 lakh, although BMET's official maximum is Tk 1,65,000 including airfare [N2, N108, N104]. For each Saudi job order it approves, BMET also sets the most the agency may take from you (step 1, Before paying) [137].

### Step 1: Find a real job

1. **Register on the Overseas Employment Platform (OEP).** You need it for training, the orientation and BMET clearance [4, 130]. Open [employee.oep.gov.bd](https://employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/login-with-otp) in Chrome, enter your mobile number and the code sent to it, and fill in the form exactly as on your NID (national ID card), including dots and spaces in your name. Pay by bKash or Nagad, then download your registration card [130]. Hotline: 09614445271 [4].
2. **Have a trade.** Almost every Saudi work visa needs a skill certificate in the trade on the visa, and a certificate can nearly double the pay of an unskilled job [N271, 1]. BMET's Technical Training Centres (TTCs) teach trades such as electrical work, air conditioning, automotive work, welding, construction and driving, in courses of 21 days to 6 months [122, N275]. To enrol, log in to OEP, open Training, then Trade/Skills, choose your division, district and centre and press Search: you see the trades that centre runs. Pick one, press Apply, then Process to enroll and Confirm; "My Course" shows your applications [130].
3. **Ask relatives or friends in Saudi Arabia whether their own company is hiring.** A visa from their employer is for a real job at a company they know; a visa from a Saudi individual who doesn't employ them is often a "free visa" (below) [N103, N105, N4]. If the company is hiring:
   1. **Your relative gives the company's HR office your name, passport copy and trade** and asks it to hire you. The company requests the work visa on Qiwa, the labour ministry's platform; only companies with enough Saudi staff and a clean labour record get visas [N296, 129].
   2. **No one pays the company for the visa.** The employer pays the visa and recruitment costs [100]. If anyone asks your relative for money for the visa, it is being sold, a sign of a free visa [N4].
   3. **The company authorises a licensed Bangladeshi agency to process your visa** on the Saudi Foreign Ministry's visa platform, using the agency's licence number [131, N295]. You can't take the visa to the Saudi embassy yourself: even with a visa from a relative's employer, a licensed agency must submit it [N103, N298]. So choose the agency first (item 4) and send its name and RL number to your relative to give the company.
   4. **The company gets the demand letter attested** by the Bangladesh Embassy online at [beriyadh.org](https://beriyadh.org/): it registers with its CR (commercial registration) number and uploads the demand letter, a power of attorney naming the agency, the employment contract, a letter of guarantee, an undertaking, its project contract and its wage protection certificate [128]. Without this, BMET won't give you clearance; it applies to single-worker visas too [N297, N312].
   5. **Then continue from step 3 (skill test) with that agency.** BMET clearance you can then get yourself, without the agency (step 8) [14]. **Need to know:** what a fair agency fee is for processing a visa the worker brings.
4. **Ask licensed agencies what Saudi job orders they have.** On [OEP's agency list](https://www.oep.gov.bd/agencies), type the agency's name or RL (recruiting licence) number into Search: the status must say "Active", and the row shows the office phone [4]. Call that number, not one a broker gives you, to check that the person you're dealing with works there, get the office address and ask which Saudi jobs they have for your trade [4, N259]. Many licences are expired, suspended or cancelled, so check the status again on the day you pay; travel agencies may not recruit workers [125, N289]. Ask to see the employer's demand letter, which names the company, the jobs and the pay, with the embassy's attestation (step 2) [N5, 12]. Bonanza Overseas (RL-0579) is the one agency with outside evidence of charging workers little [N288].
5. **Read workers' reviews of the agency** on [Recruitment Advisor](https://www.recruitmentadvisor.org/search-an-agency), a trade-union site where migrants rate agencies on fees, contract, job and pay [N279, N280].
6. **Check the company exists.** Ask the agency or your relative for its CR number and expiry date and enter them on the Saudi Ministry of Commerce's [verification page](https://v.mc.gov.sa/Verification/PageVerify.aspx?lang=en) [121, N273].

**Avoid a "free visa".** It is bought from a Saudi sponsor who has no job for you; you then work illegally, often pay the sponsor a monthly cut, and can be deported [N4]. Signs: the sponsor is a household or person who doesn't need a worker; a "house driver" visa for a factory job; a promise that you can "work freely"; a 3-month iqama instead of a year; or a job title on the visa that differs from the promised job [N4, N103, N105, N108].

**Before paying:**

- **Get the employer's name, the job, the salary and the contract in writing,** before paying, not at the airport [11, N103].
- **Find BMET's permission letter for your job.** Before an agency can process clearance for a Saudi job order, BMET issues a letter naming the agency and its RL number, the Saudi company, the block visa and demand letter numbers, the job, the number of workers, the salary and benefits, and the most the agency may take from each worker; BMET posts these letters on its website: open [bmet.gov.bd/pages/miscellaneous-infos](https://bmet.gov.bd/pages/miscellaneous-infos) (Miscellaneous information, বিবিধ তথ্য), type the agency's name or RL number into the search box and press the green button, then open the PDF in the row titled with that agency's name and "Saudi Arabia" [137, 138]. Ask the agency for the letter's date and memo number and compare it with what you are told. In one 2025 letter for kitchen workers in Riyadh, the agency could take at most Tk 50,000, only by cheque, bank draft or pay order and only after the visa, clearance and flight were confirmed; the employer paid all Saudi-side costs, including the levy and the airfare; and each worker had to sign the contract and get a Bangla translation [137]. Paying more than the letter allows is grounds for a complaint (below) [137, 14].
- **Pay into the agency's bank account by cheque, bank draft or pay order, and keep proof** [N261, 11]. Being asked to pay into a personal account or through hundi (the illegal informal channel) is a warning sign [N259].
- **Write down who took money, when and how much;** you need it to complain [N132].
- **If you need to borrow,** Probashi Kallyan Bank (PKB), the government bank for migrant workers, lends up to Tk 3 lakh without collateral at 9% simple interest, repaid in monthly instalments over up to 3 years including a grace period [139]. You must be 18 to 60 and not have defaulted on any loan, and you and your guarantors must live permanently in the branch's area [139]. At the branch, fill in the free application form and bring 3 passport-size photos; copies of your NID, passport, visa and BMET smart card (step 8), and the job contract if you have one; and 3 signed cheque leaves from a bank account in your name, with its statement. You must also open a savings account at that branch with at least Tk 500 [139]. Bring 2 guarantors: one well-off person with 3 signed cheque leaves, a bank statement and proof of income, and one family member or close relative; each brings a photo, an NID copy and proof of address such as a utility bill [139]. Fees are 0.5% for processing and 0.5% for documentation, plus VAT, and Tk 900 per lakh for risk cover; you also need 16 revenue stamps of Tk 10, and 3 adhesive stamps of Tk 100 for each guarantor [139]. The bank decides within 7 working days of complete papers; help line 16238, or +880 9677-787878 from abroad [139]. Informal lenders have charged 30-60% a year [N131].
- **If the agency takes your money and doesn't send you, or sends you to a different job,** write an application to the Director General of BMET explaining what happened, attach your proof (receipts, contract, messages, your notes of payments), and submit it with BMET's complaint form. BMET sets up an inquiry and a hearing; it's free and is meant to take 30 working days [14]. If BMET doesn't resolve it, write to BMET's Director (Employment) at diremi@bmet.gov.bd, who has 3 months; then appeal to the Secretary of the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare at secretary@probashi.gov.bd, who has 1 month; then file at [grs.gov.bd](https://www.grs.gov.bd/), the government's complaint system, which has 3 months [14].
- **You can also complain straight to the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare.** Send an application with your proof, your full address and the agency's name and RL number to its enforcement branch (sasenforcement@probashi.gov.bd, 02-41030255). It's free: both sides are called to a hearing, which starts within 3-5 working days, and the complaint is meant to be settled within 30 working days [135].
- **Need to know:** which agencies place workers in the promised job at a fair cost.

### Step 2: Get the attested demand letter

The employer applies online to the Bangladesh Embassy in Riyadh (step 1, item 3), whose labour wing checks that the employer and job are real and stamps the demand letter [128, 12, N233]. Ask the agency for a copy before paying in full [12, 11].

- **To check it is real,** take copies of the demand letter and visa to BMET or your district manpower office (step 7) [14].

### Step 3: Pass the skill test

Needed for most work visas, including cleaners and loaders, but not domestic workers; take it after the demand letter is approved. The certificate lasts 5 years [1, 43, N1, N113, N308]. Takamol, the Saudi testing company, runs it at 28 centres in Bangladesh: 27 Technical Training Centres and the BMET training centre (BRTC) in Gazipur [141].

1. **Check your trade needs it** on [pacc.qiwa.sa](https://pacc.qiwa.sa/) [N262].
2. **Register yourself,** no agent needed. On [svp-international.pacc.sa](https://svp-international.pacc.sa/) press Sign-up and enter your first name, last name, NID number, passport number, country, nationality, your own email address, date of birth, passport expiry date and a password of at least 8 characters with a capital letter, a small letter, a number and a symbol. Tick the box confirming the data are correct, then type the code sent to your email [141]. Copy everything exactly from your passport: with a wrong passport number you can't sit the test. If you have only one name, write it as the first name and your father's name as the last name; the same name in both counts as an error [141].
3. **Book.** Sign in with that email and password and the code emailed to you. In Account Dashboard press Start Verification, choose your occupation (it can't be changed later), then Direct Assessment (In-person), your division or city (the centre is assigned automatically), the test language and a date shown in green. Tick "I declare that the information entered is correct" and press Confirm [141].
4. **Pay USD 50** (about Tk 6,150) with a dollar-endorsed Visa or Mastercard; the test ticket appears in your dashboard under Reservation and is emailed to you [141, 142]. Pay no one more: there is no way to pass for extra money, and a worker caught in an illegal payment has the account locked and can't go to Saudi Arabia [142]. Report anyone offering to fix the test to the police or to BMET's Takamol and Japan Support Centre, BMET Bhaban (4th floor), Kakrail, Dhaka, 01901165981 or 01901165982 [142].
5. **Prepare if you need to;** it's optional [141]. 3-day loading and unloading courses run at 24 TTCs for Tk 2,000, and free 5- to 10-day courses run for gas station attendants (Kishoreganj TTC), sellers (BK TTC Chattogram), food and beverage counter staff (Dhaka Mohila TTC), barbers (Sylhet TTC), concrete finishers (BK TTC Dhaka) and building and construction workers (Khulna, Jhalokathi, Moulvibazar, Nilphamari and Chapainawabganj TTCs) [141]. The course certificate isn't an SVP certificate and doesn't count towards the test [141]. There are no official study materials, so anyone selling "real" questions is lying [N262].
6. **Take the test** with your original passport, a photocopy of it, the ticket and one photo; arrive before the 30-minute briefing. The computer test takes 30 minutes: 15 questions answered by clicking, in the language you chose. The practical takes 1.5 hours and is in Bangla [141]. Phones and bags go in a locker, and looking at another screen counts as cheating [141]. You need 50%, and you get at most 3 attempts [N262].
7. **To change the date,** cancel and rebook once, up to 96 hours before the test. If you miss it, you can apply and pay again 96 hours after the slot [141].
8. **Check the certificate** on [svp-international.pacc.sa](https://svp-international.pacc.sa/) [141]. To correct a wrong detail, press Support at the bottom right and raise a ticket with your name, email, a subject and description, Request Type "Suggestion", Client "Test Taker", your country and passport number, and attach your passport, ticket and certificate [141].

### Step 4: Pass the Wafid medical check

The Gulf health check for everyone going to work [5, 44].

1. **Book a slip yourself** after passing the skill test, because a result lasts only 60 days; agents can't book for you [5, N225]. On [wafid.com](https://wafid.com/en/book-appointment/), press Book an Appointment, choose Bangladesh, your city and Saudi Arabia, enter your details exactly as on your passport and check them before pressing Submit, then pay USD 10 by card (enter the card's expiry as year then month) and print the slip [134]. The centre is assigned for your city; you can't choose it [134].
2. **Go to the centre straight away** with your original passport, the printed slip and your NID; the centre takes your photo and fingerprints [134, N306]. The set fee is about Tk 8,500-10,000; centres often charge Tk 17,000-20,000 [N110, N115].
3. **Check the result** on [Wafid status search](https://wafid.com/en/medical-status-search/) with your passport number and nationality, or the slip number; it appears within 26 days [134, 5]. If a detail on it is wrong, ask the centre to submit a change request, which is free [134]. Don't buy a "fit" result: people who did were tested again in Saudi Arabia and deported [N110, N115].
4. **If unfit,** you can test again after 3 months, or appeal to the Gulf Health Council's grievance committee [N287, 5]. Unfit means, among others, HIV, hepatitis B or C, malaria, tuberculosis or other chest X-ray findings, kidney, liver or heart failure, uncontrolled blood pressure or diabetes, cancer, and very low haemoglobin [134].

### Step 5: Give fingerprints at the Tasheer visa centre

Centres are at Jamuna Future Park in Dhaka and in Chattogram [N28, N264].

1. **Book yourself** at [vc.tasheer.com](https://vc.tasheer.com/appointment) and print the appointment letter [N260, N264].
2. **Bring** your original passport (valid 6 months, 2 blank pages), the letter, the medical report and the visa authorisation [N264].
3. **Arrive 15 minutes early;** missing a slot blocks booking for 7 days. The fee is about Tk 694 [N264, N111].

### Step 6: Give the agency your papers for the visa

- **Hand over:** your passport, white-background photos, the Wafid report, the skill certificate, a police clearance, and attested education and experience certificates [N269, N220].
- **Get the police clearance yourself** at [pcc.police.gov.bd](https://pcc.police.gov.bd/ords/f?p=500) [37]. Create an account with your mobile number; enter your passport details and your permanent and current addresses (district, thana, ward); pay Tk 500 by bank challan at Sonali Bank or online A-Challan (bKash, Nagad or Rocket) at [ibas2.finance.gov.bd/acs](https://ibas2.finance.gov.bd/acs); and upload your passport page, a certificate from your chairman or councillor, your NID, a photo and the challan. Keep the reference ID [N220]. A police officer then calls or visits; show the originals. Collect the certificate from the SP office or metropolitan police counter: 7 working days in Dhaka, 10 outside [N220, N29]. Don't buy one through a middleman; fake certificates with fake QR codes have been sold [N122].
- **Get education certificates apostilled** on [mygov.bd](https://www.mygov.bd/): the board or university checks the certificate, the Foreign Ministry issues an e-apostille, and you get an SMS and download it from your MyGov dashboard [N265]. Saudi Arabia accepts apostilles instead of embassy attestation [N266, N277].
- **Pay no "embassy fee":** the embassy charges nothing for a work visa, and the employer pays the visa fee [N268, N270].
- **Need to know:** the embassy's own document list [119].

### Step 7: Check the visa yourself

- **Online:** enter the visa and passport numbers on [KSA Visa Track Application](https://ksavisa.sa/track-application) [8, 9]. Check the job title, the sponsor's name and the visa type. "No record found" means still processing, or fake [N234, N123].
- **At BMET or the district manpower office:** bring copies of the visa and the passport page with the visa; they check the visa and demand letter free within 3 working days [14, 11]. BMET's visa-check app on Google Play also checks Saudi visas [14].
- **By phone:** call Ami Probashi on 16768 with the visa details; you get an SMS saying whether the visa is valid, for a small fee [N124].
- **If the visa is refused or cancelled,** get your money back from the agency through the complaint route under step 1, Before paying [14]. **Need to know:** whether Saudi Arabia gives a reason or an appeal for a refused work visa.

### Step 8: Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight

1. **Attend the compulsory 3-day pre-departure orientation** at the Technical Training Centre in your district; BMET runs it at 70 institutes, and you pay the fee the centre sets against a money receipt [11, N302, 14]. Download the enrolment card from [OEP](https://training.oep.gov.bd/pdo-enrollment-card) and, afterwards, the certificate from [OEP](https://www.oep.gov.bd/pdo-certificate) [4].
2. **Get BMET clearance,** the smart card airport immigration checks before you board [4, 14]. After the visa is issued, the agency applies, you give fingerprints at your District Employment and Manpower Office (DEMO), then download the card from [OEP](https://www.oep.gov.bd/employee-card) [14, 4]. Welfare Board desks at the airport check the cards, and travellers without one or with a fake one are stopped [136]. If you got the visa yourself, for example through a relative, you can apply without the agency at BMET's emigration branch in Dhaka or the Chattogram district office. Bring an application, the visa copy, the contract if you have one, an attested copy of your OEP registration card, a declaration on Tk 300 stamp paper and the Welfare Board payment receipt; there is also Tk 500 income tax for a Saudi visa, and it takes 3 working days [14]. The card is free; you pay Tk 5,500 to the Wage Earners' Welfare Board and a one-time Tk 1,000 insurance premium (see If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job) [N224, 10, 34, 11].

3. **Before you fly,** leave one set of copies of all your papers at home and carry 3 sets; open a bank account in your name and one in a family member's; and note the embassy labour wing's address and phone number (Contacts) [143].

### Step 9: After arrival

Work only for the employer on your visa [13].

- **If you came without BMET clearance,** join the Welfare Board from Saudi Arabia to get its insurance and help: apply online at [portal.wewb.gov.bd](https://portal.wewb.gov.bd/) with your passport and iqama; the embassy labour wing processes it, and the fee is the equivalent of Tk 4,500 in riyals, including life insurance [140].

- **Iqama:** the employer must get it within 90 days, pay for it and renew it [N31, 100]. Your part: a medical test at an approved clinic, then fingerprints and a face scan at the Passports Directorate (Jawazat) when the employer books it; it usually takes 1-3 weeks [N309]. Without one you are illegal, so if you get only a temporary permit, complain (see If you aren't paid) [N135]. Carry it always; you need it for a bank account and a SIM card [126].
- **Check your status** on [Absher](https://www.absher.sa/), the Interior Ministry's portal [36]. To sign up, press New User, enter your iqama number and phone number, choose a username and password and enter the SMS code; then activate the account with your fingerprints at an Absher self-service kiosk, or through some banks' ATMs or apps [N303].
- **Check your contract** on [Qiwa](https://qiwa.sa/), the labour ministry's platform for contracts and job changes; log in with Nafath, the Saudi digital ID app, which takes your Absher username and password [13, N229, N301]. Ask for a copy in a language you understand [N230].
- **Keep your passport.** The employer may not hold it; if it does, ask for it in writing, then complain to the labour ministry (see If you aren't paid) [N134, N232, 124].
- **Pay:** there is no minimum wage. Typical monthly pay in 2026: cleaners SR 1,000-1,700, helpers SR 1,200-1,800, construction workers SR 1,600-3,000, drivers SR 1,500-2,500, electricians SR 1,800-3,500 [N235]. Pay must go into a bank account or salary card, with nothing deducted for visa or recruitment fees [N230].

### Your rights at work

- **Hours:** 8 a day and 48 a week (6 and 36 for Muslims in Ramadan), never more than 12 a day. Overtime pays 150% [127]. No outdoor work in direct sun from 12 to 3 pm between 15 June and 15 September [N290].
- **Rest and sick leave:** one day off a week. Sick leave: full pay for 30 days a year, three-quarters for the next 60, then unpaid [127].
- **Housing and food:** the employer gives housing or a rent allowance; check the contract also covers food or a food allowance [127, 126].
- **Health insurance:** the employer must provide it [127, N294]. Check it by iqama number on the [Council of Health Insurance site](https://www.chi.gov.sa/en/ServicesDirectory/Pages/Eservices-CheckInsurance.aspx?servicename=checkinsurance) [N292]. It doesn't cover serious illness [126].
- **Probation:** up to 90 days. In that time either side can end the contract without end-of-service pay, and you pay your own ticket home [127].

### How long you can stay

- **As long as you have a job and the employer renews your iqama.** Contracts usually run 2 years and can be renewed [126, 127].
- **No permanent residence and no pension** for ordinary workers: you get end-of-service pay when you leave [29, 127].
- **Age:** work visas are for ages 18 to 60; retirement is at 60 for men and 55 for women unless both sides agree [126, 127].
- **To leave when the contract ends,** give 30 days' notice, or 60 for an open-ended contract [127].

### What can get you jailed

- **Don't bring** alcohol, pork, khat, gutkha, pan masala, obscene material or anything linked to black magic, including on your phone [126].
- **Never carry a parcel for someone else,** even a relative: you are liable for whatever is in your bags, and drug smuggling can bring the death penalty [126, N291, 143].
- **Bring medicines** in their original packs, at most 30 days' supply, with a prescription translated into Arabic [126].

### If you aren't paid

1. **Complain to the labour ministry** online, on Qiwa or the ministry's e-services portal [124]. Choose "Friendly Settlement – File a Lawsuit", enter your details, your employer's and your job's, choose the settlement office, attach your contract, bank statements or payslips and any messages with the employer, and submit [N304]. The ministry holds a settlement session within 21 working days; if that fails, the case goes to the labour court on Najiz, the Justice Ministry's portal, where you also ask for enforcement if the employer still doesn't pay [124, N137]. File within 12 months; it is free [N137]. Helpline: 19911 [35].
2. **If you can't do it yourself, call the embassy labour wing** free on 8001000125: it helps workers settle disputes with employers and goes to the labour court with them [132, 34].
3. **From Bangladesh, you or a relative can ask the ministry to act.** Send the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare (Welfare-1 branch, saswelfare@probashi.gov.bd, 02-41030251) an application describing what happened, with copies of your passport, iqama, BMET smart card and Saudi ID number, the employer's name and full address, and the unpaid wages, allowances or insurance. It's free; within 7 working days the ministry writes to the embassy labour wing and the Welfare Board to act [135].
4. **Claim wage insurance** if the employer hasn't paid most of its workers for over 6 months: up to 6 months' wages (at most SR 17,500) plus SR 1,000 for a ticket home. Check at labor.aletihad.sa with your iqama number [N139].

### If you want to change employer

You can move without your employer's consent after a year, when the contract ends, after 3 months without pay, or when the employer misses two dispute hearings [N134, 13].

1. The new employer sends you an offer on Qiwa; accept within 10 days [N144].
2. Your employer has 14 days to respond; after a year of service, a 90-day notice period runs [N144].
3. The new employer pays the transfer fee [N144].

Don't stop working before the transfer shows as approved on Qiwa, and never pay anyone to find you a job or a sponsor [N311, N144]. A worker reported absent (huroob) who has been in Saudi Arabia over 12 months can also be transferred on Qiwa [N310]. **Need to know:** how a worker finds a new employer.

### If your employer reports you absent (huroob)

A huroob report makes you illegal; some employers file false ones to punish complaints [N8].

1. **Check Absher regularly;** it shows as "Absent from Work", often without an SMS [N147, N134].
2. **Ask the employer to withdraw it** within about 15-20 days [N151].
3. **Within 60 days,** move to a new employer or leave [N148, N163].
4. **After that,** book an appointment on Absher with the Passports Directorate (Jawazat) to prove you didn't run away [N303], or use the embassy's Special Exit Programme (see Going home without valid papers) [N151, 103]. Don't pay anyone who offers to remove a report [N147].

### Leaving at the end of the job

1. **Apply for the final exit visa** on Absher or Qiwa; the employer's consent isn't needed [13]. You have 60 days after stopping work to move or leave [N163].
2. **Collect end-of-service pay** within a week: half a month's wage for each of the first 5 years and a month's wage for each later year. If you resign, you get nothing under 2 years, a third from 2 to 5 years, two-thirds from 5 to 10 [N142, 101].
3. **The employer pays your ticket home** [100].

### Going home on leave

You get 21 days' paid leave a year, 30 after 5 years [N142]. Get an exit and re-entry visa on Absher; the employer pays for it [13, 100, N164]. When you fly back, show the re-entry visa and your ticket at the Probashi Kallyan (expatriate welfare) desk beside departure immigration at the airport; it checks the visa online and attests it, free [14, 136]. Your contract says who pays the ticket, so check it before signing [N231].

### Bringing your family

Only workers whose permit is graded skilled or high-skilled can bring a spouse and children; see Family residence [20, N244, 21]. Asylum isn't available; see Asylum [N326].

### Scams

Done to workers:

- **Free visa:** a visa bought from a sponsor with no job for you (step 1) [N4].
- **Shell-company visas:** brokers sell visas issued in the names of companies that don't exist, then disappear once the worker lands [N327].
- **Tourist visa sold as a job:** brokers promise a 15-month iqama, send the worker on a 3-month tourist visa, and the worker becomes illegal after 3 months [N327]. Check the visa type yourself (step 7).
- **A different job and a shorter iqama:** workers promised factory jobs have been made masons with a 3-month iqama instead of a year [N105].
- **Visa bribes:** in 2023 Saudi Arabia arrested two former officials of its Dhaka embassy and eight Bangladeshis for selling work visas [N121].

Tried by applicants, and punishable: paying to "fix" the skill test [142], forged police clearance certificates [N122] and bribes for a "fit" medical report [N115].

## Work visa: domestic worker

For housemaids, house drivers, cooks and gardeners working for a family [19]. Women pay nothing; agencies may not charge them [17]. No skill test is needed [N1].

1. **Check you qualify:** women must be 25 to 45, or up to 50 with work abroad before. For BMET clearance a woman brings proof of age and a no-objection letter from her legal guardian [14, 15].
2. **Women do the one-month housekeeping course,** compulsory and run at 41 Technical Training Centres; enrol on OEP as for any TTC course (company worker, step 1) and pay the centre's fee against a receipt [14, 15, 130].
3. **Accept the contract on Musaned,** the Saudi platform for domestic workers: it sends you an SMS link, and you have 5 days. Read it first; it must be translated into a language you understand [16, 19, 102].
4. **Medical check, visa and BMET clearance** as for company workers (steps 4-8) [5, 14].

**After arrival:** a weekly day off and at most 10 hours' work a day, but no minimum wage, and you can't change employer freely [18, 19, N32]. Housemaids get about SR 800-1,300 a month, drivers and cooks SR 1,000-1,900, paid through Musaned onto a bank card [N237, N151]. The employer may not keep your passport or phone [N154].

### If something goes wrong

- **You can end or move the contract** after 3 months without pay, if not collected from the airport within 15 days, if no iqama is issued, if lent to others, if abused, or if falsely reported absent [N153].
- **Complain to the labour ministry online,** which is required for domestic workers since October 2024, or ask the embassy labour wing (8001000125, free) to help; unsettled cases go to the labour court [N305, 132, 124].
- **If you have to flee, go to the embassy** at 8039 Dareen Street, Diplomatic Quarter, Riyadh [132], where women domestic workers are sheltered until they are sent home; Bangladesh runs two safe homes for women in Saudi Arabia, in Riyadh and Jeddah [136, N329], and a relative in Bangladesh can ask the Welfare Board for a place there, directly or through the district manpower office [N278, 136, 140]. Leaving without help can lead to a huroob report [N8, N7].
- **A relative in Bangladesh can ask the ministry for help:** send the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare (Welfare-2 branch, aswelfare@probashi.gov.bd) an application describing what happened, with copies of her passport, BMET smart card and iqama, the agency's name and address, and the accused person's mobile number and email. It's free; within 7 working days the ministry writes to the embassy labour wing or BMET to act [135].

### Scams

- **Doctored age:** agencies have sent girls under 25 with false ages; a 14-year-old was sent as a 25-year-old [N328].
- **Household visas with no job:** households that don't need staff get house-driver or domestic-worker visas; workers arrive to no job, and working elsewhere on these visas is punishable [N327].

### House drivers

Agencies charge Tk 4-6 lakh; new drivers earn about SR 1,500-1,800 [N238]. Check the visa says house driver and the sponsor is a household [N238]. A translated Bangladeshi licence works for 3 months; then you need a Saudi licence from a driving school (SR 2,500-3,000), with a theory test in Bangla, booked on Absher [N239, N160, N161].

## Umrah visa

Muslims 18 or over, or younger with a guardian; women don't need a mahram (a close male relative) [24]. Packages from Bangladesh cost about Tk 1.2-3 lakh including the USD 101 visa [24, N189].

1. **Buy from a licensed agency** on the Ministry of Religious Affairs' list at [hajj.gov.bd](https://hajj.gov.bd/) (helpline 16136); Bangladeshis can't apply on Nusuk, the Saudi booking platform, by themselves [113, N188, N307].
2. **Give the agency** your passport (valid 6 months), a white-background photo and a meningitis (ACWY) vaccine certificate, taken at least 10 days before arrival. Register your fingerprints and face in the Saudi Visa Bio app. The agency books a Nusuk-approved hotel and transport and gets the e-visa, usually in 3-7 working days [N307].
3. **Travel within 30 days** of the visa being issued [N190].
4. **Don't work.** Staying on to work after Umrah is illegal [24], and the Ministry of Hajj and Umrah says Umrah visas can't be used for work [N325]. Brokers sell Umrah, tourist and visit visas as jobs [N327].

## Hajj visa

Within Bangladesh's yearly quota [24, N192]. 2027 packages cost about Tk 5.1-6.2 lakh (government) or Tk 5.1-7.9 lakh (private) [N193, N194].

1. **Register** on [hajj.gov.bd](https://hajj.gov.bd/), at an Islamic Foundation office or a Union Digital Centre, for a government package or one from an approved agency [113, N15, N191].
2. **Pay the rest by the deadline** (10 Dec 2026 for Hajj 2027), with a passport valid to 31 Dec 2027 [N191].
3. **Don't do Hajj on any other visa:** fine up to SR 20,000, deportation and a ban [N16].

## Family residence

For a worker's spouse, sons under 25 and unmarried daughters, if the worker's permit is graded skilled or high-skilled [20, N244, 21]. Plan on a salary of SR 3,500-6,000 a month and a registered (Ejar) rental contract [N244, N245, N240]. The visa fee is SR 2,000, then SR 400 per family member a month [N9, N300].

1. **Get e-apostilles** for the marriage and birth certificates and your degree on [mygov.bd](https://www.mygov.bd/); Saudi Arabia accepts them instead of embassy attestation [N265, N266, N277].
2. **The worker applies on Absher:** My Services, Expatriates Services, New Family Visa Application. Enter each family member's name and passport number as on the passport, date of birth, religion, relationship and the city where the visa will be stamped (Dhaka), and submit. If Absher shows an error, apply at the recruitment (Istiqdam) office instead [N300, N9].
3. **Print and sign the form and give it to your company's HR,** which approves it online through the Chamber of Commerce (SR 30) within 30 days [N300].
4. **Pay the visa fee** through SADAD in your bank app (at Al Rajhi: Payments, Government Payments, Labor Importation) with your iqama number. After 2-3 working days Absher shows the visa number; send it to your family [N300].
5. **The family applies at the Tasheer visa centre** (booked as in company worker step 5) with passports valid 6 months, the visa number, photos, and the apostilled marriage and birth certificates translated into Arabic; it takes 3-7 working days [N300, N9].
6. **In Saudi Arabia,** within 90 days the family does a medical test, gets health insurance and gets iqamas, paying the fees through SADAD [N300, N9].

Dependants can't work without separate permission. Register a baby on Absher within 30 days and at the Bangladesh Embassy [N9, N10, 22].

## Family visit visa

For a spouse, children, parents, parents-in-law, siblings, grandparents or grandchildren of someone living in Saudi Arabia; USD 101 plus insurance, single entry, up to 30 days [23, N196, N11].

1. **Your relative applies** on the Saudi Foreign Ministry's visa platform ([visa.mofa.gov.sa](https://visa.mofa.gov.sa/)), with at least 3 months left on their iqama [131, N301, 23]. They log in with Nafath, choose Resident, then Family visit visa, press Add and enter your details exactly as on your passport (name, birth date and place, occupation in Arabic, phone, religion, relationship, passport number and dates, entry point, and single or multiple entry), accept the agreement, save and print [131, N301].
2. **Their employer approves it online;** a domestic worker's employer approves it on Absher [N301, 23]. When a "Document Number" replaces the application number, the visa is approved, and your relative sends it to you [N301].
3. **You apply at the Tasheer visa centre** (booked as in company worker step 5) with your passport, photos, the visa number and a marriage or birth certificate [23, N196].
4. **Don't work.** A visit visa can't become a job [23, N12].

## Tourist visa

Bangladeshis can't get the online e-visa unless they hold a used US, UK or Schengen visa [25].

- **Apply at the Tasheer visa centre,** booked at [vc.tasheer.com](https://vc.tasheer.com/appointment): USD 101 plus insurance [25, N264].
- **Or buy a Package Visa:** a flight-and-hotel package from an approved Saudi company such as Reserval or Almosafer, from SR 4,000 per adult, visa in 48 hours [26, N199, N200].
- **Don't work.** "Tourist visa plus job" is illegal [25, N12].

## Business visit visa

The Saudi business requests it on the KSA Visa platform, then you apply at the Tasheer visa centre: USD 101 plus insurance, 1-2 working days [27]. Meetings and events only; a "business visa" for a factory job is illegal [27].

## Student visa

Apply free on [studyinsaudi.sa](https://studyinsaudi.sa/) for up to 5 universities; agents who charge aren't authorised. Age limits are 25 for a bachelor's, 30 for a master's and 35 for a PhD, and English-taught courses usually want IELTS 5.5 [N247]. Scholarships cover airfare, housing, food, tuition and an allowance, and the university requests the visa [28, N201].

## Other visas

- **Temporary and seasonal work:** up to 90 days plus one extension, requested by the employer; it can't become an iqama [8, N250]. The Hajj-season work visa lasts 4 months: entry from 1 Shawwal to the end of Dhul Qadah, exit by the end of Muharram; the employer pays a SR 1,000 fee and a SR 2,000 refundable guarantee, and it can't be renewed or converted [N313]. **Need to know:** whether these are issued to Bangladeshis.
- **Transit:** up to 96 hours, requested when booking with Saudia or flynas [N19].
- **Premium Residency:** residence not tied to an employer, for people with high income or investment [29, 109].
- **Medical:** for treatment; USD 101 plus insurance [107, 25].

## Changing visa type

- **Visit, tourist, Umrah or business visit visa to a work iqama:** not allowed. The Passports Directorate says a visit visa can't become an iqama [N324], and Umrah visas can't be used for work [N325]. Working on one can bring a ban of up to 10 years [N243, N173].
- **Temporary or seasonal work visa to an iqama:** not allowed [8, N250].
- **Family dependant to worker:** a spouse or other dependant aged 18 or over with a valid iqama can work in health or education without changing sponsor. The employer logs in to Ajeer, chooses Ajeer Dependents Service, fills in the contract and issues the permit, free. The dependant needs the professional accreditation for the job, and the employer must not be in the red band of Nitaqat, the Saudisation rating [N322]. **Need to know:** whether a dependant can work in other sectors or move onto an employer's own work iqama.
- **Domestic worker to another household:** on Musaned, with the consent of the current employer, the worker and the new employer. Payments go through Musaned, within maximums the ministry sets: SR 16,188 for a Bangladeshi worker [N323]. **Need to know:** whether a domestic worker can move to a company job.
- **Company worker to another company:** see If you want to change employer.
- **Job title on the iqama:** the employer applies on Qiwa with the Change expatriate profession service; it is free and takes effect immediately [144]. In January 2026 the ministry suspended some job-title changes on Qiwa and listed general manager, sales representative, marketing specialist and procurement manager as titles for Saudis [N321].

## Asylum

Saudi Arabia is not a party to the 1951 Refugee Convention and has no asylum system [N326]. Its law allows political asylum "if public interest so dictates", but the government generally doesn't grant it [N326]. People in the country illegally, including those who overstayed a pilgrimage visa, are refused refugee status, and people without residency are pressed to leave or deported [N326]. UNHCR, the UN refugee agency, can get people it recognises a temporary stay as visitors, only if they hold valid identity papers [N326].

## Sending money home

- **Use a legal channel and compare fees;** STC Pay has been cheapest and banks dearest [105]. STC Pay, URpay, Mobily Pay, Western Union and MoneyGram send straight to bKash [106]. Your family gets a 2.5% government bonus on legal transfers automatically, with no documents [N174, N253].
- **Don't use hundi.** Saudi police arrest people for it, and Bangladesh freezes mobile money accounts used for it [N175, N177, N176].

## Going home without valid papers

Overstaying any visa can mean a fine of up to SR 50,000, up to 6 months in jail, deportation and a ban [N26].

- **With an expired iqama, no iqama or a huroob report:** apply to the Bangladesh Embassy's Special Exit Programme for an exit visa at [bangladeshembassy.org.sa/sepNew](https://www.bangladeshembassy.org.sa/sepNew/), with no need to visit or call, and track it by iqama number at [bangladeshembassy.org.sa/seppub](https://www.bangladeshembassy.org.sa/seppub/) [103]. If you're given an appointment, print it and bring your original iqama and a passport copy [103]. Outside Riyadh, take the embassy's letter to the labour office, then collect the exit visa from Jawazat in person [103]. It doesn't cover women domestic workers or people with police or court cases, and it can take months [103, N169].
- **Without a passport:** ask the embassy's passport wing (8001000126) for an outpass, a one-way travel permit [N211, 34]. **Need to know:** the documents needed. The employer pays the deportation ticket, or the Saudi government if you can't [N221].
- **Watch Absher for amnesties,** when people can leave without the usual penalty [36, N172].
- **To get a worker brought home,** a relative in Bangladesh applies to the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare (aswelfare@probashi.gov.bd) with proof that the person is a migrant worker, the workplace and home addresses in Saudi Arabia, the agency's name and address, and the employer's mobile number. It's free; within 7 working days the ministry asks the embassy, the Welfare Board or BMET to arrange the return [135].
- **Bans** run from 3 years for overstaying to 10 years for working on a visit, tourist or Umrah visa [N243, N173].

## If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job

1. **Call the embassy's death line,** +966570212180 [N186].
2. **The employer pays** to send the body home [100]. The family applies to the Wage Earners' Welfare Board in Dhaka, which arranges it, and pays if the employer doesn't [136]. BRAC's Migration Programme helps families apply [114, N178]. The family can also apply to the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare (aswelfare@probashi.gov.bd) with a description of what happened, the cause of death, copies of the worker's passport, iqama, BMET smart card and Saudi ID number, and the death certificate; the ministry acts within 7 working days, including for workers without valid papers [135]. If the family wants burial in Saudi Arabia instead, it says so in writing and the embassy arranges it [136]. It takes much longer if the worker had no valid papers [N178].
3. **At the airport,** the Welfare Board desk tells the family by phone what to bring, hands over the body at the cargo section and gives a Tk 35,000 cheque for transport and burial. Bring copies of the worker's passport, death certificate, air waybill and BMET card if any; a heirship certificate (warish sanad) and a family certificate from the Union Parishad chairman or ward councillor; the NID of the wife, father or mother; and the no-objection certificate (NOC) for the body. If someone else collects, bring a power of attorney. It's free [14, 136, 140].
4. **The Tk 3 lakh Welfare Board grant** goes to families of workers who left with BMET clearance or joined the Welfare Board through the embassy [136]. After the body arrives, the Board writes to the family listing the papers; send them to the Welfare Board, Probashi Kallyan Bhaban, 71-72 Eskaton Garden, Dhaka: a family-members certificate from the Union Parishad chairman, mayor or city councillor; an undertaking on Tk 400 non-judicial stamp paper; a statement of the receiving person's account at any scheduled bank, with the account and routing numbers; and 2 passport-size colour photos of each heir. Samples of the first two are on [wewb.gov.bd](https://www.wewb.gov.bd/). It's free, and the money goes straight into that account, with an SMS, within 60 working days [140].
5. **The insurance** bought at BMET clearance covers 5 years [11, 136]. It pays Tk 10 lakh for death; if the body comes home, no application is needed [11, 140]. If the worker was buried in Saudi Arabia or died after coming home, the family applies online at [portal.wewb.gov.bd](https://portal.wewb.gov.bd/) or at the Board with copies of the BMET card and passport [140]. For permanent disability it pays 10% to 100% of Tk 10 lakh depending on the damage: the worker applies with copies of the BMET card, passport and medical papers and one 6R colour photo showing the disability [140]. Claims are free and paid within 30 working days [140]. It pays Tk 50,000 if you lose the job, are injured or fall ill and return within 6 months, unless the employer paid all your migration costs; apply with copies of the BMET card and the passport pages with the departure and arrival stamps [11, 136, N180, 140]. Injured or seriously ill returnees can also get up to Tk 2 lakh from the Welfare Board for treatment: the family applies on the Board's web portal, at a district welfare centre or at the Board with the BMET card, passport, medical papers and a 6R colour photo of the worker; the Board checks by video call with the worker and pays within 60 working days [140]. The airport desk takes sick returnees to a government hospital in the Board's ambulance, free [136, 140]. It doesn't cover illness you had before leaving or coming home because of serious illness, and claims are usually rejected when the death is recorded as suicide [N180].
6. **For Saudi compensation and unpaid wages,** the family sends the embassy a power of attorney attested by Bangladesh's Foreign Ministry [N182], or applies to the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare (aswelfare@probashi.gov.bd) with copies of the worker's passport, iqama, BMET smart card and Saudi ID number, the employer's name and full address, and the wages, allowances and insurance owed; the ministry instructs the embassy within 7 working days, and the Welfare Board pays what is collected to the heirs, against the same papers as the grant (item 4) [135, 136, 140]. The Board's legal cell helps the family prepare the power of attorney [140]. Death or total disability from a work injury pays 3 years' wages, up to SR 56,000, but only if the employer registered the worker with GOSI, Saudi social insurance [N143].

## Coming home

- **Goods:** each year you can bring up to 100 grams of gold jewellery and 200 grams of silver duty-free, and one new mobile phone, or two if you hold a BMET card and stayed abroad at least 6 months [N293]. One gold bar of up to 10 tola a year is allowed at Tk 5,000 duty per tola. Declare anything above this on the form handed out on the plane, before leaving the customs hall [133, N293]. Bring no alcohol, and fill in an FMJ form for cash over USD 10,000 [133].
- **Help after return:** BRAC's Migration Programme helps returnees at the airport and with counselling, reintegration and legal help: BRAC Centre, 75 Mohakhali, Dhaka, 02-222281265 [114]. Women who return within a year after abuse or fraud get Tk 5,000 in cash at the airport welfare desk, and sick returnees are taken to hospital [14]. The Welfare Board desks also help with customs and lost luggage [136]. **Need to know:** a direct migration line at BRAC.
- **A place to stay in Dhaka:** the Welfare Board's Wage Earners' Centre at Borua, near Shahjalal airport, puts up workers flying out or back for a short stay: book at [wec.wewb.gov.bd](http://wec.wewb.gov.bd/wewb-centre/booking/search) with your BMET card, passport and air ticket; it costs Tk 100 to register and Tk 200 a night, with free transport to the airport [136, 140]. At Shahjalal airport, the Probashi Lounge gives workers food at up to 30% off, free wifi and printing; show your BMET card [140].
- **If your family is threatened or its property is seized while you are away,** apply to the Welfare Board, directly or through its district welfare centre; it writes to the district's Deputy Commissioner or Superintendent of Police to act [136, 140]. Complaints about the Board's own services go online at [wewb.gov.bd](https://www.wewb.gov.bd/) [136].
- **Business loan:** apply at a PKB branch; PKB lends returnees starting a business up to Tk 50 lakh over 10 years with collateral, or Tk 3 lakh without, at 9% [N255].

## Saudi Arabia as a destination

- **Numbers:** in 2025 over 750,000 Bangladeshis went to Saudi Arabia for work, more than two-thirds of the over 1.1 million who went abroad, and up 16% from about 628,000 in 2024 [N207]. Bangladeshis are the largest foreign group in Saudi Arabia, about 2.59 million, ahead of Indians (2.31 million), Pakistanis (2.23 million), Yemenis (2.21 million) and Egyptians (1.8 million) [N315]; another estimate is 3.5 million [N207].
- **Other markets:** Malaysia stopped normal recruitment from Bangladesh in June 2024, and recruiters say Oman and the UAE are shrinking or closed to new workers. In December 2024, 89% of Bangladeshi workers who went abroad went to Saudi Arabia [N316].
- **Past bans:** Saudi Arabia restricted recruitment from Bangladesh in 2008, reopened to women domestic workers in early 2015 and to men in mid-2016. From November 2016 to January 2017 its Dhaka embassy required a quarter of each agency's workers to be women [N317]. From 13 April 2025, ahead of Hajj, it stopped issuing business visit, tourist and family visit visas to Bangladeshis and citizens of 13 other countries, after people used such visas for Hajj [N318, N30].
- **Growth:** Saudi Arabia is building for the AFC Asian Cup 2027, the Asian Winter Games 2029, Expo 2030 and the 2034 FIFA World Cup, which needs 15 stadiums. Plumbers, pipe fitters, welders, electricians and AC mechanics are in demand [N319]. Vision 2030 and construction projects may create up to 300,000 jobs for Bangladeshis in 2026 [N207].
- **Jobs closing to foreigners:** under Saudisation, more jobs are reserved for Saudis each year. In 2026 housing supervisor jobs became Saudi-only, and firms must fill 60% of sales and marketing jobs and 30% of engineering jobs with Saudis [N320].
