Working on a tourist or Umrah visa can bring jail, deportation and a 3 to 10 year ban (Going home without valid papers).

**Seasonal work visa to an iqama: not allowed** [E0067].

## Asylum

Saudi Arabia has no asylum system, hasn't signed the 1951 Refugee Convention and generally doesn't grant asylum [E0072, E0074]. People there illegally, including pilgrimage overstayers, are refused refugee status and deported [E0074]. The only protection is UNHCR, the UN refugee agency: people it recognises may stay temporarily, with valid identity papers, until resettled or returned; those without travel documents are sometimes detained indefinitely [E0073, E0075].

Apply by email to saprstaf@unhcr.org with [E0164]:

- your full name;
- family members' names;
- date of birth, sex and nationality;
- address and phone;
- any earlier UNHCR registration;
- the reason.

Toll-free line 009668001000281, Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday [E0163]. The Riyadh office, in the Diplomatic Quarter, is by appointment only [E0165].

## Sending money home

**Use a legal channel,** never hundi (the illegal informal channel) or unlicensed agents [E0046]. Saudi police have arrested people for sending money outside legal channels, and Bangladesh has frozen bKash, Nagad and Rocket accounts linked to hundi [E0079, E0078]. Without a valid iqama you can't use a bank or send money formally [B0112].

**Compare costs** on the World Bank's price comparison, [remittanceprices.worldbank.org/corridor/SA/BD](https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org/corridor/SA/BD). Sending SR 750 cost [E0080]:

- about 7-9% through STC Pay, TeleMoney, Western Union or MoneyGram; STC Pay delivers in under an hour to cash pickup, a bank account or a mobile wallet;
- about 15-16% at bank branches, which charge SR 75.

> **Need to know**
> - What documents do STC Pay, TeleMoney and the other remittance apps need to open an account?

**To a bKash account:**

1. **You'll need** [E0083]:
   - the family member's bKash number;
   - their full name exactly as registered when the account was opened;
   - the cash or money in your app.
2. **Use a bKash partner:** Saudi Awwal Bank, STC Pay, URpay, Mobily Pay, tiqmo, barq, Western Union or MoneyGram [E0082].
3. **Give the number and name with the cash, or enter them in its app** [E0083].

The limit is Tk 2.5 lakh per transfer, as many times as you like [E0084]. Your family gets the money plus the 2.5% government incentive automatically, and can cash out at BRAC Bank, City Bank and Q-Cash ATMs for Tk 7 per thousand [E0076, E0081, E0085, E0084].

**If money doesn't arrive or goes to the wrong number:**

1. Complain to the sender company and bKash (16247) [E0083, E0173].
2. Then complain to the company's complaint cell [E0173].
3. Then complain to Bangladesh Bank's Customer Interest Protection Centre (16236 during office hours on working days, bb.cipc@bb.org.bd, or its online form) with a description, your name, address and phone, and the provider's final reply; a relative needs your written authorisation [E0172, E0174].

## Going home without valid papers

Overstaying any visa can bring a fine up to SR 50,000, up to 6 months in prison and deportation [E0053]. The re-entry ban, from departure, is usually 3 to 5 years for overstaying or huroob and 3 to 10 years for working on a tourist or Umrah visa [E0057, E0058, E0059]. Leaving without a legitimate reason means paying your own ticket [E0136].

**Special Exit Programme (SEP),** the Bangladesh Embassy's route to an exit visa.

1. **Check you qualify:**
   - You qualify if your sponsor's company is under the labour office of Riyadh, Al Qassim, Hail, Al Jouf, Northern Borders or the Eastern Province, and your iqama has expired, was never issued or carries a huroob report; house drivers and male domestic workers with an expired iqama also qualify [E0027].
   - Excluded: women domestic workers; anyone who didn't leave on an earlier exit visa; anyone with a police or court case or wanted (matlub), who must settle it with that authority first [E0028].
2. **You'll need** [E0029, E0030]:
   - your iqama number;
   - your original iqama;
   - a passport copy showing its validity.
3. **Apply online** at [bangladeshembassy.org.sa/sepsrv.html](https://www.bangladeshembassy.org.sa/sepsrv.html) ("Special Exit programme(SEP) Application Form"); no visit or call needed [E0026, E0031].
4. **Check progress** with your iqama number at [bangladeshembassy.org.sa/seppub](https://www.bangladeshembassy.org.sa/seppub/); when an appointment appears, print it and go to the embassy on that day with the documents [E0031].
5. **Get the exit visa:**
   - For a Riyadh company, the embassy gets labour office clearance; you then hand in your original iqama and passport copy, and the embassy sends them to Jawazat for the exit visa [E0029].
   - For a company under another labour office, or a huroob case with a sponsor outside Riyadh, the embassy gives you a letter to take to that labour office or Jawazat, where you collect the exit visa [E0029, E0030].

There is no time limit; workers have waited months, mostly without work [E0096].

> - What can a worker do if an SEP application is refused?

**Sponsors under other labour offices** (Makkah, Madinah, Tabuk, Asir, Jazan, Najran, Al Baha) aren't covered by SEP [E0027]. Contact the Jeddah consulate's labour wing (Contacts) [E0166].

> **Need to know**

**Any other papers problem:**

3. Then have a relative write to the ministry (below) [E0004, E0005].

- book "Consular & Welfare Service" at [passportriyadh.com/appointment](https://passportriyadh.com/appointment/) with your name, phone, passport number and OID number.

**If you are detained for deportation.** People picked up in raids have usually been held one to two weeks in a deportation centre, then flown straight home, often with nothing [SG0032, SG0033]. ## If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job

**If a worker dies in Saudi Arabia:**

1. **Call the embassy:** +966570212180, or the labour wing free on 8001000125 [E0086, E0024]. It registers the death, follows up with the employer and the authorities, and issues the no-objection certificate (NOC) needed for burial or repatriation [E0033, E0034].

   The cause of death is recorded in Saudi Arabia, usually as "natural", and Bangladesh does not check it [E0093]. The employer pays to prepare and fly the body home unless the family agrees to burial there [E0135]. If the worker had no valid papers, bringing the body home takes much longer [E0090].

   > **Need to know**
   > - How can a family ask for a post-mortem in Saudi Arabia?
   > - How can a family challenge the recorded cause of death?

2. **Tell the Wage Earners' Welfare Board** (Probashi Kallyan Bhaban, 71-72 Eskaton Garden, Dhaka) whether the family wants the body brought home or buried in Saudi Arabia; the Board relays this to the embassy and follows up until the body arrives, free [E0145, E0017]. It pays repatriation for workers who left with BMET clearance or joined it through the embassy, and asks the ministry to pay for others [E0091].

   > **Need to know**

   > - Which papers does the Board need from the family?

   Or apply to the ministry's Welfare-2 branch (aswelfare@probashi.gov.bd) by email to bring the body home; free, action within 7 working days [E0004]. **You'll need** [E0004]:
   - a description;
   - the cause of death;
   - the death certificate;
   - copies of the worker's passport, work permit or iqama, BMET smart card and Saudi ID number.

   BRAC, a Bangladeshi development organisation, helps families apply through its Migration Programme (Coming home) [E0111, E0143].
3. **At the airport,** the Board's desk calls the family, hands over the body at cargo with a Tk 35,000 cheque for transport and burial, and its ambulance carries the body from Dhaka, Chattogram or Sylhet airport, free [E0042, E0007, E0008]. **You'll need** [E0007, E0042]:
