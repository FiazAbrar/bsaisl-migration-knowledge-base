## Work visa: company worker

For a job with a Saudi company. The whole process usually takes about 1-3 months [A0004].

**Who pays what.** The Saudi employer pays the recruitment fees, the iqama (residence card), the work permit and its renewals, exit and re-entry fees and your ticket home when the job ends [A0005]; you pay that ticket only if you are unfit for the work or leave without a legitimate reason [A0006]. You pay:

- OEP registration: Tk 200 [A0028].
- A TTC course admission fee, if you take a course: Tk 748 [A0030].
- A passport: Tk 4,025 for a 48-page, 5-year e-passport, regular delivery [A0124].
- The skill test: USD 85 [A0007, A0008].
- The Wafid medical booking (USD 10) and tests (step 4) [B0004].
- Police clearance (step 6) [B0052].
- The Wage Earners' Welfare Board fee: Tk 5,500 [A0011].
- Migrant worker insurance: Tk 1,000, covering death or permanent disability for Tk 10 lakh over 5 years [A0012].
- Source income tax at BMET clearance: Tk 500 for a Saudi single visa [B0096].

For some job orders BMET caps what the agency may take, such as Tk 50,000 or Tk 58,610 (Before paying, below) [A0016, A0019].

### Step 1: Find a real job

1. **Register on the Overseas Employment Platform (OEP),** BMET's website for applying for TTC training, the compulsory 3-day pre-departure orientation and BMET clearance [A0109, A0027].

   You'll need [A0026, A0029, A0027]:

   - your NID (national ID card);
   - a mobile phone with that number;
   - a bKash, Nagad or other mobile banking account with Tk 202;
   - a recent clear photo that matches your face today.

   1. In Google Chrome open [employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/login-with-otp](https://employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/login-with-otp) (or scan the QR code on a BMET or TTC poster), go to registration and type every detail exactly as on your NID, without dots in your name; verify with the OTP (one-time code) sent to your phone, then Submit and "Save and continue" [A0026].
   2. Fill in the rest, choose Mobile Banking at payment, then bKash, Nagad or another service, and enter your account number, OTP and PIN; the fee is Tk 200 (Tk 202 with the charge), paid only online inside OEP [A0026, A0028, A0029].
   3. Download the Registration Card [A0026]. Take 2 printed copies of the registration slip to your district employment and manpower office (DEMO, BMET's office in each district) to give fingerprints [A0028]; addresses, phones and emails are in BMET's [list of DEMOs](https://bmet.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922e139933eb65569e2af6c) [A0112, A0113].

   Already registered? Log in with your mobile number and OTP [A0029, A0027]. Problems: OEP hotline 09614445271 [A0128].

2. **If a relative or friend in Saudi Arabia offers you a visa.** A visa for one or a few workers is a single visa (সিঙ্গেল ভিসা); one that comes through someone abroad is also called a personal or FNF (friends and family) visa (single and group visas, and how many go this way: Background, Channels) [SH0006, SI0023 (2020), RA0041 (2020)]. Even a visa that came free from family must then be carried by a Bangladeshi agency the Saudi embassy has approved (what to do: If a relative or friend offers you a visa, below) [SI0007].

3. **Learn a trade and get a certificate.** Workers in the trades Saudi Arabia lists must pass the Takamol skill test (step 3), which expects real skill and work experience in the trade [A0058, A0070]. BMET's Technical Training Centres (TTCs) run 3-month courses ending in a BNQF (Bangladesh National Qualifications Framework, the national skills standard) assessment [A0030, A0034]; BMET's [trades page](https://bmet.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922dc3c933eb65569e0f2b7) lists the trades and seats at every TTC [A0129].

   You'll need [A0030]:

   - your OEP registration;
   - an SSC pass or a JSC/class 8 pass, depending on the course;
   - age 18 to 40 (21 to 40 for driving);
   - Tk 748.

   1. Watch your TTC's website for the next session's admission notice, for example [TTC Gaibandha's notice board](https://ttc.gaibandha.gov.bd/pages/notices/6a1171662de6aeac14f7e3b4) [A0115]. Notices come out weeks before each three-month session, and applications stay open about four weeks (TTC Magura: 18 August to 15 September for October-December) [A0115, A0030].
   2. In OEP go to Training portal, Training, Trade/Skills, your division, district and institution, Search [A0027, A0032]; select the course, then Apply; if your photo matches your face today press Continue, then Process to Enroll and Confirm [A0027]. Applications are capped at three times the seats [A0030].
   3. Sit the written admission test at the TTC on the notice date (Bangla, English, maths, general science, overseas jobs, visas and labour law) [A0030].
   4. When an SMS says you passed, pay in OEP under Training Portal, My Course, Admission Fee: Tk 748; the driving course is free and includes the licence fee [A0030, A0031].

      > **Need to know**
      > - What happens if you fail the admission test or miss the fee deadline?
      > - Can you apply for the next session?

   5. Attend at least 80% of classes and sit the final assessment; competent trainees get Tk 150 a day for travel and food (Tk 100 for driving) when the budget allows [A0031]. The TTC helps with job placement [A0033].

4. **Ask licensed agencies what Saudi jobs they have, and check each one.** Recruiting without a licence under the Overseas Employment and Migrants Act 2013 is not allowed, and travel agencies may not send workers [A0125, A0093].

   You'll need: the agency's name or RL (recruiting licence) number.

   1. Search the name or RL number on [OEP's agency list](https://www.oep.gov.bd/agencies); the row shows licence status, validity date, office address and phone [A0089]. Go no further unless it says "Active" and the date hasn't passed (others are Expired, Suspended, Cancelled; licences are cancelled for visa fraud) [A0091, A0092].
   2. Ask the agency, at that address and phone, which Saudi jobs they have for your trade, and ask to see the employer's demand letter (company, jobs, pay) with the embassy's attestation (step 2) [A0089, A0108, A0107].
   3. Read workers' reviews on [Recruitment Advisor](https://www.recruitmentadvisor.org/search-an-agency), a trade-union site, giving what each paid, for which country, and whether they got the promised job and pay [A0094, A0095].

   **Which agencies.** No official grading of agencies exists, and the state minister for expatriates' welfare has said he could not confidently recommend any agency [SC0020, SC0021]. These had the most Saudi workers in the 1,148 permission letters BMET posted from June 2023 to December 2025 (70,145 workers from 370 agencies, counted by the team; workers means posts approved, not people who went); a high count shows activity, not fair treatment [SC0001]. Red flags are allegations or investigations, not court verdicts; the full list of agencies and flags is in the knowledge base's [agencies folder](https://github.com/FiazAbrar/bsaisl-migration-knowledge-base/tree/main/agencies).

   - **Greenland Overseas (RL0040, Active):** 44 letters, 7,421 workers [SC0001, SC0002]. Red flag: the ACC (Anti-Corruption Commission) filed a case in September 2025 against its owners, one of 13 agencies accused of forming a syndicate that charged Malaysia-bound workers nearly five times the government rate [SC0012, SC0013].
   - **Career Overseas Consultants (RL0403, Active):** 24 letters, 3,757 workers [SC0001, SC0003]. Red flag: Bloomberg (2026) reports agents' allegations that, during Malaysia's 2016-2018 hiring round, it and the other nine agencies Malaysia routed hiring through charged workers a syndicate fee of about USD 1,350; it denies collecting unlawful fees, and a 2018 lawsuit against the 10 agencies was withdrawn with no findings of wrongdoing [SC0016, SC0023, SC0022, SC0015].
   - **A.J. International (RL1304, Active):** 26 letters, 2,755 workers [SC0001, SC0004].
   - **Bashundhara Employment Services (RL1064, Active):** 12 letters, 2,090 workers [SC0001, SC0005].
   - **Amco Enterprise (RL1537, Active):** 28 letters, 1,933 workers [SC0001, SC0006].
   - **United Gulf Services (RL1036, Active):** 23 letters, 1,850 workers [SC0001, SC0007]. Red flag: Prothom Alo (September 2026) reports it is on Malaysia's 2026 list of 25 agencies and is owned by the wife of Abdul Hai, whose Greenland Overseas was in the earlier Malaysia syndicate; no allegation is made against United Gulf itself [SC0014].
   - **Bonanza Overseas (RL0579, Active, +88028881118):** 21 letters, 1,642 workers [SC0001, A0090]. The only one with outside evidence of fair recruitment: it was the Bangladeshi agency in an ILO-backed fair recruitment pilot for construction jobs in Qatar (2018-2021), not Saudi Arabia [A0096, A0097]. BMET capped its cost at Tk 58,610 per worker in one Saudi letter [A0019].
   - **Azam Overseas (RL0535, Active):** 10 letters, 1,221 workers [SC0001, SC0008].

   In July 2026 the ministry revoked 49 licences in the Malaysia syndicate case, among them Catharsis International (RL0549), Rabbi International (RL0258) and Sultan Overseas (RL0906), which held Saudi letters for 971, 936 and 887 workers; OEP shows them Cancelled [SC0019, SC0017, SC0018, SC0001, SC0009, SC0010, SC0011].

   **Brokers.** Since 2023 an agency may appoint only a person registered under the rules as its sub-agent [SG0005]. Pay the agency itself through its bank account (Before paying), not the broker [A0023], and write down the broker's name, age, parents' names and address, which a BMET complaint asks for [A0037].

5. **Check the company exists.** Saudi companies have a unified national number starting with 7, which replaced the commercial registration (CR) number [A0123, A0127].

   You'll need: the company's name or unified national number.

   1. On [mc.gov.sa/en/eservices/Pages/Commercial-data.aspx](https://mc.gov.sa/en/eservices/Pages/Commercial-data.aspx) (Ministry of Commerce, free, in Arabic), search the name or number, type the verification code and press Search; the registration data appear at once [A0098, A0099]. Help: 1900 in Saudi Arabia, cs@mc.gov.sa from Bangladesh [A0100, A0126].
   2. If the company isn't found or doesn't match the employer on your demand letter, don't pay, and complain about the agency to BMET (below) [A0098, A0107, A0056].

#### If a relative or friend offers you a visa

**The paper doesn't show where the visa came from.** A relative's visa is one of three things, and all three arrive as the same work visa [A0077 (2024), SI0007]:

- *A real job with the relative's own employer.* The employer pays the visa, recruitment fees, the iqama and their renewals, so the visa itself should cost you nothing [RA0010, A0005]. Relatives still charge: one migrant paid a close relative Tk 4 lakh for a visa the relative had got free [RA0003].
- *A visa the relative bought from a kafil (the sponsor) or a visa trader.* It has already cost about Tk 2 lakh before it leaves Saudi Arabia (Background, The root) [SI0037, SB0007], and the sponsor will pass his yearly levy and iqama fee to you (sub-step 7). These are the free (azad) visas, meaning you find your own work [SM0045], and new ones are issued as amel adi (ordinary labourer) or load-unload (লোড আনলোড) visas, because load-unload needs no skill test [RA0028, RB0045, RB0027]. The job may not be the one promised: a cousin paid Tk 4 lakh for a driver visa and the employer put the worker to herding goats, then camels [RA0001].
- *A visa the relative sells as a business.* A returnee broker in Cumilla charged Tk 3 lakh a head and passed most of it to the friends in the Gulf who supplied the visas [SL0021]. Many men present a relative as the source when they really bought from a broker [RB0006].

**What it costs, official against real.**

- *Genuine company visa:* you pay only the Bangladesh-side fees, about Tk 19,800 before the skill test, passport and ticket: those in Who pays what, plus medical about Tk 10,000, police clearance Tk 1,500, Tasheer Tk 660, orientation Tk 200 and BMET clearance Tk 250 [A0028, RB0021, B0052, RB0024, B0166, A0011, A0012, B0096]. In practice the agency's processing fee is whatever you negotiate [RB0047], and a visa seller can rent an agency's licence for Tk 15,000-20,000 a visa to get the clearance [RD0006].
- *Free visa:* the whole trip costs Tk 8-9 lakh in 2026 [RA0030]; in July 2026 two relatives of a Cumilla worker paid one man Tk 12 lakh between them, with Tk 4 lakh more due after arrival [RA0029].
- *Family isn't cheaper:* workers paid relatives Tk 286,642 on average, brokers Tk 303,351 and agencies Tk 243,531 [RD0023].

**You'll need from the relative, before any money moves** [B0076, B0073, SM0044, A0127, RB0013]:

- the 10-digit visa number;
- the sponsor's name and his Saudi ID, iqama number or company registration (CR) number;
- the occupation on the visa;
- whether the sponsor is a company, an establishment or a household;
- the wage;
- the name and RL (recruiting licence) number of the Bangladeshi agency that will hold the visa.

1. **Check the visa and the sponsor before paying anything.** On [ksavisa.sa/track-application](https://ksavisa.sa/track-application), the Saudi Foreign Ministry's visa platform, enter the visa number with the sponsor's Saudi ID, iqama or CR number (the inviter's number) and the captcha [B0073]. Check a company on the Ministry of Commerce site by its name or unified national number (item 5) [A0098, A0127]. For a household visa, the relative can have the sponsor check Musaned's Visa Status Inquiry with the sponsor's ID and the request number while you are on a call [RC0012]. Your own passport number shows nothing useful until the agency files the MOFA (sub-step 5), which attaches your passport to the visa [RB0023, SI0006].

   *The trap:* a PDF or photo on WhatsApp proves nothing. In Bishwanath, Sylhet, a family with a brother in Saudi Arabia took Tk 5 lakh for a driving visa and handed over a PDF of a different, three-month visa [RC0019, RC0020].

   *What it tells you:* if the sponsor is a person or household rather than a company, it is a household visa: you'll be a domestic worker, outside the Labour Law, working only for that household, not at the relative's company [SK0001, A0081 (2020)]. These are the classic free visas [SH0024 (2020)]: a Saudi household pays no levy on its first four domestic workers, so the visa costs it nothing a year to keep in levy [RH0001]. A load-unload or amel adi occupation carries the same warning [RA0028].

   *If it doesn't match* what you were promised (another sponsor, another job, no record), don't pay. Visas can also vanish when the labour ministry cancels applications filed on fake demand letters (Background, Current situation) [B0079, B0080].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Before the MOFA, does ksavisa.sa track-application show a work visa's sponsor and occupation from the visa number and the sponsor's ID or CR number, or nothing?

2. **Get the agency's name from the relative, check it, and get proof it holds the visa.** The worker rarely chooses: the okala goes to whatever agency the visa seller already deals with, sometimes one whose licence he rents [RD0006]. The sponsor files the okala (Arabic tafweed or wakala) on ksavisa.sa (still called Enjaz) under Authorisation on a visa, entering the visa number, his unified number, the agency's name, your city of arrival and the number of visas [RB0011, RB0012, RB0013, SI0004]. It takes one working day and the government fee starts at SR 75 (Tk 2,500); Saudi service firms charge about SR 200 to file it [RB0011, RB0015, RB0014]. This is the sponsor's cost, not yours [B0116].

   Check the agency's RL number on OEP: "Active", with a validity date not passed (item 4) [A0089]. Then, before paying the agency anything, get it to confirm in writing, or by a screenshot from its own login on the Saudi visa platform, that it holds the okala for your visa number [SI0003, A0076]. Later, check that the same agency appears on your MOFA printout (sub-step 5).

   *The trap:* once the okala is filed you are stuck with that agency. Moving means the first agency cancels both the okala and the MOFA, which costs money and time, doesn't always work, and often leaves the passport unusable for a new visa [RB0041, RB0042].

3. **Make sure the demand letter will be attested.** Since 2025 single visas, like group visas, need the employer's demand letter attested by the Bangladesh Embassy in Riyadh, which does it online on beriyadh.org for registered companies (Step 2) [A0087, A0102]. A sponsor who won't attest is a warning: companies that bring workers only to let them loose without an iqama are slow to give a demand letter [RB0033].

   > **Need to know**
   > - In November 2026, does BMET clear a one-person Saudi visa without the attested demand letter?
   > - How does a household sponsor, who can't register on beriyadh.org as a company, get one attested?

4. **Pay in stages you can check, and so you can prove it.** The relative will usually want money first, because he has already paid the kafil in riyals; your family pays taka at home to his family, outside the banks, and part leaves by hundi (হুন্ডি, informal money transfer) [RA0018, SB0010]. Tie each payment to something you have checked yourself: nothing until the visa and sponsor check out (sub-step 1) and the agency confirms it holds the okala (sub-step 2); a part once the MOFA printout shows the promised sponsor and agency (sub-step 5); the rest only once the e-visa is issued and checked (Step 7). Pay by bank or bKash to a named account, never cash: in a Banshkhali case a Saudi-based man took Tk 3.58 lakh through his wife for a visa he never sent, and police confirmed the fraud from the bank and bKash records [RA0031, RA0032].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Do families take a stamp-paper deed or a security cheque from a relative selling a visa?
   > - Has either ever won a case?

5. **Let the agency carry the file, in this order.** From medical to flight usually takes 1.5-2.5 months, a month at the fastest [RB0031].

   1. *Medical:* book the Wafid slip yourself (Step 4). Agencies say you can't, and take it for about Tk 1,500 (USD 10 plus about Tk 200 for the shop); with the centre's Tk 8,500 the medical comes to about Tk 10,000 [RB0044, RB0021, B0004]. The result is valid two months [B0017].
   2. *MOFA (মোফা):* after a fit result the agency files your visa application on Enjaz with your passport and photo; from then a search shows the sponsor's name and the application number (Step 7) [RB0023, SI0006 (2022)]. Check the sponsor and agency on it before paying the next part. Drop out after MOFA and the agency charges a "MOFA fine", usually Tk 3,000 [RB0023]. Some agencies won't file until you bring police clearance, so apply early (Step 6) [RB0025].
   3. *Skill test,* if the occupation is a listed trade (Step 3) [A0058].
   4. *Tasheer fingerprints:* the agency books the slip, which is free, so an agency asking money for it is overcharging; you pay Tk 660, or about Tk 2,200 if put in the Lounge (Step 5) [RB0026, RB0024]. Once all papers reach the Saudi mission the e-visa can be issued in 3-4 days; agencies quote a week to two months [RB0046, RB0029]. Check the e-visa yourself (Step 7) [B0075, B0077].
   5. *Orientation and BMET's own fingerprint:* the 3-day orientation (Step 8), then a second fingerprint, BMET's bio-enrolment ("bio finger"), for the clearance card [RB0028].
   6. *BMET clearance (ম্যানপাওয়ার):* the agency applies, in 3 working days (Step 8) [B0095]. BMET's charter lets a worker who got a visa on his own apply alone [A0057, B0098], but after the okala the visa sits in the approved agency's name, so the agency handles the okala, MOFA and Tasheer and in practice the clearance too [SI0007, SI0004].
   7. *Payment and ticket:* you settle the agency's bill before it takes the clearance and buys the ticket [RB0030].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Since September 2025, has BMET's emigration branch cleared a Saudi single visa for a worker who applied without an agency?

6. **Know who answers if it fails.** The visa seller signs the agency a written undertaking (মুচলেকা, muchleka) taking all liability for the workers, so on a single visa the agency is liable for nothing beyond the clearance papers [RD0007, RD0008]. The agency got perhaps Tk 20,000 of the Tk 4-5 lakh, so a BMET case against it recovers little, and single-visa workers who come back without work get back not even Tk 1 lakh [RD0010, RD0011]. If you named a relative as the source but bought from a broker, there is no one in Bangladesh to pursue [RB0006]. What to do if the relative cheats you: Before paying, "If the relative cheats you".

7. **After arrival.** In the first week set up Qiwa and Absher (Step 9): check that the job title and wage on your Qiwa contract match what you were promised, since what you approve there is the record [B0129, C0173]. Register on Absher with your iqama or border number, and every month check your iqama expiry and any "Absent from Work" flag (If your employer reports you absent (huroob)) [B0125, SM0040, C0112]. Keep your passport; the sponsor may not hold it (Step 9, Keep your passport) [B0114]. The iqama, permit, levy, renewals and any transfer fee (SR 2,000 for a first transfer) are the employer's [A0005, C0020, SM0016].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What does a free-visa worker pay a broker or kafil in 2026 to transfer his sponsorship to a real employer?

   *The iqama traps.* By law the employer gets and pays for your iqama within 90 days of arrival, and a late one costs the employer a SR 500 fine, not you [B0108, RC0033, C0020]. On a bought visa it rarely works that way. A three-month iqama is legal, but it is the classic sign of a free visa: if no job turns up before it runs out, you are illegal [RC0059, A0080 (2024)]. Nine men from Gurudaspur, Natore, each paid a villager settled in Saudi Arabia Tk 4-5 lakh; two years later none had an iqama, and his wife said it was because they hadn't paid extra for it [RC0051, RC0052]. On a free visa the kafil passes the levy and iqama fee to you (Background, The iqama) [SM0022, SG0054]. In 2026 kafils took SR 16,000-18,000 a year for making and renewing the iqama [RA0023]; one 2025 account puts the yearly cut, called faida (ফায়দা), at SR 10,000-12,000 on average, rising as the years go on [RA0027]. Anything above the levy and fees, or any levy demanded from a company worker, is the sponsor's cut. Brokers have sold a "one-year" iqama for SR 5,000 and handed over a three-month one [RA0024]. One worker flown home from a Saudi jail in November 2026 had paid a middleman Tk 3.7 lakh for an iqama that was never made [RC0056].

   *The sponsor's weapons:* working for anyone but the sponsor breaks Article 39 of the Labour Law, a SR 10,000 fine and deportation for the worker the first time [RA0009, D0030]; since February 2026 a business that lets its workers work for others faces up to SR 100,000 [RC0034]. So a sponsor whose worker is caught outside reports him absent (huroob), and one worker-writer described kafils filing theft cases, sometimes for over a crore taka, which stop the worker going home [RC0035]. Don't sign or thumbprint blank or unexplained papers for the kafil [RC0036].

   *If it fails, on a company visa:* no iqama after 90 days, complain on 19911 (Step 9) [B0109]. You can move employer without consent once your work permit has expired or was never issued [C0055], issue your own exit and re-entry or final exit visa on Absher or Qiwa [C0146], and if reported absent you have 60 days to transfer or take a final exit [C0063].

   *If it fails, on a household visa:* only the domestic worker rules apply. The ministry can move you to another employer if your iqama isn't issued or renewed within 30 days [D0031], but you cannot issue your own final exit visa; the employer does it on Absher, which is the kafil's real hold over free-visa men [SK0021].

   *Either way:* the embassy's online form has a "no iqama after arrival" option, and the Special Exit Programme covers workers whose iqama was never issued (Going home without valid papers) [E0089, E0027]. Until the end of 2026 an employer can still regularise a permit never issued within 6 months of joining [G0015].

**Women.** A woman going as a domestic worker should pay nothing and goes through a ministry-approved agency and Musaned (Work visa: domestic worker) [D0059, D0060]. A relative arranging it is a warning: in October 2026 a Muktagachha woman's cousin met her at the Saudi airport, had her sign an Arabic paper and, she says, had "sold" her to a sponsor for two years on a khadema (খাদেমা, housemaid) visa at SR 1,000 a month; he was arrested under the human trafficking law [RC0061, RC0044, RC0045].

**Avoid a "free visa".** There is no such thing [A0024]: it is a visa bought from a sponsor with no job for you, so you must find your own work and housing, often pay the sponsor a monthly cut and renewal money (Background, On a free visa), work illegally and can be deported [A0083, A0082, A0081, A0079]. Signs: the contract signed in Bangladesh isn't the one used in Saudi Arabia [A0081], and those in If a relative or friend offers you a visa (a household sponsor, a 3-month iqama). Have the papers checked (Before paying) [A0108]; report the seller to BMET or the ministry (below) or on the ministry hotline 16135 (+880 9610102030 from abroad) [A0056, A0048, A0025]; in an emergency call 999, the police emergency number [B0171].

The law reaches the relative. Section 31 of the Overseas Employment and Migrants Act 2013 (OEMA) makes it a crime for any person, not only an agency, to take money with a false promise of a job abroad or to keep a migrant's passport or visa: up to 5 years and a fine of at least Tk 1 lakh [RC0009, RC0002, RC0001]. These offences are cognisable, non-bailable and can't be settled privately [RC0010].   - **Village court (গ্রাম আদালত)** at the Union Parishad, for money owed under an agreement, receipt or other document, up to Tk 3 lakh since 2024: apply on Form 1 with a Tk 20 fee [RC0024, RC0025, RC0026].
  - **Complain to BMET or the ministry against the relative by name.** Section 41 lets anyone harmed complain against any person, not only an agency; the inquiry is meant to take 30 working days, and section 43 lets the government recover the money and pay it to you [RC0005, RC0006, RC0007]. Use the agency complaint route above, giving the person's name, age, parents' names and address [A0037].   - **If the police won't register it, file a complaint case (সি.আর. মামলা, CR case) at the magistrate's court,** as the Bishwanath victims did at the Senior Judicial Magistrate's court in Sylhet [RC0021]. ### Step 3: Pass the skill test

Workers in the listed trades must pass the Takamol skill test under Saudi Arabia's Skills Verification Program (SVP), which needs real skill and work experience in the trade [A0058, A0070]. It runs at 27 TTCs and BRTC Gazipur, the Bangladesh Road Transport Corporation's training institute [A0061, A0130].

You'll need [A0062, A0063, A0007]:

- your passport (valid);
- your NID;
- your own email address that you can open;
- a Visa or Mastercard endorsed for US dollars with USD 85 on it;
- the occupation on your visa.

1. **Check which centre tests your trade** in BMET's [list of occupations by test centre](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-bmet/2026/2/dc7e2b1f-5458-4a46-9197-958e2cd8941e.docx) ("TTC wise Occupation under SVP", on bmet.gov.bd under Training, Takamol); some centres test only loading and unloading, while Bangladesh-Korea TTC Dhaka tests 32 occupations [A0105, A0106].
2. **Register** on [svp-international.pacc.sa](https://svp-international.pacc.sa/) [A0117, A0118]: press Sign-up, enter your names, NID, passport number and expiry, country, nationality, email, date of birth and a password (8+ characters with a capital, a small letter, a number and a symbol), all exactly as in your passport; tick "Agree to acknowledge that all the data are correct" and type the code emailed to you [A0062]. With only one name, write it as the first name and your father's name as the last; the same name in both counts as an error [A0064]. With a wrong passport number you can't sit the test, so fix it first through Support at the bottom right [A0067].
3. **Book:** sign in with your email, password and the emailed code; in Account Dashboard press Start Verification and choose your occupation (fixed after this), Direct Assessment (In-person), your division or city (the centre is assigned automatically), the test language and a green date; tick "I declare that the information entered is correct" and press Confirm [A0063, A0064, A0068].
4. **Pay USD 85** with the card's number, CVV, expiry date and holder name, then press Pay; the test ticket appears under Reservation and is emailed to you [A0007, A0008, A0063, A0066]. Each retake costs the full fee again [A0009]. Pay no one more than the fee: there is no way to pass for extra money, and a worker caught paying to pass has the account locked and can't go to Saudi Arabia [A0070, A0071]. **If someone offers to fix the test for money,** report it to the police (call 999, or your local police station) [A0070, B0171], tell BMET's Takamol and Japan Support Centre (BMET Bhaban, 4th floor, Kakrail, Dhaka; 01901165981 or 01901165982) [A0072], and complain to BMET (Step 1, Before paying) [A0056].
5. **Prepare if you want:** a 3-day loading and unloading course costs Tk 2,000 at 24 TTCs, and its certificate isn't an SVP certificate [A0069]. Free courses [A0116]:

   - 5 days: gas station attendants (Kishoreganj TTC), sellers (Bangladesh-Korea TTC Chattogram);
   - 10 days: food and beverage counter staff (Dhaka Mohila TTC), barbers (Sylhet TTC), concrete finishers (Bangladesh-Korea TTC Dhaka), building and construction workers (Khulna, Jhalokathi, Moulvibazar, Nilphamari and Chapainawabganj TTCs).

6. **Take the test** with your original passport, a photocopy, the ticket and one photo, arriving before the 30-minute briefing [A0066, A0059]. The computer test is 15 questions in 30 minutes in your chosen language; the practical test is 1 hour 30 minutes, in Bangla [A0059, A0060]. Phones and bags go in a locker; looking at another candidate counts as cheating [A0066].
7. **To change the date,** cancel and rebook once, up to 96 hours before [A0068]. Miss it and you are marked absent; you can pay and book again 96 hours after the slot [A0068].
8. **Get the result** on the SVP portal, where anyone can check a certificate is genuine [A0073]. A score of at least 50% passes, and the certificate is valid for five years [A0104]. Pakistan's SVP partner says a pass is uploaded within 24 hours; after a fail you book a new test [A0119]. To correct the certificate, press Support at the bottom right and raise a ticket with your name, email, a subject and description, Request Type "Suggestion", Client "Test Taker", your country and passport number, attaching your passport, ticket and certificate [A0068].

   - full name;
   - email address;

   - your contact details;
   - the centre's name and city;
   - what happened;

   - to info@ghc.sa;
   - by post to P.O. Box 7431, Riyadh 11462, Saudi Arabia;
   - by hand to its office on Samha Street, Diplomatic Quarter, Riyadh (920020885).

   It says within 3 working days whether it accepts the complaint and decides within 30 days; the decision is final, and a centre at fault can be warned, re-checked, suspended or closed [B0151, B0152, B0019, B0025]. Its Grievance Committee hears migrants' appeals [B0026].
2. **In Bangladesh, complain about overcharging** to the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare's Enforcement-2 branch with your receipts, your full address and the centre's name and address (route: Step 1, Before paying, item 5) [B0033, A0048].

### Your rights at work

**Contract.** It must be recorded on Qiwa (Step 9), with a copy for you and the employer [C0016]. It runs for a fixed term, usually 2 years; with no length written it counts as one year, and it renews for the same length if both sides carry on [C0095, C0026]. It should state your leave, whether it is paid and who pays the airfare [C0152].

**Hours.** 8 a day and 48 a week (6 and 36 for Muslims in Ramadan), never over 12 a day in total [C0001, C0002, C0096], with a break of at least 30 minutes after 5 hours in a row [C0004]. From 15 June to 15 September, no private-sector work in direct sun from 12 noon to 3 pm; report a breach on 19911 or in the labour ministry's app (If a right is broken, below) [C0108, SG0026, C0157]. Heat stroke (confusion, slurred speech, fainting, seizures, very high temperature, hot dry or drenched skin) kills if treatment is delayed: get emergency care, move the person into shade, take off outer clothing and cool them with water [SG0027, SG0028]. Headache, nausea, dizziness, weakness and heavy sweating are heat exhaustion: get out of the heat, drink and get checked at a clinic [SG0029, SG0044].

**Overtime.** Full hourly wage plus half the basic hourly wage per extra hour; public-holiday work counts as overtime [C0005, C0010].

**Pay.** There is no minimum wage for foreign workers; the contract wage is what you can claim [C0092]. It is paid into your bank account on the contract date [C0006]. Never sign that you were paid before the money arrives; it can block a later claim [C0025]. Recruitment, iqama and work permit fees and renewals, job-title changes, exit and re-entry visas, transfer fees and your ticket home at the contract's end are the employer's and can't be taken from your pay [C0020].

**Rest and leave.**

- *Weekly rest:* one day off a week, normally Friday, not swappable for money [C0003].
- *Annual leave:* paid, at least 21 days, 30 after 5 years with the same employer, on dates the employer sets with 30 days' notice; untaken days are paid when you leave [C0007, C0009, C0008].
- *Paid holidays:* 4 days at each Eid, Founding Day (22 February), National Day (23 September) [C0010].
- *Sick leave:* up to 120 days a year, 30 at full pay, 60 at three-quarters, 30 unpaid [C0011].
- *Other paid leave:* 3 days for a child's birth, 5 for your wedding, 5 for the death of a spouse, parent or child, 10 to 15 days once for Hajj after 2 years; 12 weeks' maternity leave [C0012, C0013].

**Housing, food and transport.** The employer gives housing and transport or allowances for them [C0014]; food only if the contract says so [C0099].

**If a right is broken,** report it in the labour ministry's (Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development) app, on Google Play and the App Store, or in Tawakkalna, the Saudi government app: service Reporting Violations of Labor Regulations, sign in with Absher, pick the report type, give the workplace location, company details and proof; it is free, the ministry acts within 3 days and the status shows in the app [C0157, C0158]. Money owed is claimed through friendly settlement (If you aren't paid) [C0024]. Ministry help line: 19911, Sunday to Thursday 8 am to 5 pm, Arabic and English [C0050].

**Health insurance.** The employer must insure you [C0015]. Check your cover with the Council of Health Insurance's Insurance Information Inquiry at [chi.gov.sa](https://www.chi.gov.sa/en/ServicesDirectory/Pages/Eservices-CheckInsurance.aspx) [C0110].

If you aren't insured or the premium is taken from your pay, complain at [care.chi.gov.sa](https://care.chi.gov.sa/) (guest or account) with your details, the employer's contact details, what happened and scanned proof; it is handled within 15 working days without naming you to the employer [C0159]. The cover doesn't pay for serious illness, so go only if you are fit [C0101].

**Your passport.** No employer may keep your passport or iqama; the fine is SR 5,000, though sponsors still commonly do it [C0022, C0091]. Complain on the labour ministry's Beneficiary Page ([selfservice.hrsd.gov.sa](https://selfservice.hrsd.gov.sa/node/)) [C0023, B0120]: [hrsd.gov.sa](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/ministry-services/services/client-page) > Contact Us > Beneficiary Page > Start the Service > log in with Nafath > Complaints > Add a Request; choose the sector and categories, fill in the fields, press Send [C0154, C0155]. **Unfair dismissal.** If the employer ends the contract without a valid reason, you are owed 15 days' wages per year of service on an open-ended contract or the wages for the rest of a fixed-term one, and never less than 2 months' wages [C0033].

**Probation.** Up to 180 days if the contract sets it; either side can end the contract during it without compensation or end-of-service pay [C0017, C0018]. Who pays the ticket home: Background, What ties you to the employer, The first six months.

**Leaving without notice.** You may stop work without notice and keep all your rights if the employer doesn't meet its duties (for example, doesn't pay), moves you to another job without consent, assaults or mistreats you, leaves a known serious danger unfixed, or lied about the job when you signed [C0029, C0094]. If you just stop coming to work, the employer can report you absent (huroob, below) [C0062] and, after a written warning, dismiss you without end-of-service pay for over 30 days' absence in a contract year or 15 in a row [C0028].

### What can get you jailed

- **Don't bring** narcotic drugs, alcohol, anything with pork, poppy seeds (khas khas), khat, gutkha, pan masala, non-Islamic religious literature, obscene material, amulets or anything linked to black magic, including on your phone [C0181, C0102, C0160, C0103].
- **Never carry a parcel you haven't packed and checked yourself,** even for family [C0104]; Saudi Arabia executes foreigners for drug smuggling [C0107].
- **Medicines:** your own only, at most 30 days' supply, in original packs, with a medical report and a prescription under 6 months old, both translated into Arabic [C0105, C0106].
- **Don't work for anyone but the employer on your permit, or in another job,** even for a few hours [C0021].
- **Cybercrime** cases have brought Bangladeshis 6 months to 5 years in prison [G0003]; in October 2026 a Bangladeshi man and woman, held under the cybercrime law and on an adultery allegation, were executed for an "indecent act" in a mosque [G0007, G0001].

**Driving.** If you cause a crash that kills someone, the family can claim blood money (diya): a Riyadh court ordered an uninsured foreign driver to pay SR 300,000 per person killed and kept him in prison until he paid [SG0023]. Compulsory motor insurance pays victims up to SR 10 million, but the insurer can take that money back from a driver without the right class of licence or with an expired one [SG0020, SG0022, SG0021].

### If your employer reports you absent (huroob)

### Leaving at the end of the job

1. **Give notice:** 30 days before a fixed-term contract ends, 60 for an open-ended one [C0097]. A resignation counts as accepted after 30 days without an answer; the employer can delay it up to 60 days, and you can withdraw it within 7 [C0027].

3. **Collect what you are owed** within 1 week of the contract ending, or 2 if you ended it [C0093]. ### Bringing your family

Skilled workers may bring a spouse, sons under 25 and unmarried daughters (Family residence) [C0130]. Each work permit is graded High-skilled, Skilled or Basic on the employer's Qiwa account; you can ask for a wrong grade to be corrected, which depends on the job title in your Qiwa contract being right [C0173, C0174].

### Scams

Done to workers:

- **Free visa:** Step 1, Avoid a "free visa".
- **Shell-company visas:** visas in the names of companies that don't exist; the broker vanishes once the worker lands [C0133]. Check the company (Step 1, item 5).
- **Wrong visa or job:** brokers promise a 15-month iqama and send the worker on a 3-month tourist visa, after which the worker is illegal [C0132]; others promise one job and deliver another with a 3-month iqama [C0134]. Check the visa type and job title yourself before paying (Step 7).
- **Switched contract:** the contract handed over at the airport, or the one on arrival, can name a different employer, job or wage [SG0004, A0084]. Compare it with the attested contract (Step 2) and, after arrival, with your Qiwa contract, which is the one that counts (Step 9) [A0107, B0129]; being sent to a different job is grounds for a BMET complaint (Step 1, Before paying) [A0056].
- **Huroob removal for a fee** (huroob, above) [C0113], and **requests for your Absher or Qiwa codes,** which let someone act in your name [C0087].
