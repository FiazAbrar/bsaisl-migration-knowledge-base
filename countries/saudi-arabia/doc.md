# Saudi Arabia: migration guide

How to go to Saudi Arabia from Bangladesh, visa by visa, step by step. \[3\] cites an official source; \[N3\] a news or other third-party source, listed under "Needs an official source"; a year, as in \[N3 (2022)\], marks a source older than 2025. "Need to know" marks a fact not yet confirmed; "You'll need" lists what to have ready.

Tk is the Bangladeshi taka (1 lakh is Tk 100,000), SR the Saudi riyal and USD the US dollar. Riyal amounts are converted at SR 1 = Tk 33, the remittance rate on 7 November 2026 [SM0010]. BMET, the Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training, is the Bangladesh government agency for overseas employment and safe migration [A0111].

## Background

### In one minute

Saudi Arabia takes two of every three Bangladeshis who go abroad for work, though 2026 departures are 45% down on 2025 [E0129]. Every work visa belongs to a Saudi sponsor (kafil, কফিল), who can sell it, and it reaches the village through agents and brokers at several times its price [SB0007]. A man pays Tk 4-5 lakh or more, mostly borrowed, against a legal maximum of Tk 1.65 lakh [SB0024]. A cleaner or labourer earns SR 500-1,200 a month (Tk 16,500-40,000) [SJ0007, SL0033]. The sponsor holds the iqama (আকামা, the residence card), and a visa sold for a job that doesn't exist (a "free visa") leaves the worker without an iqama, work or pay [E0128]. A woman going as a domestic worker should pay nothing [D0059].

### Is it open now, and who can go?

Open as of 7 November 2026, and still the largest destination [E0129]. A man aged 21 to 60 can go on a visa for a named job, and usually needs a Saudi skill certificate; a woman going as a domestic worker must be 25 to 45 and needs none [SH0001, SM0036, D0039, D0161].

*Current situation (7 November 2026; re-check by 7 December 2026).* In February 2026 the Saudi labour ministry cancelled thousands of visa applications filed for jobs that didn't exist, and visas that had appeared on the Saudi system disappeared [E0127, B0079]. The Iran war and Houthi attacks on Saudi airports have cancelled over 1,000 flights since February; Biman flies Dhaka-Dammam daily from 7 November, Dhaka-Riyadh stays suspended, and Riyadh-bound workers go via Jeddah or Madinah [E0130, SH0007, SH0008, SH0009].

The market can shut suddenly: Saudi Arabia stopped hiring Bangladeshi men from 2008 to 2016, and in 2025 it stalled three times over paperwork rules, the skill test and the Hajj season [SB0031, E0126, SH0021, SH0016, SB0043].

*Three rules that hold every year.*
- Pay the bulk only after the visa is issued and you hold BMET clearance; BMET's permission letters allow the agency's fee only after visa, clearance and flight are confirmed [A0016].
- Fly before the entry visa expires: an expired Saudi work visa is very hard to renew [SH0010].
- Expect delays around Hajj: in 2025 block work visas on Qiwa paused for Bangladeshis through the Hajj season until about early July, and visit and tourist visas from 13 April [SB0043, E0123].

*Channels.* Visas come as single visas (সিঙ্গেল ভিসা) or group visas (গ্রুপ ভিসা), a group visa being 25 or more workers for one company [SH0006]. Nearly all go on single visas: 21.36 lakh workers against 90,000 on group visas in the five years to 2024, by BMET figures [RD0015]. A single visa is not the same as a visa sent by a relative. The channels:
- Resold visas: small Saudi firms sell demand letters to Bangladeshi brokers, Bangladeshis working in Saudi Arabia resell them, and Bangladeshi agencies, which often can't get job orders from employers directly, buy visas from Saudi agents and resell them down the chain (2020, 2024) [RD0005, RD0004, SB0012, SI0028].
- Relatives abroad, a major source: in IOM's (the International Organization for Migration's) 2019-2020 survey, 33.2% went on a visa relatives procured, against 42.3% through agencies [RD0021].
- Women domestic workers: through ministry-approved agencies and Musaned, the Saudi domestic-worker platform (Work visa: domestic worker) [D0060, D0157].
- Not BOESL (Bangladesh Overseas Employment and Services Limited, the state agency): under the 2025 agreement recruitment goes through private agencies [SB0003].

Whoever the visa comes from, a Bangladeshi agency the Saudi embassy has approved must carry the papers (Work visa: company worker, Step 1, item 2) [SI0007].

*Who the sponsor is.* The Bangladesh embassy in Riyadh sorts sponsors three ways:
- Company (শারিকা, sharika) [SM0044]: on a company visa the company often provides food and lodging [SH0026].
- Establishment (মুয়াসসাসা, muassasa), such as a shop, workshop or small contracting firm [SM0044].
- Household, as private driver (সায়েক খাস, sayeq khas) or household worker (আমেল মানজিলি, amel manzil) [SM0043]. Each Saudi household can recruit eight workers as drivers, gardeners or cooks, and in 2020 these household visas were being used for free visas [SH0024]. A worker on a household visa is a domestic worker in law, outside the Labour Law, even if promised a company job, and may work only for that household [C0131, SK0001].

A visa also names a profession (মিহনা, the job title on the visa): a man whose visa says farm worker but who works in a coffee shop or on a building site is fined if caught, and the kafil can deny knowing [SM0015]. Changing the profession is the employer's cost [C0020].

*Who qualifies.* The Saudi embassy in Dhaka's 2019 rules set higher minimum ages for some trades (industrial tailor 30, barber 35, garment shop worker 40), and drivers need a valid licence [SH0001, SH0003, SH0005]. The skill test, the Skills Verification Program (SVP, run by Takamol), is compulsory only for trades Takamol has listed, 108 by late 2026; the list includes street, hospital, office and roof cleaners, load-and-unload workers and construction workers, so most men going for labouring jobs need it [SM0036, SM0037, SM0038]. How to take it: Work visa: company worker, Step 3 [A0058].

*Size.* In 2025, 755,194 Bangladeshis went to Saudi Arabia for work; Qatar came next with 107,598 [SB0017]. Saudisation (reserving jobs for Saudis) closes some trades: housing supervisor became Saudi-only in 2026, and firms must give Saudis 60% of sales and marketing jobs [E0124, E0125].

### How a job reaches you, and who takes a cut

The choke point is the Saudi embassy in Dhaka: of 2,807 licensed agencies, only those it approves can file Saudi work visas, and in September 2025 BMET wrote that it did not know which agencies those are [SI0007, SI0009, SI0008].

*The root: a visa the sponsor owns.* An employer issues work visas itself, instantly, on Qiwa, the Saudi labour ministry's platform, if its Nitaqat rating (the Saudisation rating) is at least "medium green" and its government account has enough credit [SI0001, SI0002]. The visa costs about SR 2,000 (Tk 66,000) [SB0006]. A Bangladeshi labour supplier in Saudi Arabia said in October 2026 that sponsors take out two or three permits in their own name for workers they don't need and charge Tk 1-1.5 lakh each, so a permit is worth about Tk 2 lakh before it leaves Saudi Arabia [SI0036, SI0037, SB0007].

*The Saudi side: okala and MOFA.* On the Saudi visa platform the employer delegates the visa to a recruitment office [A0076]; agencies call this the okala (ওকালা, from the Arabic wakala, authorisation), and an okala visa sits in the agency's name, not a worker's [SI0004]. It becomes a named worker's visa when the agency files that worker against it; agents call this application the MOFA (মোফা, after the Saudi foreign ministry), and it shows the sponsor's name and an application number [SI0006]. Tasheer, the visa centre in Dhaka, takes the fingerprints and fills in the form on Enjaz, the Saudi foreign ministry's visa platform [SI0019, SG0043]. Since May 2023 Bangladeshis get Saudi work visas as e-visas, with no sticker in the passport [SM0001]. How to check the MOFA: Work visa: company worker, Step 7.

*The embassy's approved list.* In March 2023 Nazaha, Saudi Arabia's anti-corruption authority, arrested the Saudi embassy's own head of consular affairs and his deputy for taking money to issue work visas, so an agent's "embassy contact" is no guarantee [SB0036, SB0044]. The agencies with the most Saudi job orders, and the allegations against some, are under Work visa: company worker, Step 1, item 4 [SC0001].

*The Bangladesh side.* The Bangladesh embassy's labour wing in Riyadh attests the employer's demand letter, for single visas too since 2025 (Work visa: company worker, Step 2) [A0087]; TIB (Transparency International Bangladesh) found some officials attest without checks or for bribes [SB0009]. In October 2025 BMET's additional director general said employment contracts are found for only 3-4% of workers going to Saudi Arabia [SI0032], and workers often first see a contract at the airport [SB0013].

*The village end.* Most agencies deal with workers only through sub-agents beyond regulatory oversight [SB0013]. A village broker in Cumilla got Tk 20,000 from the agent for each man in 2024 [SJ0042]. Since 2023 an agency may use only registered sub-agents (Work visa: company worker, Step 1, "Brokers") [SG0005].

*Women domestic workers: the money should run the other way.* The Saudi household pays its recruitment office up to SR 11,750 for a Bangladeshi domestic worker (July 2026), and in 2020 the office paid the Bangladeshi agency about USD 2,000 per woman through Musaned, so she should pay nothing [SM0035, SI0029]; yet none of the returnee women IOM interviewed in 2020 had gone free [SI0030]. *The medical.* Brokers sell guaranteed "fit" results from the Wafid medical check (the Gulf health check) at centres allegedly controlled by a few influential owners who issue fake certificates; a worker who paid Tk 25,000 for a fit certificate was found unfit in Saudi Arabia and deported [SB0040, B0031]. The check itself: Work visa: company worker, Step 4.

### What you pay, and what you earn and keep

The legal maximum for a Saudi job is Tk 1.65 lakh, and the Saudi employer is meant to pay nearly every cost; in practice a man pays Tk 3.5-5 lakh or more, mostly borrowed [A0013, C0020, SJ0004, A0014].

*The rule.* The expatriates' ministry set the Tk 1.65 lakh maximum in 2016-2017 and has not changed it; in October 2026 the state minister said new country-by-country maximums are coming [SJ0019, SJ0001, SB0016]. BMET's permission letters for group visas put every Saudi-side cost, levy and airfare included, on the employer, and let the agency take only what the letter allows, Tk 50,000 or Tk 58,610 in two 2025 letters, by cheque or pay order [A0016, A0019]. The fees a worker legitimately pays in Bangladesh: Work visa: company worker, "Who pays what".

*What people really pay.*
- The whole cost: in 2026 agencies put it at Tk 3.5-5 lakh and a press investigation at about Tk 5 lakh on average, up to Tk 9 lakh [SJ0004, A0014].
- A free visa (also called an azad visa, আযাদ ভিসা): Tk 3-5 lakh through an agency and more through a broker, up to Tk 5-7 lakh [SM0045, SM0026, SM0011].
- The medical: the set fee is about Tk 10,000, but most workers pay double or more; a worker declared unfit pays Tk 30,000-80,000 for a "fit" report, and many make a "contract" (কন্ট্রাক্ট) with a broker in advance to be passed [SJ0013, SJ0014].
- Tasheer's fingerprints: about Tk 694, or about Tk 2,100 for the premium service [SJ0010].

*How they pay.* 81% of men borrow to go [SJ0025]. The lenders:
- Family: loans mostly interest-free [SJ0034].
- NGOs: 10-15% interest [SJ0034].
- Moneylenders (মহাজন): far higher interest [SJ0034].
- Probashi Kallyan Bank (PKB), the government bank for migrant workers: up to Tk 3 lakh at 9% without collateral once the worker has the visa (Work visa: company worker, Step 1, "Before paying") [A0052].

Workers pay intermediaries part in advance, often before the visa is confirmed, and mostly get no receipt [SB0013, SJ0040].

*The iqama: the employer's bill.* Each year a company worker's iqama costs the employer SR 9,150-10,350, plus compulsory health insurance [SM0022, SM0023]:
- iqama fee: SR 650;
- work permit fee: SR 100;
- expat levy: SR 800 a month (SR 700 for each foreign worker up to the number of Saudis in the firm);
- health insurance.

Every item is the employer's cost, and nothing may be deducted from wages for it [C0020, C0014]. If you are asked to pay any of it, complain to the labour ministry on 19911 (Work visa: company worker, "If you aren't paid") [B0109]. In practice some small kafils and supply companies deduct the iqama and health-insurance cost from wages [SM0033]. The work permit and levy can be renewed for 3, 6, 9 or 12 months at a time [SM0024]: that is how a kafil hands a worker a 3-month iqama and comes back for money every quarter [A0080]. Check the expiry date yourself on Absher, the interior ministry's app [SM0040].

*On a free visa you pay it yourself.* The worker bears the whole iqama renewal, SR 8,000-12,000 a year (Tk 2.6-4 lakh), plus a monthly or yearly cut to the kafil called kamishon (কামিশন) [SM0013, SM0012]. Workers on bought visas pay the kafil SR 5,000-6,000 (Tk 1.65-2 lakh) at each renewal [SG0002]. In 2026 workers told of paying a kafil Tk 4-8 lakh for a renewal that never came [SL0028].

*What the job pays.* Saudi Arabia sets no minimum wage for foreign workers; the contract wage is what counts [C0092]. Monthly wages (the first four from BMET's 2025 permission letters [SJ0012, SJ0003, SJ0002]):
- kitchen workers: SR 800 plus SR 200 for food;
- construction labourers: SR 1,100 with food and housing;
- carpenters, masons, steel fixers, electricians and painters: SR 1,200 plus SR 300 for food;
- heavy equipment operators: SR 1,500;
- cleaners, loaders and other low-skilled workers, nearly 80% of those going: SR 800-1,000 by recruiters' account [SJ0008, SJ0007]; cleaners promised SR 1,000-1,200 often get no more than SR 500 [SL0033];
- new house drivers: about SR 1,500-1,800 [D0077].

*What it costs to live.* A worker living outside a camp pays a month (November 2026) [SM0008]:
- shared room: SR 500-900;
- food he cooks himself: SR 500-800;
- transport: SR 150-300;
- phone and internet: SR 50-100.

*What reaches home, and how long the debt takes.* Many low-skilled Gulf workers can send only Tk 15,000-20,000 a month [SB0022]. ### What ties you to the employer

Your visa and iqama belong to your kafil, normally your employer [A0002]. A company worker can resign, leave, and after a year change employer without consent, but only with a valid iqama and a contract recorded on Qiwa; domestic and farm workers don't have these rights [C0053, C0146, SK0006].

- *To enter:* tied. The work visa names the sponsor and your occupation [A0002, B0075]. On arrival you get a border number (বর্ডার নম্বর), the 10-digit number Jawazat (the passports directorate) gives you until the iqama is issued [SM0030].
- *To renew:* tied. Only the employer gets and renews your iqama and work permit, and pays for them [C0020].
- *The first six months:* since 19 February 2025 a trial period of up to 180 days can be written into the contract, and either side may end the contract during it [SM0002, SM0003]. If the employer ends it, the employer pays the ticket home: the worker pays only if unfit for work or if he quits without a valid reason [SM0005].
- *To quit:* free, with notice, and without notice if the employer breaks its duties (Your rights at work) [C0027, C0029]. Just stopping work lets the employer dismiss you without end-of-service pay and report you absent [C0028].
- *To change employer (kafil bodol, কফিল বদল, or kafala, কাফালা):* tied in the first 12 months; after that you can move mid-contract with 90 days' notice, and at any time if you go unpaid 3 months, your iqama or permit expires or the employer disappears; the employer is only notified [C0053, C0055, C0057]. How: If you want to change employer.
- *To leave:* a company worker issues his own exit and re-entry visa (khuruj, খুরুজ) or final exit (khuruj nihai) on Absher or Qiwa [C0146, SM0047]; a domestic worker can't, because the employer issues it [SK0021]. A worker the sponsor reports for a crime or damage, such as theft, is declared matloob (মাতলুব, wanted) and must settle the case before he can leave [SM0046, SM0039].
- *Working for someone else:* only through Ajeer, the labour ministry's platform for temporary work for a company that isn't your sponsor; the other company applies for an Ajeer permit, the proof you are lawfully at its site [SM0006, SM0007]. Any other outside work is illegal [C0021].

*Changing employer: what it costs.* Since 2025 the law bars an employer's compensation claim against a worker who resigns [SM0004]. The new employer pays a government transfer fee: SR 2,000 for a worker's first transfer, SR 4,000 for the second, SR 6,000 from the third, plus any iqama fees the old employer left unpaid [SM0016]; anything a worker is charged beyond that is a broker's or kafil's cut, and agencies openly advertise kafils for hire [SM0034]. A firm in the red Nitaqat band cannot take you [SM0017].

Domestic workers, including house drivers, gardeners and cooks, come under the Domestic Workers Regulation (Work visa: domestic worker) [D0023], and can move without consent only in listed cases, such as 3 months' unpaid wages or no iqama in time [D0031].

*Your passport.* Your employer may not keep it: Your rights at work [C0022].

*Huroob (হুরুব), or "absent from work".* The employer's report on Qiwa can't be withdrawn [C0064]. You then have 60 days to take a final exit or move to a new employer, and you can move only if you have been in Saudi Arabia more than 12 months; a newcomer reported absent can only leave [C0063, SM0027]. After 60 days you are illegal, with a 3 to 5 year re-entry ban [C0067, E0057]. Employers file false reports, and some demand bribes to withdraw them [C0114]; kafils answer workers who protest about money or the iqama with huroob [SL0036]. What to do: If your employer reports you absent (huroob). An employer can file it only if your contract isn't on Qiwa [C0062].

*An expired iqama.* You are undocumented: no bank, money transfer or SIM, and detention and deportation if stopped [B0132]. The passports directorate fined a failure to renew SR 500, then SR 1,000, then deported (2019) [SK0018]. An expired iqama also lets you change employer without consent, if a new employer takes you [C0055]. Getting home: Going home without valid papers.

### What goes wrong most, and what people wrongly believe

Nearly every Saudi complaint is one of three things: no iqama, no work, or pay that is late, short or missing, and behind them, again and again, is a visa sold for a job that didn't exist or an employer who won't pay for the permit [E0128, SL0014].

*Complaints.* Of 114 complaints to BMET in 2023-2024 that OKUP (Ovibashi Karmi Unnayan Program, a migrant workers' organisation) studied, 47% got no work permit on arrival [SL0004]. Men without a permit were often kept until their entry visa ran out, then counted as absconders [SL0011]. Only 39% of complaints were resolved, usually for Tk 20,000 to just over Tk 1 lakh [SL0007]. How to complain: Work visa: company worker, Step 1, "Before paying", and "If you aren't paid" [A0056, C0039].

*What people believe, and the facts.*
- **"A free visa lets you work for anyone."** Brokers sell it as "go and work wherever you like" [SM0011]. In fact a worker may work only for the employer and job on the permit, or for another company through an Ajeer permit, which that company must apply for [C0021, SM0007]. Many jobs also need a baladiya (বালাদিয়া, municipal) health card, which free-visa workers don't easily get [SM0014]. Of 10 men one Cumilla broker sent, three got a proper job with a contract (2024) [SL0020]. How to spot one: Work visa: company worker, Step 1, "Avoid a free visa" [A0079].
- **"The iqama is the worker's cost: pay the kafil and he renews it."** A Bangladesh embassy official confirmed the employer pays (The iqama: the employer's bill) [SL0031].
- **"Saudi Arabia abolished the kafala system in 2025."** No Saudi announcement was made: the story began in an 18 October 2025 article by ImpACT International, a UK think tank, citing no Saudi source [SK0008].
- **"A visit, tourist or Umrah visa can be turned into an iqama once you're there."** A visit visa can't become an iqama, and working on a tourist or Umrah visa brings a ban of 3 to 10 years (Changing visa type) [E0054, E0057].
- **"Pay extra and the medical is guaranteed fit."** See The medical, above [B0032, B0031].

*Irregular routes and how they end.* Workers fall out of status in four ways:
- a free visa with no real job [A0081];
- an employer who never turns the three-month entry permit into an iqama, often to avoid the fee [B0131, SL0014];
- a kafil who keeps the renewal money or files huroob [SL0028, SL0036];
- a visit or Umrah visa overstayed [E0057].

Many free-visa men end up running or staffing a shop or workshop registered in a Saudi's name (tasattur, commercial concealment): that carries up to 5 years in prison, a fine of up to SR 5 million, and deportation with a ban on returning to work [SM0020, SM0018, SM0019, SM0021]. The end comes in a raid: detainees usually spend one to two weeks in a deportation centre and land in Dhaka with almost nothing [SG0032, SG0033], then face a re-entry ban [E0057]. Irregular returnees get no Welfare Board benefits [E0092]. Anyone who shelters, transports or helps a violator faces up to 15 years in prison and a fine of up to SR 1 million [SL0040]. The ways out without a raid (the embassy's Special Exit Programme, grace periods, and an employer regularising an expired permit by the end of 2026) are under Going home without valid papers [E0026, E0087, G0014].

## Work visa: company worker

For a job with a Saudi company. The whole process usually takes about 1-3 months [A0004].

### Who pays what

The Saudi employer pays the recruitment fees, the iqama (residence card), the work permit and its renewals, exit and re-entry fees and your ticket home when the job ends [A0005]; you pay that ticket only if you are unfit for the work or leave without a legitimate reason [A0006]. You pay:

- OEP registration: Tk 200 [A0028].
- A TTC course admission fee, if you take a course: Tk 748 [A0030].
- A passport: Tk 4,025 for a 48-page, 5-year e-passport, regular delivery [A0124].
- The skill test: USD 85 [A0007, A0008].
- The Wafid medical booking (USD 10) and tests (step 4) [B0004].
- Police clearance (step 6) [B0052].
- The Wage Earners' Welfare Board fee: Tk 5,500 [A0011].
- Migrant worker insurance: Tk 1,000, covering death or permanent disability for Tk 10 lakh over 5 years [A0012].
- Source income tax at BMET clearance: Tk 500 for a Saudi single visa [B0096].

For some job orders BMET caps what the agency may take, such as Tk 50,000 or Tk 58,610 (Before paying, below) [A0016, A0019].

2. **If a relative or friend in Saudi Arabia offers you a visa.** A visa for one or a few workers is a single visa (সিঙ্গেল ভিসা); one that comes through someone abroad is also called a personal or FNF (friends and family) visa (single and group visas, and how many go this way: Background, Channels) [SH0006, SI0023 (2020), RA0041 (2020)]. Even a visa that came free from family must then be carried by a Bangladeshi agency the Saudi embassy has approved (what to do: If a relative or friend offers you a visa, below) [SI0007].

3. **Learn a trade and get a certificate.** Workers in the trades Saudi Arabia lists must pass the Takamol skill test (step 3), which expects real skill and work experience in the trade [A0058, A0070]. BMET's Technical Training Centres (TTCs) run 3-month courses ending in a BNQF (Bangladesh National Qualifications Framework, the national skills standard) assessment [A0030, A0034]; BMET's [trades page](https://bmet.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922dc3c933eb65569e0f2b7) lists the trades and seats at every TTC [A0129].

   You'll need [A0030]:

   - your OEP registration;
   - an SSC pass or a JSC/class 8 pass, depending on the course;
   - age 18 to 40 (21 to 40 for driving);
   - Tk 748.

   1. Watch your TTC's website for the next session's admission notice, for example [TTC Gaibandha's notice board](https://ttc.gaibandha.gov.bd/pages/notices/6a1171662de6aeac14f7e3b4) [A0115]. Notices come out weeks before each three-month session, and applications stay open about four weeks (TTC Magura: 18 August to 15 September for October-December) [A0115, A0030].
   2. In OEP go to Training portal, Training, Trade/Skills, your division, district and institution, Search [A0027, A0032]; select the course, then Apply; if your photo matches your face today press Continue, then Process to Enroll and Confirm [A0027]. Applications are capped at three times the seats [A0030].
   3. Sit the written admission test at the TTC on the notice date (Bangla, English, maths, general science, overseas jobs, visas and labour law) [A0030].
   4. When an SMS says you passed, pay in OEP under Training Portal, My Course, Admission Fee: Tk 748; the driving course is free and includes the licence fee [A0030, A0031].

      > **Need to know**
      > - What happens if you fail the admission test or miss the fee deadline?
      > - Can you apply for the next session?

   5. Attend at least 80% of classes and sit the final assessment; competent trainees get Tk 150 a day for travel and food (Tk 100 for driving) when the budget allows [A0031]. The TTC helps with job placement [A0033].

4. **Ask licensed agencies what Saudi jobs they have, and check each one.** Recruiting without a licence under the Overseas Employment and Migrants Act 2013 is not allowed, and travel agencies may not send workers [A0125, A0093].

   You'll need: the agency's name or RL (recruiting licence) number.

   1. Search the name or RL number on [OEP's agency list](https://www.oep.gov.bd/agencies); the row shows licence status, validity date, office address and phone [A0089]. Go no further unless it says "Active" and the date hasn't passed (others are Expired, Suspended, Cancelled; licences are cancelled for visa fraud) [A0091, A0092].
   2. Ask the agency, at that address and phone, which Saudi jobs they have for your trade, and ask to see the employer's demand letter (company, jobs, pay) with the embassy's attestation (step 2) [A0089, A0108, A0107].
   3. Read workers' reviews on [Recruitment Advisor](https://www.recruitmentadvisor.org/search-an-agency), a trade-union site, giving what each paid, for which country, and whether they got the promised job and pay [A0094, A0095].

   **Which agencies.** No official grading of agencies exists, and the state minister for expatriates' welfare has said he could not confidently recommend any agency [SC0020, SC0021]. These had the most Saudi workers in the 1,148 permission letters BMET posted from June 2023 to December 2025 (70,145 workers from 370 agencies, counted by the team; workers means posts approved, not people who went); a high count shows activity, not fair treatment [SC0001]. #### If a relative or friend offers you a visa

##### The paper doesn't show where the visa came from

A relative's visa is one of three things, and all three arrive as the same work visa [A0077 (2024), SI0007]:

- *A real job with the relative's own employer.* The employer pays the visa, recruitment fees, the iqama and their renewals, so the visa itself should cost you nothing [RA0010, A0005]. Relatives still charge: one migrant paid a close relative Tk 4 lakh for a visa the relative had got free [RA0003].
- *A visa the relative bought from a kafil (the sponsor) or a visa trader.* It has already cost about Tk 2 lakh before it leaves Saudi Arabia (Background, The root) [SI0037, SB0007], and the sponsor will pass his yearly levy and iqama fee to you (sub-step 7). These are the free (azad) visas, meaning you find your own work [SM0045], and new ones are issued as amel adi (ordinary labourer) or load-unload (লোড আনলোড) visas, because load-unload needs no skill test [RA0028, RB0045, RB0027]. The job may not be the one promised: a cousin paid Tk 4 lakh for a driver visa and the employer put the worker to herding goats, then camels [RA0001].
- *A visa the relative sells as a business.* A returnee broker in Cumilla charged Tk 3 lakh a head and passed most of it to the friends in the Gulf who supplied the visas [SL0021]. Many men present a relative as the source when they really bought from a broker [RB0006].

##### What it costs, official against real

- *Genuine company visa:* you pay only the Bangladesh-side fees, about Tk 19,800 before the skill test, passport and ticket: those in Who pays what, plus medical about Tk 10,000, police clearance Tk 1,500, Tasheer Tk 660, orientation Tk 200 and BMET clearance Tk 250 [A0028, RB0021, B0052, RB0024, B0166, A0011, A0012, B0096]. In practice the agency's processing fee is whatever you negotiate [RB0047], and a visa seller can rent an agency's licence for Tk 15,000-20,000 a visa to get the clearance [RD0006].
- *Free visa:* the whole trip costs Tk 8-9 lakh in 2026 [RA0030]; in July 2026 two relatives of a Cumilla worker paid one man Tk 12 lakh between them, with Tk 4 lakh more due after arrival [RA0029].
- *Family isn't cheaper:* workers paid relatives Tk 286,642 on average, brokers Tk 303,351 and agencies Tk 243,531 [RD0023].

##### The risk of delay

A visa that sits too long during attestation, the medical or Tasheer can lapse, and the seller then asks for money for a new one.

**You'll need from the relative, before any money moves** [B0076, B0073, SM0044, A0127, RB0013]:

- the 10-digit visa number;
- the sponsor's name and his Saudi ID, iqama number or company registration (CR) number;
- the occupation on the visa;
- whether the sponsor is a company, an establishment or a household;
- the wage;
- the name and RL (recruiting licence) number of the Bangladeshi agency that will hold the visa.

1. **Check the visa and the sponsor before paying anything.** On [ksavisa.sa/track-application](https://ksavisa.sa/track-application), the Saudi Foreign Ministry's visa platform, enter the visa number with the sponsor's Saudi ID, iqama or CR number (the inviter's number) and the captcha [B0073]. Check a company on the Ministry of Commerce site by its name or unified national number (item 5) [A0098, A0127]. For a household visa, the relative can have the sponsor check Musaned's Visa Status Inquiry with the sponsor's ID and the request number while you are on a call [RC0012]. Your own passport number shows nothing useful until the agency files the MOFA (sub-step 5), which attaches your passport to the visa [RB0023, SI0006].

   *The trap:* a PDF or photo on WhatsApp proves nothing. In Bishwanath, Sylhet, a family with a brother in Saudi Arabia took Tk 5 lakh for a driving visa and handed over a PDF of a different, three-month visa [RC0019, RC0020].

   *What it tells you:* if the sponsor is a person or household rather than a company, it is a household visa: you'll be a domestic worker, outside the Labour Law, working only for that household, not at the relative's company [SK0001, A0081 (2020)]. These are the classic free visas [SH0024 (2020)]: a Saudi household pays no levy on its first four domestic workers, so the visa costs it nothing a year to keep in levy [RH0001]. A load-unload or amel adi occupation carries the same warning [RA0028].

   *If it doesn't match* what you were promised (another sponsor, another job, no record), don't pay. Visas can also vanish when the labour ministry cancels applications filed on fake demand letters (Background, Current situation) [B0079, B0080].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Before the MOFA, does ksavisa.sa track-application show a work visa's sponsor and occupation from the visa number and the sponsor's ID or CR number, or nothing?

2. **Get the agency's name from the relative, check it, and get proof it holds the visa.** The worker rarely chooses: the okala goes to whatever agency the visa seller already deals with, sometimes one whose licence he rents [RD0006]. The sponsor files the okala (Arabic tafweed or wakala) on ksavisa.sa (still called Enjaz) under Authorisation on a visa, entering the visa number, his unified number, the agency's name, your city of arrival and the number of visas [RB0011, RB0012, RB0013, SI0004]. It takes one working day and the government fee starts at SR 75 (Tk 2,500); Saudi service firms charge about SR 200 to file it [RB0011, RB0015, RB0014]. This is the sponsor's cost, not yours [B0116].

   Check the agency's RL number on OEP: "Active", with a validity date not passed (item 4) [A0089]. Then, before paying the agency anything, get it to confirm in writing, or by a screenshot from its own login on the Saudi visa platform, that it holds the okala for your visa number [SI0003, A0076]. Later, check that the same agency appears on your MOFA printout (sub-step 5).

   *The trap:* once the okala is filed you are stuck with that agency. Moving means the first agency cancels both the okala and the MOFA, which costs money and time, doesn't always work, and often leaves the passport unusable for a new visa [RB0041, RB0042].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What does it cost in 2026 to cancel an okala and MOFA and move to another agency?
   > - Is the office that "processes" a single visa often a sub-agent's, not the agency named on the okala?

3. **Make sure the demand letter will be attested.** Since 2025 single visas, like group visas, need the employer's demand letter attested by the Bangladesh Embassy in Riyadh, which does it online on beriyadh.org for registered companies (Step 2) [A0087, A0102]. A sponsor who won't attest is a warning: companies that bring workers only to let them loose without an iqama are slow to give a demand letter [RB0033].

   > **Need to know**
   > - In November 2026, does BMET clear a one-person Saudi visa without the attested demand letter?
   > - How does a household sponsor, who can't register on beriyadh.org as a company, get one attested?

4. **Pay in stages you can check, and so you can prove it.** The relative will usually want money first, because he has already paid the kafil in riyals; your family pays taka at home to his family, outside the banks, and part leaves by hundi (হুন্ডি, informal money transfer) [RA0018, SB0010]. Tie each payment to something you have checked yourself: nothing until the visa and sponsor check out (sub-step 1) and the agency confirms it holds the okala (sub-step 2); a part once the MOFA printout shows the promised sponsor and agency (sub-step 5); the rest only once the e-visa is issued and checked (Step 7). Pay by bank or bKash to a named account, never cash: in a Banshkhali case a Saudi-based man took Tk 3.58 lakh through his wife for a visa he never sent, and police confirmed the fraud from the bank and bKash records [RA0031, RA0032].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Do families take a stamp-paper deed or a security cheque from a relative selling a visa?
   > - Has either ever won a case?

5. **Let the agency carry the file, in this order.** From medical to flight usually takes 1.5-2.5 months, a month at the fastest [RB0031].

   1. *Medical:* book the Wafid slip yourself (Step 4). Agencies say you can't, and take it for about Tk 1,500 (USD 10 plus about Tk 200 for the shop); with the centre's Tk 8,500 the medical comes to about Tk 10,000 [RB0044, RB0021, B0004]. The result is valid two months [B0017].
   2. *MOFA (মোফা):* after a fit result the agency files your visa application on Enjaz with your passport and photo; from then a search shows the sponsor's name and the application number (Step 7) [RB0023, SI0006 (2022)]. Check the sponsor and agency on it before paying the next part. Drop out after MOFA and the agency charges a "MOFA fine", usually Tk 3,000 [RB0023]. Some agencies won't file until you bring police clearance, so apply early (Step 6) [RB0025].
   3. *Skill test,* if the occupation is a listed trade (Step 3) [A0058].
   4. *Tasheer fingerprints:* the agency books the slip, which is free, so an agency asking money for it is overcharging; you pay Tk 660, or about Tk 2,200 if put in the Lounge (Step 5) [RB0026, RB0024]. Once all papers reach the Saudi mission the e-visa can be issued in 3-4 days; agencies quote a week to two months [RB0046, RB0029]. Check the e-visa yourself (Step 7) [B0075, B0077].
   5. *Orientation and BMET's own fingerprint:* the 3-day orientation (Step 8), then a second fingerprint, BMET's bio-enrolment ("bio finger"), for the clearance card [RB0028].
   6. *BMET clearance (ম্যানপাওয়ার):* the agency applies, in 3 working days (Step 8) [B0095]. BMET's charter lets a worker who got a visa on his own apply alone [A0057, B0098], but after the okala the visa sits in the approved agency's name, so the agency handles the okala, MOFA and Tasheer and in practice the clearance too [SI0007, SI0004].
   7. *Payment and ticket:* you settle the agency's bill before it takes the clearance and buys the ticket [RB0030].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Since September 2025, has BMET's emigration branch cleared a Saudi single visa for a worker who applied without an agency?

6. **Know who answers if it fails.** The visa seller signs the agency a written undertaking (মুচলেকা, muchleka) taking all liability for the workers, so on a single visa the agency is liable for nothing beyond the clearance papers [RD0007, RD0008]. The agency got perhaps Tk 20,000 of the Tk 4-5 lakh, so a BMET case against it recovers little, and single-visa workers who come back without work get back not even Tk 1 lakh [RD0010, RD0011]. If you named a relative as the source but bought from a broker, there is no one in Bangladesh to pursue [RB0006]. What to do if the relative cheats you: Before paying, "If the relative cheats you".

7. **After arrival.** In the first week set up Qiwa and Absher (Step 9): check that the job title and wage on your Qiwa contract match what you were promised, since what you approve there is the record [B0129, C0173]. Register on Absher with your iqama or border number, and every month check your iqama expiry and any "Absent from Work" flag (If your employer reports you absent (huroob)) [B0125, SM0040, C0112]. Keep your passport; the sponsor may not hold it (Step 9, Keep your passport) [B0114]. The iqama, permit, levy, renewals and any transfer fee (SR 2,000 for a first transfer) are the employer's [A0005, C0020, SM0016].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What does a free-visa worker pay a broker or kafil in 2026 to transfer his sponsorship to a real employer?

   *The iqama traps.* By law the employer gets and pays for your iqama within 90 days of arrival, and a late one costs the employer a SR 500 fine, not you [B0108, RC0033, C0020]. On a bought visa it rarely works that way. A three-month iqama is legal, but it is the classic sign of a free visa: if no job turns up before it runs out, you are illegal [RC0059, A0080 (2024)]. Nine men from Gurudaspur, Natore, each paid a villager settled in Saudi Arabia Tk 4-5 lakh; two years later none had an iqama, and his wife said it was because they hadn't paid extra for it [RC0051, RC0052]. On a free visa the kafil passes the levy and iqama fee to you (Background, The iqama) [SM0022, SG0054]. In 2026 kafils took SR 16,000-18,000 a year for making and renewing the iqama [RA0023]; one 2025 account puts the yearly cut, called faida (ফায়দা), at SR 10,000-12,000 on average, rising as the years go on [RA0027]. Anything above the levy and fees, or any levy demanded from a company worker, is the sponsor's cut. Brokers have sold a "one-year" iqama for SR 5,000 and handed over a three-month one [RA0024]. One worker flown home from a Saudi jail in November 2026 had paid a middleman Tk 3.7 lakh for an iqama that was never made [RC0056].

   *The sponsor's weapons:* working for anyone but the sponsor breaks Article 39 of the Labour Law, a SR 10,000 fine and deportation for the worker the first time [RA0009, D0030]; since February 2026 a business that lets its workers work for others faces up to SR 100,000 [RC0034]. So a sponsor whose worker is caught outside reports him absent (huroob), and one worker-writer described kafils filing theft cases, sometimes for over a crore taka, which stop the worker going home [RC0035]. Don't sign or thumbprint blank or unexplained papers for the kafil [RC0036].

   *If it fails, on a company visa:* no iqama after 90 days, complain on 19911 (Step 9) [B0109]. You can move employer without consent once your work permit has expired or was never issued [C0055], issue your own exit and re-entry or final exit visa on Absher or Qiwa [C0146], and if reported absent you have 60 days to transfer or take a final exit [C0063].

   *If it fails, on a household visa:* only the domestic worker rules apply. The ministry can move you to another employer if your iqama isn't issued or renewed within 30 days [D0031], but you cannot issue your own final exit visa; the employer does it on Absher, which is the kafil's real hold over free-visa men [SK0021].

   *Either way:* the embassy's online form has a "no iqama after arrival" option, and the Special Exit Programme covers workers whose iqama was never issued (Going home without valid papers) [E0089, E0027]. Until the end of 2026 an employer can still regularise a permit never issued within 6 months of joining [G0015].

##### Women

A woman going as a domestic worker should pay nothing and goes through a ministry-approved agency and Musaned (Work visa: domestic worker) [D0059, D0060]. A relative arranging it is a warning: in October 2026 a Muktagachha woman's cousin met her at the Saudi airport, had her sign an Arabic paper and, she says, had "sold" her to a sponsor for two years on a khadema (খাদেমা, housemaid) visa at SR 1,000 a month; he was arrested under the human trafficking law [RC0061, RC0044, RC0045].

##### Avoid a "free visa"

There is no such thing [A0024]: it is a visa bought from a sponsor with no job for you, so you must find your own work and housing, often pay the sponsor a monthly cut and renewal money (Background, On a free visa), work illegally and can be deported [A0083, A0082, A0081, A0079]. Signs: the contract signed in Bangladesh isn't the one used in Saudi Arabia [A0081], and those in If a relative or friend offers you a visa (a household sponsor, a 3-month iqama). Have the papers checked (Before paying) [A0108]; report the seller to BMET or the ministry (below) or on the ministry hotline 16135 (+880 9610102030 from abroad) [A0056, A0048, A0025]; in an emergency call 999, the police emergency number [B0171].

- **Get the employer's name, job, salary and embassy-attested contract in writing,** and the agency's name, licence number, address and phone [A0107, A0108].

  2. Open that agency's Saudi PDF and compare company, job, salary and maximum cost with what you were told [A0017]. One letter, for example, allowed at most Tk 50,000, by cheque, bank draft or pay order only, after visa, clearance and flight were confirmed, with the employer paying all Saudi-side costs and each worker signing the contract and getting a Bangla translation [A0016, A0018].

The law reaches the relative. Section 31 of the Overseas Employment and Migrants Act 2013 (OEMA) makes it a crime for any person, not only an agency, to take money with a false promise of a job abroad or to keep a migrant's passport or visa: up to 5 years and a fine of at least Tk 1 lakh [RC0009, RC0002, RC0001]. These offences are cognisable, non-bailable and can't be settled privately [RC0010]. But section 33 makes buying or selling a visa inside Bangladesh an offence for any person, 2 to 7 years' rigorous imprisonment and at least Tk 3 lakh, so a worker who paid for a visa can be accused too, and the seller will say so [RA0011, RA0008].   - **Complain to BMET or the ministry against the relative by name.** Section 41 lets anyone harmed complain against any person, not only an agency; the inquiry is meant to take 30 working days, and section 43 lets the government recover the money and pay it to you [RC0005, RC0006, RC0007]. ### Step 3: Pass the skill test

Workers in the listed trades must pass the Takamol skill test under Saudi Arabia's Skills Verification Program (SVP), which needs real skill and work experience in the trade [A0058, A0070]. It runs at 27 TTCs and BRTC Gazipur, the Bangladesh Road Transport Corporation's training institute [A0061, A0130].

You'll need [A0062, A0063, A0007]:

- your passport (valid);
- your NID;
- your own email address that you can open;
- a Visa or Mastercard endorsed for US dollars with USD 85 on it;
- the occupation on your visa.

1. **Check which centre tests your trade** in BMET's [list of occupations by test centre](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-bmet/2026/2/dc7e2b1f-5458-4a46-9197-958e2cd8941e.docx) ("TTC wise Occupation under SVP", on bmet.gov.bd under Training, Takamol); some centres test only loading and unloading, while Bangladesh-Korea TTC Dhaka tests 32 occupations [A0105, A0106].
2. **Register** on [svp-international.pacc.sa](https://svp-international.pacc.sa/) [A0117, A0118]: press Sign-up, enter your names, NID, passport number and expiry, country, nationality, email, date of birth and a password (8+ characters with a capital, a small letter, a number and a symbol), all exactly as in your passport; tick "Agree to acknowledge that all the data are correct" and type the code emailed to you [A0062]. With only one name, write it as the first name and your father's name as the last; the same name in both counts as an error [A0064]. With a wrong passport number you can't sit the test, so fix it first through Support at the bottom right [A0067].
3. **Book:** sign in with your email, password and the emailed code; in Account Dashboard press Start Verification and choose your occupation (fixed after this), Direct Assessment (In-person), your division or city (the centre is assigned automatically), the test language and a green date; tick "I declare that the information entered is correct" and press Confirm [A0063, A0064, A0068].
4. **Pay USD 85** with the card's number, CVV, expiry date and holder name, then press Pay; the test ticket appears under Reservation and is emailed to you [A0007, A0008, A0063, A0066]. Each retake costs the full fee again [A0009]. Pay no one more than the fee: there is no way to pass for extra money, and a worker caught paying to pass has the account locked and can't go to Saudi Arabia [A0070, A0071]. **If someone offers to fix the test for money,** report it to the police (call 999, or your local police station) [A0070, B0171], tell BMET's Takamol and Japan Support Centre (BMET Bhaban, 4th floor, Kakrail, Dhaka; 01901165981 or 01901165982) [A0072], and complain to BMET (Step 1, Before paying) [A0056].
5. **Prepare if you want:** a 3-day loading and unloading course costs Tk 2,000 at 24 TTCs, and its certificate isn't an SVP certificate [A0069]. Free courses [A0116]:

   - 5 days: gas station attendants (Kishoreganj TTC), sellers (Bangladesh-Korea TTC Chattogram);
   - 10 days: food and beverage counter staff (Dhaka Mohila TTC), barbers (Sylhet TTC), concrete finishers (Bangladesh-Korea TTC Dhaka), building and construction workers (Khulna, Jhalokathi, Moulvibazar, Nilphamari and Chapainawabganj TTCs).

6. **Take the test** with your original passport, a photocopy, the ticket and one photo, arriving before the 30-minute briefing [A0066, A0059]. The computer test is 15 questions in 30 minutes in your chosen language; the practical test is 1 hour 30 minutes, in Bangla [A0059, A0060]. Phones and bags go in a locker; looking at another candidate counts as cheating [A0066].
7. **To change the date,** cancel and rebook once, up to 96 hours before [A0068]. Miss it and you are marked absent; you can pay and book again 96 hours after the slot [A0068].
8. **Get the result** on the SVP portal, where anyone can check a certificate is genuine [A0073]. A score of at least 50% passes, and the certificate is valid for five years [A0104]. Pakistan's SVP partner says a pass is uploaded within 24 hours; after a fail you book a new test [A0119]. To correct the certificate, press Support at the bottom right and raise a ticket with your name, email, a subject and description, Request Type "Suggestion", Client "Test Taker", your country and passport number, attaching your passport, ticket and certificate [A0068].

**You'll need** [B0005, B0006, B0004]:

- your NID number;
- an email address;

#### Don't buy a "fit" result

Agencies and brokers sell fit reports for about Tk 20,000-30,000, but Saudi Arabia tests you again on arrival and sends a worker found unfit home at their own cost [B0032, B0030, B0018, B0115]. Some centres declare people unfit, then ask for a bribe to change it [B0035].

   - your contact details;
   - the centre's name and city;
   - what happened;
   - any proof (receipts, messages, another lab's report).

   Send it [B0022, B0021, B0148, B0149]:

   - to info@ghc.sa;
   - by post to P.O. Box 7431, Riyadh 11462, Saudi Arabia;
   - by hand to its office on Samha Street, Diplomatic Quarter, Riyadh (920020885).

   It says within 3 working days whether it accepts the complaint and decides within 30 days; the decision is final, and a centre at fault can be warned, re-checked, suspended or closed [B0151, B0152, B0019, B0025]. Its Grievance Committee hears migrants' appeals [B0026].
2. **In Bangladesh, complain about overcharging** to the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare's Enforcement-2 branch with your receipts, your full address and the centre's name and address (route: Step 1, Before paying, item 5) [B0033, A0048].

### Your rights at work

#### Contract

It must be recorded on Qiwa (Step 9), with a copy for you and the employer [C0016]. It runs for a fixed term, usually 2 years; with no length written it counts as one year, and it renews for the same length if both sides carry on [C0095, C0026]. It should state your leave, whether it is paid and who pays the airfare [C0152].

#### Hours

8 a day and 48 a week (6 and 36 for Muslims in Ramadan), never over 12 a day in total [C0001, C0002, C0096], with a break of at least 30 minutes after 5 hours in a row [C0004]. From 15 June to 15 September, no private-sector work in direct sun from 12 noon to 3 pm; report a breach on 19911 or in the labour ministry's app (If a right is broken, below) [C0108, SG0026, C0157]. Heat stroke (confusion, slurred speech, fainting, seizures, very high temperature, hot dry or drenched skin) kills if treatment is delayed: get emergency care, move the person into shade, take off outer clothing and cool them with water [SG0027, SG0028]. Headache, nausea, dizziness, weakness and heavy sweating are heat exhaustion: get out of the heat, drink and get checked at a clinic [SG0029, SG0044].

#### Overtime

Full hourly wage plus half the basic hourly wage per extra hour; public-holiday work counts as overtime [C0005, C0010].

#### Pay

There is no minimum wage for foreign workers; the contract wage is what you can claim [C0092]. It is paid into your bank account on the contract date [C0006]. Never sign that you were paid before the money arrives; it can block a later claim [C0025]. Recruitment, iqama and work permit fees and renewals, job-title changes, exit and re-entry visas, transfer fees and your ticket home at the contract's end are the employer's and can't be taken from your pay [C0020].

#### Rest and leave

- *Weekly rest:* one day off a week, normally Friday, not swappable for money [C0003].
- *Annual leave:* paid, at least 21 days, 30 after 5 years with the same employer, on dates the employer sets with 30 days' notice; untaken days are paid when you leave [C0007, C0009, C0008].
- *Paid holidays:* 4 days at each Eid, Founding Day (22 February), National Day (23 September) [C0010].
- *Sick leave:* up to 120 days a year, 30 at full pay, 60 at three-quarters, 30 unpaid [C0011].
- *Other paid leave:* 3 days for a child's birth, 5 for your wedding, 5 for the death of a spouse, parent or child, 10 to 15 days once for Hajj after 2 years; 12 weeks' maternity leave [C0012, C0013].

#### Housing, food and transport

The employer gives housing and transport or allowances for them [C0014]; food only if the contract says so [C0099].

**If a right is broken,** report it in the labour ministry's (Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development) app, on Google Play and the App Store, or in Tawakkalna, the Saudi government app: service Reporting Violations of Labor Regulations, sign in with Absher, pick the report type, give the workplace location, company details and proof; it is free, the ministry acts within 3 days and the status shows in the app [C0157, C0158]. Money owed is claimed through friendly settlement (If you aren't paid) [C0024]. Ministry help line: 19911, Sunday to Thursday 8 am to 5 pm, Arabic and English [C0050].

#### Health insurance

The employer must insure you [C0015]. Check your cover with the Council of Health Insurance's Insurance Information Inquiry at [chi.gov.sa](https://www.chi.gov.sa/en/ServicesDirectory/Pages/Eservices-CheckInsurance.aspx) [C0110].

If you aren't insured or the premium is taken from your pay, complain at [care.chi.gov.sa](https://care.chi.gov.sa/) (guest or account) with your details, the employer's contact details, what happened and scanned proof; it is handled within 15 working days without naming you to the employer [C0159]. The cover doesn't pay for serious illness, so go only if you are fit [C0101].

#### Your passport

No employer may keep your passport or iqama; the fine is SR 5,000, though sponsors still commonly do it [C0022, C0091]. Complain on the labour ministry's Beneficiary Page ([selfservice.hrsd.gov.sa](https://selfservice.hrsd.gov.sa/node/)) [C0023, B0120]: [hrsd.gov.sa](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/ministry-services/services/client-page) > Contact Us > Beneficiary Page > Start the Service > log in with Nafath > Complaints > Add a Request; choose the sector and categories, fill in the fields, press Send [C0154, C0155]. It needs a ministry or Absher account, is free, answered within 5 days, and also in the ministry's app [C0156].

#### Unfair dismissal

If the employer ends the contract without a valid reason, you are owed 15 days' wages per year of service on an open-ended contract or the wages for the rest of a fixed-term one, and never less than 2 months' wages [C0033].

#### Probation

Up to 180 days if the contract sets it; either side can end the contract during it without compensation or end-of-service pay [C0017, C0018]. Who pays the ticket home: Background, What ties you to the employer, The first six months.

#### Leaving without notice

You may stop work without notice and keep all your rights if the employer doesn't meet its duties (for example, doesn't pay), moves you to another job without consent, assaults or mistreats you, leaves a known serious danger unfixed, or lied about the job when you signed [C0029, C0094]. If you just stop coming to work, the employer can report you absent (huroob, below) [C0062] and, after a written warning, dismiss you without end-of-service pay for over 30 days' absence in a contract year or 15 in a row [C0028].

### How long you can stay

As long as you have a contract and a valid iqama, which the employer renews and pays for [C0020]. Work visas are for ages 18 to 60 [C0100]. **No pension:** GOSI, the Saudi social insurance agency, covers foreign workers only for work injuries [C0109]; you get end-of-service pay instead (Leaving at the end of the job) [C0031].

#### Renew your passport in time

3. Bring your old passport and your NID or an online birth registration certificate in English [SG0012, SG0013, SG0014, SG0015].

A worker's 48-page, 5-year e-passport costs SR 125 (SR 190 urgent), a 10-year one SR 210 [SG0016].

### What can get you jailed

- **Don't bring** narcotic drugs, alcohol, anything with pork, poppy seeds (khas khas), khat, gutkha, pan masala, non-Islamic religious literature, obscene material, amulets or anything linked to black magic, including on your phone [C0181, C0102, C0160, C0103].
- **Never carry a parcel you haven't packed and checked yourself,** even for family [C0104]; Saudi Arabia executes foreigners for drug smuggling [C0107].
- **Medicines:** your own only, at most 30 days' supply, in original packs, with a medical report and a prescription under 6 months old, both translated into Arabic [C0105, C0106].
- **Don't work for anyone but the employer on your permit, or in another job,** even for a few hours [C0021].
- **Cybercrime** cases have brought Bangladeshis 6 months to 5 years in prison [G0003]; in October 2026 a Bangladeshi man and woman, held under the cybercrime law and on an adultery allegation, were executed for an "indecent act" in a mosque [G0007, G0001].

#### Driving

If you cause a crash that kills someone, the family can claim blood money (diya): a Riyadh court ordered an uninsured foreign driver to pay SR 300,000 per person killed and kept him in prison until he paid [SG0023]. Compulsory motor insurance pays victims up to SR 10 million, but the insurer can take that money back from a driver without the right class of licence or with an expired one [SG0020, SG0022, SG0021].

#### If you or a relative is arrested

The court provides an interpreter, but you can't choose your own [G0004]. Saudi authorities may not tell the embassy about a death sentence, even one carried out [G0006]. A relative or friend reports it to the labour wing on its online form, choosing the jail problem type, or on 8001000125, and asks the mission for legal help, which no family of the two executed in 2026 had done [E0089, E0024, G0005].

### If your employer reports you absent (huroob)

## Work visa: domestic worker

For work in a Saudi household, in one of 14 listed domestic jobs such as housemaid, private driver, nanny, cook or gardener [D0023]. Domestic workers fall outside the Saudi Labour Law; the Domestic Workers Regulation sets their rights [D0171, D0172, D0173]. Musaned, the labour ministry's domestic-worker platform, handles contracts and pay [D0157, D0045]. The employer pays recruitment (up to SR 11,750 to the recruitment office for a Bangladeshi worker), the iqama, work permit and transfer fees [SG0030, D0008]. A woman domestic worker pays nothing: her agency may take no migration cost from her [D0059], and Saudi recruitment offices may take no commission from any domestic worker, before or after she starts work [D0024]. No skill test is needed [D0161].

> **Need to know**

### Step 1: Check you qualify

- **Age.** A woman must be 25 to 45 for BMET clearance [D0039]; the housekeeping course (step 3) takes women 25 to 45, returnees up to 50 [D0050]. Saudi law bans domestic workers under 21, and the contract normally ends at 60 [D0001, D0022].
- **Children, health, education, consent** [D0051, D0050, D0054, D0052, D0039]:
  - A woman whose youngest child is under 6, who is pregnant, or who has a contagious or incurable disease can't join the course [D0051].
  - She needs class 5 (can be relaxed) and must read and write [D0050, D0054].

### After arrival: your rights

- **Who arranges what:** the employer pays your medical test, iqama and renewal, and health insurance or treatment [D0029]. The Saudi recruitment office must tell you the work and pay, and house and support you if you leave or refuse the employer [D0025, D0026].
- **Contract on Musaned:** you get an SMS link, enter your residence number and the code sent to you, and accept within 5 days; it is free (Musaned 920002866) [D0047].
- **Insurance:** a contract made through a recruitment office on Musaned is insured for its first two years [G0024]. It covers:
  - permanent disability from an accident [G0025]
  - your unpaid wages if the employer dies or is disabled [G0025]
  - sending your body and belongings home if you die [G0026]
- **Probation:** up to 90 days; either side may end the contract [D0003].
- **Hours:** at most 10 a day, a half-hour break after every 5 hours, at least 8 hours' continuous rest, and one paid day off a week [D0004, D0005].
- **Pay:** the contract wage, monthly, into a bank account or digital wallet in your name linked to Musaned through your mobile and iqama numbers [D0158, D0045, D0046]. At most a quarter can be deducted, only for damage you caused, an advance or an official order [D0013]. The Saudi-Bangladesh recruitment agreement set SR 800 a month [D0193].

  > **Need to know**

- **Housing, food and contact:** suitable housing and food or a food allowance [D0011]; the employer must let you contact your family and the embassy, and may not keep your passport, papers or belongings [D0010, D0009].
- **Leave:** up to 30 days' sick leave a year with a medical report, 15 on full pay, the rest on half [D0007]; after two years, if you renew, 30 days' paid leave and a return ticket [D0006].
- **Only your own job:** the employer may not make you work for others or in another job [D0012]. If you work for anyone else yourself: up to SR 2,000 fine, a work ban or both [D0020]; working on your own account, SR 10,000 and deportation [D0030].
- **At the end:** wages and dues within a week, or two if you end the contract; end-of-service pay of one month's wage per four years [D0015, D0014]. Ending the contract without a valid reason costs two months' wages to the other side unless the contract says otherwise [D0016]; if you leave without one, you pay your ticket home [D0019].

## Asylum

Saudi Arabia has no asylum system, hasn't signed the 1951 Refugee Convention and generally doesn't grant asylum [E0072, E0074]. People there illegally, including pilgrimage overstayers, are refused refugee status and deported [E0074]. The only protection is UNHCR, the UN refugee agency: people it recognises may stay temporarily, with valid identity papers, until resettled or returned; those without travel documents are sometimes detained indefinitely [E0073, E0075].

Apply by email to saprstaf@unhcr.org with [E0164]:

- your full name;
- family members' names;
- date of birth, sex and nationality;
- address and phone;
- any earlier UNHCR registration;
- the reason.

Toll-free line 009668001000281, Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday [E0163]. The Riyadh office, in the Diplomatic Quarter, is by appointment only [E0165].

## Going home without valid papers

Overstaying any visa can bring a fine up to SR 50,000, up to 6 months in prison and deportation [E0053]. The re-entry ban, from departure, is usually 3 to 5 years for overstaying or huroob and 3 to 10 years for working on a tourist or Umrah visa [E0057, E0058, E0059]. Leaving without a legitimate reason means paying your own ticket [E0136].

**Special Exit Programme (SEP),** the Bangladesh Embassy's route to an exit visa.

1. **Check you qualify:**
   - You qualify if your sponsor's company is under the labour office of Riyadh, Al Qassim, Hail, Al Jouf, Northern Borders or the Eastern Province, and your iqama has expired, was never issued or carries a huroob report; house drivers and male domestic workers with an expired iqama also qualify [E0027].
   - Excluded: women domestic workers; anyone who didn't leave on an earlier exit visa; anyone with a police or court case or wanted (matlub), who must settle it with that authority first [E0028].
2. **You'll need** [E0029, E0030]:
   - your iqama number;
   - your original iqama;
   - a passport copy showing its validity.
3. **Apply online** at [bangladeshembassy.org.sa/sepsrv.html](https://www.bangladeshembassy.org.sa/sepsrv.html) ("Special Exit programme(SEP) Application Form"); no visit or call needed [E0026, E0031].
4. **Check progress** with your iqama number at [bangladeshembassy.org.sa/seppub](https://www.bangladeshembassy.org.sa/seppub/); when an appointment appears, print it and go to the embassy on that day with the documents [E0031].
5. **Get the exit visa:**
   - For a Riyadh company, the embassy gets labour office clearance; you then hand in your original iqama and passport copy, and the embassy sends them to Jawazat for the exit visa [E0029].
   - For a company under another labour office, or a huroob case with a sponsor outside Riyadh, the embassy gives you a letter to take to that labour office or Jawazat, where you collect the exit visa [E0029, E0030].

There is no time limit; workers have waited months, mostly without work [E0096].
