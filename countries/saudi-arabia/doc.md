# Saudi Arabia: migration guide

How to go to Saudi Arabia from Bangladesh, visa by visa, step by step. \[3\] cites an official source; \[N3\] a news or other third-party source, listed under "Needs an official source"; a year, as in \[N3 (2022)\], marks a source older than 2025. "Need to know" marks a fact not yet confirmed; "You'll need" lists what to have ready.

Tk is the Bangladeshi taka (1 lakh is Tk 100,000), SR the Saudi riyal and USD the US dollar; SR 800 is a little over Tk 26,000 [A0003]. BMET, the Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training, is the Bangladesh government agency whose job is to increase overseas employment and make migration safe [A0111]. In Saudi Arabia a foreign worker's visa, residence and right to work are tied to a sponsor, normally the employer; this is the kafala system [A0002].

## Work visa: company worker

For a job with a Saudi company. **Need to know:** how long the whole process usually takes, from job offer to departure.

**Who pays what.** The Saudi employer pays the recruitment fees, the iqama (residence card), the work permit and its renewals, exit and re-entry fees and your ticket home when the job ends [A0005]; you pay that ticket only if you are unfit for the work or leave without a legitimate reason [A0006]. You pay OEP registration (Tk 200) [A0028]; a TTC course admission fee if you take a course (Tk 748) [A0030]; a passport (Tk 4,025 for a 48-page, 5-year e-passport, regular delivery) [A0124]; the skill test (USD 85) [A0007, A0008]; the Wafid medical booking (USD 10) and tests (step 4) [B0004]; police clearance (step 6) [B0052]; the Wage Earners' Welfare Board fee (Tk 5,500) [A0011]; migrant worker insurance (Tk 1,000, covering death or permanent disability for Tk 10 lakh over 5 years) [A0012]; and source income tax at BMET clearance, Tk 500 for a Saudi single visa [B0096]. For some job orders BMET caps what the agency may take, such as Tk 50,000 or Tk 58,610 (Before paying, below) [A0016, A0019]. **Need to know:** the medical test fee, the pre-departure orientation (PDO) fee, whether BMET still charges Tk 250 for clearance and the smart card, whether the DEMO charges for fingerprints, and the government's current maximum cost (last set at Tk 1.65 lakh in 2016 [A0013]).

### Step 1: Find a real job

1. **Register on the Overseas Employment Platform (OEP),** BMET's website for applying for TTC training, the compulsory 3-day pre-departure orientation and BMET clearance [A0109, A0027].
   You'll need: your NID (national ID card), a mobile phone with that number, a bKash, Nagad or other mobile banking account with Tk 202, and a recent clear photo that matches your face today [A0026, A0029, A0027].
   1. In Google Chrome open [employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/login-with-otp](https://employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/login-with-otp) (or scan the QR code on a BMET or TTC poster), go to registration and type every detail exactly as on your NID, without dots in your name; verify with the OTP (one-time code) sent to your phone, then Submit and "Save and continue" [A0026].
   2. Fill in the rest, choose Mobile Banking at payment, then bKash, Nagad or another service, and enter your account number, OTP and PIN; the fee is Tk 200 (Tk 202 with the charge), paid only online inside OEP [A0026, A0028, A0029].
   3. Download the Registration Card [A0026]. Take 2 printed copies of the registration slip to your district employment and manpower office (DEMO, BMET's office in each district) to give fingerprints [A0028]; addresses, phones and emails are in BMET's [list of DEMOs](https://bmet.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922e139933eb65569e2af6c) [A0112, A0113].
   Already registered? Log in with your mobile number and OTP [A0029, A0027]. Problems: OEP hotline 09614445271 [A0128]. **Need to know:** what the DEMO gives you after the fingerprints, how to correct a wrong name or date of birth, and what scams target workers at this step (fake registration sites, shops charging extra).

2. **Learn a trade and get a certificate.** Workers in the trades Saudi Arabia lists must pass the Takamol skill test (step 3), which expects real skill and work experience in the trade [A0058, A0070]. BMET's Technical Training Centres (TTCs) run 3-month courses ending in a BNQF (Bangladesh National Qualifications Framework, the national skills standard) assessment [A0030, A0034]; BMET's [trades page](https://bmet.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922dc3c933eb65569e0f2b7) lists the trades and seats at every TTC [A0129].
   You'll need: your OEP registration, an SSC pass or a JSC/class 8 pass depending on the course, age 18 to 40 (21 to 40 for driving), and Tk 748 [A0030].
   1. Watch your TTC's website for the next session's admission notice, for example [TTC Gaibandha's notice board](https://ttc.gaibandha.gov.bd/pages/notices/6a1171662de6aeac14f7e3b4) [A0115]. Courses run in three-month sessions; notices come out weeks ahead (TTC Gaibandha posted its July-September 2026 notice on 23 May) and applications stay open about four weeks (TTC Magura: 18 August to 15 September for October-December) [A0115, A0030].
   2. In OEP go to Training portal, Training, Trade/Skills, your division, district and institution, Search [A0027, A0032]; select the course, then Apply; if your photo matches your face today press Continue, then Process to Enroll and Confirm [A0027]. Applications are capped at three times the seats [A0030].
   3. Sit the written admission test at the TTC on the notice date (Bangla, English, maths, general science, overseas jobs, visas and labour law) [A0030].
   4. When an SMS says you passed, pay in OEP under Training Portal, My Course, Admission Fee: Tk 748; the driving course is free and includes the licence fee [A0030, A0031]. **Need to know:** what happens if you fail the admission test or miss the fee deadline, and whether you can apply for the next session.
   5. Attend at least 80% of classes and sit the final assessment; competent trainees get Tk 150 a day for travel and food (Tk 100 for driving) when the budget allows [A0031]. The TTC helps with job placement [A0033].

3. **Ask relatives or friends in Saudi Arabia whether their own company is hiring.** A Saudi company can get work visas only through Qiwa, the labour ministry's platform, and only if its registration and work permits are valid, it pays wages through the Wage Protection System and enough staff are Saudi [A0074, A0075].
   You'll need from your relative: the company's name and unified national number (to check it, item 5), the job title and the monthly wage [A0098, A0127, A0107].
   1. **Your relative asks the company to hire you.**
   2. **No one pays the company or sponsor for the visa:** the employer pays visa and recruitment costs, and a request for money means the visa is being sold, often for a job that doesn't exist [A0005, A0079, A0083].
   3. **The employer authorises a recruitment office** on the Saudi visa platform to deal with the Saudi embassy [A0076]. A DEMO official said in 2024 that even such a visa must go through a registered Bangladeshi agency [A0077]; check any agency first (item 4).
   4. **The company gets the demand letter attested** by the Bangladesh Embassy in Riyadh (step 2) [A0102, A0103]. Single visas need this attestation as well as group visas [A0087].
   5. **BMET clears a worker who got the visa on their own directly,** in 3 working days (step 8) [A0057]. Then continue from Step 3 (skill test).
   **Need to know:** what the company asks for (documents, trade proof) to request a visa for a named worker abroad; whether the visa can be stamped without a Bangladeshi agency, and what the employer enters to authorise one; the most an agency may charge to process a visa the worker brings.

4. **Ask licensed agencies what Saudi jobs they have, and check each one.** Recruiting without a licence under the Overseas Employment and Migrants Act 2013 is not allowed, and travel agencies may not send workers [A0125, A0093].
   You'll need: the agency's name or RL (recruiting licence) number.
   1. Search the name or RL number on [OEP's agency list](https://www.oep.gov.bd/agencies); the row shows licence status, validity date, office address and phone [A0089]. Go no further unless it says "Active" and the date hasn't passed (others are Expired, Suspended, Cancelled; licences are cancelled for visa fraud) [A0091, A0092].
   2. Ask the agency, at that address and phone, which Saudi jobs they have for your trade, and ask to see the employer's demand letter (company, jobs, pay) with the embassy's attestation (step 2) [A0089, A0108, A0107].
   3. Read workers' reviews on [Recruitment Advisor](https://www.recruitmentadvisor.org/search-an-agency), a trade-union site, giving what each paid, for which country, and whether they got the promised job and pay [A0094, A0095].
   **One agency with outside evidence:** Bonanza Overseas (RL-0579, Active, +88028881118) was the Bangladeshi agency in an ILO-backed fair recruitment pilot for construction jobs in Qatar in 2018-2021 [A0090, A0096, A0097], and BMET has approved it for Saudi job orders, with a cap of Tk 58,610 per worker in one letter [A0019].
   **Need to know:** which other agencies place workers in the promised Saudi job at a fair cost; there is no public rating of agencies.

5. **Check the company exists.** Saudi companies have a unified national number starting with 7, which replaced the commercial registration (CR) number [A0123, A0127].
   You'll need: the company's name or unified national number.
   1. On [mc.gov.sa/en/eservices/Pages/Commercial-data.aspx](https://mc.gov.sa/en/eservices/Pages/Commercial-data.aspx) (Ministry of Commerce, free, in Arabic), search the name or number, type the verification code and press Search; the registration data appear at once [A0098, A0099]. Help: 1900 in Saudi Arabia, cs@mc.gov.sa from Bangladesh [A0100, A0126].
   2. If the company isn't found or doesn't match the employer on your demand letter, don't pay, and complain about the agency to BMET (below) [A0098, A0107, A0056]. **Need to know:** which fields the result shows.

**Avoid a "free visa".** There is no such thing [A0024]: it is a visa bought from a sponsor with no job for you, so you must find your own work and housing, often pay the sponsor a monthly cut, work illegally and can be deported [A0083, A0082, A0081, A0079]. Signs: the visa is from a household or person who doesn't need a worker; you get only a 3-month iqama; the contract signed in Bangladesh isn't the one used in Saudi Arabia [A0081, A0080]. Have the papers checked (Before paying) [A0108]; report the seller to BMET or the ministry (below) or on the ministry hotline 16135 (+880 9610102030 from abroad) [A0056, A0048, A0025]; in an emergency call 999, the police emergency number [B0171].

**Before paying:**

- **Get the employer's name, job, salary and embassy-attested contract in writing,** and the agency's name, licence number, address and phone [A0107, A0108].

- **Look for BMET's permission letter for your job.** For a group job order it names the agency and RL number, the Saudi company and sponsor ID, the visa and demand numbers, the job, the number of workers, salary and benefits, and the most the agency may take from each worker [A0017].
  You'll need: the agency's name or RL number.
  1. On [bmet.gov.bd/pages/miscellaneous-infos](https://bmet.gov.bd/pages/miscellaneous-infos), search the agency's name, RL number or "Saudi" [A0020, A0021].
  2. Open that agency's Saudi PDF and compare company, job, salary and maximum cost with what you were told [A0017]. One letter, for example, allowed at most Tk 50,000, by cheque, bank draft or pay order only, after visa, clearance and flight were confirmed, with the employer paying all Saudi-side costs and each worker signing the contract and getting a Bangla translation [A0016, A0018].
  3. No letter for your job? Have the papers checked at your DEMO (next point) [A0022].
  **Need to know:** whether BMET still issues these letters for Saudi group visas, and where.

- **Have your papers checked.** Take copies of the demand letter, contract and visa to your DEMO or BMET (89/2 Kakrail, Dhaka-1000) and ask them to check they are real [A0022, A0110]; DEMO addresses and phones are in BMET's list of DEMOs (Step 1, item 1) [A0112, A0113]. **Need to know:** the cost, the time, and whether the answer is in writing.

- **Pay the agency through its bank account, by cheque, bank draft or pay order, and keep the receipt** [A0023, A0016]; complaints without written receipts often fail to win proper compensation [A0043].

- **If you need to borrow,** Probashi Kallyan Bank (PKB), the government bank for migrant workers, lends up to Tk 3 lakh without collateral at 9% simple interest, repaid monthly over up to 3 years including a grace period, once you have the visa and BMET card [A0052, A0053, A0050].
  You'll need: age 18 to 60 and no unpaid loan anywhere; 3 passport-size photos; copies of NID, passport, visa, BMET smart card (step 8) and contract if any; 3 signed MICR cheque leaves and a statement from your own bank account; Tk 500 to open a savings account; and 2 guarantors who, like you, live permanently in the branch's area: one well-off person with 3 signed MICR cheque leaves, a bank statement and proof of income, and one family member or close relative, each with a photo, NID copy and proof of address such as a utility bill [A0049, A0050, A0051].
  1. At your nearest branch ([branch list](https://pkb.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922e0de933eb65569e28f0c)), fill in the free form and open the savings account [A0050, A0120].
  2. Pay 0.5% processing and 0.5% documentation (each plus VAT) and Tk 900 per lakh risk cover; bring 16 revenue stamps of Tk 10, plus 3 adhesive stamps of Tk 100 for each guarantor [A0055, A0052].
  3. The bank must decide within 7 working days of complete papers [A0052]. Help: 16238, or +880 9677-787878 from abroad [A0054]. **Need to know:** how, when and into which account the loan is paid.
  **Scam at this step:** PKB staff have been accused of asking Tk 10,000-20,000 in bribes for a Tk 3 lakh loan; there is no such fee [A0040, A0055]. Complain to PKB's grievance officer, the General Manager (Administration and Accounts), gm2@pkb.gov.bd, +88-02-48321195, who has 30 working days [A0121]; then the Deputy Managing Director, dmd@pkb.gov.bd, +88-02-48319468, who has 20 working days [A0122]. Or call the ministry hotline 16135 [A0025] or file at [grs.gov.bd](https://www.grs.gov.bd/), the government's complaint system [A0036].

- **If the agency takes your money and doesn't send you, sends you to a different job or overcharges, complain to BMET.** It is free and meant to take 30 working days: an inquiry committee hears both sides, BMET recommends, the ministry decides [A0056].
  You'll need: your name, NID number, passport number, BMET smart card number if you have one, age, father's or husband's and mother's names, permanent address and phone; if abroad, your address there and a relative (name, relation, mobile) to attend for you; the agency's name, RL number and address, or the broker's name, age, parents' names and address; any earlier complaints against them [A0037]; and proof such as receipts and the contract [A0056].
  1. **File online** on OEP's complaint system (BMET's "online complaint" link goes to employee.oep.gov.bd); you or your family can upload documents and follow progress [A0039, A0038].
  2. **Or on paper:** fill in BMET's [complaint form (Form Ka)](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-bmet/2024/12/fe647f3d68c14792a15f496990376a8a.PDF) with the reason and what happened, sign or thumbprint it, and hand it with copies of your proof to BMET (89/2 Kakrail, Dhaka-1000), your DEMO, or the Bangladesh Embassy's labour wing if abroad [A0037, A0110].
  3. **No action in 30 working days:** write to BMET's complaint officer, the Director (Employment), diremi@bmet.gov.bd, 01711111544, who has 3 months [A0036, A0056].
  4. **Then** appeal to the Secretary, Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare, secretary@probashi.gov.bd, 02-41030444 (+880 2 41030444 from abroad), who has 1 month; then [grs.gov.bd](https://www.grs.gov.bd/), 3 months [A0036].
  5. **Or go to the ministry directly:** its Enforcement-2 branch, sasenforcement@probashi.gov.bd or 02-41030255, with an application, proof, your full address and the agency's name, RL number and address; free, action within 3-5 working days, settlement meant within 30 working days [A0048]. Hotline 16135, or +880 9610102030 from abroad [A0025].
  **Free legal help:** OKUP (Ovibashi Karmi Unnayan Program, a migrant workers' organisation), 01842773300, gives legal advice and helps file a case [A0046, A0047]. **Need to know:** the menu path to OEP's complaint form; whether a paper complaint gets a receipt or complaint number, and how to follow it up; whether ILO or IOM in Dhaka take up individual complaints.

### Step 2: Get the attested demand letter

The employer registers on the Bangladesh Embassy Riyadh's e-demand system at [beriyadh.org](https://beriyadh.org/employers/create) with its company registration number and uploads its demand letter (its request for workers), a power of attorney naming the agency, the employment contract, a letter of undertaking, a letter of guarantee, its project papers and its wage protection certificate, with a list of at least 10 current workers [A0102, A0103, A0085]. The embassy's labour wing checks and attests them online, for single and group visas alike [A0086, A0088, A0087].

1. **Ask the agency for a copy of the attested demand letter and contract** [A0107]. If it won't show them, don't pay, and complain to BMET (step 1, Before paying) [A0056].
   You'll need: nothing; the agency or employer holds the papers.
2. **Have them checked** at your DEMO or BMET (step 1, Before paying) [A0022].

**Need to know:** whether workers can check an attestation themselves on beriyadh.org; the embassy's attestation fee and processing time.

### Step 3: Pass the skill test

Workers in the listed trades must pass the Takamol skill test under Saudi Arabia's Skills Verification Program (SVP), which needs real skill and work experience in the trade [A0058, A0070]. It runs at 27 TTCs and BRTC Gazipur, the Bangladesh Road Transport Corporation's training institute [A0061, A0130]. A retake costs the full fee again [A0009].

You'll need: your passport (valid), your NID, your own email address that you can open, a Visa or Mastercard endorsed for US dollars with USD 85 on it, and the occupation on your visa [A0062, A0063, A0007].

1. **Check which centre tests your trade** in BMET's [list of occupations by test centre](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-bmet/2026/2/dc7e2b1f-5458-4a46-9197-958e2cd8941e.docx) ("TTC wise Occupation under SVP", on bmet.gov.bd under Training, Takamol); some centres test only loading and unloading, while Bangladesh-Korea TTC Dhaka tests 32 occupations [A0105, A0106].
2. **Register** on [svp-international.pacc.sa](https://svp-international.pacc.sa/) [A0117, A0118]: press Sign-up, enter your names, NID, passport number and expiry, country, nationality, email, date of birth and a password (8+ characters with a capital, a small letter, a number and a symbol), all exactly as in your passport; tick "Agree to acknowledge that all the data are correct" and type the code emailed to you [A0062]. With only one name, write it as the first name and your father's name as the last; the same name in both counts as an error [A0064]. With a wrong passport number you can't sit the test, so fix it first through Support at the bottom right [A0067].
3. **Book:** sign in with your email, password and the emailed code; in Account Dashboard press Start Verification and choose your occupation (fixed after this), Direct Assessment (In-person), your division or city (the centre is assigned automatically), the test language and a green date; tick "I declare that the information entered is correct" and press Confirm [A0063, A0064, A0068].
4. **Pay USD 85** with the card's number, CVV, expiry date and holder name, then press Pay; the test ticket appears under Reservation and is emailed to you [A0007, A0008, A0063, A0066]. Pay no one more than the fee: there is no way to pass for extra money, and a worker caught paying to pass has the account locked and can't go to Saudi Arabia [A0070, A0071]. **If someone offers to fix the test for money,** report it to the police (call 999, or your local police station) [A0070, B0171], tell BMET's Takamol and Japan Support Centre (BMET Bhaban, 4th floor, Kakrail, Dhaka; 01901165981 or 01901165982) [A0072], and complain to BMET (step 1, Before paying) [A0056].
5. **Prepare if you want** [A0069]. A 3-day loading and unloading course costs Tk 2,000 at 24 TTCs [A0069]. Free 5-day courses: gas station attendants (Kishoreganj TTC), sellers (Bangladesh-Korea TTC Chattogram); free 10-day courses: food and beverage counter staff (Dhaka Mohila TTC), barbers (Sylhet TTC), concrete finishers (Bangladesh-Korea TTC Dhaka), building and construction workers (Khulna, Jhalokathi, Moulvibazar, Nilphamari and Chapainawabganj TTCs) [A0116]. The course certificate isn't an SVP certificate [A0069].
6. **Take the test** with your original passport, a photocopy, the ticket and one photo, arriving before the 30-minute briefing [A0066, A0059]. The computer test is 15 questions in 30 minutes in your chosen language; the practical test is 1 hour 30 minutes, in Bangla [A0059, A0060]. Phones and bags go in a locker; looking at another candidate counts as cheating [A0066].
7. **To change the date,** cancel and rebook once, up to 96 hours before [A0068]. Miss it and you are marked absent; you can pay and book again 96 hours after the slot [A0068].
8. **Get the result** on the SVP portal, where anyone can check a certificate is genuine [A0073]. Pakistan's official SVP partner says a pass is uploaded within 24 hours and a candidate who fails books a new test [A0119]. To correct the certificate, press Support at the bottom right and raise a ticket with your name, email, a subject and description, Request Type "Suggestion", Client "Test Taker", your country and passport number, attaching your passport, ticket and certificate [A0068].

**Need to know:** the pass mark; how long the certificate stays valid; how to pay without a dollar-endorsed card, and whether a card in someone else's name works; the exact SVP page for checking a certificate; how to dispute a fail.

### Step 4: Pass the Wafid medical check

Wafid is the Gulf Health Council's online booking for the health check everyone going to work in a Gulf country must pass; a medical centre in your city does the tests [B0001]. A result is valid for visa issuing for only 2 months, so book when the agency is ready to apply for your visa, after step 3 [B0017].

**You'll need:** your passport (number, issue date, issue place, expiry date), your NID number, an email address, a phone that can receive a code, and a card that pays online for USD 10 or USD 25 [B0005, B0006, B0004].

1. **Book the slip yourself** at [wafid.com](https://wafid.com/en/book-appointment/): press Book an Appointment, choose Bangladesh, your city and Saudi Arabia [B0001]; Standard (USD 10, centre assigned) or Premium (USD 25, you choose centre and date) [B0004]; enter your details exactly as in your passport, typing the passport number twice, choose Work Visa, enter the code sent to you and tick the box confirming the details are correct [B0005, B0006]. Pay by card (expiry as year then month) [B0002] and print the slip; to reprint it, open Print GCC slip and enter your passport number and nationality [B0007].
2. **Go to the centre straight away** with the slip [B0003]. It takes your photo and fingerprints at reception and again during the tests [B0037]. You pay for the tests at the centre; the Council doesn't set that fee [B0038]. Pay no more than about Tk 8,500-9,000 in total [B0030].
3. **Check the result** on [Wafid status search](https://wafid.com/en/medical-status-search/) with your passport number and nationality, or the slip number; it can take up to 26 days [B0008, B0009].
4. **If a detail is wrong,** ask the centre for a free change request and note its number and date [B0010, B0011].
5. **If you paid but got no slip,** email support@wafid.com with your nationality, passport number, full name, email address, payment date and time, card number and cardholder's name, a copy of the card and a bank statement showing the payment [B0012, B0013].

**Don't buy a "fit" result.** Agencies and brokers sell fit reports for about Tk 20,000-30,000, but Saudi Arabia tests you again on arrival [B0032, B0030, B0018]. Some centres declare people unfit, then ask for a bribe to change it [B0035].

**If you are declared unfit** (for example HIV, hepatitis B or C, tuberculosis, organ failure, uncontrolled blood pressure or diabetes, cancer) [B0016], you can't retake the test for 3 months [B0027].

**If the result is wrong, the test was done badly or the centre demanded extra money:**

1. **Complain in writing to the Gulf Health Council within 2 weeks of the test;** late or anonymous complaints are refused [B0020, B0021]. Use the Complaint Form at the end of the [Wafid regulations](https://wafid.com/static/pdf/Regulations%20of%20Medical%20Examination%20of%20Expatriates%20Coming%20to%20GCC%20States%20for%20Work%20or%20Residence.pdf) [B0150], giving your contact details, the centre's name and city, what happened and any proof (receipts, messages, another lab's report) [B0023, B0024]. Send it to info@ghc.sa, by post to P.O. Box 7431, Riyadh 11462, Saudi Arabia, or by hand to its office on Samha Street, Diplomatic Quarter, Riyadh (920020885) [B0022, B0021, B0148, B0149]. It says within 3 working days whether it accepts the complaint and decides within 30 days; the decision is final, and a centre at fault can be warned, re-checked, suspended or closed [B0151, B0152, B0019, B0025]. Its Grievance Committee hears migrants' appeals [B0026].
2. **In Bangladesh, complain about overcharging** to the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare [B0033]: send an application with your receipts, your full address and the centre's name and address to its Enforcement-2 branch (sasenforcement@probashi.gov.bd, 02-41030255); it's free and meant to be settled within 30 working days [A0048]. The ministry hotline is 16135 [A0025].

**Need to know:** the official fee for the tests in Bangladesh; how and by when to appeal to the Grievance Committee.

### Step 5: Give fingerprints at the Tasheer visa centre

Every work visa applicant gives fingerprints and a photo at a Tasheer centre; Tasheer runs Saudi visa centres [B0039]. Dhaka: Jamuna Future Park (Ground Floor), KA-244, Kuril, Progoti Shoroni [B0153, B0039]. Chattogram: World Trade Center Chittagong (2nd Floor), 102/103 Agrabad C/A, Commerce College Road [B0154, B0041].

**You'll need:** your passport (valid 6+ months, 2 blank pages) and a copy, the Wafid report and the printed appointment letter, as Tasheer asks of Pakistani applicants [B0146, B0147, B0070].

1. **Book free** at [vc.tasheer.com/appointment](https://vc.tasheer.com/appointment): Bangladesh, Appointment, Schedule, then visa type, centre, date and time [B0040, B0044, B0043]. One slot per person [B0070]. Pay no website or broker to book [B0048].
2. **Change or cancel** in the Appointment menu up to the day before; a change within 3 days, or a no-show, blocks booking for 7 days [B0045, B0046].
3. **Arrive 15 minutes early,** or the slot can be cancelled, moved to the paid Lounge or delayed 7 days [B0047].
4. **At the centre** you get a token, pay the service fee, staff fill in the visa form, and you give biometrics [B0156]. Keep the original receipt to collect your passport [B0156].
5. **Track it** on vc.tasheer.com and collect the passport within 30 days, or it goes back to the embassy [B0157]. The visa is electronic, with a QR code [B0158]; check it in step 7.

**Need to know:** Tasheer's document list and fee for Bangladeshi applicants; its phone, email and complaint route in Bangladesh.

### Step 6: Give the agency your papers for the visa

The agency applies for the visa; agencies ask for a passport valid 6+ months with two blank pages, white-background photos, a police clearance certificate, the Wafid fit report and, where your trade needs it, the skill test certificate [B0064, B0065]. **Need to know:** the Saudi Embassy's own document list, including whether every trade needs police clearance; the card service charge for the police clearance fee.

**Get the police clearance certificate yourself** at [pcc.police.gov.bd](https://pcc.police.gov.bd/ords/f?p=500) [B0054].

*You'll need:* a passport valid 3+ months [B0049]; give the permanent or emergency address written in your passport as your current address [B0050]; a scan of its information page attested by a first-class gazetted officer [B0053]; if it shows no address, an attested NID, birth registration or ward councillor's certificate [B0051]; a mobile number; Tk 1,500 [B0052, B0165].

### Step 9: After arrival

**If you came without BMET clearance,** join the Welfare Board from Saudi Arabia to get its insurance and help: apply at [portal.wewb.gov.bd](https://portal.wewb.gov.bd/) with your passport and proof you work legally; the embassy labour wing processes it and you pay in riyals [E0020]. The welfare fee is Tk 5,500 [B0100, B0099].

**Iqama (residence card).** You enter on a 3-month permit; within it the employer must get your iqama and work permit (about SR 11,000 a year) [B0131], after your medical test and its health insurance for you [B0108]. Otherwise you become undocumented and can be detained and deported [B0132, B0135]. The employer pays for it and renewals [B0113, B0116]; you do the medical test and fingerprints, and it takes 1-3 weeks once papers are complete [B0144, B0143]. Check the name matches your passport; the employer corrects it at the Passports Directorate (Jawazat) [B0110]. Carry it always and show it to police on request [B0111]. Without it you can't open a bank account, use an ATM, send money or get a SIM [B0112].

*No iqama after 90 days:* complain to the labour ministry on 19911 (Sunday to Thursday, 8 am to 5 pm) [B0109, E0052]; then follow If you aren't paid and call the embassy labour wing on 8001000125 [E0024].

**Set up Absher,** the Interior Ministry's portal for your iqama, visas and appointments [B0124]. *You'll need:* your iqama number and a Saudi mobile number [B0125, B0128]. On [absher.sa](https://www.absher.sa/) press New User, enter your iqama number, a password and the SMS code [B0125, B0128]. Activate it with Nafath, the Saudi digital ID app [B0127], or at an Absher kiosk in a mall or Jawazat office (iqama number, both index fingers, mobile number, code) [B0126]. If that fails, book an appointment on Absher and activate it free at a Jawazat office [B0176]. Support: 920020405 [B0175].

**Check your contract on Qiwa,** the labour ministry's platform, where every contract must be recorded [B0117]. *You'll need:* your iqama number, date of birth as on the iqama and Nafath [B0130]. Register at [sso.qiwa.sa](https://sso.qiwa.sa/en/registration) and confirm your identity with Nafath [B0142]. The employer sends the contract to your account; what you approve there is the record of your pay and terms [B0129]: understand every term first [B0123] and keep a copy [B0118]. Support: 920000105, support@qiwa.sa [B0174].

**Keep your passport.** No employer may keep your passport or iqama, even "for safekeeping" [B0119, B0114]. If it does, call 19911 [E0052] or complain free on the labour ministry's Beneficiary Page at [selfservice.hrsd.gov.sa](https://selfservice.hrsd.gov.sa/node/) with your Absher login; it replies within 5 business days [B0120, B0168, B0169, B0170]. You can also follow If you aren't paid.

**Pay.** The employer must pay your wages on time through the Wage Protection Program, which records each payment [B0121], and give housing or a housing allowance [B0122]. Nothing may be deducted for recruitment, visa or iqama costs [B0116]. Typical monthly pay: helpers SR 1,200-1,800, cleaners SR 1,000-1,700, construction workers and masons SR 1,600-3,000, drivers SR 1,500-2,500, electricians SR 1,800-3,500 [B0137, B0136, B0138, B0139, B0140]. 

**Need to know:** the amount in riyals the labour wing now charges for Welfare Board membership; whether 19911 has a Bangla option; how a worker can see their recorded wage payments.

### Your rights at work

**Contract.** It must be recorded on Qiwa (Step 9), with a copy for you and the employer [C0016]. It runs for a fixed term, usually 2 years; with no length written it counts as one year, and it renews for the same length if both sides carry on [C0095, C0026]. It should state your leave, whether it is paid and who pays the airfare [C0152]. You may work only for your employer, in the job title on your work permit [C0021].

**Hours.** 8 a day and 48 a week (6 and 36 for Muslims in Ramadan), never over 12 a day in total [C0001, C0002, C0096], with a break of at least 30 minutes after 5 hours in a row [C0004]. From 15 June to 15 September, no private-sector work in direct sun from 12 noon to 3 pm [C0108].

**Overtime.** Full hourly wage plus half the basic hourly wage per extra hour; public-holiday work counts as overtime [C0005, C0010].

**Pay.** There is no minimum wage for foreign workers; the contract wage is what you can claim [C0092]. It is paid into your bank account on the contract date [C0006]. Never sign that you were paid before the money arrives; it can block a later claim [C0025]. Recruitment, iqama and work permit fees and renewals, job-title changes, exit and re-entry visas, transfer fees and your ticket home at the contract's end are the employer's and can't be taken from your pay [C0020].

**Rest and leave.** One day off a week, normally Friday, not swappable for money [C0003]. Paid annual leave of at least 21 days, 30 after 5 years with the same employer, on dates the employer sets with 30 days' notice; untaken days are paid when you leave [C0007, C0009, C0008]. Paid holidays: 4 days at each Eid, Founding Day (22 February), National Day (23 September) [C0010]. Sick leave: up to 120 days a year, 30 at full pay, 60 at three-quarters, 30 unpaid [C0011]. Paid leave: 3 days for a child's birth, 5 for your wedding, 5 for the death of a spouse, parent or child, 10 to 15 days once for Hajj after 2 years; 12 weeks' maternity leave [C0012, C0013].

**Housing, food and transport.** The employer gives housing and transport or allowances for them [C0014]; food only if the contract says so [C0099].

**If a right is broken,** report it in the labour ministry's (Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development) app, on Google Play and the App Store, or in Tawakkalna, the Saudi government app: service Reporting Violations of Labor Regulations, sign in with Absher, pick the report type, give the workplace location, company details and proof; it is free, the ministry acts within 3 days and the status shows in the app [C0157, C0158]. Money owed is claimed through friendly settlement (If you aren't paid) [C0024]. Ministry help line: 19911, Sunday to Thursday 8 am to 5 pm, Arabic and English [C0050].

**Health insurance.** The employer must insure you [C0015]. Check your cover with the Council of Health Insurance's Insurance Information Inquiry at [chi.gov.sa](https://www.chi.gov.sa/en/ServicesDirectory/Pages/Eservices-CheckInsurance.aspx) [C0110]. **Need to know:** which number the check asks for (iqama or passport) and what the result shows. If you aren't insured or the premium is taken from your pay, complain at [care.chi.gov.sa](https://care.chi.gov.sa/) (guest or account) with your details, the employer's contact details, what happened and scanned proof; it is handled within 15 working days without naming you to the employer [C0159]. The cover doesn't pay for serious illness, so go only if you are fit [C0101].

**Your passport.** No employer may keep your passport or iqama [C0022]. Complain on the labour ministry's Beneficiary Page [C0023]: [hrsd.gov.sa](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/ministry-services/services/client-page) > Contact Us > Beneficiary Page > Start the Service > log in with Nafath > Complaints > Add a Request; choose the sector and categories, fill in the fields, press Send [C0154, C0155]. It needs a ministry or Absher account, is free, answered within 5 days, and also in the ministry's app [C0156].

**Probation.** Up to 180 days if the contract sets it; either side can end the contract without compensation or end-of-service pay, and you pay your own ticket home [C0017, C0018, C0098].

**Leaving without notice.** You may stop work without notice and keep all your rights if the employer doesn't meet its duties (for example, doesn't pay), moves you to another job without consent, assaults or mistreats you, leaves a known serious danger unfixed, or lied about the job when you signed [C0029, C0094]. If you stop coming to work, the employer can report you absent (huroob, below) [C0062] and, after a written warning, dismiss you without end-of-service pay for over 30 days' absence in a contract year or 15 in a row [C0028].

### How long you can stay

As long as you have a contract and a valid iqama, which the employer renews and pays for [C0020]. Work visas are for ages 18 to 60 [C0100]. **No pension:** GOSI, the Saudi social insurance agency, covers foreign workers only for work injuries [C0109]; you get end-of-service pay instead (Leaving at the end of the job) [C0031].

### What can get you jailed

**Don't bring** narcotic drugs, alcohol, anything with pork, poppy seeds (khas khas), khat, gutkha, pan masala, non-Islamic religious literature, obscene material, amulets or anything linked to black magic, including on your phone [C0181, C0102, C0160, C0103]. **Never carry a parcel you haven't packed and checked yourself,** even for family [C0104]; Saudi Arabia executes foreigners for drug smuggling [C0107]. **Medicines:** your own only, at most 30 days' supply, in original packs, with a medical report and a prescription under 6 months old, both translated into Arabic [C0105, C0106]. **Don't work for anyone but the employer on your permit, or in another job,** even for a few hours [C0021].

### If you aren't paid

You can claim unpaid wages, overtime, leave pay and end-of-service pay. Act in this order.

1. **Collect proof:** your Qiwa contract, bank statements, payslips, messages about pay, and a list of missing months and amounts [C0143, C0016]. Sign nothing saying you were paid [C0025].

2. **File a friendly-settlement claim with the labour ministry.** Every wage claim starts here; unsettled claims go to the labour court [C0024, C0039].
   You'll need: your iqama number, a phone for SMS codes (a Bangladeshi number can be added), the employer's name, your job title and start date, the city where you last worked, each claim with months and amounts, and proof as PDF, JPG, JPEG, TIF or PNG files up to 5 MB each [C0039, C0048, C0034, C0049, C0043].
   1. On the [Friendly Settlement service page](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/ministry-services/services/269970) press Start Service and log in to the Individual Portal [C0034, C0039, C0161].
   2. Choose Amicable Settlement Lawsuit Filing, check your details, pick your employer (or Another Establishment to type it), enter the job details and the settlement office for your city, add each claim with its amount, attach proof, tick the declaration and press Submit Claim [C0039, C0034]. Keep it short, with dates, amounts and a list of attachments [C0143]. Template: "I worked for [company] as [job] from [date]. My contract wage is SR [amount] a month. I have not been paid for [months], a total of SR [amount]. I ask for these wages to be paid. Attached: contract, bank statements, messages." You get a claim number at once and the review result by SMS within 48 hours [C0044]. File within 12 months; settlement is meant to take 21 working days [C0035, C0042].
   3. Join the session by Webex video call and show your iqama; there is no interpreter for non-Arabic speakers [C0046, C0045]. If you miss it the claim is dismissed, but you can reopen it from My Claims within 21 working days [C0041, C0162]. If the employer doesn't come, its services are suspended and a new date is set; if it keeps not coming, you can move employer without its consent and the claim goes to court [C0040]. The settlement service also works in Urdu [C0038].
   4. If settled, print the settlement report from My Lawsuits [C0163]; if not, the claim goes to the labour court on Najiz, the Justice Ministry's online court system [C0024, C0042].
   **Need to know:** how a foreign worker first creates the Individual Portal login (Nafath or Absher); whether filing costs SR 0 or SR 100 [C0036, C0037]; whether an interpreter or the labour wing may join the session; how to enforce a settlement the employer ignores.

3. **At the same time, ask the Bangladesh Embassy's labour wing for help.** It advises workers in disputes and goes to the labour court with them [C0115, C0116]. Call free on 8001000125 in Saudi Arabia or 0114195300 (extensions 401-403), or email counselorriyadh@probashi.gov.bd or labourwing.riyadh@yahoo.com [C0145, C0175]. Or use its [online service request form](https://www.bangladeshembassy.org.sa/srspub/): name, mobile, city, iqama and passport numbers, the problem type (for wages, the housing, food and wage one), a description in Bangla or English, and copies of your iqama and passport [C0118, C0119]. Template: "I am [name], iqama [number], working for [company] in [city]. I have not been paid since [month]. I have filed a friendly-settlement claim, number [number]. Please help me with the hearing." Office: 8039 Dareen Street, Diplomatic Quarter, Riyadh [C0117]. **Need to know:** office hours, what to bring to a walk-in visit, how soon it answers the form, and whether it can act as your attorney and how to give it a power of attorney.

4. **From Bangladesh, a relative can ask the government to act.** Send the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare (Welfare-1 branch, saswelfare@probashi.gov.bd, 02-41030251) a short application saying what happened and the wages, allowances or insurance owed, with copies of your passport, iqama, BMET smart card and Saudi ID number, and the employer's name and full address; it is free, and within 7 working days the ministry asks the labour wing and the Wage Earners' Welfare Board to act [C0122]. Template: "My [brother], [name], passport [number], works for [company], [address], Saudi Arabia. He has not been paid since [month]; SR [amount] is owed. Copies attached. Please ask the labour wing in Riyadh to recover his wages." If nothing happens, contact the head of its Mission and Welfare wing: 02-41030219, 01711427769, adsmission@probashi.gov.bd [C0180]. OKUP, a Bangladeshi migrant workers' organisation, gives free legal advice and helps families take cases (01842773300) [C0140, C0144].

5. **If the whole company has stopped paying, claim wage insurance.** It pays only when the employer hasn't paid 80% or more of its workers for over 6 months, after friendly settlement [C0073]: up to 6 months' unpaid wages at your Qiwa contract rate and up to SR 1,000 for a ticket home, at most SR 17,500, not end-of-service pay [C0074, C0075, C0076]. You can claim in Saudi Arabia, even after changing employer [C0077]; domestic, seasonal, temporary and farm workers aren't covered [C0082]. Check your cover with your iqama number at [labor.aletihad.sa](https://labor.aletihad.sa/), run by the insurer Al-Etihad Cooperative Insurance (free phone 800 3040 110) [C0078, C0178]. Claim in its Worker Portal (Submit Claim) with your iqama or border number and mobile, saying whether you have left Saudi Arabia [C0177]. You or a legal representative send the claim form and declaration, proof of the last wage received, your contract, the official document showing wages owed and your iqama copy, plus your final exit visa for the ticket [C0079, C0081]. The employer has 10 working days to object [C0080]. **Need to know:** how long the insurer takes to pay, and what to do if the claim is refused.

6. **After 3 months without pay you may change employer without consent** (next section) [C0055].

### If you want to change employer

**You can move without your employer's consent** when your Qiwa contract has ended [C0052]; after 12 months' work since entering Saudi Arabia, with 90 days' notice (less if the employer agrees) and a valid contract on Qiwa [C0053, C0054, C0179]; at any time if you haven't been paid for 3 months in a row, your work permit or iqama has expired, the employer has disappeared (travel, prison, death), or you report that the business is secretly run by a foreigner in a Saudi's name and you weren't part of it [C0055, C0056]; or if the employer keeps missing your friendly-settlement sessions [C0040]. Your employer is only notified and can't refuse [C0057].

1. **Find a new employer yourself** and check the company (Step 1, item 5). Pay no one for a job offer or transfer; the new employer pays the fees [C0020]. **Need to know:** whether any official service matches foreign workers already in Saudi Arabia with employers.
2. **Accept its offer on Qiwa:** log in with Nafath, open the offer, check the job title and wage, accept or reject [C0151, C0051].
3. **Your employer is notified and your notice runs,** unless waived [C0051, C0153].
4. **The new employer completes the move;** once approved, the new work permit is issued at once, at no cost to you [C0051, C0172, C0020, C0153].

Keep working for your current employer until Qiwa shows the move is done, or you may never get a permit and stay illegal [C0150]. Never share your Absher or Qiwa password or SMS codes, even with your employer [C0087]. Some employers block moves by cancelling the iqama with a final exit visa or accusing the worker of theft or debt, and some new employers wrongly ask for the old employer's approval [C0086, C0060, C0061]; if blocked, file friendly settlement [C0072]. **Need to know:** what to do if the transfer is rejected or never completed.

### If your employer reports you absent (huroob)

Huroob ("running away") is an employer's Qiwa report that you stopped coming to work: your status becomes "Absent from Work", it can't be withdrawn, and you are removed from the company's records [C0063, C0064]. It can be filed only if you have no contract recorded on Qiwa, your permit or iqama has at least 60 days left, and you have been absent over 30 days in a contract year or 15 in a row [C0062, C0147], so make sure your contract is on Qiwa. When a Qiwa contract ends you get 60 days to move, leave or sign again before being marked absent [C0147, C0148, C0069].

1. **Check your status often** on your Absher dashboard ("Absent from Work" or blocked services) [C0112] or, with your iqama number or passport number and nationality, on the ministry's [expatriate employee inquiry](https://es.hrsd.gov.sa/services/inquiry/nonsaudiempinquiry.aspx) [C0111]. The SMS alert may not arrive if your number isn't linked to the government system [C0065, C0088].
2. **Within 60 days, move or leave:** transfer on Qiwa if you have been in Saudi Arabia over 12 months, or issue yourself a final exit visa on Absher and go home; otherwise you stay "absent" and bear the penalties [C0063, C0066, C0067]. A final exit visa needs you in Saudi Arabia with a valid iqama, a passport valid at least 60 days, traffic fines paid, fingerprints registered and no vehicle in your name [C0165, C0167, C0168]. **Need to know:** the Absher menu path, fee and validity of a worker's own final exit visa.
3. **After 60 days,** if you have been in Saudi Arabia over 12 months, a new employer can still move you on Qiwa, paying overdue permit fees [C0070, C0071]; otherwise apply to the embassy's Special Exit Programme (Going home without valid papers), which excludes people with a police or court case [C0121, C0120].
4. **If the report is false,** tell the labour wing on its online form (If you aren't paid, item 3), choosing the huroob problem type, or the court or jail type if you are also accused of a crime [C0118]. **Need to know:** whether friendly settlement or the labour court can cancel a false report, and how to answer a false theft charge.

Anyone offering to remove a huroob for a fee is a scammer; a real change shows only on Absher, Qiwa or the ministry's site [C0113].

### Leaving at the end of the job

1. **Give notice:** 30 days before a fixed-term contract ends, 60 for an open-ended one [C0097]. A resignation counts as accepted after 30 days without an answer; the employer can delay it up to 60 days, and you can withdraw it within 7 [C0027].
2. **Get your final exit visa yourself** on Absher or Qiwa, without the employer's consent; the employer is notified, and you bear the consequences of any breach of contract [C0128, C0127]. Conditions as in huroob, item 2 [C0165, C0167, C0168].
3. **Collect what you are owed** within 1 week of the contract ending, or 2 if you ended it [C0093]. End-of-service pay is half a month's last wage per year for the first 5 years and a full month per later year, part-years counted [C0031]; if you resign, you get a third after 2 to 5 years, two-thirds after 5 to 10, all after 10 [C0032]. Untaken leave is paid [C0008].
4. **Get your service certificate,** free, with your dates, job and last wage, and your papers back [C0030]. Or issue it yourself: qiwa.sa > Individual Account > Employment Certificates (Suggested Actions) > Apply Now > Service Certificate > select the job > Next Step > submit; it is approved at once for download [C0149, C0176].
5. **The employer pays your ticket home** [C0020]. If anything is unpaid, file friendly settlement within 12 months [C0035].

### Going home on leave

Apply for an exit and re-entry visa yourself on Absher; the employer is notified, its approval isn't needed, and it pays the fee [C0146, C0020]: SR 200 for one trip up to 2 months, SR 100 per extra month, SR 500 for multiple trips over 3 months [C0164, C0169]. You must be in Saudi Arabia, with a passport valid at least 90 days, fingerprints registered, no unpaid traffic or other fines, and a valid iqama and Qiwa contract; for a trip set in months the iqama must cover 90 days plus the trip [C0165, C0167, C0129]. "Return Before" on the visa is your last day to come back [C0166]. Your contract says who pays the ticket [C0152]. At the airport in Bangladesh, the Probashi Kallyan (expatriate welfare) desk checks the re-entry visa [C0125]. On Absher you declare you understand the measures taken if you don't return before your approved leave ends [C0171]. **Need to know:** what those measures are, now that the old three-year entry ban is lifted [C0170].

### Bringing your family

Skilled workers may bring a spouse, sons under 25 and unmarried daughters (Family residence) [C0130]. Each work permit is graded High-skilled, Skilled or Basic on the employer's Qiwa account, and if the grade is wrong, a correction can be requested, which depends on the job title in your Qiwa contract being right [C0173, C0174]. **Need to know:** whether a worker can see their own grade, and whether High-skilled counts as skilled here.

### Scams

Done to workers:

- **Free visa:** Step 1, Avoid a "free visa".
- **Shell-company visas:** visas in the names of companies that don't exist; the broker vanishes once the worker lands [C0133]. Check the company (Step 1, item 5).
- **Wrong visa or job:** brokers promise a 15-month iqama and send the worker on a 3-month tourist visa, after which the worker is illegal [C0132]; others promise one job and deliver another with a 3-month iqama [C0134]; Saudi embassy officials in Dhaka have been arrested for selling work visas [C0135]. Check the visa type and job title yourself before paying (Step 7).
- **Huroob removal for a fee** (huroob, above) [C0113], and **requests for your Absher or Qiwa codes,** which let someone act in your name [C0087].

## Work visa: domestic worker

For work in a Saudi household, in one of 14 listed domestic jobs such as housemaid, private driver, nanny, cook or gardener [D0023]. Domestic workers fall outside the Saudi Labour Law; the Domestic Workers Regulation sets their rights [D0171, D0172, D0173]. Musaned, the labour ministry's domestic-worker platform, handles contracts and pay [D0157, D0045]. The employer pays recruitment, the iqama (residence card), work permit and transfer fees [D0008]. A woman domestic worker pays nothing: her agency may take no migration cost from her [D0059], and Saudi recruitment offices may take no commission from any domestic worker, before or after she starts work [D0024]. No skill test is needed [D0161]. **Need to know:** what an agency may lawfully charge a man going as a house driver or other male domestic worker.

### Step 1: Check you qualify

1. **Age.** A woman must be 25 to 45 for BMET clearance [D0039]; the housekeeping course (step 3) takes women 25 to 45, returnees up to 50 [D0050]. Saudi law bans domestic workers under 21, and the contract normally ends at 60 [D0001, D0022].
2. **Children, health, education, consent.** A woman whose youngest child is under 6, who is pregnant, or who has a contagious or incurable disease can't join the course [D0051]. She needs class 5 (can be relaxed) and must read and write [D0050, D0054], and needs a no-objection letter from her legal guardian for the course and BMET clearance [D0052, D0039].
3. **Never let anyone change your age.** Agents forge a second birth certificate to get a passport for an under-age girl [D0070, D0071]. Check the birth certificate the passport will use at [everify.bdris.gov.bd](https://everify.bdris.gov.bd/), the birth registration office's check page, with the 17-digit birth registration number and the real date of birth [D0196]. If an agent offers to change the age, call the Probashi call centre on 16135 [E0021]. **Need to know:** which office takes a report of a forged birth certificate used for a passport.

### Step 2: Find the job through an approved agency

A Saudi family picks a worker through a Saudi recruitment office on Musaned, which deals with the Bangladeshi agency; the visa goes to the Saudi embassy through the Foreign Ministry's platform [D0157, D0159].

1. **Use only an agency the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare has approved to send women domestic workers.** Approval requires a Tk 15 lakh security with BMET, a woman on the office staff, and the licence at stake if a worker is abused [D0060, D0059]. Ask for the ministry's approval letter (memo number and date) and check the agency's name and RL (recruiting licence) number are in it [D0060]; check the licence is active on OEP as in Work visa: company worker, step 1. **Need to know:** whether the ministry publishes one current list of all approved agencies, where it posts the approval letters, and how to confirm a letter is genuine. Complaints about agencies: sasenforcement@probashi.gov.bd, 02-41030255 [A0048].
2. **Pay nothing.** Any request to a woman for money for the visa, ticket, training or "processing" breaks the approval conditions [D0059]; complain as in Work visa: company worker, step 1, Before paying.
3. **Get the job in writing before leaving:** contract terms, type of work, workplace, wage and rest periods [D0160]; the wage is the one in the unified contract you both sign [D0158]. The Arabic text is official and must be translated into a language you understand; get the Bangla translation before signing [D0002]. If the agency won't show the contract or its Bangla translation, or the terms differ from what you were told, don't sign, and complain as in Work visa: company worker, step 1, Before paying [A0048]. **Need to know:** how a worker in Bangladesh can see her Musaned contract herself before departure.

### Step 3: Do the one-month housekeeping course (women)

Compulsory for women going as domestic workers, at BMET's Technical Training Centres (TTCs) [D0050, D0055].

1. **You'll need:** your class 5 certificate (if any), your guardian's no-objection letter, and a photocopy of your birth certificate, NID or passport; bring the originals to the interview [D0052, D0054].
2. **Apply on OEP** ([employee.oep.gov.bd](https://employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/login-with-otp)): log in with your mobile number and code, open Training, then Trade/Skills, choose division, district and centre, press Search, pick the housekeeping course, press Apply, then Process to Enroll and Confirm; "My Course" shows your applications [A0027]. You pay the fee and choose the centre and start date online, from BMET's admission calendar [D0053]. **Need to know:** the course fee and its exact name in the OEP list.
3. **Interview** in person at the TTC: a committee checks your original papers and that you can read and write [D0054].
4. **Live at the centre for one month** (24 working days), attending 90% of sessions; it covers cooking, appliances, language, culture, personal safety, reaching the police and embassy, and first aid [D0055, D0056].
5. **Pass the final test:** 100 marks, pass mark 70%, 30 for language and 30 for practical work [D0057].
6. **Download the certificate** online; it is valid 2 years. Not gone within 2 to 3 years: a 3-day refresher; after 3 years: the full course. A returnee with a year abroad does the refresher [D0058].
7. **Don't buy a certificate;** brokers sell them [D0072].

### Step 4: Medical check, visa and BMET clearance

1. **Medical check, Tasheer fingerprints and checking the visa:** as in Work visa: company worker, steps 4, 5 and 7.
2. **BMET clearance** as in Work visa: company worker, step 8, also bringing proof of age and the guardian's letter [D0039].
3. **Before you fly,** save the embassy labour wing's free line 8001000125 [E0086, E0024] and the Probashi call centre 16135 (+880 8000102030 from abroad) [E0021], in your phone and on paper.

### After arrival: your rights

- **Who arranges what:** the employer pays your medical test, iqama and renewal, and health insurance or treatment [D0029]. The Saudi recruitment office must tell you the work and pay, and house and support you if you leave or refuse the employer [D0025, D0026].
- **Probation:** up to 90 days; either side may end the contract [D0003].
- **Hours:** at most 10 a day, a half-hour break after every 5 hours, at least 8 hours' continuous rest, and one paid day off a week [D0004, D0005].
- **Pay:** the contract wage, monthly, into a bank account or digital wallet in your name linked to Musaned through your mobile and iqama numbers [D0158, D0045, D0046]. At most a quarter can be deducted, only for damage you caused, an advance or an official order [D0013]. The Saudi-Bangladesh recruitment agreement set SR 800 a month [D0193]. **Need to know:** whether SR 800 is still the agreed wage.
- **Housing, food and contact:** suitable housing and food or a food allowance [D0011]; the employer must let you contact your family and the embassy, and may not keep your passport, papers or belongings [D0010, D0009].
- **Leave:** up to 30 days' sick leave a year with a medical report, 15 on full pay, the rest on half [D0007]; after two years, if you renew, 30 days' paid leave and a return ticket [D0006].
- **Only your own job:** the employer may not make you work for others or in another job [D0012]. If you work for anyone else yourself: up to SR 2,000 fine, a work ban or both [D0020]; working on your own account, SR 10,000 and deportation [D0030].
- **At the end:** wages and dues within a week, or two if you end the contract; end-of-service pay of one month's wage per four years [D0015, D0014]. Ending the contract without a valid reason costs two months' wages to the other side unless the contract says otherwise [D0016]; if you leave without one, you pay your ticket home [D0019].

### If something goes wrong

**You can leave and keep your rights** if the employer breaks the contract, lied about the work, assaults you or behaves immorally towards you, gives you dangerous work or rents you out [D0017]. The labour ministry can move you to a new employer without your employer's consent if wages are unpaid for three months (in a row or not), nobody collects you from the recruitment office or shelter within 15 days, no iqama is issued or renewed within 30 days, you are rented out, given dangerous work or ill-treated, the employer misses two dispute sessions, or ends the contract during probation [D0031, D0032, D0033, D0034, D0035, D0163, D0164].

**Complain in this order:**

1. **The embassy labour wing.** Call 8001000125 free in Saudi Arabia [E0086, E0024], or use the form at [bangladeshembassy.org.sa/srspub](https://www.bangladeshembassy.org.sa/srspub/) [D0048]. *You'll need:* name, mobile, city, iqama or border number, passport number, the problem type ("domestic worker problems" or "food, housing and pay problems"), a detailed description in Bangla or English, and photos of your iqama and passport if you have them [D0048, D0049]. For example: "I am a housemaid in [city]. My employer [name, phone] hasn't paid me for [N] months and keeps my passport. Please help me get my wages and go home." [D0048] Follow up on the "Your answer (SRS)" page ([bangladeshembassy.org.sa/srspub/index.php/main/viewrecord/](https://www.bangladeshembassy.org.sa/srspub/index.php/main/viewrecord/)) by reference, iqama or mobile number [D0174, D0175]. Jeddah consulate labour wing: +966 12 689 6276 ext 131 [E0036].
2. **If you must leave the house, go to the embassy or consulate,** not to a stranger: 8039 Dareen Street, Diplomatic Quarter, Riyadh [E0025]. The labour wings run safe homes for women in Riyadh, Jeddah and Madinah, paid for by the Wage Earners' Welfare Board [E0038, E0019]; the wait to fly home has run from over a month to 6 months [D0065]. Leaving without telling anyone can bring an absence (huroob) report, which makes you illegal (see If your employer reports you absent (huroob)) [D0063, D0037].
3. **The Saudi labour ministry.** File online through its "File a domestic labour lawsuit" service; the company-worker settlement service doesn't take domestic cases [D0043, D0044]. File within 12 months of the contract ending; the ministry tries to settle within 5 working days, then a committee or the labour court decides [D0018, D0027, D0043]. Musaned: 920002866 [D0061]. The labour wing helps settle disputes and attends court with workers [E0034]. **Need to know:** the service's link and steps, and whether it works without Absher.
4. **From Bangladesh, a relative can act,** if the worker can't use a phone freely [D0062]; the labour wing can then trace the employer and recover wages [D0066]. For a woman worker, apply to the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare, Welfare-2 branch (aswelfare@probashi.gov.bd), describing what happened, with copies of her passport, BMET smart card and iqama, the agency's details and the accused person's mobile and email; it's free, and within 7 working days the ministry writes to the labour wing or BMET [D0040]. To bring her home, apply the same way with proof she is a migrant worker, her workplace and home address in Saudi Arabia, the agency's name and address and the employer's mobile [D0041]. For unpaid wages of any worker, man or woman, use the Welfare-1 branch route in If you aren't paid, item 4 [E0001]. If nothing happens, write to the Joint Secretary (Mission and Welfare), 02-41030219, adsmission@probashi.gov.bd [E0002]. A relative can request a safe-home place, free, through the probashi kallyan (expatriates' welfare) branch at the Deputy Commissioner's office, the district employment and manpower office (DEMO) or the Welfare Board [E0019]. The Probashi call centre (16135) gives advice [E0021].
5. **Legal help.** OKUP, a migrants' organisation, gives free legal advice and help filing cases: 01842773300 [A0046].

### Scams

Age changes (Step 1), bought certificates (Step 3) and fees taken from women (Step 2) are covered at their steps [D0070, D0071, D0072, D0059]. Brokers also sell house-driver and domestic-worker visas from households that need no one; the worker arrives to no job, and working elsewhere is illegal [D0074, D0012].

### House drivers

1. **Cost and pay.** Agencies have asked Tk 4-6 lakh for a house-driver visa [D0076], though the employer pays recruitment (above) [D0008, D0024]. Get any payment agreed in writing and pay only into the agency's bank account (Work visa: company worker, step 1, Before paying). New private drivers earn about SR 1,500-1,800 a month [D0077].
2. **Check the visa.** Brokers promise a company job and send the worker as a private driver, or never arrange the licence [D0078]. Check the visa (Work visa: company worker, step 7): the visa shows the occupation and the sponsor's name [B0073, B0075]. The occupation must be private driver and the sponsor the household you were told about [D0023, B0075]. **Need to know:** how "private driver" is written in Arabic on the visa.
3. **Licence.** The employer arranges and pays for your Saudi licence [D0028]. With a Bangladeshi licence translated by an accredited agency, you can drive on it for at most 3 months [D0079]. For the Saudi licence, book a driving school on Absher (Traffic Services, Book Driving School), do the medical at an approved clinic and open a file at the school; a beginner course costs about SR 2,500-3,000 [D0081, D0080]. **Need to know:** whether the official theory test is offered in Bangla; ask the driving school when you open the file.

For Muslims, all year except the Hajj season (15 Dhul Qa'dah to 15 Dhul Hijjah) [D0082]. USD 80 plus USD 10.50 visa service fee, USD 10.50 insurance service fee and the insurance; single entry, valid 90 days, stay up to 90 days [D0084, D0085]. Packages cost about Tk 1.2 lakh to Tk 3 lakh or more [D0155].

4. **Don't work or stay for Hajj** [D0086, D0094]: illegal work brings a SR 10,000 fine and deportation the first time [D0030]. Brokers also sell tourist visas as jobs [D0075].
5. **If the agency cheats you,** write to the Secretary, Ministry of Religious Affairs, with what you paid, when and how, what wasn't delivered, and the agency's name, licence number and address; the ministry can order a refund within 7 working days or act against the agency [D0176]. ## Family residence

For a worker's spouse, sons under 25 and unmarried daughters, if the worker's permit is graded skilled [D0111]. Skilled means occupational groups 4 to 8 with pay above a ministry threshold and professional accreditation; elementary (group 9) jobs or lower pay are basic; the grade shows in your employer's Qiwa account and can be corrected (Bringing your family) [D0112, D0113, D0114, D0115]. The family can get entry visas with the worker's work visa, finishing residence after arrival [D0111]. **Need to know:** whether this rule replaced the salary condition below, and whether basic-level workers get family visas at all.

1. **Check you qualify:** a salary of at least SR 3,500 a month on your GOSI (General Organization for Social Insurance) record [D0116].
2. **Prepare the papers in Bangladesh:** the marriage certificate, children's birth certificates and your degree certificate, attested and translated into Arabic [D0122]. An apostille replaces embassy legalisation between the two countries [D0169, D0170]; get one on [mygov.bd](https://www.mygov.bd/) as in Work visa: company worker, step 6 [B0066, B0067]. **Need to know:** whether Absher and the Saudi embassy accept an apostille instead of attestation for these papers.
3. **Apply on Absher:** My Services, Expatriates Services, New Family Visa Application, or at the recruitment (Istiqdam) office if Absher shows an error [D0119]. Print and sign the form; your company's HR attests it on the Chamber of Commerce site within 30 days (SR 30) [D0120].
4. **Pay the SR 2,000 visa fee** through SADAD, the Saudi bill-payment system in your bank's app, with your iqama and application numbers [D0195, D0117]; after 2-3 working days Absher shows the visa number; send it to your family [D0121]. **Need to know:** whether the SR 2,000 is charged once per family or for each family member.
5. **The family applies through Tasheer** in Dhaka or Chattogram, which checks papers, takes biometrics and fees and sends the passports to the embassy [D0178, D0179]. Book as in Work visa: company worker, step 5 [B0044], bring a medical report [B0147], and track with Track Status on vc.tasheer.com [B0157]. **Need to know:** Tasheer's document list, fee and processing time for this visa, and whether a refusal can be appealed.
6. **After arrival,** each dependant costs SR 400 a month after the first 90 days [D0118]. A dependant can work in health or education through Ajeer (Changing visa type) [E0060]. Register a baby on Absher (My Services, Services, Civil Affairs, Newborn Registration) within 30 days, or pay a fine [D0123, D0124]. A child of a Bangladeshi parent is Bangladeshi [D0125]. For the baby's passport, get the Saudi birth certificate (Shahada Milad), then a Bangladeshi birth registration at the Jeddah consulate or at a parent's permanent address with Saudi Arabia as place of birth [D0167]. Apply at [passport.gov.bd](http://www.passport.gov.bd/) with the Saudi certificate, copies of both parents' passports and iqamas, the Bangladeshi birth certificate, an A4 colour photo of the baby on grey or white, and passport photos of the baby and a parent [D0166]. **Need to know:** the passport fee and processing time, and whether the Riyadh embassy follows the same steps.

## Changing visa type

**Visit, tourist, Umrah or business visit visa to a work iqama: not allowed.** A visit visa can't be converted into an iqama, family visitors may not work, and Umrah visas may not be used for work [E0054, E0056]. Paid offers to "convert" one are false [E0054, E0055]. Report breaches of residence rules to the police: 911 in Makkah, Madinah, Riyadh and the Eastern region, 999 elsewhere [E0146]. Working on a tourist or Umrah visa brings fines, jail, deportation and a ban of 3 to 10 years [E0059].

**Seasonal work visa to an iqama: not allowed** [E0067]. **Need to know:** whether a temporary work visa can be converted to an iqama.

**Family dependant to worker in health or education.** A spouse or mahram (a close male guardian relative) on a dependant iqama can work for a health or education employer, staying on the family sponsorship, through Ajeer ([ajeer.qiwa.sa](https://ajeer.qiwa.sa)), the labour ministry's platform for lending and placing workers [E0148, E0152, E0147].

1. **You'll need:** valid iqamas for you and the main worker, age 18 or over, any licence the job requires, and an offer from a health or education employer in the low green band of Nitaqat or higher; Nitaqat grades employers by the share of Saudis they employ [E0150, E0151, E0060].
2. **The employer issues the contract on Ajeer** (Contracts, Issue Contract, Contracting Service Between Workforce, Ajeer Accompanying Service); once approved, Ajeer issues your work permit, free [E0149, E0060, E0150]. If it stalls, call Ajeer on 920011040 or the labour ministry on 19911 [E0147, E0051]. **Need to know:** how long it takes.

**Need to know:** whether and how a dependant can work outside health and education.

**Domestic worker to another household,** on Musaned ([musaned.gov.sa](https://musaned.gov.sa)), the labour ministry's platform for domestic workers [E0153, E0180].

1. **You'll need:** the agreement of your current employer, you and the new employer, and your own Absher account [E0063, E0153, E0154].
2. **Your employer starts the transfer on Musaned;** the new employer accepts or rejects it there; a new contract is drawn up and the request completed through Absher [E0153].
3. **You pay nothing:** the service is free and the employer bears transfer fees; payments between employers go through Musaned, capped at SR 16,188 for a Bangladeshi worker [E0154, E0180, E0062].
4. **You get** a new fixed-period work contract [E0144]. **Need to know:** how long the transfer takes, and how you can see the new contract's terms before you agree.
5. **If your employer refuses or there is a money dispute,** file a free claim with the domestic workers' dispute committees on the ministry's Individuals Portal or unified app (My Services, Settlement Claims for Domestic Workers) with your original iqama or passport [E0155]. Musaned: 920002866 [E0140]; embassy labour wing, free: 8001000125 [E0024]. **Need to know:** whether a domestic worker can move without the current employer's consent.

**Domestic worker to a company job,** only in your first year; after your iqama is renewed the request is refused [E0133].

1. **You'll need:** a company that has signed a work contract with you, and your mobile number, to which Qiwa sends the request [E0133, E0132].
2. **The company requests the transfer on Qiwa,** naming your new job [E0132].
3. **Qiwa sends you an SMS;** approve or reject it within 10 days [E0132].
4. **Your household employer is then asked to agree** [E0134]. Qiwa support: 920000105 [E0181]. **Need to know:** what happens if the household employer refuses, what the transfer costs and how long it takes.

**Company worker to another company:** see If you want to change employer.

**Job title on the iqama.** Only an employer in the platinum or green Nitaqat band can change it, on Qiwa (Change expatriate profession); it is free, the worker pays nothing and it takes effect immediately; a job reserved for Saudis can't be given to a foreign worker [E0048, E0049, E0050, E0137]. Since January 2026, titles including sales representative, marketing specialist and procurement manager can't be given to foreign workers this way [E0064]. **Need to know:** what a worker can do if the employer refuses to change the title.

## Asylum

Saudi Arabia has no asylum system, has not signed the 1951 Refugee Convention and in practice generally does not grant asylum [E0072, E0074]. People there illegally, including pilgrimage overstayers, are refused refugee status and deported [E0074]. The only protection is UNHCR, the UN refugee agency: people it recognises may stay temporarily, with valid identity papers, until resettled or returned; those without travel documents are sometimes detained indefinitely [E0073, E0075]. Apply by email to saprstaf@unhcr.org with your full name, family members' names, date of birth, sex, nationality, address, phone, any earlier UNHCR registration and the reason [E0164]. Toll-free line 009668001000281, Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday [E0163]. The Riyadh office, in the Diplomatic Quarter, is by appointment only [E0165].

## Sending money home

**Use a legal channel,** never hundi (the illegal informal channel) or unlicensed agents [E0046]. Saudi police have arrested people for sending money outside legal channels, and Bangladesh has frozen bKash, Nagad and Rocket accounts linked to hundi [E0079, E0078]. Without a valid iqama you can't use a bank or send money formally [B0112].

**Compare costs** on the World Bank's price comparison, [remittanceprices.worldbank.org/corridor/SA/BD](https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org/corridor/SA/BD): sending SR 750 cost about 7-9% through STC Pay, TeleMoney, Western Union or MoneyGram but about 15-16% at bank branches, which charge SR 75; STC Pay delivers in under an hour to cash pickup, a bank account or a mobile wallet [E0080]. **Need to know:** what STC Pay, TeleMoney and the other apps need to open an account.

**To a bKash account:**

1. **You'll need:** the family member's bKash number and full name exactly as registered when the account was opened, and the cash or money in your app [E0083].
2. **Use a bKash partner:** Saudi Awwal Bank, STC Pay, URpay, Mobily Pay, tiqmo, barq, Western Union or MoneyGram [E0082]. Give the number and name with the cash, or enter them in its app [E0083].
3. **Limit:** Tk 2.5 lakh per transfer, as many times as you like [E0084].
4. **Your family gets** the money plus the 2.5% government incentive automatically, and can cash out at BRAC Bank, City Bank and Q-Cash ATMs for Tk 7 per thousand [E0076, E0081, E0085, E0084].
5. **If money doesn't arrive or goes to the wrong number,** complain to the sender company and bKash (16247), then the company's complaint cell [E0083, E0173]. Then Bangladesh Bank's Customer Interest Protection Centre (16236 during office hours on working days, bb.cipc@bb.org.bd, or its online form) with a description, your name, address and phone, and the provider's final reply; a relative needs your written authorisation [E0172, E0174].

## Going home without valid papers

Overstaying any visa can bring a fine up to SR 50,000, up to 6 months in prison and deportation [E0053]. The re-entry ban, from departure, is usually 3 to 5 years for overstaying or huroob and 3 to 10 years for working on a tourist or Umrah visa [E0057, E0058, E0059]. Leaving without a legitimate reason means paying your own ticket [E0136].

**Special Exit Programme (SEP),** the Bangladesh Embassy's route to an exit visa.

1. **Check you qualify:** your sponsor's company is under the labour office of Riyadh, Al Qassim, Hail, Al Jouf, Northern Borders or the Eastern Province, and your iqama has expired, was never issued or carries a huroob report; house drivers and male domestic workers with an expired iqama also qualify [E0027]. Excluded: women domestic workers, anyone who didn't leave on an earlier exit visa, and anyone with a police or court case or wanted (matlub), who must settle it with that authority first [E0028].
2. **You'll need:** your iqama number, original iqama and a passport copy showing its validity [E0029, E0030].
3. **Apply online** at [bangladeshembassy.org.sa/sepsrv.html](https://www.bangladeshembassy.org.sa/sepsrv.html) ("Special Exit programme(SEP) Application Form"); no visit or call needed [E0026, E0031].
4. **Check progress** with your iqama number at [bangladeshembassy.org.sa/seppub](https://www.bangladeshembassy.org.sa/seppub/); when an appointment appears, print it and go to the embassy on that day with the documents [E0031].
5. **Exit visa.** For a Riyadh company, the embassy gets labour office clearance; you then hand in your original iqama and passport copy, and the embassy sends them to Jawazat for the exit visa [E0029]. For a company under another labour office, or a huroob case with a sponsor outside Riyadh, the embassy gives you a letter to take to that labour office or Jawazat, where you collect the exit visa [E0029, E0030]. There is no time limit; workers have waited months, mostly without work [E0096].

**Need to know:** whether SEP costs anything, whether Saudi fines must be paid first, and what to do if refused.

**Sponsors under other labour offices** (Makkah, Madinah, Tabuk, Asir, Jazan, Najran, Al Baha) aren't covered by SEP [E0027]. **Need to know:** their procedure; contact the Jeddah consulate's labour wing (Contacts) [E0166].

**Any other papers problem:** use the embassy's online form at [bangladeshembassy.org.sa/srspub](https://www.bangladeshembassy.org.sa/srspub/), choosing the problem (huroob, no iqama, in jail, salary, domestic worker), describing it in Bangla or English and attaching iqama and passport copies [E0089]. If there's no reply, call the labour wing free on 8001000125, then have a relative write to the ministry (below) [E0024, E0004, E0005]. **Need to know:** how long the embassy takes to reply to the form.

**Without a passport** you need a travel permit (outpass) from the embassy's passport wing: call 8001000126 free, or book "Consular & Welfare Service" at [passportriyadh.com/appointment](https://passportriyadh.com/appointment/) with your name, phone, passport number and OID number [E0088, E0023, E0131]. In a deportation centre, embassy officials arrange it there [E0088]. **Need to know:** the travel permit's documents and fee, what the OID number is, and how to book without a passport number.

**Grace periods.** Saudi Arabia sometimes lets people with expired visas leave without a fine; the embassy posts these on [bangladeshembassy.org.sa/notice.html](https://www.bangladeshembassy.org.sa/notice.html) [E0176, E0175]. In a past window, people whose visit, Umrah or final exit visa expired on 25 February 2026 could leave until 18 April 2026 by going to the airport with a ticket, with no fine [E0087].

**A relative in Bangladesh can ask the government to bring a worker home.**

1. **You'll need:** proof the person is a migrant worker (passport, BMET smart card, iqama or work permit copies), the Saudi workplace and home addresses, the agency's name and address and the employer's mobile number [E0005].
2. **Send an application** describing what happened, with these papers, to the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare, Welfare-2 branch (aswelfare@probashi.gov.bd) [E0004, E0005].
3. **It is free.** Within 7 working days of the letter, the ministry asks the embassy, the Welfare Board or BMET to bring the worker home [E0005].
4. **If nothing happens,** write to the head of its Mission and Welfare wing (adsmission@probashi.gov.bd, 02-41030219, 01711427769) [E0002].

## If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job

**If a worker dies in Saudi Arabia:**

1. **Call the embassy:** +966570212180, or the labour wing free on 8001000125 [E0086, E0024]. It registers the death, follows up with the employer and the authorities, and issues the no-objection certificate (NOC) needed for burial or repatriation [E0033, E0034]. The cause of death is recorded in Saudi Arabia, usually as "natural", and Bangladesh does not check it [E0093]. **Need to know:** how a family can ask for a post-mortem or challenge the recorded cause of death. The employer pays to prepare and fly the body home unless the family agrees to burial there [E0135].
2. **Tell the Wage Earners' Welfare Board** (Probashi Kallyan Bhaban, 71-72 Eskaton Garden, Dhaka) whether the family wants the body brought home or buried in Saudi Arabia; the Board relays this to the embassy and follows up until the body arrives, free [E0145, E0017]. It pays repatriation for workers who left with BMET clearance or joined it through the embassy, and asks the ministry to pay for others [E0091]. Or email the ministry's Welfare-2 branch (aswelfare@probashi.gov.bd) with a description, the cause of death, the death certificate and copies of the worker's passport, work permit or iqama, BMET smart card and Saudi ID number; free, action within 7 working days [E0004]. BRAC, a Bangladeshi development organisation, helps families apply through its Migration Programme (Coming home) [E0111, E0143]. **Need to know:** how and with which papers the family applies to the Board, and how long it takes.
3. **At the airport,** the Board's desk calls the family, hands over the body at cargo with a Tk 35,000 cheque for transport and burial, and its ambulance carries the body from Dhaka, Chattogram or Sylhet airport, free [E0042, E0007, E0008]. **You'll need:** the worker's passport copy, death certificate, NOC, original air waybill, a heirship certificate from the Union Parishad (local council) chairman or ward councillor, the wife's, father's or mother's NID, any BMET clearance card, and a power of attorney if someone else collects [E0007, E0042].
4. **Tk 3 lakh Welfare Board grant,** for workers who left with BMET clearance or joined the Board through the embassy; the Board writes listing the papers [E0037, E0015]. **You'll need:** a family-members certificate from the Union Parishad chairman, mayor or councillor; an undertaking on Tk 400 non-judicial stamp paper (samples on [wewb.gov.bd](https://www.wewb.gov.bd/)); a statement of the receiving account at any scheduled bank showing account and routing numbers; 2 passport-size colour photos of each heir [E0015]. Send them to the Board; free; paid to the account with an SMS within 60 working days of complete papers [E0015], though families often have to chase the Board repeatedly [E0092].
5. **Tk 10 lakh life insurance,** for workers who left with BMET clearance from 12 March 2023, covering 5 years from the BMET card's issue [E0044]. No application if the body comes home; if buried in Saudi Arabia or died after returning, apply at [portal.wewb.gov.bd](https://portal.wewb.gov.bd/) or the Board with the BMET card and passport copy; free, paid within 30 working days [E0013]. Claims recorded as suicide are often rejected. Middlemen offer to speed up payouts for a large cut; the claim is free and needs no middleman [E0097, E0098].
6. **Compensation and unpaid wages from Saudi Arabia.** With the heirs' power of attorney, the embassy pursues them in Saudi courts and sends the money through BMET [E0032]. The Board tells the family when compensation is possible; the family prepares a power of attorney (the Board's legal cell helps) and the same four papers as for the grant; the Board pays heirs within 60 working days of receiving the money, free [E0016]. A relative can also email the ministry (aswelfare@probashi.gov.bd) with proof of migrant status, the employer's name and full address and what is owed; it instructs the embassy and Board within 7 working days, free [E0003].
7. **GOSI work-death compensation.** GOSI insures workers against work injury at the employer's cost; domestic workers only if its board decides so [E0100, E0101, E0160]. For a work-injury death it pays the family 84 times the average monthly wage registered with it, plus repatriation [E0157, E0158, E0159]. Claim at a GOSI office (toll-free 199044), or from Bangladesh through the embassy with a power of attorney (item 6) [E0161, E0162, E0032, E0016]. **Need to know:** whether domestic workers are now covered, which papers GOSI needs from the family, and how long payment takes.

**Injured or seriously ill in Saudi Arabia:** apply to the Welfare Board with BMET clearance or membership and a passport copy; free, the Board gets the embassy to bring the worker home, sometimes into hospital, and the airport desk takes them to hospital by ambulance [E0014, E0040].

**Treatment money after coming home,** up to Tk 2 lakh. **You'll need:** BMET clearance card or membership, passport copy, medical papers and a 6R colour photo. Apply on the Board's portal, at a district welfare centre or at the Board; it checks by video call and pays within 60 working days [E0014].

**Disability insurance:** permanent disability within the 5 years pays 10% to 100% of Tk 10 lakh [E0045, E0012]. **You'll need:** BMET clearance card, passport copy, medical papers and a 6R photo showing the disability; apply free at the Board, paid within 30 working days (Deputy Director Welfare-2, +88 02 48317085, 01321-158354, dd.welfare2@wewb.gov.bd) [E0012].

**Job lost within 6 months and you come home:** the insurance pays Tk 50,000 [E0045, E0011]. **You'll need:** BMET clearance card, passport copy and copies of the departure and arrival stamp pages; apply free at the Board, paid within 30 working days (01712-107301, jahanwewb@gmail.com) [E0011]. Illness from before leaving isn't covered, and those returning for serious illness are often refused [E0097].

**If the Board doesn't deliver,** complain in order to the officer named for the service; the Board's grievance officer (+88 02 55138267, 01684-501891, azizwelfare@gmail.com, 30 working days); the ministry's Joint Secretary (Administration) (+88 0241030261, jsadmin@probashi.gov.bd, 20 working days); then [grs.gov.bd](https://www.grs.gov.bd/), the government complaint system (60 working days) [E0022].

## Saudi Arabia as a destination

**Numbers.** Over 750,000 Bangladeshis went to Saudi Arabia for work in 2025, more than two-thirds of the 1.1 million who went abroad and up 16% on 2024 [E0118, E0119]. Bangladeshis, about 2.59 million, are the largest foreign group, ahead of Indians (2.31 million) and Pakistanis (2.23 million) [E0121]. In December 2024, 89% of Bangladeshi workers going abroad went to Saudi Arabia, as Malaysia had stopped normal recruitment [E0122].

**2026 slowdown.** Early in 2026 the Saudi labour ministry cancelled thousands of visa applications after employers filed fake demand and workers arrived to no job or iqama [E0127]. From 1 January to 17 October 2026, 315,935 Bangladeshis went, 45% fewer than a year earlier [E0129]. The Iran war that began in February 2026 and Houthi attacks disrupted Saudi airports [E0130]. Nine in ten complaints BMET got from migrant workers in 2025 were about Saudi Arabia: no iqama, no work and unpaid wages [E0128].

**Bans.** Saudi Arabia restricted recruitment from Bangladesh from 2008 to 2015-2016 [E0126]. From 13 April 2025, ahead of Hajj, Saudi Arabia stopped issuing business visit, tourist and family visit visas to Bangladeshis and citizens of 13 other countries, after people used them for Hajj [E0123].

**Growth.** Over a third of workers now go with a Takamol certificate, and BMET has started mining training; Vision 2030 (the Saudi plan to diversify beyond oil) construction and projects may create up to 300,000 jobs for Bangladeshis in 2026 [E0120].

**Jobs closing.** Under Saudisation (reserving jobs for Saudis), housing supervisor became Saudi-only in 2026, and firms must give Saudis 60% of sales and marketing and 30% of engineering jobs [E0124, E0125].
