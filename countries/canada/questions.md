# Canada open questions

These are the questions the Canada doc can't yet answer, grouped by the doc's sections. Each is a "Need to know" in `doc.md`.

## Background

- Is the student cap still in force for 2027, and what is the number for 2027?
- Were the asylum laws proposed on 19 June 2026 passed, and what exactly do they change?
- How many study permits, visit visas and work permits did IRCC issue to Bangladeshis in 2024, 2025 and 2026?
- Does BMET (the Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training) clear any Bangladeshi workers for Canada, and how many in 2025?
- What do Bangladeshi brokers charge in 2026 for a Canadian visit visa file with a host's invitation letter, and for an LMIA job offer?
- What do Bangladeshi students pay study-abroad agencies in 2026, and what does a Canadian college diploma cost a year?
- What is the study permit fee, the visit visa fee and the biometrics fee in 2026?
- Can a worker on an LMIA-based work permit change employer, and how long does a new permit take?

## Student visa

- How does a student check that a Canadian college is a designated learning institution (DLI), and where is IRCC's CIP list published?
- Where does a Bangladeshi give biometrics for a study permit (VFS Global Dhaka?), what does the permit cost, and how long does a decision take in 2026?
- What is the refusal rate for Bangladeshi study permit applications in 2025 and 2026?
- What on-campus work may a student do, and does it count towards the 24 hours?

## Visit visa

- Where does a Bangladeshi apply for a Canadian visit visa, what documents and funds are required, what does it cost, and how long does it take in 2026?
- Did the mass-cancellation power pass in Bill C-12, and has it been used against Bangladeshi visa holders?
- Where is a Bangladeshi passenger stopped (at Dhaka check-in or at a transit airport), who tells them, and does the airline refund the ticket?

## Work permit

- Do Bangladeshi recruiting agencies hold any genuine Canadian LMIA job orders, and does BMET issue permission letters for Canada?

## Permanent residence

- Which programs give permanent residence to the 33,000 work permit holders, and who qualifies?

## Irregular routes and undocumented stay

- What did the Federal Court decide in Mahin Shahriar's case, and does Canada take back its refugee claimants who cross into the US?

## Flying to Canada

- What is Biman's checked baggage allowance and excess-baggage rate on Dhaka-Toronto for students in 2026?

## Contacts

- The addresses, phone numbers and emergency helplines of the Bangladesh High Commission in Ottawa and the Consulate General in Toronto.
- Where a Bangladeshi in Canada reports trafficking or labour abuse, and whether the High Commission has a labour wing.
