## Applying for a tourist visa?

Here's what you'll need

**For a Canadian temporary resident visa, you generally need the following:**

- A [valid travel document](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/passport-travel-document.html), like a passport

- Proof of financial means to support your stay in Canada

- Travel itinerary, including hotel bookings and flight reservations

- Proof of ties to your home country, such as employment, family or property ownership

- Any other additional information based on your specific situation

[Please visit IRCC for more information on visitor visas \>\>](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship.html)
