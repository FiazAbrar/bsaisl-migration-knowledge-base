General & Appointments

Do I need an appointment for services? General

Yes. Most services require a prior appointment. Use the appointment link on the relevant page.

See: Visa Overview · E-Passport

What payment methods are accepted? Payment

In-person Credit/Debit Card only. Cash and personal cheques are not accepted. For postal, send bank draft.

See fee notes on each service page; e.g., NVR.

Can I apply by mail and get documents returned? General

Some services allow postal submission. Include a self-addressed prepaid return envelope where permitted.

See: MRP Reissue · Visa Overview


Passport (E-Passport & MRP)

How do I apply for an E-passport? Is biometric enrolment required? Passport

Yes — biometric enrolment in person with a prior appointment is mandatory.

Guide: E-Passport

My MRP is expiring. Can I reissue it? MRP

Yes. Follow the MRP Reissue instructions. Some applicants may submit by post.

Guide: MRP Reissue Application

I need a new Bangladesh passport — steps? Passport

Attend in person for required procedures. Review the page and bring required documents.

Guide: New Passport Application


Visa & Visa on Arrival (VOA)

How do I apply for a visa? Processing time? Visa

Apply with a prior appointment. Standard processing is typically about 5 working days (most categories) and ~7 days for diplomatic/official, subject to completeness and approval.

See: Visa Overview · Tourist Visa · Diplomatic/Official Visa

Which visa type should I choose? Visa
  • Tourist
  • Business
  • Research
  • Diplomatic/Official
Am I eligible for Visa on Arrival (VOA)? VOA

VOA may be granted for eligible purposes like business, investment, or official duty. Bring supporting documents and return/onward tickets.

See: VOA


No Visa Required (NVR)

Who is eligible for NVR and what documents are needed? NVR

NVR is for Bangladesh-origin foreign nationals and qualifying family members. See the page for current requirements.

Payment: Credit/Debit Card only (no cash/personal cheques).

Guide: NVR


Birth Registration & NID

How do I apply for a Birth Registration Certificate? Birth

Follow the instructions on the Birth Registration page; pay by Credit/Debit Card at the Mission.

Guide: Birth Registration

What do I need for NID enrolment? NID

You need a 17-digit Bangladeshi Birth Certificate with data matching your passport. Physical appearance is mandatory.

Guide: NID


Police Clearance

How do Bangladeshi nationals apply from abroad? Police

Complete the prescribed form and documents. Certificates are issued by the relevant Police Station in Bangladesh.

Guides: Overview · BD Nationals

Can foreign nationals obtain Clearance via the Mission? Police

Yes — submit the required application and documents. Release follows issuance by Bangladesh Police.

Guide: Foreign Nationals


Attestation & Power of Attorney

How do I attest a Power of Attorney (POA)? Fees? POA

Follow the POA attestation instructions and fee schedule. Fees may vary by page count.

Guide: Power of Attorney

Which other documents can be attested? Attestation

Educational certificates, birth/marriage certificates, driving licences, and more (per rules).

See: Attestation · Educational Documents

How do I obtain an “Alive Certificate”? Consular

Submit the filled form with required ID/status documents as listed on the page.

Guide: Alive Certificate


Address & Contacts

How do I reach the correct wing? Contact

Address: Suite 1100, 350 Sparks Street, Ottawa, ON K1R 7S8

  • Passport/Visa/NVR: consular.ottawa@mofa.gov.bd
  • Power of Attorney: poa.ottawa@gmail.com
  • Birth Registration / NID: brc.ottawa@gmail.com
