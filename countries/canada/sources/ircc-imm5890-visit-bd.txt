Singapore Visa Office
Additional Document Checklist
Temporary Resident Visa
This additional document checklist is one of the forms that you need to submit with your application.
If your documents are in a language other than English or French, you must include a certified
translation.
Failure to submit all required documentation may result in the refusal of your application or processing
delays.
False statements or submission of fraudulent documents will result in immediate refusal and possible
ban from Canada for 5 years.
The submission of an application with the documents listed below does not guarantee the issuance of a
visa. The visa officer may also request additional documents before a decision is taken.
Medical examinations: you may be required to undergo an immigration medical examination in order to
come to Canada. Instructions on completing the examination will be provided to you by the visa
office. You are not required to complete the examination before you submit your application forms.
Gather your documents in order of the checklist and check each item.

You must submit:
☐ Birth/Marriage certificates:
       You must provide a copy of birth certificate or baptismal certificate ; and
       A copy of marriage certificate where applicable.

☐ Itinerary:
       You must provide details of your itinerary in Canada such as provisional travel arrangements
        (airline and hotel booking).

☐ Proof of funds:
       You must provide proof of financial support to cover the expenses for the duration of your visit:
            o Copies of bank statements or bank book covering the past four months.
            o Any additional relevant documentation (employment letter, pay slips, proof of pension,
              business registration, investments, etc.).
       If you are not paying for your own trip:
            o Provide a letter signed by the person paying and a photocopy of their passport, or other
              official identification with photo and a bank certificate proving that they have sufficient
              funds.
            o Describe how this person is related to you.
            o These documents must be dated within three months of the date on which the application is
              submitted.

☐ Family Information Form:
       If you are a resident of Bangladesh, you must provide the Family Information Form (IMM 5707)
        fully completed, dated and signed.

If applicable, you must also submit:
☐ Visiting Friends or Family:
       If you are visiting a friend or family, you must provide:
            o A letter of invitation and supporting documents from the inviter in Canada stating the
              purpose and duration of visit;
            o Proof of relation to your inviter in Canada (birth certificate, marriage certificate, proof of
              correspondence etc.);
            o Copy of inviter’s citizenship or immigration status document (for example: Permanent
              Resident Card – please photocopy both sides, Study Permit, Work Permit, etc.).

☐ Employment Information:
       You must provide proof of employment information:
            o If you are employed, a signed original letter on company letterhead from the employer
              granting leave of absence and including:
                          the applicant’s name, position, current salary and date of hire;
                          the employer’s name and address as well as the telephone and fax number;
            o If you are self-employed, evidence of your business (business registration indicating your
              shares in the business, business license and financial documents, for example: profit and
              loss statement, balance sheet, etc.).

☐ Domestic Helper/Travelling with Employer:
       If you are a domestic helper or worker travelling with your employer, you must provide:
            o A copy of your work permit;
            o A copy of employer’s identity card or passport;
            o A proof of employer’s employment or business;
            o A letter from your employer specifying that s/he will be responsible for all expenses related
              to the trip and will ensure your return to your place of employment ;
            o A letter from your employer providing an explanation for your visit (for example: ages of
              employer’s children, elderly or disabled care, etc.; attach medical letter as applicable);
            o Details of your itinerary showing your name and the name of your employer and their family
              members travelling together.

☐ Business trip:
       If you are travelling on business, you must provide two letters:
            o A signed original letter on company letterhead from the employer indicating that the
              proposed travel to Canada has been approved. This letter must include:
                          The purpose of travel;
                          Applicant’s name, position, salary and date of hire; and
                          A statement specifying who will be responsible for all expenses related to the trip.
            o A letter of invitation from the party in Canada.
                          The invitation letter must come from the party with whom the applicant will conduct
                           direct business.
                          This excludes third parties who are only arranging or facilitating the business
                           meetings.

☐ Conference:
       If you are attending a conference, you must provide two letters:
            o A signed original letter on company letterhead from the employer indicating that the
              proposed travel to Canada has been approved. This letter must include:
                          The purpose of travel;
                          Applicant’s name, position, salary and date of hire; and
                          A statement specifying who will be responsible for all expenses related to the trip.
            o A letter of invitation from the party in Canada.
                          The invitation letter must come from the party inviting the applicant to the
                           conference.

☐ Students:
       If you are a student, you must provide an original letter from your school confirming that the school
        has approved your absence and indicating when you are required to return.

☐ Short term student:
       If travelling to Canada as a short term, one term and summer school student, you must provide a
        letter of Acceptance from the Admissions or Registrar’s Office of a Canadian school showing the
        exact amount of tuition fees the applicant is required to pay, the anticipated start and end dates,
        and the latest date by which the applicant may register.

☐ Returning student:
       If you are a returning student with valid study permit, you must provide:
            o A copy of the transcript of record for the program in which you are enrolled;
            o Copy of your valid Canadian study permit;
            o A letter from the current school in Canada confirming your future enrolment.

☐ Returning worker:
       If you are a returning worker holding a valid work permit, you must provide:
            o A signed letter from your employer in Canada confirming ongoing employment;
                          This letter must include the employer’s name and address as well as telephone and
                           email address;
                          Your position, salary and date of hire.
            o A copy of valid Canadian Work Permit;
            o A notice of assessment from the Canada Revenue Agency, Statement of Remuneration
              Paid, and pay slips for most recent year of employment in Canada.

☐ Transit visa:
       If you are applying for a transit visa, you must provide:
            o A copy of your air ticket and travel itinerary; indicate the number of times you will be
              entering Canada and the dates and times for each entry and exit;
            o A valid visa for country of final destination;
            o A letter of invitation from the inviter in the country of destination or a letter of intent stating
              the purpose of your trip.

☐ Diplomatic and official passport:
       If you are a diplomatic or official passport holders, you must provide:
            o A note verbale from your Federal or State office stating the purpose of your travel to Canada;
            o An invitation from your host in Canada; and
            o Your original passport.
