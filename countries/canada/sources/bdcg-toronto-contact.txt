Our Contacts

Before contacting us, please read carefully about the service you are looking for and the frequently asked questions (FAQs) at our website to gather required information about consular services and find answers to your questions: 

 If you need further information, you are encouraged to email your queries to the following email address: 

consular@bdcgtoronto.ca

Our Call Center Numbers (10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, Toronto Time, Working days only)

+1 4378735019 (Whatsapp) (messages and voice calls)

For official purposes other than consular services and in case of emergency:

consular@bdcgtoronto.ca 

Please drop a message regarding your query. An agent will reply to your messages as soon as possible. If necessary, the agent will call you. 

Our Location: 

1505-2235 Sheppard Avenue East, Attria II, Toronto, ON, M2J 5B5, Canada 
Fax: +1 416 492 3171
