What we do
We work to further strengthen Bangladesh–Canada cooperation across political dialogue, trade and investment, cultural and educational exchanges, and community engagement.
🕊️ Representation
Promote Bangladesh’s interests and support cooperation in bilateral and multilateral forums.
📈 Trade & Investment
Connect Bangladeshi and Canadian businesses, investors, and partners to expand opportunities.
🎓 Culture & Education
Strengthen mutual understanding through cultural diplomacy and educational exchanges.
🛂 Consular Support
Provide consular services and assistance to Bangladeshi citizens and diaspora communities.
