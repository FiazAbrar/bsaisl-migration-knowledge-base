# Refugee protection (asylum) in Canada

You can ask Canada for **refugee protection** if it’s unsafe for you to return to your home country and you’re in Canada (including airports and border crossings). Asking for refugee protection is also called **claiming asylum**.

Claiming asylum **isn’t** a shortcut to help you immigrate to Canada or stay here longer.

If Canada decides you don’t need protection, you need to leave the country.

If you lie on your claim, you could be banned from returning.

**If you return to your home country** you risk:

- having your claim rejected (if your claim is **still** being processed)
- losing your protected person status (if your claim is accepted)

[Find out more about claiming asylum in Canada](/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/refugees/asylum.html)
