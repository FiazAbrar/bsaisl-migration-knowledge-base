## Resolved conflicts

- **Number of accused in the CBSA case:** the article says five people were charged but names four, adding that one more accomplice was found in the investigation. Read as four named plus one unnamed; the doc names the four and says investigators found one more.
- **Prosecutor's mention of "Sohel and Mahfuz":** the Saskatchewan article once names the accused as "Sohel and Mahfuz", against "Sohel Haider and Mohammad Masum" everywhere else; treated as a misprint and not used.
- **Boarding denials:** "more than 2,000 Indian and Bangladeshi nationals" stopped since March 2024 (Global Affairs document) and "493 Bangladeshis" recommended not to board in 2024 (CBSA) are different measures and periods; both kept with their sources.
- **84% figures:** an 84% drop in illegal crossings to the US (June to November 2024, Global Affairs) and an 84% drop in asylum claims by temporary visa holders (April 2024 to April 2026, IRCC) are separate statistics; both kept with their periods.
- **Student cap numbers:** 360,000 a year for 2024-2025 (January 2024) and a 2026 target of about 155,000 (November 2025 levels plan) are different years, not a conflict.
- **Safe Third Country Agreement "14 days":** stated by the article as a rule; the doc attributes it to the lawyer's argument, since the article frames it as his belief about what should apply.
