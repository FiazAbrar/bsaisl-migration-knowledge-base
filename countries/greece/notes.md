# Greece: research notes

## What this version is built from

16 Prothom Alo articles (January 2024 to November 2026) from the archive chunk xchunk-greece.jsonl, read in full; 15 used, 88 facts logged (XG0001 to XG0088), all cited [N] as news. One article (6e4e8399, 28 March 2026) is a three-sentence repeat of 675621ce and was not used. No official source has been fetched and no network research was done.

The reporting covers the February 2022 Bangladesh-Greece MoU (4,000 seasonal visas a year; regularisation of 15,000 irregular Bangladeshis for five years), the regularisation's first phase, the April 2024 foreign ministers' meeting, BBC findings on coast guard pushbacks, the September 2025 law and asylum suspension, arrivals on Crete, and two Libya-to-Greece boat disasters (March 2026, Sunamganj; October 2026, Habiganj). It says nothing on how the seasonal visa actually works, wages, BMET clearance to Greece, or embassy contacts; those are "Need to know" items in the doc.

The overland Gulf-Iran-Turkey-Greece route and the Libya leg (detention, ransom) are covered in the global Illegal routes doc and linked, not repeated.

## Where the official research pass should start

- Greek Ministry of Migration and Asylum (migration.gov.gr): the 2022 Greece-Bangladesh MoU text, seasonal worker (metaklisi) procedure, the regularisation platform and its cut-off date, monthly arrival statistics, the July 2025 suspension of asylum for North Africa arrivals, and the September 2025 law.
- Greek Ministry of Foreign Affairs: which embassy issues visas to Bangladeshis (Dhaka or New Delhi), and whether an embassy in Dhaka opened after April 2024.
- Greek Ministry of Labour: minimum wage and agricultural worker rules; Manolada.
- Bangladesh Embassy in Athens: address, helpline, labour wing, travel permits, registration for regularisation.
- BMET: clearance figures for Greece by year; whether any worker has been cleared under the MoU.
- Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare and Overseas Employment: MoU status and the "second part".
- UNHCR Greece and Hellenic Coast Guard: Crete arrivals, reception centres (Heraklion, Rethymno), asylum figures for Bangladeshis.
- IOM Greece: assisted voluntary return for Bangladeshis.

## Resolved conflicts

- **Death toll of the March 2026 boat:** Prothom Alo's 29 March district report said "18 dead, 10 from Sunamganj" in its lead; the Greek coast guard, citing survivors via AFP on 28 March, said at least 22 died, and the later district reports (29 March onward) use 22. The coast guard figure (official, from survivors) is used. The global Illegal routes doc carries an open question about 18 of 38 vs 20 of 22 of 48; this set supports at least 22 dead and 26 rescued.
- **Sunamganj dead:** 4 (28 March, Derai only), 10 (29 March), 12 confirmed by police (29 March: 6 Derai, 5 Jagannathpur, 1 Dowarabazar), then Mohibur Rahman of Chhatak reported by his family on 1 April as an additional death on the same boat. A progression, not a conflict: the doc says 12 confirmed by police plus Mohibur, "at least 13".
- **Shayek Ahmad's payment:** Tk 12 lakh in two instalments (24e5fc1c) and Tk 4.5 lakh plus Tk 7.5 lakh (97ca2aaa). Consistent.
- **Mohibur's payment:** Tk 13 lakh in all including Tk 5 lakh from a moneylender (cd7fd233); the 2 April article says Tk 13 lakh "brought at moneylender interest". The fuller first article is used.
- **Regularised count:** 10,833 registered with the embassy by January 2024; "more than 10,000" given legal residence (April 2024). Different measures; both kept.
