# Greece: research notes

## What this version is built from

16 Prothom Alo articles (January 2024 to November 2026) from the archive chunk xchunk-greece.jsonl, read in full; 15 used, 88 facts logged (XG0001 to XG0088), all cited [N] as news. One article (6e4e8399, 28 March 2026) is a three-sentence repeat of 675621ce and was not used. The official research pass of 24 November 2026 (below) added official sources and facts OG0001 to OG0096.

The reporting covers the February 2022 Bangladesh-Greece MoU (4,000 seasonal visas a year; regularisation of 15,000 irregular Bangladeshis for five years), the regularisation's first phase, the April 2024 foreign ministers' meeting, BBC findings on coast guard pushbacks, the September 2025 law and asylum suspension, arrivals on Crete, and two Libya-to-Greece boat disasters (March 2026, Sunamganj; October 2026, Habiganj). It says nothing on how the seasonal visa actually works, wages, BMET clearance to Greece, or embassy contacts; those are "Need to know" items in the doc.

The overland Gulf-Iran-Turkey-Greece route and the Libya leg (detention, ransom) are covered in the global Illegal routes doc and linked, not repeated.

## Resolved conflicts

- **Death toll of the March 2026 boat:** Prothom Alo's 29 March district report said "18 dead, 10 from Sunamganj" in its lead; the Greek coast guard, citing survivors via AFP on 28 March, said at least 22 died, and the later district reports (29 March onward) use 22. The coast guard figure (official, from survivors) is used. The global Illegal routes doc carries an open question about 18 of 38 vs 20 of 22 of 48; this set supports at least 22 dead and 26 rescued.
- **Sunamganj dead:** 4 (28 March, Derai only), 10 (29 March), 12 confirmed by police (29 March: 6 Derai, 5 Jagannathpur, 1 Dowarabazar), then Mohibur Rahman of Chhatak reported by his family on 1 April as an additional death on the same boat. A progression, not a conflict: the doc says 12 confirmed by police plus Mohibur, "at least 13".
- **Shayek Ahmad's payment:** Tk 12 lakh in two instalments (24e5fc1c) and Tk 4.5 lakh plus Tk 7.5 lakh (97ca2aaa). Consistent.
- **Mohibur's payment:** Tk 13 lakh in all including Tk 5 lakh from a moneylender (cd7fd233); the 2 April article says Tk 13 lakh "brought at moneylender interest". The fuller first article is used.
- **Regularised count:** 10,833 registered with the embassy by January 2024; "more than 10,000" given legal residence (April 2024). Different measures; both kept.

## Official research pass, 2026-11-24

### What was searched

- EU: Eurofound PolicyWatch factsheet on the agreement; EUAA Digital Information Platform entries on the September 2025 return law and the July 2025 asylum suspension; a Border Violence Monitoring Network briefing hosted by EUAA (third party); AIDA Greece statistics (third party, compiling ministry data).
- Bangladesh side: Bangladesh Embassy Athens site (office and consular hours, consular fees, bilateral relations PDF, scanned consular schedule and dead-body repatriation notice, read from page images).
- Web searches on MoU implementation 2024 to 2026, BMET clearance for Greece, the asylum suspension's end, safe-country lists, Greek minimum wage.

### What was found
- The MoU text (English original in the gazette, scanned; OCR text copy in sources/law-4959-2022-greece-bangladesh-mou.txt): Article 4 gives 15,000 five-year permits for Bangladeshis already in Greece and 4,000 five-year permits a year for new workers; Article 6 requirements (passport valid two years, contract at least one year, sickness insurance); Article 8 nine months in any 12, may change employer, must leave after five years; Article 10 no family reunification or long-term residence; Article 16 entry into force on the last diplomatic notification (date not found).
- The visa channel: Greece has no embassy in Dhaka; the New Delhi embassy decides; GVCW in Banani, Dhaka (since July 2023 per news) takes all visa types; employment visa fee EUR 75 (seasonal) or EUR 180 (dependent), plus EUR 30 service, Tk 10,200 courier, EUR 30 contract attestation; medical at Dente, New Delhi.
- The employer side (MITOS, updated 19 Nov 2026): online application with TAXISnet, EUR 100 per worker, 10 days, approval emailed to the consulate; appeal within two months for EUR 50.
- September 2025 law (EUAA summary of the ministry release): illegal stay 2 to 5 years in prison and at least EUR 5,000 fine; 7-year regularisation route abolished; detention up to 24 months; 14-day voluntary departure; entry bans up to 10 years.
- Asylum suspension: Article 79 of Law 5218/2025, 11 July 2025, three months; BVMN says in force July to October 2025. No source found on renewal.
- AIDA: 3,132 asylum applications by Bangladeshis in 2025; the ministry tables it compiles publish no recognition figures for Bangladesh.
- Not found: any count of Bangladeshis who have actually come on MoU work visas; whether BMET clears workers for Greece and how many; the MoU's entry-into-force date; any safe-country-of-origin list for Greece naming Bangladesh; IOM Greece's voluntary return terms (greece.iom.int returned 403); the embassy's labour wing.

### Resolved conflicts
- **What the MoU gives people already in Greece:** Prothom Alo (XG0005) called it "temporary legal status for five years". The MoU (Article 4) and the ministry's Q&A say five-year permits on the seasonal regime: nine months a year in Greece, three months out, departure after five years. The official wording is used; XG0005 is no longer cited.
- **Penalties under the September 2025 law:** Prothom Alo (XG0022) said those not entitled to asylum must be returned or "detained for at least 24 months and fined up to EUR 10,000". The EUAA summary of the ministry's release on the passed law says illegal stay carries 2 to 5 years in prison and a fine of at least EUR 5,000, with administrative detention of up to 24 months. The EUR 10,000 figure and "minimum 3 years" appear in the EUAA entry on the draft bill (not kept), so the news figure likely reflects the draft. The official summary of the passed law is used; XG0022 and XG0020 are no longer cited.
- **Greek embassy in Dhaka:** XG0014 (April 2024, an embassy in Dhaka "on the priority list") is superseded by the Bangladesh embassy's August 2025 statement that Greece has yet to open one; XG0014 is no longer cited.
- **Arrival figures:** XG0077 (ANA, about 61,000 then 42,000 a year) replaced in the Size list by the minister's own December 2025 figures (OG0082); the two cover different periods and are not compared.
- **Passport validity:** the MoU asks for two years' validity after applying; GVCW's D visa page asks for 1.5 years and the employment checklist three months beyond the visa. Not resolved: kept as a "Need to know" in Step 3.
- **Ambassador:** XG0011 named Asud Ahmad as ambassador (January 2024); the embassy says Nahida Rahman Shumona has served since September 2024. The current name is used.
