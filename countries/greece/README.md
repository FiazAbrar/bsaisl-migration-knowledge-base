## 1 Background

<a id="in-one-minute"></a>

### 1.1 In one minute

Greece has two doors for Bangladeshis. The legal door is the 2022 MoU: up to 4,000 five-year work permits a year for Bangladeshis with a job contract in Greek farming or other sectors, plus 15,000 five-year permits for Bangladeshis who were already living in Greece when it was signed <sup>[1 (2022)](#o1)</sup>. Both permits let the holder work nine months in any twelve and give no right to bring family; at the end of the five years the holder must leave <sup>[1 (2022)](#o1)</sup>. The permits for people already in Greece were handed out: more than 10,000 got legal residence, Bangladesh's foreign minister said in April 2024, and many went home to visit as soon as they had the card, the Bangladesh ambassador at the time said <sup>[N4 (2024)](#n4), [N5 (2024)](#n5)</sup>. For new workers, the job comes first: a Greek employer applies online for approval, and only then does the worker apply for the visa at the GVCW centre in Banani, Dhaka; the file is decided by the Greek Embassy in New Delhi <sup>[5](#o5), [4](#o4), [6](#o6), [7](#o7)</sup>. The other door is a rubber boat from Libya to Crete, sold to young men from Sunamganj and Habiganj for Tk 12 to 15 lakh, part of it paid only once the young man is in Libya <sup>[N6](#n6), [N7](#n7), [N3](#n3), [N8](#n8)</sup>. Brokers call the crossing the "game" <sup>[N9](#n9)</sup>. In March 2026 a boat lost its way, drifted six days, and at least 22 people died for lack of food and water, at least 13 of them from Sunamganj; the bodies were thrown into the sea <sup>[N10](#n10), [N7](#n7), [N11](#n11), [N9](#n9)</sup>. Those who reach Crete are registered and held in camps <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>. Since September 2025, staying in Greece without the right to stay is a crime punishable by 2 to 5 years in prison, and a person can be held in detention for up to 24 months before removal <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>.

<a id="is-it-open-now-and-who-can-go"></a>

### 1.2 Is it open now, and who can go?

The legal work route exists: the MoU runs for an initial five years and can be extended, and the work visa can be applied for in Dhaka <sup>[1 (2022)](#o1), [2](#o2)</sup>. The boat from Libya to Crete, now the main entry point for asylum seekers into Greece, was still carrying Bangladeshis at the end of October 2026 <sup>[N6](#n6), [N3](#n3)</sup>.

<a id="status"></a>

#### 1.2.1 Status

- **9 February 2022:** the MoU was signed in Dhaka <sup>[1 (2022)](#o1)</sup>.
- **22 July 2022:** the Greek parliament ratified the MoU as Law 4959/2022 <sup>[9](#o9)</sup>.
- **12 January 2023:** Greece opened an online platform for Bangladeshis already in Greece to apply for the five-year permit, and began issuing permits in phases (see [Regularisation of Bangladeshis already in Greece](#regularisation-of-bangladeshis-already-in-greece)) <sup>[N5 (2024)](#n5)</sup>.
- **1 July 2023:** set as the start date for seasonal-work permits under the MoU <sup>[9](#o9)</sup>.
- **16 April 2024:** the Greek foreign minister, George Gerapetritis, told Bangladesh's foreign minister in Athens that Greece will recruit many Bangladeshi workers in agriculture, tourism, hospitality and construction, according to a Bangladesh foreign ministry press release; Bangladesh asked Greece to implement the MoU's second part <sup>[N4 (2024)](#n4)</sup>.
- **17 March 2025:** Bangladeshis may lodge any Greek visa either at the GVCW centre in Dhaka or at any GVCW centre in India <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>.
- **July to October 2025:** Greece suspended asylum applications from people arriving by sea from North Africa for three months (see [Asylum](#asylum)) <sup>[11](#o11), [N12](#n12)</sup>.
- **3 September 2025:** Greece's parliament passed a law making illegal stay a crime (see [Living without papers and deportation](#living-without-papers-and-deportation)) <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>.
- **August 2025:** Greece still had no embassy in Dhaka; its Embassy in New Delhi covers Bangladesh <sup>[12](#o12)</sup>.
- **June 2026:** the new EU migration and asylum pact was due to take effect <sup>[N13](#n13)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - How many Bangladeshis have received a Greek seasonal or dependent-employment D visa through the Dhaka centre each year since 2023?

<a id="the-channels"></a>

#### 1.2.2 The channels

- **Work visa under the MoU:** up to 4,000 five-year permits a year, for a named job with a Greek employer (see [Work visa: seasonal worker under the 2022 MoU](#work-visa-seasonal-worker-under-the-2022-mou)) <sup>[1 (2022)](#o1)</sup>.
- **Regularisation:** for Bangladeshis who were in Greece without papers before 9 February 2022 (see [Regularisation of Bangladeshis already in Greece](#regularisation-of-bangladeshis-already-in-greece)) <sup>[9](#o9)</sup>.
- **Other visas:** study and short visits (see [Other visas](#other-visas)) <sup>[4](#o4), [7](#o7)</sup>.
- **Not a legal route:** the boat from Libya to Crete (see [Irregular entry: Libya to Crete by boat](#irregular-entry-libya-to-crete-by-boat)) <sup>[N6](#n6)</sup>.
- **Not a legal route:** overland from the Gulf through Iran and Turkey (see [Irregular entry: overland through Turkey](#irregular-entry-overland-through-turkey)).

<a id="size"></a>

#### 1.2.3 Size

- **Bangladeshis in Greece:** about 45,000 in 2023, of whom 15,000 had valid residence permits <sup>[9](#o9)</sup>.
- **Regularised:** 10,337 Bangladeshis applied in 2023; 10,833 had registered with the Bangladesh embassy in Athens in the first phase by January 2024, and Bangladesh's foreign minister said in April 2024 that more than 10,000 had been given legal residence <sup>[9](#o9), [N5 (2024)](#n5), [N4 (2024)](#n4)</sup>.
- **Arrivals by sea of all nationalities:** 13,857 in the four months before 1 December 2025, against 27,324 in the same months a year earlier, when they came from Afghanistan, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sudan, Egypt and Syria, the Greek migration minister said <sup>[13](#o13)</sup>.
- **Asylum claims:** Bangladeshis made 3,132 asylum applications in Greece in 2025 <sup>[N14](#n14)</sup>.
- **Bangladeshis on the boats:** of two boats of 56 people each that reached Crete in early August 2026, one carried 25 Bangladeshis and the other 47 <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - How many Bangladeshis did BMET (Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training) clear for Greece each year from 2022 to 2026?

<a id="how-a-job-reaches-you-and-who-takes-a-cut"></a>

### 1.3 How a job reaches you, and who takes a cut

On the legal route the root is a Greek employer's approval: the employer applies online to the Ministry of Migration and Asylum, pays EUR 100 per worker, and attaches a copy of the named worker's passport <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>. On the boat route the reporting names brokers living in Libya, one broker's father collecting the money at home, brokers based in Dhaka, and payments split between home and Libya <sup>[N11](#n11), [N9](#n9), [N3](#n3), [N7](#n7), [N8](#n8)</sup>.

- **The broker in Libya.** Akhlus Mia, father of Shayek Ahmad, said he paid broker Azizul Islam of Ichgaon village Tk 12 lakh to send his son, and that Azizul lives in Libya and sends local young men from Libya to Greece <sup>[N11](#n11)</sup>. Mohibur Rahman of Chhatak upazila went through Nabi Hossain of Pagla, Shantiganj upazila, who is also in Libya <sup>[N9](#n9)</sup>.
- **The collector at home.** Nabi Hossain's father, Abdul Mannan, handled all the money in Bangladesh; he met Mohibur through masonry work and encouraged him to go to Greece, a cousin said <sup>[N9](#n9)</sup>.
- **Brokers in Dhaka.** The families of six missing Habiganj men said they had been trying to reach brokers based in Dhaka <sup>[N3](#n3)</sup>.
- **Brokers spread across three countries.** Of the 9 brokers named in two cases filed in Sunamganj in March 2026, some were in Libya, Greece and Italy <sup>[N9](#n9)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - How does a Greek farm employer get the name and passport copy of a Bangladeshi worker in Bangladesh: through relatives already in Greece, BOESL (Bangladesh Overseas Employment and Services Limited, the government's recruiting company), or private agencies, and what do they charge?

<a id="what-you-pay-and-what-you-earn-and-keep"></a>

### 1.4 What you pay, and what you earn and keep

The legal visa costs the worker EUR 135 in fees for a seasonal work visa (EUR 240 for dependent employment) plus Tk 10,200 for the courier to New Delhi (see [Step 4: Lodge the application at the GVCW centre in Dhaka](#step-4-lodge-the-application-at-the-gvcw-centre-in-dhaka)) <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>. The boat route costs Tk 12 to 15 lakh per person, raised by selling land and cattle and borrowing at high interest <sup>[N3](#n3), [N8](#n8), [N9](#n9)</sup>.

<a id="what-people-really-pay-for-the-boat"></a>

#### 1.4.1 What people really pay for the boat

- **Derai, Sunamganj:** Tk 12 lakh each, with half paid after reaching Libya, the brother of one man who died said <sup>[N7](#n7)</sup>.
- **Jagannathpur, Sunamganj:** Tk 12 lakh in two instalments: Tk 4.5 lakh before leaving, then Tk 7.5 lakh demanded from Libya before the boat <sup>[N11](#n11), [N8](#n8)</sup>.
- **Chhatak, Sunamganj:** Tk 13 lakh, of which Tk 5 lakh was borrowed from a moneylender at interest <sup>[N9](#n9)</sup>.
- **Madhabpur and Lakhai, Habiganj:** Tk 12 to 15 lakh each, raised by selling land or borrowing at high interest <sup>[N3](#n3)</sup>.

How brokers stage payments to keep families paying is explained under "The staged-payment trap, and no receipts" in the Dalal scams doc, and the moneylender loans under "Loan sharks and land mortgages" in the same doc.

<a id="the-work-and-the-pay"></a>

#### 1.4.2 The work and the pay

The Bangladeshi diaspora in Greece works mostly in agriculture and hospitality <sup>[12](#o12)</sup>. A significant number live in western Manolada and work on farms: strawberries, olives, oranges and other crops <sup>[N5 (2024)](#n5)</sup>. From 1 April 2026 Greece's minimum wage for full-time work is EUR 920 a month for salaried employees and EUR 41.09 a day for manual workers, and a seasonal worker's contract may never pay less than an unskilled worker's pay <sup>[14](#o14), [5](#o5)</sup>. The embassy said regularised workers now send money home through legal channels <sup>[N5 (2024)](#n5)</sup>.

> **Need to know**

<a id="what-ties-you-to-the-employer"></a>

### 1.5 What ties you to the employer

- **To enter:** tied; the visa needs an approval for a specific employer and a contract signed by that employer <sup>[15](#o15)</sup>.
- **To renew or come back each year:** the permit covers five years, but to come back each season the worker must show an active seasonal contract before leaving for Greece <sup>[16 (2023)](#o16), [17 (2022)](#o17)</sup>.
- **To change employer:** allowed within the five years under a new contract with another employer <sup>[1 (2022)](#o1)</sup>.
- **To leave:** the worker must spend three months of every year outside Greece and must leave for good when the five years end <sup>[17 (2022)](#o17), [1 (2022)](#o1)</sup>.
- **Which law:** workers have the same employment and social security rights as Greek law gives others <sup>[1 (2022)](#o1)</sup>.
- **Without a permit:** illegal stay is a crime punishable by 2 to 5 years in prison and a fine of at least EUR 5,000 (see [Living without papers and deportation](#living-without-papers-and-deportation)) <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - How long may a seasonal worker whose contract ends early stay in Greece to find a new employer, and how is the new contract registered on the permit?

<a id="what-goes-wrong-most-and-what-people-wrongly-believe"></a>

### 1.6 What goes wrong most, and what people wrongly believe

<a id="what-people-believe-and-the-facts"></a>

#### 1.6.1 What people believe, and the facts

- **"The broker will put you on a proper boat."** Mohibur Rahman's father said the broker promised a wooden boat and put his son on a plastic one <sup>[N9](#n9)</sup>. The March 2026 boat was rubber, and the 31 October 2026 one an inflatable rubber boat <sup>[N10](#n10), [N3](#n3)</sup>.
- **"There will be food for the trip."** These boats leave with food for 24 hours, and on the March 2026 boat the food and water ran out after one day, Shayek Ahmad's uncle said <sup>[N8](#n8)</sup>.
- **"The broker will tell the family the truth."** Broker Nabi Hossain told Mohibur's family he had reached Greece and was in a camp where he could not talk, while his body was already in the sea (see [If a relative dies or goes missing on the crossing](#if-a-relative-dies-or-goes-missing-on-the-crossing)) <sup>[N15](#n15)</sup>.
- **"Once in Greece, you can claim asylum and stay."** From July to October 2025 people arriving by sea from North Africa could not lodge asylum claims, except vulnerable people and, from mid-September, Sudanese and Eritreans; everyone who enters is now told at once that if the claim is not justified, return follows <sup>[11](#o11), [N12](#n12), [13](#o13)</sup>.
- **"After seven years in Greece you can get papers."** The September 2025 law abolished that route <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>.
- **"A Greek work permit lets you bring your family."** The MoU permit gives no right to family reunification <sup>[1 (2022)](#o1)</sup>.

<a id="irregular-routes-and-where-an-irregular-stay-ends"></a>

#### 1.6.2 Irregular routes, and where an irregular stay ends

The boat crossing from Libya ends in death at sea for some (see [Deaths on the crossing](#deaths-on-the-crossing)), in a camp on Crete for those who arrive (see [Arrival on Crete](#arrival-on-crete)), and for those not entitled to asylum, in detention and return or prison (see [Living without papers and deportation](#living-without-papers-and-deportation)) <sup>[N10](#n10), [N1](#n1), [8](#o8)</sup>. Before the regularisation, life without papers meant hiding from police raids in Athens and the risk of being sent home <sup>[N5 (2024)](#n5)</sup>.

<a id="work-visa-seasonal-worker-under-the-2022-mou"></a>

## 2 Work visa: seasonal worker under the 2022 MoU

Under the MoU, Greece grants up to 4,000 Bangladeshis a year a five-year permit for temporary work in agriculture and other sectors; each year Greece may tell Bangladesh which sectors besides agriculture are included <sup>[1 (2022)](#o1)</sup>. The holder works nine months in any 12-month period, may change employer under a new contract, cannot bring family and must leave at the end of the five years <sup>[1 (2022)](#o1)</sup>. The visa goes into the passport only once; the residence permit then covers the whole five years <sup>[16 (2023)](#o16)</sup>. Seasonal permits are given only where an employer or farmer has signed a contract <sup>[17 (2022)](#o17)</sup>.

<a id="step-1-the-greek-employer-gets-approval"></a>

### 2.1 Step 1: The Greek employer gets approval

1. The employer applies online through the Ministry of Migration and Asylum's e-services, logging in with TAXISnet (Greek tax system) codes <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>.
2. The employer uploads a contract with the worker that states the type and place of work, start date, duration, weekly or monthly hours and pay (never below an unskilled worker's pay), signed with the employer's signature authenticated by a public office <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>.
3. The employer declares the housing it gives the worker, or proves the worker has housing that meets the legal standards; if the worker pays rent, the employer gives a lease <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>.
4. The employer declares it will pay the worker's return costs where the law requires it, and names the Greek consular authority where the worker will get the visa (for Bangladesh, the Embassy in New Delhi) <sup>[5](#o5), [3](#o3)</sup>.
5. The employer attaches a copy of the worker's passport pages with his details and photo, and pays EUR 100 per worker <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>.
6. The regional Directorate for Aliens and Migration has 10 days to decide, and emails the approval and the employer-signed contract to the Greek consulate <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>.

If the employer's application is refused, an administrative appeal can be lodged within two months with the office that decided, for EUR 50; it decides within 30 days <sup>[5](#o5)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Can the worker in Bangladesh get a copy or reference number of the employer's approval to check it is real before paying anyone?

<a id="step-2-check-the-approval-has-reached-the-embassy"></a>

### 2.2 Step 2: Check the approval has reached the Embassy

1. Before applying, confirm that the approval has been issued in Greece and officially received by the Greek Embassy in New Delhi <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>. The GVCW Bangladesh assistance desk answers on +88 09606 777999 and +88 09666 911381, 9:00 to 16:30, Sunday to Thursday, and at info.bdgr@gvc24assist.eu; the Embassy's visa section is at grcon.del@mfa.gr <sup>[10](#o10), [4](#o4)</sup>.
2. The consular office calls the applicant to sign the contract and submit the application once it has the contract and approval <sup>[15](#o15)</sup>.

<a id="step-3-gather-the-papers"></a>

### 2.3 Step 3: Gather the papers

**Bring:**
- The long-stay visa application form, filled in and signed, and one recent colour passport photo on a white background <sup>[15](#o15)</sup>.
- A passport valid at least 1 year and 6 months from the day you apply, with two blank pages and issued within the past ten years <sup>[4](#o4), [15](#o15)</sup>.
- The approval from Greece for work with a specific employer <sup>[15](#o15)</sup>.
- A certified copy of the employment contract, showing the kind and period of work and pay at least equal to the monthly salary of unskilled workers <sup>[15](#o15)</sup>.
- Travel medical insurance starting two months after the day you apply and covering the whole visa (365 days for a one-year visa, 270 days for nine months, 180 days for six months) <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>.
- A police clearance certificate no more than three months old, attested by Bangladesh's Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Dhaka <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>.
- Originals with a copy of each, and an English translation of anything not in Greek or English <sup>[15](#o15)</sup>.

The medical fitness certificate comes later (see [Step 5: Interview, medical and signing the contract](#step-5-interview-medical-and-signing-the-contract)) <sup>[15](#o15)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Must the worker also get BMET clearance and a smart card for Greece, and is a BMET permission letter issued for Greek employers?
> - Does the Embassy apply the MoU's rules to Bangladeshis (passport valid two years after applying, contract of at least one year) or only GVCW's general 1.5-year passport rule?

<a id="step-4-lodge-the-application-at-the-gvcw-centre-in-dhaka"></a>

### 2.4 Step 4: Lodge the application at the GVCW centre in Dhaka

1. Book an appointment on the GVCW Bangladesh website (bd-gr.gvcworld.eu) <sup>[18](#o18)</sup>.
2. Email the appointment confirmation to info.bdgr@gvc24assist.eu with your email, phone and the visa fee in Indian rupees, asking for a payment link; the link is valid 48 hours and takes only an international credit or debit card <sup>[18](#o18)</sup>.
3. Print the payment receipt; without it the centre will not accept the application <sup>[18](#o18)</sup>.
4. Go to the GVCW centre at Borak Mehnur, 51/B, 8th Floor, Kemal Ataturk Avenue, Banani, Dhaka <sup>[6](#o6)</sup>. You may instead lodge at any GVCW centre in India <sup>[10](#o10)</sup>.

**Fees:**
- **Visa fee:** EUR 75 for seasonal work, EUR 180 for dependent employment, not refunded if refused <sup>[4](#o4), [15](#o15)</sup>.
- **GVCW service fee:** EUR 30, paid in taka <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>.
- **Courier to New Delhi:** Tk 10,200, in cash at the centre <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>.
- **Signature attestation on the contract:** EUR 30, after the interview <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>.

<a id="step-5-interview-medical-and-signing-the-contract"></a>

### 2.5 Step 5: Interview, medical and signing the contract

1. After the application is lodged, the Greek Embassy schedules an interview <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>.
2. After the interview the consular office asks for a medical fitness certificate, issued at the Dente Medical Center, 14 Nizamuddin East, New Delhi 110013 (tel. 9811157759, 9811444469); the certificate reaches the Embassy in about a week <sup>[15](#o15), [4](#o4)</sup>.
3. Processing time is entirely at the Embassy's discretion <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Must a Bangladeshi applicant travel to New Delhi for the interview and the Dente medical, and what does the medical cost?
> - How long does a seasonal work visa take from lodging in Dhaka to passport return?

<a id="step-6-after-you-arrive"></a>

### 2.6 Step 6: After you arrive

1. Apply for the residence permit in Greece immediately after entering on the D visa <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Where and how does a seasonal worker apply for the residence permit after arrival, and what does it cost?

<a id="leaving-each-year-and-at-the-end"></a>

### 2.7 Leaving each year and at the end

The worker must spend three months of every year outside Greece, and to come back must show an active seasonal contract before leaving for Greece <sup>[17 (2022)](#o17)</sup>. When the five years end the worker must leave Greece <sup>[1 (2022)](#o1)</sup>. Greece may make it easier to come back later for someone who complied fully with the MoU and Greek law <sup>[1 (2022)](#o1)</sup>.

<a id="bringing-family"></a>

### 2.8 Bringing family

The MoU permit gives no right to bring family to Greece <sup>[1 (2022)](#o1)</sup>. GVCW in Bangladesh has suspended family reunification visa applications until further notice <sup>[4](#o4)</sup>.

<a id="regularisation-of-bangladeshis-already-in-greece"></a>

## 3 Regularisation of Bangladeshis already in Greece

The MoU gave Bangladeshis who were already living in Greece when it was signed 15,000 five-year permits <sup>[1 (2022)](#o1)</sup>. It covered people in Greece before 9 February 2022 without legal status who had a binding job offer and posed no public order or security risk <sup>[9](#o9)</sup>. Applicants needed a passport valid two years after applying, a work contract or an employer's certificate, and the fees set by Greek law, and had to apply within 180 days of the MoU taking effect <sup>[1 (2022)](#o1)</sup>. Greece opened an online application platform on 12 January 2023 and issued permits in phases <sup>[N5 (2024)](#n5)</sup>. The permit works like the seasonal permit: nine months of work a year, three months out of Greece, and departure for good after five years <sup>[17 (2022)](#o17)</sup>. By January 2024, 10,833 people had registered with the Bangladesh embassy in Athens in the first phase <sup>[N5 (2024)](#n5)</sup>.

**What it changed.** Mohammad Kamal, regularised after 10 years in Greece, said he could not go home while irregular even when relatives died; with his card he came home, married, planned to go back after three months and could now come every year <sup>[N5 (2024)](#n5)</sup>. The ambassador at the time said many went to Bangladesh as soon as they got their resident card <sup>[N5 (2024)](#n5)</sup>.

> **Need to know**

<a id="irregular-entry-libya-to-crete-by-boat"></a>

## 5 Irregular entry: Libya to Crete by boat

Crete and the small island of Gavdos south of it are now the main entry point for asylum seekers into Greece; they are about 320 km from the Libyan coast <sup>[N6](#n6), [N13](#n13)</sup>. Arrivals by this route rose in 2025 <sup>[N2](#n2)</sup>. The route to Libya itself, the detention houses and ransom there are covered under "Libya to Italy by boat" in the Illegal routes doc.

<a id="how-it-is-sold"></a>

### 5.1 How it is sold

- **The deal.** The Derai men each agreed Tk 12 lakh with a broker to go to Greece, left home in February 2026 and paid half after reaching Libya <sup>[N7](#n7)</sup>.
- **The way to Libya.** Shayek Ahmad (23) went through Saudi Arabia, Kuwait and other countries to Libya; Mohibur Rahman (20) went through Saudi Arabia, and stayed in Saudi Arabia and Libya with Maruf Ahmad, another Sunamganj man who left Bangladesh with him <sup>[N8](#n8), [N9](#n9), [N15](#n15)</sup>. How brokers route people through a third country is under "A flight to a different country first" in the Dalal scams doc.
- **More money in Libya.** From Libya the broker demanded Tk 7.5 lakh more before Shayek would be put on the boat, and his father sold land and cows to pay it <sup>[N8](#n8)</sup>. Both Shayek's and Mohibur's fathers said the broker beat their sons for money before the boat <sup>[N8](#n8), [N9](#n9)</sup>.
- **Wanting to come home.** Mohibur messaged his family from the journey begging to be brought home because he was suffering, his cousin said <sup>[N9](#n9)</sup>.

<a id="the-crossing"></a>

### 5.2 The crossing

- **The "game".** Local people and brokers call the rubber-boat crossing from Libya to Greece a "game" <sup>[N7](#n7), [N9](#n9)</sup>.
- **Where from.** The March 2026 boat left Tobruk, the eastern Libyan port city, on 21 March <sup>[N10](#n10)</sup>.
- **Food.** These boats are sent off with food for 24 hours, Shayek Ahmad's uncle said <sup>[N8](#n8)</sup>. People are sent from Libya to Greece in small boats with limited food, a Bangladeshi in a Greek camp said <sup>[N11](#n11)</sup>.
- **Boarding.** Shayek hurt his leg trying to board on the first day and could not go, and was put on the next "game" on 21 March <sup>[N8](#n8)</sup>.

<a id="pushbacks-at-sea"></a>

### 5.3 Pushbacks at sea

A BBC analysis of 15 incidents between May 2020 and May 2023 found that 43 people died, more than 40 of them after the Greek coast guard forced them out of Greek waters or pushed them back to sea after they had reached Greek islands; witnesses said at least 9 were thrown into the sea <sup>[N16 (2024)](#n16)</sup>. In four incidents people were sent back after reaching Greek islands, and in some they were pushed out to sea on dinghies without engines, witnesses said <sup>[N16 (2024)](#n16)</sup>. The coast guard denied the allegations and said its own investigations found no wrongdoing <sup>[N16 (2024)](#n16)</sup>. In 2025 Frontex said it was reviewing 12 possible human rights violations by Greece, and human rights groups accuse Athens of pushing migrants back at sea and on land <sup>[N2](#n2)</sup>. Since 2019 the government has built a fence on its northern border and stepped up sea patrols <sup>[N2](#n2)</sup>.

<a id="arrival-on-crete"></a>

### 5.4 Arrival on Crete

1. <a id="arrival-on-crete-item-1"></a>**Spotted and brought in.** In August 2026 a Frontex aircraft spotted a boat of 40 that was then brought into Ierapetra port with the help of a coast guard patrol ship and a fishing boat; later that month an EU drone spotted two boats near Kali Limenes port in south Crete, and the coast guard rescued 79 people from them near Crete, Bangladeshis and at least two women and two children among them <sup>[N1](#n1), [N6](#n6)</sup>.
2. <a id="arrival-on-crete-item-2"></a>**Registered and told the rule.** At the port the migrants are registered and identity checks begin, and everyone entering is told that if the asylum claim is not justified, return follows <sup>[N1](#n1), [13](#o13)</sup>.
3. <a id="arrival-on-crete-item-3"></a>**Held in a camp.** In August 2026 all those rescued were held at a temporary shelter camp at Heraklion port, as numbers on Crete rose; at least 202 people reached Crete from Libya in under 48 hours that month <sup>[N1](#n1)</sup>. A Habiganj man who arrived on 6 March 2026 was kept in a camp, and the survivors of the 27 March boat were brought to the same camp <sup>[N17](#n17)</sup>.
4. <a id="arrival-on-crete-item-4"></a>**Moved to the mainland.** New arrivals on Gavdos and Crete used to be moved to mainland reception centres within a couple of days, up to 10; from mid-August 2025, during the asylum suspension, transfers were frozen and hundreds were held over a month in unsuitable buildings on the islands, the Border Violence Monitoring Network (BVMN), a network of non-governmental organisations, reported to the EU asylum agency <sup>[N12](#n12)</sup>.
5. <a id="arrival-on-crete-item-5"></a>**Health check, then asylum.** About 540 people rescued south of Crete in December 2025, Bangladeshis among them, were taken to Rethymno on Crete, where their asylum process was to begin after health checks <sup>[N13](#n13)</sup>.
6. <a id="arrival-on-crete-item-6"></a>**Hospital.** Two seriously ill survivors of the March 2026 boat were taken to hospital on Crete <sup>[N10](#n10), [N17](#n17)</sup>.

> **Need to know**
> - Where are Bangladeshi arrivals from Crete sent on the mainland in 2026: an open camp or a pre-removal detention centre?
> - Can a Bangladeshi in a camp phone home, and how do families find out who is in which camp?

<a id="deaths-on-the-crossing"></a>

### 5.5 Deaths on the crossing

- **March 2026, from Tobruk.** The boat left on 21 March, lost its way and drifted six days without food or water; at least 22 people died, and survivors said the bodies were thrown into the sea on the order of one of the smugglers <sup>[N10](#n10)</sup>. The fuel ran out, and the bodies were kept two days on the boat until they began to smell <sup>[N9](#n9), [N11](#n11)</sup>. A Frontex ship rescued 26 people about 53 nautical miles south of Ierapetra on 27 March: 21 Bangladeshis, 4 South Sudanese and a Chadian <sup>[N10](#n10)</sup>. Sunamganj police confirmed 12 dead from the district, six from Derai, five from Jagannathpur and one from Dowarabazar, and the family of Mohibur Rahman of Chhatak later learned he had also died on the boat <sup>[N11](#n11), [N9](#n9)</sup>. Greek authorities arrested two South Sudanese men, aged 19 and 22, suspected of being the smugglers, who were under investigation for illegal entry and negligent homicide <sup>[N10](#n10)</sup>. More on this boat is under "Deaths" in the Illegal routes doc.
- **31 October 2026, Habiganj.** Seven young men from Madhabpur and Lakhai upazilas left Libya for Greece through brokers on an inflatable rubber boat with about 50 passengers; it sprang a leak mid-sea and sank <sup>[N3](#n3)</sup>. Of the seven, only Masum Mia of Puraikala village survived: he floated for two days, washed ashore unconscious and reached Greece with the help of local people, his father said <sup>[N3](#n3)</sup>. The other six, aged 25 to 30, were still missing in mid-November 2026 <sup>[N3](#n3)</sup>.

<a id="if-a-relative-dies-or-goes-missing-on-the-crossing"></a>

### 5.6 If a relative dies or goes missing on the crossing

**How families find out.** In the reported cases, news came from a survivor or a man from the same area already in Greece, not from the broker: Rohan Ahmad, on the March boat, phoned Tarapasha village; Sohel Ahmad, living in Greece, went to the camp and phoned Shayek's family; Masum Mia phoned his own family, and his father told the families of the six missing <sup>[N7](#n7), [N8](#n8), [N3](#n3)</sup>. Broker Nabi Hossain hid Mohibur's death for two days, and only confirmed he had been put on the boat when the family went to his house in Pagla <sup>[N15](#n15)</sup>. A survivor who travelled with Mohibur then phoned home to say he had died first <sup>[N15](#n15)</sup>. The Habiganj families could not reach the brokers at all <sup>[N3](#n3)</sup>.

**Getting the authorities to search.** The Madhabpur UNO said in November 2026 that families must apply formally to the relevant government ministry, after which a search can be arranged abroad through the foreign ministry and the embassy <sup>[N3](#n3)</sup>.

**Cases against brokers.** After the March 2026 deaths the fathers of two of the dead filed two cases against 9 brokers at Jagannathpur and Derai police stations on 30 March 2026 <sup>[N9](#n9)</sup>. The Sunamganj Deputy Commissioner said the district administration would also file cases, and the Jagannathpur UNO said a list of brokers was being made for the police <sup>[N11](#n11), [N8](#n8)</sup>. Who to go to and what the law says are under "Where victims and families get help" and "The law in Bangladesh" in the Illegal routes doc, and "File a trafficking case" in the Dalal scams doc.

## 7 Asylum

On 11 July 2025 the Greek parliament suspended asylum applications from people arriving by sea from North Africa for three months and ordered their deportation without registration <sup>[11](#o11)</sup>. The suspension was in force from July to October 2025; arrivals were moved to pre-removal detention centres on the mainland such as Amygdaleza near Athens, and internal circulars exempted vulnerable people and, from mid-September, Sudanese and Eritreans, while other nationalities stayed in detention <sup>[N12](#n12)</sup>. Everyone entering Greece is now told at once that if the asylum claim is not justified, return follows <sup>[13](#o13)</sup>. The September 2025 law also limits repeat asylum applications <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>. In January 2024, 15 Bangladeshis whose asylum claims had been rejected as unfounded were flown home on a Frontex-coordinated return flight from the Amygdaleza centre <sup>[19 (2024)](#o19)</sup>. Greece planned more return flights including Bangladeshis in September 2025 <sup>[N2](#n2)</sup>. UNHCR said the 2025 law could expose people who need protection to punishment <sup>[N2](#n2)</sup>.

Under the new EU migration and asylum pact, due in June 2026, Greece gets EU help with migration, and Prime Minister Kyriakos Mitsotakis said returning those whose asylum claims are rejected will be a priority <sup>[N13](#n13)</sup>. Greece is in talks with Germany and other EU countries about return hubs outside the EU <sup>[N6](#n6)</sup>. What happened to one Bangladeshi who claimed asylum in Greece as a Rohingya is under "From the Gulf overland through Iran and Turkey to Greece" in the Illegal routes doc, and how false asylum stories are sold is under "Asylum claims built on a false story" in the same doc.

> **Need to know**

> - Does Greece list Bangladesh as a safe country of origin, and what share of Bangladeshi asylum claims did it reject in 2025?

<a id="living-without-papers-and-deportation"></a>

## 8 Living without papers and deportation

Since the law passed on 3 September 2025:
- **Illegal stay is a crime:** 2 to 5 years in prison and a fine of at least EUR 5,000 <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>.
- **Detention:** up to 24 months before removal, and an appeal does not stop it <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>.
- **Leaving voluntarily:** 14 days after a return decision, possibly with electronic monitoring <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>.
- **Entry ban:** up to ten years <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>.
- **No papers after seven years:** the route to legal status through seven years of irregular stay was abolished <sup>[8](#o8)</sup>.

Before the regularisation, Bangladeshis without papers lived in inhuman conditions and could not get treatment when sick <sup>[N5 (2024)](#n5)</sup>. In 2022 Greek police were rounding up irregular migrants in Athens every day, and more than fifty Bangladeshis were sent home in several rounds <sup>[N5 (2024)](#n5)</sup>. Under the MoU, Greece and Bangladesh cooperate on returning Bangladeshis who have no right to stay, under the EU-Bangladesh return procedures signed on 20 September 2017 <sup>[1 (2022)](#o1)</sup>. A Bangladeshi without a valid passport goes home on a Travel Permit from the Bangladesh Embassy in Athens, which costs EUR 11, or EUR 25 urgent <sup>[20 (2022)](#o20)</sup>. What happens on landing in Dhaka is under "Deportees and outpass holders" in the Departure bottlenecks doc.

## 9 Work and life in Greece

Workers under the MoU have the same employment and social security rights as Greek law gives others <sup>[1 (2022)](#o1)</sup>. From 1 April 2026 the minimum wage is EUR 920 a month for salaried employees and EUR 41.09 a day for manual workers <sup>[14](#o14)</sup>. Since the regularisation, regularised Bangladeshis work freely in various firms, and the embassy said they now send money home through legal channels <sup>[N5 (2024)](#n5)</sup>.
