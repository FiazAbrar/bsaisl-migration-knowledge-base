Description

The procedure concerns applications for approval of employment which an employer has to submit when he/she wishes to hire a third-country national as a seasonal worker in agricultural economy.

Basic information

    Title

    Approval of employment of a third-country national as seasonal worker in agricultural economy.

    Notes:

    An employer’s application for approval of employment for the purpose of seasonal work, following an invitation, must be submitted via the e-services of the Ministry of Migration and Asylum.

          Related

          Means of redress or appeal:

          Administrative appeal

          The third-country national concerned has the right to appeal against the rejection decision. The application for treatment should be submitted within two (2) months of service of the decision. Applications for treatment are subject to a fee of EUR fifty (50). The request for treatment is submitted to the department that issued the relevant decision, which decides on it within an exclusive period of thirty (30) days from the date of submission.
