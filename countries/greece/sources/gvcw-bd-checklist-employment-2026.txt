                          EMPLOYMENT VISA–SEASONAL & DEPENDENT
                                       EMPLOYMENT
 1     Application form for a visa for a long stay in Greece, fully filled in and signed by the
       applicant.
 2     One recent passport-size photograph in colour with white background, according to the
       relevantspecifications laid down by ICAO.
 3      Passport, which should:
       -be of a validity exceeding by three months the ending date of the visa length
       -include at least two blank pages
       -have been issued within the previous ten years.
 4     Prefecture’ s Approval for the provision of dependent employment to a specific employer in
       Greece
 5     Certified copy of the employment contract or original “Ypefthini dilosi” of the employer, duly
       attested by a Greek Public Authority, showing:
       -the kind and the period of employment
       -that the remuneration of the applicant is at least equivalent to the monthly salary of
       unskilled workers.
       The employment contract and the Prefecture’s Approval are sent directly to the
       Consular Office, which, after receiving them, calls the applicant to sign the contract
       and submit his
       application.
 6    Travel medical insurance covering at least the period of validity of the visa requested. The
      insurance policy should cover any eventual expenses relating to repatriation for medical reasons,
      urgent medicalattention and/or emergency hospital treatment.
       Ensure that the validity of your medical insurance starts two months after the date of
       your visa application submission.
 7     Police Clearance Certificate, recent and duly attested *
 8     Medical Fitness Certificate, upon request by the Consular Office after the Interview (see
       Note no2)

* - For India: the document must be affixed with an Apostille Sticker from the Ministry of External Affairs New Delhi
  - For Bangladesh: the document must be attested by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Bangladesh, Dhaka

  Note:
     1) Original documents and passport submitted must be accompanied along with a copy
     2) All documents submitted, if not in Greek or English, should be accompanied by an
        English translation.
     3) The applicant may be asked for additional documents.
     4) For the issuance of a medical certificate, please visit “Dente” Medical
        Center: 14 Nizamuddin East, New Delhi-110013, tel: 9811157759 &
        9811444469.
     5) The visa fee is not refunded if the visa is refused.
