Lodging visa applications in India

Dear applicants,\
Kindly be informed that as of **today, 17th March 2025**, the Bangladeshi nationals, wishing to apply for all visa categories (Schengen Visa Type C & Long-term National Visa Type D) and travel to Greece, can submit their applications either at the GVCW Visa Application Center for Greece in Dhaka or in any other GVCW Visa Application Center in India according to their preference.
