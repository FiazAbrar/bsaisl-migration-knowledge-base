# Visas

For more information regarding the submission of visa applications at the Greek Visa Application Centres operating in India,Nepal and Sri Lank, please click the following corresponding links:\
[Greece Visa Application Center in India](https://in-gr.gvcworld.eu%20)\
[Greece Visa Application Center in Nepal](https://np-gr.gvcworld.eu%20)\
[Greece Visa Application Center in Sri Lanka](https://lk-gr.gvcworld.eu%20)

Greece Visa Application Center in Bangladesh\
\
The Embassy of the Hellenic Republic in New Delhi, also accredited in Dhaka Bangladesh, is pleased to announce that from now on Bangladeshi nationals wishing to travel to Greece either for employment purposes or for tourism or for any other reason, can apply for their visa at the Visa Application Center of the Embassy's External Service Provider, GVCW, in Dhaka (for any pertinent information, please visit [https://bd-gr.gvcworld.eu](https://bd-gr.gvcworld.eu))\
