**Overview of statistical practice**

Monthly statistics are published by the Ministry of Migration and Asylum,[**^(\[1\])**](#_ftn1) however detailed data on breakdown are only scarcely included.

**Applications and granting of protection status at first instance: rates for 2025**

The MoMA [presents](https://migration.gov.gr/statistika/) an overall protection rate at first instance of 58.33%,[\[8\]](#_ftn8) but there is no information as to the methodology used to calculate the rates.[\[9\]](#_ftn9)

According to calculations made on the basis of the data provided in the table above, the in-merit protection rate at first instance would be as follows: 64.3% refugee protection and 6.3% subsidiary protection.[\[10\]](#_ftn10)

**Comparison between first instance and appeal decision rates: 2025**

It should be noted that, during the same year, the first instance and appeal authorities handle different caseloads. Thus, the decisions below do not concern the same applicants.

The MoMA presents an overall protection rate at second instance of 4.34%[\[11\]](#_ftn11).[\[12\]](#_ftn12)

According to calculations made on the basis of the data provided in the table below, the in-merit protection rate at second instance would be as follows: 4.7% refugee protection and 1.8% subsidiary protection.
