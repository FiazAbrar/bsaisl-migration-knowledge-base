Greece Visa Application Center in Bangladesh

The Embassy of the Hellenic Republic in New Delhi, also accredited in Dhaka Bangladesh, is pleased to announce that from now on Bangladeshi nationals wishing to travel to Greece either for employment purposes or for tourism or for any other reason, can apply for their visa at the Visa Application Center of the Embassy's External Service Provider, GVCW, in Dhaka (for any pertinent information, please visit https://bd-gr.gvcworld.eu)
