Bilateral Relations

Bangladesh

  1. Home
  2. Bilateral Relations
  3. Bangladesh

Greece’s Embassy in New Delhi has parallel accreditation to Bangladesh.

Bangladesh opened an Embassy in Athens in July 2009.

Bilateral Relations

Political Relations
Useful Information

Bilateral contacts between the two countries are on a satisfactory level. The political climate in our bilateral relations is very good, and there is a tradition of mutual support for candidacies in international organizations.

The existence of a Bangladeshi community in our country constitutes a significant link in our bilateral relations.
