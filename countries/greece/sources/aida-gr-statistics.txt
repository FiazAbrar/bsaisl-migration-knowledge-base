Applications and granting of protection status at first instance: rates for 2025
The MoMA presents an overall protection rate at first instance of 58.33%, [8] but there is no information as to the methodology used to calculate the rates. [9]
According to calculations made on the basis of the data provided in the table above, the in-merit protection rate at first instance would be as follows: 64.3% refugee protection and 6.3% subsidiary protection. [10]

Comparison between first instance and appeal decision rates: 2025
It should be noted that, during the same year, the first instance and appeal authorities handle different caseloads. Thus, the decisions below do not concern the same applicants.
The MoMA presents an overall protection rate at second instance of 4.34% [11] . [12]
