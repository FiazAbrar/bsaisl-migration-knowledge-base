16/07/2026

New Visa Fee Payment Method– Effective Immediately

Dear Applicants,

Please be informed that the **Visa Fee payment process in Bangladesh has changed**.

Effective immediately, all Visa Fees must be paid **after booking your appointment** using a **payment link** provided by the Call Center.

To complete your Visa Fee payment, please follow the steps below:

1.  [**Book your appointment**](https://bd-gr.gvcworld.eu/en/schedule-appointment) through the GVCW website

2.  After successfully booking your appointment, you will receive an **Appointment Confirmation Letter** by email. You can also download it as a PDF from your GVCW account under **"My Appointments."**

3.  Send your **Appointment Confirmation Letter** (email or PDF) to the Call Center at **<info.bdgr@gvc24assist.eu>**.

4.  In your email, please include the following information:

    - Your email address

    - Your phone number

    - The applicable **Visa Fee amount in INR** based on the visa category you are applying for *(all visa fee amounts in INR can be found on the website)*

    - A request for the **payment link** to complete your Visa Fee payment

5.  The Call Center will send you a **payment link**, which will remain valid for **48 hours**. You must complete the payment within this period. **Please note:** Visa Fee payments can only be made using an **international credit or debit card**.

6.  Once your payment is successful, **print the payment receipt** and bring it with you to the Visa Application Center on the day of your appointment.

**Important:** Applicants who do not present a valid Visa Fee payment receipt at the time of their appointment **will not be allowed to submit their visa application**.

Thank you for your cooperation.
