H.E. Nahida Rahman Shumona

Welcome to the official website of the Embassy of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh in the Hellenic Republic. Bangladesh and Greece share a longstanding relationship built on mutual respect, cooperation and shared values. I am committed to further strengthening these ties by expanding our collaboration in areas such as trade and investment, maritime affairs, cultural exchange, education and people-to-people connections. The Embassy remains dedicated to serving the Bangladeshi community in Greece with care, efficiency and compassion. The welfare of our citizens abroad will continue to be a central priority of our work. I also encourage members of the diaspora to actively engage with the Embassy and contribute to the strengthening of bilateral relations. I look forward to working closely with the Government and people of Greece, the diplomatic community and all stakeholders to further enhance the friendship between Bangladesh and the Hellenic Republic.
