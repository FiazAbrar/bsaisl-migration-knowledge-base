# Issuance of a long-term multiple-entry tourist visa for a family group – 5 years.

## About this service

A service that allows the issuance of a multiple-entry tourist visa for a family group for a period of five (5) years without the need for a sponsor, available to all nationalities. The visa allows its holder to stay in the country for up to 90 days.

## Terms and Conditions

- The bank statement must be submitted as an official document (stamped, signed, and in color) issued by the bank, showing a minimum balance of USD 4,000 per month or its equivalent in other currencies over the past (6) months.
- The health insurance must be issued within the United Arab Emirates and should have a validity period of 180 days.
- The visa application for a family group may be submitted if there are children included, provided that minors under the age of 18 must be accompanied by one or both parents.
- The entry visa is valid for 60 days from the date of issuance, and the applicant must enter the country within this period to avoid cancellation of the visa or the need to extend it. This requirement applies only to the first entry; once the applicant enters the country for the first time, the visa becomes multiple‑entry, and the condition no longer applies thereafter.
- The visa holder must not exceed a stay of (90) days within a single year. The year is calculated from the date of the first entry into the UAE, not from the start of the calendar year or the visa issuance date.
- The five-year visa is a multiple-entry visa, allowing multiple entries to the UAE, provided that each stay does not exceed ninety (90) days.
- The duration of stay may be extended for an additional period of the same length, provided that the total stay does not exceed one hundred eighty (180) days per year.
- If the visa holder does not stay in the UAE for the full ninety (90) days, the remaining days cannot be carried over to the following year.
- The visa will be considered invalid, and the financial guarantee will be enforced if the visa holder exceeds the permitted duration of stay in the UAE.
- The head of the family is required to enter the country together with the rest of the family members when a family visa is issued.

## List of Required Documents for Service

Passport

Personal Photo

Health insurance.

Bank statement showing a monthly income of at least USD 4,000 or its equivalent in foreign currencies for the last 6 months.

A copy of a travel ticket for a connecting flight or a return ticket.

Iraqi residence card of the service beneficiary

Pakistani identity card of the service beneficiary

Afghan identity card of the service beneficiary

Shenasnameh / Iranian identity card of the service beneficiary
