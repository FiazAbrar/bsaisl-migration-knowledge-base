### Visa for patient

Foreigners can enter the UAE for treatment under the sponsorship of a medical establishment. The sponsor ([medical establishment](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/health-and-fitness/Healthcare-access-and-insurance/healthcare-providers)) takes the responsibility of processing entry permits for treatment upon the patient’s request.

 

 

#### In Dubai

General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs in Dubai grants [a medical visa for single or multiple entries](https://www.gdrfad.gov.ae/en/services/d551ce91-52e8-11ea-0320-0050569629e8). The visa may be for 90 or 180 days. 

 

** **

**Documents required**

The following documents are needed to process the entry permit:

- patient’s passport copy

- medical report

- a photo

- a letter from the host/sponsor stating the justification for visit

- health insurance.

 

**Conditions**

The sponsor must be a hospital licensed and registered in the UAE. A deposit for financial security should be made.

 

 

#### In Abu Dhabi and the Northern Emirates

Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP) provides [a visa for treatment](https://icp.gov.ae/en/services-details/?serviceid=64afe3c1035448005bd52e60). ICP covers the emirates of Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah. The visa may be for a single entry or multiple entries.

### Visa for patient's companion

#### In Dubai

General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs grants visa to [foreigner patient’s companion](https://www.gdrfad.gov.ae/en/services/d551ce93-52e8-11ea-0320-0050569629e8). Permits are of two types single and multiple entry.

The companion's visa can be extended only once provided a duly certified medical report states that the patient needs to continue treatment and that he/she is needed in the UAE.

 

**Required documents**

The following documents are needed to process the entry permit:

- companion’s passport copy
- a photo
- copy of the patient’s visa
- a letter from the hospital.

 

 

**Conditions**

- The companion must enter UAE along with the patient. 
- He/she must have the same type pf visa as the patient (in terms of number of entries). 
- The companion’s visa will not be extended unless the patient’s entry visa is extended.\
   

 

#### In Abu Dhabi and the Northern Emirates

Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP) provides [a medical visa for a patient’s companion](https://icp.gov.ae/en/services-details/?serviceid=64afe3c1035448005bd52e60). ICP covers the emirates of Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah. The visa may be for a single entry or multiple entries.
