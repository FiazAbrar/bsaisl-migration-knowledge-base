Visa fraud syndicate busted in Rangpur

Three arrested with fake passports, forged visas and job offer letters

              Published : 26 June 2026

•

                Updated : 26 June 2026, 14:50

•

					CRIME & JUSTICE

Our Correspondent, Lalmonirhat

Photo: Star

Detectives in Rangpur have arrested three members of an organised visa fraud syndicate accused of swindling job seekers with false promises of overseas employment.
The arrests were made Thursday afternoon during a raid at a rented house in the CDT Housing Society area of Collegepara under Tajhat Police Station.

Tap here to add The Daily Star as a trusted source

Police recovered Bangladeshi and foreign passports, fake Australian visas, forged job offer letters, airline tickets, counterfeit identity cards and other materials used in the scam.
The detainees are Ruhanur Rahman, 23, Shafiq Islam, 31, and Himel Islam, 22.
A case was filed at Tajhat Police Station Thursday night on charges of fraud and forgery.
Deputy Commissioner (Detective Branch) Sanatan Chakrabarty said detectives seized 10 Bangladeshi passports with forged Australian visas, two fake Australian passports, around 30 fabricated employment letters, a laptop, four mobile phones, and envelopes bearing the name of VFS Global to make the operation appear legitimate.
He added that the suspects admitted during interrogation to running the fraud ring for a considerable period.
“They created fake Facebook pages using the name of an Australian company and lured young job seekers and expatriate families with lucrative employment offers. Their office was decorated with banners, nameplates and identity cards carrying the company’s logo,” he said.
The DB official said the group collected passports from applicants, attached forged visas, and used fake appointment letters to extort large sums of money. Police are also investigating how many victims were defrauded and the total amount of money collected, he added.
