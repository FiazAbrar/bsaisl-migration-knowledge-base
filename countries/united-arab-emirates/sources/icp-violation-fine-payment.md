# Payment of visa or residence violation fine

- 
- 
- [](javascript:; "Download pdf services")

## About this service

A service through which fines imposed on violators of entry and residence laws in the United Arab Emirates, specifically visa and residence violators, are paid.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Service Channels

- ICP Smart Services Website - Websites

This service applies to the category: **Individuals, the private sector, and the government sector**.\
You can view the terms and conditions, fees, and the list of required documents below.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Terms and Conditions

- The status must be adjusted or the individual must leave the UAE after paying any fines incurred in relation to the visa or residence permit

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Service fees

- Expiry of the permitted stay period for the foreigner in the State, or the cancellation or expiration of the (entry visa / residence permit / exit permit / newborn registration), per day. - 50 AED
- Misuse of smart services - 2000 AED
- Smart services fees - 100 AED
