- ## The National Assembly

  The National Assembly of Bangladesh known as Jatya Sangsad Bhaban, is the legislative complex of Bangladesh, serving as the meeting place for its Members of Parliament.

- ## Sixty Dome Mosque

  The Sixty Dome Mosque, also known as Shat Gombuj Masjid, is a remarkable 15th-century mosque located in Bagerhat, Bangladesh. Built by Sultan Khan Jahan Ali, it stands as the largest mosque in Bangladesh from the sultanate period (1204-1576).

- ## National Martyrs’ Memorial

  National Martyrs’ Memorial (“Jatiyo Sriti Shoudho”), a 150 feet tall monument located in Savar, is the iconic symbol of the valour and sacrifice of those who laid down their lives during the Great Liberation War of Bangladesh in 1971, leading to the nation’s independence.

- ## Worlds’ Longest Sea Beach

  Cox’s Bazar Sea Beach, located on the southeast coast of Bangladesh, boasts the longest uninterrupted natural beach (120 km) in the world with its stunning coastline, lush forests, and breathtaking views
