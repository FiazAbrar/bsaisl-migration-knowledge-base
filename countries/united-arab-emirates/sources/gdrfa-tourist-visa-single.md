### Issuance of a single-entry tourist visa

Search in Services

[Start service](https://smart.gdrfad.gov.ae/SmartChannels_Th/Login.aspx?Lang=ar-AE)

The foreigner is granted an entry visa for a single visit for the purpose of tourism for a period of 30 or 60 days, and the sponsor / host in UAE should be one of the establishments working in the field of tourism, after paying the prescribed fee and financial security deposit.\

Service Details

Requirements

1\. One personal photo.\
2\. Copy of the passport.\
3\. Identity card for the country of origin for some nationalities (Iraq - Pakistan - Iran - Afghanistan).\

Service Steps

The digital channels (GDRFA website / smart application):\
1\. Login to the smart services system using (UAE pass or username)\
2\. Search for your required service.\
3\. Fill out the application information where applicable.\
\
4\. Pay the service fees (if any).\

Fees

\- 30-day tourist visa fee: AED 200.\
\- 60-day tourist visa fee: AED 300.\
\- In addition to value added tax (5%).\
\- Security deposit: There is no security deposit amount on this type of visa\
\- Additional fees (if the sponsored person is inside UAE):\
Knowledge Dirham: AED 10.\
Innovation Dirham: AED 10.\
Fee inside UAE: 500 Dirhams.\
\
Note: Total amount may vary. The visa may increase or decrease depending on the circumstances surrounding the sponsored person or any other reasons that may require the same.\

Terms And Conditions

1\. A valid and approved passport or travel document to enter UAE and return to the country in which the passport or document holder is resident or from which the passport or document was issued. The validity period of the passport or travel document should not be less than (6) six months.\
2\. A travel ticket to continue his journey or a ticket to leave UAE.\
\
3\. Medical insurance valid in UAE.\

Service Availability Channels

Digital channels (website/smart application): The service is available 24/7\

Expected Completion Time

48.0 Hour(s)

Additional Information

Only accredited tourism offices in the emirate can issue this kind of visa.

Related Services

1\. Renewal of tourist entry visa - one entry or multiple entries. 2. Extending the tourist entry visa - one entry or multiple entries.
