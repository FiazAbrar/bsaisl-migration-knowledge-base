### Issuance of an Entry Visa for Residents of the GCC Member States and Their Accompanying Persons.

Search in Services

[Start service](https://smart.gdrfad.gov.ae/SmartChannels_Individual/Dashboard.aspx?Service=6651d523-d40d-4994-9444-998f583480b0&Lang=ar-AE)

This visa is granted to foreign nationals residing in a GCC Member State, as well as to their family members and accompanying domestic workers residing with them.

Service Details

Requirements

1\. A recent colour photograph with a white background..\
2.A copy of the passport or travel document, including its outer cover, duly certified, provided that it is valid for a period of not less than six months..\
3.A copy of the residence permit or an electronic extract showing the applicant's profession and the validity of the residence permit

Service Steps

1.Acesses the website https://smart.gdrfad.gov.ae\
2.Register as a user.\
3 select the relevant service.\
4 .Complete the required information.\
5.Pay the applicable fees.\
6.Upon approval, the visa is sent to the user’s email.

Fees

Service Fees: 52.5\
Application Fees: 100\
Issuance Fees: 100

Terms And Conditions

1.The traveller must not be subject to any restriction preventing entry into the United Arab Emirates\
2.The applicant must hold a valid residence permit issued by one of the GCC Member States.\
3\. The applicant's profession must fall within the list of professions approved for the issuance of the visa, subject to assessment by the competent authority.

Service Availability Channels

The official website of the General Directorate of Residency and Foregners Affairs- Dubai\
https://smart.gdrfa.gov.ae

Expected Completion Time

48.0 Hour(s)

Additional Information

The service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week (24/7).
