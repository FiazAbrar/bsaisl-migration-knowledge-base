# Overview

Our network of 25 Medical Fitness Centers across Dubai provides medical fitness exams, occupational health screenings, and Unified Health Screenings for both residents and newcomers to the UAE.

\
With our Premium Medical Fitness Exam service, results are delivered within 6 to 24 hours.\
For those seeking an expedited option, Smart Salem Centers offer VIP services with medical fitness results available in 30 minutes.
