H.E. Abdulla Ali Khaseif AlHmoudi, Ambassador of the United Arab Emirates to the People’s Republic of Bangladesh, met with H.E. Tarique Rahman, Prime Minister of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh.

During the meeting, the two sides discussed the distinguished bilateral relations between the United Arab Emirates and the People’s Republic of Bangladesh, as well as ways to strengthen and develop cooperation across various fields in a manner that serves the mutual interests of the two countries and their people.

The meeting also addressed a number of issues of mutual interest, in addition to exploring opportunities to broaden the horizons of cooperation and enhance coordination in support of development and economic prosperity, reflecting the depth of the relations between the UAE and Bangladesh.

The two sides reaffirmed their commitment to continuing joint efforts to further advance bilateral relations and strengthen areas of cooperation in a manner that serves the aspirations of the two friendly countries
