# Report Lost Item

FeesOptional

Duration3 Working Days

This service allows customers to report lost property or personal belongings and ensures that the owner can be contacted if the property is found. Customers may also choose to obtain a Certificate of Loss, for a fee, for use with other official entities.

## Terms and Conditions

- The lost items must be within the geographical boundaries of the Emirate of Dubai
- The certificate is valid for one month from issuance date

## Contact Details

- Email mail@dubaipolice.gov.ae

## Service Delivery Channels

• **Email**: The digital version of the certificate will be sent to your registered email address as soon as it is issued.

• **Personal Dashboard**: Log in to your Dubai Police account, navigate to "My Requests" section in your personal dashboard, search for your request, and then download the certificate directly.

• **Direct Inquiry**: You can download the certificate through "Application Status" service using only your reference number.
