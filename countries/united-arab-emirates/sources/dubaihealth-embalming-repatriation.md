# Embalming and repatriation of the deceased

[Start Service]()

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Overview

This service helps you arrange the necessary procedures and paperwork to help repatriate the deceased to their home country.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Fees

- Embalming fees: AED 1,020
- Dubai Municipality ambulance fee: AED 220
- Fees for company in charge of deceased's body: AED 1,840
- Typing fees: AED 52.50

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Required Documents

- Death certificate
- Death notification
- Letter from the police
- Letter from the embassy
- Electronic copy of the airline ticket
- Copy of the deceased's passport
- Please note, in the case of an infectious body, call the contact center on 800 342
