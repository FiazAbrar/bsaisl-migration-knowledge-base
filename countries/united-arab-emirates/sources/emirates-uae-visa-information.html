    UAE Visa Information

    Everything you need to know about UAE visas and sponsorship.
    Do I need a visa for the UAE?

    You’ll find information about visas below. Use our quick visa search tool to see if you need a visa and the passport requirements for visiting the UAE.

    UAE visa on arrival

    30-day visit eligibility

    If you are a passport holder of the below country or territory, no advance visa arrangements are required to visit the UAE. Simply disembark your flight at Dubai International and proceed to immigration, where your passport will be stamped with a 30-day visit visa free of charge.


    AndorraBruneiHong Kong, China
    KazakhstanMacau, ChinaMalaysia
    MauritiusMonacoMongolia
    Republic of IrelandUkraineUzbekistan
    Vatican City


    90-day visit eligibility

    If you are a passport holder of one of the below countries or territories, your passport will be stamped with a multiple entry 90-day visit visa that's valid for 6 months from the date of issue, and for a stay of 90 days in total. Citizens of the below European countries are also entitled to apply for a pre-arranged visit visa if their 90-day visa on arrival has been fully utilized.

    180-day visit eligibility

    If you hold a Mexican passport you’re eligible for a multiple entry 180-day visit visa that's valid for 6 months from the date of issue, and for a stay of 180 days in total.



    Dubai immigration procedures

    As an added security measure, Immigration Authorities at Dubai International are now conducting random eye screening for visitors to Dubai.

    Visitors asked to proceed for eye screening are required to present a hard copy of their visa or a printed version of the visa number confirmation page at the screening counter. If you do not have a copy with you, a charge of AED 30 per copy applies - payable only in AED.


    Important information

    Before you travel to the UAE, please check your visa requirements and make sure you have a valid visa if needed, or if your passport needs to be valid for 6 months. Holders of non-standard passports and travel documents may also have different entry requirements: find out if you need to apply for a visa and other passport requirements.


    As of 29 April 2016, GCC residence permit holders will need to apply for a UAE visa before arriving in Dubai.

    Preapproved UAE visas – visit entry permit

    New visit entry permit: visa for certain nationals of residing abroad

    Eligible nationals with a normal passport valid for a minimum of six months can obtain any of the following single-entry permits:

    • Entry permits valid for 14 days for a fee of USD 63.
    • Entry permits valid for 60 days for a fee of USD 141.36.

     

    Eligibility: India, Indonesia, Kenya, Philippines, South Africa, Thailand, Vietnam.

    If you are a national of any of the above listed countries, you must meet one of the following conditions to apply:

    Issuing countryDocument required
    Australia, Canada, Japan, New Zealand, Republic of Korea and Singapore Residency permit valid for a minimum of 6 months
    EU member state: Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czech Republic, Cyprus, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden Residency permit valid for a minimum of 6 months OR any visa issued by an EU member state valid for a minimum of 6 months
    United Kingdom Residency permit valid for a minimum of 6 months OR any valid visa, including digital e‑visa valid for a minimum of 6 months
    United States of America Permanent residency card (Green card) OR visa valid for a minimum of 6 months

     


    How and where to apply for a pre-approved UAE visa

    Eligible nationals can apply for their UAE entry permit on emirates.com through Manage your booking.

    Visa extensions

    • For a visa extension, applicants original passport and ticket copy are required.
    • Visa extensions can be requested at the airport office only.


    Required applicant documents:

    1. Colour photograph of applicant meeting the following requirements:
      • Face and pose: Look directly at the camera with your head centred in the frame. Your face should occupy most of the image.
      • Expression: Maintain a neutral, natural expression with your mouth closed. Do not smile broadly or show your teeth.
      • Eyes: Keep your eyes open and clearly visible, looking straight at the camera. Ensure no hair, shadows or eyewear frames obstruct your eyes.
      • Headwear and glasses: Do not wear hats or caps. Glasses are permitted only if the lenses are clear and free from glare or dark tint.
      • Lighting: Use even lighting from the front to avoid strong shadows on your face or the background.
      • Background and image quality: Use a plain, light-coloured background. The photo must be sharp, in focus and free from filters or colour effects.
    2. Coloured copy of the passenger’s passport external cover page.
    3. Colour copy of applicant's passport. The passport must be valid for at least six months.
    4. A confirmed hotel reservation for the duration of your stay in the UAE or a valid tenancy contract with your family member's name (father, mother, brother or sister) if staying with them on your visit.
    5. A copy of your return Emirates ticket, or codeshare flight that starts with the code “EK”, and the ticket number should start with “176”. The departure and return dates on the ticket must match the dates of your hotel reservation if staying in a hotel.
    6. Proof of relationship if applying for family member ‑ birth certificate, marriage certificate and family book.
    7. Additional documents may be required by the UAE authorities.

    Additional documents are required for visitors of some countries

    Additional documents may be required, depending on your country of residence. Please use our visa search tool above or visit our visa requirements page for more information.

    How and where to apply for your prearranged UAE visa

    Apply online

    Nationals of countries requiring an entry permit or prearranged UAE visa can now apply and pay for their visas online through Manage your booking.

    If you are travelling to or stopping over in Dubai with Emirates, you can submit an application and receive your UAE visa entirely online – without having to submit your passport for stamping. You can apply for your UAE visa through our Manage your booking after you book your Emirates flight. VFS processing fees apply. For more information please visit VFS Global

    Applicants' passports must have at least six months' validity as of their date of travel to Dubai, and must be machine readable passports – handwritten passports are not accepted.

    This service is only available to passengers with a confirmed Emirates booking where an Emirates ticket has already been issued and the flight itinerary already includes the desired time in Dubai. The itinerary into and out of Dubai should be on one ticket (PNR), and these flights must be Emirates flights. Note that any itineraries including any flights to or from Dubai on other carriers are not eligible for the online visa service.

    A minimum of four international working days prior to arrival in Dubai is required, however Emirates does recommend that online applications are made as early as possible.


    Transit visas

    48 hour and 96 hour transit visas are valid for travel with Emirates tickets outbound from Dubai.

    You can apply for a transit visa online through Manage your booking or at the Emirates Commercial Visa APT office at Dubai International airport Terminal 3.

    Sponsoring someone to visit the UAE

    Eligibility

    If you have a family member, near relative or friend who is a UAE resident, they can apply for a visa on your behalf at any of our ticketing offices in the UAE. Please note that this service is only available to residents of the UAE.


    Required visitor documents:

    1. Visa application form (available at all Emirates Ticketing Offices) in Arabic﻿ or English﻿ depending on the applicant’s nationality.
    2. Coloured copy of the passenger’s passport external cover page.
    3. Colour photograph of applicant meeting the following requirements:
      • Face and pose: Look directly at the camera with your head centred in the frame. Your face should occupy most of the image.
      • Expression: Maintain a neutral, natural expression with your mouth closed. Do not smile broadly or show your teeth.
      • Eyes: Keep your eyes open and clearly visible, looking straight at the camera. Ensure no hair, shadows or eyewear frames obstruct your eyes.
      • Headwear and glasses: Do not wear hats or caps. Glasses are permitted only if the lenses are clear and free from glare or dark tint.
      • Lighting: Use even lighting from the front to avoid strong shadows on your face or the background.
      • Background and image quality: Use a plain, light-coloured background. The photo must be sharp, in focus and free from filters or colour effects.
    4. Colour passport copy, valid for 6 months
    5. A copy of the passenger’s Emirates Airline ticket, or codeshare flight that starts with the code “EK”, and the ticket number should start with “176”.
    6. Birth certificate copy for minors (Arabic or English) is required.
    7. Proof of relationship is required.
    8. Tenancy contract (Ejari) under the sponsor’s name or hotel booking under the visitor’s name is required.
    9. Additional documents may be required by the UAE authorities.

    Terms and conditions

    1. Visitors must travel In & Out through Dubai on Emirates Airline. A copy of Emirates Airline ticket, or code share flight that starts with the code “EK”, and the ticket number that starts with “176”.
    2. Tourist Visas are valid for 60 days to enter Dubai and Transit Visas are valid for 30 days to enter Dubai.
    3. Visa processing time is approximately '2-4' working days.
    4. Visas fees are non refundable once application is submitted and processed.
    5. Visa fees must be paid upon application. 
    6. AED 50 will be deducted from the Tourist visa fee upon refund if the application is processed in the system, Sponsor requests to hold the visa application before submission or unable to provide more documents requested by the UAE Immigration or provides visa application with missing documents.
    7. Visa fees for Transit visa types are non-refundable in any case.
    8. A refundable deposit of AED 1,000 is required when sponsoring a friend or relative without proof. The sponsor must claim the deposit within 2 years from the application submission date.
    9. The sponsor is legally responsible for the visitor’s overstay fines.
    10. Visa Deposit fees are non-refundable if visitor overstayed or EK ticket misused (flown by other Airline).
    11. The Emirates ticket is non-refundable if the visa has been issued and utilized.
    12. Additional documents may be required by the UAE Authorities. Additional visa service fees may apply.
    13. Entry into the UAE is subject to Immigration approval.
    14. Failure to comply with all above Terms and Conditions, the visa deposit will be non-refundable.
