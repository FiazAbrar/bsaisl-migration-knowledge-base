Issuance of an Departure Permit

Start service

							This service allows the possibility of issuing an Departure permit for a foreigner within the country to enable him to leave the country.

Service Details

Requirements

														- Passport 
- Personal Photo
- Issuance of Birth Certificate for Newborns
- A travel ticket with a departure date within 7 days from the date of issuance 

Service Steps

														In case of issuing an exit permit for a newborn:
1- Personal presence of the father
2- Submit the application to the service officer
3- Pay the fees due
4- Receipt of the print of departure permit 

In case of lost passport:
 1- Personal presence 
2- Submit the application to the service officer
3- Pay the fees due
4- Receipt of the print of departure permit 

In case of issuing exit permits to violators and record of an eye-print:
1- Personal presence 
2- Submit the application to the service officer
3- Pay the fees due
4- Receipt of the print of departure permit 
In case of issuing an exit permit: type of (violator of an entry permit or a residency visa) 
For establishments, apply via

GDRFA website: 
1. Login to website
2. Register a new user account (in case there is no account)
3. Select the required service
4. Attach the required documents
5. Pay the fees due for the service
6. Submit the application
For individuals, apply through

AMER Centre
1. Head to one of AMER service centres
2. Select the required service at the receptionist
3. Submit the required documents to the service employee
4. Verification of a Document 
5. Pay the fees due for the service
6. Submit the application

Fees

														AED 241.

Terms And Conditions

														- Departure from the country within 7 days from the date of permit issuance.
- The regular exit permit requires the payment of all outstanding fines.
- If the individual does not depart within the validity period of the exit permit, the previous permit must be cancelled and a new application must be submitted.
- In exceptional cases involving the loss of a passport or travel document, a police report may be filed, and a travel pass can be issued by the beneficiary's consulate. The individual may then proceed with the required procedures to obtain the exit permit.   

Service Availability Channels

														Public Services Section – Al Aweer
Airport Service Center Terminal 3.
AMER Centers
 -Hatta land port

Expected Completion Time

														48.0 Hour(s)

Additional Information

														1- Financial fines to be paid, and then an Departure Permit is issued to leave the country. However, in case of non-payment of the financial fines, an IRIS is required with permanent deprivation, and then the departure permit is issued.
