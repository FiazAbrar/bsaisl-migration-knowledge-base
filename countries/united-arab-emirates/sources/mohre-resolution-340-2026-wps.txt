Summary

Ministerial Resolution No. 340 of 2026 is the current Wage Protection System (WPS)
instrument of the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation. Its text is not published on
the federal legislation portal; the official position below is taken from the UAE Government
portal page on payment of wages, last updated 14 October 2026, which states that wages
are paid in accordance with Ministerial Resolution No. 340 of 2026 and sets out the timetable
it imposes. It supersedes Ministerial Resolution No. 598 of 2022.

Establishments registered with the Ministry must pay wages on the due date through WPS,
that is through banks, exchange houses or financial institutions authorised by the Central
Bank. Salaries for the previous month fall due on the first day of each Gregorian month, and
an employer must transfer at least 85% of total wages due on time, allowing for lawful
deductions. Payment is in UAE dirhams unless the contract agrees another currency. The
Labour Law sets no minimum wage, though it requires wages sufficient to meet basic needs.

The escalation runs from the due date. From day one every establishment is monitored
electronically. On day two non-compliant establishments receive notifications. On day five
new work permits are suspended with a warning to pay. On day 11 an administrative fine
applies under Cabinet Resolution No. 21 of 2020 and, for a repeat violation within six months,
the establishment is reclassified to the third category under Ministerial Resolution No. 209 of
2022. On day 16 individual or collective labour disputes are registered automatically for the
affected workers and permits are suspended for large employers of 25 or more workers in
listed sectors or commonly owned groups meeting that threshold. On day 21 an establishment
with fewer than 50 workers faces an executive instrument for payment and one with 50 or
more faces collective dispute procedures; precautionary attachment, a travel ban on the
person in charge and referral to the Public Prosecution for repeat violations within two
consecutive months also apply.

Excluded employees are those with wage complaints before the courts, reported absconders,
those whose liberty is restricted, those on documented unpaid leave, seafarers on request,

Who it applies to

Every establishment registered with the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation and
the workers it employs, which covers mainland and non-financial free zone employers.
Excluded: nationals' fishing boats and public taxis, banks and financial institutions, houses of
worship, and listed worker categories such as absconders, workers on documented unpaid
leave and seafarers.

Obligations and deadlines

  Pay the previous month's wages through WPS by the first day of each Gregorian month.
  Transfer at least 85% of total wages due on time, documenting any lawful deduction.
  Clear any shortfall within five days, after which new work permits are suspended.
  Avoid a repeat default within six months, which brings fines and third-category reclassification.
  Notify the Ministry of unpaid leave and other exemptions with supporting documents.
  Keep WPS records and evidence of payment available for Ministry monitoring.
