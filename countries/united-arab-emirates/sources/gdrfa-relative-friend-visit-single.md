### Issuing a visit visa for a relative or friend for one entry

Search in Services

[Start service](https://smart.gdrfad.gov.ae/SmartChannels_Th/Login.aspx?Lang=ar-AE)

The foreigner is granted a single-entry visa for the purpose of visiting a relative or friend without a sponsor / host in UAE for a period of (30-60-90) days.\

Service Details

Requirements

1\. Two (2) personal photo.\
2\. A copy of the passport, and its validity should not be less than 6 months.\
3.Marriage contract.\
4\. Birth certificate.\
5\. Salary certificate for governmental and semi-governmental entities and free zones / employment contract for private companies / partnership contract for the partner.\
\
Identity card for the country of origin for some nationalities (Iraq - Pakistan - Iran - Afghanistan).\

Service Steps

Digital channels (GDRFA website / smart application):\
1\. Login to the smart services system using (UAE pass or username).\
2\. Search for your required service.\
3\. Fill out the application information where applicable.\
4\. Pay the service fees (if any).\
\
Customer Happiness Center:\
1- Visit the nearest customer happiness center.\
2- Obtain an automatic queue ticket.\
3- Submit the application fulfilling all conditions and documents (if any) to the customer service employee.\
4- Pay the service fees (if any).\
AMER Service Center:\
1\. Visit the nearest customer happiness center.\
2\. Get an automated queue ticket.\
3\. Submit the application fulfilling all conditions and documents (if any) to the customer service employee.\
\
Pay the service fees (if any).\

Fees

\- 30-day visit visa fee: AED 200.\
\- 60-day visit visa fee: AED 300.\
\- 90-day visit visa fee: AED 400.\
\- In addition to value added tax (5%).\
\- Financial security deposit: security deposit amount: AED 1000.\
\- Security deposit service fee: AED 20.\
\- Security deposit collection and reimbursement fee: AED 40.\
\- Medical insurance: 30-day visit visa insurance: AED 40.\
\- 60-day visit visa insurance: AED 60.\
\- 90-day visit visa insurance: AED 90.\
\- Additional fees (if the sponsored person is inside UAE):\
Knowledge Dirham: AED 10.\
Innovation Dirham: AED 10.\
Fee inside UAE: AED 500.\
\
Note: The total amount of the visa may vary more or less depending on the circumstances surrounding the sponsored person or any other reasons that may require the same.\

Terms And Conditions

1- The visitor should be a relative or friend of a citizen or foreigner residing in UAE.\
2\. Attach proof of relationship and justifications for the visit.\
3\. payment of the financial security deposit determined according to the purpose.\
4\. If the purpose of entry is for the foreign wife to visit her husband who is a citizen of the Gulf Cooperation Council state, she should be licensed to have a valid residence permit in the country whose nationality the husband holds.\
