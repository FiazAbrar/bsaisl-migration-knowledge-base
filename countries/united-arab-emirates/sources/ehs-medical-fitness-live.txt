Examination of Medical Fitness for Residency Visa

Share

Print

Download
Start Service 
This service enables citizens to obtain a medical fitness certificate required for residency in the country. The certificate confirms that the holder is free from communicable diseases in accordance with approved standards and legislations. It is an essential document for obtaining a new residency permit or renewing an existing one.
Service Process
1
 log in with your UAE PASS account.
2
 Submit the request
3
 Pay fees if required.
4
 Arrive at the center 20 minutes before the appointment.
5
 Perform the necessary laboratory tests and Radiology examinations as needed
6
 Send the electronic report to the customer
Conditions & Requirements
Applicants must be 18 years or older.
Valid Emirates ID or Passport
FAQ’s
Where are the medical examination centers available for new residency visas?

The Service is available at:
Emirates of Dubai: Al Nahda Center, Salah El Deen Center, Ibn Battuta Center, Dragon Mart 2 Center, Al Baraha Smart Center, Al Refaa Center.
Emirate of Sharjah: Sahara Center, Al Khibra & Al Daqqa Center (Al Sajaa), Al Taj Smart Center, Al Shurooq Center, Weqa Center, Al Khibrah Center
Emirate of Ajman: Musheirif Center, Al Nuameia Center
Emirate of RAK: Dahan Center, RAKEZ Center
Emirate of Fujairah: Mena Tower Center, Al Amal Center
Emirate of UAQ: Al Madar Center
 Service completion duration
24 Hours
 Service Fees
Category A "Employees, companies, and workers":
250 AED for medical examination + printing fees for the center
Category B "Private Drivers, Nursery Supervisors, and Domestic Help (including nannies, cleaners, and similar roles):
350 AED (includes 250 AED for the medical examination, 50 AED for vaccination (if the application is new), 50 AED for prengnacy test) + printing fees for the center
Category C "Workers in Beauty Salons, Barbershops, Health Resorts, Gyms, and Health Facilities":
300 AED (includes 250 for the medical examination, 50 AED for vaccination (if the application is new)) + printing fees for the center.

Pre- Employment Medical Examination
This service allows a job applicant to submit a request to obtain a Work Medical Fitness Certificate, which includes undergoing comprehensive medical examinations to ensure their health suitability for performing job duties and to safeguard their safety in the work environment.

This services enables to students enrolled in universities in the United Arab Emirates and abroad, enabling them to request a medical fitness certificate after undergoing a medical examination that includes a risk assessment and other laboratory tests.
