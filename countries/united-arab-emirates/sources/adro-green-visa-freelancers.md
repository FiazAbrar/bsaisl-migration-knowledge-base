## Freelancers

- ![](/-/media/Project/Expat-Bureau/Expat-Bureau/ADRO1_5/Visas/Visa-Icon-1.svg)

  ###### Duration

  5 years

- ![](/-/media/Project/Expat-Bureau/Expat-Bureau/ADRO1_5/Visas/Visa-Icon-2.svg)

  ###### Criteria

  You are eligible if you fulfil the following criteria:

  - Have obtained a freelance work permit from the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratization (MOHRE).
  - Your minimum educational level is a bachelor's degree, a specialized diploma or equivalent.
  - Your annual income from self-employment for the previous two years is not less than AED 360,000 or its equivalent in foreign currencies 

  **Or**

  You must provide proof of your financial solvency throughout the duration of residency in the UAE.

- ![](/-/media/Project/Expat-Bureau/Expat-Bureau/ADRO1_5/Visas/Visa-Icon-3.svg)

  ###### Requirements

  To apply for the Freelance Visa, you will need to provide:

   

  - A freelance work permit by the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratization (MOHRE).
  - Proof of annual income of AED 360,000 for the previous two years 

  **Or**

  A proof of financial solvency during your residence period.

   

- ![](/-/media/Project/Expat-Bureau/Expat-Bureau/ADRO1_5/Visas/Visa-Icon-4.svg)

  ###### How to Apply

  Submitting a nomination request for this category is currently unavailable through Abu Dhabi Residents Office.
