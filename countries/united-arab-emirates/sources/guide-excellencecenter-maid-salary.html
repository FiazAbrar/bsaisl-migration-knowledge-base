Many employers ask about the minimum salary for housemaid in UAE, but it’s important to clarify how salaries are regulated.

There is no single nationwide minimum wage for domestic workers. Instead, minimum salaries are guided by bilateral agreements between the UAE and workers’ home countries, as well as MOHRE-approved Tadbeer standards.

Below are the approximate minimum salaries for maids based on their nationality:

  • Filipino Maids: AED 1,500 – AED 1,800
  • Indian Maids: AED 1,000 – AED 1,500
  • African Maids: AED 850 – AED 1,200
  • Myanmmar Maids: AED 800 – AED 1,100
  • Nepalese Maids: AED 900 – AED 1,300
  • Indonesian Maids: AED 1,200 – AED 1,500

In addition to nationality-based salaries, Tadbeer centers also classify wages based on the job role required by the family. Additional Costs and Benefits for Maids in Dubai

Apart from the salary, employers must cover various expenses for their domestic workers, including:

  • Accommodation & Food: Either provided at home or compensated with an additional allowance.
  • Medical Insurance: Mandatory for all domestic workers in Dubai.
  • Annual Leave & Return Ticket: Employers must provide at least one round-trip ticket to the maid’s home country every two years.
  • Residency Visa & Work Permit: These costs range from AED 5,000 to AED 7,000 annually

How to Hire a Maid in Dubai (Legal Options Explained)

Hiring a maid in Dubai must be done through approved and legal channels to ensure compliance with UAE regulations and protection for both the employer and the domestic worker. Below are the main legal ways families can hire a maid in Dubai.

1. Hiring Through a Tadbeer Center (Recommended)

Tadbeer centers are government-approved entities regulated by the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE). Hiring through a Tadbeer center is the safest and most structured way to employ maids in Dubai.

When hiring through Tadbeer, families can choose between direct sponsorship or monthly Tadbeer packages, both fully compliant with UAE law. The process includes standardized contracts, regulated wages, medical insurance, and official documentation, ensuring transparency and legal protection for all parties.

At Excellence Center Tadbeer, we simplify the entire process. Depending on the package you choose, your maid will either be sponsored under your name or legally contracted and sponsored through Tadbeer. Visa processing, medical tests, insurance, and government requirements are handled professionally, with no hidden costs. Replacement options are also available based on package terms, giving families long-term peace of mind.

2. Hiring Through Online Platforms (Direct Selection)

Some families prefer to browse and select maids directly using online platforms such as MaidFinder.ae. These platforms allow employers to view profiles, compare experience, and communicate with potential maids.

While this method offers more flexibility in selection, employers remain responsible for completing all legal requirements, including visa sponsorship, medical tests, Emirates ID, and contract registration, unless supported by a licensed Tadbeer center.

3. Private Hiring Through Recruitment Agencies

Traditional maid service agencies in Dubai also offer recruitment services. These agencies typically manage sourcing and placement but may involve additional service fees. Employers should always verify that the agency operates within UAE regulations and that all employment documentation is processed legally.

  • Private hiring: More control, higher responsibility
