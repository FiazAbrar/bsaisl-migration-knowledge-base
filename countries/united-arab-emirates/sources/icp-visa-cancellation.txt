Visa Cancellation

About this service

A service through which an entry permit is cancelled

Service Channels

ICP Smart Services Website - Websites

ICP Smart App - Applications

Please select the appropriate category to view the terms and conditions, fees, and the list of required documents. 

 Visitor
 Tourism establishments authorized to issue visit visas for tourism purposes
 Licensed healthcare facilities in the UAE to issue entry visas for treatment or patient companions
 Government, private, or free zone entities for entry visa issuance for a work mission Or Residency
 Licensed universities, institutes, or research entities for study, training, or qualification visa

Terms and Conditions
 Valid Passport for over 6 months
 A fine of AED 50 will be imposed for each day of overstay inside the UAE after the entry permit has expired or been cancelled, starting from the day following the end of the grace period applicable to the specific type of entry permit.
 A request to cancel the tourist entry permit may not be submitted after the beneficiary has entered the country, unless the cancellation is linked to a status‑adjustment application. In this case, once the status‑adjustment request is submitted, the visa status will update to “settled/paid,” and a new visa will be issued for the beneficiary.
 Cancellation of the visa is permitted only if the applicant has not entered the country, and the request must be submitted before the end of the visa’s validity period prior to entry.
