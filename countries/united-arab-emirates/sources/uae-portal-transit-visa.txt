Transit visa

The UAE issues two types of transit visas: one for 48 hours, and another for 96 hours. Transit visas are sponsored by the UAE-based airlines and must be processed and approved before entering the UAE

Who needs a transit visa?

If you are not eligible for a 
visa on arrival or a visa-free entry
 to the UAE, and intend to step out of the airport, then you need a pre-arranged transit visa.

Where to apply for a transit visa?

You need to apply to: 

the 
UAE-based airlines
 you are travelling on

a travel agency or

airport hotel.

Duration of transit visa

A transit visa is issued for either 48 or 96 hours. It is a single-entry visa and cannot be extended.
