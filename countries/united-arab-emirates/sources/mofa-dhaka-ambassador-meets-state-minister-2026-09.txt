 H.E. Abdulla Ali AlHmoudi, Ambassador of the United Arab Emirates to Bangladesh Meets H.E. Humaiun Kobir, State Minister for Foreign Affairs

Mon 14/9/2026

                    H.E. Abdulla Ali AlHmoudi, Ambassador of the United Arab Emirates to Bangladesh, met with State Minister for Foreign Affairs H.E. Humaiun Kobir. The two sides discussed matters of mutual interest and explored opportunities to further strengthen bilateral relations and enhance cooperation between the UAE and Bangladesh. 

The meeting underscored the growing strength of Bangladesh-UAE relations, characterized by strategic alignment, mutual trust, and expanding cooperation. Discussions between H.E. Humaiun Kobir and H.E. Abdulla Ali AlHmoudi highlighted the deepening partnership between the two countries across diplomatic and economic spheres. The engagement also reflected Bangladesh’s solidarity with the UAE amid regional challenges, further reinforcing mutual trust and coordination in international forums, including the United Nations.
