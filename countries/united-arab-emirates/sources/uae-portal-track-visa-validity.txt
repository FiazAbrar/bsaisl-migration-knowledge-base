Track visa and check validity

For the emirates of Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah, 
check the status of your visa application and its validity
 on the website of Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security - ICP. For the emirate of Dubai, 
check the status of your visa application and its validity
 on the website of General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs – Dubai (DNRD).

Track visa application

If you applied for a visa to Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah 
track your visa application
 on the website of 
Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP)
.

If you applied to Dubai, 
track your visa application
 through General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs-Dubai.

Check visa validity (issuance and expiry dates)

If you already have an entry permit/residence visa to Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah 
check your visa validity
 on the website of 
Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security- ICP
.

If you already have an entry permit/residence visa in Dubai, 
check your visa validity
 through the website of General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs - Dubai.

Alternatively, for any enquiry regarding visa issuance, types and validity, communicate with 
ICP
 at the toll-free number 600522222. 

For Dubai visas, contact GDRFA on the toll-free number 800 5111 if you are in the UAE. If you are outside the UAE, call on +9714-313-9999.

For more information, please contact:

Federal Authority for Identity and Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP)

General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs
 – Dubai
