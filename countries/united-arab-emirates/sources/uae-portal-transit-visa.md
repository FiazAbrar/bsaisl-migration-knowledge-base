### Who needs a transit visa?

If you are not eligible for a [visa on arrival or a visa-free entry](https://www.visitdubai.com/en/plan-your-trip/visa-information) to the UAE, and intend to step out of the airport, then you need a pre-arranged transit visa.

### Where to apply for a transit visa?

You need to apply to: 

- the [UAE-based airlines](/en/information-and-services/visiting-and-exploring-the-uae/how-to-get-to-the-uae#getting-into-the-uae-by-air) you are travelling on
- a travel agency or
- airport hotel.

\

### Duration of transit visa

A transit visa is issued for either 48 or 96 hours. It is a single-entry visa and cannot be extended.
