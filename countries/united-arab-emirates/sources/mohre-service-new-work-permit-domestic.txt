Service Details
Issuance of a New Work Permit - Domestic Workers

Share

Print

Start Service 

Application Status 
A service provided by the Ministry to employers wishing to recruit a domestic worker from inside or outside the country.
Service Process
1
 Logging in: Login using UAE Pass through one of the approved channels
2
 Request Information Submission: Enter the required information, review the automatically-retrieved information, and attach the required documents
3
 Payment Process: Review the request and pay the required fees
4
 Domestic Worker Work Permit Issuance: The Domestic Worker Work Permit is issued upon completion of the request. You will be notified of any additional requirements if needed
Conditions & Requirements
The employer's passport must be valid
Valid original ID card of the citizen employer
The resident employer / investor must possess a valid residence permit
The proposed worker may not possess a valid work permit
The proposed worker's passport must be valid for at least three months
The worker must be 18 years of age or older
There is an age limit of 60 years for domestic workers. A domestic worker may be exempt from that rule if he / she is inside the country and his / her residence visa has been cancelled by the employer of "Domestic labor", provided that the termination date does not exceed three months.
The domestic worker must be covered by an adequate insurance policy, on the employer's file
The occupation assigned to the worker must meet the requirements of the occupation
 Permit Duration
Two years for UAE citizens, GCC citizens "Optional" (one - two years) for Diplomats and holders of golden visa - One year for residents
 Type of Employment Contracts
Full-time Employment
 Service completion duration
2 working days
 Service Fees
All Emirates except the Emirate of Dubai -Two years
All Emirates except the emirate of Dubai -One year
The Emirate of Dubai
Business Centers commission is capped at 72 dirhams
Except for federal charges, the service is free of charge via MOHRE's website and smart app.
*Please note that the fees shown above do not include tax and collection charges.

 Notes
The customer will be notified once the request has been completed. Applicants can follow up on their applications by:
Visiting the Inquiry Service at 
www.mohre.gov.ae
MOHRE smart app
Call Center 600590000
Chatbot service
Required Documents
A copy of the employer's passport, addition containing the unified number page of the citizen employer
Proof of marital status (marriage contract) and the resident visa for the spouse of the expatriate employer/investor
An expatriate employer or investor must provide proof of housing in the form of a lease contract or a title deed to the residence
A recent salary certificate from the expatriate employer, confirming that the family's income is not less than 25,000 dirhams
A bank statement of the investor/employer for the previous three months, provided that the monthly income is at least 25,000 dirhams
Copy of the domestic worker's passport
A color photograph of the domestic worker
A valid original ID card for GCC employers, residents, and investors, or a special identification card for diplomatic employees is required.
Valid original ID card of the citizen employer
Domestic worker's tourist visa if he / she is inside the country
Cancellation of the worker's previous visa, if the worker is inside the country
Employers employing more than five domestic workers must provide a written explanation outlining the reasons for hiring an additional worker
