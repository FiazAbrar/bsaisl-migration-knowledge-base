# Register / Renew Tenancy Contract

Register / Renew Tenancy Contract

Description

This service allows customers to register or renew a Tenancy contract in the Emirate of Dubai

Service Centre:\
Step 1: Visit nearest Real Estate Trustee Center, or property management company (if the property is managed by one)\
Step 2: Submit the required documents ensuring no key document is missing\
Step 3: An employee will review and approve the request through the system\
Step 4: Proceed to pay the service fees (if any) and collect the receipt\
Step 5: Receive the e-Contract Registration Certificate

Ejari System or Dubai REST:\
Step 1: Login to Ejari or App and select desired service\
Step 2: Fill in the required information and upload any necessary documents\
Step 3: Proceed to pay the service fees (if any)\
Step 4: An employee will review and approve the request through the system\
Step 5: Receive the e-Contract Registration Certificate by email

via the App:\
    1- Copy of the Unified Tenancy Contract (for individuals and companies only)\
via Real Estate Service Trustees Centers:\
    1- Original Unified Tenancy Contract\
    2- Presentation of the Emirates ID of the applicant\
    3- Presentation of the official Power of Attorney if the applicant is a representative:\
         - If issued in Dubai, the number can be entered without attaching the document\
         - If issued in another emirate, the document must be attached

via the Dubai REST App / the Department's website

1)AED 100 for registering a tenancy contract \
2) AED 10 knowledge fee \
3) AED 10 innovation fee\
4) In addition, the service partners’ fees of AED 55 plus a value-added tax (VAT) on the service partner fee of AED 2.75\

Total: 177.75 AED

via Real Estate Services Trustee Centers

1)AED 100 for registering a tenancy contract \
2) AED 10 knowledge fee \
3) AED 10 innovation fee\
4) In addition, the service partners’ fees are AED 95 + VAT for the service partners’ fees

Total: 220 AED

 

via real estate service trustees: 25 minutes (excluding waiting time)

Registration through Real Estate Trustee Offices:\
    1- The property must not be managed by a real estate company or by an owner who has Ejari system access.\
     2- The applicant must be the tenant or a legal representative with an official power of attorney.\
     3- The landlord must be the owner of the property or a legal representative with an official power of attorney.

\
Registration through Mobile Applications:\
     1- The tenant and landlord must be individuals, and the owner’s data must be up to date.

\
Registration through the System:\
Companies:\
     1- Must be licensed for one of the property management activities as per regulations.

Individual Owners (as themselves):\
     1- Must be the owner of the property and manage their own properties.

Representatives of Individual Owners:\
     1- Must have a legal power of attorney authorizing them to manage the properties.
