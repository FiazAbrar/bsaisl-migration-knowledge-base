## E-Passport Issue/ Reissue:

Content: Pages

E-passport Reissue

If your e-passport has less than one year validity or it is expired or lost or you need data change/correction, you can apply for e-passport reissue. The process is same as applying for a new e-passport. So, you need to apply online, schedule appointment and come in-person for bio-metric enrollment. In e-passport system, bio-metric enrollment is mandatory every time, even while applying for reissue.

Please read carefully all the instructions in this page before going to fill-in and submit the online application form. The link of the E-passport Online Registration Portal is given at the end of this page.

**Who can apply for e-passport?**

**To apply for e-passport, you must have either a Bangladeshi NID card or a Birth Registration Certificate (BRC) having a 17 digit number which is verifiable at** [everify.bdris.gov.bd](https://everify.bdris.gov.bd/). In case of BRC, please make sure that the webpage having your verified BRC contains the necessary data, e.g. names of the applicant and the parents in English and they match with the corresponding data in your application. If they do not match, please contact the office from which the BRC was issued to do the necessary correction

The applicants with Bangladeshi passport having more than one year validity can't apply unless proved by a supporting document justifying the legitimate necessity.

**Submission of Application and Enrollment**

While filling-in the online application, you need to choose ABU DHABI mission as your passport office. Your present address must be an address in the UAE and permanent address in Bangladesh.

It is mandatory to appear in-person for bio-metric enrollment after online submission of the application

After submission of your online application, if you find that certain data need to be corrected, please do not worry. Please correct the printout with pen. The official of the Embassy will make necessary changes to your application during bio-metric enrollment at the Embassy. Before completing your enrollment, you will be shown the draft delivery slip. You can sign the delivery slip after checking all your information.

**Once you arrive at the Embassy for submit your application to the counter of Passport & Visa Wing, it may take up to 2 to 3 hours before getting your turn to do the bio-metrics as there are six steps in the process of enrollment prior to capturing bio-metrics and most of the applicants come without the required documents; hence the concerned officials need to spend a lot of time helping those applicants prepare their application package.**

**Payment of Fees**

While filling-in online application, please select 'Offline Payment'. Only cash payment is possible to deposit fees in cash counter (counter -5) in the embassy of Bangladesh. If you want to upgrade delivery type from 'regular' to 'express', please bring the required amount for the 'express' delivery. On the contrary, those who have submitted online application for 'express' delivery are not allowed to change it to 'regular' option.

To avail discounted fee applicable for students, the applicant must bring at the time of enrollment a copy of Student ID Card and Emirates ID or any other official/school document that proves the full-time, active studentship status of the applicant.

Please note that as per Section 14(e) of Wage Earners' Welfare Board Act, 2018, all consular fees of Bangladesh missions abroad must include 10% surcharge to contribute to the Wage Earners' Welfare Fund.

**NO REFUNDS:** Fees, once received by the Embassy, cannot be refunded, even if the application is withdrawn or service cannot be rendered.

**Processing time for issuance of e-passport**:

Express delivery: 15 working days;

Regular delivery: 30 working days

(NB: Processing time may vary depending on the merit of individual cases. The applications having data mismatch among their different documents often take longer than the stipulated time)

**List of Required Documents:**

**While coming to the Embassy for bio-metric enrollment, please do not forget to bring all the required documents including**

1.  Application Summary, i.e. confirmation page of the online submission having barcode (Please make sure it shows "Application Submitted" on the top left corner of the page). If the application is not submitted, it will not be available in the server of the Embassy for enrollment.
2.  Complete application that has been submitted online,
3.  Required Fees as cash,
4.  most recent original Bangladeshi passport and its photocopy (if lost, GD/police report containing the number of the lost passport),
5.  photocopy of Bangladeshi NID (both front and back parts on one page) or photocopy of the Birth Registration Certificate (BRC) as well as a copy of the webpage with BRC data verified at [everify.bdris.gov.bd](http://everify.bdris.gov.bd/),
6.  photocopy of legal status in the UAE, e.g. valid visa, resident card,
7.  photocopy of marriage/divorce registration certificate (if applicable),
8.  if there is any change in data from the most recent Bangladeshi [MRP](https://washington.mofa.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6952669435ce18e1c05aa2d8)/E-passport, please submit relevant supporting documents as per this Circular: [https://file.mofa.gov.bd/uploads/fc62c7e3-a682-44ed-bb28-99e741c6cd53//639/abe/4af/639abe4af3075360326896.pdf](https://file.mofa.gov.bd/uploads/fc62c7e3-a682-44ed-bb28-99e741c6cd53/639/abe/4af/639abe4af3075360326896.pdf)
9.  any other documents that might be applicable to you as per the following link: [https://www.epassport.gov.bd/instructions/instructions](https://www.epassport.gov.bd/instructions/instructions)

**Delivery**

**Applicant can get delivery from delivery counter no 1in the embassy.it is mandatory to bring old passport and delivery slip at the time of delivery.**

**If applicant is sick or unable to reach in the embassy ,he may send authorised person with document (application,old passport,delivery slip and bearer Emirates ID)**

**HOME DELIVERY:**

**PLEASE CONTACT WITH COURIER SERVICE “TAWZEA” AT COUNTER NUMBER 06 IN THE BANGLADESH EMBASSY PREMISES.IT WILL COST 15 AED TO DELIVER PASSPORT WITHIN THE TERRITORY OF WHOLE UAE.**

**Tracking the Status**

At every stage of the process, you can check the status of your application at [**https://www.epassport.gov.bd/authorization/application-status**](https://www.epassport.gov.bd/authorization/application-status)
