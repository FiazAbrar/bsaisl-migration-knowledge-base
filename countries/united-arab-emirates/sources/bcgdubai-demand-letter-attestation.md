## Demand Letter Attestation

Any UAE-based company or firm wishing to recruit Bangladeshi nationals is required to submit the demand letter to the Consulate General for attestation. The Consulate General will review the documents and facilitate the attestation process if all requirements are met.

- An application addressed to the Consul General
- Demand letter on company letterhead
- Letter of Authorization
- Copy of the Agreement
- Contract Agreement (Offer Letter)
- Guarantee Letter
- Company License & Recruiting Agency’s License
- Any other supporting documents

**NOTE:** Sponsor/ Partner/ HR Manager/ PRO of the respective company must be physically present at the Consulate during document submission, along with an authorization letter from the company.

**Fee:** AED 221 \[Non-Refundable\]

**Payment Mode:** Cash only

Payment will be received once the documents are approved by the respective Consular Officer.

Please submit all required documents at the **Room No. 14** of the Consulate General of Bangladesh, Dubai.
