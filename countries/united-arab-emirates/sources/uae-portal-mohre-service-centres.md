### MOHRE service centres

Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MoHRE) approved the following centres to provide its services to workers and employers:

- [Business services and guidance centres](https://www.mohre.gov.ae/en/services/approved-services-centers/business-services-and-guidance-centers)
- [Domestic workers’ services centres](https://www.mohre.gov.ae/en/services/approved-services-centers/domestic-workers-services-centers)
- [Tawseel service providers.](https://www.mohre.gov.ae/en/services/approved-services-centers/tawseel-service-providers)
