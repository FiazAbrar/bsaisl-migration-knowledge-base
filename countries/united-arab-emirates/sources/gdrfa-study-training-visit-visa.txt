Issuing a visit visa for the purpose of study, training or rehabilitation

Search in Services

Start service

							The foreigner is granted a visit visa for a single entry for the purpose of study, training, rehabilitation, or similar to that for a period of (30-60-180) days. In this case, the sponsor / host should be one of the licensed universities, institutes, educational or research institutions.

Service Details

Requirements

														1. One personal photo.
2. A copy of the passport, and its validity should not be less than 6 months.
3. A letter from the educational entity.

Service Steps

														The digital channels (GDRFA website / smart application):
1. Login to the smart services system using (UAE pass or username).
2. Search for your required service.
3. Fill out the application information where applicable.
4. Pay the service fees (if any).

Fees

														-          30-day visit visa fee: AED 200.
   -          60-day visit visa fee: AED 300.
   -          180-day visit visa fee: AED 400.
   -          In addition to value added tax (5%).
   -          Financial security deposit:  security deposit amount: AED 1000.
   -          Security deposit service fee: AED 20. 
   -          Security deposit collection and reimbursement fee: AED 40.
   -           Additional fees (if the sponsored person is inside UAE):
   Knowledge Dirham: AED 10.
   Innovation Dirham: AED 10.
   Fee inside UAE: AED 500. 
 Note: The total amount of the visa may vary more or less depending on the circumstances surrounding the sponsored person or any other reasons that may require the same.

Terms And Conditions

														1- Submitting a letter from the host entity including the study or training program and its duration.

Service Availability Channels

														The digital channels (GDRFA website / smart application):
The service is available 24/7

Expected Completion Time

														48.0 Hour(s)

Additional Information

														Not Applicant
