### Visa fees

**Visa service fees**

Visa charges are stated on each service card on the websites of [ICP](https://icp.gov.ae/en/services/) and [GDRFA-D](https://www.gdrfad.gov.ae/en/services/72f0054f-668b-11e8-78de-0cc47ada4ec1).

![visa charges](https://assets.u.ae/api/public/content/a81a947b93f04d4ab2e51692fbcdb00f?v=179b4bf0) 

**Overstaying fines**

There is a fine on overstaying. [Check fines here](https://www.gdrfad.gov.ae/en/fines-inquiry-service).
