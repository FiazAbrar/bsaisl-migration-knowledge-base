Issuance of a residence permit for foreigner family members

Search in Services

Start service

							Both male and female foreign residents are allowed to bring their family members into the country, including the husband and any children under 25 or unmarried daughters. Following the regulations, children with special needs may also be brought in regardless of age. The family will reside where the guarantor/head of the family does for the duration of the agreement.

Service Details

Requirements

														1. A recent colour photograph with a white background for the sponsored person.
2. Copy of passport.
3. A decent medical examination approved by the concerned authorities if they are over (18) years old.

Service Steps

														Digital channels (website/smart application):
1. Log in to the smart services system through (UAE Pass or username).
2. Search for the service to be applied for.
3. Fill in the application data, where applicable.
4. Pay the service fee (if any).
Customer Happiness Centre:
1. Visit the nearest Customer Happiness Centre.
2. Get the automated turn ticket and wait.
3. Submitting the application that fulfils all conditions and documents (if any) to the customer service employee.
4. Pay the service fee (if any).
  Amer Service Centre:
1. Visit the nearest  Amer Service Centre.
2. Get the automated turn ticket and wait.
3. Submitting the application that fulfils all conditions and documents (if any) to the customer service employee.
4. Pay the service fee (if any).

• In case there are no missing documents in the application, the customer will be notified of the approval  and the approved application will be sent by e-mail.

Fees

														Residence permit fee: AED200 

Extra charge :
Knowledge Dirham: AED10 
Innovation Dirham: AED10 
Fee inside the country: AED500 

Delivery:
AED20 

Note The issuance fee increases by AED100 annually whenever the residency is over two years.

Terms And Conditions

														1. The Dubai Future Foundation in Dubai nominates this category.
2. The entrepreneur must have a pioneering project registered with the Ministry of Economy or the competent local authorities with an income of (1 million Dirhams annually).
3. Providing suitable housing for family members.
4. Proof of kinship.

Service Availability Channels

														Digital channels (website/smart application):
The service is available 24/7
Amer Service Centre:
The service is available during the normal operating hours of AMER Centers

Expected Completion Time

														48.0 Hour(s)

Additional Information

														The grace period to stay inside the country after the expiration of the residence permit or its cancellation is (60) days.
