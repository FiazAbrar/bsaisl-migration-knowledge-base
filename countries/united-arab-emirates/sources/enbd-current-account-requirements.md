## Key features

- Available in AED
- Free debit card
- Cheque book facility
- Quick account opening process
- Up to 10 free teller transactions a month (based on your banking package)
- Salaried, non-salaried and self-employed individuals can apply
- A minimum salary transfer of  5,000 for payroll customers or a minimum balance of  3,000 for non-salaried customers

No account opening fees

## Requirements and documents for opening an account

Expats

- **Identification**  - Original Emirates ID and copy
  - Copy of passport
- **In case of a salary transfer account, we'll need one of the following documents:**  - Salary transfer letter (not more than 30 days from date of issue for non-government companies. For government companies, validity as mentioned in the letter.)
  - Copy of appointment letter or contract
  - Copy of pay slip (less than a month old)
  - Statement with previous month's salary
  - **For bulk accounts:** consolidated letter from HR (with salary of each employee mentioned)
- **Additional documents**
  - In some cases, additional documents may be requested in order to proceed with opening an account
