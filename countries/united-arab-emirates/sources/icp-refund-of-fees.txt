Refund of Fees

About this service

A service through which one can request a refund of issuance fees if the service is not completed.

Service Channels

ICP Smart Services Website - Websites

ICP Smart App - Applications

Please select the appropriate category to view the terms and conditions, fees, and the list of required documents. 

 UAE Citizen
 Resident
 GCC Citizen
 Visitor

Terms and Conditions
 Only issuance fees are refundable. Application submission fees and smart services fees are non-refundable
 Refundable fees are specified in the service receipt

List of Required Documents for Service
Certificate from the bank indicating the account number and the customer's IBAN.

Related Services

 Refund of fees for approved applications 
 A service through which financial guarantees collected for approved applications, if any, are refunded.

 Start Service  

 Payment of visa or residence violation fine 
 A service through which fines imposed on violators of entry and residence laws in the United Arab Emirates, specifically visa and residence violators, are paid.

 Start Service  

 Collection of exit fees at land and sea ports 
 This service enables residents and visitors of the United Arab Emirates to pay exit fees when departing the country through land or sea ports.
