General information about attestation

  • MoFA Website: www.mofa.gov.ae 
  • Smart Mobile App: (UAE MOFA)
  • UAE Embassies and Consulates abroad
  • People of Determination and Senior Emiratis & Citizens can attest their documents by calling +97180044444
  • We also accept death certificate attestation requests by calling +97180044444
Anyone can attest documents on your behalf.
  • To attest your documents in the UAE, please check our guide for the step by step process: https://www.mofa.gov.ae/-/media/Service/Attestation-Guide/En.pdf 
  • When abroad, you can attest documents at the closest UAE Embassy or Consulate. Each Embassy or Consulate may have its own procedures in place. For additional details, please visit the Embassy’s or the Consulate’s website: www.mofa.gov.ae/en/missions/uae-missions-Abroad
  • Original document in English or Arabic (or an official translation of it)
  • Document must be attested by the appropriate governing bodies prior to submission
  • Document must not be laminated
  • Individual Affairs Documents: includes but not limited to: educational documents (certificate & transcripts are treated as two different documents) - birth certificates - death certificate - medical reports - good conduct certificate - marriage certificate, court-issued certificates – power of attorney of personal nature - to may it concern letters - employment contract - employment offer - bank statements - personal tax certificate – etc
  • Commercial Documents: includes but not limited to: contract or agreement – commercial registration - appointment of a manager or a director – minutes of meeting of board of directors or partners – company change of name – brand registration - distribution agreement - corporate insurance certificate - corporate tax certificate - board of directors decision - trade license – partnership or shareholder certificate - annexes and appendices in commercial documents – memorandums – company closures – company branch registration – bankruptcy documents – financial statement – product registration – patent registration or waiver – trademark registration – power of attorney of commercial nature – general power of attorney - signature document – end-user certificate – tax certificate - completion certificate - any other document of commercial nature.
    Commercial contracts or agreements may be considered as multiple documents based on its content. Please reach out to our call centre if you have any doubts
  • Invoices and Certificate of Origins 
    Commercial documents with proof of sale of any items or products

The original document must be sent to MoFA by courier to complete the attestation process.

  • Direct debit
  • Credit cards (visa/master)
  • Samsung Pay
  • Apple Pay (will be activated by September 1, 2022)

Attestation service charges:

  • Individual Affairs Documents: AED 150 
  • Commercial Documents: AED 2,000 
  • Commercial invoices: based on invoice amount and the scale associated. For more details, please refer to the Attestation fees guide at: www.mofa.gov.ae/ar-ae/Services/Forms/attestations-guide

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs provides all its services electronically through its website and smartphone application only.

  • Ensure that your documents are not laminated. Laminated documents cannot be attested and will be rejected.
  • Make sure the appropriate governing bodies have attested your documents before submission. Missing attestations will be rejected. For documents issued within the UAE:
    • Original documents must be attested by the governing entity, except for digital documents issued by a government entity or documents attested electronically.
      Example: A birth certificate must be attested by the Ministry of Health & Prevention or the local health authority.
    • Documents must be attested by MoFA through the Ministry’s official website www.mofa.gov.ae or the UAE MOFA smart application.
    • Documents can then be attested by one of the UAE embassies or missions abroad.
    For documents issued outside the UAE:
    • Original documents must be attested by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the issuing country.
    • Documents can then be attested by the UAE Embassy or Consulate in the issuing country by applying through MoFA online channels.
    • Original documents must be attested in the UAE through the Ministry’s official website www.mofa.gov.ae or the UAE MOFA smart application.

Consular benefits for Golden Visa holders

Yes. Consular services may extend to the family members of the Golden Visa holder, such as the spouse and children, provided they are officially registered as dependents and hold valid residence permits.
Golden Visa holders can directly contact the MoFA’s Call Center through the dedicated hotline for Golden Visa holders at 0097124931133, available 24/7 to provide immediate support, assistance, and responses to inquiries while abroad.
The Ministry offers dedicated emergency and crisis support to Golden Visa holders with valid residency while traveling abroad. This includes coordination with UAE Missions in the respective countries to provide immediate assistance and necessary response, in addition to including Golden Visa holders in emergency and evacuation plans activated during crises and exceptional situations.
The UAE Mission provides full coordination and procedural support for repatriation. However, the financial costs are borne by the family of the deceased, in accordance with the approved policies.
Requests can be made by contacting the UAE Mission in the relevant country or by calling the dedicated hotline for Golden Visa holders at 00971 2 493 1133. The required documents include a copy of the deceased’s passport, a death certificate, and any relevant medical or official documents issued by the local authorities.
The Ministry of Foreign Affairs provides a dedicated 24/7 hotline for Golden Visa holders. They can contact the MoFA’s call center at 00971 2 493 1133 from outside the UAE (ensure the country code +971 is included when dialing internationally).

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs provides a dedicated suite of consular services designed to enhance the level of support and care for Golden Visa holders while they are abroad. These include the issuance of a Return Document in case of a lost or damaged passport, a hotline exclusively dedicated for Golden Visa holders, and extended assistance in emergencies, crises, and evacuation operations. The services also cover the coordination of procedures for the repatriation of the deceased Golden Visa holder’s remains to the UAE, in collaboration with the relevant health and official authorities, to facilitate and expedite the process and support the bereaved family.
The UAE Golden Visa is a long-term residence visa granted to specific categories of foreign residents or individuals wishing to come to the UAE. It aims to attract outstanding talents, investors, entrepreneurs, and exceptional professionals from around the world, allowing them to live, work, study, and invest in the UAE.
