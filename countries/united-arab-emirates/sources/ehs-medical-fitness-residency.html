                      Examination of Medical Fitness for Residency Visa

                      Share Print Download
                      Start Service

                      This service enables an expatriate to obtain a fitness certificate for to apply for a Residency Visa, to prove that he is free from communicable and infectious diseases, thereby allowing him/her to obtain a residence permit within the UAE or to renew his/her current residence through visiting one of the residency screening centers or some of the public health centers..

                      Service Process

                      • 1

                        log in with your UAE PASS account.

                      • 2

                        Book a medical appointment.

                      • 3

                        Pay fees if required.

                      • 4

                        Arrive at the center 20 minutes before the appointment and check in through the Customer Happiness Center.

                      • 5

                        Visit the radiology department for required X-ray

                      • 6

                        Evaluate and diagnose the case by the treating physician

                      • 7

                        Examination results will be sent to the applicant via email and SMS

                      Conditions & Requirements

                      1. Applicants must be 18 years or older.

                      2. In case of residence renewal, the Emirates ID is required.

                      3. Delegate cards are required for companies with more than 10 workers.

                      FAQ’s

                      Emirate of Sharjah: Sahara Center, Al Khibra & Al Daqqa Center (Al Sajaa), Al Taj Smart Center, Al Shurooq Center, Weqa Center, Al Khibrah Center

                      Emirate of Ajman: Musheirif Center, Al Nuameia Center

                      Emirate of RAK: Dahan Center, RAKEZ Center

                      Emirate of Fujairah: Mena Tower Center, Al Amal Center

                      Emirate of UAQ: Al Madar Center

                      Service completion duration

                      24 Hours to issue the Report & Result.

                      Service Fees

                      Category A "Employees, companies, and workers":

                      • 260 AED + service center fees

                      Category B " Private drivers, nannies, housekeepers, nursery supervisors, housemaids and their equivalents":

                      • 360 AED + service center fees

                      Category C " Workers in beauty parlor, saloons, fitness clubs, spa, and health care facilities":

                      • 310 AED + service center fees

                      Related Services

                      Pre-University Fitness Screening

                      This service is provided to students enrolled in universities in the United Arab Emirates and abroad, enabling them to request a medical fitness certificate after undergoing a medical examination that includes a risk assessment and other laboratory tests.

                      Start Service
                      Pre- Employment Medical Examination

                      This service allows a job candidate to submit a request to obtain a medical fitness certificate for employment purposes, where a comprehensive set of medical examinations is conducted to ensure the individual’s health and well-being in the workplace.
