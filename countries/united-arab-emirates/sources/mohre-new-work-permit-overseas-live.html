      Service Details

      Issuance of a New Work Permit - Overseas

      Share Print
      Start Service Application Status

      A service provided by the Ministry to establishments wishing to recruit or employ a worker from outside the country.

      Service Process

      1. 1

        Logging in: Login using UAE Pass through one of the approved channels

      2. 2

        Request Information Submission: Enter the required information, review the automatically-retrieved information, and attach the required documents

      3. 3

        Payment Process: Review the request and pay the required fees

      4. 4

        The Work Permit is issued upon completion of the request. You will be notified of any additional requirements if needed

      Conditions & Requirements

      1. The establishment must have an electronic quota available

      1. The Employee must be 18 years of age or older

      1. An employee in a specialized profession or any other profession that requires a license must meet the requirements stipulated in the applicable legislation.

      1. The establishment must maintain a valid license without any registered violations, resulting in suspension of its operations in accordance with applicable laws.

      1. The Work Permit application must be submitted by the authorized signatory of the establishment

      1. Candidate for employment may not possess an active work permit.

      Permit Duration

      Two Years

      Type of Employment Contracts

      Full Time / Part Time / Temporary Work / Flexible Work / Remote Work / Job Sharing

      Service completion duration

      2 working days

      Service Fees

      1. Federal Fees - Work Permit Application - Category (1),(2),(3): 50
      2. Issuance of work permit for two years - Category (1): 250
      3. Issuance of work permit for two years - Category (2): 1200
      4. Issuance of work permit for two years - Category (3): 3450
      5. The commission for Business Centers is capped at 72 dirhams per service provided as part of the permit package (which includes printing job offer, employment contracts, and permit applications)
      6. Except for federal fees, the service is free of charge via MOHRE's website and smart app.
      7. *Please note that the fees shown above do not include tax and collection charges.

      Service channels

      1. Service Center
      2. Website
      3. Mobile Application
      4. Tawseel

      Target audience

      1. Employers

      Department name

      Digital Services Department

      Category

      1. Employer Service

      Main Service

      Issue new work permit

      Service Type

      Procedural

      Sector Type

      1. Establishments Workers

      Relation of service with other entities

      1. Federal Authority for Identity and Citizenship, Customs and Ports Security

      User Guides

      • Please check the user guide (PDF) for using the system in order to apply for the service.
      • Download

      Notes

      The customer will be notified once the request has been completed. Applicants can follow up on their applications by:

      • Visiting the Inquiry Service at www.mohre.gov.ae
      • MOHRE smart app
      • Call Center 600590000
      • Chatbot service

      Required Documents

      1. Clear colored photo with a white background

      2. Passport copy with a validity period of at least six months.

      3. The official job offer form issued by the Ministry, signed by both the employer and employee.

      4. Academic Certificates: A clear certificate bearing the name of the employee, Skill levels (1 & 2) Bachelor Degree or higher is required. Skill Levels (3 & 4) Diploma graduate or higher is required. Skill Level (5) High School Certificate is required. There is no requirement for a certificate for skill levels 6 to 9. Workers who earn less than AED 4000 per month or who do not possess a degree are not considered skilled.

      5. Professional license issued by the competent authority, example: doctor, nurse, etc. (professional license issued by the Ministry of Health - Department of Health)/ Teacher, teacher assistant (Ministry of Education - Knowledge Authority (Dubai)- Abu Dhabi Education Council- Sharjah Education Council) Fitness Trainer (Youth and Sports Authority)/ Advocate (Ministry of Justice).

      6. Workers from the following nationalities (Pakistan, Afghanistan, Iraq, and Iran) need to provide a legible and clear copy of both sides of their national ID cards issued by their home countries, and make sure it is valid.
