# Dubai Taxi Company to recruit 6,000 drivers from Bangladesh this year

Bangladeshi workers given priority for strong work ethic, company representatives tell expatriates’ welfare minister

A delegation from Dubai Taxi Company has expressed interest in recruiting 6,000 drivers from Bangladesh this year, including 1,500 new recruits in the latest phase.

The delegation paid a courtesy call on Minister of Expatriates' Welfare and Overseas Employment Ariful Haque Choudhury today at his Dhaka office to discuss the recruitment plan.

During the meeting, the minister thanked the Dubai delegation for their continued interest in hiring skilled drivers from Bangladesh and expressed hope that this recruitment trend would continue in the coming days.

He also assured the delegation of full cooperation and necessary support from the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare and Overseas Employment throughout the entire process.

The minister requested the company to arrange salaries and allowances for the recruited drivers during their training period after their arrival in Dubai. He also urged the delegation to consider recruiting "white collar" workers from Bangladesh -- including doctors and nurses -- alongside drivers.

The Dubai Taxi Company representatives informed the meeting that around 15,000 workers from 27 countries are currently employed as taxi and limousine drivers in their company, of whom approximately 8,000 are Bangladeshis.

They noted that Bangladeshi workers are given priority in recruitment due to their strong work ethic. They also mentioned that Bangladeshi women are also successfully working as drivers at Dubai Airport.

The delegation further informed that a screening and interview process for eligible Bangladeshi drivers will begin from tomorrow at the Bangladesh-Korea Technical Training Centre.

State Minister for Expatriates' Welfare and Overseas Employment, Nurul Haque Nur, raised concerns over the high migration costs borne by Bangladeshi workers travelling to Dubai.

Noting that these workers are largely from low-income backgrounds who often take out loans to fund their migration, he made a special appeal to the delegation to bring the migration cost down to within Tk 1 lakh.

The meeting was attended by Ministry Secretary Md Mokhtar Ahmed, senior representatives from Dubai Taxi Company and Al Anas Overseas, and other senior officials of the ministry.
