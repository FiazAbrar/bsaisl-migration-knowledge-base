### Green visa for investors

What is the Green visa?

The Green visa is a type of long-term residence visa, which enables foreign talents to live, work or run a business in the UAE while enjoying exclusive benefits which include:

- a renewable residence visa valid for 5 years
- the privilege of not needing a sponsor
- the ability to sponsor family members, including spouses and children.

 

Eligible categories

The following categories of individuals are eligible for a Green visa:

- Skilled workers
- Freelancers and self-employed
- Investors and business partners.

 

Source: [ICP](https://icp.gov.ae/en/green-residency/)
