Issuance of a 14‑day visa for nationals of certain countries residing  Abroad

About this service

This service allows the issuance of a 14-day entry visa to nationals of certain countries and their family members who hold a valid visa, residence permit, or Green Card issued by one of the specified countries, subject to the applicable terms and conditions

Service Channels

ICP Smart Services Website - Websites
 This service applies to the category: 
Visitor 
.
 You can view the terms and conditions, fees, and the list of required documents below.

Terms and Conditions
 For nationals of the Republic of India and their family members, the service is limited to ordinary passport holders and their family members who hold a valid visa, residence permit, or US Green Card issued by the US, EU, or UK, or a valid residence permit issued by Japan, South Korea, Australia, Canada, Singapore, or New Zealand.
 For nationals of Kenya, Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, the Philippines, and South Africa, the service is limited to ordinary passport holders and their family members who hold a valid visa, residence permit, issued by the US, EU, or UK, or a valid residence permit issued by Japan, South Korea, Australia, Canada, Singapore, or New Zealand.
 The beneficiary’s passport must be valid for at least  (6) months
 The pre-entry validity period to the United Arab Emirates is (30) days.
 The duration of stay within the United Arab Emirates is (14) days from the date of entry.
 This visa is considered a visa that can be extended only once and for the same duration.
