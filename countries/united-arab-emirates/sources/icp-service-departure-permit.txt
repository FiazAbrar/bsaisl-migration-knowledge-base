Issue Departure permit

About this service

A service whereby a departure permit is issued to violators after paying their fines.

Service Channels

ICP Smart Services Website - Websites

ICP Smart App - Applications

Please select the appropriate category to view the terms and conditions, fees, and the list of required documents. 

 Residents
 Visitors

Terms and Conditions
 Departure from the country within 7 days from the date of permit issuance
 The regular exit permit requires the payment of all outstanding fines.
 If the individual does not depart within the validity period of the exit permit, the previous permit must be cancelled and a new application must be submitted.
 In exceptional cases involving the loss of a passport or travel document, a police report may be filed, and a travel pass can be issued by the beneficiary’s consulate. The individual may then proceed with the required procedures to obtain the exit permit.
