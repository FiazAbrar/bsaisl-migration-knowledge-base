### Issuing a multi-entry tourist visa (5 years)

Search in Services

[Start service](https://smart.gdrfad.gov.ae/SmartChannels_Th/Login.aspx?Lang=ar-AE)

After paying the finance fee and guarantee, a foreigner is given a visitor visa for tourism purposes for\
several trips, valid for (5) five years from the date of issue, without needing a guarantor or host within the country.

Service Details

Requirements

1\. A personal photo.\
2\. A passport copy valid for at least 6 months.\
3\. 6 months bank statement.\
4\. Health insurance.\
5\. Round-trip ticket.

Service Steps

Digital channels (website/smart application):\
1\. Log in to the smart services system through (UAE Pass or username).\
2\. Search for the service to be applied for.\
3\. Fill in the application data, where applicable.\
4\. Pay the service fee (if any).\

3\. Submitting the application that fulfils all conditions and documents (if any) to the customer service employee.\
4\. Pay the service fee (if any).\

Terms And Conditions

1\. A passport or other travel document valid for at least six months and allows entry into the country and return to the country where the holder currently resides or from which the document was issued.\
2\. A travel ticket to continue his journey or a ticket to leave the country.\
3\. valid health insurance in the country.\
4\. Provide documentation demonstrating a bank balance of \$4,000 or equivalent in other currencies over the six months before the application's submission.\
5\. Remain in the country continuously for no longer than (90) 90 days; this period may be extended for a similar amount of time as long as the total number of days spent there in a year is at most (180).

Service Availability Channels

The service is available 24/7

48.0 Hour(s)

Additional Information

Entry Validity:\
\- Valid for entry within two months of the issuance date and can be extended for an additional two months.\
\- The year is calculated from the date of first entry into UAE.\
\
Duration of Stay:\
\- Stay in the country are permitted for a continuous or intermittent period not exceeding (90) days in any one year. This can be extended for a similar period before incurring a fine, provided that the total stay does not exceed (180) days in any one year.\
\- If the (90) days or any remaining extension is not used, the remaining days will not be carried over to the following year.\
\
Violations:\
In case of a violation, the fines will be collected at the departure port, and the financial security amount will be forfeited.\

To determine the number of days remaining, the visa holder or his representative should request a travel report to show entry and exit traffic and ensure they match the permitted duration..
