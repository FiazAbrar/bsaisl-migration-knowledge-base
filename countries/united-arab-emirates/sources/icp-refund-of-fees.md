## About this service

A service through which one can request a refund of issuance fees if the service is not completed.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Service Channels

- ICP Smart Services Website - Websites
- ICP Smart App - Applications

Please select the appropriate category to view the terms and conditions, fees, and the list of required documents.

UAE Citizen Resident GCC Citizen Visitor

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Terms and Conditions

- Only issuance fees are refundable. Application submission fees and smart services fees are non-refundable
- Refundable fees are specified in the service receipt

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## List of Required Documents for Service

- Certificate from the bank indicating the account number and the customer's IBAN.

[Start Service](https://smartservices.icp.gov.ae/echannels/web/client/default.html#/login)
