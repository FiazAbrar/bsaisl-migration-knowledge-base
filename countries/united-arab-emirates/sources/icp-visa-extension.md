## About this service

Extend entry permit is a service through which the entry permit is extended for the categories that are eligible for the extension

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Service Channels

- ICP Smart App - Applications
- ICP Smart Services Website - Websites

Please select the appropriate category to view the terms and conditions, fees, and the list of required documents.

Visitor Tourism establishments authorized to issue visit visas for tourism purposes Licensed healthcare facilities in the UAE to issue entry visas for treatment or patient companions Government, private, or free zone entities for entry visa issuance for a work mission Licensed universities, institutes, or research entities for study, training, or qualification visa

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Terms and Conditions

- The entry permit validity may be extended once, for an equivalent period, before arriving in the UAE.
- The entry permit for visiting a relative or friend may be extended by the issuing authority once or multiple times, provided the total duration does not exceed 180 days.
- The entry permit for exploring job opportunities or business establishment may be extended by the issuing authority once or multiple times, provided the total duration does not exceed 180 days.
- The 6-month multiple-entry permit for completing Golden or Blue Residence procedures may be extended once for an equivalent period only.
- Entry permits for border areas, truck drivers, workers on tourist ships, and pleasure boats are non-extendable.
- A 14-day visa issued to nationals of certain countries residing outside their country of nationality may be extended once for the same period.
- A 60-day visa issued to nationals of certain countries residing outside their country of nationality is not extendable.

- The entry permit validity may be extended once, for an equivalent period, before arriving in the UAE.
- The visit/tourism entry permit may be extended by the issuing authority once or multiple times, provided the total duration does not exceed 120 days.

- The entry permit validity may be extended once, for an equivalent period, before arriving in the UAE.
- The entry permit for medical treatment may be extended by the issuing authority once or multiple times, provided the total duration does not exceed 180 days.

- The entry permit validity may be extended once, for an equivalent period, before arriving in the UAE.
- The entry permit for study or training may be extended by the issuing authority once or multiple times, provided the total duration does not exceed 180 days.
- A work mission visa issued based on the approval of the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation may be extended once only, subject to the Ministry’s approval of the extension.
- A public-sector work mission visa may be extended once only for a period equal to the original visa duration.

## Service fees

- Application fee for all types of visa extensions - 100 AED
- Extend entry Fees - 500 AED
- Fee for extending a 14-day visa issued to nationals of certain countries residing outside their country of nationality, once for the same period - 250 AED
- Fees for extending a multiple-entry visa to complete Golden or Blue Residency procedures (6 months) - 1000 AED
- Fee for requesting the extension of entry permit validity before arriving in the UAE - 50 AED

- Application fees - 100 AED
- Extend entry Fees - 500 AED
- Fee for requesting the extension of entry permit validity before arriving in the UAE - 50 AED

## List of Required Documents for Service

- Passport
- Personal Photo
- Providing the same documents submitted at the time of issuance, according to the type of visa.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Service fines

- Expiry of the permitted stay period for the visa holder, or expiration of the entry visa (per day) - 50 AED
