Dead Body Repatriation

The Consulate General of Bangladesh in Dubai facilitates the dead body repatriation of the deceased Bangladeshi Nationals in UAE. The Consulate issues No Objection Certificate (NoC) to facilitate the process of repatriation

Death Certificate (Original + Photocopy)

Passport of the Deceased (Original + Photocopy)

Emirates ID of the Deceased (Photocopy)

Death Notification / Medical Report

Case Paper / Autopsy Report/ Police Report (Mandatory for the accidental death)

Photocopy of the Passport/ Amirates ID of the accompanied person & Contact Number

Contact detail of the close family members in Bangladesh

Name and Contact number of the person who will received the dead body at the Airport

Proof and evidence of full payment of arrear of the deceased to the nominee

No fee is required for NoC (No Objection Certificate)

Company’s Sponsor/ Partner/ Manager/ PRO/ Employee/ Relatives of the Deceased may receive this service

The service is same day service

About Dubai Consulate

The Consulate General of Bangladesh in Dubai commenced its operations on July 5, 1980. Initially located in Al Baraha, Dubai, the Consulate has since relocated twice to serve the Bangladeshi community better. 
In 1996, it moved to Al Muteena, and in 2013, it established its current office in Al-Wuheida
