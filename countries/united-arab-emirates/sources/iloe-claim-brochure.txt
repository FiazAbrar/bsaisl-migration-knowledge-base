WHAT IS THE INVOLUNTARY LOSS OF EMPLOYMENT SCHEME?
The Involuntary Loss of Employment insurance is a unique scheme that covers workers in
the federal government sector and the private sector in the United Arab Emirates. It aims
to compensate the insured with a cash amount for a maximum period of three months
per claim when they lose their job, provided that the job loss was not a result of
resignation or a disciplinary action as per the policy terms and conditions.
Mandatory Subscription as of 01 January 2023 to avoid fines

WHAT IS THE COMPENSATION BENEFIT?

The Monthly compensation is calculated at a rate of 60% of the employee's average basic
salary in the last 6 months prior to the Loss Of Employment.

             CATEGORY A                                               CATEGORY B
Basic Salary 16,000 AED or below                       Basic Salary Above 16,000 AED

Up to 10,000 AED / month                               Up to 20,000 AED / month
Up to 3 months /claim                                  Up to 3 months /claim

ELIGIBILITY FOR COMPENSATION:

     The Insured must be legally resident in the United Arab Emirates
     Compensation is provided to those enrolled in the Involuntary Loss of Employment
     scheme for at least 12 consecutive months.
     The Insured's compliance with the payment of the Involuntary Loss of Employment
     Insurance Premium according to the agreed payment plan.
     The Insured to prove that the Loss of Employment is for a reason other than
     RESIGNATION or a DISCIPLINARY ACTION.
     Submitting the Claim within 30 days from the date of the termination of the labour
     relationship, as per the policy terms and conditions.

  To check the policy terms and conditions, visit (www.iloe.ae)

WHAT ARE THE DATA REQUIRED TO Submit your claim?
To ensure the validity of the claim and to proof the loss of employment, you are advised
to cancel the work permit prior submitting the claim.

     Emirates ID card number, Unified number / Work Permit number.
     A valid UAE phone number issued in the UAE.
     Date of Birth

Ano ang INVOLUNTARY LOSS OF EMPLOYMENT SCHEME?

The Involuntary Loss of Employment insurance is a unique scheme that covers workers in
the federal government sector and the private sector in the United Arab Emirates. It aims
to compensate the insured with a cash amount for a maximum period of three months
per claim when they lose their job, provided that the job loss was not a result of
resignation or a disciplinary action as per the policy terms and conditions.
Obligatoryo na mag Subscribe ang lahat ng mangagawa sa UAE simula ika-1 ng
Enero taong 2023 para maiwasan ang pagmumulta.

Ano ang benepisyo sa nasabing kasunduan?

Ang halagang matatanggap kada buwan ay kinakalkula sa 60% ng basic na sahod ng isang
empleyado sa huling 6 na buwan bago nawalan ng trabaho.

                KATEGORYA A                                    KATEGORYA B
Ang Basic Sahod ay AED 16,000 o pababa  Ang basic sahod ay mula sa AED 16,000 pataas
Hanggang AED 10,000 / buwan             Hanggang AED 20,000 / buwan
Hanggang 3 buwan / claim                Hanggang 3 buwan / claim

KARAPAT-DAPAT PARA SA KASUNDUAN:

     Ang Insured ay dapat naninirahan ng legal sa United Arab Emirates .
     Ang mga makakakuha ay dapat nakasubaybay ng bayad sa Involuntary Loss of
     Employment scheme nang hindi bumababa sa 12 magkakasunod na buwan.
     Ang pagsunod ng Insured sa pagbabayad ng Involuntary Loss of Employment
     Insurance Premium ayon sa napagkasunduang termino sa pagbabayad.
     Pagbibigay ng Katibayan na pagkawala ng trabaho ay hindi naging dahilan ng
     pagbitiw sa trabaho o pagkatanggal nang dahil sa aksyong pandisiplina.
     Pagsusumite ng Claim sa loob ng 30 araw mula sa petsa ng huling araw ng termino
     sa trabaho, ayon sa mga tuntunin at kundisyon.

        Upang suriin ang mga tuntunin at kundisyon ng kasunduan, bisitahin ang (www.iloe.ae)
