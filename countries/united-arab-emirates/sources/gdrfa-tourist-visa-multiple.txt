Issuing a tourist visa for several entries

Search in Services

Start service

							The foreigner is granted a visit multi entries visa for the purpose of tourism for a period of 30 or 60 days, and the sponsor / host in UAE is one of the establishments working in the field of tourism, after paying the prescribed fee and financial security deposit.

Service Details

Requirements

														1. One personal photo.
2. Copy of the passport.
3. Identity card for the country of origin for some nationalities (Iraq - Pakistan - Iran - Afghanistan).

Service Steps

														Digital channels (GDRFA website / smart application):
1. Login to the smart services system via (UAE pass or username).
2. Search for your required service.
3. Fill out the application information where applicable.
4. Pay the service fees (if any).

Fees

														-30 day tourist visa fee: AED 300.
   - 60 day tourist visa fee: AED 500.
   - In addition to value added tax (5%).
   - Security deposit: There is no security deposit amount on this type of visa.          Additional fees (if the sponsored person is inside UAE):
   Knowledge Dirham: AED 10.
   Innovation  Dirham: AED 10.

Fee in UAE: AED 500.
Note: The total amount of the visa may vary more or less depending on the circumstances surrounding the sponsored person or any other reasons that may require it.

Terms And Conditions

														1. A valid and approved passport or travel document to enter the country and return to the country in which he resides or from which the passport or document was issued, and the validity period of the passport or travel document is not less than (6) six months.
2. A travel ticket to continue his journey or a ticket to leave the country.
3. valid health insurance in the country.

Service Availability Channels

														Digital channels (website/smart application): 
The service is available 24/7

Expected Completion Time

														48.0 Hour(s)

Additional Information

														Note: The total amount of the visa may vary more or less depending on the circumstances surrounding the sponsored person or any other reasons that may require it.

Related Services

														1. Renewal of tourist entry visa - one entry or multiple entries.
2. Extending the tourist entry visa - one entry or multiple entries.
