### 1.
- old: - **A UAE airline** (Emirates, Etihad, flydubai, Air Arabia) [V0185]. Emirates applies online through "Manage your booking" for passengers holding an issued Emirates ticket into and out of Dubai on one booking reference (PNR), at least four working days before arrival; processing fees of VFS Global (the company that handles Emirates' visa applications) are added [V0077, V0078, V0079].
- **A licensed UAE travel agent or hotel**, which files with ICP (the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security, for Abu Dhabi and the northern emirates) or GDRFA Dubai (the General Directorate of Identity and Foreigners Affairs, for Dubai) [V0068, V0051, V0064].
- **A Bangladeshi travel agency** selling a package with a UAE tour operator; check it is genuine before paying or sending any document copies; the UAE embassy can help verify [V0072].
- new: 1. **Pick one sponsor;** you cannot apply yourself [V0062, V0051]:

   - **a UAE airline** (Emirates, Etihad, flydubai, Air Arabia), for its own passengers only [V0185, V0069];
   - **a licensed UAE travel agent or hotel,** if you buy the ticket through it and keep the hotel booking; it files with ICP (the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security, for Abu Dhabi and the northern emirates) or GDRFA Dubai (the General Directorate of Identity and Foreigners Affairs, for Dubai) [V0070, V0068, V0064];
   - **a Bangladeshi travel agency** selling a package with a UAE tour operator [V0072].

2. **With Emirates, apply through "Manage your booking":**

   1. Buy an Emirates ticket into and out of Dubai on one booking reference (PNR), on Emirates flights only [V0077, V0078].
   2. Open the booking under "Manage your booking" and apply at least four working days before arrival [V0077].
   3. Pay the visa fee (Step 3) plus the processing fee of VFS Global, the company that handles Emirates' visa applications [V0079, V0074].

3. **With an agency, check it is genuine before paying or sending any document copies;** the UAE embassy can help verify [V0072].

### 7.
- old: A tourist visa may be extended once or more, up to 120 days in total, by applying to ICP or GDRFA with the same documents: AED 100 application plus AED 500 extension [V0053, V0060, V0058]. Leave when it expires [V0170]. Overstaying costs AED 50 a day [V0045]; check fines on GDRFA's fines inquiry service and pay through ICP or GDRFA [V0191].
- new: 1. **Extend before it expires, if you need to,** up to 120 days in total [V0053].

   You'll need [SB0006, V0060]:

   - your passport;
   - a personal photo;
   - the same documents as when the visa was issued.

   1. For an ICP visa, open ICP's "Visa Extension" service at [smartservices.icp.gov.ae](https://smartservices.icp.gov.ae/echannels/web/client/default.html#/login) and log in with UAE Pass, the UAE's digital ID [SB0006, SB0010].
   2. Upload the documents and pay AED 100 application plus AED 500 extension [SB0006, V0058].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does the visitor or the sponsoring agency, hotel or airline file a tourist visa extension, and which GDRFA service extends a Dubai-issued one?

2. **Leave when the visa expires** [V0170].
3. **If you overstay,** the fine is AED 50 a day [V0045]; check it on GDRFA's fines inquiry service and pay through ICP or GDRFA [V0191].

### 9.
