# m-B: MOHRE recruitment manual, MOHRE domestic work permit, WAM MoU, DIP e-passport fees, GDRFA relative visit (fact prefix MB)

### 1. CONFIRM

- new: with salary paid through the WPS (Wage Protection System, the UAE's electronic salary transfer) [D0138, D0139, D0140, MB0030, MB0031, MB0032, MB0033].
- why: WAM's official text of the 18 April 2018 MoU matches D0138-D0140 (Arabian Business) word for word (MB0030-MB0033).

### 2. CORRECT
- old: 1. **Age.** The UAE minimum is 18 [D0005].
- new: 1. **Age and passport.** The UAE minimum age is 18 and the maximum 60, and your passport must be valid at least three months [D0005, MB0019, MB0020].
- why: MOHRE's domestic work permit page (updated 7 Nov 2026) sets an age limit of 60 and a 3-month passport validity (MB0019, MB0020).

### 3. CONFIRM
- old: A UAE office may not ask for or accept any commission or expense from you, before or after you start, directly or through anyone else [D0009].
- new: A UAE office may not ask for or accept any commission or expense from you, before or after you start, directly or through anyone else, nor charge you for completing your employment papers [D0009, MB0001].
- why: MOHRE's operation manual for recruitment offices also bans fees for completing the employment process (MB0001).

### 4. ADD
- old: MOHRE's list of licensed offices is under Services, Authorised Service Centres [D0127].
- new: MOHRE's list of licensed offices is under Services, Authorised Service Centres [D0127]. **The work permit:** the employer applies for your MOHRE domestic work permit with UAE Pass (the national digital ID) on MOHRE's website or app, at a service centre or through Tawseel (MOHRE's document collection service); it is issued in 2 working days, free online apart from federal charges, and a centre's commission is capped at AED 72 [MB0023, MB0024, MB0025, MB0026]. A foreign-resident employer must prove a family income of at least AED 25,000 a month by salary certificate or three months' bank statements; the permit lasts two years with a UAE citizen employer and one year with a resident employer [MB0027, MB0028, MB0022].
- why: MOHRE service page "Issuance of a New Work Permit - Domestic Workers" (MB0022-MB0028); the fee amounts themselves are not in the saved page text.

### 5. ADD
- old: MOHRE tells offices that the agency in Bangladesh must explain every term in a language you understand [D0116].
- new: MOHRE tells offices that the agency in Bangladesh must explain every term in a language you understand [D0116]. The office must also tell you your rights and where to turn if they are broken [MB0004].
- why: MOHRE operation manual, office duties to the worker (MB0004).

### 6. ADD
- old: 1. Wages are due from the day you enter the UAE [D0038]. Probation can last up to six months [D0017].
- new: 1. Wages are due from the day you enter the UAE [D0038]. Probation can last up to six months [D0017]. Under the "classic package" you are recruited onto the household's file and live with it for the two-year contract [MB0005]. Under the office's "temporary" (two-year) or "flexible" (hourly to yearly) packages you stay on the office's file: the office gets your residence visa and pays your salary, and flexible-package work is capped at 8 hours a day [MB0014, MB0017, MB0015, MB0016, MB0018].
- why: MOHRE operation manual, section II, the three domestic worker packages (MB0005, MB0014-MB0018).

### 7. CORRECT
- old: 2. The employer must buy health insurance before your residence permit is issued or renewed
- new: 2. The employer must insure you on its file before MOHRE issues your work permit [MB0021], and must buy health insurance before your residence permit is issued or renewed
- why: MOHRE's work permit page requires an adequate insurance policy on the employer's file (MB0021).

### 8. CORRECT
- old: 4. **Refunds between office and employer.** If you fail probation, leave outside the permitted cases, or the agreed conditions are not met, the office refunds the employer (cost ÷ contract months) × months left, or the full cost if this happens in your first month or you are found physically unfit during probation; for example AED 7,000 of AED 12,000 after 10 of 24 months, within two weeks [D0069, D0070, D0071, D0125, D0073]. Late refunds were 106 of 135 fines on offices in the first half of 2026 [D0135]. The office must house and feed workers returned to it [D0011].
- new: 4. **Refunds between office and employer.** Under the classic package the office guarantees you to the employer for two years [MB0006]. During the six-month probation it must replace you or refund the employer if you prove incompetent or badly behaved, are medically unfit, quit or refuse to work, or the agreed conditions are not met; after probation, if you refuse to work or abscond [MB0007, MB0008, MB0009, D0069]. The refund is the full recruitment fee in your first month, the full recruitment and government fees if you are found medically unfit during probation, and otherwise (cost ÷ contract months) × months left; for example AED 7,000 of AED 12,000 after 10 of 24 months [MB0010, MB0011, MB0012, D0070, D0071, D0125]. It is due within 14 days of your return to the office or the absence report [MB0013, D0073]. Late refunds were 106 of 135 fines on offices in the first half of 2026 [D0135]. The office must house and feed you if you come back to it for any reason, including being sent back, refusing work, illness or injury [MB0002, D0011].
- why: MOHRE operation manual adds the replacement option, the two-year guarantee, the post-probation trigger (refusal or absconding) and the government-fee refund for medical unfitness (MB0006-MB0013, MB0002).

### 9. ADD
- old: A visit or tourist visa gives no right to work, and working on one brings fines and legal liability [V0179] (see Work visa: company worker, Step 1).
- new: A visit or tourist visa gives no right to work, and working on one brings fines and legal liability [V0179] (see Work visa: company worker, Step 1). To hire someone already in the UAE on a tourist visa, the household must first get a MOHRE domestic work permit for them [MB0029, V0179].
- why: MOHRE's work permit page lists the worker's tourist visa as a document when the worker is inside the UAE (MB0029).
