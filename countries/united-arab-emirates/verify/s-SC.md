- new: 1. **Get an unconditional offer and pay what the university asks before the visa.** Middlesex University Dubai, for example, wants 25% of the year's tuition paid first, a passport valid one year, and a parent's no-objection letter for anyone under 18 [F0076].

4. **Check the visa before you fly:** ICP's File Validity page for Abu Dhabi and the Northern Emirates, GDRFA's Visa Status page for Dubai (see Tourist visa, Step 4) [V0188]. Track an ICP application at smartservices.icp.gov.ae with the application number starting 010 [V0050].
5. **Finish the residence within 60 days of entry,** or overstay fines apply [F0009].

### 2.
- old: The Dubai entry permit costs AED 200 plus VAT (plus AED 520 if you are inside the UAE), and you have 60 days from entry to finish [F0092]. ICP charges AED 100 to apply, AED 100 per year and AED 100 smart service fee [F0093].
- new: **How to apply in Dubai (GDRFA):**

1. **Get the MOHRE permit first:** the work permit (skilled worker) or the self-employed permit (freelancer) [SC0002, SC0004].
2. **Apply for the green visa entry permit.** You'll need [SC0002, SC0004, SC0005]:

   - a personal photo and a passport copy valid at least 6 months;
   - skilled worker: the MOHRE permit, and the employment contract if the employer is government, semi-government or in a free zone;
   - freelancer: the MOHRE self-employed permit;
   - investor or partner: the partnership or investment contract and the trade licence.

   1. Log in to GDRFA smart services (smart.gdrfad.gov.ae) with UAE Pass or a username [SC0001].
   2. Search for the green visa service for your category, fill in the application and pay [SC0001, SC0004].
   3. Or go to a GDRFA Customer Happiness Centre or an Amer centre, take a ticket and hand in the application and papers [SC0001].

3. **Pay** AED 200 plus 5% VAT, plus AED 520 if you are inside the UAE [F0092]. GDRFA expects the skilled-worker entry visa in 48 hours [SC0003].
4. **Finish the residence within 60 days of entry** [F0092]; it is linked to an Emirates ID application [SC0006]. ICP charges AED 100 to apply, AED 100 per year and AED 100 smart service fee [F0093].

**Check it is real:** check the permit on GDRFA's Visa Status page (Dubai) or ICP's File Validity page (other emirates), as in Work visa: company worker, Step 5 [V0188].

### 3.
- old: A golden visa is a 5- or 10-year renewable residence with no sponsor; the holder may stay abroad more than six months and sponsor a spouse, children of any age and parents [F0096, F0102]. It is applied for directly on ICP's platform or by government nomination; anyone eligible but abroad gets a 6-month multiple-entry visa, renewable once [F0106, F0103]. ICP charges AED 100 to apply, AED 100 per year and AED 100 smart service fee [F0107]. Employers hire a golden visa holder on a 2-year golden visa work permit [F0085].
- new: A golden visa is a 5- or 10-year renewable residence with no sponsor; the holder may stay abroad more than six months and sponsor a spouse, children of any age and parents [F0096, F0102]. Employers hire a golden visa holder on a 2-year golden visa work permit [F0085].

**How to apply:**

1. **Check you qualify** with ICP's "Check your eligibility for Golden visa" on smartservices.icp.gov.ae; Dubai also has GDRFA's golden visa services, and creatives ask the Ministry of Culture for a recommendation at mcy.gov.ae/en/goldenvisa [SC0010].
2. **Apply directly on ICP's platform, or be nominated** by a federal or local authority [F0106]. You'll need your passport, a personal photo and proof of eligibility for your category [SC0007].
3. **Pay ICP** AED 100 to apply, AED 100 per year and AED 100 smart service fee [F0107].
4. **If you are abroad,** you get a 6-month multiple-entry visa, renewable once, to come and finish the steps [F0103].

**Check it is real:** apply only through ICP or GDRFA, never a consultancy; check on icp.gov.ae or call 600522222 (see Scam below) [F0110, F0111].

### 5.
- old: > **Need to know**
> - Is GDRFA or ICP refusing in-country status changes for Bangladeshi passport holders in 2026?
- new: **How to change status inside the UAE:**

1. **Do not let your stay lapse.** After a residence is cancelled or expires most people have 30 days' grace [G0067].
2. **The new sponsor gets the new visa** (an employer through the Work Bundle; a family sponsor as in Family residence, Step 3) [SC0008, W0089].
3. **The sponsor files the change of status inside the country** with ICP, or GDRFA in Dubai, and pays AED 500; the old residence must already be cancelled, and any fines must be settled [SC0008, F0014, F0019].
4. **Finish the new residence** (medical test, Emirates ID, residence) as that visa's section describes; the residence is linked to an Emirates ID application [SC0006].

**Check it is real:** check the new permit on ICP's File Validity page or GDRFA's Visa Status page (see Tourist visa, Step 4) [V0188].

- new: - **Fees.** An approved Bangladeshi agency or a UAE recruitment office that asks a woman domestic worker for money breaks the zero-cost rule and UAE law [D0214, D0009]; so does a UAE employer [D0032].

**Check it is real:**

1. **The Bangladeshi agency** must be one of the agencies the ministry approved for women domestic workers (21 in September 2024; see Step 3) [D0212, D0216].
2. **The UAE office:** call MOHRE on 80060, choose your language, press 2 (domestic workers) and ask an agent; or on mohre.gov.ae open Services > Enquiry services > Company Information and enter the licence number [D0134]. MOHRE also lists licensed offices under Services > Authorised Service Centres [D0127].
3. **The visa:** have BMET check it (Step 4) [D0219].

**How to report:**

1. **A UAE recruitment office:** MOHRE on 600590000, its website or app, or the Labour Claims and Advisory Centre on 80084 [D0132, D0137].
2. **A fake job or visa online, in Dubai:** the Dubai Police app or eCrime platform [W0123].
3. **Abuse or trafficking of a woman:** DFWAC's free 24-hour helpline 800111 [D0174].
4. **A Bangladeshi agency:** a written complaint with evidence to BMET's Director General (free, 30 working days); Director (Employment) 01711111544, diremi@bmet.gov.bd [D0222].
