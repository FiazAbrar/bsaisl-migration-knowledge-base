- old: 2. **Medical fitness test.** You must be found free of communicable diseases to get residence [W0029]. The tests are HIV, hepatitis B, leprosy, syphilis and tuberculosis (TB), plus pregnancy for domestic workers [W0023]. Residency is refused if HIV or leprosy is positive [W0023]. TB scars or active or drug-resistant TB bring a conditional certificate and a one-year residence while you are treated in the UAE [W0031, W0024]. Abu Dhabi uses a chest X-ray; the Emirates Health Services (EHS) centres in the northern emirates include one [W0030, W0102].
