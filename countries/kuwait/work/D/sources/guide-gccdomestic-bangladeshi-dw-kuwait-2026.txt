⚠️ Status update — Kuwait recruitment rules changed in June 2026.
 Kuwait's Interior Ministry now restricts domestic-worker recruitment to 
10 approved source countries
 (South Africa, Benin, Eritrea, Ethiopia, the Philippines, Sri Lanka, India, Vietnam, Nepal, and Senegal for male workers), and explicitly bans recruitment from 27 others. 
Bangladesh is not on the approved list
, so new domestic-worker recruitment from Bangladesh into Kuwait is not currently going through. Workers already legally in Kuwait are not affected by the change. Confirm current eligibility with PAM or the Interior Ministry before paying any agency fee. If you need someone now, the approved corridors into Kuwait include 
the Philippines
, 
Ethiopia
, 
India
, 
Sri Lanka
 (Nepal is on Kuwait's list, but Nepal's own 2017 ban on domestic work in the Gulf is still in force).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are Bangladeshi domestic workers Muslim?
Yes — Bangladesh is approximately 91% Muslim. For Kuwaiti families who prefer Muslim domestic workers who understand Islamic household practices, Bangladeshi workers are an excellent match.
Can Bangladeshi workers cook Kuwaiti food?
Many experienced Bangladeshi workers adapt well to Gulf and Kuwaiti cuisine. South Asian cooking similarities with rice dishes make the culinary transition easy.
What is the minimum age for Bangladeshi female domestic workers in Kuwait?
Bangladesh's government policy sets the minimum age at 
25 years
 (reported; confirm with BMET) for female domestic workers going to Gulf countries.
