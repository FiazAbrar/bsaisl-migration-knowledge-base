Attestation of Kuwait visa 18(Company), visa 20(Domestic Aid) and visa 22(Family visa)

General information
To safeguard the interests of Bangladeshis aspiring to come to Kuwait with ‘Single Visa’ under the ‘Personal Sponsor’ Visa (Visa-20) or ‘Small Company’ Visa (Visa-18), the ‘Single Visa’ is currently being attested at Bangladesh Embassy, Kuwait only in the presence of the sponsor (Kofil) or the recipient of the ‘Power of Attorney’ from the sponsor (Kofil).
To make the service provided by the embassy easier, from 18-06-2025, if the sponsor (Kofil) is unable to appear at the embasy in person for the ‘Single Visa’ attestation, the single visa attestation will be done in presence of the person who is holding ‘Power of Attorney’ (Towkil Khas)

from the sponsor (Kofil) or who is authorized by the sponsor (Kofil). A sample copy of ‘Letter of Authorization’ is attached herewith. In this case, the original civil ID card of the sponsor (Kofil) related to the visa must be displayed to the concerned person of the embassy.
In the case of attestation of visa-20, a photocopy of sponsor’s passport (along with photocopy of the page that bears sponsor’s signature) must be submitted to the embassy for verifying the signature of the sponsor (Kofil). In the case of the attestation of visa-18, a photocopy of Employer Signature Approval Certificate (Itemat Toukiya) provided by the Public Authority of Manpower (PAM), Kuwait has to be submitted to the embassy for verifying the signature of the sponsor.
Required Documents:
The documents those are to be deposited at the embassy for Personal Sponsor ‘Single Visa’ (Visa-20) attestation are:
1) Filled up visa attestation Contract Form, signed by the sponsor (Kofil)- 01 copy (the form is provided from Room 03 of the Embassy free of cost);
2) Visa-02 copies;
3) Employee's photo-02 copies;
4) Photocopy of the sponsor's Passport (along with photocopy of the page that bears sponsor’s signature)- 01 copy;
5) Sponsor’s (Kofil) original civil ID (to be put up to the embassy staff);
6) Photocopy of the sponsor's civil ID- 01 copy;
7) ‘Power of Attorney’ (Towkil Khas)

or ‘Letter of Authorization’ by the sponsor (Kofil) to the representative- 01 copy (if sponsor can not appear in the Embassy);
The documents those are to be deposited at the embassy for ‘Small Company’ Visa (Visa-18) attestation are:
1) Filled up visa attestation Contract Form, signed by the sponsor (Kofil)-01 copy (the form is provided from Room 03 of the Embassy, free of cost);
2) Visa-02 copies;
3) Employee's photo-02 copies;
4) Sponsor’s (Kofil) original civil ID card (to be put up to the embassy staff);
5) Photocopy of the sponsor's civil ID card-01 copy;
6) ‘Power of Attorney’ (Towkil Khas)

or ‘Letter of Authorization’ by the sponsor (kofil) to the representative-01 copy (if sponsor can not appear in the embassy);
7) Employer Signature Approval Certificate (Itemat Toukiya) provided by the Public Authority of Manpower (PAM), Kuwait- 01 copy;
8) Work permit (Tasriah Amel)- 01 copy.
Fee:
 Per Person 3.000 Kuwaiti Diner [Non-Refundable]
Payment Mode:
Cash & Knet only;
Pay at the room no-3 and collect the payment receipt;
Collect the attested visa from room no-03 at 4.00 pm on the same day of the application;
Service Hour: 8:00 am – 2:00 pm (Sunday to Thursday).

Family Visa Attestation
Husband/wife who are eligible for sponsoring family visa for family members can submit visa copy of his/her spouse and children to the Bangladesh Embassy, Kuwait for attestation. The required documents are as follows:
Copy of the visa-02 copies;
Copy of sponsor’s civil id-1 copy
Copy of the passports (husband, wife, children)-01 copy;
Copy of the Relationship Certificate-01 copy.
The Sponsor can submit the documents to the Embassy directly. The fee for family visa attestation is KD 03.00 each. The Embassy receives documents from 08.00 am to 2:00 pm from Sunday to Thursday and delivers the attested visa on the same day.
