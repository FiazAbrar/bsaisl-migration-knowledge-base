## Endorsement of Power of Attorney

Content: Pages

***Endorsement of Power of Attorney***

\

Applicants for endorsements i.e. attestation of academic certificates, marriage or divorce certificate, statement of date of birth, certificate for transfer of dead body, alive certificate, driving license, renewal of driving license, transfer of residency certificate / power of attorney PLEASE KEEP A PHOTOCOPY OF YOUR DOCUMENTS IN A SAFE PLACE before submitting to the Consular Wing of the Bangladesh Embassy. Please read the instructions thoroughly from top to bottom.

- Must know before apply for the Authentication of Power of Attorney executed outside Bangladesh: বাংলাদেশের বাহিরে সম্পাদিত পাওয়ার অব অ্যাটর্নি প্রমাণীকরণ \[[**বিধি** **১০(১)** **দ্রষ্টব্য**](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-kuwait-mofa/2024/12/d2b9611e570f45239ba2856e22804fa2.pdf)\], বাংলাদেশ গেজেট, অতিরিক্ত, জুলাই ২৩, ২০১৫ এবং [**পাওয়ার** **অব** **অ্যাটর্নি** **বিধিমালা,** **২০১৫**](https://www.dpp.gov.bd/upload_file/gazettes/13698_15157.pdf) এস. আর. ও ২৩৪-আইন/২০১৫।

**Requirements:**

- **Please submit the following documents along with the application for attestation / Power of Attorney (in person)**:

1.  Original Document and photocopy of the original document
2.  In case of any property related document, Proof of property ownership (Copy of mutation, succession/deed- RS, CS & BS)
3.  2 (two) photographs of principals (power giver) and 2 (two) photographs of the attorney (power receiver) are to be attached for verification by the Embassy. Photographs (both i.e. principal and attorney) must be passport size, colour with white background, taken recently within the last six months.
4.  A photocopy of the valid/expired hand-written Bangladesh Passport (1–7 pages) or Machine Readable Passport/E-passport (1-2 pages) or NID or Birth Certificate or Academic Certificate or any official document issued by the Government of Bangladesh that proves Bangladeshi nationality of the applicant.
5.  Photocopy of Kuwaiti Civil ID

\*\*Please keep in mind, Embassy **doesn't produce any power of attorney papers**. The applicants have to prepare the documents by themselves as per their requirement and attach the photos in proper place. They will sign in front of the consular officer which will be verified/ attested by the Embassy.

**Procedures for obtaining Attestation of Documents/Power of Attorney from Bangladesh Embassy, Kuwait.**

- **Appear in Person:** Power of Attorney or Attestation of any document such as a power of attorney (relating to sale/purchase of land in Bangladesh, transfer/purchase/sale of shares, withdrawal of money/ pension from bank, etc.) executed (signed) by any expatriate Bangladeshi national (in favour of a relative or a friend residing in Bangladesh), the executants is required to **Appear In Person** before the Consular Officer at the Bangladesh Embassy in Kuwait to put signature in front of the officer. For multiple signatories of a document, the same rule applies for all, i.e. all signatories are required to appear in person at the Embassy to put signature.
