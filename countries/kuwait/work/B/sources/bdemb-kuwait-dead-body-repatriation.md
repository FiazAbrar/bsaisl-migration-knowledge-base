## Repatriation of Dead Body

Content: Pages

[TABLE]

\

**General information**

The Embassy of Bangladesh in Kuwait facilitates the repatriation of the dead body of deceased Bangladeshi nationals in Kuwait. The Embassy issues No Objection Certificate (NOC) to facilitate the process of repatriation. The respective company/employer bears all the expenses for the repatriation of a deadbody.In the case of the absence of employer, Bangladesh Embassy bears the expenses of transfering the deadbody to Bangladesh.

**Required Documents**

- Death Certificate (Original Death Certificate & a copy of the Death Certificate );
- Passport of the Deceased (Original & a copy of the Passport );
- Kuwait Civil ID of the Deceased (Photocopy);
- Death Notification Document / Medical Report;
- Case Paper/ Autopsy Report/ Police Report (Mandatory for the accidental death);
- Photocopy of the Passport/ Kuwait Civil ID of the accompanied person & contact number;
- Contact details of the close family members in Bangladesh;
- Name and Contact number of the person who will received the dead body at the airport (Bangladesh);
- Statement of the accounts of the pending salary and service benefit of the deceased.

**Fees**

- No fee is required for NOC (No Objection Certificate);
- Company’s Sponsor/ authorized representative of the company / Relatives of the Deceased can receive this service;

\

\

**Submission & Delivery**

- The service is provided urgently on the same day basing on the availability of the Death Certificate.
- Delivery Place: Room No :02
- Service Hour: 8:00 am – 4:00 pm (Sunday to Thursday)
