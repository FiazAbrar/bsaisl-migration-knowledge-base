Leaving Kuwait? Make Sure Your Biometrics Are Updated and Verified

KUWAIT CITY, Nov 3: Many travelers have not completed their biometric fingerprinting before arriving at Kuwait’s border points, resulting in long queues and delays in departure procedures. To make travel faster and more convenient, the Ministry of Interior has urged everyone to complete the biometric process well in advance of their trip. Below are the centers, locations, and timings.
Arab Times readers have raised questions seeking clarification on the new rule. One reader asked, “Dear Arab Times, what about those who already completed the fingerprinting earlier? Is this a new regulation?”

The Ministry also announced that biometric fingerprinting will no longer be carried out at Kuwait’s airports, land borders, or sea ports. This move aims to reduce congestion and ensure smoother travel. From now on, Kuwaiti citizens must complete fingerprinting at the Criminal Evidence Department’s Personal Identification Centers or National Identity Centers before traveling, while expatriate residents can complete it at any Criminal Evidence Department center in their governorate.
