Advisory: Fake e-Visa Websites

17 November 2025

It has come to the notice of the Embassy of India, Kuwait that several fraudulent third-party websites are falsely claiming to offer Indian e-Visa services. Some of these websites have been involved in financial scams.

The following websites are not authorised by the Government of India:

indianimmigration.org

idiasevisa.org

evisaentry.com

india-immi.org

ivisa.com

india-evisa.it.com

The only official website of the Government of India for e-Visa is 
https://indianvisaonline.gov.in
. Applicants are advised to apply only through this website to avoid fraud and to protect their personal information.
