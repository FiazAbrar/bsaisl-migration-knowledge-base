# Graduate Study Excellence Scholarship

# Kuwait University

# 2016-2017

**Online Application is opened from 20 December, 2015 till 3 March, 2016**

The Scholarship intends to support outstanding Master’s and PhD students joining
thesis-based graduate programs at Kuwait University. The Scholarships are open
to national and international applicants. Kuwait University seeks candidates
with exceptional abilities qualifying them to contribute effectively to the
academic life of the University. The scholarship will include a full university
tuition waiver, as well as a generous monthly stipend of KD1000(approximately $
3500) for a Master’s degree student and KD 1400 (approximately $ 5000) for a
Ph.D. student. For successful international applicants, Kuwait University will
also offer air-tickets to and from Kuwait at the start of the scholarship and
upon graduation, accommodation, government health insurance and assist with
entry visa in accordance with Kuwait University rules and regulations.
