KUWAIT CITY, Aug 11: The Government Information Center has unveiled four categories of tourist visas tailored to meet the diverse needs of various travelers.
Visa validity for the first and second categories ranges from 30 days up to 360 days, depending on the selected options.
The first category is open to citizens of countries with powerful passports and strong economic indicators, providing a variety of visa options.

The second category covers Gulf citizens and expats with professional qualifications, along with individuals holding valid US, UK, or Schengen visas or residence permits of GCC countries.
The third category, which will be launched soon, is intended for other nationalities who can provide proof of financial statement and necessary guarantees.
The fourth category covers visitors attending events and activities in Kuwait, with conditions applied based on each specific event.
