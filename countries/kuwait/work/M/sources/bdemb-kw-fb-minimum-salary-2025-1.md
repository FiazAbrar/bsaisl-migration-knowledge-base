The following are the bare minimum monthly salaries for the employment of expatriate Bangladeshi workers in Kuwait for various occupations. The employer may pay more, according to the qualification and skill of the worker. The Bangladesh Embassy, Kuwait will verify these minimum salaries in the 'Work Permit', while attesting Visa/Demand Letter from August 01, 2025.

Minimum Monthly Salary of Bangladeshi Workers in Kuwait (Effective from 01st August 2025)

Visa-20: (Unskilled Worker)
1. House Boy: 120.000 KD (Consolidated)
2. General Labour: 120.000 KD (Consolidated)

Visa-20: (Skilled Worker)
1. Chef: 150.000 KD (Consolidated)
2. Driver: 150.000 KD (Consolidated)

Visa-18: Government Contract (Aqud Hukumi) Unskilled Worker
1. Agricultural Worker: 90.000 (For 8 hours)
2. Cleaner: 90.000 (For 8 hours)
3. General Labour: 90.000 (For 8 hours)

Visa-18: Government Contract (Aqud Hukumi) Skilled Worker
1. Driver: 120.000 (For 8 hours)

Visa-18: Private Contract (Aqud Ahali) Unskilled Worker
2. Agricultural Worker: 120.000 (For 8 hours)
3. Cleaner: 90.000 (For 8 hours)
4. General Labour: 90.000 (For 8 hours)
5. Guarder: 90.000 (For 8 hours)
6. Sponsor: 120.000 (For 8 hours)
