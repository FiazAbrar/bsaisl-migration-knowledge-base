              The complaint filing mechanism at PAM:

   Workers on Visa No. 18 can approach Public Authority of Manpower (PAM) office
for following labour disputes:

  non-payment of wages
  service-end benefits
  vacation allowances
  compensation for unfair dismissal
  financial compensation for overtime allowance
  Compensation for work injuries and other labour claims.
  local release, cancellation of residency
Steps to be taken by the individual for filing a complaint with PAM:

· Worker must appear before the Labour Unit in PAM, where the employer's file belongs
  to and can register his/her complaint.

· After registration of complaint, the worker receives the complaint registration number
  through SMS

· If the worker is out of the country, a legal representative of the worker can register
  the complaint through the competent employee in the Unit within one year of his/her
  termination/resignation.

· PAM will fix the next date of the session to investigate the complaint and inform the
  disputing parties the date and time of complaint and name of the investigator.

· On the date of hearing, the investigator tries to settle the dispute amicably between
  the two disputing parties, failing which the complaint is referred to Court within one
  month

· Then the worker will have to follow up the case in the Court.

               Open House in the Embassy (24.11.2021):

                            Frequently asked Questions (FAQs):

Q1. Is there any minimum salary for unskilled labours?
Answer: Minimum wages for various category of workers are provided on Embassy's website and can
be seen at https://indembkwt.gov.in/minimum-wages-schedule.php.

Q2: The employer has extended my contract without my consent. What should I do?
Answer: The employer is not allowed to extend the contract without your consent. If the employer has
done so, you can lodge a complaint with PAM office (Shoun) or the local labour court. If you face prob-
lems in this regard, please approach the Indian Embassy for advice/assistance.
