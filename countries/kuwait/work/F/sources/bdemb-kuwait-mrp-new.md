## MRP NEW

Content: Pages

![](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-kuwait-mofa/2024/12/6010573f9e7c488b94c305e63c785014.jpg)

# ![](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-kuwait-mofa/2024/12/6befcf643c4d48a2b8bc63eb8de73028.jpg)

# \>Eligibility

- Bangladeshi citizen
- Those who do NOT have any previous MRP.
- A Bangladeshi citizen who is not barred to have a passport by any Court of Justice or by the Government of Bangladesh.

#  \>Requirements

- Filled-up “New MRP Application Form”.
- Birth Registration Certificate (BRC) or National Identification Card (NID) as proof of citizenship.
- 4 copies of passport size recent photographs with white background. For children, parents’ photographs, 1 copy for each, are also required.
- Kuwaiti Civil ID/Eqama/Visa (any one)
- Bank receipt of deposition of Passport fees. You can also deposit to the payment counter in the embassy.

# \>Fees

- KWD 30.250 should be paid for new passport of newly born baby.

#  \>How To Pay

- Payment is to be made by the applicant in cash at the payment counter in the Embassy. Before going to pay, the applicant must fill up the “New Application Form” properly and SIGN it on the line marked for that. This form should be produced before the counter during payment.

#  \>How To Apply

**Step-1: Filling up the application form**

- Download “[New MRP Application Form](http://bdembassykuwait.org/mrp-application-form.pdf) ” and get a print, then FILL-UP the suggested sections except the one for PAYMENT.

[![](https://objectstorage.ap-dcc-gazipur-1.oraclecloud15.com/n/axvjbnqprylg/b/V2Ministry/o/office-kuwait-mofa/2024/12/0dced58a13e14575a2bf6240138bbdfa.png)](http://bdembassykuwait.org/mrp-application-form.pdf)

- You can also Fill-up the form online (Please visit [this link](http://bdembassykuwait.org/editable_mrp.pdf)) and then get a print.

**Step-2: Payment**

- Applicant needs to come to the embassy in person to make the payment. For children over 2 years, parent or authorized person should come with the applicant. For children under 2 years, only parent should come with 4 copies of child’s photographs and 1 copy for each of their own.
- Attach 4 copies of applicant’s photographs and photocopies of Bangladeshi BRC or NID and Kuwaiti Civil ID/Eqama with the “New Application Form”. For children, 1 copy of parents’(both father and mother) photographs should also be attached.
- Take a token and approach to the payment counter with completed application form.

**Step-3: Bio-matric Enrolment**

- From the payment counter, you will be put in a queue for Bio-enrolment. Our operators will take your photograph and fingerprint and also give other necessary entries. After a few moments, you will be given a print copy of all required information being submitted to the passport system. You have the opportunity to make corrections for any mistake, then you confirm and sign it. The operator will finalize the submission and give you a DELIVERY SLIP with a date mentioned for delivery. The whole process of step-3 may usually take no more than 5 minutes for each applicant.

**Step-4: Done!!**

- You are done. You can now move elsewhere on your choice.

# \>Delivery of Passports

- Usually it takes 20 days for URGENT (express) and 40 days for NORMAL(regular) delivery from the date of submission. However, unavoidable circumstances might cause delay. Currently it is taking longer than usual. Applicants are advised to follow the date mentioned in the SEAL given on the delivery slip.

1.  Delivery of Passports starts at 2.00 pm every business day and continues until 4.00 pm. The applicant or a duly authorized person may receive the passport. In each case, the recipient will be asked to produce the original old manual passport (if any) and the original delivery slip at the delivery counter.
2.  While the new passport being delivered, the manual old passport will also be given back to you with an observation seal. Please check your passport before leaving the counter to ensure your new passport is the correct one and also you have received your old passport back.
