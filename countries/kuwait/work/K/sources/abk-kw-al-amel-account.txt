                                Al Amel

    Al Amel is a labor account for corporate and commercial establishments.

                            Features & Benefits

    A personal account for corporate or commercial establishment laborers

    Salaries of less than KD 300

    Free monthly account statement

    Free debit card issue/renewal for 5 years

   Dedicated branches in Sabhan and Salmiya for Al Amel laborers

                            Rules & Eligibility

    Only expatriate customers may open an Al Amel account

    Salary transfers from other banks are not allowed

                            Requirements

     Letter from company or establishment to open an Al Amel account

     Company Commercial License and Signature Authorization

     Copy of valid civil ID and passport copy including the address page and work permit
