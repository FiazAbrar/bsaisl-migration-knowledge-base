# Civil ID fee raised from ‘KD 2 to KD 5’

[By Kuwait Times](https://kuwaittimes.com/author/32/talal/ "talal")

March 30, 2016 \| 11:19 p.m EDT

KUWAIT: The Public Authority of Civil Information (PACI)’s director Musaed Al-Assoussi said that the new civil ID fees would be in effect from Sunday and that the fee would be KD 5 instead of KD 2.

Al-Assoussi added that the decision includes increasing the fees re-issue in cases of loss or damage from KD 10 to 20. The civil ID fees could be paid online at the PACI’s website.
