KUWAIT:
 The Ministry of Interior announced on Thursday that the Criminal Security Sector, in cooperation with the Public Authority for Manpower, conducted an inspection campaign in Shuwaikh Industrial Area that resulted in the arrest of 48 residency and labor law violators. In a press statement, the Ministry said the campaign included inspections and reviews of 120 commercial establishments as part of ongoing efforts to identify and apprehend individuals violating residency and labor regulations.
The Ministry said the arrested individuals included nine violators of Article 20, 36 violators of Article 18, one violator of Article 22, and one individual wanted under an arrest warrant in connection with a non-existent company registered under Article 18. It added that legal and administrative measures were taken against all violators in preparation for their referral to the relevant authorities. The Ministry of Interior reaffirmed that inspection campaigns will continue across various areas to enforce residency and labor laws and address violations. — KUNA
