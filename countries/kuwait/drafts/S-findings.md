## a. Draft doc text

### Is it open now, and who can go?

(Two H4s for N's section. "Status" replaces or merges with N's Status; the 2026 bullet goes into N's "What closed it before".)

#### Status

**On 9 November 2026 company work visas (Article 18) are being issued to Bangladeshis again, after the 2026 war with Iran closed Kuwait's airspace and stopped entry-visa issuing in the spring; new domestic-worker visas (Article 20) stay closed to Bangladeshis** [S0001, S0013, B0016, B0020, D0001, D0002]. BMET (Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training) cleared 3,914 workers for Kuwait in October 2026 and 1,305 in the first eight days of November [B0020, S0034].

- **Private-sector company job (Article 18):** open; BMET clearances recovered from 1,376 a month in April and May 2026 to 3,749 in July and 4,538 in September [B0024, B0019, B0021, B0016]. The 2021 rule that Bangladeshis needed special Interior Ministry approval no longer applies: since May 2025 Bangladeshis get visas without it [N0041, N0014, V0096].
- **Domestic worker, house driver, cook (Article 20):** closed to new Bangladeshi workers since a June 2026 Interior Ministry circular allowed recruitment from 10 countries only, without Bangladesh, and the Bangladesh Embassy is not attesting women's domestic visas (see Work visa: domestic worker (Article 20)) [D0001, D0002, N0026, N0031].
- **An entry visa issued before the war and never used:** it lapsed; work, domestic and family entry visas held by people outside Kuwait were not extended, and the employer must apply for a new one [S0012, S0013, B0203].
- **Visit visas:** commercial visit visas were suspended for all nationalities from 10 to 19 June 2026; the wartime one-month extensions of visit visas ended on 1 September 2026 (what is open now is in Visit visas (tourist, family, business)) [S0023, S0024, S0026].
- **Family (Article 22):** see Family visa (dependants).
- **Bangladeshis with residence who are stuck abroad:** the extra absence permits Kuwait gave from 28 February 2026 ended on 1 September 2026, except for people who left Kuwait on or before 31 August 2026 [S0009, S0014, S0026, S0027]. The residence status certificate in the Sahel app shows the date by which you must be back [S0028].
- **Getting there:** Kuwait's airspace was closed from 28 February to 23 April 2026; flights from Kuwait International Airport restarted on 26 April, and Kuwait Airways planned Dhaka flights four times a week [S0001, S0002, S0003]. Flights have stopped again at short notice: an Iranian drone hit Terminal 1 on 3 June 2026, the airspace closed again on 18 July 2026, and the Bangladesh Embassy issued emergency hotlines after new missile and drone attacks in early September 2026 (flights and fares are in Step 6: Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight) [S0021, S0025, B0202].

> **Need to know**
> - On what date, and by which Interior Ministry circular, did Kuwait resume issuing new entry visas (Article 18, 20 and 22) after the spring 2026 suspension?
> - When a Bangladeshi worker's Article 18 visa expired unused during the war, did the employer apply again at its own cost, and did brokers in Dhaka re-process the visa free or charge again?
> - Is Terminal 1 at Kuwait International Airport back in use in November 2026, and which terminal do Dhaka flights use?

#### What closed it before

(Add as the last dated bullet in N's list.)

- **28 February to spring 2026, the Iran war:** Kuwait closed its airspace from 28 February to 23 April 2026, and its airlines flew from Al-Qaisumah and Dammam in Saudi Arabia, with passengers taken four to five hours by road through the Nuwaiseeb border crossing [S0001, S0004, S0006]. Entry-visa issuing stopped: in April 2026 the Foreign Ministry told embassies that unused work, domestic and family entry visas would not be extended and that employers must reapply once issuing resumed [S0012, S0013]. BMET clearances for Kuwait fell from 2,610 in January to 1,376 in April and in May, and recovered from July [S0031, B0024, B0019, B0021].

## b. Notes (for notes.md)

**Timeline, January to November 2026 (with fact ids).**
- Jan 2026: 2,610 BMET clearances for Kuwait (S0031); Feb 1,852; Mar 1,539 (S0032).
- 28 Feb: US-Israeli strikes on Iran; Iranian retaliation hits Kuwait; Kuwait airspace closed (S0001). MOI extends all visit entry visas by one month and gives residents stuck abroad an automatic 3-month absence permit, free (S0008, S0009, S0014; B0204).
- ~1-3 Mar: embassies in Kuwait suspend consular services; Kuwaiti airspace closed but land border with Saudi Arabia open (S0007). Kuwait Airways and Jazeera fly from Al-Qaisumah and Dammam, passengers bussed 4-5 hours (S0004).
- 6-11 Apr (forum, practice only): an employer's mandoub was told on 6 Apr that only valid iqama/Civil ID holders could enter, not visit or first-time work visa holders, by land (S0010); from about 11 Apr Jazeera accepted new visit/family/work visa holders via Dammam with a Saudi transit visa on arrival (S0011). Indian VFS staff said Saudi transit visas were not being given to new Kuwait work-visa holders (in the saved forum page, not logged).
- 8 Apr: US-Iran ceasefire (Wikipedia lead, not saved); open-ended extension 21 Apr (in S0001's source).
- 22 Apr: MOFA protocol circular (Al-Jarida): no extension of unused entry visas (private work, domestic, family); employers must reapply "after the announcement of the resumption of issuing entry visas" (S0012, S0013). So issuing was suspended on 22 Apr. Same circular: residents >6 months abroad get automatic 3-month absence permits from 28 Feb (S0014). ITV Bangla report of the same (B0203, B0204).
- 23 Apr: airspace reopened; 24 Apr: Kuwait Airways Dhaka via Dammam twice weekly, overland through Nuwaiseeb (S0005, S0006); 26 Apr: Kuwait Airways (T4) and Jazeera (T5) resume from KWI; Dhaka 4x weekly (S0002, S0003, S0018). 28 Apr: US Embassy still suspended all consular services (S0019).
- April and May: BMET clearances 1,376 each (B0024, B0019; B re-checked the identical figures).
- 1 May-30 Jun: PAM Decision 2/2026 transfer window for workers in SMEs, industry, agriculture, livestock, fishing, with original employer's approval, via Ashal (S0015-S0017). For L (Changing employer or leaving), not used in my draft.
- 3 Jun: Iranian drone hits KWI Terminal 1; traffic suspended (S0021). Jun 2026: MOI circular, 10 countries for domestic workers (D0001-D0002).
- 10 Jun: commercial visit visas suspended for all nationalities; 19 Jun resumed (S0023, S0024). This implies visit-visa issuing had resumed before 10 June.
- 17 Jun: foreign airlines start returning via T4, one flight a day each (S0022). June clearances 2,036 (S0033).
- 18 Jul: airspace closed again after renewed Iranian attacks (S0025). July clearances 3,749 (B0021); Aug 3,669 (in source, not logged); Sep 4,538 (B0016).
- 20 Aug: MOI announces end of automatic extensions of entry visas and leave permits from 1 Sep; those who left on or before 31 Aug keep the extra extension; Sahel certificate is the reference (S0026-S0028).
- 3 Sep: Bangladesh Embassy warns of new missile and drone attacks, issues hotlines (B0202).
- Oct: 3,914 clearances (B0020); Oct 2026 Protidiner Bangladesh (N0026): domestic visas closed to Bangladeshis. Safe Airspace: Kuwait FIR closed and reopened several times Feb-Jul; threat eased, risk remains (S0029, S0030).
- 1-8 Nov: 1,305 clearances (S0034).

Indirect evidence that issuing is running again: commercial visit visas suspended 10 Jun and resumed 19 Jun (so issuing had restarted before then), and BMET clearances back to 3,700-4,500 a month from July. The Status sentence therefore says "being issued again" on clearance evidence and leaves the date as Need to know.

**Clearances are not departures.** BMET kept clearing 1,376-1,539 workers a month in March-May while Kuwait's airspace was shut and visa issuing was suspended, so many of those people were cleared on visas issued before the war and either waited or lost the visa (S0012). Worth an insider question: how many Bangladeshi workers lost an unused visa.

**Special approval (2021) and the 10-country list.** The November 2021 seven-nationality special-approval rule (N0041, V0098) was dropped for Bangladeshis by May 2025 (N0014, V0096; V0084 says all nationalities were opened in Oct 2025). Nothing in 2026 reinstates it. The June 2026 domestic-worker circular (D0001-D0003) is current: N0026/N0027 (Protidiner Bangladesh, 19 Oct 2026) confirm Bangladesh is still not on it. Whether it also stops Bangladeshi men as house drivers is D's open question.
