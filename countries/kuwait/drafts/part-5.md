## Family visa (dependants)

A resident whose own salary is at least KD 800 a month can sponsor his wife and children on a family residence, called "Article 22" or "visa 22" (التحاق بعائل, "joining a provider") [F0001, F0002]. The sponsor (the "provider") applies in Kuwait, and must send the family home at his own cost when their residence ends [F0001, F0015]. The Ministry of Interior (MOI) says the family visa is open to every nationality that meets the salary rule, and the residence regulations in force since 23 December 2025 name no nationality [F0083, F0002, F0105]. Since May 2025 Bangladeshis get Kuwaiti visas without the special approval imposed in 2021 [V0096, N0014].

A family entry visa issued before the 2026 Iran war and not used has lapsed: in April 2026 Kuwait's Foreign Ministry said unused entry visas, including family ones, held by people outside Kuwait would not be extended, and the sponsor must apply again once entry-visa issuing resumes [S0012, S0013].

### Who can apply

#### Who qualifies

- **Salary:** at least KD 800 a month, counted only from the job the sponsor's residence was granted for (the salary on the work permit), not from overtime or side work [F0002, F0065].
- **Only one salary counts:** a husband's and wife's salaries cannot be added together; only the sponsoring parent's salary is looked at [F0084].
- **No degree needed:** the university-degree requirement of January 2024 was dropped in July 2024 [F0080].
- **Exempt from the salary rule, if the job in Kuwait matches the person's qualification** [F0004]:
  - engineers, in government or private companies [F0005];
  - Ministry of Health and military pharmacists, nurses, paramedics and medical technicians [F0006];
  - mosque imams, preachers, muezzins and Quran teachers [F0007];
  - people who wash and bury the dead [F0008];
  - government legal researchers; university and college teachers; government school supervisors, teachers, social workers and psychologists [F0009, F0010, F0011];
  - journalists of international news stations; coaches and players of government sports federations and clubs [F0012, F0013].
- **Children who can be exempted:** the Director General of Residency Affairs may waive the salary rule for a child already in Kuwait or born in Kuwait, or born abroad and aged 5 or under to parents both resident in Kuwait; it is his decision case by case, not a right [F0003].
- **Who cannot sponsor:** domestic workers (Article 20) and students (Article 23) cannot sponsor children on a family visa [F0096].

The Bangladesh Embassy's minimum salaries for company workers are mostly KD 90 to 150 a month (KD 250 for a chef), far below KD 800, so most Bangladeshi workers do not qualify [M0018, M0020, M0021, M0022, F0002]. For them the routes left are a family visit visa (see Family visit visa) or a baby born in Kuwait, for whom no minimum salary applies (see Having a child in Kuwait) [F0023, F0141].

#### Who can come

- The wife and children [F0001, F0025].
- **Sons** can stay on a parent's family visa up to 21 unless still studying (proof needed) [F0098]; for children over 20 the renewal needs a recent attested school certificate [F0074].
- **Parents and other relatives** come under Article 29, not Article 22: the residence costs KD 300 a year each, plus KD 100 health insurance, and the General Department of Residency Affairs decides each case; in practice approval is given only on humanitarian grounds, such as an only child with a parent living alone [F0026, F0027, F0053, F0094].

## Visit visas (tourist, family, business)

On 9 November 2026 the Kuwait Visa portal lets a relative in Kuwait apply online for a Bangladeshi's family visit visa, but tells a Bangladeshi with no valid US, UK or Schengen visa or residence permit and no Gulf residence permit that they are not eligible for a tourist visa [M0003, M0001]. A business visit needs a Kuwaiti company to apply [M0010]. In practice, then, Bangladeshis come on a family visit, a business visit, or a tourist visa only if they already hold one of those other visas or permits [M0003, M0010, M0002].

All visit visas are issued online on Kuwait Visa, MOI's platform at kuwaitvisa.moi.gov.kw, which is also a mobile app [V0082, V0020]. The steps on the portal are: create an account, complete the profile, submit the application, and track updates [M0013]. Since 23 December 2025 the rules come from Ministerial Decision No. 2249/2025, the executive regulations of the foreigners' residence law, which recognise twelve types of visit visa [V0057, V0061]. A visit visa holder may not work and must leave when the visit ends [M0009]. A visitor caught working is deported together with the sponsor in Kuwait, and both go on the ban list [V0086].

The 2026 Iran war disrupted visits: MOI extended visit visas by one month from 28 February 2026, these automatic extensions ended on 1 September 2026, and commercial visit visas were suspended for all nationalities from 10 to 19 June 2026 [S0008, S0026, S0023, S0024].

### Cost and time

- **Visa fee:** KD 10 for each month of the visa, for family, tourist, business, government, private, medical and multiple-entry visits [V0051, V0057, V0059].
- **Extension:** KD 10 a month [V0053].
- **Health insurance:** compulsory since 23 December 2025, bought from a private insurer approved by the Ministry of Health; no visit visa is issued or renewed without it [V0065, V0112]. The sponsor in Kuwait or the visitor pays [V0066].
- **Refunds:** none; fees are collected only once the application is accepted [V0015, V0016].
- **Time:** a complete application can be issued in about five minutes; the portal says tourist visas usually take 1 to 2 working days [V0070, V0018].
- **Payment:** online, by credit card or another online payment option [V0022].

### Family visit visa

A relative who lives in Kuwait with a valid residence applies; the visitor does not [M0004, V0069]. Since August 2025 there is no minimum salary for the sponsor, and relatives up to the fourth degree by blood or the third degree by marriage can be invited: wife, children, parents, parents-in-law, brothers and sisters, uncles and aunts, nephews and nieces, grandparents [V0069, V0078, V0116]. There is no age limit for parents [V0088]. The visit is single entry, the visa must be used within 30 days of issue, and the stay is up to 90 days from entry [M0005].

#### Step 1: The relative in Kuwait checks they can sponsor

1. **Check the relationship is within the fourth degree** by blood or third by marriage [V0116].
2. **Check your own residence is valid,** because the portal checks the sponsor's eligibility for each relationship and can refuse with "Your sponsor is not eligible to sponsor your relationship" [V0103].

In practice officials look hard at sponsors on very low salaries, around KD 250 a month, and at visits by elderly relatives, judged on the sponsor's job and salary [V0085, V0120].

1. **Register on kuwaitvisa.moi.gov.kw** (or the Kuwait Visa app) and activate the account from the email link [V0027, V0082].

**If it fails:** the portal gives the reason where it can, and you can submit a new application once the problem is fixed [V0017]. 3. **At Kuwait airport, pass the security check;** visitors who fail it are sent back [V0083].
4. **Stay no longer than the visa allows:** a single-entry family visit lasts up to 90 days from entry [M0005].
5. **To extend, apply before the visa expires:** up to three months at a time, to one year in total, at KD 10 a month [V0105, V0054, V0053].
6. **Leave on time.** Overstaying costs KD 10 a day, and the visitor may be banned from returning [V0013].

- **The residence permit:** it must show the profession and be valid more than six months from entry [V0119].

### Business visit visa

A Kuwaiti company applies on Kuwait Visa for a foreign business visitor, with proof of the business purpose [M0010]. The visa is single entry, must be used within 30 days of issue, and allows a stay of up to 30 days [M0007]. It does not allow work [M0009].

### Government and private visits

## Self-sponsorship and investor residence

These residences are not tied to an employer; each needs proof of income, an approved investment, or property in Kuwait [F0028, F0034, F0031].

### Self-sponsorship (Article 24)

A foreigner who proves a source of income for the whole stay can live in Kuwait without a job (Article 24) [F0028]. The Director General of Residency Affairs sets the conditions and decides each application himself [F0028, F0066]. He may allow the holder to work, on a letter from a government body [F0050].

- **Residence:** KD 500 a year [F0029].
- **Health insurance:** KD 100 a year [F0055].
- **Each family member:** KD 100 a year residence [F0030].

### Investor residence (Article 21)

A foreign investor approved under the Direct Investment Promotion Law 116/2013 gets residence on a letter from KDIPA (the Kuwait Direct Investment Promotion Authority), for up to 15 years, at KD 50 a year [F0034, F0035, F0036]. Investors may stay outside Kuwait more than six months [F0044].

A foreigner who proves he owns property in Kuwait can get residence for up to 10 years, at KD 50 a year, and may stay outside Kuwait more than six months [F0031, F0033, F0037, F0044].

## Changing visa type

A residence can be changed from one type or purpose to another under the General Administration of Residence Affairs' rules [V0047]. Since 2 August 2026, converting a visit visa into a residence costs KD 150 (Interior Ministry Decision No. 1091 of 2026); domestic workers are exempt [V0089, F0049].

A visit visa can become a residence in five cases only [V0055]:

1. **Government visit visa to work residence:** for university graduates and technical specialists invited by a ministry or public body, with the Director General's approval [V0106].
2. **Domestic workers** [V0107].
3. **Family or tourist visit visa to dependant residence (joining a family sponsor):** for a wife, sons under 18 and daughters under 21, the sponsor must earn at least KD 800 a month and pay the conversion, health insurance and residence fees [V0108, V0093]. A family visit visa can never become an Article 18 work permit [V0094].
4. **A worker who entered on a work entry visa, started the residence process, had to leave and came back within one month** [V0109].
5. **Other cases** the Director General of Residence Affairs accepts [V0110].

Each case is judged individually and needs the Director General's approval [V0125]. In August 2026 hundreds of applications were rejected because applicants did not meet the conditions [V0092]. A dependant on a family visa can be allowed to move to another residence without the sponsor's consent [F0014]. For a wife moving from her own work visa to her husband's family visa, see Moving your wife from a work visa to a family visa. For moving from a domestic worker visa (Article 20) to a private-sector work visa (Article 18), see Changing employer or leaving.

## Asylum

Kuwait gives no asylum: it is not a party to the 1951 Refugee Convention or its 1967 Protocol, and its law has no asylum or refugee status [F0124, F0129]. A person asking for protection is treated under the ordinary residence law, with no special status [F0125]. Only UNHCR (the UN refugee agency) decides refugee claims, under its own mandate; the government generally cooperates with it [F0126, F0130]. UNHCR counts 386 refugees and 611 asylum seekers in Kuwait, mainly from Iraq, Syria and Yemen [F0128, F0127].

> **Need to know**
> - Where is UNHCR's office in Kuwait, and does it register new claims from non-Arab nationals?

## Having a child in Kuwait

A baby born in Kuwait needs four things, in this order: a Kuwaiti birth certificate, a Bangladeshi birth registration, a Bangladeshi e-passport, and Kuwaiti residence within four months of birth [F0099, F0113, F0139, F0042]. No minimum salary is needed to sponsor a baby born in Kuwait [F0141, F0003]. The baby pays no health insurance fee for its first four months, until it gets a passport or leaves Kuwait [F0059].

### Step 1: Get the Kuwaiti birth certificate

You'll need: the marriage certificate, attested and translated into Arabic (see Step 2: Attest the marriage and birth certificates in Bangladesh) [F0138].

1. **Register the birth at the Ministry of Health** and get the birth certificate [F0099].
2. **Have it attested by Kuwait's Ministry of Foreign Affairs** [F0100, F0112].

> - What happens to a baby born to parents who are not married, or to a mother on a work or domestic visa with no husband in Kuwait?

### Step 2: Register the birth with the Bangladesh Embassy

1. **Fill in the online form** at bdris.gov.bd/br/application, print it and sign it [F0113].
2. **Fill in the Embassy's application to the Consular section,** with a photo glued on, saying why you apply through the Embassy and declaring that no digital birth certificate was taken before [F0114].
3. **Attach** the hospital discharge paper or birth document (proof of place and date of birth), and copies of the first 7 pages of the father's or mother's passport; the permanent address in that passport becomes the baby's [F0115, F0116].
4. **Pay at the Embassy, cash only:** free within 45 days of birth, KD 0.500 after; a correction costs KD 0.750 [F0117, F0118, F0119, F0120].

### Step 3: Get the baby a Bangladeshi e-passport

1. **Apply online at epassport.gov.bd** and take an appointment; attach the printout with the appointment page, a copy of the online birth registration (BRC), and the passport and Kuwait Civil ID copies the Embassy's list asks for [F0135].
2. **On the appointment day,** take a token at the information centre in the basement of building 2, pay at counter 2 or 3, do the photo and fingerprints (bio-enrolment) in room 20, and keep the delivery slip [F0136].
3. **Check the status on epassport.gov.bd;** if it shows "REWORK", see the First Secretary (Passport and Visa) in room 10; when it shows "READY FOR ISSUANCE", hand in the delivery slip between 9 am and 3 pm and collect the passport at counter 1 [F0137].

Fees for a 48-page passport [F0133, F0134]:
- **Workers and students** (labourers, drivers, cooks, painters, cleaners): 5 years KD 9.500 regular, KD 14.000 urgent.
- **Other professions:** 5 years KD 31.000 regular, KD 46.500 urgent.

> **Need to know**

> - How many weeks does a baby's e-passport take from the Embassy in Kuwait in 2026?

### Step 4: Get the baby's Kuwaiti residence and Civil ID

1. **Within four months of birth, go to the General Department of Residency Affairs** with the baby's passport for residence; otherwise the baby is given a notice to leave Kuwait [F0042].
2. **Bring** both parents' passports and Civil IDs, the attested marriage certificate, health insurance for the baby and the residence forms [F0101, F0138, F0140].
3. **Register the baby with PACI** for its Civil ID; the deadline is 120 days from birth [F0103, F0104].
