## a. Draft doc text

### Bringing your family

(For Work visa: private-sector worker (Article 18), as its own H3.)

A private-sector worker can bring a wife and children only if his monthly salary on his work permit is at least KD 800 (see Family visa (dependants)) [F0002, F0065]. The Bangladesh Embassy's minimum salaries for company visas are KD 90 to 150 (see What the job pays) [N0085]. For a worker below KD 800 the routes left are a family visit visa (see Visit visas (tourist, family, business)) or a baby born in Kuwait, for whom the salary rule does not apply (see Having a child in Kuwait) [F0003, F0141]. Combining the husband's and wife's salaries does not count: only the sponsoring parent's salary is looked at [F0084].

## Family visa (dependants)

A resident whose own salary is at least KD 800 a month can sponsor his wife and children on a family residence, called "Article 22" or "visa 22" (التحاق بعائل, "joining a provider") [F0001, F0002]. The sponsor (the "provider") applies in Kuwait, and must send the family home at his own cost when their residence ends [F0001, F0015].

**Status for Bangladeshis (9 November 2026):** the Interior Ministry says the family visa is open to all nationalities as long as the salary rule is met, and the regulations in force since 23 December 2025 name no nationality [F0083, F0002, F0105].

> **Need to know**

> - How many Article 22 family visas did Kuwait issue to Bangladeshis in 2025 and 2026?

### Who can apply

#### Who qualifies

- **Salary:** at least KD 800 a month, counted only from the job the sponsor's residence was granted for (the salary on the work permit), not from overtime or side work [F0002, F0065].
- **No degree needed:** the university-degree requirement of January 2024 was dropped in July 2024 [F0080].
- **Exempt from the salary rule, if the job in Kuwait matches the person's qualification** [F0004]:
  - engineers, in government or private companies [F0005];
  - Ministry of Health and military pharmacists, nurses, paramedics and medical technicians [F0006];
  - mosque imams, preachers, muezzins and Quran teachers [F0007];
  - people who wash and bury the dead [F0008];
  - government legal researchers; university and college teachers; government school supervisors, teachers, social workers and psychologists [F0009, F0010, F0011];
  - journalists of international news stations; coaches and players of government sports federations and clubs [F0012, F0013].
- **Children who can be exempted:** the Director General of Residency Affairs may waive the salary rule for a child already in Kuwait or born in Kuwait, or born abroad and aged 5 or under to parents both resident in Kuwait; this is his decision case by case, not a right [F0003].
- **Who cannot sponsor:** domestic workers (Article 20) and students (Article 23) cannot sponsor children on a family visa [F0096].

> **Need to know**
> - Which Bangladeshi job titles commonly reach KD 800 on the work permit (engineers aside), and how many Bangladeshis in Kuwait hold family visas?
> - Does MOI accept a work permit salary that the employer raised shortly before the application, or does it check months of salary transfers?

#### Who can come

- The wife and children [F0001, F0025].
- **Sons** can stay on a parent's family visa up to 21 unless still studying (proof needed) [F0098]; for children over 20 the renewal needs a recent attested school certificate [F0074].
- **Parents and other relatives** come under Article 29, not Article 22: the residence costs KD 300 a year each, plus KD 100 health insurance, and the General Department of Residency Affairs decides each case under its own rules; in practice approval is given only on humanitarian grounds (for example an only child with a parent living alone) [F0026, F0027, F0053, F0094].

> **Need to know**
> - Until what age can a daughter stay on her father's family visa, and what happens when she marries?

### Cost and time

- Entry visa for each family member: KD 10 [F0024].
- Health insurance with the entry visa: KD 5 a month per person [F0051].
- Residence: KD 20 per person per year (sponsor on a company or government job) [F0025].
- Health insurance with residence: KD 100 per person per year, paid when the residence is issued or renewed; it is never refunded [F0052, F0061].
- Converting a family visit visa or tourist visa into a family residence inside Kuwait: KD 150 extra (since 2 August 2026) [F0023, F0049].
- Attesting the family visa at the Bangladesh Embassy in Kuwait: KD 3 per person [F0110].

> **Need to know**
> - How long does MOI take to issue an Article 22 entry visa in 2026, from application to approval?
> - What do typing centres charge to prepare the family visa forms?

### Step 1: Check you qualify and gather the papers

1. **Check the salary on your work permit** (from Ashal, PAM's online system, or your employer) is KD 800 or more, or that your job is on the exempt list (see Who qualifies) [F0002, F0004, F0065].
2. **Gather the papers MOI asks for** [F0062, F0063, F0064, F0065, F0142, F0086, F0109, F0089]:
   - the entry visa form, prepared at a typing centre;
   - a copy of each family member's passport, valid at least two years;
   - a copy of your Civil ID;
   - proof of relationship: for non-Arab foreigners MOI asks for a certificate from the embassy, in practice the Bangladesh Embassy's Relationship Certificate, together with the attested marriage certificate and children's birth certificates (Step 2);
   - a copy of your work permit (company workers) to prove the salary, and a recent salary certificate from your employer;
   - proof of accommodation in Kuwait, often the rent contract linked to your PACI address.

> **Need to know**
> - What does the Bangladesh Embassy in Kuwait charge for a Relationship Certificate, and what papers does it need?
> - Does MOI still require a passport valid two years for a dependant, when the 2025 regulations require only six months? [F0062, F0017]

### Step 2: Attest the marriage and birth certificates in Bangladesh

You'll need: the original nikahnama (marriage certificate) and each child's online birth registration certificate (BRC) [F0123, F0122].

1. **Notarise the nikahnama and each birth certificate** with a notary public in Bangladesh [F0122, F0123].
2. **Have them attested by the Law Ministry, then by the Consular Section of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) in Dhaka,** which the Bangladesh Embassy in Kuwait requires on every Bangladeshi document [F0122, F0123, F0111].
3. **Have them attested by the Embassy of Kuwait in Dhaka, then by Kuwait's Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Kuwait** [F0088].

> **Need to know**
> - What does the Embassy of Kuwait in Dhaka charge to attest a nikahnama or birth certificate, where is its counter, and how many days does it take?
> - Does the Kuwaiti embassy accept the new MOFA e-Apostille, or only the old stamp? (Kuwait is not on MOFA's list of countries that accept a Bangladesh apostille.)
> - Must the nikahnama be translated into Arabic in Kuwait by a certified translator before MOI accepts it?

### Step 3: Police clearance for each adult

1. **Get each family member over 18 a Bangladesh police clearance** (how: Work visa: private-sector worker (Article 18), Step 4) [F0072].
2. **Have it attested by MOFA in Dhaka and by the Embassy of Kuwait in Dhaka;** it must be less than three months old when MOI gets it [F0020, F0072].

### Step 4: Apply in Kuwait

1. **Book an appointment** on the Meta platform (the Kuwaiti government's appointment system) at the residency affairs department (Jawazat) of the governorate where you live [F0085, F0087].
2. **Submit the papers from Steps 1-3 in person** with the typing-centre form; the department checks them and the manager approves [F0087, F0085].
3. **Pay** the KD 10 entry visa and the health insurance (see Cost and time) [F0024, F0051].
4. **Send the issued entry visa to your family.** Each person must enter Kuwait and finish residence within two months of arrival (Step 6) [F0016].

**If you are summoned later:** residency investigators match your salary, job and residence records automatically; in May 2025 they summoned residents whose salary had dropped below KD 800 after the family came, made them sign a pledge, and gave them one month to fix their status or send the family home [F0081, F0082, F0079].

### Step 5: Have the family visa attested at the Bangladesh Embassy in Kuwait

You'll need [F0108, F0107, F0109]:
- 2 copies of the visa;
- a copy of your Civil ID;
- copies of the passports of husband, wife and children;
- a copy of the Relationship Certificate.

1. **Go to the Embassy** in Messila, Block 7, Street 16, House 91 and 93 (phone +965 23900913), Sunday to Thursday, 8 am to 2 pm [F0121, F0131, F0110].
2. **Hand in the papers and pay KD 3 per person;** the attested visa is returned the same day [F0110].

> **Need to know**
> - Does BMET or the airport in Dhaka check this Embassy attestation when a wife or child flies to Kuwait on a family visa, or is it optional?

### Step 6: After arrival: medical, fingerprints, residence and Civil ID

Each family member must finish these within two months of arrival [F0016].

1. **Medical test** at the Ministry of Health; adults also give fingerprints at the General Department of Criminal Evidence; children under 18 are exempt from both [F0021, F0022, F0069].
2. **Apply for the residence** at the residency affairs department with [F0146, F0147, F0068, F0069, F0070, F0071, F0072, F0073]:
   - the form signed by you as sponsor;
   - the original entry visa;
   - the computer-printed medical fitness result;
   - 2 photos 4x6 cm, blue background;
   - the original passport;
   - a copy of your Civil ID;
   - the health insurance certificate;
   - for adults, the attested police clearance (Step 3);
   - for your wife, a copy of the marriage contract.
3. **Pay** KD 20 residence and KD 100 health insurance per person per year (see Cost and time) [F0025, F0052].
4. **Get the Civil ID** for each person from PACI (the Public Authority for Civil Information) and pay its fee on PACI's website [F0093].

### Keeping the family visa

- The family's residence is tied to its purpose and ends when your own job-based residence ends [F0018].
- Renewal needs the form signed by you, 2 photos, your Civil ID copy, the original passport, the health insurance certificate, a recent attested school certificate for children over 20, and a copy of the wife's valid residence for the children [F0145, F0071, F0074, F0075].
- If your salary falls below KD 800 the family can be ordered home (Step 4, after item 4) [F0079].
- A family member who stays outside Kuwait more than six months loses the residence unless an absence permit is bought, at KD 5 a month (see How long you can stay) [F0043, F0045].

> **Need to know**
> - Can Article 22 residences be renewed online (Sahel app or MOI e-services), or only in person?
> - If the sponsor loses his job, how long does the family's residence remain valid before they must leave?

### Moving your wife from a work visa to a family visa

If your wife already works in Kuwait on her own work visa (Article 18), you can move her residence to your family visa if your salary qualifies [F0002, F0087].

1. **Prepare the transfer papers at a typing centre** [F0087]:
   - her original passport and copy, residence and Civil ID copies, the attested marriage certificate and photos [F0088];
   - your Civil ID, passport copy, a recent salary certificate in Arabic, your work permit copy from Ashal or PAM, and the rent contract if asked [F0089];
   - from her employer: a no-objection certificate, the cancellation of her work residence, PACI clearance, and the PAM (Public Authority for Manpower) work-permit cancellation and transfer form; the form is signed by both the old and the new sponsor [F0091, F0144, F0076, F0077].
2. **Go to the immigration department (Jawazat) for your address with her company's representative (mandoop),** who must be present throughout [F0087, F0090].
3. **Pay the health insurance and any fines,** then the KD 20 fee at the final counter [F0143, F0092].
4. **Pay the Civil ID fee on PACI's website** [F0093].

### Can a family member work?

A family member on Article 22 may not work; to work, they must move to a work visa [F0097]. The Director General of Residency Affairs can allow a dependant to move to another residence without the sponsor's consent (see Changing visa type) [F0014].

### Family of a Kuwaiti

- **Foreign wife of a Kuwaiti man:** she is entitled to residence (Article 26); it costs KD 10 a year and she pays no health insurance fee [F0038, F0039, F0054]. A Kuwaiti woman may sponsor a foreign husband only if she did not get Kuwaiti nationality by marriage [F0038].
- **Widow or divorcee of a Kuwaiti with his children:** entitled to residence (Article 28) [F0040].
- **Children of a Kuwaiti mother:** entitled to residence, for up to 10 years [F0041, F0033].

## Self-sponsorship and investor residence

These residences are not tied to an employer; each needs proof of income, an approved investment, or property in Kuwait [F0028, F0034, F0031].

### Self-sponsorship (Article 24)

A foreigner who proves a source of income for the whole stay can live in Kuwait without a job (Article 24) [F0028]. The Director General of Residency Affairs sets the conditions and decides each application himself [F0028, F0066]. He may allow the holder to work, on a letter from a government body [F0050].

- Residence: KD 500 a year [F0029].
- Health insurance: KD 100 a year [F0055].
- Each family member: KD 100 a year residence [F0030].

> **Need to know**
> - What proof of income does MOI accept for Article 24 in 2026 (bank deposit amount, pension), and who gets it in practice (retirees, long-term residents over 60)?

### Investor residence (Article 21)

A foreign investor approved under the Direct Investment Promotion Law 116/2013 gets residence on a letter from KDIPA (the Kuwait Direct Investment Promotion Authority), for up to 15 years, at KD 50 a year [F0034, F0035, F0036]. Investors may stay outside Kuwait more than six months [F0044].

### Property-owner residence (Article 25)

A foreigner who proves he owns property in Kuwait can get residence for up to 10 years, at KD 50 a year, and may stay outside Kuwait more than six months [F0031, F0033, F0037, F0044].

> **Need to know**
> - Can a Bangladeshi individual legally buy property in Kuwait at all under Law 74/1979 on non-Kuwaiti ownership (reported to allow only Arab nationals, by decree)?

## Asylum

Kuwait gives no asylum: it is not a party to the 1951 Refugee Convention or its 1967 Protocol, and its law has no asylum or refugee status [F0124, F0129]. A person asking for protection is treated under the ordinary residence law, with no special status [F0125]. Only UNHCR (the UN refugee agency) decides refugee claims, under its own mandate; the government generally cooperates with it [F0126, F0130]. UNHCR counts 386 refugees and 611 asylum seekers in Kuwait, mainly from Iraq, Syria and Yemen [F0128, F0127].

> **Need to know**
> - Where is UNHCR's office in Kuwait, and does it register new claims from non-Arab nationals?

## Having a child in Kuwait

A baby born in Kuwait needs four things, in this order: a Kuwaiti birth certificate, a Bangladeshi birth registration, a Bangladeshi e-passport, and Kuwaiti residence within four months of birth [F0099, F0113, F0139, F0042]. No minimum salary is needed to sponsor a baby born in Kuwait [F0141, F0003]. The baby pays no health insurance fee for its first four months, until it gets a passport or leaves Kuwait [F0059].

### Step 1: Get the Kuwaiti birth certificate

1. **Register the birth at the Ministry of Health** and get the birth certificate [F0099].
2. **Have it attested by Kuwait's Ministry of Foreign Affairs** [F0100, F0112].

You'll need the marriage certificate attested and translated into Arabic (Family visa (dependants), Step 2) [F0138].

> **Need to know**
> - Which Ministry of Health office registers births of non-Kuwaitis (the hospital's births office or the governorate's births and deaths department), what does the certificate cost, and what is the deadline?
> - What happens to a baby born to parents who are not married, or to a mother on a work or domestic visa with no husband in Kuwait?

### Step 2: Register the birth with the Bangladesh Embassy

1. **Fill in the online form** at bdris.gov.bd/br/application, print it and sign it [F0113].
2. **Fill in the Embassy's application to the Consular section,** with a photo glued on, saying why you apply through the Embassy and declaring that no digital birth certificate was taken before [F0114].
3. **Attach** the hospital discharge paper or birth document (proof of place and date of birth), and copies of the first 7 pages of the father's or mother's passport; the permanent address in that passport becomes the baby's [F0115, F0116].
4. **Pay at the Embassy, cash only:** free within 45 days of birth, KD 0.500 after; a correction costs KD 0.750 [F0117, F0118, F0119, F0120].

### Step 3: Get the baby a Bangladeshi e-passport

1. **Apply online at epassport.gov.bd** and take an appointment; attach the printout with the appointment page, a copy of the online birth registration (BRC), and the passport and Kuwait Civil ID copies the Embassy's list asks for [F0135].
2. **On the appointment day,** take a token at the information centre in the basement of building 2, pay at counter 2 or 3, do the photo and fingerprints (bio-enrolment) in room 20, and keep the delivery slip [F0136].
3. **Check the status on epassport.gov.bd;** if it shows "REWORK", see the First Secretary (Passport and Visa) in room 10; when it shows "READY FOR ISSUANCE", hand in the delivery slip between 9 am and 3 pm and collect the passport at counter 1 [F0137].

Fees for a 48-page passport [F0133, F0134]:
- workers and students (labourers, drivers, cooks, painters, cleaners): 5 years KD 9.500 regular, KD 14.000 urgent;
- other professions: 5 years KD 31.000 regular, KD 46.500 urgent.

> **Need to know**
> - Which fee does the Embassy charge for a baby: the workers' rate (KD 9.500) because the parent is a worker, or the general rate (KD 31.000)?
> - How many weeks does a baby's e-passport take from the Embassy in Kuwait in 2026?

### Step 4: Get the baby's Kuwaiti residence and Civil ID

1. **Within four months of birth,** go to the General Department of Residency Affairs with the baby's passport for residence; otherwise the baby is given a notice to leave Kuwait [F0042].
2. **Bring** both parents' passports and Civil IDs, the attested marriage certificate, health insurance for the baby and the residence forms [F0101, F0138, F0140].
3. **Register the baby with PACI** for its Civil ID; the deadline is 120 days from birth [F0103, F0104].

> **Need to know**
> - What fine applies if the baby's residence is not applied for within four months?
> - Can a domestic worker or a worker on a salary below KD 800 sponsor a baby born in Kuwait, or must the baby leave?
