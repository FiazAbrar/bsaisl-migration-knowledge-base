### For "Is it open now, and who can go?" > Size (material for N)

BMET (Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training) cleared 36,445 workers for Kuwait in 2023, 33,015 in 2024, 42,746 in 2025 and 27,964 from 1 January to 8 November 2026 [B0001, B0002, B0003, B0004]. Kuwait was the 7th destination in 2023, 6th in 2024, 4th in 2025 and 5th in 2026 to 7 November [B0009, B0010, B0011, B0012]. Few women go: 1,183 in 2023, 414 in 2024, 488 in 2025 and 230 in 2026 to 8 November [B0005, B0006, B0007, B0008].

Most months see about 1,400 to 4,500 clearances, with peaks: 6,176 in July 2023, 4,274 in November 2024, 6,130 in July 2025 and 12,192 in August 2025, the month the Embassy's minimum wage scale took effect for new contracts [B0013, B0014, B0017, B0015, B0187]. In 2026 clearances fell to 1,376 in April and 1,376 in May, then rose to 3,749 in July, 4,538 in September and 3,914 in October [B0024, B0019, B0021, B0016, B0020].

### Work visa: private-sector worker (Article 18)

#### Step 1: Find a real job

There are two routes: an agency job order with a BMET permission letter (Step 1, item 3), or a single visa that a relative or contact in Kuwait gets from a sponsor (Step 1, item 4) [B0169, B0101]. Both start with OEP registration (Step 1, item 1): an agency must choose workers from BMET's database, and BMET gives no clearance to a worker outside it [B0170]. Since 1 August 2025 every Kuwait work visa must also pass the Bangladesh Embassy's attestation in Kuwait, which checks pay against the Embassy's minimum wage scale (Step 1, item 5) [B0112, B0187, B0192].

1. **Register on the Overseas Employment Platform (OEP),** BMET's website for job-seekers [B0038].

   You'll need [B0037, B0038]:
   - your NID (national ID card);
   - a mobile number;
   - Tk 200 for the government fee.

   1. Open [employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration](https://employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration) [B0038].
   2. Type your name in English, mobile and NID numbers, date of birth, division and district exactly as on the NID, the captcha, tick the terms and press "যাচাই করুন" (Verify) [B0037].
   3. Print 2 copies of the registration slip and give your fingerprints at the district office (DEMO, District Employment and Manpower Office) [B0038]. DEMO addresses are listed on BMET's website [B0039].

   **If it fails:** call the OEP hotline, 09614445271 [B0046].

2. **Learn a trade if you need one.** BMET lists every TTC (Technical Training Centre) and IMT (Institute of Marine Technology) with contacts on its website [B0040]. Skilled trades get a higher Embassy minimum wage in Kuwait (KWD 150 against KWD 90 for cleaners and general workers; see Step 1, item 5) [B0191, B0190].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does Kuwait or BMET require a skill test or NSDA certificate from Bangladeshi Article 18 workers (drivers, electricians, cooks) before the visa or clearance?

3. **Through a recruiting agency: find an agency that holds a Kuwait job order, then check its permission letter.** A Kuwait job order reaches Bangladesh as a permission letter from BMET (until December 2024 from the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare) to one licensed agency, naming the employer, each job, the pay, the contract, the conditions and the most the agency may take from you [B0169, B0177, B0179]. The Kuwait letters read for this guide are for cleaners and general labour at KWD 75 to 90 a month on 2- or 3-year contracts [B0169, B0177, B0178, B0179].

   Examples:
   - **Rowshon Habib Overseas (RL-2011), 24 December 2024:** 300 cleaners for Al Faisalya Company, Sabhan; KWD 75 a month; 3-year contract; employer pays housing, transport, medical care and insurance; 8 hours a day, 6 days a week; the agency may take at most Tk 44,370 [B0169].
   - **Times Enterprise (RL-0385), 21 July 2025:** 350 men as general labour for JM General Contracting Co.; KWD 80 a month; 2-year contract; 100-day probation; employer pays the visa fee, housing, basic furniture, transport, medical care and air ticket; at most Tk 1,05,000 [B0178].
   - **Delight Overseas (RL-2287), 7 September 2025:** 266 men as office cleaners for Prosh International, Farwaniya; KWD 90 a month; 3-year contract; 8 hours a day, 1 day off a week; employer pays housing, transport, medical care and air ticket; Embassy attestation no. 174 of 2 September 2025; at most Tk 81,730 [B0179].

   Every Kuwait letter carries the same conditions [B0170, B0171, B0172, B0173, B0174, B0175]:
   - the employer bears all Kuwait-side costs (levy, airfare);
   - the agency may take its fee only after your visa and flight are confirmed, and only by cheque, bank draft or pay order;
   - you sign the contract before clearance and get a Bangla translation attested by BMET;
   - an agency representative flies with your group or meets you at Kuwait airport;
   - if you don't get the promised job and pay, the agency has signed (on Tk 300 stamp paper) to pay your food, lodging, fare home and any compensation set by the authorities;
   - after your group arrives the agency must report to the Ministry, BMET and the Bangladesh Embassy in Kuwait.

   **Which agencies hold Kuwait job orders.** These counts are our own tally of the letters on BMET's list: 48 Kuwait letters from 29 agencies, 29 December 2024 to 8 September 2025, for at least 6,910 posts [B0180]. A post is a worker the letter lets the agency process, not a person who went [B0180]. The agencies with the most posts: Delight Overseas (RL-2287), Prestige International (RL-0232), Heaven Overseas (RL-0600), Masud Jamil Overseas (RL-1400), Loyal Manpower Service (RL-2065), Talukdar Overseas (RL-1923), Times Enterprise (RL-0385) and Rowshon Habib Overseas (RL-2011) [B0180]. A high count shows activity, not fair treatment. Red flag: in January 2025 BMET let Masud Jamil Overseas recruit 205 women cleaners for Wael Al-Nusif & Partners Trading Co. on 2-year contracts at at most Tk 1,05,000; ITV (August 2025) reports that the Embassy has since blacklisted Wael Al-Nusif, accused of bringing workers on 3- to 6-month contracts and taking Tk 3 to 4 lakh from each [B0177, B0195].

   **Check the letter.** You'll need: the agency's RL number and the employer's name [B0180].
   1. Open [bmet.gov.bd/pages/miscellaneous-infos](https://bmet.gov.bd/pages/miscellaneous-infos) and search your agency's RL number, then "কুয়েত" [B0180, B0182].
   2. Open the PDF and compare the employer, job, pay, contract length and maximum fee with what you were told [B0169, B0179].
   3. Check the Embassy attestation number on the letter matches your demand letter (it appears as "দূতাবাসের সত্যায়ন নং" or, on BMET's system page, "Block visa number") [B0179, B0176].

   This check rarely works: BMET's newest posted letter is from 10 December 2025, the 48 Kuwait letters cover a fraction of the 42,746 workers cleared for Kuwait in 2025, a search for "কুয়েত" finds only 3 of them and a search for "Kuwait" finds none [B0181, B0180, B0003, B0182, B0183]. A single visa (Step 1, item 4) has no permission letter.

   > **Need to know**
   > - Where does BMET publish Kuwait permission letters issued since December 2025, for the 27,964 workers cleared in 2026 [B0004]?
   > - How does a worker apply to an agency holding a Kuwait job order: at its office, through its brokers, or online?

4. **Through a relative or contact in Kuwait (single visa): the sponsor gets the visa and has it attested at the Bangladesh Embassy in Kuwait.** The Embassy attests a single visa from a small company (Visa 18) or a household (Visa 20) only with the sponsor (kafeel) present, or, since 18 June 2025, a person holding his power of attorney (towkil khas) or his letter of authorisation, who must show the sponsor's original Civil ID [B0101, B0102, B0103].

   The sponsor or his representative brings [B0106, B0104, B0105, B0107]:
   - the visa attestation contract form signed by the sponsor (free at Room 03 of the Embassy);
   - 2 copies of the visa and 2 photos of the worker;
   - the sponsor's original Civil ID and a copy;
   - for Visa 18: the Employer Signature Approval Certificate (Itemat Toukiya) from PAM (Public Authority for Manpower) and the work permit (Tasriah Amel);
   - for Visa 20: a copy of the sponsor's passport with the signature page.

   The fee is KWD 3 per person, non-refundable, cash or KNET (Kuwait's debit card network) at Room 3; the visa is attested the same day and collected from Room 3 at 4 pm [B0109, B0110, B0108]. Hours: 8 am to 2 pm, Sunday to Thursday [B0168].

   In July 2025 the Embassy received about 450 attestation requests a day and required employers or their registered agents to appear in person [B0197, B0198]. A worker who got a single visa applies for BMET clearance himself (Step 6, item 3) [B0086].

   **Scams:** an activist told bdnews24 (July 2025) that the visa costs KWD 3 and Embassy attestation KWD 3, while agents sell such a visa for up to KWD 2,000 (about Tk 8 lakh); workers who went in May and June 2025 paid Tk 6.5 to 7 lakh [B0200, B0199]. The Embassy says buying or selling a visa is a crime for both giver and taker [B0184].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What does a Kuwait Article 18 single visa from a relative's company sell for in 2026, and who in the chain takes what?
   > - Can a worker in Bangladesh confirm that his visa was attested by the Embassy (by phone, email or the attestation number), and how?

5. **Have the visa and demand letter checked before paying.** BMET checks job visas and demand letters free within 3 working days, but checks only Saudi, Qatari, Bahraini, Singapore and UAE visas online, not Kuwaiti ones; bring the visa and, if stamped, a copy of the passport page [B0087]. Do this at your DEMO or BMET, 89/2 Kakrail, Dhaka [B0093, B0069].

   What a genuine Kuwait job order has behind it:
   - **Embassy attestation.** Any Kuwaiti company or farm recruiting Bangladeshis must have its demand letter attested by the Embassy [B0112]. The company's representative comes in person with the demand letter, a power of attorney to the Bangladeshi agency, the employment contract, its service contract or tender, PAM work permits for the workers, licences, the owner's Civil ID and proof of the workers' hostel (ownership or lease) [B0117, B0115, B0116, B0114, B0113]. It pays KWD 0.600 per worker at counter 4 [B0118, B0119].
   - **The Embassy's minimum wage.** For contracts from 1 August 2025 the Embassy attests no visa below its minimum monthly wage, for an 8-hour day [B0187, B0192]:
     - Article 18 on a government contract: agricultural worker, cleaner, general worker KWD 90; driver KWD 120 [B0189];
     - Article 18 private contract: cleaner, general worker, guard KWD 90; agricultural worker, shepherd KWD 120 [B0190];
     - Article 18 skilled trades (AC technician, mechanic, electrician, plumber, builder, painter and others) KWD 150 [B0191];
     - Article 20 household: domestic worker and general worker KWD 120, cook and driver KWD 150 [B0188].
   - **A contract of at least 2 years.** Before attestation the company signs a certificate on a contract of at least 2 years, the wage scale and the workers' benefits [B0193].

   **Check it is real:** a job offer below these minimums, or a contract shorter than 2 years, should not have passed the Embassy [B0192, B0193].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Is the Embassy's wage scale circular (July 2025) published on kuwait.mofa.gov.bd or its Facebook page, and has it been revised since?

**Before paying:**

- **Run the checks first:** the permission letter (Step 1, item 3) and BMET's visa check (Step 1, item 5) [B0180, B0087].
- **Know the cap.** The Embassy says the government's maximum for Kuwait is Tk 1,06,780 [B0184]. Each permission letter sets its own, often lower, maximum: Tk 44,370 to Tk 1,05,000 in the 2024-2025 letters [B0169, B0179, B0178]. The worker pays nothing for the Kuwait side: the employer bears the levy and airfare [B0171]. Your own costs come on top: the passport (from Tk 4,025, Step 4, item 1) and BMET's welfare fee, insurance, clearance fee and source tax (Step 6, item 3) [B0048, B0026, B0094, B0085].
- **Pay late, by bank.** The agency may take its money only once your visa and flight are confirmed, by cheque, bank draft or pay order [B0171]; pay into the agency's bank account and keep receipts [B0092].
- **Don't go on a visit visa to work:** the Ministry warns that brokers send people to the Middle East on tourist or visit visas with false job promises [B0065].
- **Borrow from PKB** (Probashi Kallyan Bank, the government bank for migrants) once you have a visa: up to Tk 3 lakh at 9% simple interest, repaid monthly over up to 3 years, decided within 7 working days [B0030].

  You'll need [B0028, B0029]:
  - the free bank form, 3 photos, NID copy;
  - passport, visa and BMET card copies and the contract;
  - 3 signed MICR cheque leaves from your own account with a statement;
  - two guarantors: one financially solvent person with cheques, statement and proof of income, and one family member or close relative.

  You and the guarantors must live in the branch's area and you must be 18 to 60 [B0027]. Fees: 0.5% processing and 0.5% documentation, plus VAT [B0030]. Women can take PKB's women's migration loan, with one guarantor and only the documentation fee [B0032]. Apply at the branch or on mygov.bd, which sends application and tracking numbers by SMS [B0033]. Helpline 16238, or +880 9677-787878 from abroad [B0031].

> **Need to know**
> - Which is the current Kuwait cap: Tk 1,06,780 (Embassy, August 2025) or Tk 1,03,780 (2017 table), and is there a newer Ministry order [B0184, B0025]?

##### If an agency or broker takes your money

Call the Probashi call centre, 16135 (toll free, run by the Wage Earners' Welfare Board) [B0047]. Then complain in writing, with evidence, to BMET's Director General: free, decided within 30 working days; Director (Employment) 01711111544, diremi@bmet.gov.bd [B0096]. The Ministry's monitoring wing asks for clear details of who took the money, when and how much [B0201]. From Kuwait, give a written complaint to the Embassy's Labour Wing, which forwards it to the authorities in Bangladesh [B0135]. Free legal advice: 16699 [B0067].

Taking money on a false job promise or holding a migrant's passport is a crime punishable by up to 5 years' jail and a fine of at least Tk 1 lakh [B0070].

Sample complaint (in Bangla or English, with copies of receipts): "To the Director General, BMET, 89/2 Kakrail, Dhaka. I, [name], NID [number], OEP registration [number], paid Tk [amount] to [agency name, RL number / broker name, phone] on [date] at [place] for a Kuwait job as [job] with [employer]. [What happened.] I ask BMET to recover my money and act against the agency. Attached: [receipts, visa copy, messages]." [B0096, B0201]

#### Step 4: Passport, police clearance and certificates

1. **Get an e-passport.** You fill the form online and submit it at the divisional or regional passport office for your present address [B0081, B0082].

   You'll need [B0051, B0052, B0083]:
   - your NID (compulsory over 20); fill the form exactly as on it; nothing needs attesting;
   - technical certificates such as a driving licence, uploaded with the form and shown in original at the office.

   1. Fill in the form online at epassport.gov.bd [B0081].
   2. Pay through A-Challan, DGePay or ShurjoPay, or at a bank [B0050]. A 48-page passport costs Tk 4,025 / 6,325 / 8,625 for 5 years and Tk 5,750 / 8,050 / 10,350 for 10 years (regular / express / super express) [B0048].
   3. Go to the passport office with the original NID and certificates [B0083]. Delivery is within 15, 7 or 2 working days of biometric enrolment [B0049]. No police verification is needed with an online-verified NID [B0054].

   **If it fails:** passport helpline 16445 (09666716445 from abroad), 24 hours [B0055]. If the passport is lost, file a GD (General Diary, a police report) at the nearest police station and re-apply with a copy of the old passport and the GD [B0053].

2. **Get a police clearance if the agency or employer asks for one.** You'll need [B0056, B0057]:
   - a passport valid at least 3 months;
   - a present address that is the permanent or emergency address in the passport, where you live;
   - a scan of the passport data page attested by a first-class gazetted officer;
   - Tk 1,500 by treasury challan, code 1-7301-0001-2681, at any Bangladesh Bank or Sonali Bank branch, or online by card with a service charge.

   1. Register at [pcc.police.gov.bd](https://pcc.police.gov.bd/), log in and click "Apply" [B0078].
   2. Fill in your personal details, then your present and permanent addresses; police verify you at the present address [B0079].
   3. Upload the scanned documents [B0080].
   4. Track it by SMS: "PCC S <reference>" to 26969. Collect it with your passport or application copy, or send someone with an attested authorisation letter [B0058].

   **If it fails:** helpdesk Sunday-Thursday 9 am-5 pm, 01320001824, 01320001825, help.pcc@police.gov.bd [B0059].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does Kuwait require a police clearance for an Article 18 work visa for Bangladeshis, and must it be attested by MoFA Dhaka and the Kuwait Embassy in Dhaka (see Step 2: The job offer and contract)?

3. **Certificates, if your job needs them.** Kuwait is not one of the 114 countries that accept Bangladesh's online Apostille alone, so every document needs both steps below [B0100]. Any Bangladeshi document the Bangladesh Embassy in Kuwait later attests (marriage or birth certificate, police clearance) must first carry MoFA Dhaka's attestation [B0163].
   1. **Issuing authority and Ministry of Education.** SSC and HSC certificates are verified by the Education Board, degrees by the university, then by the Ministry of Education [B0064]. Experience certificates instead must be attested by BMET or the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare and notarised; MoFA then attests them free [B0063].
   2. **MoFA online.** On mygov.bd, register, choose the attestation service for the document, upload it as JPEG/JPG/PNG and pay Tk 200 a page; it takes 5 working days, and an SMS tells you when the Apostille is issued [B0060, B0061].
   3. **MoFA consular counter.** Take the Apostille and the documents to MoFA's consular counter for attestation [B0100, B0062].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What does the Kuwait Embassy in Dhaka charge to attest a certificate after MoFA, and how long does it take (W covers degree attestation for professionals)?

#### Step 6: Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight

1. **Do the pre-departure orientation (PDO), 3 days.** It is compulsory, at one of 70 BMET institutes; pay against a money receipt (Tk 200 at Feni TTC; pay no more) [B0089, B0041]. Download the certificate at training.oep.gov.bd/pdo-certificate: type your passport or NID number and the captcha [B0042].
2. **Check the contract** at least 2 days before you go: job, employer, workplace, length, pay, leave and airfare [B0043], against the permission letter (Step 1, item 3) and the Embassy's minimum wage (Step 1, item 5) [B0169, B0187]. You must have signed it before clearance and been given a BMET-attested Bangla translation [B0172]. Keep a copy: the Embassy asks workers to know the pay and type of work before signing [B0186].
3. **BMET clearance (the smart card).** For a group visa the agency applies to BMET's emigration branch with the visa copy, the attested contract, your attested registration copy, a Tk 300 stamp undertaking, the pay order for the welfare fee and the source-tax challan; BMET clears within 3 working days of the visa [B0084]. Your costs:
   - welfare fee Tk 5,500 to the Wage Earners' Welfare Board, from 1 October 2026 [B0026];
   - insurance Tk 1,000 once (5 years, Tk 10 lakh cover) [B0094];
   - clearance fee Tk 250 [B0085];
   - source tax Tk 1,200 (skilled) or Tk 800 (unskilled) on a group visa; Tk 250 on an attested single visa, Tk 400 on an unattested one [B0085].

   **Single visa:** apply yourself at BMET's emigration branch in Dhaka or the Chattogram DEMO with your application, visa copy, any contract, attested OEP registration copy, a Tk 300 stamp undertaking and the pay order for the welfare fee and insurance; 3 working days [B0086]. Women on a single visa need a guardian's no-objection letter on stamp paper [B0044].

   **Check it is real** at portal.wewb.gov.bd/bmet-wewbm-check: type your passport number (or membership ID) and the captcha [B0045]; the airport welfare desks check smart cards [B0090]. **If it fails:** BMET's Director (Emigration), +8802222222415, 01711-135901, diremi@bmet.gov.bd, or the OEP hotline 09614445271 [B0088, B0046].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does BMET refuse clearance for a Kuwait visa that lacks the Embassy's attestation, and is the attestation number checked on OEP (RAIMS) at clearance [B0194]?

4. **The flight.** The employer pays the airfare, and an agency representative must fly with your group or meet you at Kuwait airport; the agency sends the flight schedule and worker list to the Embassy and BMET [B0171, B0173].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which airlines fly Dhaka or Chattogram to Kuwait in November 2026 (Kuwait Airways, Jazeera, Biman, US-Bangla, others), and what does a one-way ticket cost?

5. **Before you fly:** keep one set of copies at home and 3 with you, open 2 bank accounts, and never carry a packet you don't know [B0095]. Save the Embassy's Labour Wing number (see Contacts).

### Coming home (Bangladesh-side items)

- **Taking your household goods home for good: get a TR certificate.** A Bangladeshi leaving Kuwait for good applies to the Embassy's Labour Wing for a TR (transfer of residence) certificate under NBR's SRO 165 of 2000; the form is free at the Embassy reception and on its website [B0166].
- **No money for papers.** The Embassy says paying for iqama (residence) renewal, leave or a transfer is illegal; get a receipt for any payment [B0185].
- **Airport help.** The Welfare Board's desks at Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet airports help returnees, take complaints of abuse or cheating, and give women returning within a year after abuse or fraud Tk 5,000 cash [B0073, B0074, B0090]. A free ambulance takes injured, sick or dead workers from Dhaka and Chattogram airports; Dhaka desk +8802 8901040, 01404 413388; Chattogram 01913631797 [B0071]. Sylhet desk 01558277904 [B0091].
- **A place to stay.** The Wage Earners' Centre (Lonjonipara, Borua, Khilkhet, Dhaka-1229, near Shahjalal airport): Tk 100 admission, Tk 200 a night; book at wec.wewb.gov.bd or in person with proof of clearance or membership, a passport copy and the ticket; 01310 350555, 01754 715720 (24 hours) [B0075]. The Probashi Lounge at Shahjalal airport gives free wifi and phone calls and food at up to 30% off with proof of clearance or membership [B0076].
- **If your family needs legal help in Bangladesh, such as protecting its property,** apply to the Welfare Board (directly, through the Deputy Commissioner's expatriate welfare branch or the DEMO) [B0077].
- **Business loan:** PKB's rehabilitation loan, up to Tk 50 lakh (Tk 3 lakh without collateral), 9% over up to 10 years, at the branch for the project's area with a trade licence and return papers [B0034, B0035]; fees 0.5% processing (max Tk 15,000) and 0.5% documentation [B0036].
- **BRAC** gives returnees airport help, counselling, money or materials to restart earning, and legal help [B0066].

### Contacts (Bangladesh side and Embassy)

#### Help from the Bangladesh Embassy in Kuwait

- **Labour Wing advice and complaints.** Any Bangladeshi worker can get advice and help from the Labour Wing, and give it written complaints to forward to Bangladesh [B0134, B0135]. For a labour dispute the Embassy points workers to PAM, in person or on PAM's online platform (see If you aren't paid) [B0136].
- **Monthly public hearing (gono shunani).** On the second Sunday of each month at the Embassy; the October 2026 hearing was on 11 October [B0205, B0162].
- **Welfare Board card for workers without a BMET card.** Apply online at portal.wewb.gov.bd/login, print the form, attach copies of your passport and Civil ID and a colour photo, and hand it in at Room 13; pay KWD 17 at counter 4; the card is ready in 7 working days [B0120, B0123, B0124, B0122, B0125, B0127, B0126]. People under 18 or over 65 can't register [B0121]. The card covers help freeing detained workers and bringing them home, Tk 1 lakh for bringing home and treating injured or sick workers, Tk 3 lakh to the family of a worker who dies abroad, and legal help through the Labour Wing [B0132, B0130, B0131, B0133]. A lost card can be downloaded from wewb.gov.bd with your passport number, or a duplicate requested from the Labour Wing [B0129, B0128].
- **Passport renewal in Kuwait.** E-passport fees at the Embassy for workers and students (labourer, driver, cook, painter, cleaner), 48 pages: KWD 9.500 regular or 14.000 urgent for 5 years; KWD 15.500 or 23.500 for 10 years [B0157]. Professionals pay KWD 31.000 or 46.500 for 5 years and 38.500 or 54.000 for 10 years [B0158]. Come in person with the old passport copy and a copy of your Civil ID, pay cash at the counter, give biometrics and keep the delivery slip; passports are handed out 2 to 4 pm against the old passport and the slip [B0154, B0152, B0153, B0156]. New passport lists are posted on the Embassy's Facebook page [B0206].
- **Detained or in a deportation centre.** Embassy staff visit the Duara Azam and Sofor Mogfor deportation centres and the Central Jail, issue travel permits (outpasses) and help send detained Bangladeshis home (see Going home without valid papers) [B0137, B0138].
- **Sick in hospital.** On request of the hospital or relatives, the Embassy gives money to send sick Bangladeshis home [B0139].
- **Death.** The Embassy issues a free NOC (no-objection certificate) the same day for sending the body home; the employer pays, or the Embassy if there is no employer; for an accidental death the heirs give the Embassy a power of attorney and its lawyer claims compensation, paid through the Welfare Board (see If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job) [B0159, B0160].
- **Power of attorney for land or money at home.** Sign it in person before the consular officer; the Embassy doesn't draft it; bring the original and a copy, 2 photos each of the giver and receiver, proof of Bangladeshi nationality and a Civil ID copy; KWD 3.250 (6.250 urgent) [B0164, B0167].
