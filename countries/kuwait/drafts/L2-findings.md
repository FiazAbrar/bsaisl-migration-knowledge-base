# L findings (second L agent): private-sector law and work

Written 9 November 2026. Facts L0112 to L0304 in `work/L/facts.csv` are this agent's; L0001 to L0111 in the same file were logged by another agent working in the same folder at the same time (see Notes). The draft below cites only L0112 to L0304.

## a. Draft doc text

### Your rights at work

#### Which law covers you

Company workers on an Article 18 (private-sector) residence are covered by the Private Sector Labour Law, Law 6 of 2010 [L0198]. Domestic workers on Article 20 are not (see Work visa: domestic worker) [L0198]. The law is enforced by PAM (Public Authority for Manpower), whose labour relations departments in each governorate take workers' complaints (see If you aren't paid) [L0216, L0222].

#### Contract

- **Written, three copies:** the contract states the signing date, start date, wage, length and the work, and is made in three copies, one for you, one for the employer and one for the Ministry (now PAM) [L0112].
- **No paper is not the end:** if there is no written contract, the job still counts as a contract and you can prove your rights with any evidence, such as bank statements and messages [L0113, L0235].
- **Arabic wins:** the contract is in Arabic; a translation may be attached, but if they differ the Arabic text counts [L0116].
- **Length:** a fixed-term contract lasts between one and five years and can be extended only by agreement [L0117]; the employer may not extend it without your consent [L0204].
- **Probation:** at most 100 working days, once per employer; either side can end the contract during it without notice, but if the employer ends it, it still pays end-of-service indemnity for the time worked [L0118, L0119].
- **Same job, same pay:** the wage may not be cut while the contract runs, and any agreement to cut it is void [L0114]; the employer may not put you on work that doesn't match the contract or your skills [L0115]. If you are forced into a different job, complain to PAM with a copy of your work permit [L0205].
- **Never sign a blank paper** or a second agreement after you arrive [L0214]. A common trick is a new contract on arrival at a lower wage: one worker's contract said KD 200 and the company paid KD 90 [L0224].

#### Pay and deductions

- **Minimum wage:** KD 75 a month for private-sector workers, set in June 2017 for new or renewed work permits and contracts [L0243, L0244].
- **When:** at least once a month, on a working day, in Kuwaiti dinars, and no later than the 7th day after it falls due [L0148, L0149]. PAM's Director General said in October 2026 that salaries must be paid before the 7th of the following month [L0229].
- **How:** into your own account at a bank in Kuwait [L0150]; cash is allowed only in a new worker's first two months in Kuwait [L0230]. An automated system suspends a company's PAM file as soon as salary payments become irregular [L0228].
- **Basic salary matters:** only the basic salary counts for end-of-service pay, so check the basic figure in the contract, not only the total [L0199]; your dues are calculated on the last salary paid [L0155].
- **Deductions:** no more than 10% of the wage to repay a loan from the employer, with no interest [L0151]; all deductions together for debts (including the employer's) at most 25% [L0152]; disciplinary deductions at most five days' wage a month [L0121]. You may be punished only after being told in writing and heard [L0122].
- **Never charged:** safety and protective equipment [L0175]. You can't be forced to buy food or goods from the employer or a particular shop [L0153].
- **If the company shuts down for reasons that are not your fault,** fully or partly, it must keep paying you [L0154].

#### Hours, rest and leave

**Hours and overtime**
At most 8 hours a day and 48 a week, and 36 hours a week in Ramadan [L0156]. No more than five hours in a row without a break of at least one hour, and breaks don't count as work [L0157]. Overtime needs a written order from the employer and is capped at 2 hours a day, 180 hours a year, 3 days a week and 90 days a year [L0158]. It is paid at your ordinary rate plus 25%; you can prove overtime by any means, and the employer must keep an overtime record [L0159, L0160]. The posted schedule of daily hours, break, rest day and holidays must hang where you can see it [L0176].

**Rest day and holidays**
One fully paid rest day of 24 hours in a row after every six working days [L0304]. If you are made to work on it, you get at least 50% extra plus another day off [L0161]. Paid public holidays: Hijri New Year, Isra and Miraj, Arafat Day, Prophet's Birthday, National Day (25 February), Liberation Day (26 February) and Gregorian New Year, one day each, plus three days each for Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha [L0162, L0302]. Work on a public holiday is paid at double wage plus a day off in lieu [L0163].

**Annual leave**
30 days' paid leave a year; in the first year you earn it only after nine months, and part years count pro rata [L0166]. Leave pay is paid before you go [L0167]. The employer sets the dates, and leave can be saved up to two years' worth [L0168, L0169]. Leave not taken is paid in cash when the contract ends [L0170]. Working for another employer during leave lets the employer take back the leave pay [L0171].

**Sick and other leave**
Sick leave a year: 15 days on full pay, 10 on three-quarter pay, 10 on half pay, 10 on quarter pay, then 30 days unpaid [L0164], with a certificate from the employer's doctor or a government health centre [L0165]. After two continuous years, a Muslim worker who hasn't done Hajj gets 21 days' paid Hajj leave [L0172]. Three days' paid leave on the death of a close relative [L0173]. A woman gets 70 days' paid maternity leave and can't be dismissed during it [L0174, L0303]. The employer may not dismiss you, or give you notice, while you are on any of these leaves [L0127].

#### Heat

Outdoor work is banned daily from 11:00 am to 4:00 pm from 1 June to 31 August under PAM's Ministerial Resolution No. 3 of 2026 [L0236, L0237, L0296]. PAM may also stop outdoor work in dust storms, heavy rain, extreme heat or humidity and other emergencies [L0238]. Urgent technical work is exempt only with prior approval from PAM's Labour Inspection Department [L0239]. Employers must give cold drinking water, shaded rest areas, first aid and rest breaks [L0240]. Report a site working in the banned hours to PAM's inspection team on 24936192; breaches can get the employer's PAM file suspended [L0241, L0242].

#### Housing and health care

**Housing**
An employer working on a government contract or in remote areas must give free suitable housing and transport, or a housing allowance [L0120].

**Health insurance**
No residence permit is issued or renewed without health insurance [L0246]. Since 23 December 2025 the yearly health fee on residence issue or renewal is KD 100 for most categories (it was KD 50), KD 10 for agricultural labourers, fishermen and herders, and KD 5 for a private-sector work entry visa [L0245, L0247, L0248].

#### Your passport

Keeping a worker's passport is illegal but remains widespread in Kuwait [L0259]. If your company won't give it back, complain to the PAM office where your residency was stamped (see If you aren't paid, item 2) [L0203]. When the contract ends, the employer must return all your documents and give you a service certificate stating your time, job and last salary [L0146, L0147].

> **Need to know**
> - Which article of Law 6/2010 or Decree-Law 114/2024 bans keeping a private-sector worker's passport, and what is the penalty [L0259]?

#### End-of-service pay (indemnity)

**How much**
For a monthly-paid worker: 15 days' wage for each of the first five years, then one month's wage for each later year, at most 18 months' wage in total [L0137]. For daily, weekly, piece-rate or hourly workers: 10 days' wage a year for the first five years, then 15 days a year, at most one year's wage [L0138]. Part years count pro rata [L0139]. It is worked out on the last basic salary [L0155, L0199]. Any debt or loan you owe the employer is taken off [L0140].

Example: a monthly-paid worker on a KD 100 basic salary who leaves after 3 years at the end of the contract gets 3 x 15 days = 45 days' wage, about KD 150 [L0137, L0142].

**Full, part or none**
- **Full:** if the employer ends the contract; if a fixed-term contract ends and isn't renewed; if you quit for the employer's fault (Article 48); on death or disability; or on the company's closure or bankruptcy [L0141, L0142, L0143].
- **Part, if you resign from an open-ended contract:** half after 3 to 5 years, two-thirds after 5 to 10 years, full after 10 years; under 3 years the law gives nothing for resigning [L0144, L0145].
- **None:** if dismissed for causing gross loss, getting the job by fraud or disclosing company secrets [L0197]. Other dismissals for misconduct keep the indemnity, and an arbitrary dismissal can be challenged at the Labour Court for indemnity plus compensation [L0196].

> **Need to know**

### What can get you jailed or deported

#### Leaving your employer and working for someone else

If you stop going to work and work for another employer, your employer files an absence ("absconding") report and must send you home (see If your employer reports you absent) [L0192]. After the report goes to the Ministry of Interior, it can end in residency cancellation, deportation and a blacklist that stops you coming back [L0293]. A deported worker is banned from entering Kuwait again [L0210]; a worker who leaves without going through the deportation centre can come back on a new valid visa with Kuwait's permission [L0211]. Brokers who sell visas sometimes file false absconding reports to free the quota and sell it again [L0273].

#### The residence law (Decree-Law 114/2024)

Kuwait's residence law, Decree-Law 114 of 2024, replaced the 1959 law and took effect on 5 January 2025 [L0277, L0295]. Its fourth chapter punishes residency trafficking, the buying and selling of visas [L0277]. The Interior Minister can deport a foreigner even with a valid residence permit in the cases the law lists, and hold him up to 30 days, renewable, until he leaves [L0278, L0279]. The cost of deportation falls on the employer, or on whoever employs or houses the foreigner [L0280]. Some violations can be settled by paying instead of going to court [L0281]:
- not getting a residence permit after entering on a work visa: KD 2 a day in the first month, then KD 4 a day, up to KD 1,200 [L0282];
- overstaying a visa: KD 10 a day [L0283].

> **Need to know**
> - What prison terms and fines does Decree-Law 114/2024 set for a worker (not the trader) who paid for a traded visa, works for someone other than his sponsor, or overstays, and which articles?
> - What are the three cases in which the 2025 executive regulations (Ministerial Decision 2249 of 2025) allow deportation of a worker with a valid residence?

#### Traffic offences

Under Kuwait's new traffic law, police arrest drivers under the influence of alcohol or drugs, drivers who cause injury or death, racers and those who flee [L0284]. Driving without a valid licence costs KD 75, or in court up to 3 months and KD 150 to 300 [L0285]. Running a red light is a felony: up to three years and up to KD 1,000 [L0286]. A phone at the wheel costs KD 75 and reckless driving KD 150 [L0288]. Carrying passengers without a permit (the illegal "taxi" many drivers run on the side) is a serious offence [L0289]. An expatriate may own only one car [L0287]. Deportation for a serious traffic offence is an administrative decision after an investigation and does not need a repeat offence; about 15 people were deported this way in May to July 2025 [L0290, L0291]. In October 2026, 300 delivery workers were deported in a few days for violations [L0232].

#### Drugs

Kuwait's anti-drug law, Decree-Law 159 of 2025, took effect on 15 December 2025 [L0298]. Smuggling or producing drugs to sell can bring death or life imprisonment and a fine of KD 100,000 to KD 2 million; holding drugs to sell or promoting them, death or life imprisonment and KD 50,000 to KD 500,000 [L0299, L0300]. Planting drugs on someone to accuse them falsely is also a crime [L0301].

> **Need to know**
> - What does Decree-Law 159/2025 set for possession for personal use, and is a foreign worker always deported after the sentence?

#### Strikes, protests and what you post

Joining a strike or protest is illegal for migrant workers and can get you sent home [L0212]. Don't post anything against Kuwait's religion or sensitivities, especially on social media [L0213]. Inside a work dispute, a worker charged by his own employer is suspended, not dismissed, until a final verdict; if acquitted, the employer pays the lost wages and compensation [L0124].

> **Need to know**
> - What penalties apply in Kuwait to alcohol (drinking, making, selling) for a foreign worker, and is deportation automatic?
> - Can an unpaid phone bill, loan or bounced cheque lead to a travel ban that stops a worker leaving, given about 4,000 travel bans were issued in the first seven months of 2025 [L0260]?

### If you aren't paid

PAM handles unpaid wages, end-of-service pay, leave pay, unfair dismissal, overtime, work-injury compensation, release and residency cancellation for Article 18 workers [L0215]. The law makes you go to PAM first; only PAM sends the case to court [L0188]. Complaining is free, and so is the court [L0186]. Claims die one year after the contract ends, so don't wait [L0185].

**If you aren't paid, in this order:**
1. **Gather the proof.** You'll need: your contract (or any proof of the job), bank statements showing the missing or short payments, and your messages with the employer about pay [L0235, L0113].
2. **File a labour complaint (shakwa a'maliya) at PAM.** Go in person to the PAM labour relations department that holds your employer's file (the office where your residency was stamped) [L0202, L0216], or to PAM in Hawally, Jabriya, Block 6, Street 104, Building 7 [L0222].
   1. Say: "I want to file a labour complaint against [company name]. My contract salary is KD [amount]. I have not been paid for [months] / I was paid only KD [amount]."
   2. You get the complaint number by SMS [L0217].
   3. PAM sets a hearing and tells both sides the date, time and the investigator's name [L0219].
3. **Go to the hearing.** The investigator tries to settle [L0220]. Some employers don't answer PAM's calls to drag the case out [L0258].
4. **Court.** If there is no settlement, PAM must send the case to the Court of First Instance within one month, and the court sets a hearing within 3 days of receiving it [L0188, L0189]. You then follow the case in court yourself [L0220]. Workers' claims come before the employer's other debts if the company is wound up [L0187].
5. **Quit and transfer if it goes on.** Non-payment is a breach that lets you quit without notice and keep full indemnity [L0130, L0143], and since May 2026 PAM can let you transfer to another employer before one year in such cases (see Changing employer or leaving) [L0265]. In May 2026 the Labour Court ordered a company that hadn't paid a worker for six months to release him without its consent and pay six months' wages, full indemnity for four years, leave and overtime, and threw out its absconding report [L0233, L0234].

**Already home?** Authorise a friend or lawyer in Kuwait to file for you, within one year of leaving the job [L0218, L0223, L0200].

**Don't run away instead.** Leaving without filing first can end in an absconding report, residency cancellation and a blacklist [L0225, L0293].

**Free legal help:** the Kuwait Society for Human Rights gives legal aid to expatriates [L0226].

> **Need to know**

**Abuse and violence:** report it to the local police and insist on a medical examination that records the injuries [L0206]. Assault by the employer is grounds to quit without notice and keep full indemnity [L0131].

### If your employer reports you absent

The employer can treat you as having resigned if you are absent without acceptable reason for 7 days in a row or 20 days in a year [L0123]. It must report the absence within 15 days of the first day you were away [L0270]. Since March 2026 the employer files it in PAM's Ashal portal, and you get an alert at once in the Ashal app [L0274]. The employer prints the notice and must file it with the Ministry of Interior's Residency Affairs Directorate within 3 working days [L0276].

**If you get the alert, in this order:**
1. **Object within 60 days.** Go to the PAM labour relations department that holds your employer's file and file an objection [L0275]. Bring proof that the absence was justified or the report false: medical certificates, travel documents, a resignation you sent, and messages with the employer [L0272].
2. **Ask the employer to withdraw it.** The employer can cancel the report in PAM's portal [L0294].
3. **File your own labour complaint** for any unpaid wages at the same time (see If you aren't paid), because a false absconding report filed after you asked for your pay can be thrown out by the court [L0234].
4. **Ask PAM for a transfer.** Since May 2026 a false or malicious report is one of the cases in which PAM can let you move to another employer before one year [L0264].

If the case is not settled, after 90 days it goes from PAM to the Ministry of Interior, which can cancel your residence and deport and blacklist you [L0292, L0293]. Cases often show in the system 15 to 20 days after the report [L0209]. Going home after an absconding case is covered in Going home without valid papers.

> **Need to know**
> - Does a worker reported absent lose his indemnity, given the law treats 7 days' absence as resignation [L0123, L0144]?
> - Does PAM still use the 30-day and 60-day objection windows, or only the 60 days announced in March 2026 [L0275]?

### Changing employer or leaving

**The rules**
- **Under one year:** you need your employer's consent, and a transfer before one year costs KD 300 [L0268]. On a fixed-term contract still running, you can't transfer or get a release without the employer's approval [L0207].
- **After three years** with the same sponsor on an open-ended contract, you can move without the employer's approval [L0208].
- **Early transfer without consent (since May 2026):** under Administrative Decision 680 of 2026, PAM can investigate your complaint and let you transfer before one year if the employer didn't get or renew your work permit or residency through its own fault, its PAM file is suspended or blocked, it filed a false absconding report, it broke the Labour Law in a way that lets you quit without notice (unpaid wages, abuse, unsafe work, contract fraud, false criminal charges), or the company went bankrupt or closed for good [L0261, L0262, L0264, L0265, L0266, L0267]. PAM can then stop the employer's new permits and recruitment [L0263].
- **Fee:** each work permit now carries an extra KD 150 under Ministerial Resolution 4 of 2025 [L0269]. PAM said in October 2026 that it won't allow transfers "without sufficient reasons" [L0231].

**Quitting**
On an open-ended contract, give three months' written notice (monthly-paid); if you skip it, you owe the wage for the notice period [L0125]. On a fixed-term contract, leaving early without a legal reason can make you pay damages up to your wage for the rest of the contract [L0129], and the employer may ask you to repay recruitment costs in proportion to the time left; PAM can be asked to waive this [L0201]. You can quit at once with full indemnity if the employer breaks the contract or law, assaults you, endangers your health (decided by the Medical Arbitration Committee at the Ministry of Health), or cheated you over the contract terms [L0130, L0131, L0132, L0133].

> **Need to know**
> - Is the 2016 KD 300 fee for transfer before one year still charged on top of the KD 150 permit fee in 2026 [L0268, L0269]?
> - Who pays the transfer fees in practice, the old employer, the new one or the worker?
> - Can a worker transfer between sectors (for example out of the restricted SME, industrial, agricultural, fishing and livestock sectors) outside the May to June 2026 window?

### Leaving at the end of the job

1. **Collect what you're owed.** Full indemnity if the fixed-term contract ends and isn't renewed (see End-of-service pay (indemnity)) [L0142], cash for leave not taken [L0170], and any unpaid wages and overtime [L0215].
2. **Get your service certificate and documents back.** The employer must give a certificate of your time, job and last salary, and return your documents [L0146, L0147].
3. **Get the exit permit** (see Going home on leave, item 1) [L0249].
4. **If anything is unpaid,** file a PAM complaint before you fly, or within one year through someone in Kuwait (see If you aren't paid) [L0185, L0218].

> **Need to know**
> - Does Kuwait law or the standard PAM contract make the employer pay the worker's ticket home at the end of the contract? Law 6/2010 makes it pay only when it recruited workers it didn't need or after an absconding report [L0192, L0193].
> - Is the residence cancelled before departure (and by whom), and can a worker leave with his Civil ID still active?

### Going home on leave

1. **Get an exit permit in Sahel.** Since 1 July 2025 every Article 18 worker needs an employer-approved exit permit to travel abroad [L0249]. Domestic workers don't [L0256].
   You'll need: the Sahel app and your departure and return dates [L0250].
   1. Open Sahel, go to Services, then Public Authority of Manpower, then Expatriate Workers Services, then Exit Permit [L0250].
   2. Enter the departure and return dates and submit [L0250]. Apply no earlier than 7 days and no later than 24 hours before you fly [L0251].
   3. Your employer approves it in Sahel Business or Ashal [L0252].
   4. Download and print the permit with its barcode and show it at the airport [L0255]. It is valid for one week [L0297].
   **If it fails:** if the employer refuses or delays without reason, complain to the PAM labour relations unit that holds the company's file [L0253]. The rule came by Interior Ministry circular and sets no penalty for an employer who blocks a worker's exit [L0257].
2. **Take your leave pay.** You get 30 days' paid leave a year, paid before you go (see Your rights at work: Hours, rest and leave) [L0166, L0167].
3. **Come back on time.** Seven days in a row without acceptable reason lets the employer treat you as resigned and file an absence report [L0123, L0270].

> - Who pays the ticket for annual leave? Law 6/2010 is silent; is it in the standard contract?

## If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job

### Injury at work

1. **Report it.** The employer must report a work injury at once, including one on the way to or from work, to the area police station, PAM's labour department and the insurer; you or someone for you can report it too [L0178].
2. **Treatment is on the employer.** The employer pays treatment, medicine and transport, and must insure workers against work injuries and occupational diseases [L0179, L0177].
3. **Your wage.** Full wage for the whole treatment period; after six months, half wage until you recover, are declared disabled or die [L0181].
4. **Disability and compensation.** The treating doctor's report sets the treatment period and the disability percentage; you (or the employer) can object within one month to the Medical Arbitration Committee at the Ministry of Health [L0180]. Compensation follows a schedule set by ministerial resolution [L0182]. It is lost if you hurt yourself on purpose or through gross misconduct, such as being drunk or breaking posted safety rules, unless the injury kills or disables you over 25% [L0183]. An occupational disease that shows up within a year of leaving counts too [L0184].
5. **If you can't go on working,** the contract ends and you are owed full indemnity [L0134, L0143].

> **Need to know**
> - What are the compensation amounts in the ministerial schedule (for example for death, or for loss of a hand) [L0182]?
> - How does a worker claim compensation after going home disabled, and how long does it take?

### Death

The contract ends on the worker's death, and full indemnity is due to the family [L0134, L0143]. If death came from a work injury, the heirs are owed compensation under the schedule [L0182].

> **Need to know**
> - Who pays to send the body home from Kuwait (the employer, Kuwait's government, or the Bangladesh Embassy / Wage Earners' Welfare Board), and what papers are needed (police report, Ministry of Health death certificate, residency cancellation, embassy no-objection)?
> - How do heirs in Bangladesh collect the indemnity, unpaid wages and compensation from Kuwait (through the Bangladesh Embassy's labour wing and a power of attorney)?
> - Is blood money (diya) paid in Kuwait when a worker is killed in a traffic accident, and how much?

### Losing the job

If the company closes for good or goes bankrupt, the contract ends and full indemnity is due; if it is sold or merged, your contract carries on with the new owner [L0135, L0136, L0143]. Closure is one of the cases in which PAM can let you transfer early (see Changing employer or leaving) [L0266]. If the employer ends an open-ended contract, it owes three months' notice or pay in lieu, and during notice you may take one paid day (or eight hours) a week to look for work [L0125, L0126]. You can't be dismissed for your sex, origin or religion [L0128]. If the employer recruited you without having real work, it must pay your way home [L0193].
