## a. Draft doc text

## Visas not open to Bangladeshis

- **Tourist e-visa or visa on arrival on a Bangladeshi passport from Bangladesh:** not open; only the nationals of about 53 listed countries get it, and Bangladesh is not on the list [V0006]. The tourist category for "other nationalities", who would prove funds with a bank statement, was not switched on when the system launched in August 2025 [V0080, V0124].
- **Visa-free entry:** only for nationals of countries named by decree, on a reciprocal basis; Bangladesh is not one [V0049].
- **Changing a family or tourist visit visa into a private-sector work visa (Article 18) inside Kuwait:** not allowed; a visit visa can become a work residence only if it is a government visit visa (see Changing visa type) [V0055, V0094].

In November 2021 Bangladeshis were one of seven nationalities (with Pakistan, Iraq, Yemen, Iran, Syria and Sudan) who needed special Interior Ministry permission for business, family and tourist visas [V0098, V0099]. In May 2025 the Bangladesh Ambassador to Kuwait, Major General Syed Tareque Hossain, said Bangladeshis were getting Kuwaiti visas without special approval [V0096]. In October 2025 a Kuwaiti security source said the visa system had been opened to all nationalities, lifting earlier restrictions on certain countries [V0084].

> **Need to know**
> - On kuwaitvisa.moi.gov.kw in November 2026, what does the nationality check show when "Bangladesh" is selected for each visa type (tourist, family, business): "Apply Now", "apply through Kuwait embassy", or "not eligible"?
> - Has the "other nationalities" tourist category (bank statement, hotel booking, card deposit) been switched on since August 2025, and does it include Bangladesh?

## Visit visas (tourist, family, business)

**Status, November 2026:** Kuwait's Interior Ministry says all visit visa types are open to every nationality except Israelis [V0079, V0081]. For Bangladeshis this means in practice: a family visit visa, applied for by a relative living in Kuwait; a business visit visa, applied for by a Kuwaiti company; and a tourist visa only for those living in a Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) country, the US, the UK or the Schengen area (see Visas not open to Bangladeshis) [V0001, V0002, V0118].

All visit visas are issued online on Kuwait Visa, the Ministry of Interior's platform at kuwaitvisa.moi.gov.kw, which is also a mobile app [V0082, V0020]. Since 23 December 2025 the rules come from Ministerial Decision No. 2249/2025, the executive regulations of the foreigners' residence law, which recognise twelve types of visit visa [V0057, V0058, V0061]. A visit visa holder may not work and must leave when the visit ends [V0012]. Visitors caught working are deported together with their sponsor in Kuwait and put on the ban list [V0086].

### Cost and time

- **Visa fee:** 10 KWD for each month of the visa, for family, tourist, business, government, private, medical and multiple-entry visits [V0051, V0057, V0059].
- **Extension:** 10 KWD per month [V0053].
- **Health insurance:** compulsory since 23 December 2025, bought from a Ministry of Health-approved private insurer; no visit visa is issued or renewed without it [V0065, V0112]. The sponsor in Kuwait or the visitor pays [V0066].
- **Refunds:** none; fees are collected only once the application is accepted [V0015, V0016].
- **Time:** a complete application can be issued in about five minutes; the portal says tourist visas usually take 1 to 2 working days [V0070, V0018].
- **Payment:** online, by credit card or another online payment option [V0022].

> - What does visitor health insurance cost for a one-month family visit, and is it bought inside the Kuwait Visa application or separately?

### Family visit visa

A relative who lives in Kuwait with a valid residence applies; the visitor does not apply [V0001, V0069]. Since August 2025 there is no minimum salary for the sponsor, and relatives up to the fourth degree by blood or the third degree by marriage can be invited: wife, children, parents, parents-in-law, brothers and sisters, uncles and aunts, nephews and nieces, grandparents [V0069, V0078, V0116]. There is no age limit for parents [V0088].

#### Step 1: The relative in Kuwait checks they can sponsor

1. **Check the relationship is within the fourth degree** by blood or third by marriage [V0116].
2. **Check your own residence is valid,** because the portal checks the sponsor's eligibility for each relationship and can refuse with "Your sponsor is not eligible to sponsor your relationship" [V0103].

In practice officials look hard at sponsors on very low salaries, around 250 KWD a month, and at visits by elderly relatives, judged on the sponsor's job and salary [V0085, V0120].

#### Step 2: The family in Bangladesh sends the papers

You'll need:

- the visitor's passport, valid at least 6 months [V0009];
- the document proving the relationship: usually the birth certificate, sometimes the marriage certificate [V0075];
- that document in Arabic, or translated into Arabic by an authorised translation office [V0076, V0116];
- a colour face photo, under 12 months old, 40 mm high by 30 mm wide, on a plain white or light grey background, as a JPEG file under 2 MB [V0025, V0102, V0026, V0023, V0024].

For attesting Bangladeshi birth and marriage certificates, see Family visa (dependants).

> **Need to know**
> - Does the family visit application need the Bangladeshi birth or marriage certificate attested (Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Dhaka, Kuwait Embassy in Dhaka), or only an Arabic translation done in Kuwait?

#### Step 3: The relative applies on Kuwait Visa

1. **Register on kuwaitvisa.moi.gov.kw** (or the Kuwait Visa app) and activate the account from the email link [V0027, V0082].
2. **Add your Civil ID to the profile and confirm it with Kuwait Mobile ID,** the government identity app; the portal asks for this before a family application [V0028].
3. **Choose "Family", enter the visitor's details and upload the papers from Step 2** [V0001, V0076].
4. **Give your address in Kuwait,** where the visitor will stay [V0077].
5. **Choose the visa:** single entry for 1, 2 or 3 months, or multiple entry for 3 months, 6 months or 1 year with each stay up to one month [V0074, V0040].
6. **Buy the health insurance and pay the fee by card** (see Cost and time) [V0065, V0051, V0022].
7. **Track the application** by logging in; the portal also emails status updates [V0021].

**You get back** the visa online, without visiting a residence affairs office [V0131].

**If it fails:** the portal gives the reason where it can, and you can submit a new application once the problem is fixed [V0017]. The authorities can refuse or cancel a visa at any time, even after issue, without giving reasons [V0019]. For parents, a medical report is sometimes requested, and approval can stall without one [V0122]. Help desk: Electronic Services Department, General Administration of Residence Affairs, Dajeej, Sunday to Thursday 8:00 am to 2:00 pm, or the portal's online inquiry form at any time [V0029, V0030].

#### Step 4: Travel and stay

1. **Fly within 30 days of the visa's issue date** [V0010]. Any airline, or land or sea, is allowed [V0071].
2. **Check the name, date of birth and passport number on the visa match the passport;** a mismatch voids the visa and the visitor is refused entry [V0034].
3. **At Kuwait airport, pass the security check;** visitors who fail it are sent back [V0083].
4. **Stay no longer than the visa allows:** a family visit may last up to three months under the 2025 regulations [V0035].
5. **To extend, apply before the visa expires:** up to three months at a time, to one year in total, at 10 KWD a month [V0105, V0054, V0053].
6. **Leave on time.** Overstaying costs 10 KWD a day, and the visitor may be banned from returning [V0013].

### Tourist visa

Bangladeshis can get one only if they live in a GCC country, the US, the UK or the Schengen area [V0118].

- **Who qualifies:** residents of those places in a listed profession: doctors, pharmacists, nurses, engineers, lawyers, consultants, university and school teachers, journalists, pilots, systems analysts and programmers, accountants, managers and businessmen [V0007, V0133, V0134]. The portal shows the allowed professions while you apply [V0115].
- **The residence permit:** it must show the profession and be valid more than six months from entry [V0119].
- **Stay:** single entry for 1, 2 or 3 months, or multiple entry for 3 months, 6 months or 1 year, with each stay up to a month [V0135].

> **Need to know**
> - Kuwait Times reported in August 2025 that the profession condition for GCC residents was dropped [V0073], but officials still described it in October 2025 [V0121]: can a Bangladeshi labourer or driver with a Saudi, UAE or Qatar iqama get a Kuwait tourist visa in 2026?

You'll need [V0008]:

- a passport copy (valid at least 6 months) [V0009];
- a copy of the GCC residence permit showing the profession [V0008];
- a confirmed travel ticket [V0008];
- a hotel reservation [V0008];
- a recent face photo (rules in Family visit visa, Step 2) [V0008].

1. **Register on kuwaitvisa.moi.gov.kw** and activate the account by email [V0027].
2. **Choose "Tourist", select your nationality and answer "Are you a GCC Resident?"**; if the portal says you are not eligible, you cannot apply online [V0132, V0004, V0005].
3. **Upload the documents, pay the fee and buy the health insurance** (see Cost and time) [V0008, V0051, V0065].
4. **Wait** 1 to 2 working days [V0018].

Hotels must register foreign guests with the Interior Ministry within 24 hours [V0111].

### Business visit visa

A Kuwaiti company applies on Kuwait Visa for a foreign business partner or visitor, with proof of the business purpose [V0002, V0113]. The stay is up to one month, single or multiple entry [V0037, V0040]. It does not allow work (see Visit visas (tourist, family, business)) [V0012].

> - Are "business visit" visas being sold in Dhaka in 2026 as a cheap way into Kuwait to look for work, and at what price?

### Government and private visits

- **Government visit:** a Kuwaiti ministry or public body requests it; the stay is up to three months [V0038]. There are no fixed requirements; the inviting body decides [V0123]. It is the only visit visa that can become a work residence (see Changing visa type) [V0055].
- **Private visit:** requested by a citizen or resident, for up to three months [V0036].

### Scams

**A visit visa sold as a job visa.** No visit visa allows work, and a family or tourist visit visa can never be converted into a private-sector work visa inside Kuwait [V0012, V0094]. A visitor caught working is deported, and the sponsor too, and both go on the ban list [V0086]. A Bangla report gives the same warning: there is no chance to change a tourist or family visa into a work visa [V0100].

**Paying a broker to "process" an online visa.** Visit visas are applied for only on the official Kuwait Visa website or app, by the relative or company in Kuwait [V0020, V0001, V0002]. The fee is 10 KWD a month plus insurance [V0051, V0065]. The Bangladesh ambassador has warned against forged Kuwaiti visas [V0097].

> - What do Dhaka agencies charge in 2026 for a Kuwait "family visit" or "business visit" visa, against the official 10 KWD a month?

## Transit and medical treatment

### Transit

- **Transit visa:** for travellers passing through Kuwait who hold a visa for their destination; the stay is up to seven days [V0042, V0031]. It costs 10 KWD [V0052].
- **Emergency entry visa:** issued at a port of entry in an emergency; the holder must leave within seven days [V0043].
- **Overland transit from Iraq or Iran (Abdali border):** a seven-day transit visa approved in June 2025 for Gulf citizens, GCC residents, Europeans and diplomats [V0127].

> - Do Bangladeshi Hajj or Umrah pilgrims connect through Kuwait, and do they need a Kuwaiti transit visa?

### Medical treatment

A medical treatment visa is issued on a certificate from Kuwait's Ministry of Health, or from a private hospital with the Ministry of Health's certification, proving the patient has been accepted for treatment; the stay is up to three months [V0039]. The fee is 10 KWD a month, as for other visit visas [V0051].

> **Need to know**
> - Who applies for a medical treatment visa (hospital, relative in Kuwait or the patient), on which platform, and do any Kuwaiti hospitals accept Bangladeshi patients from abroad?

## Student visa

A study entry visa is requested by a Kuwaiti government educational body or a private university, stating that the student has been admitted [V0044, V0104]. After arrival the student has two months to get residence, which is granted on the institution's certified admission certificate and renewed with a certificate that the student is still studying [V0048, V0045].

- **Residence fee:** 20 KWD [V0056].
- **Government health insurance:** 100 KWD a year [V0068].
- **Police certificate:** a home-country criminal record certificate under 90 days old, given to the Kuwaiti embassy [V0033].
- **Kuwait University:** in January 2024 it charged international students 100 KWD per credit unit and a 10 KWD application fee, and planned to admit at least 300 of them, in seats Kuwaiti students did not take [V0101, V0130].
- **Scholarship:** Kuwait University's Graduate Study Excellence Scholarship for Master's and PhD students has been open to international applicants, with air tickets, housing, government health insurance and help with the entry visa [V0128, V0129].

Changing a study residence to another purpose follows rules set by the Director General of Residence Affairs [V0046].

## Changing visa type

A residence can be changed from one type or purpose to another under the General Administration of Residence Affairs' rules [V0047]. Since August 2026, converting a visit visa into a residence costs 150 KWD (Interior Ministry Decision No. 1091 of 2026); domestic workers are exempt [V0089, V0090, V0091].

A visit visa can become a residence in five cases only [V0055]:

1. **Government visit visa to work residence:** for university graduates and technical specialists invited by a ministry or public body, with the Director General's approval [V0106].
2. **Domestic workers** [V0107].
3. **Family or tourist visit visa to dependant residence (joining a family sponsor)** [V0108]. For a wife, sons under 18 and daughters under 21, the sponsor must earn at least 800 KWD a month and pay the conversion, health insurance and residence fees [V0093]. A family visit visa can never become an Article 18 work permit [V0094].
4. **A worker who entered on a work entry visa, started the residence process, had to leave and came back within one month** [V0109].
5. **Other cases** the Director General of Residence Affairs accepts [V0110].

Each case is judged individually and needs the Director General's approval [V0125]. In August 2026 hundreds of applications were rejected because applicants did not meet the conditions [V0092]. For moving from a domestic worker visa (Article 20) to a private-sector work visa (Article 18), see Changing employer or leaving.
