## a. Draft doc text

### Step 2: The job offer and contract

1. **Check the visa type is "18".** Private-sector jobs need an Article 18 work visa ("Visa No. 18") [W0074]. A commercial visit visa ("Visa No. 14") lasts about three months, gives no right to transfer to Visa 18 and is not covered by the labour law (see Changing visa type) [W0074, W0083]. Nurses, home nurses and caretakers must come on Article 18, never on Article 20 (domestic worker), and an Article 20 visa cannot be changed to Article 18 after arrival [W0077, W0078].
2. **Check the job title on the offer matches what you will do and what you can prove.** PAM (Public Authority for Manpower, which issues work permits) checks a worker's qualification, by level, field and whether Kuwait's Ministry of Higher Education recognises it, when it issues, renews or amends a work permit [W0050]. PAM staff check that the field of study matches the profession on the application [W0051]. An employer who makes a worker do work other than what the work permit says faces up to 3 years in jail and fines of KD 2,000 to 10,000 per worker [W0035]. A worker who works for anyone other than his own employer can be deported even with a valid residence (see What can get you jailed or deported) [W0017, W0075].
3. **Never sign a second contract or a blank paper in Kuwait** (your rights under the contract are under Your rights at work) [W0076].

### Step 3: The employer gets your work permit and visa

This happens in Kuwait, between the employer, PAM and the Ministry of Interior (MOI) [W0002, W0009].

1. **The employer gets the work permit from PAM.** The private-sector work entry visa is issued only on the basis of a work permit from PAM [W0002]. Since 1 June 2024 an employer may recruit from abroad as many workers as its licence is estimated for, instead of the old limit of 25% from abroad [W0034]. Since the same date a first-time work permit costs an extra KD 150 [W0033, W0036]. If the job needs a degree, the employer applies through Ashal or the Sahel Business app, and an engineering application without the required approval is rejected automatically [W0050, W0052].
2. **The employer gets the entry visa from MOI.** The entry visa for private-sector work costs KD 10 [W0020]. Since 23 December 2025 a worker arriving on an entry visa to take up residence also pays a KD 5 monthly health insurance fee until the residence is issued [W0029, W0019].
3. **The criminal record.** Everyone coming for residence from a country with a Kuwaiti embassy must have a criminal record certificate (police clearance) attested by that embassy [W0010]. Getting the police clearance in Bangladesh is in Step 4 [W0010].
4. **Drivers and mandoubs (company representatives) who lived in Kuwait before** cannot get a new work visa in either job until two years after they left [W0014].

> **Need to know**

> - How long is a Kuwaiti work entry visa valid for travel after issue?
> - What does an Article 18 visa sell for in Dhaka in 2026, against the KD 150 + KD 10 the employer pays officially [W0033, W0020]?
> - Does PAM charge any other fee for a first permit (the base work-permit fee) on top of KD 150?

### Step 5: Medical check and checking your visa is real

1. **Take the Wafid medical in Bangladesh.** Wafid (formerly GAMCA) is the Gulf states' pre-departure medical, run by the Gulf Health Council; booking, the centres and the fee are as in the Bangladesh-side steps (see Step 4) [W0068, W0070]. In May 2026 Kuwait's Ministry of Health (MOH) brought in the updated Wafid rules (54 articles) [W0068]. You are tested before departure and tested again after you arrive in Kuwait (Step 7, item 2) [W0069, W0071].
   - The certificate is valid for two months from issue for the visa [W0070].
   - Wafid says the result appears within 26 days [W0072].
   - **Scams:** workers accuse Wafid centres of charging booking fees and arbitrary extra costs for vaccinations and other tests [W0073].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which Wafid centres in Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet take Kuwait-bound workers, and what do they charge in 2026?
   > - Does the employer or the worker pay the Wafid fee for Kuwait in practice?

2. **Check the visa is real.**
   1. MOI's residency e-services page (moi.gov.kw/main/eservices/residence) lists "Enquire Visa Application Status" [W0060]. Its links point to kuwaitvisa.moi.gov.kw and rnt.moi.gov.kw [W0060].
   2. Phone MOI Residency Affairs: 25582960, 25582961, 97288200 or 97288211 [W0061].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Can a worker check an Article 18 work entry visa online, on which MOI page, and with which number (visa number, MOI reference number, passport number)? In November 2026 the rnt.moi.gov.kw status page returned "not found" and kuwaitvisa.moi.gov.kw handles visit e-visas.
   > - What does a genuine Kuwaiti work entry visa look like (fields, QR code), and what do fake ones sold in Bangladesh look like?
   > - Does Sahel show a worker's entry visa before he has a Civil ID?

### Step 7: After arrival

You have two months from entry to finish the residence procedures; otherwise you are in Kuwait illegally [W0003].

1. **Keep your passport valid.** For residence your passport must be valid for at least six months; the residence period no longer depends on the passport's expiry [W0004].
2. **Take the arrival medical.** A first residence needs an MOH health certificate showing you have no communicable disease [W0011]. This is the second test after Wafid [W0069, W0071]. MOI's online "medical result readiness" enquiry shows, by reference number, whether your medical result is ready for the residence paperwork [W0063].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which MOH expatriate medical centres take new male Article 18 workers in 2026 (Shuwaikh, Sabhan, Jahra, Ali Sabah Al-Salem, Fahaheel?), at what hours, and what is the fee (KD 10?) and who pays it?
   > - What happens to a worker found unfit on arrival: deportation at whose cost, and is the agency's money refunded?

3. **Give your fingerprints.** A first residence needs fingerprints taken by the General Department of Criminal Evidence [W0011]. Expatriates give them at any Criminal Evidence centre in their governorate, 8:00 to 14:30, not at the airport [W0066, W0067]; centres for company workers include Ali Sabah Al-Salem (Umm Al-Hayman) and the old Jahra Traffic building [W0067]. Companies book fingerprint appointments on MOI's "company fingerprinting" platform [W0064]. To check you are enrolled, open Sahel (Kuwait's government app) > Services > Ministry of Interior > Security Services > "Verifying the necessity of performing a biometric enrollment" [W0065].
4. **The employer gets your residence (iqama).** Article 18 residence is granted on your PAM work permit [W0009]. It costs KD 20 a year and needs government health insurance at KD 100 a year (KD 10 for farm, fishing, shepherd and dairy workers) [W0021, W0025, W0027]. The sponsor must pay the health insurance [W0030].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Do employers deduct the KD 20 residence fee, the KD 100 health insurance or the KD 150 permit fee from workers' pay in practice?

5. **Get your Civil ID.** Your residence is recorded on the Civil ID card issued by PACI (Public Authority for Civil Information) [W0013].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What is PACI's fee for a first Civil ID (KD 5?), how is it collected (PACI kiosk, delivery), and how long does it take?
   > - How does a new worker activate Kuwait Mobile ID (the digital Civil ID app) and Sahel?

6. **Open a bank account and get a SIM.**

   > **Need to know**
   > - Can a new worker open a salary account before he has a Civil ID, and which banks open accounts for low-wage workers?
   > - What do Zain, Ooredoo and stc need for a prepaid SIM before and after the Civil ID, and do SIMs need biometric verification?

7. **Know the Residency Affairs offices** (Jawazat), where residence problems are handled: Shuwaikh (Block 4); Farwaniya (Block 4); Jahra (near Jahra Court, Marzouk Al-Miteb Street, Al-Waha); Mubarak Al-Kabeer (near Lulu Hypermarket, Qurain); Ahmadi (Block 9); and Mishref (Al-Masjid Al-Aqsa Street, Mubarak Al-Abdullah) [W0082, W0087, W0088, W0089, W0090].

**If you had to leave Kuwait before the residence was done:** a worker who entered on a work entry visa, had started the residence formalities, had to leave and stayed out no more than one month can have his visa converted to residence [W0008].

### How long you can stay

You can stay as long as your employer keeps your work permit and residence valid; residence for private-sector work rests on the PAM work permit and ends when its purpose (the job) ends, even before its expiry date [W0009, W0005].

- **Length of each residence:** up to five years [W0006, W0026]. It is never longer than your health insurance [W0007].
- **Renewal fees:** KD 20 a year for the residence (doubled on 23 December 2025) and KD 100 a year for health insurance (up from KD 50) [W0025, W0027, W0028]. No residence is renewed without health insurance [W0031]. Renewing a work permit for the same job needs no new degree papers; a change of job title does (see Work visa: professional and skilled) [W0053].
- **Over 60 without a degree:** from 2020-21 Kuwait banned renewal for workers over 60 without a university degree, and many had to leave [W0040]. In 2021 it was replaced by a high fee, later KD 250 a year plus private health insurance, about KD 900 in all [W0041, W0037, W0038]. In November 2024 this was cancelled: workers over 60 with high-school education or less renew and transfer on the same fees as everyone else [W0037, W0038, W0039].
- **Six months abroad:** your residence is lost if you stay outside Kuwait more than six months in a row [W0015, W0042]. MOI can give permission to stay away longer, while the residence is valid, for KD 5 a month [W0016, W0023].
- **When the job ends:** if your residence ends or is not renewed, Residency Affairs may give you a departure notice of up to three months, renewable once, up to six months from the end of your service, at KD 10 a month [W0012, W0022]. Temporary residence costs KD 10 a month [W0024]. Leaving properly is under Leaving at the end of the job [W0012].

> **Need to know**
> - Does PAM issue Article 18 work permits for one or two years at a time in 2026, and is the iqama issued for the same period?
> - How does a worker apply for permission to stay abroad more than six months: on Sahel, at Jawazat, or only through the employer?

### Your residence permit

The details of your residence ("iqama") are recorded on your Civil ID [W0013]. The employer applies for it and renews it, because Article 18 residence rests on the PAM work permit [W0009]. You cannot move it to another employer without the competent government body's (PAM's) permission (see Changing employer or leaving) [W0009].

1. **Check your Civil ID shows your residence and expiry date** [W0013].
2. **Check your fingerprints are enrolled** in Sahel, as in Step 7, item 3 [W0065].
3. **If your residence expires or is cancelled,** call Residency Affairs (25582960, 25582961, 97288200, 97288211) or go to your governorate's Jawazat office (Step 7, item 7) [W0061, W0082].
4. **If you are trafficked or held,** MOI's Public Moral Protection and Anti-Human Trafficking department answers on 25589648, 25589655 and 25589696 [W0062].

> **Need to know**
> - Can an Article 18 worker see his own residence and work-permit status in Sahel, and what does each status mean?

## Work visa: professional and skilled

The route is the same Article 18 work permit and residence as for any private-sector worker (see Work visa: private-sector worker (Article 18)) [W0009]. The difference is that your job title must match a recognised degree [W0051, W0047].

### Degree attestation

1. **Attest the degree in Bangladesh:** by the university, the education ministry, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and finally the Kuwait Embassy [W0045].
2. **In Kuwait,** the attested degree must be stamped by Kuwait's Ministry of Foreign Affairs [W0046].
3. **Equivalency:** the degree goes to the Ministry of Higher Education for equivalency [W0044]. PAM's system records it as unrecognised, preliminary recognition, administrative recognition or accredited [W0050].
4. **Job title:** PAM checks that your field of study matches the profession on the work permit [W0051].

> **Need to know**
> - Which Bangladeshi office attests a degree for Kuwait (Ministry of Education, then MOFA), and what does the Kuwait Embassy in Dhaka charge?
> - How long does Kuwait's Ministry of Higher Education equivalency take, and does it accept Bangladeshi private-university degrees?

### Engineers

PAM classifies engineers under an engineering job title, and that title is needed both for the work permit and for the profession on the Civil ID [W0047]. The Kuwait Society of Engineers (KSE) no longer runs the accreditation exam; PAM and the Ministry of Higher Education now do degree checks, equivalency and classification from documents, with no exam [W0044, W0048]. Some employers still ask for KSE membership, but it does not replace government recognition [W0049]. An engineering work-permit application without the required approval is rejected automatically [W0052].

> **Need to know**
> - Which Bangladeshi engineering degrees (BUET, other public, private) does Kuwait's Ministry of Higher Education recognise in 2026?
> - What happens to a diploma-holder who comes on an "engineer" visa?

### Nurses and doctors

A valid Kuwait MOH nursing licence is required for any nursing job, including assistant nurse; working without it can lead to deportation and jail [W0080]. Nurses come only on Article 18 [W0077]. Hospitals and agencies in Kuwait have confiscated nurses' certificates and refused release or transfer: keep your originals [W0079]. A nurse recruited inside Kuwait should get a written contract attested by MOH and check the hospital has a valid MOH licence and MOH/PAM quota [W0081].

> **Need to know**
> - What is the MOH licensing exam or Prometric/Dataflow route for Bangladeshi nurses and doctors, and its fee?
> - Does Kuwait's MOH recruit Bangladeshi nurses or doctors directly in 2026?

### Driving licences

A non-Kuwaiti needs more than two years' legal residence, a university degree and a salary of at least KD 600 a month for a private licence (Ministerial Decision 270/2020) [W0054]. Exempt from all three:
- government drivers and agents, and general delegates (mandoubs), with a valid licence from home [W0085];
- domestic workers who worked two years for the same sponsor and changed their residence profession to driver [W0055];
- oil-sector technicians and medical technicians [W0086, W0056].

Engineers and doctors with a degree are exempt from the residence and salary conditions [W0057, W0087]. Managers and accountants need the KD 600 salary and a degree, but not the two years [W0058]. The licence is cancelled if you move to a job not on the list or your residence is cancelled [W0059].

> **Need to know**
> - Do company drivers (heavy, light, bus) on Article 18 get a licence under the "general delegate" exemption, or do companies convert a Bangladeshi licence through a test?
> - How long is an expatriate's licence valid in 2026 (one year, the residence period?), and what does the test cost?
