## Work visa: private-sector worker (Article 18)

Article 18 is the work visa for company jobs in Kuwait's private sector ("Visa No. 18"): cleaning companies, construction, shops, farms, drivers and trades [W0074, M0018, M0022]. Your residence rests on your employer's work permit from PAM (Public Authority for Manpower, which issues work permits), so the employer applies for it, renews it and must agree to most changes [W0002, W0009]. There are two ways in: an agency job order with a BMET permission letter, or a single visa a relative or contact in Kuwait gets from a sponsor (Step 1) [B0169, B0101]. Both pass the Bangladesh Embassy in Kuwait, which since 1 August 2025 checks the salary on the work permit against its minimum salaries before attesting a visa or demand letter [B0112, M0014].

### Step 1: Find a real job

1. **Register on OEP (the Overseas Employment Platform),** the job-seekers' website of BMET (Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training, Bangladesh's government office for overseas jobs) [B0038]. An agency must choose workers from BMET's database, and BMET gives no clearance to a worker outside it [B0170].

   You'll need [B0037, B0038]:
   - your NID (national ID card);
   - a mobile number;
   - Tk 200 for the government fee.

   1. Open [employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration](https://employee.oep.gov.bd/employee/registration) [B0038].
   2. Type your name in English, mobile and NID numbers, date of birth, division and district exactly as on the NID, the captcha, tick the terms and press "যাচাই করুন" (Verify) [B0037].
   3. Print 2 copies of the registration slip and give your fingerprints at your DEMO (District Employment and Manpower Office); DEMO addresses are on BMET's website [B0038, B0039].

   **If it fails:** call the OEP hotline, 09614445271 [B0046].

2. **Learn a trade if you can.** BMET lists every TTC (Technical Training Centre) and IMT (Institute of Marine Technology), with contacts, on its website [B0040]. A skilled trade carries a higher Embassy minimum salary: KD 150 (about Tk 60,500) for a mason, electrician, plumber, painter, welder or mechanic, against KD 90 (about Tk 36,300) for a cleaner or general labourer (KD is the Kuwaiti dinar; KD 1 = about Tk 403) [M0022, M0021, M0020, G0002].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does Kuwait or BMET require a skill test or NSDA (National Skills Development Authority) certificate from Bangladeshi Article 18 workers (drivers, electricians, cooks) before the visa or clearance?

3. **Through a recruiting agency: find an agency with a Kuwait job order, then check its permission letter.** A Kuwait job order reaches Bangladesh as a permission letter from BMET (until December 2024 from the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare) to one licensed agency, naming the employer, each job, the pay, the contract, the conditions and the most the agency may take from you [B0169, B0177, B0179]. The Kuwait letters read for this guide are for cleaners and general labour at KD 75 to 90 a month on 2- or 3-year contracts [B0169, B0177, B0178, B0179].

   Examples:
   - **Rowshon Habib Overseas (RL-2011), 24 December 2024:** 300 cleaners for Al Faisalya Company, Sabhan; KD 75 a month; 3-year contract; employer pays housing, transport, medical care and insurance; 8 hours a day, 6 days a week; the agency may take at most Tk 44,370 [B0169].
   - **Times Enterprise (RL-0385), 21 July 2025:** 350 men as general labour for JM General Contracting Co.; KD 80 a month; 2-year contract; 100-day probation; employer pays the visa fee, housing, basic furniture, transport, medical care and air ticket; at most Tk 1,05,000 [B0178].
   - **Delight Overseas (RL-2287), 7 September 2025:** 266 men as office cleaners for Prosh International, Farwaniya; KD 90 a month; 3-year contract; 8 hours a day, 1 day off a week; employer pays housing, transport, medical care and air ticket; Embassy attestation no. 174 of 2 September 2025; at most Tk 81,730 [B0179].

   Every Kuwait letter carries the same conditions [B0170, B0171, B0172, B0173, B0174, B0175]:
   - the employer bears all Kuwait-side costs (levy, airfare);
   - the agency may take its fee only after your visa and flight are confirmed, and only by cheque, bank draft or pay order;
   - you sign the contract before clearance and get a Bangla translation attested by BMET;
   - an agency representative flies with your group or meets you at Kuwait airport;
   - if you don't get the promised job and pay, the agency has signed (on Tk 300 stamp paper) to pay your food, lodging, fare home and any compensation set by the authorities;
   - after your group arrives the agency must report to the Ministry, BMET and the Bangladesh Embassy in Kuwait.

   **Which agencies hold Kuwait job orders.** Our own count of the letters on BMET's list finds 48 Kuwait letters from 29 agencies, 29 December 2024 to 8 September 2025, for at least 6,910 posts; a post is a worker the letter lets the agency process, not a person who went [B0180]. The agencies with the most posts are Delight Overseas (RL-2287), Prestige International (RL-0232), Heaven Overseas (RL-0600), Masud Jamil Overseas (RL-1400), Loyal Manpower Service (RL-2065), Talukdar Overseas (RL-1923), Times Enterprise (RL-0385) and Rowshon Habib Overseas (RL-2011); a high count shows activity, not fair treatment [B0180]. Red flag: in January 2025 BMET let Masud Jamil Overseas recruit 205 women cleaners for Wael Al-Nusif & Partners Trading Co. on 2-year contracts, and ITV (August 2025) reports that the Embassy has since blacklisted that company, accused of bringing workers on 3- to 6-month contracts and taking Tk 3 to 4 lakh from each [B0177, B0195].

   **Check the letter.** You'll need: the agency's RL number (the recruiting licence number every licensed agency has) and the employer's name [B0180].
   1. Open [bmet.gov.bd/pages/miscellaneous-infos](https://bmet.gov.bd/pages/miscellaneous-infos) and search your agency's RL number, then "কুয়েত" [B0180, B0182].
   2. Open the PDF and compare the employer, job, pay, contract length and maximum fee with what you were told [B0169, B0179].
   3. Check the Embassy attestation number on the letter matches your demand letter; it appears as "দূতাবাসের সত্যায়ন নং" or, on BMET's system page, "Block visa number" [B0179, B0176].

   This check rarely works: BMET's newest posted letter is from 10 December 2025, the 48 Kuwait letters cover a fraction of the 42,746 workers cleared for Kuwait in 2025, and a search for "কুয়েত" finds only 3 of them and "Kuwait" none [B0181, B0180, B0003, B0182, B0183]. A single visa (item 4) has no permission letter [B0101].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Where does BMET publish Kuwait permission letters issued since December 2025, for the 27,964 workers cleared in 2026 [B0004]?
   > - How does a worker apply to an agency holding a Kuwait job order: at its office, through its brokers, or online?

4. **Through a relative or contact in Kuwait (single visa): the sponsor gets the visa and has it attested at the Bangladesh Embassy in Kuwait.** The Embassy attests a single visa from a small company (Visa 18) only with the sponsor (kafeel) present or, since 18 June 2025, a person holding his power of attorney (towkil khas) or his letter of authorisation, who must show the sponsor's original Civil ID (Kuwait's identity card) [B0101, B0102, B0103].

   The sponsor or his representative brings [B0106, B0104, B0105]:
   - the visa attestation contract form signed by the sponsor (free at Room 03 of the Embassy);
   - 2 copies of the visa and 2 photos of the worker;
   - the sponsor's original Civil ID and a copy;
   - the Employer Signature Approval Certificate (Itemat Toukiya) from PAM and the work permit (Tasriah Amel).

   The fee is KD 3 per person, non-refundable, in cash or by KNET (Kuwait's debit card network) at Room 3; the visa is attested the same day and collected from Room 3 at 4 pm [B0109, B0110, B0108]. The attestation counters are open 8 am to 2 pm, Sunday to Thursday [B0168]. A worker on a single visa applies for BMET clearance himself (Step 6, item 3) [B0086].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Can a worker in Bangladesh confirm that his visa was attested by the Embassy (by phone, email or the attestation number), and how?

5. **Have the visa and demand letter checked before paying.** BMET checks job visas and demand letters free within 3 working days at your DEMO or at BMET, 89/2 Kakrail, Dhaka; bring the visa and, if stamped, a copy of the passport page [B0087, B0093, B0069]. BMET checks only Saudi, Qatari, Bahraini, Singapore and UAE visas online, not Kuwaiti ones [B0087].

   What must stand behind a genuine Kuwait job order:
   - **Embassy attestation of the demand letter.** Any Kuwaiti company or farm recruiting Bangladeshis must have its demand letter attested by the Embassy [B0112]. The company's representative comes in person with the demand letter, a power of attorney to the Bangladeshi agency, the employment contract, its service contract or tender, PAM work permits for the workers, licences, the owner's Civil ID and proof of the workers' hostel, and pays KD 0.600 per worker at counter 4 [B0117, B0115, B0116, B0114, B0113, B0118, B0119].
   - **The Embassy's minimum salary.** The Embassy's Labour Wing circular No. BDEM/LW/Kuwait/Ad/2025 of 7 July 2025, signed by Mohammad Abul Hossain, Minister (Labour), sets monthly minimums for an 8-hour day; employers may pay more [M0015, M0023, M0014]:
     - government contract (aqud hukumi): agricultural worker, cleaner and general labour KD 90; driver KD 120 [M0018, M0019];
     - private contract (aqud ahali): cleaner, general labour and guard KD 90; agricultural worker KD 120 [M0020];
     - private-contract skilled jobs (AC and auto mechanic, barber, driver, waiter, mason, electrician, plumber, painter, steel fixer, welder, machine operator, tailor and others) KD 150; chef KD 250 [M0021, M0022].
   - **A contract of at least 2 years.** Before attestation the company signs a certificate on a contract of at least 2 years, the wage scale and the workers' benefits [B0193].

   **Check it is real:** a job offer below these minimums, or a contract shorter than 2 years, should not have passed the Embassy [B0192, B0193, M0014]. How to check the visa itself is in Step 5: Medical check and checking your visa is real [B0087].

6. **Know the cap, and pay late, by bank.** The Embassy says the government's maximum for Kuwait is Tk 1,06,780, and taking more is a punishable offence [B0184]. Each permission letter sets its own, often lower, maximum: Tk 44,370 to Tk 1,05,000 in the 2024-2025 letters [B0169, B0179, B0178]. The employer bears the Kuwait-side levy and airfare [B0171]. Your own costs come on top: the passport (Step 4, item 1) and BMET's welfare fee, insurance, clearance fee and source tax (Step 6, item 3) [B0048, B0026, B0094, B0085]. The agency may take its money only once your visa and flight are confirmed, by cheque, bank draft or pay order into its bank account; keep the receipts [B0171, B0092].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which is the current Kuwait cap: Tk 1,06,780 (Embassy, August 2025) or Tk 1,03,780 (2017 table), and is there a newer Ministry order [B0184, B0025]?

7. **Borrow from PKB if you need to** (Probashi Kallyan Bank, the government bank for migrants), once you have a visa: up to Tk 3 lakh at 9% simple interest, repaid monthly over up to 3 years, decided within 7 working days [B0030].

   You'll need [B0028, B0029]:
   - the free bank form, 3 photos and an NID copy;
   - copies of your passport, visa and BMET card, and the contract;
   - 3 signed MICR cheque leaves from your own account, with a statement;
   - two guarantors: one financially solvent person with cheques, statement and proof of income, and one family member or close relative.

   You and the guarantors must live in the branch's area, and you must be 18 to 60 [B0027]. Fees are 0.5% processing and 0.5% documentation, plus VAT [B0030]. Women can take PKB's women's migration loan, with one guarantor and only the documentation fee [B0032]. Apply at the branch or on mygov.bd, which sends application and tracking numbers by SMS; helpline 16238, or +880 9677-787878 from abroad [B0033, B0031].

### Step 2: The job offer and contract

1. **Check the visa type is "18".** Private-sector jobs need an Article 18 work visa ("Visa No. 18") [W0074]. A commercial visit visa ("Visa No. 14") is short-term, gives no right to transfer to Visa 18 and is not covered by the Labour Law (see Changing visa type) [W0074, W0083]. Nurses, home nurses and caretakers must come on Article 18, never on Article 20 (domestic worker), and an Article 20 visa cannot be changed to Article 18 after arrival [W0077, W0078].
2. **Check the job title matches what you will do and what you can prove.** PAM checks a worker's qualification (level, field, and whether Kuwait's Ministry of Higher Education recognises it) when it issues, renews or amends a work permit, and checks that the field of study matches the profession on the application (see Work visa: professional and skilled) [W0050, W0051]. An employer who makes a worker do work other than what the work permit says faces up to 3 years in jail and fines of KD 2,000 to 10,000 per worker [W0035].
3. **Check the pay and contract length** against the permission letter (Step 1, item 3) and the Embassy's minimum salary (Step 1, item 5) [B0169, M0014, B0193].
4. **Get the contract at least 2 days before you go** and check the job, employer, workplace, length, pay, leave and airfare [B0043]. You must sign it before BMET clearance and be given a Bangla translation attested by BMET [B0172]. Keep a copy: the Embassy asks workers to know the pay and type of work before signing [B0186].
5. **Never sign a second contract or a blank paper in Kuwait** (your rights under the contract are in Your rights at work) [W0076].

### Step 3: The employer gets your work permit and visa

This happens in Kuwait, between the employer, PAM and MOI (the Ministry of Interior, which issues visas and residence) [W0002, W0009].

1. **The employer gets the work permit from PAM.** The private-sector work entry visa is issued only on a PAM work permit [W0002]. Since 1 June 2024 an employer may recruit from abroad as many workers as its licence is estimated for, instead of the old limit of 25% from abroad, and a first-time work permit costs an extra KD 150 (about Tk 60,500) [W0034, W0033, W0036, G0002]. If the job needs a degree, the employer applies through Ashal or the Sahel Business app, and an engineering application without the required approval is rejected automatically [W0050, W0052].
2. **The employer gets the entry visa from MOI.** The entry visa for private-sector work costs KD 10 [W0020]. Since 23 December 2025 a worker who arrives on an entry visa to take up residence also pays a KD 5 monthly health insurance fee until the residence is issued [W0029, W0019].
3. **The visa must be used in time.** In April 2026, during the Iran war that began on 28 February 2026 and closed Kuwait's airspace, Kuwait's Foreign Ministry told embassies that work, domestic and family entry visas held by people outside Kuwait who could not enter would not be extended, and that issuing of new entry visas was suspended [S0001, S0012, S0013]. The employer must apply for a new entry visa once issuing resumes [S0013, B0203]. BMET clearances for Kuwait fell to 1,376 in April and in May 2026 and rose to 3,914 in October 2026 [B0024, B0019, B0020].
4. **Drivers and mandoubs (company representatives) who lived in Kuwait before** cannot get a new work visa in either job until two years after they left [W0014].

### Step 4: Passport, police clearance and certificates

1. **Get an e-passport.** You fill the form online and submit it at the divisional or regional passport office for your present address [B0081, B0082].

   You'll need [B0051, B0052, B0083]:
   - your NID (compulsory over 20); fill the form exactly as on it; nothing needs attesting;
   - technical certificates such as a driving licence, uploaded with the form and shown in original at the office.

   1. Fill in the form at epassport.gov.bd [B0081].
   2. Pay through A-Challan, DGePay or ShurjoPay, or at a bank: a 48-page passport costs Tk 4,025 / 6,325 / 8,625 for 5 years and Tk 5,750 / 8,050 / 10,350 for 10 years (regular / express / super express) [B0050, B0048].
   3. Go to the passport office with the original NID and certificates; delivery is within 15, 7 or 2 working days of biometric enrolment, and no police verification is needed with an online-verified NID [B0083, B0049, B0054].

   For residence in Kuwait the passport must be valid for at least six months [W0004].

   **If it fails:** passport helpline 16445 (09666716445 from abroad), 24 hours [B0055]. If the passport is lost, file a GD (General Diary, a police report) at the nearest police station and re-apply with a copy of the old passport and the GD [B0053].

2. **Get a police clearance.** Everyone coming to Kuwait for residence from a country with a Kuwaiti embassy must present a criminal record certificate to that embassy for attestation [W0010].

   You'll need [B0056, B0057]:
   - a passport valid at least 3 months;
   - a present address that is the permanent or emergency address in the passport, where you live;
   - a scan of the passport data page attested by a first-class gazetted officer;
   - Tk 1,500 by treasury challan, code 1-7301-0001-2681, at any Bangladesh Bank or Sonali Bank branch, or online by card with a service charge.

   1. Register at [pcc.police.gov.bd](https://pcc.police.gov.bd/), log in and click "Apply" [B0078].
   2. Fill in your personal details and your present and permanent addresses; police verify you at the present address [B0079].
   3. Upload the scanned documents [B0080].
   4. Track it by SMS: "PCC S <reference>" to 26969; collect it with your passport or application copy, or send someone with an attested authorisation letter [B0058].

   **If it fails:** helpdesk Sunday to Thursday, 9 am to 5 pm, 01320001824, 01320001825, help.pcc@police.gov.bd [B0059].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Who takes the police clearance to the Kuwait Embassy in Dhaka for attestation (the agency, the processing agency), must MoFA attest it first, and what does it cost?

3. **Certificates, if your job needs them.** Kuwait is not one of the 114 countries that accept Bangladesh's online Apostille alone, so every document needs both steps below [B0100].
   1. **Issuing authority and Ministry of Education.** SSC and HSC certificates are verified by the Education Board, degrees by the university, then by the Ministry of Education [B0064]. Experience certificates must instead be attested by BMET or the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare and notarised; MoFA (the Ministry of Foreign Affairs) then attests them free [B0063].
   2. **MoFA online.** On mygov.bd, register, choose the attestation service for the document, upload it as JPEG/JPG/PNG and pay Tk 200 a page; it takes 5 working days, and an SMS tells you when the Apostille is issued [B0060, B0061].
   3. **MoFA consular counter.** Take the Apostille and the documents to MoFA's consular counter for attestation [B0100, B0062].
   4. **Kuwait Embassy in Dhaka.** A degree is attested last by the Kuwait Embassy (degree attestation for professionals is in Work visa: professional and skilled) [W0045].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What does the Kuwait Embassy in Dhaka charge to attest a certificate after MoFA, and how long does it take?

### Step 5: Medical check and checking your visa is real

1. **Take the Wafid medical in Bangladesh.** Wafid (formerly GAMCA) is the Gulf states' pre-departure medical, run under the Gulf Health Council; it covers some 50 tests [W0068, K0014]. Since May 2026 Kuwait's MOH (Ministry of Health) applies the updated Wafid rules, which cover the fees applicants pay and attestation of results by GCC missions abroad [W0068, K0015]. Your result is attested by the Kuwait Embassy as part of the visa process, and you are tested again after you arrive (Step 7, item 2) [K0008, W0069, W0071].
   - The certificate is valid for two months from issue for the visa [W0070].
   - Wafid says the result appears within 26 days [W0072].

   **Scams:** workers accuse Wafid centres of charging booking fees and arbitrary extra costs for vaccinations and other tests [W0073].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which Wafid centres in Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet take Kuwait-bound workers, and what do they charge in 2026?
   > - Does the employer or the worker pay the Wafid fee for Kuwait in practice?

2. **Check the visa is real before you pay the rest.** BMET does not check Kuwaiti visas online, and MOI's online visa enquiry is for visit visas, so check the visa through the offices that must have handled it [B0087, W0060, K0071, K0072].

   You'll need: a copy of the visa, the demand letter and the BMET permission letter if there is one (Step 1, item 3) [B0087, B0180].

   1. Read the visa: it must be an Article 18 work visa ("Visa No. 18"), not a visit visa (No. 14) or a domestic visa (No. 20) [W0074, W0077].
   2. Take the visa and demand letter to your DEMO or to BMET for the free check (Step 1, item 5) [B0087, B0093].
   3. Ask the agency which processing agency in Dhaka is handling your visa: every Kuwait work visa must pass through one of the agencies the Kuwait Embassy in Dhaka authorises, at least 10 of them [N0002, N0003].
   4. Ask for the Bangladesh Embassy in Kuwait's attestation of the demand letter (company visa) or of the single visa (Step 1, item 4): a company recruiting Bangladeshis must have it [B0112, B0101].
   5. Online, MOI's residency e-services page (moi.gov.kw/main/eservices/residence) lists "Enquire Visa Application Status", but its links go to kuwaitvisa.moi.gov.kw, which handles tourist, family, business and government visit visas, and rnt.moi.gov.kw [W0060, K0072, K0071]. MOI Residency Affairs answers on 25582960, 25582961, 97288200 and 97288211 [W0061].

   **Scams:** a worker who came through a Bangladeshi agency to Kuwait Oil Al Naseef Company was told on arrival that his visa was fake, that he still owed money for it, and that he would be sent home unless the broker paid [K0050]. The Kuwait Embassy in Dhaka gave journalists only an email address for questions and did not answer them [K0051].

### Step 6: Orientation, BMET clearance and the flight

1. **Do the pre-departure orientation (PDO), 3 days.** It is compulsory, at one of 70 BMET institutes; pay against a money receipt (Tk 200 at Feni TTC; pay no more) [B0089, B0041]. Download the certificate at training.oep.gov.bd/pdo-certificate: type your passport or NID number and the captcha [B0042].
2. **Check the contract once more** against Step 2: The job offer and contract [B0043, B0172].
3. **Get BMET clearance (the smart card).** For a group visa the agency applies to BMET's emigration branch with the visa copy, the attested contract, your attested registration copy, a Tk 300 stamp undertaking, the pay order for the welfare fee and the source-tax challan; BMET clears within 3 working days of the visa [B0084].

   Your costs:
   - welfare fee Tk 5,500 to the Wage Earners' Welfare Board, from 1 October 2026 [B0026];
   - insurance Tk 1,000 once (5 years, Tk 10 lakh cover) [B0094];
   - clearance fee Tk 250 [B0085];
   - source tax Tk 1,200 (skilled) or Tk 800 (unskilled) on a group visa; Tk 250 on an attested single visa, Tk 400 on an unattested one [B0085].

   **Single visa:** apply yourself at BMET's emigration branch in Dhaka or the Chattogram DEMO with your application, the visa copy, any contract, an attested OEP registration copy, a Tk 300 stamp undertaking and the pay order for the welfare fee and insurance; it takes 3 working days [B0086]. Women on a single visa need a guardian's no-objection letter on stamp paper [B0044].

   **Check it is real:** at portal.wewb.gov.bd/bmet-wewbm-check type your passport number (or membership ID) and the captcha; the airport welfare desks also check smart cards [B0045, B0090].

   **If it fails:** BMET's Director (Emigration), +8802222222415, 01711-135901, diremi@bmet.gov.bd, or the OEP hotline 09614445271 [B0088, B0046].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Does BMET refuse clearance for a Kuwait visa that lacks the Embassy's attestation, and is the attestation number checked on OEP at clearance [B0194]?

4. **The flight.** The employer pays the airfare, and an agency representative must fly with your group or meet you at Kuwait airport; the agency sends the flight schedule and worker list to the Embassy and BMET [B0171, B0173]. Kuwait's airspace was closed from 28 February to 23 April 2026; Kuwait Airways and Jazeera Airways restarted from Kuwait International Airport on 26 April, with Kuwait Airways planning Dhaka flights four times a week [S0001, S0002, S0003]. The airspace closed again on 18 July 2026, and the Bangladesh Embassy issued emergency hotlines after new missile and drone attacks in early September 2026 (see Contacts) [S0025, B0202].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which airlines fly Dhaka or Chattogram to Kuwait in November 2026, and what does a one-way ticket cost?

5. **Before you fly:** keep one set of copies at home and 3 with you, open 2 bank accounts, and never carry a packet you don't know [B0095].

### Step 7: After arrival

You have two months from entry to finish the residence procedures below; otherwise you are in Kuwait illegally [W0003].

1. **Keep your passport valid.** For residence your passport must be valid for at least six months; the residence period no longer depends on the passport's expiry [W0004].
2. **Take the arrival medical at an MOH centre.** A first residence needs an MOH health certificate showing you have no communicable disease [W0011, K0008]. Everyone except domestic workers needs an appointment booked in advance [K0006].
   1. Go on the appointment: MOH's Expatriate Labor Screening Unit runs five main centres, Shuwaikh (the main one), Fahaheel, Rumaithiya, Jahra and Sabhan, open Sunday to Thursday, 7:30 am to 6 pm; Ali Sabah Al-Salem (Umm Al-Hayman) has also been listed [K0003, K0007].
   2. You are tested for HIV, hepatitis B and C and tuberculosis, and for malaria and filariasis depending on your country, and may be given vaccinations, including meningitis [K0009, K0001].
   3. The result goes straight into MOI's system for your residence; ask the company for the reference number and check MOI's online "medical result readiness" enquiry [K0002, W0063].

   **If it fails:** a suspected infection is re-tested to confirm it; cases that pose a significant public-health threat are deported, and treatable cases are referred to specialised centres [K0004]. MOH has said that a worker found with a listed disease is deported and put on the banned list [K0013]. Since May 2025 an "indeterminate" HIV or hepatitis C result makes a new arrival medically unfit [K0011, K0012].

   > **Need to know**
   > - What does the arrival medical cost in 2026, who pays it in practice, and is it booked on eservices.moh.gov.kw or on Sahel?
   > - Who pays the ticket of a worker deported as medically unfit, and does the agency refund him?

3. **Give your fingerprints.** A first residence needs fingerprints taken by the General Department of Criminal Evidence [W0011]. Expatriates give them at any Criminal Evidence centre in their governorate, 8:00 to 14:30, not at the airport; centres for company workers include Ali Sabah Al-Salem (Umm Al-Hayman) and the old Jahra Traffic building [W0066, W0067]. Companies book the appointments on MOI's "company fingerprinting" platform [W0064]. To check you are enrolled, open Sahel (Kuwait's government services app) > Services > Ministry of Interior > Security Services > "Verifying the necessity of performing a biometric enrollment" [W0065].
4. **The employer gets your residence (iqama).** Article 18 residence is granted on your PAM work permit [W0009]. It costs KD 20 a year and needs government health insurance at KD 100 a year (KD 10 for farm, fishing, shepherd and dairy workers), and the sponsor must pay the health insurance [W0021, W0025, W0027, W0030].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Do employers deduct the KD 20 residence fee, the KD 100 health insurance or the KD 150 permit fee from workers' pay in practice?

5. **Get your Civil ID.** The Civil ID, issued by PACI (Public Authority for Civil Information), is your residence permit: since 10 March 2019 no residence sticker goes in the passport, and the card carries your passport number and profession and is needed to leave and re-enter Kuwait [W0013, K0016, K0017]. After the medical, fingerprints and residence, your employer requests the card through PACI's online system [K0018].

   You'll need: your Civil ID number, which the company's HR department can give you [K0020].

   1. Check the card: open services.paci.gov.kw/card/inquiry, choose "Card Status", type your Civil ID number and submit; when the card is ready the page says so and names where to collect it [K0077, K0021]. Or call PACI on 188988 [K0022].
   2. Pay the KD 5 fee online on PACI's website; delivery to your home costs KD 2 more, and a lost or damaged card costs KD 20 to replace [K0027, K0028, K0029].
   3. Collect it from PACI's vending machines, between 9 am and 8 pm, within 3 months [K0030, K0031]. In 2020 more than 200,000 cards lay uncollected, over 90% of them belonging to Article 18 workers [K0032, K0033].
   4. Check that your name and passport details on the card match your passport exactly, or you may be stopped when leaving or re-entering [K0019].
   5. Set up the digital Civil ID in the Kuwait Mobile ID app (Hawyti) once you have the number, even before the card arrives [K0023]:
      1. Install "Kuwait Mobile ID" from Google Play, the App Store or Huawei AppGallery, then tap "Get started", then "registration" [K0073, K0074].
      2. Choose "enter data manually", type your Civil ID and passport numbers, then your email and mobile number, and tap "next" [K0075, K0024, K0076].
      3. Take a face photo with the front camera; once it is verified you get an OTP (one-time password) by SMS; enter it [K0026].
      4. Set a PIN of 4 to 12 digits, not in sequence or repeated, and confirm it [K0025].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Is the first Civil ID still KD 5 in 2026, and does the employer pay it or deduct it?
   > - Where do new Article 18 workers collect their cards in 2026, and does the company often collect them for workers?

6. **Open a bank account and get a SIM.**
   - **Bank account.** Since January 2025 the Central Bank of Kuwait requires banks to open accounts for everyone, including low-paid service and craft workers, and forbids refusing an account because of salary [K0034, K0035]. Banks must open these "worker accounts" when asked but do not advertise them, so ask for one [K0036]. An employer with five or more workers must pay wages into each worker's bank account (see Sending money home) [G0029].
     - **ABK's Al Amel account** (Al Ahli Bank of Kuwait) is for company workers earning less than KD 300, expatriates only, with dedicated branches in Sabhan and Salmiya [K0037, K0038, K0039]. You'll need: a letter from your company asking ABK to open the account, the company's commercial licence and signature authorisation, copies of your Civil ID, passport (with the address page) and work permit, and the account form [K0040, K0041, K0042]. It gives a free debit card for 5 years; salaries from other banks can't be paid into it [K0043, K0044].
     - **Ordinary salary accounts** often need a higher salary: Burgan Bank's Al Rateb account needs at least KD 400 for expatriates [K0045].
   - **SIM card.** Residents register a SIM with their Civil ID; before you have it, a visitor's SIM is registered on the original passport [K0047, K0048]. Zain, Ooredoo and stc must verify every new customer's identity [K0049].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Can a new worker open any bank account before his Civil ID is issued, and which banks other than ABK open accounts for KD 90-150 workers?
   > - Do employers keep workers' ATM cards or PINs, and where does a worker complain?

7. **Know the Residency Affairs offices** (Jawazat), where residence problems are handled: Shuwaikh (Block 4); Farwaniya (Block 4); Jahra (near Jahra Court, Marzouk Al-Miteb Street, Al-Waha); Mubarak Al-Kabeer (near Lulu Hypermarket, Qurain); Ahmadi (Block 9); and Mishref (Al-Masjid Al-Aqsa Street, Mubarak Al-Abdullah) [W0082, W0087, W0088, W0089, W0090].

**If you had to leave Kuwait before the residence was done:** a worker who entered on a work entry visa, had started the residence formalities, had to leave and stayed out no more than one month can have his visa converted to residence [W0008].

### Scams

#### Done to workers

- **The "free visa" (ফ্রি ভিসা, আজাদ ভিসা):** no such visa exists in Kuwait law; a ring in Bangladesh uses the word to sell visas for lakhs of taka, and workers find on arrival a visa tied to a sponsor with no guaranteed job [N0089, N0091]. Bangladesh's ambassador met community groups in May 2025 to warn against it [N0092].
- **The bought single visa:** an activist told bdnews24 (July 2025) that agents sell a single visa for up to KD 2,000 (about Tk 8 lakh), and workers who went in May and June 2025 paid Tk 6.5 to 7 lakh [B0200, B0199]. The Embassy says buying or selling a visa is a crime for both giver and taker [B0184].
- **The fake or unpaid visa:** the company says on arrival that the visa is fake or not fully paid, and demands money from the broker or sends the worker home (how to check is in Step 5: Medical check and checking your visa is real) [K0050, N0008].
- **The short contract:** an employer blacklisted by the Embassy was accused of bringing workers on 3- to 6-month contracts after taking Tk 3 to 4 lakh from each (Step 1, item 3) [B0195].
- **The processing overcharge:** the Kuwait-Embassy-authorised processing agencies in Dhaka were accused in early 2025 of taking USD 150-300 (Tk 17,000-35,000) per visa against an official fee of Tk 6,270 [N0001].
- **The visit visa sold as a job:** brokers send people on tourist or visit visas with false job promises [B0065]. A visit visa holder may not work and must leave when the visit ends (see Visit visas (tourist, family, business)) [M0009].
- **Wafid extras:** booking fees and extra charges for vaccinations and tests (Step 5, item 1) [W0073].
- **Women's domestic jobs:** rings are sending women domestic workers illegally, though the Embassy is not attesting these visas (see Work visa: domestic worker (Article 20)) [N0101, N0031].

#### Tried by workers

- **Working outside your sponsor:** working for anyone but your own employer can get you deported even with a valid residence (see What can get you jailed or deported) [W0017, W0075, N0090].
- **Coming on a visit visa to switch to a job:** a commercial visit visa gives no right to transfer to Visa 18 and is not covered by the Labour Law [W0074, W0083].
- **Buying a residence or trading permits:** in November 2026 Kuwait's Criminal Court jailed a Bangladeshi sanitation worker for four years with hard labour for trading residence permits through a company, forgery and bribery; the company was fined KD 10,000 per worker and lost its licence [N0056, N0057].

#### If an agency or broker takes your money

1. **Call the Probashi call centre,** 16135 (toll free, run by the Wage Earners' Welfare Board) [B0047].
2. **Complain in writing, with evidence, to BMET's Director General:** it is free and decided within 30 working days; Director (Employment) 01711111544, diremi@bmet.gov.bd [B0096]. The Ministry's monitoring wing asks for clear details of who took the money, when and how much [B0201].
3. **From Kuwait,** give a written complaint to the Embassy's Labour Wing, which forwards it to the authorities in Bangladesh [B0135].
4. **Get free legal advice** on 16699 [B0067].

Taking money on a false job promise or holding a migrant's passport is a crime punishable by up to 5 years' jail and a fine of at least Tk 1 lakh [B0070].

Sample complaint (in Bangla or English, with copies of receipts): "To the Director General, BMET, 89/2 Kakrail, Dhaka. I, [name], NID [number], OEP registration [number], paid Tk [amount] to [agency name, RL number / broker name, phone] on [date] at [place] for a Kuwait job as [job] with [employer]. [What happened.] I ask BMET to recover my money and act against the agency. Attached: [receipts, visa copy, messages]." [B0096, B0201]
