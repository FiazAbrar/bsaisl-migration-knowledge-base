## a. Draft doc text

### Your rights at work

#### Which law covers you

The Private Sector Labour Law (Law 6 of 2010) covers private-sector employees, that is, workers on an Article 18 work residence [L0002, L0087]. Domestic workers such as housemaids and house drivers on Article 20 are not covered; they have their own law (see Work visa: domestic worker (Article 20)) [L0001]. The law sets only the minimum: better terms in your contract or the company's rules apply over it [L0003]. A worker on a visit visa (Article 14) has no rights under the Labour Law, even if a company brought him to work [L0110].

> **Need to know**
> - Which of Law 6/2010's articles have been changed since 2010 (amendments of 2013, 2017, 2018 and any after the 2024 residence law), and is there an official consolidated English text on PAM's site?

#### Contract

Your contract must be written and must state the date signed, the start date, the wage, the period (if fixed) and the kind of work, in three copies: one for you, one for the employer and one for PAM (the Public Authority for Manpower, which issues work permits and handles labour complaints) [L0004]. It is in Arabic; a translation may be attached, but the Arabic text wins if they differ, so ask for the contract in Arabic and English [L0007, L0104]. A fixed-term contract runs from one to five years and can be extended only if both sides agree; the employer may not extend it without your consent [L0008, L0091]. Probation is at most 100 working days, only once with the same employer; during it either side may end the contract without notice, and if the employer ends it, it still owes you end-of-service pay for the time worked [L0009, L0010].

Once signed, the wage can't be cut: any agreement to pay less, made before or after, is void [L0005]. The employer may not give you work that doesn't match the job in the contract [L0006]. Don't sign a second contract or a blank paper in Kuwait [L0111]. A common trick is a new, lower contract on arrival: one worker promised KD 200 a month was paid KD 90 after the company changed the contracts without consent [L0356].

#### Pay and deductions

**Minimum wage**
The legal minimum in the private sector is KD 75 a month (PAM Decision 14 of 2017), for contracts and work permits made or renewed since then [L0424, L0425]. Separately, since 1 August 2025 the Bangladesh Embassy in Kuwait refuses to attest a new visa that pays less than its own minimums, all for an 8-hour day: KD 90 for an unskilled worker on a government contract (akud hukumi), KD 120 for an unskilled worker on a private contract (akud ahli), KD 120 for a skilled driver and KD 150 for a skilled worker [L0341, L0342].

> **Need to know**
> - Has Kuwait raised the KD 75 private-sector minimum since 2017 (Law 6/2010 requires a review at least every five years) [L0086]?
> - Does the Bangladesh Embassy's KD 90 to 150 floor still apply in November 2026, and does it cover visas attested before 1 August 2025 [L0341]?

**When and how you are paid**
Monthly-paid workers must be paid at least once a month, and pay may not be more than seven days late [L0036, L0035]. Wages go into your account at a bank in Kuwait; PAM says cash is allowed only for a new worker's first two months in the country [L0037, L0351]. An employer that doesn't pay through the bank can be fined up to the total it owes and must still pay [L0075]. Since February 2026 PAM's monitoring system detects a short or missing salary deposit, suspends the company's file and sends you the reason and amount through the Sahel app (the government's app for residence and labour services) [L0355, L0352]. Regular allowances count as part of your wage when your dues are calculated, and dues are worked out on your last salary [L0034, L0042].

**Deductions**
No more than 10% of your wage may be deducted to repay a loan from the employer, with no interest, and all deductions for debts together may not exceed 25% [L0038, L0039]. Penalty deductions may not exceed five days' wage a month [L0012]. You can't be forced to buy food or goods from the employer or from particular shops [L0040]. Nothing may be charged or deducted for safety equipment [L0063].

#### Hours, rest and leave

**Hours and overtime**
At most 8 hours a day and 48 a week; in Ramadan at most 36 hours a week [L0043]. After at most five hours in a row you get a break of at least one hour, which doesn't count as working time [L0044]. The employer must post the daily hours, breaks, weekly day off and holidays at the workplace [L0064]. Overtime needs a written order from the employer and is capped at 2 hours a day, 3 days a week, 90 days and 180 hours a year [L0047, L0045]. It is paid at least 25% above your normal rate; the employer must keep an overtime record, and you may prove overtime by any means, so keep your own log and photos of your timesheets [L0046, L0048]. Unpaid overtime and food allowance are among the commonest complaints to the Indian Embassy's labour wing [L0110].

**Rest day and holidays**
One fully paid day off (24 hours) after every six working days [L0050]. If you work on it, you get at least 50% extra plus another day off [L0049]. Paid public holidays include three days each for Eid al-Fitr and Eid al-Adha; work on a holiday is paid double plus a day off in lieu [L0052, L0051].

**Leave**
Annual leave is at least 30 working days on full pay, earned in the first year after six months; weekly days off, public holidays and sick days falling in it don't count as leave [L0408, L0409]. Leave pay is paid before you go [L0056]. The employer sets the dates, and leave can be carried over up to two years' worth [L0057, L0058]. Untaken leave is paid in cash when the contract ends [L0059]. Working for someone else during your leave lets the employer take the leave pay back [L0060]. The employer may not dismiss you while you are on leave [L0019]. Sick leave each year is 15 days on full pay, 10 at three-quarters, 10 at half, 10 at a quarter and 30 unpaid, with a certificate from the employer's doctor or a government health centre [L0053, L0054]. After two years with the same employer, a worker who hasn't done Hajj gets 21 days' paid Hajj leave [L0061]. You get three days' paid leave when a close relative dies [L0062]. Going home on leave needs an exit permit (see Going home on leave).

#### Heat

From 1 June to 31 August every year, work in open areas is banned from 11 am to 4 pm under Ministerial Resolution 3 of 2026 [L0403, L0404]. PAM can extend the ban to dust storms, heavy rain or extreme heat and humidity, and urgent work in those hours needs prior approval from its Labour Inspection Department [L0347]. The employer must give cold drinking water, shaded rest areas, first aid and rest breaks [L0346]. Report a site working in the banned hours to PAM's inspection team on 24936192; the employer's file can be suspended until it complies [L0348, L0349].

#### Housing, food and health care

**Housing and transport**
An employer working on a government project or in a remote area must give free suitable housing and transport, or a housing allowance [L0011]. The Indian Embassy tells workers to make sure the contract charges nothing for housing or transport to work and says whether food is given or paid as an allowance [L0103].

> **Need to know**
> - Which ministerial decision sets the minimum standards for workers' housing (space per worker, beds, toilets) under Article 34, and where do workers report bad housing?
> - Must a company that is not on a government project provide housing at all, or only if the contract says so [L0011]?

**Health insurance**
You can't get or renew residence without health insurance; under the Ministry of Health regulations in force since 23 December 2025, the fee for an Article 18 private-sector worker is KD 10 [L0343, L0344]. Dhaman (the Health Insurance Hospitals Company, a public-private partnership) runs hospitals and clinics meant for insured non-Kuwaitis [L0345]. A work injury is treated at the employer's cost (see If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job) [L0068].

> **Need to know**
> - Is the 2025 health insurance fee for an Article 18 worker KD 10 a year, as one report of the regulations says, or KD 100, as another says for most categories [L0344, L0343]? Who pays it in practice, the employer or the worker?
> - Which Dhaman hospital or clinic serves which area, and do company workers still use government clinics for a fee?

#### Your passport

Keeping a worker's passport is illegal but still widespread [L0320]. If your company won't give it back, complain to the PAM office where your residence was stamped (see If you aren't paid, item 2) [L0094]. You must show your passport when the authorities ask and report a lost or damaged one to MOI within two weeks [L0383, L0423].

> **Need to know**
> - Which article of the 2024 residence law or a PAM decision bans an employer keeping an Article 18 worker's passport, and what is the penalty?

#### End-of-service pay

The indemnity (end-of-service pay) for a monthly-paid worker is 15 days' wage for each of the first five years and one month's wage for each year after, up to one and a half years' wage [L0026]. Daily, weekly, hourly and piece-rate workers get 10 days per year for the first five years and 15 days per year after, up to one year's wage [L0027]. Part years count pro rata, and any loan you owe the employer is deducted [L0028]. You get it in full when the employer ends the contract, when a fixed-term contract ends without renewal, and when the contract ends through the employer's fault, your death or incapacity, or the firm's bankruptcy or closure [L0029, L0030, L0405]. Dismissal for misconduct under Article 41(b) doesn't take it away [L0013]. If you resign from an open-ended contract, you get half after 3 to 5 years, two-thirds after 5 to 10 years and all of it after 10 years [L0031]. The Indian Embassy says only the basic salary counts for end-of-service pay, so check how your salary is split in the contract [L0103].

> **Need to know**
> - Does a worker who resigns before 3 years on an open-ended contract get no indemnity at all, as Article 53 implies [L0031]?
> - Is the indemnity calculated on basic salary only, as the Indian Embassy says, or on the full wage with regular allowances, as Article 55 suggests [L0103, L0034]?

### What can get you jailed or deported

The Interior Minister can deport a foreigner even with a valid residence, for example one with no legitimate income or one working for someone else, and the order can extend to dependants [L0369, L0417, L0377]. The 2025 executive regulations add deportation after a felony sentence or a crime of honour or trust, or after four criminal judgments of any kind within five years [L0419]. A person ordered deported can be held up to 30 days, renewable [L0370]. Once deported, you can't come back unless the Interior Minister personally approves [L0099, L0382]. The employer pays the deportation costs, and anyone who employed or housed you illegally pays them in full [L0418].

#### Working for someone else and visa trading

You may not work for anyone except your own employer without permission [L0411, L0106]. Under the 2024 residence law (Decree-Law 114/2024, Article 19, in force since 5 January 2025), this carries up to two years in prison and/or a KD 5,000 to 10,000 fine, which can be settled by paying KD 3,000 [L0368, L0372, L0413, L0414]. The same article bars the employer from using you outside the job you were recruited for, hiring you out to others, or withholding your dues [L0412]. Selling or buying a visa or residence for money, for a real or fake job, is residency trading: 3 to 5 years and/or KD 5,000 to 10,000 for each worker for the seller, and up to one year and/or up to KD 1,000 for the worker who paid [L0373, L0380, L0416]. A worker who paid is exempt if he reports the crime to the authorities, even after getting the permit [L0415]. Sheltering, employing or housing someone without valid residence is also a crime [L0376].

> **Need to know**
> - Has any Bangladeshi worker who reported his visa seller been spared punishment under the informant exemption [L0415]?

#### Absence and late papers

Being reported absent leads to arrest and deportation (see If your employer reports you absent) [L0099]. If your residence ends without renewal or transfer, you must leave within the period MOI sets, at most six months from the end of service [L0422]. Late residency is fined KD 2 a day for the first month and KD 4 a day after, up to KD 1,200, and an expired visa KD 10 a day [L0389, L0388]. The Interior Minister may waive these fines for a person who is deported and leaves [L0387].

#### Strikes and protests

Strikes and protests are illegal; workers who strike can be arrested, jailed and deported [L0105]. In January 2025, 80 Bangladeshi cleaners who stopped work over overtime, residence papers, civil IDs and late pay were told their tickets home were ready and said they were pressed to sign resignations [L0400, L0401].

#### Drugs and alcohol

Alcohol is banned and drinking it is a serious crime [L0108]. Under the anti-drug decree-law of November 2025, trafficking, producing or growing drugs for trade carries death or life imprisonment and fines of KD 100,000 to 2 million [L0398]. Never carry a parcel for someone else, and carry medicines only with a doctor's prescription [L0109, L0107]. An addict who asks for treatment before being charged is immune from prosecution [L0399].

> **Need to know**
> - What does the 2025 anti-drug law set for simple possession or use by a foreigner, and is deportation automatic after a sentence?

#### Religion and social media

Don't post or say anything against Islam or Kuwait's customs, including on social media [L0102].

#### Driving

Most low-wage workers can't get a private driving licence: it needs two years' residence, a KD 600 salary and a university degree, except for 12 exempt categories such as professional drivers [L0396]. Under the traffic law in force since April 2025, driving without a valid licence costs KD 75, or up to 3 months in jail and KD 150 to 300 in court [L0391]. Running a red light is a felony with up to 3 years and up to KD 1,000, and using a phone while driving costs KD 75 [L0390]. Police arrest drivers under drink or drugs, drivers who injure or kill someone, and drivers who flee an accident [L0392]. Carrying passengers for money without a permit is a serious offence [L0394]. A foreigner can be deported for a single serious traffic offence after investigation; 15 were deported in May to July 2025 [L0395]. In October 2026 the Interior Minister said 300 delivery workers had been deported for violations in a few days [L0354].

> **Need to know**
> - Which traffic offences lead to deportation of a foreigner, and is a first serious offence enough, as the Traffic Department said in October 2025, or only a repeat, as officials said in April 2025 [L0395, L0393]?

#### Debts and travel bans

Unpaid debts and court cases can bring a travel ban (see Sending money home) [L0317].

### If you aren't paid

Complain early and in writing: your claim can't be heard more than one year after the contract ends, and labour cases are free of court fees [L0076, L0077]. Your wage is late if it is not in your bank account seven days after the due date [L0035]. If the employer breaks the contract or the law, for example by not paying, you may quit without notice and keep your full end-of-service pay [L0022, L0023]. Walking away without complaining first gets you reported absent (see If your employer reports you absent) [L0361].

1. **Keep your proof.** Keep your contract, bank statements showing what was and wasn't paid, payslips, your overtime log, and messages with the company about pay [L0360].
2. **Complain to PAM's Labour Relations Department.** Go in person to the PAM labour unit that holds your employer's file (usually the governorate office where your residence was stamped) and ask to file a labour complaint (shakwa amaliya, শাকওয়া আমালিয়া) [L0363, L0092, L0357]. The PAM office in Hawally is in Jabriya, Block 6, Street 104, Building 7 [L0356].

   You'll need: your Civil ID, a copy of your passport and work permit, the contract and your proof from item 1 [L0093, L0360].

   1. Say your agreed salary, what you received and for how many months; PAM can take claims for wages, end-of-service pay, leave pay, overtime, unfair dismissal, work injury, and a local release or cancellation of residence [L0357, L0362].
   2. You get a complaint number by SMS [L0364].
   3. PAM sets a hearing and tells both sides the date, time and the investigator's name [L0365].
   4. At the hearing the investigator tries to settle the dispute; if there is no settlement, PAM must send the case to the court within one month [L0365, L0080].

   **What to say:** "I want to file a labour complaint (shakwa amaliya) against [company name], file number [number]. My contract salary is KD [amount]. I have not been paid for [months] / I am paid only KD [amount]. I also claim overtime and leave pay."

   > **Need to know**
   > - Is there now an online or Sahel route for an Article 18 worker's labour complaint, and what is it called on screen (the April 2026 Sahel service is for Kuwaitis' employment-support grievances) [L0355]?
   > - What are the addresses and phone numbers of PAM's Labour Relations Departments in Capital, Farwaniya, Ahmadi, Jahra and Mubarak Al-Kabeer (the Indian Embassy's list is a scanned PDF)?
   > - How long does PAM take in practice from complaint to hearing, and to referral to court?

3. **The Labour Court.** The court sets a hearing within 3 days of receiving the case, and labour cases are handled quickly and free of charge [L0081, L0077, L0078]. You can use a lawyer [L0407]. Workers' dues rank ahead of the employer's other debts, except its private home [L0079]. In May 2026 the Labour Court ordered a company that had not paid a worker for six months to pay his wages, full end-of-service pay, leave and overtime, let him transfer without its consent, and threw out its absconding report and its claim for recruitment costs [L0359].

   > **Need to know**
   > - How long does a Labour Court case take in practice, and how does a worker enforce a judgment against a company that still won't pay?

4. **Ask to transfer.** Since May 2026 PAM can let you move to another employer before one year if wages aren't paid through the official procedure or the employer breaks the law (see Changing employer or leaving) [L0335, L0330].
5. **If you must leave Kuwait first.** Within one year of the contract ending, a lawyer or representative in Kuwait can file the complaint at PAM for you [L0366, L0088]. A friend in Kuwait can also be authorised [L0358].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Which Bangladesh Embassy lawyer panel or NGO (Kuwait Society for Human Rights) files PAM cases for workers who have gone home, and what does it cost?

6. **The Bangladesh Embassy's labour wing.** Report the problem to the labour wing as well (see Embassy services in Kuwait) [L0400].

PAM's enforcement has tightened: in October 2026 it said it had suspended the files of 10,000 companies that had not paid workers, some for a year or more [L0350].

### If your employer reports you absent

If you stay away from work without an acceptable reason for 7 days in a row or 20 days in a year, the employer may treat you as having resigned [L0015]. It can then file an absence report (ghiyab, called "absconding" or "case" by workers); the report has to reach MOI within 15 days of your first day absent [L0323]. Since March 2026 the employer files it online on PAM's Ashal portal, and you get an alert in the Ashal app [L0321]. The employer must take the printed report to MOI's Residency Affairs Directorate within 3 working days [L0322]. Some employers file false reports after a worker asks for his pay, and visa traders file them to free the visa for resale [L0361, L0324]. The case often appears in the system 15 to 20 days after filing [L0098].

1. **Check your status.** Watch the Ashal and Sahel apps for an absence alert [L0321].

   > **Need to know**
   > - Where in Sahel or Ashal does a worker see an absence report against him, and what does the alert say?

2. **Object within 60 days.** Go to the Labour Relations Department and object that the absence was justified or wrongly reported [L0321, L0325]. Bring proof you were working or were unpaid (see If you aren't paid, item 1) [L0360].
3. **Ask the employer to withdraw it.** The employer can cancel the report on PAM's portal [L0327]. A joint PAM and MOI panel may drop a report the employer withdraws or that is malicious, fraudulent or filed in error [L0326].
4. **Ask to transfer.** A false or malicious report is a ground to transfer before one year (see Changing employer or leaving) [L0329].
5. **If it can't be cleared.** A worker with an absence case who has his passport can surrender to the deportation authorities with a ticket; once deported he can't return (see Going home without valid papers) [L0098, L0099].

> **Need to know**
> - Is the 60-day objection window counted from the alert or from the report date, and what happens to a worker who objects late [L0321]?
> - Can an absent worker be arrested while his objection is pending?

### Changing employer or leaving

#### If you want to change employer

The basic rule: you can transfer only after one year with your employer and with its consent [L0333]. Changing sponsor needs the current employer's approval or MOI's [L0385]. On an open-ended contract after 3 years with the same sponsor, you can move without the employer's approval [L0096]. On a fixed-term contract still running, you can't transfer or get a release without the employer's approval [L0095].

Since May 2026 (Administrative Decision 680 of 2026), PAM can allow a transfer before one year without consent if it finds that [L0328, L0334]:

- the employer failed to issue or complete your work permit or residence through its own negligence, or its file is suspended [L0332];
- it filed a false or malicious report against you [L0329];
- it broke the Labour Law so that you may quit without notice: unpaid wages, abuse, unsafe work, fraud in the contract terms, false criminal charges [L0330];
- wages weren't paid through the official procedure, or you won a final court ruling against it [L0335];
- the firm went bankrupt or closed for good [L0331].

Apply through a complaint at PAM's Labour Relations Department (see If you aren't paid, item 2); PAM investigates first [L0334]. PAM's director general said in October 2026 that transfers without sufficient reason won't be allowed [L0353].

> **Need to know**
> - What does a transfer cost the new employer in 2026: KD 300 for a transfer before one year (2016 decision), plus the KD 150 per permit fee of 2025 [L0336, L0338]? Who pays it in practice?
> - Is the 3-year rule for open-ended contracts still applied by PAM [L0096]?

#### Resigning or being dismissed

On an open-ended contract either side gives at least three months' written notice for a monthly-paid worker [L0017]. During the employer's notice you may take one paid day or 8 hours a week to look for a new job [L0018]. Whoever ends a fixed-term contract without the right to do so owes the other side compensation of up to the wage for the time left [L0021]. Many contracts make a worker who resigns early repay recruitment, visa, travel and medical costs; PAM can be asked to waive it [L0090, L0089, L0319]. You can't be dismissed for claiming your rights, or for your sex, origin or religion [L0020]. A dismissed worker can challenge the dismissal at the Labour Court and, if it was arbitrary, gets end-of-service pay plus compensation [L0014].

#### Leaving at the end of the job

1. **Collect what you're owed.** Your end-of-service pay, untaken leave in cash and your last wages (see End-of-service pay) [L0026, L0059]. The employer must give you a service certificate with your period of service, job and last salary, and return your documents [L0033, L0032]. Check the contract for who pays the ticket home [L0406].
2. **Get the exit permit** (see Going home on leave, item 1) [L0305].
3. **If anything is unpaid, complain before you go**, or within one year through a representative (see If you aren't paid, item 5) [L0076, L0366].

> **Need to know**
> - Does the employer have to cancel the residence and pay the ticket home at the end of a contract under any Kuwaiti rule, or only if the contract says so?

#### Going home on leave

1. **Get an exit permit.** Since 1 July 2025 every Article 18 worker needs his employer's exit permit to leave Kuwait, for a holiday or for good [L0305]. Domestic workers don't [L0316].

   You'll need: a smartphone with Sahel (in English or Arabic only), your Civil ID, and your departure and return dates [L0309, L0318].

   1. Open Sahel, go to Services, choose the Public Authority of Manpower, then Expatriate Workers Services, then Exit Permit [L0311, L0309].
   2. Enter your departure and return dates and submit; the request goes to your employer, who approves it on Sahel Business or Ashal [L0309, L0306].
   3. Apply no earlier than 7 days and no later than 24 hours before you fly [L0310].
   4. Once approved, the permit is valid for 7 days; print it or show it in Sahel at the airport [L0313, L0314].

   **If you have no smartphone or it's an emergency:** the employer can apply for you on Sahel Business or Ashal [L0312]. How fast you get it depends on how fast the employer approves [L0315].

   **If the employer refuses or delays:** complain to the Labour Relations unit that holds the company's file [L0307]. There is no limit on how many permits you can get in a year [L0308].

   **Scams:** sponsors of "free visa" workers have been reported charging about KD 50 for an exit permit [L0317].

   > **Need to know**
   > - How long does PAM take to act on a complaint about a refused exit permit, and can it issue the permit itself?

2. **Take your leave pay before you go** (see Hours, rest and leave) [L0056].
3. **Come back within six months**, or get MOI's permission before the six months end, or your residence lapses [L0421].
4. **Don't work for anyone else while on leave** [L0060].

## If a worker dies, is injured or loses the job

### If you are injured at work

1. **Report it.** The employer must report a work injury, including one on the way to or from work, at once to the area police station, the Labour Department and the insurer; you or someone for you can report it too [L0067, L0066]. If your employer beat or injured you, complain to the police and insist on a medical examination to record the injury [L0097].
2. **Treatment.** The employer pays your treatment, medicine and transport [L0068]. The employer must insure you against work injuries [L0065].
3. **Pay while off work.** Full wage for the whole treatment period, half wage after six months, until you recover, are declared disabled or die [L0070].
4. **Compensation.** You or your heirs get compensation by the ministerial schedule of injuries [L0071]. You lose it if you hurt yourself on purpose, were drunk or on drugs, or broke posted safety rules, unless the injury kills you or leaves over 25% permanent disability [L0072]. An occupational disease that appears up to one year after you leave is covered [L0073].
5. **If you disagree with the disability rating,** object within one month to the Ministry of Health's Medical Arbitration Committee [L0069].
6. **If the employer won't pay,** claim at PAM (see If you aren't paid, item 2) [L0362].

> **Need to know**
> - What does the current ministerial compensation schedule pay for death and for each degree of disability?
> - Which insurer covers work injuries for Article 18 workers in practice, and how does a worker claim from it?

### If a worker dies

The contract ends on the worker's death, and his heirs are owed the full end-of-service pay [L0024, L0405]. Heirs can claim his dues and work-injury compensation [L0080, L0071].

> **Need to know**
> - Who pays to send a Bangladeshi worker's body home from Kuwait: the employer by law, or only under the contract?
> - What are the Kuwait-side steps (police report, Ministry of Health death certificate, General Department of Criminal Evidence, Bangladesh Embassy no-objection letter, cargo) and how long do they take in 2026?
> - How do heirs in Bangladesh collect the dead worker's end-of-service pay and wages from Kuwait?

### If you lose the job

The contract ends if the employer is declared bankrupt or the firm closes for good; you get full end-of-service pay [L0025, L0405]. If the firm is sold or merged, your contract continues with the new owner [L0025]. If the firm is shut for reasons that aren't your fault while the employer wants you to keep working, you are paid for the closure [L0041]. A bankruptcy or closure is a ground to transfer to another employer before one year (see Changing employer or leaving) [L0331]. If your residence ends without a transfer, you must leave within the period MOI sets, at most six months from the end of service [L0422].
