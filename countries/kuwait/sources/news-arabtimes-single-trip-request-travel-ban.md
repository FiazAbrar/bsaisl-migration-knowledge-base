KUWAIT CITY, March 10: The Ministry of Justice announced on Sunday the launch of a new single-trip request service through the unified government e-services application, Sahel. This service allows applicants (defendants) who have been issued a travel ban to submit a request for a single trip and follow up on its status.

In a press statement, the ministry explained that the service is part of the General Department of Enforcement services offered through the Sahel application. The ministry emphasized its ongoing efforts towards digital transformation and its commitment to enhancing public service by introducing electronic solutions to simplify procedures for the public.
