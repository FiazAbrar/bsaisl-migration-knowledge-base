# Kuwait imposes over 4,300 travel bans on debtors

**Published on:** August 7, 2026 12:49 PM

![Travel Ban In Kuwait For Expats: Causes, How To Check Status, And How To Resolve It \| Kuwait Local News](https://kuwaitlocal.com/files/news/images/travel-ban-Kuwait-2026-07-06-14-41-11.webp)

Kuwait issued 4,357 travel ban requests against debtors during June, according to new figures from the Ministry of Justice. The Court Rulings Enforcement General Department released the latest enforcement statistics. The data highlighted extensive action to recover unpaid debts. Officials recorded more than 400,000 enforcement procedures during the month. These actions covered all governorates across Kuwait.

Authorities also requested the arrest and detention of 2,645 debtors. Officials said the measures support the enforcement of court rulings. The largest number of procedures involved seizing debtors’ assets held by third parties. Authorities issued 83,380 seizure reports during June.

Meanwhile, officials processed 39,377 seizure release reports. They also registered 5,826 statutory case reports. Authorities received 4,983 requests to lift asset seizures. In addition, they handled 4,481 requests to remove enforcement seizures from vehicles.

Officials also recorded 2,068 reports involving funds received outside the enforcement department. Another 699 reports involved debtors appearing to settle payments. The Capital Governorate recorded the highest enforcement activity. Authorities completed 120,778 procedures in the area. Ahmadi ranked second with 75,634 procedures. Hawally followed with 67,452 completed actions.

Farwaniya registered 56,736 procedures during the month. Jahra recorded 48,744 enforcement actions. Mubarak Al-Kabeer completed 29,860 procedures. Border crossings, Sulaibiya, and the Kuwait Lawyers Association recorded another 1,315 actions.

The figures reflect Kuwait’s continued efforts to enforce court judgments. Officials aim to improve debt recovery through legal measures. Travel bans remain one of the country’s key enforcement tools. Authorities use them to prevent debtors from leaving Kuwait before resolving legal obligations.

The Ministry of Justice says these procedures help strengthen the legal system. They also encourage debtors to settle outstanding financial obligations promptly.
