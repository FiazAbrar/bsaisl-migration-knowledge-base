Kuwait Visa Check 2026: How to Check Your Kuwait Visa Validity Status Online

Last updated: Jun 3, 2026

Booked a trip to 
Kuwait
 and waiting on your e-Visa? You can verify it online in minutes through the official Ministry of Interior (MoI) portal.

This guide covers checking authenticity, tracking your application, and fixing common errors.

What You Need Before You Start

Have these details ready before opening the portal:

Your passport number, exactly as printed (including any leading zeros)

Your e-Visa reference number, used to confirm authenticity

Your visa application number, used to track the application status

Both numbers appear in the confirmation email you received after applying through the official Kuwait e-Visa platform. Haven’t applied yet? Start with our 
step-by-step Kuwait e-Visa guide
.

Where to Check Your Visa Status

Kuwait relaunched its e-Visa service in 2025 under a new platform run by the Ministry of Interior.

The current official portal is 
kuwaitvisa.moi.gov.kw
, which now handles tourist, family, business, and government visit visas. A separate visa application status check is also available at 
rnt.moi.gov.kw
.

This portal is for visit e-Visas. If you hold a Kuwait residency or work permit, that status is checked separately — see our 
Kuwait work permit guide
. For how Kuwait’s e-Visa fits alongside other Gulf visas, see 
GCC unified visa vs national e-Visas
.

Always rely on these government domains. Avoid third-party lookalike sites that ask for payment to “check” your visa.

How to Check e-Visa Authenticity

Use the authenticity check to confirm that the e-Visa document you received is genuine and matches MoI records.

Open the official 
Kuwait e-Visa portal

Choose the visa enquiry or visa check option

Enter your passport number and e-Visa reference number

Complete the CAPTCHA and submit

If the visa is approved and valid, the system returns your visa details, including the issue and validity dates. If the reference is wrong, you will see an “invalid number” message and should re-check the digits in your approval email.

Physical Authenticity Check

You can also confirm a Kuwait e-Visa by inspecting the printed document for these features:

Title at the top reading “Welcome to the State of Kuwait – Visa” in English and Arabic

Subheading showing the visa type, such as “Tourist Visa – Application,” in both languages

Kuwait police badge on the left and the Kuwaiti coat of arms on the right

Main body in English on the left and Arabic on the right

An English approval line beginning “Dear [Your Name], We are pleased to inform you that your tourist visa application for Kuwait has been approved,” followed by your 12-digit application number

A 9-digit visa number with validity dates printed below

How to Check Visa Application Status

If you have applied but not yet received approval, use the application status tool to see where your file stands.

Go to the 
Kuwait visa application status page
 or open the status option from the main MoI portal

Enter your visa application number

Complete the CAPTCHA and select Submit

You will see one of three results:

Status

What It Means

In Progress

Your application is still under review by the MoI

Approved

Your visa has been issued and is ready to download or print

Rejected

Your application was not approved; review the reason and reapply if eligible

Most tourist e-Visa applications are processed within a few working days. Plan ahead, especially if your travel date is close, since the Kuwaiti weekend falls on Friday and Saturday and processing pauses then.

Troubleshooting Common Errors

Invalid number:
 Re-enter the e-Visa reference number carefully. Make sure you are not using the application number in the authenticity field, or vice versa.

Name or number mismatch:
 Check that the passport number matches your travel document exactly, including any leading zeros.

CAPTCHA loop or page timeout:
 Refresh the page, clear your browser cache, or try a different browser or device.

Reference number missing:
 Search your inbox and spam folder for the original approval email from the MoI. If you cannot recover it, contact the Kuwait MoI helpdesk for assistance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the official website to check my Kuwait visa?

The official Kuwait visa portal is kuwaitvisa.moi.gov.kw, run by the Ministry of Interior. Application status can also be checked via rnt.moi.gov.kw.

How long does a Kuwait e-Visa take to process?

Most tourist e-Visa applications are reviewed within a few working days. Times can vary based on the visa type and the volume of applications.

What is the difference between the e-Visa reference number and the application number?

The application number is used while your file is still under review. The e-Visa reference number is issued once the visa is approved and is used to verify the authenticity of the e-Visa document.

Can I check my Kuwait visa using only my passport number?

No. The portal requires both your passport number and either the e-Visa reference number or the application number, depending on whether you are checking authenticity or application status.

What should I do if my Kuwait visa shows as rejected?

Review the reason given on the portal. You may be able to reapply with corrected details or supporting documents, or apply through a different visa category. For complex cases, contact a Kuwaiti embassy or consulate.

Is the Kuwait e-Visa portal safe to use?

Yes, when you use the official Ministry of Interior domain (moi.gov.kw). Avoid third-party sites that charge extra fees to “check” your visa, as these are not affiliated with the Kuwaiti government.
