    Work permit related complaints - Article 18/20
                          Visa holders

Such cases include Workers asking for the following:
    cancellation of his work permit and transferring it to another Employer,
    the final cancellation of work permit and exiting Kuwait
    Changing the Work Visa, like transfer to the Government Sector (Article 17) or
       Domestic Worker (Article 20) or Family Visa (Article 22).

For the above complaints, the worker should approach Public Authority for Manpower

(PAM)/       for Visa 18 and Domestic Labour Office(DLO)/

   for Visa 20. One may note that:

    Worker himself should be present at the aforementioned places and have to
       register complaint.

    Once the complaint is registered, PAM will give a date of hearing. The worker
       should attend the hearing and if PAM/DLO permits, her/his legal representative
       can also join the hearing.

    In the event where, the worker's request is approved and release order is issued by
       PAM, she/he should complete the transfer formalities to the prospective Employer
       within one month.

    In the event of non-approval of the demand of the worker, the worker can
       challenge the decision at the Central Administration Office in Abu Fatira area
       within 15 days from the date of the decision.

    In case where the worker is unable to resolve his matter, despite taking all the
       necessary measures, the worker must exit Kuwait (and travel to India) after
       canceling her/his residency.

Note: If the worker is not able to transfer his residency in the name of the prospective
Employer within one month from the order of the PAM or in case, PAM does not accede
to the request of worker, the worker must apply for cancellation of residency and exit
Kuwait, as the former Employer is bound by Law to file an absconding case against the
worker.
