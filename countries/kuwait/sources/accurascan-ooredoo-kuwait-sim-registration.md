## The Challenge: Fast Onboarding with Strong Fraud Protection

SIM registration is a critical process in Kuwait, governed by strict regulatory requirements. Telecom providers must ensure that every new customer identity is verified accurately, while also preventing fraudulent document submissions, spoofing attempts, or forged IDs.

For Ooredoo Kuwait, the goal was clear:

- Deliver a smooth digital onboarding experience

- Automate document capture and verification

- Ensure high accuracy across multiple ID types

- Add strong fraud detection layers before OCR extraction

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Accura Scan’s Two-Layer Verification Approach

To meet these requirements, Ooredoo Kuwait deployed Accura Scan’s advanced [**identity verification solutions**](https://accurascan.com/), employing multiple checks ensuring only genuine documents reach the OCR stage.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 

### Layer 1: ID/Document Validation

 

Before any data is extracted, the document must first pass through Accura Scan’s template matching check for validation.

This ensures that the uploaded document matches the correct structure, layout, and design of an approved ID type. It prevents errors caused by incorrect document uploads, unsupported formats& manipulated or tampered templates

This step guarantees that only valid Kuwait and GCC issued identity documents proceed further.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 

### Layer 2: Document Liveness & ID Forgery Detection

 

Once template validation is complete, the document undergoes Accura Scan’s Document Liveness & [**ID Forgery Detection**](https://accurascan.com/id-liveness/).

This advanced AI check confirms whether the document is physically real and not a spoof attempt using printed copies, screenshots, digital or photo overlays and fake document reproductions.

Beyond simple authenticity checks, this layer strengthens protection against sophisticated ID card tampering attempts, ensuring that digitally manipulated or altered documents are detected before data extraction begins.

This layer significantly reduces fraud risk and strengthens onboarding compliance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## High-Accuracy MRZ OCR for Kuwait & GCC Documents

After clearing both verification layers, Accura Scan’s MRZ OCR engine performs fast and accurate capture of identity data from a wide range of documents, including:

- Kuwait Civil ID &Resident ID Cards

- Bedouin ID Cards

- Passports and Visas

- Other GCC National IDs

The system delivers structured, reliable data instantly into Ooredoo’s registration process, reducing manual effort and improving customer experience.
