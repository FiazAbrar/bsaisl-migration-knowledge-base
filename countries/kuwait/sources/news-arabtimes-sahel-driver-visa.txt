KUWAIT CITY, May 13: Kuwait’s Ministry of Interior announced the launch of a new electronic service allowing citizens to issue entry visas for new domestic drivers through the unified government services application “Sahel,” without the need to visit citizen service centers.
The new service was introduced by the Human Resources and Information Technology Sector in cooperation with the Residency Affairs Sector as part of the Ministry’s ongoing digital transformation efforts to simplify procedures and improve public services.
The Ministry said the initiative is designed to enhance efficiency, save users time and effort, and support Kuwait’s broader vision of building an integrated digital government system through expanded electronic services.
