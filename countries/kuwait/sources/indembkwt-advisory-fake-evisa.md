# Advisory: Fake e-Visa Websites

17 November 2025

It has come to the notice of the Embassy of India, Kuwait that several fraudulent third-party websites are falsely claiming to offer Indian e-Visa services. Some of these websites have been involved in financial scams.

The following websites are not authorised by the Government of India:

1.  indianimmigration.org
2.  idiasevisa.org
3.  evisaentry.com
4.  india-immi.org
5.  ivisa.com
6.  india-evisa.it.com

The only official website of the Government of India for e-Visa is [https://indianvisaonline.gov.in](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/). Applicants are advised to apply only through this website to avoid fraud and to protect their personal information.

Page last updated: 18 October 2026
