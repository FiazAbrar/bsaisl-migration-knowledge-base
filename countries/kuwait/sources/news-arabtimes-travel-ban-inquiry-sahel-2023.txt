KUWAIT CITY, Aug 28: The Ministry of Justice incorporated  new feature into the Sahel application. This service will now be accessible to both Kuwaiti citizens and expats, enabling them to inquire about any civil court orders that might hinder their travel plans. Hashem Al-Qallaf, the Undersecretary of the Ministry of Justice, highlighted in a press release that this service will provide comprehensive information regarding travel bans, including associated outstanding payments.
