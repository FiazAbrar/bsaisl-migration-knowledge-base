Terms and Conditions:

1. The Contract is for 2 (two) years / 3 (three) years / 4 (four) years tenure and renewable.
    The workers will be on probation for 100 days from the date of arrival at Kuwait. They will
    be confirmed on successful completion of probationary period. During probation period
    the workers will get full salary and all facilities as per the contract.

2. During the probationary period if any worker is found unsuitable for continuing the work
    under this contract then it has to be based on some logical reason. The logical reason is
    to be immediately {or at least 15(fifteen) days prior to the repatriation of the worker from
    Kuwait, whichever is early} informed to the Bangladesh Embassy, Kuwait in writing,
    clearly mentioning the logical reason along with proof, why he or she is / they are found
    unsuitable for continuing the work under this contract.

3. The Contract shall come into effect from the date the worker enters into Kuwait. If the
    existing project expires, the company shall transfer the worker to another project to cover

    2 (two) years / 3 (three) years / 4 (four) years of job tenure without charging any money
    from the worker.
4. Visa will be provided by the company free of cost.
5. The Company will provide the following to the workers at company's cost:
     Accommodation with basic furniture.
     Transportation from residence to place of work and back.
     Free medical facilities. The company shall insure the worker against injuries and work

         maladies. It shall also commit to the health insurance value in accordance with the
         Kuwait Law no. (1) of the year 1999 and Kuwait Labour Law (Law No. 6 of the year
         2010).
     Air ticket for initial travel to Kuwait and for return to home country at the end of the
    contract.
6. Working hours will be 8 (Eight) hours per 6 (Six) consecutive days per week with one
    day paid leave as per Kuwait Labour Law (Law No. 6 of the year 2010).
7. Overtime allowance will be paid for any additional hours at work in accordance with the
    Kuwait Labour Law (Law No. 6 of the year 2010).
8. In case of death of any worker, the company shall forward the mortal remains of the
    worker to his home country at company's cost and settle all dues of the worker in
    coordination with the Bangladesh Embassy, Kuwait.
9. In case of injury to the worker, the company will pay compensation to the worker in
    accordance with the Kuwait Labour Law (Law No. 6 of the year 2010).
10. The worker shall be entitled to paid annual leave of at least 30(thirty) days. However,
    the worker shall not be entitled to leave in the first year except after at least 6(six)
    months of service under the employer.
11. The company will keep suitable accommodation facilities ready for the workers and will
    allow the Embassy officials to inspect the company accomodation at least 30 (thirty)
    days prior to the arrival of the workers to Kuwait.
12. The company will notify the Bangladesh Embassy, Kuwait of the arrival of the
    Bangladeshi workers within 7(seven) days of their arrival at Kuwait and allow the
    Bangladesh Embassy officials to meet and address all the Bangladeshi workers within
    30(thirty) days of their arrival at Kuwait.
13. The company shall hand over a signed copy of job contract to each worker, within
    30(thirty) days of their arrival at Kuwait.
