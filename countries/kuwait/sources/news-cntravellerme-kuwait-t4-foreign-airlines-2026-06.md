# Kuwait International Airport's Terminal 4 to open to international airlines

Terminal 1, meanwhile, continues to undergo repair works following an Iranian drone attack

By [Hana Al-Khodairi](/contributor/hana-al-khodairi)

June 16, 2026

![A plane of Kuwait Airways approaches Kuwait International Airport in Farwaniya Governorate Kuwait April 26 2026. Kuwait...](https://media.cntravellerme.com/photos/6a30fd4fe3e27f90fffacaa3/16:9/w_2560%2Cc_limit/2273014497)

Xinhua News Agency/Getty Images

International and Gulf airlines will begin flying from [Kuwait International Airport](https://www.cntravellerme.com/story/kuwaits-airspace-reopens-with-flights-taking-off-from-sunday)'s Terminal 4, as the country's international terminal remains closed following an Iranian drone attack.

The Public Authority for Civil Aviation announced the decision to open the terminal, which usually only serves state carrier, Kuwait Airways, to other airlines on Sunday, 14 June, stating it “reflects its national responsibility and commitment to serving travellers”.

Operations will be rolled out over the coming weeks as the authority works with airlines that wish to resume their network to the country. According to local reports, the flights will begin on Wednesday, 17 June, with the first phase allowing participating airlines to operate one flight per day. The [airlines](https://www.cntravellerme.com/story/these-middle-east-airlines-let-unaccompanied-minors-fly-alone) operating from Terminal 4 are yet to be confirmed, but they will include Gulf carriers, Arab and international airlines.

The authority advised passengers “to follow updates regarding their flights and contact their respective airlines before heading to the airport”.

Terminal 1 was hit by an Iranian drone in the early hours of Wednesday, 3 June, resulting in air traffic being suspended and flights diverted. Terminal 1 remains closed, after [Kuwaiti](https://www.cntravellerme.com/story/kuwait-launches-visas-on-arrival-for-gcc-residents) state media confirmed the attack caused one death, multiple injuries and “severe damage”.

![Flights resume at Kuwait International Airport](https://media.cntravellerme.com/photos/6a30fe0aa076fe8743422573/master/w_1600%2Cc_limit/2278525438)

Flights resume at Kuwait International Airport

Anadolu/Getty Images

The country had been slowly rebuilding its flight schedule after months of disruption caused by regional security tensions that forced the suspension of operations earlier this year, bringing direct travel back to the country after the airport’s longest temporary closures in recent years.

Since 26 April, Kuwait-based airlines Kuwait Airways and Jazeera have resumed flights, with operations concentrated across Terminals 4 and 5. The process of getting Kuwait's air traffic back to pre-war levels remains gradual, with both airlines adding routes and frequencies in stages as authorities continue to prioritise safety and operational stability.

The initial closure, which began on 28 February, affected more than 200,000 passengers during the busy spring travel period, forcing many to reroute through neighbouring Gulf hubs, including [Dubai](https://www.cntravellerme.com/destinations/dubai), [Riyadh](https://www.cntravellerme.com/destinations/riyadh) and [Doha](https://www.cntravellerme.com/destinations/doha). Kuwait's airspace reopened on 23 April, with passenger services beginning their return three days later.

Passengers [travelling through Kuwait](https://www.cntravellerme.com/story/can-you-transit-through-the-middle-east-right-now-latest-updates-for-dubai-abu-dhabi-and-doha) in the coming weeks are being advised to check directly with their airline before departure, as schedules may continue to change. Enhanced security measures remain in place, and travellers should allow additional time at the airport, with authorities recommending arrival at least three hours before departure.
