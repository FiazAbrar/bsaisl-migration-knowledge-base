Residency permit ‘trader’ arrested

                                        By B Izzak

                                    July 21, 2025 | 11:39 a.m

EDT

KUWAIT: Residency detectives arrested a citizen who facilitated illegal residency permits for expats in exchange for money, the interior ministry said on Monday. The ministry said the suspect, who is authorized to sign for and represent 29 private companies, confessed to facilitating illegal residency permits for 56 expatriate workers against amounts ranging between KD 350 and KD 1,200 per person.

The suspect facilitated the residencies for expats without providing work. The expat beneficiaries included three residency violators and another three in violation of work permit conditions, the ministry said. Furthermore, some workers were found to be employed by companies other than those from which their residency permits were obtained. The transactions were done through Syrian and Indian intermediaries, the manager told investigators.
The ministry said the bust comes as part of its ongoing efforts to combat trafficking in persons crimes and facilitating residency permits through illegal ways. All suspects involved in the crime have been referred to concerned authorities for further legal action.
The ministry reiterated it will continue its efforts to clamp down on trafficking in persons crimes and called on the public to cooperate with authorities by informing them about such crimes.
