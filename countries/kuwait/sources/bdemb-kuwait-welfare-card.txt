Wage Earner Welfare Membership Card (Expatriate Welfare Card)

General information
Any Bangladeshi expatriate working in Kuwait who does not possess the BMET card can apply for the Wage Earner Welfare Membership Card.
Who can apply for this card?
Bangladeshi expatriates under the age of 18 and over the age of 65 are not eligible to register for this membership card.
It is advisable for the applicant to be physically present when submitting the Expatriate Welfare Card application.
Documents to be submitted
Online Application-01 Copy;
Bangladeshi passport-01 Copy;
Kuwait Civil ID-01 Copy;
Colored passport size photo-01 copy
Website link for online application: 
https://portal.wewb.gov.bd/login
Application fee
17 Kuwait Dinar.
Where to submit the application?
Room No : 13;
Service Hour: 8:00 am – 2:00 pm (Sunday to Thursday).
Application Process
The application must be submitted online;
After submitting the application online, print the submitted application form, attach the necessary documents, and submit it to room no-13;
Pay the application fee at counter no-4 and collect the payment receipt;
The card can be collected from room no-13;
Processing Time
07 (Seven) working days.
What to do if you lose your Expatriate Welfare Card:
If you lose your Expatriate Welfare Card, you can download an online copy of the lost card from the website 
www.wewb.gov.bd
. Please keep your passport number in hand while downloading the online copy.
Apply to the Labor Wing of the Bangladesh Embassy in Kuwait to get another duplicate card similar to the lost card.
Benefits of Expatriate Welfare Card
Providing scholarships to talented children of expatriate workers;
Providing assistance in school/college/university admission for children of expatriate workers;
Providing legal assistance to expatriate workers through the Labor Welfare Wing in Bangladesh Missions;
Providing assistance in the release of detained workers abroad and bringing them back to the country;
Providing financial assistance of 1 (one) lakh taka for the repatriation and treatment of injured, sick and physically disabled workers;
Providing financial assistance in bringing back the bodies of deceased workers;
Ambulance service provided from the airport to transport sick and deceased workers returning from abroad. A cheque of Tk 35,000 (Thirty five thousand) is paid for the transportation and burial expenses of the dead body at the time of handing over the dead body to the relatives of the deceased from the airport;
Financial assistance of 3 (three) lakh taka to the family of the deceased worker who dies abroad;
Collection of death compensation/insurance/arrears of salary/service benefits of deceased workers abroad and distributing to the heirs;
Providing opportunities for inclusion in future programs undertaken for the rehabilitation of affected workers abroad;
Rehabilitation loan is disbursed in just 2 (two) weeks through Probashi Kalyan Bank;
Providing assistance through local administration to protect the assets of expatriate workers and eliminating various difficulties;
Providing services to expatriate workers and their families through the District Employment and Manpower Office and the Expatriate Welfare Desk of the Deputy Commissioner Office of the respective district.
