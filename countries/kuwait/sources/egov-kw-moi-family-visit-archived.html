Family Visit

This service is provided by Ministry Of Interior
Kuwait Government Online > Citizens and Residents > Family Visit

Service Description

  • ​This service enables the issuance of an entry visa for relatives for visit purposes.​

Eligibility or Criteria

  • ​N/A

Required Documents

  • The designated visit application form.
  • A copy of the visitor’s passport, provided that its validity period is no less than six months.
  • Proof of kinship, noting that oath certificates issued by embassies shall not be recognized.
  • A recent salary certificate for a foreign sponsor employed in the government sector, or verification through the computerized system, as applicable.
  • A copy of the sponsor’s Civil ID

Procedures

If the applicant is a Kuwaiti citizen, the required documents shall be submitted to the General Department of Immigration in any governorate. If the applicant is a non-Kuwaiti resident, the required documents shall be submitted to the General Department of Immigration in the relevant governorate. ​

The designated service form shall be completed. ​

The prescribed fees shall be paid.​
