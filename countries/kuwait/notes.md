## 2026 war and visa status (prefix S)

**Timeline, January to November 2026 (with fact ids).**
- Jan 2026: 2,610 BMET clearances for Kuwait (S0031); Feb 1,852; Mar 1,539 (S0032).
- 28 Feb: US-Israeli strikes on Iran; Iranian retaliation hits Kuwait; Kuwait airspace closed (S0001). MOI extends all visit entry visas by one month and gives residents stuck abroad an automatic 3-month absence permit, free (S0008, S0009, S0014; B0204).
- ~1-3 Mar: embassies in Kuwait suspend consular services; Kuwaiti airspace closed but land border with Saudi Arabia open (S0007). Kuwait Airways and Jazeera fly from Al-Qaisumah and Dammam, passengers bussed 4-5 hours (S0004).
- 6-11 Apr (forum, practice only): an employer's mandoub was told on 6 Apr that only valid iqama/Civil ID holders could enter, not visit or first-time work visa holders, by land (S0010); from about 11 Apr Jazeera accepted new visit/family/work visa holders via Dammam with a Saudi transit visa on arrival (S0011). Indian VFS staff said Saudi transit visas were not being given to new Kuwait work-visa holders (in the saved forum page, not logged).
- 8 Apr: US-Iran ceasefire (Wikipedia lead, not saved); open-ended extension 21 Apr (in S0001's source).
- 22 Apr: MOFA protocol circular (Al-Jarida): no extension of unused entry visas (private work, domestic, family); employers must reapply "after the announcement of the resumption of issuing entry visas" (S0012, S0013). So issuing was suspended on 22 Apr. Same circular: residents >6 months abroad get automatic 3-month absence permits from 28 Feb (S0014). ITV Bangla report of the same (B0203, B0204).
- 23 Apr: airspace reopened; 24 Apr: Kuwait Airways Dhaka via Dammam twice weekly, overland through Nuwaiseeb (S0005, S0006); 26 Apr: Kuwait Airways (T4) and Jazeera (T5) resume from KWI; Dhaka 4x weekly (S0002, S0003, S0018). 28 Apr: US Embassy still suspended all consular services (S0019).
- April and May: BMET clearances 1,376 each (B0024, B0019; B re-checked the identical figures).
- 1 May-30 Jun: PAM Decision 2/2026 transfer window for workers in SMEs, industry, agriculture, livestock, fishing, with original employer's approval, via Ashal (S0015-S0017). For L (Changing employer or leaving), not used in my draft.
- 3 Jun: Iranian drone hits KWI Terminal 1; traffic suspended (S0021). Jun 2026: MOI circular, 10 countries for domestic workers (D0001-D0002).
- 10 Jun: commercial visit visas suspended for all nationalities; 19 Jun resumed (S0023, S0024). This implies visit-visa issuing had resumed before 10 June.
- 17 Jun: foreign airlines start returning via T4, one flight a day each (S0022). June clearances 2,036 (S0033).
- 18 Jul: airspace closed again after renewed Iranian attacks (S0025). July clearances 3,749 (B0021); Aug 3,669 (in source, not logged); Sep 4,538 (B0016).
- 20 Aug: MOI announces end of automatic extensions of entry visas and leave permits from 1 Sep; those who left on or before 31 Aug keep the extra extension; Sahel certificate is the reference (S0026-S0028).
- 3 Sep: Bangladesh Embassy warns of new missile and drone attacks, issues hotlines (B0202).
- Oct: 3,914 clearances (B0020); Oct 2026 Protidiner Bangladesh (N0026): domestic visas closed to Bangladeshis. Safe Airspace: Kuwait FIR closed and reopened several times Feb-Jul; threat eased, risk remains (S0029, S0030).
- 1-8 Nov: 1,305 clearances (S0034).

**Clearances are not departures.** BMET kept clearing 1,376-1,539 workers a month in March-May while Kuwait's airspace was shut and visa issuing was suspended, so many of those people were cleared on visas issued before the war and either waited or lost the visa (S0012).
