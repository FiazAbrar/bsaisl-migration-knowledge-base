How can I request for a longer validity of my visa?
Updated by 
ICA

1y ago
5
 found this helpful
Each visa application, including the duration of the visa granted, is considered on its own merits and subject to approval. You may wish to indicate the reason for requiring a visa with longer validity in the "Additional Information" field, for ICA's consideration. Please note that you may be required to submit supporting documents for our consideration.
You will be notified on the validity of the visa granted if your visa application is approved.
