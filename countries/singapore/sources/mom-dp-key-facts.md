# Key facts on Dependant's Pass

Get a overview and key facts about the Dependant's Pass for spouses and children of eligible Employment Pass or S Pass holders.

## Pass facts 

|  |  |
|----|----|
| Who it is for | Legally married spouse or unmarried children under 21 years of [eligible Employment Pass or S Pass holders](/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/eligibility). |
| Who can apply | An employer or appointed employment agent must apply on behalf of the candidate. |
| What is the duration | The duration is tied to the validity of the main work pass or up to the duration requested, whichever is shorter.   |
| Is it renewable | Yes. Find out [how to renew](/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/renew-a-pass). |
| Relevant legislation | [Immigration Act](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/SL/IA1959-RG1) |
| Related eServices and forms | Access [online services and forms](/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/eservices-and-forms) for Dependant’s Pass. |

## Features of the pass

The key features of the pass are as follows:

- [Eligible pass holders](/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/eligibility#working-in-singapore) may be able to work in Singapore.
- No foreign worker levy or quota required.

## Pass map

Overview of what you need to do before, during and after you apply for the Dependant’s Pass.

## Before applying

- ### 

  - ![know requirements](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/know-requirements.png)
    Know the [eligibility and requirements](/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/eligibility) to apply.
  - ![know requirements](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/know-requirements.png)
    Find out the [documents required](/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/documents-required) for applying.

## Before arrival

- ### 

  - ![Apply](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/apply.png)
    [Apply for the Dependant's Pass](/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/apply-for-a-pass) and wait for the outcome.
  - ![Apply](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/apply.png)
    (If the pass is approved) Get an in-principle approval (IPA) letter.
  - ![declaration](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/declaration.png)
    Ensure that your dependant complies with the latest [travel requirements](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore).

## Upon arrival

- ### 

  - ![issuance](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/inssuance.png)
    [Get the pass issued](/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/apply-for-a-pass#get-the-pass-issued) and receive a notification letter stating whether registration is required.
  - ![register fingerprints](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/register-fingerprints.png)
    (If required) Make an appointment for the dependant to [register their fingerprints and photo](/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/apply-for-a-pass#if-required-register-fingerprints-and-photo).
  - ![receive card](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/receive-card.png)
    [Receive the pass card](/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/apply-for-a-pass#receive-the-card) at the address provided 5 working days after registration or document verification.

## When required

- ### 

  - ![notify mom](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/notify-mom.png)
    [Notify MOM](/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/notify-mom-of-changes) if there are any changes to your dependant’s personal information.
  - ![replace card](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/replace-card.png)
    [Apply for a replacement](/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/replace-a-pass-card) if the pass card is lost, stolen or damaged.
  - ![cancel pass or permit](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/cancel-pass-or-permit.png)
    [Cancel the pass](/passes-and-permits/dependants-pass/cancel-a-pass) if they no longer require it.
