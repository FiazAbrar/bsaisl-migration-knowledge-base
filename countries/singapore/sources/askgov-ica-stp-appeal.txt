How can I appeal if my application for a Student's Pass is rejected?
Updated by 
ICA

1y ago
44
 found this helpful
If your application is unsuccessful, you should inform your school before submitting an appeal to ICA. You may then 
submit an appeal via the enquiry form
, along with your application reference number. Each appeal will be considered on its own merits. The general processing time for appeal cases is about 4 weeks and the outcome of the appeal will be made known to both you and the school.
