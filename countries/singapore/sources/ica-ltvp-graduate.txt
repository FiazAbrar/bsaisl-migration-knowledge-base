            Overview

The Graduate from an Institute of Higher Learning (IHL) Seeking Employment scheme supports foreign graduates who wish to remain in Singapore to explore job opportunities.
 If you are a recent Institute of Higher Learning (IHL) graduate, you must obtain a Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP) under this scheme to legally remain in Singapore for a period of time after your Student's Pass is cancelled.



            Eligibility

To apply, you must have graduated from an approved Institute of Higher Learning (IHL) in Singapore and are seeking employment in Singapore. 
You do not require a local sponsor for this application.



            Documents Required

You will need the following documents ready before you apply. 
From the applicant    
A coloured digital   photograph taken recently in dimensions 400 x 514 pixels. Please refer to   the  
Photo Guidelines
 for more details.
Travel document
Educational certificate(s) and transcripts



Note

You will need to provide original documents and official translations (for documents in languages other than English).
ICA accepts:
Translations provided by the embassy of the country/place that issued the document
Translations produced by a notary public in Singapore or the country/place that issued the document
Privately created translations attested by the embassy of the country/place that issued the document, or notarised by a notary public in Singapore or the country/place that issued the document 
ICA does not endorse any private translation companies or entities. Insufficient documentation may result in non-acceptance of your application. You may be required to provide additional documents to support your application.

            Fees

At Submission 
At Completion of Formalities

S$45 for each application

S$60 for issuance of LTVP

S$30 for a Multiple Journey Visa
(if applicable)

All fees are non-refundable.

You can pay online using any of the following:
Visa or MasterCard credit/debit card
American Express (AMEX) credit card
Internet Direct Debit (DBS/POSB, OCBC, UOB and Standard Chartered Bank Internet Banking accounts in Singapore)
PayNow

Successful applicants will be notified of other applicable fees subsequently.



            Processing Time

Applications will be processed within
 6 weeks
, provided all the required documents are submitted and in order. Some applications may take longer to process.

You will be informed of the outcome of the LTVP application via email. You may also log in to check the LTVP application status online.

 for queries on LTVP matters.

Upon course completion, students will have to cancel their Student's Pass (STP) and surrender any physical STP card that was issued to them for cancellation to ICA. Upon cancellation of their STP, they may be granted a short-term visit pass to stay in Singapore (up to 89 days).
If you are studying in an Institute of Higher Learning (IHL) and wish to have a longer period of stay to look for jobs upon completion of your course, you may apply for a 1-year (non-renewable) Long-Term Visit Pass.
