# Visa Guidelines

Check if you need an entry visa based on the travel document you hold and read the entry requirements before travelling to Singapore. 

Last updated 28 Oct 2026



## Visa and Entry Information

If you hold a travel document issued by one of the countries or places listed below, you will need a valid\
Singapore entry visa to travel to, and seek entry, into Singapore.

**Things to Keep in Mind**

- **A visa does not guarantee entry.** A Singapore entry visa is a pre-entry permission. It is not an immigration pass. ICA officers at the checkpoint will determine if you can enter Singapore. As a valid visa holder, you must adhere to all travel advisories and check the latest entry requirements\] before you travel.
- **The SG Arrival Card is not a visa.** You must still submit your SG Arrival Card within 3 days before arriving in Singapore. Read more about the requirements for the [SG Arrival Card with electronic health declaration before your trip](/information-and-e-services/entry-and-departure/entering-singapore/sg-arrival-card-with-electronic-health-declaration).
- **Visa validity and period of stay.** The period of stay granted is not tied to the validity of your visa. Your period of stay is determined by the [electronic visit pass (e-Pass)](/information-and-e-services/entry-and-departure/entering-singapore/short-term-visit-pass/electronic-visit-pass-(e-pass)-retrieval-for-short-term-visitors) issued to you at the checkpoint.
- **Visa applications.** ICA does not accept submission of visa applications over the immigration counters.\
  \
  If you do not have a Local Contact with a Singpass/Corppass account, you can apply either:
  - Through an authorised visa agent appointed by the Singapore Overseas Missions
  - At the nearest Singapore Overseas Mission

Contact your nearest [Singapore Overseas Mission](/redirect-links/mfa--overseas-mission) for details on how to apply through them or their authorised visa agents.

- **e-Pass**. From 11 March 2022, all foreign visitors arriving in Singapore will be notified [digitally of their Visit Pass (e-Pass)](/about-ica/news-and-publications/public-advisories/roll-out-of-e-pass-to-all-checkpoints-and-launch-of-ica's-e-pass-enquiry-portal) through email. There will not be any endorsement on your passport. The e-Pass notification will indicate your allowed period of stay and your last day in Singapore. You must provide a valid email address when submitting your [SG Arrival Card](/information-and-e-services/entry-and-departure/entering-singapore/sg-arrival-card-with-electronic-health-declaration).
- **Short-Term Visit Pass Extension**. To stay longer, you must [apply to extend your Short-Term Visit Pass (STVP). Find out how to apply for an STVP extension](/information-and-e-services/entry-and-departure/entering-singapore/short-term-visit-pass/short-term-visit-pass-extension).\

**Other Travel Documents that Require a Visa**

You will also need a visa if you are travelling on:

- A Refugee travel document. This travel document is subject to assessment of recognition for entry into Singapore
- An Alien's passport. This travel document is subject to assessment of recognition for entry into Singapore
- A [Palestinian Authority passport](/information-and-e-services/entry-and-departure/entering-singapore/visa-guidelines/palestinian-authority-passport)
- A [temporary passport issued by the United Arab Emirates](/information-and-e-services/entry-and-departure/entering-singapore/visa-guidelines/temporary-passport-issued-by-united-arab-emirates)
- A [Document of Identity issued by Hong Kong Special Administrative Region](/information-and-e-services/entry-and-departure/entering-singapore/visa-guidelines/document-of-identity-issued-by-hong-kong-special-administrative-region)
- A [Travel Permit issued by Macao Special Administrative Region](/information-and-e-services/entry-and-departure/entering-singapore/visa-guidelines/travel-permit-issued-by-macao-special-administrative-region)
- A [PRC Travel Document](/information-and-e-services/entry-and-departure/entering-singapore/visa-guidelines/prc-travel-document)\

You may [contact us via the enquiry form](/redirect-links/feedback/formsg--visa) with a copy of your travel document biodata page and front cover for further assistance and verification.

## Visa Free Transit Facility

You may be eligible for the Visa Free Transit Facility (VFTF) if you are a national of:

- The Commonwealth of Independent States
- Georgia
- India
- Turkmenistan
- Ukraine

If you are a National of the People’s Republic of China (PRC), you may also be eligible if you hold a PRC Travel Document, Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (SAR) Document of Identity, or Macau SAR Travel Permit. PRC nationals holding ordinary, diplomatic, public affairs, or service passports do not qualify. Check the full eligibility criteria for the [Visa Free Transit Facility](/information-and-e-services/entry-and-departure/entering-singapore/entry-requirements/visa-free-transit-facility).
