# Renew an Employment Pass

You can apply to renew an Employment Pass (EP) if you want the pass holder to continue working for you. If the EP is expiring from 1 September 2024, they will need to pass the [COMPASS requirements](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/renew-a-pass#renewal-criteria).

## Renew early

- Remember to apply for renewal early. Your new work pass validity will not be reduced or lost when you renew early. Any remaining validity from your current pass (up to six months) is added on to your renewal.
- If you miss the deadline, you need to apply for a new pass.
- You’ll have to apply for renewal using the [renewal form](/-/media/mom/documents/services-forms/passes/ep_sponsor_renewal_form.pdf) for EP (Sponsorship) holders.

## Renewal criteria

The candidate needs to:

- Earn at least the [EP qualifying salary](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility#ep-qualifying-salary). 
- Work for the same employer in a managerial, executive, or specialised job.
- Pass the points-based [Complementarity Assessment Framework (COMPASS)](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility#compass).

- Candidates earning at least \$22,500 [fixed monthly salary](/faq/employment-pass/what-is-a-fixed-monthly-salary) are [exempted from COMPASS](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility#exemptions-from-compass).
- Renewals are not guaranteed. 
- Once a candidate’s [qualification has been verified](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/documents-required#changes-to-verification-requirement), there is no need to get it verified again at renewal, or when there is a change of employer. If a candidate’s qualifications need to be verified, you will be prompted when filling in the EP application form.

## How to renew

Log in to [*myMOM* Portal](/eservices/services/mymom-portal) to apply for renewal.

Ensure that your organisation’s [turnover information](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/notify-mom-of-changes#change-in-companys-name-or-financial-information) for the past 3 years has been updated before submitting the application.

### For EP (Sponsorship)

To renew EP (Sponsorship):

1.  Download the [EP (Sponsorship) renewal application form](/-/media/mom/documents/services-forms/passes/ep_sponsor_renewal_form.pdf) and complete it as a soft copy.
2.  Submit the completed EP (Sponsorship) renewal application form and the related supporting documents using the [online form](https:\\go.gov.sg\renew-ep-sponsorship).

The outcome will be emailed to the contact person indicated in the application.

## When you can renew

You can apply to renew 6 months (180 days) before the pass expires.

Enter the pass expiry date to check when you can start renewing.

By using this service, you agree to accept the [terms of use](/calculator-terms-of-use).

## Upon renewal

If your renewal is successful, you:

1.  Will get an in-principle approval (IPA) letter. It is valid for **3 months**.
2.  Must follow the instructions in the IPA to [get the pass issued](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/apply-for-a-pass#get-the-pass-issued).

You must get the pass issued before the IPA or current pass expires, whichever occurs first. Otherwise, an overstaying fine will be imposed. The duration of the renewed pass will start when the current pass expires.
