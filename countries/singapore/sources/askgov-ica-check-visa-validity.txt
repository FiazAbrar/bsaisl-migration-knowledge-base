How do I check my visa validity?
Updated by 
ICA

2y ago
35
 found this helpful
The visa validity is indicated on the e-Visa. You can retrieve your e-Visa using the 
Enquire Application Status
 function and key in your Visa Reference Number and Travel Document Number.
If you do not have a visa application reference number, you may 
contact us via the enquiry form
 and indicate the applicant's name and passport number for the retrieval of visa application reference number.
