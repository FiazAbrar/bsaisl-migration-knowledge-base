## Pass map

Get an idea of what you need to do before, during and after you apply for a Work Permit.

## Before applying

- ### 

  - ![know requirements](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/know-requirements.png)
    Understand the [general requirements](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/work-permit-conditions) and [sector-specific rules](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules) to apply.
  - ![quota levy security bond](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/quota-levy-security-bond.png)
    Understand the foreign worker [quota and levy requirements](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/foreign-worker-levy).
  - ![declaration](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/declaration.png)
    If you have never applied for a Work Permit, you need to [declare your business activity](/eservices/services/declare-your-business-activity).

    *For first-time employers*

## Before worker's arrival

- ### Apply for Work Permit

  - ![Apply](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/apply.png)
    [Apply for the Work Permit](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/apply-for-work-permit). Once approved, you will get an in-principle approval (IPA) to bring the worker into Singapore.

- ### Prepare for worker's arrival

  - ![medical](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/medical.png)
    Ensure that the worker complies with the latest [travel requirements](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore).
  - ![registration](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/registration.png)
    Book a slot at the [Onboard centre](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/onboard-centre) before securing your worker's flight.

    *For all non-Malaysian male CMP Work Permit holders on IPA*
  - ![quota levy security bond](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/quota-levy-security-bond.png)
    Buy a [security bond](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/security-bond).

    *For non-Malaysian Work Permit holders*
  - ![medical](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/medical.png)
    Buy [medical insurance](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/medical-insurance) for each Work Permit holder and submit the insurance details online.
  - ![medical](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/medical.png)
    Buy the [Primary Care Plan](/primary-care-plan).

    *For workers who stay in dormitories or work in CMP sectors*
  - ![registration](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/registration.png)
    Register the worker for the [Settling-in Programme (SIP)](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/settling-in-programme#register-for-sip) once you know their arrival date.

    *For employers of all non-Malaysian Work Permit holders in the manufacturing sector and female CMP workers*

## Upon worker's arrival

- ### Before permit can be issued

  - ![registration](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/registration.png)
    Send the worker to the [Onboard centre](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/onboard-centre) directly after they arrive in Singapore.

    *For employers of all non-Malaysian male CMP Work Permit holders on IPA*
  - ![registration](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/registration.png)
    Send the worker to attend [SIP](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/settling-in-programme) within first 14 days of arrival in Singapore.

    *For employers of all non-Malaysian Work Permit holders in the manufacturing sector and female CMP workers*
  - ![registration](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/registration.png)
    Register [worker's residential and workplace addresses](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/housing/employers-responsibilities).
  - ![medical](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/medical.png)
    Send the worker for a [medical examination](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/medical-examination) by a Singapore-registered doctor **within 2 weeks** of arrival.

- ### Get the Work Permit issued

  - ![issuance](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/inssuance.png)
    When the worker arrives, you can [get the permit issued](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/apply-for-work-permit#get-the-permit-issued) and you will get a notification letter stating whether registration is required.
  - ![register fingerprints](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/register-fingerprints.png)
    If required, make an appointment for the worker to [register their fingerprints and photo](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/apply-for-work-permit#register-fingerprints-and-photo).
  - ![receive card](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/receive-card.png)
    [Receive the Work Permit card](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/apply-for-work-permit#receive-the-card) at the address provided 4 working days after registration or document verification.

## Monthly

- ### 

  - ![quota levy security bond](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/quota-levy-security-bond.png)
    [Pay the foreign worker levy](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/foreign-worker-levy/paying-the-levy) by GIRO.

## As and when required

- ### 

  - ![notify mom](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/notify-mom.png)
    [Notify MOM](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/notify-mom-of-changes) if the company's or worker's information changes.
  - ![quota levy security bond](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/quota-levy-security-bond.png)
    You can [apply for levy waiver](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/foreign-worker-levy/apply-for-levy-waiver) in certain situations.
  - ![renewal](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/renewal.png)
    [Renew the Work Permit](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/renew-a-work-permit) before it expires.
  - ![replace card](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/replace-card.png)
    [Apply for a replacement](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/replace-a-card) if the permit card is lost, stolen or damaged.
  - ![cancel pass or permit](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/cancel-pass-or-permit.png)
    [Cancel the permit](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/cancel-a-work-permit) if the worker no longer works for you.
  - ![medical](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/medical.png)
    Cancel the worker’s PCP by contacting your [PCP Anchor Operator](/primary-care-plan/buying-a-pcp#find-your-workers-zone-and-anchor-operator).

Note: Pass holders may be required to pay [income tax](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/new-to-tax/working-out-your-tax-residency).
