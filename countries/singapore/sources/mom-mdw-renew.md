# Renew a Work Permit for a migrant domestic worker (MDW)

You must renew your MDW's Work Permit before it expires. Before renewing, you also need to complete certain steps, such as buying an insurance package (security bond, medical and personal accident insurance).

- From 1 September 2025,  if there are children in your household below 7 years old who are not fully vaccinated against measles, you must ensure that your helper has [immunity against measles](https://www.mom.gov.sg/newsroom/press-releases/2025/0610-mandatory-measles-immunity-requirement-MDWs).
- If you no longer need your helper, you can consider [transferring her to another employer](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/transfer-to-a-new-employer).
- **Additional step for employment agencies**: Please get the employer’s authorisation using our [FDW eService](/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-domestic-helpers-and-confinement-nannies) **before you can perform any transactions for them**. For more details, you can refer to [our FAQs](/-/media/mom/documents/work-passes-and-permits/faq-2fa.pdf) and [infographic](/-/media/mom/documents/work-passes-and-permits/ea-infographic.pdf).

## At a glance

|  |  |
|----|----|
| When to renew | After you receive the renewal letter, you can [renew your helper’s Work Permit online](/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-domestic-helpers-and-confinement-nannies). You should receive the letter from MOM **about 8 weeks** before the Work Permit expires. |
| Who can renew | Employer, sponsor or authorised employment agency (EA) |
| How much it costs | \$35 per renewal |
| What is the duration of the renewed permit | Up to 2 years. It may be shorter [in these situations](#duration-of-renewal). |
| How long it takes | The outcome is immediate |

You can [make an appeal](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/appeal-for-renewal) if your helper is not eligible for renewal. However, any appeal is unlikely to be successful unless you provide new information for our consideration. Alternatively, you can consider [part-time help with household chores](/faq/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/what-is-household-services-scheme).

## When to renew

You should receive the renewal letter from MOM **about 8 weeks** before your helper’s Work Permit expires. Otherwise, an overstaying fine will be imposed.

After you receive the renewal letter, you can [renew her Work Permit online](/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-domestic-helpers-and-confinement-nannies).

If your helper plans to travel overseas just before the expiry date, you should renew the Work Permit before she leaves Singapore.

If you didn’t receive the renewal letter, you can [print a copy](https://service2.mom.gov.sg/workpass/keepmyhelper).

## Late renewals

If you renew a Work Permit late, you are still liable for levy after it expires. Levy penalties or overstaying fine, if applicable, may also be imposed.

You can [request for an extension](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/get-a-short-extension-for-work-permit) if you’re unable to renew the Work Permit on time.

## How to renew

*Processing time: Immediate*

To renew the permit:

1.  Log in to [submit the application and upload the required documents](/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-domestic-helpers-and-confinement-nannies). Refer to the **renewal letter** for specific instructions.
2.  Pay \$35 for each renewal. You can pay by Visa, Mastercard or PayNow.

## Duration of renewal

The Work Permit is **usually valid for 2 years**. However, it may be shorter in these situations:

| In this situation | The duration will be until |
|----|----|
| The validity of the insurance package is less than 26 months | 2 months before the insurance package expires |
| The helper is approaching 60 years old | The day the helper turns 60 years old |

The helper’s passport is no longer tied to her Work Permit duration. Thus, you can renew her Work Permit even if her passport has expired or has a validity of less than 2 years.

You should make sure your helper’s passport is valid if she is travelling out of Singapore. Embassies may impose requirements on the renewal of passports.
