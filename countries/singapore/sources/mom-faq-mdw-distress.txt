        I am a migrant domestic worker (MDW) in distress. Who can I call?

You can:

Call the Police at 
999
 if you are in danger.

    OR

Call the MDW Helpline at 
1800 339 5505
 (Mon – Fri, 8.30am to 5.30pm) to speak to an MOM officer. 

    The helpline is strictly for MDWs in distress. For other matters, 
contact MOM
.

Note that airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines.
