# Frequent changes of MDWs

If you have changed migrant domestic workers (MDWs) multiple times within a year, you have to meet certain requirements before you can employ another worker.

You are a Frequent Change Employer if you have changed more than 3 MDWs within a 12-month period.

If you are employing your 4^(th) MDW within a 12-month period, you will be required to attend the **CONNECT Workshop** conducted by the **Association of Employment Agencies Singapore (AEA(S))**. Details will be provided in the email sent to you by MOM.

## What to expect during the CONNECT Workshop

The CONNECT workshop is conducted by an experienced consultant from AEA(S).

With a network of reputable employment agencies, AEA(S) can help employers better understand their hiring needs and facilitate a better match.

You will be given a questionnaire to complete before the session. This would assist the consultant in understanding your previous hiring practices and management of MDWs and make recommendations to assist you.

## Attending CONNECT Workshop

[TABLE]

Attendance for the consultation is **mandatory**. You will need to provide notice in writing of at least 3 working days to AEA(S) before the consultation if you are not able to attend. Otherwise, the fee of \$80 will be forfeited.

An appointment will only be confirmed upon successful payment.
