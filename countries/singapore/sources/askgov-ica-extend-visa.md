# How can I extend my Visa?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

![Immigration & Checkpoints Authority logo](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Flogos.ask.gov.sg%2Ficalogo.png&w=3840&q=75)

Updated by ICA 3y ago

------------------------------------------------------------------------

23 found this helpful

There is strictly no extension of visa validity or change of visa type. You can submit a fresh application via our [e-Service](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/esvclandingpage/save) upon the expiry or upon utilisation of your current visa. However, please note that each visa application is assessed on its own merits.
