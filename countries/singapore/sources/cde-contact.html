Contact us

Email address

contact@cde.org.sg

For assistance on domestic employment-related issues, please call our 24-hour helpline at 1800 2255 233 (1800-CALL-CDE) or visit our Facebook page at www.facebook.com/cde.singapore to drop us a message.

For migrant domestic worker interview-related enquiries, please call us at 6303 6840 or 6303 6841 or drop us an email at interview@cde.org.sg.

For all media enquiries, please drop us an email at stratcomms@ntuc.org.sg.

For general matters, such as how to collaborate with us to improve the domestic employees' landscape, please write in to contact@cde.org.sg
