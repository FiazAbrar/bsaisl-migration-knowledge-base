### Creating pathways to safe and dignified migration for Bangladeshi workers

BRAC Probashbandhu Ltd is a government-approved recruiting agency that facilitates safe, ethical, and low-cost migration.

We connect professional, skilled and semi-skilled workers with employers in a range of sectors across multiple countries. We handle the full migration cycle, from sourcing and recruitment to visa checks, orientation, training, and deployment.

Our goal is to disrupt unethical recruitment practices, helping migrants pursue opportunities with dignity and security.
