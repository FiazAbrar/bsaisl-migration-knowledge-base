# Key facts on Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass

Get a quick overview of the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass to decide if it is the right pass for you.

Candidates may apply for the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass under the AI and Tech track from 28 January 2027

## Features of the pass

The key features of the pass include the following:

- Exemption from Complementarity Assessment Framework (COMPASS) and Fair Consideration Framework job advertising requirements
- Flexibility to concurrently start, operate and work for multiple companies at any one time (subject to your employment contract terms, where relevant)
- No need to reapply for a pass if you change jobs
- Availability of [passes for eligible family members](/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families)
- Your spouse will be able to apply to work on a [Letter of Consent](/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families/dependants-pass-for-overseas-networks-expertise-pass/working-in-singapore).

You may be required to pay [income tax](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/tax-residency-and-tax-rates/working-out-my-tax-residency).

## Pass map

Overview of what you need to do before, during and after you apply for an Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass.

## Before applying

- ### 

  - ![know requirements](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/know-requirements.png)
    Know the [eligibility and requirements](/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/eligibility) to apply.
  - ![know requirements](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/know-requirements.png)
    Find out the [documents required](/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/documents-required) for applying.

## Before arrival

- ### 

  - ![Apply](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/apply.png)
    [Apply for the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass](/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/apply-for-a-pass) and wait for the outcome.
  - ![Apply](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/apply.png)
    (If your pass is approved) Get an in-principle approval letter.
  - ![medical](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/medical.png)
    Ensure that you comply with the latest [travel requirements](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore).

## Upon arrival

- ### 

  - ![issuance](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/inssuance.png)
    [Get your pass issued](/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/apply-for-a-pass#get-the-pass-issued) and receive a notification letter stating whether registration is required.
  - ![register fingerprints](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/register-fingerprints.png)
    (If required) Make an appointment for the candidate to [register fingerprints and photo](/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/apply-for-a-pass#register-fingerprints-and-photo).
  - ![receive card](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/receive-card.png)
    [Receive your pass card](/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/apply-for-a-pass#receive-the-card) at the address provided 5 working days after registration or document verification.

## As and when required

- ### 

  - ![notify mom](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/notify-mom.png)
    [Notify MOM](/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/notify-mom) about your professional activities and annual income by 31 January of the next year.

    *You should also notify MOM about any changes to your residential address or personal information.*
  - ![replace card](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/replace-card.png)
    [Apply for a replacement](/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/replace-a-pass-card) if your pass card is lost, stolen or damaged.
  - ![cancel pass or permit](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/cancel-pass-or-permit.png)
    [Cancel your pass](/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/cancel-a-pass) if you no longer require it.
