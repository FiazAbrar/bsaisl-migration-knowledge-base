# Can I get a refund for my employment agency fee if I resign from my employment?

If you resign from your employment, you will not be entitled to any refunds of your agency fees.\
\
If you are terminated from your employment, and was instructed by your [employment agency](https://form.gov.sg/64e5cb37f8c88e0012480ed1) or [employer](https://www.mom.gov.sg/eservices/services/report-an-employment-act-violation) to submit a resignation letter, you should contact MOM for assistance.\
