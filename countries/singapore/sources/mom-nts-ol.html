Non-Traditional Sources (NTS) Occupation List

Firms in Manufacturing and Services sectors can hire Work Permit holders from NTS for a restricted set of occupations in the NTS Occupation List.

What is the NTS Occupation List

Firms in the Manufacturing and Services sectors can hire Work Permit holders from the following countries or regions:

  • Malaysia
  • People’s Republic of China (PRC)
  • North Asian sources (NAS):
    • Hong Kong (HKSAR passport)
    • Macau
    • South Korea
    • Taiwan

Under the NTS Occupation List, firms will be allowed to hire Work Permit holders from the following countries or regions for a restricted set of occupations:

  • Bangladesh
  • Bhutan
  • Cambodia
  • India
  • Laos
  • Myanmar
  • Philippines
  • Sri Lanka
  • Thailand

Requirements

Firms must meet all of the following requirements:

  • Keep within a sub-Dependency Ratio Ceiling (DRC) of 8% for NTS Work Permit holders in the NTS Occupation List.
  • Pay their Work Permit holder performing occupations in this list a fixed monthly salary of at least $2,000.
  • Work Permit holders must only perform the occupation stated on their Work Permit. 

NTS Occupation List

The NTS Occupation List contains the following occupations:

Occupation You need to ensure that 
Cabin attendants

The firm holds a valid Civil Aviation Authority of Singapore (CAAS)-issued:

  • Singapore Air Operator Certificate (AOC).

Or

  • Foreign Operator’s Permit (FOP) and operating scheduled services to/from Singapore.

Childcare workers comprising:

  • Babysitters/Infant caregivers (also known as infant educator and childminder)
  • Child/After school care centre worker (also known as educarer or early years educator)
  • Teacher aides (also known as assistant infant educator and assistant early years educator)

The firm:

  • Has a valid Early Childhood Development Centre (ECDC) licence.

Or

  • Is appointed by the Early Childhood Development Agency (ECDA) under the ChildMinding Pilot.

Food processing workers, but excluding:

  • Food processing supervisors and general foremen.
  • Other food and beverage jobs like tasters, graders, bakers, pastry-cooks and confectionery makers.
  • The firm has a valid SFA Food Processing Establishment or Slaughterhouse licence.
  • The licence must be under the firm's name as registered with ACRA.

Food services workers comprising:

  • Butchers, fishmongers and related food preparers
  • Cooks
  • Food/drink stall assistants
  • Kitchen assistants
  • Waiters
  • The firm has a work pass account under the Services sector and has a valid Singapore Food Agency (SFA) Food Shop or Supermarket licence.
  • The licence must be under the firm’s name as registered with the Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA).

Heavy vehicle drivers, such as:

  • Bus drivers
  • Lorry drivers
  • Trailer truck drivers
  • Workers have a class 4 or 5 licence as of 1 September 2025.
  • Workers should not be driving cars or light good vehicles which are considered as Class 3.
Housekeeping workers and porters  The firm is a licensed hotel. 

Manufacturing workers, such as:

  • Assemblers
  • Machine operators
  • Metal moulders and coremakers
  • Quality checkers & testers
  • Riggers and cable splicers
  • Sheet metal workers
  • Structural metal preparers and erectors
  • Welders and flame cutters
 The firm has a work pass account under the Manufacturing sector. 

Levy requirements

You must pay a monthly levy for Work Permit holders. For your worker to qualify for higher-skilled worker levy, they must meet the requirements for their respective sector:

  • Manufacturing
  • Services
