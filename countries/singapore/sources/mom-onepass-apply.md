# Apply for an Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass

You can submit an Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass application online.

## At a glance

[TABLE]

You may only **submit 1 work pass application per candidate** at a time. If the candidate has an existing work pass application, they cannot apply for an Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass.

Although you can apply for their passes at the same time as the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass, you may lose the application fees for each family pass if your Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass application gets rejected.

## Submit an application

Show

*Processing time: 4 weeks for most cases*

### How to apply

1.  Prepare the [documents required](/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/documents-required).
2.  Complete the [online application form](https://service2.mom.gov.sg/workpass/op/landing).
3.  Pay the \$105 application fee via Visa or Mastercard.

To avoid processing delays, please ensure all [required documents](/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/documents-required) are properly completed before submission.

### How to check your application status

You may [check your application status online](/eservices/services/check-work-pass-and-application-status) after 4 weeks.

If additional information or documents are required:

- Your processing time may take longer than 4 weeks
- We will email you (please reply directly to our email)

### Receiving your application results

We will send your results to the **email** indicated in your application form.\
\
If your pass is approved, you should also receive an IPA letter. Your IPA letter:

- **Is a pre-approved single-entry visa for you to enter Singapore.** It gives you 6 months to enter Singapore and get your pass issued.
- **States whether you need to go for a medical examination** after your arrival in Singapore.

Applying on behalf of a candidate

A company can apply for the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass on behalf of a candidate that it wishes to employ.

**During application**, you should:

1.  Inform the candidate of the:
    - Contents of the application.
    - Documents submitted as part of the application.
    - Declaration made as part of the application.
2.  Send a copy of the submitted application form to the candidate for their reference.

**After the application has been approved**, the pass holder will need to:

1.  Sign the relevant declaration form.
2.  Submit it during his or her pass issuance appointment at the Employment Pass Services Centre.

If you are an authorised third-party service provider who is not an employment agent, you can still submit on behalf of the applicant. In the form, please select ‘I am applying on behalf of the candidate as an employment agency’ and enter ‘NA’ when prompted to key in the EA Licence Number.

## Prepare for arrival in Singapore

Show

### Before departing

You should:

- Print your IPA letter.
- Ensure that you comply with the [latest travel requirements](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore).

### After arriving

You should:

- Show your IPA letter to the immigration officer.
- Complete any required medical examinations.

## Get the pass issued

Show

*When: Within 6 months of IPA*

*Processing time: Immediate*

You must be in Singapore to get your pass issued.  

[TABLE]

### EP eService

For candidates with Singpass accounts to get the pass issued:

1.  [Log in to our eService](/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice) and provide the [required information and documents](#what-you-need).
2.  Pay the fees using GIRO, Visa, Mastercard or Amex: 
    - \$225 for each pass
    - (If applicable) \$30 for each [Multiple Journey Visa](https://www.ica.gov.sg/#/visitor/visitor_entryvisa)
3.  Once the pass is issued, you will receive a **notification letter** by email.

### EPSC

For candidates without Singpass accounts, you can get your pass issued over the counter at our EPSC. Please refer to the following steps:

1.  [Make an appointment](/eservices/services/make-an-appointment) to have your pass issued at our [Employment Pass Services Centre](/contact-us/locations#Tab-EP).
2.  Bring the [supporting documents](#what-you-need) for your appointment.
3.  Pay the fees using VISA, MasterCard, AMEX, NETS or CashCard (if your credit or debit card was not issued in Singapore, please ensure it has been activated for overseas use):
    - \$225 for each pass
    - (If applicable) \$30 for each [Multiple Journey Visa](https://www.ica.gov.sg/#/visitor/visitor_entryvisa)
4.  You will be issued a **notification letter**. 

&nbsp;

The notification letter:

- Allows you to start work and travel in and out of Singapore while waiting for the pass card.
- States if you need to have your fingerprints and photo taken for the card registration.
- Is valid for **1 month** from the date of issue. If you need more time to complete the card registration procedures, you can [request to extend the validity of the notification letter](https://www.go.gov.sg/change-date-epsc).

## (If required) Register fingerprints and photo

Show

*When: Within 2 weeks after the pass is issued (if required)*

### Make an appointment

If your notification letter states that you need to register your fingerprints and photo, you should [make an appointment](/eservices/services/make-an-appointment) at our [Employment Pass Services Centre (EPSC)](/contact-us/locations#Tab-EP).

You must register your fingerprints and photo **within 2 weeks** after your pass has been issued.

### What to bring for your appointment

Please bring the following documents for your appointment:

- Original passport 
- Appointment letter
- Notification letter
- Your mobile phone (with a local mobile number) with both Singpass and [SGWorkPass](/eservices/sgworkpass) apps downloaded.

During registration, we will capture your fingerprints, take your photo and register you for a Singpass account which allows you to access your digital work pass in the [SGWorkPass](/eservices/sgworkpass) app.

## Set up digital work pass

Show

*When: On the day of card registration*

After you are registered for a Singpass account, you can:

1.  Download [SGWorkPass](/eservices/sgworkpass) (if you have not done so yet)
2.  Log into the app with your Singpass
3.  View your digital work pass and employment information

SGWorkPass app allows you easy and secure access to your digital work pass. You can check your status, expiry date and procure services earlier, without having to wait for delivery of your physical work pass card.

If the candidate is not required to report to EPSC for card registration, please inform them to set up their digital work pass.

## Receive the card

Show

*When: Within 5 working days after registration or document verification* 

### Card delivery

We will deliver the card to the given address **within 5 working days** after you register at the [Employment Pass Services Centre (EPSC)](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility#Tab-EP) and get your documents verified. If you do not need to register, we will deliver the card within 5 working days after verifying your documents. 

The authorised recipients will get an SMS or email with the delivery details at least 1 working day before the delivery. 

Set up your [SGWorkPass](/eservices/sgworkpass) app to view your digital work pass and employment information.

When collecting the card, you will need to show these documents for identification:

| Person receiving the card | Required documents            |
|:--------------------------|:------------------------------|
| Candidate                 | Passport or digital work pass |
|  Authorised person        | NRIC, FIN or passport         |

If you do not receive the card within 5 working days, [check if you have uploaded the correct documents](/faq/work-pass-general/ive-registered-for-my-card-but-mom-has-not-yet-contacted-me-about-the-card-delivery-date-what-should-i-do). 

### Unsuccessful card delivery

If we are unable to deliver your card after 2 attempted deliveries, you or an authorised person needs to collect the card at [EPSC](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility#Tab-EP).\
\
You can collect the card **3 working days** after the second attempted delivery. You do not need an appointment.
