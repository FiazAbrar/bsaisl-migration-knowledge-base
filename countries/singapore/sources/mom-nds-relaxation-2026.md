# Interim relaxation of New Dormitory Standards (NDS) for migrant worker dormitories

6 November 2026 [Work passes and permits](/newsroom/announcements?category=Work+passes+and+permits)

As part of the Government’s continuous [efforts to build up sufficient supply of dormitory beds for migrant workers](/newsroom/press-releases/2026/0911-launch-of-sites-for-purpose-built-dormitories-in-2026-2027), MOM will introduce two interim relaxations to the New Dormitory Standards (NDS) from 1 January 2027.

Dormitory operators can choose to adopt these relaxations, subject to securing the relevant approvals from the technical agencies and MOM. Relevant Foreign Employee Dormitories Act (FEDA) requirements will also be correspondingly modified to facilitate the operational relaxation to NDS.
