Your digital work pass to support your employment in Singapore.

step-by-step guide

What is SGWorkPass

SGWorkPass allows you to access your digital work pass to:

View your latest work pass details
 without scanning the QR code on your physical card.

Verify the validity of your work pass 
by showing your digital work pass.

Enjoy greater data privacy 
as only you can see your detailed pass information after confirming your identity on your mobile phone.

Reduce the risk of lost or damaged cards
 by carrying your digital work pass on  your mobile phone.  

With SGWorkPass, you can also:

Receive notifications
 from MOM which are unique and relevant to you.

Change the language settings
 to access our services in your preferred language.

SGWorkPass allows you to verify the validity of MOM-issued passes by:

Viewing the pass holder’s digital work pass.

Using your SGWorkPass app to scan the QR code on the pass holder’s digital work pass or work pass card.

You can also get the pass holder’s FIN by scanning the barcode on their digital work pass.
