            Overview

Use
 this e-Service to cancel your Student's Pass (STP) when you complete or terminate
    your studies. Schools can also use this service to cancel an STP on a student's behalf.

Check eligibility

Check fees



            Eligibility

You can use this e-Service to cancel an STP if you are:
A school representative
An STP holder with a valid Singpass account



            Fees

There is no fee for the cancellation of your STP.



            Procedure 

Submit your cancellation application online at least 3 working days before your STP expires.
You must cancel and surrender your physical STP card (if you were issued one) for cancellation within 7 days of completing or terminating your studies. You can return it to an ICA officer at the checkpoint when leaving Singapore. 
If you or your school encounter issues during the submission, contact us via the 
enquiry form
.
