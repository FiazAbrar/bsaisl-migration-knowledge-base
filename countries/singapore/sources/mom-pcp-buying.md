# Buying a Primary Care Plan (PCP) from Anchor Operators

You must buy a PCP from the Anchor Operator (AO) that manages the zone where your workers stay. AOs are medical service providers appointed by MOM.

## Find your worker's zone and Anchor Operator

Each Anchor Operator manages a zone (from Zone A to Zone F).

To find the zone where your worker stays, check the first 2 digits of their postal code and match it to the zone below to view the AO, PCP price and buying instructions.

If your worker's postal code is 65XXXX, you must buy a PCP from zone F's AO, which is St Andrew's Mission Hospital.

## How to buy

Select based on ‘Zone: \[first 2 digits of postal code\]’ to find out the Anchor Operator’s details and how to buy a PCP.
