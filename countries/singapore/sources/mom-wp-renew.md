# Renew a Work Permit

You can renew a Work Permit online 7 to 12 weeks before it expires. Before renewing, you also need to complete certain steps, such as checking your quota and extending the worker's security bond and medical insurance.

## At a glance

|  |  |
|----|----|
| When to renew | 7 to 12 weeks before the permit expires. You can go to [WP Online](/eservices/services/wp-online-for-businesses-and-employment-agencies) to view the list of workers eligible for renewal |
| Who can renew | Employer or appointed employment agent (EA) |
| How much it costs | \$35 per renewal |
| How long it takes | The outcome is immediate |

You can [make an appeal](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/appeal-for-renewal) if your worker is not eligible to get a renewal. However, your appeal is unlikely to be successful unless you provide new information when you appeal.

## When to renew

You should renew the Work Permit **7 to 12 weeks before it expires**. Otherwise, an overstaying fine will be imposed.

If your worker plans to travel overseas just before the Work Permit expiry date, you should renew the Work Permit before he leaves Singapore.

You can go to [WP Online](/eservices/services/wp-online-for-businesses-and-employment-agencies) to:

- View the list of workers eligible for renewal.
- Print a copy of the renewal letter if you didn’t receive it.

## Late renewals

If you renew the Work Permit late, you’re still liable for the levy after it expires. Levy penalties or overstaying fine, if applicable, may also be imposed.

You can [request for an extension](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/get-a-short-extension-for-work-permit) if you’re unable to renew the Work Permit on time.

## How to renew

*Processing time: Immediate*

You can renew the Work Permit online.

To renew the permit:

1.  Register the worker’s [residential address and mobile number](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/housing/employers-responsibilities) using the [Online Foreign Workers Address Service (OFWAS)](/eservices/services/ofwas) – at least an hour before renewing the pass online.
2.  Log in to [WP Online](/eservices/services/wp-online-for-businesses-and-employment-agencies) to submit the application and upload the required documents. You may be asked to upload more or fewer documents during online renewal depending on what you have entered.\
    \
    Print and refer to the **renewal notice and application form** in WP Online under the “Renew” function for specific instructions.
3.  Pay \$35 for each renewal. You can pay by GIRO, VISA, Master Card or eNETS debit.

After the Work Permit is renewed, **print and check the notification letter** to see:

Guide your worker to set up their [SGWorkPass](/eservices/sgworkpass) app. They will need a Singpass account. They can view their digital work pass and employment information in the app.

The notification letter:

- Is valid for **1 month** from the date of issue. [Request to extend the validity](https://form.gov.sg/5b68f6c561cffc000f885453) if you have a change of travel plans.
- Should be given to the worker. It allows the worker to travel in and out of Singapore while waiting for the Work Permit card.

\[For CMP workers or those staying in dormitories\] Before their passes can be renewed, ensure that you have:

- Completed the purchase of the [Primary Care Plan](/primary-care-plan) (PCP).
- Declared on [WP Online](/eservices/services/wp-online-for-businesses-and-employment-agencies) that you have bought the PCP.

## Duration of renewal

The Work Permit is **usually valid for 2 years**. However, it may be shorter depending on:

- The expiry of the worker’s passport. If it expires in less than 2 years, the duration will be until 1 month before the expiry date.
- The validity of the security bond. If it is less than 26 months, the duration until 2 months before the security bond expires.
- The validity of the prior approval (if applicable).
- The maximum employment period of your workers (see [Work Permit requirements for your sector](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules)).

To get the full 2-year duration, you should renew your worker’s passport and security bond before you renew the Work Permit. Embassies may impose requirements on the renewal of passports.
