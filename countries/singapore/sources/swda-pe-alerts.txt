Seen an ad by Singapore Business School (SBS)? Here's what you need to know
Singapore Business School is not a registered Private Education Institution with SWDA

It has come to the attention of the Skills and Workforce Development Agency (“SWDA”) that an organisation known as “Singapore Business School” (“SBS”) has been advertising Master's Degree programmes through its website, 
www.sbs-online.world
. SWDA would like to inform members of the public that SBS is NOT a private education institution (“PEI”) registered with SWDA.

SWDA is unable to verify the authenticity of SBS's claims that its Master's Degree programmes are endorsed or accredited by the associations listed on its website, or the existence of these associations.

In view of recent enquiries concerning SBS, SWDA wishes to reiterate that SBS is not a registered PEI in Singapore and its courses are not permitted by SWDA. Members of the public who intend to enrol in SBS's programmes are advised to exercise caution and undertake their own due diligence before making any enrolment or payment decisions.

Members of the public who believe they have been scammed by SBS, including those who have made payments to SBS and suspect fraudulent activities, are advised to lodge a Police report directly with the Singapore Police Force (“SPF”) for further investigation.

Members of the public who wish to enrol in private education courses offered by registered PEIs are strongly advised to verify the institution's registration status and approved courses before enrolling or making payment. The list of registered PEIs is available on the 
TPGateway PEI Listing
.
