## Our services

If you require medical care, we can help with a range of services to ensure that your journey is stress-free and comfortable. For more details, view the [International Patients Guide](/international-patients-guide).

### Medical services

- Professional recommendations of treatment options and doctors
- Securing an appointment within 24 hours
- Advice on estimated cost of treatments and procedures
- Arranging a teleconsultation

### Travel arrangements

- Evacuation and repatriation assistance
- Flight and accommodation arrangements
- Visa application and extensions
- Airport transfer arrangements

### Language and special services

- Multi-language translation/interpreter service
- Special food and beverage/religious arrangements
- Post-care support
- Other services upon request
