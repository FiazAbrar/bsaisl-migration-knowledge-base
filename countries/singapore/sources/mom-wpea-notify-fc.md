# Notify MOM for Work Pass Exempt Activities

You must notify us if you are coming to Singapore to work in a work pass exempt activity. You can submit the notification online when you arrive.

## When to notify

You must notify us of your work pass exempt activity:

- After you have arrived in Singapore and obtained a Short Term Visit Pass at immigration.
- Before you start the activity.

## How to notify us

To notify us:

1. Submit an e-notification. You will need the following information:

   - Your personal particulars, such as name and date of birth
   - Passport number and expiry date
   - Disembarkation/Embarkation card number
   - Short Term Visit Pass expiry date
   - Type of activity you are performing
   - Period of activity (start date and end date)
   - Workplace address
2. After applying, print the acknowledgement letter.

It is an offence to carry out work pass exempt activities without first notifying MOM.
