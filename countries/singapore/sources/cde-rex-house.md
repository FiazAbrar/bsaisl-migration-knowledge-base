# CDEConnect at Rex House

## Information for Employers

**CDEConnect at Pasir Panjang has relocated to CDEConnect at Rex House. \
All interviews are now conducted at our new location.**

**New Location:**

******73 Bukit Timah Road\
Level 6, Rex House\
Singapore 229832**

- MDW interviews will now be conducted at CDEConnect at Rex House.
- Employers are required to **schedule appointments according to the timeline stated in the appointment letters sent to them.**
- **Walk-in visits **are not encouraged to ensure smooth coordination and reduce waiting time. Do make an appointment in advance to ensure smooth coordination and minimise waiting time.
- Nearest drop-off point with wheelchair access is at **Alfa Centre**. 

**Please note: Information on this page is based on current arrangements and may be updated from time to time.**

****

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### General Information

**Why is CDE relocating to Rex House?\**
CDE is relocating to improve accessibility and enhance service delivery, allowing us to better support employers and MDWs.

 

**When will operations begin at the new location?**\
Operations at Rex House will commence on **3 August 2026**.

 

**Will there be any disruption to services during the move?**\
No, all services will continue without interruption, with Rex House being fully functional from mid-August.

 

### Location & Accessibility

**What is the new address of CDEConnect at Rex House?\**
73 Bukit Timah Road\
Level 6, Rex House\
Singapore 229832

 

**How can I get to Rex House?**

**By MRT**

Rex House is within walking distance of Little India Station. Take Exit A and walk towards Mackenzie Road. Tap/click [here](https://alep-p-001.sitecorecontenthub.cloud/api/public/content/bbd9dfd2af384367a4a2c13f192ada94?v=dc28b2d6 "Directions from Little India Station to Rex House") for the directional video.

**By Bus**

The nearest bus-stop (bus-stop number: 40011) to Rex House is located next to Little India Station Exit A. Tap/click [here](https://alep-p-001.sitecorecontenthub.cloud/api/public/content/8fd2086a742c421086f699059f694f4d?v=f356c3a3) for the directional video.

 

Upon alighting at the Little India Station bus stop, proceed to cross at the traffic light towards Little India Station  Exit A Bus Stop. Continue walking towards Rex House, which is visible from the bus stop.

(Note: Due to ongoing major construction along the road, you may prefer to use the MRT underpass to cross safely.)

**Wheelchair Access**

Nearest drop-off point with wheelchair access is at **Alfa Centre**. 

 

 

### Appointments & Visits

**Will my existing appointment still be valid?\**
Yes. As you already have selected **Rex House** at time of scheduling your appointment, it will remain valid and will take place at Rex House from 3 August 2026 onwards.

** **

**Do I need to reschedule my appointment?\**
Rescheduling is not required unless you are contacted directly by CDEConnect.

** **

**Can I visit without an appointment?**\
Employers are required to book appointments in accordance with the timeline stated in the appointment letters. This ensures smooth coordination and efficient service and waiting time.

 

### Facilities & Amenities

**Is parking available at Rex House?**

1.  **Car park M0059**, along Mackenzie Road, an open-air car park located behind Rex House
2.  **Car park M0040**, just outside of Little India Station Exit A, an open-air car park

There is **no public parking facility** available at the building premises.

 

 

**Are there any amenities nearby?**\
Yes. There are various food outlets and convenience options within walking distance, including:

- Halal Canteen at \#03-01 of Boon Siew Building (Beside Rex House)
- Old Chang Kee (quick local snacks)
- Ananda Bhavan (casual dining at 663 Buffalo Rd, \#01-10, Singapore 210663)
- Tekka Centre
- Warm Up Café (Facing Carpark M0059) Non-Halal

 

In addition, there are convenience stores and other eateries in the surrounding Little India area.

 

**Is the building accessible?**\
Yes. Rex House is accessible for elderly and differently abled visitors.

For those arriving via public transport, assistance can be arranged at **Level 1**, where a wheelchair ramp is available upon request.

 

### Service & Operations 

**Will there be any changes to CDEConnect services?**\
No. All services, processes, and support provided by CDEConnect will remain unchanged.

 

**Will contact details change after the relocation?**\
All existing phone numbers, email addresses, and communication channels will remain the same.

** **

**Will operating hours change?**\
There are no changes to operating hours unless otherwise stated.

 

**Stay Updated:**

Employers are encouraged to refer to this page for updates and clarifications.
