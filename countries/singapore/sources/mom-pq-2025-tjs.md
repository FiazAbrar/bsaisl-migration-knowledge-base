# Written Answer to PQ on Number of foreign workers who applied for and are allowed to work under the Temporary Job Scheme

7 January 2025

NOTICE PAPER NO. 3310 OF 2024 FOR THE SITTING ON 07 JANUARY 2025\
QUESTION NO. 6385 FOR WRITTEN ANSWER

MP: Mr Leong Mun Wai

To ask the Minister for Manpower (a) since 2022, what is the annual number of foreign workers who have applied for and are granted permission to work under the Temporary Job Scheme while under investigation by the authorities; and (b) what are the main reasons for applications that are unsuccessful.

Answer:

1. Foreign workers who are required to remain in Singapore to assist in investigations will be issued a Special Pass by the respective law enforcement agencies. These Special Pass Holders (SPHs) are generally allowed to work under the Temporary Job Scheme (TJS), unless there are concerns over public safety or interference with investigations.

2. SPHs do not need to apply to participate in the TJS. They will be informed of their eligibility to work under the scheme. If an SPH chooses to take up this option, their potential employer can submit an application for a TJS work permit to the Ministry of Manpower to hire the SPH.

3. Since 2022, an annual average of about 850 SPHs have worked under the TJS. SPHs may choose not to seek employment under the TJS if they are able to support themselves. SPHs who face issues with their upkeep can approach the Ministry of Manpower for assistance.
