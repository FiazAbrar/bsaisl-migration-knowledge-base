**Note:**

- Getting an EntrePass is not a guarantee that your business registration and licences will get approved.
- You will still need to get approval from the [Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA)](https://www.acra.gov.sg) for registering your business. You also have to apply separately for any licences needed to start your business.
- Processing time is around 6** weeks**. You will not be able to extend your Short Term Visit Pass if it expires before your application is approved.
- Apply for [family passes](/passes-and-permits/entrepass/passes-for-family) after the main pass is approved. Although you can apply for family passes at the same time as the EntrePass, you will lose the application fees for each family pass if the EntrePass application gets rejected.

## Submit an application

Show

*Processing time: Within 6 weeks. Some cases will take more time.*

### How to apply

1.  Prepare the [documents required](/passes-and-permits/entrepass/documents-required).
2.  Complete the [application online](https://service2.mom.gov.sg/workpass/pe/ent/landing).
3.  Pay the \$105 application fee by Visa, Mastercard or Amex.

### How to check your application status

You may [check your application status](/eservices/services/check-work-pass-and-application-status) after 2 weeks.

If additional information or documents are required:

- We will email you (please reply directly to our email).
- The processing time may take longer than 6 weeks.

### Receiving your application results

We will send your results to the notification email indicated in your application form.

If your pass is approved, you will also receive an in-principle approval (IPA) letter. The IPA:

- Is a **pre-approved single-entry visa** for you to enter Singapore. It gives you 6 months to enter Singapore and get your pass issued.
- **States whether you need to go for a medical examination **after your arrival in Singapore.

## Enter Singapore

Show

### Before departing

You should:

- Print your in-principle approval (IPA) letter.
- Ensure that you comply with the [latest travel requirements](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore).

### After arriving

You should:

- Show your IPA letter to the immigration officer.
- Complete any required medical examinations.

## Get the pass issued

Show

*When: Within 6 months of in-principle approval*

*Processing time: Immediate*

You must be in Singapore to get your pass issued.

[TABLE]

### EP eService

For candidates with Singpass accounts, company representatives or employment agents to get the pass issued:

1.  [Log in to our eService](/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice) and provide the [required information and documents](#see-what-you-ll-need).
2.  Pay the fees using GIRO, Visa, Mastercard or Amex:
    - \$225 for each pass

    &nbsp;

    - (If applicable) \$30 for each [Multiple Journey Visa](https://www.ica.gov.sg/#/visitor/visitor_entryvisa)
3.  Once the pass is issued, the candidate and the company representative/employment agent will receive a **notification letter** by email. 

&nbsp;

### EPSC

For candidates without Singpass accounts, you can get your pass issued over the counter at our EPSC. Please refer to the following steps:

1.  [Make an appointment](/eservices/services/make-an-appointment) to have your pass issued at our [Employment Pass Services Centre](/contact-us/locations#Tab-EP).
2.  Bring the [supporting documents](#see-what-you-ll-need) for your appointment.
3.  Pay the fees using VISA, MasterCard, AMEX, NETS or CashCard (If your credit or debit card was not issued in Singapore, please ensure it has been activated for overseas use):
    - \$225 for each pass
    - (If applicable) \$30 for each [Multiple Journey Visa](https://www.ica.gov.sg/#/visitor/visitor_entryvisa)
4.  You will be issued a **notification letter**. 

&nbsp;

The notification letter:

- Allows the candidate to start work and travel in and out of Singapore while waiting for the pass card. 
- States if the candidate needs to have their fingerprints and photo taken for the card registration.
- Is valid for **1 month** from the date of issue. If the candidate needs more time to complete the card registration procedures, you can [request to extend the validity of the notification letter](https://www.go.gov.sg/change-date-epsc).

### What you'll need

You will need the following information to get the pass issued:

- Candidate’s passport details
- Candidate’s Singapore contact details 
- Candidate’s current Short-Term Visit Pass or immigration pass details
- Candidate’s Singapore residential address, which must meet the [housing requirements](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/housing/various-types-of-housing) for you to get the pass issued. 
- Singapore residential or office address to receive the candidate’s card
- Details of up to 3 authorised recipients to receive the card (their mobile number and NRIC number / FIN / passport number) 

You may also need to submit PDF copies of these documents:

- Candidate’s passport page showing the date of arrival in Singapore 
- Completed medical declaration form

## (If required) Register fingerprints and photo

Show

 

*When: Within 2 weeks after pass is issued (if required)*

Check the notification letter for whether you need to register your fingerprints and photo.

For registration, you must [make an appointment](/eservices/services/make-an-appointment) to visit the [Employment Pass Services Centre (EPSC)](/contact-us/locations#Tab-EP). You need to register **within 2 weeks** after the pass is issued.

Bring along these documents for your appointment:

- Original passport.
- Appointment letter.
- Notification letter.
- Your mobile phone (with a local mobile number) with both Singpass and [SGWorkPass](/eservices/sgworkpass) apps downloaded.

During registration, we will capture your fingerprints, take your photo and register you for a Singpass account which allows you to access your digital work pass in the [SGWorkPass](/eservices/sgworkpass) app.

## Set up digital work pass

Show

*When: On the day of card registration*

After they are registered for a Singpass account, inform the candidate to:

1.  Download [SGWorkPass](/eservices/sgworkpass) (if they have not done so yet)
2.  Log into the app with their Singpass
3.  View their digital work pass and employment information

SGWorkPass app allows them easy and secure access to their digital work pass. They can check their status, expiry date and procure services earlier, without having to wait for delivery of their physical work pass cards.

If the candidate is not required to report to EPSC for card registration, please inform them to set up their digital work pass.

*\*

 

## Receive the card

Show

*When: Within 5 working days after registration or document verification* 

We will deliver the Employment Pass card to the given address within **5 working days** after you register and get your documents verified at EPSC. If you did not have to register your fingerprints and photo, we will deliver the card within 5 working days after checking your documents.

Set up your [SGWorkPass](/eservices/sgworkpass) app to view your digital work pass and employment information.

The authorised recipients will get an SMS or email with the delivery details at least 1 working day before the delivery.

When collecting the card, you will need to show these documents for identification:

| Person receiving the card | Required documents            |
|:--------------------------|:------------------------------|
| Candidate                 | Passport or digital work pass |
|  Authorised person        | NRIC, FIN or passport         |

If you do not receive the card within 5 working days, [check if you have uploaded the correct documents](/faq/work-pass-general/ive-registered-for-my-card-but-mom-has-not-yet-contacted-me-about-the-card-delivery-date-what-should-i-do).

You can also check your card delivery status using [EP eService](/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice).

### If card delivery fails

After two unsuccessful deliveries, you’ll have to collect the card at [EPSC](/contact-us/locations#Tab-EP).

You or an authorised person can collect it **3 working days** after the second attempted delivery. You don’t need an appointment.

Bring along these documents for card collection:

- Pass holder’s original passport or digital work pass
- Notification letter

If you authorise someone to collect it on your behalf, make sure they bring these along:

- An authorisation letter from the pass holder
- NRIC or passport for verification
- Pass holder’s original passport
- Notification letter
