                                                                                                                                               Singapore takes in a carefully controlled number of new citizens and PRs each year. We
                                                                                                                                               are intentional in granting citizenships and permanent residencies to assimilable individuals

                                                                                                                                               through marriage), or have studied, worked or lived in Singapore for some time.

It is important for new citizens and PRs to be integrated into our communities. All new citizens
aged 16 to 60 must complete the Singapore Citizenship Journey before obtaining their citizenship.
The programme helps new citizens understand Singapore's history, our norms and values,
and build stronger ties with the local community.20 A nation-wide grassroots network of more
than 1,500 Integration and Naturalisation Champions (INCs) also helps drive integration
efforts in neighbourhoods.21

20 Launched in 2011, the Singapore Citizenship Journey comprises an online e-learning module with a quiz component, an experiential learning
programme and a Community Sharing Session with local community leaders.
21 INCs develop and implement programmes to welcome and integrate new citizens. They play a pivotal role in bridging cultural gaps and fostering
