        Hiring an existing manufacturing or services worker

You can hire existing workers, without them having to leave Singapore first. Find out the requirements and how to apply.

At a glance

Who are eligible

Existing manufacturing and services workers from North Asian sources, People's Republic of China (PRC) or Non-Traditional Sources (NTS). You can only hire from your own business sector.

Note:

You can also find out about 
hiring existing Malaysian workers
.  

When you can start applying

With the current employer's consent:

Anytime during the worker’s employment in Singapore. You can find out 
more details
.

Without the current employer's consent:

From 
40 to 21 days before
 their current Work Permit expires. You first need to 
apply for pre-approval
 from MOM.

Why hire an existing worker
You may consider hiring workers who are already working in Singapore to:

Benefit from experienced workers and a faster deployment time.

Save on the cost of bringing in new workers. 

Apply for a transfer worker

With current employer's consent 
Show

To apply for the transfer when the worker's Work Permit has a validity of 14 days or more:

Submit your Work Permit application by following the general 
Work Permit application process
. If the worker is eligible for transfer, we will send an SMS or email notification to the current employer.

You should opt for email notifications during your application, so that you will receive:

A summary of your application. 

Notifications to check your application status once there are updates

Wait for the current employer to give consent. The current employer needs to log in to 
myMOM 
Portal

within 7 days
 to approve or reject the transfer request. Otherwise, your Work Permit application will be rejected.

Check the application outcome by logging in to 
myMOM 
Portal
 > Work Passes > Quick menu > View details. You will also be notified by email if you had opted for it.

Upon approval, the employer can proceed to have the new Work Permit (WP) issued, and the existing WP will be automatically cancelled once the new WP is issued. If you need an earlier transfer, have a discussion with the worker and current employer to come to an agreement on when to cancel the existing Work Permit.

If the worker's Work Permit is expiring in less than 14 days, you first need to 
apply for pre-approval
 from MOM. You will need the current employer to sign the 
consent form
.

Without current employer's consent
Show
When the workers’ Work Permits are within 40 to 21 days from expiry, you can apply for new Work Permits for them without their current employer’s consent.

To apply for a new Work Permit for the transfer:

Apply for pre-approval

Once pre-approval is obtained, you can 
apply for a new Work Permit

Upon approval, you need to get the Work Permit issued.

You can only request for the Work Permit to be issued when the 
current permit has been cancelled or expires
.

As a current employer, what could I do to keep my workers
As a current employer, you are given priority to renew your workers’ Work Permits.

If you want to keep your workers, you should 
renew their Work Permits early
, upon receiving the renewal notice. 

When your workers’ Work Permits are 
within 40 to 21 days from expiry
, another employer can apply for new Work Permits for them without your consent.  

If the new employer’s application is approved:

You can continue to employ the worker, as long as the existing Work Permit is valid.

You are responsible for settling any outstanding salary while the worker was under your employment.
