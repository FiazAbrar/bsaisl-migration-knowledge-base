Key facts on Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass

Get a quick overview of the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass to decide if it is the right pass for you.

Candidates may apply for the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass under the AI and Tech track from 28 January 2027

Pass facts

Who it is for

Top talent in business, arts and culture, sports, academia and research, AI and Tech.

Tip: If you are an experienced founder who is building your next venture, find out more about EDB’s Global Founder Programme (GFP) and the support package including manpower and visa facilitation, capital, and connections.
Who can apply Candidates who meet the eligibility criteria can apply.

Appointed employment agencies and employers can also apply on behalf of their candidate.

Note: You may only submit 1 work pass application per candidate at a time. For example, you may only apply for either an Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass or Employment Pass for your candidate at any one time.
What is the eligibility criteria

Candidates need to meet one of the criteria below: 

  • Earned a fixed monthly salary of at least S$30,000 (or its equivalent in foreign currency) for the 12 consecutive months leading up to the date of application
  • Will earn a fixed monthly salary of at least S$30,000 under their future employer in Singapore 

Tip: Even if you do not meet the salary criterion, you may still qualify if you have outstanding achievements in certain sectors.
What is the duration

The duration of the pass is as follows:

  • First-time candidates: Up to 5 years
  • Subsequent renewals: Up to 5 years
Is it renewable Yes, upon meeting the renewal criteria.
Foreign worker levy or quota No foreign worker levy or quota required.
Related eServices and forms Access online services and forms for Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass.

Learn more about working in Singapore and connect with more than 80,000 professionals and entrepreneurs through the Singapore Global Network.

Features of the pass

The key features of the pass include the following:

  • Exemption from Complementarity Assessment Framework (COMPASS) and Fair Consideration Framework job advertising requirements
  • Flexibility to concurrently start, operate and work for multiple companies at any one time (subject to your employment contract terms, where relevant)
  • No need to reapply for a pass if you change jobs
  • Availability of passes for eligible family members
  • Your spouse will be able to apply to work on a Letter of Consent.

You may be required to pay income tax.

When required

Before applying

    • Know the eligibility and requirements to apply.
    • Find out the documents required for applying.

Before arrival

    • Apply for the Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass and wait for the outcome.

    • (If your pass is approved) Get an in-principle approval letter.

    • Ensure that you comply with the latest travel requirements.

Upon arrival

    • Get your pass issued and receive a notification letter stating whether registration is required.
    • (If required) Make an appointment for the candidate to register fingerprints and photo.
    • Receive your pass card at the address provided 5 working days after registration or document verification.

As and when required

    • Notify MOM about your professional activities and annual income by 31 January of the next year.

      You should also notify MOM about any changes to your residential address or personal information.

    • Apply for a replacement if your pass card is lost, stolen or damaged.
    • Cancel your pass if you no longer require it.
