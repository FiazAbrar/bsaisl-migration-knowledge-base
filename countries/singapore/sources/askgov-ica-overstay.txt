What will happen if I overstay in Singapore?
Updated by 
ICA

3y ago
39
 found this helpful
Overstaying is an offence under the Immigration Act. Depending on the number of days you have overstayed, you will be subjected to a composition fine or prosecution in court.
You can submit your application for a Short-Term Visit Pass (STVP) extension via 
ICA e-Service
 for ICA's assessment.
