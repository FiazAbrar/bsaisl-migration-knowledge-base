30 October 2026

To: All firms employing construction Work Permit holders (WPHs)

[From 1 Jan 2027] Bringing in New Construction Work Permit Holders (WPHs) from the
People's Republic of China (PRC) and Thailand

1. On 30 April 2026, it was announced that to expedite the process of bringing in new
workers, the pre-entry Skills Evaluation Certificate (Knowledge) (SEC(K)) testing1 will cease
for workers from the People's Republic of China (PRC) and Thailand from January 2027. This
cessation will be extended to all source countries from January 2028.

2. Prospective workers who have attained SEC(K) through overseas testing in PRC and
Thailand prior to 1 January 2027 will be allowed to enter and work as new Construction WPHs
since they possess the relevant SEC(K) qualification, subject to prevailing Work Permit
criteria, including the firm's Dependency Ratio Ceiling (DRC).

3. From 1 January 2027, firms that wish to bring in new Construction WPHs who do not
have the relevant SEC(K) qualifications from PRC and Thailand are required to tap on the
Alternative Entry Pathway (AEP) programme.2

Vocational Certifications recognised by BCA under AEP
4. Under the AEP, firms can bring in new workers from source countries that have
obtained vocational certifications recognised by BCA. To date, BCA has recognised nine
vocational certifications offered in PRC and Thailand, spanning six trades already available
for testing at PRC and Thailand Overseas Testing Centres (OTCs) today.

5. As the standards of these vocational certifications have been recognised to be on par
with existing SEC(K) skills standards, new workers from PRC and Thailand who possess the
recognised vocational certification can be brought in to work in Singapore without obtaining
the SEC(K) locally.

6. Furthermore, these nine BCA-recognised vocational certifications are assessed to be
equivalent to attaining SEC(K) at a higher skill level. New PRC and Thai workers who possess
these vocational certifications and earn a minimum fixed monthly salary of $1,600 will be
eligible for R1 (Higher-skilled) status via the Direct R1 pathway.

7. For the full list of recognised vocational certifications, please refer to Alternative Entry
Pathway (AEP) Programme | Building and Construction Authority.

Process to bring in new workers possessing Vocational Certifications recognised by BCA
under AEP
8. For firms intending to bring in new Construction WPHs from PRC and Thailand from 1
January 2027 with vocational certifications recognised by BCA, the process is as follows:

Step  Details
1     Firms are to approach the vocational centres directly to either:

        i. Arrange for their prospective workers to enrol in the relevant vocational
               training and certification, or

        ii. Indicate that they would like to employ the vocational centres' graduates
               who hold the recognised vocational certification.

      The respective vocational centres' contact information can be found at Alternative

      Entry Pathway (AEP) Programme | Building and Construction Authority.

2     The vocational centres will provide BCA with the following:

      i. Information about prospective graduates or workers

      ii. Verification of their vocational certification

      This process will take up to approximately 4­5 weeks. Once this process has been

      completed, the vocational centre will notify the firms.

3     After being notified, firms may proceed to submit a Work Permit application to the

      Ministry of Manpower (MOM) on myMOM Portal. The Work Permit application

      process with MOM remains unchanged.

9. To ensure that the recognised vocational certifications remain on par with existing
skills standards, BCA may conduct sample audits.

Employer-led Process under AEP
10. Firms can also apply to BCA to pilot their own recruitment channel under AEP to bring
in new Construction WPHs from PRC and Thailand without SEC(K) or BCA-recognised
vocational certifications.

11. To apply, firms must submit a proposal to BCA outlining how they intend to select and
train better quality Construction workers from PRC and Thailand in specific trades, using this
form: Alternative Entry Pathway (AEP) to bring in Better Quality Worker | FormSG

12. Firms bringing in new Construction WPHs from PRC and Thailand must continue to
meet MOM's prevailing Work Permit criteria, including DRC. This applies to all new
Construction WPHs, whether they had previously attained SEC(K) in PRC or Thailand, or are
being brought in via employer-led AEP processes or with recognised vocational certifications.

13. These changes will enable firms to bring in new Construction WPHs from PRC and
Thailand faster while maintaining the quality of new workers.

14. For any questions, you may contact us via the BCA Feedback Form
(https://www2.bca.gov.sg/feedback/).

FAQs
1. When is the last date to enrol workers for overseas testing in PRC and Thailand?

Leading up to 1 January 2027 when overseas testing will cease for PRC and Thailand, firms
should approach the existing OTCs in PRC and Thailand early for more information on the
final enrolment and testing dates.

2. How did BCA select the vocational certifications from PRC and Thailand to be
recognised under AEP?

Based on the vocational schools' long-running track record in training workers who perform
well in SEC(K) tests, BCA has assessed that these vocational certifications are on par with
existing skills standards.

3. From 1 January 2027, how should firms source workers from other source
countries besides PRC and Thailand?

As part of a phased implementation leading up to the full removal of the overseas testing
requirement from January 2028, mandatory pre-entry SEC(K) testing will continue in all other
source countries. New Construction WPHs from all other source countries must continue to
obtain SEC(K) overseas before entering Singapore. Alternatively, firms may bring in new
Construction WPHs without SEC(K) via their own AEP pilots. More details will be shared
nearer to January 2028, to prepare firms for upcoming changes.
