# Can I apply for visa if my passport is less than 6 months?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

![Immigration & Checkpoints Authority logo](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Flogos.ask.gov.sg%2Ficalogo.png&w=3840&q=75)

Updated by ICA 1y ago

------------------------------------------------------------------------

36 found this helpful

You will not be able to apply for visa if your passport has less than 6 months' validity. You will require a passport that is valid for at least 6 months from the date of arrival in Singapore as part of the [entry requirements](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-depart/entry_requirements).
