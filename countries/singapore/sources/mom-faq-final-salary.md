# I submitted my resignation letter, but my employer is now asking me to leave earlier/immediately. Am I entitled to notice pay and when will I receive my final salary?

As your employer is now asking you to leave earlier/immediately even though you were already serving notice, your employer should pay you salary in lieu of notice for the rest of the notice period.

You should receive your [final salary](/employment-practices/salary/paying-salary#how-often-salary-must-be-paid) on your last day of work or within 3 working days from you last day of work.

The Tripartite Alliance for Dispute Management (TADM) handles salary related claims and disputes on employment termination. If your employer did not pay you, you can visit [TADM's website](https://www.tal.sg/tadm) and tap on “ASK TADM” chatbot advisory service for assistance.
