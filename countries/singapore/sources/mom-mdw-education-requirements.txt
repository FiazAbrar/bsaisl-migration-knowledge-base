Educational Requirements for First-time Migrant Domestic Workers

Migrant Domestic Workers (MDWs) must have a minimum of 8 years of formal education and possess
the necessary educational certificates as documentary proof of her education. A list of acceptable
educational certificates is given below.

When the migrant domestic worker arrives in Singapore, she will have to submit a photocopy of the
educational certificates before she is allowed to attend the Settling-In-Programme (SIP). The Ministry
of Manpower (MOM) may require MDWs who are unable to produce acceptable educational
certificates to be repatriated.
