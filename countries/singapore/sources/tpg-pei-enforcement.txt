Between 24 Apr 2019 and 4 July 2019
PekSiew Gek, Manager of Kings International Business School
Entity Number: 200919036Z
Conviction
Following the regulatory cancellation of Kings International Business School’s registration on 5 May 2015, the accused was convicted and sentenced for: 

a.  Failing to ensure that Kings International Business School had complied with a  direction to fully refund or place out 34 students. This is an offence under Section 60(5) read with Section 61(2) of the PE Act, Cap 247A. The accused was convicted of the offence by the Court on 24 Apr 2019 and was sentenced to 4 weeks imprisonment. 

b.  Furnishing of documents (examination scripts) which are false in a material particular and which one does not believe to be true. This is an offence under Section 61(1)(b) of the PE Act, Cap 247A. The accused was convicted of the offence by the Court on 4 July 2019 and was sentenced to 6 weeks imprisonment. 

c.  Furnishing of documents (attendance lists) which are false in a material particular and which one does not believe to be true. This is an offence under Section 61(1)(b) of the PE Act, Cap 247A. The accused was convicted of the offence by the Court on 4 July 2019 and was sentenced to 4 weeks imprisonment on 4 July 2019.

In addition, the accused was convicted of the offence, Section 204a read with Section 116 of the Penal Code, on 22 May 2018 by the Court for abetting another person to obstruct the course of justice. The accused was sentenced to 3 months imprisonment. The accused’s appeal against the conviction and sentence was dismissed on 10 Jun 2019.
24 Apr 2019
