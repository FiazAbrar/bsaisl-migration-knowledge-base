## Overview

Use this e-Service to submit your appeal online, upload supporting documents, and check your appeal status for citizenship, permanent residence, and Long-Term Visit Pass appeals.

## Eligibility

You can only appeal if your previous application was officially rejected.

Do not submit an appeal if:

- Your application is still pending. Wait for the outcome instead.
- You have not applied before. Submit a new application instead.

Each appeal is assessed on its own merits. ICA considers factors such as the individual’s family ties to Singapore Citizens, economic contributions, qualifications, age, family profile, and length of residency. These help us assess the applicant’s ability to contribute to Singapore, integrate into our society, and their long-term commitment to Singapore.

\



## Documents Required

You can upload new documents and official translations to support your appeal.



Note

You will need to provide original documents and official translations (for documents in languages other than English).

ICA accepts:

- Translations provided by the embassy of the country/place that issued the document
- Translations produced by a notary public in Singapore or the country/place that issued the document
- Privately created translations attested by the embassy of the country/place that issued the document, or notarised by a notary public in Singapore or the country/place that issued the document

ICA does not endorse any private translation companies or entities. Insufficient documentation may result in non-acceptance of your application. You may be required to provide additional documents to support your appeal.



## Fees

There is no fee for the submission of appeals.



## Procedure

Log in with Singpass to submit your appeal.

- **If the original application had a sponsor:** The sponsor must log in.
- **If the original application had no sponsor:** The main applicant must log in. 

If the original application was submitted without Singpass, you must submit the appeal using the same email address used in your original application.

## Processing Time

ICA assesses appeals on their own merits, taking into account the specific circumstances of each case.\
The processing time varies by appeal.

## FAQs

You can only submit an appeal if your previous application was rejected within the last 12 months.

The sponsor of the rejected application must submit the appeal using Singpass. If there was no sponsor, the main applicant must submit the appeal.

If the original application was submitted without Singpass, you must submit the appeal using the same email address.\

If your appeal for Singapore citizenship, permanent residence, or Long-Term Visit Pass is pending, you can withdraw your appeal by logging in to the e-Service and selecting "Withdraw Appeal".

If you have a pending application for Singapore citizenship, permanent residence, or Long-Term Visit Pass, you cannot submit a new appeal for the same pass or status.

If you wish to apply for a new applicant or a new pass, you can submit a new application via [e-Service](/redirect-links/e-services/e-services--appeal-for-singapore-citizenship--permanent-residence-or-long-term-visit-pass). The applicant must not have any pending application for the pass or status they are applying for.
