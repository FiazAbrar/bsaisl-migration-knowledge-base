# Inter-agency advisory on safe transportation of workers

Jointly issued by the Ministry of Manpower (MOM), Ministry of Transport (MOT), Land Transport Authority (LTA), Singapore Police Force (SPF), and Building and Construction Authority (BCA).

31 December 2022 [Workplace safety and health](/newsroom/announcements?category=Workplace+safety+and+health) [Foreign manpower](/newsroom/announcements?category=Foreign+manpower)

1\. This advisory sets out practical guidance for employers and workers on new and existing legal requirements and recommended practices for the industry to enhance safety of workers on lorry transportation.

### B. Requirements and recommendations for employers

[TABLE]

2\. To improve workers’ safety, employers should **consider using other modes of transportation (e.g. buses) where possible**. Where it is not practical to use alternative modes of transportation, employers should ensure the safety of workers travelling in lorries by adopting the recommended practices to ensure safety of the driver and all passengers in the lorry.

3\. Employers should:

1.  Set the right tone and culture to emphasise the importance of safety, so that workers do not feel pressured to rush;
2.  Ensure safe means of access and egress to and from the lorry deck;
3.  Avoid ferrying workers together with heavy goods/equipment;
4.  Avoid incentives or disincentives that may encourage drivers to rush or drive recklessly;
5.  Be prepared to activate a back-up driver where necessary; and
6.  Provide avenues for reporting unsafe driving (e.g. display hotline number to report unsafe driving).

4\. Employers are encouraged to:

1.  Reward good driving behaviors. For example, incentive bonus for clean driving history (e.g. 0 demerit point) or achieving accident-free record for a period of time;
2.  Deploy vehicular safety technologies (VSTs)[³](#footnote) to improve driving safety. This can include (a) Advanced Driver Assistance System (ADAS) to enhance the situational awareness of drivers and provide warning of collision risks, (b) Driver Status Monitoring System (DSMS) to detect driver fatigue and distraction such as mobile device usage, and (c) Driver Behavior Management System (DBMS) to monitor reckless driving behavior (e.g. harsh turning, acceleration or braking); and
3.  Provide raincoats and rain boots to workers to protect them against inclement weather.

### C. Requirements and recommendations for drivers

[TABLE]

5\. Drivers should adopt safe and good driving habits, have sufficient rest and not rush. **If the driver feels unfit to drive (e.g. unwell, drowsy from medication), the driver or the vehicle buddy should inform his/her supervisor or employer immediately and seek the necessary medical treatment.**

6\. Drivers should:

1.  Before driving,\
    1.  Perform daily checks on the general condition of the vehicle.
    2.  Check that the workers are seated properly and have their seat belts fastened (where applicable) before moving off.
2.  When driving,\
    1.  Always stay focused on the road.
    2.  Adopt safe driving habits including:\
        - No harsh turning, acceleration, and braking;
        - Slow down when approaching road humps; and
        - During adverse weather conditions, drive slower, keep a longer safe distance from the vehicle in front as longer braking distance may be required, and turn on headlights.
3.  When vehicle stops at a destination,\
    1.  Pull handbrake and make sure vehicle comes to a complete stop before letting passengers alight; and
    2.  Switch on hazard lights if stopping at the roadside.

### D. Recommendations for vehicle buddies

\

8\. Vehicle buddies should:

1.  Remind the driver to drive safely (e.g. stay within the speed limits, no reckless driving);
2.  Assist the driver to look out for obstruction, hazard, or danger; and
3.  Assist the driver with navigating to the destination if asked, to allow the driver to focus on driving.

### E. Requirements and recommendations for workers in lorry rear decks

[TABLE]

9\. Workers in lorry rear decks should:

1.  Board and alight the lorry with care. Do not rush or jump down from the lorry;
2.  Not move around when the lorry is moving;
3.  Report defects on vehicle to employers or driver so that timely repairs can be carried out;
4.  Report unsafe driving to their employers or the authority; and
5.  Report unsafe behavior of other lorry passengers during commute to their employers.
