        How do I apply for Special Pass for my injured worker who is pending work injury compensation claim?

You must:

Download and complete the 
Special Pass application form
.

Accompany your injured worker to the 
MOM Services Centre
 to apply for Special Pass. Bring the following documents:

Request letter with company’s letterhead stating:

Reasons for cancelling work permit.

Request to issue the special pass.

Work permit holder’s original passport.

Work permit holder’s medical appointment card, if any.

Work permit holder’s work permit card.

At least 3 photos that clearly show the condition of Work Permit holder’s accommodation.
