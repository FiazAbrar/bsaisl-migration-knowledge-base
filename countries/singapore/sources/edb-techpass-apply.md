# Tech.Pass - application & renewal

**From 28 January 2027, MOM/EDB will no longer accept new and renewal Tech.Pass applications.** Existing Tech.Pass holders can remain on their pass until its expiry.

## Application process

### Apply for Tech.Pass

- Download the Tech.Pass application form for yourself and dependants, if applicable.
- Complete the application form as a soft copy and get it signed.
- Obtain a payment reference number that is automatically generated in the form.
- Prepare the relevant supporting documents indicated in the application form.

### Payment

- Pay the application fee (S$105) for each application. You can pay the application fee by PayNow, Visa, Mastercard or Amex when using our online submission form (FormSG) to upload your application documents. Payment must be made separately for each Tech.Pass and dependant's application.
- To ensure successful payment, please follow the instructions stated in the application form. Application fees are non-refundable.

### Upload completed application form and required documents

Please make sure you have the following documents for submission:

- Original completed and signed application form.
- Travel document page.
- Supporting documents.

Please submit your applications [here](https://form.gov.sg/60055cbf0a4eb30011a7cc8f).

### Processing timeline

It takes around 8 weeks to process Tech.Pass applications, from when all documents are in order. It may take longer if additional information is required.

If and when your application is approved, you will receive an email from MOM, containing formal notification of approval, along with the in-principle approval (IPA) letter.

### Get the pass issued

The pass must be issued within 6 months of receiving the in-principle approval. This can be done in person at the MOM Employment Pass Services Centre with an appointment.

You must be in Singapore to have your pass issued. You will need your issued pass to start work or business activities in Singapore.

Fees involved:

- S$225 for each pass.
- S$30 for each Multiple Journey Visa, if applicable.

No extension to the in-principle approval will be granted.

### Can I still apply for the Tech.Pass before the introduction of the new ONE Pass (AI and Tech) track in January 2027?

New applications for Tech.Pass will remain open until 27 January 2027, 2359hrs. Applications submitted before this date will continue to be processed under the existing framework.

### Do I need to secure employment in Singapore to apply for Tech.Pass?

No, you do not need to secure employment in Singapore prior to application. Individuals can apply for the Tech.Pass without employer sponsorship.
