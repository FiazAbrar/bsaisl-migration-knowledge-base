# My migrant worker and I disagree on the destination where the worker will be repatriated. Can I get help to resolve the dispute?

Sending your migrant worker or migrant domestic worker home after employment ends is part of your responsibility as an employer. This is a condition of the security bond.

Employers may also send their MWs to another destination by mutual agreement. Both employers and workers are encouraged to be understanding, and to exercise flexibility when doing so. You can inform MOM of this arrangement when cancelling the Work Permit using the [Work Permit eService in myMOM Portal](/eservices/services/work-permit-transactions-for-workers-employed-by-businesses).

However, in cases where both employers and workers are unable to come to an agreement, you can [contact MOM](https://form.gov.sg/655319129520f10012b5887b) for a decision.
