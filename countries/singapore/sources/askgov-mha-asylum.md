# Does Singapore accept refugees or asylum seekers?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

![Ministry of Home Affairs logo](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Flogos.ask.gov.sg%2Fmha.png&w=3840&q=75)

Updated by MHA 5mo ago

Singapore is not in a position to accept any persons seeking political asylum or refugee status, given our constraints as a small city state. Nonetheless, we will assist and ensure the safe departure of such persons to a third country by coordinating with organisations such as the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR), where appropriate. Singapore respects the principle of non-refoulement under customary international law.
