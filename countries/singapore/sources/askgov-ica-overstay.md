# What will happen if I overstay in Singapore?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

![Immigration & Checkpoints Authority logo](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Flogos.ask.gov.sg%2Ficalogo.png&w=3840&q=75)

Updated by ICA 3y ago

Overstaying is an offence under the Immigration Act. Depending on the number of days you have overstayed, you will be subjected to a composition fine or prosecution in court.\
You can submit your application for a Short-Term Visit Pass (STVP) extension via [ICA e-Service](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/esvclandingpage/extend) for ICA's assessment.
