Application of Student's Pass

Category
Actions / Information Required
1.
Full-Time International Students
You must hold a valid Student's Pass issued by the Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA) of Singapore for your studies in NTU.
2.
Dependant's Pass (DP) holder
Immigration Exemption Order (IEO)
You are permitted to study at NTU with a Dependant's Pass (DP)/Immigration Exemption Order (IEO). However, if your DP/IEO expires or is terminated before you finish your course, you must apply for a Student's Pass to continue your studies at NTU. 
To apply for the Student's Pass (STP), you need to submit a service request through the 
NTU Student One Stop Portal
3. 
Part-Time Students
Not eligible
 to apply for Student's Pass.
4. 
Application of Student’s Pass
You must submit an application 
not more than

2 months 
before the commencement of the course, through ICA Student’s Pass Online Application and Registration (SOLAR) System.
Guide on how to apply for Student’s Pass online can be downloaded  
HERE.
More information on Student’s Pass can be found below.

Prior to your Student's Pass submission to ICA, NTU will need to register your particulars through the 
ICA SOLAR system
 with the following details:
Your name, date of birth, gender, and nationality (as appear in your travel      document)
Course details, course commencement and end dates
SOLAR application (AKA Registration Acknowledgement Letter)
Undergraduate students can access the 
Undergraduate Acceptance Link
 after obtaining the matriculation number.
Postgraduate students can look out for the Confirmation of Acceptance email sent to the inbox/ junk folder after confirming acceptance. The SOLAR application (AKA Registration Acknowledgement Letter) details will be indicated in the email.

Please ensure the following when submitting eForm 16 at 
ICA SOLAR system
:
All your particulars* including programme name and start date are correctly captured in eForm 16. 
NRIC/FIN number of your parents if they are Singaporeans or foreigners working or      living in Singapore. Otherwise, leave it 
blank
.
A Singapore address and contact details. You may leave it 
blank
 if      there is no local address/contact details.
A Reachable email address.
*Note
: Should there be any discrepancies, please inform us through 
Ask One Stop
 and do not proceed with the submission

You have to pay a non-refundable processing fee of $45 within 7 days upon submission of your eForm 16 with completed documents. Failing which, the application will be withdrawn. ICA SOLAR system only accepts payment via credit/debit card and internet banking only. 
Some applications may take longer time to process as stated below.  You may enquire about the application status using the 
e-Service
.
Application without Visa – Within One Week 
Application with Visa – Within Two Weeks 

You may check your Student Pass application status via     
ICA SOLAR system
. 
If approved, you may print the outcome letter of your Student's Pass.
If rejected, you may 
submit an appeal via the enquiry form
, along with your STP application reference number. Each appeal will be considered on its own merits. The general processing time for appeal cases is about 4 weeks, and the outcome of the appeal will be made known to both you and the school.

A Single Journey Visa will be incorporated in the IPA letter if you require visa. You need not apply separately and may enter Singapore with the IPA letter at the Singapore checkpoints.

Access 
ICA SOLAR system
 to complete Step 1 and Step 2 as shown below

Step 1: Acknowledgement and Document Upload
Click “Term & Conditions” to acknowledge the terms & conditions before viewing the required documents for submission.
Medical Examination Report (if applicable)
If the period of your studies is 6 months or more, you may download the ICA prescribed Medical Examination Report after acknowledging the Terms & Conditions
Upon satisfactory completion of the medical examination, you are required to submit the completed medical examination report via SOLAR system for the completion of the Student's Pass formalities with ICA in Singapore, to be issued the digital student’s pass.

Step 2: Make Payment
Once ICA approves the document(s) for Step 1, you will be notified to make payment within 7 days for Step 2.
If payment is not made within the stipulated deadline, ICA will withdraw your application. You will need to repeat the whole application process.

Step 3: Completion of Formalities
You may proceed to make an appointment at 
ICA Services Centre
 (Please make appointment 1 day after STP issuance fee has been paid). Please note that the completion of Student's Pass formalities with ICA is strictly by 
e-appointment 
ONLY
. ICA will 
NOT
 entertain students who walk-in without an e-appointment. This must be done within the validity of your Short-Term Visit Pass or before the expiry date of your IPA letter, whichever is earlier. 
Do note that the IPA letter is not an immigration pass and does not confer any valid stay to the applicant. You are required to attend an additional in-person session after your arrival in Singapore to complete your formalities with ICA to have the Student’s Pass issued.
If the ICA e-appointment date is beyond the validity of your Short-Term Visit Pass, please extend your Short-Term Visit Pass at ICA 
website
.
Important Notes:
1) Singapore Mobile Number
The Singapore mobile number that   can receive SMS, must be ready by the time of the biometrics   enrolment/completion of formalities (COF) of the Student's Pass as a one-time   OTP will be sent to the Singapore mobile number provided. In the event if you   do not have a Singapore mobile number, you can use the Singapore mobile   number of an authorized proxy (e.g., sponsor or parent, etc.), that can   receive SMS, so long as the owner of the mobile number is aware that his/her   mobile number is being used for the retrieval of the digital Student's Pass.   
2) Overstaying
Please be informed that   Overstaying is a punishable offence under the Immigration Act. If you   overstay beyond the validity of your immigration pass, you may be subjected   to a composition fine and prosecution in court.  Application/in-principle   approval of Student’s Pass does not constitute legal extension of stay in   Singapore. 
You must have a valid immigration pass at all times.   

Upon successful completion of formalities (i.e. enrolment of biometrics), students should expect to receive the “Login email” from 
no-reply@file.gov.sg
 which will be sent to their email addresses as provided in their eForm16, not more than 7 working days from the date of enrolment.
