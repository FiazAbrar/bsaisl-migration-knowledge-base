Safeguard Against Scams and Account Misuse

Work Permit Holders can maintain only one DBS/POSB deposit account, which must be a POSB Payroll Account. The account has a daily transaction limit of S$7,000.

Eligibility & Fees

Eligibility

Only work permit holders may apply

Initial deposit

Not required

Fees

  • A S$2 monthly service fee if average daily balance falls below S$500.
    (For MDW Only: Accounts opened via the MOM system (for new or transfer MDWs) or online (for existing MDWs) will enjoy a waiver of service fee).
  • A S$2 monthly ATM withdrawal fee will apply if the customer makes more than 4 ATM cash withdrawals in the month.
  • A S$2 fee will apply for each cash withdrawal of S$2,000 or less, at any DBS/POSB branch.

Important notes

Work Permit Holders can maintain only one DBS/POSB deposit account1, which must be a POSB Payroll Account. The POSB Payroll Account has a daily limit of S$7,000 for outgoing transactions. This limit cannot be increased and is designed to better protect your money from scams and reduce potential losses.

The limit includes:

  • Local and overseas fund transfers
  • ATM fund transfers
  • QR Scan & Pay transactions
  • NETS debit card payments
  • Cash withdrawals
  • GIRO and Standing Instructions
  • Top-ups
  • Transactions funded from your deposit account through PayLah!
  • Investment-related purchases

If you have payments or recurring transactions that may bring you above the S$7,000 daily limit, please plan them in advance.  

There is no limit on incoming transactions. You can continue to receive any amount into your account.

For more information, please refer to our FAQs.

1Applications for other account types are subject to eligibility assessment at our branches.

Suspension of Accounts

Following the cancellation of work permit and departure from Singapore, work permit holders' accounts will be suspended. To avoid disruption, work permit customers are encouraged to ensure all funds are withdrawn, transferred or that the account is closed prior to departure.
For Work Permit HoldersFor Employers
This is to lower the risk of criminal syndicates using work permit holders' (WPHs') banking credentials for unlawful purposes if the accounts remain active after WPHs leave Singapore. As part of the government's efforts to combat scams and money laundering, the Singapore Police Force (SPF), Ministry of Manpower (MOM), and Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) are working with banks to progressively suspend the accounts of former work permit holders who have left Singapore (e.g., cancelled or expired work passes) to prevent account misuse by criminal syndicates.
Your account will be suspended (frozen) when your work permit expires or is cancelled and you have left Singapore. Your account will be suspended (frozen), meaning it will not be closed, and your funds will remain in the account. However, you will be unable to access the account or its balance during the suspension. Your funds remain safe, and you can access them again upon returning to Singapore and reactivating your account.
