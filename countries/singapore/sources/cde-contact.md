# Contact us

![mail-line.svg](https://alep-p-001.sitecorecontenthub.cloud/api/public/content/75b11dd00857466fa8d361df288d0d0c?v=e04e51f0&h=54&w=53&la=en)

### Email address

<contact@cde.org.sg>

For assistance on domestic employment-related issues, please call our **24-hour helpline at 1800 2255 233 (1800-CALL-CDE)** or visit our Facebook page at [www.facebook.com/cde.singapore](https://www.facebook.com/cde.singapore) to drop us a message.

For migrant domestic worker interview-related enquiries, please call us at **6303 6840 or 6303 6841** or drop us an email at [interview@cde.org.sg](mailto:interview@cde.org.sg?subject=MDW%20Interview).

For all media enquiries, please drop us an email at [stratcomms@ntuc.org.sg](mailto:stratcomms@ntuc.org.sg?subject=Contact%20us).

For general matters, such as how to collaborate with us to improve the domestic employees' landscape, please write in to [contact@cde.org.sg](mailto:contact@cde.org.sg?subject=Contact%20us)

 

Thank You.
