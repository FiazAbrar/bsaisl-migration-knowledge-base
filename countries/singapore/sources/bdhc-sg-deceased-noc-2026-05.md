## Bringing Our Migrant Workers Home: NOC & Repatriation Process Explained

The Bangladesh High Commission in Singapore conveys its heartfelt condolences to the bereaved family on the sad demise of their loved one, who had been serving in Singapore as a migrant worker. To arrange the repatriation of the deceased — who held a W-Pass or S-Pass — to Bangladesh, obtaining a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from this mission is a mandatory requirement. On behalf of the Bangladesh Government, this mission, along with the Probashi Kallayan Desk at Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (HSIA), Dhaka, remains committed to extending all possible support to the family during this difficult time. Kindly submit the following documents and follow the steps outlined below (process flowchart) to proceed with the NOC process.

Documents Required:
1. Death Certificate- (Original + 1 Copy)
2. NEA Certificate (National Environment Agency) - (Original + 1 Copy)
3. Embalming Certificate - (Original + 1 Copy)
4. Sealing Coffin Certificate - (Original + 1 Copy)
5. Identity Documents:
   (a) Deceased Passport Copy - (Colour – 2 Copies)
   (b) Work Permit Copy - (Colour – 2 Copies)
   (c) FIN Copy (if any)
6. Company authorization to a Casket Company:
   Please submit an official letter from the deceased’s last working company/organization addressed to Bangladesh High Commission informing the death issue and authorizing of a licensed ‘Casket Company’ to arrange the repatriation.
7. Flight Arrangement: Flight Booking Confirmation (1 Copy)
