# SG Arrival Card with Electronic Health Declaration

Submit your SG Arrival Card and electronic health declaration for free before arriving in Singapore.

Last updated 28 Oct 2026



## Submit the SG Arrival Card with Electronic Health Declaration

You can submit the SG Arrival Card (SGAC) and electronic health declaration via:

- SGAC e-Service 
- MyICA Mobile application \
  Download the official app for free from the [App Store (iOS)](/redirect-links/app-links/myica-app-store) or [Google Play (Android)](/redirect-links/app-links/myica-google-play) for seamless submissions. The app’s profile-saving function can auto-populate your information for future trips to Singapore.  [Learn more about using the MyICA Mobile app on the go](/myica-mobile-application).

## Who Needs to Submit

**All travellers** must submit the SGAC with electronic health declaration before arriving in Singapore, except:

- Travellers transiting or transferring through Singapore without clearing immigration
- Residents (Singapore Citizens (SCs), Permanent Residents (PRs), Long-Term Pass holders) travelling through Woodlands and Tuas Checkpoints



Note

Long-Term Pass holders include holders of Student’s Pass, Dependant’s Pass, Long-Term Visit Pass and Work Pass.

As part of the SGAC submission, you must submit the electronic health declaration to guard against the importation of diseases of concern and to protect the health of Singapore residents. Those who make false declarations may face prosecution under the Infectious Diseases Act.



## When to Submit

You must submit the SGAC **within 3 days** (including the day of arrival) **before you arrive in Singapore**. For example, if you are arriving in Singapore on 30 June, you can submit your declaration from 28 June onwards. This avoids unnecessary delays during immigration clearance.

If there is a change in your health status after you have submitted your declaration, you must update it using the "Update SGAC" feature on the SGAC e-Service. It is an offence to submit a false declaration.



## At the Immigration Counter

After submitting your SGAC, you only need to present your travel document to the ICA officer at the immigration counter when you arrive.
