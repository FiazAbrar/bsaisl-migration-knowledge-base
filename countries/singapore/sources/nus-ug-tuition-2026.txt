Notes for Students:

1. The cohort-based fee system was introduced for the new undergraduate intake of all
     College/Faculties/Schools from AY2008/2009. Under this fee system, tuition fees for an intake cohort of
     students will be based on the fees for that cohort throughout the student's entire candidature. While fees
     continue to be reviewed annually and adjustments made as and when necessary, with each new cohort
     of students, each new cohort will know the exact amount of tuition fees they have to pay per year
     throughout their entire candidature. This enables students to plan better for the financing of their
     undergraduate studies.

2. The per annum fee amount, covering Semesters 1 and 2 of an Academic Year (AY), is payable in two
     equal instalments at the start of each semester. Semestral fees are payable as long as a student's
     candidature remains active.

Students who read courses in the Special Term will pay a per-course fee amount. Students who register
for course(s) in the Special Term immediately following the expiry of their normal candidature period will
continue to pay subsidised fees on a per course basis. However, for the Special Terms beyond the normal
candidature period, the Special Term fees payable will be the full non-subsidised semestral fees pro-rated
based on the number of Units read. The related policy on Tuition Fee beyond Normal Candidature can be
found here.

3. Students who are not in receipt of the Tuition Grant will have to pay the non-subsidised fee amounts as
     quoted here (which are inclusive of the prevailing GST). The prevailing GST is 9% with effect from 1st

     January 2024.

4. For the time being, College/Faculties/Schools that are not on a modular curriculum structure (esp.
     Medicine and Dentistry) do not offer Special Term courses.

5. SkillsFuture Mid-Career Enhanced Subsidy:
     Students who are Singapore Citizens, aged 40 and above (based on the year which the student turns 40
     years old) and enrolled in MOE-subsidised full-time undergraduate programmes may be eligible for higher
     subsidy under the SkillsFuture Mid-Career Enhanced Subsidy. The fees payable will be 60% lower than
     the standard subsidised fees payable by other Singaporean students. The amount of fees payable will be
     reflected in their individual student bill. Students who are not eligible for MOE Tuition Grant are also not
     eligible for the SkillsFuture Mid-Career Enhanced Subsidy. This subsidy does not apply to courses read
     in the Special Term.

For more information regarding Skills Future Credits, please refer to http://www.myskillsfuture.gov.sg/.

6. The Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) comprises its Member States. For more
     information, please refer to https://asean.org/member-states/.

7. With effect from 1st July 2021, the Department of Real Estate, which was previously under School of
     Design and Environment has moved to NUS Business School.

8. With effect from AY2024, the Bachelor of Science (Real Estate) programme is renamed as Bachelor of
     Business Administration (Real Estate) under NUS Business School. Students currently enrolled in the
     Bachelor of Science (Real Estate) programme from the AY2023 intake who successfully transition to the
     new Bachelor of Business Administration (Real Estate) programme, as well as returning National
     Servicemen who accepted offer to enroll in the Bachelor of Science (Real Estate) programme prior to
     AY2023 and will join the common-intake Bachelor of Business Administration programme in AY2024 and
     thereafter, will pay the prevailing fees for the Real Estate major in the year of their acceptance of offer.
