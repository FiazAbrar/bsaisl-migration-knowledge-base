# Bangladesh High Commission Singapore www.singapore.mofa.gov.bd

No.:19.01.6501.103.36.051.25
18 April 2025

## NOTICE

Considering the ever-expanding expatriate Community in Singapore, the Government of Bangladesh has taken a milestone decision to waive the attestation of In Principle Approval (IPA) from now onward (vide Ministry of Expatriate Welfare memo no. 49.00.0000.048.11.017.21.119 dated 17 April 2025).

Therefore, with effect from 18 April 2025, there will be no requirement of IPA attestation by the High Commission. We hope it will further ease entry of our manpower in the human resource market of Singapore.

The High Commission has the honour and pleasure to share this long waited information with the esteemed employers and community at large.

Sheikh Md. Mostafa Ala Min
Second Secretary & Head of Chancery
