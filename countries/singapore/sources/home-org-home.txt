HOME and the migrant workers we support were recently featured in this CNA news story. Over the past three years, we have seen a significant rise in migrant workers from Myanmar seeking assistance after being deceived by recruitment agents and employers about the jobs they would be doing in Singapore.
We hope greater awareness of these cases will lead to stronger safeguards in Singapore’s work pass system, and more serious efforts to address the recruitment debt and deceptive practices that continue to place migrant workers at risk.

HOME in The News

How HOME Helps
Direct assistance, shelter and food for domestic workers who experience abuse and exploitation.
Employment advice and legal aid for migrant workers in Singapore, and when they have returned to their countries of origin.
Skills training such as English language, computer, baking, cooking, hairdressing and caregiving courses for domestic workers.
Advocacy and public education on issues affecting migrant workers.
