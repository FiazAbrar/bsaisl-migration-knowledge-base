## Features of the pass

The key features of the pass are as follows:

- Flexibility to hold a job in any sector. However, the PEP does not exempt you from complying with registration requirements to practise in Singapore for professions such as medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, architecture, law, etc.
- You do not need to reapply for a pass if you change jobs.
- You can stay in Singapore for a continuous period of up to 6 months without a job to search for new employment.
- Availability of [passes for eligible family members](/passes-and-permits/personalised-employment-pass/eligibility#passes-for-family-members).
- No foreign worker levy or quota required.

**Note:**

- If your PEP is expiring, you will need to get an [Employment Pass](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass) or [S Pass](/passes-and-permits/s-pass) to continue working in Singapore. You do not have to cancel the existing PEP for your employer to apply for the new pass.
- You may be required to pay [income tax](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/new-to-tax/working-out-your-tax-residency).

## Pass map

Overview of what you need to do before, during and after you apply for a Personalised Employment Pass.

## Before applying

- ### 

  - ![know requirements](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/know-requirements.png)
    Know the [eligibility and requirements](/passes-and-permits/personalised-employment-pass/eligibility) to apply.
  - ![know requirements](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/know-requirements.png)
    Find out the [documents required](/passes-and-permits/personalised-employment-pass/documents-required) for applying.

## Before arrival

- ### 

  - ![Apply](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/apply.png)
    [Apply for the Personalised Employment Pass](/passes-and-permits/personalised-employment-pass/apply-for-a-pass) and wait for the outcome.
  - ![Apply](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/apply.png)
    (If your pass is approved) Get an in-principle approval letter.
  - ![medical](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/medical.png)
    Ensure that you comply with the latest [travel requirements](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore).

## Upon arrival

- ### 

  - ![issuance](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/inssuance.png)
    [Get the pass issued](/passes-and-permits/personalised-employment-pass/apply-for-a-pass#get-the-pass-issued) and receive a notification letter stating whether registration is required.\
     
  - ![register fingerprints](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/register-fingerprints.png)
    (If required) Make an appointment to [register your fingerprints and photo](/passes-and-permits/personalised-employment-pass/apply-for-a-pass#if-required-register-fingerprints-and-photo).
  - ![receive card](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/receive-card.png)
    [Receive your pass card](/passes-and-permits/personalised-employment-pass/apply-for-a-pass#receive-the-card) at the address provided 5 working days after registration or document verification.

## When required

- ### 

  - ![notify mom](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/notify-mom.png)
    [Notify MOM](/passes-and-permits/personalised-employment-pass/notify-mom-of-changes) about your annual income by 31 January of the next year. You should also notify MOM about any changes to your employment or personal information.
  - ![replace card](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/replace-card.png)
    [Apply for a replacement](/passes-and-permits/personalised-employment-pass/replace-a-pass-card) if your pass card is lost, stolen or damaged.
  - ![cancel pass or permit](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/cancel-pass-or-permit.png)
    [Cancel your pass](/passes-and-permits/personalised-employment-pass/cancel-a-pass) if you no longer require it.
