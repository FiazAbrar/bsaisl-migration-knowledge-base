Entry Requirements

For all travellers including Singapore residents.

Overview

Answer a few questions to find out about the entry requirements and documents you may need for your travel to Singapore. Based on your responses, an initial overview of the relevant requirements and links to more detailed guidance will be displayed.

This tool provides general guidance only. Entry into Singapore is subject to prevailing immigration requirements and ICA’s decision at the point of entry.
