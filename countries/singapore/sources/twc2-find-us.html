The building known as “The Bencoolen” can be confusing to visitors because there are three parts to it: the shopping mall, the residential tower and the office tower. Please use the office tower entrance which faces Bencoolen Street directly and which is just 50m from Ibis Hotel.

OFFICE HOURS

Our office is open from 9:30am to 6:00pm Monday to Friday except public holidays. However, we have a hybrid work-from-home arrangement and not all staff are in on all days. It is therefore advisable to email or WhatsApp us in advance to check that the right person you wish to meet will be in the office on a particular day.
