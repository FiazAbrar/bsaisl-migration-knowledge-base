# Employment agencies and personnel search (EA Directory)

You can use this service to find employment agencies that match your needs, check the agencies' service quality and performance, and shortlist and compare different employment agencies.

[Search for employment agencies and personnel](https://service2.mom.gov.sg/eadirectory/)

Service is online

**Availability**\
24 hours\

MOM officers will **never** ask you to transfer money or disclose bank log-in details over a phone call or email. Find out more on how to [transact safely and securely](/transact-safely-and-securely). Call the 24/7 ScamShield Helpline on [1799](tel:1799) if you are unsure if something is a scam.

## How do I search for employment agencies?

Show

Download the [user guide](/-/media/mom/documents/foreign-manpower/employment-agencies/ea-personnel-search-user-guide-web.pdf).

## What types of employment agencies can I search for?

Show

You can search for employment agencies for:

- Foreign workers
- Foreign domestic workers
- Local workers (Singaporeans / PRs)

## Which employment agencies should I avoid?

Show

You can find the list of EAs to avoid at the **“Under revocation / suspension / surveillance”** tab in EA Directory.
