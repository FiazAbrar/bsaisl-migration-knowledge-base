Levy concession for a migrant domestic worker (MDW)

As an MDW employer, you can get a concessionary rate of $60 per month if you meet the qualifying criteria.

Who can qualify

As an employer, you can qualify for levy concession if there is an eligible person living in your household.

Levy concession can only be granted for 1 helper per eligible person and is capped at 2 helpers per household. To qualify for levy concession for a second helper, there must be 2 eligible persons in your household.
Find out the qualifying criteria and what you need to do to enjoy levy concession for each of the eligible persons below:

  • Is certified by a Singapore-registered doctor as requiring help with at least 1 activity of daily living (ADL), such as washing, feeding, dressing, moving or walking around, transferring from bed to chair and vice-versa, or toileting.

When all the conditions are met:

  • Apply with Agency for Integrated Care (AIC) and receive a recommendation letter from them.

 

When will the levy concession be reflected in your bill

When you have met the qualifying criteria and completed the required steps, levy concession will be automatically granted. You will see the following changes within your next 2 levy bills:

  • The new, lower levy rate.
  • Levy adjustments backdated to the date you completed the required steps.

Check why you were not granted levy concession

If you were not automatically granted the levy concession, you can submit a form to check why.

Eligible person Form
Child Check why levy concession for a child was not granted
Elderly or PWD Check why levy concession for an elderly or PWD was not granted

After completing the form, we will email you the outcome and next steps within 2 working days.

Home Caregiving Grant

If you hire a helper to take care of a family member who needs permanent assistance with 3 or more ADLs, you may qualify for the Home Caregiving Grant, a monthly grant of up to $400. For more information on the criteria and how to apply, you can:
  • Visit AIC’s website.
  • Call AIC Hotline 1800 650 6060.
