# Visa Free Transit Facility

Check the eligibility and entry requirements for the 96-hour Visa Free Transit Facility when travelling through Singapore.

Last updated 28 Oct 2026



## Overview of Eligible Nationals

If you are transiting to or from a third country via Singapore, you may be eligible for the 96-hour Visa Free Transit Facility (VFTF) if you are a national of:

- The Commonwealth of Independent States (CIS) (Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Moldova, Russia, Tajikistan and Uzbekistan)
- Georgia
- India
- Turkmenistan
- Ukraine

It is also available to holders of the following documents (excluding ordinary, diplomatic, public affairs or service PRC passport holders):

- People's Republic of China (PRC) Travel Document
- Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (SAR) Document of Identity (DOI)
- Macau SAR Travel Permit

Learn more about the eligibility requirements below.



Note

Meeting the VFTF criteria does not guarantee entry into Singapore. You must also meet prevailing entry requirements. ICA officers will assess and determine your entry at the checkpoint. You cannot extend your stay under the VFTF.



## Nationals of India and Eligible PRC, Hong Kong SAR and Macau SAR Document Holders

If you are transiting to or from a third country via Singapore, you may be eligible for the 96-hour VFTF if you meet these requirements:\

- **Transport and onward ticket**\
  You may enter Singapore by any mode of transport but depart only via air or sea. You must have a valid onward air, ferry, or cruise ticket departing Singapore within 96 hours.\
- **Valid visa or long-term pass (Applicable to Indian nationals only)** \
  You must have a valid visa or long-term pass that is valid for at least 1 month from your date of entry into Singapore. It must be issued by, or valid for entry into, one of the following countries:\
  \
  \- Australia\
  \- Canada\
  \- Germany\
  \- Japan\
  \- New Zealand\
  \- Switzerland\
  \- United Kingdom\
  \- United States of America

**Travelling on a Single Journey Visa**

If you have a Single Journey Visa (SJV) issued by one of the countries listed above, you may be granted VFTF on your return journey (even if the SJV has been used and is no longer valid), provided you:

- Travel directly from the country that issued the SJV, en route through Singapore, back to your home country
- Have not returned to your home country since you last used the SJV



## Nationals of the CIS, Georgia, Turkmenistan and Ukraine

If you are transiting to or from a third country via Singapore, you may be eligible for the 96-hour VFTF if you meet these requirements:\

- **Transport and onward ticket**\
  You may enter Singapore by any mode of transport but depart by air. You must have a valid onward air ticket departing Singapore within 96 hours.\
