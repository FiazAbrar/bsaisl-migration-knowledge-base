# How do I check on the visa application status?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

![Immigration & Checkpoints Authority logo](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Flogos.ask.gov.sg%2Ficalogo.png&w=3840&q=75)

Updated by ICA 1y ago

The processing time of a visa application is within 3 working days (excluding the day of submission). However, some applications may take a longer time to process. You may use the [ICA e-Service](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/esvclandingpage/save) to enquire your application status. Do note that you will require the visa reference number and applicant's travel document number.
