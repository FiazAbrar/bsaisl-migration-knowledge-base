# Which Country Should I Choose A Maid From?

-  May 08, 2021
-  [Ray Tay](https://searchmaid.com.sg/author/ray)

![which-country-should-i-choose-a-maid-from](https://searchmaid.com.sg/storage/posts_banner/which-country-should-i-choose-a-maid-from_AlAUmBAUbvAxjG2EuOpdIAAusG14gMy55kEjGKEx.jpg)

At a Glance

- MOM approves 13 source countries or regions for helpers: Bangladesh, Cambodia, Hong Kong, India, Indonesia, Macau, Malaysia, Myanmar, Philippines, South Korea, Sri Lanka, Taiwan and Thailand. Pakistan is not on the list.
- Most Singapore families hire from the Philippines, Indonesia, Myanmar or India.
- Pick on factors that matter to your home: language, experience, diet and religion, cost and paperwork.
- Some embassies set their own minimum salary. The Philippines has the highest floor in 2026.
- Every helper must be female, aged 23 to below 50 at application, with at least 8 years of formal education.

Choosing a helper's country is really choosing a set of practical trade-offs. Nationality tells you about language, paperwork and typical salary. It tells you nothing about one person's character. Interview the person, then use this guide for the rest.

## Which countries can I hire a maid from in Singapore?

MOM sets the rules. A helper needs to meet all of these to get a Work Permit:

|  |  |
|:---|:---|
| Requirement | MOM rule |
| Sex | Female |
| Age | 23 to below 50 at application. Helpers aged 50 and above can only renew until 60. |
| Source country or region | Bangladesh, Cambodia, Hong Kong, India, Indonesia, Macau, Malaysia, Myanmar, Philippines, South Korea, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand |
| Education | Minimum 8 years of formal education with a recognised certificate |

Swipe to see more. Source: Ministry of Manpower, MDW eligibility page (checked Oct 2026).

## What should I compare when choosing a helper's country?

Language

Who will she talk to most? Kids doing homework, a grandparent who speaks Malay, or you?

Experience

Ex-Singapore and transfer helpers know local homes already. First-timers cost less but need more training.

Religion and diet

Ask directly about handling pork, fasting months and rest-day worship. Fit matters both ways.

Cost and paperwork

Embassy salary floors and extra steps (like Philippine embassy verification) change your budget and timeline.

## How do Filipino, Indonesian, Myanmar and Indian helpers compare?

|  |  |  |  |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| Country | Language | Indicative salary (2026) | Things to check |
| Philippines | English is widely used in schooling | From S\$650 (Philippine minimum) | Embassy contract verification and Philippine-side clearance add time |
| Indonesia | Bahasa Indonesia, close to Malay. English varies | About S\$600 to S\$650 expected minimum | Ask your agency for the current embassy rules before you commit |
| Myanmar | Burmese. English varies, often basic for first-timers | About S\$550 to S\$750 | Departure paperwork in Myanmar can shift. Ask the agency about current timelines |
| India | Varies by state. Many speak some English or Hindi | Agency quotes vary. Confirm with your agency | Smaller pool, so fewer candidates to choose from |

Swipe to see more. Salary ranges are indicative only, gathered Oct 2026 from filipinos.sg (Philippine S\$650 minimum), HelperChoice (Indonesia) and Upwill (Myanmar). Experience and duties push pay up. Always confirm with your agency.

## Do embassies set a minimum salary for maids in Singapore?

- **Singapore:** no legal minimum wage for helpers. You agree salary in the contract.
- **Philippines:** the DMW set the minimum for Filipino household workers in Singapore at S\$650 a month.
- **Indonesia:** expected minimum is reported at about S\$600 to S\$650 in 2026.
- **Myanmar:** we could not confirm a current published embassy minimum. Ask your agency.
