                                      Singapore Visa Application (Dhaka)
                                    Frequently Answered Questions (FAQ)

Q: What are the requirements for Bangladeshi to apply for Singapore Visa?
A: Documents required to apply for Singapore Visa can be found on https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-
transit-depart/entering-singapore/visa_requirements/visa-detail-page/bangladesh Bangladesh
holding diplomatic, official and service passports do not require a visa for entry.

Q: How can I apply for Singapore Visa in Bangladesh?
A: Applicants may submit the applications through Singapore Authorised Visa Agents located in
Dhaka. The contacts can be found on https://www.mfa.gov.sg/Overseas-
Mission/Dhaka/Consular-Services/Overview-visa-information/visa-information

Q: What can I do if my visa application is still pending after 5 working days?
A: Applicant may approach the AVA for assistance. Singapore High Commission will consider the
application on case-by-case basis.

Q: Why was my visa application been rejected?
A: Singapore High Commission will not reveal the reason for rejection. Applicant may submit the
appeal for reassessment through the AVA. Singapore High Commission will consider the
application on case-by-case basis.

Q: Why am I issued with Double or Single Journey Visa (DJV/SJV) when I applied for Multiple
Journey Visa (MJV)?
A: Singapore High Commission follows the set of requirements matrix and applications are
assessed on case-by-case basis. Applicant will not be guaranteed with the MJV applied for.

Q: Can I apply for medical visa or business visa with tourism agency letter of invitation (LOI)?
A: No. Medical visa will require LOI from the medical industry and business visa will require LOI
from Singapore business company.

Q: Who can I contact pertaining to my visa applications?
A: Applicant may contact the Singapore Authorised Visa Agents for assistance.
