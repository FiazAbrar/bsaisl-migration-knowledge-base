Student's Pass Application/Renewal

Check if you need a Student's Pass to study full-time in Singapore and select your educational institution to read its specific requirements before applying or renewing. 

Last updated 29 Oct 2026



                Requirements 

If you are a foreigner, you must apply for or renew a Student's Pass to pursue full-time studies in Singapore. 
Before you apply or renew, you must meet the following pre-requisites:
Approved courses: 
You must be accepted into an approved full-time course. You cannot apply for a Student's Pass for part-time, evening, or weekend courses.
Vaccination requirements:
 If you are a foreign-born applicant aged 12 and below, you must submit your vaccination information to the Communicable Diseases Agency (CDA). Visit the  
CDA website
 for the details.



                Exemptions

You do not need a Student's Pass if you hold a valid pass or exemption order and meet the conditions listed in the table below:
Pass Type or Exemption Order   
Conditions for studying without a Student’s Pass   
Dependant's Pass (DP)
Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP)
Immigration Exemption Order (IEO)   

You are pursuing full-time, non-religious studies.

Note: If your pass or IEO expires or is cancelled   during your studies, you must apply for a Student's Pass to continue   studying.   
Short-Term Visit Pass (STVP)   
You are attending a short-term course that   meets all the following criteria:
It is completed within 30 days or within your STVP validity, whichever is   shorter.
It is a complete and stand-alone module (courses with multiple modules   require a Student's Pass).
It does not include practical occupational training or industrial   attachments involving walk-in customers.
It is not conducted at premises that are also places of business (such as a   hair or beauty salon).   



                Procedure

Application requirements and procedures depend on the type of educational institution you intend to study at. Select the education institution below for the specific requirements and apply for or renew a Student’s Pass:
Approved Private Education Institutions
Foreign System Schools and Privately-Funded Schools
Government/Government-Aided/Independent Schools
Institutes of Technical Education
Kindergartens and Childcare Centres
Institutes of Higher Learning



Note

If you want to transfer to another school after your application is approved but before you collect your Student's Pass, you must submit a new application.
