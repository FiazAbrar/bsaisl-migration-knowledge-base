Overview

You can use this e-Service to submit and track your citizenship application online if you fall under an eligible category. You may also sponsor applications for your spouse, children, or aged parents. 

Application Process

  1. Check eligibility
  2. Check National Service obligation
  3. Prepare required documents
  4. Apply online
  5. Make payment
  6. Check processing time
  7. Check application status
  8. Complete formalities


ICA considers factors such as the individual's family ties to Singaporeans, economic contributions, qualifications, age, family profile, and length of residency, to assess the applicant's ability to contribute to Singapore, integrate into our society, and their commitment to sinking roots.

ICA has no affiliation with any other external migration agency/commercial entity that claims to be a Singapore immigration specialist/consultant or partner. ICA does not support or endorse services by self-proclaimed professional immigration consultancy service providers for Singapore citizenship applications.



National Service Obligation

Under the Enlistment Act 1970, all male Singapore citizens and permanent residents, must serve National Service (NS) unless exempted. This includes all male applicants who are granted Singapore citizenship or Singapore permanent residence status as a foreign student or under their parents’ sponsorship.

All male applicants must access www.cmpb.gov.sg for information on NS before proceeding with this application.

Enlistment and Deferment Conditions for Male Applicants

If you are required to serve NS:

  • Registration and Enlistment: You must register for NS upon reaching 16½ years old. You will be scheduled for enlistment into full-time NS at the earliest opportunity upon reaching 18 years old.
  • Pre-Tertiary Studies: MINDEF allows students to attain educational qualifications up to the ‘A’ Levels, polytechnic diploma, or an equivalent pre-tertiary qualification before serving NS. You will be enlisted at the earliest opportunity after finishing pre-tertiary education, or after turning 18, whichever is later.
  • University Studies: MINDEF does not grant deferment for university studies, regardless of whether such studies have begun. If you are granted citizenship while in university, you must disrupt your studies to enlist if you cannot complete them before your enlistment date.
  • Operationally Ready NS: Following the completion of full-time NS, you will become an Operationally Ready National Serviceman (NSman). Your obligation will end at age 50 (for Officers) or 40 (for other ranks).

Consequences of Defaulting      

Failing to discharge your NS liabilities carries severe legal and immigration consequences:

Consequences Details
Legal penalties

Defaulting on NS obligations is an offence under the Enlistment Act 1970. Penalties include a fine of up to $10,000, imprisonment of up to 3 years, or both.

Impact on immigration and family

Renouncing or losing Singapore citizenship or permanent residency without completing full-time NS will impact your current and future applications for:

  • Work or study in Singapore
  • Long-term immigration facilities

Doing so will also adversely affect your family members’ ability to:

  • Apply for or sponsor long-term immigration facilities
  • Renew their Re-Entry Permits
  • Apply for Singapore citizenship or permanent residency

The Government may also withhold citizenship renunciation applications from male SCs who have not discharged their NS liabilities.

 For further enquiries, please contact the Central Manpower Base at 1800-3676767 (local) or 65-65676767 (overseas) or email [email protected].

Documents Required

Please refer to the checklist on the documents required(PDF, 97KB) for your application.

Note

You will need to provide original documents and official translations (for documents in languages other than English).

ICA accepts:

  • Translations provided by the embassy of the country/place that issued the document
  • Translations produced by a notary public in Singapore or the country/place that issued the document
  • Privately created translations attested by the embassy of the country/place that issued the document, or notarised by a notary public in Singapore or the country/place that issued the document
ICA does not endorse any private translation companies or entities. Insufficient documentation may result in non-acceptance of your application. You may be required to provide additional documents to support your application.


Procedure

All applicants and sponsors must log in with Singpass. If you are ineligible for Singpass, use the unique login credentials provided by ICA. If you do not have a Singpass account, apply for one before starting your citizenship application. 



With a Local Sponsor

Your sponsor must log in to complete the form on your behalf. Before your sponsor can submit the application, you must log in to review the form and make your declarations.



Without a Local Sponsor

You must log in to complete the form, make your declarations, and submit the application.

If you are aged below 15, apply using the non-Singpass option.



Processing Time

ICA generally processes applications within 12 months. For children born overseas to SCs, the application will be processed within 1 month, provided you submit all required documents in order. Some applications may take longer to process. 



Application Status

You will receive your application outcome via email. You can also check your citizenship application status by enquiring below.

Check Status
(opens in a new tab)


Completion of Formalities

Once approved, you will receive an In-Principle Approval (IPA) letter. If you are aged 16 to 60, you must complete the mandatory Singapore Citizenship Journey (SCJ) programme before completing the formalities for the issuance of your Identity Card.

FAQs

Please refer to the Singapore Citizenship Roadmap(PDF, 139KB) on ICA website for the requirements at each stage of the citizenship application. The main applicant/ sponsor can log in to view the final instructions.

Please address the letter to the ICA Services Centre. The letter must be dated within 3 months of your application date and state your:

  • Occupation
  • Date of employment
  • Basic and gross monthly salary

For Singpass users, please log in to the ICA e-Service and select 'Update information' within your case summary page to provide the update. 

For Non-Singpass users, please log in the e-Service with your submitted application Reference ID and select 'Update information' within your case summary page to provide the update.

If the document(s) you wish to submit is not listed under ‘Update Information’, please contact us via the enquiry form and upload the latest documents reflecting the changes.
