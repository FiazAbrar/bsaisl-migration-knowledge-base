# What is the validity period of a Singapore Visa?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

![Immigration & Checkpoints Authority logo](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Flogos.ask.gov.sg%2Ficalogo.png&w=3840&q=75)

Each visa application, including the duration of the visa granted, is considered on its own merits and subject to approval.

Applicants who are applying for a visa via our [e-Service](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/esvclandingpage/save) are advised to submit the application about 30 days prior to the intended arrival into Singapore so that the visa validity will be sufficient for the visit.
