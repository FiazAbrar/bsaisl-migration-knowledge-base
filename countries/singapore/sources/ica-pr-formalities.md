# Completion of Formalities for Permanent Residence

Complete the required formalities after receiving your In-Principle Approval for permanent residence application to officially become a Singapore Permanent Resident.

Last updated 06 Nov 2026



## Overview

Once your Singapore permanent residence application receives In-Principle Approval (IPA), you must log in to the ICA e-Services to complete the required tasks. After you have completed all necessary formalities, ICA will officially grant your permanent residence status. 



Note

- IPA is not an immigration pass. It does not grant you a legal stay in Singapore. Overstaying is an offence. You must have a valid immigration pass to remain in Singapore while completing these formalities.



## Fees

Once ICA has processed your submissions, you will receive a notification to pay the issuance fees online.

- S\$20 for Entry Permit
- S\$50 for 5-year Re-Entry Permit
- S\$50 for Singapore Identity Card

You can pay online using any of the following:

- Visa or MasterCard credit/debit card
- American Express (AMEX) credit card
- Internet Direct Debit (DBS/POSB, OCBC, UOB and Standard Chartered Bank Internet Banking accounts in Singapore)
- PayNow\



## Procedure

**Complete All Required Tasks**

When you receive the IPA, log in to the e-Service for [Singpass holder](/redirect-links/myica) and [non-Singpass holder](/redirect-links/e-services/e-services--ipses) to complete the required tasks before the approval expires.

Before you proceed:

- Ensure all entries are accurate. Errors may affect the processing of your application and the issuance of your official documents.
- If you need to upload a photograph, ensure it meets ICA's photo specifications. **Avoid using filters or heavy retouching as this can affect facial recognition**. Refer to ICA’s [Photo Guidelines](/photo-guidelines) for more information on the requirements.

**Pay Issuance Fees**

Once ICA has processed your submissions, you will receive a notification to pay the issuance fees. Make your online payment by the deadline to avoid having your application withdrawn.

**Complete Formalities**

After you have successfully made your payment, you can download a letter indicating whether an in-person visit to the ICA Services Centre is required to complete your formalities.

- **If an in-person visit is required:** Your appointment date and time will be included in the letter. If you need to reschedule, you can do so via [e-Appointment](/redirect-links/online-appointment---check-status).
- **If an in-person visit is not required:** ICA will issue your Entry Permit and Re-Entry Permit, upon successful payment. You will receive an email with a link to download them via the e-Service for your retention.\

You will receive a separate notification when your Identity Card is ready for collection.
