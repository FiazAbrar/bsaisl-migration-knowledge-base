# Who are the authorised visa agents?

You are advised to liaise directly with the nearest Singapore Overseas Mission for information of their authorised visa agents, if any. For locations of the Singapore overseas missions, please visit the [Ministry of Foreign Affairs' website](https://www.mfa.gov.sg/Overseas-Missions) under "Overseas Missions".
