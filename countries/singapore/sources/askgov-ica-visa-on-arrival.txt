Can I apply for visa if my passport is less than 6 months?
Updated by 
ICA

1y ago
36
 found this helpful
You will not be able to apply for visa if your passport has less than 6 months' validity. You will require a passport that is valid for at least 6 months from the date of arrival in Singapore as part of the 
entry requirements
.
