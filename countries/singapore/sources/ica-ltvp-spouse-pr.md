## Overview

If you are a foreigner married to a Singapore Permanent Resident (PR), you must obtain a Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP) to reside in Singapore long-term. Foreign spouses who wish to work in Singapore must obtain the relevant work authorisation.

## Eligibility

To apply, your PR spouse must be your local sponsor.



## Documents Required

Refer to the [checklist on the documents required](/docs/ica/ica/ipses/ltvp-document-checklist.pdf?sfvrsn=95400065_0)(PDF, 88KB) for your application.



Note

You will need to provide original documents and official translations (for documents in languages other than English).\

ICA accepts:

- Translations provided by the embassy of the country/place that issued the document
- Translations produced by a notary public in Singapore or the country/place that issued the document
- Privately created translations attested by the embassy of the country/place that issued the document, or notarised by a notary public in Singapore or the country/place that issued the document\

ICA does not endorse any private translation companies or entities. Insufficient documentation may result in non-acceptance of your application. You may be required to provide additional documents to support your application.\



## Procedure

Your PR spouse must log in with Singpass to sponsor your application.

If you are unable to complete or submit your LTVP or LTVP renewal application due to unique circumstances, you may [contact us via the enquiry form](/redirect-links/feedback/formsg--ltvp-enquiry) for further assistance.

## Fees

[TABLE]

All fees are non-refundable.

You can pay online using any of the following:

- Visa or MasterCard credit/debit card
- American Express (AMEX) credit card
- Internet Direct Debit (DBS/POSB, OCBC, UOB and Standard Chartered Bank Internet Banking accounts in Singapore)
- PayNow\

Successful applicants will be notified of other applicable fees subsequently.



## Processing Time

ICA processes applications within **6 weeks**, provided you submit all the required documents. Some applications may take longer to process.



## Application Status

You will be informed of the outcome of the LTVP application via email. You can also log in to check the LTVP application status online or via [MyICA](/redirect-links/myica) portal.

## Completion of Formalities

Once approved, you will receive an In-Principle Approval (IPA) letter and must [complete the formalities for the issuance of your LTVP](/information-and-e-services/long-term-visit-and-study/long-term-visit-pass/completion-of-formalities-and-issuance-of-long-term-visit-pass).\

## FAQs

You can apply for a Letter of Consent to work in Singapore if you are an LTVP or LTVP+ holder who is the spouse or unmarried child of a Singapore Citizen or Singapore Permanent Resident. Please refer to [MOM](/redirect-links/mom-website-newborn) for further information.

If you are unable to submit your LTVP renewal application due to unique circumstances, you can [submit a request](/redirect-links/feedback/formsg--ltvp-enquiry) for further assistance.

There is no need for the applicant to be physically present in Singapore while the application is being processed. However, please note that you and/or your sponsor may be required to attend an interview or submit additional documents during the processing of LTVP.
