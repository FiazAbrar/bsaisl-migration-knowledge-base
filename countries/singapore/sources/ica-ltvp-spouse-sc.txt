            Overview

Singapore Citizens (SCs) may sponsor their foreign spouse for long-term residence through the Spouse of an SC scheme, which provides structured pathways for family reunification. If you are a foreign spouse, you must obtain a Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP) under this scheme to reside in Singapore long-term.
Before marriage: 
You and your SC partner are strongly encouraged to complete the free online 
Pre-Marriage LTVP Assessment (PMLA)
 to check your chances of getting an LTVP (as marriage does not guarantee LTVP approval). Eligible couples will receive a Letter of LTVP Eligibility (LLE) valid for 1 year. Submitting this LLE with your LTVP application after marriage shortens the processing time from 6 months to just 6 weeks.

            Eligibility

To apply, you must meet the following requirements:
Local Sponsor: 
You must be legally married to an SC. Your SC spouse must act as your local sponsor for the application.
Work Permit Holders:
 If you are an existing or former Work Permit holder (including Training Work Permit), you must obtain approval from the Controller of Work Passes before marrying an SC or PR in Singapore or overseas, regardless the type of marriage (civil, religious or customary). Failure to do so may result in the withdrawal of your work privileges and you may be prevented from entering Singapore for a period of time. Visit the 
Ministry of Manpower (MOM) website
 for details.

Note

You will need to provide original documents and official translations (for documents in languages other than English).
ICA accepts:
Translations provided by the embassy of the country/place that issued the document
Translations produced by a notary public in Singapore or the country/place that issued the document
Privately created translations attested by the embassy of the country/place that issued the document, or notarised by a notary public in Singapore or the country/place that issued the document

ICA does not endorse any private translation companies or entities. Insufficient documentation may result in non-acceptance of your application. You may be required to provide additional documents to support your application.



            Procedure

You may apply for an LTVP after marriage. Your SC spouse must log in with Singpass to sponsor your application.
If you are unable to complete or submit your LTVP or LTVP renewal application due to unique circumstances, you may 
contact us via the enquiry form
 for further assistance.

            Processing Time

ICA processes applications within 
6 months
, provided you submit all the required documents. If you completed the PMLA before applying, the processing time is shortened to 
6 weeks
. Some applications may take longer.

            FAQs

You can apply for a Letter of Consent to work in Singapore if you are an LTVP or LTVP+ holder who is the spouse or unmarried child of a Singapore Citizen or Singapore Permanent Resident. Please refer to 
MOM
 for further information.

If you are unable to submit your LTVP renewal application due to unique circumstances, you can 
submit a request
 for further assistance.

Foreign spouses of Singapore Citizens may be eligible for LTVP+ if the foreign spouse has at least 1 child who is a Singapore Citizen from the marriage; or is pregnant. 
Other consideration factors may include the marriage duration, ability of the sponsor to financially support the family, and good conduct of both the sponsor and the applicant. In general, applicants may be considered more favourably for LTVP+ if they have been married for at least 3 years.  

An LTVP+ provides the holder with a greater certainty of stay and a longer period of residency in Singapore.
