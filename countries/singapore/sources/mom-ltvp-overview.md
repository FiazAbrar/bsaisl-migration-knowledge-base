# Long-Term Visit Pass

The Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP) is for a common-law spouse, step-child or handicapped child of an Employment Pass or S Pass holder. Pass holders earning over \$12,000 can also get a pass for parents. Find out about the pass and how to apply. For Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass holder, you can also [apply for a Long-Term Visit Pass](/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families/long-term-visit-pass-for-overseas-networks-expertise-pass).
