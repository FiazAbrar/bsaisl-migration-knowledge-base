# How can I appeal if my application for a Student's Pass is rejected?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

![Immigration & Checkpoints Authority logo](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Flogos.ask.gov.sg%2Ficalogo.png&w=3840&q=75)

Updated by ICA 1y ago

------------------------------------------------------------------------

44 found this helpful

If your application is unsuccessful, you should inform your school before submitting an appeal to ICA. You may then [submit an appeal via the enquiry form](https://go.gov.sg/ica-stp-enquiry), along with your application reference number. Each appeal will be considered on its own merits. The general processing time for appeal cases is about 4 weeks and the outcome of the appeal will be made known to both you and the school.
