## Remittance

With an aim to serve the money transfer needs of Non-Resident Bangladeshi population, Nagad has launched Remittance Services. Through Nagad remittance, our honorable NRB community can easily send money to their families and friends back home in Bangladesh in a fast & secure away.

## How to?

Step 1 Sender Visits partner MTO Agent/Bank branch/Exchange House

Step 2 Sender provides information to Nagad’s partner MTO Agent/Bank branch/Exchange House

Step 3 Sender deposits cash

Step 4 Money Received by Nagad User

**Send Money to Nagad accounts by Western Union:**

Now our honorable Expatriate Bangladeshi community can also remit money to their loved ones’ Nagad accounts in Bangladesh by Western Union. Remitters can send money through Western Union by below mentioned methods:

1. Visit Western Union Agents worldwide
2. Using Western Union Mobile App
3. Online money transfer through Western Union web portal

Beneficiaries will instantly receive the money sent by Western Union in their Nagad accounts along with 2.5% government incentive.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Any customer from abroad can send money (remittance) to the Nagad account. After sending the remittance, you will know about it through SMS notification in Nagad account. Nagad account recipient will get 2.5% government incentive on remitted money if sending money to Nagad account in Bangladesh through legal channels. In this case the customer can see the details of principal amount and 2.5% government incentive in 1 SMS.

Regular (Nagad/Islamic) cash out charges will be applicable to cash out the remittance amount.
