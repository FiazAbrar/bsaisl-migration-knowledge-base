Our International Offices

IHH Healthcare Singapore's international offices serve patients from more than 20 cities worldwide. Our warm and dedicated staff are trained to attend to your needs before, during and after your arrival and treatment at our hospitals.

View all

Our services

If you require medical care, we can help with a range of services to ensure that your journey is stress-free and comfortable. For more details, view the International Patients Guide.

Medical services

  • Professional recommendations of treatment options and doctors
  • Securing an appointment within 24 hours
  • Advice on estimated cost of treatments and procedures
  • Arranging a teleconsultation

Travel arrangements

  • Evacuation and repatriation assistance
  • Flight and accommodation arrangements
  • Visa application and extensions
  • Airport transfer arrangements

Language and special services

  • Multi-language translation/interpreter service
  • Special food and beverage/religious arrangements
  • Post-care support
  • Other services upon request
