High Commission for the People’s Republic of Bangladesh in Singapore

## Documents Attestation

1. For documents originated from Singapore Organization/ Office/ Company : The documents need to be notarized and attested by Minsitry of Foreign Affairs Singapore before submission to the counter.

2. For documents originated from Bangladesh (Marriage certificate, Birth certificate / Police report / Educational/Driving license certificate etc) : The documents need to be attested by Minsitry of Foreign Affairs of Bangladesh before submission to the counter.

3. Fee :S$ 22.00 ( To be paid by NETS/Flash pay only)
