# Visiting Singapore

Last updated 09 Oct 2026

We welcome all visitors to Singapore. To ensure you have a successful and memorable trip, please read on to find out what to prepare before your trip to Singapore.

### Before Arrival

You should submit your personal information, trip details and health declaration electronically within three days prior to the date of your arrival in Singapore. This can be done using the SG Arrival Card e-Service.

Please note the SG Arrival Card is not a visa and the use of the SG Arrival Card e-Service is free of charge.

You can submit the SG Arrival Card via [https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/sgarrivalcard/](/redirect-links/e-services/e-services--sgac) or through "MyICA" mobile application that can be downloaded for free from either the [App Store (iOS)](/redirect-links/app-links/myica-app-store) or [Google Play (Android)](/redirect-links/app-links/myica-google-play). Please refer to [entry requirements](/information-and-e-services/entry-and-departure/entering-singapore/entry-requirements) for more information on entering Singapore.

You will receive your SG Arrival Card in the form of a PDF document (applicable for web service only, see below for a specimen) or email once ICA has successfully processed your submission. Please note that the SG Arrival Card comes with a unique DE Number that accompanies your passport number.

### Basic Entry Requirements

You must present yourself for immigration clearance at a point of entry into Singapore. You must satisfy the basic entry requirements before you can be considered for entry. The grant of an immigration pass will be determined by the ICA officers at the point of entry.

From 11 March 2022, all foreign visitors arriving in Singapore will be notified digitally of their Visit Pass (e-Pass) through email. There will not be any endorsement on your passport. The e-Pass notification will indicate the period of stay granted and last day of stay allowed. Please ensure that you provide a valid email address when submitting the [SG Arrival Card](/redirect-links/e-services/e-services--sgac).

Please refer to [entry requirements](/information-and-e-services/entry-and-departure/entering-singapore/entry-requirements) for more information.

### Visa Requirements

If you hold a travel document issued by one of the listed countries or places, you will require a valid Singapore visa to travel to, and seek entry, into Singapore. Please refer to [visa guidelines](/information-and-e-services/entry-and-departure/entering-singapore/visa-guidelines) to check if you require a Singapore visa and for more information.

Upon successful processing of your visa application, you will be issued an e-visa in the form of a PDF document.

Please note that possession of a valid visa does not guarantee entry into Singapore. The grant of an immigration pass will be determined by the ICA officers at the point of entry. The period of stay granted is not tied to the validity of your visa.
