# How do I apply for a Visa?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

![Immigration & Checkpoints Authority logo](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Flogos.ask.gov.sg%2Ficalogo.png&w=3840&q=75)

Updated by ICA 1y ago

ICA does not accept submission of visa application over the immigration counters.

You can apply for a visa by using our [e-Service](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/esvclandingpage/save).

If you do not have a Local Contact with a Singpass/ Corppass account, you may submit the visa application:

- Through an authorised visa agent appointed by the Singapore Overseas Missions; or

- At the nearest [Singapore Overseas Mission](https://www.mfa.gov.sg/Overseas-Missions)

Please liaise directly with the nearest [Singapore Overseas Mission](https://www.mfa.gov.sg/Overseas-Missions) for the detailed visa application procedure if the application is to be submitted through their authorised visa agents or at the Singapore Overseas Mission.
