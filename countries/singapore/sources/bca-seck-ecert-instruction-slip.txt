Request of Skills Evaluation Certificates (SEC(K)) or Validation Test Certificates for New Workers
We are pleased to inform you that Skills Evaluation Certificates (SEC(K)) or Validation Test Certificates
will be issued electronically for new workers from 4 May 2026 onwards.
Please follow these steps to obtain your certificate:

1  Ensure your Singpass account is activated.
   *For Singpass enquiries, please visit https://ask.gov.sg/singpass.

2 Access the online form: Go to FormSG: @https://form.gov.sg/682c24da56d1d932efc14a63

3  Log in using your personal Singpass.

   *Do not use Singpass accounts belonging to others (eg: employer or employment agent).

   Complete all required fields:

   · Passport number: For all nationalities except Malaysian and PRC nationals (use the

        number from your test registration)

   · Identification Card Number: For Malaysian and PRC nationals only

   · Handphone number

   · Employer name (as stated on IPA)

4  · Work permit number (as stated on IPA)

   · Email address
   · Click "Verify" to receive a One-Time Password (OTP) in your email
   · Enter One-Time Password (No OTP will be sent if the email address is incorrect)
   · Trade (refer to the trade videos in FormSG if unsure)
   · Test year
   · Upload a copy of the IPA

5  Click "Submit Now".

   *Do note only one certificate request per worker is permitted.

Once submitted, BCA will process your request and send your certificate to your email address within
two weeks. For assistance, please contact us at https://www2.bca.gov.sg/Feedback/. Thank you.
