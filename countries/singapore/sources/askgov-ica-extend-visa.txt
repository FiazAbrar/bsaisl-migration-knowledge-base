How can I extend my Visa?
Updated by 
ICA

3y ago
23
 found this helpful
There is strictly no extension of visa validity or change of visa type. You can submit a fresh application via our 
e-Service
 upon the expiry or upon utilisation of your current visa. However, please note that each visa application is assessed on its own merits.
