# 24-Hour Helpline

![IMG_1227.jpg](https://alep-p-001.sitecorecontenthub.cloud/api/public/content/c46f296fced248ec97ab179980d7913f?v=3c95c0c2)

 

MWC operates a 24-hour helpline for migrant workers. The helpline is also made available in about 10 different languages native to migrant workers.

 

Migrant workers in need of assistance on employment-related issues can call MWC's 24-hour helpline at 6536 2692.
