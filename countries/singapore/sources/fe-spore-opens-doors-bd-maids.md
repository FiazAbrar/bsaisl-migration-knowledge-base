# S'pore opens doors to Bangladeshi maids

Tuesday, 21 September 2010

------------------------------------------------------------------------

![](https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/uploads)

Munima Sultana\
back from Singapore\
Prospects for sending Bangladeshi domestic helps to Singapore have brightened on the back of stringent Filipino regulation on the deployment of maids.\
Officials, however, disputed with the reported figure on the potential recruitment of 45,000 maids from Bangladesh as hiring is dependent on their English language proficiency.\
Shirley Ng, president of Association of Employment Agencies Singapore (AEAS) told the Strait Times that no specific number has been confirmed yet.\
She, however, refused to make further comment on the matter, despite this correspondent's phone call and e-mail.\
An AEAS delegation visited Bangladesh last month and met with Bangladeshi officials to advance the proposal.\
An estimated 120,000 Bangladeshi migrant workers are working in Singapore, but officials at Bangladesh High\
Commission say, hardly any Bangladeshi-origin maids are working there.\
Bangladesh is an approved sending nation, which can legally send maids to Singapore.\
The association said domestic workers from Bangladesh could form up to 20 per cent of the market in the next five to 10 years if they can meet criteria set by the Ministry of Manpower and employers, according to local media reports.\
Both Strait Times and MediaCorp of Singapore reported that a new source of foreign domestic workers from Bangladesh has been created after the Philippines made its law tough to bring their maid and Indonesian maids prefer to go to Hong Kong and Taiwan for better salary.\
Bangladeshi diplomats are hopeful that Singapore is an ideal destination for Bangladeshi female workers, who can be better protected in the city state.\
All the foreign maids have to pass some tests including safety and security issues to get work permit in Singapore.\
Employers also look for their quality to communicate and understand more than one language as it is a country of several natives including Malay, Indian and Chinese.\
