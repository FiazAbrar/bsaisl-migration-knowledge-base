# Apply for an Employment Pass

You can apply for an Employment Pass online as an employer or appointed employment agent.

## How to apply

[TABLE]

- See [pass map](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/key-facts#pass-map) for an overview of what you need to do before, during and after you apply for an Employment Pass.
- Use the [Self-Assessment Tool](/eservices/services/employment-s-pass-self-assessment-tool) to check if your candidate qualifies. You should not apply if the Self-Assessment Tool shows that your candidate does not qualify.
- Apply for family passes after the main pass is approved. Although you can apply for family passes at the same time as the EP, you will lose the application fees for each family pass if the EP application gets rejected.

You are required to advertise your job opening on MyCareersFuture to [consider all applicants fairly](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/consider-all-candidates-fairly), before you can apply for an Employment Pass.

## Submit an application

Show

You can submit an application using [EP eService](/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice). 

***Processing time:** Processed or given an update within 10 business days.*

### How to apply

*Candidates do not need to be in Singapore when you apply.*

1.  [Get a written consent to apply for Employment Pass](/faq/work-pass-general/why-must-i-get-a-foreigners-written-consent-before-applying-for-a-work-pass-for-them) from the candidate.
2.  Complete the [candidate’s form](/-/media/mom/documents/work-passes-and-permits/ep-and-s-pass-candidate-form.pdf). It contains information you’ll need for the application and can be sent to the candidate to fill in. 
3.  [Submit the EP application](/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice) and upload the [required documents](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/documents-required).\
    Ensure that your organisation’s [turnover information](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/notify-mom-of-changes#change-in-companys-name-or-financial-information) for the past 3 years has been updated before submitting the application.
4.  Pay the \$105 fee by GIRO, Visa, Mastercard or Amex.

[Learn what to do if the educational institution is not listed](/faq/employment-pass/what-should-i-do-if-i-cant-find-my-candidates-qualification-in-the-self-assessment-tool) on the application form

**How to check your application status**

You may [check your application status online](/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice) after **10 business days**.

If your application is still pending after 10 business days, please check your email for our request of additional documents.

If additional information or documents are required:

- Your processing time may take longer than 10 working days.
- You will need to [submit the documents](/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice). Click on ‘Work Passes’ on the left menu \> Choose the application record \> Select ‘Submit documents’. Alternatively, select ‘View details’ to see the action required from you.
- Otherwise, our email will inform you to submit them using our [online form](https://form.gov.sg/#!/5f8d4eadef8dab00112fd313).

### **If the pass is approved**

1.  [Print the in-principle approval (IPA) letter](/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice)

2.  Send the pass holder's copy of the IPA to the candidate.

    The IPA:

    - **Is a pre-approved single-entry visa for the candidate to enter Singapore**. It gives the candidate **6 months** to enter Singapore and get their pass issued. 
    - **States whether the candidate needs to go for a** **medical examination** after their arrival in Singapore.

3.  Make arrangements to bring in your candidates.

You should only bring in candidates when the pass is approved, as we will not be able to extend their visit pass.

For overseas company without a Singapore-registered office

**For companies not registered in Singapore:** you need to get a local sponsor to submit the application.

***Processing time:** Within 6 weeks for most cases.*

### How to apply

*Candidates do not need to be in Singapore when you apply.*

1.  [Get a written consent to apply for Employment Pass](/faq/work-pass-general/why-must-i-get-a-foreigners-written-consent-before-applying-for-a-work-pass-for-them) from the candidate.
2.  Download the [Employment Pass (Sponsorship) application form (for online submission)](/-/media/mom/documents/services-forms/passes/ep_sponsor_form8.pdf).
3.  Complete the application form in soft copy and get it signed.
4.  Submit the following application documents and pay the **\$105 application fee** by [PayNow](https://www.abs.org.sg/consumer-banking/pay-now), Visa, Mastercard or Amex using [our online form](https://go.gov.sg/submit-ep-sponsorship). You will need a Corppass account to complete this form. If you do not have one, [apply for Corppass](https://www.corppass.gov.sg) first.
    - Completed and signed application form
    - Supporting documents listed in the application form

Watch this video to find out [how to fill in educational details in the application](https://youtu.be/7ohEbY3vdJo).

### **How to check your application status**

You may [check your application status online](/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice) after **6 weeks**.

If additional information or documents are required:

- Your processing time may take longer than 6 weeks.
- We will email you to [submit the requested documents](https://form.gov.sg/#!/5f8d4eadef8dab00112fd313).

### **If the pass is approved**

1.  We will email the **in-principle approval (IPA) letter** to the contact person indicated in the application. 

2.  Send the pass holder's copy of the IPA to the candidate.

    The IPA:

    - **Is a pre-approved single-entry visa for the candidate to enter Singapore**. It gives the candidate **6 months** to enter Singapore and get their pass issued. 
    - **States whether the candidate needs to go for a** **medical examination**, which can be done after they arrive in Singapore.

3.  Make arrangements to bring in your candidates.

You should only bring in candidates when the pass is approved, as we will not be able to extend their visit pass.

If you have questions on the application, [send us your enquiry on EP (Sponsorship) application matters](https://service2.mom.gov.sg/efeedback/Forms/eFeedbackWithReferrer.aspx?option=19).

## Prepare for arrival in Singapore

Show

### Before departing

The candidate should:

- Print their in-principle approval (IPA) letter.
- Ensure that they comply with the [latest travel requirements](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore).

### After arriving

The candidate should:

- Show their IPA letter to the immigration officer. 
- Complete any required medical examinations.

## Get the pass issued

Show

*When: Within 6 months of in-principle approval (IPA)*

*Processing time: Immediate*

The candidate must be in Singapore when you carry out these steps to get the pass issued:

1.  Log in to [EP eService](/eservices/services/employment-pass-eservice) and provide the [required information and documents](#what-you-ll-need).
2.  Pay the fees using GIRO, Visa, Mastercard or Amex:
    - \$225 for each pass
    - (If applicable) \$30 for each [Multiple Journey Visa](https://www.ica.gov.sg/#/visitor/visitor_entryvisa)
3.  Once the pass is issued, the candidate and you will both receive the notification letter by email. The notification letter is valid for 1 month from the date of issue and:
    - Allows the candidate to start work and travel in and out of Singapore while waiting for the pass card.
    - States if registration of fingerprints and photo is required.

If the candidate needs more time to complete the card registration procedures, you can [request to extend the validity of the notification letter](https://go.gov.sg/change-date-epsc).

### What you'll need

You will need the following information to get the pass issued: 

- Candidate's passport details
- Candidate’s Singapore contact details
- Candidate's current Short-Term Visit Pass (STVP) or immigration pass details
- Candidate's Singapore residential address, which must meet the [housing requirements](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/housing/various-types-of-housing)
- [](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/housing/various-types-of-housing)Singapore residential or office address to receive the candidate’s card
- Details of up to 3 authorised recipients to receive the card (their mobile number and NRIC number, FIN or passport number)

You may also need to upload PDF copies of these documents:

- Candidate’s passport page showing the date of arrival in Singapore
- Completed medical examination form or medical declaration form
- Completed declaration form (attached to the candidate's IPA letter). The employer’s declaration of the form should be signed by an authorised human resource personnel, or an employee holding at least a managerial position.

If you need more time to get the pass issued as your candidates are waiting for their medical results, you can [extend their STVP](https://www.mom.gov.sg/extend-stay). Submit this request **7 to 14 days before the STVP expires** and upload the doctor's memo stating the medical results collection date.

**Example:** If the STVP expires on 17 February, you can only submit the request from 4 to 10 February. **Overstaying fines** may be incurred for late request.

## (If required) Register fingerprints and photo

Show

*When: Within 2 weeks after pass is issued*

**Check the notification letter** for whether the candidate needs to register fingerprints and photo.

If required, the candidate needs to complete registration **within 2 weeks** after pass is issued.

For registration, you must [make an appointment](/eservices/services/make-an-appointment) for the candidate to visit the [Employment Pass Services Centre (EPSC)](/contact-us/locations#Tab-EP).

For the appointment, the candidate should bring:

- Original passport
- Appointment letter
- Notification letter
- Their mobile phone (with local mobile number) with both Singpass and SGWorkPass apps downloaded

During registration, we will capture your fingerprints, take your photo and register you for a Singpass account which allows you to access your digital work pass in the [SGWorkPass ](/eservices/sgworkpass)app.

## Set up digital work pass

Show

*When: On the day of card registration*

After they are registered for a Singpass account, inform the candidate to:

1.  Download [SGWorkPass](/eservices/sgworkpass) (if they have not done so yet)
2.  Log into the app with their Singpass
3.  View their digital work pass and employment information

SGWorkPass app allows them easy and secure access to their digital work pass. They can check their status, expiry date and procure services earlier, without having to wait for delivery of their physical work pass cards.

If the candidate is not required to report to EPSC for card registration, please inform them to set up their digital work pass.
