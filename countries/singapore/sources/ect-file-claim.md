# File an employment claim

Find out more about the Employment Claims Tribunals and the process of filing an employment claim.

## About the Employment Claims Tribunals

The Employment Claims Tribunals (ECT) provide employees and employers with a speedy and low-cost forum in which to resolve their salary-related claims and wrongful dismissal claims.

Mediation at the [Tripartite Alliance for Dispute Management (TADM)](https://www.tal.sg/tadm/mediation-guide-3) is **compulsory** before claims can be filed at the ECT. You may only file a claim at the ECT if **both** of the following applies:

- Your dispute is unresolved after mediation at [TADM](https://www.tal.sg/tadm).
- You have been issued a claim referral certificate by an approved TADM mediator.

> [!NOTE]
> You should submit a mediation request to TADM as soon as your claim arises, as there are limits to the claim period.
>
> You may refer to [TADM's mediation guide](https://www.tal.sg/tadm/mediation-guide-3) for more information.

## File an employment claim step-by-step

This is the process of filing an employment claim. 

If you file a claim, you are the **claimant**.

The other party is the **respondent**. If you have a claim made against you, refer to [Respond to an employment claim](/civil/respond-employment-claim) instead. 

> [!NOTE]
> Lawyers are not allowed to represent parties for ECT matters.

Before filing

Understand employment claims

Find out if your employment claim can be heard at the ECT.

- [Cases eligible for an employment claim](/civil/cases-eligible-employment-claim "Cases eligible for an employment claim")

When filing a claim

File and serve an employment claim

You need to file your employment claim in the Community Justice and Tribunals System (CJTS) and serve it on the respondent.

- [How to file and serve an employment claim](/civil/how-to-file-serve-employment-claim "How to file and serve an employment claim")

Resolving the dispute

Resolve the dispute online

Parties have a chance to settle their dispute online without going to court by online negotiation (eNegotiation) or upon request, online mediation (eMediation) on CJTS.

- [Settle a tribunal or protection from harassment dispute online: eNegotiation or eMediation](/alternatives-to-trial/enegotiation-emediation "Settle a tribunal or protection from harassment dispute online: eNegotiation or eMediation")

Attend court

If the dispute is not resolved, both you and the respondent must attend a case management conference (CMC). At the CMC, the registrar will attempt to mediate the dispute between the parties.\
\
If the matter cannot be settled at the CMC, the case will be fixed for a hearing before a tribunal magistrate.

- [Before going to court for an employment claim](/civil/before-going-to-court-employment-claim "Before going to court for an employment claim")
- [At your employment claims case management conference](/civil/at-employment-claims-CMC "At your employment claims case management conference")
- [At your employment claims hearing](/civil/at-employment-claims-hearing "At your employment claims hearing")

After an ECT order is made

File an application, if needed

You may file an appeal against an employment claims order in some cases, or set aside an order made in your absence if you missed a court session. If the ECT makes an order in your favour but the other party does not comply, you may apply to enforce it.

- [Appeal against an employment claims order](/civil/appeal-employment-claims-order "Appeal against an employment claims order")
- [Enforce an employment claims order](/civil/enforce-employment-claims-order "Enforce an employment claims order")
- [Set aside an employment claims order made in your absence](/civil/set-aside-employment-claims-order-made-in-absence "Set aside an employment claims order made in your absence")
