# Eligibility for EntrePass

This work pass is intended for serial entrepreneurs, high-calibre innovators or experienced investors that want to operate a business in Singapore that is venture-backed or owns innovative technologies.

## Who is eligible

EntrePass is open to all nationalities/citizenships.

You can apply for an EntrePass if you meet **both** conditions:

- Have started, or intend to start, a private limited company that is:
  - Registered with [ACRA](http://www.acra.gov.sg) (Pass holder must hold at least 30% of registered company)
  - **[Venture-backed or owns innovative technologies](/faq/entrepass/which-companies-are-considered-venture-backed-or-own-innovative-technologies)**.

If the registered company is more than 12 months old as of the date of application, you will be assessed according to the [renewal criteria](/passes-and-permits/entrepass/renew-a-pass).

- Meet at least one of the following criteria: 

Raised funding for a past or current business

You have raised at least SGD 100,000 from a **single funding round** from investors for any past or current business.

Examples of eligible investors include:

- [SEEDS Capital](https://www.seedscapital.sg/)
- [Vertex Ventures](https://www.vertexventures.sg/)
- Partners under Enterprise Singapore's [Startup SG Equity (SSGE)](https://www.startupsg.gov.sg/programmes/4895/startup-sg-equity/partners) Programme
- Internationally renowned Venture Capital (VC) firms, corporates, business angels, etc

 

Supported by a government-recognised or internationally renowned incubator or accelerator

The business is or will be supported by an incubator or accelerator that is either:

- Government-recognised
- Internationally renowned

Examples of eligible investors include:

- Partners under Enterprise Singapore’s [Startup SG Accelerator (SSGA)](https://www.startupsg.gov.sg/programmes/4900/startup-sg-accelerator/partners) or [Startup SG Founder (SSGF)](https://www.startupsg.gov.sg/programmes/4894/startup-sg-founder/accredited-mentor-partners) programmes
- Internationally renowned incubators or accelerators such as Y-Combinator, MassChallenge, Alchemist Accelerator, etc.

Founded and sold a technology business

You have founded and sold a business that is [venture-backed or owns innovative technologies](/faq/entrepass/which-companies-are-considered-venture-backed-or-own-innovative-technologies).

Holds intellectual property

The registered business or proposed business holds Intellectual Property (IP) that is registered with an [approved national IP institution](http://www.wipo.int/).\

The IP must provide significant competitive advantage which cannot be easily replicated to the business.

Have a research collaboration in Singapore

The business has a relevant ongoing research collaboration in Singapore with at least one of the following:

- Institute of Higher Learning (IHL)
- Research Institution (RI)

Examples of eligible IHLs and RIs include:

- [IHLs](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/LTVP/apply/graduate-from-an-institute-of-higher-learning-seeking-employment-in-singapore/list_ihl) as listed by the Immigrations & Checkpoints Authority (ICA)
- RIs under the Agency for Science, Technology and Research (A\*STAR)
- [Universities and institutions](https://www.nrf.gov.sg/programmes/create/) under the Campus for Research Excellence and Technological Enterprise (CREATE)

## Requirements

The research collaboration must:

- Have your involvement
- Be related to the business
- Be verified by a contact person from the institution

Contracts of services are not considered research collaborations.
