        Frequent changes of MDWs

If you have changed migrant domestic workers (MDWs) multiple times within a year, you have to meet certain requirements before you can employ another worker.

You are a Frequent Change Employer if you have changed more than 3 MDWs within a 12-month period.

If you are employing your 4
th
 MDW within a 12-month period, you will be required to attend the 
CONNECT Workshop
 conducted by the 
Association of Employment Agencies Singapore (AEA(S))
. Details will be provided in the email sent to you by MOM.
What to expect during the CONNECT Workshop
The CONNECT workshop is conducted by an experienced consultant from AEA(S).

With a network of reputable employment agencies, AEA(S) can help employers better understand their hiring needs and facilitate a better match.

You will be given a questionnaire to complete before the session. This would assist the consultant in understanding your previous hiring practices and management of MDWs and make recommendations to assist you.
Attending CONNECT Workshop

How to attend

In-person or virtual consultation

MOM requires Frequent Change Employers to attend the CONNECT Workshop conducted by AEA(S), either virtually or in-person at the AEA(S)'s office located at:

            Blk 9 Jalan Kukoh #03-77

            Singapore 160009

When

You need to 
make an appointment
 for the CONNECT Workshop after receiving the email notification. The email will provide instructions on how to book an appointment.

Please note that AEA(S)'s office operates on an appointment basis.

Language

AEA(S) can arrange for consultants who are fluent in Mandarin, Malay, Tamil, and dialects like Cantonese and Hokkien, to conduct the CONNECT Workshop. The employer's next-of-kin can accompany the employer for the workshop if the employer is not proficient in these languages.

Duration

1 hour

Fee

$80

Attendance for the consultation is 
mandatory
. You will need to provide notice in writing of at least 3 working days to AEA(S) before the consultation if you are not able to attend. Otherwise, the fee of $80 will be forfeited.

 An appointment will only be confirmed upon successful payment.
