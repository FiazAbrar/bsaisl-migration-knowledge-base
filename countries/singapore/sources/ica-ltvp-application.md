## Overview

The Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP) is granted to eligible foreigners who wish to live in Singapore for an extended period. Local sponsors can use this platform to conveniently submit applications for their family members and track their status online.\

## Eligibility

As a foreigner, you are eligible to apply for an LTVP if you fall under any of the categories below. If you are not a graduate of an Institute of Higher Learning, you require a local sponsor to submit the application on your behalf using their Singpass. Check the table to see if you require a local sponsor.\

[TABLE]

ICA considers factors such as the individual's family ties to Singaporeans, economic contributions, qualifications, age, family profile, and length of residency, to assess the applicant's ability to contribute to Singapore, integrate into our society, and their commitment to sinking roots.\

ICA has [no affiliation with any external migration agency/commercial entity](/about-ica/news-and-publications/public-advisories/application-for-long-term-immigration-facilities-through-commercial-entities-or-consultants) that claims to be a Singapore immigration specialist/consultant or partner. ICA does not support or endorse services by self-proclaimed professional immigration consultancy service providers for LTVP applications.\



Note

**For intended Spouses of Singapore Citizens:** To check your chances of getting an LTVP, take the free Pre-Marriage LTVP Assessment (PMLA) with your intended SC spouse before marriage. Couples who complete this assessment enjoy a shorter processing time of 6 weeks, instead of the usual 6 months.

## Procedure

Whether you are applying for a new LTVP or renewing one, all applicants and sponsors must log in with Singpass. If you are ineligible for Singpass, use the unique login credentials provided by ICA. If you do not have a Singpass account, [apply for one](/redirect-links/singpass-register-account) before starting your LTVP application.



### With a Local Sponsor

Your sponsor must log in to complete the form on your behalf. Before your sponsor can submit the application, you must log in to review the form and make your declarations.



### Without a Local Sponsor

You must log in to complete the form, make your declarations, and submit the application.

Submit your Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP) renewal application at least 3 months before your current LTVP expires. The renewed LTVP will be valid from the date of issuance (upon payment of the issuance fee). ICA will not carry forward any remaining validity from your current LTVP to the renewed LTVP.

## Fees

[TABLE]

You can pay online using any of the following:

- Visa or MasterCard credit/debit card
- American Express (AMEX) credit card
- Internet Direct Debit (DBS/POSB, OCBC, UOB and Standard Chartered Bank Internet Banking accounts in Singapore)
- PayNow\

ICA will notify successful applicants of any other applicable fees.



## Processing Time

ICA processes most applications within **6 weeks**, provided you submit all required documents in order.

Some applications may take longer to process.

## Completion of Formalities

Once approved, you will receive an In-Principle Approval (IPA) letter and must [complete the formalities for the issuance](/information-and-e-services/long-term-visit-and-study/long-term-visit-pass/completion-of-formalities-and-issuance-of-long-term-visit-pass) of your LTVP.

## FAQs

The Ministry of Manpower issues the Dependant's Pass (DP) to dependants of eligible work pass holders. Meanwhile, ICA generally issues the LTVP to dependants of eligible SCs or PRs.

If your LTVP renewal application is being processed and your current LTVP is expiring, you will receive an email notification about the auto-conversion of your LTVP to a Short-Term Visit Pass (STVP).

If you did not receive any notification from ICA during the renewal application process, you may cancel your LTVP by submitting the Cancellation of LTVP form.

The sponsor should cancel the LTVP holder's LTVP by submitting the Cancellation of Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP) form.
