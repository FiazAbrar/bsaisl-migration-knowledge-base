Each visa application, including the duration of the visa granted, is considered on its own merits and subject to approval.
Applicants who are applying for a visa via our 
e-Service
 are advised to submit the application about 30 days prior to the intended arrival into Singapore so that the visa validity will be sufficient for the visit.
