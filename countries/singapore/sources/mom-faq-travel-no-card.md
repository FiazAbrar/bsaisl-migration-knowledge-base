# Can pass holders travel in and out of Singapore if they have not received the pass card?

Yes. Pass holders can travel in and out of Singapore using their digital work pass in their [SGWorkPass](/eservices/sgworkpass) app or their valid notification letter.

Encourage your pass holder to use their digital work pass in the SGWorkPass app for verification when required to lower their risk of losing or damaging their physical card. Digital work passes are free and available immediately upon set up of the SGWorkPass app. Your pass holder can download the SGWorkPass app and set up their digital work pass if they have not done so. They can [register](https://go.gov.sg/register-singpass) or [reset](https://www.singpass.gov.sg/home/ui/online-reset-password/user-detail) their Singpass, if needed.

If your pass holder has to leave Singapore urgently and may not be back before receiving the card, you can request to extend the 1-month validity period:

- [For Work Permit or S Pass holders](https://form.gov.sg/forms/mom/5b68f6c561cffc000f885453)
- [For Employment Pass holders](https://form.gov.sg/forms/mom/5b6d4563ca1efb000f3b03a4)
