# May I seek asylum in Singapore?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

![Ministry of Home Affairs logo](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Flogos.ask.gov.sg%2Fmha.png&w=3840&q=75)

Updated by MHA 5mo ago

As a small, densely populated country with limited land, Singapore is not in a position to accept any persons seeking political asylum or refugee status, regardless of their ethnicity or place of origin.
