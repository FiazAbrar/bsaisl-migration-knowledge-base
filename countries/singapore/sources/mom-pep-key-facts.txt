        Key facts on Personalised Employment Pass

Get a quick overview of the Personalised Employment Pass to decide if it is the right pass for you.

Pass facts

            Who it is for

            High-earning Employment Pass holders or overseas foreign professionals.

            Who can apply

            Candidate who meet the 
eligibility criteria
 can apply directly.

            Minimum salary

Earn a fixed monthly salary of at least $22,500.

            What is the duration

            Up to 3 years.

            Is it renewable

            No. The PEP is issued only once.

            Relevant legislation

Conditions of Work Pass

Employment of Foreign Manpower Act

            Related eServices and forms

            Access 
online services and forms
 for PEP.

Features of the pass
The key features of the pass are as follows:

Flexibility to hold a job in any sector. However, the PEP does not exempt you from complying with registration requirements to practise in Singapore for professions such as medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, architecture, law, etc.

You do not need to reapply for a pass if you change jobs.

You can stay in Singapore for a continuous period of up to 6 months without a job to search for new employment.

Availability of 
passes for eligible family members
.

No foreign worker levy or quota required.

Note: 

If your PEP is expiring, you will need to get an 
Employment Pass
 or 
S Pass
 to continue working in Singapore. You do not have to cancel the existing PEP for your employer to apply for the new pass.

You may be required to pay 
income tax
.

Pass map
Overview of what you need to do before, during and after you apply for a Personalised Employment Pass.

Before applying

Know the 
eligibility and requirements
 to apply.

Find out the 
documents required
 for applying.

Before arrival

Apply for the Personalised Employment Pass
 and wait for the outcome.

(If your pass is approved) Get an in-principle approval letter.

Ensure that you comply with the latest 
travel requirements
.

Upon arrival

Get the pass issued
 and receive a notification letter stating whether registration is required.

(If required) Make an appointment to 
register your fingerprints and photo
.

Receive your pass card
 at the address provided 5 working days after registration or document verification.

When required

Notify MOM 
 about your annual income by 31 January of the next year. You should also notify MOM about any changes to your employment or personal information.

Apply for a replacement
 if your pass card is lost, stolen or damaged.

Cancel your pass
 if you no longer require it.
