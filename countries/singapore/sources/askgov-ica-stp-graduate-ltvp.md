# Will a Student's Pass Holder be issued a Long-Term Pass automatically upon course completion?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

![Immigration & Checkpoints Authority logo](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Flogos.ask.gov.sg%2Ficalogo.png&w=3840&q=75)

Updated by ICA 1y ago

------------------------------------------------------------------------

29 found this helpful

Upon course completion, students will have to cancel their Student's Pass (STP) and surrender any physical STP card that was issued to them for cancellation to ICA. Upon cancellation of their STP, they may be granted a short-term visit pass to stay in Singapore (up to 89 days).

If you are studying in an Institute of Higher Learning (IHL) and wish to have a longer period of stay to look for jobs upon completion of your course, you may apply for a 1-year (non-renewable) Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP).

You may refer to [LTVP for Graduate from IHL Seeking Employment in Singapore](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/LTVP/apply/graduate-from-an-institute-of-higher-learning-seeking-employment-in-singapore) for more details.
