Apply for Singapore Visa from Bangladesh

Saimon Global is the Authorized Visa Agent of High Commission of the Republic of Singapore in Dhaka. You will need a visa to enter Singapore, however if you are holders of diplomatic, official and service passports do not need a visa for entry.

Call to Apply

Singapore Visa Requirements for Bangladeshi

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED :

 

Step-by-Step Guide & Requirement to Applying for a Singapore Visa from Bangladesh

To apply for an Singapore entry Or Visit visa for business or social visits, you will need the following documents:

  • Valid Passport with at least six months validity from the date of arrival in Singapore.
  • Visa Application Form-14A completely filled in with original sign as per Passport
  • 2 recent passport sized Color photographs (Met/Semi Met Paper, white background). MUST be taken during the last 3 months.
  • Air Ticket Booking copy
  • Bank Statement & Solvency Certificate
  • Hotel Booking Copy
  • Invitation Letter MUST (Any 1 of following) Form V39A completely filled:
    • COMPANY INVITATION: (Authorized NRIC Number, Designation, Company Registration Number (UEN), Address, phone number and relationship with applicant).
    • INDIVIDUAL: (Authorized NRIC Number, Address, phone number, relationship with applicant).
  • Some applicants may require additional documents & a longer processing time due to additional verifications required.

For Government Service Holders

For Govt. Service Holders Letter of Introduction (LOI) letter signed by officials of Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA).

 

SINGAPORE VISA PROCESSING TIME

Minimum 5(five) working days (excluding day of submission) to 1 month or more. Do take note that some applications may require more than 1 month for processing and it completely depends on the Singapore Embassy Dhaka.

 

APPLICATION RECEIVING HOURS

Sunday – Thursday  9:00 AM – 2:00 PM

 

PASSPORT DELIVERY HOURS  

Sunday – Thursday  9:00 AM – 4:00 PM
