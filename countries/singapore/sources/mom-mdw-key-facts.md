## Pass map

Get an idea of what you need to do before, during and after you apply for a Work Permit.

## Before applying

- ### 

  - ![know requirements](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/know-requirements.png)
    Know the [eligibility and requirements](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements) to apply.
  - ![know requirements](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/know-requirements.png)
    Understand the [guidelines](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/employers-guide) to help create a good working relationship with your MDW.

    *For employers*
  - ![registration](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/registration.png)
    Register for the [Employers' Orientation Programme](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/employers-orientation-programme-eop).

    *For first-time employers*

## Before MDW's arrival

- ### Apply for Work Permit

  - ![Apply](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/apply.png)
    Know the [eligibility](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/apply-under-a-special-scheme) to apply under the Joint-Income or Sponsorship schemes.

    *Lower-income employers or senior citizens may be eligible*
  - ![Apply](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/apply.png)
    [Apply for the Work Permit](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/apply-for-work-permit). Once approved, you will get an in-principle approval to bring the MDW into Singapore.

- ### Prepare for MDW's arrival

  - ![medical](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/medical.png)
    Ensure that the MDW complies with the latest [travel requirements](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore).
  - ![quota levy security bond](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/quota-levy-security-bond.png)
    Buy a [security bond](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/security-bond).

    *For non-Malaysian MDW*
  - ![medical](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/medical.png)
    Buy [medical and personal accident insurance](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/insurance-requirements) for the MDW and submit the insurance details online.
  - ![registration](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/registration.png)
    Register the MDW for the [Settling-in Programme (SIP)](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/settling-in-programme-sip) once you know their arrival date.

    *For first-time MDWs*

## Upon MDW's arrival

- ### Before permit can be issued

  - ![registration](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/registration.png)
    Send the MDW to attend the [SIP](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/settling-in-programme-sip) within 7 days of their arrival in Singapore, excluding Sunday and public holidays.

    *For first-time MDWs*
  - ![medical](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/medical.png)
    Send the MDW for a [medical examination](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/medical-examination) by a Singapore-registered doctor within 2 weeks of arrival.

- ### Get the Work Permit issued

  - ![issuance](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/inssuance.png)
    When the worker arrives, you can get the [permit issued](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/apply-for-work-permit#get-the-permit-issued) and you will get a Temporary Work Permit stating whether registration is required.
  - ![register fingerprints](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/register-fingerprints.png)
    (If required) Make an appointment for the MDW to [register their fingerprints and photo](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/apply-for-work-permit#register-fingerprints-and-photo).
  - ![receive card](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/receive-card.png)
    [Receive the Work Permit card](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/apply-for-work-permit#receive-the-card) at the address provided 4 working days after registration or document verification.

## Monthly

- ### Every month

  - ![quota levy security bond](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/quota-levy-security-bond.png)
    [Pay the MDW levy](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/foreign-domestic-worker-levy/paying-levy) by GIRO.

- ### Every 6 months

  - ![medical](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/medical.png)
    Send the MDW for a [medical examination](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/eligibility-and-requirements/six-monthly-medical-examination) every 6 months.

## As and when required

- ### 

  - ![notify mom](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/notify-mom.png)
    [Notify MOM](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/notify-mom-of-changes) if you or your MDW's information changes.
  - ![quota levy security bond](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/quota-levy-security-bond.png)
    You can [apply for levy waiver](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/foreign-domestic-worker-levy/apply-for-levy-waiver) in certain situations.
  - ![renewal](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/renewal.png)
    [Renew the Work Permit](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/renew-a-work-permit) before it expires.
  - ![replace card](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/replace-card.png)
    [Apply for a replacement](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/replace-a-card) if the permit card is lost, stolen or damaged.
  - ![cancel pass or permit](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/cancel-pass-or-permit.png)
    [Cancel the permit](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-domestic-worker/cancel-a-work-permit) if the MDW no longer works for you.
