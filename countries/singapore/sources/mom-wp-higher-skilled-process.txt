        Higher-skilled workers for process sector

You can get the higher-skilled worker (R1) levy rate if your workers meet the new requirements.

The higher-skilled worker levy (R1) framework has been enhanced to ensure that workers in the process sector are equipped with relevant skills. 

  Process workers previously on the R1 levy tier who 
could not
 meet the new requirements by 31 December 2025 would have been automatically adjusted to basic-skilled levy (R2) from 1 January 2026.
Requirements 
To get the R1 levy rate, your workers must meet any of these criteria:

Skills qualifications and salary

Skills qualifications and experience

Skills qualifications and working in a company with Productivity Certification Framework (PCF) rating of at least Bronze

For more details on the PCF certification process, you can 		
contact Singapore Chemical Industry Council Limited
 (SCIC).

Note: 
Companies need to renew their PCF certification every 2 years. This is to ensure they continue improving their productivity and remain current with industry standards and practices.

How to upgrade your worker

We will automatically upgrade your worker to the R1 status once they meet the necessary requirements. You do not need to apply for the upgrade.

The new levy rate will start on the 1
st
 of next month after your worker is on the R1 levy rate.

You may refer to the 
current levy rates
 in the Process sector. You can check your worker’s new skill status and levy rate in 
WP Online
