    International Patient Services

    Raffles Hospital earns a reputation for itself as the destination of choice among international patients. More than 35 per cent of Raffles Hospital’s patients are foreigners, comprising nationals from more than 100 countries.

    Located conveniently in the heart of Singapore, Raffles Hospital is a full service private hospital offering comprehensive specialist services combined with advanced medical technology. Raffles Hospital aims to maintain Singapore’s leadership as a regional healthcare hub by providing more complex procedures such as neurovascular interventional procedures, targeted cancer treatments, heart surgeries, orthopaedic surgeries, and fertility treatments.

    The Raffles Hospital Group Practice Model of collaborative care also allows our staff medical specialists to offer team-based care which is especially important for patients who have multiple medical conditions and require the care of more than one doctor. As a team, specialists diagnose and treat patients efficiently. At the same time, costs are kept low by not repeating consultations or examinations unnecessarily.

    Understanding how it feels to be ill and in a foreign land, Raffles Hospital has set up the Raffles International Patients Centre (Raffles IPC) to provide personalised care for our international patients and tend to all non-medical needs during the stay.

    Services

    • Medical enquiries and appointment-making,
    • Medical translation and interpreter services,
    • Medical evacuation and repatriation,
    • Arrangement for accommodation in Singapore,
    • Visa application and extension,
    • Airport meeting and transfer service,
    • Travel planning and visitor information,
    • Secretarial support services,
    • Concierge services.
