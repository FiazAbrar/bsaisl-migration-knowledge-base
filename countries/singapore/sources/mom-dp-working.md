# Working in Singapore

Dependant’s Pass holders who wish to work in Singapore can get a work pass to do so.\

 

Dependant's Pass (DP) holders are welcome to contribute to our economy and society while in Singapore. If you are:

## Looking to work for an organisation

Show

Prospective employers can apply for an Employment Pass (EP), S Pass, or DP-Work Permit (DP-WP) for their DP holders.

**Current DP-WP holders** must get their existing employer to cancel their Work Permit **before** the prospective employer can submit a new application.

**DP holders who get an EP or S Pass:**

- Are required to meet prevailing EP or S Pass qualifying criteria.
- Will be granted the work and stay privileges associated with an EP or S Pass.
- Will no longer be dependent on the main pass holder for their stay in Singapore. They will need to have their DP cancelled before their EP or S Pass is issued.**\**

Prospective employers can use the [Self-Assessment Tool (SAT)](/eservices/services/employment-s-pass-self-assessment-tool) to check if the candidate qualifies for an EP or S Pass.

**DP holders who get a DP-WP:**

- Can be of any nationality/citizenship.
- Are not subject to a minimum qualifying salary.
- Will have their DP-WP’s validity period tied to their DP. 
- Are not subject to the [six-monthly medical examination](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/six-monthly-medical-examination) and [security bond](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/security-bond) requirements.
- Do not need their employer to provide [medical insurance](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/medical-insurance) for them if they are already covered under a medical insurance plan that meets MOM’s [minimum mandatory insurance coverage requirement](/passes-and-permits/work-permit-for-foreign-worker/sector-specific-rules/medical-insurance).
- Will be subject to the relevant Work Permit quota and levy. The DP-WP levy rates are aligned to the lowest levy rates for each business sector (and the applicable levy tier for Manufacturing and Services sectors), as shown in the table below:

**Note: **

- A Dependency Ratio Ceiling (DRC) or quota is the maximum ratio of foreign workers to the total workforce that a company can employ in each sector. 
- From 2028, the current Tier 1 and Tier 2 in the Services and Manufacturing sectors will be merged. The monthly levy rate for the merged tier will be \$300 in the Manufacturing sector and \$400 in the Services sector. MOM will release more details on the implementation timeline in due course. 

**DP holders of Overseas Networks & Expertise Pass holders:**

You can find out more about [working in Singapore](/passes-and-permits/overseas-networks-expertise-pass/passes-for-families). 

## Looking to set up or operate your own business

Show

You can apply for a [Letter of Consent](/passes-and-permits/loc-for-dependants-pass-business-owners).

## Looking to volunteer

Show

You do not need a work pass to participate in volunteer activities, provided that:

- No payment is involved
- The activities are for a charitable purpose
