# Representative Offices

To ensure a comfortable and pleasant experience for you, our regional representative offices can assist to your enquires and facilitate your visit to Singapore.

### Our services include:

- Medical inquiries and information on Raffles Hospital services
- Medical appointments with [specialists](https://www.rafflesmedicalgroup.com/services/specialist-centres/) in Raffles Hospital
- Flight reservation and confirmation
- Arrangement and providing accommodation in Singapore
- Airport pick- up and transfer
- Visa arrangement
- Assistance before, during and after hospitalization
- Other services upon request
