Information and e-Services

Electronic Visit Pass (e-Pass) Retrieval for Short-Term Visitors

Retrieve your Electronic Visit Pass to check your allowed period of stay in Singapore. 
Last updated 03 Nov 2026


Overview

An Electronic Visit Pass (e-Pass) is issued to all arriving foreign visitors. You must provide a valid email address when submitting your SG Arrival Card (SGAC), as your e-Pass will automatically be sent to this email address after you clear immigration.

Your e-Pass contains your Disembarkation/Embarkation (D/E) number and visit pass details, including the maximum number of days and the last day you are allowed to stay in Singapore.


Note
  • ICA does not issue e-Passes to Permanent Residents and Long-Term Pass holders upon arrival in Singapore.


e-Pass Retrieval

You can also retrieve a copy of your e-Pass from the  e-Pass Enquiry Portal on the SGAC e-Service using your:

  • Disembarkation/Embarkation (D/E) number issued after your SGAC submission
  • Passport details


Retrieval of Disembarkation/Embarkation Number

You can retrieve your D/E number from the following:

  1. Your e-Pass

    e-Pass
  2. Your SGAC

    You can also find your D/E number after successful submission of your SGAC through any of these channels:

    Submission Channel Description Thumbnail
    e-Service and MyICA Mobile Application

    SG Arrival Card acknowledgement email

    Sent to the email address declared in your SGAC.
    If you have multiple SGACs, refer to the submission for your current trip. 

      


Enquiry on e-Pass

If you encounter any problems with the e-Pass Enquiry Portal or need help retrieving your D/E number, you may  submit your enquiry to ICA.
