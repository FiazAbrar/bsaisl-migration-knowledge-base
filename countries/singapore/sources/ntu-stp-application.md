# Application of Student's Pass

\

[TABLE]

 

 

Prior to your Student's Pass submission to ICA, NTU will need to register your particulars through the [ICA SOLAR system](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/solar/index.xhtml) with the following details:

- Your name, date of birth, gender, and nationality (as appear in your travel document)
- Course details, course commencement and end dates
- SOLAR application (AKA Registration Acknowledgement Letter)

Undergraduate students can access the [Undergraduate Acceptance Link](https://wis.ntu.edu.sg/pls/webexe/adm_acceptance_form.login) after obtaining the matriculation number.

Postgraduate students can look out for the Confirmation of Acceptance email sent to the inbox/ junk folder after confirming acceptance. The SOLAR application (AKA Registration Acknowledgement Letter) details will be indicated in the email.

Please ensure the following when submitting eForm 16 at [ICA SOLAR system](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/solar/index.xhtml):

- All your particulars\* including programme name and start date are correctly captured in eForm 16.
- NRIC/FIN number of your parents if they are Singaporeans or foreigners working or living in Singapore. Otherwise, leave it **blank**.
- A Singapore address and contact details. You may leave it **blank** if there is no local address/contact details.
- A Reachable email address.

**\*Note**: Should there be any discrepancies, please inform us through [Ask One Stop](https://ntuadminonestop.service-now.com/ntussp?id=sc_cat_item&sys_id=2a2b48fddba02c50061f0e1dd3961999&case_type=cb7b60e6dbfcecd0061f0e1dd3961975) and do not proceed with the submission

You have to pay a non-refundable processing fee of \$45 within 7 days upon submission of your eForm 16 with completed documents. Failing which, the application will be withdrawn. ICA SOLAR system only accepts payment via credit/debit card and internet banking only. 

Some applications may take longer time to process as stated below.  You may enquire about the application status using the [e-Service](https://www.ica.gov.sg/eservicesandforms/solar "https://www.ica.gov.sg/eservicesandforms/solar").

- Application without Visa – Within One Week
- Application with Visa – Within Two Weeks

You may check your Student Pass application status via [ICA SOLAR system](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/solar/index.xhtml).

If approved, you may print the outcome letter of your Student's Pass.

If rejected, you may [submit an appeal via the enquiry form](https://go.gov.sg/ica-stp-enquiry), along with your STP application reference number. Each appeal will be considered on its own merits. The general processing time for appeal cases is about 4 weeks, and the outcome of the appeal will be made known to both you and the school.

A Single Journey Visa will be incorporated in the IPA letter if you require visa. You need not apply separately and may enter Singapore with the IPA letter at the Singapore checkpoints.

Access [ICA SOLAR system](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/solar/index.xhtml) to complete Step 1 and Step 2 as shown below

![](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/media/images/default-source/onestop/step-5.png?sfvrsn=f5c44437_1 "STEP 5")

 

#### Step 1: Acknowledgement and Document Upload\

Click “Term & Conditions” to acknowledge the terms & conditions before viewing the required documents for submission.\

**Medical Examination Report (if applicable)**

If the period of your studies is 6 months or more, you may download the ICA prescribed Medical Examination Report after acknowledging the Terms & Conditions

Upon satisfactory completion of the medical examination, you are required to submit the completed medical examination report via SOLAR system for the completion of the Student's Pass formalities with ICA in Singapore, to be issued the digital student’s pass.

** **

#### **Step 2: Make Payment**

Once ICA approves the document(s) for Step 1, you will be notified to make payment within 7 days for Step 2.

If payment is not made within the stipulated deadline, ICA will withdraw your application. You will need to repeat the whole application process.

 

[TABLE]

 

#### ![](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/media/images/default-source/onestop/step-6.png?sfvrsn=48a788ee_1 "STEP 6")**Step 3: Completion of Formalities**

You may proceed to make an appointment at [**ICA Services Centre**](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/eappt/) (Please make appointment 1 day after STP issuance fee has been paid). Please note that the completion of Student's Pass formalities with ICA is strictly by [e-appointment ](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/ibook/index.do)**ONLY**. ICA will **NOT** entertain students who walk-in without an e-appointment. This must be done within the validity of your Short-Term Visit Pass or before the expiry date of your IPA letter, whichever is earlier. 

Do note that the IPA letter is not an immigration pass and does not confer any valid stay to the applicant. You are required to attend an additional in-person session after your arrival in Singapore to complete your formalities with ICA to have the Student’s Pass issued.

If the ICA e-appointment date is beyond the validity of your Short-Term Visit Pass, please extend your Short-Term Visit Pass at ICA [website](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-depart/extend_short_stay).
