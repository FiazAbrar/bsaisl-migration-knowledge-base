What should I do if an MDW has not been paid, is overworked or not given enough food?

You can report the infringement online.

Alternatively, you can:

  • Advise her to call
    MDW Helpline 1800 339 5505
    +65 6339 5505 for overseas callers
    (Mon – Fri, 8.30am to 5.30pm)
    Centre for Domestic Employees 1800 225 5233
    (24 hrs)

    Airtime charges apply for mobile calls to 1800 service lines.
  • Accompany her to make a complaint personally at:
    MOM Services Centre – Hall A
    1500 Bendemeer Road
    Singapore 339946
