# How many days can a foreign visitor stay in Singapore?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

![Immigration & Checkpoints Authority logo](/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Flogos.ask.gov.sg%2Ficalogo.png&w=3840&q=75)

Updated by ICA 11d ago

If you are eligible for entry, you will receive a Visit Pass or e-Pass which will indicate the period of stay granted. The granting of visit passes to visitors is determined by the Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA) officers at the point of entry.

You may refer to the [e-Pass Enquiry Portal](https://www.ica.gov.sg/redirect-links/e-services/e-services--e-pass-enquiry) (for e-Pass issued) for more information on your stay validity. Alternatively, you will receive a Visit Pass or e-Pass through the email which you have provided when submitting the [SG Arrival Card](https://eservices.ica.gov.sg/arrivalcard/sgac) at the point of entry.

The Visit Pass or e-Pass will expire after the last day of validity or when the traveller departs Singapore.
