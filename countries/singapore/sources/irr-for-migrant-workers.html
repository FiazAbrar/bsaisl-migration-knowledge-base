    Ongoing Programs

    FOR MIGRANT WORKERS

    Here is a list of programs we offer at IRR. If you have any questions or need additional assistance, please feel free to reach out to us on Facebook.

    Airport Transfers

    If you’re a Tuas resident flying back home and have a valid flight ticket, our friendly driving volunteers can provide a ride to the airport. Alternatively, our generous donors may sponsor a cab for you.

    Please contact Lakshmi at +65 9001 4936 to request.

    Bicycles

    Do you live in a remote area with limited access to public transport? Bicycles may be provided to help you travel more easily and get around more conveniently.

    Please note that this is a waitlist, and the waiting time can be more than a year.

    Please sign up here

    Dental Care

    If you’re having minor dental problems like tooth pain, we’re here to help. Our team can arrange a dental appointment for you, completely free of charge. Don’t let small issues become big ones—let us help you take care of your teeth today!

    Please sign up here

    Free Shopping @ inspIRRe

    inspIRRe is a first of its kind FREE store in Singapore located at 217 Henderson Road. Male migrant workers on Work Permits can pick up essential items such as clothes, toiletries, bags, snacks, and electronics for free.

    The sign-up form will only be open on Sundays at 4PM, and can only be filled then.

    Please sign up here

    Gardens by the Bay Tours

    If you’d like to visit Gardens by the Bay, come join us! This offer is open to first-time participants only. Please note, no walk-ins are allowed. All visitors MUST have a booking confirmation from IRR before attending. Tickets will be provided free of charge.

    Please sign up here

    Laptops

    Are you enrolled in a paid higher education or diploma course lasting longer than 4 months and do not have a laptop? We may be able to provide one, subject to meeting the eligibility criteria.

    Please sign up here

    Luggage

    Are you going on home leave and need luggage to bring your items back? We can provide either cabin or check-in preloved luggage a month prior to your flight date.

    Please sign up here

    MADWISH (English Classes)

    We offer free in-person English classes to help you improve your language skills. Our friendly volunteers are here to teach and support you. Whether you’re just starting or want to get better, we’re here to help.

    Please sign up here

    Mandai Zoo Tours

    If you’d like to visit the Singapore Zoo, come join us! This offer is open to first-time participants only. Please note, no walk-ins are allowed. All visitors MUST have a booking confirmation from IRR before attending. Tickets will be provided free of charge.

    Please sign up here

    Vision Care

    Do you need glasses or have trouble seeing clearly? Our eye doctors are here to help with any vision problems you may have. Eye check-ups and spectacles will be provided free of charge.

    Please sign up here
