# Student's Pass Sponsorship

Check if you can sponsor a foreigner to pursue full-time study in Singapore based on the type of educational institutions they have been accepted into.

Last updated 30 Oct 2026



## Sponsorship Requirements

As a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident, you can sponsor a foreigner to study in Singapore if he/she has been accepted by an educational institution for full-time study.

A local sponsor is only required for the following types of educational institutions. Select the institution below for information on the application forms you need to submit:

- [Government/Government-Aided/Independent Schools](/information-and-e-services/long-term-visit-and-study/student-s-pass/student's-pass-application-renewal/government--government-aided--independent-schools)
- [Institutes of Technical Education](/information-and-e-services/long-term-visit-and-study/student-s-pass/student's-pass-application-renewal/institutes-of-technical-education)
