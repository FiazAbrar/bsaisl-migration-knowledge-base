About housing issues for migrant workers

Address registration for Work Pass Holder – Unblock address

This form is for:

OFWAS/EP eService users who encounter an error message as the address is blocked or cannot be used to house foreign employees.

Homeowners who are unable to login to Foreign Worker Tenant Enquiry Service (FWTES) to provide their consent to allow pass holder declarations at this address.

Address registration for Work Pass Holder – HDB occupier

This form is for employers whose workers are occupiers at HDB addresses and are unable to register the workers after:

Property owner has registered the workers as occupiers with HDB

The workers’ FIN number has been updated with HDB

Address registration for Work Pass Holder – Update address type for residential use

This form is for OFWAS/EP eService users who encounter an error message that the address is not approved for residential use, and the employer has relevant documents to show that the premise is approved for residential use by the respective agencies.

Address registration for Work Pass Holder – Maximum occupancy load reached

This form is for property owners / employers / agents acting on behalf of property owners to request:

Update of the correct occupancy load of the premise

Removal of pass holders no longer staying at the address

Address registration for Work Pass Holder – Address not found

This form is for OFWAS/EP eService users who encounter an error message that the postal code, floor or unit number is invalid although it is a valid address. 

Removal of existing tenants 

This form is for property owners who require assistance to remove ex-tenants from their address as they are unable to access MOM’s Foreign Worker Tenant Enquiry Service (FWTES) to remove their ex-tenants/check on the tenants registered at their property. (Property agents may also submit on their behalf.)
