        Enhancements to Work Permit eService on myMOM Portal

    Building on the introduction of Apply and Appeal functions in 2025, Work Permit transactions for migrant workers will continue moving from Work Permit Online to 
myMOM
 Portal through 2027, allowing you to perform transactions on one platform more seamlessly.

From January 2027: Renew Work Permits, manage your organisation's contact details, and view quota

Resources for employers, employment agencies and medical service providers on how to renew Work Permits, manage your organisation's contact details, and view quota details. 

From November 2026: Apply for a Temporary Permit, view and update Work Permit holders' particulars and extend in-principle approvals

Resources for employers and employment agencies on how to apply for a Temporary Permit, view and update Work Permit holders' particulars and employment details, and extend the validity of in-principle approvals.

From July 2026: Cancel Work Permit function on myMOM Portal

Resources for employers and employment agencies to cancel Work Permits, request for Special Passes, and for current employers to provide consent for migrant workers to transfer to a new employer.

From May 2026: Issue Work Permit 

Resources for employers, employment agencies and medical service providers on the process to issue Work Permits for migrant workers.
