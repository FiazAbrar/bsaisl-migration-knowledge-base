# Student's Pass Online Application and Registration (SOLAR) For International Students Pursuing Full-Time Studies (Information accurate as of 5 Oct 2026)

National University of Singapore, user guide (Annex-STP-Application), PDF at https://nusgs.nus.edu.sg/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/annex-stp-application.pdf

### Important Notes for New Graduate Research Students:

• If you accept our offer of admission, the University will apply for the Student's Pass (STP) on your behalf via the Singapore Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA) SOLAR system.

• Please start the STP application only after receiving an email from the University.

• ICA will issue only one STP to a student at any given time. You may apply to multiple institutions, but you can only accept ONE offer.

• If you have previously accepted an offer from another institution but now wish to accept an offer from NUS, you must first inform the other institution to cancel their STP application before submitting your eForm 16.

### Procedure to Submit eForm 16

### Step 1

Go to the ICA website (https://www.ica.gov.sg), click on "Pass Holder", followed by "Apply/Renew Student's Pass".

### Step 2

Select "Institutes of Higher Learning", followed by "Apply Online".

### Step 3

Select "Foreign Student" icon to begin.

Apply online at least two months and not more than three months before course begins.

### Step 4

Enter the required information and click "Login".

STP Application Number: For security reasons, your unique STP application number will be provided to you via email

Please check that your particulars submitted to ICA are correct and correspond exactly with the information printed on your passport. Please inform the University immediately if there are any errors. DO NOT proceed with the STP application.

• Your name must be entered in FULL (i.e. to include first, middle and last name/surname) and must correspond exactly with the name printed on your passport. Initials will not be accepted.

### Step 5

Click "Submit eForm 16" icon and fill in the required information.

c) Please provide a valid "Email Address". All future correspondence, including the issuance of the digital STP (if approved), will be sent to this email address.

d) You may leave the "Singapore Mobile Number (if available at time of application)" and "Residential Address in Singapore (if available)" fields blank if unavailable at the time of application.

### Step 6

After clicking "Next", you will see the "Document Required" page. Please submit the required documents by the deadline indicated. Otherwise, the draft application will be deleted.

a) Signed Form16

Click on the "Download Form 16" button to download the form, sign it and save a scanned copy.

b) Recent Passport Size Photo

The uploaded photo must not be digitally enhanced, as it will affect their ability to use government transactions requiring facial recognition.

c) Travel Document Biodata

You will be required to upload a clear image of the passport biodata page. Blurred images will result in delays and ICA may request another copy to be uploaded.

### Step 7

Upload the required documents and click "Proceed".

Please submit a passport sized colour photograph which is taken within the last three months and against a white background

### Step 8

Verify the information and click "Submit" at the bottom of the page.

Please note that amendments of eForm 16 are not allowed after submission. If you realise that there are mistakes after submission, you may contact ICA for assistance.

DO NOT click "withdraw" button on the ICA portal unless you are instructed to do so. Clicking the "Withdraw" button will withdraw your STP application.

### Step 9

Click "Make Payment" to pay the $45 non-refundable processing fee by the deadline indicated. Please note that your application will only be processed after the payment is made.

### Step 10

The processing time is typically within one week (or up to two weeks if a visa is required), excluding the date of submission.

Please check your email and login to ICA SOLAR system regularly for updates. If you are required to upload any clarification document(s), you must do so within 14 days of the email notification. Failure to do so will result in the STP application being withdrawn. Please also check your Junk / Spam folder.

### Step 11

Once your Student's Pass application is approved, click on the "Print Outcome Letter" to obtain your In-Principle-Approval (IPA) letter. a) The IPA letter must be presented to the Immigration Officer upon arrival in Singapore. b) For visa-required nationals, the IPA letter also serves as a single-entry visa.

### After IPA

Step 1: Click "Terms & Conditions" to complete the Terms & Conditions of Student's Pass (STP) Acknowledgement.

Step 2: Upload the completed Medical Examination Report.

a) The ICA-prescribed Medical Examination Report must be completed by a registered medical doctor before it is uploaded to the ICA SOLAR system. New applicants can undergo the medical examination in their home country. Those submitting an application for renewal / transfer must have their examination done in Singapore.

Incomplete or blank medical reports will result in the IPA being withdrawn. A new application will need to be submitted for consideration.

You must ensure that the Medical Examination Report meets the following requirements before uploading:

i. Student's information (as shown in the travel document) is correctly filled in

ii. Clinic's stamp is endorsed

iii. Examining Doctor's name and date of medical examination (no more than 3 months ago) clearly stated

iv. An official English translation is provided, if written in a foreign language

b) ICA requires up to 7 working days to process the above-mentioned documents.

Step 4: Once ICA approves the documents, you will be notified via email to make payment for the issuance fee and multiple-journey visa fee (if applicable).

Step 5: Make an e-appointment for the Completion of Formalities (COF) for the Student's Pass at least one day after the payment has been made (if applicable).

b) Students who walk in without an e-appointment during the OSE period will be turned away.

c) Walk-ins to ICA Services Centre without an appointment will be turned away.

d) If your e-appointment date is beyond the validity of Short-Term Visit Pass (STVP), please extend your STVP at ICA website to avoid overstaying. Please note that overstaying without a valid pass is an offence under the Immigration Act.

For any queries regarding Student's Pass Online Application, please contact ICA by submitting the ICA online contact form.
