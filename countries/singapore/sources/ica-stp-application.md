# Student's Pass Application/Renewal

Check if you need a Student's Pass to study full-time in Singapore and select your educational institution to read its specific requirements before applying or renewing. 

Last updated 29 Oct 2026



## Requirements

 

If you are a foreigner, you must apply for or renew a Student's Pass to pursue full-time studies in Singapore.

Before you apply or renew, you must meet the following pre-requisites:

- **Approved courses:** You must be accepted into an approved full-time course. You cannot apply for a Student's Pass for part-time, evening, or weekend courses.
- **Vaccination requirements:** If you are a foreign-born applicant aged 12 and below, you must submit your vaccination information to the Communicable Diseases Agency (CDA). Visit the [CDA website](/redirect-links/cda-nir) for the details.
