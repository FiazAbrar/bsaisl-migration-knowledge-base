# Key facts on Employment Pass

Get a quick overview of the Employment Pass (EP) to decide if it is the right pass for you. 

## Pass facts

[TABLE]

## Features of the pass

The key features of the pass are as follows:

- Availability of [passes for eligible family members.](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/passes-for-family)
- No foreign worker levy or quota required.

Pass holders may be required to pay [income tax](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/new-to-tax/working-out-your-tax-residency).

## Pass map

Overview of what you need to do before, during and after you apply for an Employment Pass.

## Before applying

- ### 

  - ![consider all candidates fairly](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/consider-all-candidates-fairly.png)
    Advertise on MyCareersFuture and [consider all candidates fairly](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/consider-all-candidates-fairly).
  - ![know requirements](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/know-requirements.png)
    Know the [eligibility and requirements](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/eligibility) to apply.
  - ![know requirements](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/know-requirements.png)
    Find out the [documents required](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/documents-required) for applying.

## Before candidate's arrival

- ### 

  - ![Apply](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/apply.png)
    [Apply for the EP](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/apply-for-a-pass) and wait for the outcome.
  - ![Apply](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/apply.png)
    (If your pass is approved) Get an in-principle approval letter.
  - ![medical](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/medical.png)
    Ensure that the candidate complies with the latest [travel requirements](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore).

## Upon candidate's arrival

- ### 

  - ![issuance](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/inssuance.png)
    [Get the pass issued](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/apply-for-a-pass#get-the-pass-issued) and receive a notification letter stating whether registration is required.
  - ![register fingerprints](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/register-fingerprints.png)
    (If required) Make an appointment for the candidate to [register their fingerprints and photo](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/apply-for-a-pass#register-fingerprints-and-photo).
  - ![receive card](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/receive-card.png)
    [Receive the pass card](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/apply-for-a-pass#receive-the-card) at the address provided 5 working days after registration or document verification.

## As and when required

- ### 

  - ![notify mom](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/notify-mom.png)
    [Notify MOM](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/notify-mom-of-changes) if the company's or pass holder's information changes.
  - ![renewal](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/renewal.png)
    [Renew the pass](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/renew-a-pass) before it expires.
  - ![replace card](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/replace-card.png)
    [Apply for a replacement](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/replace-a-pass-card) if the pass card is lost, stolen or damaged.
  - ![cancel pass or permit](/-/media/mom/img/pass-map/process-icons---desktop/cancel-pass-or-permit.png)
    [Cancel the pass](/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/cancel-a-pass) if the pass holder no longer works for you.
