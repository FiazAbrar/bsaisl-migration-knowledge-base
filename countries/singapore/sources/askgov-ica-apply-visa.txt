How do I apply for a Visa?
Updated by 
ICA

1y ago
26
 found this helpful
ICA does not accept submission of visa application over the immigration counters.
You can apply for a visa by using our 
e-Service
.
If you do not have a Local Contact with a Singpass/ Corppass account, you may submit the visa application:
Through an authorised visa agent appointed by the Singapore Overseas Missions; or
At the nearest 
Singapore Overseas Mission
Please liaise directly with the nearest 
Singapore Overseas Mission
 for the detailed visa application procedure if the application is to be submitted through their authorised visa agents or at the Singapore Overseas Mission.
