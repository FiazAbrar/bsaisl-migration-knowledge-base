## **FAQs**

### [CAN I GET A LEARNING LICENSE WITHOUT SPONSRS LETTER OR SIGNATURE](#collapse160)

For the the first issuance of the learning license, you must get your sponsor's approval.

### [Can I convert my Philippine driver's license to Omani license?](#collapse158)

No you can't swap your Philippine driving license for an Omani one.

### [How long can I use my country's driver's license here in Oman with a short visit visa?](#collapse157)

You can drive a car in Oman as long as have a visit visa valid for 3 months. In case you intend to reside in the country, you must get an Omani driving license.

### [What age is the ROP granting licence for Omani citizens and Non-Omani?](#collapse156)

Minimum age for Granting Omani Driving licence:

- For Omani citizens : (18 years)
- For Non-Omani : (18 years old)
