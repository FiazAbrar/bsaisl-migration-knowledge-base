								Get Permit to Work at Noon During Summer

Service that enables establishments to apply for a permit to work during the midday period from 1 June to 31 August between 12:30 PM and 3:30 PM, in accordance with approved occupational safety and health regulations and standards, to ensure the protection of workers from the risks associated with exposure to high temperatures.

Special conditions

Provide workers with enough cold drinking water
Set up a shaded rest area near the worksite, closed from all sides, furnished and air-conditioned for all workers 
Provide first aid supplies
Provide water for washing, with covered tanks to keep it cool
Keep an air-conditioned bus near the worksite
Follow any other conditions required by the job circumstances
