33
Property Owner Visa
It is granted by the competent authority without a sponsor to a foreigner who owns a real estate unit, based on a certificate issued by the competent authority. It is also granted to the legal representatives of the legal person that owns a real estate unit. The holder shall enter the Sultanate of Oman within three (3) months of the visa issuance date. The stay period is two (2) years.
