# ROP eVisa (evisa.rop.gov.om): Full conditions of visa 28B GCC Citizen Companion Visa

Saved 2026-11-09 by submitting the public form on evisa.rop.gov.om/visa-eligibility and extracting the result text.

28B GCC Citizen Companion Visa
 List of Countries for 28B GCC Citizen Companion Visa
 Visit to Oman by a person who has current residency in Bahrain, Qatar or United Arab Emirates and who is accompanied by a GCC citizen.
 Conditions
 Fee
 There is no fee for this visa. 
 Applicant
 Must be outside the Sultanate of Oman at time of application. 
 Applicant
 Must be a resident of a GCC country, with at least 6 months residency in the country remaining on entry to Oman. 
 Applicant
 Must be accompanied by a GCC citizen. 
 Applicant
 Must be either a domestic servant of, or a family member of the GCC citizen. 
 Applicant
 Must be a resident of Bahrain, or Qatar or UAE. 
 Sponsor
 This visa does not require a sponsor in Oman. 
 Submit
 Applications for this visa can be submitted on arrival at the border in Oman. 
 Document
 A photograph of the applicant (digital)
 Document
 A copy of the applicant's passport.
 Document
 Proof of the family relationship. (May not be required if the names and passport details indicate the relationship between the foreign resident and the joining family member).
 Document
 A copy of applicants Residency card
 Document
 A copy of the sponsor passport or ID card (must be valid for more than 6 months)
 Grant
 Must not have other current Oman visa. 
 Entry
 Passport must be valid for at least 6 months on entry to Oman.
 Entry
 Traveller must enter Oman with the GCC citizen. 
 Entry
 Traveller must enter Oman directly from a GCC country.
 Entry
 Visa can only be used for a single entry to Oman.
 Entry
 A female servant can only enter Oman if the accompanying employer is also accompanied by his family 
 Stay
 Length of stay in Oman is 1 month. 
 Stay
 If the visitor overstays in Oman, there is a fine of OMR 10.000 for each day of overstay. 
 Extension/ Renewal
 Extension of stay allowed is 1 month. 
 Extension/ Renewal
 The stay in Oman can be extended once only.
 General
 Possession of a valid visa does not guarantee that a traveller will be granted entry to Oman. Entry to Oman is at the discretion of the Royal Oman Police officer at the port of entry.
