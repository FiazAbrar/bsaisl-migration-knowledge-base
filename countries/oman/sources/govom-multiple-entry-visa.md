# Get Multiple Entry Visa

This service allows the responsible authority to grant a visa to foreigners who wish to enter the Sultanate multiple times. The visa permits the holder to enter and stay in the country for the specified duration in it..

## Special conditions

- The passport must be valid for more than 6 months.
- The applicant should be outside the country.
- The applicant must not have another valid visa.
- This visa allows the holder to enter the country during its validity period and stay for up to 3 months each time.
