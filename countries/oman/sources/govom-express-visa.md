# Get an Express Visa

This service enables the competent authority to issue an express visa upon request by a local employer for skilled professionals and businesspersons. The visa is provided through Omani representatives abroad in coordination with the relevant authority.

## Special conditions

- The applicant must be a senior professional or a businessperson.
- The applicant must be outside the country when applying for the visa.
- The applicant must not have another valid visa.
- The applicant's passport must be valid for no less than (6) months.
