Post Arrival Medical Fitness Exam Service

Start Service

Service Provider

Target Audience

Business owners and individuals wishing to import expatriate workers.
Private sector hospitals and centers that provide labor screening services for expatriates. 

Service Delivery Channels

eHealth Portal

Sanad Services Center

Service Completion Period

10 minutes

Details

The service of obtaining a medical examination form for workers, to complete the procedures for obtaining or renewing residency cards, obtaining a visa, or obtaining citizenship.

User Manual 

Service Steps

1.  Visit eHealth Portal
2.  Log in to the system or register a new account in the system using the PKI feature of the mobile phone or ID card.
Note: You can follow the steps for PKI for a mobile phone via the following link in the Tam service: 
Digital certification Portal 
3.  Enter the required data. After verifying the data, you can proceed to the payment process.
4. Visit the medical center
5. Visit the nearest medical center and view the QR code that you received via email or text message after completing the payment process.

Required Documents

                                    ID card 

                                    Passport 

Terms and Conditions

You must enter the system using the user’s ID card(PKI)

The form remains valid for 30 days from the date of extraction

It is not possible for those under 18 years of age to register for a work visa.

Children under 16 years old are not required to submit the form to obtain a visit or residence visa

Fees Details

30 Riyals for non-food handler

40 Riyals for food handler
