# Get Joining Relatives Visa

This service enables you to obtain residence for a husband, wife or children (under 21 years), and to accompany a foreign resident, landlord or investor working in the Sultanate of Oman

## Required documents

- Digital photographs of the applicant
- Copy of the applicant's passport
- Copy of passport and foreigner resident's card to be joined by his family
- Proof of relationship with resident
- Letter from the employer assuming responsibility for the applicant's visa during his stay in the Sultanate
