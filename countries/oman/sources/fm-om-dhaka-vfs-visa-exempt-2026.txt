Bangladesh: Ambassador  meets with State Minister for Foreign Affairs

10 May 2026

Jameel Albalushi Ambassador of the Sultanate of Oman to Bangladesh
, paid a courtesy call in Dhaka on 
Ms. Shama Obaed Islam, State Minister at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Bangladesh.

The meeting discussed strengthening cooperation in trade, investment, culture, sports, and the labour sector, reaffirming a commitment to further advancing Bangladesh-Oman relations.
