Labour Complaints

Labour disputes support system

Indian nationals with grievances related to salary dues, non-payment  of wages, medical treatment, leave, boarding & lodging, harassment, over time  and  other labour disputes, as well as  issues pertaining to court and police cases   can approach  Community Welfare  Wing of the Embassy. They can either visit the Embassy in person , or contact  via telephone, email or other social media platforms..

During  office hours (7:00 AM to 3:30 PM), please contact

General number: +968-24684500

Community Welfare Wing:  +968-24684567

Attaché (CW): +968-24684570,  +968-24684522

24/7  Toll Free Helpline No. 80071234

Email Id: 
cw[dot]muscat[at]mea[dot]gov[dot]in

Open  House

Embassy conducts an Open House on every month last Friday in hybrid mode, where Indian nationals with their issues and concerns can meet and interact with Ambassador in person or virtually without prior appointment. Community Welfare and Consular officials are also present in the Open House where the aggrieved can register/seek redressal of their grievances.
