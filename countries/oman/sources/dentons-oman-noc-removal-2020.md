# Expatriate employees will not require NOC in Oman but T&Cs apply

June 10, 2020

On 31 May 2020, Lieutenant General Hassan Mohsin Al Shuraiqi, the Inspector-General of Police and Customs, announced a decision of the Royal Oman Police 157/2020 (Decision), which permits the transfer of an expatriate employee to another employer in Oman, in cases where the employment contract with the existing employer has ended. This Decision shall be published in the Official Gazette and will be implemented from 1 January 2021.

The Decision, once published, will amend Article 24 of the executive regulations of the Law on the Residence of Foreigners. Since 2014, the Law on the Residence of Foreigners does not permit the transfer of an expatriate employee to another employer unless the employee has obtained a no-objection certificate (NOC) from the existing employer. This law requires that, if the expatriate employee does not obtain an NOC from their current employer to join another company in Oman, the expatriate employee is banned from working in Oman for any other employer for two years.

Article 1 of the Decision provides: Article 24 of the executive regulations of the Law on the Residence of Foreigners is replaced by the following text:

"It is permissible for a foreign employee to transfer from one employer to another, provided the employer has a licence to recruit expatriate employees, and on condition that they provide proof of the end or termination of the employment contract. They must also provide evidence of the approval of the competent government authority on allowing the signed contract with the second employer and it must be in accordance with the controls set by the competent authority."
