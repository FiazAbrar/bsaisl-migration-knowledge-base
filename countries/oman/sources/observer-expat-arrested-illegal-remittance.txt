Expat arrested for illegal remittance service

Observer Web Team

                                        Published: 1:15 PM, Aug 18, 2026

                    SHARE

An Asian national was arrested for conducting international money transfers to a number of expatriate workers through unlicensed applications in exchange for financial gain.
The arrest was carried out by Al Buraimi Governorate Police Command, and legal procedures are being completed against him.
