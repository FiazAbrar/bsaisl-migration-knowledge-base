Golden Residency

For business owners, entrepreneurs, and investors looking to establish or expand their ventures in Oman.
Who Can Apply?

You may qualify if you:

  • Start or invest in a business.
  • Own property in tourism zones
  • Buy bonds or listed shares
  • Hold a fixed-term deposit
  • Employ 50+ Omanis
  • Are nominated by an invested company

Why Oman?

At the crossroads of Asia, Africa, and the Middle East, Oman offers unmatched access, long-term stability, and a globally ranked quality of life.
Strategic Location

Oman boasts a 3,165 km coastline, opening to the Arabian Sea, Gulf of Oman, and the Indian Ocean. This prime position makes it a natural hub for global trade, logistics, and maritime connectivity

Economic and Political Stability

Oman’s currency (OMR) is the 3rd strongest, pegged to the US dollar, ensuring stability. Its neutral policy and low risk create a secure business environment

Economic Vision

Guided by Oman Vision 2040, the country is committed to sustainable growth, economic diversification, and innovation.

Business-Friendly Environment

With streamlined processes, competitive costs, and world-class infrastructure, Oman makes it easy to start and grow your business.

Cultural Richness

Oman’s rich heritage, warm hospitality, and high quality of life create a welcoming environment for investors and their families.
