## Bangladesh: Ambassador receives representatives of VFS Global

22 May 2026

[Jameel Albalushi, Ambassador of the Sultanate of Oman to the People’s Republic of Bangladesh,](https://www.fm.gov.om/en/missions/om-bd-dhaka-em/) met at the embassy in Dhaka with representatives of [VFS Global.](https://www.vfsglobal.com/en/individuals/index.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The meeting discussed existing cooperation in electronic attestation services and reviewed mechanisms for organising the processing of visa exempt applications for business and tourism categories, in a way that enhances procedures, improves the quality of consular services and facilitates them for beneficiaries.

The meeting also touched on opportunities to strengthen cooperation in the fields of investment and tourism in Oman.

This is an unofficial English version of an Arabic report. To view the official Arabic text, [click here](https://www.fm.gov.om/ar/46375/).
