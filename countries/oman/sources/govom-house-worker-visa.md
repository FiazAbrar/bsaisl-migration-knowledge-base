# Get House worker Visa

This service enables the employer to get a visa for a domestic worker based on a license from the Ministry of Labour, unless the employer is a government entity.

## Special conditions

- The visa holder must be at least (21) years old at the time of application submission.
- The applicant must not hold any other valid visa for the Sultanate of Oman.
- The required documents must be valid to complete the visa application.
- The applicant's residency in Oman is valid for up to two years and can be renewed for the same duration through the Ministry of Labour.
- Fees are not refundable.
- The visa applicant must have a passport valid for no less than (6) months.
