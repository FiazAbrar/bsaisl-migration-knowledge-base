How to Apply for E-Passsport

Content: Pages

How to Apply:

  1. Please fill-in the online e-passport application. Please carefully check the spelling & accuracy of the information you have provided.

  2. Head to over amardutabashmuscat.org to book an appointment for passport enrolment.

  3. Make the online payment for your passport fees. Please click here to see the applicable fees.

  4. Take a print out of the following:

  • Appointment paper

  • Submitted online application form with barcode

  • Proof of payment (Bank Slip)

  • Passport copy

  • Oman labour card copy

  • Birth Registration Certificate (BRC) OR National Identity Card (NID) copy

  • For students, last payment receipt (not exceeding 06 months) from the educational institution.

  1. Visit our office as per the scheduled appointment with full application package. In-person visit by the applicant (over age of 6) is mandatory to provide biometric data (live photo, fingerprint, iris etc,)

  2. Newborn babies and children below the age of 06 (six) years are exempt from in-person attendance. You must bring a recently taken 3R sized color photograph (Lab Printed) with white background of the child, Birth Registration Certificate (BRC) of the child and Passport/NID/BRC copy of parents.

  3. Please wait for your turn for biometric enrollment. Biometric enrollment often requires longer than expected time. Therefore, your waiting time may be longer depending on the length of queue (sometimes several hours). You are requested to visit our office having sufficient time at hand.

  4. After successful enrolment, you will be handed over a delivery slip which you retain carefully. It will be required for any further query, receiving the printed passport etc.

Collection/Delivery of Printed Passport:

Once you receive a message on your mobile phone you may:

  • Apply with Oman Post for OMR 1.050 to receive your passport within 7 working days. (Any application made sooner will be dismissed)

  • Book an appointment on amardutabashmuscat.org and come to the Embassy on the appointed time slot.

  • You may also come on any Saturday from 9.00 am to 3.00 pm for collection without prior appointment.

If you do not receive a message within the estimated date, you may check the status of your passport here.

Please carefully note the following:

  • If you had previously applied with a NID, the new E-passport MUST be applied with NID as well. If not, your application will be rejected.

  • According to the latest circular of the Ministry of Home Affairs, the age correction using a BRC compared to the previous MRP should not exceed 08 (eight) years.

  • No application will be accepted without prior appointment.

  • The Embassy of Bangladesh, Muscat reserves all rights to accept/ reject an e-Passport application and request for any additional document(s) at any stage of e-Passport process for necessary scrutiny.
