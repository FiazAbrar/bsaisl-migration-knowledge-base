								Get Family Visa

This service allows foreigners, at the discretion of the competent authorities and upon the employer's request and responsibility, to obtain a family visa for relatives, such as the foreign employee's parents, siblings, or relatives of an Omani citizen, etc.

								Royal Oman Police

							Updated:
						December 28, 2025

										You are starting the service as:

												Personal account

												gup-business-account-label

										Required documents

Personal photo
Copy of the relative's passport
Copy of the resident’s passport and resident card
Proof of family relationship for residency sponsorship
Copy of employment contract
Application letter
