								Get GCC Resident Visa

This service enables approved professionals residing in GCC countries and their companions to get a tourist visa to visit the Sultanate of Oman.

								Royal Oman Police

Special conditions

The applicant must have a passport valid for no less than 6 months

The applicant must hold a valid residency in one of the GCC countries for no less than 3 months
The applicant must be employed by any institution
