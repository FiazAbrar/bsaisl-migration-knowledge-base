Establishment 

of 

(OHRC) 

The Omani Human Rights Commission (OHRC) was established as per to a Royal Decree No.124/2008 as an independent national entity to disseminate the culture of human rights among all segment of society and protect human rights as per national and international laws in the Sultanate.
