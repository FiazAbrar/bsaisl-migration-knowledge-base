# Newspaper - Oman: Labour Ministry launches inspections to enforce midday work ban [Lexis Middle East]

Source: https://www.lexismiddleeast.com/news/2026-06-09_49/en (Firecrawl scrape, 2026-11-09). Summary of The Arabian Stories, 9 June 2026.

## Summary

The Arabian Stories, 9 June 2026: The Ministry of Labour in Oman has intensified enforcement of the annual midday work ban, warning construction firms and outdoor employers of fines up to RO 1,000 as temperatures approach 50°C.

Inspection teams have been deployed nationwide to monitor compliance with the three‑month ban, which prohibits outdoor work between 12:30 p.m. and 3:30 p.m. during June, July, and August.

Officials confirmed that violators face fines ranging from RO 500 to RO 1,000, alongside administrative penalties under labour law. The ministry stressed that **inspection campaigns** are ongoing across governorates, targeting construction sites, industrial zones, and other outdoor workplaces.

The ban is designed to protect workers from heat stress, dehydration, and exhaustion. Employers are required to adjust schedules, provide shaded rest areas, ensure access to drinking water, and strictly adhere to occupational safety rules.
