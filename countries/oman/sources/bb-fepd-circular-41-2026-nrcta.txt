              Opening and Operation of Non-Resident Convertible Taka Accounts (NRCTA)

Dear Sirs,

         Please refer to FEPD-1 Circular No. 14, dated June 23, 2026, regarding the opening, operation, and
permissible uses of Non-Resident Convertible Taka Accounts (NRCTAs) for Non-Resident Bangladeshis
(NRBs).

         02. With a view to broadening the scope and enhancing the utility of NRCTAs, it has been decided,
upon review of the provisions of FEPD-1 Circular No. 14/2026, to modify the said circular as follows:

         A. Eligibility and account features

         (1) NRCTAs may be opened and maintained by NRBs with Offshore Banking Units (OBUs) and
Authorized Dealers (ADs) of scheduled banks in any form, including savings, current or fixed deposit
accounts, against inward remittances and other permissible sources as specified in Section B below.

         (2) In addition to existing account-opening procedures applicable to NRBs, Bangladeshi nationals
intending to proceed abroad for employment may open NRCTAs without initial deposit before their
departure. NRCTAs may also be opened by NRBs during their temporary visit to Bangladesh, subject to
compliance with applicable account-opening and documentation requirements.

         B. Permissible fund transfer to NRCTAs

         (1) NRCTAs may be credited with:

         (a) inward remittances received through banking channels and transfers from private foreign
currency accounts/non-resident foreign currency deposit accounts, including remittances received through
authorized exchange houses, money transfer operators (MTOs), and other legitimate payment service
providers, upon conversion of foreign currency into Taka at the prevailing exchange rate;

         (b) transfers from other NRCTAs;

         (c) interest/profit accruing on funds held in such accounts;

         (d) repatriable funds derived from foreign direct investment, foreign portfolio investment or other
investments permitted by Bangladesh Bank, where the underlying investment was originally made from
NRCTAs or inward remittances;

         (e) refunds of share subscriptions to new issues of Bangladeshi companies, or portions thereof,
where the subscription amount was paid from NRCTAs or by debit to private foreign currency
accounts/non-resident foreign currency deposit accounts;

         (f) other refunds where the original payment was made by debit to NRCTAs; and

         (g) any other payment eligible under general or special authorization of Bangladesh Bank.

         (2) In case of inward remittances received through ADs, the designated foreign currency funds shall
be transferred to the respective OBUs through the BD-RTGS System, unless the respective OBU accepts the
equivalent amount in Taka.

         C. Nature, operation and repatriation

         (1) NRCTAs maintained in deposit form shall be market-driven interest/profit-bearing accounts
denominated in Taka. The rates offered on NRCTAs shall be comparable with domestic Taka deposits.

         (2) Both principal and accrued interest/profit shall be freely repatriable abroad.

         (3) Balances held in NRCTAs may be utilized for:

         (a) legitimate local disbursements;
         (b) transfers to other NRCTAs or Non-Resident Taka Accounts (NRTAs);
         (c) transfers to private foreign currency accounts or non-resident foreign currency deposit accounts
in equivalent foreign currency;
         (d) purchase of shares under foreign direct investment (FDI), or transfer to Non-Resident Investors'
Taka Accounts (NITAs) for portfolio investment, or placement of funds in such investments covered by
regulations permitting repatriation of investment proceeds, including profit/earnings, abroad;
         (e) foreign currency notes in equivalent foreign currency to account holders while on return visit
from Bangladesh, subject to applicable limits; and
         (4) Incentives on inward remittances intended for credit to NRCTAs will generally not apply, unless
otherwise specifically specified.

         (5) Income earned through business done or services rendered in Bangladesh shall not be credited to
NRCTAs.

         D. Utilization and placement of NRCTA funds by OBUs

         (1) OBUs may utilize NRCTA funds for extending Taka-denominated financing to Type A
industrial enterprises in specialized zones only for admissible current expenses in Bangladesh, including
salaries, wages and utility payments, and shall be repaid exclusively from their export proceeds.

         (2) For utilization through DBUs, OBUs may place NRCTA funds in Taka accounts maintained
with their respective DBUs. Such accounts and placements shall be separately identifiable and accounted for
as NRCTA-related funds.

         (3) Taka funds placed by an OBU with its DBU out of NRCTA proceeds shall be adjustable against
the applicable regulatory limit governing placement/transfer of foreign currency by the DBU with the OBU.

         (4) OBUs may sell foreign currency received as NRCTA deposits to their respective DBUs and
credit the equivalent Taka proceeds to the respective NRCTAs at the prevailing exchange rate.

         (5) OBUs may purchase foreign currency from their respective DBUs against Taka balances held in
NRCTAs for repatriation by account holders or for other permissible foreign exchange transactions.

         E. Utilization of NRCTA funds by ADs

         ADs may utilize funds mobilized through NRCTAs for extending Taka-denominated financing to
eligible customers as regular Taka lending, subject to applicable credit norms, prudential requirements, risk
management guidelines and other instructions of Bangladesh Bank.

         F. Lending against NRCTA deposits as lien

         (1) Banks may extend Taka-denominated loans against NRCTA deposits maintained with
OBUs/DBUs as lien for NRBs and/or their designated third parties for personal or legitimate business
purposes, including non-repatriable direct investment in Bangladesh and acquisition of residential property
for own use, subject to applicable regulations.

         (2) Such loans shall not be used for relending, agricultural or plantation activities, or real estate
business. There shall be no direct or indirect foreign exchange consideration for pledging NRCTA deposits.

         (3) Repayment shall be made through adjustment of NRCTA deposits or fresh inward remittances
through banking channels. In case of loans disbursed to designated third party, adjustments can also be made
from eligible local sources, subject to applicable regulations.

         (4) Usual credit norms and considerations shall apply to such credit facilities.

         G. Customer acquisition and facilitation Services

         Prior approval of Bangladesh Bank shall be required for an OBU/AD intending to avail customer
acquisition and facilitation services from a service provider located outside Bangladesh. The application for
such approval shall be submitted subject to, among others, fulfillment of the following conditions:

         (1) a draft agreement/contract with the service provider, duly approved by the Board of Directors of
the concerned bank;

         (2) proposed arrangement shall comply with the applicable provisions of BRPD Circular No. 02,
dated January 19, 2015 as amended from time to time; and

         (3) arrangement shall be entered into on an arm's-length basis and on commercially reasonable and
competitive terms.

         H. Other operational matters
         (1) ADs/OBUs may permit operation of NRCTAs through power of attorney (PoA) or other legal
authority granted by NRBs to resident individuals, subject to applicable regulations.
         (2) In the event of death of an account holder, banks may allow remittance of funds lying in
NRCTAs to the non-resident nominee, subject to the account agreement and applicable regulations.
         (3) NRCTAs may be maintained after permanent return of account holders to Bangladesh. Such
accounts may be credited with retirement benefits, pensions, superannuation benefits or other bonafide
income from external sources. Balances held in such accounts may be used for settlement of legitimate
payments abroad, subject to documentary evidence and deduction of applicable taxes.
         (4) All transactions shall be executed at the prevailing exchange rate applicable to the respective
currency at the time of the transaction.

         I. Accounting, compliance and reporting
         (1) NRCTA funds and financing extended there from shall be separately accounted for by respective
banks so that their source and utilization can be distinctly identified.
         (2) Banks maintaining NRCTAs shall ensure compliance with applicable account-opening and
maintenance requirements, KYC procedures, AML/CFT standards, tax regulations, prudential requirements
and reporting instructions issued by Bangladesh Bank.
         (3) Banks are encouraged to offer NRCTAs with online account opening and electronic/online
banking facilities, subject to applicable regulations. Prior to launching such facilities, banks shall notify
Bangladesh Bank of product features, operational modalities, risk management arrangements and
compliance framework.

         J. Supersession and savings
         (1) FEPD-1 Circular No. 14, dated June 23, 2026, shall stand superseded by this circular.
         (2) NRCTAs opened and transactions undertaken under the superseded circular shall remain valid
and shall be governed by the provisions of this circular.
