Oman extends mandatory sick leave and extraordinary leave insurance to expatriates

13 July 2026

1 min read

By 
Hamood Al Rawahi

Related topics

At a glance

Oman will extend compulsory sick leave and extraordinary leave insurance coverage to expatriate employees from 19 July 2026.

The change is introduced through Oman Decision No. 13 of 2026, issued by the Social Protection Fund.

Participation in the insurance scheme will become mandatory for expatriate workers in both the public and private sectors.

The requirement applies to employees working in the State Administrative Apparatus, other public legal entities, and private sector establishments.

The development expands social protection coverage for expatriate workers and places new compliance obligations on employers.

From 19 July 2026, expatriate employees in Oman will be required to participate in compulsory sick leave and extraordinary leave insurance schemes under Oman Decision No. 13 of 2026, issued by the Social Protection Fund.

The measure extends insurance coverage to expatriate workers employed across the public and private sectors, including those working within the State Administrative Apparatus, other public legal entities, and private sector establishments. As participation in the scheme will be mandatory, affected employers should ensure they are prepared to comply with the new requirements before the implementation date.
