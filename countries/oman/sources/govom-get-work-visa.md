# Get a Work Visa

This service allows the employer to issue a visa to a foreigner coming to work in the Sultanate of Oman, based on their request and under their responsibility. This visa is granted under a license from the Ministry of Labour, unless the employer is a government entity.

## Special conditions

- The sponsor must be a local, fully qualified individual and is responsible for the accuracy of the information provided in the application.
- The applicant for the work visa must be at least (21) years old.
- The gender of the sponsored individual must align with the gender specified on the work permit issued by the Ministry of Labour.
- The profession on the work permit must match the profession on the visa application.
