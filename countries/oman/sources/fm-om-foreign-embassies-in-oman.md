# Foreign embassies and consulates in Oman

This page provides information about foreign Embassies and Consulates in Oman. It tells you how to contact the missions of countries which provide services in Oman. To contact representatives of international organisations in Oman please see the page on [multilateral relations](https://www.fm.gov.om/en/policy/multilateral-relations/).

For a more detailed search, enter search terms – such as a city or ambassador's name – in the search box and click the search button.
