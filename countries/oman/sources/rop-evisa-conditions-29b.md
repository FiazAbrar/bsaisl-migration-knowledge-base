# ROP eVisa (evisa.rop.gov.om): Full conditions of visa 29B GCC Resident Visa

Saved 2026-11-09 by submitting the public form on evisa.rop.gov.om/visa-eligibility and extracting the result text.

29B GCC Resident Visa
 List of Countries for 29B GCC Resident Visa
 Visit to Oman for a person who resides in the GCC and is accompanied by another GCC resident who is on the list of approved professions.
 Conditions
 Fee
 The fee for this visa is OMR 5.000.
 Applicant
 Must be outside the Sultanate of Oman at time of application. 
 Applicant
 Must be a resident of a GCC country, with at least 6 months residency in the country remaining on entry to Oman. 
 Applicant
 Must be accompanied by a GCC resident on the list of approved professions, and must be either a domestic servant of, or a family member of that GCC resident.

here
 to see list of approved professions
 Sponsor
 This visa does not require a sponsor in Oman. 
 Document
 A photograph of the applicant (digital)
 Document
 A copy of the applicant's passport.
 Document
 Visa Copy of the main applicant
 Grant
 Must not have other current Oman visa. 
 Entry
 Passport must be valid for at least 6 months on entry to Oman.
 Entry
 Traveller must enter Oman with the eligible GCC resident. 
 Entry
 Visa can only be used for a single entry to Oman.
 Entry
 A female servant can only enter Oman if the accompanying employer is also accompanied by his family 
 Stay
 Length of stay in Oman is 4 weeks. 
 Stay
 If the visitor overstays in Oman, there is a fine of OMR 10.000 for each day of overstay. 
 Extension/ Renewal
 First time Extension of Stay can be done by the applicant. Second and subsequent extensions can only be done by visiting a DGPCS office.
 Extension/ Renewal
 Extension of stay allowed is 1 week. 
 General
 Possession of a valid visa does not guarantee that a traveller will be granted entry to Oman. Entry to Oman is at the discretion of the Royal Oman Police officer at the port of entry.
