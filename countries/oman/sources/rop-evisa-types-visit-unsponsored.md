36
Visit Visa Multi-Entry 1 year
Granted by the competent authority to nationals of designated countries, upon their request, for a period of one year. It is also granted by the Sultanate's representations abroad or upon the request and under the responsibility of a local employer authorized to conduct tourism activities. Holders of this visa may enter the Sultanate more than once during its validity period, provided that the duration of stay each time does not exceed one month.
Tourism
29
GCC Resident Visa
Granted by the competent authority to foreign residents of GCC countries who wish to visit the Sultanate for tourism purposes, provided they hold professions specified by the authority. It does not require a sponsor. This visa allows its holder a single entry into the Sultanate and a stay of four weeks, extendable for one week.
Tourism
20
Oman / Qatar Common Visa
Visit to Oman by a person who is on the list of eligible nationalities, and who must travel first to Oman then to Qatar directly.
Tourism
21
Dubai / Oman Common Visa
Allows a visit to Oman for a person who is on the list of eligible nationalities, and who has a current tourist visa which was used to enter the Emirate of Dubai, and now wishes to enter Oman directly from Dubai.
Tourism
26
Tourist Visit Visa
Granted by the competent authority to nationals of specified countries, upon their request, for a period of ten (10) days, fourteen (14) days or one month subject to the conditions specified by the competent authority.
Tourism
28
GCC Citizen Companion Visa
Granted to foreign residents of GCC countries, in categories determined by the competent authority, provided they enter the Sultanate accompanied by their sponsor or a member of their family. It may also be granted to the foreign wife of a GCC citizen, provided she enters the Sultanate accompanied by her husband or a member of his family. The visa allows the holder a single entry into the Sultanate and a stay of one month, renewable for another month.
Tourism
23
Airport Transit Visa
Granted by the competent authority at airports to a foreigner upon a request submitted by the carrier airline. It allows the holder a single entry into the country and a stay of up to 72 hours, provided they have sufficient funds to cover their expenses and a valid onward ticket.
Transit
27
Short Transit Visa
Granted by the Sultanate's representations abroad in coordination with the competent authority. It may also be granted at land border posts designated by the competent authority, in accordance with to the regulations and procedures it establishes. The visa is valid for a period not exceeding six months and allows the holder to transit through the Sultanate to a neighboring country via land border posts designated by this authority, within a period not exceeding five days from the date of entry. To obtain this visa, the foreigner must be a national of the neighboring country or holds a valid visa for entry into the country.
Transit
12
Truck Driver Visa
Granted to foreign truck or bus drivers and their assistants, in accordance with the regulations set by the competent authority. It allows the holder to enter the country more than once and is valid for sixty (60) days, provided that it is used within three (3) months from its issuance date, provided that the number of assistants does not exceed one.
This visa allows its holders to enter the country during its validity period and stay for a period not exceeding one month at a time in the case of unloading goods within the country, and for a period not exceeding five (5) days in case of transit by truck or similar vehicle to a neighboring country. The visa period may be extended, if necessary, for a period not exceeding five (5) days from the date of entry.
Business
25
Multi-entry Visit Visa
Granted by the competent authority to a foreigner wishing to enter the Sultanate more than once. It allows the holder to enter and stay in the country for a specified period. Provided that the holder enters the Sultanate within three months from the visa's issuance date.
Business
39
Diplomat Visit
Allows single and Multientry Visit to Oman for holders of Diplomatic/Special/Service/Official passports of Special list of countries, issued directly at Border-locations.
Business
35
Patient Visa
Allows a short visit to Oman for a person suffering from a medical problem.
Emergency
38
Off the Plan Owner Visa
Allows multiple visits to Oman for a foreign off-the-plan buyer of a housing unit in an approved tourist complex in Oman by the approving authority.
