## Bangladesh: Ambassador meets with State Minister for Foreign Affairs

10 May 2026

[Jameel Albalushi Ambassador of the Sultanate of Oman to Bangladesh](https://www.fm.gov.om/en/missions/om-bd-dhaka-em/), paid a courtesy call in Dhaka on [Ms. Shama Obaed Islam, State Minister at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Bangladesh.](https://mofa.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/699a83f92a584f9da901e8cd?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The meeting discussed strengthening cooperation in trade, investment, culture, sports, and the labour sector, reaffirming a commitment to further advancing Bangladesh-Oman relations.

This is an unofficial English version of an Arabic report. To view the official Arabic text, [click here](https://www.fm.gov.om/ar/44967/).
