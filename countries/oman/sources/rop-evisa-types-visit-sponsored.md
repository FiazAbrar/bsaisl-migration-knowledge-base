Cruise Ship Passenger And Crew Visa
Granted by the competent authority upon the request and under the responsibility of the cruise ship's agent, to passengers and crew wishing to visit the Sultanate for tourism purposes. It allows the holder to enter and stay for a period (10) ten days or one month.

7
Official Visit Visa
Granted by the competent authority to a foreigner wishing to enter the Sultanate for a visit upon the request of the government entity the foreigner is visiting. It allows the holder to stay in the country for three months, renewable for a similar period.

22
Festivals Visa
Allows a visit to Oman for a person to take part in a festival or exhibition which has the approval of the Ministry of Commerce & Industry.

8
Express Visa
Granted by the competent authority to foreign businessmen whose work requires to visit the country for business purposes. It may also be granted to holders of rare, high-level professions upon the request and under the responsibility of a local sponsor. The visa must be used within one month from its issuance date. It allows the holder a single entry into the country and a stay of not more than three (3) weeks, extendable for one (1) week.
Work
11
Multi-entry Visit Visa (sponsored)
Allows multiple visits to Oman for a professional or business person employed in certain occupations sponsored by a local sponsor.
Work
13
Contractor Visit Visa
Granted by the competent authority upon the request and under the responsibility of an employer to a foreigner whose nature of work involves preliminary testing by some government entities prior to employment. Provided that the sponsor presents evidence of the concerned government organizationвҖҷs approval. The visa allows its holder to enter the Sultanate and stay for 2 months and it is renewable for 1 more month.
Work
17
Band Visa
Granted upon the request and under the responsibility of a local sponsor to a foreigner visiting to perform artistic shows, provided that the sponsor has obtained approval from the competent authorities. The visa shall be used within three months from the date of issuance.
This visa allows its holder to enter the country once and stay for a period of three months. The visa is renewable for a similar period.
Work
24
Short Work Visa
Granted upon the request and under the responsibility of a local sponsor to a foreigner visiting for the purpose temporary work, provided that they are not under twenty-one years of age, and the sponsor must have a license to bring in workers (permit) unless the sponsor is a government entity. In all cases, the visa expires after three months from the date of issuance.
Work
4
Family Visit Visa
Granted by the competent authority to relatives and friends of an Omani citizen upon their request and under their sponsorship. It is also granted to relatives of a foreigner residing in the country upon the sponsorвҖҷs request and his/her responsibility. It may also be granted to relatives of a residential unit owner upon his/her request and under his/her responsibility.
The visa allows its holder to stay in the country for three (3) months, renewable for a period not exceeding one (1) month.
Family
31
Scientific Research Visa
Granted by the competent authority upon the request and under the responsibility of a local competent authority to a foreigner visiting for scientific research purposes, provided that the requesting authority presents evidence of approval from other competent authorities. It is also granted by the Sultanate's representations abroad in coordination with the competent authority. The visa is valid for use within three months of its issuance date and allows the holder to enter and stay in the country for one month, extendable for a period not exceeding two months.
Research
18
Sailor's Transit Visa
Granted by the competent authority, upon the request and under the responsibility of the ship's agent to crew members wishing to depart the Sultanate via airports. It is also granted to crew members wishing to enter the Sultanate via airports to join their ships. The visa allows its holder to enter and stay in the country for a period not exceeding seven days.
Transit

Emergency Visa
