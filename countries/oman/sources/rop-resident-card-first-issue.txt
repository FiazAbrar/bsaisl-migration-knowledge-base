Civil Status & Passport Services

First-time issuance of residence card 

Description

Every expatriate who is resident in Oman must obtain a Residence Card within 30 days from the date of entry in Oman. 

Conditions 

Applicant must appear in person.

The required documents.

Required Documents

Application form for a new residence card.

Original passport.

Original and copies of the Manpower form after medical examination.

A letter from the employer (Government employment ).

Procedures 

Application is to be checked by the registrar.

The applicant collects their residence card.

Fees 

6 
  for one year.

 11 
 for two years.

 10 
 for each month of delay in application for the card.
