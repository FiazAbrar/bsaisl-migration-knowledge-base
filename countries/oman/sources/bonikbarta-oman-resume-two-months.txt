Oman assures to resume visas for Bangladeshis within two months

Bonik Barta Online   

The assurance came during a meeting between Dr Asif Nazrul, Adviser for Expatriates’ Welfare and Overseas Employment, and the Omani labour minister on the sidelines of the Global Labour Market Conference held in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

Oman visa services for Bangladeshi nationals will resume within the next two months, reopening a pathway that has remained closed since 2023. Oman’s Minister of Labour, Dr Mahad bin Said bin Ali Ba’owain Salim Al Busaidi, has assured the information.
The assurance came during a meeting between Dr Asif Nazrul, Adviser for Expatriates’ Welfare and Overseas Employment, and the Omani labour minister on the sidelines of the Global Labour Market Conference held in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, on Monday (January 26). During the meeting, Dr Asif Nazrul praised Oman’s initiative to regularise irregular Bangladeshi migrant workers without imposing fines.
At the discussion, the adviser sought the Omani labour minister’s cooperation in facilitating access for skilled Bangladeshi workers to Oman’s labour market. He also requested a review of the suspension imposed on visas for unskilled and semi-skilled workers. In response, the Omani minister explained that priority had been given to regularising existing irregular and undocumented migrant workers, prompting the temporary suspension of new work visas for unskilled and semi-skilled workers since 2023. He, however, assured that work visas for Bangladeshi nationals would be reinstated within the next two months.
Dr Asif Nazrul also briefed the Omani labour minister on various initiatives undertaken by Bangladesh’s interim government, including the use of technology in migration management to ensure transparency and accountability, legal reforms, skills assessment prior to deployment, and pre-departure training to familiarise workers with the host country’s language, culture, and legal framework.
