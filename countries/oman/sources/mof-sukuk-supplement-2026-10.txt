Subscription Conditions and Procedures

Investors will be invited to participate in the offering of the Trust Certificates through (i) a competitive
bidding process (Category I) and (ii) a non-competitive subscription process (Category II), with
Category I and Category II together constituting the Offering.

Category I will be available to institutional investors, individuals and other eligible investors subscribing
on a competitive basis (Category I Applicants). Category II will be available to eligible individual
investors subscribing on a non-competitive basis (Category II Applicants).

Individual investors may elect to participate through either Category I on a competitive basis or
Category II on a non-competitive basis. Category I Applicants and Category II Applicants are together
referred to as the Applicants and each as an Applicant.

Subscription Price

The subscription price for Category II Applicants will be OMR 1 per Trust Certificate, being the face
value of each Trust Certificate. The subscription price payable by Category I Applicants will be
determined by reference to the yield specified in the successful bid and the applicable yield reference
table provided along with the Application Form.

Profit Rate / Yield

The profit rate applicable to the Trust Certificates will be 4.85% per annum. Category II Applicants will
subscribe to the Trust Certificates at such profit rate.
