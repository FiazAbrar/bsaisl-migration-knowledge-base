Breadcrumb

								Transfer Non-Omani Workers Services

This service enables the transfer of workers' services based on a request submitted by the employer, representative, or authorized signatory. It allows (individuals, employers, and government entities) to transfer the services of non-Omani workers under their sponsorship.

Special conditions

-The worker whose services are to be transferred must be actively employed.
-The new employer must have a vacant license to transfer the worker to their establishment.
