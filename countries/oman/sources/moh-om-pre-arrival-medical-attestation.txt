Pre-Arrival Medical Report Attestation Service

Start Service

Service Provider

Target Audience

Business owners and individuals interested in recruiting expatriate workers.

Service Delivery Channels

eHealth Portal

Service Completion Period

5 minutes (for Wafid reports) / upon MOH approval (for Oman issued reports)

Details

A service that facilitates the Pre-Arrival Medical Report Attestation to obtain a visa before arriving in the country. 

User Manual 

Service Steps

1.Visit eHealth Portal

2.Log in to the system or register a new account in the system using the PKI feature of the mobile phone or ID card.
Note:
 You can follow the steps for PKI for a mobile phone via the following link in the Tam service:
 Digital certification Portal 

3. Enter the required data. After verifying the data, you can proceed to the payment process.

4. Visit the Royal Oman Police website to complete the visa procedures

Terms and Conditions

You must access the system using the user’s ID card (PKI)
