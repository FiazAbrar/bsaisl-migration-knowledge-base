# Oman open questions

These are the questions the Oman migration guide can't yet answer, grouped by the guide's sections. Each is a "Need to know" in `doc.md`; an answer from someone who knows (an agency, a returnee, the Bangladesh Embassy in Muscat, Oman's Ministry of Labour or ROP) can close it.
