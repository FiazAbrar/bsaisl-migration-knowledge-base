# Oman doc: coverage audit

**1. The migrant's questions.** Most are answered: how long you can stay (Your residence card), pay (What you pay, and what you earn and keep; Pay and deductions), rights at work, bank account and SIM (Step 8 of the company worker visa, with Need to know lines), pension (Probash scheme; the SPF savings scheme for foreigners due by July 2027), family (Family joining visa) and what you can bring home. There are three gaps. Sex outside marriage is a jailable offence, but it appears only under Having a child in Oman, not under What can get you jailed or deported. There is no Need to know line on social-media or cybercrime offences. The doc also doesn't say what to do if nobody meets you on arrival.

**2. Against the original aim.** The visa-by-visa coverage is complete for the open routes. Each has its requirements, where to apply, cost, time, refusal (as a Need to know where no appeal is known), scams both ways and whether the holder can work. Changing visa type, asylum (written to discourage: economic hardship doesn't qualify) and the corridor (numbers against India, Pakistan, Nepal, Egypt and Myanmar; the 2023 ban; the farm and fishing quotas) are all covered. Small gaps in the company worker visa: no legal route to work temporarily for another firm, no mention that an employer can replace you with an Omani under Omanisation, and nothing on the priority of wages when an employer has debts. Visit visas don't mention the humanitarian extension.

**3. The insider test.** Covered: the October 2023 suspension and why, free visas and sponsor fees, the "five workers per young Omani" approvals, trafficking by boat through Iran, Al Hail market, the medical-slip syndicate, the BAIRA statements, Galfar, Asyad and the two agencies with 2023 job orders, the 2018 detentions, the 2025 amnesty, the 2026 MoL crackdown, the spouse-visa fraud at Dhaka airport, the UAE-Buraimi trafficking of women, deaths (1,893 bodies, 2016-2022), real costs (Tk 1.8 to 3.5 lakh, OKUP's Tk 5 lakh) and remittances. Prosecutions of visa sellers and the price of the 2026 farm visas are already open Need to know lines.

**Uncited facts (389).** Most duplicate cited facts word for word (the English Labour Law and residence-law rows E0001 to E0114 and E0193 to E0208 duplicate KC, KA and KB ids; D0009 to D0120 duplicate KD ids; R0011 to R0019, V0075 to V0099 and B0186 to B0188 repeat news already cited under C ids). Others are older counts that newer figures replace: C0003, C0007 to C0012, C0025, C0057, C0060, C0063 and C0100. Others are superseded: P0062, P0079, E0184, E0186, B0266, E0215. Others are statistics or trivia that change no decision (C0038, B0073, D0125, E0110, P0051, D0093, D0095). The ones that fill a gap are E0006, E0042, E0087, E0152, E0182, D0021, D0098, D0115, D0116, P0044 and B0131. Note: E0182 is from a news source, and its quote supports only consent, six months' service and no move into professions reserved for Omanis, so the fix uses only those.

### FAIL Sex outside marriage missing from the jailing section
old: #### Bribes
new: #### Sex outside marriage

Sex between a man and a woman without a marriage contract carries six months to three years in prison for both, and at least two years if either is married (see If the parents are not married) [P0138, P0139].

new: The ROP's Inspector General can cancel a residence and deport someone who breaches public order or morals or has no visible lawful means of living [KC0078].

### FAIL The legal way to work temporarily for another firm
old: The Minister can also deport a worker who breaks the labour rules, at the employer's expense, and ban his entry [KC0049].
new: The Minister can also deport a worker who breaks the labour rules, at the employer's expense, and ban his entry [KC0049]. Since December 2024 your employer may lend you to another establishment only with your consent, after you have worked at least six months for it, and not into a profession reserved for Omanis [E0182].

### FAIL Replacement by an Omani not mentioned
old: During the notice period you get 10 paid hours a week to look for a job [KC0067].
new: During the notice period you get 10 paid hours a week to look for a job [KC0067]. The employer may also end your contract to replace you with an Omani in the same occupation under its Omanisation plan [E0042].

### FAIL Company grievance system not mentioned
old: 1. **Ask first.** Ask your manager for the wage; if he does not answer, go to MOL [E0155].
new: 1. **Ask first.** Ask your manager for the wage; if he does not answer, go to MOL [E0155]. An employer with 50 or more workers must have a complaints and grievances system approved by MOL [E0006].

### FAIL Wage priority when the employer has debts
old: Workers' lawsuits under the Labour Law are free of court fees [KC0062].
new: Workers' lawsuits under the Labour Law are free of court fees [KC0062]. Your wages and dues come before all the employer's other debts, except maintenance a court has ordered [E0087].

### FAIL No one meets you on arrival
old: If your labour or resident card is not done within a month, tell your direct boss, and call MoL on 80077000 [R0150, R0153].
new: If nobody meets you or you cannot find your workplace on arrival, call MoL on 80077000 or go to the Ministry of Labour [E0152]. If your labour or resident card is not done within a month, tell your direct boss, and call MoL on 80077000 [R0150, R0153].

### FAIL Advertising a maid for transfer is banned
old: From 2027, only a licensed office may recruit a domestic worker [KD0039].
new: From 2027, only a licensed office may recruit a domestic worker [KD0039]. An employer may not advertise a domestic worker by religion or colour, or in a degrading way, to pass her to another employer [D0021].
