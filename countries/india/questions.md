# India open questions

These are the questions the India doc can't yet answer, grouped by the doc's sections. Each is a "Need to know" in `doc.md`.

## Background

- How many Bangladeshis does Bangladesh's High Commission in New Delhi estimate live in India without papers, and how many were returned through flag meetings in 2025 and 2026?
- How do workers who cross find jobs in Delhi, Haryana or Chennai: through relatives, contractors at the border, or the brokers themselves, and do the brokers take a cut of wages?
- What does a Bangladeshi labourer earn a day in Delhi, Haryana and Chennai in 2026, and how is money sent home: hundi (informal transfer), or carried across?
- Does BMET (the Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training, the Bangladesh government body that clears workers going abroad) issue emigration clearance for India, and how many workers did it clear for India in 2024 to 2026?

## Working in India without papers

- Which other Indian cities and trades employ Bangladeshis without papers in 2026 (brick kilns, rag-picking, domestic work), and what do they earn?

## Arrest, detention and being sent back

- Which Bangladeshi body verifies the nationality of people held in India (the Ministry of Home Affairs, the police Special Branch or the district administration), how long it takes, and how can a family in Bangladesh send papers to speed up a relative's case?
- Do officials from the Bangladesh Deputy High Commission in Kolkata visit West Bengal's holding centres, and does any lawyer or legal-aid body represent detainees there?

## Coming home

- Which section do police actually use in cases against people returned by India, what do courts usually give, and are returnees pushed in by India treated differently from those caught leaving?

## Indian visas

- The IVAC site still showed an advisory in November 2026 that only limited appointment slots for urgent medical and emergency visas were open: are tourist visa appointments open to everyone in November 2026, and how long is the wait for a slot?
- Can a refused applicant reapply at once, and is any reason given for refusal?
- What sums has India notified for compounding an overstay, and is a Bangladeshi who overstays barred from future visas?

## Trafficked and held in India

- What does the 2026 trafficking and migrant smuggling act say about victims who crossed the border illegally: are they protected from prosecution, and which body arranges their return from India?

## Contacts

- Does the Bangladesh High Commission in New Delhi have a 24-hour number for a Bangladeshi just arrested in India, and do the WhatsApp lines answer outside office hours?
