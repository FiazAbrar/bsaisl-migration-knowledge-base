## Where an official research pass should start

- India's Ministry of Home Affairs: Foreigners Division (visa categories, overstay penalties, the Immigration and Foreigners Act 2025 if in force), and the Bureau of Immigration / FRRO pages.
- Indian Visa Application Centres in Bangladesh (IVAC, indianvisa-bangladesh.nic.in): which visas are open to Bangladeshis in 2026, fees, processing times.
- High Commission of India in Dhaka: notices on visa restrictions since August 2024.
- Bangladesh High Commission, New Delhi, and Deputy High Commissions in Kolkata, Guwahati and Agartala: consular help for detained Bangladeshis, nationality verification, travel permits, phone numbers.
- Bangladesh Ministry of Home Affairs and Special Branch: how nationality verification of people held in India works and how long it takes.
- BGB press releases: flag-meeting handovers and push-in counts for 2025-2026.
- BMET (oep.gov.bd country clearance reports): whether any workers are cleared for India.
- West Bengal government orders on holding centres (May-June 2026); Assam's Foreigners Tribunals and the Immigrants (Expulsion from Assam) Act 1950 (the "1950 law" in XI0050).
- Bangladesh law under which returnees are charged for illegal crossing (Passport Act 1920 / Bangladesh Control of Entry Act), and sentences actually given.

## Resolved conflicts

- Number sent back from West Bengal: the June 2026 Al Jazeera article first says Adhikari claimed "about 5,000" had been sent back, then quotes him as saying 4,800 had been sent back from the detention centres and 836 more were held. Used the quoted, specific 4,800 (XI0126).
- Pending nationality verification: 2,369 on India's list (22 May 2025, XI0038), more than 2,860 cases pending (7 May 2026, XI0100), and "more than 2,800" suspected Bangladeshis' details sent (media, June 2026, XI0130). Different dates, consistent; all kept with dates.
- Push-in counts: 167 in five districts on 4-7 May 2025 (XI0031) and "more than 300" pushed from Assam since May 2025 (XI0044) cover different scopes; not a conflict. Al Jazeera says most people pushed in were Indian citizens (XI0140) and BGB says those pushed in included Indian, Myanmar and Bangladeshi nationals (XI0034); both kept.
- Visa restriction start: "from July" (bankers, November 2024, XI0023) and "after the August political change" (March 2025, XI0026). Kept both as July-August 2024.
- Credit card figures: June 2024 India Tk 92 crore appears in both the November 2024 and March 2025 articles; consistent.

## Unsettled
