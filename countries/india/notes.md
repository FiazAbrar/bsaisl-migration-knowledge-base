### What was found
- No work route for ordinary workers: the employment visa is for professionals appointed by the Government of India or on assignment with reputed organisations, with an invitation and proof of employment from a registered Indian company (OI0001-OI0002, OI0009-OI0010).
- Visa process, papers, fee (no Indian visa fee; IVAC fee BDT 1,500 from 10 Aug 2025), 5-10 working days, refusal final, five IVACs, paid-slot fraud warning and report number.
- Indian law: illegal entry up to 5 years/Rs 5 lakh; overstay up to 3 years/Rs 3 lakh; forged papers 2-7 years and Rs 1-10 lakh; burden of proof on the person; warrantless arrest by head constable and above; compounding possible; Foreigners Act 1946 repealed.
- Exemption Order 2025: six non-Muslim minority communities from Bangladesh who entered by 31 Dec 2024 exempt from passport and visa requirements.
- Bangladesh side: leaving without a passport is Passport Order Art. 3, punishable under Art. 11(3) (up to 3 months, Tk 500, or both); the Control of Entry Act covers Indian citizens only. Overseas Employment and Migrants Act 2013: returnees count as migrant workers; right to legal aid (s27), right to return and mission help (s29), cost recoverable (s29(2)); brokers arranging exit through non-designated points up to 10 years and at least Tk 5 lakh (s34); false job promises up to 5 years and at least Tk 1 lakh (s31).
- Travel Permit for detainees/victims: proof of nationality, release or deportation order, photos, Rs 1,800; issued only after nationality verified in Bangladesh.

### Resolved conflicts
- CAA and the 2024 cut-off: Prothom Alo (XI0107) said that after a 2024 change, the covered communities who entered by 31 Dec 2024 "are treated as legal citizens". The official sources say the CAA citizenship cut-off is 31 Dec 2014 (OI0053) and the 31 Dec 2024 date comes from the Exemption Order 2025, which exempts those people from passport and visa requirements, not grants citizenship (OI0052). Official version used; XI0107 dropped from the doc. The earlier doc line "the cut-off was later moved to 31 December 2024" was replaced the same way.
- Number of CAA communities: XI0106 says seven; the MHA Q&A (OI0053) and the Exemption Order (OI0052) list six (Hindu, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi, Christian). Official used; XI0106 dropped.
- IVAC fee: the High Commission FAQ (undated, OI0032) says Tk 800; IVAC's own notice says BDT 1,500 from 10 Aug 2025 (OI0038). Newer IVAC figure used.
- Tourist visas: the IVAC capture of 10 Nov 2026 still carries "Advisory 1" (limited slots for urgent medical and emergency visas only), while the High Commissioner announced tourist visas from 28 June 2026 (OI0104) and the HCI document list of 13 Aug 2026 includes tourist (T-1) papers. Kept the reopening and put the advisory question as a Need to know.
- Contacts: the Bangladesh mission list (undated) names heads of mission who may have changed; names were left out, addresses and phone numbers used. Third-party directories give other WhatsApp numbers for the New Delhi mission (+91 85275 08281, +91 88002 13179); the mission's own page gives +91 9311 638983 and +91 93194 05078, which are used.
- Mission weekly holidays: the mission list says Saturday and Sunday; used as stated.
