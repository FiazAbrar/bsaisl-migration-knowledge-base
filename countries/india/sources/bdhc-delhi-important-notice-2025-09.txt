04. Travel Permit:

   Travel Permit for Newborn Baby:
   The applicant is required to submit following documents to the Mission while applying
   for Travel Permit for Newborn Baby (s):
   1. An application form Parents
   2. Birth Certificate of Baby/Babies issued by Local Municipal Corporation
   3. Birth Certificate of Baby/Babies issued by local Govt. of Bangladesh.
   4. 02 (Two) recent Passport-size photos of baby/babies with white background
   5. Passport & Visa copy of the parents
   6. Marriage certificate of parents
   7. Discharge certificate from Hospital
   8. National Identity Card (NID) copies of Parents
   9. Fees: NEFT of Rs. 1800.00 (One Thousand Eight Hundred) in favour of Bangladesh
       High Commission, New Delhi (You have to pay here using your debit or credit card).


Travel Permit for Lost Passport:
The applicant is required to submit following documents to the Mission while applying for
Travel Permit for Lost Passport:
   1.   An application form Applicant.
   2.   Applicant’s Passport & Visa copy
   3.   FIR copy of local police station
   4.   Form-C/Residence proof
   5.   02 (Two) recent Passport-size photos white background.
   6.   National Identity Card (NID)/Birth Registration Certificate (BRC) copy of Applicant
   7.   Fees: NEFT of Rs. 1800.00 (One Thousand Eight Hundred) in favour of Bangladesh
        High Commission, New Delhi (You have to pay here using your debit or credit card).


Travel Permit for Passport Expiry:
The applicant is required to submit following documents to the Mission while applying for
Travel Permit for Passport Expiry:
   1.   An application form Applicant.
   2.   Applicant’s Passport & Visa copy
   3.   Form-c/Residence proof.
   4.   02 (Two) recent Passport-size photos white background.
   5.   National Identity Card (NID)/Birth Registration Certificate (BRC) copy of Applicant
   6.   Fees: NEFT of Rs. 1800.00 (One Thousand Eight Hundred) in favour of Bangladesh
        High Commission, New Delhi (You have to pay here using your debit or credit card).
Travel Permit for Victims/Detainees:
The applicant is required to submit following documents to the Mission while applying for
Travel Permit for Victims/Detainees:
   1. An application form Applicant.
   2. Proof of Nationality
   3. Court Release Order/Deportation order from India Government
   4. 02 (Two) recent Passport-size photos white background (35×45 mm)
   5. NEFT of Rs. 1800.00 (One Thousand Eight Hundred) in favour of Bangladesh High
      Commission, New Delhi (You have to pay here using your debit or credit card).
   6. [N.B.- The Mission will issue Travel Permit (T.P.) after receiving the above-mentioned
      documents and once the nationality of the detainees/victims has been verified by the
      appropriate authority of Bangladesh]


05. Repatriation Of Dead Body
For repatriation of a Dead Body to Bangladesh the following documents should be
submitted/emailed to the Mission:
   1.   Request Letter from the Relative/Attendant (Available at the Website)
   2.   Undertaking letter from the Relative/Attendant and Undertaker/Agent
   3.   Death Certificate from Hospital.
   4.   Passport & Visa copies of deceased and relative.
   5.   NOC from local police station.
   6.   Fees: No Fees Applicable.
   7.   Email Address: consular.newdelhi@mofa.gov.bd
