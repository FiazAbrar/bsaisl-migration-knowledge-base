# United Kingdom open questions

These are the questions the United Kingdom doc can't yet answer, grouped by the doc's sections. Each is a "Need to know" in `doc.md`.

## Background

- How many workers did BMET clear for the UK in 2025 and 2026, and in which jobs?
- What do Dhaka and Sylhet student-recruitment agencies charge students, and how much do UK universities pay them per student?
- How is a UK Skilled Worker visa sold in Bangladesh, and for how much?
- How long does a Skilled Worker or care worker whose job ends have before the Home Office cuts their visa, and how often does that happen in practice?

## Student visa

- Is the Financial Times' 23.5% figure for Bangladeshi students in January-June 2026 a refusal rate or a withdrawal rate? Prothom Alo calls it a withdrawal rate in a report about refusals.
- Where can a student check whether a given university currently accepts Bangladeshi applicants, or applicants from Sylhet?
- Which Bangladeshi banks' statements does UKVI accept, and does it check them with the bank?
- Where exactly are the UK visa application centres in Bangladesh (Dhaka, Sylhet, Chattogram), who runs them, and what are their hours and optional paid services?
- What does the UKVI credibility interview ask Bangladeshi students?
- What is the Ministry of Education's own deadline and selection process for Commonwealth Master's nominations each year?

## Standard Visitor visa

- What do visa officers most often cite when refusing Bangladeshi visitor applications, and what do Dhaka agents charge to prepare one?

## Care-worker and Skilled Worker visa

- Can a worker ask UKVI or the employer to confirm a CoS reference number is genuine before applying, and how?

## Irregular entry and removal

- How many Bangladeshis has the UK returned each year since the 2024 agreement, forced and voluntary?
- Does the Bangladesh High Commission in London give help or legal referrals to Bangladeshis facing removal?

## Voting in Bangladesh elections from the UK

- Will postal voting from the UK be offered again for later elections, and when does registration open?

## Online harassment from other Bangladeshis

- Does the ICO act on videos misusing an adult's personal data when no children are involved?

## Contacts

- The address and hours of the Bangladesh Assistant High Commission in Manchester and of any office in Birmingham.
