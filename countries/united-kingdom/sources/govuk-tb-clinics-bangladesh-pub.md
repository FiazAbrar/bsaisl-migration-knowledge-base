## Details

You’ll need to have a tuberculosis (TB) test if you’re coming to the UK for 6 months or more and both of the following apply:

- you’ve lived in Bangladesh for 6 months or more
- you were living there (or another [listed country](https://www.gov.uk/tb-test-visa/countries-where-you-need-a-tb-test-to-enter-the-uk)) within the last 6 months

You’ll need a TB test if you’re coming to the UK as:

- a fiancé, fiancée or proposed civil partner on a [family visa](https://www.gov.uk/uk-family-visa/partner-spouse)
- a [returning resident](https://www.gov.uk/returning-resident-visa) and you’ve been away for more than 2 years
