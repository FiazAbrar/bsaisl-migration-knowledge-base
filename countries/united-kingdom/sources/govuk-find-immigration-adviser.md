# Search for an adviser

You can get immigration advice from an immigration adviser if you need help with getting permission to stay in the UK.

Immigration advisers can help you with most things to do with immigration, including helping you to fill in the right forms and representing you at a tribunal. They do not make immigration decisions.

Check if the adviser is registered and if they charge a fee before you use them.

If you’re from the EU, Switzerland, Norway, Iceland or Liechtenstein, you might still be able to [apply to the EU Settlement Scheme](/settled-status-eu-citizens-families) if you were living in the UK by 31 December 2020. The deadline to apply for most people was 30 June 2021. You can apply if you have ‘reasonable grounds’ for not applying by the deadline - for example, you had an illness or were the victim of domestic abuse.

## Find a registered immigration adviser

Use the Immigration Advice Authority (IAA) Adviser Finder to [find a registered adviser near you](https://portal.oisc.gov.uk/s/adviser-finder).

All immigration advisers must be registered with IAA or be a [member of an approved professional body](/government/publications/how-to-become-a-regulated-immigration-adviser/how-to-become-a-regulated-immigration-adviser).

### If you want legal help

You can find solicitors who give immigration advice through:

- [The Law Society](https://solicitors.lawsociety.org.uk/) if you live in England or Wales
- [The Law Society of Scotland](https://www.lawscot.org.uk/find-a-solicitor/)
- [The Law Society of Northern Ireland](https://lawsoc-ni.org/using-a-solicitor)

A solicitor can help manage your case and find a barrister.

If you’re managing your own case but want legal advice or advocacy, you can find a barrister through:

- [The Bar Council’s Direct Access scheme](https://www.barcouncil.org.uk/bar-council-services/for-the-public/direct-access-portal.html) if you live in England or Wales
- [The Faculty of Advocates](https://www.advocates.org.uk/) if you live in Scotland
- [The Bar of Northern Ireland](https://www.barofni.com/directory/search/search&channel=barristers&barrister_areas=immigration)

## Make a complaint

You can [complain about an immigration adviser](/find-an-immigration-adviser/complain-about-an-adviser) if you think you’ve had a bad service.

You cannot get your money back if an adviser is not regulated.
