WELCOME TO THE OFFICIAL WEBSITE OF THE BANGLADESH HIGH COMMISSION IN LONDON

The website provides useful information and guidance on consular and visa services, together with an overview of the enduring and multifaceted partnership between Bangladesh and the United Kingdom. It also offers an introduction to Bangladesh's rich history, vibrant culture, growing trade and investment opportunities, and diverse tourism destinations.

A New Chapter for Bangladesh

Following the 13th National Parliamentary Election, held in a free, fair, participatory and peaceful manner, Bangladesh has embarked on a new chapter of democratic renewal and national progress. On 17 February 2026, Mr Tarique Rahman took oath as the Prime Minister of Bangladesh, following an election that reflected strong popular support. The new Parliament elected Mr Mirza Fakhrul Islam Alamgir as President, who took oath on 21 August 2026.

With a youthful population and nearly two-thirds of its people of working age, Bangladesh possesses immense energy, dynamism and potential. Under the vision of its new democratic government, the country is moving forward with renewed confidence towards building a more inclusive, prosperous and progressive society.

Bangladesh's global community is an integral part of this journey. More than 14 million Bangladeshis live abroad, making the country's diaspora an important source of knowledge, enterprise, investment and goodwill. In the United Kingdom, a vibrant British Bangladeshi community of more than 800,000 people constitutes a valued bridge between our two countries and contributes significantly to the social, cultural and economic life of both Bangladesh and Britain.

Strengthening the Bangladesh–UK Partnership

The relationship between Bangladesh and the United Kingdom is rooted in history and strengthened by shared values, extensive people-to-people connections and growing cooperation across a wide range of areas. From trade, investment and development to education, culture, climate action and people-to-people links, there is enormous scope to further expand the breadth and depth of our partnership.

The Bangladesh High Commission in London remains firmly committed to advancing this historic and enduring partnership, exploring new avenues of cooperation and expanding the opportunities for mutual benefit and shared prosperity.

At the same time, we remain committed to serving, supporting and engaging the British Bangladeshi community and all those who seek to build stronger connections with Bangladesh.

Discover Bangladesh. Connect with Bangladesh. Partner with Bangladesh.

We invite you to explore our website, discover the many facets of Bangladesh, access our consular and visa services, and learn more about the opportunities for engagement between Bangladesh and the United Kingdom.
