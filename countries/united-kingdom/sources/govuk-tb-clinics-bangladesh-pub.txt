 Bangladesh: tuberculosis test clinics for a UK visa

 Where to get tested for tuberculosis (TB) in Bangladesh for your UK visa application.

Tuberculosis testing in Bangladesh

HTML

 Details

You’ll need to have a tuberculosis (
TB
) test if you’re coming to the UK for 6 months or more and both of the following apply:

you’ve lived in Bangladesh for 6 months or more

you were living there (or another 
listed country
) within the last 6 months

You’ll need a 
TB
 test if you’re coming to the UK as:

a fiancé, fiancée or proposed civil partner on a 
family visa

a 
returning resident
 and you’ve been away for more than 2 years
