# Brunei open questions

These are the questions the Brunei migration guide can't yet answer, grouped by the guide's sections. Each is a "Need to know" in `doc.md`; an answer from someone who knows (BOESL, an agency, a returnee, the Bangladesh High Commission in Bandar Seri Begawan, Brunei's Department of Labour or Immigration) can close it.

## Background

- From what date did the Employment Act, Chapter 278 replace the Employment Order 2009, and did JobCentre Brunei merge into the DME?
- Is BOESL still the only channel in November 2026, or may private Bangladeshi agencies again send workers to Brunei employment agencies that have signed with BOESL?
- BMET cleared 1,681 workers for Brunei in 2025 and 1,726 by 7 November 2026, but only 25 had been hired under the government-to-government arrangement by September 2024: through which channel did the rest go?
- Do Bangladeshi women go to Brunei as domestic workers or cleaners, through which channel, and how many did BMET clear in 2025-26? (The US State Department's 2026 trafficking report says Brunei penalised four agencies for overcharging domestic workers.)
- BMET's yearly table gives 1,081 for 2023 and 205 for 2024 (matching BMET's figure of 3,473 from January 2020 to January 2024), while its online clearance report gives 5,959 and 3,058: what does the clearance report count that the table does not, and are its 2025 and 2026 figures departures?
- In 2026, does a Brunei visa outside BOESL still sell in Bangladesh, and for how much?
- Is the High Commission's "List of Registered Brunei Employment Agencies" the list of agencies that have signed with BOESL, and is it current?
- How does a Brunei visa reach a Bangladeshi village in 2026: through whom, and what does each hand take?
- What does a Bangladeshi construction worker or cleaner in Brunei earn a month in 2026, before and after deductions?
- How many Bangladeshis die in Brunei each year, and of what causes?

- Which embassy's visa fee does BOESL's circular mean (Brunei's High Commission in Dhaka for the endorsement, or Bangladesh's in Brunei for attestation), and how much is it?
- How much do the approved medical centres in Dhaka charge?
- When was the Tk 1,20,780 ceiling for Brunei set, is it still in force, and does it apply to the BOESL route?
- Do BOESL or Brunei employers ask for a police clearance certificate, a trade test certificate or experience papers for the Brunei route?
- How does a worker whose relative's Brunei company wants him by name get onto a BOESL demand letter, and does he still pay the full BOESL charge?
- Which of the three Dhaka centres does BOESL send Brunei workers to, are their Brunei approvals still current, what do they charge, and how long is the report valid?
- Will BOESL show a worker the attested visa and contract before he buys the pay order, and how can he confirm the High Commission attested his visa?
- In practice, does BOESL take the passports to Brunei's High Commission in Dhaka, or must each worker go in person, and how long does endorsement take?
- What do the 2-year employment pass, the re-entry visa and the green IC cost, and who pays?
- Do Brunei employers of Bangladeshi workers pay wages into a bank account or in cash, and can a worker whose passport is held by the employer open an account?
- In 2026, are brokers still selling Brunei "company visas" outside BOESL, and at what price?
- Are visit visas being sold in Bangladesh as Brunei jobs, and how do those workers end up?
- Did the revision of the Employment Order 2009 into the Employment Act (Chapter 278) change any of the rights below?
- Does Brunei set minimum standards for foreign workers' staff houses (space per worker, toilets), and who inspects them?
- Which Brunei law, if any, makes it an offence for an employer to keep a worker's passport, and has anyone been charged?
- Does the Department of Labour order unpaid wages to be paid to the worker, and can a worker stay and work elsewhere while the case runs?
- Does a worker need his current employer's release letter to transfer, and can he transfer if the employer has not paid him?
- What is the BND 600 "exit fine" in law, and how does a worker whose employer refuses approval leave legally?
- Where a BOESL circular says the worker pays his own food, does the employer still give a food allowance, as the MoC requires?
