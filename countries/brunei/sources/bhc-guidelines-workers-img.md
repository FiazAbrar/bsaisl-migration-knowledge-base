প্রবাসী বাংলাদেশি কর্মীদের জন্য করণীয় (What expatriate Bangladeshi workers should do)
ক: Follow the host country's laws, rules, workplace policies, local general law, social media rules, decency, social and cultural values.
খ: Use social media to keep in touch with family, friends and well-wishers.
গ: Follow the organisation's policy before sharing any information, photo or video about the job, employer or workplace.
ঘ: Be careful with unknown people, unknown links, files or messages, and beware of scams and phishing.
ঙ: Use social media responsibly; check accuracy before sharing; stay away from suspicious or provocative online activity.

প্রবাসী বাংলাদেশি কর্মীদের জন্য বর্জনীয় (What expatriate Bangladeshi workers must avoid)
ক: Do not share, like or comment on communal, provocative or banned-organisation posts, rumours or false information.
খ: Do not share provocative posts on Israel-Palestine, Iran-Israel or other international conflicts that may break local law.
গ: Do not take part in banned political or online campaigns in the host country.
ঘ: Do not donate to any person, organisation or online fund without proper checking.
ঙ: Do not photograph or publish workplaces, airports, ports, government or security sites.
চ: Do not publish your own or others' passport, visa, national ID, bank or other personal information on social media.
ছ: Do not take part in posts or comments that incite religious, ethnic or racial hatred.
জ: Do not get involved in fake job adverts, illegal visas, lures of quick income or suspicious cryptocurrency investment promotions. (Bangla: ভুয়া চাকরির বিজ্ঞাপন, অবৈধ ভিসা, দ্রুত আয়ের প্রলোভন বা সন্দেহজনক ক্রিপ্টোকারেন্সি-সংক্রান্ত প্রচারণায় জড়িত না হওয়া)
ঝ: Do not post, comment or react in support or praise of any crime, violence or illegal activity.
