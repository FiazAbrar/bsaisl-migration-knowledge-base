BANDAR SERI BEGAWAN: The Department of Manpower and Employment (DME) is investigating an employer over four alleged labour offences involving local and foreign workers at a retail shop in Kampong Manggis Satu.

The offences were detected during Operation Usai 259/2026 conducted by the department’s Law Enforcement Division on Nov 11 to ensure compliance with the Employment Order, 2009.

The alleged offences included failure to pay overtime, requiring employees to work beyond the stipulated hours, failure to provide paid annual leave and breaching foreign worker licence conditions.

The department said a foreign worker employed as a general labourer was found performing office paperwork duties, which were not in line with the occupation approved under the worker’s licence.

Further investigations are ongoing. If found guilty, the employer may face a compound fine of up to BND1,000 for each offence. Failure to pay the compound fine may result in court action.

The department reminded employers to comply with the Employment Order, including paying overtime to employees who work more than eight hours a day or 44 hours a week.

Employees must not work more than eight hours a day, while shift workers may work up to 12 hours a day. Overtime must not exceed 72 hours a month.

Employers are also required to provide paid annual leave after employees have completed three months of service with the same company, and ensure foreign workers perform duties in accordance with their approved occupations. - Borneo Bulletin/ANN
