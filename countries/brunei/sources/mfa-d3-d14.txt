BUSINESS VISITS

PROCEDURES

Business Visit Visa is issued for the purpose of investment or business related activities in Brunei Darussalam.

Business Visit Visa is required to be applied by the registered business company or government-linked company in Brunei Darussalam on behalf of the applicant.

The visa application is to be submitted to the Visa and Diplomatic Section, Department of Immigration and National Registration, Brunei Darussalam.

Subject to the information stated in the approval letter, the applicant can proceed to the nearest Brunei Darussalam Mission Abroad to be issued with the relevant visa stamp. 

PROCESSING PERIOD/TIME 

The general processing time for Business Visit Visa is as follows: 

3 working days for application submitted at Headquarters, Department of Immigration and National Registration and Kuala Belait branch office.

5 working days for application submitted at Temburong branch office.

Some applications may take longer to process, should it require further assessment.

Required Documents:

Applicants for Business Visit Visa are required to attach the following documents with the complete visa application form:

 Application letter and any supporting documents from the guarantor /company in Brunei Darussalam.

 Supporting letter from the Government of Brunei Darussalam or relevant registered company.

 Any supporting documents from the applicant.

Terms and conditions apply.

PROFESSIONAL VISITS

PROCEDURES

Professional Visit Visa issued for the purpose of professional work/expertise required for a particular project awarded in Brunei Darussalam.

Professional Visit Visa is required to be applied by the government agencies / government-linked company / business registered company in Brunei Darussalam on behalf of the applicant.

The visa application is to be submitted to the Visa and Diplomatic Section, Department of Immigration and National Registration, Brunei Darussalam.

Subject to the information stated in the approval letter, the applicant can proceed to the nearest Brunei Darussalam Mission Abroad to be issued with the relevant visa stamp.

PROCESSING PERIOD/TIME 

The general processing time for Professional Visit Visa is as follows: 

3 working days for application submitted at Headquarters, Department of Immigration and National Registration and Kuala Belait branch office.

5 working days for application submitted at Temburong branch office.

Some applications may take longer to process, should it require further assessment. 

Required Documents:

Applicants for Professional Visit Visa are required to attach the following documents with the complete visa application form.

Application letter and any supporting documents from the guarantor company in Brunei Darussalam.

Supporting letter from the Government of Brunei Darussalam or relevant registered company.

Any supporting documents from the applicant.

Terms and conditions apply.
