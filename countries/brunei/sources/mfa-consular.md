- Co-ordinate audiences and courtesy calls for VVIP and Head of Missions

- Handles all official messages from foreign missions and Brunei Darussalam missions

**Consular Affairs Section**

Provides consular assistance to Brunei Darussalam's nationals and its interest abroad in accordance with the provisions of the 1963 Vienna Convention on Consular Relations.

 

[TABLE]

.

Provides and administers Diplomatic Privileges and Immunities accorded to Foreign Diplomatic Missions to Brunei Darussalam and its diplomatic and non-diplomatic individuals, in accordance with the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations 1961and for other matters connected therewith.

 

The Consular Affairs Section is responsible for obtaining diplomatic clearance for foreign military/private vessels and aircrafts entering the territory of Brunei Darussalam and Brunei military/private vessels and aircrafts. The Consular Affairs Section authenticates public documents issued, signed or stamped by the authorities that are not parties to the 1961, " [Hague Convention Abolishing the Requirement of Legislation for Foreign Public Document](http://www.hcch.net/index_en.php?act=conventions.text&cid=41)."
