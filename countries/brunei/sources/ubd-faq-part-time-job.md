# I am an international student and my scholarship application was unsuccesful, can I get a part time job in Brunei to fund my studies?

Updated3 February 2023

International student will be holding a student pass/student visa during their studies in UBD. Holders of this student pass/visa is not allowed to undertake any form of employment.
