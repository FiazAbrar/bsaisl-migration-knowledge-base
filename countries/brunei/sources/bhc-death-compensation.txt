 Death Compensation 

Content: Pages

Private

The Embassy facilitates the realization of the death compensation money from the sponsor or the company for those who died by accident in the workplace or road accident or killed through court procedure. The legal heirs of the deceased authorize the Embassy through Power of Attorney to take legal course for the realization of the compensation money. Once the Power of Attorney is received by the Embassy from the legal heir of the deceased, the Embassy submit the file to the enlisted lawyer to move. After successful completion of the court procedure, the compensation money cheque is deposited to the Bank account of the Embassy of Bangladesh. Then the Embassy remits the money to the account of the Wage Earners Welfare Board which disburse the money to the legal heirs of the deceased.
Government

In the event of a Government Compensation (Workmen's Compensation) case, the company must first submit the relevant report to the Labour Department (LD), with Shena also being informed or involved in the process. The Labour Department will then require a medical report from RIPAS or the hospital where the patient is receiving treatment. Once the medical report is submitted, RIPAS prepares the necessary report, which is assessed by the Labour Department. Following the assessment, the legal representative holding the Power of Attorney (POA) on behalf of the family becomes involved. At this stage, BHC is also involved to facilitate the process. The matter is then referred to the MFA, which coordinates with Brunei High Commission in Dhaka. Arrangements are subsequently made for the transfer of funds, and the compensation will be remitted directly to the beneficiary in Bangladesh.

NO COMPANSATION WILL BE PROVIDED FOR ILLEGAL WORKERS OR WORKERS WITHOUT ANY INSURANCE
