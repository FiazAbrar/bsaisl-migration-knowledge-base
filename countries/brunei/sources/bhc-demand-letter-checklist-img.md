Demand Letter checklist
(Revised Checklist - October 2026)

The Bangladesh High Commission requests the agencies and companies to arrange the documents according to the list below. You are requested to bring a copy of this list attached to each demand letter during submission.

No. | Document | Attached? | Checked by BHC
1 Agency Demand Letter (with contact number and email)
2 Company Demand Letter (with contact number and email of recruiting officer)
3 Authorization Letter
4 Worker's Contract (With Company Seal and Sign)
5 Valid Job Centre Approval
6 Company Profile
7 Company X Form (OR) 16/17 License
8 Certificate of Registration
9 Identity Card Copy of Owner/s
10 List of existing Bangladeshi workers (Name, Passport Number and contact number)
11 Projects Completed and Upcoming Projects (with pictures)
12 Business Establishment/Office Agreement
13 Pictures of Business Establishment/Office
14 Staff House Agreement
15 Staff House Pictures
16 Additional Documents (Such as Employment Agency License, Builder's License, Certificates and etc)
17 Company's updated LD of all workers.
18 Wage receipts of all Bangladeshi workers for the past three months.

For recommended workers (maximum 2 workers), you have to submit the following documents
1 Hiring of Recommended Workers (Agency Letterhead + Worker's Contact Number in Bangladesh)
2 Hiring of Recommended Workers (Company Letterhead + Worker's Contact Number in Bangladesh)
3 Declaration for Recommendation (Pre-Selection) of Aspirant Migrant Workers
4 Workers Passport Copy
5 Worker's Employment Pass (if he has previously worked in Brunei)
