  CODE OF PRACTICE FOR THE REGISTRATION OF MOBILE PREPAID SUBSCRIBER IDENTITY MODULE (SIM) CARDS
                                    (MOBILE PREPAID SIM CARDS REGISTRATION CODE)

In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 26(1) of the Telecommunications Order 2001, the Authority for
Info-communications Technology Industry of Brunei Darussalam ("Authority") hereby issues the following
Code of Practice.

1. PRELIMINARY

1.1. Citation and commencement

         This Code of Practice may be cited as the Code of Practice for the Registration of Mobile Prepaid
         Subscriber Identity Module (SIM) Cards, also referred to as the Mobile Prepaid SIM Cards Registration
         Code ("the Code"). The Code shall commence on a date to be appointed by the Authority.

1.2. Purpose of the Code

         The purpose of this Code is to ensure accurate subscriber records and safeguarding subscribers by
         preventing SIM cards from being fraudulently registered and used under their names.

1.3. Definitions

         In this Code, unless the context otherwise requires ­

         1.3.1. "AITI Order, 2001" means the Authority for Info-communications Technology Industry of
                   Brunei Darussalam Order, 2001.

         1.3.2. "Authority" means the Authority for Info-communications Technology Industry of Brunei
                   Darussalam (also known by the acronym AITI) established by Section 3 of the AITI Order, 2001.

         1.3.3. "Mobile Service Provider(s)" means a licensee that is granted a licence under Section 5 of the
                   Telecommunications Order 2001 and engaged in the provision of public mobile
                   telecommunications services.

1.4. Legal Effect of the Code

         1.4.1. Mobile Service Providers must comply with all provisions in this Code.

         1.4.2. The obligations contained in this Code are in addition to those contained in the
                   Telecommunications Order, 2001, as well as any other regulations, licences, codes of practice
                   or standard of performance, advisory guidelines or directions issued by the Authority.

         1.4.3. To the extent that any provision of this Code is inconsistent with the provisions of the AITI
                   Order, 2001, and/or the Telecommunications Order, 2001, the provisions of the AITI Order,
                   2001 or the Telecommunications Order, 2001 shall prevail.

         1.4.4. To the extent that this Code is inconsistent with the provision of any other codes of practice or
                   standard of performance or licence terms and conditions issued by the Authority in relation to

                   the same, whether currently in force or implemented in the future, the terms of this Code shall
                   prevail.

         1.4.5. If any provision of this Code is held to be unlawful, all other provisions will remain in full force

1.5. Application of the Code to Mobile Service Providers

         Unless otherwise stated, the provisions of the Mobile Prepaid SIM Cards Registration Code shall apply
         to all Mobile Service Providers in Brunei Darussalam.

1.6. Modification of provisions

         1.6.1. The Authority, where appropriate, will modify the provisions of this Code to reflect changes
                   made in other ancillary regulations, the adoption of new best practices and technology
                   advancement.

         1.6.2. This Code may be modified by the Authority pursuant to periodic review or on its own initiative
                   at any time. The Authority may conduct consultations prior to adopting such modifications.

2. REGISTRATION OF MOBILE PREPAID SUBSCRIBER IDENTITY MODULE (SIM) CARDS

2.1. Compulsory Recording of Subscriber Details

2.1.1. Mobile Service Providers shall maintain accurate records of mobile prepaid SIM card users or
         subscribers. This shall contain, at the very least, personal details (including full name, IC
         number and address) for accurate identification of the user or subscriber.

2.1.2. Registration can be done either by physical attendance (in person) at the respective Mobile
         Service Provider's or authorised resellers' storefront or online through electronic platforms
         maintained by the respective Mobile Service Providers.

2.1.3.  Mobile Service Providers shall require subscribers to notify them of a change in ownership of
        any mobile prepaid SIM card registered under the subscriber's name. Mobile Service Providers
        shall update the records of the existing subscriber and new subscriber and register the mobile
        prepaid SIM card under the new subscriber's name. In the case where the existing subscriber
        of a mobile prepaid SIM card cannot be contacted, the Mobile Service Provider shall obtain
        information, documentation or any data that serves as evidence of ownership transfer from
        the existing subscriber of that mobile prepaid SIM card to the new subscriber.

2.2. Age Eligibility

2.2.1. Mobile Service Providers shall ensure that subscribers of mobile prepaid SIM cards are at least
         twelve (12) years old and possess a valid national identity card.

2.2.2.  For subscribers under twelve (12) years old ("child subscriber"), Mobile Service Providers shall
        ensure that the parent or legal guardian of the child subscriber registers the mobile prepaid
        SIM card under the parent or legal guardian's name and lists down the child subscriber as the
        user.

2.3. Documents Required for Registration

2.3.1. Brunei Citizens and Permanent Residents:

        2.3.1.1. Valid original yellow identity cards; or
        2.3.1.2. Valid original Brunei Military or Police Personnel identity cards; or
        2.3.1.3. Valid original purple identity cards for permanent residents.

2.3.2. Foreign Workers:

        2.3.2.1. Valid original green identity card; or
        2.3.2.2. Valid original passport and employment pass.

2.3.3. Visitors/Tourists:

        2.3.3.1. Valid original passport from their country of origin.

2.3.4. Local Businesses:

        2.3.4.1. Letter of authorisation from the business owner(s).
        2.3.4.2. Registered Company: Form X and Notice of Situation of Registered Office.
        2.3.4.3. Sole Proprietors or Partnerships: Form 16 & 17.

2.4. Use of Online or Electronic Registration Platform

2.4.1. Mobile Service Providers must ensure that their online or electronic registration platforms are
         able to perform the following functions:

        2.4.1.1. Identification: Collection of user or subscriber information; and

        2.4.1.2. Verification: Ensuring that the user or subscriber information provided belongs to
                    the user or subscriber either by:

               a) Capturing images or videos of the user or subscriber via recording or sighting
                     sufficient documents of the user or subscriber to attribute such information to
                     a particular user or subscriber; or

               b) Adopting biometric technology to scrutinise users or subscribers virtually, in
                     real-time.

2.4.2.  Prior to implementation of online or electronic registration platforms, Mobile Service Providers
        are required to submit details of the proposed online or electronic registration platform and
        its processes and demonstrate it to the Authority for approval to ensure compliance with the
        requirements in this Code.

3. COMPLIANCE

3.1. Authorised resellers

3.1.1. Mobile Service Providers shall furnish a list of its authorised resellers of mobile prepaid SIM
         cards to the Authority.

3.1.2. Mobile Service Providers and authorised resellers shall ensure compliance with the
         requirements of this Code.

3.2. Non-compliance

3.2.1.  In the case of any non-compliance to the Code, the Authority may suspend or cancel the Mobile
        Service Provider's licence in whole or in part, reduce the period for which the licence is in force,
        or impose a financial penalty of such amounts to be determined by the Authority in accordance
        to Section 8 of the Telecommunications Order, 2001.
