Country category for Visa Application

CATEGORY A

Foreign nationals of countries in this category will require a visa to enter Brunei Darussalam, which can be applied through the nearest Brunei Darussalam's Missions Abroad.

CATEGORY B

F
oreign nationals of countries in this category will require a visa to enter Brunei Darussalam, which can be applied through the nearest Brunei Darussalam's Missions Abroad.

CATEGORY C

Holders of all types passport from countries under this category could apply for Visa on Arrival upon entry into Brunei Darussalam.

CATEGORY D

Foreign nationals of countries under this category do not require any visas to enter Brunei Darussalam.
