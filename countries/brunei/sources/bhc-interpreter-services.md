Government agencies will send the Bangladesh High Commission an official letter asking whether the interpreter is available on the requested date and time. Based on that, the High Commission will communicate with the respective agency to set the session.

***With regards to court cases***

**Defendants:** The court will ask for the charge and penalty to be read to the defendant. They are then asked whether they understand what has been read to them. Based on that, they are asked if the plead guilty or maintain their innocence.

If they plead guilty, then the ‘Statement of Facts’ is read to them. Then they are asked whether they understand and agree with what has been read to them. If they do not agree, they will be asked of which particular point/s they do not agree with and the prosecution will adjust accordingly. If they agree, the court will proceed to mitigation phase where the defendant is given the chance to speak. The defendant is allowed to say what they want and those are taken into the court’s record.

If a defendant maintains innocence, the court will go to trial where witnesses will be brought forward to give evidence against the defendant. Equally, the defendant is also given the chance to bring forward their own witnesses to defend them. During trial, the defendant is also allowed the opportunity to defend themselves during cross examination where they can question the witness that is present. They can also hire a lawyer if they want.

**Witnesses:** The prosecution will ask questions to the witness in order to build up their case against the defendant. The defendant can be represented by a lawyer or remain unrepresented.
