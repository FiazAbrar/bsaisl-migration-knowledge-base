Checklist for the Bangladeshi Male Worker's Visa Attestation

1. Application for visa attestation/Demand letter using company letterhead.
2. Documents verified by the court (if applicable);
 a. Visa copy
 b. LPA/LD(Original and Photocopy).
 c. Contract of Employment Agreements(Signed by the employer with company seal).
 d. Sub-Contract Agreement Copy
3. Company profile:
 a. Portfolio of the company.
 b. License copy/16-17 & form x of the company
 c. All partners/shareholders IC Copy with contact no & signature.
 d. Bank Statement of the company (last 03 Months).
 e. Sijil ABCi (summarized the total employee information).
4. Employee details:
 a. New worker's Passport copy.
 b. Existing workers list with Passport no, IC no, Staff house address, and contact number.
 c. New workers list with contact number (including Guardian and Personal).
5. Project/Business details:
 a. Current/proposed projects summary list attached with agreements/LOA and photographs according to format.
 b. List of completed projectslist attached with photographs.
 c. Business License, Agreement copy and photographs (Shop/Restaurant/Mini Mart/Agriculture/Farm)
6. Office Details:
 a. Registered office address
 b. Tenancy Agreement copy with the photographs.
7. Staff House (Labour Camp) Details:
 a. Permanent Staff House address and living capacity of the workers with the photographs.
 b. Tenancy agreement copy.

NB: After the confirmation message of the approval need to make payment of the attestation fee for each employee B$15 through Bank Islam Brunei Darussalam (BIBD) in favor of this (Account Name. HighCommission of the People's Republic of Bangladesh, Account Number: 0000-101-0072146). official account.
