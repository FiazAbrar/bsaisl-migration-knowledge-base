 Bangladesh e-Passport (ePP) Application Checklist 

Content: Pages

Please ensure you have all original documents and their corresponding photocopies ready before attending your appointment.
1. General Documents (For All Applicants)
Application Forms:

Printed copy of your online e-Passport application form (with barcode) and the application summary/appointment slip.
Proof of Legal Status in Brunei:
 Original and a photocopy of your valid 
Brunei Visa
, 
Work Permit / Labor Card (LD)
, or 
Brunei Green Smart Identity Card (IC)
.
Primary Identification:
Original National ID (NID) card.
If you do not have an NID:
 Original English Birth Registration Certificate (BRC), which must be 17-digit and online-verifiable.
Previous Passport:

Original previous Bangladeshi passport (for renewal or reissue) plus one photocopy of the bio-data page.
2. Profession-Specific Documents (For Discount & Verification)
For Laborers / General Workers (LD Copy)
Labor Card (LD):
 Original and photocopy of your valid 
Brunei Labor Card (LD)
 to verify your registered employment and profession.
Employment Contract / Proof:
 Copy of your current employment agreement or official letter from your Brunei employer (if requested for profession verification).
For Students (Student Copy)
Student ID Card:

Photocopy of your valid University/College/School Student ID card.
Bonafide Student Certificate:

A formal letter or current registration paper from your educational institution in Brunei confirming your active enrollment (must show start and end dates of study).
3. For Applicants Under 18 Years
Birth Certificate:

Original Birth Registration Certificate (English version, online-verifiable).
Parents' ID:
 Copies of Father's and/or Mother's National ID (NID) cards.
Photograph:

One recent 
3R-size photograph
 with a white background (only required if the child is 
under 6 years old
).
4. Supporting Documents for Changes (If Applicable)
If you are requesting corrections or updates to your existing passport information (like changing your marital status or name), you must provide supporting legal proof:
Name Change (Marriage):

Registered Marriage Certificate.
Marital Status Change:

Registered Divorce Certificate/Decree (if applicable).
Legal Declarations:
 Court order or affidavit.
