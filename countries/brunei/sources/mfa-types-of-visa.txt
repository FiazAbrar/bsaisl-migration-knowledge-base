Types of Visa
Diplomatic Visa
: Visa issued to foreign nationals holding 
Diplomatic
 passport.
Social visit Visa
: Visa issued for the purpose of social visit or for a tourist.
Professional Visit Visa
: Visa issued for the purpose of professional work/expertise required for a particular project awarded in Brunei Darussalam.
Business Visit Visa
: Visa issued for the purpose of investment or business related activities in Brunei Darussalam.
Employment Visa
: Visa issued for the purpose of employment in Brunei Darussalam.
Dependent Visa: 
Visa issued to spouse and Children (under the age of 18 years old), whose husband is working in Brunei Darussalam.
Student Visa
: Visa issued for the purpose of studying in Brunei Darussalam.

Transit Visa:
  Visa issued to any person who is in transit and enroute to a third country via Brunei Darussalam.

Note: 
Transit Visa is only applicable and issued at the 
BRUNEI INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT ONLY.
Transit
 visa may be granted to the nationals of the People’s Republic of China, Australia, Russia, Tajikistan, Mongolia, India, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia and Taiwan, if they are 
on
 transit to or from a third country 
by 
air
. However, they are required to 
fulfil
 the following conditions:
Have a valid passport
Have a confirmed onward 
air-ticket
A valid visa to the third country, where appropriate
The passenger continue their journey to the third country within the 72 hours from transit visa granted
Sufficient funds throughout the period of transit in Brunei Darussalam
Transit
 visa may also be granted to all nationals holding valid
Residence Permit
 issued by Australia, New Zealand, United States of America (USA), Japan and 
United Kingdom
 and required to fulfill the conditions mentioned above.
Extension of visa is not permitted.
Types of Entry
Single Entry Visa
: Visa is used for a single journey to enter Brunei Darussalam.
Multiple Entry Visa
: Visa is used for multiple journeys into Brunei Darussalam.
