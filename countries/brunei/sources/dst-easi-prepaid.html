Frequently Asked Questions

1. How much is the Easi SIM Pack?

The Easi Sim Pack is $0, you will only need to purchase a $10 Easi Recharge to activate the account.  Customers can use the $10 credit balance to buy add-ons!

Note: Customers will need to recharge their Easi for the first time within 30 days from the day of the SIM purchase date.

2. Where can customers get the Easi SIM Pack?

You can get the Easi SIM Pack at:

1. DST Website here

2. DST Branches. Learn More >

3. DST Exclusive Dealers. Learn More >

4. DST Sales Agents

5. DEE on Whatsapp. Learn More >

6. 16888

3. Can the Easi SIM Pack services be used before it is successfully registered?

No. All the services on the new Easi SIM Pack will be inactive until the number/SIM is successfully registered.

4. How can customers register their new Easi SIM?

Customers can register their Easi SIM at the following;      

1. Self-Registration on MyDST

2. Assisted by staff members at DST Branches, DST Dealers, and Sales Agents

5. What happens after the SIM Pack is successfully registered?

Once the SIM Pack is successfully registered, all services will be automatically activated, and the SIM card will be fully functional.

6. What documents are required to self-register the SIM through MyDST?

You will need to have their official documentation ready to self-register through MyDST;

a. Bruneian Citizens and Permanent Residents – Valid original Identification Cards (IC)

b. Valid original Brunei Military or Police Personnel Identity cards;

c. Foreigners – Valid original Passport and employment pass

7. How do I self-register through the MyDST app?

You can self-register your Easi SIM through the MyDST app by following the steps below;

a) Without MyDST Login

b) With MyDST Login – This flow is only applicable if you already have a MyDST account

8. How will I know if my registration was successful?

After you have completed your self-registration process a ‘SIM Successfully Registered’ will be displayed.

9. Do I need to be connected to the Internet to do the self-registration?

Yes, you will need to be connected to the Internet to access MyDST and do your self-registration.

10. Do I need to use a smartphone to do the self-registration?

Yes, you will need to download the MyDST app into your smartphone and then do the self-registration process.

11. What happens if I don’t recharge my SIM within 30 days of activation?

Upon successful SIM activation, you must recharge your number within 30 days to keep it active.

12. Do I need to activate my SIM immediately after registering my number?

Yes, you will need to activate and recharge your number immediately after registering

1. How to activate the Easi SIM card?

You will need to recharge your Easi SIM card with a $10 Easi Recharge and call any number to activate it.

2. What happens when the Easi SIM is not registered?

Your Easi will not be activated and you will not be able to use the services

3. What are the USSD codes for Easi?

Dial the USSD codes below to:

  • Roaming Calls – *101#
  • Check your credit balance – *102#
  • Recharge your number – *103*(12-digit PIN number)#
  • Last call details – *104#
  • Credit transfer – *105*(Easi mobile number)*(credit transfer denomination)#
  • Check your credit bonus – *106#
  • Recharge another Easi number – *107*(Easi mobile number)*(12-digit PIN number)#
  • Credit conversion – *109#
  • Roaming add-on – *110#
  • IDD add-on *117#
  • Check and purchase add-on data packages – *133#
1. What are Add-Ons?

Add-ons are data packages that serve as a supplement to your existing data plan/bundle.

2. How to buy the Easi Add-Ons?

You can buy the Easi add-ons by dialing *133# or via MyDST app and website.

3. How to pay for the Add-Ons bought?

The amount to buy for the add-on will be deducted from your credit balance.

4. Will I be able to buy TWO (2) add-ons at the same time?

You are only allowed to purchase ONE (1) add-on at a time.

5. Can I cancel an add-on subscription?

No, once you have bought an add-on you will not be able to cancel it.

6. How does the add-on data package validity work?

The validity period is measured/counted to the exact minute of the predetermined validity period of each package. For example, if the add-on data package is valid for 1 day (24 hours) and the time of the purchase is at 10:59 AM, then the add-on data package will expire at 10: 58 AM the next day.

7. Will my add-on subscription be renewed automatically?

No, the add-on subscription will not be automatically renewed upon its expiry.

8. Will I be able to use my add-on if my credit balance is zero?

Yes, you can still utilize your add-on if your credit balance is zero.

9. Will I be able to use my add-on if my credit has expired?

No, you need to recharge your Easi before you continue to use any add-on.

10. How do I check my add-on balance?

You can check your add-on by dialing *133# or via MyDST app and website.

11. Will I be notified if I have fully utilised my add-on?

Yes, you will be notified via an SMS notification once your add-on is utilised by 50%, 80% and once it has been fully utilised (100%).

Note: You will not receive an SMS notification once your add-on data package expires.

1. What is a Combo Plan?

A Combo Add-on is an add-on that bundles calls, SMS and data in one add-on. 

2. If I have purchased a Combo Add-on and have fully utilised the data bundle, will I be able to purchase a new Combo add-on?

No, you will not be able to buy a new Combo Add-on if you still have an active Combo Add-on. You can only repurchase a Combo Add-on after you have utilised all your bundles (Calls, SMS and Data) or once it expires, whichever comes first.

3. If I had purchased a Combo Add-on, could I purchase a data add-on at the same time?

Yes, you can buy a data add-on on top of your active Combo Add-on. However, you will not be able to buy an additional data add-on if you are currently on an active data add-on.

4. I am currently subscribed to an Easi Combo Plan and an add-on data plan. Which data bundle will be utilised first?

The data bundle with the shorter remaining validity period will be utilised first.

1. How much is the Easi Tourist SIM Pack?

The Easi Tourist SIM pack is $15, which includes 3GB local data and $3 credit bonus for local voice calls and SMS valid for 7 days from the activation date.

2. How long can I use the Easi Tourist SIM for?

The Easi Tourist SIM is only valid for 30 days and once the 30 days is up, the SIM will automatically expire. During this 30 day period, you can recharge your credit balance anytime to continue using our services.

Note: If you’re staying in Brunei for more than 30 days, you may buy our $0 Easi SIM pack. Learn More >

3. Where can I purchase an Easi Tourist SIM Pack?

Once you’ve landed in Brunei, turn right at the arrivals and you’ll see our DST Airport branch! Our sales representative will be happy to assist you in the registration process.

If you’re in a rush and haven’t managed to get your SIM, don’t worry. You can still get one at any of our selected DST Branches.

Don’t forget to bring your passport and travel documents with you.

4. I’ve got my SIM! How do I activate it?

Activating your SIM is simple. Make your first call or text and your SIM should be good to go.

5. What are the charges, pre-load credit and validity for Easi Tourists?

The Easi Tourist SIM pack is $15, which includes 3GB local data and $3 credit bonus for local voice calls and SMS valid for 7 days from the activation date.

6. What services are available for the Easi Tourist SIM Pack?

The services offered for our Easi Tourist SIM Pack are:

1. Incoming and outgoing calls

2. Incoming and outgoing SMS

3. Data services i.e. Internet browsing

7. What are the USSD codes for Easi Tourists?

Dial the USSD codes below to:

  • Check your credit balance – *102#
  • Recharge your number – *103*(12-digit PIN number)#
  • Last call details – *104#
  • Check your credit bonus – *106#
  • Recharge another Easi number – *107*(Easi mobile number*(12-digit PIN number)#
  • Check and purchase add-on data packages – *133#
8. How do I check my balance for the $3 bonus?

You can check your bonus credit by dialing *106# or via MyDST app and website.

9. How do I check my balance for the 3GB internet?

You can check your add-on balance by dialing *133# or via MyDST app and website.

10. Will I be notified if I have fully utilised my 3GB local data package?

Yes, you will be notified via an SMS notification once your add-on is utilised by 50%, 80% and once it has been fully utilised (100%).

11. Can I make International Direct Dialing (IDD) calls by using my Easi Tourist SIM?

Yes, you can make IDD calls by using our ’00’ or ’099’ published rates or IDD ‘095’ depending on the rates and country. For more information on our International services including rates and charges, click here.

12. Will I be able to upgrade my Easi Tourist SIM Pack to Mobi?

No, you will not be able to upgrade or migrate your Easi Tourist SIM Pack to Mobi.

13. Can I change my plan from Easi Tourist to Normal Easi Plan?

No, you will not be able to change your plan from Easi Tourist to Normal Easi Plan.
