# BDCB Alert List

Brunei Darussalam Central Bank (BDCB) would like to alert the members of the public to a list of persons (i.e. companies and individuals) who, based on information available to BDCB at the time of publication, may be or may have been wrongly perceived as being licensed, authorised or regulated by BDCB.

This list is without prejudice to any further assessment by BDCB regarding the activities of the persons concerned.

Search

Type of Activity

- ☐ Investment
  Offering investment advice, investment products, and/or a platform or avenue for investment services. Such activity may constitute a regulated activity under the [Securities Markets Order, 2013](https://www.agc.gov.bn/AGC%20Images/LAWS/Gazette_PDF/2013/EN/s059.pdf) if the activities involve:

  1.  Any securities instruments as defined under the Order; or
  2.  Managing investments by taking money from investors (public) to invest and manage the money; or
  3.  Any other regulated activity as defined under the Order.

  Carrying out investment activities without a license may be a breach of the [Securities Market Order, 2013](https://www.agc.gov.bn/AGC%20Images/LAWS/Gazette_PDF/2013/EN/s059.pdf).
- ☐ Money Lending
  Providing money lending service without a license, which may be a breach of the [Moneylenders Act, Cap 62](https://www.agc.gov.bn/AGC%20Images/LAWS/ACT_PDF/M/CHAPTER%20062(2019%20ED).pdf). This service may include the lending of money to settle an existing bank loan or financing by charging interests or service fee.
- ☐ Money Changer
  Carrying out money changing business without a license, which may be a breach of the [Money-Changing and Remittance Businesses Act Cap 174](https://www.agc.gov.bn/AGC%20Images/LAWS/ACT_PDF/M/CHAPTER%20174.pdf).
- ☐ Money Remittance
  Carrying out money remittance/transfer business without a license, which may be a breach of the [Money-Changing and Remittance Businesses Act Cap 174](https://www.agc.gov.bn/AGC%20Images/LAWS/ACT_PDF/M/CHAPTER%20174.pdf).
- ☐ Pawn Broker
  Carrying out pawnbroker business without a license, which may be a breach of the [Pawnbrokers Order, 2002](https://www.agc.gov.bn/AGC%20Images/LAWS/BLUV/PAWNBROKERS%20ORDER,%202002.pdf).
- ☐ Deposit Taking
  'Deposit' is a sum of money paid on terms under which it will be repaid (with or without interest or at a premium), and either on demand or at a time or in circumstances agreed by or on behalf of the persons making the payment and the persons receiving it.

  Receiving, taking or accepting deposits, may be a breach of the [Banking Act, Cap 95](https://www.bdcb.gov.bn/docs/regulatory/banking/Banking%20Act,%20Cap%2095.pdf) or [Islamic Banking Act, 2008](https://www.agc.gov.bn/AGC%20Images/LAWS/BLUV/ISLAMIC%20BANKING%20ORDER,%202008.pdf).
- ☐ Any other suspicious and/or unethical activities
  Activities that are deemed suspicious and/or unethical, which may have a negative financial implication to the public.

  If the activity involves the account issuing services, meaning:

  The service of issuing a payment account to any person in Brunei Darussalam or any service relating to any operation required for operating a payment account including:

  1.  Any service that enables money to be placed in a payment account; or
  2.  Any service that enables money to be withdrawn from a payment account,

  the activity may fall under the [Notice on Requirements for Payment Systems, 2020](https://www.bdcb.gov.bn/docs/consumer-issues/alert-list/Notice-on-Requirements-for-Payment-Systems-Amendment.pdf).

## 

The BDCB Alert List was created to inform the public about entities that are suspicious, or not licensed or regulated by BDCB. Entities offering regulated financial activities\* must be licensed by BDCB. This is to protect Brunei Darussalam's financial system, as well as financial consumers.

\*such as dealing with currency options, money changing, money lending, money remittance and providing financial investments.

Purpose of the Alert List

- To inform and remind the public against participating in any unlicensed financial schemes.
- To advise members of the public against making any deposits or investments with persons that are not regulated under the relevant laws.
- To provide early warning to consumers by highlighting to them that if they choose to deal with an unregulated entity, they do not have all the protection afforded under the relevant laws.
- To remind the public to conduct the necessary checks with the relevant authorities.

Benefits of Dealing with Licensed Financial Institutions

- Protected by Laws of Brunei Darussalam.
- Assurance that you are dealing with qualified professionals and experts.
- Can seek BDCB's assistance if you suspect any misconduct by licensed financial institutions.

## 

Only **banks, insurance companies, takaful operators, capital market and securities institutions, money changers, remittance service providers, as well as other entities and approved payment system operators under the relevant laws and regulations administered by BDCB** are allowed to provide financial services in Brunei Darussalam.

The list of the licensed financial service providers can be accessed [here](https://www.bdcb.gov.bn/regulatory/list-of-bdcb-licensees)

## 

![Icon](https://www.bdcb.gov.bn/img/consumer-issues/bdcb-alert-list/consequences.svg)

Consequences

- Protection under the laws administered by **BDCB is not applicable** should the members of the public choose to deal with illegal financial service providers.
- Members of the public participating in illegal financial activities could also **be charged** under the law.

![Icon](https://www.bdcb.gov.bn/img/consumer-issues/bdcb-alert-list/advise.svg)

Thus, members of the public are advised not to

- Deal with any of illegal financial service providers.
- Reveal or disclose personal financial credentials to any illegal financial service providers.
- Make any deposit or investment with individuals and entities that are not regulated under the relevant laws.

## 

Recognise the signs of financial scams!

![icon](https://www.bdcb.gov.bn/img/consumer-issues/bdcb-alert-list/high-returns.svg)

Financial scams often offer high returns, with little to no risk within a short period of time.

![icon](https://www.bdcb.gov.bn/img/consumer-issues/bdcb-alert-list/recruitment-of-members.svg)

They often disguise themselves as Multi Level Marketing (MLM), but without any clear product offering. Instead, they focus on recruitment of members, where their membership fees are used to create income or returns for early members.

![icon](https://www.bdcb.gov.bn/img/consumer-issues/bdcb-alert-list/sms-scams.svg)

Financial scams may ask for you to come up with several payments to claim your rewards. For example, in the case of investment scams, victims are told to pay a processing fee before they can obtain their money in lump sum.

Make sure you only deal with licensed financial institutions.

![icon](https://www.bdcb.gov.bn/img/consumer-issues/bdcb-alert-list/licensed-financial-institutions.svg)

Click [here](https://www.bdcb.gov.bn/regulatory/list-of-bdcb-licensees) to find the list of licensed financial institutions.

![icon](https://www.bdcb.gov.bn/img/consumer-issues/bdcb-alert-list/bdcb-alert-list.svg)

Click [here](https://www.bdcb.gov.bn/consumer/bdcb-alert-list) for the BDCB Alert List. This is a list of companies and websites which are neither authorised nor approved under the relevant laws and regulations administered by BDCB or whose activities raise a suspicion of illegal activity.

![icon](https://www.bdcb.gov.bn/img/consumer-issues/bdcb-alert-list/bdcb-app.svg)

Download BDCB’s app where you can search for entities to check whether they are licensed or not.

Never reveal your bank details to any unauthorised or unknown persons!

![icon](https://www.bdcb.gov.bn/img/consumer-issues/bdcb-alert-list/bank-details.svg)

Some scammers may impersonate bank officers or staff, and will ask for your bank details. Make sure you do not reveal your bank details to people you do not know.

Members of the public are advised to be careful and not fall prey to these schemes. Be alert and look out for the following common features of these illegal schemes:

- They may approach you via mail, telephone, internet, or in person, with the intention of taking money from you for
  goods or services that they have no intention to provide.
- They may promise high returns aimed to attract the members of the public to participate in the schemes
- You may not be given copies of any documentation including agreements on the investment.
- No assurance that they can continue to pay the high returns.

**Note:** These financial scams will fail eventually when there are no new deposits being continually received by them. At such time, they will collapse and the depositors or investors will lose their investments.

Should you fall victim to a financial scam, please lodge a report to the Royal Brunei Police Force at the nearest police station or call 993.
