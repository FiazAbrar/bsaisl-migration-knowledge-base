# Privacy Policy – Migration Navigator (test version)

Last updated: 24 November 2026

The Migration Navigator answers questions about working abroad from Bangladesh, using guides researched by the BSAISL team. You can use it on WhatsApp, on Telegram and on its web page. It is a prototype under development. The same text is published on the bot's web page at `/privacy` (`web/privacy.html`); keep the two the same.

## What we keep
- **Your messages and the bot's replies**, so the bot can follow the conversation.
- **A short note about your situation** (for example the country you are asking about, your stage in the process, and what you have already paid), so the bot can help you next time without asking again.
- **A scrambled code instead of your identity.** Your WhatsApp number, Telegram id or web session is turned into a code (a keyed hash) before anything is stored. The code can't be turned back into your number.
- **On the web page:** a random session cookie, valid for 24 hours. Your internet (IP) address is used only in scrambled form, to limit how many messages one network can send.
- **Counters** that limit how many messages one person or network can send. They are deleted after about a day.

Numbers that look like a phone, national ID (NID), passport or bank card number are removed from your message before it is stored or sent to the AI service. Please never send passwords, bank or card numbers, NID or passport numbers.

## How long we keep it
- Messages: deleted after **90 days**.
- The note about your situation: deleted **12 months after your last message**.
- Send **delete my data** (or **আমার তথ্য মুছে দিন**, or /delete on Telegram, or the button on the web page) at any time to delete everything at once. **reset** also deletes everything and starts a new conversation.

Using the bot means you agree to this. The bot's first reply to a new person says so.

## How we use it
- To answer your questions and keep the context of your conversation.
- To test and improve the accuracy of the answers.

## Who it is shared with
- **Meta (WhatsApp)** and **Telegram**, which carry the messages on those apps.
- **An AI service** (accessed through OpenRouter), which reads your question and the relevant guide text to write the answer. We ask for providers that do not store or train on this data.
- **Our hosting providers**, which run the bot and its database (servers in Singapore).
- **Sentry**, which receives error reports so we can fix problems. Error reports do not include your messages, number or address.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
