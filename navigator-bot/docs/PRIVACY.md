# Privacy Policy – Navigator Bot (test version)

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Navigator Bot is a test WhatsApp assistant that answers questions about working abroad from Bangladesh, using a published guide. It is a prototype under development and is used only by a small group of testers.

## What we collect
- The messages you send to the bot and the replies it gives.
- A WhatsApp user identifier, which we store in scrambled (hashed) form, so we can keep your conversation in order.
- On the web chat: a random session cookie, valid for 24 hours, and your internet (IP) address. Both are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, and only to keep your conversation in order and to limit how many messages one person or network can send. Limit counters are deleted after about a day.
- Technical data needed to deliver messages (time sent, delivery status).

We do not ask for, and you should not send, passwords, bank or card numbers, national ID or passport numbers.

## How we use it
- To answer your questions and keep the context of your conversation.
- To test and improve the accuracy of the answers.

## Who it is shared with
- **Meta (WhatsApp)**, which carries the messages.
- **An AI service** (accessed through OpenRouter), which reads your question and the relevant guide text to write the answer. We ask for providers that do not store or train on this data.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

## How long we keep it
Conversation history is deleted after 30 days. Anonymous test results (questions and answers without your identifier) may be kept to measure the bot's accuracy.

You can stop using the bot at any time. To have your conversation deleted, contact us at the address below.
