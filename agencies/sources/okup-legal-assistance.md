# OKUP Legal Assistance Service

### Standing by Migrant Workers. Defending Rights. Restoring Dignity.

At **OKUP**, we believe every migrant worker deserves justice, dignity, and a voice — no matter where they are. Our **Legal Assistance Service** is designed to protect the rights of migrant workers and their families through accessible, professional, and empathetic legal support.

## What We Do

## ⚖️

#### Legal Counseling

Free legal advice to returnee migrants, trafficking victims, exploited workers, and their families.

## 📋

#### Case Filing & Representation

Direct legal support for filing cases in local and international jurisdictions, working with government agencies and courts.

## 👩‍⚖️

#### Women & Vulnerable Groups

Gender-sensitive and trauma-informed support prioritizing female migrants, trafficking survivors, and children.

## 📚

#### Legal Awareness & Training

Legal literacy workshops empowering migrant workers and communities to understand and claim their rights.

## Who Can Access Our Services?

-  Current and returnee migrant workers
-  Families of migrants in distress
-  Survivors of trafficking and exploitation

All services are **confidential**, **non-discriminatory**, and provided **free of cost**.
