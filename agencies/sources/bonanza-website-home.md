HELP & SUPPORT

## Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding our recruitment process, visa procedures, and requirements.

## How can a candidate apply for overseas jobs through Bonanza Overseas?

Candidates can apply by submitting their CV through our online "Form" page, visiting our Gulshan-2 office directly, or responding to our official circulars published on BMET and our social platforms.

## What is the license number of Bonanza Overseas?

Bonanza Overseas is an officially approved and licensed recruiting agency in Bangladesh under Recruiting License No. \*\*RL-579\*\*, affiliated with BAIRA and the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare.

## How long does the entire deployment process take?

The mobilization timeframe depends on the employer's visa issuance, trade testing, medical clearance (GAMCA), and BMET smart card processing, which typically takes between 2 to 4 weeks.
