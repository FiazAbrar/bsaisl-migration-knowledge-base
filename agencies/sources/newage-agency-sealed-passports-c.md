# Recruiting agency sealed for keeping passports of Malaysia job aspirants

\

The Bureau of Manpower Employment and Training on Thursday sealed a recruiting agency in Dhaka and seized more than 600 passports of people aspiring to migrate to Malaysia, before formal recruitment for the country’s labour market had been announced.

A BMET mobile court, led by executive magistrate and deputy director Syed Shamsul Tabrize, conducted the drive against Triple A Overseas of in the capital’s Paltan and fined the agency Tk 50,000 for illegally keeping the passports, according to a press release.

The mobile court also seized the passports from the agency.

‘Keeping passports and interviewing aspirant migrants without demand letters is punishable offence under the Migration act,’ Tabrize said.

The action came as Bangladesh and Malaysia were preparing to resume recruitment of Bangladeshi workers following the suspension of Malaysian labour market in May 2024.

Expatriates’ Welfare and Overseas Employment Minister Ariful Haque Choudhury said the government would not allow any recruiting agency to deceive or exploit migrant workers or aspiring migrants.

The passports would be handed back to their owners following due process, while further action would be taken against the recruiting agency, the release said.

Prime Minister Tarique Rahman’s visit to Malaysia in June raised hopes of reopening the Malaysian labour market for Bangladeshi workers.
