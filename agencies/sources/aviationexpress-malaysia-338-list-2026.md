## 338 Bangladeshi agencies, Including BOESL, Added to Malaysia recruitment list

প্রকাশ: শুক্রবার । আগস্ট ২৮, ২০২৬

![338 Bangladeshi agencies, Including BOESL, Added to Malaysia recruitment list](https://aviationexpress.news/storage/news/5kniBPKZgQET0zTI0zdTHbHGV2FnXTkwHG43JhXR.jpg)

Malaysia's Foreign Workers Centralised Management System (FWCMS) has published a new list of 338 recruiting agencies authorized to send workers from Bangladesh to Malaysia, expanding significantly on an earlier list of just 25 agencies.

\

The new list, released Thursday, includes Bangladesh Overseas Employment Services Limited (BOESL), the country's state-run recruiting agency, under which 62 agencies have been placed. Additionally, 250 agencies have been added as sub-agents under the 25 recruiting agencies that were named in the earlier list.

\

Notably, since the first list included only 25 agencies, stakeholders had called for Malaysia's labour market to be kept open to all recruiting agencies.
