Labour Recruiters

Labour Recruiters participating in the IRIS Certification Program

We are pleased to share a list of labour recruiters that have applied for IRIS Certification. As labour recruiters move through the IRIS certification process, each will be listed below, updated as the labour recruiter achieves certification milestones.

*A Certification Status of “Withdrawn” indicates that an accredited certificate previously issued under the IRIS Certification Scheme is no longer valid. This status demonstrates that, at one time, the Labour Recruiter’s operations were successfully certified as fully IRIS compliant, but are no longer continuously validated as meeting the certification scheme’s requirements.

Note: As indicated in the IRIS Scheme General Requirements, an IRIS Certificate may be voluntarily discontinued for many reasons – consequently a ‘Withdrawn’ status should not be construed as an indication that the Labour Recruiter is no longer operating in an fair and ethical manner, or that the Labour Recruiter is no longer capable of meeting the principles of the IRIS standard. Interested parties seeking further assurance of a Labour Recruiter’s present IRIS capabilities must reach-out to that recruiter directly.
