# Malaysia: Founder of co. Bestinet & proprietor of Bangladeshi recruitment co. Catharsis Intl. linked to extortion & trafficking of migrants; incl. cos comments

5 Nov 2024. Author: By Anders Melin, Bloomberg (UK)

Bangladeshi police have asked the Malaysian government to arrest & extradite two businessmen as part of a probe of alleged "money laundering, extortion and trafficking of migrant workers". One of the men, Aminul Islam, founded Bestinet, and the police have also asked that the use of the company's software (used to process and monitor migrant workers in Malaysia) is temporarily halted. The other man, Ruhul Amin, is proprietor of the Dhaka-based recruitment firm Catharsis International.

Action taken: Aminul's lawyer said they have not received any information about the letter and categorically denied the allegations raised in it. A representative for Bestinet said the company isn’t aware of the letter from Bangladesh’s police including the request that Malaysian authorities stop using Bestinet software, and declined to comment.

A representative for Catharsis said by email that Ruhul refuted any allegations and has “always been operating legally, legitimately and ethically.”
