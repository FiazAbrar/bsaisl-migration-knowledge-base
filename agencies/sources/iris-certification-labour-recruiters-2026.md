*\*A Certification Status of “Withdrawn” indicates that an accredited certificate previously issued under the IRIS Certification Scheme is no longer valid. ***Note**: As indicated in the [IRIS Scheme General Requirements](https://nam02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Firiscertification.org%2Firis-documents%2F&data=05%7C02%7CLStoddard%40sa-intl.org%7C5e5c3f15110c4c631e2a08deff07aa2b%7C1b310f9b1f3d4b1b933ca97340c65749%7C0%7C0%7C639228605548540962%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=cuJO0EFDK%2FoKwIAj9W0A25cVUE%2BbE%2BszqCPAaovLNAU%3D&reserved=0), an IRIS Certificate may be voluntarily discontinued for many reasons – consequently a ‘Withdrawn’ status should not be construed as an indication that the Labour Recruiter is no longer operating in an fair and ethical manner, or that the Labour Recruiter is no longer capable of meeting the principles of the IRIS standard. Interested parties seeking further assurance of a Labour Recruiter’s present IRIS capabilities must reach-out to that recruiter directly.*
