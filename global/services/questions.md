# Services for migrants and their families open questions

These are the questions the Services for migrants and their families doc can't yet answer, grouped by the doc's sections. Each is a "Need to know" in `doc.md`.

## Before you go

- Did the Probashi Kallyan cells promised in March 2024 open, and where does someone go to find one?
- Has any district opened the one-stop service described in May 2026, and which?
- Is the SIMS project running a second phase in 2026, and in which upazilas?
- Do the CV builder and the online-doctor service still run in 2026, and does a doctor's consultation through Ami Probashi cost anything?
- Was KOICA's USD 1 crore for the Chattogram Bangladesh-Korea TTC signed, and what courses does the centre now run?
- Where does NSDA's caregiving training run, what does it cost, and does its certificate count for caregiver jobs abroad?
- Were the language training centres the Prime Minister ordered in March 2026 opened, and where?
- Has the BRTC driver training started, where does someone apply, and does it cost anything?
- When does the next Korean batch at the Dhaka Mohila TTC open, and do other TTCs run the same course?
- Does the course prepare for the EPS-TOPIK test that South Korea's work route uses, and how many of its trainees have gone to Korea?
- After a MyGov approval, does the worker still have to go to the branch with the guarantors to sign and to collect the money, and how many visits does that take?
- How does a family or a returnee claim the write-off: at which branch, with which papers (death certificate, passport with arrival stamp)?
- Has the government announced an interest-free loan for people going abroad, and on what terms?
- Is this the same loan as the Tk 10 lakh solvency loan for students announced in March 2026, and how many people have taken it?
- Has the special bank-loan programme for meritorious students going abroad for higher study, proposed in the 2026-27 budget, started, and is it separate from PKB's loan?
- What is the "certificate of eligibility" PKB asks for, and who issues it?
- Which training abroad has the loan paid for so far (which countries and courses), and is the money sent straight to the institution?
- How large is the discount, is the fare still offered in 2026, and what proof of a first work visa does Flight Expert ask for?
- Apart from the Foreign Language and Skills Centre (see Online courses at BMET's Foreign Language and Skills Centre), which TTCs run language courses in 2026, for how long, and at what fee?
- When does FLSC open its next batch, and does it run classroom courses as well as online ones?
- Which EU countries does the Talent Partnership train for, where is the training held, and how does a worker apply?
- Is the Tk 900 per lakh risk-cover fee paid in cash at the branch or deducted from the loan before it is paid out?
- The ministry's press release of March 2026 described this loan as collateral-free up to Tk 10 lakh, for up to 3 years, with N5 for Japan and tuition sent straight to the institution, unlike circular 22/2025: has PKB revised the circular, and which terms apply now?
- Is this 11% loan still offered alongside the 9% loan (see The skills and language loan funded by Bangladesh Bank), or has that scheme replaced it?
- What is the Guardian Probashi Prohori premium, how much does each benefit pay, and where does a worker buy it: at a BRAC branch, or only as a BRAC microfinance member?

## At the airport

- Does the third terminal have a Probashi Kallyan desk in 2026, and where in the terminal is it?
- Where does a worker on leave now print the leave permission if the third terminal has no desk?
- How does a migrant recognise and reach one of the IOM-sponsored helpers at the airport?
- Do card holders get the Biman discount and Balaka lounge in practice, and how large is the discount?
- BMET lists a desk at Osmani airport, Sylhet, but the minister said in February 2026 that Sylhet had no such service centre: is the Sylhet desk staffed in 2026, and where in the terminal is it?
- Where exactly is the Probashi lounge (which terminal, before or after immigration), and is it open to returning workers as well as departing ones?

## While abroad

- Has the Board or the ministry hired legal firms abroad, in which countries, and how does a worker in trouble reach one?
- Has any Bangladesh Support Centre opened, at which embassies, and how does a worker in jail or in a dispute reach it?
- Does a worker evacuated with Welfare Board money, as from Iran in 2026, have to repay any of it later?
- How did the ministry help migrants whose visas expired because flights were cancelled in 2026, and how many got that help?
- Which missions take NID registration in 2026, and what does a migrant without an NID bring to apply?
- Can a migrant whose MRP and NID differ get an e-passport at the mission on the MRP's data in 2026?
- What does a letter writer charge, and which items (medicines, food) may not be sent to the Gulf by post?
- Which countries, fees and deadlines apply to BOU's overseas programmes for the 2026 batch?

## If a worker dies abroad

- How does a family get a second post-mortem in Bangladesh: whom do they apply to (police, the magistrate, the Welfare Board desk at the airport), and does it cost anything?
- For a worker who was legal and had clearance, who pays the cost of sending the body home from abroad (the employer, the Welfare Board or the family), and is a collection among workers still needed in 2026?

## Help for the family at home

- Is the disabled-child allowance open to new applicants in 2026, and when?
- Does filing under the 2010 Act at the court cost anything, and does the upazila women affairs officer help a woman fill in the application?
- When is a winner's first scholarship payment made after the list is published, and is it paid into the student's bank account?

## Coming home

- Do PKSF's partner NGOs give returnees priority for farm demonstrations or loans, and how does a returnee find the partner NGO in his upazila?
- What help (seed, training, advice) does an upazila agriculture office give a returnee starting a farm, and does it cost anything?
- Was the migrant-owned hospital company formed, and can migrants buy shares in 2026?
- Has the returnee rehabilitation fund, or any of the budget's returnee programmes, started; how large is it, and who can apply?
- Has the seven-day arrangement for migrants' land disputes started, and where does a migrant or the family apply?
- Can a returnee migrant apply to these schemes, at which banks, with which papers, and is there a migrant quota?
- Has RAISE been extended or followed by a new project after June 2026, and where does a returnee register now?
- How does a returnee join Prottasha: in which districts does it work, where does he or she register, who qualifies, and what papers are needed?

## How the services are paid for

- Has the Welfare Board published an annual report or accounts for 2024-25 or 2025-26, and what did it collect in welfare fees and spend on migrants and families?

## Women workers

- Has the ILO-backed cash scheme for returnee women started, and how does a woman apply?
