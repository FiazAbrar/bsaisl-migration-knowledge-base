## REINTEGRATION OF MIGRANT WORKERS IN BANGLADESH

 

## Sustainably reintegrate returnee migrants into their communities and support the institutionalization of reintegration services.

![](data:image/svg+xml;base64,PHN2ZyBwcmVzZXJ2ZWFzcGVjdHJhdGlvPSJub25lIiB2aWV3Ym94PSIwIDAgMTAwIDEwMCIgaGVpZ2h0PSIxMDAiIHdpZHRoPSIxMDAlIiB2ZXJzaW9uPSIxLjEiIHhtbG5zPSJodHRwczovL3d3dy53My5vcmcvMjAwMC9zdmciIHN0eWxlPSJiYWNrZ3JvdW5kOiA7Ij48cGF0aCBkPSJNMCAwIEMgNTAgMTAwIDgwIDEwMCAxMDAgMCBaIiBzdHlsZT0iZmlsbDogI2U1ZTVlNTsgc3Ryb2tlOiAjZTVlNWU1OyI+PC9zdmc+)

## The four-year project, supported by Switzerland, aims to sustainably reintegrate returnees into society. It provides tailored support at every stage of the returnee migrants’ journey—from facilitating immediate assistance at the airport to delivering comprehensive, gender-transformative reintegration services. Simultaneously, the project strengthens government frameworks to ensure effective economic and social reintegration.

## NUMBERS

 

 

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/awareness.png)

### Awareness

**6,86,991 (Women: 4,03,528, Men: 2,83,463)** people reached through awareness activities.

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/migration-icon-4.png)

### Returnee reintegration

**2,480** returnees were reported to be either socially/economically reintegrated.

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/capacity-development.png)

### Capacity development of returnees

**4,483 (Women: 325, Men: 4,158)** returnees received entrepreneurship development/trade-specific skills training.

 

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/counselling.png)

### Psychosocial support

**1,074 (Women: 156, Men: 918)** vulnerable returnees received psychosocial counselling support.

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/referral-support.png)

### Referral support

**1,724 (Women: 145, Men: 1,581)** returnees received **2,329** referral support.

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/migration-icon-2.png)

### Immediate assistance

**139** vulnerable women returnees received immediate assistance.

## WHAT WE DO

 

 

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/wwd-immediate-assistance1.jpg)

#### Immediate assistance

ensures vulnerable women returnee migrants' dignified return

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/wwd-tailored-reintegration-plan1.jpg)

#### Tailored reintegration plan

works as a roadmap to achieve sustainable reintegration

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/wwd-psychosocial-counselling.jpg)

#### Psychosocial counselling

boosts returnees' mental strength and ensures well-being

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/wwd-career-counselling2.jpg)

#### Career counselling

offers innovative solutions to unlock returnees' potential

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/wwd-skills-development.jpg)

#### Skills development

make returnee migrants more confident

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/wwd-referral-support.jpg)

#### Referral support

ensures returnee migrants' multidimensional needs

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/wwd-awareness.jpg)

#### Awareness

to build an informed and active community about reintegration services and increase the social acceptance of the returnees

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/wwd-sensitization.jpg)

#### Sensitization

to ensure a supportive network and environment is in place for returnee migrants

 

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/wwd-collaboration.jpg)

#### Collaboration

with local employers/private organizations to assist the returnees sustainable reintegration through capacity building/job placement/mentorship support

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/wwd-partnership.jpg)

#### Partnership

with the government to create returnee-friendly migration management
