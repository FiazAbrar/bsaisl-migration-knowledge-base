## PROTTASHA-II

## Improved Sustainable Reintegration of Bangladeshi Returnee Migrants

 

## Contributing to the sustainable reintegration of Bangladeshi returnee migrants and promoting safe migration across Bangladesh, the European Union (EU) funded Prottasha II project is being implemented and co-financed by BRAC. This four-year project focuses on interventions at every step of the returnee migrants’ journey—from enhancing access to tailored immediate assistance at airport to sustainable and gender-sensitive reintegration services (psychosocial, social, and economic).

## NUMBERS

 

 

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Immediate-Assistance.png)

### Immediate Assistance

**4,242** (Women: **165**, Men: **4,077**) Vulnerable Returnees received Immediate Assistance.

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Psychosocial-Support.png)

### Psychosocial Support

**1,159** (Women: **417**, Men: **742**) Vulnerable Returnees received Psychosocial Counselling Support.

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Capacity-Development-of-Returnees.png)

### Capacity Development of Returnees

**3,707** (Women: **1,289**, Men: **2,418**) Vulnerable Returnees received various skills training.

 

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Financial-Material-Assistance.png)

### Financial/Material Assistance

**3,091** (Women: **1,134**, Men: **1,957**) Vulnerable Returnees received Material Assistance.

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Awareness.png)

### Awareness

**1,64,24,361** (Women: **34,79,876**, Men: **1,29,44,485**) People reached through Awareness Activities.

 

\*Up to 30 September 2025.

## WHAT WE DO

#### Immediate Assistance

Immediate assistance ensures vulnerable returnee migrants' dignified return

#### Psychosocial Counselling

Psychosocial counselling boosts returnees' mental strength and ensures well-being

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/thumb-career-counselling.jpg)

#### Career Counselling

Career counselling offers innovative solutions to unlock returnees' potential

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/thumb-tailored-reintegration-plan-2.jpg)

#### Tailored Reintegration Plan

Tailored reintegration plan works as a roadmap to achieve sustainable reintegration

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/thumb-skills-development.jpg)

#### Skills Development

Skills development make returnee migrants more confident

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/thumb-material-assistance.jpg)

#### Material Assistance

Material assistance creating viable livelihood opportunities for returnees sustainable future

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/thumb-referral-support.jpg)

#### Referral Support

Referral support ensures returnee migrants' multidimensional needs

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/thumb-awareness.jpg)

#### Awareness

Awareness to build an informed and active community about safe migration practices and the risk of irregular migration

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/thumb-probashbandhu-forum1.jpg)

#### Probashbandhu Forums

Probashbandhu forums contribute to exploitation-free migration for potential and returnee migrants at community level

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/thumb-women-migrant-forums.jpg)

#### Women Migrant Forums

Women migrant forums empowering women returnee migrants to claim their rights

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/thumb-stakeholders-engagement.jpg)

#### Stakeholders' Engagement

Stakeholders' engagement ensures a supportive network and environment is in place for returnee migrants

 

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/thumb-diaspora.jpg)

#### Diaspora Engagement

Diaspora engagement members will be engaged as ambassadors to raise awareness on safe migration and sustainable reintegration

![](//explore.brac.net/program/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/thumb-partnership-2.jpg)

#### Partnership

Partnership with the government for creating returnee-friendly migration management
