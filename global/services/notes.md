## Resolved conflicts (official research pass)

- PKB migration loan term: July 2024 news [GV0005] said three years for a new visa and two for a re-entry visa, with a two-month grace period; PKB's page (Sep 2026) says at most 3 years including grace, with no separate re-entry term [OV0010]. Official stands.
- PKB migration loan papers: news [GV0007-GV0009] listed four attested photos and address certificates and said applying "costs nothing"; PKB's page lists three photos, the borrower's own three MICR cheques, a Tk 500 savings account, utility-bill proof of address for guarantors, and fees of 0.5% + 0.5% plus VAT and Tk 900 per lakh [OV0003-OV0016]. The form is free; the loan is not. Official stands; GV0007-GV0009 and GV0011 are no longer cited.
- PKB language/skills loan: PKB's circular 22/2025 (16 Nov 2025) gives N4 for Japan, 2-5 years with a 6-month grace period, tiered collateral above Tk 3 lakh, one guarantor and payment to the borrower by account-payee cheque [OV0026-OV0036]. The ministry's release carried by BSS on 31 Mar 2026 [OV0037-OV0040] and Prothom Alo in April 2026 [GV0178-GV0181] say N5, up to 3 years, collateral-free to Tk 10 lakh and tuition sent straight to the institution. No revised circular was found on pkb.gov.bd (the skills-loan page, updated 27 Jan 2026, links only circular 22/2025). The doc states the circular and keeps one Need to know naming the differences.
- Wage Earners' Centre price: Prothom Alo (May 2024) said Tk 200 a day; the Welfare Board says Tk 100 admission plus Tk 200 a night [OV0070]. Official stands.
- Lounge benefits: the adviser's April 2025 remarks [GV0119] (food 30% cheaper, Wi-Fi, phone, charging) match the Board's page [OV0067]; GV0119 dropped in favour of the official list.
- Scholarship portal: older rounds named www.wewb.gov.bd or stipen.wewb.gov.bd/stipend [GV0030, GV0032]; the 2026 notice names https://portal.wewb.gov.bd [OV0098]. Official stands.
- Sylhet desk: the minister said on 19 Feb 2026 that Sylhet's airport lacked the service centres seen at Dhaka [GV0170]; BMET's (undated) desk list names a Sylhet desk with an assistant director and phone [OV0066]. Not settleable from the sources; kept as a Need to know.
- Tk 13,500: RMMRU's figures (Tk 39,84,93,000 to 29,518 returnees) divide to exactly Tk 13,500 each, matching the state minister's figure [OV0121, GV0303]. The doc says "averaged Tk 13,500"; no official page states a flat RAISE grant amount.
- Probashi card: the 13 Oct 2026 "piloted in four to five countries" remark [GV0268] and the ministry's 12 Nov 2026 "not launched, no applications" release [OV0105] both stay, dated; the release is the latest official word.
