## Resolved conflicts

- PKB women returnees: the FAQ cites PKB's MD (March 2024) saying returnee women's loans carry "no service charge" (সেবা মাশুল) [CQ0105]; in July 2024 the same MD said PKB lends to returnee women for self-employment at 4 to 7% interest [GV0016]. A service charge is a fee, not interest, so the two are compatible; both kept.
- PKB guarantors: the FAQ asks whether PKB wants one or two guarantors. The July 2024 report [GV0009] lists papers for two guarantors and three cheques signed by one of them, matching PKB's own page [F0116 in faq]. Not edited in the FAQ (other agent's folder); noted here.
- Lounge helpers: Asif Nazrul called them 100 staff appointed; IOM called them about 100 volunteers it sponsored [GV0027, GV0047]. Both kept, attributed.

## Resolved conflicts (chunk services-3)

- PKB language-study loan terms: the December 2025 report (pa-2025-12-03-6dlq7doam9) gives a tiered security scheme (no collateral to Tk 3 lakh, property deed Tk 3-5 lakh, registered mortgage Tk 5-10 lakh), a 2-5 year term and N-4 for Japan; the April 2026 report (pa-2026-04-01-stplucaf70) calls the whole loan collateral-free up to Tk 10 lakh, with a term of up to 3 years and N5 for Japan. Both are secondhand newspaper summaries of PKB's terms, neither quotes a PKB circular, and the newer one may reflect a change (the PM's adviser announced a collateral-free guarantee of up to Tk 10 lakh on 18 April 2026, FAQ CQ0160) or may be a simplification. Not settleable from the sources: the doc gives the December tiers as PKB's stated terms, dates both, and asks in a Need to know. PKB's own loan page or circular would settle it; pkb.gov.bd blocks automated access.
- Interest: both reports say 11%; no conflict.

- Probash-Bondhu number from abroad: the 20 Oct 2026 scholarship article prints +880 9610 203030 [GV0257]; the April 2024 article [GV0036], BMET's safe migration leaflet and the Welfare Board's 2023-24 annual report (both in global/dalal-scams/sources) give 09610102030. - PKB language/skills loans: the 9% Bangladesh Bank-funded scheme (collateral-free to Tk 10 lakh, two guarantors, 3 years plus extension to 5, students excluded) differs from the 11% loan reported in Dec 2025 (tiered collateral). The April 2026 report's "collateral-free up to Tk 10 lakh" matches the new scheme, so it may have described it early, but no source says whether the 11% loan was replaced. Kept as a Need to know.
