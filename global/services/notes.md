Structure designed for growth by when a person needs a service: In one minute; Before you go (advice near home, training centres, the PKB migration loan); At the airport (the Probashi Kallyan desk, the lounge); While abroad (BOU programmes); If a worker dies abroad; Help for the family at home (scholarships, disabled-child allowance); Coming home (PKB loans to restart); Women workers. Later chunks should add sections inside these (labour wings, legal aid, Welfare Board claims, reintegration programmes) rather than new top-level parts where one fits.

Checked against global/faq/doc.md (Loans; Problems and coming home) and global/departure/doc.md (Coming back) so nothing contradicts them; basic PKB terms, insurance, death payments and scholarship amounts stay in the FAQ and are linked, not repeated.

## Resolved conflicts

- PKB women returnees: the FAQ cites PKB's MD (March 2024) saying returnee women's loans carry "no service charge" (সেবা মাশুল) [CQ0105]; in July 2024 the same MD said PKB lends to returnee women for self-employment at 4 to 7% interest [GV0016]. A service charge is a fee, not interest, so the two are compatible; both kept.
- PKB guarantors: the FAQ asks whether PKB wants one or two guarantors. The July 2024 report [GV0009] lists papers for two guarantors and three cheques signed by one of them, matching PKB's own page [F0116 in faq]. Not edited in the FAQ (other agent's folder); noted here.
- Lounge helpers: Asif Nazrul called them 100 staff appointed; IOM called them about 100 volunteers it sponsored [GV0027, GV0047]. Both kept, attributed.
