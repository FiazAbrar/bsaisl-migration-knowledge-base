 Bangladesh Immigration Police

Bangladesh Immigration Police

The Bangladesh Immigration Police plays a crucial role in managing the flow of people entering
and exiting the country. Established to ensure national security and facilitate legal migration,
this agency operates under the Ministry of Home Affairs.
Functions and Responsibilities
The primary responsibilities of the Bangladesh Immigration Police include:
1. Border Control: 
Monitoring and regulating the entry and exit of individuals at airports,
land borders, and seaports.
2. Visa Management:
 Processing visa applications and ensuring compliance with
immigration laws.
3. Detaining Illegal Immigrants: 
Identifying and detaining individuals who attempt to enter
or reside in the country illegally.
4. Documentation: 
Issuing travel documents and identity cards to citizens and foreigners
as required.
5. Collaboration with Other Agencies: 
Working alongside law enforcement and
international organizations to combat human trafficking and smuggling.

Visa on Arrival (VoA)
Visa on Arrival (VoA) is a type of visa that allows travelers to obtain a visa upon entering a
country rather than applying for one in advance. Currently, Bangladesh Immigration Police has
launched a Visa on Arrival (VoA) online portal. Through this, it is possible to apply for a visa very
easily for respected passenger.
