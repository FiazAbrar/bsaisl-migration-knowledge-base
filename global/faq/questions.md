# Frequently asked questions about going abroad: open questions

Questions this doc can't yet answer. Each is a "Need to know" in `doc.md`.

- Is the list's Congo actually Kosovo, and is Cambodia on it or Croatia, as other reports of the same 4 November 2026 order give?
- Are UAE recruitment offices getting domestic-worker entry permits for Bangladeshi women in November 2026, or are they refused at the visa stage?
- Has the Ministry issued the new country-by-country maximums the State Minister promised in October 2026?
- Is there a public list of registered sub-agents that a worker can check a dalal's name against?
- Does BMET still issue permission letters after 10 December 2025, and can a worker get a copy from BMET?
- Is the fee Tk 1,500 (the portal) or Tk 500 (Bangladesh Police's guidance)?
- Does the Ministry publish one current list of all agencies approved to send women domestic workers?
- Do customs officers still apply USD 5,000, the figure on Bangladesh Customs' own page?
- Did labour migration from Bangladesh to the UAE stop in 2024, as RMMRU (the Refugee and Migratory Movements Research Unit) reported, or did about 47,000 workers go that year, as Prothom Alo reported in 2026?
- Was the amendment to the Overseas Employment and Migrants Act that brought sub-agents (registered dalals) under the law passed in 2023 or in 2024?
  - Can a worker search RAIMS (the Recruitment Agents Information System, BMET's agency database) to check an agency or sub-agent?
- Has the grading system for recruiting agencies that the State Minister for expatriates' welfare announced in May 2026 been introduced, and where are the grades published?
- When a worker dies abroad, is the payment the family gets at the airport for transport and burial Tk 35,000 or Tk 25,000 in 2026?
- When a Bangladeshi is arrested in Saudi Arabia, does the labour wing of the Bangladesh Embassy learn of it?
  - Does the labour wing arrange a lawyer or an interpreter?
  - Whom should the family in Bangladesh contact first?
- How many Bangladeshis are on death row in Saudi Arabia in 2026, and for which charges?

## Deciding

- Which nursing degrees and licences do the UK, Japan and the Gulf countries accept from Bangladesh, and which exam or registration must a Bangladeshi nurse pass for each?
