## Our Purpose

The purpose of the The Immigration service, Bangladesh Police is to provide immigration related service and security to the Bangladesh through the well managed entry and out of people. The immigration service is provided by Special Branch of Bangladesh Police.

## Executives

The authority is headed by the Additional Inspector General of Police
