### Resolved conflicts

- bKash agent cash-out: Prothom Alo (April 2024, GM0036) gave Tk 18.60 per Tk 1,000; bKash's own cash-out page (archived 19 Sept 2025) gives Tk 18.50. The bKash page is newer and the provider's own; Tk 18.50 is used and GM0034-GM0037 are no longer cited.
- US dollar bonds: Prothom Alo (Aug 2025, GM0424) gave 7.50% and 6.50% at maturity; the Department of National Savings sheets (March 2026) show those are the rates for up to USD 100,000, with lower rates on larger amounts. The official tiered rates replace GM0424.
- Wage Earners' Development Bond above Tk 1 crore: Prothom Alo gave the 9% band as "Tk 50 lakh to Tk 1 crore" (GM0142, GM0242); the 2026 official sheet's band is "Tk 50,00,001 and above" with no upper limit. The official band is used.
- Tax on remittances to other relatives: the 2024 news (GM0075) spoke of a budget decision to tax them; the law as published in Oct 2025 makes any gift income (s.66(d)) and exempts only gifts from spouse, parents and children (para 35), while para 17 exempts the migrant's own foreign income brought in legally. The doc now states the law; whether NBR applies s.66(d) to remittances to siblings and in-laws, and whether later finance laws changed it, stays a Need to know.
