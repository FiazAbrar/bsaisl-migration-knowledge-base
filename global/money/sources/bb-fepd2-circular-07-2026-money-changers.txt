Consolidated Circular - Licenses for Authorized Dealers, Money Changers and Gold Dealers

Dear Sirs,

Licenses for dealing in foreign exchange and gold are regulated under the Foreign Exchange
Regulation (FER) Act, 1947 and the circulars/instructions issued thereunder from time to time.

02. With a view to streamlining the operational procedures relating to the issuance and renewal of
licenses for Authorized Dealers, Money Changers and Gold Dealers, the existing
regulations/instructions have been consolidated into a single document, with necessary
modifications and updates, as enclosed herewith.

03. Upon issuance of this circular, the existing instructions in this regard shall stand repealed to the
extent covered by this circular.

04. This circular is issued in exercise of the powers conferred upon Bangladesh Bank under Section
20(3) of the FER Act, 1947.

05. The instructions contained in this circular shall remain valid for a period of one year from the
date of its issuance. Any instructions, amendments or clarifications issued by Bangladesh Bank
during this period shall be read in conjunction with this circular.

Please bring the contents of this consolidated circular to the notice of your relevant constituents.

                                             PART-A
                                AUTHORIZED DEALERS
1. General

Bangladesh Bank issues licenses normally to scheduled banks to deal in foreign exchange. To
obtain authorized dealer (AD) license for a branch, a scheduled bank operating in Bangladesh
must submit a formal application through its Head Office to Director (FEPD-2), Foreign Exchange
Policy Department-2, Bangladesh Bank, Head Office, Dhaka along with the following documents/
Information (Annexure-1):

(a) A declaration regarding compliance with the Foreign Exchange Risk Management Guidelines
issued vide FE Circular Letter No. 12, dated February 20, 2025, and subsequent amendments
thereto, as well as with the instructions contained in foreign exchange circulars/circular letters
issued from time to time, including the prescribed reporting routines to Bangladesh Bank;

(b) Year-wise data for the preceding 05 (Five) years in respect of all AD branches of the bank
relating to import, export, non-funded exposure, forced loan/funded liabilities created against such
exposure and remittances, showing the respective number and amount;

(c) The number of overdue outstanding bills of entry against retired/settled import bills and the
corresponding foreign currency amount, expressed in million USD;

(d) A feasibility assessment report justifying the necessity and business potential of the proposed
license for the concerned branch;

(e) Certification/details regarding the availability of adequate skilled and experienced officers at
the proposed branch for conducting foreign exchange transactions;

(f) True copy of license issued by Banking Regulation and Policy Department-1 (BRPD-1) of
Bangladesh Bank in favor of the proposed branch; and

(g) The audited financial statements of the preceding financial year.

2. Cancellation/ Revocation of license
If a licensed AD bank fails to fulfill the duties assigned to it, and/or fails to comply with
instructions issued under the Foreign Exchange Regulation (FER) Act, 1947 (as amended in
2015), Bangladesh Bank reserves the absolute right to cancel or revoke the license granted to such
bank at any time after giving a reasonable opportunity of explaining its position on the matter.

                                             PART-B
                                   MONEY CHANGERS

1. Money changer license

Bangladesh Bank may issue "Money Changer" licenses to persons/firms desirous of undertaking,
as their sole line of business, the purchase and sale of foreign currency notes and coins from and to
incoming and outgoing tourists. ADs are advised to process applications for licenses on behalf of
their customers provided Bangladesh Bank decides to issue new licenses and forward the same
with recommendation to Director (FEPD-2), Foreign Exchange Policy Department-2, Bangladesh
Bank, Head Office.

2. Use of office space

Money changer shall have no branch office. The premise to be used for money changing business
shall not be used for any other business activity.

3. Passport endorsement fee by money changers

Money changers may charge maximum Tk. 300 (Three hundred) for the endorsement of an
outgoing passenger's passport, irrespective of the amount. To ensure regulatory compliance and
consumer transparency, the amount of the endorsement fee must be clearly displayed in a
prominent place at the money changer’s business premises and a written receipt must be provided
to the customer for this specific fee. Money changers must keep a proper record of all fees
collected from customers for future auditing and inspection purposes.

4. Buying of foreign currency notes and coins

Money changers are allowed to buy foreign currency notes and coins from incoming foreign and
Bangladesh nationals coming/returning from abroad. For each such purchase an encashment
certificate shall be furnished to the seller in prescribed format as per Appendix-1.

5. Releasing foreign currency notes and coins

Money changers may sell foreign currency notes and coins only to outgoing Bangladesh nationals
against their annual private travel entitlements (per calendar year) subject to a maximum limit of
USD 3,000 (Three thousand) in the form of USD notes and coins at any one instance. The balance
of the applicable entitlement may be sold in the form of notes and coins denominated in other
freely convertible/usable foreign currencies subject to availability of the respective customer’s
remaining travel entitlement. Release of foreign exchange up to USD 200 (Two hundred) or
equivalent requires a valid ticket, while release in excess of USD 200 (Two hundred) or equivalent
requires a valid visa, where applicable.

Money changers may also sell foreign currency notes and coins to outgoing foreign nationals
having duly issued encashment certificates, subject to a maximum limit of USD 500 (Five

                                                 2
hundred) or equivalent by reconversion of Bangladesh Taka proceeds of foreign exchange sold by
a tourist during his/her stay in Bangladesh. Any licensed money changer whether it encashed
earlier or not may reconvert the unspent Bangladesh Taka belonging to the foreign tourist, up to
USD 500 (Five hundred) or equivalent provided that the tourist submits system generated
encashment certificate provided by any licensed money changer. While providing reconversion,
money changer shall stamp on the original encashment certificate to the effect that reconversion
has been executed and preserve a copy thereof.

With regard to Hajj Pilgrimage, licensed money changers shall follow the instructions issued for
releasing foreign currency in terms of Hajj Policy of the Government. Money changers may
release foreign exchange on account of overseas allowances of the cockpit and cabin crews
allowed by Biman Bangladesh Airlines Ltd. as mentioned in paragraph 3(2), Part-B of FEPD-1
Circular No. 40, dated September 30, 2026.

While releasing foreign exchange for travel abroad, money changers shall verify and satisfy
themselves that the travel for which the foreign exchange released earlier was actually undertaken
or the issued foreign exchange was duly encashed. Each sale of foreign exchange shall be recorded
in the passport and air ticket (in case of air travel) of the outgoing passenger with seal and
signature of authorized person of the money changer. In each case of release of foreign exchange
for travel abroad, photocopies of first six pages (in case of Machine Readable Passport,
photocopies of two pages), pages containing visa (if available) and pages containing endorsement
of foreign exchange of the passport duly signed by the authorized person of the money changer
with seal shall be retained at least for 05 (Five) years for inspection by Bangladesh Bank officials.

In case of release of foreign exchange to a foreign tourist against encashment certificate
evidencing conversion of foreign currency into Taka, copy of the encashment certificate issued to
the outgoing tourist shall be retained at least for 05 (Five) years along with the receipts/vouchers
and other records of sale. The money changers shall verify to satisfy themselves that the journey is
to be undertaken not later than 02 (two) weeks after the date on which the exchange is issued. In
case of air travel the date of departure needs to be determined on the basis of air ticket and an
undertaking from the passenger may be treated as the determining criterion for other cases. No
foreign exchange should be sold unless the date of departure is specified within the stipulated
time.

6. Stock of foreign currency and foreign currency account

The initial stock of foreign currency notes and coins shall be built up by the money changers as
prescribed at paragraph 4 and 5 above. The maximum stock of cash foreign currency must not
exceed USD 25,000 (Twenty five thousand) or equivalent at the close of each business day. Cash
foreign currency beyond this limit shall either be encashed with any AD or deposited to the
foreign currency (FC) account maintained with the designated AD of the concerned money
changer. Cash foreign currency can be withdrawn from this FC account. The balance of that
account must not exceed USD 50,000 (Fifty thousand) or equivalent at any point of time.
Moreover, the maximum stock of Bangladesh Taka must not exceed Tk. 50.00 (Fifty) lac at any


                                                 3
point of time. Bangladesh Taka beyond this limit shall be deposited to money changers’ current
deposit account.

7. Fixation/display of buying/selling rate

Money changers shall fix their own exchange rates for purchase and sale of foreign currency notes
& coins and the rates shall be displayed prominently at their counters.

8. Reporting

(a) Submission of monthly statement

Details of all sales and purchases shall be recorded in the books properly and completely. All such
records and books of accounts of the licensee must be made available for inspection by
Bangladesh Bank officials as and when directed. Periodical returns/statements in prescribed form
(as per Appendix-2) shall be furnished to FEOD-2, Head Office or concerned office of Bangladesh
Bank on monthly basis within 10 (Ten) days from the end of each month.

(b) Online money changer monitoring system

Bangladesh Bank has developed 'Online Money Changer Monitoring System', a web based real
time transaction reporting platform. Each transaction of money changer with customers shall have
to be reported to the above mentioned system. Such reporting will generate an acknowledgement
in PDF format with unique transaction ID and Bangladesh Bank monogram for each transaction.
The printed copy of the acknowledgement report shall have to be delivered to the client. For
details, money changers shall follow the instructions mentioned in the user guide attached with the
system.

9. Renewal of license

The license shall be valid for a period of 01 (one) year. For renewal, the licensee shall apply to
Director (FEOD-2), Foreign Exchange Operation Department-2, Bangladesh Bank, Head Office,
Dhaka (for Dhaka region) or to the Executive Director of concerned office of Bangladesh Bank
(for other areas) through the concerned AD bank not later than 02 (two) months before the expiry
of license.

Bangladesh Bank may not consider prayer for renewal unless volume of transactions in the
previous year as per statements submitted to Bangladesh Bank reaches the threshold amount fixed
by Bangladesh Bank from time to time. Presently for renewal of license, the minimum required
annual volume of foreign exchange transactions by money changers is USD 5.00 (Five) lac or
equivalent foreign currency in the city corporation areas of Dhaka and Chattogram and USD 3.50
(Three and a half) lac or equivalent foreign currency for other areas. Money changers failing to
achieve the above target shall be considered ineligible for renewal of their licenses.



                                                4
Application for renewal shall be accompanied by copies of deed of house rent (in case of rented
house) for the proposed renewal period, up-to-date tax payment certificate, VAT Payment
Certificate, up-to-date trade license, updated membership certificate of relevant association
representing the sector, return of asset-liability (Form IT-10B) submitted to Income Tax Office,
audited balance sheet of the last year (where the applicant is a company). For non-submission of
certificate evidencing payment of tax for the preceding year, an undertaking for submitting the
same later on along with latest tax payment certificate should be furnished. The returns so
submitted to tax authority must be accompanied by documents evidencing minimum net asset of
the licensee of Tk. 10.00 (Ten) lac for sole proprietorship and partnership firms. For joint stock
company, at the time of renewal, minimum paid up capital shall be Tk. 1.00 (One) crore or more
as per audited balance sheet.

Subject to satisfactory performance in the previous year, licensee shall be advised by Bangladesh
Bank in writing to deposit non-refundable renewal fee Tk. 10,000 (Ten thousand) in favor of
Bangladesh Bank through Bangladesh Electronic Fund Transfer Network (BEFTN) to A/c No-
2316100055; Account name- BBGenA/cHO_FEOD. Certificate of renewal of license shall be
provided through AD bank if renewal fee is deposited accordingly. Money changers must preserve
the license and renewals issued by Bangladesh Bank with reasonable care.

10. Change of office
Any change in money changer's office address shall require prior approval of Bangladesh Bank.
Applications for changing office address should be forwarded to Director (FEPD-2), Foreign
Exchange Policy Department -2, Bangladesh Bank along with up-to-date money changer license,
deed of rent (in case of rented house), reasons for change etc. along with the documents mentioned
in Annexure-2.

11. Conversion to limited company and transfer of ownership
Conversion of the status of a money changer from sole proprietorship or partnership to joint stock
company and transfer of ownership of a money changer may be done only with prior approval of
the Bangladesh Bank. Such request may be accommodated by Bangladesh Bank upon establishing
the bonafides of the cases. Money changers shall submit such requests to Director (FEPD-2),
Foreign Exchange Policy Department-2, Bangladesh Bank, Head Office through their nominated
ADs showing proper reasons along with the documents mentioned in Annexure-3.

12. Cancellation of money changer license

Any contravention of, violation of, or failure to comply with any term or condition of the license
or any instruction issued by Bangladesh Bank from time to time shall render the concerned
licensed money changer liable to cancellation or revocation of its license. Bangladesh Bank may,
after providing a reasonable opportunity to explain its position, cancel or revoke the license and/or
take any other regulatory or supervisory action as deemed appropriate.

                                            PART-C
                              LIMITED MONEY CHANGERS

1. General

Under Section 3 of the FER Act, 1947, Bangladesh Bank issues AD licenses. As per the existing
system, AD licenses are issued in favor of scheduled bank branches or their central processing
centers. Limited money changer licenses are also issued to bank branches/bank booths, hotels, gift
shops etc. The procedures for applying for a license, eligibility, scope of operations, license
renewal process, responsibilities and other related matters are described below.

2. Application procedure for obtaining a limited money changer license

For the purpose of obtaining a limited money changer license, scheduled banks operating in
Bangladesh shall submit the application through their head office, and in the case of other
institutions, through an AD bank, to Director (FEPD-2), Foreign Exchange Policy Department-2,
Bangladesh Bank, Head Office, Dhaka, along with the documents mentioned below (Annexure-4):

(a) For bank branches/booths:

   (i) Copy of license/approval issued by the Banking Regulation and Policy Department-1
   (BRPD-1) of Bangladesh Bank in favor of the relevant branch/booth;
   (ii) Details (with appropriate proof) on manpower experienced in foreign exchange,
   infrastructure, security arrangements and use of ICT; and
   (iii) Justification and evidence of foreign exchange related transactions.

(b) For other institutions:

   (i) Business type/nature and documents justifying the need for the license;
   (ii) Copy of updated trade license;
   (iii) TIN certificate, BIN certificate and all relevant and updated licenses/registrations;
   (iv) If the applicant is a registered company: copy of registration certificate, Memorandum of
   Association and Articles of Association and previous year’s financial statement;
   (v) Ownership deed or lease agreement, depending on whether the business operates from
   owned or rented premises;
   (vi) Brief description on business experience, infrastructure, security and ICT capacity (with
   evidence); and
   (vii) Upon receiving initial clearance, submit a non-refundable pay order/bank draft of Tk.
   50,000 (Fifty thousand) in favor of Director (FEPD-2), Foreign Exchange Policy Department-
   2, Bangladesh Bank, Head Office, Dhaka.



                                                6
3. Scope for licensed institutions

(a) For bank branches/booths:
   (i) The license issued shall not be transferable under any circumstances;
   (ii) A licensed institution may change its place of business with the approval of Bangladesh
   Bank;
   (iii) Branches/booths of licensed banks will be able to carry out other transactions specified in
   the issued license, including buying and selling foreign currency, opening and maintenance of
   admissible foreign currency accounts, dollar endorsement on the passports of citizens
   travelling abroad and the encashment of foreign currency;
   (iv) Authorized bank branches/booths in Dhaka region shall submit monthly statement of
   foreign exchange transactions as per Appendix-3 through their AD Bank to Director (FEOD-
   2), Foreign Exchange Operation Department-2, Bangladesh Bank, Head Office, Dhaka and in
   the case of licensed institutions located in other regions to the relevant office of Bangladesh
   Bank; and
   (v) The licensed institution shall comply with all relevant instructions issued or to be issued by
   Bangladesh Bank from time to time.

(b) For other institutions:

   (i) The license shall not be transferable under any circumstances;
   (ii) A licensed institution shall not change its place of business without the prior approval of
   Bangladesh Bank;
   (iii) The licensed establishment may accept foreign currency in cash only from foreign
   passport holders against the sale of its goods/services. The foreign currency received shall be
   converted into local currency through an approved AD bank within the next working day.
   However, for the convenience of transactions, a maximum of USD 1,000 (One thousand) or
   other approved foreign currency of equivalent value may be retained;
   (iv) Institutions located within Dhaka region shall submit monthly foreign exchange
   transaction reports as per Appendix-4 on a monthly basis through their AD Bank to Director
   (FEOD-2), Foreign Exchange Operation Department-2, Bangladesh Bank, Head Office, Dhaka
   and in the case of licensed institutions located in other regions, to the relevant offices of
   Bangladesh Bank; and
   (v) The licensed institution shall comply with all relevant instructions issued or to be issued by
   Bangladesh Bank from time to time.

4. License validity period and renewal procedure

(a) In the case of bank branches/booths, there is no need to renew the license.




                                                  7
(b) In case of other institutions, the license period will be 02 (two) years. All these institutions
should apply through the concerned AD Bank to the Foreign Exchange Operation Department-2,
Bangladesh Bank, Head Office, Dhaka and in case of licensed institutions located in other regions,
to the relevant offices of Bangladesh Bank along with the following documents 02 (two) months
before the expiry of the license for renewal for the next 02 (two) years :

   (i) Copy of updated trade license;
   (ii) Latest income certificate/income tax assessment as determined by the Income Tax
   authority;
   (iii) Authentic documents in support of payment of VAT against goods or services sold, such
   as VAT payment certificate for the previous financial year;
   (iv) Information on foreign exchange transactions for the last one year;
   (v) License renewal fee of Tk. 10,000 (Ten thousand) (non-refundable) should be deposited
   through EFT from the bank account maintained with AD Bank of the institution to Director
   (FEOD-2), Foreign Exchange Operation Department-2, Bangladesh Bank, Head Office, Dhaka
   and for institutions located outside Dhaka, the fee shall be deposited through the AD Bank to
   the concerned regional office of Bangladesh Bank through EFT as per the prevailing
   procedure;
   (vi) Updated copies of all types of licenses/registrations/certificates etc. obtained under other
   relevant authorities in the name of the applicant organization; and
   (vii) Ownership documents if the organization is located in a self-owned location/copy of the
   updated rental agreement executed with the owner of the relevant location if the organization
   is located in a rented location.

5. Authority of Bangladesh Bank to grant/reject permission and inspect

Bangladesh Bank reserves the right to grant or refuse a license to conduct limited foreign
exchange transactions. Bangladesh Bank may also conduct on-site inspections of the applicant
bank branches/booths and other institutions. Information and supporting documents of foreign
currency transactions must be properly preserved.

6. AML/CFT compliance
In order to prevent money laundering and terrorist financing, the licensed institution must ensure
compliance with the prevailing laws, regulations of the country and the circulars, circular letters
and other instructions issued by the Bangladesh Financial Intelligence Unit and Money
Laundering & Terrorist Financing Prevention Department from time to time.

7. Visible notice board

Notice boards should be installed in a place easily visible to customers, clearly written in Bengali
and English, which will contain notices regarding limited money changers with licenses and the
daily exchange rate in foreign currencies.

8. Cancellation of license

If a licensed bank branch/booth or institution fails to fulfill the duties assigned to it, and/or fails to
comply with instructions issued under the FER Act, 1947, Bangladesh Bank reserves the absolute
right to cancel or revoke the license granted to such bank/institution, without assigning any reason
whatsoever.

                                            PART-D
                       AUTHORIZED GOLD DEALER (AGD)

1. General
In accordance with paragraph 3.1 of the Gold Policy-2018 (amended, 2021) and section 8 of the
FER Act, 1947, Bangladesh Bank may issue Authorized Gold Dealer licenses to AD banks or
other eligible institutions for the import of gold bars and gold ornaments, subject to compliance
with the applicable rules, regulations, and instructions of Bangladesh Bank. Subject to a decision
by Bangladesh Bank to commence issuance of such licenses, ADs shall process license
applications either on their own behalf or on behalf of their customers and forward the
applications, along with their recommendations, to Director (FEPD-2), Foreign Exchange Policy
Department-2, Bangladesh Bank, Head Office, Dhaka.
2. Scope for licensed institutions
(a) For AD bank
    (i) Issued gold dealer license is non-transferable;
    (ii) With Bangladesh Bank's prior approval, a licensee can change its business location;
    (iii) Licensees must comply with import policy orders and other relevant import laws and
    regulations to import gold bars and gold ornaments;
    (iv) No other commercial activities are allowed at the licensed premises;
    (v) Licensed institutions must follow all directives issued or to be issued by Bangladesh Bank;
    (vi) Subject to following the directives of the Gold Policy-2018, the licensed AD bank can
    import gold bars/ gold ornaments in accordance with the demand of licensed institutions under
    Gold (Procurement, Storage and Distribution) Order, 1987 upon prior approval from
    Bangladesh Bank;
    (vii) Each import of gold bars/gold ornaments requires separate prior approval from
    Bangladesh Bank, including details of the import demand and the foreign currency to be paid.
    In this regard, holding a gold license does not automatically allow import in every case; and
    (viii) Licensed institutions must comply with the conditions mentioned in the approval letter
    issued by Bangladesh Bank for the import of gold bars/gold ornaments.

(b) For other institutions
   (i) Issued gold dealer license is non-transferable;
   (ii) With Bangladesh Bank's prior approval, a licensee can change their business location
   (Only within city corporation/Municipality area mentioned on the license);
   (iii) Licensees must comply with import policy orders, other relevant import laws and
   regulations to import gold bars/gold ornaments and obtain bond license following Customs
   Act (if applicable);
   (iv) No other commercial activities are allowed at the licensed premises;
   (v) Licensed institutions must follow all directives issued or to be issued by Bangladesh Bank;
   (vi) Subject to following the directives of the Gold Policy-2018, the licensed AD bank can
   import gold bars/ gold ornaments in accordance with the demand of licensed institutions under


                                                10
   Gold (Procurement, storage and distribution) Order, 1987 upon prior approval of Bangladesh
   Bank;
   (vii) Each import of gold bars/gold ornaments requires separate prior approval from
   Bangladesh Bank, including details of the import demand and the foreign currency to be paid.
   In this regard, holding a gold license does not automatically allow import in every case; and
   (viii) Licensed institutions must comply with the conditions mentioned in the approval letter
   issued by Bangladesh Bank for the import of gold bars/gold ornaments.
3. Import of gold bars for the purpose of exporting gold under the Gold Ornaments Export
Scheme
The instructions of paragraph 11, Part-O of FEPD-1 Circular No. 30, dated August 13, 2026 will
be applied for the import of gold bars on back-to-back basis by the exporting organizations having
bonded warehouse license under the Gold Ornaments Export Scheme.

4. License validity and renewal procedure
The license is valid for 02 (two) years from the date of issue. For renewal, the applicant must
submit an application to Director (FEPD-2), Foreign Exchange Policy Department-2, Bangladesh
Bank, Head Office, Dhaka at least 03 (three) months before expiry, along with the following
documents:

(a) For AD bank
   (i) A non-refundable pay order or bank draft of Tk. 2.00 (Two) lac issued in favor of Director
   (FEPD-2), Foreign Exchange Policy Department-2, Bangladesh Bank, Head Office, Dhaka as
   renewal fee; and
   (ii) Updated copies of all other relevant license/registrations/certificate.

(b) For other institutions
   (i) Copy of updated trade license;
   (ii) Income tax payment certificate along with the latest income certificate/income tax
   assessment order and copy of IT-10B as assessed by the Income Tax Authority;
   (iii) A non-refundable pay order or bank draft of Tk. 2.00 (Two) lac issued in favor of
   Director, FEPD-2, Bangladesh Bank, Head Office, Dhaka as renewal fee; and
   (iv) Updated copies of all other relevant licenses/registrations/certificate.
5. Authority of Bangladesh Bank to grant/reject permission and inspect
Bangladesh Bank retains the authority to conduct on-site inspections of the applicant bank/
institution prior to issuing permission. Bangladesh Bank also reserves the power to inspect the AD
bank/institution. If a licensed AD bank/institution fails to fulfill the duties assigned to it, and/or
fails to comply with instructions issued under the FER Act, 1947, Bangladesh Bank shall reserve
the absolute right to cancel or revoke the license granted to such bank/institution, without
assigning any reason whatsoever.

                                             PART-E
    AUTHORIZED IMPORTER OF GOLD ORE/DORE FOR REFINING

1. General

In accordance with the directives of paragraph 3.2A of the Gold Policy-2018 (Amended, 2021)
issued by Ministry of Commerce, Government of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh vide
Notification No. 26.00.0000.100.42.008.21-112 dated 02 June 2021 (Appendix-5), a decision has
been taken to grant permission as an authorized importer under Section 8 of the FER Act, 1947 for
the purpose of importing unrefined gold (ore) and partially refined gold (dore) in favor of gold
refinery establishments licensed by the Ministry of Commerce.

2. Eligibility criteria for obtaining permission as an authorized importer

   (a) The applicant entity must be approved by the Ministry of Commerce (MoC) as a gold
   refinery establishment or to establish such a refinery;
   (b) The applicant must be a resident sole proprietorship, a partnership concern or a registered
   limited company in Bangladesh;
   (c) The applicant shall undertake the necessary actions to establish a gold refinery at a location
   as per the conditions approved by the MoC;
   (d) Adequate arrangements for the storage of imported unrefined gold (ore) and partially
   refined gold (dore) must be available at the refinery;
   (e) The applicant shall be financially sound (in terms of capital/net worth, working capital,
   etc.) as determined by the MoC;
   (f) The applicant must have advanced and secured telecommunication system through which
   regular reports can be submitted to Bangladesh Bank;
   (g) The applicant must hold all up-to-date licenses and registrations required by the prevailing
   laws of the country; and
   (h) The applicant must be a valid member of a trade organization approved by the MoC.

3. Application for permission

To obtain permission as an authorized importer for the import of unrefined gold (ore) and partially
refined gold (dore), an application shall be submitted to Director (FEPD-2), Foreign Exchange
Policy Department-2, Bangladesh Bank, Head Office, Dhaka through an AD bank along with the
following documents (Annexure-5):
   (a) Copy of the MoC approval for establishment of the gold refinery;
   (b) Updated copies of trade license, TIN certificate, VAT registration, BIN certificate, and
   relevant trade association membership certificates;
   (c) Latest income tax certificate/assessment order issued by the tax authority and IT-10B copy
   along with tax payment certificate;
   (d) Required supporting documents as per clause 2(e) above;

   (e) Bank solvency report including detailed credit status (CIB report) of the applicant issued
   by a scheduled bank;
   (f) The resume of the applicant institution’s CEO and authorized officials involved in business
   operations along with passport size photograph;
   (g) The company's registration certificate, the MOA and AOA (for limited companies);
   (h) Detailed information regarding owners/partners/directors, including police clearance
   certificates, tax payment certificates, solvency certificates, and CIB reports (for demonstrating

   (i) Copy of the deed of ownership of the location designated for the gold refinery;
   (j) A non-refundable pay order of Tk. 30.00 (Thirty) lac in favor of Director (FEPD-2),
   Foreign Exchange Policy Department-2, Bangladesh Bank as the permission fee; and
   (k) A brief description of the applicant's experienced personnel, infrastructure, security, and
   communication systems (with appropriate documents).

4. Validity of permission

The permission shall remain valid for 05 (Five) years from the date of issuance. For renewal, the
applicant must submit an application to Director (FEPD-2), Foreign Exchange Policy Department-
2, Bangladesh Bank, Head Office, Dhaka at least 03 (three) months before expiry, along with the
following documents:

   (a) Copy of updated trade license;
   (b) Latest income certificate/assessment order issued by the tax authority and IT-10B copy
   along with tax payment certificate;
   (c) A non-refundable pay order of Tk. 5.00 (Five) lac in favor of Director (FEPD-2), Foreign
   Exchange Policy Department-2, Bangladesh Bank, Head Office, Dhaka as the renewal fee; and
   (d) Updated copies of all other licenses/registrations/certificates required under the applicable
   law in Bangladesh.

5. Authority of Bangladesh Bank to grant/reject permission and inspect:

Bangladesh Bank retains the authority to conduct an on-site inspection of the applicant gold
refinery establishment prior to issuing permission. Bangladesh Bank also reserves the power to
inspect the authorized institution and approve or reject permission of the authorized institution.

       DEALING IN FOREIGN CURRENCY NOTES AND COINS ETC.
1. Unauthorized dealings

No person, firm or company other than an AD or authorized money changer (money changer or
limited money changer authorized by Bangladesh Bank to deal in certain foreign exchange
transactions) is permitted to deal in foreign currency in any form. Other persons entering into
transactions involving the buying or otherwise acquiring or borrowing from or selling or otherwise
transferring or lending to or exchanging with, a person who is not an AD or authorized money
changer any foreign currency, will, therefore, be deemed to be contravening the provisions of the
FER Act, 1947.

2. Conditions under which ADs/Authorized money changers may purchase foreign currency

ADs and money changers may freely buy foreign currency from incoming passengers regardless
of nationality and regardless of whether or not a declaration on form FMJ (Appendix-6) is
produced at the time of encashment. If this form is produced, the amount encashed should be
endorsed on it. ADs/Money changers shall also issue encashment certificate against purchase of
foreign exchange from incoming passengers (Appendix-1).

ADs may also purchase foreign currency notes, coins and other travel instruments freely from
authorized money changers without the production of Form FMJ.

3. Disposal of foreign currency notes and coins by ADs

(a) ADs are permitted to dispose of foreign currency notes etc. by way of sales to other ADs and
the general public in accordance with the instructions of Bangladesh Bank.

(b) They may also export foreign currency notes and coins to agents or correspondents abroad for
credit to their foreign currency accounts with the prior approval of Bangladesh Bank and office of
the Chief Controller of Imports & Exports (CCI&E). Applications in duplicate with copy of duly
attested agreement should be made to FEOD-1, Bangladesh Bank, Head Office; approval will be
given in the duplicate copy. For reasons of security, strict confidentiality should be maintained in
respect of communication with Bangladesh Bank; this may preferably be done through personal
calls on the senior officials of the department.

4. ADs requirement of foreign currency notes

In addition to normal purchases from the public, authorized money changers and other ADs, an
AD may supplement its holdings of foreign currency notes from abroad with approval from
Bangladesh Bank.




                                                14
5. Responsibility for the realization of the value of foreign currency notes purchased by ADs

All purchases of foreign currency notes and coins made by ADs and authorized money changers
are entirely on their own responsibility and they must make their own arrangements for realizing
the proceeds of surplus foreign currency notes and coins purchased by them which cannot be sold
to their customers as permitted under the regulations. ADs may realize the value of surplus foreign
currency notes and coins by repatriating them through their foreign branches or correspondents
and having the proceeds credited to their appropriate foreign currency accounts.

6. Release of foreign exchange in the form of USD notes to outgoing persons

The following instructions shall be applicable for releasing foreign exchange in the form of
foreign currency notes against different entitlements or balances held in different foreign currency
accounts as stated in different circulars issued by Bangladesh Bank:

(a) Foreign exchange in the form of USD notes & coins not exceeding USD 5,000 (Five thousand)
may be issued in favor of outgoing Bangladesh nationals against their respective entitlements such
as private travel quota/business travel quota/travel for treatment abroad/study abroad etc. or any
other general or special permission per person per trip; the remainder amount of entitlement or the
entire entitlement may be released in the form of notes/coins of other freely convertible foreign
currencies. To mention, the entitlements may be utilized also through international cards
(credit/debit/pre-paid as applicable) as mentioned in FEPD-1 Circular No.40, dated September 30,
2026.

(b) The limit mentioned in '(a)' shall also be applicable to outgoing Bangladesh/foreign nationals
for issuing USD notes/coins against balances held in their respective foreign currency accounts
(Private Foreign Currency Accounts, Resident Foreign Currency Deposit Accounts, Exporters'
Retention Quota Accounts etc.) or from the balances of Convertible Taka accounts (or against
equal amount of foreign exchange on a back-to-back basis) per person per trip; the remainder
amount or the entire amount may be released in the form of notes/coins of other freely convertible
foreign currencies from the balances of the respective foreign currency accounts in a trip. Other
relevant regulations regarding issuance of foreign exchange against each entitlement shall have to
be followed meticulously as stated in different circulars issued by Bangladesh Bank.

1. An incoming/outgoing passenger may bring in/take out upto Taka 10,000 (Ten thousand) in
Bangladesh currency at the time of arrival into/departure from Bangladesh.
১। ব াংলাদেশে আগমন/ব াংলাদেশ হইতে বহির্ গমনকালে ক োন ব্যক্তি ব াংলাদেশী মুদ্রা অনধিক ১০,০০০ (দশ হাজার)
টাকা আনিতে/লইয়া যাইতে পারিবেন।

2. Any amount of foreign currency may be brought in by an incoming passenger with declaration to
the customs authorities in Form FMJ. No declaration, however, is necessary for amounts brought in
upto USD 10,000 (Ten thousand) or equivalent. For a foreigner, the entire amount brought in with
declaration or upto USD 10,000 (Ten thousand) brought in without declaration may be taken out freely
at the time of departure. Upto USD 10,000 (Ten thousand) brought in without declaration by a
Bangladeshi may also be retained and taken out freely while proceeding abroad. Amount in excess of
USD 10,000 (Ten thousand) brought in by the resident Bangladeshis, should, however, be encashed or
deposited in appropriate foreign currency account within 30 (thirty) days of arrival. Such amounts
brought in by non-resident Bangladeshis can be encashed or deposited in foreign currency account any
time after return to Bangladesh.
২। শুল্ক কর্ত ৃপক্ষের নিকট এফএমজে ফরমে ঘ োষণা প্রদান করিয়া যে ক োন পরিমাণ বৈদেশিক মুদ্রা ব াংলাদেশে
আগমনকালে সঙ্গে আনা যাইতে পারে। তবে আনীত বৈদেশিক মুদ্রার পরিমাণ অনধিক ম াঃডঃ ১০,০০০ (দশ হাজার)
বা সমতু ল্য হইলে শুল্ক কর্ত ৃপক্ষের নিকট ঘ োষণা প্রদান আবশ্যক হইবে না। বিদেশীগণের ক্ষেত্রে ঘ োষণা প্রদান
করিয়া আনীত সমুদয় বৈদেশিক মুদ্রা এবং ঘ োষণা প্রদান ব্যতিরেকে আনীত অনধিক ম াঃডঃ ১০,০০০ (দশ হাজার)
ব াংলাদেশ ত্যাগকালে অবাধে প্রত্যাবাসনয োগ্য হইবে। ঘ োষণা প্রদান ব্যতিরেকে আনীত অনধিক ম াঃডঃ ১০,০০০
(দশ হাজার) ব াংলাদেশী নাগরিকগণের ক্ষেত্রেও সঙ্গে রক্ষণয োগ্য এবং ব াংলাদেশ হইতে বর্হিগমনকালে সঙ্গে
বহনয োগ্য হইবে; এতদ্ব্যতিরিক্ত যে ক োন অংক নিবাসী ব াংলাদেশীদের ক্ষেত্রে ব াংলাদেশে আগমনের ৩০ (ত্রিশ)
দিনের মধ্যে টাকায় ভাঙ্গাইতে/প্রয োজ্য ধরণের বৈদেশিক মুদ্রা হিসাবে জমা করিতে হইবে। অনিবাসী ব াংলাদেশী
কর্ত ৃক আনীত এইরূপ অংক ব াংলাদেশে ফেরত আসিবার পর যে ক োন সময় টাকায় ভাঙ্গাইতে পারিবেন অথবা
বৈদেশিক মুদ্রা হিসাবে জমা করিতে পারিবেন।
