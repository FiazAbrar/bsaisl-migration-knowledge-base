The exchange rate margin is the cost resulting from the fact that the firm applies a rate that is different from the market reference rate. The margin is the percentage difference between the market reference rate and the exchange rate actually applied to the transaction.

[Total cost (%)](/corridor/Qatar/Bangladesh?order=field_cc1_total_cost_percent_cal&sort=desc#tab-1)

The total cost of sending a remittance transfer includes: the fee charged to the sender plus the exchange rate margin.
