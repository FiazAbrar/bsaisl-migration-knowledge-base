# Illegal routes: open questions

Questions this doc can't yet answer. Each is a "Need to know" in `doc.md`.

- What are the Bangladesh Embassy in Tripoli's working phone, WhatsApp and email for families of people held in Libya? Its website's contact page is empty.
- Does IOM Bangladesh take requests from families directly to trace a person held in Libya, and on what number?
- What is the street price in 2026 of a Serbia, Croatia or Romania "work permit" sold in Dhaka or Sylhet, and how is it paid?
- What is the exact list of 12 countries in the Ministry's 4 November 2026 letter?
- What does a Dubai or Saudi tourist visa "with job" sell for in Dhaka in 2026?
- What does the Cambodia-Thailand-Malaysia land route cost in late 2026, and do any boats still leave from Teknaf or Cox's Bazar for Malaysia?
- What phone or email does the CID's THB Unit give the public for trafficking tips?
- How many people, and how many Bangladeshis, were on the boat that left Libya for Greece in March 2026, and how many Bangladeshis died? Reports give at least 18 of 38, or 20 of 22 dead or missing out of 48 aboard.
- How many Bangladeshis reached Italy by sea in 2025? Figures given are 20,164; 20,259 (BRAC, from Frontex, the EU border agency, for all of Europe); and 17,000 sent to Italy by illegal routes "in the past year" (CID information cited in a Prothom Alo editorial of 1 April 2026).
- How many Bangladeshis reached Italy by sea in January to June 2026: 4,249 (BRAC, from Frontex) or 3,691 (Prothom Alo, 5 September 2026)?
- How many Bangladeshis die crossing the Mediterranean in a typical year? Two Prothom Alo editorials give "at least 100" and "at least 500"; which is right, and from what source?
- Can a Bangladeshi on a UAE visit visa change it to an employment visa inside the UAE in 2026? Agency leaders said in June 2024 that it could be done, a January 2026 report says it has been closed to Bangladeshis for several years, and the Bangladesh Embassy said in September 2025 that no UAE visa ban had been announced.
- Which five TTCs (Technical Training Centres) did Qatar's labour minister name in May 2026 as places Qatar wants to hire skilled workers from directly?
  - How does a trainee at one of those TTCs get onto that direct channel?
- What does UNHCR's (the UN refugee agency's) own publication give for the number of people missing at sea on the Bay of Bengal route in 2023 (a UNHCR official said about 5,787 in June 2026), and how many of them were women and children?
- How many Bangladeshis came home from Cambodia in June 2026: 583 or 646?
- In BRAC's survey of people who came home from Cambodia, is the 86-87% figure for people whose passports and papers were taken, or for people carrying forged papers?
- Of the group of 30 Bangladeshi men sent to Russia in May 2026 and made to fight in the war, is the "Sohel" the families list among the dead the same man as Ali Hasan Sohel of Goalanda, Rajbari, whom Prothom Alo lists as wounded and in a Russian hospital?
- Was that group held in a hotel in Russia for three days, as the families' complaint says, or four, as Rajon Hossain, one of the men who came home, says?
- BMET's yearly figures for clearances to Russia (4,725 in 2025 and 3,105 in 2026 to 15 October) add up to more than the 7,449 clearances BMET gives for 1 January 2025 to 27 October 2026: which count is right?
- For 2024, RMMRU gives 993 Bangladeshis going to Russia and BMET gives 1,395 clearances: does one count departures and the other clearances?
- Is the Wage Earners' Welfare Board membership fee Tk 400 or Tk 300 in 2026?
  - How does a worker already abroad without BMET clearance pay it?
- What phone number does the Airport Armed Police Battalion give trafficking victims who want to file a case?
- What phone numbers do BNWLA and Justice and Care (an anti-trafficking NGO) give families of women trafficked to India?
