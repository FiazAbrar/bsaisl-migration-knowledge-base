Built from 64 Prothom Alo articles (kb4 xchunk-other-destinations, 2024 to Nov 2026), prefix GO. 52 articles used, 12 not used (reasons in the ledger gledger-other-destinations.json). Countries with their own doc (countries/ list plus lebanon, greece, canada, united-kingdom) are held in the ledger, not written here.

## Resolved conflicts

- Australian student work limit: a July 2024 report (GO0004) says it was raised from 20 to 24 hours a week; an October 2025 promotional article (5a9307ab) says 20 hours. Kept the specific, dated July 2024 statement; the 2025 article is a general feature, not cited for this. Official figure (hours per fortnight) still to be confirmed on Home Affairs: listed as Need to know.
- Australian Temporary Graduate (485) visa length: July 2024 report (GO0011) gives 4/5/6 years (bachelor/master/doctorate); a November 2025 article (GO0153) gives 2/2/3/3. The 2025 figure is newer and reflects the July 2024 change; GO0011 is no longer cited. The October 2025 article's "two to four years" is vague and not cited.
- Cyprus case (Shahriar Ahmed Emon), settled 23 Nov 2026: our source (Prothom Alo 23 June 2026, the family's account) says he last spoke to his mother on the afternoon of 11 June 2026, sent his roommate his location at 9 pm local time and was missing from then; a ransom message came from his WhatsApp at about 10 pm, and the roommate reported him to police the next morning (12 June). The Illegal routes doc had 12 June from a Prothom Alo report of 22 June built on Cyprus police and CID statements. 11 June wins: the family account is first-hand and internally consistent, the body was found on Sunday 21 June, which our source calls ten days after he left, and the 22 June report's "nine days" of search fits a police case opened on 12 June, the likely origin of its date. The Illegal routes doc now says 11 June too, citing the same 23 June article.

**Resolved conflicts**

- Australia student work limit: the July 2024 report's "24 hours a week" (GO0004) is replaced by Home Affairs' 48 hours a fortnight (OD0001).
- Australia 485 fee: AUD 2,300 (GO0154, 2025) rose to AUD 4,600 on 1 Mar 2026 and AUD 5,750 on 1 Jul 2026 (OD0018); the doc gives 5,750.
- Ireland: the 2024 guide's embassy address (C-17 Malcha Marg, GO0038) is replaced by the embassy's own page (Thapar House, 124 Janpath Road, OD0120). The guide's VFS appointment with biometrics (GO0037) and 3,518-rupee VFS charge (GO0023) describe applying from India; the embassy's table says Bangladesh residents post their documents (OD0034), pay by INR demand draft (OD0036) and give no biometrics. - Spain: Prothom Alo said no work contract was needed, only a passport and police clearance (GO0156); the official FAQ adds that applicants who never sought international protection must also show work in Spain, a family unit, or certified vulnerability (OD0062). Prothom Alo said holders could apply for permanent residence after a year (GO0347, GO0350); the FAQ says the permit lasts one year and holders must then move into ordinary permits (OD0070). Official wins in both.
- Germany: blocked account 1,027 euros a month (GO0067, 2024) is now 1,091 (OD0040); applications through the Consular Services Portal (OD0039), not VIDEX (GO0083); processing at least about 6 weeks (OD0044) replaces "a few weeks to six months" for the wait time. German student visas: Prothom Alo said the portal starts on 1 January 2027 (GO0284); the embassy page of 22 Nov 2026 says the new process is already in effect and the last old-list appointments were in December 2026 (OD0055, OD0056); the doc states the embassy's version.
- Austria: the guide's booking by email (GO0042 kept only for fingerprints and photo) is replaced by the embassy's online appointment system (OD0090); "proof of accommodation" (GO0032 context) replaced by the official rule that students need not prove accommodation but rent counts against means (OD0095); the guide's work permit "after a job offer, valid a year" (GO0046, GO0047) replaced by the AMS employment permit rule (OD0092).
- Denmark: "fewer spouses and dependants" (GO0120) replaced by SIRI's rule that no accompanying family permits are granted for applications from 1 Oct 2026 (OD0077).
- Taiwan: the guide's funds rule (sponsor's return) is now cited with TECC's own list (OD0104), and the guide's address replaced by TECC's page (OD0103).
- Thailand e-visa timing: the January 2025 report (GO0087) says the e-visa comes within about 10 days; the embassy's tourist visa page says 7 working days (OD0133, last updated Dec 2023). The doc gives the embassy's 7 working days and drops "10 working days" from the GO0087 sentence. Fee: unofficial guides' Tk 4,500 is confirmed by the embassy's own notice (OD0126).
- Portugal: GO0125 (Sept 2025) said the new law awaited the president's approval; it came into force on 23 Oct 2025 as Law 61/2025 (OD0155). The doc now says so.
- Egypt: the Al-Azhar facts first came from Prothom Alo (GO0282, GO0297, GO0299, GO0304); the Ministry of Education's own notice (OD0211, OD0215) now carries them and those news ids are no longer cited. Prothom Alo's "Al-Sharif Institute" is "Al-Sharif Institutes" in the notice.
