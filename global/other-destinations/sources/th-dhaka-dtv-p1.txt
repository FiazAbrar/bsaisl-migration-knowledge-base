THAI VISA APPLICATION FOR NON-THAI NATIONALS
Consular Section | Royal Thai Embassy, Dhaka

DESTINATION THAILAND VISA “DTV”
(Workcation, Soft Power related activities, spouse/children under 20 years old)
Multiple Entry | 5 Year
**Application for DTV requires approval from the Royal Thai Embassy
prior to making visa fee payment**

This type of visa will issue to the applicant who wishes to enter the Kingdom of Thailand
with the following purposes:

To enjoy workcation

To participate in following activities: Muay Thai courses, Thai cooking classes,
sport training, serious and continuous medical treatment, seminars, and music
festival.

. Spouse and dependent children (age under 20 years old) of DTV holder.

DTV visa allows holder to stay in Thailand for 180 days per entry. Holder may extend
the period of stay for 1 time per each entry, for a period not exceeding 180 days

(maximum stay will be 180 days + 180 days per entry). Holder may reenter Thailand as

often as required for as long as the visa remains valid.

CHECKLIST

FOR WORKCATION

An application form completely filled out and signed by the applicant;

A valid passport for travelling to Thailand with sufficient remaining validity:

the validity of 24 months is required for five-year DTV visa application;

One set of passport information page photocopy, last Thai visa page & renewal
page (if any);

Two colour passport-sized photos (3.5 x 4.5cm), taken against white background
and within the past 6 months;

Visa request letter from the applicant;

Document indicating current location e.g. house registration ;
