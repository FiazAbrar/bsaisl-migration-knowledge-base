Facilitation of Payment for e-Visa Application

The Royal Thai Embassy is pleased to introduce additional online payment
options to enhance convenience for e-Visa applicants. Starting on September 1*,
2025, applicants may choose to make payments securely through either of the
following bank platforms:

Commercial Bank of Ceylon Eastern Bank Limited (EBL)

https://www.combank.net.bd/thaievisa_| https://onboarding.ebl-bd.com/thaipayment/start

The payment window is from 09.00 to 12.00 hrs on working days of the
Embassy. Applicants must upload the Payment Information Summary (PIS)
page to the e-Visa platform for the Embassy’s verification.

Please note that (1) the amount paid must exactly match the total amount
stated on the PIS; (2) Split payments are not accepted; (3) Applications with
incorrect payment amounts will be rejected; and (4) payments will not be
refunded.

As part of ongoing efforts to maintain accuracy and consistency with current
exchange rates, the Embassy will also be updating certain service fees in BDT in
the near future. Details will be shared through official channels.

We appreciate your understanding and continued support. The Royal Thai
Embassy remains committed to providing efficient and secure services for all
genuine travelers to Thailand.
