Al- Azhar Scholarship Nomination Guidelines:

1. Al-Azhar Secondary Certificate, or its equivalent, duly authenticated by the Ministry of
Foreign Affairs of the candidate’s country, certified by the Egyptian Embassy, and stamped with
the Seal of the Emblem of the Republic.

2. A statement of the subjects studied by the candidate as part of the secondary school certificate
or its equivalent, duly authenticated and certified by the Egyptian Embassy, to be used as a
reference when making the nomination in accordance with the candidate’s preferences.

3. Original birth certificate, clearly indicating the mother’s name and nationality, duly
authenticated by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the candidate’s country and certified by the
Egyptian Embassy. If the mother’s name and nationality are not stated on the birth certificate,
any other document indicating such information may be attached, provided that it is duly
authenticated and certified by the two aforementioned authorities.

4. Six (6) recent photographs of the candidate, with the candidate’s name and nationality written
on the back of each photograph.

5. A copy of the candidate’s passport, provided that the passport is newly issued or renewed and
valid for use.

6. A Certificate of Good Conduct issued by the authority nominating the student.

7. A medical certificate, approved by the Ministry of Health of the candidate’s country,
confirming that the candidate is medically fit and free from infectious diseases (including
HIV/AIDS, hepatitis, tuberculosis, etc.), and is physically and mentally fit (with no disabilities).
The certificate must be authenticated by the Egyptian Embassy in order for the nomination
procedures to be completed.

8. Data Form.

9. Declaration/Undertaking Form for Al-Azhar Islamic Missions City (Madinat al-Bu‘uth al-
Islamiyya).

10. An official statement confirming that the qualification obtained by the student nominated for
an Al-Azhar scholarship qualifies him/her for admission to a university in his/her home country.
The statement must be authenticated by both the Egyptian Embassy and the Ministry of Foreign
Affairs, in order to determine whether the student is eligible for university admission and to
enable the necessary procedures to be completed.
