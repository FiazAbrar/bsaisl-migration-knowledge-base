                        Special Notice to the Short Term Visitors to Sri Lanka

•   With effect from 25.05.2026, nationals of the following 40 countries are eligible to obtain
    tourist visa (ETA) free-of-charge for a period of 30 days.

      1   AUSTRALIA          11   FRANCE             21   MALAYSIA         31   SAUDI ARABIA
      2   AUSTRIA            12   GERMANY            22   NEPAL            32   SOUTH KOREA
      3   BAHARAIN           13   INDIA              23   NETHERLAND       33   SPAIN
      4   BELARUS            14   INDONESIA          24   NEW ZEALAND      34   SWEDEN
      5   BELGIUM            15   IRAN               25   NORWAY           35   SWISTZERLAND
      6   CANADA             16   ISRAEL             26   OMAN             36   THAILAND
      7   CHINA              17   ITALY              27   PAKISTAN         37   TURKEY
      8   CZECH REPUBLIC     18   JAPAN              28   POLAND           38   UAE
      9   DENMARK            19   KAZAKAZTHAN        29   QATAR            39   UNITED KINGDOM
     10   FINLAND            20   KUWAIT             30   RUSSIA           40   UNITED SATATES OF AMERICA


•   Nationals of the above-mentioned countries holding Diplomatic, Official, Service, or Ordinary
    passports are eligible to obtain tourist visa (ETA)free-of-charge under this scheme.
•   Please note that all foreign nationals, including nationals of the above-mentioned 40 countries
    and nationals with the bilateral reciprocal agreements with Sri Lanka ( i.e. Maldives, Seychelles and
    Singapore) are required to obtain an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) prior to their arrival in
    Sri Lanka. As per the bilateral reciprocal agreement between Sri Lanka and Maldives, Maldivian
    nationals will receive 90 days tourist visa through ETA system.
•   The Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) for nationals of the above-mentioned 40 countries and for
    nationals of Maldives, Seychelles and Singapore will be processed free of charge. How ever, ETA
    fee paid by the applicants prior to 25.05.2026 are non-refundable
•   Under this scheme, tourists are entitled to a tourist visa (ETA) free of charge valid for 30 days
    (Maldivian nationals will receive 90 days), with double-entry facility permitted from the date of first
    arrival in Sri Lanka within the 30-day validity period.
•   Applicants who have obtained a free visa and wish to remain in Sri Lanka beyond 30 days may apply
    for a visa extension upon payment of the applicable visa fee.
•   Please note that except the national eligible to apply ETA free of Charge mentioned above, nationals
    of all other countries shall continue to be subject to the general rules and regulations governing the Sri
    Lanka ETA system.
