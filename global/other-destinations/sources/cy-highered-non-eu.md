# Applications from candidate students outside the European Union (EU)

Students from non-EU countries need to obtain a visa/entry permit to enter Cyprus from the competent authority of the Civil Registry and Migration Department.

Additionally, it is required that students submit an application to the [Civil Registry and Migration Department](http://www.moi.gov.cy/moi/crmd/crmd.nsf/index_en/index_en?OpenDocument) for a residence permit within 10 days of their arrival in Cyprus.

For further information regarding the procedures for obtaining a visa/entry permit to enter Cyprus, prospective students are advised to contact the Diplomatic Missions of the Republic of Cyprus in their countries of origin.
