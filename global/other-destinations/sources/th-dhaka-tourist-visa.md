## TOURIST VISA (TR)

วันที่นำเข้าข้อมูล 7 Dec 2022

วันที่ปรับปรุงข้อมูล 7 Dec 2023

\| 151,283 view

\*\*\* For first-time applicants, submission of both previous (if any) and current passports is necessary \*\*\*  

 

**Note:** Thai tourist visa takes 7 working days to process from the day the application is submitted.\
Applicants are advised to submit their application form and relevant documents at least two weeks before their planned date for the trip.
