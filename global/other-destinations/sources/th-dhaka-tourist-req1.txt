THAI VISA APPLICATION
Consular Section | Royal Thai Embassy, Dhaka

TOURIST VISA (TR)
This type of visa is issued to applicants who wish to enter the Kingdom for
tourism or medical treatment purposes.

CHECKLIST
(Single Entry)

| An application form completely filled out and signed by the applicant (signature
must match with the signature in the current passport);

0 A valid passport for travelling to Thailand with sufficient remaining validity (not
less than six months);

0 Passport must have at least 2 blank pages to accommodate immigration stamps;

OU A photocopy of the last Thai visa page (if any);

O 1 colour passport-sized photos (3.5 x 4.5cm), taken against a white background
and within the past 6 months;

0 Evidence of adequate finance e.g. current bank solvency and bank statement of the
last 6 months with satisfactory transaction (at least 20,000 Baht per person and
40,000 Baht per family or 60,000 Taka per person and 120,000 Taka per family).
Only original bank statement and bank solvency letter are acceptable. In case the
applicant is sponsored by his/her company, the company’s trade license, the
company’s bank statement and bank solvency letter must be submitted;

0 Evidence shows applicant will leave Thailand after the trip, such as round-trip air
ticket or travelling to a third country;

(1 Visa request letter from the applicant;

o No Objection Certificate (NOC) from employer if employed;

o BMDC certificate or Letter from hospital if the applicant is a Doctor

o Bar Council Certificate or letter from a law firm if the applicant is a lawyer

o Recommendation letter from the school / university or student card / evidence
of school / university enrolment;

(| Trade license in Bengali and a photocopy of notarized English translation;

O Salary Bank Statement (preferably) / Pay Slip / Salary Certificate are required for
employees. In case applicant is unable to submit salary certificate, details must be
mentioned in the Office NOC;

Copy of Marriage Certificate (Nikah nama) and a photocopy of notarized English
translation;

O Only for those who wishes to enter Thailand for a medical treatment purpose:

o Copy of appointment letter from hospital in Thailand
o Sponsorship letter along with sponsor’s bank statement and bank solvency
letter for attendants / house maids.
