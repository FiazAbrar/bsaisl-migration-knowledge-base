    Latest information on travel to Austria

    We ask for your understanding that due to high demand there may be a longer waiting time for appointments to submit visa applications. We therefore recommend booking an appointment as early as possible (6 months before the planned travel at the earliest) via the appointment system (VFS Global). If there are currently no appointments available, please try again later, as new appointment slots are regularly released.

    Information on how to apply can be found here.

    Service for citizens

    Has your passport been stolen? Do you live in India, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Maldives, Nepal or Sri Lanka (jurisdiction of the Embassy) and would like to participate in an Austrian election? Would you like to know about the security situation in our jurisdiction? Our online guide "Service for Citizens" will provide you with comprehensive advice!

    New edition of the “Researcher’s Guide to Austria”

    The EURAXESS Austria 'Researcher's Guide to Austria' was edited and published by the OeAD Austrian Agency for International Cooperation in Education and Research, and is intended to support researchers, scientists and their families to get a good start in Austria. To the guide

    ReFocus Austria

    ReFocus Austria is the country’s largest global outreach programme to support Austrian Businesses abroad and a key component of the Austrian federal government‘s economic comeback plan during and after the Covid-19 pandemic.
