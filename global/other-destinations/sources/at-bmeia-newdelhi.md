### 

Latest information on travel to Austria

We ask for your understanding that due to high demand there may be a longer waiting time for appointments to submit visa applications. We therefore recommend booking an appointment as early as possible (6 months before the planned travel at the earliest) via the appointment system ([VFS Global](https://visa.vfsglobal.com/ind/en/aut/)). If there are currently no appointments available, please try again later, as new appointment slots are regularly released.

Information on how to apply can be found [here](https://www.bmeia.gv.at/en/austrian-embassy-new-delhi/travels-to-austria/entry-and-residence/how-to-apply).
