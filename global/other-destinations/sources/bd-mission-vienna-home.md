Ambassador and Permanent Representative

[](/pages/static-pages/695266b335ce18e1c05aadb8)

H.E. Mr. Toufique Hasan

Welcome to the official website of the Embassy and Permanent Mission of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh in Vienna, Austria. Whether you are a citizen of Bangladesh residing in our accredited region, a foreign friend seeking to learn more about our country, or a prospective traveler or investor, this platform is designed to serve as your gateway to Bangladesh. As the Ambassador of Bangladesh to Austria—and concurrently accredited to neighboring countries including Hungary, Slovenia, and Slovakia—my foremost priority is to further strengthen our vibrant bilateral ties. We are actively working to expand cooperation in areas of trade, investment, education, renewable energy, and cultural exchange. In addition, as the Permanent Mission of Bangladesh to the United Nations Office and other International Organizations in Vienna (including the IAEA, UNOV, UNIDO and CTBTO), we are committed to amplifying Bangladesh’s voice on the global stage in the areas of peaceful uses of nuclear technology, inclusive industrial development, and global security.
